# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 21

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 21. Additional voluntary contributions and shared cost additional voluntary contributions (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-1a999711d2aa2771036040e910d39c49][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An active member may elect to pay additional voluntary contributions (“AVCs”) into a scheme established under contract between the Committee and a body approved for the purposes of the Finance Act 2004 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^f00018] (“an additional voluntary contributions arrangement”). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) The additional voluntary contributions arrangement must be a money purchase pension scheme registered in accordance with the Finance Act 2004 and administered in accordance with that Act, the Pensions Act 2004 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^f00019] and the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 2005 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^f00020].

(3) Where the member’s employing authority, at its discretion, contributes to the arrangement, the additional voluntary contributions arrangement is known as a shared cost additional voluntary contributions arrangement and contributions to it as “SCAVCs”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(4) Such AVCs and SCAVCs are in addition to any other contributions the member may pay under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 19 (payment of additional regular contributions). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(5) Where a member elects to pay AVCs or SCAVCs, he must first— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) notify his employing authority in writing; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) in the notification specify— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (i) the percentage of his pensionable pay he wishes to pay or the amount he wishes to pay on his usual pay days from his pay; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] whether he wishes any of his AVCs or SCAVCs to be used to provide benefits payable on his death (“death benefits”); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] if he does, the proportion so to be used. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(6) Subject to paragraph (7), a member may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) vary— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (i) the amount of his AVCs or SCAVCs; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] the proportion of them to be used to provide death benefits; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) stop paying AVCs or SCAVCs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(7) Where he wishes to take the steps in paragraph (6), he must first notify his employing authority in writing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(8) An active member may transfer, by notifying the Committee in writing within 12 months of joining, or such longer period as the Committee may allow, into his additional voluntary contributions scheme constituted under this regulation the accumulated value of any other additional voluntary contributions scheme to which he has subscribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(9) An election to pay AVCs or SCAVCs may be made in respect of each employment in respect of which a person is a member. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(10) The maximum a person may specify under paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (5)(b)(i) or notify under paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (7) as the varied amount in respect of each employment for which he makes such an election is 50% of the pensionable pay of that employment or an amount equal to 50% of the pensionable pay of that employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
