# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 15

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 15. Contributions during reserve forces service leave (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8b135bff1d3bbffb1b12260681df8d0c][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2015-04-01).**

(1) This regulation applies to a person who— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) is a member or who has applied to be a member; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) goes on reserve forces service leave. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) He must pay contributions under regulation 3 (contributions payable by active members) of the Benefits Regulations and any payments under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 19 (payment of additional regular contributions) of these Regulations or regulation 57 (payments to increase total membership) of the 2002 Regulations that he was paying immediately before his relevant reserve forces service began if (and only if) his reserve forces pay during that service equals or exceeds the pensionable pay he would have received if he had continued to be employed in his former employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) Those contributions continue to be payable to the fund at the same rates on that pensionable pay. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(4) If he is not obliged to pay contributions under paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (2) he must be treated as if he had paid them and also any payments under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 19 or 20A (payment of additional contributions: survivor benefits (ASBCs)) of these Regulations[^c-76925f257f2c8c4c9967c666f8cdd777] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] of these Regulations or regulation 57 of the 2002 Regulations that he would have been liable to pay if he had continued to be employed in his former employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(5) If he was paying any contributions under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 21(1) (additional voluntary contributions and shared cost additional voluntary contributions) immediately before his leave began— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) he may continue to pay, or stop paying, them; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) unless he has stopped paying them, the Committee must throughout the period of his relevant reserve forces service, continue to pay such contributions which were to be used to provide benefits for him on his death. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(6) His relevant reserve forces service counts as a period of membership in his former employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(7) If during that service, he— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) dies; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) attains his normal retirement age; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (c) becomes incapable for health reasons of working efficiently in local government employment, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

he shall be treated as if he were in that employment at that time. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
