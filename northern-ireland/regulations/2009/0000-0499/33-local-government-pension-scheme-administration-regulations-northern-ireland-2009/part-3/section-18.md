# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 18

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 18. Applications to make absence contributions (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-d62e9fe35c51ebf66af8665f240e53fd][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) To make contributions under regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 14(5) (contributions during child-related leave), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 16 (contributions during trade dispute absence) or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 17(6)(a) (contributions during absences with permission), a person must apply to his employing authority in writing before the expiry of a period of 30 days beginning with the day— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) on which he returns to work, if he returns to work following the absence; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) on which he ceases to be employed, if he ceases to be employed by that employing authority without returning to work. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) In either case, such longer period as the Committee may allow. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) A person’s personal representatives may make an application under paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (1) if he has died without making an application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
