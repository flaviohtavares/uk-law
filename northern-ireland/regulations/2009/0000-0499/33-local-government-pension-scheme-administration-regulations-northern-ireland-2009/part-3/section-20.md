# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 20

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 20. Discontinuance of ARCs (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-7f45444c245b04df603aaae0cb97a3da][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A member— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) may stop paying his ARCs before the end of the ARC payment period if he notifies the Committee and his employing authority in writing that he wishes to do so; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) must stop doing so if he ceases to be an active member. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) If a member stops paying his ARCs before the end of the ARC payment period— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) on leaving his employment on the grounds of ill-health and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] the Committee[^c-1eb2e7ec9c28994d24949328eaffd560] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] makes a determination in respect of him under regulation 20(2) or (3) (early leavers: ill-health) of the Benefits Regulations; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) on his death, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

he is treated as having paid his ARCs up to the end of that period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) If a member stops paying his ARCs and paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (2) does not apply to him, he must be credited with additional pension of an amount determined by the Government Actuary, having regard to the ARCs he paid before he stopped. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
