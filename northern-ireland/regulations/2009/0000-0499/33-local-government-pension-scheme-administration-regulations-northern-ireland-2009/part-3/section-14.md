# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 14

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 14. Contributions during child-related leave (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-2ddfbe3c83e1140e9d9ef725aa33a4cd][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2015-04-01).**

(1) If a person who is a member, or has applied to be a member, goes on maternity, paternity or adoption leave, the person must make contributions as respects any part of his period of maternity, paternity or adoption absence for which the person is a member and entitled to receive pay (including statutory pay). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) But that pay does not include any amount that reduces the member’s actual pay on account of possible entitlement to statutory pay. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) Such contributions must be made at the contribution rate on that pay. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(4) If a person who is a member or has applied to be a member— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) goes on ordinary maternity leave, paternity leave or ordinary adoption leave; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) is not entitled to receive pay (including statutory pay) for all or any part of that period of leave, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

the person shall be treated as if the person had paid contributions under paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (1) for the unpaid period of that leave and on the pay that the person would have received during that period but for the absence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(5) If a person who is a member or has applied to be a member— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a)[^c-a1b63c73c2361432b0965d99b0615757] is on maternity, paternity or adoption leave (other than ordinary maternity, paternity or adoption leave); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) for all or part of that period *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] of maternity, paternity[^c-0b1216fe5bec0c94a7e7221004b57668] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] or adoption absence is not entitled to receive pay (including statutory pay) but is a member, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

the person may make contributions at the contribution rate as respects the unpaid period of that absence as if the person’s pay in the employment were equal to the adjusted pay. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(6) The adjusted pay shall be the pay the person was entitled to receive immediately before the unpaid period began (including statutory pay) but— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) not including any amount that reduces his actual pay on account of the person’s possible entitlement to statutory pay; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) disregarding any amount he receives on account of a day’s work carried out under regulation 12A of the Maternity and Parental Leave etc. Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^f00011] or regulation 21A of the Paternity and Adoption Leave Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^f00012].

(7) A member to whom paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (5) applies may continue to pay contributions under regulation 21 (additional voluntary contributions and shared cost additional voluntary contributions) which he was paying immediately before the leave began. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(8) If an active member goes on maternity, paternity or adoption leave, the member must continue to make any payments that member was making under regulation 19 (payment of additional regular contributions) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] or 20A (payment of additional contributions: survivor benefits (ASBCs)) of these Regulations[^c-4fe664acf86a8ad68f5672e3a5e00622] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] or regulation 57 (payments to increase total membership) of the 2002 Regulations on the pay the member would have received during the period but for the leave. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(9) In this regulation— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- “additional paternity leave” means leave under the Additional Paternity Leave Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010;[^c-ccaaeb89e811167744020a5126f0982a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- “ordinary adoption leave” means leave under Article 107A of the Employment Rights (Northern Ireland) Order 1996 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^f00013];
- “ordinary maternity leave” means leave under Article 103 of that Order *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^f00014];
- “paternity leave” means leave under regulation 4 or 8 of the Paternity and Adoption Leave Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- “period of maternity, paternity or adoption absence” means any period throughout which a member is absent from duty because he is exercising his right to take— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (a) ordinary maternity or adoption leave; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (b) additional maternity or adoption leave under Article 105 or 107B of the Employment Rights (Northern Ireland) Order 1996 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^f00015]; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (c) paternity leave; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] or[^c-e9b820ab21446ffdf7d3b87537098466] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (d) additional paternity leave; and[^c-d16a2a93a71353f38678209948d469cf] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- “statutory pay” means any statutory maternity, paternity or adoption pay payable under the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^f00016].
