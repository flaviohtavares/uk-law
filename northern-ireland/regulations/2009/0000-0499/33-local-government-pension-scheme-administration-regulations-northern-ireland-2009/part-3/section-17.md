# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 17

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 17. Contributions during absences with permission (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-b2e3afcfb3cebead37a018c03cc26a51][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2015-04-01).**

(1) If a member— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) is away from his employment with permission (otherwise than because of illness or injury)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (i) for a continuous period of less than 31 days; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] on jury service for any period; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) is receiving reduced pay or no pay, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

he must make the payments specified in paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (2) on the pensionable pay he would have received during the period but for his absence (“his deemed pay”). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) The payments are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) contributions at the contribution rate; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) any payments he was making under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 19 (payment of additional regular contributions) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] or 20A (payment of additional contributions: survivor benefits (ASBCs))[^key-c535011e13b59d6dadb70b0ff22bd70b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] of these Regulations or regulation 57 (payments to increase total membership) of the 2002 Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) The member may continue to pay contributions under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 21 (additional voluntary contributions and shared cost additional voluntary contributions) which he was paying immediately before the absence began. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(4) If a member— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) is away from his employment with permission (otherwise than because of illness or injury) for a continuous period of more than 30 days; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) is receiving reduced pay or no pay, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

he must make payments specified in paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (5) on his deemed pay. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(5) The payments are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) contributions at the contribution rate for the first 30 days’ absence; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) any payments he was making under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 19 or 20A[^key-703d578a3df9ab02d572042ea3ab8a34] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] of these Regulations or regulation 57 of the 2002 Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(6) The member may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) make contributions at the contribution rate on his deemed pay for the remainder of the period of absence subject to a maximum of 36 months; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) continue to make any payments he was making under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 21 which he was paying immediately before his absence began. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
