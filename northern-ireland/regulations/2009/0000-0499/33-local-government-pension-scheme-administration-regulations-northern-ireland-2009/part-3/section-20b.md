# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 20B

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 20B. Discontinuance of ASBCs (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-1874f520b96d8d893982a70b106568d5][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A member— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) may stop paying ASBCs before the end of the ASB payment period if the member notifies the Committee and the employing authority (if different) in writing; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) must stop doing so if he ceases to be an active member. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) If a member stops paying ASBCs before the end of the ASB payment period— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) on leaving their employment on grounds of ill-health where the Committee makes a determination under regulation 20(2) or (3) of the Benefits Regulations; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) on the member’s death, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

the member is to be treated as having paid ASBCs up to the end of that period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) If a member stops paying ASBCs and paragraph (2) does not apply, the member must be credited with additional survivor benefits of an amount determined by the Government Actuary, having regard to the ASBCs that were paid before the member stopped paying them. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
