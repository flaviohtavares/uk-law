# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 22

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 22. Use of accumulated value of AVCs and SCAVCs (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-44ea9cfe5ceaaf331964e564ed84fb58][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2015-04-01).**

(1) This regulation applies where a person who has paid AVCs or SCAVCs during his employment or made a transfer under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 21(8) (additional voluntary contributions)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) leaves his employment with the employing authority notified under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 21(5)(a)—
  - (i) without entitlement to immediate payment of retirement benefits; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] with such entitlement under regulation 16 (normal retirement), 17 (retirement after the normal retirement age), 18 (flexible retirement), 19 (early leavers: inefficiency and redundancy), 30 (choice of early payment of pension) or 31 (early payment of pension: ill-health) of the Benefits Regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) stops being an active member without leaving that employment; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (c) becomes entitled to ill-health benefits under regulation 20 (early leavers: ill- health) of the Benefits Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) A person mentioned in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] paragraph (1)(a)(i) or (b) must notify the Committee[^c-f3bf6f0d207cbf75e31197ef51168c18] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] in writing that he wishes the accumulated value of the AVCs or SCAVCs (“the accumulated value”) to be used in one or more of the permissible ways specified in the notification. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) The permissible ways are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) to subscribe to a registered pension scheme (other than the Scheme); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) to purchase an appropriate policy from one or more insurance companies (within the meaning of section 275 of the Finance Act 2004). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(4) A person mentioned in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] paragraph (1)(a)(i), (1)(a)(ii) or (1)(c)[^c-a9304c3002a984fa519f6da1688c0686] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] may notify his employing authority in writing that he wishes the accumulated value to be used to provide additional pension for him under the Scheme, or partly to provide such pension for him. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(5) If he does so, he becomes entitled to such additional pension as is shown as appropriate in guidance issued by the Government Actuary. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(6) The employing authority must send notification under paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (2) or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (4) to the Committee as soon as possible. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(7)[^c-4e42042e43d611eee8b0efe129ca3f6a] In the case of a person mentioned in paragraph (1)(b) the employing authority must, as soon as possible, inform the Committee that the person has stopped being an active member. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(8) The Committee must make such arrangements as are necessary for the use of the accumulated value in accordance with a notification under paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (2) or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (4) [^c-9455e977f52b797a376202738d366f42]....

(9)[^c-5e31b4c43c7082a355a512400b84fcb1] In the event that a member dies before the policy is entered into, the accumulated value is payable to his personal representatives. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
