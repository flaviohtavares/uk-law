**6.**[^c-4f23eaeba9ad1928839036669a915e5c] An undertaking from the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] [^c-38efe37c97a283d23a70c8bb17005923]... admission body that it will promptly notify the Committee in writing of any material change in the terms and conditions of employment which affect entitlement to benefits under the Scheme for its employees who are members and of any terminations of employment by virtue of redundancy or in the interests of efficiency. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

**No longer in force (ended 2015-04-01).**

**No longer in force (ended 2015-04-01).**

**8.**[^c-1e8642e13a5f4e247b9fa8fc93c3f4ae] A requirement that a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] [^c-38efe37c97a283d23a70c8bb17005923]... admission body— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) notifies the Committee of any matter which may affect, or is likely to affect, its participation in the Scheme; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) gives immediate notice to the Committee of any actual or proposed change in its status which may give rise to a termination, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

and, for these purposes, a termination includes a take-over, reconstruction or amalgamation, liquidation or receivership and a change in the nature of the body’s business or constitution. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

**No longer in force (ended 2015-04-01).**

**10.**[^c-441dc8eab6b92df41a238bed9a06ee53] A right for the Committee to terminate the agreement in the event of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) the insolvency, winding up or liquidation of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] [^c-38efe37c97a283d23a70c8bb17005923]... admission body; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) a breach by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] [^c-38efe37c97a283d23a70c8bb17005923]... admission body of any of its obligations under the admission agreement (but where the breach is capable of remedy only where it has not been remedied within a reasonable time); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (c) a failure by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] [^c-38efe37c97a283d23a70c8bb17005923]... admission body to pay any sums due to the fund within a reasonable period after receipt of a notice from the Committee requiring it to do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

**11.**[^c-8a0dbe3570ff29d955d3b6306b2a6817] A requirement that the admission agreement in its final form shall be available for public inspection at the appropriate offices of the Committee. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

**No longer in force (ended 2015-04-01).**

**12.**[^c-e54d7020c158c896a022c1ad3ea73f88] In relation to a transferee admission body under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 5(2)(a)—

- (c) a provision requiring the employing authority to keep under assessment the level of risk arising as a result of the matters set out in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] regulation 6(3)[^c-6e9a754be9ae352f2e48caa4cae1c2ae] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc];
- (d) provision that where a representation or notification must be given to the Committee under paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 5, 6, 7 or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 8, it must also be given to the employing authority; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (e) a requirement that the admission agreement in its final form shall be available for public inspection at the appropriate offices of the employing authority, if different from the Committee. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
