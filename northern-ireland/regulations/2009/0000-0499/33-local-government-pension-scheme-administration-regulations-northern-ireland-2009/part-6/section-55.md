# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 55

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 55. Notice of decisions by the appointed person under regulation (affected by repeal, see note) 54[^c-4e50b9a89ef423171590770c8c48e516][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A decision on the matters raised by an application under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 54 (right to apply for an appointed person to review a decision) must be issued by the appropriate appointed person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) to the applicant; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) to the Committee, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

by notice in writing before the expiry of the period of 2 months beginning with the date the application was received. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) But, if no such notice is issued before the expiry of that period, an interim reply must immediately be sent to the persons mentioned in paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (1) setting out the reasons for the delay and an expected date for issuing the decision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) A notice under paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (1) must include— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) a statement of the decision; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) reference to any legislation or provisions of the Scheme relied upon; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (c) in the case where the disagreement relates to the exercise of a discretion, a reference to the provisions of the Scheme conferring the discretion; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (d) a reference to the rights of the applicant to refer the disagreement for reconsideration by the Committee under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 56 (reference of disagreement to the Committee), specifying the time within which he may do so; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (e) a statement that the Pensions Advisory Service *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^f00033] is available to assist members and beneficiaries of the Scheme in connection with any difficulty with the Scheme which remains unresolved and the address at which it may be contacted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
