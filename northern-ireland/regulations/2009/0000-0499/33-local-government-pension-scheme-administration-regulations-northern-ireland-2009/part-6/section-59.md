## 59. Referral of decisions under regulation (affected by repeal, see note) 50(1) to the county court (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-2de9653c89279e6ed802f7cdb9e15af6][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

Where an employing authority has either decided or failed to decide such question as is mentioned in regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 50(1) (first instance decisions: general), that question shall be determined by the county court *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] [^c-47abde05e5ba6a278d07460d198892ec]... *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc].
