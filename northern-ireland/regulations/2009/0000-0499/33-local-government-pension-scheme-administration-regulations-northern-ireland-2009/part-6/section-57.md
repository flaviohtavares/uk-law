# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 57

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 57. Notice of decisions by the Committee under regulation (affected by repeal, see note) 56[^c-b0069d2e7d1198efbf801fb6fa908da9][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Committee must issue its decision on the matters raised by an application under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 56 (reference of disagreement to the Committee) to the parties to the disagreement by notice in writing before the expiry of the period of 2 months beginning with the date the application was received (but see paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (2)).

(2) If no such notice is issued before the expiry of that period, an interim reply must be sent immediately to those parties, setting out the reasons for the delay and the expected date for issuing the decision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) A notice under paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (1) must include— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) a statement of the decision; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) in a case where there has been a decision made under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 55 (notice of decisions by the appointed person under regulation 54), an explanation as to whether and, if so, to what extent that decision is confirmed or replaced; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (c) a reference to any legislation or provisions of the Scheme relied upon; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (d) in a case where the disagreement relates to the exercise of a discretion, a reference to the provisions of the Scheme conferring the discretion; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (e) a statement that the Pension Advisory Service is available to assist members and beneficiaries of the Scheme in connection with any difficulties with the Scheme which remain unresolved and of the address at which the Pension Advisory Service may be contacted; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (f) a statement that the Pensions Ombudsman may investigate and determine any complaint or dispute of fact or law in relation to the Scheme made or referred in accordance with the 1993 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^f00034] and of the address at which he may be contacted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
