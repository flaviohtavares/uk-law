# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 58

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 58. Rights of representation (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-fbaa4a7ca7f042415d55f4b282f52b18][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An application under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 54 (right to apply for an appointed person to review a decision) or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 56 (reference of disagreement to the Committee) may be made or continued on behalf of the applicant by a representative nominated by him. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) Where a person who has the right to make or has made such an application dies, the application may be made or continued on his behalf by his personal representative. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) Where such a person is a minor or is or becomes otherwise incapable of acting for himself, the application may be made or continued on his behalf by a member of his family or some other person suitable to represent him. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(4) Where a representative is nominated before an application is made, the application must specify his full name and address and whether that is to be used for service on the applicant of any documents in connection with the application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(5) Where a representative’s address is not to be so used, the appropriate appointed person or the Committee, as the case may be, must send to the applicant a copy of a decision under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 55(1) (notice of decisions by the appointed person under regulation 54) or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 57(1) (notice of decisions by the Committee under regulation 56) or an interim reply under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 55(2) or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 57(2).
