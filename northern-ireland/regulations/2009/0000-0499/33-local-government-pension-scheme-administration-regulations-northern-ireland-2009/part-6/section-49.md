# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 49

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 49. Interpretation of Part (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-659d70ecbe926de230f7801107a5e889][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

In this Part a reference to the employing authority of a prospective member is a reference to the body that would be his employer if he were to become an active member in the employment by virtue of which he would be eligible to join the Scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
