# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 54

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 54. Right to apply for an appointed person to review a decision (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-e8cdf77f4d7cd5e11435108cb0e3a7c1][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where there is a disagreement about a matter in relation to the Scheme between a member or an alternative applicant and the Committee, the member or, as the case may be, the alternative applicant may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) apply directly to the appropriate appointed person to decide a disagreement; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) apply to the Committee for it to refer the disagreement to an appointed person for decision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) These persons are alternative applicants— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) a widow, widower, surviving civil partner or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] [^key-c273305f724f98de153187efdbed9f20]... *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] cohabiting partner (as defined in regulation 25 of the Benefits Regulations) of a deceased member; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) a dependant of a deceased member or any other person to whom benefits in respect of him may be paid; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (c) a prospective member; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (d) a pension credit member; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (e) a person entitled to a pension credit; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (f) a person who ceased to be a member, or to fall within sub-paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (a), (b), (c), (d) or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (e) during the period of 6 months ending with the date of the application; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (g) in the case of a disagreement relating to the question as to whether a person claiming to be a member or to fall within sub-paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (a), (b), (c), (d), (e) or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (f) does so, the claimant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) The application for a decision must set out particulars of the disagreement, including a statement as to its nature with sufficient details to show why the applicant is aggrieved. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(4) An application by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) a member or a prospective member; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) a person who has ceased to be a member or prospective member during the period of 6 months ending with the date of the application; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (c) a person claiming to be a person within paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (a) or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (b),

must set out his full name, address, date of birth, his national insurance number (if any) and the name of his employing authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(5) An application by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) a person entitled to a pension credit or a pension credit member; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) a person who ceased to be such a person or member during the period of 6 months ending with the date of the application; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (c) a person claiming to be a person within paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (a) or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (b),

must set out his full name, address and date of birth. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(6) An application by any other person must set out— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) his full name, address and date of birth; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) his relationship to the member; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (c) the member’s full name, address, date of birth, national insurance number and the name of his employing authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(7) The application must be signed by or on behalf of the applicant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(8) The application must be accompanied by a copy of any written notification issued under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 52 (notification of first instance decisions). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(9) The application must be made before the end of the period of 6 months beginning with the relevant date or such further period as the appropriate appointed person considers reasonable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(10) Where the disagreement relates to a decision under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 51 (first instance determinations: ill-health), the relevant date is the date notification of it is given under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 52.

(11) Otherwise, the relevant date is the date of the act or omission which is the cause of the disagreement or, if there is more than one, the last of them. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
