# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 52

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 52. Notification of first instance decisions (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-e1715ba86f2c989b9d220b7a239eae40][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Every person whose rights or liabilities are affected by a decision under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 50 (first instance decisions: general) or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 51 (first instance determinations: ill-health) shall be notified of it by the Committee or the employing authority which made it as soon as is reasonably practicable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) A notification shall include— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) in the case of a decision that the person is not entitled to a benefit, the grounds for the decision; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) in the case of a decision as to the amount of a benefit, a statement showing how the amount is calculated; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (c) in the case of a notification under paragraphs *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (a) or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (b) a conspicuous statement directing the person’s attention to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (i) the address from which he may obtain further information about the decision, including details of any calculation of service or benefits; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] his right under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 54 (right to apply for an appointed person to review a decision) to make an application to an appointed person, the address at which he may be contacted and his job title; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] his right under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 56 (reference of disagreement to the Committee) to apply to the Committee for a reconsideration of the matter within the time limit referred to in that regulation; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (d) in the case of a decision by an employing authority made under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 50, a conspicuous statement directing the person’s attention to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (i) the employing authority’s address from which he may obtain further information about the decision; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] his right of appeal to the county court under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 59 (referral of decisions under regulation 50(1) to the county court). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
