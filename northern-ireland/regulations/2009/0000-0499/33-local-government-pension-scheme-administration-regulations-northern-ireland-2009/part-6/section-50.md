# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 50

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 50. First instance decisions – general (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-f6f8b972d1327a6a00c2c8dcdfa91a67][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The employing authority shall decide in relation to each of its employees who is a member— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) whether he is a whole-time, variable-time or part-time employee; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) which of his emoluments are remuneration on which contributions are payable; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (c) what rate of contribution the employee is liable to pay to the fund or admission agreement fund; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (d) if he is a part-time employee the proportion which his contractual minimum hours of employment in each week bear to those of a comparable whole-time employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) In relation to any employment in which a person is a member, the Committee is to decide— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) what previous service or employment (if any) he is entitled to count as a period of membership; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) whether any, and if so what, periods of service as a part-time employee are included in such period; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (c) what proportion of whole-time service his service during any such period represents; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (d) whether a payment in lieu of contributions has been made or equivalent pension benefits have been assured under Part III of the National Insurance Act (Northern Ireland) 1966 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^f00030] in respect of any period of non-participating employment; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (e) any question about counting additional periods as membership or crediting additional pension. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) The questions specified in paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (1) shall be decided as soon as is reasonably practicable after— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) the person becomes a member; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) any change occurs in the number of the member’s regular or casual hours of employment; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (c) any other material change occurs in or in relation to the employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(4) The questions specified in paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (2) shall be decided as soon as is reasonably practicable after the person becomes a member of the Scheme in respect of the relevant employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(5) Any question as to whether a person is entitled to a benefit under the Scheme shall be decided by the Committee. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(6) Where a person is or may become entitled to benefit payable out of the fund or an admission agreement fund, the Committee shall decide the amount of the benefit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(7) The decision under paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (5) shall be made as soon as is reasonably practicable after the cessation of the employment or, as the case may be, the death of the employee and that under paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (6) as soon as is reasonably practicable after the occurrence of the event by virtue of which the entitlement arises or may arise. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(8) In this regulation “benefit” includes a return of contributions and a benefit specified in regulation F6(11) or (14) of the Local Government (Superannuation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^f00031], Part N (pension sharing) of the Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^f00032] and Part VI (pension sharing) of the 2002 Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
