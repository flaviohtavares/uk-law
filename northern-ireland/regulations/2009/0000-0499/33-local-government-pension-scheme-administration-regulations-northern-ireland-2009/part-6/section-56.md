# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 56

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 56. Reference of disagreement to the Committee (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-318fa2a26367693ed0973beee1f8bf4f][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where an application about a disagreement has been made under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 54 (right to apply for an appointed person to review a decision), an application may be made to the Committee to reconsider the disagreement by the person who applied under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 54.

(2) The application must set out particulars of the grounds on which it is made, including a statement that the applicant under this regulation wishes the disagreement to be reconsidered by the Committee. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) An application made by the person who applied under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 54 must set out the matters required by paragraph (4), (5) or (6) as the case may be, of that regulation to be included in his application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(4) The application must be accompanied by a copy of any written notification issued under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 52 (notification of first instance decisions). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(5) Where notice of a decision on the application under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 54 has been issued, the application under this regulation must state why the applicant is dissatisfied with that decision and be accompanied by a copy of that notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(6) The application must be signed by or on behalf of the person making it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(7) An application for reconsideration may only be made before the expiry of the period of 6 months beginning with the relevant date. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(8) Where notice of a decision on the matters raised by the application under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 54 has been issued, the relevant date is the date of that notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(9) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) an interim reply has been sent out under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 55(2) (notice of decisions by the appointed person under regulation 54); but *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) no notice of decision has been issued before the expiry of the period of one month beginning with the date specified in the reply as the expected date for issuing the decision, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

the relevant date is the date with which that period expires. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(10) Where no notice of decision has been issued or interim reply has been sent before the expiry of the period of 3 months beginning with the date the application under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 54 was made, the relevant date is the date with which that period expires. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
