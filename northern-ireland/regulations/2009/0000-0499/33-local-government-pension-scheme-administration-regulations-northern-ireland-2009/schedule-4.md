# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, SCHEDULE 4

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 4 (affected by repeal, see note) — COUNCILLOR MEMBERS (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^c-33068dcfd3fb594c5e79c5d919a6fd95][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

Regulation 3A *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

### Membership (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

**1.** Regulation 12(1) (re-employed and rejoining deferred members) shall only apply to a councillor member in respect of any councillor membership where both the former membership and the membership as an active member referred to in that paragraph are councillor membership. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

**2.** Delete regulation 13 (concurrent employments). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

### Additional voluntary contributions and shared cost AVC schemes (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

**3.** Delete regulation 21(3) (additional voluntary contributions and shared cost additional voluntary contributions). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

**4.** In regulations 21 to 23 delete “and SCAVCs” and “or SCAVCs” where it occurs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

### Members’ contributions (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

**5.** In regulation 22(1)(a)(ii) (use of accumulated value of AVCs and SCAVCs) delete “18 (flexible retirement), 19 (early leavers: inefficiency and redundancy),”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

### Transfers (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

**6.** In regulation 41 (rights to return of contributions) delete paragraphs (4) and (5). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

**7.** Delete regulations 77 to 81 (bulk transfers (transfers of undertaking), etc., calculation of amount of transfer payment under regulation 77, inward transfers of pension rights, right to count a credited period and community scheme transferees). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
