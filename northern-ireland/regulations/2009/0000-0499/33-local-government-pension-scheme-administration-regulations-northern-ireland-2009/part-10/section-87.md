# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 87

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 87. Safeguarded rights (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-e5b81998935e5ed778d19b77d812b3cc][^key-de9b827c01ebc2a042a04f509abadd56][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

[^key-de9b827c01ebc2a042a04f509abadd56]

(1) A pension credit member’s safeguarded rights for the purposes of the Scheme and of the 1993 Act and regulations made under that Act are such of his rights falling with section 64A(1) of that Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^f00048] as represent the safeguarded percentage of the rights acquired by him in the Scheme by virtue of the pension credit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) The “safeguarded percentage” is the percentage of the shareable rights by reference to which the amount of the pension credit is determined which are contracted-out rights. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) “Contracted-out rights” has the meaning given in section 64A(5) of the 1993 Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
