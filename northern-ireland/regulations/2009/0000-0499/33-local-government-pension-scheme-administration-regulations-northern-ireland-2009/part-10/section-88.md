## 88. Valuation date (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-5895bcbef30d18a029db258fa1ccc3a3][^c-de9b827c01ebc2a042a04f509abadd56][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

[^c-de9b827c01ebc2a042a04f509abadd56]

For the purposes of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

- (a) calculating the cash equivalent referred to in Article 27(2) of the 1999 Order; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
- (b) regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 84(3) and (4) (discharge of liability for pension credit rights), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

the valuation date shall be the first day of the implementation period as defined in Article 31(1) of that Order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
