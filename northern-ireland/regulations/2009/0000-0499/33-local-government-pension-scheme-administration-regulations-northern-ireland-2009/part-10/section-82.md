# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 82

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 82. Pension debit member (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-14fd493d563693e705adf0def6cde264][^key-de9b827c01ebc2a042a04f509abadd56][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

[^key-de9b827c01ebc2a042a04f509abadd56]

(1) The benefits payable to a pension debit member shall be reduced to take into account the debit to which the shareable rights of the pension debit member are subject under a pension sharing order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) The amount of the reduction shall be calculated in accordance with guidance issued for this purpose by the Government Actuary. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
