# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 92

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 92. Death grants: pension credit members (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-dcf30289d8ad029694992e788a242787][^key-de9b827c01ebc2a042a04f509abadd56][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

[^key-de9b827c01ebc2a042a04f509abadd56]

(1) Regulations 23 (death grants: active members), 32 (death grants: deferred members) and 35 (death grants: pensioner members) of the Benefits Regulations shall apply in relation to a pension credit member. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) The Committee at its absolute discretion may make payments of a death grant to or for the benefit of the pension credit member’s nominee or personal representatives, or any person appearing to the Committee to have been his relative or dependant at any time. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
