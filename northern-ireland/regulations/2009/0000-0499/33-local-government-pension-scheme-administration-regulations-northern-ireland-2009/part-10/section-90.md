# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 90

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 90. Calculation (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-5e75c42177e413382c9c6d19d611206f][^c-de9b827c01ebc2a042a04f509abadd56][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

[^c-de9b827c01ebc2a042a04f509abadd56]

(1) The annual rate of the pension at normal benefit age shall be the pension calculated as referred to in regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 84(4) (discharge of liability for pension credit rights), increased in accordance with the Pensions (Increase) Act (Northern Ireland) 1971 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^f00049] and, if applicable, the Pensions Increase (Northern Ireland) Order 1974 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^f00050] from the day on which the pension sharing order takes effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) Where applicable, the lump sum grant shall be equal to 3 times the annual rate of pension. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
