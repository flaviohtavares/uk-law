# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 86

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 86. Death of a person entitled to a pension credit before discharge (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-aa5c7217317e07724af8a8375809a12c][^c-de9b827c01ebc2a042a04f509abadd56][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

[^c-de9b827c01ebc2a042a04f509abadd56]

(1) Where a person entitled to a pension credit dies before liability in respect of his pension credit has been discharged in accordance with regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 84(1) (discharge of liability for pension credit rights), such liability shall be discharged by the Committee by payment of a lump sum. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) The lump sum shall be equal to 3 times the annual rate of the pension that would have been paid to him if on the date of his death he had become entitled to a pension as a pension credit member, calculated in accordance with guidance issued by the Government Actuary. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) The Committee shall pay the lump sum to the deceased’s personal representatives. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
