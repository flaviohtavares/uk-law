# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 83

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 83. Interpretation (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-fd1e30dc374a38766eaf69be3d2ef14e][^c-de9b827c01ebc2a042a04f509abadd56][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

[^c-de9b827c01ebc2a042a04f509abadd56]

References in this Part to a pension credit member are to that person in relation to his pension credit rights and not in relation to any other rights he may have under the Scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
