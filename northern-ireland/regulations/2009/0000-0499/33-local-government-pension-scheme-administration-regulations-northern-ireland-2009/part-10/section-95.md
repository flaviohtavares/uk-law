# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 95

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 95. Transfers in (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-7352c1f7e56283bd0e51c05816103600][^key-de9b827c01ebc2a042a04f509abadd56][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

[^key-de9b827c01ebc2a042a04f509abadd56]

(1) A pension credit member is not entitled to request the Committee to accept a transfer value for relevant pension rights and the Committee must not accept a transfer value of such rights where they have accrued to a pension credit member. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) “Relevant pension rights” has the meaning given in regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] 79(2) (inward transfers of pension rights). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
