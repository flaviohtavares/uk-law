# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 91

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 91. Payment of benefits (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-edb7b621e63dd6817c34165ffe3aa832][^c-de9b827c01ebc2a042a04f509abadd56][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

[^c-de9b827c01ebc2a042a04f509abadd56]

(1) Subject to paragraph (4), a pension credit member[^c-22c0e9fb4018b3d8cdce8ee23a207916] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] who attains normal benefit age is entitled to the immediate payment of a pension and, if applicable, a lump sum grant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) The pension and if applicable the lump sum grant are payable from the fund or admission agreement fund. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(3) The pension is payable for life. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(4)[^c-38dd00892d2d541fed9d1cd25d583ae3] A pension credit member may apply in writing to the Committee for the benefits payable under paragraph (1) to be paid at or after *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] age 55[^c-470b3fbc068224f4e401c564760a16f1] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc] (but before normal benefit age), and such benefits shall be reduced by the amounts as are shown to be appropriate in guidance issued by the Government Actuary. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(5) Where paragraph (4) applies the benefits shall be payable from the date the Committee receives the application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
