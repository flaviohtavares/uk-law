# Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, section 93

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 93. Commutation: small pensions (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-e78dca424cb5a6e06ff5e2d8fa823774][^key-de9b827c01ebc2a042a04f509abadd56][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc][^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

*Extent: N.I.*

[^key-de9b827c01ebc2a042a04f509abadd56]

(1) Regulation 39 (commutation: small pensions) of the Benefits Regulations shall apply if as a result of a pension sharing order the annual rate of the retirement pension which a member who has attained normal benefit age is entitled to be paid falls below the sum mentioned in that regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]

(2) Regulation 39 of those Regulations shall apply in respect of the retirement pension payable to a pension credit member in respect of a pension credit and the amount of that pension shall for the purpose of this regulation be aggregated with any other pension payable to that member under the Scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4ff113e077177dfd69afa0039c815dc]
