# The Libraries (2008 Act) (Commencement No 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 123 (C. 7)

Libraries

The Libraries (2008 Act) (Commencement No 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2009

Made

18th March 2009

The Department of Culture, Arts and Leisure, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 12(2) of the Libraries Act (Northern Ireland) 2008 [^f00001], makes the following Order.

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Libraries (2008 Act) (Commencement No.2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2009.

(2) In this Order “the Act” means the Libraries Act (Northern Ireland) 2008.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the following provisions of the Act —

- (a) Section 1(3) and (4) (The Northern Ireland Library Authority);
- (b) Section 2 (Duty of Authority to provide library service);
- (c) Section 6 (Charges for certain library services);
- (d) Section 7 (Byelaws in respect of use of library facilities);
- (e) Section 8 (Grants for or in connection with library services);
- (f) Section 10(2);
- (g) Schedule 4 (Repeals) except in so far as it relates to Article 73(2) of the Education and Libraries (Northern Ireland) Order 1986 [^f00002]

is 1st April 2009.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Culture, Arts and Leisure on 18<sup>th</sup> March 2009

(L.S.)

Linda Wilson

A senior officer of the Department of Culture, Arts and Leisure

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 1 April 2009 for the coming into operation of Sections 1 (part), 2, 6, 7, 8 and 10 (2) and of part of Schedule 4 to the Libraries Act (Northern Ireland) 2008 being further provisions of that Act, relating to establishing the Northern Ireland Library Authority as the library authority for Northern Ireland, giving it certain powers and removing responsibility for the public library service from the five education and library boards.
