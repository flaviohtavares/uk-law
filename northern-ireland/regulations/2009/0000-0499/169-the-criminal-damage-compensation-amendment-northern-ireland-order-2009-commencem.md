# The Criminal Damage (Compensation) (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 2009 (Commencement) Order 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 169 (C. 10)

Criminal Damage

The Criminal Damage (Compensation) (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 2009 (Commencement) Order 2009

Made

18th April 2009

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 1(3) of the Criminal Damage (Compensation) (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 2009 [^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Criminal Damage (Compensation) (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 2009 (Commencement) Order 2009.

## 2. Commencement

Article 3 of the Criminal Damage (Compensation) (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 2009 shall come into operation on 19th April 2009.

Paul Goggins

Minister of State

Northern Ireland Office

18 April 2009

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into operation Article 3 of the Criminal Damage (Compensation) (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 2009 on 19th April 2009.
