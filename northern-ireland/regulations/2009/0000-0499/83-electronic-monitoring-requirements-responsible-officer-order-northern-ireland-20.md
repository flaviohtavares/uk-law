# Electronic Monitoring Requirements (Responsible Officer) Order (Northern Ireland) 2009

> Text as at 2025-07-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

2009 No. 83

Criminal Law

Electronic Monitoring Requirements (Responsible Officer) Order (Northern Ireland) 2009

Made

5th March 2009

Coming into operation

1st April 2009

To be laid before Parliament

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 40 (3) of the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2008[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement[^key-b5384582bebfad40519203e5d9a2c684]

This Order may be cited as the Electronic Monitoring Requirements (Responsible Officer) Order (Northern Ireland) 2009 and shall come into operation on 1 April 2009.

## 2. Responsible officer[^key-7330b961c2f9da2cedaa289cff1b4727]

The person responsible for the monitoring under an electronic monitoring requirement shall be an employee of G4S Security Services (UK) Limited, Sutton Park House, 15 Carshalton Road, Sutton, Surrey SM1 4LD , or an employee of Buddi Limited, Talbot House, 17 Church Street, Rickmansworth (Herts) WD3 1DE[^key-20ff1d6cfba6b09dbff3df386590b2aa] .

Paul Goggins

Minister of State

Northern Ireland Office

5 March 2009

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order specifies the person responsible for the electronic monitoring of a person’s compliance with an electronic monitoring requirement.
