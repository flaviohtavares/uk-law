# The Motor Hackney Carriages (Belfast) (Amendment) By-Laws (Northern Ireland) 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 127

Road Traffic And Vehicles

The Motor Hackney Carriages (Belfast) (Amendment) By-Laws (Northern Ireland) 2009

Made

19th March 2009

Coming into operation

1st October 2009

The Department of the Environment makes the following By-Laws in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 65(1) and (2) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These By-Laws may be cited as the Motor Hackney Carriages (Belfast) (Amendment) By-Laws (Northern Ireland) 2009 and shall come into operation on 1<sup>st</sup> October 2009.

## 2. By-Laws amended

In the Schedule to the By-Laws made in respect of Motor Hackney Carriages standing or plying for hire made by the Council of the County Borough of Belfast on 4<sup>th</sup> June 1951[^f00002] for the entry for Glengall Street substitute—

> |  |  |  |  |
> | --- | --- | --- | --- |
> | GLENGALL<br>STREET | North Side: from a point 31 metres east of the east kerbline of Durham Street for a distance of 14 metres. Carriages shall stand in a single line by the kerbline and parallel therewith, facing east. | 2 | At any time |
> |  |  |  |  |
> |  | South Side: from a point 73 metres east of the east kerbline of Durham Street for a distance of 32 metres. Carriages shall stand in a single line by the kerbline and parallel therewith, facing east. | 5 | At any time |

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 19<sup>th</sup> March 2009.

Stanley Duncan

A senior officer of the Department of the Environment

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the By-Laws)

These By-Laws amend the Schedule to the Motor Hackney Carriages (Belfast) By-Laws made by the Council of the County Borough of Belfast on 4<sup>th</sup> June 1951 by—

- (a) altering the position of the taxi stand on the south side of Glengall Street and decreasing the number of taxis which may stand from 6 to 5; and
- (b) making provision for a new taxi stand on the north side of Glengall Street.
