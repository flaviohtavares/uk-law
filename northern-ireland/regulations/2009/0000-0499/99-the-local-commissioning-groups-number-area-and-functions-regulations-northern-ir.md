The Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety makes the following regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 9(1), 9(2) and 9(3)(a) of the Health and Social Care (Reform) Act (Northern Ireland) 2009 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e06c2e7c4485c59eafd61e8560b4031e][^f00001]:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-e06c2e7c4485c59eafd61e8560b4031e]

(1) These regulations may be cited as the Local Commissioning Groups (Number, Area and Functions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009 and shall come into operation on 1st April 2009. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e06c2e7c4485c59eafd61e8560b4031e]

(2) In these regulations— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e06c2e7c4485c59eafd61e8560b4031e]

- “the Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e06c2e7c4485c59eafd61e8560b4031e]” means the Health and Social Care (Reform) Act (Northern Ireland) 2009; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e06c2e7c4485c59eafd61e8560b4031e]
- “Local Commissioning Group” means a committee referred to in section 9(1) of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e06c2e7c4485c59eafd61e8560b4031e] the Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e06c2e7c4485c59eafd61e8560b4031e];
- “Local Government District” has the same meaning as in section 1 of the Local Government (Boundaries) Act (Northern Ireland) 1971 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e06c2e7c4485c59eafd61e8560b4031e][^f00002].

## 2. Number and area of Local Commissioning Groups (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-e06c2e7c4485c59eafd61e8560b4031e]

(1) For the purposes of section 9(1) of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e06c2e7c4485c59eafd61e8560b4031e] the Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e06c2e7c4485c59eafd61e8560b4031e] the prescribed number of Local Commissioning Groups shall be 5. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e06c2e7c4485c59eafd61e8560b4031e]

(2) The name of each Local Commissioning Group shall be the name specified in column 1 of the Schedule and the area of that Group shall be the area of the local government districts specified opposite the name of that Group in column 2 of the Schedule. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e06c2e7c4485c59eafd61e8560b4031e]

## 3. Functions of Local Commissioning Groups (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-e06c2e7c4485c59eafd61e8560b4031e]

Each Local Commissioning Group shall exercise the following functions with respect to the commissioning of health and social care for people in its area— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e06c2e7c4485c59eafd61e8560b4031e]

- (a) assessing health and social care needs; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e06c2e7c4485c59eafd61e8560b4031e]
- (b) planning health and social care to meet current and emerging needs; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e06c2e7c4485c59eafd61e8560b4031e]
- (c) securing the delivery of health and social care to meet assessed needs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e06c2e7c4485c59eafd61e8560b4031e]

EXPLANATORY NOTE *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e06c2e7c4485c59eafd61e8560b4031e]

(This note is not part of the regulations) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e06c2e7c4485c59eafd61e8560b4031e]

These regulations prescribe the number of Local Commissioning Groups to be appointed by the Regional Health and Social Care Board established under the Health and Social Care (Reform) Act (Northern Ireland) 2009 (regulation 2(1)). They also prescribe the areas of the Local Commissioning Groups (regulation 2(2)) and the functions they will exercise with respect to the commissioning of health and social care (regulation 3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e06c2e7c4485c59eafd61e8560b4031e]
