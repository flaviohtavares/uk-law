The Department of the Environment makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Articles 5 and 36 of the Water (Northern Ireland) Order 1999 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-609e862e09cec989565bd9e9fb987358][^f00001], and, being a department designated *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-609e862e09cec989565bd9e9fb987358] [^f00002] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-609e862e09cec989565bd9e9fb987358] [^f00003] in relation to the environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by that section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-609e862e09cec989565bd9e9fb987358]

## 1. Citation and commencement (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-6a3a12de62657038dda4f55cf89087f8][^key-609e862e09cec989565bd9e9fb987358]

These Regulations may be cited as the Surface Waters (Shellfish) (Classification) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009 and shall come into operation on 27th March 2009. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-609e862e09cec989565bd9e9fb987358]

## 2. Interpretation (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-86d31ebe37d9afd91b1d07ebcaabeeef][^key-609e862e09cec989565bd9e9fb987358]

The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-609e862e09cec989565bd9e9fb987358] [^f00004] applies to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Northern Ireland Assembly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-609e862e09cec989565bd9e9fb987358]

These Regulations amend the Surface Waters (Shellfish) (Classification) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 1997 (“the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-609e862e09cec989565bd9e9fb987358] 1997 Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-609e862e09cec989565bd9e9fb987358]”).

These Regulations update the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-609e862e09cec989565bd9e9fb987358] 1997 Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-609e862e09cec989565bd9e9fb987358] to reflect the revocation of Council Directive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-609e862e09cec989565bd9e9fb987358] [79/923/EEC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1979/0923)[^key-609e862e09cec989565bd9e9fb987358] as amended, and the consolidation of its terms with minor amendments in Directive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-609e862e09cec989565bd9e9fb987358] [2006/113/EC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0113)[^key-609e862e09cec989565bd9e9fb987358] of the European Parliament and of the Council on the quality required of shellfish waters, (O.J. No L376, 27.12.2006, p.14). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-609e862e09cec989565bd9e9fb987358]

The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-609e862e09cec989565bd9e9fb987358] 1997 Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-609e862e09cec989565bd9e9fb987358] as amended prescribe a system for classifying the quality of shellfish waters, and apply to coastal and brackish waters requiring protection or improvement in order to support shellfish life and growth, to contribute to the high quality of shellfish products directly edible by man. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-609e862e09cec989565bd9e9fb987358]
