# Licensing and Registration of Clubs (Amendment) (2011 Act) (Commencement No.2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2012 No. 28 (C. 6)

Licensing

Licensing and Registration of Clubs (Amendment) (2011 Act) (Commencement No.2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012

Made

30th January 2012

The Department for Social Development makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 18 of the Licensing and Registration of Clubs (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2011[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Licensing and Registration of Clubs Amendment (2011 Act) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012.

## 2. Appointed Day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the Licensing and Registration of Clubs Amendment Act (Northern Ireland) 2011 listed in the Schedule is 1st May 2012.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 30th January 2012

(L.S.)

Michael Daly

A senior officer of the Department for Social Development
