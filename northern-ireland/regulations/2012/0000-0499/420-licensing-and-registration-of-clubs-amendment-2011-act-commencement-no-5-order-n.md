# Licensing and Registration of Clubs (Amendment) (2011 Act) (Commencement No.5) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2012 No. 420 (C. 42)

Licensing

Licensing and Registration of Clubs (Amendment) (2011 Act) (Commencement No.5) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012

Made

26th November 2012

The Department for Social Development makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 18 of the Licensing and Registration of Clubs (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2011[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Licensing and Registration of Clubs Amendment (2011 Act) (Commencement No. 5) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012.

## 2. Appointed Day

10th December 2012 is the day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the Act specified in column 1 of the Schedule, for the purposes set out in column 2 of the Schedule.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 26<sup>th</sup> November 2012

(L.S.)

Michael Daly

A senior officer of the

Department social Development

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the coming into operation of the provisions of the Licensing and Registration of Clubs (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2011 listed in the Schedule to this Order, on 10th December 2012.
