# The Road Traffic (Immobilisation, Removal and Disposal of Vehicles) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2012 No. 146

Road Traffic And Vehicles

The Road Traffic (Immobilisation, Removal and Disposal of Vehicles) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012

Made

26th March 2012

Coming into operation

23rd April 2012

The Department of the Environment makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 13 of, and Schedule 1 to, the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 2007[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Immobilisation, Removal and Disposal of Vehicles) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012 and shall come into operation on 23<sup>rd</sup> April 2012.

## 2. Amendment of the Road Traffic (Immobilisation, Removal and Disposal of Vehicles) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012

(1) The Road Traffic (Immobilisation, Removal and Disposal of Vehicles) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012[^f00002] are amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (3).

(2) In regulations 8(2)(a) and 19(2)(a) (false or misleading statements,etc) for “to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale” substitute “to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum”.

(3) In regulation 20(2) (disputes) for “the appropriate court of summary jurisdiction” substitute “a court of summary jurisdiction”.

Sealed with the official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 26<sup>th</sup> March 2012

Iain Greenway

A senior officer of the

Department of the Environment

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

These Regulations make minor corrections to the wording used in regulations 8(2)(a), 19(2)(a) and 20(2) to the Road Traffic (Immobilisation, Removal and Disposal of Vehicles) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012.
