# Superannuation (Chief Inspector of Criminal Justice in Northern Ireland) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2012 No. 49

Pensions

Superannuation (Chief Inspector of Criminal Justice in Northern Ireland) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012

Made

17th February 2012

Coming into operation

14th March 2012

The Department of Finance and Personnel makes the following Order, in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 3(4) and (7) of the Superannuation (Northern Ireland) Order 1972[^f00001] and now vested in it[^f00002].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Superannuation (Chief Inspector of Criminal Justice in Northern Ireland) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012, and shall come into operation on 14th March 2012.

## 2. Addition to list of offices

There shall be added to the offices listed in Schedule 1 to the Superannuation (Northern Ireland) Order 1972 with effect from 1st February 2012, the office of Chief Inspector of Criminal Justice in Northern Ireland[^f00003].

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance and Personnel on 17th February 2012

(L.S.)

John McKibbin

A senior officer of the

Department of Finance and Personnel

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order adds the office of Chief Inspector of Criminal Justice in Northern Ireland to the offices listed in Schedule 1 to the Superannuation (Northern Ireland) Order 1972 for which the Department of Finance and Personnel may make pension schemes.
