# The Labour Relations Agency Arbitration Scheme Order (Northern Ireland) 2012, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6. Application of Terms of Reference

Section 46(1)(b) of the Arbitration Act 1996, reproduced in Appendix B of the Schedule, shall apply to arbitrations conducted in accordance with the Scheme, modified such that the arbitral tribunal shall decide the dispute, if the parties so agree, in accordance with the Terms of Reference in paragraph 10 of the Schedule.
