# The Labour Relations Agency Arbitration Scheme Order (Northern Ireland) 2012, section 5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5. Application of Part I of the Arbitration Act 1996

The provisions of Part I of the Arbitration Act 1996[^f00009] referred to in paragraphs 40, 89, 108, 110 to 115, 119, 123 and 124 of the Schedule and reproduced in Appendix B of the Schedule shall apply with the modifications specified in those paragraphs to arbitrations conducted in accordance with the Scheme.
