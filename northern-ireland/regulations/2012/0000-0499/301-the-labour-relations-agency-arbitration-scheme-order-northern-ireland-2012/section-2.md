# The Labour Relations Agency Arbitration Scheme Order (Northern Ireland) 2012, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order—

- “the 1996 Order” means the Employment Rights (Northern Ireland) Order 1996;
- “the Fair Employment Tribunal” means the Fair Employment Tribunal for Northern Ireland established under Article 81 of the Fair Employment and Treatment (Northern Ireland) Order 1998;
- “the Scheme” means the arbitration scheme set out in the Schedule with the exception of paragraphs 40, 89, 108, 110 to 115, 119, 123 and 124.
