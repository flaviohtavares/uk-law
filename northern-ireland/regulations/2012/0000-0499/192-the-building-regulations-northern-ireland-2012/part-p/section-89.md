# The Building Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012, section 89

> Text as at 2025-05-06.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 89. Reducing the risk of scalding

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The domestic hot water distribution system shall be so designed and installed as to incorporate measures to ensure that the temperature of water that can be delivered is not excessive.

(2) The hot water supply to any fixed bath shall be so designed and installed as to incorporate measures to ensure that the temperature of water that can be delivered to that bath does not exceed 48 ºC.
