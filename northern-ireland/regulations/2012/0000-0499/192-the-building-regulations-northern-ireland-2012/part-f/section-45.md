# The Building Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012, section 45

> Text as at 2025-05-06.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 45. Notice of commissioning

*Extent: N.I.*

Where this regulation applies, the person carrying out the work, for the purpose of ensuring compliance with regulation 39(b) and (c), shall give, not more than 5 days after completion of the commissioning, a notice in writing to—

- (a) the building owner stating that the fixed building services have been commissioned; and
- (b) the district council stating that the requirements of sub-paragraph (a) have been met.
