# The Building Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012, section 43B

> Text as at 2025-05-06.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 43B. Nearly zero-energy requirements for new buildings[^c-70fd59190707047a5a757ee85892fb87]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where a building is erected, it must be a nearly zero-energy building.

(2) For the purposes of paragraph (1)—

- (a) in respect of new buildings occupied and owned[^c-f7bd40ad57cd3e5f0794d06c84cd2fbb] by public authorities, this regulation shall apply from 1<sup>st</sup> January 2019; and
- (b) in respect of all new buildings, this regulation shall apply from 31<sup>st</sup> December 2020.
