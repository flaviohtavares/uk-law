# The Building Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012, section 40

> Text as at 2025-05-06.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 40. Target carbon dioxide emission rate

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Without prejudice to the requirements of regulation 39, where a building is to be erected, or a building other than a dwelling is extended as described in regulation 38(3)(b)[^c-a0346c62c15e253b318f47e754fc65bf] , minimum energy performance requirements in the form of[^c-2c1b6b0eccca5fed70121876f5a4b9b4] a target carbon dioxide emission rate for that building shall be calculated and expressed[^c-84040dd85b9858e175cb47d05d998437] using a national calculation methodology.

(2) The building, or extension as described in regulation 38(3)(b)[^c-1075c42dd50e94348a11a0c9f4f07345] , shall be so designed and constructed as not to exceed its calculated target carbon dioxide emission rate , with all carbon dioxide emission rates calculated and expressed using a national calculation methodology[^c-59eef58ed3105ee8f76662fe6a0508c3] .
