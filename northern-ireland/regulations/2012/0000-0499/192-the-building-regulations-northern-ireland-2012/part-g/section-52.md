> Text as at 2025-05-06.

## 52. Acoustic conditions in schools

*Extent: N.I.*

Each room or space in a school building shall be designed and constructed in such a way that it has—

- (a) acoustic conditions; and
- (b) reasonable resistance to the passage of sound,

appropriate to its intended use.
