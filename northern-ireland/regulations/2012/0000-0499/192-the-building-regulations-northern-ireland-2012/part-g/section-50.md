# The Building Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012, section 50

> Text as at 2025-05-06.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 50. Protection against sound within a dwelling or room for residential purposes

*Extent: N.I.*

Within a dwelling or room for residential purposes reasonable resistance to the passage of airborne sound shall be provided by—

- (a) internal walls that separate—
  - (i) a bedroom; and
  - (ii) a room containing a water closet,

from any other room; and

- (b) all internal floors.
