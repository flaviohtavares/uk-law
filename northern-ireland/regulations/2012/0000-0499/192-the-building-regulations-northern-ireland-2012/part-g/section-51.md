# The Building Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012, section 51

> Text as at 2025-05-06.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 51. Reverberation in the common internal parts of buildings containing flats or rooms for residential purposes

*Extent: N.I.*

Any common internal part of a building that contains a flat or a room for residential purposes shall be designed and constructed in such a way as to limit reverberation around those common parts to a reasonable level.
