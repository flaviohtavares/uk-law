> Text as at 2025-05-06.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 15. Testing of drains and private sewers

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A district council may—

- (a) require in writing any person by whom or on whose behalf work on drains and private sewers was carried out to carry out such tests of any drain or private sewer as may be necessary to establish compliance with any of the provisions of Part N; or
- (b) carry out, or arrange to have carried out, such tests itself.

(2) A person who carries out any tests under paragraph (1)(a) shall give notice of the results to the district council not more than 5 days after the tests are carried out.
