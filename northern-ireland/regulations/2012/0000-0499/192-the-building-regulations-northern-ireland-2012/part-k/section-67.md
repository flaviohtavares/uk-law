# The Building Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012, section 67

> Text as at 2025-05-06.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 67. Notification of testing and commissioning

*Extent: N.I.*

Where this regulation applies, the person carrying out the work, for the purpose of ensuring compliance with regulation 65(2), shall give, not more than 5 days after completion of the testing and commissioning, a notice in writing to the district council stating—

- (a) the results of air flow tests; and
- (b) that any fixed mechanical ventilation system and associated controls have been commissioned.
