# The Building Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012, section 65

> Text as at 2025-05-06.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 65. Means of ventilation

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Adequate means of ventilation shall be provided for people in a building.

(2) Fixed mechanical ventilation systems and any associated controls shall be tested and commissioned to ensure that the requirement of paragraph (1) is met.
