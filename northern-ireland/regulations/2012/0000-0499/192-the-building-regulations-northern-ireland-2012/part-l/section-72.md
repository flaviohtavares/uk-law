> Text as at 2025-05-06.

## 72. Warning of the presence of carbon monoxide gas

*Extent: N.I.*

Where a combustion appliance is installed in a dwelling, reasonable provision shall be made to detect and give warning of the presence of carbon monoxide gas at levels harmful to people.
