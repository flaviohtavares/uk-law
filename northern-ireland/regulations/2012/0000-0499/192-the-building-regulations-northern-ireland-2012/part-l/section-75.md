> Text as at 2025-05-06.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 75. Protection of liquid fuel storage tanks

*Extent: N.I.*

Reasonable provision shall be made to ensure that a liquid fuel storage tank and connecting pipework is so constructed and separated from adjacent buildings and the boundary of the premises as to reduce to a reasonable level the risk of the fuel contained inside it igniting in the event of a fire occurring in adjacent buildings or premises.
