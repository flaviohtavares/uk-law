> Text as at 2025-05-06.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 2 — CLASSES OF EXEMPTED BUILDINGS

*Extent: N.I.*

Regulation 4

In Classes **3** and **4** of the Table to this Schedule “height**”** shall be measured from the lowest level of the ground adjoining the building to the highest point of the building.

| **4.** | **Buildings for agriculture and for keeping animals**<br>A building used for agriculture or for keeping animals, but not including a building used principally for retailing (including storage for retailing), packaging, exhibiting, or as an office, provided that—<br>- (a) no part of the building is used as a dwelling;<br>- (b) no point of the building is less than one and half times its height from—<br>  - (i) any point of a building which contains sleeping accommodation;<br>  - (ii) the nearest part of a road; or<br>  - (iii) the nearest point of the boundary of the land on which it is to be erected; and<br>- (c) the building is provided with a fire exit which is not more than 30 m from any point in the building. |
| **5.** | **Temporary buildings**<br>A building, including an exhibition stand and a stand for viewing a public display, which is not intended to remain where it is erected for more than 28 days. |
| **6.** | **Ancillary buildings**<br>- (a) A building on a construction site, being a building which is intended to be used only in connection with the disposal of buildings or building plots on that site and which contains no sleeping accommodation.<br>- (b) A building on or close to the site of construction or civil engineering work, which is intended to be used only by people engaged on those works during the course of those works and which contains no sleeping accommodation.<br>- (c) A building, other than a building containing a dwelling or used as an office or showroom, erected for use on the site of and in connection with a mine or quarry. |
| **7.** | **Small detached buildings**<br>- (a) A detached single storey building, having a floor area which does not exceed 30 m<sup>2</sup>, which contains no sleeping accommodation and is a building which is either—<br>  - (i) constructed substantially of non-combustible material; or<br>  - (ii) at no point less than 1 m from—<br>    - (aa) the nearest point of a dwelling;<br>    - (bb) the nearest part of a road; or<br>    - (cc) the nearest point of the boundary of the land on which it is to be erected.<br>- (b) A detached building designed and intended to shelter people from the effects of nuclear, chemical or conventional weapons, and not used for any other purpose, if—<br>  - (i) its floor area does not exceed 30 m<sup>2</sup>; and<br>  - (ii) the excavation for the building is no closer to any exposed part of another building or structure than a distance equal to the depth of the excavation plus 1 m.<br>- (c) A detached single storey building, having a floor area which does not exceed 15 m<sup>2</sup>, which contains no sleeping accommodation and is not less than 1 m from a dwelling. |

| **8.** | **Extensions** |

| **9.** | **Other Buildings**<br>- (a) An air-supported structure which—<br>  - (i) does not exceed 15 m in length or diameter;<br>  - (ii) has alternative means of escape;<br>  - (iii) is not less than 2 m from any building to which these regulations apply; and<br>  - (iv) is used solely in connection with a dwelling.<br>- (b) An air-supported structure which—<br>  - (i) after deflation is re-erected in accordance with plans previously approved and with any conditions imposed in connection with the approval of those plans; and<br>  - (ii) is used for the same purpose as that for which it was previously erected.<br>- (c) A tent or marquee.<br>- (d) A movable dwelling including a tent, caravan, shed or similar structure used for human habitation, but not a structure that would require disassembly to facilitate being moved on or off site.<br>- (e) Mobile accommodation used in a static condition, including a houseboat, caravan or railway carriage.<br>- (f) Any part of a dock, wharf, pier, lighthouse, dam, reservoir, railway line, airfield, pavement, canal (including locks and other structures associated therewith), oil rig, tunnel, bridge, road, path, ramp, stairway, walkway or work of public utility which does not form part of, give support to or gain support from, a building to which these regulations apply and which is not required for the purposes of Part R of these regulations; sewage disposal works other than buildings designed to house plant or provide covered accommodation for persons.<br>- (g) A structure (other than a chimney) not incorporating any covered space with headroom exceeding 1.5 m, except where the structure is attached to or within a building to which these regulations apply; a tower mast not attached to a building to which these regulations apply.<br>- (h) Unclad plant or machinery, including a vending or weighing machine. |
