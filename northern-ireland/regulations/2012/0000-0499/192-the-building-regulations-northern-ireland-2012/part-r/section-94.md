# The Building Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012, section 94

> Text as at 2025-05-06.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 94. Sanitary conveniences in dwellings

*Extent: N.I.*

In a dwelling a sanitary convenience shall be provided—

- (a) in the entrance storey; or
- (b) where the entrance storey contains no habitable rooms – in the principal storey.
