# The Building Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012, section 91

> Text as at 2025-05-06.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 91. Access and use

*Extent: N.I.*

Reasonable provision shall be made for people to have access to, into, within and to use a building and its facilities:

Provided that in a dwelling this requirement shall be limited to the entrance storey or, where that storey contains no habitable room, to the principal storey.
