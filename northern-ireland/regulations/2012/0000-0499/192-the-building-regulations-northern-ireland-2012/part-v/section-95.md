# The Building Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012, section 95

> Text as at 2025-05-06.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 95. Application and interpretation

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraphs (2) and (3) this Part shall apply to any building or part of a building.

(2) Regulation 97 shall not apply to glazing in a dwelling.

(3) Regulation 99 shall not apply to glazing that is not intended to be cleaned.

(4) In this Part—

“**Glazing**” includes glass, plastic and other transparent or translucent materials.
