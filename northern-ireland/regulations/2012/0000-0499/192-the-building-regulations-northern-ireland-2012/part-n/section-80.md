# The Building Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012, section 80

> Text as at 2025-05-06.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 80. Sanitary pipework

*Extent: N.I.*

Sanitary pipework shall—

- (a) consist of pipes and fittings—
  - (i) of such size, layout, construction and watertightness; and
  - (ii) with sufficient ventilation,

  to ensure the hygienic conveyance of foul water to an underground foul drainage system; and
- (b) have such means of access as is necessary to facilitate the clearance of blockages.
