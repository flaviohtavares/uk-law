> Text as at 2025-05-06.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 79. Drainage systems

*Extent: N.I.*

Every building shall be provided with such sanitary pipework, underground foul drainage and rainwater drainage as may be necessary for the hygienic and adequate disposal of foul water and rainwater from that building.
