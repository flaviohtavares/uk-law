# The Building Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012, section 55

> Text as at 2025-05-06.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 55. Provision of stairs in dwellings

*Extent: N.I.*

Within every dwelling of more than one storey there shall be provided between such storeys access by means of a stair complying with the relevant provisions in this Part: Provided that nothing in this regulation shall require the provision of a stair to any storey within a dwelling if that storey is used only as general storage accommodation.
