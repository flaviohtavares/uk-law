# The Medicines (Products for Human Use) (Fees) Regulations 2012, section 56

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 56. Amendment of the Homoeopathic Regulations

The Homoeopathic Regulations are amended by omitting the following definitions in regulation 1(2)[^f00026] (citation, commencement and interpretation)—

- (a) “administrative variation”,
- (b) “the Board”,
- (c) “concerned member State”,
- (d) “set of applications”, and
- (e) “standard variation”.

## Annotations

[^f00026]: Footnote: The definition of “administrative variation” was inserted by [S.I. 1998/574](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/574). The definitions of “concerned member state” and “set of applications” were inserted by [S.I. 2005/2753](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/2753). The definition of “standard variation” was inserted by [S.I. 1998/574](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/574) and amended by [S.I. 2001/795](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/795).
