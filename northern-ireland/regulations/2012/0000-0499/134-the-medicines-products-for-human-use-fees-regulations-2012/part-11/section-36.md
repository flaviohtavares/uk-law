# The Medicines (Products for Human Use) (Fees) Regulations 2012, section 36

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 36. Fees for applications for membership and certificates

(1) The fee payable by an applicant for membership of the good clinical practice accreditation scheme is £126.

(2) The fee payable by an applicant for a certificate of membership of the good clinical practice accreditation scheme is £67.
