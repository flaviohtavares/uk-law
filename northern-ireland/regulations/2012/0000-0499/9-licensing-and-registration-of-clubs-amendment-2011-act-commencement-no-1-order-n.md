# Licensing and Registration of Clubs (Amendment) (2011 Act) (Commencement No.1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2012 No. 9 (C. 1)

Licensing

Licensing and Registration of Clubs (Amendment) (2011 Act) (Commencement No.1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012

Made

13th January 2012

The Department for Social Development makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 18 of the Licensing and Registration of Clubs (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2011[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Licensing and Registration of Clubs Amendment (2011 Act) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012.

## 2. Appointed Day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of sections 13 and 14 of the Licensing and Registration of Clubs (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2011 is 1st March 2012.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 13th January 2012

(L.S.)

Michael Daly

A senior officer of the Department for Social Development

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the coming into operation of sections 13 and 14 of the Licensing and Registration of Clubs (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2011 on the 1st March 2012.
