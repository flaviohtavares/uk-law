# The Rules of the Court of Judicature (Northern Ireland) (Amendment No.2) 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2012 No. 273

Court Of Judicature, Northern Ireland

Procedure

The Rules of the Court of Judicature (Northern Ireland) (Amendment No.2) 2012

Made

4th July 2012

Coming into operation

1st August 2012

The Northern Ireland Court of Judicature Rules Committee[^f00001] makes the following Rules in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 55 and 55A of the Judicature (Northern Ireland) Act 1978[^f00002].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Rules may be cited as the Rules of the Court of Judicature (Northern Ireland) (Amendment No.2) 2012 and shall come into operation on 1st August 2012.

## 2. Amendment to the Rules of the Court of Judicature (Northern Ireland) 1980

Order 116B of the Rules of the Court of Judicature (Northern Ireland) 1980[^f00003] shall be amended as follows—

- (a) in rule 13(2)(b), for “application” substitute “appeal”;
- (b) in rule 28—
  - (i) in paragraph (1)(b)(ii), insert “or” after “case;”; and
  - (ii) omit paragraphs (1)(b) (iii) and (1A).

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules make minor amendments to Order 116B of the Rules of the Court of Judicature (Northern Ireland) 1980 \[[S.R. 1980 No. 346](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1980/346)\] to provide greater clarity in relation to the process for an appeal to the High Court in respect of a designation decision under the Terrorist Asset-Freezing etc. Act 2010; and to omit the obligation in rule 28 (Search for lodging of and service of material) to lodge and serve material which the disclosing party does not rely upon, but which adversely affects the other party’s case.
