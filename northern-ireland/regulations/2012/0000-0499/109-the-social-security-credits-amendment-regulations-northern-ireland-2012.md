# The Social Security (Credits) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2012 No. 109

Social Security

The Social Security (Credits) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012

Made

9th March 2012

Coming into operation

5th April 2012

The Department for Social Development makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 22(5) and 171(1) and (3) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992[^f00001] and now vested in it[^f00002].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Social Security (Credits) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012 and shall come into operation on 5th April 2012.

## 2. Amendment of the Social Security (Credits) Regulations

In regulation 9C of the Social Security (Credits) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1975[^f00003] (credits for adoption pay period and maternity pay period)—

- (a) in the heading after “adoption pay period” insert “, additional paternity pay period”;
- (b) in paragraph (1)(a) after “paragraph (2)(a)” insert “or (aa)”;
- (c) in paragraph (2) after sub-paragraph (a) insert—

  > - (aa) the additional paternity pay period in respect of which additional statutory paternity pay was paid to a person, or

  ; and
- (d) in paragraph (5)—
  - (i) after ““adoption pay period”,” insert ““additional paternity pay period”,”, and
  - (ii) after ““statutory adoption pay”” insert “, “additional statutory paternity pay””.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 9th March 2012

(L.S.)

Anne McCleary

A senior officer of the Department for Social Development

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend regulation 9C of the Social Security (Credits) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1975 to provide for an award of credits in respect of any week during which a person was paid additional statutory paternity pay.

These Regulations make in relation to Northern Ireland only provision corresponding to provision contained in Regulations made by the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions in relation to Great Britain and accordingly, by virtue of section 149(3) of, and paragraph 10 of Schedule 5 to, the Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992, are not subject to the requirement of section 149(2) of that Act for prior reference to the Social Security Advisory Committee.
