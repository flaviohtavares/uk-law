# The Health (2006 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2012 No. 307 (C. 27)

Dangerous Drugs

Health And Social Care

The Health (2006 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012

Made

1st August 2012

The Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety makes the following Order, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 83(6) of the Health Act 2006[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Health (2006 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012.

(2) In this Order “the Act” means the Health Act 2006.

## 2. Appointed day for provisions relating to the supervision and management of controlled drugs

1st August 2012 is the appointed day for the coming into operation of –

- (a) Chapter 1 of Part 3 of the Act as it applies in relation to Northern Ireland, in so far as it is not already in operation[^f00002]; and
- (b) Sections 76 and 77 of the Act, in so far as they relate to offences under Chapter 1 of Part 3 (including offences created in regulations under that Chapter).

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety on 1st August 2012

Dr Norman Morrow

A senior officer of the

Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order commences Chapter 1 of Part 3 of the Health Act 2006, in relation to Northern Ireland and sections 76 and 77 of that Act so far as they relate to offences under that Chapter 1 of Part 3 (including offences created in regulations under that chapter). Chapter 1 of Part 3 contains measures relating to the safe management and use of controlled drugs in certain health and social care settings. In particular, it provides for the appointment of accountable officers who are to ensure that specified organisations have appropriate arrangements for the safe and effective handling of controlled drugs, and it contains duties of collaboration between responsible bodies and rights of entry and inspection to relevant premises.
