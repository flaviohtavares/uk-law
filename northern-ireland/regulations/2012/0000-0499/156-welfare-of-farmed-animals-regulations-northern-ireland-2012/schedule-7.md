# Welfare of Farmed Animals Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012, SCHEDULE 7

> Text as at 2019-12-14.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 7 — Additional conditions that apply to the keeping of cattle

*Extent: N.I.*

Regulation 4

**1.** Where lactating dairy cows or calving cows are kept in a building, they shall have access at all times to a well-drained and bedded lying area.

**2.** Where any calving cows are kept in a building, they shall be kept—

- (a) in a pen or a yard which is of such a size as to permit a person to attend the cows; and
- (b) separate from other livestock other than calving cows.
