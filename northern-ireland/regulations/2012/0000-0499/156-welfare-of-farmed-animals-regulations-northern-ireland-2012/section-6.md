# Welfare of Farmed Animals Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012, section 6

> Text as at 2019-12-14.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 6. Offences

*Extent: N.I.*

A person commits an offence if, without lawful authority or excuse, that person—

- (a) contravenes, or does not comply with a duty in, regulations 3, 4 or 5;
- (b) makes an entry in a record, or gives any information for the purposes of these Regulations which they know to be false in any material particular or, for those purposes, recklessly makes a statement or gives any information which is false in any material particular; or
- (c) causes or permits any of the above.
