# The Road Traffic (2007 Order) (Commencement No. 5) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2012 No. 337

Road Traffic And Vehicles

The Road Traffic (2007 Order) (Commencement No. 5) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012

Made

13th September 2012

The Department of the Environment makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 1(3) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 2007[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic (2007 Order) (Commencement No. 5) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012.

## 2. Amendment

In the Schedule to the Road Traffic (2007 Order) (Commencement No. 5) Order (Northern Ireland) 2010[^f00002]—

- (a) for the entry “Article 51” substitute “Article 51 (1) to (5)”; and
- (b) for the entry “Article 52 (1) to (5)” substitute “Article 52”.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 13<sup>th</sup> September 2012

Iain Greenway

A senior officer of the

Department of the Environment

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order corrects a drafting error in the Road Traffic (2007 Order) (Commencement No. 5) Order (Northern Ireland) 2010 ([S.R. 2010 No. 226 (C. 13)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2010/226)).
