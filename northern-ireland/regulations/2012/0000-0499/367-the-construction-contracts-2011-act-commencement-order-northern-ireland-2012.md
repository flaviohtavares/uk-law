# The Construction Contracts (2011 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2012 No. 367 (C. 34)

Construction

The Construction Contracts (2011 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012

Made

20th September 2012

The Department of Finance and Personnel makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 9(2) of the Construction Contracts (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2011[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Construction Contracts (2011 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the Construction Contracts (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2011 shall be 14th November 2012.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance and Personnel on 20<sup>th</sup> September 2012

(L.S.)

David Carson

A senior officer of the Department of Finance and Personnel

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the provisions of the Construction Contracts (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2011 into operation on 14th November 2012.
