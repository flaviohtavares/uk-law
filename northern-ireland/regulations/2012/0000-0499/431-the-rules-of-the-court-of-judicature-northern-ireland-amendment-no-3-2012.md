# The Rules of the Court of Judicature (Northern Ireland) (Amendment No.3) 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2012 No. 431

Court Of Judicature, Northern Ireland

Procedure

The Rules of the Court of Judicature (Northern Ireland) (Amendment No.3) 2012

Made

30th November 2012

Coming into operation

1st January 2013

The Northern Ireland Court of Judicature Rules Committee[^f00001] makes the following Rules in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 55 and 55A of the Judicature (Northern Ireland) Act 1978[^f00002].

## 1. Citation and Commencement

These Rules may be cited as the Rules of the Court of Judicature (Northern Ireland) (Amendment No.3) 2012 and come into operation on 1st January 2013.

## 2. Amendments to the Rules of the Court of Judicature (Northern Ireland) 1980[^f00003]

In Order 79—

(1) In rule 11, omit, “, including bail under section 91 of the Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 2011,”; and

(2) After rule 11, insert—

> **11A. Recommittal in respect of order under section 91 of the Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 2011**
>
> Where an application by the prosecutor or a surety for the review of an order under section 91 of the Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 2011 (power of a magistrates’ court to grant bail on compassionate grounds), the High Court decides to exercise its jurisdiction, the Court may, if it appears to it any of the grounds mentioned in rule 11 paragraphs (a) to (c) are satisfied, order that the person released on bail on foot of the order be recommitted to custody and issue a warrant for his arrest .

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules amend the Rules of the Court of Judicature (Northern Ireland) 1980 \[[S.R. 1980 No.346](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1980/346)\] to make it clear that the High Court may, where it decides to exercise its jurisdiction, enforce a breach of bail granted by the magistrates’ courts under section 91 of the Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 2011.
