# The Northern Ireland Poultry Health Assurance Scheme (Fees) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012

> Text as at 2016-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

2012 No. 305

Animals

Animal Health

The Northern Ireland Poultry Health Assurance Scheme (Fees) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012

Made

27th July 2012

Coming into operation

27th August 2012

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development is designated for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 [^c21336191] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Union [^c21336201].

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 8(1), (2) and (5) of the Diseases of Animals (Northern Ireland) Order 1981 [^c21336211] and section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 having consulted with the bodies which appeared to it to be substantially representative of the interests concerned and with the approval of the Department of Finance and Personnel.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Northern Ireland Poultry Health Assurance Scheme (Fees) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012 and shall come into operation on 27th August 2012.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order—

“**the Department**” means the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development.

## 3. Fees

The fees and amounts specified in (or determined in accordance with) columns (1) and (2) of the Table in the Schedule shall be paid to the Department (in accordance with the Note 1 to the Table) by the person specified in column (3) within 14 days of demand.

## 4. Consequential amendment

In paragraph 1 (Interpretation) of the Schedule to The Northern Ireland Poultry Health Assurance Scheme Order (Northern Ireland) 2011 [^c21336221] for the definition of “Fees Order” substitute “ “**Fees Order**” means The Northern Ireland Poultry Health Assurance Scheme (Fees) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012; and ”.

## 5. Revocation

The Northern Ireland Poultry Health Assurance Scheme (Fees) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011 [^c21336231] is revoked.

### 1. Note

The re-inspection fee shall be determined on a case by case basis and shall comprise of a charge for an administration officer’s time spent providing administrative support, the charge for which shall be £26.25 per hour (or part thereof) and the veterinary inspector’s time based on a charge of £55.97 per hour (or part thereof) and a charge for mileage, that is, 45 pence for each mile travelled by the veterinary inspector to and from the operator’s establishment in order to carry out the re-inspection.

*(This note is not part of the Order)*

This Order revokes and remakes the Northern Ireland Poultry Health Assurance Scheme (Fees) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011. The Order makes provision for the Department to charge fees for activities required under Council Directive [2009/158/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0158) (on animal health conditions governing intra-Community trade in, and imports from third countries, of poultry and hatching eggs). The activities and fees are listed in the Schedule.
