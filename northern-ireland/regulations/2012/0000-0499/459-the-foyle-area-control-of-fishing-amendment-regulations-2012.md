# The Foyle Area (Control of Fishing) (Amendment) Regulations 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2012 No. 459

Fisheries

The Foyle Area (Control of Fishing) (Amendment) Regulations 2012

Made

12th December 2012

Coming into operation

31st January 2013

The Foyle, Carlingford and Irish Lights Commission, with the approval of the North/South Ministerial Council, makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 13(1) of the Foyle Fisheries Act 1952[^f00001] and section 13(1) of the Foyle Fisheries Act (Northern Ireland) 1952[^f00002] and now vested in it[^f00003].

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Foyle Area (Control of Fishing) (Amendment) Regulations 2012 and shall come into operation on 31<sup>st</sup> January 2013.

(2) These Regulations apply to the Foyle Area.

## 2. Amendment of the Foyle Area (Control of Fishing) Regulations 2010

(1) The Foyle Area (Control of Fishing) Regulations 2010[^f00004] are amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

(2) In regulation 2(1) (interpretation)—

- in the definition of “catch and release” for “7 mm” substitute “12 mm”.

Given under the Seal of the Foyle, Carlingford and Irish Lights Commission on 12th December 2012.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These regulations amend the Foyle Area (Control of Fishing) Regulations 2010 by making an amendment to the definition of “catch and release”, increasing the minimum gape of hook permitted for use while angling for salmon, sea trout or brown trout from 7 mm to 12 mm in any waters to which restrictions to angling to catch and release applies.
