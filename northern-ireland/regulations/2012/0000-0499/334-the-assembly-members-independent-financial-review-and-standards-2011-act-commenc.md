# The Assembly Members (Independent Financial Review and Standards) (2011 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2012 No. 334 (C. 30)

Northern Ireland Assembly

The Assembly Members (Independent Financial Review and Standards) (2011 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012

Made

10th September 2012

The Northern Ireland Assembly Commission makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 38(2) of the Assembly Members (Independent Financial Review and Standards) Act (Northern Ireland) 2011[^f00001]

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Assembly Members (Independent Financial Review and Standards) (2011 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012.

## 2. Appointed Day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the following provisions of the Assembly Members (Independent Financial Review and Standards) Act (Northern Ireland) 2011 is 17th September 2012—

- (a) section 17 (functions of the Commissioner);
- (b) sections 25 to 27 (investigations of the Commissioner);
- (c) section 28 to 33 (investigatory powers of the Commissioner).

Signed by authority of the Assembly Commission on 10th September 2012

T Reaney

Clerk to the Assembly

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into operation on 17th September 2012 sections 17 and 25 to 33 of the Assembly Members (Independent Financial Review and Standards) Act (Northern Ireland) 2011.

The other provisions of the Act came into operation on Royal Assent.
