# Superannuation (Commission for Victims and Survivors for Northern Ireland) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2012 No. 393

Pensions

Superannuation (Commission for Victims and Survivors for Northern Ireland) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012

Made

29th October 2012

Coming into operation

17th December 2012

The Department of Finance and Personnel makes the following Order, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 3(4) and (7) of the Superannuation (Northern Ireland) Order 1972[^f00001] and now vested in it[^f00002].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Superannuation (Commission for Victims and Survivors for Northern Ireland) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012, shall come into operation on 17th December 2012 and shall have effect from 23rd May 2008.

## 2. Addition to list of offices

There shall be added to the offices listed in Schedule 1 to the Superannuation (Northern Ireland) Order 1972, the office of member of the Commission for Victims and Survivors for Northern Ireland [^f00003].

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance and Personnel on 29th October 2012

(L.S.)

John McKibbin

A senior officer of the

Department of Finance and Personnel

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order adds the office of member of the Commission for Victims and Survivors for Northern Ireland to the offices listed in Schedule 1 to the Superannuation (Northern Ireland) Order 1972 for which the Department of Finance and Personnel may make pension schemes. It has retrospective effect from 23 May 2008 by virtue of Article 3(7) of that Order.
