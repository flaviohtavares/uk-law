# The Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2012 No. 150

Road Traffic And Vehicle

The Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012

Made

28th March 2012

Coming into operation

1st May 2012

The Department of the Environment, makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 55(1) and (2) and 110(2) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1995[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012 and shall come into operation on 1<sup>st</sup> May 2012.

## 2. Amendment to regulation 24 of the Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999

(1) In regulation 24(5) of the Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999[^f00002] (maintenance and efficiency of brakes), after item 1 in Table I insert—

> | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
> | 1A | A vehicle included in item 1 of the table in regulation 20 or which complies with Community Directive 79/489, 85/647, 88/194, 91/422 or 98/12 or with ECE Regulation 13.03, 13.04, 13.05 or 13.06 which is first used on or after 1st January 2012— |  |  |  |
> |  | (a) when not drawing a trailer; | 58 | 25 |  |
> |  |  |  |  |  |

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend regulation 24(5) of the Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999 in regards to the efficiency limit for brakes. This new limit is to apply to vehicles first used on or after 1st January 2012.
