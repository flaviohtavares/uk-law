# The Local Government (2005 Order) (Commencement No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2012 No. 417 (C. 41)

Local Government

The Local Government (2005 Order) (Commencement No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012

Made

21st November 2012

The Department of the Environment makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 1(2) of the Local Government (Northern Ireland) Order 2005[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Local Government (2005 Order) (Commencement No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of Article 33 (Indemnities for members and officers) of the Local Government (Northern Ireland) Order 2005 shall be 26th November 2012.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 21st November 2012

Linda MacHugh

A senior officer of the Department of the Environment

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into operation the last remaining provision, Article 33 (Indemnities for members and officers), of the Local Government (Northern Ireland) Order 2005.
