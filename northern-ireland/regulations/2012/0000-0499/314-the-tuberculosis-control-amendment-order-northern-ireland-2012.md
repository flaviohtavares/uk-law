# The Tuberculosis Control (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2012 No. 314

Animals

The Tuberculosis Control (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012

Made

8th August 2012

Coming into operation

1st September 2012

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development[^f00001] makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Articles 18(7) and 60(1) of, and paragraph 4 of Part II of Schedule 2 to, the Diseases of Animals (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00002].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Tuberculosis Control (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012 and shall come into operation on 1st September 2012.

## 2. Amendment of the Tuberculosis Control Order (Northern Ireland) 1999

(1) The Tuberculosis Control Order (Northern Ireland) 1999[^f00003] shall be amended as provided by paragraphs (2) and (3).

(2) For article 11(1) there shall be substituted the following paragraph—

> **11.** (1) Subject to paragraph (1A), where the Department slaughters or causes an animal to be slaughtered under Article 10(1), the compensation payable by the Department for the animal shall be the market value of the animal.

.

(3) After article 11(1) there shall be inserted the following paragraph—

> (1A) The amount of compensation payable by the Department under this Article in respect of any animal shall be reduced by any amount payable as compensation for the loss of that animal (howsoever that loss is expressed) under a contract of insurance.

.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 8th August 2012.

Dr Mark Browne

A senior officer of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Tuberculosis Control Order (Northern Ireland) 1999 by providing that the amount of compensation payable by the Department in respect of any animal slaughtered or caused to be slaughtered by the Department under that Order shall be reduced by any amount payable as compensation for the loss of that animal (howsoever that loss is expressed) under a contract of insurance.
