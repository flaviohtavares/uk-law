> Text as at 2025-11-26.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 2. Service on added defendant

*Extent: N.I.*

Where any person is ordered to be added or substituted as defendant, except under Rule 8, the amended civil bill or other originating process shall, be served on the added or substituted defendant according to the rules applicable to the service of a civil bill\[[^f00112] . . .\].
