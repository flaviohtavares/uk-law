# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 26

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 26. Assessment hearing

*Extent: N.I.*

The officer shall notify the parties of the date on which the amount of the claim will be assessed by the judge.

**27.** Where a decree has been issued under Rule 16(b) and the party against whom the decree has been made wishes to attend the assessment hearing, he must notify the small claims office and the other parties in writing.

**28.** The judge shall issue a decree for the amount of the claim, as assessed.
