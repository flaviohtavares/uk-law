> Text as at 2025-11-26.

## 30. Procedure on a small claims hearing

*Extent: N.I.*

A small claims hearing—

- (a) shall be informal; and
- (b) shall not be subject to the strict rules of evidence.

**31.** The judge may adopt any procedure which he considers to be fair.
