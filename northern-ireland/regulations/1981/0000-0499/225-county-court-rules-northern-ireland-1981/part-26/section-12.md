> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 12. Accepting Liability

*Extent: N.I.*

If the respondent accepts liability for the claim, he must, within 21 days of receiving the copy Form 125, lodge with the small claims office a notice of acceptance of liability in Form 126B.

**13.** On receiving a notice of acceptance of liability, the chief clerk may issue a decree, together with a copy of Form 126B, or may refer the notice to the judge if he considers that would be more appropriate.
