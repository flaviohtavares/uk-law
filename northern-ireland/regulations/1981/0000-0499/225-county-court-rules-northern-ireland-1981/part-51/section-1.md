# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 1

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 1. Interpretation

*Extent: N.I.*

In this \[[^f00420] Part of this\] Order—

- (a) a section referred to by number means the section so numbered in the Child Abduction and Custody Act 1985[^f00421]; and
- (b)
  - “**the Hague Convention**” means the Convention defined in section 1(1);
  - “**the High Court**” includes the High Court of Justice in England and Wales \[[^f00422] and the High Court of Justice of the Isle of Man\].
