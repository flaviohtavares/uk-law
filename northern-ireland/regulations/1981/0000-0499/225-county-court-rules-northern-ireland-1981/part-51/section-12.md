# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 12

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 12. Interpretation

*Extent: N.I.*

In this Part—

- “**the Act**” means the Maintenance and Affiliation Orders Act (Northern Ireland) 1966[^f00432];
- “**maintenance order**” has the meaning assigned to it by section 10(2) of the Act.
