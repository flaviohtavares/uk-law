# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 3

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 3. Directions

*Extent: N.I.*

In any action or matter \[[^f00135] the judge\] or, where the application is authorised to be made to the \[[^f00136] district judge\], the \[[^f00136] district judge\] may at any time on the application on notice of any party or of his own motion give such directions as he thinks proper.
