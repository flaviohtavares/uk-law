# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 15

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 15. Time limit for an application under rule 13

*Extent: N.I.*

Save where \[[^f00148] the judge\] for special reason to be stated in \[(a) the judge’s\] order allows, an application for an order under rule 13 shall not be made later than 56 days before the date on which the proceedings are first listed for hearing.\]
