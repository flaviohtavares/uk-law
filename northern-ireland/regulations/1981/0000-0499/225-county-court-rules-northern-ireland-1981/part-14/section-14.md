# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 14

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 14. Application for an order under rule 13

*Extent: N.I.*

An application by a party for an order under rule 13 shall be made by notice of motion and shall, unless \[[^f00148] the judge\] otherwise orders, be supported by an affidavit.
