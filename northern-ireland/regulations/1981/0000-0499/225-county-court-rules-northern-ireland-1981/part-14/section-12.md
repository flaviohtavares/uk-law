# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 12

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 12. Interpretation

*Extent: N.I.*

In this Part of this Order—

- (a) “**an ADR process**” means mediation, conciliation or another dispute resolution process approved by \[[^f00145] the judge\], but does not include arbitration;
- (b) “\[[^f00145] judge\]” includes District \[[^f00145] judge\]; and
- (c) “**party**” includes the personal representative of a deceased party.
