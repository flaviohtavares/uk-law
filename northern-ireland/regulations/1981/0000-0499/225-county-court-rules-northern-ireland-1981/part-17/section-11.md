# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 11

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 11. Right of application to \[ the judge\][^f00172]

*Extent: N.I.*

Any party may, before the proceedings before the officer are concluded, apply by way of motion on notice to \[[^f00172] the judge\] for his ruling upon any matter arising in the course of the proceedings.
