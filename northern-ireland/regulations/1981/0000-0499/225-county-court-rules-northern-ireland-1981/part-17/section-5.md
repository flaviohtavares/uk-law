# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 5

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 5. General account

*Extent: N.I.*

Unless \[[^f00170] the judge\] otherwise orders, every order for a general account of the estate of a deceased person shall direct an inquiry as to what parts (if any) of such estate are outstanding or undisposed of.
