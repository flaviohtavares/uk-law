> Text as at 2025-11-26.

## 10. Judge may require production of writ, etc.

*Extent: N.I.*

\[[^f00213] The judge\] may at any time require a next friend, guardian ad litem, \[[^f00214] controller\] or widow to obtain and produce the writ, pleadings and any other document used in the proceedings in the High Court.
