# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 9

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 9. Costs payable out of money received

*Extent: N.I.*

Where an order has been made by the High Court for payment of any costs to a solicitor out of the money recovered, the amount of such costs, if not paid before the money was received by the county court in accordance with Article 21 of the Order, shall, on the application of the solicitor, supported by the certificate of the taxing master or other appropriate officer of the High Court, be paid out of the money received, and any investments may be sold for that purpose if \[[^f00213] the judge or district judge as the case may be\] or, in his absence, the chief clerk thinks fit.
