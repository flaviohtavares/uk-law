# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 2

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 2. Application for payment out, etc.

*Extent: N.I.*

Where a person desires to apply to the court for the investment or payment out of court of any money or securities paid into court under this Order, the following provisions shall apply—

- (a) the application shall be made to \[[^f00261] the judge\] *ex parte*:
- (b) \[[^f00261] the judge\] on the hearing of the *ex parte* application may require notice of the application to be served on such persons as he thinks fit, and fix a day for the further hearing;
- (c) evidence in support of the application may be given by affidavit or in such other manner as \[[^f00261] the judge\] may direct.
