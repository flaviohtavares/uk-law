# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 2

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 2. Approval of \[ judge\] or reserved bidding[^f00333]

*Extent: N.I.*

The sale shall be subject to the approval of \[[^f00333] the judge\] or to a reserved bidding to be fixed by the court.
