# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 9

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 9. Where sale is subject to approval of \[ judge\][^f00336]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where a sale is subject to the approval of \[[^f00336] the judge\]—

- (a) the certificate of the \[[^f00335] district judge\] shall be in Form 159; and
- (b) the solicitor having carriage shall on notice to the proposed purchaser and the other parties make application to \[[^f00336] the judge\] by notice of motion in Form 160 for confirmation of the sale, and \[[^f00336] the judge\] may either confirm the sale by order in Form 161 or make such other order thereon as he thinks fit having regard to the court valuer’s report and the biddings.
