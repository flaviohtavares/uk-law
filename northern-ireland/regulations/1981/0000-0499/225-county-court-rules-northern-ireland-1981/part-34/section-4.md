# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 4

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 4. Bidding by parties

*Extent: N.I.*

A party to the suit shall not bid at the sale unless liberty to bid is given in the order for sale or the permission of \[[^f00333] the judge\] is obtained on application made on notice to the other parties to the suit.
