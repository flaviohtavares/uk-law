# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 4

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 4. Privilege

*Extent: N.I.*

Where privilege is claimed for any document, \[[^f00156] the judge or district judge as the case may be\] may inspect the document for the purpose of deciding whether the claim of privilege is valid.
