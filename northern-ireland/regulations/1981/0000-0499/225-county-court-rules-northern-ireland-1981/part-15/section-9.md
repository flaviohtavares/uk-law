# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 9

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 9. Non-compliance with the order

*Extent: N.I.*

Where an order for discovery, inspection or production of documents is not complied with, \[[^f00161] then \[[^f00160] the judge or district judge as the case may be\] may make such order as he thinks just, including, in particular, an order that the proceedings be dismissed or, as the case may be, an order that the notice of intention to defend be struck out and judgment be entered accordingly.\]
