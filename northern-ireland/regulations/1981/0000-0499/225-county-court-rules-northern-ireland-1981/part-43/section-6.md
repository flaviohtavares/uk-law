# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 6

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 6. Service on solicitor of party

*Extent: N.I.*

Where a party acts by a solicitor, service of any proceedings or document upon such solicitor, or delivery of the same at his office, or sending the same to him by post, shall be deemed to be good service upon the party for whom such solicitor acts, \[[^f00367] . . .\] except in cases where by these Orders or any other enactment personal service upon a party is required.
