# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 19A

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 19A. \[ Date when documents served by post deemed to be served[^f00377]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where a civil bill or any other document is served by ordinary first class post under these Rules, unless the contrary is proved, the document shall be deemed to have been served on the \[[^f00378] seventh business day\] after posting (excluding the day of posting).

(2) In this Rule “**business day**” has the same meaning as in Rule 27.\]
