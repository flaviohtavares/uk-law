# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 24

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 24. Searches

*Extent: N.I.*

Searches may be made on such days in each week as shall be fixed from time to time by \[[^f00379] the judge or district judge as the case may be\], and notice thereof shall be printed and kept posted on some conspicuous place in the Office.
