# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 7

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 7. Service by advertisement

*Extent: N.I.*

Where, by reason of the absence of any party or from any other sufficient cause, the service of any notice, proceedings, or document (other than a civil bill or petition) cannot be effected, \[[^f00368] the judge or district judge as the case may be\] may order notice by advertisement or otherwise in lieu of said service.
