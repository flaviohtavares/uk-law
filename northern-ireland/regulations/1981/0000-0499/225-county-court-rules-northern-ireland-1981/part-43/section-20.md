# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 20

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 20. Documents to be free from erasures and alterations

*Extent: N.I.*

Unless \[[^f00376] the judge or district judge as the case may be\] otherwise orders, no decree, dismiss, order, recognizance, or other document shall be received by the chief clerk or other officer unless the same is free from all unsightly or ambiguous erasures, interlineations or other alterations.
