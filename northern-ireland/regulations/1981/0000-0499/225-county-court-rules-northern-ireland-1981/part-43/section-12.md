# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 12

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 12. Interest on legacies

*Extent: N.I.*

Interest shall be computed on legacies at the rate of \[[^f00371] 6\] per centum per annum from the end of one year from the date of the death of the testator, unless otherwise ordered, or a different rate or time of payment is directed by the will or established by law.
