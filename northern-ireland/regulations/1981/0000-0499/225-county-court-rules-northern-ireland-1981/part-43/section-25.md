# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 25

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 25. Instructing solicitor to attend counsel

*Extent: N.I.*

Except by leave of \[[^f00379] the judge or district judge as the case may be\] on such terms as to costs or otherwise as to \[[^f00379] the judge or district judge as the case may be\] may seem just, no counsel shall be heard unless he is instructed by a solicitor who is either personally present or represented by someone in attendance upon counsel at the hearing.
