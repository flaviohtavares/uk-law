# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 6

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 6. Rules apply to civil proceedings except as otherwise provided

*Extent: N.I.*

Without prejudice to Part II of Order 32, in its application to cases stated under Article 61 of the Order on appeal from magistrate’s courts, \[[^f00550] or to Part IIA of Order 32,\] these Rules shall apply only to civil proceedings.
