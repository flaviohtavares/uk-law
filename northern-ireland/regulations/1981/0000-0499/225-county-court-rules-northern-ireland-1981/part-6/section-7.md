# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 7

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 7. Service deemed good

*Extent: N.I.*

\[[^f00069] The judge or district judge as the case may be\] may in any case declare the service actually effected sufficient.

**8.** \[[^f00070] A process server shall, as soon as possible after he has received a civil bill serve it upon the defendant.\]
