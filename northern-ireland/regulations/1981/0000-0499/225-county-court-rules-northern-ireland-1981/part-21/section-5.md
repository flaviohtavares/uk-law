# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 5

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 5. Payments into court not to be communicated to \[ judge or district judge as the case may be\][^f00202]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where money has been paid into court under Rule 2 that fact shall not be communicated to \[[^f00202] the judge or district judge as the case may be\] before the determination of all questions of liability and the amount of debt or damages.
