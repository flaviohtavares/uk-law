# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 1

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 1. Generally

*Extent: N.I.*

Actions or matters pending in the same court may be heard together by order of \[[^f00132] the judge or district judge as the case may be\] of his own motion or on the application of any party on notice.
