> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 8. Costs

*Extent: N.I.*

\[[^f00098] The judge or district judge as the case may be\], in the exercise of his discretion as to costs, may take into consideration the extent to which a defendant has or has not availed himself of such of the powers given by the preceding Rules of this Order as were available to him.
