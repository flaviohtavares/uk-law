# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 6

> Text as at 2025-11-26.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 6. Witnesses’ expenses

*Extent: N.I.*

The expenses of witnesses shall be measured by the arbitrator, as nearly as possible in accordance with the scales prevailing in the court from which the arbitration is referred and such expenses shall be set forth in the award and shall be subject to review by \[[^f00182] the judge\].
