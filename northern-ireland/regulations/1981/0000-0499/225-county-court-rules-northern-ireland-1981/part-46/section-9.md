# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 9

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 9. Certificate of decree and decree

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The certificate of the decree of \[[^f00393] the judge\] to be transmitted by the chief clerk to the Principal or District Registry shall be in Form 189.

(2) The decree shall be in Form 190 or 191 or, where the suit is stopped for want of jurisdiction, in Form 192.
