# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 10

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 10. Where all parties are not present at the hearing

*Extent: N.I.*

Upon the hearing of any testamentary civil bill, \[[^f00393] the judge\] may, whether or not all the parties to the suit are present, proceed to consider the subject-matter of the civil bill and make a decree thereon, or he may adjourn the proceedings from time to time as he thinks fit.
