# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 2

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 2. Payment out and transfer of funds and securities in court

*Extent: N.I.*

Save as is otherwise provided in Court Funds Rules, money paid into court under Rule 1(2) or securities purchased under Rule 1(3) and, the dividends or interest thereon shall not be sold, transferred or paid out to the party entitled thereto, except pursuant to the order of \[[^f00383] the judge or district judge as the case may be\].
