# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 19

> Text as at 2025-11-26.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 19. Records, costs and court fees

*Extent: N.I.*

Subject to Order 26 proceedings heard before the \[[^f00250] district judge\] shall for all purposes, including records, costs and court fees, be treated in like manner as if they had been heard before \[[^f00249] the judge\], save that as regards costs, Rule 19(1)(*a*) of Order 55 shall not apply.
