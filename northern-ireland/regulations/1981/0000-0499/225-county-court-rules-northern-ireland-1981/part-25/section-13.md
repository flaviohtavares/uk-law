# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 13

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 13. Inspection by \[ judge or district judge as the case may be\][^f00244]

*Extent: N.I.*

\[[^f00244] The judge or district judge as the case may be\] may inspect any property or thing concerning which any question may arise in any proceedings.
