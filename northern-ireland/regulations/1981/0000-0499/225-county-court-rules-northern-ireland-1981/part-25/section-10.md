# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 10

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 10. Vexatious or irrelevant question

*Extent: N.I.*

\[[^f00243] The judge or district judge as the case may be\] may disallow any question put in cross-examination to any party or witness which appears to \[[^f00243] the judge or district judge as the case may be\] to be vexatious or irrelevant.
