# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 5

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 5. \[Remuneration[^f00180]

*Extent: N.I.*

Every assessor shall receive for each half day’s attendance a fee of £43.00[^c-b438c2101a4e0b5b218084726310e6ba] together with such sum for his expenses as the judge or district judge may order.\]
