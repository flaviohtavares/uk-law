# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 4

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 4. Absence of assessor

*Extent: N.I.*

If at the time and place appointed for the hearing the assessor appointed does not attend, \[[^f00179] the judge\] may hear the action or matter without his assistance.
