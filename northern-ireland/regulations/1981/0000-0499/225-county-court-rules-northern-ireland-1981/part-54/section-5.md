# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 5

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 5. Payment into court not to be communicated to \[ the judge\][^f00476]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where money has been paid into court in accordance with this Order that fact shall not be communicated to \[[^f00476] the judge\] before the determination of the appeal.
