# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 1

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

**1.** (1) Where application is made under section 17 of the Married Women’s Property Act 1882[^f00263] particulars of the question to be submitted to the decision of \[[^f00264] the judge\] shall be filed in the Office and thereupon a summons shall be issued in Form 135 and shall be served together with a copy of the particulars \[[^f00265] not less than 28 days before the date fixed for the hearing\].

(2) All proceedings subsequent to the issue of the summons shall be had as if the proceeding had been commenced by an equity civil bill.
