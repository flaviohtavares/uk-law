# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 1

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

**1.** Where application is made under Articles 35, 38 or 40 of the Matrimonial Causes (Northern Ireland) Order 1978, particulars of the question to be submitted to the decision of \[[^f00281] the judge\] shall be filed in the Office and thereupon a summons shall be issued in the form specified in Form 137 and shall be served together with a copy of the particulars \[[^f00282] not less than 28 days before the date fixed for the hearing\].
