## 1. \[ Interpretation[^f00268]

*Extent: N.I.*

In this Order “**the Act**” means the Consumer Credit Act 1974[^f00269], a section referred to by number means the section so numbered in the Act and expressions which are defined in the Act have the same meaning in this Order as they have in the Act.
