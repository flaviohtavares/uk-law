# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 15

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 15. Declarations

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An application for a declaration under section 142(1)(*b*) or 142(2) shall be made by notice in Form 285.

(2) \[[^f00277] Paragraph (5) of Rule 9\] shall apply to a notice of application under paragraph (1) of this Rule.
