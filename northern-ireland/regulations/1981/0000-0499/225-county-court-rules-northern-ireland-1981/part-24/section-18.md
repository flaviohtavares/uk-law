> Text as at 2025-11-26.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 18. Proof of handwriting

*Extent: N.I.*

In any proceedings \[[^f00234] the judge or district judge as the case may be\] may, upon such terms as he may think proper, receive in evidence proof of the handwriting of any party or of any subscribing witness to any instrument whatsoever.
