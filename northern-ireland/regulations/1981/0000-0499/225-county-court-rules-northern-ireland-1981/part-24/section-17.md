# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 17

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 17. Proof of valuation of lands

*Extent: N.I.*

Without prejudice to any other enactment regarding proof of the valuation of lands, a copy or extract certified by the Commissioner of Valuation or an officer on his behalf to be a true copy of the latest \[[^f00235] capital value list or NAV list\] relating to the hereditament shall, for the purposes of any proceedings in a county court, be sufficient proof of the valuation of such hereditament until the contrary is shown.
