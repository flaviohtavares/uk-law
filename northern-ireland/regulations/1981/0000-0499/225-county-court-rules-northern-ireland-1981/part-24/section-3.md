> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 3. Petitions

*Extent: N.I.*

Evidence in support of or in opposition to a petition may be by affidavit unless \[[^f00223] the judge or district judge as the case may be\] otherwise directs.
