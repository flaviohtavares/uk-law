> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 1. Notice to insurer

*Extent: N.I.*

The notice of proceedings to be given by a claimant to an insurer (or the giver of a security) under Article 98 of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00435] shall be in Form 292, with such variations as the circumstances may require.
