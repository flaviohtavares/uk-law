# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 25

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 25. Proceedings in respect of judicial act: Notice to appropriate person

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where proceedings under section 7(1)(*a*) of the Act in respect of a judicial act are brought in a county court in accordance with section 9(1)(*a*) of the Act, the Court shall give notice of those proceedings in Form 398 to the appropriate person.

(2) The notice referred to in paragraph (1) shall be given by the Court having had regard to the list of authorised Northern Ireland Departments or authorised Departments of the Government of the United Kingdom published in pursuance of section 17 of the Crown Proceedings Act 1947[^f00460].
