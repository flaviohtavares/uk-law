# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 12

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 12. Application for order for taxation under Article 71F of the Order

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An application to the court under Article 71F(1) of the Order shall be made by notice in Form 317 and an order upon such application shall be in Form 318.

(2) An application to the \[[^f00444] district judge\] for an order under Article 71F(2) shall be made by notice in Form 319 and an order upon such application shall be in Form 320.

(3) The notice under paragraph (1) or (2) shall be served on the respondent and a copy thereof on the chief clerk together with a copy, if available, of the bill of costs to which the application relates, not less than \[[^f00448] 28 days before the date fixed for the hearing\].

\[[^f00449] . . .\]

(5) Unless otherwise directed, the application shall be heard and determined in chambers.
