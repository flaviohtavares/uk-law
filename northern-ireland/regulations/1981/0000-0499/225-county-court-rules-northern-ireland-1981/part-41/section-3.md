# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 3

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 3. Evidence

*Extent: N.I.*

It shall lie with the applicant to satisfy \[[^f00360] the judge or district judge as the case may be\] by oral evidence or affidavit of such facts as would under Rule 1 enable the application to be granted.
