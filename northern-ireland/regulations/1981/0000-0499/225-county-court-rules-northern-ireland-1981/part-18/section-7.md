# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 7

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 7. Review by judge

*Extent: N.I.*

The receiver or any person dissatisfied with the allowance or disallowance by the \[[^f00176] district judge\] of any item in the account may within eight days from receiving notice of the signing of the certificate under Rule 6 apply to \[[^f00175] the judge\] on notice for a review of the decision of the \[[^f00176] district judge\], and the judge may make such order on the application as he thinks fit.
