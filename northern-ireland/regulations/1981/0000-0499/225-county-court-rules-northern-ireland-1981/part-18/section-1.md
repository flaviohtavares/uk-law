# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 1

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 1. Appointment

*Extent: N.I.*

Where before, at or after the hearing of any proceedings it appears to \[[^f00175] the judge\] expedient that a receiver be appointed, such appointment may be made by \[[^f00175] the judge\] of his own motion or on the application of a party.
