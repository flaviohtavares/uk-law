# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 6

> Text as at 2025-11-26.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 6. Certificate of \[ district judge\][^f00176]

*Extent: N.I.*

The \[[^f00176] district judge\] shall after examining the account make and sign a certificate stating the result of the examination.
