# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 2

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 2. Security

*Extent: N.I.*

Every receiver other than an officer of the court shall, unless otherwise ordered, give such security to the \[[^f00176] district judge\] for the faithful discharge of his duties, and the payment over of money, as \[[^f00175] the judge\] shall direct.
