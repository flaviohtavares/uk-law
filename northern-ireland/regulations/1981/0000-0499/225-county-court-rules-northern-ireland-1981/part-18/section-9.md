# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 9

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 9. Payment of balance into court

*Extent: N.I.*

Any balance certified to be due from the receiver shall, subject to any direction given by \[[^f00177] the judge\] on review under Rule 7, be paid into court within fourteen days from the date of the certificate of the \[[^f00178] district judge\].
