> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 14. Decree rectifying or setting aside deed

*Extent: N.I.*

A decree rectifying a deed shall be in Form 151 and a decree setting aside a deed shall be in Form 152.
