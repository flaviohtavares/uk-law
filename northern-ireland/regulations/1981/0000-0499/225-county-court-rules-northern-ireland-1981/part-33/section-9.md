> Text as at 2025-11-26.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 9. Stay of execution of decrees in ejectment

*Extent: N.I.*

In all cases of decrees in ejectment, \[[^f00326] the judge\] may grant such stay of execution as he considers reasonable in the circumstances.
