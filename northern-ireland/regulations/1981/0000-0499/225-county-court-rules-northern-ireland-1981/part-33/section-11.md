## 11. Decree for balance of rent and costs under section 61 of the Landlord and Tenant Law Amendment Act, Ireland, 1860

*Extent: N.I.*

A decree for the balance of rent and costs under section 61 of the Landlord and Tenant Law Amendment Act, Ireland, 1860[^f00329] shall be in Form 146.
