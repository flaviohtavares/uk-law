# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 5

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 5. Amounts for which decree issued to be in decimal currency

*Extent: N.I.*

A decree for the payment of a sum of money must state the sum in decimal currency.
