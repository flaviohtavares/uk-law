# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 15

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 15. Order for preparation and execution of a deed

*Extent: N.I.*

Where an order directs any deed to be prepared and executed, it shall state by what party the deed is to be prepared and to whom it is to be submitted for approval and who is to bear the costs thereof; and if the parties cannot agree as to the form of the deed, \[[^f00331] the judge\] may, on the application of either party, settle the same himself or name counsel by whom the same is to be settled, subject to the final approval of \[[^f00331] the judge\] and his discretion as to costs.
