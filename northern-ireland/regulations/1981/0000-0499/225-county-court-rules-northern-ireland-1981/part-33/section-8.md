# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 8

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 8. Decrees for recovery of land

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A decree for the recovery of land shall be in \[[^f00325] Form 56,\] Form 143 or Form 144 as appropriate.

(2) A dismiss of a civil bill claiming recovery of land shall be in Form 145.
