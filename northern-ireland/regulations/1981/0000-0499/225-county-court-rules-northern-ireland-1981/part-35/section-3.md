# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 3

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 3. Costs

*Extent: N.I.*

\[[^f00338] The judge or district judge as the case may be\], upon the hearing of any ejectment proceedings where there is more than one defendant, may order that the costs of the proceedings shall be paid by and recovered from one or more of such defendants and not from the other or others of them.
