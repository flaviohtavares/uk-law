# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 7

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 7. Affidavit in support of petition

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The petition shall be supported by an affidavit by the petitioner giving particulars of every person whose interest may be effected by the proceedings and his relationship to the petitioner.

Provided that if the petitioner is under the age of 18 the affidavit shall be made by his next friend.
