# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 2

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 2. Effect of service of notice

*Extent: N.I.*

Subject to any order of \[[^f00122] the judge or district judge as the case may be\], the notice shall have effect as if it were a civil bill served by the defendant on the third party.
