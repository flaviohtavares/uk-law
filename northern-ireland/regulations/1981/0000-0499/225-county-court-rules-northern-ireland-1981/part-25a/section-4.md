> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 4. Representations by defendant[^M_F_7fd11175-2fd2-440c-84c9-59beca05f22f]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The defendant may lodge representations in writing with the chief clerk within 14 days of service on him of the application and shall, at the same time, serve a copy on the plaintiff.
