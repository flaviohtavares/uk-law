# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 2

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 2. Proceedings consequent on order under rule 1

*Extent: N.I.*

A warrant for committal to prison under an order made under rule 1 shall be in Form 267; and all other warrants shall be in the forms specified in the Fines Act (Ireland) 1851[^f00538].
