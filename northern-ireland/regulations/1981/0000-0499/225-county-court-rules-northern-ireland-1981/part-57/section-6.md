> Text as at 2025-11-26.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 6. Notice of motion for decree enforceable by committal

*Extent: N.I.*

A notice of motion for a decree which may be enforced by committal may be in Form 269 and shall, except where \[[^f00539] the judge\] otherwise determines, be supported by affidavit of the party making the application.
