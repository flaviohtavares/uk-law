# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 4

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 4. Repayment of fine

*Extent: N.I.*

If, in any case, after a fine has been paid the person on whom it was imposed shows cause which satisfies \[[^f00539] the judge\] that if cause had been shown at an earlier date, he would not have imposed a fine or would have imposed a smaller fine, or would not have ordered payment or full payment to be enforced, \[[^f00539] the judge\] may order the fine or any part thereof to be repaid.
