# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 14

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 14. Where judge may appoint next friend

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where proceedings in which a next friend is required are commenced without such next friend \[[^f00034] the judge or district judge as the case may be\] may—

- (a) on the application of any party or of his own motion appoint as next friend any person who consents to act and gives an undertaking in Form 5; or
- (b) order the proceedings to be struck out.

(2) Where a next friend is appointed under paragraph (1), Order 4 Rule 2(2) shall apply.
