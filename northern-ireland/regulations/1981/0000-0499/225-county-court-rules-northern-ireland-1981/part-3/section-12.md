# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 12

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 12. \[ Patients\][^f00035]

*Extent: N.I.*

A \[[^f00035] patient for whom a controller has been appointed\] may sue and defend by his \[[^f00036] controller\] and \[[^f00035] a patient for whom a controller has not been appointed\] may sue by his next friend and may defend by his guardian ad litem.
