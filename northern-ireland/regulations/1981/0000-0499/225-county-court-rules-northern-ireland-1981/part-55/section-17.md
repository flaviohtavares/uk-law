# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 17

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 17. \[Services fee for postal service[^f00522]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where service of a civil bill or other document has been effected by post under Rule 3(2)(*a*) of Order 6, the solicitor shall be entitled to a fee inclusive of outlay in lieu of the fees payable to process servers as follows £4.00[^c-6fa2392823b3e189a338e3919ede8fed]\].
