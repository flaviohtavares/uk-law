# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 15

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 15. Lands not separately rated

*Extent: N.I.*

In any proceedings for recovery of possession of lands not separately valued for rating \[[^f00521] the judge\] may make any apportionments of valuation necessary for the determination of the costs applicable.
