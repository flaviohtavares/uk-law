# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 8

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 8. Exercise of discretion in absence of party

*Extent: N.I.*

Any discretion exercisable by \[[^f00493] the judges\] as to the amount to be allowed for any fees or costs may be exercised notwithstanding that the party liable to pay such fees or costs is not present or represented.
