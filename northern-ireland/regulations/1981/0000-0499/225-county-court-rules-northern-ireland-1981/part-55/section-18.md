# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 18

> Text as at 2025-11-26.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 18. Injunctions claimed other than in equity proceedings

*Extent: N.I.*

In proceedings where an injunction is claimed under Article 13 of the Order, not being proceedings within the equity jurisdiction, the costs in relation to the hearing of the claim for an injunction shall be in the discretion of \[[^f00521] the judge or district judge as the case may be\], both as to incidence and amount.
