# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 13

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 13. Additional services

*Extent: N.I.*

Where it is necessary to serve more than one copy of a civil bill or other document initiating proceedings there shall be payable for each copy after the first a further sum of \[[^f00517] £1.00\].
