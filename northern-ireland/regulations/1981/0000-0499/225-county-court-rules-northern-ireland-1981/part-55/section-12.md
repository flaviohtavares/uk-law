# County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981, section 12

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 12. Costs of party appearing in person

*Extent: N.I.*

The amount of any costs awarded to a party other that a solicitor appearing in person in accordance with section 1 of the Litigants in Person (Costs and Expenses) Act 1975[^f00516] shall be such as may be determined by \[[^f00514] the judge or district judge as the case may be\].
