The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development, being a Department designated *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f4269c78393880ad194e275fb62ed21] [^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f4269c78393880ad194e275fb62ed21] [^f00002] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community, makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by that section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f4269c78393880ad194e275fb62ed21]

## 1. Citation and commencement (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-d00dcc0e92551d00393de3921e7b3707][^key-6f4269c78393880ad194e275fb62ed21]

These Regulations may be cited as The European Fisheries Fund (Grants) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008 and shall come into operation on 9th January 2009. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f4269c78393880ad194e275fb62ed21]

## 2. Amendment of the European Fisheries Fund (Grants) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008 (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-570f69801683984cb12febe2ce85030b][^key-6f4269c78393880ad194e275fb62ed21]

EXPLANATORY NOTE *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f4269c78393880ad194e275fb62ed21]

(This note is not part of the Regulations) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f4269c78393880ad194e275fb62ed21]

These Regulations correct a drafting error in The European Fisheries Fund (Grants) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008. Those Regulations provide that the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development may pay grants for the purposes of Title *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f4269c78393880ad194e275fb62ed21] IV *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f4269c78393880ad194e275fb62ed21] of Council Regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f4269c78393880ad194e275fb62ed21] [(EC *(affected by repeal, see note)*) 1198/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1198)[^key-6f4269c78393880ad194e275fb62ed21] on the European Fisheries Fund. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f4269c78393880ad194e275fb62ed21]
