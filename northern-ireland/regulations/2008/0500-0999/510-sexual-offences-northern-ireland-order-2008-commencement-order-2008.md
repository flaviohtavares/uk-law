# Sexual Offences (Northern Ireland) Order 2008 (Commencement) Order 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 510 (C. 30)

Criminal Law

Sexual Offences (Northern Ireland) Order 2008 (Commencement) Order 2008

Made

24th December 2008

The Secretary of State makes the following Order, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 1(3) of the Sexual Offences (Northern Ireland) Order 2008[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Sexual Offences (Northern Ireland) Order 2008 (Commencement) Order 2008.

## 2. Commencement

The Sexual Offences (Northern Ireland) Order 2008, in so far as not already in operation, shall come into operation on 2<sup>nd</sup> February 2009.

Paul Goggins

Minister of State

Northern Ireland Office

24 December 2008

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order commences the Sexual Offences (Northern Ireland) Order 2008 as from the 2<sup>nd</sup> February 2009. Part 1 and Articles 80 and 82 of the 2008 Order came into operation one week after the day on which that Order was made.
