# The Road Traffic (Traffic Wardens) (Revocation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 341

Road Traffic And Vehicles

The Road Traffic (Traffic Wardens) (Revocation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2008

Made

8th August 2008

Affirmed by resolution of the Assembly on

21st October 2008

Coming into operation in accordance with Article 1

The Department for Regional Development[^f00001] makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 44 of the Road Traffic Regulation (Northern Ireland) Order 1997[^f00002] and now vested in it[^f00003].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as The Road Traffic (Traffic Wardens) (Revocation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2008 and shall come into operation on the day after it is affirmed by resolution of the Assembly.

## 2. Revocation

The Road Traffic (Traffic Wardens) Order (Northern Ireland) 1999[^f00004] is revoked.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Regional Development on 8th August 2008

(L.S.)

R Sherman

A senior officer of the Department for Regional Development

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Road Traffic (Traffic Wardens) Order (Northern Ireland) 1999.
