# The Court Funds (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 156

Judicature, Northern Ireland

The Court Funds (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2008

Made

2nd April 2008

Coming into operation

19th May 2008

To be laid before Parliament

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 82(1) of the Judicature (Northern Ireland) Act 1978[^f00001] and with the concurrence of the Treasury, makes the following Rules:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Rules may be cited as the Court Funds (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2008 and shall come into operation on 19th May 2008.

## 2. Amendments

The Court Funds Rules (Northern Ireland) 1979[^f00002] are amended as follows.

**3.** In rule 2(1)—

- (a) for “the Common Investments Funds Scheme” substitute “the Common Investment Scheme 2004”[^f00003]; and
- (b) for “section 1 of the Administration of Justice Act 1965” substitute “section 42 of the Administration of Justice Act 1982”[^f00004].

**4.** In rule 6(2) omit “of Ireland at its principal office in Belfast”.

**5.** In rule 31 for “the Common Investment Funds Scheme 1965” substitute “the Common Investment Scheme 2004”.

**6.** After Form 8 insert the new Form 9 set out in the Schedule.

## SCHEDULE

Rule 6

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules amend the Court Funds Rules (Northern Ireland) 1979 \[[S.R. 1979 No. 105](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1979/105)\] so as to—

- amend a number of references in respect of the Common Investment Scheme;
- remove a reference to the Bank of Ireland so that the Accountant General is no longer obliged to maintain the County Court Account at that particular institution; and
- add a new form of lodgment schedule for use in situations where a lending institution finds itself holding on behalf of an untraceable mortgagor the proceeds of sale of property it has sold as mortgagee and wishes to lodge same in court.
