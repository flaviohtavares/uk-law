# The Taxis (Enniskillen) (Revocation) Bye-Laws (Northern Ireland) 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 257

Road Traffic And Vehicles

The Taxis (Enniskillen) (Revocation) Bye-Laws (Northern Ireland) 2008

Made

18th June 2008

Coming into operation

25th July 2008

The Department of the Environment makes the following Bye-Laws in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 65(1) and (2) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Bye-Laws may be cited as the Taxis (Enniskillen) (Revocation) Bye-Laws (Northern Ireland) 2008 and shall come into operation on 25<sup>th</sup> July 2008.

## 2. Revocation

The Taxis (Enniskillen) Bye-Laws (Northern Ireland) 2006[^f00002] are hereby revoked.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 18<sup>th</sup> June 2008.

Stanley Duncan

A senior officer of the

Department of the Environment

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Bye-Laws)

These Bye-Laws revoke the Taxis (Enniskillen) Bye-Laws (Northern Ireland) 2006. The effect of the revocation is to abolish the taxi stands at Paget Lane.
