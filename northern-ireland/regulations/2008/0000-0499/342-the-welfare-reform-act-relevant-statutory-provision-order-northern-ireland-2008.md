# The Welfare Reform Act (Relevant Statutory Provision) Order (Northern Ireland) 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 342

Social Security

The Welfare Reform Act (Relevant Statutory Provision) Order (Northern Ireland) 2008

Made

8th August 2008

Coming into operation

1st September 2008

The Department for Social Development makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 39(7) of the Welfare Reform Act (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Welfare Reform Act (Relevant Statutory Provision) Order (Northern Ireland) 2008 and shall come into operation on 1st September 2008.

## 2. Relevant statutory provision

A relevant statutory provision for the purposes of section 39(1) of the Welfare Reform Act (Northern Ireland) 2007 (information relating to certain benefits) is Article 4 of the Housing Support Services (Northern Ireland) Order 2002[^f00002] (grants for housing support services).

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 8th August 2008

(L.S.)

John O’Neill

A senior officer of the Department for Social Development

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

Section 39(1) of the Welfare Reform Act (Northern Ireland) 2007 (“the Act”) provides that information relating to certain benefits may be supplied to certain persons for purposes connected with the application of grant paid under a “relevant statutory provision” towards expenditure related to welfare services. This Order provides that a relevant statutory provision is Article 4 of the Housing Support Services (Northern Ireland) Order 2002. That Article provides that the Northern Ireland Housing Executive may pay grants to eligible persons towards expenditure incurred by them in relation to housing support services.

Section 39 of the Act, the enabling provision under which this Order is made, was brought into operation, for the purpose only of authorising the making of orders or regulations, on 8th August 2008 and, in so far as not already in operation, on 1st September 2008 by virtue of the Welfare Reform (2007 Act) (Commencement No. 6 and Transitional and Savings Provisions) Order (Northern Ireland) 2008 ([S.R. 2008 No. 339 (C. 18)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2008/339)).
