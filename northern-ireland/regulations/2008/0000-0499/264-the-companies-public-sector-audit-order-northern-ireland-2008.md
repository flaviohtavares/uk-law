## 1. Title and commencement (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-f7310065e29f7abd730664c0df55fa85][^c-4621fe478586df29603702fdb2277ed0]

This Order may be cited as the Companies (Public Sector Audit) Order (Northern Ireland) 2008 and shall come into operation on 1st July 2008. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4621fe478586df29603702fdb2277ed0]

## 2. Listed Bodies (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-6065babb45ce2b81ddd10abc09b1f71c][^c-4621fe478586df29603702fdb2277ed0]

In respect of any financial year ending after the coming into operation of this Order the accounts of bodies listed in the Schedule shall be audited by the Comptroller and Auditor General. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4621fe478586df29603702fdb2277ed0]
