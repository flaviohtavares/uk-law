# Reporting of Prices of Milk Products Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

2008 No. 240

Agriculture

Reporting of Prices of Milk Products Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008

Made

3rd June 2008

Coming into operation

1st July 2008

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development is designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community.

These Regulations make provision for a purpose mentioned in that section and it appears to the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development that it is expedient for the references in these Regulations to a Community instrument or to any provision of a Community instrument to be construed as a reference to that instrument or that provision as amended from time to time.

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 2(2) of, and paragraph 1A of Schedule 2 to, the European Communities Act 1972[^f00003].

## 1. Citation and commencement[^key-5f014d3efdf58a8ddc37d2dbc49dd980]

These Regulations may be cited as the Reporting of Prices of Milk Products Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008 and shall come into operation on 1<sup>st</sup> July 2008.

## 2. Interpretation[^key-1774f94d8a808c7bc58097130546b6a7]

(1) In these Regulations—

- [^key-0cf61602c8e229c4fc0287c14e76107d]...
- “the Department” means the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development;
- “milk processor” means a person operating an establishment which manufactures milk products;
- “[^key-7e787e8af4e94c9f6183a7079ec129eb]**milk products**” means whey powder, skimmed milk powder, whole milk powder, butter, cheeses (including commodity cheeses) and raw milk.

(2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00005] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

## 3. Provision of information on prices of milk products[^key-a864f692335322beb74f39e54a65bc31]

(1) A milk processor shall provide to the Department such information relating to the prices of milk products as the Department may by notice require [^key-593961ccdbd4a524ba10f62863761ae8]....

(2) The notice referred to in paragraph (1) may require the milk processor to provide the information on a regular basis, and may specify when, and in what format, the information shall be provided.

## 4. Offences[^key-5e882ce4cf38f0a17de335188d8aefa7]

A person who fails to comply with a notice referred to in regulation 3(1) shall be guilty of an offence, and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale.

## 5. Revocation[^key-97b20fc5b7e16e196903187112a68c00]

The Reporting of Prices of Milk Products Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005[^f00006] are revoked.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 3<sup>rd</sup> June 2008.

(L.S.)

Liam McKibben

A senior officer of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations, which come into operation on 1<sup>st</sup> July 2008, revoke and remake with amendments the Reporting of Prices of Milk Products Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005 ([S.R.2005 No. 286](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2005/286)).

The Regulations implement Article 6 of [Commission Regulation (EC) No. 562/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/0562) (OJ No L95, 14.4.05, p. 11) laying down rules for the implementation of Council Regulation [(EC) No 1255/1999](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1999/1255) as regards communications between the Member States and the Commission in the milk and milk products sector, as amended from time to time.

Regulation 3 requires milk processors to provide the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development with such information relating to the prices of certain milk products as the Department may require by notice. Regulation 4 sets out offences.
