# The Motor Hackney Carriages (Belfast) (Amendment) By-Laws (Northern Ireland) 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 245

Road Traffic And Vehicles

The Motor Hackney Carriages (Belfast) (Amendment) By-Laws (Northern Ireland) 2008

Made

5th June 2008

Coming into operation

1st August 2008

The Department of the Environment makes the following By-Laws in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 65(1) and (2) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These By-Laws may be cited as the Motor Hackney Carriages (Belfast) (Amendment) By-Laws (Northern Ireland) 2008 and shall come into operation on 1<sup>st</sup> August 2008.

## 2. By-Laws amended

For by-law 26(1) of the By-Laws relating to Motor Hackney Carriages standing or plying for hire made by the Council of the County Borough of Belfast on 4<sup>th</sup> June 1951[^f00002] there shall be substituted—

> **26.** (1) The fares payable for the hire of a Motor Hackney Carriage within the prescribed limits shall be—
>
> - (a) where not more than 4 passengers are carried—
>
>   | (1)<br>*Item* | (2)<br>*Fare Title* | (3)<br>*Day/Time* | (4)<br>*Calculation*<br>*of fare* | (5)<br>*Rate* |
>   | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
>   | 1\. | Rate 1 | Mon-Fri 06.00-20.00 | For the first 0.5 miles (805 metres) or part thereof, or for the first 2 minutes and 55 seconds (175 seconds) or part thereof.<br>For every subsequent 135.3 yards (123.7 metres) or part thereof, or 27 seconds or part thereof. | Minimum £3.00<br>Maximum £3.00<br>Minimum £0.10<br>Maximum £0.10 |
>   | 2\. | Rate 2 | Mon-Fri 20.00-24.00 and Sat-Sun 06.00-24.00 | For the first 0.25 mile (402.2 metres) or part thereof, or 1 minutes 35 seconds or part thereof.<br>For every subsequent 125.7 yards (115 metres) or part thereof, or 27 seconds or part thereof. | Minimum £3.00<br>Maximum £3.00<br>Minimum £0.10<br>Maximum £0.10 |
>   | 3\. | Rate 3 | Any Day 24.00-06.00 | For the first 0.125 mile (201.1 metres) or part thereof, or 51 seconds or part thereof.<br>For every subsequent 117.3 yards (107.2 metres) or part thereof, or 27 seconds or part thereof. | Minimum £3.00<br>Maximum £3.00<br>Minimum £0.10<br>Maximum £0.10 |
>   | 4\. | Rate 4 | 14.00 Christmas Eve – 06.00 27 Dec. and 20.00 New Year’s Eve – 24.00 1 Jan. | For the first 0.25 miles (402.2 metres) or part thereof, or 1 minutes 35 seconds (95 seconds) or part thereof.<br>For every subsequent 125.7 yards (115 metres) or part thereof, or 27 seconds or part thereof. | Minimum £4.00<br>Maximum £4.00<br>Minimum £0.20<br>Maximum £0.20 |
> - (b) additional fare—
>   - (i) for each passenger exceeding 4 carried for the entire distance—
>
>     |  |  |
>     | --- | --- |
>     | minimum | 40p |
>     | maximum | 40p |
>   - (ii) for each piece of luggage carried on the outside of the Motor Hackney Carriage—
>
>     |  |  |
>     | --- | --- |
>     | minimum | 20p |
>     | maximum | 20p. |
>
>   In assessing additional fares, every child under the age of 6 years and/or one child over 6 years and under 12 years shall be disregarded. Two children between the ages of 6 years and 12 years shall be counted as one passenger.

.

## 3. Revocation

By-law 2(4) of the Motor Hackney Carriages (Belfast) (Amendment) By-Laws (Northern Ireland) 2006[^f00003] and the Motor Hackney Carriages (Belfast) (Amendment No. 2) By-Laws (Northern Ireland) 2006[^f00004] are revoked.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 5<sup>th</sup> June 2008.

Stanley Duncan

A senior officer of the

Department of the Environment

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the By-Laws)

These By-laws amend the Motor Hackney Carriages (Belfast) By-laws made by the Council of the County Borough of Belfast on 4<sup>th</sup> June 1951 by revising the rates of fare to be charged.

The percentage rate of increase varies from 13% to 19% depending on the rate band employed.

By-law 2(4) of the Motor Hackney Carriages (Belfast) (Amendment) By-Laws (Northern Ireland) 2006 and the Motor Hackney Carriages (Belfast) (Amendment No. 2) By-Laws (Northern Ireland) 2006 are revoked as a consequence of these By-Laws.
