# The Motor Vehicles (Taxi Drivers’ Licences) (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 387

Road Traffic And Vehicles

The Motor Vehicles (Taxi Drivers’ Licences) (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008

Made

18th September 2008

Coming into operation

31st October 2008

The Department of the Environment makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 79A(2) and 218(1) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Taxi Drivers’ Licences) (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008 and shall come into operation on 31st October 2008.

## 2. Amendment of the Motor Vehicles (Taxi Drivers’ Licences) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1991

In regulation 4 of the Motor Vehicles (Taxi Drivers’ Licences) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1991[^f00002] (fee for a licence), for paragraph (1) substitute—

> (1) The fee for a first licence shall be £140.00 and for renewal of a licence shall be £105.00.

.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 18<sup>th</sup> September 2008.

Stanley Duncan

A senior officer of the

Department of the Environment

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend regulation 4(1) of the Motor Vehicles (Taxi Drivers’ Licences) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1991 by increasing the fee for a first licence from £110.00 to £140.00 and the fee for the renewal of a licence from £75.00 to £105.00.
