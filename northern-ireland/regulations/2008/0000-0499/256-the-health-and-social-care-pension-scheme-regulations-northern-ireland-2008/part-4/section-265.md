## 265. Cases with more than 10 years of officer service

*Extent: N.I.*

Where a member has more than 10 years of pensionable service before first becoming a [^key-46d399dbdf8cbfffc4ee5a7b253b6950]... medical practitioner, the reference amount shall be the better of—

- (a) the amount determined by calculation method A, and
- (b) the base amount.
