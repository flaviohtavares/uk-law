# The Health and Social Care (Pension Scheme) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008, section 134

> Text as at 2025-06-18.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 134. General prohibition on unauthorised payments

*Extent: N.I.*

Nothing in these Regulations[^c-997087c8169a43dd50c04653c29a28c0] requires or authorises the making of any payment, which, if made, would be an unauthorised payment for the purposes of Part 4 of the Finance Act 2004 (see section 160(5) of that Act).
