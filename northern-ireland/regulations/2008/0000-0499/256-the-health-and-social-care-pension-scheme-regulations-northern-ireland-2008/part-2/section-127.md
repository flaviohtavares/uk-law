# The Health and Social Care (Pension Scheme) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008, section 127

> Text as at 2025-06-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 127. Beneficiaries who are incapable of looking after their affairs

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) In the case of a beneficiary who, in the opinion of the Department, is by reason of illness, mental disorder, minority or otherwise unable to look after the beneficiary's affairs, the Department may—

- (a) use any amount due to the beneficiary under the Scheme for the beneficiary's benefit, or
- (b) pay it to some other person to do so.

(2) Payment of an amount to a person other than the beneficiary under paragraph (1) discharges the Department from any obligation under the Scheme in respect of the amount.
