# The Health and Social Care (Pension Scheme) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008, section 117

> Text as at 2025-06-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 117. Reduction of pension

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) If the condition in paragraph (2) is met, the amount of the old service pension for any financial year[^key-6d0425ae6fc601239833341f47287544] is reduced.

(2) The condition is that the employed pensioner's relevant income for the financial year[^key-26d3739b3156f1d06772e966799454f4] exceeds the employed pensioner's previous pay.

(3) The amount of the reduction under paragraph (1) is equal to that excess but cannot exceed the enhancement amount.

(4) For the meaning of “relevant income” and “enhancement amount” see regulation 118.

(5) For the meaning of “previous pay” see regulation 119.

(6)[^key-2a5607acad096a26c5a1b1f5f9a34e4d] For the purpose of calculating the reduction to be made under paragraph (1) in respect of any part of a financial year, the amount of the member’s previous pay will be reduced proportionately.

(7)[^key-2a5607acad096a26c5a1b1f5f9a34e4d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(8) If the member has a guaranteed minimum under section 10 of the 1993 Act in relation to the old service pension, nothing in this regulation requires the reduction of the old service pension below the amount of the member's guaranteed minimum in relation to it.
