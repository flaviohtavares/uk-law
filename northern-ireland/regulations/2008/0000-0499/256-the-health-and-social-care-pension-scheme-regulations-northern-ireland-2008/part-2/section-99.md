> Text as at 2025-06-18.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 99. Effect of transfers-out

*Extent: N.I.*

If a transfer value payment is made under this Chapter in respect of a person's rights under this Section of[^key-bc3cdae6122cb0d1162fa89cdb6fbf8e] the Scheme, those rights are extinguished.
