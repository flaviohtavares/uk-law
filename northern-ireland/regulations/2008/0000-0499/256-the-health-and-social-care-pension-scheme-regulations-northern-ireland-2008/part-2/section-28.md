# The Health and Social Care (Pension Scheme) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008, section 28

> Text as at 2025-06-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 28. Contribution rate for members other than non-GP providers[^c-d7dc157480fbcf798ef0144bd10b7fb1][^c-82196db9db30ae30d9a54b1fc9475e60]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Contributions under regulation 27(1)(a) must be paid at the member’s contribution rate for the scheme year in question.

(2)[^c-381ef4d10f30b1f71f8c5ea374b179b9] A member’s contribution rate for each scheme year from 2015-2016[^c-310dd406961a391e520c23073dce7f65] is the percentage specified in column 2 of the following table in respect of the corresponding pensionable pay band specified in column 1 of that table into which the member’s pensionable pay falls.

Scheme Years from 2015-2016[^c-9cfeb05beec9002bba26c07d73b2a163]

| *Column 1*<br>*Pensionable Pay band* | *Column 2*<br>*Contribution percentage rate* |
| --- | --- |
| Up to £15,431 | 5% |
| £15,432 to £21,477 | 5.6% |
| £21,478 to £26,823 | 7.1% |
| £26,824 to £47,485 | 9.3% |
| £47,486 to £70,630 | 12.5% |
| £70,631 to £111,376 | 13.5% |
| £111,377 to any higher amount | 14.5% |

(2A)[^c-641c3b3055cb7ffc4c5e4d1fcfc8eddf] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) The pensionable pay bands and contribution percentage rates shall be determined in accordance with the tables set out in this regulation[^c-1b88939780b3590acc42dcfdf49f81e4] in respect of each scheme year.
