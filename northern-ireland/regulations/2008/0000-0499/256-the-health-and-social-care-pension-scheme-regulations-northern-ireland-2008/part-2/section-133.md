# The Health and Social Care (Pension Scheme) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008, section 133

> Text as at 2025-06-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 133. Determination of questions

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Except as otherwise provided by this Part, any question arising under the Scheme is to be determined by the Department.

(2) Any such disagreement as is referred to in Article 50 of the 1995 Order (resolution of disputes) must be resolved by the Department in accordance with any arrangements applicable under that Article.
