# The Health and Social Care (Pension Scheme) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008, section 60

> Text as at 2025-06-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 60. Reduction in pension debit member’s benefits[^c-b9c3ea99be7b628c63ab3b349fc78633]

*Extent: N.I.*

 

(1) The benefits to which a pension debit member is entitled under this Chapter are subject to the reduction to be made under Article 28 of the 1999 Order.

(2) In the case of a 2008 Section Optant, this regulation is subject to paragraph (2) of regulation 136H.
