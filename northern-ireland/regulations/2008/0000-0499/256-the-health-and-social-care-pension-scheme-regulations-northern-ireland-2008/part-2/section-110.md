# The Health and Social Care (Pension Scheme) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008, section 110

> Text as at 2025-06-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 110. General rule: separate treatment of service etc

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The general rule is that, in accordance with regulations 65 and 91—

- (a) the re-employed member's pensionable service in respect of the earlier service and the later service are treated separately, and
- (b) the re-employed member's reckonable pay in respect of the earlier service and the later service is determined separately.

(2) This regulation is subject to the provisions mentioned in regulations 65(4) and 91(5).
