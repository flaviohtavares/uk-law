# The Health and Social Care (Pension Scheme) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008, section 260X

> Text as at 2025-06-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 260X. Application of Chapter 11 of this Part[^c-19483b2d3af8f38e66b91dd8dd2f7487]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This Chapter makes provision in relation to pensioner members of the 1995 Section of the Scheme who are eligible to be members of this Section of the Scheme in accordance with paragraph (1)(b)(ii) of regulation 153A (Eligibility: transitional).

(2) A member of this Section of the Scheme to whom this Chapter applies is referred to as a “Waiting Period Joiner”.
