# The Health and Social Care (Pension Scheme) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008, section 187

> Text as at 2025-06-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 187. Reduction in pension debit member’s benefits[^c-475a36ef0286d06a3c89453a1c208cc9]

*Extent: N.I.*

 

(1) The benefits to which a pension debit member is entitled under this Chapter are subject to the reduction to be made under Article 28 of the 1999 Order (Reduction under pension sharing order following divorce or nullity of marriage).

(2) In the case of a 2008 Section Optant, this regulation is subject to paragraph (2) of regulation 260H.
