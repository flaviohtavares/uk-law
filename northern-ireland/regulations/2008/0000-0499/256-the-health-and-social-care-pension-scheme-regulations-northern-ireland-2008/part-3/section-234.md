# The Health and Social Care (Pension Scheme) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008, section 234

> Text as at 2025-06-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 234. Bulk transfers in

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies if—

- (a) the employment of one or more persons (“the transferred employees”) is transferred without their consent to a new employer,
- (b) on that transfer the transferred employees cease to be active members of an occupational pension scheme (“**the former employer's scheme**”),
- (c) after that transfer the transferred employees become active members of this Section of[^c-a653b875a1b2b34dbd6730a797e8c4c4] the Scheme,
- (d) the Department has agreed special terms for the acceptance of transfer value payments in respect of the transferred employees from the former employer's scheme, after consulting the Scheme actuary, and
- (e) the transferred employees have consented in writing to their rights being transferred in accordance with those terms.

(2) This Section of[^c-b290c93053ea72368aa2fb94cbd202a9] the Scheme has effect with such modifications as are necessary to give effect to the terms mentioned in paragraph (1)(e).

(3) If the transfer is directly or indirectly attributable to a statutory provision, this Section of the Scheme has effect with such modifications as the Department considers necessary in consequence of the transfer.
