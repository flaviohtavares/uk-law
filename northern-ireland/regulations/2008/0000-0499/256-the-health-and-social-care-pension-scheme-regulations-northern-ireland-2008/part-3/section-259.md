# The Health and Social Care (Pension Scheme) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008, section 259

> Text as at 2025-06-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 259. Prohibition on assignment or charging of benefits

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Any assignment of, or charge on, or any agreement to assign or charge, any right to a benefit under this Section of[^c-310033b3f67d6609db24f2a3a21f963d] the Scheme is void.

(2) On the bankruptcy of any person entitled to a benefit under this Section of the Scheme, no part of the benefit may be paid to the person's trustee in bankruptcy or other person acting on behalf of the creditors, except in accordance with an order under Article 280 or 283 of the Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989 [^c19303511] (income payments orders).
