# The Health and Social Care (Pension Scheme) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008, section 150

> Text as at 2025-06-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 150. Exclusions and deductions from pensionable earnings — all practitioners

*Extent: N.I.*

Any sum that is withheld or otherwise recovered from a practitioner under regulation 26 of and Schedule 5 to the Health and Personal Social Services (General Medical Service Contracts) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004 [^c19303321] will be excluded or deducted from the practitioner's pensionable earnings in such manner and to such extent as the Department may approve.
