# The Health and Social Care (Pension Scheme) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008, section 151

> Text as at 2025-06-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 151. Limit on pensionable earnings — dentist performers carrying on deceased person's business

*Extent: N.I.*

In the case of a dentist performer employed by persons carrying on a deceased practitioner's dentistry business, pensionable earnings cannot exceed the total of the amount paid to him by those persons, plus any amounts paid to him by a Health and Social Services Board that those persons allow him to retain.
