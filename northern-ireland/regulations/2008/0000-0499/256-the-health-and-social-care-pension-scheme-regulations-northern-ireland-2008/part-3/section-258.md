## 258. General prohibition on unauthorised payments

*Extent: N.I.*

Nothing in these regulations requires or authorises the making of any payment, which, if made, would be an unauthorised payment for the purposes of Part 4 of the Finance Act 2004 (see section 160(5) of that Act).
