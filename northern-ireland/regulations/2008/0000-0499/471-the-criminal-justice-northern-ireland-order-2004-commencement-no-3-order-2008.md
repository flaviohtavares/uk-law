# The Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2004 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 471 (C. 27)

Criminal Law

The Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2004 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2008

Made

20th November 2008

The Secretary of State makes the following Order, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 1(3) of the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2004 [^f00001].

## 1. Citation and Interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2004 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2008.

(2) In this Order

- “the 2004 Order” means the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2004

## 2. Commencement

The provisions of the 2004 Order specified in the Schedule to this Order shall come into operation on 2<sup>nd</sup> February 2009.

Paul Goggins

Minister of State

Northern Ireland Office

20th November 2008

## SCHEDULE

Article 2

**1.** Article 10 (live links in criminal proceedings)

**2.** Article 11 (effect of, and rescission of, direction)

**3.** Article 12 (Magistrates’ Court permitted to sit at other locations)

**4.** Article 13 (warning to jury)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into operation on 2<sup>nd</sup> February 2009 provisions of the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2004 dealing with the provisions of live links in criminal proceedings.
