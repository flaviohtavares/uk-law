## 1. Citation and commencement (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-4c1962471503e4450f01132a6d57fe58][^key-02b376f59ff78572379565bca4a86978][^key-71e381a4c80299d0343d3d89572cce28]

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008 and shall come into operation on 12th February 2008. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-71e381a4c80299d0343d3d89572cce28]

(3) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-71e381a4c80299d0343d3d89572cce28][^f00004] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Northern Ireland Assembly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-71e381a4c80299d0343d3d89572cce28]
