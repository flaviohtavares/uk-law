## 100. Interpretation[^c-ee19c8c64ad9607307bcc10644a15143]

*Extent: N.I.*

In this Chapter “special account” means, where a claimant is carrying on a commercial activity in respect of which assistance is received under the self-employment route, the account into which the gross receipts from that activity are payable during the period in respect of which such assistance is received.
