## 113. Calculation of capital in the United Kingdom[^key-340e13f172f3dc67d4ae6df75006afbe]

*Extent: N.I.*

Capital which a claimant possesses in the United Kingdom is to be calculated at its current market or surrender value less—

- (a) where there would be expenses attributable to sale, 10 per cent.; and
- (b) the amount of any encumbrance secured on it.
