## 84. Income of participants in the self-employment route[^c-3b8ea6db8be9e934f77fd6c8541b8276]

*Extent: N.I.*

Chapters 2, 3, 4, 6, 8 and 9 and regulations 132 to 137, 142 and 143 do not apply to any income which is to be calculated in accordance with Chapter 5 (participants in the self-employment route).
