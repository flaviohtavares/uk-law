## 135. Relationship with amounts to be disregarded under Schedule 8[^c-a801d16b16049a21738d508a6b0b71db]

*Extent: N.I.*

No part of a student’s covenant income or grant income is to be disregarded under paragraph 15 of Schedule 8 (charitable and voluntary payments).
