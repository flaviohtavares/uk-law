## 87. Calculation of income and capital of students[^c-8dea69025d61c1d8419d49246d77d835]

*Extent: N.I.*

The provisions of Chapters 2 to 7 (income and capital) are to have effect in relation to students and their partners subject to the provisions set out in Chapter 10 (students).
