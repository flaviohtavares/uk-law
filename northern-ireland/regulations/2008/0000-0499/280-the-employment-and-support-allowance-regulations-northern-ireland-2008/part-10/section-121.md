## 121. Disregard of payments treated as not relevant income[^key-3d6fa650805021a0a09a8f173736b4bc]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where the Department treats any payment as not being relevant income for the purposes of section 72A of the Administration Act[^f00118] (payment of benefit where maintenance payments collected by Department), that payment is to be disregarded in calculating a claimant’s income.
