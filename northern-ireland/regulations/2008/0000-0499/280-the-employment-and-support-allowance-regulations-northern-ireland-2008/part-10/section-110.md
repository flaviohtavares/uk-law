## 110. Capital limit[^c-d29ae7f7759f3656548dcd53d56d059c]

*Extent: N.I.*

For the purposes of paragraph 6(1)(b) of Schedule 1 to the Act as it applies to an income-related allowance (no entitlement to benefit if capital exceeds prescribed amount), the prescribed amount is £16,000.
