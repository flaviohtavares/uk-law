## 120. Treatment of ... liable relative payments[^key-34eb94f65ef262e7ac5dea8b60c51f35][^key-570ec3b6441e3c5038a167210186a26b]

*Extent: N.I.*

Subject to regulation 121 [^key-fc07906ef84a8590e245a6714d9d32a9]... , a payment—

- (a) to the extent that it is not a payment of income, is to be treated as income;
- (b) is to be taken into account in accordance with the following provisions of this Chapter.
