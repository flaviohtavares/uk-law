## 24. Matters to be taken into account in determining good cause in relation to regulation 22 or 23[^c-07d91d07d402b4f3b66c404dc1047430]

*Extent: N.I.*

The matters to be taken into account in determining whether a claimant has good cause under regulation 22 or 23 include—

- (a) whether the claimant was outside Northern Ireland at the relevant time;
- (b) the claimant’s state of health at the relevant time; and
- (c) the nature of any disability the claimant has.
