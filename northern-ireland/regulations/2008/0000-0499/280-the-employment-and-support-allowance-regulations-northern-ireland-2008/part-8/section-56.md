# The Employment and Support Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008, section 56

## 56. Notification of interview[^c-932f5274b2b7f3d2015d043bb506d15b]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department must notify the claimant of the requirement to take part in[^c-c12e29e48f018a70fe7632ddaa9c34db] the work-focused interview including details of the date, time and if required to attend in person, the[^c-7fd19a4858ec4c7e490bb0c48eb79963] place of the interview.

(2) A work-focused interview may take place at a claimant’s home if it is determined that requiring the claimant to attend elsewhere would cause undue inconvenience to, or endanger the health of, the claimant.

(3) The notification referred to in paragraph (1) may be in writing or otherwise.
