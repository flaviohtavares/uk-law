## 60. Requirement to take part in a work-focused interview not to apply[^c-f6eb1d8cd37f570cce257fd8a8f52beb]

*Extent: N.I.*

The Department may determine that a requirement on a claimant to take part in a work-focused interview is not to apply, or is to be treated as not having applied, if that interview would not be, or would not have been, of assistance because the claimant is, or was likely to be, starting or returning to work.
