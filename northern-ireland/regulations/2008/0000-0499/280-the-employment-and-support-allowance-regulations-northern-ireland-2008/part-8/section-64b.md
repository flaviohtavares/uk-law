## 64B. Circumstances in which a claimant is to be treated as being in hardship[^c-1b5e36ddaf871dc7491928594a613893]

*Extent: N.I.*

A claimant is to be treated as being in hardship if the claimant’s partner, or a child or qualifying young person for whom the claimant or the claimant’s partner is responsible, is or will be in hardship unless a hardship payment is made.
