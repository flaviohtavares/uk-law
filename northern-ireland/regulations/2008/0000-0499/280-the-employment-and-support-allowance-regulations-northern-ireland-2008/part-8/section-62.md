## 62. Contracting out certain functions relating to work-focused interviews[^c-c215f436e713e30b3fec42cd5496a0a4]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Any function of the Department specified in paragraph (2) may be exercised by, or by employees of, such person (if any) as may be authorised by the Department.

(2) The functions are any function under—

- (a) regulation 54(1) (requirement to take part in a work-focused interview);
- (b) regulation 56(1) and (2) (notification of interview);
- (c) regulation 57(1)(b) and (c) (taking part in a work-focused interview);
- (d)[^c-f94670dd848bf528daca189578b269a6] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (e) regulation 59(1) and (3) (deferral of requirement to take part in a work-focused interview);
- (f) regulation 60 (requirement to take part in a work-focused interview not to apply).
