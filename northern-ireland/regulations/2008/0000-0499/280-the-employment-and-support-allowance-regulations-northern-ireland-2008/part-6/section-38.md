# The Employment and Support Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008, section 38

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (28).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisr/2008/280
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 38. Claimant may be called for a medical examination to determine whether the claimant has limited capability for work-related activity[^c-14c3210ca63b781c894e811dd735cec8]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where it falls to be determined whether a claimant has limited capability for work-related activity, that claimant may be called by or on behalf of a health care professional approved by the Department to attend for a medical examination in person, by telephone or by video[^c-7f469b441d34d7b32de530c9cd6487b6] .

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), where a claimant fails without good cause to attend for or to submit to an examination mentioned[^c-0c73f01d681c123335d138f0eae63e05] in paragraph (1), the claimant is to be treated as not having limited capability for work-related activity.

(3)[^c-ea6cf9028a5849ef0b1f3230b0415d4d] Paragraph (2) does not apply unless—

- (a) written notice of the date, time and place for the examination was sent to the claimant at least 7 days in advance; or
- (b) the claimant agreed to accept a shorter period of notice whether given in writing or otherwise.
