# The Employment and Support Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008, section 18

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisr/2008/280
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 18. Circumstances in which the condition that the claimant is not receiving education does not apply[^c-c180a647685004ed69fe95646eea697c]

*Extent: N.I.*

Paragraph 6(1)(g) of Schedule 1 to the Act does not apply where the claimant is entitled to armed forces independence payment[^c-c06338c43831c9321af70879d3a60543], disability living allowance[^M_F_fb4a1f1b-d55d-469c-e69c-f4d8fb354c36] , Scottish adult disability living allowance[^c-0406f88fff13cb500b94d292dc649a34][^M_F_e6bded41-2e18-4468-9842-2b6fabcc9a4f] , child disability payment ,[^c-caf3111b10b841025cef091884a93966] personal independence payment or adult disability payment[^c-a04e2171555e1e3ee96cb8316ea95546] .
