## 15. Claimants to be treated as receiving education[^c-13de01ebaac48e7470a4d24e589872b6]

*Extent: N.I.*

Subject to regulation 18, a qualifying young person is to be treated as receiving education for the purposes of paragraph 6(1)(g) of Schedule 1 to the Act.
