## 163. Applicable amounts in urgent cases

*Extent: N.I.*

For the purposes of calculating any entitlement to an income-related allowance under this Part— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7282e73c8612c95fc17d68d80a0d2749]

- (a) except in a case to which paragraph (b) or (c) applies, a claimant’s weekly applicable amount is to be the aggregate of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7282e73c8612c95fc17d68d80a0d2749]
  - (i)[^key-69ffe8fba49a50796ce5707ba4cdd3bd] 90 per cent. of the amount applicable or, as the case may be, of the reduced amount. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7282e73c8612c95fc17d68d80a0d2749]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7282e73c8612c95fc17d68d80a0d2749] the amount, if applicable, specified in Part 3 of Schedule 4 (premiums), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7282e73c8612c95fc17d68d80a0d2749]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7282e73c8612c95fc17d68d80a0d2749] the amount, if applicable, specified in Part 4 of Schedule 4 (components), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7282e73c8612c95fc17d68d80a0d2749]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7282e73c8612c95fc17d68d80a0d2749] any amounts applicable under regulation 67(1)(c) or, as the case may be, 68(1)(d) (housing costs); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7282e73c8612c95fc17d68d80a0d2749]
- (b) in the case of a claimant to whom any paragraph of Schedule 5 (special cases) applies, the applicable amount is to be the aggregate of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7282e73c8612c95fc17d68d80a0d2749]
  - (i)[^key-3bec30fd8e790033b5f077a29a1bb4d6] 90 per cent. of the amount applicable in respect of the claimant and partner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7282e73c8612c95fc17d68d80a0d2749]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7282e73c8612c95fc17d68d80a0d2749] the amount, if applicable, specified in Part 3 of Schedule 4 (premiums), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7282e73c8612c95fc17d68d80a0d2749]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7282e73c8612c95fc17d68d80a0d2749] the amount, if applicable, specified in Part 4 of Schedule 4 (components), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7282e73c8612c95fc17d68d80a0d2749]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7282e73c8612c95fc17d68d80a0d2749] any amounts applicable under regulation 67(1)(c) or, as the case may be, 68(1)(d) (housing costs); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7282e73c8612c95fc17d68d80a0d2749]
- (c) in the case of a claimant to whom regulation 162(2) applies, where that claimant is appealing to an appeal tribunal constituted under Chapter 1 of Part 2 of the 1998 Order, against a decision which embodies a determination that the claimant does not have limited capability for work, the applicable amount is to be the aggregate of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7282e73c8612c95fc17d68d80a0d2749]
  - (i)[^key-2c671378dda1d4107dd6b7a86e56a60c] 90 per cent. of the amount applicable or, as the case may be, of the reduced amount. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7282e73c8612c95fc17d68d80a0d2749]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7282e73c8612c95fc17d68d80a0d2749] the amount, if applicable, specified in Part 3 of Schedule 4 (premiums), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7282e73c8612c95fc17d68d80a0d2749]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7282e73c8612c95fc17d68d80a0d2749] any amounts applicable under regulation 67(1)(c) or, as the case may be, 68(1)(d) (housing costs). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7282e73c8612c95fc17d68d80a0d2749]
