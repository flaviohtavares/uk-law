## 164. Assessment of income and capital in urgent cases

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-7282e73c8612c95fc17d68d80a0d2749] The claimant’s income is to be calculated in accordance with Part 10 subject to the following modifications— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7282e73c8612c95fc17d68d80a0d2749]

- (a) any income other than— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7282e73c8612c95fc17d68d80a0d2749]
  - (i) a payment of income or income in kind made under the Macfarlane Trust, the Macfarlane (Special Payments) Trust, the Macfarlane (Special Payments) (No. 2) Trust, the Fund, the Eileen Trust or the Independent Living Fund (2006), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7282e73c8612c95fc17d68d80a0d2749]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7282e73c8612c95fc17d68d80a0d2749] income to which paragraph 9 (but only to the extent that a concessionary payment would be due under that paragraph for any non-payment of an income-related allowance under regulation 162 or of jobseeker’s allowance under regulation 147 of the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (urgent cases)), 34, 40(2), (3) or (4), 41, 61 or 62 of Schedule 8 (sums to be disregarded in the calculation of income other than earnings) applies, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7282e73c8612c95fc17d68d80a0d2749]

  possessed or treated as possessed by the claimant, is to be taken into account in full notwithstanding any provision in that Part disregarding the whole or any part of that income; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7282e73c8612c95fc17d68d80a0d2749]
- (b) any income to which regulation 118 (calculation of tariff income from capital) applies is to be disregarded; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7282e73c8612c95fc17d68d80a0d2749]
- (c) income treated as capital by virtue of regulation 112(1), (2), (3) and (7) (income treated as capital) is to be taken into account as income; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7282e73c8612c95fc17d68d80a0d2749]
- (d) in a case to which regulation 162(2) (urgent cases) applies, any income to which regulation 107(1) applies is to be disregarded. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7282e73c8612c95fc17d68d80a0d2749]

(2) The claimant’s capital calculated in accordance with Part 10, but including any capital referred to in— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7282e73c8612c95fc17d68d80a0d2749]

- (a) paragraph 3; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7282e73c8612c95fc17d68d80a0d2749]
- (b) paragraph 10 (to the extent that such assets as are referred to in that paragraph consist of liquid assets); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7282e73c8612c95fc17d68d80a0d2749]
- (c) paragraph 11 (except to the extent that the arrears referred to in that paragraph consist of arrears of housing benefit payable under Part 7 of the Contributions and Benefits Act or any arrears of benefit due under regulation 162, or regulation 147 of the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (urgent cases)); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7282e73c8612c95fc17d68d80a0d2749]
- (d) paragraphs 13(b), 24 and 32, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7282e73c8612c95fc17d68d80a0d2749]

of Schedule 9 (capital to be disregarded), is to be taken into account in full and the amount of an income-related allowance which would, but for this paragraph be payable under this regulation, is to be payable only to the extent that it exceeds the amount of that capital. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7282e73c8612c95fc17d68d80a0d2749]
