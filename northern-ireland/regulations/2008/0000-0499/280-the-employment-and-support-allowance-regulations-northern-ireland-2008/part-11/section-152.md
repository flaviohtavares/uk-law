## 152. Short absence[^c-b0234cf99a89f3663042905dfebe7b96]

*Extent: N.I.*

A claimant is to continue to be entitled to an employment and support allowance during the first 4 weeks of a temporary absence from Northern Ireland if—

- (a) the period of absence is unlikely to exceed 52 weeks; and
- (b) while absent from Northern Ireland, the claimant continues to satisfy the other conditions of entitlement to that employment and support allowance.
