# The Waste (Amendment) (2007 Order) (Commencement No.2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 75 (C. 2)

ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION

The Waste (Amendment) (2007 Order) (Commencement No.2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2008

Made

27th February 2008

The Department of the Environment makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 1(3) of the Waste (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 2007 [^f00001]:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Waste (Amendment) (2007 Order) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2008.

(2) In this Order “the 2007 Order” means the Waste (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 2007.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of Article 10 of the 2007 Order shall be 7<sup>th</sup> April 2008.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 27<sup>th</sup> February 2008.

Wesley Shannon

A senior officer of the Department of the Environment

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings Article 10 of the Waste (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 2007 (“the 2007 Order”) into operation on 7<sup>th</sup> April 2008.

(This note is not part of the Order)
