# Pig Production Development (Levy) Revocation (No 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Rule has been made in consequence of a defect in [SR 2008 No. 25](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2008/25) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Rule.

2008 No. 83

Agriculture

Pig Production Development (Levy) Revocation (No 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2008

Made

5th March 2008

Coming into operation

1st April 2008

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development [^f00001] makes the following Order upon the dissolution of the Pig Production Development Committee [^f00002] and in exercise of the powers conferred by section 5(1), (2) and (3) of the Pig Production Development Act (Northern Ireland) 1964 [^f00003]:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Pig Production Development (Levy) Revocation (No 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2008 and shall come into operation on 1st April 2008.

## 2. Revocations

The following Orders are revoked:

- (a) the Pig Production Development (Levy) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996 [^f00004]; and
- (b) the Pig Production Development (Levy) Revocation Order (Northern Ireland) 2008 [^f00005].

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 5th March 2008.

John Speers

A senior officer of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order revokes the Pig Production Development (Levy) Order (NI) 1996 consequential upon the dissolution of the Pig Production Development Committee (by [S.R. 2007 No. 354](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2007/354)). The Committee is dissolved and liability for levy ceases on 31 March 2008.

The Order also revokes the Pig Production Development (Levy) Revocation Order (Northern Ireland) 2008 ([S.R. 2008 No.25](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2008/25)) which contained an error rendering it ineffective.
