# The Mesothelioma, etc., (2008 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 351 (C. 19)

Social Security

The Mesothelioma, etc., (2008 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2008

Made

26th August 2008

The Department for Social Development makes the following Order, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 12(1) of the Mesothelioma, etc., Act (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Mesothelioma, etc., (2008 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2008.

(2) In this Order “the Act” means the Mesothelioma, etc., Act (Northern Ireland) 2008.

## 2. Appointed days

(1) The day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the Act specified in the Schedule—

- (a) for the purpose only of authorising the making of regulations, is 27th August 2008; and
- (b) for all other purposes, is 1st October 2008.

(2) In so far as not already in operation, the day appointed for the coming into operation of the Act is 1st October 2008.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 26th August 2008

(L.S.)

B McGahan

A senior officer of the Department for Social Development

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the coming into operation of provisions of the Mesothelioma, etc., Act (Northern Ireland) 2008 as follows—

- 27th August 2008 for provisions listed in the Schedule for the purpose only of authorising the making of regulations; and
- 1st October 2008 for all other purposes.
