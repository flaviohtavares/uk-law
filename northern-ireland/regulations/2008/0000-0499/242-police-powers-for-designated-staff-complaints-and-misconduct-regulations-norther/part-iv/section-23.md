# Police Powers for Designated Staff (Complaints and Misconduct) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008, section 23

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 23. Expenses of complainants and witnesses

The Ombudsman may, if he thinks fit, pay to the person by whom a complaint is made—

- (a) sums in respect of expenses properly incurred by him, excluding legal expenses, and
- (b) allowances by way of compensation for the loss of his time,

in accordance with such scales and subject to such conditions as the Secretary of State may determine.
