# Police Powers for Designated Staff (Complaints and Misconduct) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008, section 49

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 49. Documents to be supplied to the panel conducting the hearing

There shall be supplied to the panel conducting the hearing—

- (a) a copy of the notice given under regulation 44(1)(a); and
- (b) where a summary of the facts has been prepared under regulation 48, a copy of that summary and of any response from the designated person concerned.
