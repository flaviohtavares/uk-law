# Police Powers for Designated Staff (Complaints and Misconduct) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008, section 66

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 66. Record of conduct proceedings

The Chief Constable shall cause a book of record to be kept in which shall be entered every case brought against a designated person, together with the finding thereon and a record of the decision in any further proceedings in connection therewith.
