# Police Powers for Designated Staff (Complaints and Misconduct) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008, section 53

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 53. Non-attendance

(1) The designated person concerned shall be instructed to attend the hearing.

(2) If the designated person concerned fails to attend the hearing it may be proceeded with and concluded in his absence.

(3) Where the designated person concerned informs the presiding officer in advance that he is unable to attend due to ill-health or some other unavoidable reason the hearing may be adjourned.

(4) Where, owing to the absence of the designated person concerned, it is impossible to comply with any of the procedures set out in these regulations, that procedure shall be dispensed with.
