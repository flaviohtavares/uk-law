# Police Powers for Designated Staff (Complaints and Misconduct) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008, section 61

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 61. Personal record to be considered before recommendation made

Where the question of making a recommendation under regulation 60(3) is being considered—

- (a) the members of the panel conducting the hearing shall have regard to the record of the service of the designated person concerned in the police support staff, and
- (b) the panel conducting the hearing may receive evidence from any witness whose evidence would, in the opinion of the members of the panel or the designated person concerned, assist in determining the question, and
- (c) the designated person concerned, or his representative, shall be afforded an opportunity to make oral or, if appropriate, written representations as respects the question or to adduce evidence relevant thereto.
