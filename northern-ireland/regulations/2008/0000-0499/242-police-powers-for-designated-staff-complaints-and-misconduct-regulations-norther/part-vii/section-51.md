# Police Powers for Designated Staff (Complaints and Misconduct) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008, section 51

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 51. Conduct of hearing

(1) The panel conducting the hearing may from time to time adjourn if it appears to them to be necessary or expedient to do so for the due hearing of the case.

(2) Any decision of the panel conducting the hearing shall be based on a simple majority, but shall not indicate whether it was taken unanimously or by a majority.
