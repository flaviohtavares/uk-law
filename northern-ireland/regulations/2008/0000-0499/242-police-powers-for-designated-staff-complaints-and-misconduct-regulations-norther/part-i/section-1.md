# Police Powers for Designated Staff (Complaints and Misconduct) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) These regulations may be cited as the Police Powers for Designated Staff (Complaints and Misconduct) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008.

(2) These regulations shall come into operation on 1st July 2008.
