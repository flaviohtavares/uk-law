# Police Powers for Designated Staff (Complaints and Misconduct) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008, section 34

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 34. Police support staff: supervising member

A case which arises from a complaint, report or allegation which indicates that the relevant conduct of a designated person who is a member of the police support staff did not meet the appropriate standard may be referred for examination to a police officer of at least the rank of superintendent, who must not be an interested party (“the supervising member”).
