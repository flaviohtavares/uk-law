# The Health and Social Services Trusts (Originating Capital) Order (Northern Ireland) 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 142

HEALTH AND PERSONAL SOCIAL SERVICES

The Health and Social Services Trusts (Originating Capital) Order (Northern Ireland) 2008

Made

21st March 2008

The Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety[^f00001] makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 14(1) of the Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1991[^f00002] and with the consent of the Department of Finance and Personnel[^f00003].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Health and Social Services Trusts (Originating Capital) Order (Northern Ireland) 2008.

## 2. Originating Capital of HSS Trusts

The originating capital of an HSS trust specified in column 1 of Schedule 1 shall be of an amount specified opposite it in column 2.

## 3. Amendment

In the Schedule to the Health and Social Services Trusts (Originating Capital Debt) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00004] reference to the Causeway HSS Trust and its Originating Capital Debt, Initial Loan and Public Dividend Capital shall be deleted.

## 4. Revocation

The statutory rules listed in Schedule 2 are revoked.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety on 21st March 2008.

Julie Thompson

A senior officer of the

Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety

The Department of Finance and Personnel hereby consents to the foregoing Order.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance and Personnel on 26th March 2008.

Ciaran Doran

A senior officer of the

Department of Finance and Personnel

(This note is not part of the Order)

Article 14(1) of the Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1991 (as amended) requires the making of an Order specifying the originating capital of HSS Trusts. This Order specifies the originating capital of each of the HSS Trusts listed in Schedule 1 (Article 2). The HSS Trusts were established from 1st April 2007.

The Order also revokes or amends the legislation specifying the originating capital debts of HSS Trusts dissolved on 31st March 2007 (Articles 3 and 4).
