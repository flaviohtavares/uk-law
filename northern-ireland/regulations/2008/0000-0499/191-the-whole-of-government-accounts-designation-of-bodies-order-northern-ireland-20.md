# The Whole of Government Accounts (Designation of Bodies) Order (Northern Ireland) 2008

> Text as at 2014-04-11.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

2008 No. 191

Government Resources And Accounts

The Whole of Government Accounts (Designation of Bodies) Order (Northern Ireland) 2008

Made

*28t*h April 2008

Coming into operation

23rd May 2008

The Department of Finance and Personnel [^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by section 15(1) of the Government Resources and Accounts Act (Northern Ireland) 2001[^f00002], makes the following Order.

The bodies listed in the Schedule to this Order appear to the Department of Finance and Personnel (“the Department”)—

- (a) to exercise functions of a public nature; or
- (b) to be entirely or substantially funded from public money.

The Department intends the accounts under section 14 of the Government Resources and Accounts Act (Northern Ireland) 2001 (“the Act”) for the financial year ending with 31st March 2008 to relate in part to those bodies:

## 1. Title and commencement[^c-15d568ba2cdef1b15eb8e9ff2600b975]

This Order may be cited as the Whole of Government Accounts (Designation of Bodies) Order (Northern Ireland) 2008 and shall come into operation on 23rd May 2008.

## 2. Designation of Bodies[^c-c18ae806138f1b79860350737f21f8c5]

For the purposes of section 15 of the Act, the Department hereby designates the bodies listed in the Schedule in respect of the financial year ending with 31st March 2008.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance and Personnel on 28th April 2008

David Thomson

A senior officer of the Department of Finance and Personnel

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates bodies, for the financial year ending with 31st March 2008, for the purposes of section 15 of the Government Resources and Accounts Act (Northern Ireland) 2001. The Department of Finance and Personnel (“the Department”) intends to pass the information provided by bodies designated in the Schedule to this Order to HM Treasury for use in the preparation of the Whole of Government Accounts (WGA). The designation enables the Department to require those bodies to provide financial information in relation to that year.

WGA are financial statements prepared on the basis of Generally Accepted Accounting Practice (GAAP) which will cover the whole of the public sector as far as practically possible.
