# The Departments (1999 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 480 (C. 37)

NORTHERN IRELAND DEPARTMENTS

The Departments (1999 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 1999

Made

30th November 1999

In exercise of the powers conferred by Article 1(2) of the Departments (Northern Ireland) Order 1999[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling me in that behalf, I hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Departments (1999 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 1999.

## 2. Appointed day

The provisions of the Departments (Northern Ireland) Order 1999 (except for Article 8 and the repeal of section 4 of the Ministries Act (Northern Ireland) 1944)[^f00002] shall come into operation on 1st December 1999.

Peter Mandelson

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Dated this 30th day of November 1999

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order brings into operation on 1st December 1999 the provisions of the Departments (Northern Ireland) Order 1999 except for Article 8 and the repeal of section 4 of the Ministries Act (Northern Ireland) 1944.
