# Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999, section 50

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 50. Construction

The requirements specified in Schedule 6 shall apply to a minibus first used on or after 1st October 1990 except a vehicle—

- (a) manufactured by Land Rover UK Limited and known as the Land Rover; or
- (b) constructed or adapted for the secure transport of prisoners.
