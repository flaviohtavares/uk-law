# Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999, section 26

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 26. General requirement as to wheels and tracks[^c-91a4d8f1b5403d6cdad15d8515a79e94]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Every motor cycle and invalid carriage shall be a wheeled vehicle, and every other motor vehicle and every trailer shall be either a wheeled vehicle or a track-laying vehicle.
