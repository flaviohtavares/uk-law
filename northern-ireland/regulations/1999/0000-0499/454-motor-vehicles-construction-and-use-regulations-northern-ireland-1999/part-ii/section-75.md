# Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999, section 75

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 75. Closets, etc.[^c-1768153356af674797f669599fd3034a]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) A wheeled vehicle first used after 15th January 1931 shall not be equipped with a closet or urinal which can discharge directly on to a road.

(2) A tank, into which a closet or urinal with which a vehicle is equipped empties, and every closet or urinal which does not empty into a tank, shall contain chemicals which are non-inflammable and non-irritant and provide an efficient germicide.
