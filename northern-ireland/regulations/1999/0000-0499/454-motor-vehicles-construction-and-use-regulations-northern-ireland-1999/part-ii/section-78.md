# Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999, section 78

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 78. Maintenance of spray suppression devices

Every part of a containment device with which a vehicle is required to be fitted by regulation 77 shall when the vehicle is on a road be maintained free from obvious defects which would be likely to affect adversely the effectiveness of the device.
