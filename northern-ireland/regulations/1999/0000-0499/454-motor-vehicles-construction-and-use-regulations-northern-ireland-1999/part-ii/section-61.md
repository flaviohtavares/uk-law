# Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999, section 61

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 61. Maintenance of sideguards[^c-054385e81400df445457211e53093f7e]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

A sideguard fitted to a vehicle in compliance with the requirements of regulation 60 shall at all times when the vehicle is on a road be maintained free from any obvious defect which would be likely to affect adversely its effectiveness.
