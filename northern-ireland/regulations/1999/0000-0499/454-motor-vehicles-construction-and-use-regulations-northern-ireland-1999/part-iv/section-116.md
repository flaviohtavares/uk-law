# Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999, section 116

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 116. Restrictions on speed of low platform trailers, etc.

(1) No person shall use, or cause or permit to be used, on a road a vehicle displaying the rectangular plate described in the definition of “low platform trailer” in regulation 2(2) or anything resembling such a plate at a speed exceeding 40 mph.

(2) No person shall use, or cause or permit to be used on a road a vehicle displaying the rectangular plate described in Schedule 16 (Plate for Restricted Speed Vehicle) or anything resembling such a plate at a speed exceeding 50 mph.
