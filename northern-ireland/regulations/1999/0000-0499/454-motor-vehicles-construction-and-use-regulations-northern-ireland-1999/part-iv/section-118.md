# Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999, section 118

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 118. Passengers on motor cycles

If a person in addition to the driver is carried astride a two-wheeled motor cycle on a road (whether a sidecar is attached to it or not) suitable supports or rests for the feet shall be available on the motor cycle for that person.
