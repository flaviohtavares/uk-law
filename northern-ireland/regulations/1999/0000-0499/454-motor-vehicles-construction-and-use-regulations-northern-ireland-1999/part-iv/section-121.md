# Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999, section 121

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 121. Opening of doors[^c-63972cc0ead3eccd417bf73145f81229]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

A person shall not open, or cause or permit to be opened, any door of a vehicle on a road so as to injure or endanger anyone.
