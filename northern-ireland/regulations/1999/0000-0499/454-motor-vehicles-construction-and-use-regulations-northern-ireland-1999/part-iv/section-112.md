# Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999, section 112

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 112. Avoidance of excessive noise

A motor vehicle shall not be used on a road in such manner as to cause excessive noise which could have been avoided by the exercise of reasonable care on the part of the driver.
