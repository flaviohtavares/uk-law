# Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999, section 92

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 92. Saving for the Motor Vehicles (Authorised Weight) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999

Nothing in regulations 87 to 91 shall be taken to prohibit the use of a vehicle in circumstances where the maximum authorised weight for the vehicle, for any vehicle combination of which the vehicle forms part and for any axle of the vehicle, as determined in accordance with the Motor Vehicles (Authorised Weight) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999[^f00060], is not exceeded.
