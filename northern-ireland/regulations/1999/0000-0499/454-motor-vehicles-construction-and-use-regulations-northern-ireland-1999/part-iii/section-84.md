# Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999, section 84

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 84. Plate relating to dimensions

(1) This regulation applies to a vehicle which is either—

- (a) a bus or a heavy motor car and which was manufactured after 19th April 1999; or
- (b) a trailer used in combination with a vehicle falling within paragraph (*a*) and manufactured after 19th April 1999.

(2) A vehicle to which this regulation applies shall not be used unless—

- (a) the vehicle is equipped with a plate securely attached to the vehicle in a conspicuous and readily accessible position and containing the particulars as to the dimensions of the vehicle specified in Annex III of Community Directive [96/53/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0053)[^f00059]; or
- (b) those particulars are included in the particulars shown on the plate with which the vehicle is equipped in accordance with regulation 79.
