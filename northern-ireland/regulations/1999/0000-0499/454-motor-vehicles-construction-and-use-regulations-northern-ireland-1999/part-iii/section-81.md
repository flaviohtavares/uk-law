# Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999, section 81

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 81. Plates — agricultural trailed appliances

(1) Subject to paragraph (3), a wheeled agricultural trailed appliance manufactured on or after 1st December 1985 shall be equipped with a plate which is affixed to the vehicle in a conspicuous and readily accessible position clearly and indelibly marked with the particulars specified in paragraph (2).

(2) Those particulars are—

- (a) the name of the manufacturer of the appliance;
- (b) the year in which the appliance was manufactured;
- (c) the maximum gross weight;
- (d) the unladen weight; and
- (e) the maximum load which would be imposed by the appliance on the drawing vehicle.

(3) In the case of a towed roller consisting of several separate rollers used in combination, a single plate shall satisfy the requirement specified in paragraph (2).
