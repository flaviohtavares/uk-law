# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 1999, section 17

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 17. Disability working allowance

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In the Disability Working Allowance (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992[^f00028]—

- (a) in regulation 51(4) (determination of appropriate maximum disability working allowance) the capital sum prescribed is £3,000;
- (b) in regulation 51(4), (5) and (6) the amount specified for the allowance in respect of a child or young person is nil;
- (c) in regulation 52(1) (applicable amount)—
  - (i) in sub-paragraph (a) for “£59·25” there shall be substituted “£60·50”, and
  - (ii) in sub-paragraph (b) for “£79·00” there shall be substituted “£80·65”; and
- (d) in Schedule 5 (determination of appropriate maximum disability working allowance) the sums prescribed shall be as set out in Schedule 3 to this Order.
