# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 1999, section 18

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 18. Applicable amounts for income support

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The sums relevant to the calculation of an applicable amount as specified in the Income Support Regulations shall be the sums set out in the following provisions of this Article and Schedules 4 to 8 to this Order; and for this purpose a reference in this Article to a numbered regulation or Schedule is a reference to the regulation of, or Schedule to, the Income Support Regulations bearing that number.

(2) In regulations 17(1)(b), 18(1)(c), 21(1) and 71(1)(a)(ii), (b)(ii) and (iii), (c)(ii) and (d)(i), in paragraph 14(a) of Part III of Schedule 2 and in paragraph 1(2) of Part I of Schedule 4, the sum specified is in each case £3,000.

(3) The sums specified in Part I of Schedule 2 (applicable amounts: personal allowances) shall be as set out in Schedule 4 to this Order.

(4) In paragraph 3 of Part II of Schedule 2[^f00029] (applicable amounts: family premium)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (1)(a) the sum specified is £15·75; and
- (b) in sub-paragraph (1)(b) for “£11·05” there shall be substituted “£13·90”.

(5) The sums specified in Part IV of Schedule 2 (applicable amounts: weekly amounts of premiums) shall be as set out in Schedule 5 to this Order.

(6) In paragraph 18 of Schedule 3[^f00030] (housing costs: non-dependant deductions)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (1)(a) for “£45·00” there shall be substituted “£46·35”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (1)(b) for “£7·00” there shall be substituted “£7·20”;
- (c) in sub-paragraph (2)(a) for “£78·00” there shall be substituted “£80·00”;
- (d) in sub-paragraph (2)(b) for “£78·00”, “£116·00” and “£16·00” there shall be substituted “£80·00”, “£118·00” and “£16·50” respectively;
- (e) in sub-paragraph (2)(c) for “£116·00”, “£152·00” and “£22·00” there shall be substituted “£118·00”, “£155·00” and “£22·65” respectively;
- (f) in sub-paragraph (2)(d) for “£152·00”, “£200·00” and “£36·00” there shall be substituted “£155·00”, “£204·00” and “£37·10” respectively; and
- (g) in sub-paragraph (2)(e) for “£200·00”, “£250·00” and “£41·00” there shall be substituted “£204·00”, “£255·00” and “£42·25” respectively.

(7) In paragraph 6(2) of Schedule 4 (applicable amounts of persons in homes for persons in need and nursing homes) for “£247·00” there shall be substituted “£252·00” and, subject to paragraph (2), those other sums relevant to the calculation of an applicable amount which are specified in that Schedule shall be as set out in Schedule 6 to this Order.

(8) Those sums relevant to the calculation of an applicable amount which are specified in Schedule 7 (applicable amounts in special cases) shall be as set out in Schedule 7 to this Order.

(9) The sums specified in any provision of the Income Support Regulations set out in column (1) of Schedule 8 to this Order are the sums set out in column (2) of that Schedule.
