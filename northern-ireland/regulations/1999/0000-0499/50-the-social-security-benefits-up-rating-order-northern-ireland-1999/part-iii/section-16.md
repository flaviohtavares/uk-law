# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 1999, section 16

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 16. Family credit

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In the Family Credit (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1987[^f00027]—

- (a) in regulation 46(4) (determination of appropriate maximum family credit) the capital sum prescribed is £3,000;
- (b) in regulation 46(4), (5) and (6) the amount specified for the credit in respect of a child or young person is nil;
- (c) in regulation 47(1) (applicable amount) for “£79·00” there shall be substituted “£80·65”; and
- (d) in Schedule 4 (determination of maximum family credit) the sums prescribed shall be set out in Schedule 2 to this Order.
