# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 1999, section 7

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 7. Workmen’s compensation

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In paragraph 2(6)(c) of Schedule 8 to the Contributions and Benefits Act (maximum weekly rate of lesser incapacity allowance supplementing workmen’s compensation) for “£38·70” there shall be substituted “£39·95”.
