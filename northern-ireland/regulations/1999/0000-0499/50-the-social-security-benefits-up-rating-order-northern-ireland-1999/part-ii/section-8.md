# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 1999, section 8

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 8. Earnings limits

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In section 80(4) of the Contributions and Benefits Act (earnings limits in respect of child dependency increases)—

- (a) in paragraph (a) for “£140” there shall be substituted “£145”; and
- (b) in paragraph (b) for “£18” and “£140” there shall be substituted “£19” and “£145” respectively.
