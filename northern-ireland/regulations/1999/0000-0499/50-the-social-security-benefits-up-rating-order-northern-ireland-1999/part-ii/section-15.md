# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 1999, section 15

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 15. Transitional invalidity allowance in long-term incapacity benefit cases

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In regulation 18(2) of the Social Security (Incapacity Benefit) (Transitional) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995[^f00026] (rate of long-term incapacity benefit in transitional cases)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a) for “£13·60” there shall be substituted “£14·05”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b) for “£8·60” there shall be substituted “£8·90”; and
- (c) in sub-paragraph (c) for “£4·30” there shall be substituted “£4·45”.
