# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 1999, section 13

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 13. Child benefit

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In regulation 2(1) of the Child Benefit and Social Security (Fixing and Adjustment of Rates) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1976[^f00024] (weekly rates of child benefit)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a)(i) for “£11·45” there shall be substituted “£14·40”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (a)(ii) the sum specified is £17·10; and
- (c) in sub-paragraph (b) for “£9·30” there shall be substituted “£9·60”.
