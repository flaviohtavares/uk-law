# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 1999, section 22

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 22. Age-related amounts of contribution-based jobseeker’s allowance

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In regulation 79(1) of the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (weekly amounts of contribution-based jobseeker’s allowance)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a) for “£30·30” there shall be substituted “£30·95”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b) for “£39·85” there shall be substituted “£40·70”; and
- (c) in sub-paragraph (c) for “£50·35” there shall be substituted “£51·40”.
