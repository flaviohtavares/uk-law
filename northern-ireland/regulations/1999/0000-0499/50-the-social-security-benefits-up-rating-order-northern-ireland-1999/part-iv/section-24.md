# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 1999, section 24

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 24. Prescribed sum for jobseeker’s allowance

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In regulation 170 of the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (trade disputes: prescribed sum) for “£27·00” there shall be substituted “£27·50”.
