## SCHEDULE 11 — Part I of Schedule 1 to the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations as amended by this Order

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Article 23(3)

> **SCHEDULE 1 — Applicable Amounts**
>
> **Part I — Personal Allowances**
>
> **1.** [^f00072]The weekly amounts specified in column (2) in respect of each person or couple specified in column (1) shall be the weekly amounts specified for the purposes of regulations 83 and 84(1) (applicable amounts and polygamous marriages).

> **3.** (1) The weekly amount for the purposes of regulations 83(c) and 84(1)(d) (residential allowance) in respect of a person who satisfies the conditions specified in sub-paragraph (2) shall be £59·40.
