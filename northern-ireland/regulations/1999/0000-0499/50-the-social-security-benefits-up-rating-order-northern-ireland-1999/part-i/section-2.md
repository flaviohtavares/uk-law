# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 1999, section 2

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 2. Interpretation

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) In this Order—

- “the Administration Act” means the Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992;
- “the Contributions and Benefits Act” means the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992[^f00004];
- “the Pension Schemes Act” means the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993[^f00005];
- “the Computation of Earnings Regulations” means the Social Security Benefit (Computation of Earnings) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00006];
- “the Housing Benefit Regulations” means the Housing Benefit (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1987[^f00007];
- “the Income Support Regulations” means the Income Support (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1987[^f00008];
- “the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations” means the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00009].

(2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00010] shall apply to this Order as it applies to a Measure of the Assembly.
