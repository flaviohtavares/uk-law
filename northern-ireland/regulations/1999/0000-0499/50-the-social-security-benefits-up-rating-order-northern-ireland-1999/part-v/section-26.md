# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 1999, section 26

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 26. Revocations

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

The following are hereby revoked—

- (a) Articles 16(a) and 17(a) of the Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00040];
- (b) the Social Security Benefits Up-rating (No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00041];
- (c) Article 21(b) of the Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 1997[^f00042]; and
- (d) the Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 1998[^f00043].
