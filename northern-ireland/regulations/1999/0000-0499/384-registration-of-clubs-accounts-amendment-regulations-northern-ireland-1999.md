# Registration of Clubs (Accounts) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 384

REGISTRATION OF CLUBS

Registration of Clubs (Accounts) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999

Made

15th September 1999

Coming into operation

25th October 1999

The Department of Health and Social Services, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 40(1) of the Registration of Clubs (Northern Ireland) Order 1996[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Registration of Clubs (Accounts) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999 and shall come into operation on 25th October 1999.

**2.** In Schedule 2 (Systems of Control) to the Registration of Clubs (Accounts) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997[^f00002]—

- (a) in paragraph 5 (Membership records), sub-paragraph (2) shall be omitted and the existing sub-paragraph “(3)” shall be re-numbered “(2)”;
- (b) in paragraph 12(1) (Summary of receipts) for the words “A record shall be kept” there shall be substituted “At least once each week a record shall be prepared”;
- (c) in paragraph 14 (Summary of creditors) for the words “A record shall be maintained” there shall be substituted “At least once each week a record shall be prepared”;
- (d) in paragraph 18(1) (Bar staff) the words “and addresses” shall be omitted;
- (e) for paragraph 24 (Monthly reports) there shall be substituted the following paragraph—

  > **24. Monthly reports**
  >
  > Each month a report shall be prepared summarising the receipts and payments of the club since the date of the last report; or the date of registration whichever is the later.
- (f) in paragraph 25(b) (Contents of reports, records and receipts) the words “and address” shall be omitted.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health and Social Services on

Don Hill

Under Secretary

15th September 1999.

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations amend the Registration of Clubs (Accounts) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997 to relax the systems of control set out in Schedule 2—

- (i) a quarterly report on membership records is no longer required;
- (ii) a record summarising receipts and creditors will only need to be completed on a weekly basis as is already the case with the records of payments and debtors;
- (iii) there is no longer a need for the addresses to be shown on the record of persons engaged in the supply of intoxicating liquor;
- (iv) a monthly report of all sums due to the club is no longer required; and
- (v) there is no longer a need for anyone preparing reports, vouchers, receipts, etc., to record their address on such documents.
