# Education (Assessment Arrangements for Key Stage 3) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 222

EDUCATION

Education (Assessment Arrangements for Key Stage 3) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1999

Made

11th May 1999

Coming into operation

9th June 1999

The Department of Education, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 7(1)(b), (2)(a)(ii), (6) and (7) of the Education Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 1989[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Education (Assessment Arrangements for Key Stage 3) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1999 and shall come into operation on 9th June 1999.

## 2. Amendment of the Education (Assessment Arrangements for Key Stage 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997

In Article 4 of the Education (Assessment Arrangements for Key Stage 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997[^f00002] for the definition of “the Assessment Document” there shall be substituted the following definition—

> - “the Assessment Document” means a document entitled “Assessment Arrangements in Key Stage 3”, prepared by the Northern Ireland Council for the Curriculum, Examinations and Assessment, approved by the Department and published by the Council;

.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Education on

C. Jendoubi

Assistant Secretary

11th May 1999.

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order amends the Education (Assessment Arrangements for Key Stage 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997.

It amends the definition of “the Assessment Document” to provide for the detailed arrangements for the assessment of pupils in Key Stage 3 to be prescribed in a document prepared and published by the Northern Ireland Council for the Curriculum, Examinations and Assessment.
