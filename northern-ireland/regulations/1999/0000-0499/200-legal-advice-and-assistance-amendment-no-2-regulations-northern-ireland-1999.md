# Legal Advice and Assistance (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 200

LEGAL AID AND ADVICE

Legal Advice and Assistance (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999

Made

26th April 1999

To be laid before Parliament

Coming into operation

1st June 1999

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 5(3) and 27 of the Legal Aid, Advice and Assistance (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002] hereby makes the following Regulations:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Legal Advice and Assistance (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999 and shall come into operation on 1st June 1999.

## 2. Assistance by way of representation for certain applications under the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 1998

In paragraph 2 of Schedule 3 to the Legal Advice and Assistance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1981[^f00003] after sub-paragraph (j) there shall be added the following sub-paragraph—

> - (k) for a sex offender order under Article 6 of the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 1998[^f00004]

.

Irvine of Lairg, C.

Dated 26th April 1999

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations amend the Legal Advice and Assistance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1981 to extend assistance by way of representation to proceedings in a court of summary jurisdiction brought for an order under Article 6 of the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 1998.
