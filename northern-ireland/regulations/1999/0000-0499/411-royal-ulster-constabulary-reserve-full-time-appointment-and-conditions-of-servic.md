# Royal Ulster Constabulary Reserve (Full-time) (Appointment and Conditions of Service) (Amendment) Regulations 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 411

POLICE

Royal Ulster Constabulary Reserve (Full-time) (Appointment and Conditions of Service) (Amendment) Regulations 1999

To be laid before Parliament

Made

4th October 1999

Coming into operation

12th November 1999

The Secretary of State, in pursuance of section 26 of the Police (Northern Ireland) Act 1998[^f00001], and after consulting, in accordance with section 26(6) of that Act, the Police Authority and the Police Association, hereby makes the following regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Royal Ulster Constabulary Reserve (Full-time) (Appointment and Conditions of Service) (Amendment) Regulations 1999 and shall come into operation on 12th November 1999.

## 2. Amendment of the Royal Ulster Constabulary Reserve (Full-time) (Appointment and Conditions of Service) Regulations 1996

(1) The Royal Ulster Constabulary Reserve (Full-time) (Appointment and Conditions of Service) Regulations 1996[^f00002] shall be amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

(2) In regulation 2(1) the definition of public holiday shall be amended to insert the words “31st December 1999,” preceding the words “New Year’s Day”.

Marjorie Mowlam

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Northern Ireland Office

4th October 1999

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations further amend the Royal Ulster Constabulary Reserve (Full-time) (Appointment and Conditions of Service) Regulations 1996 to allow for an extra day of leave to members of the Royal Ulster Constabulary Reserve (Full-time) on 31st December 1999, to mark the Millennium.
