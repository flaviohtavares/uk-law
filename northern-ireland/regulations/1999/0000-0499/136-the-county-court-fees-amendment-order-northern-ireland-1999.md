# The County Court Fees (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 136

COUNTY COURTS

The County Court Fees (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1999

Made

16th March 1999

Coming into operation

12th April 1999

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 116(1) of the Judicature (Northern Ireland) Act 1978[^f00001], after consultation with the Lord Chief Justice and with the concurrence of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the County Court Fees (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1999 and shall come into operation on 12th April 1999.

## 2. Fee to be taken on an application for a warrant of arrest

In Section IV of Schedule 1 to the County Court Fees Order (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00002] after paragraph 3 there shall be added the following new paragraph—

> |  |  |  |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> |  | UNDER ARTICLE 5 OF THE PROTECTION FROM HARASSMENT (NORTHERN IRELAND) ORDER 1997[^f00003] |  |
> | 4\. | On an application for a warrant of arrest | £10.00 |

.

Irvine of Lairg, C.

Dated 11th March 1999

We concur,

Jane Kennedy

Bob Ainsworth

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

Dated 16th March 1999

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order amends the County Court Fees Order (Northern Ireland) 1996 so as to prescribe the fee to be taken on an application for a warrant of arrest under Article 5(3) of the Protection from Harassment (Northern Ireland) Order 1997.
