# Fair Employment and Treatment (1998 Order) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 355 (C. 27)

FAIR EMPLOYMENT

Fair Employment and Treatment (1998 Order) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 1999

Made

13th August 1999

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by Article 1(2) of the Fair Employment and Treatment (Northern Ireland) Order 1998[^f00001] I hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Fair Employment and Treatment (1998 Order) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 1999.

## 2. Appointed day

The following provisions of the Fair Employment and Treatment (Northern Ireland) Order 1998, that is to say—

- (a) Articles 80, 96 and 98;
- (b) Schedule 3 insofar as it amends Article 53A of the Sex Discrimination (Northern Ireland) Order 1976; and
- (c) paragraphs 2(b), 3(b) and 4(b) of Schedule 4,

shall come into operation on 16th August 1999.

Marjorie Mowlam

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Northern Ireland Office

13th August 1999

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order brings into operation on 16th August 1999 all the remaining provisions of the Fair Employment and Treatment (Northern Ireland) Order 1998 (“the Order”). These provisions deal with the effect of certificates signed by the Secretary of State under the Order, the Sex Discrimination (Northern Ireland) Order 1976 and the Race Relations (Northern Ireland) Order 1997.

The other provisions of the Order were brought into operation by the Fair Employment and Treatment (1998 Order) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 1999 ([S.R. 1999 No. 81 (C. 10)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1999/81)).
