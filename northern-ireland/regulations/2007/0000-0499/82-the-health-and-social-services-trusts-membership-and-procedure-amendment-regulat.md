# The Health and Social Services Trusts (Membership and Procedure) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 82

health and personal social services

The Health and Social Services Trusts (Membership and Procedure) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made

12th February 2007

Coming into operation

5th March 2007

The Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 10(6) of the Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1991[^f00002], makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Health and Social Services Trust (Membership and Procedure) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 and shall come into operation on 5th March 2007.

(2) In these Regulations—

- “the principal Regulations” means the Health and Social Services Trusts (Membership and Procedure) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1994[^f00003].

## 2. Amendment of regulation 2 of the principal Regulations

In regulation 2 (*Maximum number of Directors*) of the principal Regulations for the word “eleven” substitute the word “thirteen”.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety on 12th February 2007.

Dr Denis McMahon

A senior officer of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations provide for an increase in the number of directors of an Health and Social Services Trust from eleven to thirteen.
