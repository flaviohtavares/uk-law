The Department of Finance and Personnel[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 30B (1), (3), (4), and (5) and 42(2A) and (2B) of the Rates (Northern Ireland) Order 1977[^f00002], makes the following Regulations:

## PART 1 (affected by repeal, see note) — Introductory (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972][^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]

### 1. Citation and commencement (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-5b6f3fa0b20129619cd796c5cf7585f1][^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]

These Regulations may be cited as the Rate Relief (Education, Training and Leaving Care) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 and shall come into operation on 1st April 2007. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]

### 2. Interpretation (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-1e340045608ba6e2df4271de70a36ee2][^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]

(1) In these Regulations— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]

- “the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972] 1977 Order *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]” means the Rates (Northern Ireland) Order 1977; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]
- “the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972] 1995 Order *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]” means the Children (Northern Ireland) Order 1995 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972][^f00003];
- “academic year” means the period of twelve months beginning on 1st January, 1st April, 1st July or 1st September of the calendar year in which the academic year of the course in question begins according to whether that academic year begins on or after 1st January and before 1st April, on or after 1st April and before 1st July, on or after 1st July and before 1st August or on or after 1st August and on or before 31st December, respectively; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]
- “approved course of training” shall be construed in accordance with regulation 8; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]
- “course for the initial training of teachers” includes such a course leading to a first degree unless otherwise indicated but excludes an employment-based teacher training scheme; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]
- “designated course” shall be construed in accordance with regulation 7; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]
- “institution” in relation to a designated course means an educational institution and in relation to an approved course of training means a training institution approved by the Department of Employment and Learning for the purposes of these Regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]
- “periods of work experience” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]
  - (a) periods of industrial, professional or commercial experience associated with full-time study at an institution but at a place outside that institution; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]
  - (b) periods during which a person is employed and residing in a country whose language is one that he is studying for his course (provided that the period of residence in that country is a requirement of his course and the study of one or more modern languages accounts for not less than one half of the total time spent studying on the course); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]
- “postgraduate course” means a course for entry to which a first degree (or equivalent qualification) or higher is normally required; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]
- “publicly funded” means maintained or assisted by recurrent grants out of public funds and related expressions are to be interpreted accordingly; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]
- “quarter” in relation to an academic year means the first three months, and any subsequent period of three months, of that year; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]
- “rate relief” means rate relief under Article 30B of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972] 1977 Order *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972] as read with these Regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]
- “relevant institution of higher education in the Republic of Ireland” means an institution listed in Schedule 2. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]

(2) In relation to a designated course or an approved course of training provided by an institution which does not have academic years, any reference to such a year shall (except for the purposes of paragraph (3)), be treated as a reference to a calendar year beginning on the day on which the course begins. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]

(3) In relation to any designated course or approved course of training which begins part way through an academic year of the institution providing it, the course shall be treated as beginning on the first day of that academic year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]

(4) A person shall, for the purposes of these Regulations, be treated as undertaking a designated course or a course of approved training from the date the course begins or is treated under these Regulations as beginning. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]

(5) A course is provided by an institution if it provides the teaching or training and supervision which comprise the course, whether or not the institution has entered into an agreement with the student or trainee to provide the course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]

### 3. Gap Year Student (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-363b9dac992911d8a20616bbf4ef011b][^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]

(1) This paragraph applies where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]

- (a) a person had on or before 1st August in any year received an offer, whether conditional on obtaining specified qualifications or not, of a place on a designated course; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]
- (b) the academic year of the course begins on or after 1st September in the same year but before 1st September in the next following year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]

(2) This paragraph applies where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]

- (a) a person had received an offer (“the first offer”) of a place on a designated course which begins on or before 1st September in the same year (“the current year”); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]
- (b) he was unable to take up the offer because a particular qualification or grade was not awarded to him; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]
- (c) he appealed against the decision not to award him the qualification or grade; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]
- (d) the appeal was allowed after the last date on which he could have taken up the offer; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]
- (e) as a result, he was offered a place on that or a similar designated course; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]
- (f) the first academic year of the course referred to in sub-paragraph (e) begins on or after 1st September in the current year but before 1st September in the next year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]

(3) Where paragraph (1) applies a person shall be treated as a gap year student from the date he received the offer until— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]

- (a) where he takes up the offer, the first day of the academic year in which the course in question begins; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]
- (b) in any other case, the date he indicates that he does not intend to take up the offer or if he gives no such indication the first day of the academic year for the course to which the offer relates, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]

as the case may be. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]

(4) Where paragraph (2) applies a person shall be treated as a gap year student from the date he received the first offer until— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]

- (a) where he takes up that offer or any other offer referred to in that paragraph, the first day of the academic year in which the course in question begins; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]
- (b) in any other case, the date he indicates that he does not intend to take up that offer or any other offer referred to in that paragraph or, if he gives no such indication, the first day of the academic year for the course to which the first offer relates, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]

as the case may be. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]

(5) Where a person has— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]

- (a) completed at least one academic year of any designated course but not the entire course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]
- (b) without withdrawing from or abandoning the course and with the agreement of the institution providing it has, for any purpose (including private study, work experience, training, voluntary work, recreation or travel) absented himself from the course for not more than one academic year; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]
- (c) at the end of his period of absence resumes undertaking the course, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]

he shall be treated as a gap year student for the period of his absence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]

### 4. Transfer Student (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-e88f711f1c40ae100f1646d28e82ea09][^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]

(1) This paragraph applies where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]

- (a) a person is undertaking a designated course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]

(2) Where paragraph (1) applies a person shall be treated as a transfer student from the date of his withdrawal from the course until he begins to undertake another designated course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]

### 5. Sandwich Course (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-a6ac15d816013d5a54bfb2c6f07123b0][^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]

(1) For the purposes of these Regulations, a course is a “sandwich course” if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]

- (a) it is not a course for the initial training of teachers; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]
- (b) it consists of alternate periods of full-time study in an institution and periods of work experience; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]
- (c) taking the course as a whole, the person undertaking that course is required by the institution to attend (whether at the premises of the institution or elsewhere) for full-time study for an average of not less than 18 weeks in each academic year and in each such week that person is required to attend he is expected to undertake periods of study or tuition amounting to at least 21 hours on average. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]

(2) A person is to be regarded as undertaking a sandwich course from the beginning of the first period of full-time study until the end of the last such period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]

(3) For the purposes of paragraph (1), where periods of full-time study and work experience alternate within any week of the course, the days of full-time study are aggregated with each other and with any weeks of full-time study in determining the number of weeks of full-time study in each year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]

## PART 2 (affected by repeal, see note) — Rate Relief (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972][^c-920541f5455a1cc0c64fce2a511c8972]

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Article 30B of the Rates (Northern Ireland) Order 1977, as amended, provides that Regulations may grant full rates relief in respect of premises if they are occupied by persons in education or training, persons under the age of 18 or persons leaving care and if certain requirements are satisfied. Among these requirements are that—

- (a) the persons concerned meet certain eligibility conditions relating to education, training or leaving care;
- (b) the premises in question are used wholly as a private dwelling and must be the principal residence of such persons; and
- (c) every occupier of the premises must qualify for the relief.

Part 2 of the Regulations makes provision for and in connection with this relief.

Regulation 6 provides for the grant of the relief and for the period a person in education or training is to be regarded as occupying premises as his principal residence.

Regulation 7 (as read with regulations 3 to 5) specifies the conditions that a person in education must satisfy to be eligible for the relief. That person must—

- (a) be undertaking a designated course listed in Schedule 1 provided by publicly funded institutions; or
- (b) be a gap year or transfer student; and
- (c) where he is undertaking a sandwich course satisfy the minimum requirements for full-time study.

Regulation 8 specifies the conditions that a person in training must satisfy to be eligible for the relief. The training must in particular be provided under arrangements approved by the Department of Education and Learning and must lead to a recognised qualification.

Regulation 9 specifies the conditions a person leaving care must satisfy to be eligible for the relief.

Regulation 10 requires an applicant for the relief to provide the Department of Finance and Personnel with such information as it may reasonably require.

Part 3 of the Regulations (regulations 11 and 12) provides for reviews of decisions relating to the relief and appeals to the Valuation Tribunal.

Part 4 of the Regulations allows for exemptions from rates for Halls of Residence owned or managed by bodies listed in Schedule 3 which are provided predominantly for the accommodation of persons in education or training and who satisfy the conditions set out in regulation 7 and 8 respectively.
