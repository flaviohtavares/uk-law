# The Disability Discrimination (2006 Order) (Commencement No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 430 (C. 28)

disabled persons

The Disability Discrimination (2006 Order) (Commencement No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made

5th October 2007

The Office of the First Minister and deputy First Minister makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 1(2) of the Disability Discrimination (Northern Ireland) Order 2006[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Disability Discrimination (2006 Order) (Commencement No.3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007.

(2) In this Order—

- “the Order” means the Disability Discrimination (Northern Ireland) Order 2006; and
- “the 1995 Act” means the Disability Discrimination Act 1995[^f00002].

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the following provisions of the Order is 31st October 2007—

- (a) Article 18 (meaning of “disability”) to the extent that it is not already in operation;
- (b) paragraphs 2, 26, 27, 28, and 32 of Schedule 1 (minor and consequential amendments) and Article 19(1) in so far as it relates to those entries;
- (c) paragraph 3 of Schedule 1 (minor and consequential amendments) to the extent that it is not already in operation and Article 19(1) in so far as it relates to that entry;
- (d) the entries in Schedule 2 (repeals) in respect of paragraphs 1(1) of Schedule 1 and 2(1) of Schedule 8 to the 1995 Act and Article 19(2) in so far as it relates to those entries.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Office of the First Minister and deputy First Minister on 5th October 2007.

Gerard Mulligan

A senior officer of the

Office of the First Minister and deputy First Minister

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This is the third Commencement Order to be made under the Disability Discrimination (Northern Ireland) Order 2006 (“the Order”). The Order extensively amends the Disability Discrimination Act 1995 (“the 1995 Act”).

Article 2 brings into operation the Order’s provisions on the meaning of disability, specified paragraphs of Schedule 1 (minor and consequential amendments) and specified entries in Schedule 2 (repeals).
