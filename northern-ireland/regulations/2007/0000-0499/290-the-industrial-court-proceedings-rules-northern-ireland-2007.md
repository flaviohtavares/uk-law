# The Industrial Court (Proceedings) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2007

> Text as at 2007-12-15.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

2007 No. 290

eMPLOYMENT

The Industrial Court (Proceedings) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made

29th May 2007

Coming into operation

3rd June 2007

The Department for Employment and Learning makes the following Rules in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 92(3)(f) of the Industrial Relations (Northern Ireland) Order 1992[^f00001] and now vested in it[^f00002]:

## 1. Citation and commencement[^key-99af4a817238bcab9279c0ea78904e2b]

These Rules may be cited as the Industrial Court (Proceedings) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2007 and shall come into operation on 3rd June 2007.

## 2. Appearance in certain proceedings before the Industrial Court by counsel or solicitor[^key-792e54a7856dd58715fcabfa842f7fcd]

For the purposes of Article 92(3)(f) of the Industrial Relations (Northern Ireland) Order 1992 any party to a case under a provision listed in the Schedule may appear by counsel or solicitor in proceedings before the Industrial Court.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Employment and Learning on 29th May 2007.

Sir Reg Empey

Minister for Employment and Learning

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules allow for any party to be represented by counsel or solicitor in proceedings before the Industrial Court arising from the legislative provisions listed in the Schedule.
