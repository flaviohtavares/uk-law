# The Street Works (Inspection Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 29

roads

Street works

The Street Works (Inspection Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made

18th January 2007

Coming into operation

1st April 2007

The Department for Regional Development[^f00001] makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 35 of the Street Works (Northern Ireland) Order 1995[^f00002] and now vested in it[^f00003]:

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

These Regulations may be cited as The Street Works (Inspection Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 and shall come into operation on 1st April 2007.

## 2. Amendment

In regulation 3(1) of The Street Works (Inspection Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005[^f00004] (fees, chargeable inspections, units of inspection, phases of works and estimated numbers of units of inspection) for “£21.00” there shall be substituted “£24.00”.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Regional Development on 18th January 2007.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend The Street Works (Inspection Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005 by increasing the fee prescribed for each chargeable inspection from £21.00 to £24.00.
