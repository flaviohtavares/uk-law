# The Health and Social Services Trusts (Dissolution) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 132

HEALTH AND PERSONAL SOCIAL SERVICES

The Health and Social Services Trusts (Dissolution) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made

5th March 2007

Coming into operation

1st April 2007

The Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety[^f00001] makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 23(1) of Schedule 3 to, the Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1991[^f00002] having considered it appropriate in the interests of the health and personal social services.

In accordance with paragraph 23(3) of Schedule 3 to that Order the prescribed consultation has taken place with the relevant Health and Social Services Councils and staff interests [^f00003].

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

This Order may be cited as the Health and Social Services Trusts (Dissolution) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007 and shall come into operation on 1st April 2007.

## 2. Dissolution of Health and Social Services Trust

The Health and Social Services trusts listed in Schedule 1 are dissolved.

## 3. Revocation

The statutory rules listed in Schedule 2 are revoked

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety on 5th March 2007.

Dr Andrew McCormick

A senior officer of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the dissolution of the health and social services trusts listed in Schedule 1 on 1<sup>st</sup> April 2007.

It revokes the health and social services trust establishment orders, as amended, listed in Schedule 2 from that date.
