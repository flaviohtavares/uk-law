# The Electricity (Single Wholesale Market) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 458

ELECTRICITY

The Electricity (Single Wholesale Market) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made

30th October 2007

Coming into operation

30th October 2007

The Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment[^f00001] makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 2(6) of the Electricity (Single Wholesale Market) (Northern Ireland) Order 2007[^f00002]:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Electricity (Single Wholesale Market) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007.

## 2. Amendment of the Electricity (Single Wholesale Market) (Northern Ireland) Order 2007

For the definition of “the Irish Minister” in Article 2(5) of the Electricity (Single Wholesale Market) (Northern Ireland) Order 2007 there shall be substituted the following definition—

> “the Irish Minister” means the Minister for Communications, Energy and Natural Resources (being a Minister of the Government of Ireland).

.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment on 30th October 2007

Fiona Hepper

A senior officer of the

Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends Article 2(5) of the Electricity (Single Wholesale Market) (Northern Ireland) Order 2007 to provide a revised definition of “the Irish Minister” in consequence of the change in the title of the Minister in question.
