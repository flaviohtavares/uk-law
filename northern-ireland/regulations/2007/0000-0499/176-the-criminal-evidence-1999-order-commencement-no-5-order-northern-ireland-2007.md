# The Criminal Evidence (1999 Order) (Commencement No. 5) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 176 (C. 9)

CRIMINAL LAW

The Criminal Evidence (1999 Order) (Commencement No. 5) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made

27th February 2007

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by Article 1(2) of the Criminal Evidence (Northern Ireland) Order 1999[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Criminal Evidence (1999 Order) (Commencement No.5) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007.

## 2. Appointed Day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of Article 36 of the Criminal Evidence (Northern Ireland) Order 1999 is 1st March 2007.

David Hanson

Minister of State

Northern Ireland Office

27th February 2007

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into operation on 1st March 2007 Article 36 of the Criminal Evidence (Northern Ireland) Order 1999.

Article 36 makes provision for when it is permissible for inferences from silence to be drawn. It amends Articles 3, 5 and 6 of the Criminal Evidence (Northern Ireland) Order 1988 to provide that such inferences shall not be drawn unless a person has had the opportunity to consult a solicitor.

(This note is not part of the Order)

The following provisions of the Criminal Evidence (Northern Ireland) Order 1999 came into operation on 30th June 2003 insofar as they related to special measures directions for persons under 17.
