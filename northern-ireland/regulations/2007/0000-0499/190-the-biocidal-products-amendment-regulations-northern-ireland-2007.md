The Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-80c06a1e078ec2da98527f65e484a977][^f00001], being the Department concerned *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-80c06a1e078ec2da98527f65e484a977][^f00002], makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-80c06a1e078ec2da98527f65e484a977][^f00003] (“the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-80c06a1e078ec2da98527f65e484a977] 1972 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-80c06a1e078ec2da98527f65e484a977]”) and Articles 17(1), (2), (3) and (5), 40(2) and (4) and 55(2) of, and paragraphs 1(1), (4) and (5), 3(1), 12(1), 14(1) and 15 of Schedule 3 to the Health and Safety at Work (Northern Ireland) Order 1978 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-80c06a1e078ec2da98527f65e484a977][^f00004] (“the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-80c06a1e078ec2da98527f65e484a977] 1978 Order *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-80c06a1e078ec2da98527f65e484a977]”).

The Department was designated *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-80c06a1e078ec2da98527f65e484a977][^f00005] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-80c06a1e078ec2da98527f65e484a977] 1972 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-80c06a1e078ec2da98527f65e484a977] in relation to the notification and control of substances and to measures relating to biocides. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-80c06a1e078ec2da98527f65e484a977]

These Regulations make provision for a purpose mentioned in section 2(2) of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-80c06a1e078ec2da98527f65e484a977] 1972 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-80c06a1e078ec2da98527f65e484a977] and it appears to the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment that it is expedient for references in the Biocidal Products Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-80c06a1e078ec2da98527f65e484a977][^f00008] to Directive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-80c06a1e078ec2da98527f65e484a977] [98/8/EC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0008)[^key-80c06a1e078ec2da98527f65e484a977] of the European Parliament and the Council *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-80c06a1e078ec2da98527f65e484a977][^f00009], [Commission Regulation (EC) No. 1896/2000 *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2000/1896)[^key-80c06a1e078ec2da98527f65e484a977][^f00010] and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-80c06a1e078ec2da98527f65e484a977] [Commission Regulation (EC) No. 2032/2003 *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/2032)[^key-80c06a1e078ec2da98527f65e484a977][^f00011] to be construed as references to those instruments as amended from time to time. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-80c06a1e078ec2da98527f65e484a977]

- (a) either after a decision is made not to include one of those substances in Annex I, IA or IB, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-80c06a1e078ec2da98527f65e484a977]
- (b) after the decision has been taken to include all of those substances in Annex I, IA or IB. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-80c06a1e078ec2da98527f65e484a977]

**14.** In Great Britain the corresponding Regulations are the Biocidal Products (Amendment) Regulations 2007 ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-80c06a1e078ec2da98527f65e484a977][S.I. 2007/293](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/293)).
