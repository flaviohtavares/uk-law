# The Special Educational Needs and Disability (2005 Order) (Commencement No 1) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 25

education

The Special Educational Needs and Disability (2005 Order) (Commencement No 1) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made

16th January 2007

The Department of Education makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 1 (2) of the Special Educational Needs and Disability (Northern Ireland) Order 2005[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Special Educational Needs and Disability (2005 Order) (Commencement No 1) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007.

## 2. Amendment

(1) The Special Educational Needs and Disability (2005 Order) (Commencement No 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2005[^f00002] shall be amended as provided by paragraph (2).

(2) In the Schedule for the entry “Articles 43 to 48 so far as relating to Chapters I and II of Part III” there shall be substituted the following entry—

> Articles 43 to 48 so far as relating to Chapters I and III of Part III

.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Education on 16th January 2007.

Dorothy Angus

A senior officer of the Department of Education

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order corrects a drafting error in the Special Educational Needs and Disability (2005 Order) (Commencement No 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2005 ([S.R. 2005 No. 336](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2005/336)).
