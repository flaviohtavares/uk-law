# The Travelling Expenses and Remission of Charges (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 264

HEALTH AND PERSONAL SOCIAL SERVICES

The Travelling Expenses and Remission of Charges (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made

30th April 2007

Coming into operation

1st May 2007

The Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety[^f00001] makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 45, 98, 106 and 107(6) of, and paragraphs 1(b) and 1B of Schedule 15 to, the Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1972[^f00002] and with the approval of the Department of Finance and Personnel in so far as they relate to the remission of charges, and in conjunction with the Department of Finance and Personnel in so far as they relate to travelling expenses.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Travelling Expenses and Remission of Charges (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 and shall come into operation on 1st May 2007.

(2) In these Regulations—

- “the principal Regulations” means the Travelling Expenses and Remission of Charges Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004[^f00003]; and
- “the Income Support Regulations” means the Income Support (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1987[^f00004].

## 2. Amendment of Schedule 1 to the principal Regulations

(1) Schedule 1 to the principal Regulations (Modifications of the Income Support (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1987) is amended as follows.

(2) In Table A, in the modifications of—

- (a) regulation 45 of the Income Support Regulations, for “£21,000” substitute “£21,500”; and
- (b) regulation 53 of the Income Support Regulations—
  - (i) for “£12,750” substitute “£13,000”, and
  - (ii) for “£21,000” substitute “£21,500”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations further amend the Travelling Expenses and Remission of Charges Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004 (“the principal Regulations”), which provide for remission and payment of certain charges which would otherwise be payable under the Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1972 and for the payment by the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety of travelling expenses incurred in attending a hospital.

Regulation 2 amends Schedule 1 of the principal Regulations to increase the amounts used as the basis for calculating entitlement to the payment of travel expenses and the remission of charges under those Regulations.
