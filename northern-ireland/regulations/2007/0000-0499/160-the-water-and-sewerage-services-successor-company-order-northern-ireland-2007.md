# The Water and Sewerage Services (Successor Company) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 160

water and sewerage

The Water and Sewerage Services (Successor Company) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made

9th March 2007

Coming into operation

1st April 2007

The Department for Regional Development, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 269(1) of the Water and Sewerage Services (Northern Ireland) Order 2006[^f00001], and with the consent of the Department of Finance and Personnel, makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Water and Sewerage Services (Successor Company) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007 and shall come into operation on 20th March 2007.

## 2. Successor Company

Northern Ireland Water Limited (NIO 54463) is nominated as the successor company for the purposes of Part X of the Water and Sewerage Services (Northern Ireland) Order 2006.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Regional Development on 9th March 2007

John Mills

A senior officer of the

Department for Regional Development

The Department of Finance and Personnel consents to the forgoing Regulations.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance and Personnel on 9th March 2007

Mary McIvor

A senior officer of the

Department of Finance and Personnel

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order nominates Northern Ireland Water Limited (NIO 54463) as the successor company for the purposes of Part X of the Water and Sewerage Services (Northern Ireland) Order 2006.
