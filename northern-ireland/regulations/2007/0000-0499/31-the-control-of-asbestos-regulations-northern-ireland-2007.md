### 3. Application (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-4fcef5f201bfa10dcccd9f749ddda138][^key-7d3883cbac231c5972531e334b84dece]

(1) These Regulations shall apply to a self-employed person as they apply to an employer and an employee and as if that self-employed person were both an employer and an employee. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d3883cbac231c5972531e334b84dece]

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), regulations 8 (licensing), 9 (notification of work with asbestos), 15(1) (arrangements to deal with accidents, incidents and emergencies), 18(1)(a) (asbestos areas) and 22 (health records and medical surveillance) shall not apply where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d3883cbac231c5972531e334b84dece]

- (a) the exposure of employees to asbestos is sporadic and of low intensity; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d3883cbac231c5972531e334b84dece]
- (b) it is clear from the risk assessment that the exposure of any employee to asbestos will not exceed the control limit; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d3883cbac231c5972531e334b84dece]
- (c) the work involves— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d3883cbac231c5972531e334b84dece]
  - (i) short, non-continuous maintenance activities; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d3883cbac231c5972531e334b84dece]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d3883cbac231c5972531e334b84dece] removal of materials in which the asbestos fibres are firmly linked in a matrix; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d3883cbac231c5972531e334b84dece]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d3883cbac231c5972531e334b84dece] encapsulation or sealing of asbestos-containing materials which are in good condition; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d3883cbac231c5972531e334b84dece]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d3883cbac231c5972531e334b84dece] air monitoring and control, and the collection and analysis of samples to ascertain whether a specific material contains asbestos. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d3883cbac231c5972531e334b84dece]

(3) No exposure to asbestos will be sporadic and of low intensity within the meaning of paragraph (2)(a) if the concentration of asbestos in the atmosphere when measured in accordance with the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d3883cbac231c5972531e334b84dece] 1997 WHO recommended method *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d3883cbac231c5972531e334b84dece] or by a method giving equivalent results to that method approved by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d3883cbac231c5972531e334b84dece] the Executive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d3883cbac231c5972531e334b84dece] exceeds or is liable to exceed the concentration approved in relation to the specified reference period approved for the purposes of this paragraph by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d3883cbac231c5972531e334b84dece] the Executive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d3883cbac231c5972531e334b84dece].

(4) Where a duty is placed by these Regulations on an employer in respect of his employees, he shall, so far as is reasonably practicable, be under a like duty in respect of any other person, whether at work or not, who may be affected by the work activity carried out by the employer except that the duties of the employer— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d3883cbac231c5972531e334b84dece]

- (a) under regulation 10 (information, instruction and training) shall not extend to persons who are not his employees unless those persons are on the premises where the work is being carried out; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d3883cbac231c5972531e334b84dece]
- (b) under regulation 22 (health records and medical surveillance) shall not extend to persons who are not his employees. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d3883cbac231c5972531e334b84dece]

(5) Regulation 17, insofar as it requires an employer to ensure that premises are thoroughly cleaned, shall not apply to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d3883cbac231c5972531e334b84dece]

- (a) the fire and rescue authority for Northern Ireland within the meaning of article 3 of the Fire and Rescue Services (Northern Ireland) Order 2006 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d3883cbac231c5972531e334b84dece][^f00014], in respect of premises attended by its employees for the purpose of fighting a fire or in an emergency; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d3883cbac231c5972531e334b84dece]
