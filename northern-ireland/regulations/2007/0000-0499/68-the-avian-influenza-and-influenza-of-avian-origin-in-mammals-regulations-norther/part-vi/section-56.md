# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007, section 56

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 56. Size of zones[^c-9e6551eb4fe492d590c6dd5ecf7e02cc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A low pathogenic avian influenza restricted zone must—

- (a) be centred on the outbreak point; and
- (b) subject to regulation 29(8), have a radius of at least 1 kilometre.

(2) “Outbreak point” has the meaning specified in regulation 29.
