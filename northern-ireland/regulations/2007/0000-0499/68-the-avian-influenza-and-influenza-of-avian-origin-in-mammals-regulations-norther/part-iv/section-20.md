> Text as at 2025-02-27.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 20. Killing of birds on premises[^c-e5b204a500c86399923203fab935f695]

*Extent: N.I.*

Subject to regulation 21, the Department must ensure that poultry and other captive birds to be killed on infected premises under paragraph 11 of Part I of Schedule 2 to the 1981 Order are killed there without delay.
