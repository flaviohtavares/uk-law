# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007, section 26

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 26. Identification of contact premises[^key-1960db600e28abd2d03e10946af54b64]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department must serve a notice on the occupier of any premises if it suspects that highly pathogenic avian influenza may have been carried—

- (a) there from other premises; or
- (b) to other premises from there.

(2) Premises in respect of which a notice is served are contact premises for the purposes of this Part.
