# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007, section 25

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 25. Veterinary inquiry at infected premises[^key-d57dff40c321c9eafea66c9a1983cd00]

*Extent: N.I.*

The Department must ensure that veterinary inquiries continue at all infected premises for such period as it considers necessary.
