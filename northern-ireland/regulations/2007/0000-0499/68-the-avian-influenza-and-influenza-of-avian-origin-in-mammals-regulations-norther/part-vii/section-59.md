# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007, section 59

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 59. Killing of mammals[^key-cc5cd42cfae405220e6a48d25ade454f]

*Extent: N.I.*

The Department must ensure that mammals to be killed under paragraph 10 of Part I of Schedule 2 to the 1981 Order[^f00010] because they are affected or suspected of being affected with influenza virus of avian origin or have been exposed to such disease are—

- (a) killed as soon as possible, in accordance with the instructions of a veterinary inspector; or
- (b) transported in accordance with the instructions of a veterinary inspector (if they are moved off the premises for slaughter) to a designated slaughterhouse.
