# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007, section 86

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 86. Disapplication[^c-ff98a7c2edeb1d913afb03fda8341256]

*Extent: N.I.*

The provisions of the Diseases of Poultry Order (Northern Ireland) 1995[^f00018] are disapplied insofar as they apply to avian influenza.
