# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007, section 77

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 77. Disapplication of measures[^c-5da204ba11b337f91bd6d02aa271b2a5]

*Extent: N.I.*

Measures prohibiting or restricting the movement of any person or the use of any thing do not apply to the Department or a district council, or any person authorized by either of them to act, in the execution of these Regulations.
