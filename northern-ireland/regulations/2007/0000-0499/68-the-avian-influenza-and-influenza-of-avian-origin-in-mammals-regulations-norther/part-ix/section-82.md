# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007, section 82

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>

## 82. Provision of information[^key-9d1b3b1a2ccedba4d73e05c5d11aa5d1]

*Extent: N.I.*

A person shall not provide information which he knows to be false or misleading to a person executing these Regulations.
