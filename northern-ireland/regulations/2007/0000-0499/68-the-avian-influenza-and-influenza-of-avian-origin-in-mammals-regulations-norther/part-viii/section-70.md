> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 70. Measures at other restocked premises[^key-ceef698f9a80e155443fb363d48d8ca0]

*Extent: N.I.*

The Department may, in relation to other restocked premises where poultry or other captive birds are kept,—

- (a) apply the measures in regulation 68; and
- (b) by notice served on the occupier of the premises, require him to carry out the measures in regulation 69.
