# The Welfare Reform (2007 Act) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 335 (C. 23)

social security

The Welfare Reform (2007 Act) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made

18th July 2007

The Department for Social Development makes the following Order, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 60(1) of the Welfare Reform Act (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Welfare Reform (2007 Act) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007.

## 2. Appointed days

The day appointed for the coming into operation of section 52 of, and Schedule 6 to, the Welfare Reform Act (Northern Ireland) 2007—

- (a) for the purpose only of authorising the making of an order, is 19th July 2007; and
- (b) for all other purposes, is 10th August 2007.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 18th July 2007

(L.S.)

John O’Neill

A senior officer of the Department for Social Development

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the coming into operation of section 52 of, and Schedule 6 to, the Welfare Reform Act (Northern Ireland) 2007 (provision relating to “relevant employer” for the purposes of the Pneumoconiosis, etc., (Workers’ Compensation) (Northern Ireland) Order 1979 ([S.I. 1979/925 (N.I. 9)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1979/925))) on 19th July 2007 for the purpose only of authorising the making of an order and 10th August 2007 for all other purposes.
