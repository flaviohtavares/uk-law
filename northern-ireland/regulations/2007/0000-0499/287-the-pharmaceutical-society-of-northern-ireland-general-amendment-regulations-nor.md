# The Pharmaceutical Society of Northern Ireland (General) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Rule has been printed in substitution of the SR of the same number and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Rule.

2007 No. 287

PHARMACY

The Pharmaceutical Society of Northern Ireland (General) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made

4th May 2007

Coming into operation

1st June 2007

The Council of the Pharmaceutical Society of Northern Ireland, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 5 of the Pharmacy (Northern Ireland) Order 1976[^f00001], makes the following Regulations with the approval of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety[^f00002].

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Pharmaceutical Society of Northern Ireland (General) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 and shall come into operation on 1st June 2007.

(2) In these Regulations “the principal regulations” means the Pharmaceutical Society of Northern Ireland (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1994[^f00003].

## 2. Amendment of the principal regulations

In regulation 4 of the principal regulations (retention fees)—

- (a) in paragraph (1)(a) for “£295” there shall be substituted “£319”;
- (b) in paragraph (2)(a) for “£135” there shall be substituted “£146”;
- (c) in paragraph (2)(b) for “£108” there shall be substituted “£117”;
- (d) in paragraph (2)(c) for “£49” there shall be substituted “£53”.
