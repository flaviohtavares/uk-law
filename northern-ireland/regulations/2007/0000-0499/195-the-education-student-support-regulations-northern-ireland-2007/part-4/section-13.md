# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007, section 13

> Text as at 2008-07-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 13. Old system students[^c-ffe51cc118fdc30b4d0f07ae1449741b]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An old system student qualifies for a grant for fees in respect of the fees payable by him in connection with his attendance on a designated course in accordance with Chapter 3 of this Part.

(2) An old system student qualifies for a fee contribution loan in respect of the fees payable by him in connection with his attendance on a designated course in accordance with Chapter 4 of this Part.
