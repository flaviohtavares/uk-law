> Text as at 2008-07-17.
>

## 14. Requirement to enter a contract for a loan for fees[^key-071361ee97ee1fe6b3b1b81e15c490f1]

*Extent: N.I.*

To receive a loan for fees payable under this Part, an eligible student must enter into a contract with the Department.
