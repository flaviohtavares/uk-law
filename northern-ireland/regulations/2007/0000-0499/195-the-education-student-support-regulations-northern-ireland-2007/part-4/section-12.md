## 12. Current system students[^key-dcbff403b548ebb13487542b476ca0fb]

*Extent: N.I.*

A current system student qualifies for a fee loan in respect of the fees payable by him in connection with his attendance on a designated course in accordance with Chapter 2 of this Part.
