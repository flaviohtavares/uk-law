## 38. Old system students[^c-1fdb0d378165b4d7f19748742056588a]

*Extent: N.I.*

The following grants are available to an old system student in connection with a designated course if he meets the relevant qualifying conditions in this Part—

- (a) disabled students’ allowance;
- (b) grant for students who have left care;
- (c) grant for dependants;
- (d) grant for travel;
- (e) higher education bursary.
