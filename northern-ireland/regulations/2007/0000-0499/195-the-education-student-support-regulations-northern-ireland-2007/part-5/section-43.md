# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007, section 43

> Text as at 2008-07-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 43. General[^c-e1d9029d8bc59350a99ac717365c7147]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The grant for dependants consists of the following elements—

- (a) adult dependants’ grant;
- (b) childcare grant;
- (c) parents’ learning allowance.

(2) The qualifying conditions for each element and the amounts payable are set out in regulations 45 to 48.
