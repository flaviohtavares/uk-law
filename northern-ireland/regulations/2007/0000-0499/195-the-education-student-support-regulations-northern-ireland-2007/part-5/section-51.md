# The Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007, section 51

> Text as at 2008-07-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 51. Qualifying conditions for the grant for travel – courses in medicine and dentistry[^c-713f82651565292028da26183ca541f1]

*Extent: N.I.*

A grant is available to an eligible student attending a course in medicine or dentistry (a necessary part of which is a period of study by way of clinical training) in respect of the reasonable expenditure which he is obliged to incur in an academic year for the purpose of attending in connection with his course any hospital or other premises in the United Kingdom or the Republic of Ireland (not comprised in the institution) at which facilities for clinical training are provided other than expenditure incurred for the purpose of residential study away from the institution.
