> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 37. Current system students[^key-0b26fd77f02b3728446de466802e6763]

*Extent: N.I.*

The following grants are available to a current system student in connection with a designated course if he meets the relevant qualifying conditions in this Part—

- (a) disabled students’ allowance;
- (b) grant for students who have left care;
- (c) grant for dependants;
- (d) grant for travel;
- (e) maintenance grant or special support grant.
