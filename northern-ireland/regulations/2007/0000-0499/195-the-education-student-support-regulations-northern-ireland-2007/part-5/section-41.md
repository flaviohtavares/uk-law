## 41. Qualifying conditions for the disabled students’ allowance[^c-6cc18f2bffe05e87e0d34ce38c230868]

*Extent: N.I.*

An eligible student qualifies for a grant to assist with the additional expenditure which the Department is satisfied that he is obliged to incur in connection with his attendance on a designated course by reason of a disability to which he is subject.
