# The Housing (Amendment) (2006 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 37 (C. 1)

HOUSING

The Housing (Amendment) (2006 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made

25th January 2007

The Department for Social Development makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 1(3) of the Housing (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 2006[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Housing (Amendment) (2006 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007.

## 2. Appointed Day

Article 3 of, and the Schedule to, the Housing (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 2006 shall come into operation on 1st April 2007.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 25th January 2007.

D. M. Crothers

A senior officer of the

Department for Social Development

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the remaining provisions of the Housing (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 2006 into operation on 1st April 2007.
