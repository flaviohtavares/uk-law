The Department for Employment and Learning makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 15 of the Work and Families (Northern Ireland) Order 2006 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808][^f00001]:

## 1. Citation and commencement (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-2befcb52f5e0ceff13f66964d9d00525][^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]

These Regulations may be cited as the Working Time (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 and shall come into operation on 1st October 2007. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]

## 2. Amendment to the Working Time Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1998 (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-2659ee36abc18d2971c0c4aeaf89d7b3][^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]

(1) The Working Time Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1998 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808][^f00002] shall be amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) to (9). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]

(2) After regulation 13 (entitlement to annual leave), insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]

> **13A. Entitlement to additional annual leave (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]**
>
> (1) Subject to regulation 25C and paragraphs (3) and (5), a worker is entitled in each leave year to a period of additional leave determined in accordance with paragraph (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]
>
> (2) The period of additional leave to which a worker is entitled under paragraph (1) is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]
>
> - (a) in any leave year beginning on or after 1st October 2007 but before 1st April 2008, 0.8 weeks; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]
> - (b) in any leave year beginning before 1st October 2007, a proportion of 0.8 weeks equivalent to the proportion of the year beginning on 1st October 2007 which would have elapsed at the end of that leave year; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]
> - (c) in any leave year beginning on 1st April 2008, 0.8 weeks; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]
> - (d) in any leave year beginning after 1st April 2008 but before 1st April 2009, 0.8 weeks and a proportion of another 0.8 weeks equivalent to the proportion of the year beginning on 1st April 2009 which would have elapsed at the end of that leave year; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]
> - (e) in any leave year beginning on or after 1st April 2009, 1.6 weeks. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]
>
> (3) The aggregate entitlement provided for in paragraph (2) and regulation 13(1) is subject to a maximum of 28 days. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]
>
> (4) A worker’s leave year begins for the purposes of this regulation on the same date as the worker’s leave year begins for the purposes of regulation 13. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]
>
> (5) Where the date on which a worker’s employment begins is later than the date on which his first leave year begins, the additional leave to which he is entitled in that leave year is a proportion of the period applicable under paragraph (2) equal to the proportion of that leave year remaining on the date on which his employment begins. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]
>
> (6) Leave to which a worker is entitled under this regulation may be taken in instalments, but it may not be replaced by a payment in lieu except where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]
>
> - (a) the worker’s employment is terminated; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]
> - (b) the leave is an entitlement that arises under paragraph (2)(a), (b) or (c); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]
> - (c) the leave is an entitlement to 0.8 weeks that arises under paragraph (2)(d) in respect of that part of the leave year which would have elapsed before 1st April 2009. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]
>
> (7) A relevant agreement may provide for any leave to which a worker is entitled under this regulation to be carried forward into the leave year immediately following the leave year in respect of which it is due. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]

(3) After regulation 25B insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]

> **25C. Entitlement to additional annual leave under a relevant agreement (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]**
>
> (1) Regulation 13A does not apply in relation to a worker whose employer, as at 1st October 2007 and by virtue of a relevant agreement, provides each worker employed by him with an annual leave entitlement of 1.6 weeks or 8 days (whichever is the lesser) in addition to each worker’s entitlement under regulation 13, provided that such additional annual leave— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]
>
> - (a) may not be replaced by a payment in lieu except in relation to a worker whose employment is terminated; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]
> - (b) may not be carried forward into a leave year other than that which immediately follows the leave year in respect of which the leave is due; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]
> - (c) is leave for which the worker is entitled to be paid at not less than the rate of a week’s pay in respect of each week of leave, calculated in accordance with Articles 17 to 20 of the 1996 Order, modified such that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]
>   - (i) references to the employee are references to the worker; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]
>   - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808] references to the employee’s contract of employment are references to the worker’s contract; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]
>   - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808] the calculation date is the first day of the period of leave in question; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]
>   - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808] the references to Articles 23 and 24 do not apply. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]
>
> (2) Notwithstanding paragraph (1), any additional annual leave in excess of 1.6 weeks or 8 days (whichever is the lesser) to which a worker is entitled, shall not be subject to the conditions of that paragraph. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]
>
> (3) This regulation shall cease to apply to a worker from the day when an employer ceases to provide additional annual leave in accordance with the conditions in paragraph (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]

(4) Regulation 13(6) shall be omitted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]

(5) In regulations 14(1)(b) and (3), 15(1), 16(1) and paragraph 1(a) of Schedule 2, after “regulation 13” insert “and regulation 13A”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]

(6) In regulations 15(2)(a) and 15A(1), after “regulation 13” insert “or regulation 13A”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]

(7) In regulation 15A(2), after “regulation 13(1)” insert “and regulation 13A(2), subject to the limit contained in regulation 13A(3),”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]

(8) In regulation 18(2) after “13” insert “, 13A”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]

(9) In regulation 30(1)(a) for head (i) substitute— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]

> - (i) regulation 10(1) or (2), 11(1), (2) or (3), 12(1) or (4), 13 or 13A; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]

## 3. Amendment to the Industrial Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005 (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-08765abd64c84de140e32cb9107d2108][^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]

In rule 22(5)(e) of Schedule 1 to the Industrial Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808][^f00003] after “13,” insert “13A,”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Employment and Learning on 20th July 2007. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]

Sir Reg Empey *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]

Minister for Employment and Learning *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]

EXPLANATORY NOTE *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]

(This note is not part of the Regulations) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]

These Regulations, made under powers contained in Article 15 of the Work and Families (Northern Ireland) Order 2006, amend the Working Time Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1998 (the “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]Working Time Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]”).

The Regulations add a new regulation 13A to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808] Working Time Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808] to increase the annual leave entitlement from 4 weeks to 5.6 weeks. Regulation 13A(2) provides that workers become eligible for the first additional 0.8 weeks from 1st October 2007; for the second 0.8 weeks from 1st April 2009. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]

Regulation 13A(3) caps the aggregate entitlement provided for by regulations 13 and 13A at 28 days. Regulation 13A(6) provides that leave may not be replaced by a payment in lieu except where the employment is terminated or for an initial transitional phase. Regulation 13A(7) provides for the possibility of limited carry over of the additional annual leave in that a relevant jagreement may provide for leave to be carried forward into the leave year immediately following the leave year in respect of which the entitlement is due. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]

A new regulation 25C is added to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808] Working Time Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808] to provide that regulation 13A shall not apply to a worker whose employer, as at 1st October 2007, already provides each worker employed by him with an entitlement to additional annual leave of 1.6 weeks or 8 days (whichever is the lesser) by virtue of a relevant agreement provided such additional leave meets the conditions in paragraph (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]

Regulation 2(4) revokes regulation 13(6). Regulation 2(5) to (9) contains consequential amendments to other provisions in the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808] Working Time Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808], to add additional leave provided for under regulation 13A to the existing regime of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808] Working Time Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808] in respect of compensation payable on termination of employment, leave during the first year of employment, notice requirements in respect of when leave may be taken, entitlement to pay for leave, sectors partially or wholly excluded, remedies for breach of the provision and provision for those workers employed in agriculture. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]

Regulation 3 makes a consequential amendment to the Industrial Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4c09c853eb719494846731a05425e808]
