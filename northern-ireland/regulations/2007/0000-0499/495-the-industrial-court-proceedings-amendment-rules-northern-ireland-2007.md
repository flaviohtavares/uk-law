# The Industrial Court (Proceedings) (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 495

EMPLOYMENT

The Industrial Court (Proceedings) (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made

5th December 2007

Coming into operation

15th December 2007

The Department for Employment and Learning makes the following Rules in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 92(3)(f) of the Industrial Relations (Northern Ireland) Order 1992[^f00001] and now vested in it[^f00002]:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Rules may be cited as the Industrial Court (Proceedings) (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2007 and shall come into operation on 15th December 2007.

## 2. Appearance in proceedings before the Industrial Court by counsel or solicitor

For the purposes of Article 92(3)(f) of the Industrial Relations (Northern Ireland) Order 1992, in the Schedule to the Industrial Court (Proceedings) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00003], after the entry related to “The European Cooperative Society (Involvement of Employees) Regulations 2006” there shall be inserted—

- (a) in the first column “The Companies (Cross Border Mergers) Regulations 2007”;
- (b) in the second column “[S.I. 2007/2974](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/2974)”; and
- (c) in the third column “regulations 24, 27(1), 32(1), 33(4), 41(6), 42(2), 46(2), 49(2), 53(1), 54(1), 55, 56 and 57, as read with Schedule 2”.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Employment and Learning on 5th December 2007.

Sir Reg Empey

Minister for Employment and Learning

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules allow for any party to be represented by counsel or solicitor in proceedings before the Industrial Court arising from the Companies (Cross Border Mergers) Regulations 2007 by amending the Industrial Court (Proceedings) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2007.
