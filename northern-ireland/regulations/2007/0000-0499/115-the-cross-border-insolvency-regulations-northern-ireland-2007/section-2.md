## 2. Interpretation[^c-843f2a8959dfe0de149dcd256a8fbd83]

*Extent: N.I.*

In these Regulations “the UNCITRAL Model Law” means the Model Law on cross-border insolvency as adopted by the United Nations Commission on International Trade Law on 30<sup>th </sup>May 1997.
