# The Cross-Border Insolvency Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007, section 4

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 4. Modification of Northern Ireland insolvency law[^c-31fe19035ed9ff38d1e2c7b317879208]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Northern Ireland insolvency law (as defined in article 2 of the UNCITRAL Model Law as set out in Schedule 1) and Part 4 of the Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989[^f00004] shall apply with such modifications as the context requires for the purpose of giving effect to the provisions of these Regulations.

(2) In the case of any conflict between any provision of Northern Ireland insolvency law or of Part 4 of the Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989 and the provisions of these Regulations, the latter shall prevail.
