# The Cross-Border Insolvency Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007, section 7

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 7. Disapplication of Article 3 of the Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989[^c-02c0f5bbe39128e7a14cd9835e1b3074]

*Extent: N.I.*

Nothing in Article 3 of the Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989[^f00005] applies to anything done by a foreign representative—

- (a) under or by virtue of these Regulations;
- (b) in relation to relief granted or cooperation or coordination provided under these Regulations.
