# The Residential Family Centres Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007, section 33

> Text as at 2020-04-02.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 33. Appointment of liquidators etc.[^key-a10fe9cb721f090ff552bae6e5d0987e]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Any person to whom paragraph (2) applies must—

- (a) forthwith notify the RQIA[^key-1ab6982513a93aea57feba0c8096e8f0] of his appointment indicating the reasons for it;
- (b) appoint a manager in any case where there is no registered manager; and
- (c) within 28 days of his appointment, notify the RQIA of his intentions regarding the future operation of the residential family centre.

(2) This paragraph applies to any person appointed as—

- (a) the receiver or manager of the property of a company or a partnership which is a registered provider of a residential family centre;
- (b) a liquidator or provisional liquidator of a company which is the registered provider of a residential family centre; or
- (c) the trustee in bankruptcy of a registered provider of a residential family centre.
