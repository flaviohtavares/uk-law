# The Residential Family Centres Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007, section 27

> Text as at 2020-04-02.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 27. Review of quality of care[^c-d5734a5b6841390e418a10a3135ab1db]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The registered person shall introduce and ensure systems are maintained for reviewing at appropriate intervals the quality of care and other service provision in or for the purposes of the family residential centre and that any such review is undertaken no less than annually.

(2) The registered person shall supply to the RQIA[^c-1ab6982513a93aea57feba0c8096e8f0] a report in respect of any review conducted by him for the purpose of paragraph (1) and make a copy of the report available to residents.

(3) The system referred to in paragraph (1) shall provide for consultation with residents and their representatives.
