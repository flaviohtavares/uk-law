# The Residential Family Centres Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007, section 21

> Text as at 2020-04-02.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 21. Staff views as to conduct of residential family centre[^c-96d8c9c475cf6ae9ea625ce50c134d45]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies to any matter relating to the conduct of the centre so far as it may affect the care, safety or welfare of residents.

(2) The registered person shall make arrangements to enable staff to inform the registered person and the RQIA[^c-1ab6982513a93aea57feba0c8096e8f0] and the local HSS trust in the area of which the centre is situated, in confidence of their views about any matter to which this regulation applies.
