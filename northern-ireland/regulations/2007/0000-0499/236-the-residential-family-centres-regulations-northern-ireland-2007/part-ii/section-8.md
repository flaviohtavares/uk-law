# The Residential Family Centres Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007, section 8

> Text as at 2020-04-02.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 8. Appointment of registered manager[^c-dc838dbb5958a0c284e17d734b83da6d]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The registered provider shall appoint an individual to manage a residential family centre if—

- (a) there is no registered manager in respect of the residential family centre; and
- (b) the registered provider—
  - (i) is an organisation or a partnership;
  - (ii) is not a fit person to manage a residential family centre; or
  - (iii) is not, or does not intend to be, managing the residential family centre himself.

(2) Where the registered provider appoints a person to manage the residential family centre, he shall forthwith give notice to the RQIA[^c-1ab6982513a93aea57feba0c8096e8f0] of—

- (a) the name of the person so appointed; and
- (b) the date on which the appointment is to take effect.
