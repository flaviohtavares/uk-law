# The Residential Family Centres Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007, section 11

> Text as at 2020-04-02.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 11. Registered person — notification of offences[^key-ce24810d320ff3bf9352143c14c80a64]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where the registered person or the responsible individual is convicted of any criminal offence, whether in Northern Ireland or elsewhere, he shall forthwith give notice in writing to the RQIA[^key-1ab6982513a93aea57feba0c8096e8f0] of—

- (a) the date and place of the conviction;
- (b) the offence of which he was convicted; and
- (c) the penalty imposed on him in respect of the offence.
