# The Residential Family Centres Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007, section 9

> Text as at 2020-04-02.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 9. Fitness of registered manager[^key-c10fc7eb8ff9ccc1f3ba80a1a2f6ca99]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A person shall not manage a residential family centre unless he is fit to do so.

(2) A person is not fit to manage a residential family centre unless—

- (a) he is of integrity and good character;
- (b) having regard to the size of the residential family centre, the statement of purpose, and the number and needs of the residents—
  - (i) he has the qualifications, skills and experience necessary to manage the centre; and
  - (ii) he is physically and mentally fit to manage the centre;
  - (iii)[^key-32f687348aadce7de6a84d17158a30a4] subject to head (iv), he is registered in the relevant part of the register;
  - (iv) head (iii) does not apply to a person who, in order to perform the duties for which the person is so employed is required by any statutory provision, to be registered with any person or body other than the Northern Ireland Social Care Council and is so registered.
- (c) full and satisfactory information is available in relation to him in respect of the matters specified in Schedule 2.
