# The Offshore Installations (Safety Case) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007, section 8

>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 8. Safety case for non-production installation[^key-7baa626c1ff2ecb53b67c3f90f769c65]

*Extent: N.I.*

Subject to regulation 27, the owner of a non-production installation shall ensure that it is not moved in internal waters[^key-9570d7d1035b0a8a4dd806a57f56e416] with a view to its being operated there unless—

- (a) he has prepared a safety case containing the particulars specified in regulation 12 and Schedule 4;
- (b) he has sent the safety case to the Executive at least three months (or such shorter period as the Executive may specify) before the movement of the installation in those waters with a view to its being operated there; and
- (c) the Executive has accepted the safety case.
