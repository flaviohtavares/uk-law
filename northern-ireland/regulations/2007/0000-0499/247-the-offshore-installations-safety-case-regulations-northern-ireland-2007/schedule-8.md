# The Offshore Installations (Safety Case) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007, SCHEDULE 8

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 8 — MATTERS TO BE PROVIDED FOR IN A VERIFICATION SCHEME

*Extent: N.I.*

Regulation 19(2)

**1.**[^key-ca82a9cf44babd59d85ff6bebad9ad9f] The principles to be applied by the duty holder for the installation in selecting persons—

- (a) to perform functions under the scheme; and
- (b) to keep the scheme under review.

**2.**[^key-4295f45adb901b375b6c2b9f9ccf9ed4] Arrangements for the communication of information necessary for the proper implementation, or revision, of the scheme to the persons referred to in paragraph 1.

**3.**[^key-c1f96372266b15e27277d6e2bc2d9e6b] The nature and frequency of examination and testing.

**4.**[^key-9395876bc037b69e40ab12138963ba07] Arrangements for review and revision of the scheme.

**5.**[^key-a06c7ee2c90e3de03184c945e5b0c8cf] The arrangements for the making and preservation of records showing—

- (a) the examination and testing carried out;
- (b) the findings;
- (c) remedial action recommended; and
- (d) remedial action performed.

**6.**[^key-361afd24504259d7e31579b5ea1f086c] Arrangements for communicating the matters specified in paragraph 5 to an appropriate level in the management system of the duty holder for the installation.
