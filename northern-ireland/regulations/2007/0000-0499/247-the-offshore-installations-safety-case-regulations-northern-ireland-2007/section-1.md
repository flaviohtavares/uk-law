# The Offshore Installations (Safety Case) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007, section 1

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation and commencement[^key-0f7b06a14ea7af45dd72d093b0743637]

*Extent: N.I.*

These Regulations may be cited as the Offshore Installations (Safety Case) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 and shall come into operation on 21st May 2007.

*Extent: N.I.*

**1.** These Regulations replace the Offshore Installations (Safety Case) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1993 ([S.R. 1993 No. 221](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1993/221)) (“the 1993 Regulations”) to provide for the preparation of safety cases for offshore installations and for the notification of specified activities to the Health and Safety Executive for Northern Ireland (“the Executive”).
