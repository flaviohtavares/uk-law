# The Road Traffic (2007 Order) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 302 (C. 19)

ROAD TRAFFIC AND VEHICLES

The Road Traffic (2007 Order) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made

15th June 2007

The Department of the Environment makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 1(3) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 2007[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic (2007 Order) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007.

(2) In this Order “the Order” means the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 2007.

## 2. Appointed Day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the Order specified in the Schedule shall be 27th June 2007.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 15th June 2007.

Stanley Duncan

A senior officer of the

Department of the Environment
