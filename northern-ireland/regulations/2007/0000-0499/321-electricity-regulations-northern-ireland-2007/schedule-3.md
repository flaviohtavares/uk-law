# Electricity Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007, SCHEDULE 3

> Text as at 2007-11-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 3 — Amendments to Energy Order

*Extent: N.I.*

Regulation 61

**1.**[^key-0ae8270e3264b8e444973d94a8b761a7] In Article 12(2) for the “; and” at the end of sub-paragraph (b) there shall be substituted a full stop and sub-paragraph (c) shall be repealed.

**2.**[^key-a95b70cddd7abc2a52600a3557c3bc55] In Article 20(1)(a) there shall be omitted “or (2)”.

**3.**[^key-f5a156e48018d1c0d9885e4e6bebcd12] In Article 41—

- (a) in paragraph (1) at the end of sub-paragraph (a) there shall be added “and”, at the end of sub-paragraph (b) for “; and” there shall be substituted a full stop and sub-paragraph (c) shall be repealed.
- (b) in paragraph (2) after “26,” there shall be inserted “43A”.

**4.**[^key-27ba9f3ec10472282b9f71dc405906a3] In Article 52(3) there shall be omitted “or (2)”.

**5.**[^key-1aaf7ae45b8e7c009c743df73cf7a41a] In paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 3 there shall be omitted the words from “in the definition” to “and” where it first occurs.

**6.**[^key-7080b48f04acd1bed37478e79f723f91] In Schedule 5 in the entry relating to Article 3 of the 1992 Order there shall be omitted the words from “and in the definition of” to the end.
