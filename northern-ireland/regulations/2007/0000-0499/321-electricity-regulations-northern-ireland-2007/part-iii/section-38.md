# Electricity Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007, section 38

> Text as at 2007-11-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 38. Provision of Information[^key-817eec506817f91493fe2b5882fa5bae]

*Extent: N.I.*

It shall be the duty of each holder of a licence under Article 10 of the 1992 Order and any person who is a nominated associate to provide the Authority with all such information and other assistance as he may require for the purposes of or in connection with the exercise of any function conferred on it by regulation 35 or 36 in relation to such licences.

**39.**[^key-ced37372a0ff2bb62514fa7a17e6f33c] The Authority shall not exercise any function conferred on it by regulation 35 or 36 except after consultation with—

- (a) the Department;
- (b) the nominated associate; and
- (c) such holders of licences under Article 10 of the 1992 Order[^f00011],

as it considers appropriate.
