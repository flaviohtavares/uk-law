## 3. Placing on the market of quick-frozen foodstuffs (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_1159ceb3-5dff-46bb-d1f8-e267bde76ec2]

No person shall place a quick-frozen foodstuff on the market for human consumption unless the conditions set out in paragraph 1 of Schedule 2 are satisfied in relation to it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_1159ceb3-5dff-46bb-d1f8-e267bde76ec2]

## 4. Packaging of quick-frozen foodstuffs intended for the ultimate consumer (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_1159ceb3-5dff-46bb-d1f8-e267bde76ec2]

No person shall place any quick-frozen foodstuff intended for the ultimate consumer on the market unless— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_1159ceb3-5dff-46bb-d1f8-e267bde76ec2]

- (a) it has been packed by its manufacturer or packer in such prepackaging as is suitable to protect it from microbial and other forms of external contamination and against dehydration; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_1159ceb3-5dff-46bb-d1f8-e267bde76ec2]
- (b) the quick-frozen foodstuff has remained in such prepackaging up to the time of placing on the market. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_1159ceb3-5dff-46bb-d1f8-e267bde76ec2]

## 5. Labelling of quick-frozen foodstuffs[^key-ad40b132254ad879d8843693edf83181]

(1) The description “quick-frozen” or any other description listed in Article 8.1(a) of Directive 89/108 shall not be used in the labelling for the purpose of placing on the market of—

- (a) a quick-frozen foodstuff; or
- (b) a food which by virtue of that labelling becomes a quick-frozen foodstuff.

(2) A quick-frozen foodstuff intended for supply, without further processing, to the ultimate consumer or to a catering establishment shall, in addition to the description “quick-frozen” (and, as the intending supplier may see fit, any other description listed in Article 8.1(a) of Directive 89/108) added to its sales name, be marked or labelled on its packaging, container or wrapping, or on a label attached thereto, with-

- (a) an indication of the date of minimum durability;
- (b) an indication of the maximum period during which it is advisable to store it;
- (c) an indication of—
  - (i) the temperature at which; and/or
  - (ii) the equipment in which,

  it is advisable to store it;
- (d) a reference allowing identification of the batch to which it belongs; and
- (e) a clear message of the type “do not refreeze after defrosting”.

(3) Any other quick-frozen foodstuff shall, in addition to the description “quick-frozen” (and, as the supplier may see fit, any other description listed in Article 8.1(a) of Directive 89/108) added to its sales name, be marked or labelled on its packaging, container or wrapping, or on a label attached thereto, with-

- (a) a reference allowing identification of the batch to which it belongs; and
- (b) the name or business name and address of the manufacturer or packer, or of a person established within the European Union[^key-e915449bcc39ca1e2b3004334be258a1] who places that foodstuff on the market.

(4) No person shall place on the market any food intended for the ultimate consumer or any catering establishment where its labelling or marking contravenes paragraph (1) or (2) and no person shall place on the market any food intended for any other person where its labelling or marking contravenes paragraph (1) or (3).

## 6. Equipment (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_1159ceb3-5dff-46bb-d1f8-e267bde76ec2]

Each food operator handling a quick-frozen foodstuff intended for placing on the market for human consumption shall ensure during each stage during which it is within his care and control that the equipment used in respect of that foodstuff is such as to ensure that no act or omission on his part would cause the placing on the market of the foodstuff for human consumption to contravene these Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_1159ceb3-5dff-46bb-d1f8-e267bde76ec2]

## 7. Sampling and method of measuring temperatures (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_1159ceb3-5dff-46bb-d1f8-e267bde76ec2]

Where, further to an inspection, an authorised officer has reasonable grounds to believe that the temperatures that are being or have been maintained in respect of any quick-frozen foodstuff are not the temperatures prescribed for such foodstuff in paragraph 1(e) and (f) of Schedule 2, he shall further inspect such quick-frozen foodstuff and such temperatures in accordance with the provisions of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_1159ceb3-5dff-46bb-d1f8-e267bde76ec2] Directive 92/2 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_1159ceb3-5dff-46bb-d1f8-e267bde76ec2].

## 8. Records (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_1159ceb3-5dff-46bb-d1f8-e267bde76ec2]

## SCHEDULE 2 (affected by repeal, see note) — Conditions that have to be satisfied in relation to a quick-frozen foodstuff if it is to be placed on the market for human consumption (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_1159ceb3-5dff-46bb-d1f8-e267bde76ec2][^M_F_1159ceb3-5dff-46bb-d1f8-e267bde76ec2]

Regulations 3 and 7 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_1159ceb3-5dff-46bb-d1f8-e267bde76ec2]

**1.** The conditions are that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_1159ceb3-5dff-46bb-d1f8-e267bde76ec2]

- (a) a quick-frozen foodstuff has been manufactured from raw materials of sound, genuine and merchantable quality and no other materials; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_1159ceb3-5dff-46bb-d1f8-e267bde76ec2]
- (b) no raw material has been used in the manufacture of the quick-frozen foodstuff unless, at the time of its use, it would have been lawful for the raw material to be placed on the market for human consumption; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_1159ceb3-5dff-46bb-d1f8-e267bde76ec2]
- (c) the preparation and quick-freezing of the quick-frozen foodstuff have been carried out with sufficient promptness, and by use of appropriate technical equipment, to minimise any chemical, biochemical and microbiological changes to the food comprised in it; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_1159ceb3-5dff-46bb-d1f8-e267bde76ec2]
- (d) no cryogenic medium other than an authorised cryogenic medium has been used in direct contact with any food comprised in the quick-frozen foodstuff; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_1159ceb3-5dff-46bb-d1f8-e267bde76ec2]
- (e) the quick-freezing of each food comprised in the quick-frozen foodstuff has resulted in the temperature of that food after thermal stabilisation being –18 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_1159ceb3-5dff-46bb-d1f8-e267bde76ec2]<sup>o</sup>C or colder; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_1159ceb3-5dff-46bb-d1f8-e267bde76ec2]
- (f) following the quick-freezing and thermal stabilisation of each food comprised in the quick-frozen foodstuff in compliance with sub-paragraph (e) the temperature of that food has been maintained at a level no warmer than –18 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_1159ceb3-5dff-46bb-d1f8-e267bde76ec2]<sup>o</sup>C except where a permitted exception relating to the temperature of the food applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_1159ceb3-5dff-46bb-d1f8-e267bde76ec2]

**2.** For the purposes of paragraph 1— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_1159ceb3-5dff-46bb-d1f8-e267bde76ec2]

- (a) an authorised cryogenic medium is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_1159ceb3-5dff-46bb-d1f8-e267bde76ec2]
  - (i) air, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_1159ceb3-5dff-46bb-d1f8-e267bde76ec2]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_1159ceb3-5dff-46bb-d1f8-e267bde76ec2] nitrogen, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_1159ceb3-5dff-46bb-d1f8-e267bde76ec2]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_1159ceb3-5dff-46bb-d1f8-e267bde76ec2] carbon dioxide; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_1159ceb3-5dff-46bb-d1f8-e267bde76ec2]
- (b) a permitted exception relating to the temperature of any food applies when— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_1159ceb3-5dff-46bb-d1f8-e267bde76ec2]
  - (i) that food is kept within brief periods during transport (including local distribution) at a temperature warmer than *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_1159ceb3-5dff-46bb-d1f8-e267bde76ec2] -18<sup>o</sup>C but not warmer than -15 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_1159ceb3-5dff-46bb-d1f8-e267bde76ec2]<sup>o</sup>C; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_1159ceb3-5dff-46bb-d1f8-e267bde76ec2]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_1159ceb3-5dff-46bb-d1f8-e267bde76ec2] that food is kept in a retail display cabinet at a temperature warmer than -18 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_1159ceb3-5dff-46bb-d1f8-e267bde76ec2]<sup>o</sup>C to an extent consistent with good storage practice but not warmer than -12 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_1159ceb3-5dff-46bb-d1f8-e267bde76ec2]<sup>o</sup>C.
