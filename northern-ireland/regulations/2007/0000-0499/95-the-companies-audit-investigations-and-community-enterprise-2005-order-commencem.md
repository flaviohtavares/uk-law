# The Companies (Audit, Investigations and Community Enterprise) (2005 Order) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 95 (C. 5)

COMPANIES

The Companies (Audit, Investigations and Community Enterprise) (2005 Order) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made

19th February 2007

The Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 1(2) of the Companies (Audit, Investigations and Community Enterprise) (Northern Ireland) Order 2005[^f00002] makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Companies (Audit, Investigations and Community Enterprise) (2005 Order) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007.

(2) In this Order –

- “the 2005 Order” means the Companies (Audit, Investigations and Community Enterprise) (Northern Ireland) Order 2005.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of Article 10 of the 2005 Order is 6th April 2007.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment on 19th February 2007

M Bohill

A senior officer of the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the law relating to auditors’ rights to information and comes into operation on 6th April 2007.
