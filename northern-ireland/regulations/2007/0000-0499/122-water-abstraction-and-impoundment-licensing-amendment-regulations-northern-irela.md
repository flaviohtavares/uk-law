# Water Abstraction and Impoundment (Licensing) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 122

environmental protection

Water Abstraction and Impoundment (Licensing) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made

28th February 2007

Coming into operation

1st April 2007

The Department of the Environment makes the following Regulations in exercise of its powers under Articles 20(1) and (3), and 61(2) of the Water (Northern Ireland) Order 1999[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Water Abstraction and Impoundment (Licensing) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 and shall come into operation on 1<sup>st</sup> April 2007.

## 2. Amendment of the Water Abstraction and Impoundment (Licensing) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006

(1) The Water Abstraction and Impoundment (Licensing) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006[^f00002] shall be amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) to (6).

(2) After regulation 9, insert the following regulation–

> **9A.** (1) The Department shall, within 14 days of receipt of an application under regulation 8, serve notice of that application on every water undertaker.
>
> (2) A water undertaker may make representations in writing to the Department within 28 days from the date on which the notice under paragraph (1) was served.

.

(3) In regulation 10(2)(b) for “regulation 9(4)” substitute “regulations 9(4) and 9A(2)”.

(4) In regulation 13(3) for “Regulations 9 and 10(2)” substitute “Regulations 9,9A and 10(2)”.

(5) In regulation 17, for paragraph (1) substitute the following paragraph—

> (1) For the purposes of considering representations made—
>
> - (a) under regulation 9 and under that regulation as applied by regulations 13 and 15; and
> - (b) under regulation 9A(2),
>
> the Department may direct the Appeals Commission to hold a public local inquiry.

.

(6) In Schedule 4, in paragraph 3, for “Regulations 9 and 19” substitute “Regulations 9, 9A and 19”.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 28 February 2007

Wesley Shannon

A senior officer of the Department of the Environment

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations amend the Water Abstraction and Impoundment (Licensing) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006 (“the principal Regulations”) by inserting a new regulation 9A. The Regulations include some amendments that are consequential to the insertion of regulation 9A.

Regulation 9A provides for a water undertaker to be notified of any licence applications made to the Department under regulation 8 of the principal Regulations and for the water undertaker to make representations to the Department within 28 days of receipt of the notification.
