# Salaries (Comptroller and Auditor General) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 259

SALARIES

Salaries (Comptroller and Auditor General) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made

23rd April 2007

Coming into operation

8th June 2007

To be laid before Parliament under paragraph 7(3) of the Schedule to the Northern Ireland Act 2000

In exercise of the powers conferred by Article 4(1) of the Audit (Northern Ireland) Order 1987 [^f00001] I make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and Commencement

This Order may be cited as the Salaries (Comptroller and Auditor General) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007 and shall come into operation on 8<sup>th</sup> June 2007.

## 2. Revocation

The Salaries (Comptroller and Auditor General) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006 [^f00002] is revoked.

## 3. Salary

The annual salary payable to the holder of the office of Comptroller and Auditor General shall be increased as follows -

- (a) from £130,219 to £132,172 with effect from 1<sup>st</sup> April 2007
- (b) from £132,172 to £133,422 with effect from 1<sup>st</sup> November 2007

Peter Hain

One of Her Majesty’s Principle Secretaries of State

Northern Ireland Office

23rd April 2007

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for an increase in the annual salary payable to the Comptroller and Auditor General following recommendations made in the Report of the Review Body on Senior Salaries in 2007 (HMSO CM 7030).

The increase, which is to be staged, is made with effect from 1<sup>st</sup> April 2007 and 1<sup>st</sup> November 2007 under Article 4(1) of the Audit (Northern Ireland) Order 1987.

Article 2 revokes the [Salaries (Comptroller and Auditor General) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006 SR 302/06](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/302), which took effect from 1<sup>st</sup> April 2006.
