# The Export Restrictions (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 503

ANIMALS

Animal Health

The Export Restrictions (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made

13th December 2007

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development, is designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002], in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community.

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred upon it by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as The Export Restrictions (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 and come into operation on 13th December 2007.

## 2. Interpretation

The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00003] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

## 3. Revocation

(1) Regulations 4 to 7 and 9 to 12 of The Export Restrictions (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00004] are hereby revoked.

(2) The Export Restrictions (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007, except insofar as revoked by paragraph (1) shall be revoked on 31st December 2007.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 13th December 2007.

L.S.

Elizabeth Redmond

A senior officer of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke The Export Restrictions (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 ([S.R. 2007 No. 357](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2007/357) as amended by [S.R. 2007 No. 364](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2007/364), [S.R. 2007 No. 399](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2007/399) and [S.R. 2007 No. 441](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2007/441)). The Regulations give effect to the Commission Decision of 13th December 2007 amending Commission Decision [2007/554/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2007/0554) concerning certain protection measures against foot-and-mouth disease in the United Kingdom (not yet published in the Official Journal) .
