# The Local Government (2014 Act) (Commencement No. 4) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 209 (C.15)

Local Government

The Local Government (2014 Act) (Commencement No. 4) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015

Made

31st March 2015

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 129 of the Local Government Act (Northern Ireland) 2014[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Local Government (2014 Act) (Commencement No. 4) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015.

(2) In this Order “the Act” means the Local Government Act (Northern Ireland) 2014.

(3) In this Order any reference to a numbered section is a reference to a section of the Act.

## 2. Provisions of the Act coming into operation on 1st April 2015

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the Act listed in Schedule 1 is 1st April 2015.

## 3. Provisions of the Act coming into operation on 1st April 2016

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the Act listed in Schedule 2 is 1st April 2016.

## 4. Provisions of the Act coming into operation on 1st April 2017

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the provision of the Act listed in Schedule 3 is 1st April 2017.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 31st March 2015.

(L.S.)

Linda MacHugh

A senior officer of the Department of the Environment
