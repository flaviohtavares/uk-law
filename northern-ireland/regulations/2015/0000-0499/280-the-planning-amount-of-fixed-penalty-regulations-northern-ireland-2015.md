# The Planning (Amount of Fixed Penalty) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 280

Planning

The Planning (Amount of Fixed Penalty) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015

Laid before the Assembly in draft

Made

23rd June 2015

Coming into operation

6th July 2015

The Department of the Environment makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred on it by sections 153(9), 154(9) and 247(1) of the Planning Act (Northern Ireland) 2011[^f00001].

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Planning (Amount of Fixed Penalty) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015 and shall come into operation on 6th July 2015.

(2) In these Regulations “the 2011 Act” means the Planning Act (Northern Ireland) 2011.

## 2. Amount of fixed penalty

The amount prescribed for the purposes of—

- (a) section 153(9) of the 2011 Act is £2,000; and
- (b) section 154(9) of the 2011 Act is £300.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 23rd June 2015

Angus Kerr

A senior officer of the Department of the Environment

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations prescribe the amount of the penalty payable under a fixed penalty notice served under section 153(9) or 154(9) of the Planning Act (Northern Ireland) 2011.
