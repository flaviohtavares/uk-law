# The Superannuation (District Councils) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 141

Pensions

The Superannuation (District Councils) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015

Made

10th March 2015

Coming into operation

1st April 2015

The Department of Finance and Personnel makes the following Order, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 3(4) of the Superannuation (Northern Ireland) Order 1972[^f00001] and now vested in it[^f00002].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Superannuation (District Councils) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015 and shall come into operation on 1<sup>st</sup> April 2015.

## 2. Addition to list of employments

There shall be added to the employments listed in Schedule 1 to the Superannuation (Northern Ireland) Order 1972 with effect from 1<sup>st</sup> April 2015, employment in the Northern Ireland district councils[^f00003].

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance and Personnel on 10th March 2015.

(L.S.)

Grace Nesbitt

A senior officer of the

Department of Finance and Personnel

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

This Order adds employment in the Northern Ireland district councils to the employments listed in Schedule 1 to the Superannuation (Northern Ireland) Order 1972 for which the Department of Finance and Personnel may make pension schemes.
