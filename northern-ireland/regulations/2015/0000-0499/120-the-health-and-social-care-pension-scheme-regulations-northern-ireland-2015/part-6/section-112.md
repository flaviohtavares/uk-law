# The Health and Social Care Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 112

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 112. Surviving adult dependant pension

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) If an active member, a deferred member or a pensioner member dies leaving a surviving adult dependant, the surviving adult dependant is entitled to a pension payable for life.

(2) In this Part, “**surviving adult dependant**” means, in relation to a deceased member of former member—

- (a) the surviving spouse,
- (b) the surviving civil partner, or
- (c)[^c-41227828e36235ef7027902732fe1da9] a surviving scheme partner.

(3) For the rate at which the pension is payable, see regulations 114 to 118.
