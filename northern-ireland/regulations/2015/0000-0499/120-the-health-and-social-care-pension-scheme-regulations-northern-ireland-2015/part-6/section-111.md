# The Health and Social Care Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 111

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 111. Lump sum on death

*Extent: N.I.*

A lump sum is payable in accordance with Schedule 12 in respect of the death of—

- (a)[^c-cc8d5c1add6267a74596cb7b6c330689] an active member;
- (b) a deferred member;
- (c) a pensioner member;
- (d) a recent leaver (within the meaning of that Schedule);
- (e) a re-employed pensioner (within the meaning of that Schedule);
- (f) a partial retirement pensioner (within the meaning of that Schedule); or
- (g) a pension credit member.
