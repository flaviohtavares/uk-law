# The Health and Social Care Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 23

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 23. Effect of break in pensionable service

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) In the following table a break in a period of pensionable service of a member (M) in circumstances set out in Column 1 has the effect described in Column 2.

Table

<table>
<tbody>
<tr><th><em>Column 1</em></th><th><em>Column 2</em></th></tr>
<tr><th colspan="2"><strong>Part 1</strong><strong>M leaves active membership, is neither qualified for a retirement pension pursuant to regulation 71 nor is a pensioner member because of rights resulting from that membership, then returns to active service</strong></th></tr>
<tr><td>1.The break-<br>(a) does not exceed one month; or<br>(b) is due to a trade dispute (within the meaning of Article 2(2) of the Jobseekers (Northern Ireland) Order 1995) .<br>2. No transfer payment is made to another scheme in respect of pensionable service before the break<br>3. It is immaterial if a refund of contributions is paid to M in respect of the period before the break.</td><td>The period of pensionable service before the break is treated as continuous with the period after the break</td></tr>
<tr><td>1. The break does not exceed 5 years.<br>2. No transfer payment is made to another scheme in respect of M's pensionable service before the break.<br>3. If a refund of contributions in respect of the period before the break is paid to M, it is repaid before the end of the period of 6 months starting on the first day of pensionable service after the break.</td><td>The period of pensionable service before the break is treated as continuous with the period after the break.</td></tr>
<tr><td>1. The break exceeds 5 years.<br>2. M may claim a refund of contributions in respect of pensionable service before the break.</td><td>The period of pensionable service before the break is not treated as continuous with the period after the break and does not count towards qualifying service.</td></tr>
<tr><th colspan="2"><strong>Part 2</strong><strong>M leaves active membership, is qualified for a retirement pension pursuant to regulation 71 but is not a pensioner member because of rights resulting from that membership, then returns to active service</strong></th></tr>
<tr><td>1. The break does not exceed 5 years.<br>2. No transfer payment is made to another scheme in respect of M's pensionable service before the break.</td><td>The period of pensionable service before the break is treated as continuous with the period after the break.</td></tr>
<tr><td>1. The break exceeds 5 years.<br>2. No transfer payment has been made to another scheme in respect of M's pensionable service before the break.</td><td>The period of pensionable service before the break is not treated as continuous with the period after the break, but does count towards qualifying service.</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>

[^c22440431]

(2) In paragraph (1) a reference to the repayment of a refund of contributions includes the repayment of any interest on the contributions.
