# The Health and Social Care Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 46

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 46. Determination of contributions payable

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The scheme manager must, after consultation with the scheme actuary, determine the amount of monthly payments to be paid in respect of a buy-out election.

(2) The scheme manager—

- (a) may determine the amount of the monthly payments by reference to the length of the period between the date of the buy-out election and the date when the member will reach prospective normal pension age; and
- (b) may exercise the functions under this paragraph so as to re-determine the amount of the monthly payments during that period.

(3) Unless the scheme manager re-determines the rate, monthly payments following a gap in service not exceeding 5 years are the same as before the gap.
