> Text as at 2026-03-02.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 55. Annual rate of additional (self only) pension

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The annual rate of additional (self only) pension specified in an additional pension election must be a whole number multiple of—

- (a) £250; or
- (b) such other amount as is determined by the scheme manager.
