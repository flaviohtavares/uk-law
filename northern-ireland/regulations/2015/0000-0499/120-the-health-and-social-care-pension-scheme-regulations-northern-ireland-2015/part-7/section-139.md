# The Health and Social Care Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 139

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 139. Effect of transfer-out

*Extent: N.I.*

If a transfer value payment is made under this Section in respect of a person's rights under the scheme, those rights are extinguished.
