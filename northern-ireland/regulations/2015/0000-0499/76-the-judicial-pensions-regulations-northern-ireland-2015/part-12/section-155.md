> Text as at 2024-05-24.

## 155. Scheme manager to be scheme administrator for the purposes of Part 4 of Finance Act 2004

*Extent: N.I.*

The scheme manager is appointed to be responsible for all functions that are functions conferred or imposed on the scheme administrator under Part 4 (pension schemes etc.) of the 2004 Act.
