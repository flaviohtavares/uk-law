# The Judicial Pensions Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 162

> Text as at 2024-05-24.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 162. Determination of questions

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Except as otherwise provided by these Regulations, any question arising under this scheme is to be determined by the scheme manager, whose decision on it is final.

(2) The scheme manager must, in consultation with the Judicial Pension Board, establish a procedure for the determination of disputes relating to members or other persons with an interest in the scheme.

(3) A procedure established under paragraph (2) must meet the requirements of Article 50 (requirement for dispute resolution arrangements) of the 1995 Order [^c22374691].
