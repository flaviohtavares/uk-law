> Text as at 2024-05-24.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 10. Scheme advisory board: membership

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The scheme advisory board is to consist of a chair appointed by the Department of Justice and at least 2 and no more than 16 persons appointed by the chair.

(2) Equal numbers of employer representatives and member representatives must be appointed under paragraph (1).

(3) The chair cannot be an employer representative or a member representative.

(4) The appointment of member representatives must be approved by the Lord Chief Justice.

(5) The appointment of employer representatives must be approved by the scheme manager.

(6) A member of the scheme advisory board is to hold and vacate office in accordance with the terms of that member's appointment.
