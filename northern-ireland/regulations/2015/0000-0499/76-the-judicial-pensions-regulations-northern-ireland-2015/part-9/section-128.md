> Text as at 2024-05-24.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 128. Deduction of payments for extra pension under Schedule 1

*Extent: N.I.*

Schedule 1 has effect for the purpose of enabling deductions of payments for—

- (a) added pension; and
- (b) an effective pension age.
