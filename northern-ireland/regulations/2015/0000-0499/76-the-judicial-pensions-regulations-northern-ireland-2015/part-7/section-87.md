> Text as at 2024-05-24.

## 87. Reduction in pension debit member's benefits

*Extent: N.I.*

The benefits to which a pension debit member is entitled under this Part are subject to the reduction to be made under Article 28 (reduction of benefit) of the 1999 Order.
