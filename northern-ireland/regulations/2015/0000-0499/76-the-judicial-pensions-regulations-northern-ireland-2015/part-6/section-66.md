# The Judicial Pensions Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 66

> Text as at 2024-05-24.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 66. Meaning of “permanent breakdown in health”

*Extent: N.I.*

For the purposes of this Chapter, a member's breakdown in health is “permanent” if it is likely to continue until the member reaches prospective normal pension age.
