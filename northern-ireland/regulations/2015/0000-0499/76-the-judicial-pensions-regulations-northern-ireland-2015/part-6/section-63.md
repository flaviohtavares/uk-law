> Text as at 2024-05-24.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 63. Entitlement to partial retirement pension or full retirement pension

*Extent: N.I.*

An active member of this scheme (P) who exercises the partial retirement option is entitled to the immediate payment for life of—

- (a) if P claims payment of part only of P's accrued pensions, a partial retirement pension calculated in accordance with regulation 51; or
- (b) if P claims payment of the whole of P's accrued pensions, a full retirement pension calculated in accordance with regulation 48.
