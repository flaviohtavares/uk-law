# The Judicial Pensions Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 82

> Text as at 2024-05-24.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 82. Adjustment of allocated benefit (members who have reached the age of 75)

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The amount of allocated pension payable to the beneficiary of an allocation election may be adjusted in a manner determined by the scheme manager if—

- (a) the member who made the allocation election dies after reaching the age of 75; and
- (b) on the death of the member, the amount of allocated pension payable to the beneficiary does not qualify as a dependants' scheme pension under section 167 (pension benefit rules) of the 2004 Act [^c22374391].

(2) In this regulation, “**allocated pension**” has the meaning given in regulation 81(1)(b).
