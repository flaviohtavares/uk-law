> Text as at 2024-05-24.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 73. Exercising an option under this Chapter

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A member may exercise an option under this Chapter by notice to the scheme manager in a form required by the scheme manager.

(2) In this Chapter, “**option notice**” means a notice by which a member exercises an option under this Chapter.
