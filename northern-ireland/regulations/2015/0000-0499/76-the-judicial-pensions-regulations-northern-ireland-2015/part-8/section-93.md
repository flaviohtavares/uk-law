## 93. Meaning of “surviving adult's pension”

*Extent: N.I.*

In these Regulations, “**surviving adult's pension**” means any of the following pensions payable to a surviving adult under this Chapter—

- (a) a dependant's earned pension;
- (b) a dependant's ill-health pension;
- (c) a dependant's added pension.
