# The Judicial Pensions Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 116

> Text as at 2024-05-24.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 116. Pension protection lump sum death benefit

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A lump sum death benefit is treated for the purposes of the 2004 Act as a pension protection lump sum death benefit if and to the extent that—

- (a) the member has given written notice to the scheme manager that the lump sum death benefit is to be so treated; and
- (b) any other conditions that are required to be met for the lump sum death benefit to be so treated are met (see paragraph 14 of Part 2 (lump sum death benefit rule) of Schedule 29 to the 2004 Act [^c22374441]).

(2) Tax may be deducted from the lump sum death benefit if the scheme manager is liable for tax under section 206 (special lump sum death benefits charge) of the 2004 Act [^c22374451] in respect of a pension protection lump sum death benefit.
