# The Judicial Pensions Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 91

> Text as at 2024-05-24.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 91. Surviving adults

*Extent: N.I.*

In these Regulations—

- “**surviving adult**”, in relation to a deceased member of this scheme, means the member's surviving spouse, surviving civil partner or surviving nominated partner;
- “**surviving civil partner**”, in relation to a deceased member of this scheme, means a person who was in a civil partnership with the member as at the date of the member's death; and
- “**surviving spouse**”, in relation to a deceased member of this scheme, means a person who was married to the member as at the date of the member's death.
