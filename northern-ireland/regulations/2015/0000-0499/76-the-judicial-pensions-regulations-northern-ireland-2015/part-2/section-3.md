# The Judicial Pensions Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 3

> Text as at 2024-05-24.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 3. Establishment and scope

*Extent: N.I.*

A career average revalued earnings scheme is established as a defined benefits scheme for the payment of pensions and other benefits to or in respect of holders of judicial office.
