# The Judicial Pensions Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 41

> Text as at 2024-05-24.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 41. Closure of active member's account

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The scheme manager must close an active member's account in relation to a period of service when the scheme manager establishes in relation to that period of service—

- (a) a deferred member's account under regulation 43; or
- (b) a full retirement account under regulation 47.

(2)[^key-d2c2675d8c73adb4c9ceb3edb1b17ce5] The scheme manager must re-establish as active member’s account under this Chapter when the scheme manager closes a deferred member’s account under regulation 46.
