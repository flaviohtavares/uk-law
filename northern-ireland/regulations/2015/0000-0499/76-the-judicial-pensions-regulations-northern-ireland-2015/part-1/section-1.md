# The Judicial Pensions Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 1

> Text as at 2024-05-24.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation and commencement

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Judicial Pensions Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015.

(2) Part 1, Part 3, regulations 20, 128, 166, 167, Schedule 1, Schedule 2 and paragraph 12 of Schedule 3 come into operation on 28th February 2015.

(3) The remaining provisions come into operation on 1st April 2015.
