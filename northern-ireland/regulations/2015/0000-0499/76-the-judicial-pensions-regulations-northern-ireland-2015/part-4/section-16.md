# The Judicial Pensions Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 16

> Text as at 2024-05-24.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 16. Application of Chapter

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This Chapter applies in relation to a continuous period of service in a qualifying judicial office.

(2) If a person is in service in 2 or more qualifying judicial offices, this Chapter applies separately in relation to each of the offices.
