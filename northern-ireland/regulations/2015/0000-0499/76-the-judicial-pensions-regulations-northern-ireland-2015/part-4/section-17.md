# The Judicial Pensions Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 17

> Text as at 2024-05-24.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 17. Enrolment

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A person (P) begins pensionable service under this scheme in relation to a qualifying judicial office on the first day on which P becomes an eligible person in relation to service in that office unless—

- (a) regulation 21 applies; or
- (b) P is a transition member with continuity of service and paragraph (2) applies.

(2) This paragraph applies if—

- (a) the first day on which P becomes an eligible person in relation to service in a qualifying judicial office is the day after P's transition date; and
- (b) on P's transition date—
  - (i) P was in service in the same office; and
  - (ii) P has opted out of a relevant[^key-126087b2cf32b44cb3ebc926ba0e412c] scheme in relation to that service.
