## 137. Surviving adult's pension payable in respect of pension debit member

*Extent: N.I.*

The annual rate of surviving adult's pension payable in respect of a pension debit member is calculated by reference to the pension debit member's rights under these Regulations as reduced under Article 28 of the 1999 Order.
