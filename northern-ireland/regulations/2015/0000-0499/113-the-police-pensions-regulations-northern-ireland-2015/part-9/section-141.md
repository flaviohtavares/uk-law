## 141. Reduction in pensions in cases of wide age disparity

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies if, on the death of a member of this scheme, a surviving adult's pension becomes payable to a surviving adult who is more than 12 years younger than the member.

(2) The annual rate of the surviving adult's pension is reduced by the lower of—

- (a) 50% of the amount of the annual rate of the pension calculated under regulation 138,139 or 140; and
- (b) [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2015/113)
