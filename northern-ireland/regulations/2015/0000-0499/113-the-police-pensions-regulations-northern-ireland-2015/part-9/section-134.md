## 134. Meaning of “surviving adult partner”

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A person (P) is a surviving adult partner of a deceased member of this scheme if—

- (a) the following conditions are met[^c-4a8dd9e4a9804093363b04ea046849e9] —
  - (i) P and the member were cohabiting as partners in an exclusive, committed long-term relationship;
  - (ii) either P was financially dependent on the member or P and the member were financially interdependent;
  - (iii) the member was able to marry or form a civil partnership with P; and
  - (iv)[^c-439631178563eb01d163e081fa9023ca] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b)[^c-9b237a491e8ab86ee7edfe76243a2e18] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) P has satisfied the scheme manager that—
  - (i) the circumstances in sub-paragraphs (i) to (iii) of paragraph (a) continued to subsist at the time of the member's death; and
  - (ii) the period of cohabitation had been of at least 2 years' duration at the time of the member's death.

(2) The scheme manager may in its discretion accept a shorter period of cohabitation if satisfied in the particular circumstances of the case that the member and P would have cohabited as partners for at least 2 years had the member not died.
