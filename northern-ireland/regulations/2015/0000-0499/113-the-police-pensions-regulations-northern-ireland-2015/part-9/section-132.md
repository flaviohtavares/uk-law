# The Police Pensions Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 132

> Text as at 2026-02-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 132. Application of Part

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This Part applies in relation to death benefits payable in respect of a continuous period of pensionable service under this scheme (“period of service”).

(2) In this Part, “**active member**”, in relation to this scheme, means a person—

- (a) who is an active member of this scheme; or
- (b) who, for the purpose of determining entitlement to payment of benefits to or in respect of the member, is taken to be an active member of this scheme [^c22512241].

## Annotations

[^c22512241]: Marginal citation: See Chapter 1 of Part 4 (active membership) for when a member of the police service is taken to be an active member of this scheme.
