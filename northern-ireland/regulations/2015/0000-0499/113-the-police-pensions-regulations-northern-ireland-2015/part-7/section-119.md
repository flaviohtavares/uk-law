> Text as at 2026-02-01.

## 119. Exercising an option under this Chapter

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A member may exercise an option under this Chapter by written notice to the scheme manager in any form the scheme manager requires.

(2) In this Chapter, “**option notice**” means a notice by which a member exercises an option under this Chapter.
