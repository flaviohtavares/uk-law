# The Police Pensions Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 187

> Text as at 2026-02-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 187. Interpretation of Chapter

*Extent: N.I.*

In this Chapter—

- “**club transfer statement**” means a statement under regulation 192 of the amount of club transfer earned pension;
- “**transfer date**” means the earlier of—
  - (a) if the scheme manager has provided a transfer statement or a club transfer value statement, the last day of the period of 2 months beginning with the date of the statement; and
  - (b) the day on which the transfer payment is received by the scheme manager;
- “**transfer payment request**” means a request to the scheme manager under this Chapter that a transfer payment be accepted from another pension scheme;
- “**transfer statement**” means a statement under regulation 189(2).
