# The Police Pensions Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 177

> Text as at 2026-02-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 177. Application of Part

*Extent: N.I.*

This Part—

- (a) supplements the rights conferred by or under Chapter 4 of Part 4 of the 1993 Act (transfer values) and is without prejudice to that Chapter; and
- (b) supplements the rights conferred by or under Chapter 5 of that Part (early leavers: cash transfer sums and contribution refunds [^c22512321] and is without prejudice to that Chapter.

## Annotations

[^c22512321]: Marginal citation: [Chapter 5](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2015/113/part/5) of Part 4 of the Pensions Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993 was inserted by Article 241 of the [Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 2005 (S.I.2005/255)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/255).
