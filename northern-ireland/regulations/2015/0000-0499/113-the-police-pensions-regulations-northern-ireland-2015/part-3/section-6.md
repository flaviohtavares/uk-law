# The Police Pensions Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 6

> Text as at 2026-02-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 6. Scheme manager

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The police pension authority for a member of the police service is responsible for managing and administering this scheme and any statutory pension scheme that is connected with it [^c22511841] in relation to that member.

(2) Pensions and other benefits payable under this scheme are payable by the scheme manager.

(3) A statutory pension scheme that is a defined contributions scheme [^c22511851] is not connected with this scheme.

(4) In these Regulations “ **scheme manager**” in relation to a member of the police service means the police pension authority for that member acting in exercise of its functions under paragraph (1).

## Annotations

[^c22511841]: Marginal citation: see [section 4(6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2015/113/section/4/6) of the Act which sets out when statutory pension schemes are “connected”;

[^c22511851]: Marginal citation: See section 34 of the Act for the meaning of “defined contributions scheme”;
