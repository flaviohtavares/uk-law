## 14. Payment of fees and expenses

*Extent: N.I.*

The scheme manager may—

- (a) pay fees to or in respect of members of the Police Pension Board of such amounts as the scheme manager may determine; and
- (b) reimburse members of the Police Pension Board in respect of any reasonable expenses incurred by them in performance of their duties in relation to this scheme.
