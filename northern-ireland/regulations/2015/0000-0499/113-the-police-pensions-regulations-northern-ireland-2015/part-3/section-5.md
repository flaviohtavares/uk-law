## 5. Police pension authority

*Extent: N.I.*

In these Regulations “**police pension authority**” means—

- (a) for a member of the police service who falls within Regulation 4(1); the Board.
- (b) for a member of the police service engaged on relevant service under section 27 of the 1998 Act; the Board.
