## SCHEDULE 2 — Progressive medical conditions

*Extent: N.I.*

Regulation 106

### The progressive medical conditions mentioned in regulation 110 are—

- AIDS
- Alzheimer's disease;
- Cancer;
- Creutzfeld-Jacob disease;
- Huntington's chorea;
- Motor neurone disease;
- Multiple sclerosis;
- Nieman Pick disease;
- Non-variant Creutzfeld-Jacob disease;
- Parkinson's disease; and
- Variant Creutzfeld-Jacob disease.
