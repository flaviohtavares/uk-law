# The Police Pensions Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 39E

> Text as at 2026-02-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 39E. Scheme manager notifications[^key-a007457b7a119191fe5203b67f26cd77]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) If following the scheme managers receipt of P’s election application the scheme manager determines that P is eligible for benefits under this Chapter, the scheme manager must give P notice of terms on which P has agreed to pay revised contributions.

(2) That notice must be in writing.
