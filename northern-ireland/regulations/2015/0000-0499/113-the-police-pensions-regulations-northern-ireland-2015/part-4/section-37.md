# The Police Pensions Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 37

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 37. Appeal against selected medical practitioner's report (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24][^key-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) P may appeal to the scheme manager against the selected medical practitioner's report if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]

- (a) the scheme manager determines under this Chapter that the risk that P will retire on grounds of permanent medical unfitness is such that the likely cost of providing P with ill-health benefits is disproportionately high (“the scheme manager's determination”); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]
- (b) P is given written notice of the scheme manager's determination; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]
- (c) P is dissatisfied with the opinion of the selected medical practitioner's report on the likelihood or likely timing (or both) of P becoming permanently medically unfit for performing the ordinary duties of a member of the police service. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]

(2) An appeal is made under this regulation if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]

- (a) within 28 days of receiving notice of the scheme manager's determination, P gives the scheme manager notice of intent to appeal; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]
- (b) within 2 months (or such longer period as may be agreed by the scheme manager) of receiving notice of the scheme manager's determination, P provides the scheme manager with evidence that P has been examined by a registered medical practitioner (“the appellant's medical practitioner”) who disagrees with the opinion in the selected medical practitioner's report. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]

(3) On an appeal under this regulation, the scheme manager must ask the selected medical practitioner— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]

- (a) to reconsider the selected medical practitioner's report in light of the evidence provided by P; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]
- (b) if necessary, to produce a revised report on the likelihood and likely timing of P becoming permanently medically unfit for performing the ordinary duties of a member of the police service (“the selected medical practitioner's revised report”). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]

(4) The selected medical practitioner must send a revised report and certificate to the scheme manager and to P. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]

(5) Subject to regulation 38 (revised report by third medical practitioner) for the purpose of this Chapter that revised report is the final medical report. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]
