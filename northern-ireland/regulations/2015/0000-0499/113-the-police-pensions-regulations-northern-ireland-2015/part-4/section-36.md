# The Police Pensions Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 36

> Text as at 2026-02-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 36. Selected medical practitioner's report (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24][^c-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A selected medical practitioner who examines a person (P) under this Chapter must send a report and certificate (“the selected medical practitioner's report”) to the scheme manager giving an opinion on the likelihood and likely timing of P becoming permanently medically unfit for performing the ordinary duties of a member of the police service. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]

(2) The selected medical practitioner, in giving an opinion on the likelihood of P becoming permanently medically unfit for performing the ordinary duties of a member of the police service, must consider— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]

- (a) the likelihood of P becoming unable to perform those duties; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]
- (b) the likelihood of that inability continuing until the member reaches normal pension age under this scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]

(3) A copy of the selected medical practitioner's report and certificate must be given to P. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]

(4) Subject to an appeal under regulation 37 (appeal against selected medical practitioner's report) for the purpose of this Chapter the selected medical practitioner's' report and certificate is final. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]
