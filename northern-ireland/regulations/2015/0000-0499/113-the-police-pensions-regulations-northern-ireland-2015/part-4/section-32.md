# The Police Pensions Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 32

> Text as at 2026-02-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 32. Interpretation

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24] For the purposes of this Chapter “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]**final medical report *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]**” has the meaning given in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]

- (a) regulation 36(1) (selected medical practitioner's report); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]
- (b) regulation 37(3) (appeal against selected medical practitioner's report); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]
- (c) regulation 38(4)(revised report by third registered medical practitioner). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]

(2) For the purpose of this Chapter, the likely cost of providing a person with benefits under this scheme is disproportionately high if it is more than 50% greater than the likely cost of providing benefits under this scheme to a person who has not been identified as presenting a risk of retiring on grounds of permanent medical unfitness. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]
