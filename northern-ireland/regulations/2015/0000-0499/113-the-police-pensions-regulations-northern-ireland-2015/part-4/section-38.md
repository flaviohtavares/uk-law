# The Police Pensions Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 38

## 38. Revised report by third registered medical practitioner (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24][^c-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) If P within 28 days of receiving the selected medical practitioner's revised report gives written notice to the scheme manager that P is dissatisfied with the opinion contained in the revised report, the scheme manager must appoint a third registered medical practitioner to examine P. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]

(2) The third registered medical practitioner must be— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]

- (a) a person who is acceptable to both the selected medical practitioner and the appellant's medical practitioner; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]
- (b) if the medical practitioners fail to agree, a person whom the scheme manager considers appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]

(3) The third registered medical practitioner must send the scheme manager and P a written statement giving an opinion on the likelihood and likely timing of P becoming permanently medically unfit for performing the ordinary duties of a member of the police service. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]

(4) If the third registered medical practitioner disagrees with any part of the selected medical practitioner's revised report— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]

- (a) the third registered medical practitioner's written statement must be in the form of a revised report and certificate which must be sent to the scheme manager and to P; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]
- (b) for the purpose of this Chapter that revised report is the final medical report. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]
