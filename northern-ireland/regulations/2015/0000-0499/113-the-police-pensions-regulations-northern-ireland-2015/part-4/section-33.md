# The Police Pensions Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 33

> Text as at 2026-02-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 33. Determination of eligibility for payment of ill-health benefits (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24][^c-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies in relation to the following persons— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]

- (a) every new member of the police service; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]
- (b) every member of the police service who opts under Chapter 2 (automatic enrolment) to become an active member of this scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]

(2) This regulation does not apply to a person who, not more than 5 years before being appointed to the police service— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]

- (a) was an active member of a police pension scheme; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]
- (b) was eligible under that scheme for payment of benefits on grounds of permanent medical unfitness. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]

(3) Before P joins this scheme, the scheme manager may require P to submit to an examination by a selected medical practitioner in order that the scheme manager may determine P's eligibility under this scheme for payment of ill-health benefits. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]

(4) If the scheme manager requires P to submit to an examination under this regulation, the scheme manager, by applying the opinion set out in the final medical report and advice from the scheme actuary, must determine whether the risk that P will retire on the grounds of permanent medical unfitness (“the risk”) is such that the likely cost of providing P with ill-health benefits is disproportionately high. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]

(5) P is ineligible under this scheme for payment for ill-health benefits under this scheme if the scheme manager determines, under this regulation that the risk is such that the likely cost of providing P with ill-health benefits under this scheme is disproportionately high. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]

(6) If the scheme manager determines under this regulation that the risk is such that the likely cost of providing P with ill-health benefits is not disproportionately high, P is eligible under this scheme for payment of ill-health benefits from the first day of pensionable service under this scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]

(7) A person to whom this regulation applies by virtue of paragraph (1)(b) must pay any fee charged for an examination or report under this regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]
