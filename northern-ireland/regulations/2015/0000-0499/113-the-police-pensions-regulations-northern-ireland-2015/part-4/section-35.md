# The Police Pensions Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 35

> Text as at 2026-02-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 35. Reduced rate of member contributions (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24][^key-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A reduced member contributions rate applies to an active member of this scheme who is ineligible for ill-health benefits under this scheme for payment of ill-health benefits. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]

(2) If the scheme manager determines under regulation 34 (re-determination of eligibility for ill-health benefits) that the member is eligible under this scheme for payment of ill-health benefits, the reduced member contributions rate ceases to apply from the date of that determination. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]

(3) In this regulation, “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]**reduced member contributions rate *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]**” means a reduced rate of member contributions which applies to the member under Part 10 (contributions). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-eec0b1b141f380bcea560cb178f0ee24]
