>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 39A. Elections and Chapter 4A[^key-a007457b7a119191fe5203b67f26cd77]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies, as from 1 April 2015 to a person who falls within regulation 33(5).

(2) Where such a person makes an election under Chapter 4A in respect of the ill-health benefits referred to in it —

- (a) this Chapter ceases to apply to that person;
- (b) Chapter 4A applies to that person; and
- (c) a determination under regulation 33(5) does not prevent the making of an election under Chapter 4A.
