# The Police Pensions Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 77

> Text as at 2026-02-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 77. Application of Chapter

*Extent: N.I.*

This Chapter applies in relation to a member of the police service (“the member”)—

- (a) who is an active member of this scheme; or
- (b) who, for the purpose of determining entitlement to payment of benefits to or in respect of the member, is taken to be an active member of this scheme [^c22512071].
- (c) This Part does not apply to a member who falls within regulation 4(1)(d).

## Annotations

[^c22512071]: Marginal citation: See Chapter 1 Part 4 (active membership) for where a member of the police service is taken to be an active member of the scheme.
