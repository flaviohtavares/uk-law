# The Police Pensions Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 83

> Text as at 2026-02-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 83. Application of Chapter

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This Chapter applies in relation to a member of a police service(“the member”) who is a deferred member of this scheme.

(2)[^key-22d95b57d07f7ca06f6a6ad25a6bb630] For the purpose of this Chapter—

- (a) a former member of the police service is taken to be a member of the police service; and
- (b) “**police pension authority**” in relation to the former member of the police service, means the police pension authority for the police service.
