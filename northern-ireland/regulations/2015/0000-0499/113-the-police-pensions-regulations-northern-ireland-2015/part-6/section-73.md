# The Police Pensions Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 73

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 73. Permanent medical unfitness

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) In these Regulations, a reference to a member of the police service who falls within regulation 4(1)(a),(b) or (c)(“the member”) being permanently medically unfit is taken to be a reference to—

- (a) the member being medically unfit at the time the selected medical practitioner decides the question; and
- (b) that medical unfitness being at that time likely to be permanent.

(2)[^key-59af86ed37f751fca1e7b3cec10f93a8] For the purpose of deciding whether or not the member's medical unfitness is likely to be permanent, the member is taken to receive normal appropriate medical treatment.

(3)[^key-59af86ed37f751fca1e7b3cec10f93a8] In this regulation, “**appropriate medical treatment**” does not include medical treatment that the police pension authority acting in exercise of its functions as scheme manager decides is reasonable for the member to refuse.

(4)[^key-59af86ed37f751fca1e7b3cec10f93a8] In respect of a member who falls within regulation 4(1)(b) “**appropriate medical treatment**” does not include medical treatment that the Chief Constable decides is reasonable for the member to refuse.

(5) The member may appeal under regulation 206 (appeals to the Department) against a decision of the police pension authority as to whether a refusal to accept medical treatment is reasonable.
