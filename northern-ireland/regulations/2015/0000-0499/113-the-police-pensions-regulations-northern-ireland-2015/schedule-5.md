## SCHEDULE 5 — Amendment of the Royal Ulster Constabulary Pensions Regulations 1988

*Extent: N.I.*

Regulation 226

**1.** The Royal Ulster Constabulary Pension Regulations 1988 are amended as follows.

**2.** After regulation A3(2) insert —

> (3) Where any person's entitlement to receive payment on account of a pension or a gratuity under the old cases regulations was terminated by virtue of paragraph 20 of the Schedule to the 1949 Order the pension or gratuity shall be reinstated and become payable as from 1st July 2014.
