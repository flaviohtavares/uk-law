## 201. Late payment of retirement index adjustment or retirement PIA index adjustment

*Extent: N.I.*

Nothing in these Regulations requires any part of a pension attributable to a retirement index adjustment or retirement PIA index adjustment to be paid before the end of the last active scheme year.
