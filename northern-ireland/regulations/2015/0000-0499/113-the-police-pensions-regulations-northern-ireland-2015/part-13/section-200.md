> Text as at 2026-02-01.

## 200. Reduction in pension debit member's benefits

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The benefits to which a pension debit member is entitled under Part 7 are subject to the reduction to be made under article 28 of the 1999 Order.

(2) Member contributions and payments for added pension refunded under Part 10 to a pension debit member are subject to the reduction to be made under article 28 of the 1999 Order.
