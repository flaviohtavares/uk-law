# The Police Pensions Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 170

> Text as at 2026-02-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 170. Payment of member contributions

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Member contributions for each period are to be deducted by the member's employer in respect of the member's pensionable earnings for that pay period.

(2) A member's employer may make contributions on the member's behalf in circumstances determined by the scheme manager.
