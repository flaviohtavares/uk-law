# The Police Pensions Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 1

> Text as at 2026-02-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation, commencement and effect

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) These Regulations—

- (a) may be cited as the Police Pensions Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015; and
- (b) apply to members of the police service in Northern Ireland.

(2) Subject to paragraph 3, these Regulations come into operation on 1st April 2015.

(3) Schedule 5 has effect from 1st July 2014.
