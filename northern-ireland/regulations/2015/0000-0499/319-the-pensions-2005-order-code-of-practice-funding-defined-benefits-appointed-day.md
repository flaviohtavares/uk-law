# The Pensions (2005 Order) (Code of Practice) (Funding Defined Benefits) (Appointed Day) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 319

Pensions

The Pensions (2005 Order) (Code of Practice) (Funding Defined Benefits) (Appointed Day) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015

Made

24th July 2015

The Department for Social Development makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 86(7) of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 2005[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Pensions (2005 Order) (Code of Practice) (Funding Defined Benefits) (Appointed Day) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the purposes of Article 86(7) of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 2005 (procedure for issue and publication of codes of practice) for the coming into operation of the Pensions Regulator Code of practice no. 3: Funding defined benefits: Second issue is 29th July 2015.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 24th July 2015

(L.S.)

Anne McCleary

A senior officer of the Department for Social Development

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints the day for the purposes of Article 86(7) of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 2005 for the coming into operation of the Pensions Regulator Code of practice no. 3: Funding defined benefits: Second issue as 29th July 2015.

The Code of Practice is available on the Pensions Regulator’s website: http://www.thepensionsregulator.gov.uk/doc-library/codes.aspx.
