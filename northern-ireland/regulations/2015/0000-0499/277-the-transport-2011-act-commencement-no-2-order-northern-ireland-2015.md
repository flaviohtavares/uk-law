# The Transport (2011 Act) (Commencement No.2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 277 (C. 21)

Public Passenger Transport

The Transport (2011 Act) (Commencement No.2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015

Made

*18*th June 2015

The Department for Regional Development makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 48 of the Transport Act (Northern Ireland) 2011[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Transport (2011 Act) (Commencement No.2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015.

(2) In this Order—

- “the Act” means the Transport Act (Northern Ireland) 2011.

## 2. Appointed day

The appointed day for the coming into operation of the provisions of the Act specified in the Schedule for the purposes only of making regulations is 23rd June 2015.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Regional Development on 18<sup>th</sup> June 2015.

Tom Reid

A senior officer of the Department for Regional Development

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This is the second Commencement Order to be made under the Transport Act (Northern Ireland) 2011.

Article 2 brings into operation sections 1, 4, 5, 8, 13 and 14 for the purposes only of making regulations.
