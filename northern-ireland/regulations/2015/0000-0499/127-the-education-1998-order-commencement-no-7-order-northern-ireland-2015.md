# The Education (1998 Order) (Commencement No. 7) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 127 (C. 8)

Education

The Education (1998 Order) (Commencement No. 7) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015

Made

6th March 2015

The Department of Education, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 1(3) of the Education (Northern Ireland) Order 1998[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Education (1998 Order) (Commencement No.7) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015.

## 2. Appointed Day

The day appointed for coming into operation of Article 81(2) of the Education (Northern Ireland) Order 1998 is 6th March 2015.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Education on 6th March 2015.

David Hughes

A senior officer of the Department of Education

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into operation Article 81(2) of the Education (Northern Ireland) Order 1998 which makes it necessary for the Department of Education to approve all courses, provided in schools, leading to external qualifications for pupils who have gained the upper limit of compulsory school age.
