# The Natural Mineral Water, Spring Water and Bottled Drinking Water Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 36

> Text as at 2023-10-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 36. Revocations

*Extent: N.I.*

The following Regulations are revoked—

- (a) the Natural Mineral Water, Spring Water and Bottled Drinking Water Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 [^c22616401];
- (b) the Natural Mineral Water, Spring Water and Bottled Drinking Water (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009 [^c22616411];
- (c) the Natural Mineral Water, Spring Water and Bottled Drinking Water (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010 [^c22616421];
- (d) the Natural Mineral Water, Spring Water and Bottled Drinking Water (Amendment) (No.2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010 [^c22616431]; and
- (e) the Natural Mineral Water, Spring Water and Bottled Drinking Water (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011 [^c22616441]
