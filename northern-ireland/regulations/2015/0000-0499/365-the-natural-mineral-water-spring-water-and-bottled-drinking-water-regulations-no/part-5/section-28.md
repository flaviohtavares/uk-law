# The Natural Mineral Water, Spring Water and Bottled Drinking Water Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 28

> Text as at 2023-10-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 28. General

*Extent: N.I.*

The district council must ensure that each sample is representative of the quality of the water concerned consumed throughout the year in which the sample is taken.
