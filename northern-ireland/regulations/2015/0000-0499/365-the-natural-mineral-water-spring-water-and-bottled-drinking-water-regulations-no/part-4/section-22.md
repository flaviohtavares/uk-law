# The Natural Mineral Water, Spring Water and Bottled Drinking Water Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 22

> Text as at 2023-10-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 22. Sale of bottled drinking water

*Extent: N.I.*

A person must not sell bottled drinking water which is—

- (a) bottled in contravention of regulation 19; or
- (b) labelled in contravention of regulation 20; or
- (c) advertised in contravention of regulation 21.
