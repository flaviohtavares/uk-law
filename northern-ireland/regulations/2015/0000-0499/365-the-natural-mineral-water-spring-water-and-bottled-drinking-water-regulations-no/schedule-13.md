# The Natural Mineral Water, Spring Water and Bottled Drinking Water Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, SCHEDULE 13

> Text as at 2023-10-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 13 — Consequential Amendments

*Extent: N.I.*

Regulation 37

### 1. The Private Water Supplies Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009

In regulation 4(a) of the Private Water Supplies Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009 [^c22616451] (exemptions), for “the Natural Mineral Water, Spring Water and Bottled Drinking Water Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007” substitute “the Natural Mineral Water, Spring Water and Bottled Drinking Water Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015”

### 2. The Food Safety (Sampling and Qualifications) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013

In Schedule 1 to the Food Safety (Sampling and Qualifications) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013 [^c22616461] omit the references to “The Natural Mineral Water, Spring Water and Bottled Drinking Water Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007”.

### 3. The Food Information Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014

In Schedule 7 to the Food Information Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014 omit paragraph 16 of Part 2
