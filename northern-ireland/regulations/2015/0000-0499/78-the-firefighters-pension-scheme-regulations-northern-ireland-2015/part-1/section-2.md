## 2. Establishment of Firefighters' Pension Scheme 2015

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) These Regulations establish a scheme for the payment of pensions and other benefits to or in respect of fire and rescue workers [^c22393941] who are firefighters in Northern Ireland.

(2) This scheme is to be known as the Firefighters' Pension Scheme 2015.

## Annotations

[^c22393941]: Marginal citation: See paragraph 6 of schedule 1 to the Public Service Pensions Act (Northern Ireland) 2014 for the definition of fire and rescue workers.
