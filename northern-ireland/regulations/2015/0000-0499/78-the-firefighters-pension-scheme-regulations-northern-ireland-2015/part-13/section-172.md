# The Firefighters' Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 172

> Text as at 2026-05-01.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 172. Limitation on assignment of benefits

*Extent: N.I.*

An assignment of an award under these Regulations is void to the extent that it is in favour of a person other than a dependant of the person entitled to the award.
