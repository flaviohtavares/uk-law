## 179. Scheme administrator for the purposes of the 2004 Act

*Extent: N.I.*

The scheme manager is appointed to be responsible for all liabilities and responsibilities connected with the functions conferred or imposed on the scheme administrator by or under Part 4 of the 2004 Act [^c-ef113310cda23722a034d4a97775afc5]....
