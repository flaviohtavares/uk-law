# The Firefighters' Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 22

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 22. Automatic re-enrolment

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies if, on the automatic re-enrolment date, an eligible person (P) in relation to service in a scheme employment is not in pensionable service under this scheme in relation to service in that employment.

(2) On the automatic re-enrolment date, the scheme manager must enrol P in this scheme in relation to service in that employment if the employer is required under section 5 (automatic re-enrolment) of the Pensions (No. 2) Act (Northern Ireland) 2008 [^c22394311] to make arrangements for P to be an active member of a pension scheme.
