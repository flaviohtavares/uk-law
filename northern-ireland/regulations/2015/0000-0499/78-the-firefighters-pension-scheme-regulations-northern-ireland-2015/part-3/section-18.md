# The Firefighters' Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 18

> Text as at 2026-05-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 18. Application of Chapter

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This Chapter applies in relation to a continuous period of service in a scheme employment.

(2) If a person is in service in two or more scheme employments at the same time, this Chapter applies separately in relation to each of the employments.
