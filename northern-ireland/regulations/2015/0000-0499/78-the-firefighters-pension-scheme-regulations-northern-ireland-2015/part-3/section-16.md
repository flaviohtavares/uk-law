# The Firefighters' Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 16

> Text as at 2026-05-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 16. Eligible persons

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) For the purpose of this Part, an eligible person is a person who is eligible to be an active member of this scheme.

(2) A person (P) who is in service in a scheme employment is an eligible person in relation to that employment unless in relation to service in that employment—

- (a) P is a protected member of the FPS or the NFPS; or
- (b) P is a member of any other pension scheme and the Board pays contributions to that scheme in respect of P.

(3) P is an eligible person whilst on reserve forces service leave.
