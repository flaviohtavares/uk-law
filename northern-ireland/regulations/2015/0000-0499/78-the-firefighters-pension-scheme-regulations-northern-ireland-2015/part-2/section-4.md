# The Firefighters' Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 4

## 4. Scheme manager

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Northern Ireland Fire and Rescue Service Board is the scheme manager for this scheme and any statutory pension scheme that is connected with it [^c22394251].

(2) The scheme manager is responsible for managing and administering this scheme and any statutory pension scheme that is connected with it.

(3) A statutory pension scheme that is a defined contributions scheme [^c22394261] is not connected with this scheme.

## Annotations

[^c22394251]: Marginal citation: See section 4(6) of the 2014 Act which sets out when statutory pension schemes are “connected”.

[^c22394261]: Marginal citation: See section 34 of the 2014 Act for the meaning of “defined contributions scheme”.
