# The Firefighters' Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 139

> Text as at 2026-05-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 139. Application of Part

*Extent: N.I.*

This Part—

- (a) supplements the rights conferred by or under chapter 4 of Part 4 of the Pension Schemes Act (transfer values); and
- (b) supplements the rights conferred by or under Chapter 5 of that Part (early leavers: cash transfer sums and contribution refunds) [^c22394401] and is without prejudice to that Chapter.
