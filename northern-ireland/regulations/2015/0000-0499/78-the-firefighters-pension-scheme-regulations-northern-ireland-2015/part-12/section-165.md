# The Firefighters' Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 165

> Text as at 2026-05-01.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 165. Appeals on other issues[^key-a533e9088e3d2463b03672ce6f239a89]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where a person makes a formal complaint to the Scheme Manager which does not involve an issue of a medical nature, the Scheme Manager shall deal with the complaint under the arrangements implemented by it pursuant to the requirements of article 50 of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1995 (requirement for dispute resolution arrangements) and the Occupational Pension Schemes (Internal dispute Resolution Procedures) (Consequential and Miscellaneous Amendments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008.
