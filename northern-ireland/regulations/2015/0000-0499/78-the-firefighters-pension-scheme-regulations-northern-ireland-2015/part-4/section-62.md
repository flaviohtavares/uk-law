# The Firefighters' Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 62

> Text as at 2026-05-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 62. Amount of pension payable to survivor member

*Extent: N.I.*

The survivor member's account must be credited with an amount equal to the annual rate of pension payable to the survivor member calculated in accordance with the appropriate regulation in Chapter 2 (pensions for surviving partners) or Chapter 3 (pensions for eligible children) of Part 6 (death benefits), as the case may be, which confers the pension on the survivor member.
