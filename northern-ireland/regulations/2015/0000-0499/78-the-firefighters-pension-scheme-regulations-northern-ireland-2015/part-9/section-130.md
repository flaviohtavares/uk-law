## 130. Establishment of Firefighters' Pension Fund

*Extent: N.I.*

The Firefighters' Pension Fund (“FPF”) established and maintained by the scheme manager for the purposes of the FPS, and used by the scheme manager for payments and receipts required or authorised to be made under the NFPS must also be used for the purpose of payments and receipts required or authorised to be made under these Regulations.
