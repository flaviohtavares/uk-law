# The Firefighters' Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 138

> Text as at 2026-05-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 138. Duty to provide information

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The scheme manager must provide the Department with such information relevant to the exercise of the Department's functions under this Part as it may, by written notice, require.

(2) The scheme manager must provide the information required within the period of time specified by the Department in that notice or within such additional period as may be allowed by the Department.
