## 103. Person to whom lump sum death benefit payable

*Extent: N.I.*

The scheme manager may, at its absolute discretion, pay the lump sum death benefit payable under this Chapter to or for the benefit of the member's nominee, personal representatives or any person appearing to the scheme manager to have been a relative or dependent of the member.
