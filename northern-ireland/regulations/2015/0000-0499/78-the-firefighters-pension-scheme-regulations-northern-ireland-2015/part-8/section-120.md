# The Firefighters' Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015, section 120

> Text as at 2026-05-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 120. Contributions during absence from work on reserve forces service leave

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An active member (P), who is on reserve forces service leave and who is treated as receiving assumed pensionable pay, must pay contributions at the contribution rate ascertained in accordance with regulation 118 (member contributions).

(2) The amount of the contributions paid is calculated by multiplying the contribution rate by the lesser of the following amounts—

- (a) assumed pensionable pay;
- (b) the total of the actual pay received and any additional payment made by the scheme employer.
