# The Charities (2013 Act) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 256 (C.19)

Charities

The Charities (2013 Act) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015

Made

22nd May 2015

The Department for Social Development makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 10(2) of the Charities Act (Northern Ireland) 2013[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Charities (2013 Act) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of section 4 of the Charities Act (Northern Ireland) 2013 (educational endowments) is 1st June 2015, except in so far as it relates to applications made to the Department before that date.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 22nd May 2015

(L.S.)

Chris Hughes

A senior officer of the Department for Social Development

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into operation section 4 of the Charities Act (Northern Ireland) 2013 (which provides for the transfer of functions exercisable by the Department for Social Development in relation to educational endowments to the Charity Commission for Northern Ireland) on 1st June 2015, except in so far as it relates to applications made to the Department before that date.
