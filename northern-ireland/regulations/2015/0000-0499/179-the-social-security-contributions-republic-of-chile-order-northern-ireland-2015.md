# The Social Security (Contributions) (Republic of Chile) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 179

Social Security

The Social Security (Contributions) (Republic of Chile) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015

Made

23rd March 2015

Coming into operation-

1st June 2015

The Secretary of State for Business, Innovation and Skills makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 155(1)(a) and (2) of the Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Social Security (Contributions) (Republic of Chile) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015 and shall come into force on 1st June 2015.

## 2. Modification of Acts

The Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992 and the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992[^f00002] are modified to such extent as may be required to give effect to the provisions contained in the Convention set out in the Schedule, so far as those provisions relate to Northern Ireland.

Vince Cable

Secretary of State for Business, Innovation and Skills

23rd March 2015

This Order makes provisions for the modification of the Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act [1992 (c. 8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/8) and the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act (Northern Ireland) [1992 (c. 7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/7) in order to give effect to the Convention on Social Security between the Government of Great Britain and Northern Ireland and the Government of the Republic of Chile.
