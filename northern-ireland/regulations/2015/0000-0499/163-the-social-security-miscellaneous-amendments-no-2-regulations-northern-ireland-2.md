# The Social Security (Miscellaneous Amendments No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 163

Housing; Rates; Social Security

The Social Security (Miscellaneous Amendments No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015

Made

16th March 2015

Coming into operation

6th April 2015

The Department for Social Development makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 39(1) and 47(1) of the Child Support (Northern Ireland) Order 1991[^f00001], sections 122(1)(a) and (d), 132(3) and (4)(b), 132A(2) and (3) and 171(1), (3) and (4) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992[^f00002], Articles 6(5), 14(1) and (4) and 36(1) and (2) of the Jobseekers (Northern Ireland) Order 1995[^f00003], Articles 11(6) and 74(1) and (5) of the Social Security (Northern Ireland) Order 1998[^f00004], and now vested in it[^f00005] and sections 17(1) and (3) and 25(2) of the Welfare Reform Act (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00006].

Regulations 6 and 7 are made with the consent of the Department Finance and Personnel[^f00007].

The Social Security Advisory Committee has agreed that proposals in respect of these regulations should not be referred to it[^f00008].

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Security (Miscellaneous Amendments No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015 and shall come into operation on 6 April 2015.

(2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00009] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

## 2. Amendments of the Income Support (General) Regulations

In regulation 39(2)(a) of the Income Support (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1987[^f00010] (deduction of tax and contributions for self-employed earners)—

- (a) for “11(1)” substitute “11(2)”;
- (b) for “11(3)” substitute “11(8)”; and
- (c) for “small earnings exception” substitute “small profits threshold”.

## 3. Amendment of the Child Support (Maintenance Assessments and Special Cases) Regulations

In paragraphs 2A(4)(a) and 3(6)(a) of Schedule 1 to the Child Support (Maintenance Assessments and Special Cases) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992[^f00011](calculation of N and M) for “section 11(1) or, as the case may be, (3)” substitute “section 11(2) or, as the case may be, (8)”.

## 4. Amendment of the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations

In regulation 102(2)(a) of the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00012](deduction of tax and contributions for self-employed earners)—

- (a) for “11(1)” substitute “11(2)”;

## 5. Amendment of the Child Support (Maintenance Calculations and Special Cases) Regulations

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend a number of different regulations relating to social security in consequence of certain provisions of Schedule 1 to the National Insurance Act [2015 (c. 5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/5) (“the 2015 Act”) coming into operation. Paragraph 12 of Schedule 1 to the 2015 Act substitutes section 11 of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act [1992 (c.7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/7), which relates to liability for Class 2 National Insurance contributions. From 6<sup>th</sup> April 2015 self-employed earners with profits in a tax year of £5,965 (the Small Profits Threshold) or over will pay Class 2 contributions via the Self-Assessment system.

Regulation 1 provides for citation, commencement and interpretation.

Regulations 2 to 8 amend the following regulations:

- the Income Support (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1987;
- the Child Support (Maintenance Assessments and Special Cases) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992;
- the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996;
- the Child Support (Maintenance Calculations and Special Cases) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001;
- the Housing Benefit Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006;
- the Housing Benefit (Persons who have attained the qualifying age for state pension credit) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006;
- the Employment and Support Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008,

in particular so as to align the language and legislative references with those contained in the 2015 Act.
