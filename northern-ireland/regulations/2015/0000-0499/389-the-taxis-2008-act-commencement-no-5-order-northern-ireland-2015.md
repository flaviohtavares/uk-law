# The Taxis (2008 Act) (Commencement No. 5) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 389 (C. 35)

Road Traffic And Vehicles

The Taxis (2008 Act) (Commencement No. 5) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015

Made

1st December 2015

The Department of the Environment makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 59 of the Taxis Act (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Taxis (2008 Act) (Commencement No. 5) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015.

(2) In this Order “the Act” means the Taxis Act (Northern Ireland) 2008.

## 2. Appointed days

(1) The day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the Act specified in Part 1 of the Schedule, for the purpose only of making regulations, is 2<sup>nd</sup> December 2015.

(2) The day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the Act specified in Part 2 of the Schedule, for the purpose only of making an application for a taxi licence, is 9<sup>th</sup> May 2016.

(3) The day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the Act specified in Part 3 of the Schedule, for all purposes, is 31<sup>st</sup> May 2016.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 1<sup>st</sup> December 2015

Elizabeth Loughran

A senior officer of the

Department of the Environment

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into operation the provisions of the Taxis Act (Northern Ireland) 2008 specified in—

- (a) Part 1 of the Schedule, for the purpose only of making regulations, on 2<sup>nd</sup> December 2015;
- (b) Part 2 of the Schedule, for the purpose only of making an application for a taxi licence, on 9th May 2016; and
- (c) Part 3 of the Schedule, for all purposes, on 31<sup>st</sup> May 2016.
