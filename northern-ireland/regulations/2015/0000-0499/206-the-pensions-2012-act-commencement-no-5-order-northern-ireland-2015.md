# The Pensions (2012 Act) (Commencement No. 5) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 206 (C. 14)

Pensions

The Pensions (2012 Act) (Commencement No. 5) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015

Made

30th March 2015

The Department for Social Development makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 34(3) of the Pensions Act (Northern Ireland) 2012[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Pensions (2012 Act) (Commencement No. 5) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of section 8 of the Pensions Act (Northern Ireland) 2012 (timing of automatic re-enrolment) in so far as it is not already in operation is 1st April 2015.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 30th March 2015

(L.S.)

Anne McCleary

A senior officer of the Department for Social Development

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into operation section 8 of the Pensions Act (Northern Ireland) 2012, in so far as it is not already in operation, on 1st April 2015.

Section 8 amends section 6(1)(b) of the Pensions (No. 2) Act (Northern Ireland) 2008 ([c. 13 (N.I.)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nia/2008/13)) so that three years is substituted by two years and nine months. This will ensure that automatic re-enrolment will not happen more frequently than once in every period of two years and nine months. The two years and nine months interval applies to the employer rather than the jobholder.
