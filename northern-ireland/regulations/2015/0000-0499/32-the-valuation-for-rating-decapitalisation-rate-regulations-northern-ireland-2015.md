## 1. Citation and commencement (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-099948315ea73c3a7c3ddbcf1d82d69b][^c-cc658bb528fe67f6640b690950f77a79]

These Regulations may be cited as the Valuation for Rating (Decapitalisation Rate) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015 and shall come into operation on 24th February 2015. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc658bb528fe67f6640b690950f77a79]

## 2. Interpretation (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-72066bcd749a25d1452e1f0c41e1a23a][^c-cc658bb528fe67f6640b690950f77a79]

(1) In these Regulations— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc658bb528fe67f6640b690950f77a79]

- “the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc658bb528fe67f6640b690950f77a79] 1977 Order *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc658bb528fe67f6640b690950f77a79]” means the Rates (Northern Ireland) Order 1977; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc658bb528fe67f6640b690950f77a79]
- “capital value”, in relation to a hereditament, means the price which a willing seller would reasonably expect to realise for the fee simple absolute in possession in the hereditament, in its actual state and with vacant possession, at the time by reference to which the estimate of net annual value falls to be made, on the assumption that there was then an adequate supply of similar properties available to meet a reasonable demand; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc658bb528fe67f6640b690950f77a79]
- “church hereditament” means any hereditament which falls within Article 41(2)(b) of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc658bb528fe67f6640b690950f77a79] 1977 Order *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc658bb528fe67f6640b690950f77a79];
- “contractor’s principle” means the method of ascertaining the net annual value of a hereditament by reference to the actual or notional cost of constructing or providing it or to its capital value; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc658bb528fe67f6640b690950f77a79]
- “decapitalisation rate” means the percentage rate which is applied to the actual or notional cost of construction or provision or to the capital value of any hereditament for the purpose of the contractor’s principle; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc658bb528fe67f6640b690950f77a79]
- “hereditament” means any hereditament or any part of it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc658bb528fe67f6640b690950f77a79]

(2) In these Regulations “educational hereditament” means any hereditament— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc658bb528fe67f6640b690950f77a79]

- (a) which is constructed or adapted for use, and which is wholly or mainly used, for the purposes of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc658bb528fe67f6640b690950f77a79]
  - (i) a school within the meaning of Article 2(2) of the Education and Libraries (Northern Ireland) Order 1986 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc658bb528fe67f6640b690950f77a79][^f00002];
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-cc658bb528fe67f6640b690950f77a79] an institution of further education within the meaning of Article 2(2) of the Further Education (Northern Ireland) Order 1997 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc658bb528fe67f6640b690950f77a79][^f00003]; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc658bb528fe67f6640b690950f77a79]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-cc658bb528fe67f6640b690950f77a79] a higher education institution within the meaning of Article 30(3) of the Education and Libraries (Northern Ireland) Order 1993 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc658bb528fe67f6640b690950f77a79][^f00004]; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc658bb528fe67f6640b690950f77a79]
- (b) which consists of premises with respect to which a person is registered under Article 118(1)(b) of the Children (Northern Ireland) Order 1995 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc658bb528fe67f6640b690950f77a79][^f00005] and which are wholly or mainly used for the purpose of providing day care for children who have attained the age of two years. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc658bb528fe67f6640b690950f77a79]

(3) In these Regulations “healthcare hereditament” means any hereditament constructed or adapted wholly or mainly either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc658bb528fe67f6640b690950f77a79]

- (a) for the reception and treatment of persons suffering from any illness, injury or infirmity; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc658bb528fe67f6640b690950f77a79]
- (b) as a maternity home, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc658bb528fe67f6640b690950f77a79]

and used for such a purpose. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc658bb528fe67f6640b690950f77a79]

(4) Any reference in these Regulations to a hereditament used for any particular purpose includes a reference to a hereditament which is not for the time being in use but which when last in use was used for that purpose. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc658bb528fe67f6640b690950f77a79]

## 3. Application of Regulations (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-679428fb621f2654151ea82a6fca6238][^c-cc658bb528fe67f6640b690950f77a79]

These Regulations apply in relation to the ascertainment of the net annual value of any hereditament for the purpose of any NAV list which comes into force on or after 1st April 2015. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc658bb528fe67f6640b690950f77a79]

## 4. Decapitalisation Rate (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-726eb189512adb50ff9e630a8882475e][^c-cc658bb528fe67f6640b690950f77a79]

Where, for the purposes of paragraph 1 of Part 1 of Schedule 12 to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc658bb528fe67f6640b690950f77a79] 1977 Order *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc658bb528fe67f6640b690950f77a79], the net annual value of a hereditament is ascertained by reference to the contractor’s principle in circumstance where these Regulations apply, the decapitalisation rate shall be— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc658bb528fe67f6640b690950f77a79]

- (a) 2.67 per cent in the case of a church hereditament, an educational hereditament or a healthcare hereditament; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc658bb528fe67f6640b690950f77a79]
- (b) 4 per cent in any other case. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc658bb528fe67f6640b690950f77a79]

## 5. Revocation and saving (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8b117b51e312d0798d982534e53a05dd][^c-cc658bb528fe67f6640b690950f77a79]

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the Valuation for Rating (Decapitalisation Rate) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc658bb528fe67f6640b690950f77a79][^f00006] are revoked. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc658bb528fe67f6640b690950f77a79]

(2) The Valuation for Rating (Decapitalisation Rate) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc658bb528fe67f6640b690950f77a79][^f00007] shall continue to have effect in relation to the ascertainment of the net annual value of any hereditament for the purpose of the valuation list which came into force on 1st April 2003. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc658bb528fe67f6640b690950f77a79]

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations prescribe the decapitalisation rate to be applied when determining the net annual value of any hereditament by reference to the contractor’s principle for the purposes of any NAV list coming into force on or after 1st April 2015. The contractor’s principle is the method of ascertaining the net annual value of a hereditament by reference to its actual or notional cost of construction or provision or to its capital value.

The decapitalisation rate is 2.67% in the case of a church hereditament, educational hereditament or healthcare hereditament, all of which are defined in regulation 2, and 4% in any other case. These Regulations have the effect of moving defence hereditaments from the lower of the two decapitalisation rates to the higher rate following consultation on this issue.

These Regulations revoke the Valuation for Rating (Decapitalisation Rates) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009 made to take effect for the 2010 non-domestic revaluation which was subsequently cancelled.

The corresponding decapitalisation rates for the purposes of the valuation list which came into force on 1st April 2003 were 3.67% and 5.5% respectively and provision is made for those rates to continue to have effect in relation to that list.
