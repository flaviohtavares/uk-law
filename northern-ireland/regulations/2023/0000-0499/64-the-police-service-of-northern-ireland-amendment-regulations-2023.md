# The Police Service of Northern Ireland (Amendment) Regulations 2023

> Text as at 2023-04-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

2023 No. 64

Police

The Police Service of Northern Ireland (Amendment) Regulations 2023

Made

4th April 2023

Coming into operation

28th April 2023

The Department of Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 25(1) and 26(1) of the Police (Northern Ireland) Act 1998[^f00001], makes the following Regulations. Those powers are now vested in the Department[^f00002].

In accordance with sections 25(8) and 26(6) of that Act the Department has consulted the Northern Ireland Policing Board and the Police Association for Northern Ireland before making these Regulations.

## 1. Citation and commencement[^key-12bc4f5769d4c17cf4e4c5b9c416f37b]

These Regulations may be cited as the Police Service of Northern Ireland (Amendment) Regulations 2023 and shall come into operation on 28th April 2023.

## 2. Amendment of the Police Service of Northern Ireland Regulations 2005[^key-891e66b87e4a124759f55dffc555867e]

(1) The Police Service of Northern Ireland Regulations 2005[^f00003] shall be amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

(2) In regulation 3(1) in paragraph (a) of the definition of “public holiday”, for 2023 only, after “first Monday in May” insert “, second Monday in May”.

## 3. Amendment of the Police Service of Northern Ireland and Police Service of Northern Ireland Reserve (Injury Benefit) Regulations 2006[^key-5872a3337e670ce16f35615bdcc66ba1]

(1) The Police Service of Northern Ireland and Police Service of Northern Ireland Reserve (Injury Benefit) Regulations 2006[^f00004] shall be amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

(2) In Schedule 1 in the definition of “public holiday”, for 2023 only, after “first Monday in May” insert “, second Monday in May”.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Justice on 4th April 2023

Richard Pengelly

A senior officer of the Department of Justice

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Police Service of Northern Ireland Regulations 2005 and the Police Service of Northern Ireland and Police Service of Northern Ireland Reserve (Injury Benefit) Regulations 2006 to allow for the additional public holiday on 8th May 2023, to mark the coronation of His Majesty King Charles III.
