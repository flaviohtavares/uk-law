# The Insolvency (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2023, section 3

> Text as at 2023-03-13.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 3. Saving provisions[^key-da1a3d0e134438535aff70f28fcdb3e6]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This Rule applies where before 30th March 2022—

- (a) a moratorium under Part 1A[^f00005] of the Order has come into force; or
- (b) in the case of a moratorium for a company to which either Article 13BA or 13BB[^f00006] applies, an application has been made to the High Court.

(2) Where this Rule applies—

- (a) the amendments made by Parts 2 and 3 of these Rules do not apply; and
- (b) Part 3 of Schedule 8 to the Corporate Insolvency and Governance Act 2020[^f00007] continues to have effect,

in relation to that moratorium.

**4.**[^key-9a2ecade66cf6e276b474b2b56c527d5] Nothing in Parts 2 and 3 of these Rules affects the operation of the principal Rules in relation to a moratorium under Schedule A1 to the Order which has come into operation before repeal of that schedule taking effect on 25th June 2020[^f00008].
