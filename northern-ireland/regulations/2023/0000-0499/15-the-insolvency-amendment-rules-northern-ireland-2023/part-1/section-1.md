# The Insolvency (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2023, section 1

> Text as at 2023-03-13.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation[^key-3b9127e793b5488d0a1687654f183371]

*Extent: N.I.*

These Rules may be cited as the Insolvency (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2023 and shall come into operation on 13th March 2023.

**2.**[^key-51f60e74b470538bce66c375cb6663d8] In these Rules—

(1) “**the principal Rules**” means the Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991[^f00004]; and

(2) “**the Order**” means the Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989 and any reference to a numbered Article is a reference to an Article of that Order.
