# The Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) (Offences) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2023

> Text as at 2023-05-15.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

2023 No. 70

Road Traffic And Vehicles

The Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) (Offences) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2023

Made

20th April 2023

Coming into operation

15th May 2023

The Department for Infrastructure[^f00001], makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 57(2) of the Road Traffic Offenders (Northern Ireland) Order 1996[^f00002] and now vested in it[^f00003].

In accordance with Article 91(1) of that Order the Department has consulted with such representative organisations as it thinks fit.

## 1. Citation and commencement[^key-21ad830fe4dbb0dfc3b8290b0a7e5fb8]

This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) (Offences) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2023 and shall come into operation on 15th May 2023.

## 2. Amendment of the Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) (Offences) Order[^key-bc7bcc8bd50e5d2d62f2dfbf8126fdb5]

In Schedule 1 to the Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) (Offences) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997[^f00004] (fixed penalty offences), after the heading “Offences under the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1995” and before item 20 insert—

> |  |  |  |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 19A | Article 12 | Driving mechanically propelled vehicle on a road or other public place without due care and attention, or without reasonable consideration. |

.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Infrastructure on 20th April 2023

Chris Hughes

A senior officer of the

Department for Infrastructure

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends Schedule 1 to the Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) (Offences) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997 to specify an additional offence, relating to the driving of a mechanically propelled vehicle on a road or other public place without due care and attention, or without reasonable consideration for other persons using the road or place, to be a fixed penalty offence for the purposes of the Road Traffic Offenders (Northern Ireland) Order 1996.

An Explanatory Memorandum has been produced and is available from Safe and Accessible Travel Division, Department for Infrastructure, Clarence Court, 10-18 Adelaide Street, Town Parks, Belfast BT2 8GB or online alongside this Statutory Rule at http://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr.
