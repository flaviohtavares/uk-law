# The Public Service Vehicles Accessibility (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2023

Road Traffic And Vehicles

The Public Service Vehicles Accessibility (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2023

Made

5th September 2023

Coming into operation

1st October 2023

The Department for Infrastructure[^f00001], makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 41(2) and 45(1)(b) of the Disability Discrimination Act 1995[^f00002] and now vested in it[^f00003].

## 1. Citation and commencement[^c-2593d569a4387a7c0fb23ea2eac148c9]

These Regulations may be cited as the Public Service Vehicles Accessibility (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2023 and shall come into operation on 1st October 2023.

## 2. Amendment of the Public Service Vehicles Accessibility Regulations[^c-d5593f5f6dc54c6a353374912441f1dd]

(1) The Public Service Vehicles Accessibility Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00004] are amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

(2) In regulation 7 (applications and fees for accessibility certificates), in paragraph (2)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a)—
  - (i) in head (i) for “£28.00” substitute “£46.00”; and
  - (ii) in head (ii) for “£55.00” substitute “£70.50”; and
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b)—
  - (i) in head (i) for “£10.00” substitute “£17.00”; and
  - (ii) in head (ii) for “£20.00” substitute “£38.00”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Public Service Vehicles Accessibility Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003 in order to increase the fees payable in respect of applications for accessibility certificates.

On first application, where the vehicle is required to comply with the only one of Schedules 1, 2 or 3 the fee is increased from £28.00 to £46.00 and on subsequent application the fee is increased from £10.00 to £17.00.

On first application, where the vehicle is required to comply with Schedules 1 and 2 or Schedules 1 and 3 the fee is increased from £55.00 to £70.50 and on subsequent application the fee is increased from £20.00 to £38.00.

An Explanatory Memorandum and Regulatory Impact Assessment have been produced and are available from Safe and Accessible Travel Division, Department for Infrastructure, Clarence Court, 10-18 Adelaide Street, Town Parks, Belfast BT2 8GB or online alongside this Regulation at http//www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr
