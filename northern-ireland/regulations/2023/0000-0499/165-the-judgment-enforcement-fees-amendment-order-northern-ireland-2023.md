# The Judgment Enforcement Fees (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2023

> Text as at 2023-11-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

2023 No. 165

Judgments (enforcement)

The Judgment Enforcement Fees (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2023

Made

10th October 2023

Coming into operation

1st November 2023

The Department of Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 116(1) of the Judicature (Northern Ireland) Act 1978[^f00001] and now vested in it[^f00002], makes the following Order with the concurrence of the Department of Finance[^f00003].

The Department has, in accordance with section 116(1) of that Act, consulted with the Lady Chief Justice.

## 1. Citation and commencement[^c-3c5225bb8680b173cab6d7d94f53138b]

This Order may be cited as the Judgment Enforcement Fees (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2023 and shall come into operation on 1st November 2023.

## 2. Amendment of the Judgment Enforcement Fees Order (Northern Ireland) 1996[^c-315a2918242f56d9c3cbf987fedeac6e]

The Judgment Enforcement Fees Order (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00004] is amended as follows.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Judgment Enforcement Fees Order (Northern Ireland) 1996 ([S.R. 1996 No. 101](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1996/101)) to increase the majority of fees payable in respect of the enforcement of judgments under the Judgments Enforcement (Northern Ireland) Order 1981.

An Explanatory Memorandum and a Regulatory Impact Assessment have been produced and are available from Northern Ireland Courts and Tribunals Service, Central Management Team, Laganside House, 23-27 Oxford Street, Belfast BT1 3LA or online alongside this Statutory Rule at http://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr.
