# The General Teaching Council for Northern Ireland (Constitution) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014

> Text as at 2015-10-15.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

2014 No. 218

Education

The General Teaching Council for Northern Ireland (Constitution) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014

Made

5th August 2014

Coming into operation

8th September 2014

The Department of Education makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 34 of and paragraph 1 of Schedule 1 to the Education (Northern Ireland) Order 1998 [^c22190631].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the General Teaching Council for Northern Ireland (Constitution) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014 and shall come into operation on 8th September 2014.

## 2. Amendment of the General Teaching Council for Northern Ireland (Constitution) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001

(1) The General Teaching Council for Northern Ireland (Constitution) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001 [^c22190641] are amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (3).

(2) In regulation 8 (term of office), in paragraph (1) at the beginning insert “ Subject to regulation 8A, ”.

(3)[^key-da59f9498f0d01e0d36e5b80eb10de5a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Education on 5th August 2014.

Faustina Graham

A senior officer of the

Department of Education

*(This note is not part of the Regulations)*

These Regulations amend the General Teaching Council for Northern Ireland (Constitution) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001 to extend the term of office of Council members whose term would otherwise have ended on 14 October 2014 by a further year.
