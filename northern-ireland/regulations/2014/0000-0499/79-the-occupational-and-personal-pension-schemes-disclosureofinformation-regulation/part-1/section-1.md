# The Occupational and Personal Pension Schemes (DisclosureofInformation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 1

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation, commencement and expiry[^c-18e24de19b2e779ee2327e7cf4f875be]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Occupational and Personal Pension Schemes (Disclosure of Information) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014 and shall come into operation on 6th April 2014.

(2) On 6th April 2015 the following provisions cease to have effect—

- (a) paragraphs 2 and 3 of Schedule 6, and
- (b) paragraph 11(1)(b) and (2) of Schedule 8.
