# The Occupational and Personal Pension Schemes (DisclosureofInformation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 18

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 18. Lifestyling[^c-038feeb0303bd591cb8a8163c0d9fa50]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The information listed in Part 3 of Schedule 2 must be given to a member in accordance with this regulation where the scheme contains provision for lifestyling.

(2) The information must, subject to paragraph (3), be given between 5 and 15 years before the member’s retirement date.

(3) Information need not be given under paragraph (2) where it has been given in the previous 12 months under regulation 6.
