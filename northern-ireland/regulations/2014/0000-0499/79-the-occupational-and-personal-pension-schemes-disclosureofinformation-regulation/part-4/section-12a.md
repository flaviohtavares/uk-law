# The Occupational and Personal Pension Schemes (DisclosureofInformation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 12A

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 12A. Additional information available on request: pooled funds[^key-16523bda0b19263d29395ef910e70a0f]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where the scheme is a relevant scheme within the meaning of the Occupational Pension Schemes (Scheme Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997, a statement containing the information listed in paragraph 35 of Schedule 3 must be given on request to a member, or to a recognised trade union on behalf of the member, in accordance with this regulation.

(2) The statement must be given within 2 months of the date the request is made and it must be given in accordance with regulation 26.

(3) A statement is not required to be given where the request is made—

- (a) less than 6 months after the last occasion on which information was given to or in respect of the member under this regulation, or
- (b) by a recognised trade union unless it is relevant to the rights of a member who is in that recognised trade union.
