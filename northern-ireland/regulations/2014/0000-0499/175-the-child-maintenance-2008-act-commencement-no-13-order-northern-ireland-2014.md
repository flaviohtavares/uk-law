# The Child Maintenance (2008 Act) (Commencement No. 13) Order (Northern Ireland) 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 175 (C. 10)

Family Law

Child Support

The Child Maintenance (2008 Act) (Commencement No. 13) Order (Northern Ireland) 2014

Made

17th June 2014

The Department for Social Development makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 41(1) of the Child Maintenance Act (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Child Maintenance (2008 Act) (Commencement No. 13) Order (Northern Ireland) 2014.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of—

- (a) section 11 (transfer of cases to new rules), so far as relating to the paragraphs referred to in paragraph (b);
- (b) paragraphs 2, 3, 5, 6 and 7 of Schedule 2 (maintenance calculations: transfer of cases to new rules),

for the purpose only of making regulations is the day after the day on which this Order is made.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 17th June 2014

(L.S.)

Anne McCleary

A senior officer of the Department for Social Development

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the coming into operation of section 11 of, and paragraphs 2, 3, 5, 6 and 7 of Schedule 2 to, the Child Maintenance Act (Northern Ireland) 2008, for the purpose of making of regulations. This Order commences powers to allow the Department to make Regulations to require parties in existing cases to choose whether or not to remain in the statutory scheme under the 2012 rules for calculating child maintenance.
