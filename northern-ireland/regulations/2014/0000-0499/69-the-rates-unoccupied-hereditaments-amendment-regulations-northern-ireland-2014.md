# The Rates (Unoccupied Hereditaments) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 69

Rating

The Rates (Unoccupied Hereditaments) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014

Made

5th March 2014

Coming into operation

1st April 2014

The Department of Finance and Personnel makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 25A(6) of, and paragraph 1(1) to (3) of Schedule 8A to, the Rates (Northern Ireland) Order 1977[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Rates (Unoccupied Hereditaments) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014 and shall come into operation on 1st April 2014.

## 2. Amendment of the Rates (Unoccupied Hereditaments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011

In paragraph 2(1)(a)(iv) of the Schedule to the Rates (Unoccupied Hereditaments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011[^f00002] for the words “thirty months” there shall be substituted the words “forty two months”.

## 3. Revocation

The Rates (Unoccupied Hereditaments) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013[^f00003] are revoked.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance and Personnel on 5th March 2014

(L.S.)

Brian McClure

A senior officer of the Department of Finance and Personnel

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend paragraph 2 of the Schedule to the Rates (Unoccupied Hereditaments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011 ([S.R. 2011 No. 36](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2011/36)).

Regulation 2 provides that the temporary exemption from unoccupied rates for a developer who is entitled to possession of a domestic hereditament, which first appeared in the valuation list before 1st April 2012 and has never been occupied, is extended from thirty months to forty two months.

Regulation 3 revokes the Rates (Unoccupied Hereditaments) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013.
