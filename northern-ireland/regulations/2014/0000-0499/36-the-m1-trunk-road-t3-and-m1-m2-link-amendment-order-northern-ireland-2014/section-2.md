# The M1/Trunk Road T3 and M1-M2 Link (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Designation and direction

(1) The lengths of road described in Part I of Schedule 1 shall become special road and trunk road and be part of the Belfast-Ballygawley-Londonderry-Land Frontier Trunk Road T3;

(2) The lengths of road described in Part II and the road and lengths of road described in Part III of Schedule 1 shall become trunk road and be part of the Belfast-Ballygawley-Londonderry-Land Frontier Trunk Road T3;

(3) The road and lengths of road described in Part IV of Schedule 1 shall cease to be trunk road.
