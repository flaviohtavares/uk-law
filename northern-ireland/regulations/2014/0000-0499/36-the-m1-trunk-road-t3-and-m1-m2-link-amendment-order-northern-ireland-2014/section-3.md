# The M1/Trunk Road T3 and M1-M2 Link (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Classification of traffic

The lengths of road described in Part I of Schedule 1 shall be used only by traffic of Classes I and II as set out in Schedule 1 to the Roads (Northern Ireland) Order 1993.
