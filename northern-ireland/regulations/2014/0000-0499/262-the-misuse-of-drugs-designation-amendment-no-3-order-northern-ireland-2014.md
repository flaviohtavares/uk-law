# The Misuse of Drugs (Designation) (Amendment No.3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 262

Dangerous Drugs

The Misuse of Drugs (Designation) (Amendment No.3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2014

Made

21st October 2014

Coming into operation

11th November 2014

The Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 7(4) of the Misuse of Drugs Act 1971 [^f00001] as adapted by section 7(9) of that Act and now vested in it [^f00002] and after consultation with the Advisory Council on the Misuse of Drugs in accordance with section 7(7) of that Act.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Misuse of Drugs (Designation) (Amendment No.3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2014 and comes into operation on 11th November 2014.

(2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 [^f00003] shall apply to this Order as it applies to an Act of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

## 2. Amendment to the Misuse of Drugs (Designation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2001

(1) The Misuse of Drugs (Designation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2001 [^f00004] is amended as follows:

(2) In paragraph 1(a) of Part 1 of the Schedule, after “Gamma-butyrolactone” insert—

> Khat

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety on 21st October 2014

Dr. Anne Kilgallen

A senior officer of the

Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

Section 7(3) of the Misuse of Drugs Act 1971 requires Regulations to be made to allow the use for medical purposes of the drugs which are subject to control under that Act. Section 7(3) does not apply to any drug designated by order under section 7(4) as a drug to which section 7(4) is to apply. This Order amends the Misuse of Drugs (Designation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2001 by inserting Khat into Part 1 of the Schedule to that Order.
