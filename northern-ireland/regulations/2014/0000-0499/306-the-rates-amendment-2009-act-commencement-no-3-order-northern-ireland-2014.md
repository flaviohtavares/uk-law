# The Rates (Amendment) (2009 Act) (Commencement No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 306 (C. 18)

Subject

The Rates (Amendment) (2009 Act) (Commencement No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2014

Made

10th December 2014

The Department of Finance and Personnel makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 19(1) of the Rates (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2009[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Rates (Amendment) (2009 Act) (Commencement No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2014.

(2) In this Order “the 2009 Act” means the Rates (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2009.

## 2. Appointed day

1st April 2015 is the day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the 2009 Act listed in the Schedule.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance and Personnel on 10th December 2014

(L.S.)

Brian McClure

A senior officer of the Department of Finance and Personnel

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the coming into operation of those provisions of the Rates (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2009 which have not already been brought into operation. They will come into operation on 1st April 2015.
