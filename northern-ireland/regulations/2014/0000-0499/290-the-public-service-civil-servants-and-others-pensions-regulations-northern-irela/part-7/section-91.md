## 91. Entitlement to pension credit members’ pension[^key-4d1a2a648dd80a92214cb2a32b0bda53]

*Extent: N.I.*

A pension credit member (P) of this scheme is entitled to the immediate payment for life of a pension credit member’s pension under this scheme if—

- (a) P has reached normal pension age under this scheme;
- (b) the pension sharing order under which P is entitled to the pension credit has taken effect; and
- (c) P has claimed payment of the pension.
