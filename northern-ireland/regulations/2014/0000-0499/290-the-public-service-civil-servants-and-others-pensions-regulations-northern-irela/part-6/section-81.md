> Text as at 2026-06-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 81. Exercising an option under this Chapter[^key-b88a733dfef01c764f73ccc0e8d5e27f]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A member may exercise an option under this Chapter by notice to the scheme manager in any form the scheme manager requires.

(2) In this Chapter, “option notice” means a notice by which a member exercises an option under this Chapter.
