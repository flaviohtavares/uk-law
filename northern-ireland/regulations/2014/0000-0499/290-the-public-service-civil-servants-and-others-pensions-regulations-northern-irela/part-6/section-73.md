# The Public Service (Civil Servants and Others) Pensions Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 73

>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 73. Meaning of “upper tier payment threshold”[^key-91afa0520dee5d486db8406a8eabe363]

*Extent: N.I.*

An active member of this scheme meets the upper tier threshold for payment of an ill-health pension (“upper tier payment threshold”) if—

- (a) the member is not partially retired; and
- (b) the member’s breakdown in health involves total incapacity for employment.
