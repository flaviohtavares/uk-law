# The Public Service (Civil Servants and Others) Pensions Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 59

> Text as at 2026-06-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 59. Qualifying service[^key-7679077d164e1311e2438ab421798768]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) In these Regulations, “qualifying service” means the total of—

- (a) any continuous period of active membership of[^key-7aa9a8645de3271205e21f8814b9dfd8] this scheme;
- (aa)[^key-a13083c10b604b99fc00276bba3b90dc] the member’s service in another occupational pension scheme that the Department has determined should be counted for the purposes of this regulation;
- (b) if a transfer payment has been received by this scheme in respect of a member’s accrued rights under another occupational pension scheme, the members pensionable service under that scheme; and
- (c) for a transition member with continuity of service, the member’s pensionable service under the PCSPS(NI) before the transition date[^key-1f0f39ccaeabdd18aee5436b74e469e9] for that member.

(2) None of the following counts as qualifying service—

- (a) any pensionable service under this scheme in respect of which a person’s rights under this scheme are extinguished[^f00053];
- (b) any pensionable service under the PCSPS(NI) in respect of which a person’s rights under that scheme are extinguished;
- (c) any unauthorised absence from scheme employment.
