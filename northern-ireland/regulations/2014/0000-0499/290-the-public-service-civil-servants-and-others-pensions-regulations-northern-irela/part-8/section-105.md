# The Public Service (Civil Servants and Others) Pensions Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 105

> Text as at 2026-06-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 105. Meaning of “dependant’s upper tier top up earned pension”[^c-71980e567f377d889728ce5f9ff86bfd][^c-b70f6c635be7aeb4ed9532e97b51c4d3]

*Extent: N.I.*

A dependant’s upper tier top up earned pension is a pension payable on the death of a member of this scheme if the member was entitled to the immediate payment of an upper tier top up earned pension as at the date of the member’s death.
