# The Public Service (Civil Servants and Others) Pensions Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 109

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 109. Annual rate of surviving adult’s pensions payable on death of deferred member[^c-72f30fd507e0f17f0d55f3286d4562b4]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies on the death of a deferred member of this scheme.

(2) The annual rate of a dependant’s earned pension is an amount equal to 37.5% of the sum of the provisional amount of deferred earned pension specified in the deferred member’s account.

(3) The annual rate of a dependant’s added pension is an amount equal to 37.5% of the provisional amount of deferred added (all beneficiaries) pension specified in the deferred member’s account.
