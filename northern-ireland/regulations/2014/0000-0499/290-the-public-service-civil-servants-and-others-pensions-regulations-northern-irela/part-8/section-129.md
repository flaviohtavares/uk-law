# The Public Service (Civil Servants and Others) Pensions Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 129

> Text as at 2026-06-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 129. Meaning of “annualised final pay”[^c-4382b1a3751aec2b14d271837f452af5]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of this Chapter, for a continuous period of pensionable service that is less than 12 months, a member’s annualised final pay is—

[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2014/290)

where—

- *FP* is the amount of the member’s permanent pensionable earnings payable in respect of[^c-519479eb084f704fbc2df1591a87f90e] that period of service; and
- *N* is the number of days in that period.

(2) But if the continuous period of pensionable service includes 29th February, paragraph (1) has effect with the substitution for “365” of “366”.

(3)[^c-5311bedb7469e80f1ed5d2803d7824c7] In this regulation—

- (a) if the member is a transition member with continuity of service, “pensionable earnings” in respect of any period includes the member’s pensionable earnings under the PCSPS(NI) before the transition date for that member; and
- (b) in respect of a period of assumed pay, “**pensionable earnings**” means the member’s assumed pay.
