# The Public Service (Civil Servants and Others) Pensions Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 140

> Text as at 2026-06-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 140. Application of Part[^c-4ae7a95817116c5c0a964c1898aebd50]

*Extent: N.I.*

This Part—

- (a) supplements the rights conferred by or under Chapter 1 of Part 4ZA[^c-c60590a060549ba43c116b384a17dbe0] of the 1993 Act (transfer rights: general) and is without prejudice to that Chapter; and
- (b) supplements the rights conferred by or under Chapter 2[^c-6583ee387b7ab06a049fcba4fa8b1967] of that Part (early leavers: cash transfer sums and contribution refunds)[^f00072] and is without prejudice to that Chapter.
