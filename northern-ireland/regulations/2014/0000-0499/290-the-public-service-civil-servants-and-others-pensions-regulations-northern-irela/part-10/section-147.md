# The Public Service (Civil Servants and Others) Pensions Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 147

> Text as at 2026-06-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 147. Effect of a transfer out[^key-3f2aebece34e07eb048632ae45a4df06][^key-d6aaf33276febcb2cdb6601428c88a85]

*Extent: N.I.*

If a transfer payment is made under this Chapter in respect of a member’s accrued rights to benefits under this scheme, those rights are extinguished.
