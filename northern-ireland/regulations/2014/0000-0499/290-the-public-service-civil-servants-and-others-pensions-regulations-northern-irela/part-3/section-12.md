## 12. Scheme advisory board: conflicts of interest[^c-56373f011fa0b0d70be847b2bdc3a7d3]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Before appointing, or approving the appointment of, any person under regulation 11(1), the Department must be satisfied that that person does not have a conflict of interest[^f00046].

(2) The Department must be satisfied from time to time that none of the members of the scheme advisory board has a conflict of interest.

(3) If the Department determines that a member of the scheme advisory board has a conflict of interest, the Department must terminate the appointment of the member.

(4) A member of the scheme advisory board, or a person proposed to be appointed as such a member, must provide the Department with any information the Department may reasonably require for the purpose of paragraph (1) or (2).
