## 11. Scheme advisory board: membership[^key-c2d5b0ac743ed3ccaa59ca6e47103cf8]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The scheme advisory board is to consist of the following members—

- (a) a chair appointed by the Department; and
- (b) at least 2, and no more than 16, members appointed by the chair with the approval of the Department.

(2) A member of the scheme advisory board is to hold and vacate office in accordance with the terms of that member’s appointment.
