## 4. Scheme manager[^key-398b8a053ef6ef8c436732b24f4ba10b]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department is the scheme manager for this scheme and any statutory pension scheme that is connected with it[^f00038].

(2) The scheme manager is responsible for managing and administering this scheme and any statutory pension scheme that is connected with it.
