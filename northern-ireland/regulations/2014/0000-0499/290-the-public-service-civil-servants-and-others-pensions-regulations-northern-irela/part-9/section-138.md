> Text as at 2026-06-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 138. Deduction of payments for extra pension under Schedule 1[^c-cf32be69b42d4381e356f1d10a66d088]

*Extent: N.I.*

Schedule 1 has effect for the purpose of enabling deductions of payments for—

- (a) added pension; and
- (b) an effective pension age.
