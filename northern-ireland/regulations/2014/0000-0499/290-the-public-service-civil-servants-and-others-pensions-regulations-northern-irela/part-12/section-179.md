>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 179. Provision of benefit information statements to members[^key-17e2fe6fc9eb1b9b2d469661b0a97064]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The scheme manager must provide benefit information statements to each active member of this scheme in accordance with—

- (a) section 14 of the Act (information about benefits); and
- (b) directions made under that section (Department directions).
