# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 4

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 4. Connected Schemes

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) In these Regulations, “**connected scheme**” means another statutory pension scheme that is connected, within the meaning of section 4(6) of the Act, with this scheme.

(2) The scheme established by the Teachers' Superannuation (Additional Voluntary Contributions) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 1996 [^c22298671] is not a connected scheme.
