## 2. Establishment of Northern Ireland Teachers' Pension Scheme 2015

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) These Regulations establish a scheme for the payment of pensions and other benefits to or in respect of teachers [^c22298521] in Northern Ireland.

(2) This scheme is to be known as the Northern Ireland Teachers' Pension Scheme 2015.
