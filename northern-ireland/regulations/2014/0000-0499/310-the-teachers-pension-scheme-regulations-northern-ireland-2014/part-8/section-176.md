# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 176

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 176. Cessation of benefits where no entitlement

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies if after paying a benefit the Department determines that there was no entitlement or there is no longer an entitlement to the benefit.

(2) The Department may—

- (a) cease to pay the benefit;
- (b) withhold the whole or any part of the benefit; or
- (c) recover any payment made if there was no entitlement to the benefit.
