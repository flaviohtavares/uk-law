# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 167

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 167. Member declaration

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department may not pay a member a lump sum under this Chapter unless the member declares in writing that, on payment of the lump sum, paragraph 3A of Schedule 29 to the Finance Act 2004 Act [^c22298811] would not apply.

(2) The declaration must be—

- (a) signed by the member;
- (b) in a form specified by the Department; and
- (c) provided by a date determined by the Department.
