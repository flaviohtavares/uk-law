# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 215

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 215. Application of Chapter

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This Chapter applies in relation to all persons who were in an eligible employment.

(2) This Chapter does not apply in relation to any person without that person's agreement.
