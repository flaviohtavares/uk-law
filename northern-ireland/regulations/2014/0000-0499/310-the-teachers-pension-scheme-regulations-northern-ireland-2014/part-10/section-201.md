# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 201

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 201. Application for a statement of entitlement

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A person (P) may apply for a statement of entitlement by written notice to the Department.

(2) The application must be made within 12 months after the day on which P becomes subject to the receiving scheme.
