# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 12

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 12. Membership of scheme advisory board

*Extent: N.I.*

The Department may determine—

- (a) the membership of the Northern Ireland Teachers' Pension Scheme Advisory Board;
- (b) the manner in which members of the Northern Ireland Teachers' Pension Scheme Advisory Board may be appointed and removed; and
- (c) the terms of appointment of the members of the Northern Ireland Teachers' Pension Scheme Advisory Board.
