# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 9

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 9. Membership of pension board

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the Department may determine—

- (a) the membership of the Northern Ireland Teachers' Pension Scheme Pension Board;
- (b) the manner in which the Northern Ireland Teachers' Pension Scheme Pension Board members may be appointed and removed; and
- (c) the terms of appointment of the members of the Northern Ireland Teachers' Pension Scheme Pension Board.

(2) The Northern Ireland Teachers' Pension Scheme Pension Board is to include equal numbers of employer representatives and member representatives [^c22298681].

## Annotations

[^c22298681]: Marginal citation: See section 5(7) of the Act for the meaning of “employer representatives” and “member representatives”.
