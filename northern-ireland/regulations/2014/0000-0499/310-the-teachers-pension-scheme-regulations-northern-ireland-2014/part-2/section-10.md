# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 10

>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 10. Northern Ireland Teachers’ Pension Scheme Pension Board–Conflict of interest[^c-6649ff26b78945b85c90cbc92d703488]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department must be satisfied that any person to be appointed as a member of the Northern Ireland Teachers' Pension Scheme Pension Board does not have a conflict of interest [^c22298691].

(2) The Department must be satisfied from time to time that none of the members of the Northern Ireland Teachers' Pension Scheme Pension Board has a conflict of interest.

(3) A person who is to be appointed as a member of the Northern Ireland Teachers' Pension Scheme Pension Board must provide the Department with such information as it reasonably requires for the purposes of paragraph (1).

(4) A person who is a member of the Northern Ireland Teachers' Pension Scheme Pension Board must provide the Department with such information as it reasonably requires for the purposes of paragraph (2).
