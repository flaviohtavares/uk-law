# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 11

> Text as at 2025-04-01.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 11. Establishment of scheme advisory board

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A scheme advisory board (“the Northern Ireland Teachers' Pension Scheme Advisory Board”) is established.

(2) The Northern Ireland Teachers' Pension Scheme Advisory Board is responsible for providing advice to the Department, at its request, on the desirability of changes to this scheme.

(3) The Department is to determine the process by which the Northern Ireland Teachers' Pension Scheme Advisory Board discharges its functions.
