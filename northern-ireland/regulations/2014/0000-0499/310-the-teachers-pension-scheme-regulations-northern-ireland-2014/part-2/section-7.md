# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 7

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 7. Scheme Manager

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department is the scheme manager for this scheme and any connected scheme.

(2) The Department is responsible for managing this scheme and any connected scheme.

(3) The Department may delegate its functions as scheme manager under these Regulations, including this power to delegate.

(4)[^key-b4c3d1465fb37bac7a9fd2f2a752736c] Where the Department’s functions as scheme manager have been delegated to a person, any reference to the Department in relation to those functions in Parts 3 to 11 and Schedules 1 to 4 shall be construed as a reference to that person.
