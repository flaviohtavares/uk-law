# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 49

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 49. Closure of pension accounts on transfer out or repayment of balance of contributions

*Extent: N.I.*

All pension accounts relating to a member (P) (other than a pension credit member's account) must be closed if—

- (a) a transfer payment is made respect of P's accrued rights under this scheme; or
- (b) P is repaid the balance of contributions under regulation 189.
