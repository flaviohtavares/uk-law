# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 55

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 55. Ill-health pension ceases to be payable

*Extent: N.I.*

If an ill-health pension ceases to be payable to a person (P) under regulation 114 or 118 and P re-enters pensionable service—

- (a) the pensioner member's account must be closed;
- (b) the active member's account must be re-established and credited with an amount equal to the annual rate of pension payable immediately before the ill-health pension ceased to be payable.
