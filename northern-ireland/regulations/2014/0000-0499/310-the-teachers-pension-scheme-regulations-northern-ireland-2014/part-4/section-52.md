# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 52

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 52. Receipt of club transfer values

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies if a club transfer is received from another club scheme in relation to an active member of this scheme.

(2) On receipt of the transfer value payment, the active member's account must be credited with an amount of club transfer earned pension the member is entitled to under Part 10 (transfers).
