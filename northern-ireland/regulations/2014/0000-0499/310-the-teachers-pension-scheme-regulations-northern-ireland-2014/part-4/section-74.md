# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 74

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 74. Meaning of “actuarial adjustment”

*Extent: N.I.*

In these Regulations, “**actuarial adjustment**” means the actuarial adjustment (to be determined by the Department after taking advice from the scheme actuary) that is applied when calculating the annual rate of pension payable—

- (a)[^c-68081a870338c2169cc8034ba7e9e876] to a member who, on the entitlement day for a pension other than an age retirement pension or an ill health pension, has not reached normal pension age; or
- (b) to an active member who becomes a pensioner member after reaching normal pension age.
