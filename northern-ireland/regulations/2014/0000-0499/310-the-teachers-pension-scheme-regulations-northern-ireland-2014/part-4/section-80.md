# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 80

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 80. Pension credit member's pension account

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A pension account must be established for each pension credit member (“the pension credit member's account”).

(2) The pension credit member's account must specify the amount of credited pension and for that amount—

- (a) the actuarial adjustment (if any); and
- (b) the commutation amount (if any).

(3) In these Regulations, “**amount of credited pension**” means an amount equal to the pension credit calculated in accordance with regulations made under paragraph 5(b) of Schedule 5 to the 1999 Order.
