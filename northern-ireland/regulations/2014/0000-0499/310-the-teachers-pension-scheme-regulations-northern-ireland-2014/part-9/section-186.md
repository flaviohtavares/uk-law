# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 186

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 186. Contributions under Schedule 2

*Extent: N.I.*

Schedule 2 has effect for the purpose of enabling—

- (a) payment of additional pension contributions by lump sum; and
- (b) monthly payments of—
  - (i) faster accrual contributions;
  - (ii) additional pension contributions; and
  - (iii) buy-out contributions.
