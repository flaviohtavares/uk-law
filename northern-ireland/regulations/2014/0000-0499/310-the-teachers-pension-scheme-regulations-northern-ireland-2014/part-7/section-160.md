# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 160

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 160. Payment of death grant

*Extent: N.I.*

On the death of a pension credit member, the death grant must be paid—

- (a) to the death grant beneficiary;
- (b) if there is more than one death grant beneficiary, to those beneficiaries in the shares determined in accordance with regulation 157(4) and (5); or
- (c) if there is no death grant beneficiary, to—
  - (i) the member's surviving spouse or surviving civil partner; or,
  - (ii) if there is no such person, to the member's personal representatives as part of the member's estate.
