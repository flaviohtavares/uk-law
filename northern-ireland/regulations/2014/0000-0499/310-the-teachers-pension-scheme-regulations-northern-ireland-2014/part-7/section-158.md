# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 158

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 158. Death grant: death of pension credit member before pension becomes payable

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A death grant is payable under this regulation on the death of a pension credit member (D) who dies before a pension credit retirement pension becomes payable to D under regulation 155.

(2) The amount of the death grant is found by—

- (a) taking the amount of credited pension which would have become payable to D at the date of D's death if D had reached normal pension age; and
- (b) multiplying that amount by 2.25.
