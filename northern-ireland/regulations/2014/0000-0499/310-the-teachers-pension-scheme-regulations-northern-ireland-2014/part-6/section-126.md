# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 126

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 126. General

*Extent: N.I.*

In this Part—

- “**member**” means a member other than a pension credit member;
- “**survivor's benefits**” means one or more of the following—
  - (a) a death grant;
  - (b) a survivor's pension.
