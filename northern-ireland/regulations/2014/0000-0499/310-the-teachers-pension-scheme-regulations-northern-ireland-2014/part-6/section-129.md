# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 129

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 129. Meaning of “dies as a pensioner member”

*Extent: N.I.*

In this Part, a member (D) dies as a pensioner member if a retirement pension becomes payable before D's death.
