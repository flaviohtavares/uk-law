# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 134

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 134. Meaning of “surviving adult”

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) For the purpose of a death grant, the “surviving adult” of a member who has died means the member's—

- (a) surviving spouse;
- (b) surviving civil partner; or
- (c) surviving nominated partner.

(2) For the purpose of a survivor's pension, the “surviving adult” of a member who has died means the member's—

- (a) surviving spouse;
- (b) surviving civil partner;
- (c) surviving nominated partner; or
- (d) surviving nominated beneficiary.
