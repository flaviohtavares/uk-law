# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 141

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 141. Supplementary death grant payable on death of pensioner member

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A death grant is payable under this regulation (“supplementary death grant”) if—

- (a) a member (D) dies as a pensioner member; and
- (b) AR is greater than AP.

(2) The amount of the death grant is AR-AP, where— AR is 5 x D's annual rate of retirement pension payable as at the date of D's death; and AP is the total amount of pension which was payable to D up until D's death.
