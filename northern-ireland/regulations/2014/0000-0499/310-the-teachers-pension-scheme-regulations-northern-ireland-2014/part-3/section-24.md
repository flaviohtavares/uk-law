# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 24

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 24. Service not pensionable unless member's contributions are paid

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department may decide that a period of service in respect of which member's contributions are not paid is not a period of pensionable service if—

- (a) the Department has sent the member a demand under regulation 194 (“the demand”) in respect of those contributions; and
- (b) within 3 years after the date of the demand, the member has not paid the whole of the amount and interest stated in the demand.

(2) The period of service is not a period of pensionable service if the Department —

- (a) gives the member written notice to that effect; and
- (b) repays the member any amount paid in part satisfaction of the demand.
