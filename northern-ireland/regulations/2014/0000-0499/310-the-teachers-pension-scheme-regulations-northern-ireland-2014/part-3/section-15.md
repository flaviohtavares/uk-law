# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 15

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 15. Meaning of “P's automatic enrolment date” and “P's automatic re-enrolment date”

*Extent: N.I.*

For the purpose of this Part—

- (a) “P's automatic enrolment date” is the date on which section 3 of the 2008 Act;
- (b) applies to a person (P) in relation to P's eligible employment; and
- (c) “P's automatic re-enrolment date” is a date determined under regulation 12 of the Occupational and Personal Pension Schemes (Automatic Enrolment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010 [^c22298701] in relation to P's eligible employment.
