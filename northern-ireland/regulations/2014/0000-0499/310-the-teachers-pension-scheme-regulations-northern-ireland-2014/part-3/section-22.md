## 22. Payment of retirement benefits to dual capacity members

*Extent: N.I.*

Retirement benefits under this scheme are payable to a dual capacity member as if the member were 2 or more members and the amounts payable are determined accordingly [^c22298711].

## Annotations

[^c22298711]: Marginal citation: The result is that 2 or more pensions lump sums may be payable in respect of the one member.
