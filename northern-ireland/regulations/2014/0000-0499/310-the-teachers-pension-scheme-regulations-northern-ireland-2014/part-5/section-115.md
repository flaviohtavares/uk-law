# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 115

> Text as at 2025-04-01.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 115. Meaning of “entitlement day” (total incapacity pension)

*Extent: N.I.*

The entitlement day for a total incapacity pension payable with an ill-health pension is the same as the entitlement day for the ill-health pension.
