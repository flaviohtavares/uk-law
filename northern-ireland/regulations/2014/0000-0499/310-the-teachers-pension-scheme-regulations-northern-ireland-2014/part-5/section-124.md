# The Teachers' Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 124

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 124. Entitlement to short-service annuity

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A person (P) is entitled to payment from the entitlement day of a short-service annuity in respect of post-benefit service if—

- (a) P enters a period of post-benefit service;
- (b) P is not re-qualified for retirement benefits in respect of that service;
- (c) P leaves all eligible employment; and
- (d) P has applied under regulation 162 for payment of the annuity.

(2) A short-service annuity is payable for life.
