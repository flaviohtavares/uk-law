# The Health and Social Care Bodies (Membership) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 318

Health And Social Care

The Health and Social Care Bodies (Membership) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014

Made

*16th December 2014*

Coming into operation

*6th January 2015*

The Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety makes the following regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 7(2), 12(2) and 14(2) of, and paragraph 3 of Schedule 1, paragraph 3 of Schedule 2 and paragraphs 3 and 5 of Schedule 3 to the Health and Social Care (Reform) Act (Northern Ireland) 2009[^f00001].

## 1. Citation, interpretation and commencement

(1) These regulations may be cited as the Health and Social Care Bodies (Membership) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014 and shall come into operation on 6th January 2015.

(2) In these Regulations “the principal Regulations” means the Regional Health and Social Care Board (Membership) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009[^f00002]; the Regional Agency for Public Health and Social Well-being (Membership) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009[^f00003]; and the Regional Business Services Organisation (Membership and Procedure) Regulations 2009[^f00004].

## 2. Amendment of the principal regulations

For regulation 4(1) in the principal regulations (*Disqualification for appointment)* substitute the following—

> **4.** (1) Subject to regulation 5, a Chair or a person appointed under regulation 2(1) shall be disqualified for appointment if that person –

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety on 16th December 2014

L Devlin

A senior officer of the

Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These regulations amend the principal regulations to provide that the Chairs of the Regional Health and Social Care Board, the Regional Agency for Public Health and Social Well-being and the Regional Business Services Organisation are disqualified for appointment on the same basis as other members appointed by the Department to these bodies.
