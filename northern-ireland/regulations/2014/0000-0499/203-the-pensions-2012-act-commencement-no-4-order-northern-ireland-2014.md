# The Pensions (2012 Act) (Commencement No. 4) Order (Northern Ireland) 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 203 (C. 14)

Pensions

The Pensions (2012 Act) (Commencement No. 4) Order (Northern Ireland) 2014

Made

3rd July 2014

The Department for Social Development makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 34(3) of the Pensions Act (Northern Ireland) 2012[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Pensions (2012 Act) (Commencement No. 4) Order (Northern Ireland) 2014.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of section 27 of the Pensions Act (Northern Ireland) 2012 (definition of money purchase benefits) is 24th July 2014.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 3rd July 2014

(L.S.)

Andrew M. Hamilton

A senior officer of the Department for Social Development

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into operation section 27 of the Pensions Act (Northern Ireland) 2012 on 24th July 2014.

Section 27 inserts section 176B into the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act [1993 (c. 49)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1993/49), which supplements the definition of “money purchase benefits” in section 176(1) of that Act, with retrospective effect to 1st January 1997. It also inserts section 78A into the Pensions (No. 2) Act (Northern Ireland) [2008 (c. 13 (N.I.)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nia/2008/13)), which supplements the definition of “money purchase benefits” in section 78 of that Act, with retrospective effect to 5th July 2010.
