# The Lands Tribunal (Salaries) Order (Northern Ireland) 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 259

Lands Tribunal

The Lands Tribunal (Salaries) Order (Northern Ireland) 2014

Laid before the Assembly in draft

Made

15th October 2014

Coming into operation

16th October 2014

The Department of Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(5) of the Lands Tribunal and Compensation Act (Northern Ireland) 1964[^f00001] and section 18 of the Administrative and Financial Provisions Act (Northern Ireland) 1962[^f00002], and now vested in it[^f00003] makes the following Order.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Lands Tribunal (Salaries) Order (Northern Ireland) 2014 and shall come into operation on 16th October 2014.

## 2. Increases in salaries

(1) The annual salary payable to the President of the Lands Tribunal shall be increased from £129,579 to £130,875 from 1st April 2014.

(2) The annual salary payable to any other member of the Lands Tribunal shall be increased from £121,993 to £123,213 from 1st April 2014.

## 3. Revocation

The Lands Tribunal (Salaries) Order (Northern Ireland) 2013[^f00004] is revoked.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Justice on 15th October 2014

David Ford

Minister of Justice

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for changes in the annual salaries payable to members of the Lands Tribunal for Northern Ireland following recommendations made in the Report of the Review Body on Senior Salaries in 2014 (TSO CM 8822) as presented to Government.

It increases by 1 percent, with effect from 1st April 2014, under section 18 of the Administrative and Financial Provisions Act (Northern Ireland) 1962, the annual salary of the President of the Lands Tribunal and all other members of the Tribunal.

Article 3 revokes the Lands Tribunal (Salaries) Order (Northern Ireland) 2013.
