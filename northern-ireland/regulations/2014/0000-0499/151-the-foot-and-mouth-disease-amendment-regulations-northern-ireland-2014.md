# The Foot and Mouth Disease (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 151

Animals

Animal Health

The Foot and Mouth Disease (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014

Made

30th May 2014

Coming into operation

1st July 2014

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002], makes the following Regulations.

## 1. Citation and Commencement

These Regulations may be cited as The Foot and Mouth Disease (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014 and shall come into operation on 1st July 2014.

## 2. Interpretation

The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00003] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

## 3. Amendment

The Foot and Mouth Disease Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006[^f00004] are amended as follows—

- (a) In regulation 15(1), for words “a contemporary control zone” substitute “a temporary control zone”;
- (b) In regulation 30(4)—
  - (i) For sub-paragraph (b), substitute “any protection zone extends from that border such that its boundary is at least three kilometres from the infected premises”; and
  - (ii) For sub-paragraph (c), substitute “any surveillance zone extends from that border such that its boundary is at least ten kilometres from the infected premises”.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 30th May 2014.

Geraldine Fee

A senior officer of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Foot and Mouth Disease Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006 ([S.R. 2006 No. 42](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/42)). The Regulations amend regulations 15(1) and 30(4)(b) and (c) to correct some technical errors.
