# The Child Support, Pensions and Social Security (2000 Act) (Commencement No. 12) Order (Northern Ireland) 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 138 (C. 6)

Family Law

Child Support

The Child Support, Pensions and Social Security (2000 Act) (Commencement No. 12) Order (Northern Ireland) 2014

Made

15th May 2014

The Department for Social Development makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 68(2)(a) of the Child Support, Pensions and Social Security Act (Northern Ireland) 2000[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and interpretation

This Order may be cited as the Child Support, Pensions and Social Security (2000 Act) (Commencement No. 12) Order (Northern Ireland) 2014.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of section 24 of the Child Support, Pensions and Social Security Act (Northern Ireland) 2000 (regulations), in so far as not already in operation, is 16th May 2014.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 15th May 2014

(L.S.)

Dave Wall

A senior officer of the Department for Social Development

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the coming into operation of section 24 of the Child Support, Pensions and Social Security Act (Northern Ireland) 2000 (regulations), in so far as not already in operation, on 16th May 2014.
