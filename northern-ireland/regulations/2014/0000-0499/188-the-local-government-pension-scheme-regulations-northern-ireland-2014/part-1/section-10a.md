# The Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 10A

> Text as at 2024-08-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 10A. Separate employments, etc.[^key-c41fa5b9aa6077dc74872133c2ed8f5b]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where a person holds separate employments under one employing authority, these Regulations apply as if each of them were with a different employer.

(2) For the purposes of these Regulations, a clerk of a district council who performs functions under Article 9(2) (conduct of elections) of the Electoral Law (Northern Ireland) Order 1972 shall, in relation to those functions, be treated as if that person were employed by that district council and that person’s pay in relation to those functions shall be treated as pensionable pay by that district council.
