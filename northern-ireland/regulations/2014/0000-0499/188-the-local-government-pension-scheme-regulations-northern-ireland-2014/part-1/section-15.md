# The Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 15

> Text as at 2024-08-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 15. Contributions during reserve forces service leave

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An active member on reserve forces service leave shall pay contributions in accordance with regulation 11 (contributions) or regulation 12 (temporary reduction in contributions).

(2) The contributions under paragraph (1) are payable on the active member's assumed pensionable pay calculated under regulation 23 (assumed pensionable pay).
