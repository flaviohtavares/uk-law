# The Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 59

> Text as at 2024-08-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 59. Pension debits

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Committee shall have regard to actuarial guidance issued by the Department as to the reduction of benefits payable under these Regulations in consequence of a pension debit.

(2) The Committee shall make such adjustments to a member's pension accounts as are required to give effect to a pension debit.
