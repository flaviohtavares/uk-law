> Text as at 2024-08-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 117. The board: appointments[^key-feb41d688133d427875970954bd616a7]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where the Board is established under regulation 116(7), the Committee shall determine—

- (a) the manner in which members of the Board may be appointed and removed; and
- (b) the terms of appointment of members of the Board.
