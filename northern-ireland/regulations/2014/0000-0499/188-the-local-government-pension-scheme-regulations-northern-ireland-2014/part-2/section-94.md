# The Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 94

> Text as at 2024-08-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 94. Non-assignability

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Every benefit to which a person is entitled under the Scheme is payable to or in trust for that person.

(2) No such benefit is assignable or chargeable with that person's, or any other person's, debts or other liabilities.

(3) On the bankruptcy of a person entitled to a benefit under the Scheme no part of the benefit passes to any trustee or other person acting on behalf of the creditors, except in accordance with an income payments order or an income payments agreement under Article 283 or 283A of the Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989 [^c22152281].
