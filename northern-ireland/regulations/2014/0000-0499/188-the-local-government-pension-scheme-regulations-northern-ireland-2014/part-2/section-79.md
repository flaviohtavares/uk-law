# The Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 79

> Text as at 2024-08-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 79. First instance decisions[^key-716a222787d9926212a5bc33c354c29b]

*Extent: N.I.*

For regulations 79 to 89 (decisions), a reference to the employing authority of a prospective member is a reference to the body that would be the prospective member's employer, if that prospective member were to become an active member in the employment by virtue of which the prospective member would be eligible to join the Scheme.
