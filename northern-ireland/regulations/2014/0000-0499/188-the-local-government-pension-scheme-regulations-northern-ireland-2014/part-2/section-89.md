## 89. Referral of decisions under regulation 80(1) to the county court[^key-716a222787d9926212a5bc33c354c29b]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where an employing authority has either decided or failed to decide such question as is mentioned in regulation 80(1) (first instance decisions: general), that question shall be determined by the county court [^key-d586cc11f5f826e12eabf899d7012eb2]....
