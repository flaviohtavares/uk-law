# The Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 97

> Text as at 2024-08-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 97. Tax

*Extent: N.I.*

The Committee is responsible for deducting from any payment of benefits under the Scheme any tax to which it may become chargeable under the 2004 Finance Act.
