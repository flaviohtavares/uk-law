# The Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 98

> Text as at 2024-08-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 98. Pension increases

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where a pension to which the 1971 Act applies is payable out of the pension fund any increase under that Act or the Pensions (Increase) (Northern Ireland) Order 1974 [^c22152301] shall be paid from that pension fund.

(2) Any increase in a pension required by reason of Chapter 3 of Part 4 of the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993 (Protection of Increases in Guaranteed Minimum Pensions: “Anti-Franking”) [^c22152311] shall be paid from the pension fund.
