# The Building Society Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 115

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 115. Application of rules in this Part

The rules in this Part apply only in relation to the liquidation committee established after a full payment resolution has been passed.
