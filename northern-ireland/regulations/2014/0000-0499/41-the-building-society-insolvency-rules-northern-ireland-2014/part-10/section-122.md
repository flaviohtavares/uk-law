# The Building Society Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 122

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 122. Quorum

Subject to rule 4.179(6A) of the 1991 Rules, as inserted by rule 134(3) a meeting of the committee is duly constituted if due notice of it has been given to all the members, and at least two members are present or represented.
