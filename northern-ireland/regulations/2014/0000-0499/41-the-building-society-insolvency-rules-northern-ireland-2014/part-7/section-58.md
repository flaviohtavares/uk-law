# The Building Society Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 58

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 58. Meaning of “prove”

(1) Apply rule 4.079 of the 1991 Rules.

(2) In paragraph (5), for “or a Government Department” substitute “, a Government Department or the FSCS”.

(3) Omit paragraphs (2), (6) and (8).
