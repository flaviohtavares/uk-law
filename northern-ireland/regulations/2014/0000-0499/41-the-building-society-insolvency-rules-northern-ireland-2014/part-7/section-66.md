# The Building Society Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 66

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 66. Appeal against decision on proof

(1) Apply rule 4.089 of the 1991 Rules.

(2) For paragraph (7) substitute—

> (7) The building society liquidator is not personally liable for costs incurred by any person in respect of an application under this rule unless the court makes an order to that effect.
