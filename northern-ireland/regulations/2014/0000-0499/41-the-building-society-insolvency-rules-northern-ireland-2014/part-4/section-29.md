# The Building Society Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 29

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 29. Limited disclosure

(1) Apply rule 4.039 of the 1991 Rules.

(2) In paragraph (1), for “official receiver”, substitute “building society liquidator”.

(3) After paragraph (1), insert—

> (1A) The building society liquidator may also apply to the court for an order of limited disclosure in respect of those depositors of the society who, at the time of the making of the statement of affairs, still have a claim against the society in respect of their deposits.
