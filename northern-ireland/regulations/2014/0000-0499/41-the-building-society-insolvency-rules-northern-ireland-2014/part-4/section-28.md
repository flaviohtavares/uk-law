# The Building Society Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 28

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 28. Verification and filing

(1) Apply rule 4.036 of the 1991 Rules.

(2) For “the official receiver” substitute “the building society liquidator”.

(3) For paragraph (6), substitute—

> (6) The building society liquidator shall file the statement of affairs in court and shall send a copy of it to the appropriate regulator.

.

(4) Omit paragraph (7).
