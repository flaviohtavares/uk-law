# The Building Society Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 31

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 31. Expenses of statement of affairs

(1) Apply rule 4.041 of the 1991 Rules[^f00015].

(2) For “official receiver”, substitute “building society liquidator”.

## Annotations

[^f00015]: Footnote: Rule 4.041 was amended by [S.R. 2008 No. 118](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2008/118).
