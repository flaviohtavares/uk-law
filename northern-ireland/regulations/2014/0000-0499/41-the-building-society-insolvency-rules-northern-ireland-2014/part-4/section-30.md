# The Building Society Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 30

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 30. Release from duty to submit statement of affairs; extension of time

(1) Apply rule 4.040 of the 1991 Rules[^f00014].

(2) For “official receiver” substitute “building society liquidator.”
