# The Building Society Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 222

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 222. Restriction on concurrent proceedings and remedies

Where in a building society insolvency, the court makes an order staying any action, execution or legal process against the property of the society, service of the order may be effected by delivering a sealed copy to the address for service of the plaintiff or other person having the carriage of the proceedings to be stayed.
