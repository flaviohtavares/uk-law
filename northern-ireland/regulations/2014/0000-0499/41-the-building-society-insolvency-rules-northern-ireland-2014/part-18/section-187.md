# The Building Society Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 187

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 187. Notice of application under Article 150A(5)

(1) Apply rule 7.08A[^f00023] of the 1991 Rules.

(2) Omit from “save that notice” to “State liquidator”.

## Annotations

[^f00023]: Footnote: Rule 7.08A was inserted by [S.R. 2006 No. 47](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/47).
