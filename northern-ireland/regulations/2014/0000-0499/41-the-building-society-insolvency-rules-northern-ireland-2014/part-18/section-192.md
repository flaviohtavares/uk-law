# The Building Society Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 192

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 192. Use of reports

(1) Unless the application involves other parties, or the court orders otherwise, a report may be filed in court instead of an affidavit by—

- (a) the building society liquidator,
- (b) the provisional building society liquidator, or
- (c) the special manager.

(2) In any case where a report is filed instead of an affidavit, the report shall be treated for the purposes of rule 191 and any hearing before the court as if it were an affidavit.
