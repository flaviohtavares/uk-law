# The Building Society Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 197

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 197. Orders enforcing compliance with the Rules

(1) The court may on application of the building society liquidator, or the provisional building society liquidator as the case may be, make such orders as it thinks necessary for the enforcement of obligations falling on any person in accordance with—

- (a) Article 121(2) of the 1989 Order (“General functions in winding up by the High Court”); or
- (b) Article 199 of the 1989 Order (“Duty to cooperate with office holder”).

(2) An order of the court under this rule may provide that all the costs of and incidental to the application for it shall be borne by the person against whom the order is made.
