# The Building Society Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 185

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 185. Application under Article 150A(5) to disapply Article 150A

(1) Apply rule 7.07A[^f00022] of the 1991 Rules.

(2) In paragraph (1), omit “administrator or receiver”.

(3) Omit paragraph (2)(a).

## Annotations

[^f00022]: Footnote: Rule 7.07A was inserted by [S.R. 2006 No. 47](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/47).
