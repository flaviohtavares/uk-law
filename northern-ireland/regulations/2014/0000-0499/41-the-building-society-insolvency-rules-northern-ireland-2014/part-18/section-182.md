# The Building Society Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 182

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 182. Preliminary

This Part applies to any application made to the court under Part 2 of the Banking Act or these Rules except an application under section 95 of the Banking Act for a building society insolvency order.
