# The Building Society Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 200

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 200. Title of proceedings

Every proceeding under Part 2 of the Banking Act shall, with any necessary additions, be titled “IN THE MATTER OF ………. (naming the building society to which the proceedings relate) AND IN THE MATTER OF THE BANKING ACT 2009”.
