# The Building Society Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 198

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 198. Warrants (general provisions)

(1) A warrant issued by the court under any provision of the 1989 Order shall be addressed to such officer of the court as the warrant specifies, or to any constable.

(2) In this Chapter references to property include books, papers and records.
