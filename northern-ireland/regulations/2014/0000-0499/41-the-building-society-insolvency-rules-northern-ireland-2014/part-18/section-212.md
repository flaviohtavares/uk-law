# The Building Society Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 212

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 212. Certificate of taxation

Order 62, rule 22 of the Rules of the Court of Judicature (Northern Ireland) 1980[^f00025] applies in respect of the certification of such measurement as it applies in respect of the issue of a certificate of taxation, with the necessary modifications.
