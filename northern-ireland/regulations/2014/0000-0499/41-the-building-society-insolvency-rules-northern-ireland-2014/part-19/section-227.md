# The Building Society Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 227

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 227. Definition of “proxy”

(1) Apply rule 8.1 of the 1991 Rules.

(2) In paragraph (2) omit “, company”.

(3) In paragraph (4), for “chairman of the meeting” to “official receiver” substitute: “chair of the meeting or the building society liquidator”.
