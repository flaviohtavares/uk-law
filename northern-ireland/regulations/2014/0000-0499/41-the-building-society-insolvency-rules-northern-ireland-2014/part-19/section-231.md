# The Building Society Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 231

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 231. Right of inspection

(1) Apply rule 8.5 of the 1991 Rules.

(2) In paragraph (1)(b) for “a company’s members or” substitute “the building society’s” and for “company or of its” substitute “building society’s”.

(3) For paragraph (2) substitute—

> (2) The reference in paragraph (1) to creditors is to those creditors who have proved their debts but does not include a person whose proof has been wholly rejected for purposes of voting, dividend or otherwise.

.

(4) Omit paragraph (3)(b).
