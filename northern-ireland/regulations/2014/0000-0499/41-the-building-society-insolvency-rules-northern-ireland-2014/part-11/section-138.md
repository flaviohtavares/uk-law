# The Building Society Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 138

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 138. General qualification on powers

In exercising any power conferred on the building society liquidator by this Part before a full payment resolution has been passed, the building society liquidator shall exercise it consistently with Objective 1.
