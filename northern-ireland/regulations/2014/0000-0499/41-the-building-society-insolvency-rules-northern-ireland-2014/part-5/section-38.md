# The Building Society Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 38

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 38. General rule as to reporting

(1) Apply rule 4.051 of the 1991 Rules.

(2) In paragraph (1), for “official receiver” substitute “building society liquidator”.
