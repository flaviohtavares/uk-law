# The Building Society Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 36

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 36. Report where statement of affairs lodged

(1) Apply rule 4.049 of the 1991 Rules.

(2) For “official receiver”, substitute “building society liquidator”.
