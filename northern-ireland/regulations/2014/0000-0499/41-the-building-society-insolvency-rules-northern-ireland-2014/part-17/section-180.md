# The Building Society Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 180

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 180. Appropriate regulator’s directions under section 116 of the Banking Act

Where the appropriate regulator gives a direction under section 116 of the Banking Act (application by an interested person for postponement of dissolution) the appropriate regulator shall send a copy of the direction to that applicant[^f00021].

## Annotations

[^f00021]: Footnote: The Building Societies (Insolvency and Special Administration) Order 2009 ([S.I. 2009/805](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/805)) modifies the application of section 116.
