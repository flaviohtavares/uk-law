# The Building Society Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 43

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 43. General power to call meetings

(1) Apply rule 4.061 of the 1991 Rules.

(2) Where the building society liquidator has been directed to summon a meeting of creditors under Article 164 of the 1989 Order (as applied by section 109 of the Banking Act) for the purpose of removing the building society liquidator, the building society liquidator shall give notice of the meeting to the Bank of England and the appropriate regulator.
