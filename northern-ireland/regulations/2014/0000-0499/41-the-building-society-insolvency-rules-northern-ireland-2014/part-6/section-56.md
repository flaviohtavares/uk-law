# The Building Society Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 56

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 56. Admission and rejection of proof (creditors’ meetings)

(1) Apply rule 4.076 of the 1991 Rules.

(2) For paragraph (6) substitute—

> (6) The chair is not personally liable for costs incurred by any person in respect of an application under this rule unless the court makes an order to that effect.
