# The Building Society Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 11. Verification of application

(1) This rule applies where an application has been filed at the court under rule 7.

(2) An affidavit shall be attached to the application to state that the statements in the application are true, or are true to the best of the applicant’s knowledge, information and belief.

(3) The affidavit shall identify the person making the statement and shall include the capacity in which that person makes the statement and the basis for that person’s knowledge of the matters set out in the application.

(4) The affidavit is, unless proved otherwise, evidence of the statements in the application.
