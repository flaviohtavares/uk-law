# The Building Society Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10. Other persons to receive copies of application

(1) The applicant shall send two copies of the application to—

- (a) the proposed building society liquidator;
- (b) the Bank of England (if it is not the applicant);
- (c) the appropriate regulator (if it is not the applicant);
- (d) the FSCS;
- (e) any person who has given notice to the appropriate regulator in respect of the society under section 90D of the Building Societies Act[^f00011];
- (f) if there is in force for the society a voluntary arrangement under Part II of the 1989 Order, the supervisor of that arrangement; and
- (g) if an administrative receiver has been appointed in relation to the society, that receiver;

in accordance with paragraph (2).

(2) One copy shall be sent electronically as soon as practicable and the other (a sealed copy) shall be sent by first class post on the business day on which the application is served on the society.

(3) Any of the persons in sub-paragraph (1) will have the right to attend and be heard at the hearing of the application.
