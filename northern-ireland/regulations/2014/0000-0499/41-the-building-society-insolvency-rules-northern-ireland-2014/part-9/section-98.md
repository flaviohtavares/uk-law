# The Building Society Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 98

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 98. Rule as to reporting

(1) Apply rule 4.132A[^f00018] of the 1991 Rules.

(2) In paragraph (1) for “rule 4.131 or 4.132” substitute “rule 97 of the 2014 Rules”.

## Annotations

[^f00018]: Footnote: Rule 4.132A was inserted by [S.R. 2006 No. 47](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/47).
