# The Building Society Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 101

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 101. Building society liquidator’s remuneration where he realises assets on behalf of chargeholder

(1) Apply rule 4.134B of the 1991 Rules.

(2) In paragraph (1), for the words from “liquidator” to “and” substitute “building society liquidator”.

(3) In paragraphs (2) and (3), after “Schedule 4” add “to the 1991 Rules as applied by the Schedule to the 2014 Rules”.
