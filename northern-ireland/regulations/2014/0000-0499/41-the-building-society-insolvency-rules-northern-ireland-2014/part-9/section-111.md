# The Building Society Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 111

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 111. Notice to Bank of England of intention to vacate office

(1) This rule applies where the building society liquidator was appointed by a meeting of creditors.

(2) Apply rule 4.144 of the 1991 Rules.

(3) For “official receiver”, wherever it appears, substitute “Bank of England”.

(4) In paragraph (1) for “receive his resignation” substitute “be notified of his resignation”.
