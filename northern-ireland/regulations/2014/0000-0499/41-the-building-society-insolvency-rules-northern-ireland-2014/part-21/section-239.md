# The Building Society Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 239

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 239. Preliminary

(1) The rules in this Part relate to the declaration and payment of dividends in a building society insolvency.

(2) In this Part—

- “**creditors**” means those creditors of the society of whom the building society liquidator is aware, or who are identified in the society’s statement of affairs, and
- “**the insolvent**” means the building society.
