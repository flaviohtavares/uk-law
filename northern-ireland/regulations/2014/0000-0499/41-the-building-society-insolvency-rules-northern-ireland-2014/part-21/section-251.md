# The Building Society Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 251

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 251. Debt payable at future time

(1) Apply rule 11.13 of the 1991 Rules.

(2) For paragraph (3) substitute—

> (3) In paragraph (2) “**relevant date**” means the date of the commencement of the building society insolvency.
