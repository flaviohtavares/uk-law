# The Building Society Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 279

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 279. “Insolvent estate”

References to “the insolvent estate” are to the assets of the building society.
