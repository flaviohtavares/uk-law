# The Building Society Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 275

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 275. Expressions used generally

(1) Apply rule 0.2 of the 1991 Rules.

(2) For the definition “insolvency proceedings” after “Order” insert “, the Banking Act 2009”.
