# The Building Society Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 278

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 278. Notice to joint building society liquidators

Where two or more persons are acting jointly as the building society liquidator, delivery of a document to one of them is to be treated as delivery to them all.
