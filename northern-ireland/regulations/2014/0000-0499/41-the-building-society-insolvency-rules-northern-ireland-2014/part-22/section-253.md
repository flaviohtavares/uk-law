# The Building Society Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 253

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 253. Costs, expenses, etc

(1) All fees, costs, charges and other expenses incurred in the course of building society insolvency, except for any money paid by the FSCS to eligible depositors in pursuance of Objective 1, and any expense incurred by the FSCS in this process, are to be regarded as expenses of the building society insolvency.

(2) The costs associated with the prescribed part (within the meaning of Article 150A of the 1989 Order) shall be paid out of that prescribed part.
