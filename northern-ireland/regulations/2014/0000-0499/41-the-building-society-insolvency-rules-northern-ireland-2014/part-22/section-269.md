# The Building Society Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 269

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 269. Right to have list of creditors

(1) Where one or more creditors have the right under these Rules to inspect documents on the court file, they may require the building society liquidator to send them a list of the society’s creditors and the amounts of their respective debts.

(2) Paragraph (1) does not apply if a statement of the society’s affairs has been filed in court or filed with the appropriate regulator.

(3) The building society liquidator must respond to the request in paragraph (1) but may charge the appropriate fee for doing so.
