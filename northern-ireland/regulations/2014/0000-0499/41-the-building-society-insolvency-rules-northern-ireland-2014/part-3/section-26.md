# The Building Society Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 26

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 26. Termination of appointment

(1) Apply rule 4.034 of the 1991 Rules.

(2) After paragraph (2) insert—

> (3) On the making of a building society insolvency order the appointment of the provisional building society liquidator shall terminate.
