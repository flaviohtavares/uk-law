# The Building Society Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 2014, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Application of Rules

These Rules apply in relation to a building society undergoing the procedure in Part 2 of the Banking Act 2009[^f00003], as applied and modified by section 90C of the Building Societies Act 1986[^f00004] and by any order made under section 130 of the Banking Act, known as building society insolvency.
