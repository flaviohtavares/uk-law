# The Police Act 1997 (Criminal Record Certificates: Relevant Matters) (Amendment No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 207

Criminal Law

The Police Act 1997 (Criminal Record Certificates: Relevant Matters) (Amendment No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2014

Laid before the Assembly in draft

Made

1st July 2014

Coming into operation

8th July 2014

The Department of Justice makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 113A(7)[^f00001] of the Police Act 1997 as modified by section 126A(1) and (8)[^f00002]of that Act.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

This Order may be cited as the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Record Certificates: Relevant Matters) (Amendment No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2014, and shall come into operation on 8<sup>th</sup> July 2014.

## 2. Amendment to the Police Act 1997

In section 113A(6E)(b)(ii) of the Police Act 1997[^f00003] for “aged 18 or under” substitute “aged under 18”.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Justice on 1<sup>st</sup> July 2014

David Ford

Minister of Justice

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends an error in section 113A(6E)(b)(ii) of the Police Act 1997 in relation to the reference to a person aged under 18 years of age. It corrects the existing reference of “aged 18 or under” so that the substituted reference of “aged under 18” reads consistently with the words “aged under 18” in paragraphs (c)(ii) and (d).

This Order comes into operation on 8th July 2014.
