# Marketing of Potatoes (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 223

Agriculture

Marketing of Potatoes (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011

Made

*15*th June 2011

Coming into operation

*1s*t September 2011

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development [^f00001], with the approval of the Department of Finance and Personnel [^f00002], makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 3(3), 7(2) and 11(1) of the Marketing of Potatoes Act (Northern Ireland) 1964[^f00003].

## 1. Citation and Commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Marketing of Potatoes (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011 and shall come into operation on 1st September 2011.

## 2. Amendment of the Marketing of Potatoes Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1989

The Marketing of Potatoes Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1989[^f00004] shall be amended as follows—

- (a) in paragraph (1) of regulation 7 (Fees for the inspection of ware potatoes sent out of Northern Ireland) for “£3.90” there shall be substituted “£4.30”,
- (b) in regulation 8 (Fees for the examination of ware potatoes for sale in Northern Ireland) for “£3.90”, there shall be substituted “£4.30”.

## 3. Revocation

The Marketing of Potatoes (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005[^f00005] are hereby revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke and replace the Marketing of Potatoes (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005.

The Regulations increase the fee payable for the inspection or examination of ware potatoes from £3.90 per tonne or part of a tonne to £4.30 per tonne or part of a tonne.
