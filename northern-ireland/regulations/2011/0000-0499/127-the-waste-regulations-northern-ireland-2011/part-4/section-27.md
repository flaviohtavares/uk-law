# The Waste Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011, section 27

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 27. Amendment to the Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999[^c-8ca115b6fe9df9bdb9d36a2d77798583]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999[^f00013] shall be amended as follows.

(2) In Schedule 1—

- (a) for paragraph 9 substitute the following—

  > **9.** Waste disposal installations for the incineration, chemical treatment (as defined in Annex I to Directive [2008/98/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0098) or landfill of hazardous waste (as defined in regulation 6 of the Hazardous Waste Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005.

  .
- (b) in paragraph 10 for “Annex IIA to Directive [75/442/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1975/0442)” substitute “Annex I to Directive [2008/98/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0098)”.
