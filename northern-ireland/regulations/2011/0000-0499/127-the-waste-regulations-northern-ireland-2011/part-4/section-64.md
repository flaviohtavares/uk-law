# The Waste Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011, section 64

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 64. Amendment to the List of Wastes Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005[^key-ee3a6098afef13c75c30890050b46f28]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The List of Wastes Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005[^f00058] shall be amended in accordance with regulation 64(2) to 65.

(2) In regulation 2—

- (a) in paragraph (a) for the definition of “the Waste Directive” substitute—

  > - the Waste Directive” means Directive [2008/98/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0098) of the European Parliament and of the Council on waste

  .
- (b) omit paragraph (c);
- (c) in paragraph (d) for the definition of “the List of Waste Decision” substitute—

  > - the List of Waste Decision” means Commission Decision [2000/532/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2000/0532) of 3<sup>rd</sup> May 2000 replacing Decision [94/3/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1994/0003) establishing a list of wastes pursuant to Article 1(a) of Council Directive EEC/75/442 on waste as replaced by Article 7 of Directive [2008/98/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0098) of the European Parliament and of the Council on waste

  .
- (d) in paragraph (e) for the definition of “the List of Wastes” substitute—

  > - (e) the List of Wastes” means the “List of Wastes pursuant to Article 7 of Directive [2008/98/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0098) of the European Parliament and of the Council on waste,” set out in the Annex to the List of Wastes Decision. The list of waste shall include hazardous waste and—
  >   - (i) in relation to hazardous waste, in accordance with the regulatory procedure laid down in Article 7 of Directive [2008/98/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0098) on the basis of—
  >     - (aa) Annex III (properties of waste which render it hazardous) to that Directive,
  >
  > - and a reference to the List of Wastes includes a reference to the Introduction thereto (“the Introduction to the List”).

  .

(3) In regulation 4 for paragraph (a) substitute—

> - (a) one of more of the properties listed in Annex III to Directive [2008/98/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0098) of the European Parliament and of the Council on waste.

.

**65.**[^key-10dc570527d10e0e6c07df78a629a8ac] In the Schedule—

(1) In paragraph 1, for “Article 18 of Directive [75/442/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1975/0442)” substitute “Article 39 of Directive [2008/98/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0098)” and for “Article 1(a) of Directive [75/442/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1975/0442)” substitute “Article 3(1) of Directive [2008/98/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0098).”.

(2) For paragraph 2, substitute “Wastes included in the list are subject to the provisions of Directive [2008/98/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0098) except where Article 2(1) and (2) applies.”.

(3) For paragraph 4, substitute “Any waste marked with an asterisk (\*) is considered as hazardous waste pursuant to Directive [2008/98/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0098) on waste, and subject to the provisions of that Directive unless Article 20 of that Directive applies.”.

(4) In paragraph 6, for “Council Directive [91/89/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0089)” substitute “Council Directive [2008/98/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0098)”.
