# The Waste Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011, section 70

> Text as at 2025-02-27.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 70. Amendment of the Environmental Liability (Prevention and Remediation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009[^c-975524a2f4aa2d6273d7d33b69c5f81b]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Environmental Liability (Prevention and Remediation) Regulations (Northern Ireland 2009[^f00063] shall be amended as follows.

(2) Amendment of Schedule 2—

- (a) In paragraph 2(1) for the words from “Directive [2006/12/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0012)” to the end, substitute “Directive [2008/98/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0098) of the European Parliament and of the Council on waste.”

(3) Amendment of Schedule 3—

- (a) In paragraph 2(1) for the words from “Directive [2006/12/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0012)” to the end, substitute “Directive [2008/98/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0098) of the European Parliament and of the Council on waste.”
- (b) In paragraph 2(2) for the words from “Directive [2006/12/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0012)” to the end, substitute “Directive [2008/98/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0098) of the European Parliament and of the Council on waste.”
