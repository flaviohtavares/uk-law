## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation[^c-0155a9dbac4b1b2175f2b4a01d84f39f]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Waste Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011 and except as provided in paragraphs (2), (3) and (4) shall come into operation on 8th April 2011.

(2) Regulation 17 shall come into operation on 8th October 2011.

(3) Regulation 18 shall come into operation on 1st January 2015.
