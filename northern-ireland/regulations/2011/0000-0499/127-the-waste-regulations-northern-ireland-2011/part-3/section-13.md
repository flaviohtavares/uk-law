## 13. Review and modification of waste prevention programmes[^key-f03fd69fa9cafb1b451b6b1610dd710c]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department [^key-420c14b4b578dc65b9c27be2d4d2495e]...—

- (a) shall review each waste prevention programme at least every sixth year; and
- (b) may from time to time modify the waste prevention programmes in accordance with Article 30 of the Waste Framework Directive.
