## 20. Duty in relation to collected waste by private operators[^c-b352c721935454de1039d97469668f9f]

*Extent: N.I.*

The duties under regulations 18 and 19 shall apply equally to a person required to be registered as a carrier of controlled waste for the purposes of the Controlled Waste (Registration of Carriers and Seizure of Vehicles) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999[^f00006] as they apply to district councils.
