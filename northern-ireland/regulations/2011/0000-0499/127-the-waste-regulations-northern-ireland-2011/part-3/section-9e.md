# The Waste Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011, section 9E

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 9E. Modification of Directive [2000/60/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2000/60) of the European Parliament and of the Council[^c-d0abcf56d4bb11c7ff3bed246d3cdaab]

*Extent: N.I.*

For the purposes of regulation 9C(3)(a), Article 11(3)(j) of Directive [2000/60/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2000/60) of the European Parliament and of the Council is to be read as if—

(1) the reference to “Member States” were a reference to the appropriate authority or local authority which, immediately before IP completion day, was able to exercise the discretion described in the Article in respect of Northern Ireland; and

(2) in the words after the sixth indent, “**environmental objectives**” means—

“in relation to a river basin district in Northern Ireland, the objectives set under regulation 12, in accordance with regulation 13, of the Water Environment (Water Framework Directive) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2017;
