# The Rate Relief (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 43

Rates

The Rate Relief (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011

Made

18th February 2011

Coming into operation

1st April 2011

The Department of Finance and Personnel makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 30A of the Rates (Northern Ireland) Order 1977[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Rate Relief (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011 and shall come into operation on 1st April 2011.

> - (lza) as if the definition of “equity-sharing lease” was omitted;

.

(3) In regulation 17(2)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (f)(iii) for “.” substitute “;”;
- (b) after sub-paragraph (f) insert—

>   - (i) in sub-paragraph (2) after “Schedule 6”;
>   - (ii) in sub-paragraph (4) after “Schedule 5”;

(7) In regulation 33(j) for “regulations 102 to 104” substitute “regulation 101A, and regulations 102 to 104”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Rate Relief (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 and the Rate Relief (Qualifying Age) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 which apply with modifications the Housing Benefit Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006 and the Housing Benefit (Persons who have attained the qualifying age for state pension credit) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006 (“the Housing Benefit Regulations”) for the purposes of the low income rate relief scheme.

Regulation 2 at paragraph (2) and paragraphs (4) to (7) makes minor amendments to the Rate Relief (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 to take account of amendments to the Housing Benefit Regulations.

Regulation 3 at paragraphs (2), (4) and (5) makes minor amendments to the Rate Relief (Qualifying Age) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 to take account of amendments to the Housing Benefit Regulations.

Regulation 2(3) amends the Rate Relief (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 by uplifting the amount of carer premium used in the calculation of low income rate relief. Regulation 3(3) makes an equivalent amendment to the Rate Relief (Qualifying Age) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007.
