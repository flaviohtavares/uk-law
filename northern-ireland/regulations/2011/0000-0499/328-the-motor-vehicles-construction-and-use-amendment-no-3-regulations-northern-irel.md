# The Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) (Amendment No. 3) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 328

Road Traffic And Vehicles

The Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) (Amendment No. 3) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011

Made

22nd September 2011

Coming into operation

21st October 2011

The Department of the Environment makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Articles 55(1) and (2) and 110(2) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1995[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) (Amendment No. 3) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011 and shall come into operation on 21<sup>st</sup> October 2011.

## 2. Amendment to the Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999

For regulation 44(5)(g) of the Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999[^f00002] substitute—

> - (g) used as a search and rescue vehicle on the authority of the Chief Constable on search and rescue operations;

.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 22<sup>nd</sup> September 2011

Deirdre Kenny

A senior officer of the

Department of the Environment

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations further amend the Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999 which govern the construction, equipment, maintenance and use of motor vehicles.

Regulation 44 (audible warning instruments) is amended to permit the fitting of a bell, gong, siren or two-tone horn to a motor vehicle which is used for search and rescue operations.
