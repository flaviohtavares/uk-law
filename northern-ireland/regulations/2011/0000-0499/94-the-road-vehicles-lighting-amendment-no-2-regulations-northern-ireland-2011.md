# The Road Vehicles Lighting (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Rule has been made to correct a drafting error in [S.R. 2011 No. 49](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2011/49) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Rule.

2011 No. 94

Road Traffic And Vehicles

The Road Vehicles Lighting (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011

Made

11th March 2011

Coming into operation

24th March 2011

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 55(1), (2), (4) and (6) and 110(2) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1995[^f00001], makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Vehicles Lighting (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011 and shall come into operation on 24<sup>th</sup> March 2011.

## 2. Amendment

In regulation 17A(1) and (2) of the Road Vehicles Lighting Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000[^f00002] as set out in regulation 4 of the Road Vehicles Lighting (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011[^f00003] for “1<sup>st</sup> June 2010” substitute “1<sup>st</sup> June 2011”.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 11<sup>th</sup> March 2011

Deirdre Kenny

A senior officer of the

Department of the Environment

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations correct an error in regulation 4 of the Road Vehicles Lighting (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011. In new regulation 17A of the Road Vehicles Lighting Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000 as set out in the said regulation 4 the date 1<sup>st</sup> June 2010 is amended to 1<sup>st</sup> June 2011.
