# The Supervision and Treatment Orders (Maximum Period) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 115

Mental Health

The Supervision and Treatment Orders (Maximum Period) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011

Made

16th March 2011

Coming into operation

30th June 2011

The Department of Justice makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 1(2) and (3) of Schedule 2A to the Mental Health (Northern Ireland) Order 1986 [^f00001] and now invested in it [^f00002].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Supervision and Treatment Orders (Maximum Period) (Northern Ireland) Order 2011 and shall come into operation on 30th June 2011.

## 2. Amendment to the Mental Health (Northern Ireland) Order 1986

(1) Schedule 2A to the Mental Health (Northern Ireland) Order 1986 (supervision and treatment orders) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1(1)(a) for “2 years” substitute “3 years”.

(3) In paragraph 8(2) for “2 years” substitute “3 years”.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Justice on 16th March 2011

(L.S.)

David Ford

Minister of Justice

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends paragraph 1(1)(a) of Schedule 2A to the Mental Health (Northern Ireland) Order 1986 (“the 1986 Order”) to increase, from 2 years to 3 years, the maximum period for supervision and treatment orders. It also makes a related amendment to paragraph 8(2) of Schedule 2A to the 1986 Order.
