# The Aquatic Animal Health (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 118

Aquatic Animal Health

The Aquatic Animal Health (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011

Made

18th March 2011

Coming into operation

1st May 2011

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development is a department designated [^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Union.

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Aquatic Animal Health (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011 and come into operation on 1<sup>st</sup> May 2011.

## 2. Amendment of Aquatic Animal Health Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009

(1) The Aquatic Animal Health Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009[^f00003] are amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

(2) In Schedule 1 (list of diseases) after the entry relating to Spring viraemia of carp add—

> Ostreid herpesvirus 1 µvar       Pacific oysters (*Crassostrea gigas).*

.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 18th March 2011

Liam McKibben

A senior officer of the

Department of Agriculture and Rural Development

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Aquatic Animal Health Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009 by adding Ostreid herpesvirus 1µvar (OsHV1- µvar) to Schedule 1 List of Diseases.
