# The Civil Registration (2011 Act) (Commencement No1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 323 (C. 24)

Civil Registration

The Civil Registration (2011 Act) (Commencement No1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011

Made

*15th September 2011*

The Department of Finance and Personnel makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 34 of the Civil Registration Act (Northern Ireland) 2011 [^f00001]:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Civil Registration (2011 Act) (Commencement No.1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011.

## 2. Appointed day

The following provisions of the Civil Registration Act (Northern Ireland) 2011 shall come into operation on 3rd October 2011 -

- (a) section 1(1), (2), (3)(a) and (5) (registration of births);
- (b) section 5 (time limit on registration of still-births);
- (c) section 6 (registration of still-births where parents not married);
- (d) section 9(1), (2), (3), (4)(a) and (6) (registration of deaths);
- (e) section 12 (discharge of functions of the Registrar General); and
- (f) section 18 (issue of short birth certificate).

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance and Personnel on 15th September 2011.

TN Caven

A senior officer of the

Department of Finance and Personnel

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into operation on 3rd October 2011 the following provisions of the Civil Registration Act (Northern Ireland) 2011-

- (a) section 1(1) to (3)(a) and (5) (registration of births);
- (b) section 5 (time limit on registration of still-births);
- (c) section 6 (registration of still-births where parents not married);
- (d) section 9(1) to (4)(a) and (6) (registration of deaths);
- (e) section 12 (discharge of functions of the Registrar General); and
- (f) section 18 (issue of short birth certificate).
