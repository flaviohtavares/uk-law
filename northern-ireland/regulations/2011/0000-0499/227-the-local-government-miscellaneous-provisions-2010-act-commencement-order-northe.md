# The Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) (2010 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 227 (C. 15)

Local Government

The Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) (2010 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011

Made

15th June 2011

The Department of the Environment makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 26(2) of the Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act (Northern Ireland) 2010[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) (2010 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011.

## 2. Appointed day

In so far as not already in operation, the day appointed for the coming into operation of Part 1 (contracts of councils) of the Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act (Northern Ireland) 2010 shall be 1st August 2011.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 15th June 2011.

Wesley Shannon

A senior officer of the Department of the Environment

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the coming into operation of Part 1 (contracts of councils) of the Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act (Northern Ireland) 2010. Section 3 of Part 1 (insofar as it enabled the Department to make regulations), Part 2 and Part 3 came into operation on the day after the day on which the Act received Royal Assent.
