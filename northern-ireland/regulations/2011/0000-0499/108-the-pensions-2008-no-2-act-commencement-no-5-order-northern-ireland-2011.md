# The Pensions (2008 No. 2 Act) (Commencement No. 5) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 108 (C. 7)

Pensions

The Pensions (2008 No. 2 Act) (Commencement No. 5) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011

Made

15th March 2011

The Department for Social Development makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 118(1) of the Pensions (No. 2) Act (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the [Pensions (2008 No. 2 Act) (Commencement No. 5) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2011/108).

(2) In this Order “the Act” means the Pensions (No. 2) Act (Northern Ireland) 2008.

## 2. Commencement of provisions

(1) The following provisions of the Act shall come into operation on 16th March 2011—

- (a) section 63 (objectives of the Regulator); and
- (b) section 99 (charges in respect of pension sharing etc.) for the purpose only of conferring the power to make regulations.

(2) The provisions of the Act specified in the Schedule shall come into operation, in so far as they are not already in operation, on 6th April 2011.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 15th March 2011

(L.S.)

Anne McCleary

A senior officer of the Department for Social Development

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into operation section 63 of the Pensions (No. 2) Act (Northern Ireland) 2008 (“the Act”) on 16th March 2011. Section 63 amends Article 4(1) of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 2005 ([S.I. 2005/255 (N.I. 1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2005/255)) to provide a new statutory objective for the Pensions Regulator.

The Order brings section 99 of the Act into operation on 16th March 2011, for the purpose only of conferring power to make regulations. It also brings sections 86 to 99 and 101 of, and Schedule 4 (except paragraph 11), Schedule 5 and Part 4 of Schedule 10 to, the Act into operation, in so far as they are not already in operation, on 6th April 2011. Those sections and Schedules make provision for pension compensation to be shared on divorce, dissolution of a civil partnership or in cases of nullity, for attachment orders to be made on divorce etc. in relation to pension compensation and for the Board of the Pension Protection Fund to recover charges in respect of pension sharing etc.
