# The Dogs (Amendment) (2011 Act) (Commencement No.1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 281 (C. 19)

Animals

The Dogs (Amendment) (2011 Act) (Commencement No.1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011

Made

21st July 2011

Coming into operation

28th July 2011

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development, makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Section 18 of the Dogs (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2011[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Dogs (Amendment) (2011 Act) (Commencement No.1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011.

(2) In this Order “the Act” means the Dogs (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2011.

## 2. Appointed Day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the Act listed in the Schedule is 28<sup>th</sup> July 2011.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 21<sup>st</sup> July 2011.

C McMaster

A senior officer of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development
