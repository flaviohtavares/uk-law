# Pensions Increase (Review) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 129

Pensions

Pensions Increase (Review) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011

Made

21st March 2011

Coming into operation

11th April 2011

In accordance with section 132 of the Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992[^f00001] the Department for Social Development[^f00002] has given a direction[^f00003] that the sums which are the additional pensions in long-term benefits are to be increased by a specified percentage.

The Department of Finance and Personnel now makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 69(1), (2), (5) and (5ZA) of the Social Security Pensions (Northern Ireland Order 1975[^f00004] and now vested in it[^f00005].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Pensions Increase (Review) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011 and shall come into operation on 11th April 2011.

## 2. Interpretation

(1) In this Order—

- “the 1971 Act” means the Pensions (Increase) Act (Northern Ireland) 1971[^f00006];
- “the 1975 Order” means the Social Security Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1975;
- “basic rate” and “derivative pension” have the meanings given by section 15(1) of the 1971 Act [^f00007];
- “existing Orders” means the Orders referred to in the Schedule;
- “official pension” has the meaning given by section 5(1) of the 1971 Act;
- “pension authority” has the meaning given by section7(1) of the 1971 Act;
- “qualifying condition” means one of the conditions laid down in section 3 the 1971 Act[^f00008];
- “relevant injury pension”, “substituted pension” and “widow’s pension” have the meanings given by section 15(1) of the 1971 Act.

(2) For the purpose of this Order the time when a pension “begins” is that stated in section 8(2) of the 1971 Act[^f00009], and the “beginning date” shall be construed accordingly.

(3) Where, for the purposes of this Order, it is necessary to calculate the number of complete months in any period an incomplete month shall be treated as a complete month if it consists of at least 16 days.

## 3. Increases in certain pensions

(1) The pension authority may, if any of the conditions in paragraph (2) are fulfilled, increase the annual rate of an official pension in respect of any period on or after 11th April 2011 as follows—

- (a) a pension beginning before 12th April 2010 may be increased by 3.1 per cent of the basic rate (as increased by the amount of any increase under section 1 of the 1971 Act or the existing Orders);
- (b) a pension beginning on or after 12th April 2010 and before 11th April 2010 and before 11th April 2011 may be increased by 3.1 per cent multiplied by—

  [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2011/129)

  where

  - *A* is the number of complete months in the period between the beginning date of the pension and 11th April 2011; and
  - *B* us 12.

(2) Those conditions are—

- (a) a qualifying condition is satisfied;
- (b) the official pension is—
  - (i) a derivative pension;
  - (ii) a substituted pension; or
  - (iii) a relevant injury pension.

## 4. Increase in certain lump sums

(1) This Article applies to any lump sum or instalment of a lump sum which became payable on or after 12th April 2010 but before 11th April 2011.

(2) The pension authority may increase a lump sum or instalment of a lump sum to which this Article applies by 3.1 per cent of the amount of the lump sum or instalment (as increased by the amount of any increase under section 1 of the 1971 Act or under the existing Orders) multiplied by—

[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2011/129)

where

- (a) *A* is the number of complete months in the period between the beginning date for the lump sum or, if later, 12th April 2010 and the date on which it became payable; and
- (b) *B* is 12.

## 5. Reduction in respect of guaranteed minimum pensions

Where—

- (a) a person is entitled to a guaranteed minimum pension on 11th April 2011; and
- (b) entitlement to that guaranteed minimum arises from an employment from which (either directly or by virtue of the payment of a transfer credit) entitlement to the official pension also arises

the amount by reference to which any increase in the rate of an official pension provided for by this Order is to be calculated shall be reduced by any amount equal to the rate of the guaranteed minimum pension unless the Department of Finance and Personnel[^f00010] in accordance with the provisions of Article 69A[^f00011] of the 1975 Order, otherwise directs.

**6.** The amount by reference to which any increase in the rate of a widow’s or widower’s pension provided for by this Order is to be calculated shall, where the pensioner becomes entitled on the death of the deceased spouse to a guaranteed minimum pension, be reduced in accordance with Article 69(5ZA)[^f00012] of the 1975 Order.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for increases in the rates of public service pensions.

The increase to be made in the rates of such pensions is the percentage (or in some circumstances a fraction of the percentage) by which the Department for Social Development has increased the sums which are the additional pensions in long-term benefits, namely the additional pension entitlements accruing to employees in respect of their earnings after 5th April 1978.

For pensions which began before 12th April 2010 the increase is 3.1%.

For pensions which began on or after 12th April 2010 the increases are as follows:—

| Pensions Beginning | Percentage Increase | Pensions Beginning | Percentage Increase |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 12th April 2010 to<br>26th April 2010 | 3.1% | 27th October 2010 to<br>26th November 2010 | 1.29% |
| 27th April 2010 to<br>26th May 2010 | 2.84% | 27th November 2010 to<br>26th December 2010 | 1.03% |
| 27th May 2010 to<br>26th June 2010 | 2.58% | 27th December 2010 to<br>26th January 2010 | 0.78% |
| 27th June 2010 to<br>26th July 2010 | 2.33% | 27th January 2010 to<br>26th February 2010 | 0.52% |
| 27th July 2010 to<br>26th August 2010 | 2.07% | 27th February 2010 to<br>26th March 2010 | 0.26% |
| 27th August 2010 to<br>26th September 2010 | 1.81% |  |  |
| 27th September 2010 to<br>26th October 2010 | 1.55% |  |  |
| 27th October 2010 to<br>26th November 2010 | 1.29% |  |  |
| 27th November 2010<br>26th December 2010 | 1.03% |  |  |

The Order provides for increases on certain deferred lump sums which became payable on or after 12th April 2010 and before 11th April 2011.

The Order also makes provision for the amount by reference to which any increase in the rate of an official pension is to be calculated to be reduced by an amount equal to the rate of the guaranteed minimum pension entitlement deriving from the employment which gives rise to the official pension.
