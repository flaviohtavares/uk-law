# The Foyle Area and Carlingford Area (Tagging and Logbook) (Amendment) Regulations 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 363

Fisheries

The Foyle Area and Carlingford Area (Tagging and Logbook) (Amendment) Regulations 2011

Made

14th October 2011

Coming into operation

1st December 2011

The Foyle, Carlingford and Irish Lights Commission, with the approval of the North/South Ministerial Council, makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 13(1) of the Foyle Fisheries Act 1952[^f00001] and section 13(1) of the Foyle Fisheries Act (Northern Ireland) 1952[^f00002] and now vested in it[^f00003].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Foyle Area and Carlingford Area (Tagging and Logbook) (Amendment) Regulations 2011 and shall come into operation on 1st December 2011.

## 2. Amendment of the Foyle Area and Carlingford Area (Tagging and Logbook) Regulations 2001

For regulation 5(1)(e) of the Foyle Area and Carlingford Area (Tagging and Logbook) Regulations 2001[^f00004] substitute—

> - (e) have in his possession any tag which has been forged or altered;

Given under the Seal of the Foyle, Carlingford and Irish Lights Commission on 14th October 2011.

R.J.D Anderson

Chief Executive of the

Loughs Agency

Approved by the North/South Ministerial Council on 14th October 2011.

A Barrington

Joint Secretary of the

North/South Ministerial Council

M Bunting

Joint Secretary of the North/South Ministerial Council

being persons authorised by the North/South Ministerial Council to signify approval on its behalf.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Foyle Area and Carlingford Area (Tagging and Logbook) Regulations 2001 by prohibiting the possession of forged or altered tags.
