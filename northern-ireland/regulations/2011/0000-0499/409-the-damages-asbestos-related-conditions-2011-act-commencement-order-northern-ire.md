# The Damages (Asbestos-related Conditions) (2011 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 409 (C. 30)

Damages

The Damages (Asbestos-related Conditions) (2011 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011

Made

5th December 2011

The Department of Finance and Personnel, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 4(1) of the Damages (Asbestos-related Conditions) Act (Northern Ireland) 2011[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Damages (Asbestos-related Conditions) (2011 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011.

## 2. Appointed Day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the Damages (Asbestos-related Conditions) Act (Northern Ireland) 2011 is 14<sup>th</sup> December 2011.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance and Personnel on 5th December 2011

O G Paulin

A senior officer of the Department of Finance and Personnel

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the Damages (Asbestos-related Conditions) Act (Northern Ireland) 2011 to come into operation on 14<sup>th</sup> December 2011.
