# The Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) (Offences) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 287

Road Traffic And Vehicles

The Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) (Offences) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011

Made

28th July 2011

Coming into operation

12th September 2011

The Department of the Environment makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 57(2) of the Road Traffic Offenders (Northern Ireland) Order 1996[^f00001].

In accordance with Article 91(1) of that Order the Department has consulted with such representative organisations as it thinks fit.

## 1. Citation and Commencement

This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) (Offences) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011 and shall come into operation on 12<sup>th</sup> September 2011.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) (Offences) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997 by specifying additional offences to be fixed penalty offences for the purposes of the Road Traffic Offenders (Northern Ireland) Order 1996. The offences relate to drivers’ hours, bus, coach and goods vehicles’ authorisations, certificates of professional competence and trade licences.

An Explanatory Memorandum and a Regulatory Impact Assessment has been produced and is available from Road Safety Division, Department of the Environment, Clarence Court, 10-18 Adelaide Street, Belfast BT2 8GB or viewed online at http;//www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr.
