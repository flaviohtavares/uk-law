# The Road Traffic (2007 Order) (Commencement No. 8) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 433 (C. 32)

Road Traffic And Vehicles

The Road Traffic (2007 Order) (Commencement No. 8) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011

Made

19th December 2011

The Department of the Environment makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 1(3) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 2007[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic (2007 Order) (Commencement No. 8) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011.

(2) In this Order “the Order” means the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 2007.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of Article 36 of the Order shall be 6<sup>th</sup> February 2012.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 19<sup>th</sup> December 2011

Deirdre Kenny

A senior officer of the Department of the Environment

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into operation Article 36 of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 2007.

Article 36 inserts a new Article 19I into the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981 so as to allow the Department of the Environment to grant access to driver licensing information held by it and to make regulations specifying the purposes for which that information may be used.
