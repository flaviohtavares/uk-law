# The County Court (Amendment No. 3) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 421

County Courts

The County Court (Amendment No. 3) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2011

Made

9th December 2011

Coming into operation

1st January 2012

The County Court Rules Committee makes the following Rules in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 47 of the County Court (Northern Ireland) Order 1980[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and Commencement

These Rules may be cited as the County Court (Amendment No. 3) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2011 and shall come into operation on 1st January 2012.

## 2. Amendment to the County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981

The County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981[^f00002] are amended as follows—

(1) in Order 32—

- (a) after rule 6E (Discharge or variation of a special measures direction), insert—

  > **6EA. Application to disapply or disapply in part the primary rule**
  >
  > (1) An application to disapply or disapply in part the primary rule under Article 9(4)(ba) of the 1999 Order shall be made in writing and shall include such information as the court requires to make a determination.
  >
  > (2) An application under paragraph (1) shall be served, by the applicant, on the chief clerk and on each party to the proceedings as soon as reasonably practicable after the witness has expressed that wish.
  >
  > (3) Paragraphs (6) to (12) of Rule 6B shall apply to an application to disapply or disapply in part the primary rule as they apply to an application for a direction.

  ; and
- (b) in rule 6G, –
  - (i) in paragraph (2), omit “Subject to paragraph (3),”; and
  - (ii) omit paragraphs (3) and (4).

(2) in Appendix 1, in Form 137A, in PART 2, after “State why it is believed that this person should accompany the witness” insert “(include the witness’ views)”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules make a number of amendments the County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981 ([S.R 1981 No.225](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1981/225)) (“the principal Rules”) which take account of the amendments made to Article 9 of the Criminal Evidence (Northern Ireland) Order 1999 (“the 1999 Order”) (‘special measures relating to child witnesses’) by the Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 2011. Specifically,

- Rule 2(1) inserts new rule 6EA to Order 32 of the principal Rules to provide the procedure for an application to disapply or disapply in part the primary rule in Article 9 of the 1999 Order.
- Rule 2(2) amends rule 6G of Order 32 of the principal Rules.
- Rule 2(3) amends Form 137A in the principal Rules to ensure a witness’ views are given when considering the identity of a specified person to accompany the witness while the witness is giving evidence by live link.
