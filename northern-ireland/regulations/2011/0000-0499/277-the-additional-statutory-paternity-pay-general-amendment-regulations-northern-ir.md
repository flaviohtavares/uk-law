# The Additional Statutory Paternity Pay (General) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 277

Employment

Work And Families

The Additional Statutory Paternity Pay (General) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011

Approved by resolution of the Assembly on 5th December 2011

Made

18th July 2011

Coming into operation

14th August 2011

The Department for Employment and Learning makes the following Regulations in exercise of the power conferred by section 167ZEE(2) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Additional Statutory Paternity Pay (General) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011 and shall come into operation on 14th August 2011.

## 2. Amendment to the Additional Statutory Paternity Pay (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010

In regulation 14 of the Additional Statutory Paternity Pay (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010[^f00002] (additional statutory paternity pay period (adoption)), in paragraph (1), for “before” substitute “after”.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Employment and Learning on 18th July 2011.

Dr Stephen Farry

Minister for Employment and Learning

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Additional Statutory Paternity Pay (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010 (“the Principal Regulations”) to correct a drafting error in regulation 14 of those Regulations. The error concerns the date of the beginning of the additional statutory paternity pay period (adoption).

An impact assessment has not been prepared for these Regulations since they merely correct a drafting error.
