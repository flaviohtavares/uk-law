# The Local Government Finance (2011 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 306 (C. 22)

Local Government

The Local Government Finance (2011 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011

Made

9th September 2011

The Department of the Environment makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 47(2) of the Local Government Finance Act (Northern Ireland) 2011[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Local Government Finance (2011 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011.

(2) In this Order, “the Act” means the Local Government Finance Act (Northern Ireland) 2011.

## 2. Appointed days

(1) 10th September 2011 is the day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the Act specified in column 1 of the Schedule, for the purposes set out in column 3 of the Schedule.

(2) 1st November 2011 is the day appointed for the coming into operation of section 13 of the Act (duty to determine affordable borrowing limit) for the purpose of enabling a council to determine its affordable borrowing limit for the financial year commencing on 1st April 2012.

(3) 1st April 2012 is the day appointed for the coming into operation of all provisions of the Act not otherwise brought into operation by this Order.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 9th September 2011

(L.S.)

Elizabeth Loughran

A senior officer of the Department of the Environment

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the coming into operation of the provisions of the Local Government Finance Act (Northern Ireland) 2011 (”the Act”).

10th September 2011 is the day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions specified in column 1 of the Schedule (for the purposes specified in column 3 of the Schedule).

1st November 2011 is the day appointed for the coming into operation of Section 13 of the Act (duty to determine affordable borrowing limit)for the purpose of of enabling a council to determine its affordable borrowing limit for the financial year commencing on 1st April 2012.

1st April 2012 is the day appointed for the coming into operation of all provisions of the Act not otherwise brought into operation by this Order.
