# Police Service of Northern Ireland and Police Service of Northern Ireland Reserve (Full-Time) (Severance) (Amendment) Regulations 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 408

Police

Police Service of Northern Ireland and Police Service of Northern Ireland Reserve (Full-Time) (Severance) (Amendment) Regulations 2011

Made

5th December 2011

Coming into operation

31st December 2011

The Department of Justice makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred on it by sections 25(1) and (2)(k), 26(1) and (2)(g), and 72(2) of the Police (Northern Ireland) Act 1998[^f00001] (“the 1998 Act”) and section 49 of the Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000[^f00002]. These powers are now vested in the Department[^f00003].

In accordance with section 26(6) of the 1998 Act the Department has consulted the Northern Ireland Policing Board and the Police Association for Northern Ireland. In accordance with section 62(3) of the Police Act 1996[^f00004] it has consulted with the Police Negotiating Board for the United Kingdom. In accordance with section 72(2A)[^f00005] of the 1998 Act it has obtained the consent of the Department of Finance and Personnel.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Police Service of Northern Ireland and Police Service of Northern Ireland Reserve (Full-Time) (Severance) (Amendment) Regulations 2011 and shall come into operation on 31 December 2011.

## 2. Amendment of the Police Service of Northern Ireland and Police Service of Northern Ireland Reserve (Full-Time) (Severance) Regulations 2003

In regulation 3(a) (length and review of the scheme) of the Police Service of Northern Ireland and Police Service of Northern Ireland Reserve (Full-Time) (Severance) Regulations 2003[^f00006] for “Treasury” substitute “Department of Finance and Personnel”.

## 3. Amendment of the Police Service of Northern Ireland Reserve (Full-time) Severance Regulations 2006

In regulation 3(3)(b) (length and termination of the Scheme) of the Police Service of Northern Ireland Reserve (Full-time) Severance Regulations 2006[^f00007] for “Treasury” substitute “Department of Finance and Personnel”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations transfer the requirement for HM Treasury consent to a determination by the Department of Justice under regulation 3(a) of the Police Service of Northern Ireland and Police Service of Northern Ireland Reserve (Full-Time) (Severance) Regulations 2003 and regulation 3(3)(b) of the Police Service of Northern Ireland Reserve (Full-time) Severance Regulations 2006 to the Department of Finance and Personnel.
