# The Magistrates’ Courts (Criminal Justice (Children)) (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 60

Magistrates’ Courts

The Magistrates’ Courts (Criminal Justice (Children)) (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2011

Made

1st March 2011

Coming into operation

25th March 2011

The Magistrates’ Courts Rules Committee makes the following Rules in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 13 of the Magistrates’ Courts (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001], after consultation with the Department of Justice and with the agreement of the Lord Chief Justice.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Rules may be cited as the Magistrates’ Courts (Criminal Justice (Children)) (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2011 and shall come into operation on 25th March 2011.

## 2. Amendment of the Magistrates’ Courts (Criminal Justice (Children)) (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 1999[^f00002]

In Form 1 of the Magistrates’ Courts (Criminal Justice (Children)) Rules (Northern Ireland) 1999, in the heading, for “Articles 14 and 15;” substitute “Article 14;”.

Liam McNally

Peter Luney

John Rea

Nigel Broderick

Dated 1st March 2011

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules amend the Magistrates’ Courts (Criminal Justice (Children)) Rules (Northern Ireland) 1999 \[[S.R.1999 No.7](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1999/7)\] to remove the reference to Article 15 of the Criminal Justice (Children) (Northern Ireland) Order 1998 in the heading of Form 1.
