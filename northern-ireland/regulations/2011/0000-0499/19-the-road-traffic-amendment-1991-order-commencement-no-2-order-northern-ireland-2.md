# The Road Traffic (Amendment) (1991 Order) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 19 (C. 4)

Road Traffic And Vehicles

The Road Traffic (Amendment) (1991 Order) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011

Made

27th January 2011

The Department of the Environment makes the following Order, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 1(2) of the Road Traffic (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 1991[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic (Amendment) (1991 Order) (Commencement No.2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of Article 7 of the Road Traffic (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 1991[^f00002] (compulsory training courses for riders of motor cycles) is 21<sup>st</sup> February 2011.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 27<sup>th</sup> January 2011

Deirdre Kenny

A senior officer of the

Department of the Environment

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into operation on 21<sup>st</sup> February 2011 Article 7 of the Road Traffic (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 1991 which has the effect of requiring motor cyclists, who have yet to pass a test of competence to drive a motor bicycle, to undergo compulsory training unless exempted by Regulations from having to do so.
