# The Pensions (2008 No. 2 Act) (Commencement No. 6) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 441 (C. 33)

Pensions

Social Security

The Pensions (2008 No. 2 Act) (Commencement No. 6) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011

Made

23rd December 2011

The Department for Social Development makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 118(1) of the Pensions (No. 2) Act (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the [Pensions (2008 No. 2 Act) (Commencement No. 6) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2011/441).

## 2. Commencement of provisions

The following provisions of the Pensions (No. 2) Act (Northern Ireland) 2008 shall come into operation on 3rd January 2012—

- (a) section 49 (monitoring of employers’ payments to personal pension schemes) in so far as it relates to the powers to make regulations under section 107A of the Pension Schemes Act[^f00002], for the purposes only of making regulations;
- (b) section 83 (additional State Pension etc.: minor and consequential amendments) in so far as it relates to the provisions listed in paragraph (c), and
- (c) in Schedule 3 (additional State Pension etc.: minor and consequential amendments)—
  - (i) paragraph 1 in so far as it relates to the provisions listed in sub-paragraphs (ii) to (xii) of this paragraph;
  - (ii) paragraphs 2 and 3;
  - (iii) paragraph 4(1) in so far as it relates to the provisions listed in sub-paragraph (iv) of this paragraph;
  - (iv) paragraph 4(2) and (3), except for sub-paragraphs (2)(b) and (3)(b) in so far as they relate to Schedule 4C to the Contributions and Benefits Act[^f00003] (additional pension consolidation);
  - (v) paragraph 5;
  - (vi) paragraph 6(1) in so far as it relates to paragraph 6(2);
  - (vii) paragraph 6(2);
  - (viii) paragraph 7, except for sub-paragraph (a);
  - (ix) paragraph 8, except for sub-paragraph (a);
  - (x) paragraph 9(1) in so far as it relates to the provisions listed in sub-paragraph (xi) of this paragraph;
  - (xi) paragraph 9(2) and (3), except for sub-paragraph (2)(c), and
  - (xii) paragraphs 11 and 12.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 23rd December 2011

(L.S.)

Anne McCleary

A senior officer of the Department for Social Development

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings section 49 of the Pensions (No. 2) Act (Northern Ireland) 2008 (“the Act”) into operation on 3rd January 2012, for the purpose only of conferring power to make regulations under section 107A of the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993. Section 49 amends section 107A so that the term “employee” includes a “jobholder”.

The Order also brings into operation certain provisions of Schedule 3 to the Act (and section 83 in so far as it relates to those provisions) on 3rd January 2012. Those provisions make minor and consequential amendments to the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992 in relation to retirement pensions under Part II of that Act.
