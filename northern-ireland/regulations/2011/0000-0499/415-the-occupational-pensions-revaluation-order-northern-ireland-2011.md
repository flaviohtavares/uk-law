# The Occupational Pensions (Revaluation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 415

Pensions

The Occupational Pensions (Revaluation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011

Made

9th December 2011

Coming into operation

1st January 2012

This Order is made in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 2(1) of Schedule 2 to the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993[^f00001] and now vested in the Department for Social Development[^f00002].

The Secretary of State for Work and Pensions has made an Order[^f00003] under paragraph 2(1) of Schedule 3 to the Pension Schemes Act 1993[^f00004].

Accordingly the Department for Social Development makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Occupational Pensions (Revaluation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011 and shall come into operation on 1st January 2012.

(2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00005] shall apply to this Order as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

## 2. The higher and lower revaluation percentages for each revaluation period

For the purposes of paragraph 2(1) of Schedule 2 to the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993, for each revaluation period specified in column 1 of the Schedule, the higher revaluation percentage is the percentage specified in column 2, and the lower revaluation percentage, where applicable, is the percentage specified in column 3, in relation to that period.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 9th December 2011

(L.S.)

Anne McCleary

A senior officer of the Department for Social Development

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which corresponds to an Order ([S.I. 2011/2867](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/2867)) made by the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions under paragraph 2(1) of Schedule 3 to the Pension Schemes Act 1993, specifies appropriate revaluation percentages. The percentages specified are relevant to the revaluation of benefits under occupational pension schemes, as required by section 80 of, and Schedule 2 to, the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993. The lower revaluation percentage introduced by the Pensions (No. 2) Act (Northern Ireland) [2008 (c. 13 (N.I.)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nia/2008/13)) does not apply to pensionable service before 6th April 2009.
