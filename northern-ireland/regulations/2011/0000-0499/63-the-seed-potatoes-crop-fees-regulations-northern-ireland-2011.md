EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations increase the fee payable to the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development in respect of the certification of seed potato crops arising under the Seed Potatoes Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001 (regulations 2, 3 and the Schedule).

In the case of an application for the certification and classification of seed potato crops, except for pre-basic tissue culture, received by the Department on or before the 30<sup>th</sup> May in any calendar year:

- (a) where the total area of the crop does not exceed 1 hectare the fee is increased from £43.00 to £46.00.
- (b) where the total area of the crop exceeds 1 hectare the fee is increased from £21.50 to £23.00 half hectare or part of a half hectare.

In the case of an application for the certification and classification of seed potato crops, except for pre-basic tissue culture received by the Department after 30<sup>th</sup> May in any calendar year:

- (a) where the total area of the crop does not exceed 1 hectare the fee is increased from £53.00 to £56.00;
- (b) where the total area of the crop exceeds 1 hectare the fee is increased from £26.50 to £28.00 per half hectare or part of a half hectare.
