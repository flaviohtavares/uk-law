# The Strategic Investment and Regeneration of Sites (Maze/Long Kesh Development Corporation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 234

Strategic Investment

The Strategic Investment and Regeneration of Sites (Maze/Long Kesh Development Corporation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011

Laid before the Assembly in draft

Made

1st June 2011

Coming into operation

1st October 2010

The Office of the First Minister and deputy First Minister makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 15(1) and 15(3) of, and paragraph 2(1)(b) of Schedule 1 to, the Strategic Investment and Regeneration of Sites (Northern Ireland) Order 2003[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Strategic Investment and Regeneration of Sites (Maze/Long Kesh Development Corporation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011 and shall come into operation on 1st October 2010.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order—

- “the 2003 Order” means the Strategic Investment and Regeneration of Sites (Northern Ireland) Order 2003;
- “the Corporation” means the Maze/Long Kesh Development Corporation established under Article 3;
- “the Maze/Long Kesh site” means the area of land described in the Schedule.

## 3. Establishment of Development Corporation

There is hereby established a development corporation within the meaning of Article 15(1) of the 2003 Order, to be known as the Maze/Long Kesh Development Corporation.

## 4. Relevant Site

The Corporation shall exercise its functions in relation to the relevant site known as the Maze/Long Kesh site.

## 5. Membership of the Corporation

In addition to the chairman, the corporation shall consist of 10 other members.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Office of the First Minister and deputy First Minister on 1<sup>st</sup> June 2011.

Alan Maitland

A Senior Officer of the

Office of the First Minister and deputy First Minister

## SCHEDULE — THE MAZE/LONG KESH SITE

Article 2

All that and those lands situated in the townland of Maze, Barony of Iveagh Lower (Upper Half) and County of Down registered in the Land Registry of Northern Ireland as being part of the following Folios and are shown outlined in black on the map attached:

- Folio No 29420 County Down
- Folio No 30155 County Down

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Statutory Rule establishes a Development Corporation under Article 15(1) of the Strategic Investment and Regeneration of Sites (Northern Ireland) Order 2003 initially to progress the development of the Maze/Long Kesh site. The Rule also specifies the number of members the Development Corporation shall have, in accordance with Article 15(3) of, and paragraph 2(1)(b) of Schedule 1 to, that Order.
