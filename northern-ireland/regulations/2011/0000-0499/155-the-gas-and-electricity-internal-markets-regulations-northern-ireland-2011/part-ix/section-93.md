# The Gas and Electricity (Internal Markets) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011, section 93

> Text as at 2013-07-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 93. Existing members of the Authority[^c-b36265710a771f72e01fd512db07283a]

*Extent: N.I.*

For the purposes of sub-paragraph (3) of paragraph (3) of Schedule 1 to the Energy Order, a person who at the date these Regulations come into operation has been appointed to hold office as chairman or other member of the Authority for one or more terms in total not exceeding 7 years shall be treated as having been appointed only once.
