# The Gas and Electricity (Internal Markets) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011, section 89

> Text as at 2013-07-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 89. Amendments to the Energy Act (Northern Ireland) 2011[^key-ab178020f56b5b90a71e569024be0311]

*Extent: N.I.*

In Section 17(5) of the Energy Act (Northern Ireland) 2011, in paragraph (a) of the definition of “relevant licence”, after the words “Article 10(1)(b)” there shall be inserted the words “or Article 10(1)(bb)”.
