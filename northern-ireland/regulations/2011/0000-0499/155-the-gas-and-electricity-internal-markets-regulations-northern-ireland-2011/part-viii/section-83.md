# The Gas and Electricity (Internal Markets) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011, section 83

> Text as at 2013-07-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 83. Amendments to the Energy Order[^c-f5a973d75c5b99799d27b61208f9c129]

*Extent: N.I.*

In Article 2 of the Energy Order, there shall be inserted at the appropriate places the following definitions—

> - “the Agency” means the Agency for the Cooperation of Energy Regulators, established under the Agency Regulation;

;

> - “designated regulatory authority” means an authority designated in accordance with Article 35 of the Electricity Directive or Article 39 of the Gas Directive;

;

> - “exemption holder” means any person who is—
>   - (a) an electricity generation exemption holder;
>   - (b) an electricity distribution exemption holder;
>   - (c) an electricity supply exemption holder;
>   - (d) a gas conveyance exemption holder;
>   - (e) a gas storage exemption holder; or
>   - (f) a gas supply exemption holder;

;

> - “the Agency Regulation” means Regulation [(EC) No 713/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0713) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 July 2009 establishing an Agency for the Cooperation of Energy Regulators;

;

> - “the Electricity Directive” means Directive [2009/72/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0072) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 July 2009 concerning common rules for the internal market in electricity and repealing Directive [2003/54/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2003/0054);

;

> - “the Electricity Regulation” means Regulation [(EC) No. 714/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0714) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 July 2009 on conditions for access to the network for cross-border exchanges in electricity and repealing Regulation [(EC) No. 1228/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1228);

;

> - “electricity distribution exemption holder” means a person who carries on the activity referred to in Article 8(1)(bb) of the Electricity Order under the authority of an exemption granted pursuant to Article 9 of that Order;

;

> - “electricity generation exemption holder” means a person who carries on the activity referred to in Article 8(1)(a) of the Electricity Order under the authority of an exemption granted pursuant to Article 9 of that Order;

;

> - “electricity supply exemption holder” means a person who carries on the activity referred to in Article 8(1)(c) of the Electricity Order under the authority of an exemption granted pursuant to Article 9 of that Order;

;

> - “the Gas Directive” means Directive [2009/73/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0073) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 July 2009 concerning common rules for the internal market in natural gas and repealing Directive [2003/55/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2003/0055);

;

> - “the Gas Regulation” means Regulation [(EC) No. 715/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0715) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 July 2009 on conditions for access to the natural gas transmission networks and repealing Regulation [(EC) No. 1775/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/1775);

;

> - “gas conveyance exemption holder” means a person who carries on the activity referred to in Article 6(1)(a) of the Gas Order under the authority of an exemption granted pursuant to Article 7 of that Order;

;

> - “gas storage exemption holder” means a person who carries on the activity referred to in Article 6(1)(b) of the Gas Order under the authority of an exemption granted pursuant to Article 7 of that Order;

;

> - “gas supply exemption holder” means a person who carries on the activity referred to in Article 6(1)(c) of the Gas Order under the authority of an exemption granted pursuant to Article 7 of that Order;

; and

> - “Third Package” means—
>   - (a) the Electricity Directive;
>   - (b) the Gas Directive;
>   - (c) the Agency Regulation;
>   - (d) the Electricity Regulation; and
>   - (e) the Gas Regulation.

.

**84.**[^c-9a34aa73f70d10106017278d2a75bb3f] In Article 38 of the Energy Order—

- (a) In paragraph (1), for the words “European Parliament and Council Directive [2003/54/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2003/0054) concerning common rules for the internal market in electricity and repealing Directive [96/92/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0092)” there shall be substituted the words “the Electricity Directive”; and
- (b) in paragraph (2), for the words “European Parliament and Council Directive [2003/55/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2003/0055) concerning common rules for the internal market in natural gas and repealing Directive [98/30/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0030)” there shall be substituted “the Gas Directive”.
