# The Gas and Electricity (Internal Markets) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011, section 2

> Text as at 2013-07-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 2. Interpretation[^c-e4ffafce6e6749c74fa79ebf6da8fb02]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00003] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

(2) In these Regulations—

- “the Authority” means the Northern Ireland Authority for Utility Regulation;
- “the Department” means the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment;
- “the Electricity Directive” means European Parliament and Council Directive [2009/72/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0072) concerning common rules for the internal market in electricity and repealing Directive [2003/54/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2003/0054);
- “the Electricity Order” means the Electricity (Northern Ireland) Order 1992[^f00004];
- “the Energy Order” means the Energy (Northern Ireland) Order 2003[^f00005];
- “the Gas Directive” means European Parliament and Council Directive [2009/73/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0073) concerning common rules for the internal market in natural gas and repealing Directive [2003/55/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2003/0055); and
- “the Gas Order” means the Gas (Northern Ireland) Order 1996[^f00006].
