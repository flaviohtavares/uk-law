# The Gas and Electricity (Internal Markets) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011, section 16

> Text as at 2013-07-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 16. Interpretation[^key-1b629c5ca9684eb8ac5cdb6d85a5e0ef]

*Extent: N.I.*

In Article 3 (Interpretation of Part II) of the Electricity Order—

- (a) after the definition of “distribute” there shall be inserted the following definition—

  > - “distribution licence” means a licence under Article 10(1)(bb);

  ; and
- (b) in the definition of “electricity distributor” for “10(1)(b)” there shall be substituted “10(1)(bb)”.
