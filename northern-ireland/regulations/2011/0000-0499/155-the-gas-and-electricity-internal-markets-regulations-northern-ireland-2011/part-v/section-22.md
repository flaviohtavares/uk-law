## 22. General duties of electricity distribution system operators[^c-e48536df5e100b75f8f29aeefbd9372b]

*Extent: N.I.*

In Article 12(1)(a) of the Electricity Order after “distribution” there shall be inserted “which has the long-term ability to meet reasonable demands for the distribution of electricity”.
