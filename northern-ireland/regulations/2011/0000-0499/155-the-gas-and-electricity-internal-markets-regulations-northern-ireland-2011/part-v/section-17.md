## 17. Prohibition on unlicensed distribution[^c-2a85cfb4e3efeb7c90eb89139cdde237]

*Extent: N.I.*

In Article 8(1) (Prohibition on unlicensed supply, etc) of the Electricity Order, after paragraph (b) there shall be inserted the following sub-paragraph—

> - (bb) distributes electricity for that purpose;
