# The Gas and Electricity (Internal Markets) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011, section 31

> Text as at 2013-07-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 31. Existing electricity distribution system operators[^key-15034d1e231ab77dd4252860d90f402f]

*Extent: N.I.*

Article 8(1)(bb) of the Electricity Order shall not apply to any person, other than—

- (a) the holder of a licence under Article 10(1)(bb) of the Electricity Order; or
- (b) the former holder of such a licence which has been revoked other than with his agreement,

until 30 September 2012 or such later date as the Department may determine under regulation 32[^key-a052a5e407e8d732ce60b98ffc38ea1b] .

**32.**[^key-202c887cce82943ca782c655bcf239f4] The Department may determine a later date if it thinks there are reasonable grounds for doing so and that date is not later than 12 months after the date in regulation 31.
