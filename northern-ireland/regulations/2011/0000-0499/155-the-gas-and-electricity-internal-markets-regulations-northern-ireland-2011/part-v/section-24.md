# The Gas and Electricity (Internal Markets) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011, section 24

> Text as at 2013-07-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 24. Miscellaneous[^c-a0065fc7cd023b1fe34f41f190fc0c5c]

*Extent: N.I.*

In Article 33 (Electrical inspectors) of the Electricity Order in paragraph (2)(a) after “supply” there shall be inserted “, distribute”.

**25.**[^c-605e9341ef72ed72c6c5f2f847f15701] In Article 38 (Provisions supplementary to Article 37) of the Electricity Order—

- (a) in paragraph (1) after “transmission licence” there shall be inserted “or a distribution licence”; and
- (b) in paragraph (2) after “licence to” there shall be inserted “distribute or”.

**26.**[^c-c75ef77710cbd815bc488bab7a015f23] In Article 46 (Functions with respect to competition) of the Electricity Order in paragraphs (2A) and (3) after “transmission” in each place where the word occurs there shall be inserted “, distribution”.

**27.**[^c-c232deb182c3b232e0db478c286167b9] In Article 58 (Directions for preserving security of electricity supplies, etc.) of the Electricity Order—

- (a) in paragraph (1)(a) after “transmission” there shall be inserted “, distribution”; and
- (b) in paragraph (6) after “generate” there shall be inserted “, distribute”.

**28.**[^c-6bf8d121e90f8f837a3886579bd6e93e] In Article 59 (Provision of statistical information) of the Electricity Order—

- (a) in paragraph (1) after “transmission” there shall be inserted “, distribution”; and
- (b) in paragraph (1) after “generate” there shall be inserted “, distribute”.

**29.**[^c-0d8b101b843b022d6ca4fe194dbaa11e] In Article 60 (Promotion of new techniques in national interest) of the Electricity Order—

- (a) in paragraph (1) after “transmission” there shall be inserted “, distribution”; and
- (b) in paragraph (2) after “generate” there shall be inserted “, distribute”.

**30.**[^c-405de6bf2d256de655749a2009fca16f] In Article 62(2) (Directions restricting the use of certain information) of the Electricity Order, after “supply” there shall be inserted “, distribute”.
