# The Gas and Electricity (Internal Markets) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011, section 33

> Text as at 2013-07-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 33. Amendments to the Electricity (Applications for Licences and Extensions of Licences) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007[^c-3b07544f8e5b4636416484fa688523b1]

*Extent: N.I.*

The Electricity (Applications for Licences and Extensions of Licences) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00007] shall be amended as follows—

- (a) in regulation 2 (Interpretation) after the definition of “application” there shall be inserted the following definition—

- “distribution licence” means a licence under Article 10(1)(bb) of the Order;
  - (a) in regulation 3 (Form of applications) after paragraph (5) there shall be inserted the following paragraph—

    > (6) An application for a distribution licence shall be in the form, and shall contain the particulars, specified in Part I of Schedule 6.

    ;
  - (b) in regulation 4 (Documents to accompany applications) after paragraph (5) there shall be inserted the following paragraph—

    > (6) An application for a distribution licence shall be accompanied by the documents and particulars specified in Part II of Schedule 6.

    ;
  - (c) in regulation 6(1)(b)(i) (Manner of publishing applications), after “transmission licence” there shall be inserted—

    > , a distribution licence

    ;
  - (d) in regulation 7(1) (Fees payable with applications), after “transmission” there shall be inserted—

    > , distribution

    ;
  - (e) in Part II of Schedule 3—
    - (i) in paragraph 2(b), after “systems” there shall be inserted “or distribution systems”;
    - (ii) in paragraph 2(g), after “underground;” omit “and”; and
  - (f) after Schedule 5 there shall be inserted the Schedule set out in Schedule 2 to these Regulations.
