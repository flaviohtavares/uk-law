# The Gas and Electricity (Internal Markets) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011, section 67

> Text as at 2013-07-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 67. Amendments to the Gas Order[^c-761105979f6907c500f73458346be145]

*Extent: N.I.*

In Article 27(2) of the Gas Order for the words “give general directions” there shall be substituted with the words “issue guidance”.

**68.**[^c-eb56e6c639d790be39f17252029fd121] In Article 30(2) of the Gas Order the words “, with the consent of the Department,” shall be omitted.
