# The Gas and Electricity (Internal Markets) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011, section 57

> Text as at 2013-07-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 57. Authority’s monitoring functions in relation to electricity[^key-84defb27db732c1d5298404ba67a3b93]

*Extent: N.I.*

After Article 50(3A) of the Electricity Order there shall be inserted the following paragraph—

> (3B) the activities to which paragraph (1) applies include, in particular, the matters specified in the following provisions of the Directive as matters to be monitored—
>
> - (a) Article 26(3);
> - (b) Article 37(1)(g) to (k), (m) and (q) to (t); and
> - (c) where an independent system operator has been designated under Article 10H of this Order, Article 37(3)(a), (b) and (f).

.
