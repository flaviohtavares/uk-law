# Eggs and Chicks (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 248

Food

Eggs and Chicks (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011

Made

30th June 2011

Coming into operation

1st August 2011

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development is designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Union.

These Regulations make provision for a purpose mentioned in section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972, and it appears to the Department that it is expedient for any reference to the following Regulations to be construed as a reference to those Regulations as amended from time to time—

- (a) Council Regulation [(EC) No. 1234/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/1234)[^f00003] establishing a common organisation of agricultural markets and on specific provisions for certain agricultural products (Single CMO Regulation);
- (b) [Commission Regulation (EC) No. 589/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/0589)[^f00004] laying down detailed rules for implementing Regulation [(EC) No. 1234/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/1234) as regards marketing standards for eggs; and
- (c) [Commission Regulation (EC) No. 617/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/0617)[^f00005] laying down detailed rules for implementing Regulation [(EC) No. 1234/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/1234) as regards marketing standards for eggs for hatching and farmyard poultry chicks.

There has been open and transparent public consultation during the preparation of the following Regulations as required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council[^f00006] laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety.

Accordingly, the Department makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of, and paragraph 1A[^f00007] of Schedule 2 to, the European Communities Act 1972.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Eggs and Chicks (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011 and shall come into operation on 1<sup>st</sup> August 2011.

## 2. Interpretation

The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00008] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

## 3. Amendment of the Eggs and Chicks Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010

The Eggs and Chicks Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010[^f00009] shall be amended in accordance with regulations 4 to 7.

**4.** In regulation 2(1) (Interpretation) after the definition of “eggs for hatching”, there shall be inserted—

> “electronic communication” has the same meaning as in section 4(1) of the Electronic Communications Act (Northern Ireland) 2001[^f00010]

.

**5.** In paragraphs (2)(e) and 6(d) of regulation 21 (Compliance notices) the words “to an appointed person” shall be omitted.

**6.** For Regulation 22 there shall be substituted—

> **22.** (1) A person may, within 21 days of the notification of a decision to which this regulation applies, make written representations to appeal against the decision to a person appointed for this purpose by the Department.
>
> (2) The appointed person shall consider the representations and report in writing to the Department.
>
> (3) The Department shall give the person who made representations written notification of its final determination and the reasons for it.
>
> (4) A person who is aggrieved by the final determination of the Department under paragraph (3) may, within 21 days of the notification of the determination, appeal against that determination to a magistrates’ court.
>
> (5) This regulation applies to—
>
> - (a) a decision by the Department to refuse to register an establishment as a pedigree breeding establishment, other breeding establishment or hatchery under Article 2(1) of [Commission Regulation (EC) No. 617/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/0617), or to withdraw such a registration;
> - (b) a decision by the Department to refuse to authorise an undertaking as a packing centre to grade eggs under the first sub-paragraph of Article 5(2) of [Commission Regulation (EC) No. 589/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/0589), or to withdraw such an authorisation;
> - (c) a decision by an authorised officer to impose a regulation 21(1) compliance notice; and
> - (d) a decision by an authorised officer to impose a regulation 21(4) compliance notice.
>
> (6) The withdrawal of an authorisation or registration referred to in paragraph (5) does not take effect until the time for appealing against it has expired, and, if an appeal is lodged, until the appeal is finally disposed of or withdrawn.

**7.** Paragraph (6) of regulation 28 shall be omitted.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 30<sup>th</sup> June 2011.

Keith Morrison

A senior officer of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Eggs and Chicks Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010 ([S.R. 2010 No. 125](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2010/125)) that give effect to Council Regulation [(EC) No. 1234/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/1234) (O.J. No. L 299, 16.11.2007, p. 1), [Commission Regulation (EC) No. 617/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/0617) (O.J. No. L 168, 28.6.2008, p. 5), [Commission Regulation (EC) No. 589/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/0589) (O.J. No. L 163, 24.6.2008, p. 6) and Regulation [(EC) No. 2160/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/2160) (O.J. No. 325, 12.12.2003, p. 1) on EC marketing standards relating to eggs for hatching and chicks, eggs in shell for consumption and new *Salmonella* related controls.

The Eggs and Chicks Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010 are amended to—

- (a) include a revised definition for “electronic communication” (regulations 4 and 7);
- (b) include an appeal to a magistrates’ court (regulations 5 and 6).

A Regulatory Impact Assessment has not been prepared for these Regulations because no additional requirements are being imposed.
