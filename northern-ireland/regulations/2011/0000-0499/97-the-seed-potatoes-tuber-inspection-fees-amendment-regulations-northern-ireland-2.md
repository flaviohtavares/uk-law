# The Seed Potatoes (Tuber Inspection Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 97

Agriculture

The Seed Potatoes (Tuber Inspection Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011

Made

11th March 2011

Coming into operation

1st September 2011

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development [^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by section 1 of the Seeds Act (Northern Ireland) 1965[^f00002], after consultation with representatives of such interests as appear to it to be concerned, makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Title and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as The Seed Potatoes (Tuber Inspection Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011 and shall come into operation on 1st September 2011.

## 2. Amendment of The Seed Potatoes (Tuber Inspection Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006

For the Schedule to The Seed Potatoes (Tuber Inspection Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006 substitute:

> **SCHEDULE**
>
> regulation 2
>
> |  |  |
> | --- | --- |
> | Matter | Fee |
> |  | £ |
> | **1.** Fee for the inspection of seed potato tubers prior to their being sealed under regulation 14(2) of the Seed Potatoes Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001 and for the provision of an appropriate number of labels for packages per tonne | 4.80 per tonne or part of a tonne |

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 11th March 2011.

John Speers

A senior officer of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend The Seed Potatoes (Tuber Inspection Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006.

The Regulations increase the seed potatoes tuber inspection fees from £4.40 per tonne or part thereof to £4.80 per tonne or part thereof from 1st September 2011.
