# Local Government (Defined Activities) (Exemptions) Order (Northern Ireland) 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 43

LOCAL GOVERNMENT

Local Government (Defined Activities) (Exemptions) Order (Northern Ireland) 2000

Made

1st March 2000

Coming into operation

1st April 2000

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Articles 4(8) and 16(3) of the Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Northern Ireland) Order 1992[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Title, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Local Government (Defined Activities) (Exemptions) Order (Northern Ireland) 2000 and shall come into operation on 1st April 2000.

(2) In this Order “the exemption end date for a council” shall be construed in accordance with Article 3.

## 2. Activities not to be treated as defined activities

Where the activity specifed in column 2 of the Schedule is carried out by a council specified in column 1 of the Schedule on or before the exemption end date for that council, that activity shall not be treated as a defined activity on or before the exemption end date for that council.

## 3. Provision as to end of exemption

In respect of each council specified in column 1 of the Schedule, the exemption end date for that council is, in respect of maintenance of ground, the date specified opposite that council in column 2 of that Schedule.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 1st March 2000.

J. McConnell

Assistant Secretary
