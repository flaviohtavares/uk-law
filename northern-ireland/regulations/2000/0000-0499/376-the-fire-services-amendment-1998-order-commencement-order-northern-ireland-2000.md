# The Fire Services (Amendment) (1998 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 376 (C. 19)

FIRE SERVICES

The Fire Services (Amendment) (1998 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2000

Made

30th November 2000

The Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety[^f00001] in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 1(3) of the Fire Services (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 1998[^f00002], and now vested in it[^f00003], and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Fire Services (Amendment) (1998 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2000.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of Articles 5 and 6 of the Fire Services (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 1998 (constitution of the Fire Authority) is 30th September 2001.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety on 30th November 2000.

N. Lunn

Senior Officer of the

Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order brings into operation on 30th September 2001, Articles 5 and 6 of the Fire Services (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 1998 (“the 1998 Order”). The 1998 Order amends the Fire Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1984 so as to make new provision with respect to members of the Fire Authority for Northern Ireland nominated by certain councils and associations.

All provisions of the 1998 Order are now commenced.
