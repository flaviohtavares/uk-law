# The Undersized Whiting (Revocation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 158

FISHERIES

The Undersized Whiting (Revocation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2000

Made

8th May 2000

Coming into operation

16th June 2000

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred on it by sections 127(1) and (3A) of the Fisheries Act (Northern Ireland) 1966[^f00002] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Undersized Whiting (Revocation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2000 and shall come into operation on 16th June 2000.

## 2. Revocation

The Undersized Whiting Order (Northern Ireland) 1993[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 8th May 2000.

G. Lavery

A Senior Officer of the

Department of Agriculture and Rural Development

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order revokes the Undersized Whiting Order (Northern Ireland) 1993 which prescribed a minimum size for whiting.
