# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, SCHEDULE F1

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE F1 (affected by repeal, see note) — Elections as respects widowers' pensions etc. (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

Regulation F8 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

**1.**[^key-a71a0d2d91b13e9a4670d69f8948a897] (1) A married woman who— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) was a member on 27th July 1989 and has since that date continuously been a member, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) is entitled to count a period of membership before 1st April 1972, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

may, subject to sub-paragraph (3), give notice in writing to the Committee of any additional period which she wishes to count for the purposes of calculating any widower’s long-term pension payable in respect of her. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) For the purposes of sub-paragraph (1), the reference to a woman having continuously been a member includes a reference to a woman who, having ceased to be a member— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) became or becomes a member again within one month of so ceasing, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) became or becomes a member again within one month of returning to work in accordance with Article 111 of the Employment Rights (Northern Ireland) Order 1996 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^f00101] (which confers the right to return to work following pregnancy or confinement). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) The additional period of which notice may be given by a woman under sub-paragraph (1) shall not exceed the length of her period of membership which is not, or is not treated for the purposes of regulation F7(3) as being, membership after 31st March 1972. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(4) Notice given under sub-paragraph (1) shall be irrevocable, but, subject to the provisions of this paragraph, notice may be given on more than one occasion. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 2. Elections under regulation E12 of the 1992 regulations (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-71aa1bab46d5e4cd761526068f6c92e0][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(1) Where any woman— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) duly elected under regulation E12(1)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*b*) or (2)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*b*) of the 1992 regulations (election by wife of dependant and permanently incapacitated husband), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) was a person to whom regulation E12(1)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*a*) of those regulations (unmarried woman with potentially eligible child) applied immediately before she ceased to be a pensionable employee (within the meaning of those regulations), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

then, subject to sub-paragraph (2), the provisions of Part D relating to the amount of the retirement pension and the retirement grant, Parts F and G and paragraph 3 of Schedule BI apply in relation to her as if she were a man, and where sub-paragraph ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*a*) applies, as if the husband in relation to whom the election was made were a woman. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) Sub-paragraph (1) does not apply if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) the woman has given notice under paragraph 1(1) that she wishes to count any of her membership before 1st April 1972 for the purposes of calculating a widower’s long-term pension, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) if she ceases to be married to the husband in respect of whom the election under regulation E12(1) or (2)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*b*) of the 1992 regulations was made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 3.[^key-d0ff60e9ee902fbc1f3a4ed850f8f241]

Where a woman died before 6th April 1988, Part G only applies in respect of her if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) she was a person to whom regulation E12(1)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*a*) of the 1992 regulations (unmarried woman with potentially eligible child) applied, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) in respect of whom an election under regulation E12(1)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*b*) or (2)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*b*) of the 1992 regulations had effect immediately before her death. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
