# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section D4

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## D4. References to members leaving employment to include optants-out (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-35e605ae6c9de8166911f27db5611ff1][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

In this Part any reference to a member ceasing to hold a local government employment (except the reference in regulation D7) includes a reference to a person who was a member in such an employment but ceased to be so by virtue of a notification under regulation B11(2) (leaving the Scheme) and other references in these regulations shall be construed accordingly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
