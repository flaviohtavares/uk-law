# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section D20

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## D20. Limitations on benefits etc. (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-853b11d6ae7509b9274ecb657437ff0d][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

The provisions of this Part are subject to Schedule C4 (limitations on contributions and benefits) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] or, as the case may be, the corresponding provisions of the 2002 Regulations (in accordance with Schedule 2 to the Transitional Regulations)[^key-caaecce738c6bcc60f1b3c60e57e6350] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] and to the general provisions in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] regulation H2 and regulations 51, 52, 95 to 97 and 114 to 118 of the 2002 Regulations.[^key-b9f633f750f194075fad0f1cc6ba6fce] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111].
