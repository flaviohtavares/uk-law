# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section D19

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## D19. National insurance (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-4d4e37d18f7b29d964f87aba9366218f][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), where a pension is payable to a member who attains state pensionable age in respect of a period of service in a non-participating employment, which counts for the purpose of calculating any benefits payable to the member (other than excepted service), no provision in these regulations— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) for the surrender or assignment of a pension, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) for the reduction, termination or suspension of a pension, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

shall apply so as to reduce the pension below the minimum rate of equivalent pension benefits applicable in respect of that period of service under the Insurance Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) Paragraph (1) does not apply to any provision for the reduction, termination or suspension of a pension, which is used for a purpose prescribed by regulations made, or deemed to have been made, under section 56(1)(c) of the Insurance Act (equivalent pension benefits). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) For the purposes of paragraph (1) “excepted service”, in relation to any person, means any earlier period of such service as there mentioned, being service in respect of which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) a payment in lieu of contributions has been made, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) equivalent pension benefits satisfying the requirements of the Insurance Act have already been assured to him. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
