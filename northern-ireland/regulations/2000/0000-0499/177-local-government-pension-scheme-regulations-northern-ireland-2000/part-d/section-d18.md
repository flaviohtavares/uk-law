# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section D18

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## D18. Equivalent pension benefits (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-caeed78a34560728021e358338ad428a][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) If when a member ceased to hold a local government employment— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) he was not entitled in relation to that employment to a retirement pension under regulation D5, D6, D7, D9 or D11, and he receives a return of contributions; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) the whole or some part of his period of membership was in service in a non-participating employment or in service which relates to employment with a non-local government employer in a non-participating employment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c) a period of his service in a non-participating employment came to an end by reason— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (i) of the repeal of section 55(1) of the Insurance Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^f00026]; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] of the provisions of regulation 2(2) of the National Insurance (Non-Participation — Assurance of Equivalent Pension Benefits) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1960 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^f00027] (as modified by regulation 10(2)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*a*) or ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*b*) of the Transitional Provisions Regulations); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (d) at some time during the settlement period (within the meaning of regulation 2 of the Transitional Provisions Regulations) he became, and has remained, assured of equivalent pension benefits; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

then he is entitled in relation to that employment to an annual retirement pension payable at the rate of the equivalent pension benefits applicable to him in respect of any period of membership in service in a non-participating employment or which relates to service with a non-local government employer in a non-participating employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) A retirement pension to which a person has become entitled by virtue of paragraph (1) is payable from the first date on which he— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) has attained state pensionable age, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) is no longer in any local government employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) For the purposes of these regulations a member to whom this regulation applies shall be treated as having ceased to hold the employment in respect of which he receives a return of contributions on the day before the date of receipt. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
