# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section D8

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## D8. Ill-health retirement grants (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-294fd16f4478e3f3737e74f05fd6b0ee][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where a member— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) ceases to hold a local government employment by reason of being permanently incapable of discharging efficiently the duties of that employment by reason of ill-health or infirmity of mind or body, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) regulation D7 does not apply to him because he does not have a statutory pension entitlement, but *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c) his total period of membership is at least one year, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

then, subject to paragraph (3), he is entitled to be paid a lump sum (“an ill-health retirement grant”). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) The amount of the ill-health retirement grant is the lesser of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) one twelfth of the member’s pensionable remuneration, multiplied by the length in years of his total period of membership, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) three eightieths of his pensionable remuneration, multiplied by the length in years of the total period of membership he would have been entitled to count if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (i) he had continued as a member of the Scheme until the age of 65, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] any added period payments had been completed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) Paragraph (1) does not apply if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) the member ceased to hold his employment in consequence of any such offence or misconduct as are mentioned in regulation C21(4), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) apart from this regulation, the member is entitled to any payment out of the fund, other than a return of contributions, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c) where the grant to which a member would be entitled under paragraph (1), calculated in accordance with paragraph (2), is no more than the net amount he would receive if a return of contributions were made to him (including an increase under regulation K19 where that regulation applies) he shall instead be entitled to such a return of contributions (so increased where appropriate). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(4) Without prejudice to any subsequent decision under regulation J3 (decisions as to benefits) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] or regulation 98 of the 2002 Regulations (first instance decisions)[^key-c3a1cf19e5d03951029bd13075ed4732] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111], the Committee shall notify a person who falls within paragraph (1) (other than one to whom paragraph (3)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*a*) applies), in writing and as soon as is reasonably practicable, of the amount of the ill-health retirement grant to which he would be entitled if he were and remained a person to whom this regulation applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
