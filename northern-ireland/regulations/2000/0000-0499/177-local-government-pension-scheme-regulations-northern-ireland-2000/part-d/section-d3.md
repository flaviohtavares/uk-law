# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section D3

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## D3. Statutory pension entitlement (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-2f46978ed790948d374efcd5840afc66][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

For the purposes of these regulations, a person has a “statutory pension entitlement” if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) his total period of membership is not less than two years, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) a transfer value from the trustees or managers of a personal pension scheme or self-employed pension arrangement has been and remains credited to the fund in relation to him, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c) neither paragraph ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*a*) nor ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*b*) applies and he— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (i) has attained state pensionable age; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] will attain state pensionable age before the following 6th April. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
