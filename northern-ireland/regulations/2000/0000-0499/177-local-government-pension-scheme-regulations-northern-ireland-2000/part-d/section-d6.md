# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section D6

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## D6. Early entitlement to retirement benefits: redundancy etc. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-d6dad62778f6adaf2738e08bae09d337][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to the following provisions of this Part, if a member who ceases to hold a local government employment— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) has a statutory pension entitlement, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) has attained the age of 50, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c) satisfies one of the conditions mentioned in paragraph (2), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

he is entitled— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (i) to a standard retirement pension, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] to a standard retirement grant, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

which are payable immediately on his ceasing to hold that employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) The conditions mentioned in paragraph (l)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*c*) are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) that the employing authority certifies that he has ceased to hold the local government employment— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (i) by reason of redundancy; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] in the interests of the efficient exercise of its functions; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) that he was one of the holders of a joint appointment and his appointment has been terminated because the other ceased to hold his appointment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
