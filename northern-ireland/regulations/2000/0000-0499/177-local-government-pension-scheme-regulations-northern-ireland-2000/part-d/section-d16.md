# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section D16

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## D16. Guaranteed minimum pensions for members in contracted-out employment, etc. (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-02604e66eb3ac1a1501cc90cc511a91c][^key-b05ddfe427f81e28e85d95211c3c0d0f][^key-ed4c48de46f869ca1b9e4e0321622d86][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-02604e66eb3ac1a1501cc90cc511a91c] Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) the employment of a member in any local government employment is contracted-out employment; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) the member has a guaranteed minimum pension under section 10 of the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993 in relation to benefits under these regulations, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

then paragraphs (2) to (4) apply as respects the member’s employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2)[^key-02604e66eb3ac1a1501cc90cc511a91c] The member who ceases to hold his local government employment is from the date on which he attains state pensionable age entitled to a pension at a weekly rate equal to that guaranteed minimum (unless on ceasing to hold his local government employment he is entitled to a retirement pension at a higher rate). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) If the member— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) attains state pensionable age while in local government employment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) continues in the same employment for a further period of 5 years; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c) does not then cease to hold it, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

then he is entitled from the end of that period to so much of his retirement pension as equals that guaranteed minimum (unless he consents to a postponement of the entitlement). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(4)[^key-02604e66eb3ac1a1501cc90cc511a91c] The guaranteed minimum referred to in paragraphs (2) and (3) shall, so far as it is attributable to earnings factors for the tax year 1988-89 or for subsequent tax years, be increased in accordance with the requirements of section 105 of the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(5) A person’s retirement pension is not to be reduced under regulation D13 to less than the aggregate of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) any minimum rate of equivalent pension benefits applicable under the Insurance Act, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) the annual rate obtained by multiplying one eightieth of the person’s pensionable remuneration by the length in years of the whole period of the person’s membership in contracted-out employment during the period beginning with the relevant date and ending with 30th April 1995. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(6)[^key-02604e66eb3ac1a1501cc90cc511a91c] In paragraph (5) “the relevant date” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) in the case of a man, 17th May 1990; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) in the case of a woman, 6th April 1978. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(7)[^key-02604e66eb3ac1a1501cc90cc511a91c] Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) if these regulations had come into force on 17th May 1990, a man would have been entitled to make an election under regulation D9(3), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) he gives notice of such election within six months of the commencement date, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

the election may be expressed to have the effect as if these regulations had commenced on 17th May 1990 and the notice had been given on the first day on which he would in that case have been entitled to give notice of the election. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(8)[^key-02604e66eb3ac1a1501cc90cc511a91c] Where a person’s local government employment is or was contracted-out employment, a surrender under regulation D14 (together with any previous surrenders) shall not result— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) in the annual rate of the retirement pension being less than one eightieth of the pensionable remuneration, multiplied by the length in years of the whole period of his membership in contracted-out employment after 5th April 1978, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) (disregarding the effect of the preceding paragraphs) in the weekly rate of the retirement pension being less than his guaranteed minimum, if any. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(9)[^key-02604e66eb3ac1a1501cc90cc511a91c] Where a person making a surrender under regulation D14— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) is in local government employment, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) has attained normal retirement age, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c) has a statutory pension entitlement, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

references in paragraph (8) to the retirement pension are references to the retirement pension which would become payable if he were to cease to hold his employment on the day on which the surrender takes effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(10)[^key-02604e66eb3ac1a1501cc90cc511a91c] Where this regulation applies it overrides any provision in these regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] or the 2002 Regulations or the Transitional Regulations[^key-d4ec0e85c174f7e3829e1095c32ea390] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] to the extent to which it conflicts with it, except— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) regulation D15 and Part I of Schedule D5 (reduction of retirement pension in the case of certain re-employed pensioners) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] or regulation 112 or 113 of the 2002 Regulations (abatement) or any provision of the Transitional Regulations relating to those provisions[^key-77d5d473dee7bd5e18eb9524c091d6f7] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111];
- (b) regulation H4 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] or regulation 114 of the 2002 Regulations or regulation 117 of those Regulations (so far as it relates to regulation 114)[^key-962f45e765cccff63f601c3f1c61de03] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] (forfeiture of rights); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c) regulation H5 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] or regulation 51 of the 2002 Regulations.[^key-4dde1e1aaf84d50c0da5a1ccd8570479] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] (commutation of small pensions). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
