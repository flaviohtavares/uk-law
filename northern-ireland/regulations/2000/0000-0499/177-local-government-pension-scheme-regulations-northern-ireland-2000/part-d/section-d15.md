# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section D15

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## D15. Adjustments to retirement pensions and grants for certain re-employed pensioners (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-9362e85dee77a32021ced02d8d2adc74][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

Schedule D5 shall have effect for the purpose of making provision as to the retirement benefits in respect of certain pensioners who are re-employed by LGPS employers; and the provisions of this Part have effect subject to Part I of that Schedule (reduction of retirement pensions), Part II of that Schedule (combined benefits) and Part III of that Schedule (separate benefits) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] or, as the case may be, the corresponding provisions of the 2002 Regulations (in accordance with Schedule 2 to the Transitional Regulations)[^c-d3b5b041a9902c67306130ae510a579b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111].
