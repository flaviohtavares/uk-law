# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section D14

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## D14. Surrender of part of retirement pension in favour of spouse or dependant (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-c7e5ab2692d030555eabf119bd64afd0][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to the following provisions of this regulation and to regulation D16, a person who— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) has become entitled to receive payments in respect of a retirement pension, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) holds local government employment and has attained normal retirement age, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

may surrender, as from the relevant date, in favour of his spouse or any dependant of his (“the beneficiary”), a part of the retirement pension which is or may become payable to him, and a person who has surrendered part of a retirement pension may surrender further parts of it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) The part of the retirement pension surrendered on any occasion— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) shall be an exact number of pounds, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) shall secure for the beneficiary a pension of at least £97·50 per annum. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) The surrendered part (together with any parts previously surrendered) shall not exceed— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) the amount which would result in the reduction of the retirement pension to less than the rate of the pension which would become payable to the beneficiary, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) one third of the retirement pension. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(4) Where a person who has made a surrender under this regulation dies, an annual pension at a rate which is (according to tables to be prepared from time to time by the Government Actuary) actuarially equivalent at the relevant date to the value of the surrendered part of the retirement pension becomes payable to the beneficiary. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(5) For the purposes of this regulation “the relevant date” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) the date of becoming entitled to receive payments in respect of the retirement pension; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) in the case of a person who dies while still in local government employment, the day immediately before the date of his death. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(6) Where the person surrendering holds local government employment and has attained normal retirement age, references in paragraphs (2) and (3) to the retirement pension are references to the retirement pension which would become payable if he were to cease to hold his employment on the day on which the surrender takes effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(7) Schedule D4 shall have effect for the purpose of making further provision as respects the procedure for surrenders under this regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
