# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section D5

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## D5. Retirement on or after NRD (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-d4d49a43549a24ac7765d70e09d2412e][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

Subject to the following provisions of this Part, if a member who ceases to hold a local government employment— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) has a statutory pension entitlement, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) has attained normal retirement age, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

he is entitled— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (i) to a standard retirement pension, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] to a standard retirement grant, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

which are payable immediately on his ceasing to hold that employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
