# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section D11

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## D11. Entitlement to deferred retirement benefits (“preserved benefits”) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-41907cf2ceda72e54413eb24db1f7f8f][^c-27388683eff66fd10338f53ae026adda][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-41907cf2ceda72e54413eb24db1f7f8f] If a member who ceases to hold a local government employment, is not entitled under regulation D5, D6, D7 or D9 to retirement benefits which are payable immediately on his ceasing to hold that employment, has a statutory pension entitlement, then, subject to regulation D13, he becomes entitled in relation to that employment to a standard retirement pension and a standard retirement grant payable from the appropriate date; and in these regulations benefits to which a person becomes entitled under this paragraph and which have not yet become payable are called “preserved benefits”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) For the purposes of paragraph (1) “the appropriate date”, in relation to any person, is his 65th birthday or, if earlier, the earliest of the following— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a)[^c-41907cf2ceda72e54413eb24db1f7f8f] his NRD; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b)[^c-41907cf2ceda72e54413eb24db1f7f8f] any date on which he becomes permanently incapable, by reason of ill-health or infirmity of mind or body, of discharging efficiently the duties of the employment he has ceased to hold; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c)[^c-41907cf2ceda72e54413eb24db1f7f8f] any date after he has attained the age of 50 years from which the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] the Committee[^c-ff2e7232dab05d14638474d1eeb63e2b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111], [^c-54eb013245ed1cbfedc765469f70b5ca]... *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] determines on compassionate grounds that the benefits are to become payable; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (d)[^c-41907cf2ceda72e54413eb24db1f7f8f] in the case of a person who has attained the age of 60 years and has completed 10 years' membership provided that he was a contributory employee before 1st April 1972, that birthday; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (e)[^c-32baebebe68525cb1c962b737e04729a] in the case of a person who— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (i) has ceased the employment under which the preserved benefits were accrued (but may be in local government employment unrelated to the preserved benefits); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] has duly elected to receive payment from a date on or after the date upon which that person attains the age of 55, the date so elected. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
