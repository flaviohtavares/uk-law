# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section D1

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## D1. “Pensionable remuneration” (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-5209d6ac5a1dc6fedc33b0117ba350d9][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A person’s pensionable remuneration, in relation to a local government employment, is his remuneration for so much of the relevant period as he is entitled to count as a period of membership in relation to that employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) For the purposes of this regulation, the relevant period is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) the year ending with the day on which the person ceases to be a member; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) such other period as in accordance with Schedule D1 is to be regarded as the relevant period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) Paragraph (1) has effect subject to the further provisions concerning pensionable remuneration in that Schedule and in Schedule C4 (limitations on contributions and benefits). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
