# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section D12

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## D12. Further provisions about preserved benefits (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-2bc4f18501ae527544984072fcfe24da][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A person who is entitled to preserved benefits under regulation D11(1) ceases to be entitled to them— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (i) the whole of the aggregate amount of his contributions to the fund has been returned to him (with or without interest) under regulation C21 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] or regulation 88 of the 2002 Regulations[^key-67425d5074e9ccd32ed5699003a12567] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] or regulation C15 of the 1992 regulations or regulation 16 of the 1981 regulations, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] after receiving the return of contributions, he has no further right to count any period of membership to which a transfer value accepted under regulation K14 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] or regulation 124 of the 2002 Regulations[^key-930aafca5c7df45e3cc4e3d240c92e14] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] or regulation J8 of the 1992 regulations or regulation 69 of the 1981 regulations relates, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) if rights in respect of the period of membership he was entitled to count in relation to the employment he ceased to hold have been transferred to a non-local government scheme, a personal pension scheme, a self-employed pension arrangement, a retirement annuity contract or an appropriate policy by virtue of the payment of a transfer value, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c) if he re-enters local government employment (unless he elects to remain entitled to those benefits), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (d) if the body which employed him in the employment which he ceased to hold certifies under paragraph 4 of Schedule D1 that on ceasing to hold it he suffered a material reduction in remuneration. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) An election by a person for the purposes of paragraph (1)(c) shall be made by giving notice in writing to the Committee— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) before the expiry of the period of three months beginning with the date on which he re-enters local government employment, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) such longer period as the Committee may allow. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3)[^key-19d647be6ceb1dfa1e55ac2f78e81705] In the case of a member who first becomes a member on or after 2nd April 2001 any period of membership in the employment of a non-associated admission body (as defined in regulation B6(16)(c)) shall not be aggregated with any other periods of membership for the purpose of calculating his retirement grant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
