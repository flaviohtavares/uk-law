# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section D10

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## D10. No double entitlement (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-c70d111cbe12ccc0f043240096c0b204][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where (apart from this regulation) any person would be entitled to a retirement pension or to a retirement grant under two or more regulations in respect of the same period of membership— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) he may elect by notice in writing, given to the Committee before the expiry of the period of three months beginning with the day on which he becomes so entitled, under which provision he is to be paid those benefits; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) if he makes no such election, the Committee may notify him in writing of the provision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) Paragraph (1) is without prejudice to regulations D16, D18 and D19 and section 9(6) of the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^f00023].
