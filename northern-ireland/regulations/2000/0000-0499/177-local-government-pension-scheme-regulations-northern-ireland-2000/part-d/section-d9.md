# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section D9

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## D9. Retirement at or after 60 but before NRD (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-b226e9322441a1d37fd0f4e01fe3f3c0][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to the following provisions of this Part, if a member who ceases to hold a local government employment after attaining the age of 60 but before his NRD satisfies the relevant conditions, he is entitled— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) to a standard retirement pension (reduced in accordance with regulation D13), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) to a standard retirement grant (so reduced), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

which are payable immediately on his ceasing to hold that employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) The conditions referred to in paragraph (1) are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) that the member has a statutory pension entitlement; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) that he duly makes an election for his retirement benefits to be payable immediately on his ceasing local government employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) An election under this regulation shall be made by notice in writing to the Committee given within the period of three months beginning with the day on which the member ceases to hold local government employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
