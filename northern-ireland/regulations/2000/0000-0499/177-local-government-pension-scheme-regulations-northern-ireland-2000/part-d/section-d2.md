# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section D2

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## D2. “Standard retirement pension” and “standard retirement grant” (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-9a3295c5bf1dc62b52a270ca1b52485f][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraphs (2), (3) and (4),[^c-949f1eb2f114bc16910760155842e03e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] in relation to any person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) “standard retirement pension” means a pension payable at an annual rate equal to one eightieth of his pensionable remuneration, multiplied by the length in years of his total period of membership; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) “standard retirement grant” means a lump sum of an amount equal to three eightieths of his pensionable remuneration, multiplied by the length in years of his total period of membership. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) In the case of a person who— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) is entitled under regulation B15 or C9 or paragraph 2( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*a*) or 4 of Schedule C5 to count an additional period as a period of membership, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) had at the appropriate time (within the meaning of regulation C10(7)) attained the age of 45 years, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

then— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (i) the standard retirement pension is increased by one two hundred and fortieth of his pensionable remuneration, multiplied by the length in years of that additional period of membership, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] that additional period of membership is excluded from his total period of membership for the purposes of calculating the standard retirement grant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) Paragraphs (1) and (2) are subject to regulations D7(2), D13, D16(5), D18(1), paragraph 3 of Schedule B1 and Schedule C4 (limitations on contributions and benefits); and paragraph (1)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*b*) is subject to the provisions of Schedule D2 which relates to the reduction in retirement grants on account of contingent spouses' pensions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(4)[^c-168aa73f3eb28fd14e149e6b2eeabfe4] In the case of a pension debit member the “standard retirement pension” and, if not already paid, the “standard retirement grant” shall be reduced in accordance with guidance issued by the Government Actuary to take into account the debit to which the shareable rights of the pension debit member are subject under a pension sharing order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
