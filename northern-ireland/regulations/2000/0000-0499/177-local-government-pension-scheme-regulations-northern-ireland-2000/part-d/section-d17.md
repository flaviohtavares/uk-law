# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section D17

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## D17. Revaluation of guaranteed minimum in certain cases (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-c72e4edfa8fdfe7dc819c282a8fbdb95][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) a person has ceased to be a member; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) the guaranteed minimum in relation to his pension is appropriately secured (within the meaning of section 15 of the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) The earnings factors of such a person shall be determined for the purposes of section 10(2) of that Act— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) by reference to the last order under article 23 of the Social Security Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1975 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^f00024] or section 130 of the Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^f00025] to come into force before the end of the tax year in which he ceased to be a member, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) without reference to the last such order to come into force before the end of the final relevant year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) In this regulation “final relevant year” has the meaning given in section 12(5) of the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
