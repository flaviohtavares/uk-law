# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, SCHEDULE D4

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE D4 (affected by repeal, see note) — Procedure for surrender of part of retirement pension (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

Regulation D14(7) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

**1.**[^c-e28fcc959299d0a89f9422214b8e9cbc] The Committee shall send to each person who becomes eligible to surrender part of his retirement pension, on his first becoming so eligible, a notice stating— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) that provision has been made by these regulations for the surrender of part of a retirement pension to a spouse or dependant; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) that he may, on application to the Committee, obtain further information on the subject. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

**2.**[^c-d6e8884b9a17b05c3bc618b37a2afbc7] (1) Subject to sub-paragraph (3), where a person wishes to make a surrender he may within the relevant period notify the Committee in writing of his wish to surrender a part of that retirement pension. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) In sub-paragraph (1) “the relevant period” in relation to a member or former member, means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) in the case of a person who on ceasing to hold his employment becomes entitled to a retirement pension (in this Schedule referred to as “a retiring employee”) not more than two months before or within one month after the date on which he ceases to be employed; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) in the case of a person who would, if he were to retire from his employment, become entitled to a retirement pension (in this Schedule referred to as “a continuing employee”), within two months before or at any time after becoming a continuing employee and while he is still employed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) Where the Committee is satisfied that it has not been reasonably practicable for a retiring employee to notify his wish to surrender a part of a retirement pension within the relevant period owing to circumstances beyond his control, it may extend that period to a date not more than six months after the date on which he ceases to be employed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

**3.**[^c-47ff3bd101b9ffec01dd82f55c11a996] (1) Subject to sub-paragraphs (2) and (3), where the Committee receives a notification given by a person under paragraph 2, it shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) immediately arrange for him to be examined at his own expense by a registered medical practitioner nominated by it and for a report to be given to it by the practitioner stating whether, in his opinion that person is in good health for his age; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) require the person to furnish at his own expense— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (i) a certificate of his birth (except where the date of birth has been duly recorded by the Committee and is not disputed); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] where the beneficiary is the person’s spouse, the spouse’s birth certificate and their marriage certificate; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] where the beneficiary is a dependant, the dependant’s birth certificate and such evidence as may be appropriate to prove dependency, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

  and any other information or evidence which the Committee considers necessary. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) Where the report under sub-paragraph (1)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*a*) is to the effect that the person is not in good health, the Committee shall notify him accordingly and offer him an opportunity of a further examination at his own expense by some other registered medical practitioner nominated by it with a view to that practitioner reporting to it on the state of the person’s health. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) If for any reason a birth or marriage certificate cannot be supplied as mentioned in sub-paragraph (1), the Committee may accept such other evidence of birth or marriage as it thinks fit in order to determine the age or, as the case may be, the marital status of the person concerned. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

**4.**[^c-814a607d05096390ff18b8e154142ae9] (1) Subject to the provisions of this Schedule, unless *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) the Committee is of the opinion, on consideration of a report obtained by it under paragraph 3, that the person to whom the report relates is not in good health; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) it is of the opinion that the evidence produced concerning marriage or dependency is not satisfactory, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

it shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (i) allow the surrender of such part of the retirement pension as is specified in the person’s notification and as is in conformity with this Schedule; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] grant to the beneficiary named in the notification a pension, payable in the event of the beneficiary’s surviving the person and to be calculated in accordance with regulation D14(4). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) a person is named as spouse in the notification given under paragraph 2; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) the Committee is dissatisfied with the evidence of marriage, but is satisfied, on the evidence already before it or after making such further inquiries as it thinks necessary, that the named person is a dependant of the person who gave the notification, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

it shall treat the notification as if the person named in it as spouse had been named as a dependant of the person giving the notification. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) A decision by the Committee to allow a surrender by a retiring employee shall not be made before the date on which he ceases to be employed, and a decision by the Committee to allow a surrender by a continuing employee shall not be made before the date on which he becomes such an employee. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(4) As soon as is reasonably practicable after deciding whether to allow a surrender of any part of the retirement pension of a person who has given it a notification under paragraph 2, the Committee shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) notify him of its decision; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) if it has allowed a surrender, furnish him— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (i) with a statement as to the amount of the pension to which the beneficiary may become entitled after his death; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] if the person who gave the notification under paragraph 2 is a retiring employee, with a statement as to the amount of the reduced retirement pension to which he is entitled. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(5) A notification of a decision not to allow a surrender shall state the grounds for the decision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(6) A notification sent to a person under sub-paragraph (4) shall, if it has been posted in a prepaid envelope addressed to him, be deemed to have been received by him at the time at which a letter would be delivered in the ordinary course of post. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

**5.**[^c-4e06bb63753bfb3ef5f6c782b5526165] A person who has given a notification under paragraph 2 of his wish to surrender a part of his retirement pension may, at any time before he has received notification from the Committee of its decision, cancel or amend that notification by a notice in writing addressed to the Committee and posted in a prepaid envelope to, or left at its offices. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

**6.**[^c-a50c1c2abce7eee664a0a17d95736a65] (1) A notification given by a person under paragraph 2 shall become null and void if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) the beneficiary dies before the person receives notification from the Committee that his surrender has been allowed; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) the person dies at any time before midnight on the day on which the Committee decides to allow the surrender. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) Subject to sub-paragraph (1), a surrender allowed in pursuance of a notification given by a person shall have effect as from the date on which the person ceases to hold his employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
