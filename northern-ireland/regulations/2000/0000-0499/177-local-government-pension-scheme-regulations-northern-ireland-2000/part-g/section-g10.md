# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section G10

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## G10. Children over 17 in paid training (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-2065a7c59200d787734be355db358e6a][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) If a child in respect of whom a children’s long-term pension is payable has attained the age of 17 years and is receiving remuneration in respect of full-time training for a trade, profession or vocation at an annual rate in excess of the indexed training rate, then— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) the annual rate of the pension is to be reduced by the amount of the excess, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) if it results in a smaller reduction, the child is to be disregarded for the purpose of calculating the pension. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) In paragraph (1) “the indexed training rate” means the annual rate at which an official pension (within the meaning of the Pensions (Increase) Act (Northern Ireland) 1971 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^f00029]) would for the time being be payable if it had begun on 1st April 1994 and had then been payable at an annual rate of £1,450. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
