# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section G3

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## G3. Member’s children’s long-term pension (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-e1a6d9befc0158e2eb3b9d427263e4cd][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (6), where a member dies leaving one or more eligible children, the eligible children are entitled until they cease to be children to or to the benefit of a children’s long-term pension payable from the member’s death. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) Subject to paragraph (5) and regulations G9 and G10, the annual amount of a children’s long-term pension is the appropriate fraction of the deceased person’s retirement pension. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) Subject to regulation G10, for the purposes of paragraph (2), “the appropriate fraction” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) if there is one eligible child and a surviving spouse’s pension under Part F is for the time being payable, one quarter; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) if there is one eligible child and no such surviving spouse’s pension is payable, one third; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c) if there are two or more eligible children and a surviving spouse’s pension under Part F is for the time being payable, one half; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (d) if there are two or more eligible children and no such surviving spouse’s pension is payable, two thirds; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

but references in paragraphs ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*a*) to ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*d*) to a surviving spouse’s pension do not include a short-term pension under regulation F1 in circumstances where no long-term pension is payable under regulation F2. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(4) Subject to regulation G9, for the purposes of paragraph (2) the retirement pension of a member shall be taken to be the pension to which he would have become entitled if he had at the time of his death become entitled to a pension under regulation D7 (ill-health, etc.) and on the relevant assumptions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(5) In sub-paragraph (4) “the relevant assumptions” means that there is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) no increase in the pension by virtue of regulation D2(2)(i), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) no reduction in the pension under regulation D13 (early payments), regulation H2 (national insurance), or Part I of Schedule D5 (re-employed pensioners) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] or, as the case may be, regulation 113 (abatement) of the 2002 Regulations[^c-744a2844c6a437125e33686dc5060652] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111], or by virtue of a surrender under regulation D14. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(6) Where a children’s short-term pension— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) is payable under regulation G4, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) would be so payable apart from regulation G8(2), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

the children’s long-term pension does not become payable until the expiry of the period in respect of which that pension is payable under regulation G4 or, as the case may be, would be so payable apart from regulation G8(2) (but subject to regulation G8(3) and (4)). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
