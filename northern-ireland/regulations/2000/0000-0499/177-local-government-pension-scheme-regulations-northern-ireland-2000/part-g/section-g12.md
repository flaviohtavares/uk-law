# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section G12

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## G12. Adjustments to children’s pensions for certain re-employed pensioners etc. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-ed5a82cc08f33ca070e28a19432eec99][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

The provisions of this Part have effect subject to Part IV of Schedule D5 (re-employed pensioners) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] or, as the case may be, regulation 50 of the 2002 Regulations (in accordance with the Transitional Regulations)[^c-b6e0a87a58a8bcf36d6c3e9f98dba4aa] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] and to paragraph 3 of Schedule F1 (women dying before 1st April 1972). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
