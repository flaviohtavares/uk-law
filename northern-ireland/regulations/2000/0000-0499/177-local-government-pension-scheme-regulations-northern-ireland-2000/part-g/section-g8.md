# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section G8

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## G8. Limitations on entitlement to children’s short-term pensions (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-50fa56a6bc5164385e8a23d46c9149b7][^key-873c51c31c59fd07eefa0b819c116693][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-50fa56a6bc5164385e8a23d46c9149b7] Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) a widow’s short-term pension is calculated in accordance with regulation F6(1) or a widower’s short-term pension is payable by virtue of regulation F1(1) or F4(1), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) the child who is entitled to or to the benefit of a children’s short-term pension is in the care of the widow or widower, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

the rate of the child’s short-term pension is reduced by the rate of the widow’s or widower’s pension. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2)[^key-50fa56a6bc5164385e8a23d46c9149b7] While— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) a widow’s short-term pension is payable under regulation F1(1) or F4(1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) a widower’s short-term pension is payable under either of those regulations at a rate not less than that of the children’s short-term pension; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c) a widower’s short-term pension is payable under regulation F1(1) in circumstances where no long-term pension is payable under regulation F2(1), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

a children’s short-term pension is not payable unless the child is not in the care of the widow or widower, and then for only three months after the death (or until he ceases to be a child, if sooner). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) (apart from paragraph (2), or this paragraph, or paragraph (4)) a children’s short-term pension would be payable to or for the benefit of a child in the care of a parent to whom a surviving spouse’s short-term pension would be payable; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) if a children’s long-term pension and a surviving spouse’s long-term pension were payable instead of those short-term pensions, that would result in a greater aggregate pension (having regard to paragraph (2)), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

those long-term pensions shall be payable instead of those short-term pensions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(4)[^key-50fa56a6bc5164385e8a23d46c9149b7] Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) apart from paragraph (3) and this paragraph, a children’s short-term pension would be payable to or for the benefit of a child who is not in the care of a parent to whom a surviving spouse’s short-term pension is payable, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) a children’s long-term pension would be greater, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

the long-term pension shall be payable instead of the short-term pension. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
