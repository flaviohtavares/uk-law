# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section G2

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## G2. Meaning of “eligible child” (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-686ec411cbd0122c249f0a3b1c32033b][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of these regulations a child is an eligible child of a deceased person who was in a local government employment when he died and was then a member or a former member, if he is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) the deceased’s legitimate child born before the first anniversary of the member’s or former member’s death, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) the deceased’s adopted child, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c) the deceased’s step-child or illegitimate child, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (d) an adopted child of a person who has been married to the deceased, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (e) a child accepted by the deceased as a member of the family, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

and, in the case of a child within paragraph ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*c*), (*d*) or ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*e*), is wholly or mainly dependent on the deceased at the time of his death. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) For the purposes of these regulations a child is an eligible child of a person who has died after becoming entitled to a retirement pension if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) he is a legitimate child of a marriage of the deceased which took place before the date on which he became entitled to the pension, and was born before the first anniversary of that date, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) he is a child adopted by the deceased before he became entitled to the pension, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c) he is a child who was wholly or mainly dependent on the deceased both before he became entitled to the retirement pension and at the time of his death and is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (i) the deceased’s step-child or illegitimate child, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] an adopted child of a person who married the deceased before he became entitled to the pension, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] a child accepted by the deceased as a member of the family. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
