# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section G1

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## G1. Meaning of “child” (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-d4c3eb48e4e4f6d0789f969168b2f9a6][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

- For the purposes of these regulations “child” means a person who— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (a) has not attained the age of 17 years, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (b) has attained that age and has since been— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
    - (i) receiving full-time education, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
    - (ii) undergoing a full-time course of training of not less than 2 years' duration for a trade, profession or vocation, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

    either continuously or continuously with the exception of a period which the Committee has in its discretion decided to disregard (on being satisfied that his education or training ought not to be regarded as completed), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (c) has attained that age and is incapacitated by reason of ill-health or infirmity of mind or body which arose either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
    - (i) before he attained that age, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
    - (ii) while receiving such full-time education or training, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
    - (iii) during a period which the Committee has decided to disregard under paragraph ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*b*).
