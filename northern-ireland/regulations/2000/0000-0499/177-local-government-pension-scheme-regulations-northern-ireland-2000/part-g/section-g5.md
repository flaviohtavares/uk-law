# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section G5

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## G5. Deferred pensioner’s children’s long-term pension (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-4720e6e29f50b96917c200fcff4c6195][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where a person who is not a member dies leaving one or more eligible children and at the time of his death he— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) was entitled to preserved benefits, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) was in a local government employment and would have been so entitled if he had ceased to hold that employment immediately before that time, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

those children are entitled to or to the benefit of a children’s long-term pension until they cease to be children. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) Subject to regulations G9 and G10, the annual amount of a children’s long-term pension is the appropriate fraction (within the meaning of regulation G3(3)) of the deceased person’s retirement pension. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) Subject to regulation G9, for the purposes of paragraph (2) the retirement pension of a person shall be taken to be the retirement pension to which he would have become entitled if at the time of his death he had become entitled under regulation D5 (retirement on or after NRD)and on the relevant assumptions (within the meaning of regulation G3(5)). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
