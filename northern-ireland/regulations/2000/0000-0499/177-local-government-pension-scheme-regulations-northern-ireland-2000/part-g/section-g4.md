# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section G4

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## G4. Member’s children’s short-term pension (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-7308a6bc285742b17838a9e6736d9a02][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to regulation G8, where a member dies leaving one or more eligible children, they are entitled to or to the benefit of a children’s short-term pension for 6 months after the death or, if less, until they cease to be children. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) Subject to regulation G8, the annual rate of a children’s short-term pension is the rate at which a surviving spouse’s short-term pension would have been payable by virtue of regulation F1. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
