# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section G9

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## G9. Long-term pensions: increase of period of membership in certain cases (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-a1d4030beb95adb296040c7ca678cd7c][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

Unless the retirement pension determined under regulation G3(4), G5(3) or G6(3) would be greater, the retirement pension of a deceased person shall be taken to be that to which he would have been entitled if he were entitled to count the lesser of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) 10 years of membership; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) the period of membership which he would have been entitled to count if he had continued to be a member until his 65th birthday. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
