# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section G6

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## G6. Pensioner’s children’s long-term pension (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-ed275e3d1edb96cfb936df6fb09a8fbc][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (4), where a person who— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) was entitled to receive payments in respect of a retirement pension (other than a pension sole entitlement to which is under regulation D16 or a pension under regulation D18), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) would have been so entitled but for the operation of regulation H6 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] or, as the case may be, regulation 52 of the 2002 Regulations[^c-e346a7bcb4d7b53549c6f261b9f455d1] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] (commutation in exceptional circumstances of ill-health) or Part I of Schedule D5 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] or, as the case may be, regulation 113 (abatement) of the 2002 Regulations[^c-ff8301fffe22529f341e6bd8014822f7] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] (re-employed pensioners), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

dies leaving one or more eligible children, they are entitled until they cease to be children to or to the benefit of a children’s long-term pension payable from the death. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) Subject to regulations G9 and G10, the annual amount of a children’s long-term pension is the appropriate fraction (within the meaning of regulation G3(3)) of the deceased person’s retirement pension *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] which if the deceased person was a pension debit member, is calculated by reference to the retirement pension to which the deceased would have been entitled had his shareable rights not been subject to a pension debit[^c-324c1c182a92776b634871c74121abbf] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111].

(3) Subject to regulation G9, for the purposes of paragraph (2) the retirement pension of a person who is entitled at the time of his death to receive payments in respect of a retirement pension (or would have been so entitled apart from regulation H6 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] or, as the case may be, regulation 52 of the 2002 Regulations[^c-21d0b6c4d844624980e5d3173438385c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] or Part I of Schedule D5 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] or, as the case may be, regulation 113 (abatement) of the 2002 Regulations[^c-8921322553c498a105beb68c9c9b4462] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]) shall be taken to be the retirement pension that would have been payable on the assumption that no payment under regulation H6 had been made and on the relevant assumptions (within the meaning of regulation G3(5)). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(4) In a case where a children’s short-term pension is payable under regulation G7, the children’s long-term pension does not become payable until the expiry of the period in respect of which that pension is payable under that regulation (or would be so payable if it were not for payment being suspended under regulation G8(2)). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
