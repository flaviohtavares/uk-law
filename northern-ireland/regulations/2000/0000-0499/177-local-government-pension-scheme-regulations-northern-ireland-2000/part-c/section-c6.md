# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section C6

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## C6. Maternity absence (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-3098a4eee3b4251c894b8f4d2d4b6a14][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) If a person who is a member or has applied to be a member goes on maternity leave, she must make contributions at the standard contribution rate, as respects any part of her period of maternity absence for which she is a member and entitled to receive pay (including any statutory maternity pay payable to her under the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992), on that pay. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) That pay includes any such statutory pay but not any amount by which her actual pay is reduced on account of her possible entitlement to such statutory pay. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) If a person who is a member or has applied to be a member– *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) goes on ordinary maternity leave; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) is not entitled to receive pay (including statutory maternity pay payable to her under the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act (Northern Ireland) 1992) for all or any part of that period of leave, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

for these Regulations she shall be treated as if she had paid contributions under paragraph (1) for the unpaid period of that ordinary maternity leave and on the pay that she would have received during that period but for her absence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(4) If– *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) a person who is a member or has applied to be a member is on maternity leave, other than ordinary maternity leave; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) for the whole or part of the period of her maternity absence (“the unpaid period”) she is not entitled to receive pay (including any statutory maternity pay payable to her under the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992) but is a member, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

she may elect to make contributions at the standard contribution rate as respects the unpaid period, as if her pay in the employment were equal to the pay she was entitled to receive immediately before the unpaid period begun (including any such statutory pay, but not any amount by which her actual pay is reduced on account of her possible entitlement to such statutory pay). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(5) An election under paragraph (4) shall be made by notice in writing to the employing authority given before the expiry date of the period of 30 days beginning with the earlier of– *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) the day on which the member returns to duty; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) the day on which she ceases to be employed by that employing authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
