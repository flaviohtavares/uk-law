# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section C7

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## C7. Absence owing to trade dispute (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-a4143e872ec181f0c664595b480192c7][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies to a person who— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) has been absent from duty, otherwise than on leave of absence, for a period of one or more days during and in consequence of a trade dispute, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) was a member immediately before— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (i) that period, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] where two or more periods of absence occurred in consequence of a single trade dispute, the first of those periods; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

  and in this regulation a period for which a person to whom this regulation applies was so absent is referred to as a “relevant absence”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) Subject to paragraph (4) and regulation C26, if a person to whom this regulation applies gives notice in writing that he wishes this paragraph to apply (or, in a case within paragraph (7), his personal representatives do so), an amount equal to 16 per cent. of the difference between— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) the person’s remuneration (if any) for the relevant contribution period, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) the remuneration he would have received for that period if it had not included any relevant absence or part of a relevant absence, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

is payable in respect of the relevant contribution period to the authority to whom notice was given; and in this regulation “relevant contribution period” means a period which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (i) is coextensive with one of the intervals at which a person to whom this regulation applies was required under regulation C3 to make standard contributions, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] includes all or part of a relevant absence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) An authority shall pay to the fund any sum it receives by way of full or part payment of the amount mentioned in paragraph (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(4) Notice under paragraph (2) shall be given in writing to the authority which is or, as the case may be, was last the employing authority in relation to the person to whom the notice relates, before the expiry— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) of the period of three months beginning with the day after the last day of the relevant contribution period, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) where all or part of more than one relevant contribution period is included in a relevant absence which occurred in consequence of a single trade dispute, of the period of three months beginning with the last day of the last of the relevant contribution periods, in respect of which the notice is given, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c) in the case of a notice within paragraph (7), of the period of twelve months beginning with the date of the deceased employee’s death, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (d) within such longer period as the authority may allow; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

and, in any case where the notice relates to more than one relevant contribution period included in a relevant absence which occurred in consequence of a single trade dispute, the notice is of no effect unless it is given in respect of all the relevant contribution periods. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(5) A period of absence from duty without remuneration (otherwise than on leave of absence) does not count as a period of membership unless— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) it was a relevant absence, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) the amount specified in paragraph (2) has been paid in respect of every relevant contribution period all or part of which was included in that relevant absence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(6) Where the amount specified in paragraph (2) has been paid in respect of a relevant contribution period, so much of any relevant absence as was included in that period may count as a period of membership, whether or not a contract of employment continued to subsist during the relevant absence or any part of it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(7) Where a person to whom this regulation applies dies before the end of the period of three months specified in paragraph (4)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*a*) without giving notice under paragraph (2), his personal representatives may give that notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(8) For the purposes of paragraph (1)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (i) a person’s contract of employment is terminated in consequence of a trade dispute, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] not later than the day after the end of the trade dispute, he again becomes an employee of the same LGPS employer and a member, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

  notwithstanding the termination, he is to be treated as having been absent from duty; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) it is immaterial whether or not— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (i) the person was participating in or financing or otherwise directly interested in the trade dispute, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] the employing authority was a party to the trade dispute. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(9) In this regulation “trade dispute” has the meaning given by Article 2(4) and (7) of the Industrial Relations (Northern Ireland) Order 1992 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^f00013].
