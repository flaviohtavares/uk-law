# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section C20

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## C20. Effect of opting out of membership on certain additional payments (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-51d1533c54912058c12a245b3ec2f599][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where a member— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) is making additional periodical payments by virtue of having made an election under regulation C9(1), C13 or C14; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) gives notification in accordance with regulation B11 (leaving the Scheme); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

then— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (i) no further additional periodical payments shall be payable from the day on which he ceases to be a member; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] the benefits to which he is entitled in relation to those payments shall be calculated in accordance with regulation C19(7) and (8) as if the payments had been discontinued under regulation C18. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) Paragraph (1) shall not preclude a person who has elected under regulation B11 making a further election under regulation C9(1), C13 or C14 after again becoming a member. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
