# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section C17

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## C17. No elections to make payments after 64 (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-7dbbe8df84bef0ed3be28ae2ac79329e][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

A person may not make an election under regulation C9, C13 or C14 if he has attained the age of 64. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
