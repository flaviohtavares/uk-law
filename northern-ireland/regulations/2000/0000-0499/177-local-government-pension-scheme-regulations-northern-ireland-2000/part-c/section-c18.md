# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section C18

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## C18. Notice to discontinue payments (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-84adee10d1b937ae03edd4ca10778f8e][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

Payment in accordance with regulation C9(2) or C16(2) may be discontinued if the member notifies the Committee and the employing authority in writing that he wishes it to be discontinued. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
