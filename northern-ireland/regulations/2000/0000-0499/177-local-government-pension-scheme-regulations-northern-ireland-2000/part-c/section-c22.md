# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section C22

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## C22. Meaning of “aggregate amount of contributions” for purposes of regulation C21 (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-ce5d18cc5fd92ebd6f02e38d7c6f2ebe][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) In regulation C21 references to the aggregate amount of a person’s contributions to the fund include references to the contributions and amounts specified in paragraph (2) in so far as they— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) have not been returned to the person or, if returned, have subsequently been repaid by him, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) are attributable to a period of membership which might have counted under these regulations in relation to the employment he has ceased to hold, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c) are not attributable to any earlier period of membership in respect of which a benefit has been paid under Part D, E, F or G of these regulations or the corresponding provisions of the 1992 regulations or the 1981 regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) The contributions and amounts mentioned in paragraph (1) are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) any contributions or payments paid by him to the fund— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (i) under regulation C3, C5 or C6, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] under regulation C1 or C2 of the 1992 regulations, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) any amount paid by him— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (i) by way of additional contributory payments or added period payments, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] under regulation C7 or C13, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] under regulation C3, C6, C7 or C8 of the 1992 regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
