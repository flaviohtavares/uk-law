# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section C24

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## C24. Additional voluntary contributions (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-7354395b32ed8c05aaafc0faf7c928ea][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to regulation C26 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] and C26A[^key-07bdb533910d9f23a13c782181186e34] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111], a member may at any time elect to pay contributions under this regulation in addition to those provided for by the previous regulations in this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) Schedule C3 shall have effect— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) in relation to such an election and in relation to contributions payable under this regulation; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) in relation to receipt of a transfer value in respect of an additional voluntary contributions provision or an additional voluntary contributions scheme (so far as permitted by regulation K14(2)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*b*)).

(3) In these regulations— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- “additional voluntary contributions provision” means a provision of an occupational pension scheme approved by the Commissioners of Inland Revenue under section 591 of the Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1988 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^f00021] (which provides for the payment by employees of voluntary contributions); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- “additional voluntary contributions scheme” means a scheme approved by the Commissioners of Inland Revenue under that section, to which an employer is not a contributor and which provides benefits additional to those provided by an occupational pension scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
