# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section C19

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## C19. Uncompleted periodical payments (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-d5aeffb83a3c762958f9244b5adec809][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) a member has made an election under regulation C9(1), C13 or C14 to make additional periodical payments, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) he has commenced payment, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c) before the presumed termination date a relevant event occurs, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (d) in the case of payments under regulation C9(1), any payment made to him under regulation C21 does not include the amount already paid by him under regulation C9(2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) In paragraph (1) “presumed termination date” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) in the case of an election under regulation C9(1), the member’s NRD, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) in the case of an election under regulation C13 or C14, the birthday specified under regulation C16(1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) For the purposes of this regulation the relevant events are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) the discontinuance of payment under regulation C18, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) where there has been no such discontinuance of payment— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (i) the member’s ceasing to hold his employment, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] the death of the member while in local government employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(4) Where the relevant event is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) the death of the member, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) his ceasing to hold his employment by reason of ill-health or infirmity of mind or body, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

he is to be treated as having completed payment in accordance with regulation C9(2) or, as the case may be, C15 and C16. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(5) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) the relevant event is the member’s ceasing to hold his employment, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) condition ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*a*) or ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*b*) in regulation D6(2) is satisfied, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c) his employment ends not less than 12 months after the date of receipt of his notice of election, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (d) he gives notice in writing for the purpose to the Committee not later than the expiry of the period of three months beginning on the day after the last day of his employment, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

then, if he pays to the fund, within the period of one month beginning on the date on which he is notified by the Committee of the amount calculated by the fund’s actuary to represent the capital value of the additional periodical payments remaining to be paid, a sum equal to that amount, he shall be treated as having completed payment in accordance with regulation C9(2) or, as the case may be, C15 and C16. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(6) The Committee may accept a notice given under paragraph (5)(d) notwithstanding that paragraph (5)(c) is not satisfied. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(7) Subject to paragraph (9), where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) the relevant event is discontinuance of payment under regulation C18, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) the relevant event is the member’s ceasing to hold his employment and neither paragraph (4)(b) nor paragraph (5) applies, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

the period of membership in respect of which the election was made is to be treated as having been the appropriate proportion of the period in respect of which it was originally made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(8) In paragraph (7) “appropriate proportion” means the proportion which the length of the period during which additional payments have been paid bears to the length of the period during which they were to have been paid (each period being expressed in complete years and any fraction of a year). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(9) Where, apart from this paragraph, paragraph (7)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*b*) would apply and the member, having elected to make additional payments under regulation C9(1), C13 or C14— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) has within 12 months after ceasing to hold his employment again entered local government employment, without having— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (i) become entitled in relation to the first employment to the payment of any benefit, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] received any payment under regulation C21 which includes the amount already paid by him under regulation C9, C13 or, as the case may be, C14 or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] made a request for earlier payment under regulation C21(6), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) has not made an election for the purposes of regulation D12(1)(c) (retention of right to preserved benefits), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c) within three months after his again entering local government employment pays to his new employing authority an amount equal to any additional periodical payments that would have been payable if he had not ceased to hold the first employment, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

then, the election under regulation C9(1), C13 or, as the case may be, C14 continues to have effect as if the relevant event had not occurred. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
