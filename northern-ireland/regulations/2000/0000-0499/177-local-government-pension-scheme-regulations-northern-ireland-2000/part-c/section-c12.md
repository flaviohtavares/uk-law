# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section C12

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## C12. Purchase by part-time employees of additional periods under regulation C9 (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-bb00b429a9bdd341889fbf9b7b11fb96][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to the following provisions of this regulation, where a person makes an election under regulation C9 in relation to a part-time employment to make additional payments in respect of a period— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) the period he may count as a period of membership under paragraph (1) of that regulation, is the appropriate fraction of the period he would be entitled to count in respect of that period if he were a whole-time employee (“the notional whole-time period”); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) the maximum which applies in his case by virtue of regulation C10, is the appropriate fraction of the maximum which would apply in his case if he were a whole-time employee; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c) the amount to be paid by him in accordance with regulation C11 shall be calculated in the same manner as if he were a whole-time employee purchasing the notional whole-time period, but taking his remuneration as his actual remuneration for the time being (and not the remuneration he would have been paid for a single comparable whole-time employment). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) In paragraph (1) “appropriate fraction”, in relation to an employee, means the fraction of which the numerator is the number of his contractual hours and the denominator is the number of contractual hours of a single comparable whole-time employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) any person has made an election under regulation C9 which has effect in relation to part-time employment; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) that employment ceases to be part-time and becomes whole-time employment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

then— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (i) he may continue to pay contributions under the election in relation to the whole-time employment at the same percentage of his pensionable remuneration as the contributions he paid in relation to the part-time employment; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] the additional period in respect of the contributions paid in relation to the whole-time employment shall be calculated on the same basis as if he had been in that whole-time employment when he made the election. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(4) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) any person has made an election under regulation C9 which has effect in relation to whole-time employment; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) that employment ceases to be whole-time and becomes part-time employment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

then— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (i) he may continue to pay contributions under the election in relation to the part-time employment at the same percentage of his pensionable remuneration as the contributions he paid in relation to the whole-time employment, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] the additional period in respect of the contributions paid in relation to the part-time employment shall be calculated on the same basis as if he had been in that part-time employment when he made the election. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(5) The previous provisions of this regulation do not apply to an election made by a person under regulation C5 of the 1992 regulations (or having effect as if so made) but, if he so elects by notice in writing to the Committee, then— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) payments made by him on and after the date when the election takes effect shall be made by reference to his actual remuneration for the time being; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) the period of membership which he is entitled to count by virtue of them shall be calculated on the same basis as if the previous provisions of this regulation had always applied in respect of his election. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(6) A person may not make an election under paragraph (5) after the beginning of the period of one year ending with his NRD. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
