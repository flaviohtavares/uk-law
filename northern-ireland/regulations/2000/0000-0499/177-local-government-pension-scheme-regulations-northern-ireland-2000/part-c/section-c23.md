# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section C23

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## C23. Effect of return to local government on right to a return of contributions (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-324838150fd45235d17e125f70d62d7f][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

A person’s right to a payment under regulation C21 is extinguished if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) after leaving local government employment with a right to such a payment he returns to such employment without having received the payment; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) he has not given written notice to the Committee— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (i) before the expiry of the period of three months beginning with the date on which he returns to such employment; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] within such longer period as the Committee may allow, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

  that he wishes to receive an immediate payment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
