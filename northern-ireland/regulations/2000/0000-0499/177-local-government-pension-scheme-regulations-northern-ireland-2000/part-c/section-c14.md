# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section C14

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## C14. Payments to increase widower’s pension by counting membership before 1st April 1972 (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-c9ab16180053df0e65487151fd3440b9][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to the following provisions of this regulation, a member who— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) is a married woman, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) is entitled to count a period of membership before 1st April 1972, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c) was not a member on 27th July 1989 or has not been a member continuously since that date, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

may, by notice in writing given to the Committee, elect to make additional periodical payments in order to count all or part of her period of membership before 1st April 1972 as membership for the purposes of calculating a widower’s pension in accordance with regulation F7(2)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*b*)(iii). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) A notice under paragraph (1) shall specify whether it relates to the whole or only a specified part of the woman’s period of membership which is membership before 1st April 1972. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) A notice under paragraph (1) may not be given in respect of a period of less than one year unless— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) the whole of the woman’s period of membership before 1st April 1972, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) where she has previously given a notice in respect of part of that membership, the remainder of it, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

amounts to less than one year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(4) Notice under paragraph (1) may be given by a member on more than one occasion. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(5) For the purposes of paragraph (1)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*c*), the reference to a woman having been a member continuously includes a reference to a woman who, having ceased to be a member became or becomes a member again— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) within one month of so ceasing, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) within one month of returning to work in accordance with Article 111 of the Employment Rights (Northern Ireland) Order 1996 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^f00018] (which confers the right to return to work following pregnancy or confinement). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
