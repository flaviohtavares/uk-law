# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section C2

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## C2. Meaning of “normal retirement age” and “NRD” (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-1648fda68b9e08f71f4705b6274025ca][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) In these regulations, in relation to any member, “normal retirement date” (“NRD”) means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) in the case of a member who by his 60th birthday has a total period of membership of at least 25 years, that birthday; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) in the case of a member who first has such a total period of membership by a date after his 60th birthday but before his 65th birthday, the day after that date; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c) in the case of a member who by his 60th birthday has a total period of membership of at least 10 years, provided that he was a contributory employee before 1st April 1972, that birthday; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (d) in the case of a member who does not fall within paragraphs ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*a*), (*b*) or ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*c*), his 65th birthday; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

and “normal retirement age” means his age on his NRD. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) Where for any purpose of the regulations it is necessary to determine a person’s NRD or normal retirement age before he attains that age, it shall be assumed that his local government employment and membership of the Scheme will be continuous. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
