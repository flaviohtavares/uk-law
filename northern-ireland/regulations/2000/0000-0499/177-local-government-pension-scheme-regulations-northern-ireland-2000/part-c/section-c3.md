# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section C3

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## C3. Member’s standard contributions (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-da9ab7ec1e4ecdfb89034065ff3ad3bc][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

- Subject to regulations C5 to C7 and C26, a member shall, at such intervals as the Committee may determine, make contributions in respect of every employment in relation to which he is a member— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (a) in the case of a manual worker, at the rate of five per cent. of his remuneration in the employment, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (b) in the case of an officer, at the rate of six per cent. of that remuneration. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
