# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section C11

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## C11. Amounts of payments under regulation C9 (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-eea4e374358c1503edb94f604099c7f0][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The amount to be paid by way of additional periodical payments by a member who has made an election under regulation C9(1) in respect of an additional period is the appropriate percentage of his remuneration for the time being, multiplied by the length of that period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) For the purposes of paragraph (1)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) “the appropriate percentage”, in relation to a member, is the percentage in the relevant Table in Part I of Schedule C2 appropriate to his age on his next birthday after the date of the election and to his normal retirement age; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) the lengths of periods are to be expressed in complete years and any fraction of a year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
