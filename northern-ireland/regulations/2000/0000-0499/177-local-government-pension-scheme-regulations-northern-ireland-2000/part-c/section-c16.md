# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section C16

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## C16. Further provisions as to making of payments under regulations C13 and C14 (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-a4f26262510a1d2f78e3b0923b3ee1cd][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A member shall specify in a notice of election to make additional periodical payments under regulation C13 or C14 the birthday (“the specified birthday”) up to which additional payments are to be paid, which may be— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) his 65th birthday, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) any earlier birthday which is or is after his NRD. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) Such additional periodical payments are to be paid, at such intervals as the Committee may determine, from the member’s next birthday after the date of the election. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
