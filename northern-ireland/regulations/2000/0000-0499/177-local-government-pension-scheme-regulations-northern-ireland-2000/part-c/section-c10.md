# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section C10

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## C10. Maximum length of additional periods to be purchased under regulation C9 (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-94d33b873c2ea2e402ceae201e8e713d][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to regulation C26 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] and C26A[^key-58d945942c3dd33e794b2e177c0dc3ae] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111], the maximum length of the period in respect of which payment may be made under regulation C9 in respect of a person is the length (expressed in years and fractions of a year) of the period (if any) by which his potential period of membership falls short of the relevant maximum number of years at the appropriate time. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) In paragraph (1) “potential period of membership”, in relation to any person, means the period which (apart from the payment) he would be entitled to count as a period of membership in relation to his local government employment if he continued in it until he attained the age of 65. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) Subject to paragraph (4) and to Schedule C4 (limitations on contributions and benefits), “the relevant maximum number of years”, in relation to any person, means 40 years. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(4) In the case of a person (other than an excluded member) who at the appropriate time was entitled to, or had received, superannuation benefits in respect of any local government employment or under any non-local government scheme, the relevant maximum number of years specified in paragraph (3) in relation to him is to be reduced, in accordance with the certificate of an actuary, to the extent necessary to ensure that the aggregate of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) the relevant income benefits: and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) the pension equivalent of the relevant capital benefits, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

will not exceed two-thirds of his pensionable remuneration. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(5) In paragraph (4)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- “excluded member” means a member whose pensionable remuneration in the first year of his employment during which he is a member does not exceed one quarter of the permitted maximum for the purposes of section 590C of the Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1988 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^f00016] (earnings cap) for the year of assessment in which that first year ends); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- “pension equivalent” has the meaning given in regulation 5(5)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*b*) of the Retirement Benefits Schemes (Restriction on Discretion to Approve) (Additional Voluntary Contributions) Regulations 1993 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^f00017];
- “the relevant income benefits”, in relation to a member, means the aggregate annual amount of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (i) the actuarial value, expressed as an annuity payable to him, of the pension benefits mentioned in paragraph (4); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (ii) the part of his retirement pension attributable to his period of membership before his NRD: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- “the relevant capital benefits”, in relation to a member, means the aggregate amount of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (i) his retirement grant, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (ii) any lump sum comprised in the pension benefits mentioned in paragraph (4). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(6) For the purposes of paragraphs (4) and (5)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) it is to be assumed that the person will, until his NRD, continue in the same local government employment and on the same terms and conditions (including, in particular, his scale of remuneration) as at the date of the election; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) any period of membership on or after the date of the election is to be disregarded; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c) regard is to be had to any advice from the Commissioners of Inland Revenue as to the calculation of the value of the earlier benefits. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(7) The appropriate time is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) except where paragraph ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*b*) applies, the first day of the earliest period that the person is entitled to count as a period of membership in relation to his local government employment; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) if that period is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (i) a period of service under an officer of a LGPS employer or former local authority; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] a period during which the person was subject to a non-local government scheme other than one which was or became a statutory scheme, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

  the first day of the earliest period of local government employment that the person is entitled to count as a period of membership in relation to his local government employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
