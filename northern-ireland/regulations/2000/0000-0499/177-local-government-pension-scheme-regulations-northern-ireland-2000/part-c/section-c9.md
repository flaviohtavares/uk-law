# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section C9

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## C9. Payments to increase membership: calculation of all benefits (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-894d3fb5400bb13ed661e24401abb8bb][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to the following provisions, if a member elects at any time to make additional periodical payments under this regulation, then, in relation to the relevant employment, he may count as a period of membership (but not for the purposes of determining entitlement to any benefit)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) if he completes the additional payments, the period in respect of which payment was made; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) if he begins making the additional payments but does not complete payment, an additional period calculated in accordance with regulation C19. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) The additional payments— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) are payable from the member’s next birthday after the date of the election at such intervals as the Committee may determine, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) cease to be payable on the day before— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (i) his NRD, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] if his NRD is not his birthday, the last birthday before his NRD. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) An employee may not make an election under paragraph (1) if the Committee has resolved that he should undergo a medical examination at his own expense and he has not done so to its satisfaction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(4) An election under this regulation shall be made by notice in writing given to the Committee. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
