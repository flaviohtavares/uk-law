# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section C25

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## C25. Deduction and recovery of member’s contributions (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-5f84f06edf53825f00d4801a42d0044b][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An employing authority may deduct from the remuneration payable by it to a person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) contributions or payments payable by him under regulations C3, C5 and C6, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) any amount payable by him under regulation C7, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c) any instalments or additional periodical payments payable by him to the fund, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (d) contributions payable by him under regulation C24. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) The former employer of a person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) by whom sums are payable under regulation C8(2) or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) in respect of whom any sums are paid under paragraph 6(4) of Schedule C3, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

may deduct those sums from any payment the employer makes to that person under Part V of the Reserve and Auxiliary Forces (Protection of Civil Interests)(Northern Ireland) Order 1953 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^f00022], so far as those sums are payable in respect of the period in respect of which that payment is made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) If and so far as deductions are not made under paragraph (1) or (2), the Committee may recover any sum remaining due— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) as a simple contract debt in any court of competent jurisdiction (but, in the case of a sum payable under regulation C8 or paid under paragraph 6(4) of Schedule C3, only if it is not paid within 12 months of the person ceasing to perform relevant service as defined in that regulation), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) by deducting it from any payment by way of benefits to or in respect of the person in question under these regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
