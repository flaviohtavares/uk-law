# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section C15

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## C15. Amount of payments under regulation C13 and C14 (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-1d499868ca98456657a2d8683a97d7b2][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The amount to be paid by a member who has under regulation C13 or C14 elected to make additional periodical payments in respect of a period of membership specified in the notice of election is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) in the case of a male member, the appropriate percentage of his remuneration for the time being, multiplied by the length of that period; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) in the case of a female member who made such an election as is mentioned in paragraph 2 of Schedule F1, the appropriate percentage of her remuneration for the time being, multiplied by four times the length of the period of membership before 1st April 1972 specified in her notice of election; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c) in the case of a female member who has given notice under paragraph 1 of Schedule F1, the appropriate percentage of her remuneration for the time being, multiplied by three times the length of the period of membership specified in her notice of election which is membership before 1st April 1972. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) For the purposes of this regulation “the appropriate percentage” is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) in the case of a male member, the percentage specified in the relevant Table in Part III of Schedule C2 appropriate to his age on his next birthday after the date of the election and, in the case of paragraph (1)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*a*), to his specified birthday; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) in the case of a female member, the percentage specified in the relevant Table in that Part of that Schedule appropriate to her age on her next birthday after the date of the election and, in the case of the paragraph (1)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*b*) or ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*c*), to her specified birthday; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

and in this paragraph “specified birthday” means the birthday specified in accordance with regulation C16(1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) For the purposes of this regulation the lengths of periods are to be expressed in complete years and any fraction of a year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(4) References in paragraph (1)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*c*) to a period of membership before 1st April 1972 include references to a period treated for the purposes of paragraph 2(2) of Schedule D2 as being such a period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
