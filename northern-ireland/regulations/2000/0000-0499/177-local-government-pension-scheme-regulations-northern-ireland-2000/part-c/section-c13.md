# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section C13

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## C13. Payments to avoid reduction of retirement grant and death grant (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-cfa9eebb0dadbe83a62d56a440919cad][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to the following provisions of this regulation, a member— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) whose retirement grant would be subject to reduction under paragraph 1, 2(1) or 3(1) of Schedule D2 (reduction in standard retirement grant on account of contingent spouse’s pension); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) whose death grant would be subject to reduction under regulation E5 (surviving spouse deductions from certain death grants), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

may, by notice in writing given to the Committee, elect to make additional periodical payments under this regulation in order to avoid all or part of the reduction— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (i) in his retirement grant; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] in any death grant that may become payable under Part E. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) A notice under paragraph (1) shall specify whether the reduction is to be avoided in respect of the whole or only a specified part— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) in the case of a male member, of his membership before 1st April 1972 or before any earlier date on which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (i) he was judicially separated from his wife: or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] his marriage was dissolved: or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] he became a widower, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) in the case of a female member who has given notice under paragraph 1(1) of Schedule F1, of her membership which is or is treated for the purposes of paragraph 2(2) of Schedule D2 as being membership before 1st April 1972; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c) in the case of a female member who has made such an election as is mentioned in paragraph 2 of Schedule F1, of her membership before 1st April 1972. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) An election may not be made in respect of a period of membership of less than one year unless— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) the whole of the person’s period of membership in the case of a person described in paragraph (2) before 1st April 1972; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) where he has previously made an election in respect of part of that membership, the remainder of it, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

amounts to less than one year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(4) An election under this regulation may be made by any person from time to time, but not— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) if in the particular case the Committee so resolves, without his having, at his own expense, undergone a medical examination to its satisfaction; nor *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) after making an election under regulation C9(5)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*b*) of the 1992 regulations (or any corresponding previous provision) to make payment by instalments. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(5) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) this regulation applies to a woman by virtue of her having made such an election as is mentioned in paragraph 2 of Schedule F1; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) she has made (or is treated as having made) an election under paragraph (1) or regulation C9 of the 1992 regulations (or any corresponding previous provision); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c) this regulation subsequently applies to her by virtue of paragraph 2(1) of Schedule D2; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (d) she makes a further election under paragraph (1), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

then— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (i) any payments made under this regulation pursuant to the election referred to in sub-paragraph (b) shall be deemed to have been made pursuant to the further election, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] any additional sums payable by her pursuant to the further election shall be determined accordingly by the fund’s actuary. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
