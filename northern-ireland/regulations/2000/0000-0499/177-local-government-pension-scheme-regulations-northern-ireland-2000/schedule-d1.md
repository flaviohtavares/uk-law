# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, SCHEDULE D1

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE D1 (affected by repeal, see note) — Further provisions about pensionable remuneration (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

Regulation D1 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 1. Increase of pensionable remuneration where not all relevant period is a period of membership (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-148c2b3f915c6ba461d6d07ec4cc9c8a][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

Where a person is entitled to count as a period of membership in relation to the employment which he ceases to hold only part of the year specified in regulation D1(2)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*a*), his pensionable remuneration is his remuneration during that part multiplied by 365 and divided by the number of days in that part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 2. Optional alternative relevant periods (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-320d42225839eec341ff068212617fad][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(1) Where the circumstances mentioned in any of paragraphs 3 to 5 apply, then if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) the member or former member so elects, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) in the event of his death, the Committee so determines, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

for the purposes of regulation D1 the relevant period is the period mentioned in that paragraph. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) An election under sub-paragraph (1) shall be made by a notice in writing given by the member or former member to the Committee not later than one month after he is notified under regulation J4 of his entitlement to a benefit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) Where a person dies while still in local government employment or without having made an election under sub-paragraph (1), the Committee may give a determination in respect of any matter as to which an election under sub-paragraph (1) may have been made by him. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 3.[^c-38f13a583077214f473298ceeb02677b]

Subject to paragraphs 4 and 5, where a person is not entitled to count the whole of the period which would otherwise be the relevant period under regulation D1(2)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*a*) as a period of membership because he has been absent from duty (otherwise than by reason of illness or injury), the relevant period is the last 365 days which he is entitled to count as a period of membership. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 4.[^c-7325dec460be507983b63b7e767ee289]

(1) Where a reduction in a member’s remuneration is certified as material under this paragraph, then the relevant period is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) such one of the last 5 of the 13 years ending with the day on which he ceases to be a member, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) such consecutive 3 of those 13 years, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

as he may elect (or, in a case within paragraph 2(3), as may be determined by the employing authority). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) Where by virtue of this paragraph the relevant period is a period of 3 consecutive years, the member’s pensionable remuneration is the aggregate of his remuneration during that period divided by three. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) Subject to sub-paragraph (7), where a member suffers a material reduction in remuneration he is entitled to be issued with a certificate to that effect by the employing authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(4) A member suffers a reduction in remuneration if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) the remuneration of an employment which he continues to hold is reduced, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) he is transferred to another employment under the same LGPS employer at a reduced remuneration. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(5) Subject to sub-paragraph (6), a reduction in remuneration is only material if it is such that the member’s pensionable remuneration would be likely to be less if the relevant period were the period applicable under regulation D1(2)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*a*) than if it were the period applicable under sub-paragraph (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(6) A reduction in remuneration is not material if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) it did not result from circumstances beyond the member’s control, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) it was temporary, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c) it consisted in the termination of, or a reduction in, a temporary increase in remuneration. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(7) The employing authority may issue a certificate without an application from the member, but need not issue a certificate if he does not apply for one within 12 months after the date of reduction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(8) A certificate issued under this paragraph is to specify the date of the material reduction and the authority is to keep, for 10 years from that date, a record of the certificate including such information as would be necessary for applying sub-paragraph (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 5.[^c-1dfeefa351e63a3e442503c3f30acc90]

Where the member’s pensionable remuneration would be higher if either of the two years immediately preceding the period applicable under regulation D1(2)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*a*) were the relevant period instead of that period, the relevant period is whichever of those years yields the highest amount. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 6. Periods of absence (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-7ce2db08175c3dccf9c6d1deaad23570][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

If during the 13 years ending with the day mentioned in regulation D1(2)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*a*) the member’s remuneration was reduced or suspended during absence from duty— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) if the reduction or discontinuance was by reason of illness or injury, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) if it was otherwise than by reason of illness or injury and he— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (i) made contributions under regulation C5 (leave of absence), C6 (maternity absence) or a payment under regulation C7 (absence due to trade dispute), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] contributed under regulation 14(4) or (5) of the 1981 regulations or the corresponding provisions of the former regulations, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

  he is for the purposes of regulation D1 and this Schedule to be treated as having received for any period in respect of which he made such contributions or payment the remuneration which he would have received but for the reduction or discontinuance. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 7. Part-timers (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-497206acb8087b147780eaa348fb262a][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

Subject to regulations E7 and F1(3) and paragraph 9(2) of Schedule M2, for the purposes of regulation D1 and this Schedule, a member is, in respect of any period of part-time local government employment, to be treated as having received the remuneration which would have been paid in respect of a single comparable whole-time employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 8. Multiple employments (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-a7e4687a88b6cfc0dbd6d1f4fe9a8fdf][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) a person was at any time employed in a single local government employment (“the first employment”), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) he becomes entitled to a benefit in relation to one of two or more concurrent local government employments (“the second employment”), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c) his remuneration in the first employment becomes material for the purpose of calculating that benefit, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

that remuneration shall for that purpose be reduced to such amount as bears the same relation to it as the annual rate of remuneration of the second employment at the date when he ceased to hold that employment bears to the total of the annual rates of remuneration of all the concurrent employments at that date. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 9. Fee earners (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-67434757b5cacac9df4eee071d6c0cff][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

Where the whole or a part of the member’s remuneration consists of fees, his pensionable remuneration in respect of them is the annual average of the fees earned by him— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) during the period of three years ending with the last day of the period which is the relevant period for the purposes of regulation D1, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) during such more favourable period, of more than three but not more than five years, ending with the last day of the period which is the relevant period for the purposes of regulation D1, as his last employing authority may allow, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c) if he was entitled to receive fees during part only of the period mentioned in paragraph ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*a*), during that part of the period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 10. Employees with notional remuneration (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-1831aa2bdd5319975e8708e1c624496f][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(1) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) any of a member’s remuneration during the period which is the relevant period for the purposes of regulation D1 was determined in accordance with an agreement under paragraph 7 of Schedule C1 (notional remuneration), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) his average weekly earnings from his local government employment in that period (other than payments for overtime and payments by way of bonus) are within the relevant limits, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c) his pensionable remuneration would be greater if determined by reference to those earnings, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

his pensionable remuneration is to be determined by reference to those earnings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) For the purposes of sub-paragraph (1) earnings are within the relevant limits if they— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) exceed by more than 50 per cent. the lower earnings limit at the end of the relevant period, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) do not exceed the upper earnings limit at the end of that period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) In this paragraph “lower earnings limit” and “upper earnings limit” have the same meaning as in the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
