### Certain female nurses, physiotherapists, midwives and health visitors (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

[^key-7d8b39a9b27271bb567373d9c0cfdd2c]

In their application to members to whom regulation 50 of the 1981 regulations applied these regulations shall have effect subject to the modifications in the following paragraphs— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(1) regulation B2(4) shall have effect as if for the figure “65” there was substituted the figure “60”; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) regulation C2(1)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*a*) and ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*c*) shall have effect as if for the words “60th birthday” there were substituted the words “55th birthday”; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) paragraph 8(1)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*a*)(ii) of Schedule C4 shall have effect as if for the words “60 years” there were substituted the words “55 years”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
