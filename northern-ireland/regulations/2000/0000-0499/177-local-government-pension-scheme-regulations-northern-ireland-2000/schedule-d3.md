## SCHEDULE D3 (affected by repeal, see note) — Additional membership in cases of ill-health (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

Regulation D7(2) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 1. “Relevant service” and “relevant membership period” (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-2706ffb9fe83ab5d4eb8b3f7706f1526][^key-209e71c475cb061ed24b896452e2bdca][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(1) For the purposes of this Schedule— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a)[^key-2706ffb9fe83ab5d4eb8b3f7706f1526] a member’s relevant service is so much of his contributing service and non-contributing service as did not consist of years added to his service— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (i) under regulation 43 of the 1981 regulations, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] the corresponding provisions of the former regulations, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b)[^key-2706ffb9fe83ab5d4eb8b3f7706f1526] a member’s relevant membership period is the total of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (i) any period of membership deriving from relevant service which became reckonable service by virtue of regulations 39 and 40(1)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*a*) of the 1981 regulations, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] any subsequent period of membership (other than a period excluded by sub-paragraph (2)), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c) in sub-paragraph 1( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*b*)(ii) “subsequent period of membership” does not include an additional period purchased by lump sum or additional contributions except, subject to sub-paragraph ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*d*) below, the additional period was purchased on or after 1st November 1991 under regulation 11 of the Local Government (Superannuation) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1991 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^f00097] or regulation C7 of the 1992 regulations, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (d) where the additional membership period to which a person is to be treated as being entitled would be a longer period if, in calculating a person’s relevant membership period, an additional period purchased under regulation 11 of the Local Government (Superannuation) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1991 or under regulation C7 of the 1992 regulations were to be disregarded instead of being taken into account, the additional period so purchased shall be disregarded. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2)[^key-2706ffb9fe83ab5d4eb8b3f7706f1526] The subsequent period of membership mentioned in sub-paragraph (1)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*b*)(ii) does not include an additional period purchased by a lump sum or additional contributions unless— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) it was purchased on or after 1st November 1991 under regulation 46A of the 1981 regulations or under regulation C7 of the 1992 regulations as continued in effect by Schedule C5, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) taking that period into account results in an increase in the additional period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 2. Calculation of additional membership period (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-67af0a9ecc72cc7b61b912b2a0efc3f2][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(1) Subject to paragraphs 3 and 4, the additional membership period is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) in a case where the length of the relevant membership period is less than 10 years, a period equal to the length of the relevant membership period; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) in any other case— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (i) the period by which the length of the relevant membership period falls short of 20 years, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] if longer, 6 243/365 years. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) The additional membership period is not to exceed the period by which the member’s period of membership would have been increased if he had continued in the employment which he has ceased to hold until— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) he attained the age of 65 years, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) if earlier, the date when his period of membership amounted to 40 years. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 3. Alternative additional periods (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-414b51c0529149dccf7cbdd0660380b9][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(1) Subject to paragraph 4, where before entering the employment which he has ceased to hold the person had, on ceasing to hold a previous employment, become entitled to benefits— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) under regulation D7(1) or regulation E2(1)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*b*)(i) of the 1992 regulations, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) under regulation 9(1)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*a*)(i) of the 1962 regulations, and regulation 18(1)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*b*)(i) of the 1981 regulations, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

the additional period in relation to a person shall be a period calculated as mentioned in paragraph 2, but— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (i) on the assumption that his relevant membership period includes his relevant membership period (or, as the case may be, the period of his relevant service) in relation to the previous employment, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] with the deduction of the appropriate period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) In sub-paragraph (1)(ii) “the appropriate period” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) in the case mentioned in sub-paragraph (1)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*a*), the additional period which became reckonable in relation to the previous employment by virtue of regulation D7(2) and this Schedule or, as the case may be, regulation E3(9) of and Schedule 9 to the 1992 regulations, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) in the case mentioned in sub-paragraph (1)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*b*), if the person’s retirement pension in respect of a previous employment was calculated under the proviso to regulation 10(1) of the 1962 regulations before the amendment of those regulations by the Local Government (Superannuation) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1973 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^f00098]—
  - (i) the period by which his relevant service fell short of 20 years, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] the period by which it would have been increased if he had continued in the previous employment until he reached the age of 65 years, as the case may be. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 4. Part-timers (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-0678ed13dfae250f66ac073e37f7391c][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(1) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) the whole of a member’s relevant membership period is in respect of part-time service, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) the reduction under regulation B14(1)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*b*) is by the same proportion in respect of the whole period, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] each such apportioned part of the unreduced period shall be reduced as mentioned in that regulation, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] those reduced periods shall be aggregated together. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) Subject to sub-paragraph (4), where part only of a member’s relevant membership period is in respect of part-time service, the additional membership period for the whole of his relevant membership period shall be determined in accordance with paragraphs 1 to 3 (and, in so far as the membership in respect of part-time service is concerned, by reference to the unreduced period), and then the period resulting from the application of those provisions (“the aggregate unreduced period”) shall be reduced by multiplying it by the fraction of which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (b) the divisor is the aggregate unreduced period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(4) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) apart from this sub-paragraph, sub-paragraph (3) would apply, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
