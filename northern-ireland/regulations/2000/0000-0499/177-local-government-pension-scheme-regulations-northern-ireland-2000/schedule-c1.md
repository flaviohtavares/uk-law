# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, SCHEDULE C1

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE C1 (affected by repeal, see note) — Further provisions concerning meaning of “remuneration” (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

Regulation C1 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 1. Part-timers (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-3b76926345922c1142e35ddf833eeb8b][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

For the purpose of calculating a member’s standard contributions under regulation C3, the remuneration of a part-time employee for any period (other than a period during which he was absent from duty by reason of illness or injury with reduced or no remuneration) is to be taken to be the remuneration he would have received if during that period he had worked no more and no less than the contractual hours. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 2. Statutory payments during absence (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-864a9dc32b35336b317096fb4a16f3a1][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

Subject to paragraph 3( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*b*), for the purpose of calculating a member’s standard contributions under regulation C3, any reduction in remuneration by reason of the actual or assumed enjoyment by the employee during any period of absence from duty of any statutory entitlement shall be disregarded. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 3.[^c-29323c464dfc2900dc99af66084b498a]

For the purposes of regulation C6— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) a woman’s remuneration includes any statutory maternity pay payable to her under the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^f00083], and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) in calculating the contributions which an employee would have been required to make under regulation C3, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

regard shall be had to any reduction in her remuneration during a period of maternity absence by reason of the actual or assumed enjoyment of such statutory maternity pay. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 4.[^c-7444022ef0a9180b56d292c4a1373d63]

In regulation C7 “remuneration” does not include any guarantee payment under the Employment Rights (Northern Ireland) Order 1996. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 5.[^c-945fb9e65535f6d08d8346adc2617078]

For the purpose of calculating the amount of any benefit payable under these regulations to a person to whom regulation C8 applies, his remuneration in respect of any period of his relevant service (as defined in that regulation) shall be deemed to be the amount by reference to which the calculation would have been made if he had continued to be employed in his former employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 6. Additional periodical payments: “remuneration for the time being” (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-c0548164494e0a62a760826e3fb2e844][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(1) Subject to sub-paragraph (2), for the purposes of regulations C11(1) and C15(1), in relation to any additional periodical payment falling to be paid by a member, his remuneration for the time being is the remuneration received by him for the interval at the end of which the payment falls to be paid. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) For the purposes of sub-paragraph (1)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) a member is to be taken to have received for any period for which, while a contract of employment subsisted, he was absent from duty with reduced or no remuneration (otherwise than by reason of illness or injury) the remuneration that he would have received but for his absence from duty, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) any reduction in remuneration by reason of the actual or assumed enjoyment by the member during any period of absence from duty (other than a period of maternity absence), of any statutory entitlement shall be disregarded. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 7. Power to agree notional remuneration (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-fd60e78b8d1d3bb1124d280b1a900470][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(1) A LGPS employer may from time to time enter with the bodies or persons representative of any class or description of its employees into an agreement specifying the method by which there shall be determined— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) an amount representing the whole of the remuneration, in respect of the period during which the agreement remains in force, of a member of that class or, as the case may be, an employee of that description, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) such part of his remuneration in respect of that period as is so specified. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) Where such an agreement is in force, then the whole or, as the case may be, the specified part of the remuneration of an employee who is a member of the class or, as the case may be, is of the description specified, shall, in respect of the period during which that agreement remains in force and the employee remains in employment with the LGPS employer in question as an employee of that class or description, be deemed for the purposes of these regulations to be the amount determined in accordance with the method specified. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) Where a LGPS employer enters into an agreement under sub-paragraph (1), it shall notify in writing all its employees who are members of a class or, as the case may be, an employee of a description, to which the agreement relates, including in the notification a conspicuous statement directing the attention of the employee to the place where he may obtain information about details of the agreement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(4) The notification required by sub-paragraph (3) shall be sent to an employee— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) if he is in the employment of the LGPS employer on the date the agreement was made, as soon as is reasonably practicable after that date; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) if he enters the employment later, within three months after entering it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
