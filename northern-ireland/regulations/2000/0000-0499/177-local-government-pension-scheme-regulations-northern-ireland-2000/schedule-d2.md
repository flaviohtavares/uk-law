# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, SCHEDULE D2

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE D2 (affected by repeal, see note) — Retirement Grants (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

Regulation D2(3) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 1. Reduction in standard retirement grant on account of contingent spouse’s pension (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-c9aa00ed8a7f1d71b2ca72d22c06e395][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

Subject to paragraph 4, where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) a person who is a married man has a period of membership before 1st April 1972; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) a widow’s pension may become payable in respect of him under Part F, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

the standard retirement grant is reduced by two-eightieths of his pensionable remuneration, multiplied by the length in years of that period of membership. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 2.[^key-80c79d3fb25125d56cb776baf0d2a133]

(1) Subject to paragraph 4, where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) a woman has a period of membership of which notice has been given under paragraph 1(1) of Schedule F1 and which is or is treated for the purposes of this paragraph as being a period of membership before 1st April 1972, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (i) she is a married woman and a widower’s pension may become payable under Part F in respect of that service, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] she is a widow or a woman whose marriage has been dissolved and the death or, as the case may be, the dissolution occurred after 31st March 1972, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

  the standard retirement grant is reduced by three one hundred and sixtieths of the pensionable remuneration, multiplied by the length in years of any such membership as is mentioned in paragraph ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*a*).

(2) For the purposes of this paragraph a period of membership which a person is entitled to count under regulation K15 shall be treated as being a period of membership after 31st March 1972 if the relevant transfer value was accepted after 5th April 1988. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 3.[^key-d756354a35371911f0665f87320bd4b4]

(1) Subject to paragraph 4, where the person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (i) is a widower; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] is judicially separated from his wife; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] is a man whose marriage has been dissolved; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

the standard retirement grant is reduced by two eightieths of his pensionable remuneration, multiplied by the length in years of any period of membership before 1st April 1972, or, if earlier, the date of the death, separation or dissolution. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 4.[^key-06b506e64a45c6957b8b69c6d062e620]

In calculating any reduction under paragraph 1, 2 or 3, no account shall be taken of any period of membership in respect of which payment under regulation C13 has been or is to be treated as having been completed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
