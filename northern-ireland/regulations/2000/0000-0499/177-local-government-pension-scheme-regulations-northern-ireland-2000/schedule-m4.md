- (b) in regulation 2 for “Regulation B5(10)” substitute “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]regulation B6(3)[^key-657b728920106d86b504b8eb68bb062c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]” and for “Regulation B5” substitute “regulation B6”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
