# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section E5

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## E5. Surviving spouse deductions from certain death grants (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-94f699d4808165b293557aae48f48df7][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A death grant is reduced under this regulation by the appropriate amount if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) it is payable under regulation E1 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] or[^key-f403dc81d53e9d969f19d653dc38dbb2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] E2 [^key-e0870a523d4670a55104e1b6b71777a1]..., and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) a surviving spouse’s long-term pension is payable under Part F. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) the deceased was a man, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) the deceased was a woman who was treated as a man by virtue of paragraph 2 of Schedule F1, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

the appropriate amount for the purposes of paragraph (1) is an amount equal to two eightieths of the deceased’s pensionable remuneration multiplied by the length in years of any period of membership before 1st April 1972 in respect of which the widow's, or as the case may be, widower’s pension is payable under Part F. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) Where the deceased was a woman (other than one to whom paragraph (2)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*b*) applies), the appropriate amount for the purposes of paragraph (1) is an amount equal to three one hundred and sixtieths of the deceased’s pensionable remuneration, multiplied by the length in years of any period— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) of which notice of which was given under paragraph 1(1) of Schedule F1, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) which is treated for those purposes as a period of membership before 1st April 1972, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c) in respect of which a widower’s pension is payable under Part F. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(4) In calculating any reduction under this regulation, no account shall be taken of any period of membership in respect of which payment under regulation C13 has been or is to be treated as having been completed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
