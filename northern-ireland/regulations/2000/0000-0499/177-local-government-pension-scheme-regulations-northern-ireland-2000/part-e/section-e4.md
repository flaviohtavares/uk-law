# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section E4

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## E4. Death grant: pensioners (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-ef9843da560d9dc46aead8455a42a183][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) If at the time of his death a person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) was entitled to receive payments in respect of a retirement pension (excluding a pension to which he would not be entitled apart from regulation D16 or D18), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) would have been so entitled but for the operation of Schedule D5 (re-employed pensioners) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] or regulation 113 (abatement) of the 2002 Regulations[^c-2e971252e0e33f0d115a4434e5ee1bd1] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111],

there shall be paid a lump sum death grant in accordance with regulation E8. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) The amount of the death grant payable under paragraph (1) is an amount equal to the total amount that would (or would but for Schedule D5 (re-employed pensioners) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] or regulation 113 (abatement) of the 2002 Regulations[^c-dd4bf475277dc8780138117e9728184a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] or his death, or both) have been paid to him by way of retirement pension for the first 5 years after he became (or would but for Schedule D5 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] or regulation 113 (abatement) of the 2002 Regulations[^c-b57e881ca1295bf2e11d2261b541345b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] have become) entitled to receive payments in respect of the pension, reduced by the total of any payments made to him in respect of the pension. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
