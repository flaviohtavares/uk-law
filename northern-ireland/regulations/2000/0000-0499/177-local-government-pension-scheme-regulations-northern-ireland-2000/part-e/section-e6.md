# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section E6

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## E6. Adjustments to death grants for certain re-employed pensioners (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-95d5726f677255ec1ae75bd19aaf38c9][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

The provisions of this Part have effect subject to Part III of Schedule D5 (separate benefits of certain persons re-employed by LGPS employers) and Part IV of that Schedule (death in further employment of such employees) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] or, as the case may be, the corresponding provisions of the 2002 Regulations (in accordance with Schedule 2 to the Transitional Regulations)[^key-f8f4e5ad2ca471ca0c97fb07e744fb94] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111].
