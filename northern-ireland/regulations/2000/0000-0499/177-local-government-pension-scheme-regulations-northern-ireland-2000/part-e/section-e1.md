# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section E1

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## E1. Death grant: members in service (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-f5e6c7d10d8e3e77a929510a63dac9b0][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) If at the time of his death a person was a member, there shall be paid a lump sum death grant in accordance with regulation E8. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) Where paragraph (1) applies the amount of the death grant is the greater of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) twice the deceased’s pensionable remuneration, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) three eightieths of his pensionable remuneration multiplied by the length in years of his period of membership, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

less, in a case where a surviving spouse’s long-term pension is payable under Part F, the appropriate amount to be calculated under regulation E5. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
