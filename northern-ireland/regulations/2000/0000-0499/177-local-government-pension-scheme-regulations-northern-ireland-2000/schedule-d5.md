#### 1. Application of Part I (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-390fe25aed0185f7522eb4cade4e8858][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(1) Subject to sub-paragraph (3), this Part applies to a person who, since becoming entitled to a retirement pension in relation to a former employment, has entered a new employment with a LGPS employer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) In this Part “retirement pension” includes an annual pension under the former regulations and a retirement pension under the 1992 regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) This Part does not apply where the new employment is employment by virtue of which the person is entitled to participate in benefits provided under regulations made under Article 11 of the Order of 1972 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^f00099] (superannuation of teachers). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

#### 2. General reduction rule (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-0c44cbe4539255ad0cd8f2950b711f94][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

Subject to paragraphs 3, 7 and 9, while the person holds the new employment the annual rate of the retirement pension is reduced— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) if the annual rate of remuneration of the new employment, equals or exceeds the indexed annual rate of remuneration of the former employment, to zero; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) otherwise, by the amount (if any) which is necessary to secure that the potential receipts during the new employment do not exceed the indexed annual rate of remuneration of the former employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
