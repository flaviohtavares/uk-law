# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section B11

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## B11. Leaving the Scheme (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-ebeb336b82e67f2c63670d5fcf1c9d1a][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to regulation B2(2) and (4), a person shall cease to be a member of the Scheme if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) he wishes to do so, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) he ceases to be eligible for membership. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) A person who wishes to cease to be a member of the Scheme shall so notify his employer in writing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) Subject to paragraphs (5) and (6), a person giving a notification under paragraph (2) shall cease to be a member of the Scheme— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) in a case where a date of cessation (being a date not earlier than the date of the notification) is specified in the notification, from that date, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) otherwise, from the first day of the first, or if the LGPS employer so determines, the second payment period following the notification. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(4) In paragraph (3)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*b*), “payment period” means a period of service to which the employee’s payment of wages or salary relates. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(5) Where a person gives a notification under paragraph (2) within three months after first becoming a member of the Scheme, he shall be treated as never having been a member. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(6) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) a person to whom regulation C8 (absence on reserve forces service) applies has given notification under paragraph (2) before ceasing his former employment or beginning his leave of absence in order to perform his relevant service (within the meaning of that regulation), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) he is still a member immediately before he commences his relevant service, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

then, subject to paragraph (7), the notification shall be of no effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(7) A person may elect that paragraph (6) is not to apply, by notice given in writing to the Committee before the end of the period of 12 months beginning with the end of his relevant service (or within such longer period as it may allow), and it shall take all practicable steps to ensure that a person entitled to make such an election is notified of his entitlement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(8) Any contributions paid by a person with respect to a period during which, by virtue of a notification under paragraph (2), he is not a member of the Scheme shall be returned to him. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
