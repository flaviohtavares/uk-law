## B2. Age restrictions: meaning of “latest retirement age” and “LRD” (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-1e5ff5f4ab9497e71c8ccd1ca986dc54][^c-83d842d209c480a32f85ee8168c6d345][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A person is only eligible to be a member of the Scheme if he has attained the age of 16 years. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2)[^c-07a07da858ad252ada4441424e4b170c][^c-83d842d209c480a32f85ee8168c6d345] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c-f7b4c5b506dc799a1a40a0a080e92630][^c-83d842d209c480a32f85ee8168c6d345] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^c-83d842d209c480a32f85ee8168c6d345] Subject to paragraph (5), a person is not eligible to be a member of the Scheme after he has attained the age of 65 and in these regulations, in relation to any person, “latest retirement age” (“LRA”) means that age and “latest retirement date” (“LRD”) means the date by which he attains that age. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(5)[^c-83d842d209c480a32f85ee8168c6d345] A person is only eligible to be a member of the Scheme on and after his LRD if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) his total period of membership does not exceed the maximum referred to in paragraph (7); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) at least one of the conditions mentioned in paragraph (6) is satisfied in relation to him. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(6)[^c-83d842d209c480a32f85ee8168c6d345] The conditions mentioned in paragraph (5) are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) at least one of the following has become payable to him— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (i) a pension which is liable to be reduced or suspended under Schedule D5 (re-employed pensioners), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] an ill-health retirement grant under regulation D8, or under regulation E4 of the 1992 regulations, or under regulation 28A *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^f00005] of the 1981 regulations, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) he has received or is entitled to receive compensation under any enactment for loss of employment or loss or diminution of emoluments attributable to the provisions of an enactment, and the compensation is liable to be reduced or suspended, in consequence of his taking up employment with a LGPS employer, in the like manner and to the like extent as it would have been if he had remained eligible to belong to the occupational pension scheme to which he belonged or was eligible to belong immediately before suffering the loss. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(7)[^c-83d842d209c480a32f85ee8168c6d345] The maximum referred to in paragraph (5)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*a*) is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) in the case of a Class A member (as defined in paragraph 1(1) of Schedule C4) 40 years, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) in the case of a Class B member or a Class C member (as so defined), the aggregate of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (i) his total period of membership before he attained the age of 60 years (disregarding any period in excess of 40 years), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] his total period of membership since he attained that age (but not exceeding 5 years plus any period by which the period mentioned in paragraph (i) fell short of 40 years); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

  and in paragraph (5) and this paragraph “total period of membership” has the meaning given in regulation B13, except that it also includes any additional period of membership which the member has been treated as being entitled to count for the purposes of regulation D7 (enhancement in cases of retirement on grounds of ill-health) or any corresponding earlier provision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
