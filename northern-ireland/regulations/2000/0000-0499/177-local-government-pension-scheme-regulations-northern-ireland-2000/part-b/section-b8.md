# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section B8

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## B8. Other cases of ineligibility (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-86712e631e064f7fa908602084abf973][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Notwithstanding anything in regulations B1 to B3, the following persons are not eligible to be members of the Scheme— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) an existing officer, unless he exercised the option conferred by regulation 23 of the 1950 regulations or the corresponding provision of the Belfast Corporation Superannuation Scheme; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) any member of the fire brigade as defined in Article 2(2) of the Fire Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1984 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^f00009];
- (c) any officer in the employment of the Fire Authority of Northern Ireland who was employed by the Northern Ireland Fire Authority immediately before 1 May 1954, and who did not exercise the option available to him under the relevant proviso to regulation 3(1) of the 1950 regulations to avail himself of the benefits of those regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (d) any person in contributory service within the meaning of the Teachers (Superannuation) Act (Northern Ireland) 1950 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^f00010]; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (e) any person who as a member of staff of the University of Ulster is eligible to participate in the Universities' Superannuation Scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) A person is not eligible to be a member of the Scheme in respect of his part-time employment as a member of a fire brigade maintained in pursuance of the Fire Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1984 on terms under which he is or may be required to engage in fire fighting. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3)[^c-a2d1b9d2996bd22bf1513ba3cc7be8d6] A person who is a member and is an employee of a transferee admission body is treated as leaving a local government employment when he ceases to be employed in connection with the services or assets referred to in the transfer arrangement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
