# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section B7

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## B7. Ineligibility of employees eligible to join other statutory schemes (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-970d234f19a0c31711e34f666ef72c53][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A person is not eligible to be a member of the Scheme by virtue of any employment which also entitles him to belong to another occupational pension scheme provided by or under an enactment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) The reference in paragraph (1) to an enactment— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) includes an enactment in a local Act, but *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) excludes Article 9 of the Order of 1972. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
