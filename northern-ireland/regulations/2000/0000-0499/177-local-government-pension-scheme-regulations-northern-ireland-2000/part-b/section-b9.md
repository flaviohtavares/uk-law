# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section B9

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## B9. Applications for membership (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-73879e812fdf8c17d06c70409a8ab364][^key-012f73f58234a6e5fed83765eed7dc5a][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-73879e812fdf8c17d06c70409a8ab364] Subject to the following provisions of this Part, a person who wishes to become a member of the Scheme shall apply to do so by notice given in writing to his employer or future employer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) Subject to paragraphs (3) and (4), an employee is deemed to have made an application to become a member, unless— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) before commencing his employment, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) in the case of a person to whom this paragraph began to apply after he commenced his employment, before the date on which it began to apply to him, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

he notified his employer in writing that he did not wish to become a member of the Scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3)[^key-73879e812fdf8c17d06c70409a8ab364] Paragraph (2) does not apply— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) to a person whose employment is of a casual nature, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) to a person who has previously been a member of the Scheme but has ceased to be a member after giving notification under regulation B11, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c) to a person who before the commencement date was not an employee working at least 30 hours per week. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(4)[^key-73879e812fdf8c17d06c70409a8ab364] Where a person who has ceased to be employed in an employment in which he was a member at the time of cessation commences a new employment in which he is eligible for membership, he is deemed to have made an application to become a member unless, before commencing his new employment, he notified his employer in writing that he did not wish to be a member of the Scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(5)[^key-73879e812fdf8c17d06c70409a8ab364] An application for membership may be withdrawn at any time before the applicant becomes a member. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
