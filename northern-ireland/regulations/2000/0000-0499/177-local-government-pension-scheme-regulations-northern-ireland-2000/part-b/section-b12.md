# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section B12

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## B12. Rejoining the Scheme (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-5682f82c3a65dd86617ecd9325440709][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (3), a person who has once given a notification under regulation B11 may subsequently apply to become a member of the Scheme again. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) An application under paragraph (1) shall be made by notice in writing given to the applicant’s employer or future employer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) A person who has made an application under paragraph (1) and subsequently gives another notification under regulation B11(2) may only make a further application under paragraph (1) if his employer or future employer consents or— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) he is beginning a new employment with a new employing authority by virtue of which he is eligible to be a member of the Scheme, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) he applies before or within the period of three months beginning with the day on which he begins that employment, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c) he is a person to whom regulation K17 applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(4) Regulation B10 shall apply to an application under paragraph (1) as it applies to an application under regulation B9(1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
