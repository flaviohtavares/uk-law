# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section B14

> Text as at 2019-11-18.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## B14. Length of period of membership: calculation of benefit (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-a05c59f9f9d1c1571b876705183d5629][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) For the purpose of calculating the amount of any benefit under these regulations— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) a period of membership in excess of a number of complete years shall be counted as the appropriate fraction of a year (and accordingly references to the length in years of membership shall be taken as references to the number of complete years and any fraction of a year in the period of membership); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) subject to paragraph (3), a period of membership in part-time service in local government employment shall be treated as though it had been a proportionately reduced period of membership in whole-time local government employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) In paragraph (1)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- “the appropriate fraction” means the fraction of which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (a) the numerator is the number of complete days comprised in the excess; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (b) the denominator is 365; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- “proportionately reduced” means reduced in the proportion which the number of contractual hours during the period of part-time service in the employment, bears to the number of contractual hours of that employment if it were on a whole-time basis. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) Paragraph (1)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*b*) does not apply in determining a member’s total period of membership for the purposes of regulation D7(2) (qualification for enhancement of pension in cases of ill-health) and is subject to paragraph 4 of Schedule D3 (additional membership in such cases). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
