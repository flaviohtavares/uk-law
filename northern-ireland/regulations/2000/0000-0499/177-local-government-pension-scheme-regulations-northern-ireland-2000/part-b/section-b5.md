# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section B5

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## B5. Separate employments etc. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-a1f9c0799ebb43e78f6289f88b028be9][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where a person holds two or more separate employments under one LGPS employer then these regulations apply in relation to each of those employments as if the other or others were held by him under another LGPS employer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) For the purposes of these regulations, a clerk of a district council who performs functions under Article 9(2) (conduct of elections) of the Electoral Law (Northern Ireland) Order 1972 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^f00006] shall, in relation to those functions, be treated as if he were employed by that council and any fees paid to him in connection with those functions shall be treated as remuneration paid to him by that council. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
