# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section B15

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## B15. Special power of employing authority to increase period of membership (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-64bc39e1139ca281dfd4f57398573c78][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraphs (2) and (3), if the body employing an employee who is eligible to be a member of the Scheme (in these regulations referred to as “the employing authority”) is satisfied that, having regard to the interests of the efficient exercise of its functions, there are exceptional reasons for doing so, it may resolve to add an additional period of membership to a member’s period of membership. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) A resolution under paragraph (1)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) may only be passed before or within 6 months after the person becomes a member in the authority’s employment, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) may not be passed after he has attained the age of 59 years, unless he did so after becoming such a member. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) The additional period is to be specified in the resolution and is not to exceed the maximum period which would be applicable under regulation C10(1) (taking the references in regulation C10(6)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*a*) and ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*b*) to the date of the election as references to the date of the resolution and, in the case of a person who at the date of the resolution had not become a member in the employment of the authority, treating him as if he had on that date become such a member on the scale of remuneration at which the employment was offered to him). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(4) Where the employing authority has passed a resolution under paragraph (1) and the member— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) remains in his employment under that authority until his NRD (within the meaning of regulation C2(1)), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) on ceasing to hold that employment before his NRD is incapable of discharging efficiently the duties of the employment by reason of ill-health or infirmity of mind or body, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c) dies while in that employment, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

the additional period specified in the resolution may be counted as a period of membership. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(5) In any other case where the employing authority has passed such a resolution the member is entitled to count as a period of membership the appropriate proportion of the additional period of membership specified in the resolution. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(6) In paragraph (5) “the appropriate proportion” means the proportion which the period during which the member has been in the employment of the employing authority bears to the period during which the member would have been in that employment if he had remained in it until his NRD (within the meaning of regulation C2(1)). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
