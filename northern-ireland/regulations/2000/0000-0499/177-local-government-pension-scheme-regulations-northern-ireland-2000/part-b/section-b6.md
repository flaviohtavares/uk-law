# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section B6

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## B6. Agreements to enable employees of non-LGPS employers to be members (“admission agreements”) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-d9388e06df9fba8a8e4fdaa60df847f9][^c-48a54f9a51e7495b0b5c82677a390bd9][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Committee may make an admission agreement with any admission body. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) An admission agreement is an agreement that all or any specified class of the admission body’s employees may be members. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) These are admission bodies— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) a body which provides a public service in the United Kingdom otherwise than for the purposes of gain and which either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (i) has sufficient links with a LGPS employer for the body and the LGPS employer to be regarded as having a community of interest, whether because the operations of the admission body are dependent on the operations of the LGPS employer or otherwise, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] is approved by the Department for the purpose of admission to the Scheme; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) a body to the funds of which any LGPS employer contributes; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c) a body to which any monies are payable from monies appropriated by or under an enactment as defined in section 98(1) of the Northern Ireland Act 1998; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (d) a body representative— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (i) of local authorities, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] of local authorities and officers of local authorities, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] of officers of local authorities, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] of LGPS employers; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (e) statutory undertakers; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (f) non-statutory undertakers; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (g) the managers of a voluntary school *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] or grant maintained integrated school[^c-bee88b8b3a49b91dd16fcead3ace66e5] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] within the meaning of Article 2(2) of the Education and Libraries (Northern Ireland) Order 1986; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (h) the governing body of an institution of further education within the meaning of the Further Education (Northern Ireland) Order 1997; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (i) a body which provides services or assets referred to in a transfer arrangement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(4) An admission body referred to in paragraph (3)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*d*)(iii) is only an admission body if it is formed for the purpose of consultation as to the common interests of local authorities and the discussion of matters relating to local government. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(5) Approval under paragraph (3)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*a*)(ii) may be subject to such conditions as the Department thinks fit and it may withdraw approval at any time if such conditions are not met. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(6) It must be a term of an admission agreement made with a non-associated admission body within paragraphs (3)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*b*) and ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*c*) that the body which provides the funding or from which monies are appropriated (and, if more than one, all of them) guarantees the liability of the admission body to pay all amounts due from it under the Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(7) In the case of an admission agreement with a transferee admission body— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) the transferor LGPS employer, if not the Committee, must be a party to the admission agreement; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) only those employees of the transferee admission body who are employed in connection with the provision of the services or assets referred to in the transfer arrangement are eligible to be members of the Scheme; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c) the admission agreement shall require an indemnity or bond in an approved form and with an authorised insurer or a relevant institution; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (d) provision shall be made for the matters set out in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] Schedule A3[^c-5f2c1a1bdbc365bb3de8f8ef86ede7eb] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111].

(8) Where a transferor LGPS employer and a transferee admission body undertake to meet the requirements of paragraph (7), the Committee must admit to the Scheme the eligible employees of the transferee admission body and, where it does so, the terms on which it does so are the admission agreement for the purposes of these Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(9) An admission agreement must terminate if the admission body ceases to be such a body. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(10) An admission agreement may make such other provision about its termination as the parties to the agreement consider appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(11) When the Committee makes an admission agreement, it must promptly inform the Department of the date the agreement takes effect, the admission body’s name and, in the case of an admission agreement with a transferee admission body, the name of the relevant transferor LGPS employer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(12) The Committee must notify the Commissioners of Inland Revenue of the admission of an admission body within the time prescribed in regulations made under section 605 of the Taxes Act and provide such information as may be so prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(13) Any question which may arise between the Committee and any other party to an admission agreement relating to the construction of the agreement or to the rights and obligations under that agreement shall be referred in writing for determination to the Department. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(14) An employee of an admission body may not be a member if he is a member of another occupational pension scheme (within the meaning of section 1 of the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993) other than where the accrual of benefits under that pension scheme would not affect approval of the Scheme as an approved scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(15) These Regulations apply to employment with an admission body in which the employee is a member in the same way as if the body were a LGPS employer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(16) In this regulation and in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] Schedule A3[^c-a5af68acc7f0a278323bc77a87410ebd] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] (where applicable)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) “authorised insurer” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (i) an insurance company authorised under section 3 or 4 of the Insurance Companies Act 1982 to carry on general business, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] an EC company which is lawfully carrying on general business, or providing insurance of an appropriate class within general business, in the United Kingdom, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

  and expressions used in this definition and the Insurance Companies Act 1982 have the same meaning as in that Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) “indemnity or bond in an approved form” means an indemnity or bond to meet a level of risk exposure arising on premature termination of the transfer arrangement actuarially assessed to the satisfaction of the Committee and the transferor LGPS employer if not the Committee; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c) “non-associated admission body” means a body described in— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (i) paragraph (3)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*a*)(ii), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] paragraph (3)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*b*) where, at the date that the admission agreement is made, the contributions paid to a body by any one or more LGPS employers equal in total 50% or less of the total amount it receives from all sources, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] paragraph 3( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*c*);
- (d) “non-statutory undertakers” means a body who, though not authorised by any statutory provision to do so, is primarily engaged in carrying on— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (i) any railway, light railway, road transport, water transport, canal, inland navigation, dock, harbour, pier; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] any undertaking for the promotion of industrial development or the promotion of the development of tourist traffic; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (e) “relevant institution” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (i) an institution authorised under Part I of the Banking Act 1987 (regulation of deposit-taking business), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] a person to whom the restriction on acceptance of deposits in section 3 of that Act does not apply because he is specified in Schedule 2 to that Act (Central banks etc.), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] a European authorised institution which has lawfully established a branch in the United Kingdom for the purpose of accepting deposits, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

  and “European authorised institution” has the same meaning as in the Banking Co-ordination (Second Council Directive) Regulations 1992; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (f) “statutory undertakers” means a body authorised by any statutory provision to carry on— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (i) any railway, light railway, road transport, water transport, canal, inland navigation, dock, harbour, pier; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] any undertaking for the promotion of industrial development or the promotion of the development of tourist traffic; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (g) “transferee admission body” means an admission body described in paragraph (3)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*i*);
- (h) “transfer arrangement” means a contract or other arrangement made with a transferor LGPS employer for the provision of, or making available of, services or assets, for the purposes of or in connection with the exercise of a function of that transferor LGPS employer; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (i) “transferor LGPS employer” means an authority or body which is a LGPS employer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
