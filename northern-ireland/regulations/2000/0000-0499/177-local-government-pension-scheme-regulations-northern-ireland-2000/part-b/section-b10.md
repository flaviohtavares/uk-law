# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section B10

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## B10. Admission to the Scheme (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-cbac7594d9fd235516bda762c0f64882][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A person who has or is deemed to have applied under regulation B9 and is eligible to be a member of the Scheme shall become a member of it on the appropriate day. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) In this regulation, “the appropriate day” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) in the case of a person who has applied under regulation B9(1) at least one month before the date on which he commences his employment (or such lesser period before that date as his employer allows), the date on which he commences his employment or such later date as he has specified in his application; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) in any other case where an application is made under that regulation, the first day of the first payment period following the application; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c) in the case of a person who is deemed to have applied under regulation B9(2)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (i) if he is a person to whom that paragraph applied on the date when he commenced his employment, that date, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] otherwise, on the date on which it began to apply to him. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) In paragraph (2)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*b*), “payment period” means a period of service to which the employee’s payment of wages or salary relates. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
