# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section B1

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## B1. General eligibility of employees of LGPS employers (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-5723fbea039010f1cce3fc70ddb26760][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to the provisions of this Part, a person is only eligible to be a member of the occupational pension scheme constituted by these regulations (in these regulations referred to as “the Scheme”) if he is an employee of a LGPS employer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) In these regulations— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) “employee” means an employee whether permanent or temporary; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) “a LGPS employer” is a local authority as defined in Article 2 of the Order of 1972 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^f00004].
