# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, SCHEDULE A2

> Text as at 2019-11-18.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE A2 (affected by repeal, see note) — Interpretation for pension sharing on divorce (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-9ec8c94fcf17cadea8321ca425fe8c76][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

Regulation 2 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

In these Regulations— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- “the 1999 Act” means the Welfare Reform and Pensions Act 1999; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- “the 1999 Order” means the Welfare Reform and Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1999; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- “Deferred pensioner” and “pensioner” shall be construed in accordance with Part E; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- “normal benefit age” means 65; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- “pension credit” means a credit under Article 26(1)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*b*) of the 1999 Order or under section 29(1)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*b*) of the 1999 Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- “pension credit benefits” means benefits payable under the Scheme to or in respect of a pension credit member by virtue of rights under the Scheme attributable to a pension credit; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- “pension credit member” means a person who has pension credit benefits under the Scheme; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- “pension credit rights” means rights to future benefits under the Scheme which are attributable to a pension credit; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- “pension debit” means a debit under Article 26(1)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*a*) of the 1999 Order or under section 29(1)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*a*) of the 1999 Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- “pension debit member” means a member, a deferred pensioner or pensioner, whose shareable rights under the Scheme are subject to a pension debit; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- “pension sharing order” in relation to a pension credit member, a pension debit member, a person entitled to a pension credit or pension credit rights means the order or provision by virtue of which Article 26 of the 1999 Order or section 29 of the 1999 Act takes effect; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- “shareable rights” has the same meaning as in Article 24(2) of the 1999 Order or under section 27(2) of the 1999 Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- “transfer date” in relation to a pension credit member and pension credit rights means the day on which the pension sharing order takes effect; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- “transferor” in relation to a pension credit member or a person entitled to a pension credit means the person to whose shareable rights the pension sharing order relates. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
