# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, SCHEDULE C3

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE C3 (affected by repeal, see note) — Additional voluntary contributions (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

Regulation C24 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 1. Making and variation of elections (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-a77450b0b1e09915182cb8b65cd09b67][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(1) An election by a member to pay contributions under regulation C24 shall specify— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) the amount of the additional contributions which he wishes to pay (expressed either as a percentage of his remuneration or as a sum payable at the times mentioned in paragraph 4); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) the proportion (if any) of the contributions which he wishes to be used to provide benefits payable in the event of death. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) A member who has made an election under regulation C24 may at any time elect— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) to vary the amount of his contributions or the proportion of them that he is to continue to pay which are to be used to provide benefits payable on death; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) to discontinue those contributions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) An election by a member under regulation C24 or this paragraph shall be made by notice given in writing to the member’s employing authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 2. Payment and amount of AVCs (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-0f12ae8d096c83ff4a70529653f2a19e][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(1) The Committee may require, before any contributions are first made pursuant to the election, that the amount of the contributions payable under regulation C24 in any tax year shall be not less than the amount specified in regulation 2(8) of the Pension Schemes (Voluntary Contributions Requirements and Voluntary and Compulsory Membership) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1987 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^f00084].

(2) The amount of the contributions payable in any tax year is limited in accordance with paragraph 5 of Schedule C4. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 3.[^c-296bd6e9defc317a77fcdc7971bd3740]

No contributions shall be payable under regulation C24 with respect to any period— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) during which the person contributing is not a member, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) subject to paragraph 4, after the time when he ceases to be employed by the employing authority to which notice was given under paragraph 1. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 4.[^c-3304c435b84a82480965ec4554690f36]

Contributions under regulation C24 shall normally be payable on the member’s usual pay day. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 5. Functions of employing authorities and the Committee (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-9dcf21ea1897d283bfa0c7c76ebe3edc][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(1) As soon as an employing authority receives a notice under paragraph 1 it shall forward it to the Committee. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) The Committee shall give effect to a notice under paragraph 1 as soon as reasonably practicable, and in any event— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) in the case of a notice under paragraph 1(1), so that the member may begin to pay his contributions under paragraph 4 before the expiry of the period of six months beginning with the date on which he gives notice, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) in the case of a notice under paragraph 1(2), so that the member may pay his contribution at the varied amount or in the varied proportions, or, as the case may be, cease to pay his contributions, before the expiry of the period of three months beginning with that date. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 6.[^c-25d468bfb950fb44f2d4779b585c541f]

(1) Where a notice under paragraph 1 specifies that all or part of the contributions are to be used to provide benefits payable in the event of death, the Committee shall make arrangements for the provision of those benefits under a pension policy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) The pension policy shall provide that the Committee is to make payments to the insurance company with which the policy is entered into of the same amounts as the contributions to be used as mentioned in sub-paragraph (1) within one month of the day on which they are payable by the member under paragraph 4. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) The policy shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) be subject to the conditions mentioned in paragraphs 2 and 3 of this Schedule; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) allow the member to vary his election under paragraph 1(2); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c) make provision for the arrangements mentioned in paragraphs 10 to 14 and 21 in the event of a change of employment by the member or, as the case may be, potential overpayment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(4) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) a person to whom regulation C8 applies was before the commencement of his relevant service (as defined in that regulation) paying contributions to be used to provide benefits payable in the event of death, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) he has not elected to discontinue those contributions, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

the Committee shall continue to make the appropriate payments in respect of him during the period of his relevant service in accordance with the arrangements made under this paragraph. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 7.[^c-cbaab28254b919c7d2d8bc61bd802d5b]

(1) The Committee shall invest with a relevant body any contributions which are not to be used to provide benefits payable in the event of death. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) Subject to sub-paragraph (3) and paragraphs 10 to 16, on the member ceasing to be a member of the Scheme the Committee shall as soon as reasonably practicable apply the accumulated value of the contributions invested as mentioned in sub-paragraph (1) to the provision of additional pension benefits under a pension policy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) If the member dies before such a policy is entered into, the accumulated value shall be payable to his personal representatives. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(4) The contributions invested in accordance with sub-paragraph (1) shall not be used to provide benefits in the form of a lump sum unless— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) the aggregate of the pension benefits provided under Parts D to G and the additional pension benefits provided by the pension policy mentioned in sub-paragraph (2) do not exceed the annual rate or amount referred to in regulation H5; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) the pension benefits provided under those Parts are being compounded in accordance with that regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 8.[^c-58f2a813bcae2dd5b1f94b73d8f97e88]

The benefits under a pension policy entered into under paragraph 6 or 7 shall be money purchase benefits the value of which is reasonable having regard to the amount of the contributions paid. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 9.[^c-8ac14191f1bd6aaf3ed5f08131007a82]

The Committee— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) shall consult the member before entering into a pension policy under paragraph 6 or 7; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) shall give effect, so far as is practicable and subject to paragraph 8, to his wishes with respect to the benefits to be provided under it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 10. Changes of employment in which membership is continued (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-4f0261570a70211b08efe548d1028f63][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(1) If a member— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) ceases to be employed by the employing authority to which notice was given under paragraph 1(1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) was making payments of additional contributions up to the date of cessation, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c) before the expiry of the period of one month and one day beginning with that date enters a new employment in which he is also a member; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

he may elect that his election to pay contributions under regulation C24 should continue to have effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) An election under sub-paragraph (1) shall be made by notice given in writing to the member’s new employing authority before the expiry of the period of one month beginning with the date on which the new employment begins. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 11.[^c-dabeb630a15d7b2be18b00b6926d655c]

As soon as the new employing authority receives a notice of election under paragraph 10 it shall forward it to the Committee. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 12.[^c-43f1b9bfc239395796a980a77fa5b752]

(1) Subject to sub-paragraph (2), the member shall, with effect from the next pay day after the day on which notice was given under paragraph 10, make payments of contributions under regulation C24 in his new employment under his notice under paragraph 1(1) (as it has effect for the time being). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) No payment of contributions under regulation C24 shall be made with respect to the period (if any) between the two employments mentioned in paragraph 10. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 13.[^c-b41a3cb62cdfb70c47f84540ec00e29c]

The Committee shall in respect of contributions made under regulation C24 by the member in his new employment— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) apply any continuing contributions, which are specified to be used to provide benefits payable in the event of death, towards the pension policy mentioned in paragraph 6; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) continue to invest all contributions paid by him and not specified to be so used in the manner mentioned in paragraph 7. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 14.[^c-584c0b0ee5520036e66ab3727ef85524]

Paragraphs 7(2) and 10 to 16 shall have effect in relation to a cessation of the new employment as if the new employment were the one in relation to which notice was given under paragraph 1(1) . *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 15. Cessation of membership or employment (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-6be0a641e577b84abd1f69fb361c1477][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(1) Where a person who has given notice under paragraph 1(1) of an election under regulation C24 to his employing authority— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) has ceased to be employed by that authority; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) has ceased to be a member without ceasing to be so employed, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

he may elect to have the accumulated value of the invested additional contributions mentioned in paragraph 7(2), used— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (i) in the case mentioned in paragraph ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*a*), in one or more of the ways mentioned in sub-paragraph (2); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] in the case mentioned in paragraph ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*b*) in one or both of the ways mentioned in paragraphs ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*b*) and ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*e*) of that sub-paragraph. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) The ways referred to in sub-paragraph (1) are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) to subscribe to an occupational pension scheme; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (c) to subscribe to a self-employed pension arrangement; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (d) to purchase an appropriate policy from one or more insurance companies; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (e) to subscribe to an additional voluntary contributions scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 16.[^c-966b26bd87d63835f8987bf27f82ed3d]

Where a person receives a refund under regulation C21 as a consequence of ceasing— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) to be employed by an employing authority; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) to be a member, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

he must receive immediate payment of the accumulated value of the invested additional contributions mentioned in paragraph 7(2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 17.[^c-66c3628ece56fb09ba66d9f33b73f264]

Paragraph 3 shall not preclude a member who has again become employed by a LGPS employer (and has not elected under paragraph 10) making a fresh election under regulation C24 by notice under paragraph 1 in relation to his new employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 18. Separate treatment of AVCs from other contributions (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-f3073f7d291fb21edf31928082429d4d][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

Regulations C21 and C23 (return of contributions) and paragraphs 3 and 4 of Schedule C4 (limitations on contributions) do not apply to contributions payable under (or interest on late payments which relate to contributions under) regulation C24. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 19.[^c-16e8a68f16aafa5e005d7975b8f84f45]

The making of contributions under regulations C24, and any rights or liabilities arising under a pension policy entered into under paragraphs 6 or 7, shall be left out of account in calculating the amount of a transfer value payable under Part K; and regulations H4 (forfeiture etc.) and L9 (recovery or retention) and Part J (decisions and appeals) do not apply in relation to benefits under such a policy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 20. Inward transfer of AVC rights (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-116c7c67489b391c30458fd82ead726d][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(1) A member may, when giving a notice under regulation K14(1), also give written notice to the Committee that he wishes it to accept a transfer value relating to some or all of the rights to benefits arising out of an additional voluntary contributions provision or an additional voluntary contributions scheme to be used to provide money purchase benefits as specified in paragraphs 6, 7 and 8. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) Where such a notice is given the transfer value shall be accepted and so used by the Committee, and paragraphs 6 to 9, 18 and 19 shall apply in relation to the transfer value as they apply to contributions, except that for references to a notice under paragraph 1(1) or (2) there shall be substituted a reference to the notice under sub-paragraph (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

### 21. Over-provision: calculation and return of surplus funds (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-041d39de5395ca385e60c2cf54907047][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) In this paragraph— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
