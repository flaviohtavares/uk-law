# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section H2

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## H2. National Insurance (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-97b64550ee6409c9accabe5e0e928e03][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where, but for the revocation of the 1981 regulations, the amount of a benefit would have fallen to be reduced under Part V of those regulations, the amount shall be reduced as if that Part had not been revoked. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
