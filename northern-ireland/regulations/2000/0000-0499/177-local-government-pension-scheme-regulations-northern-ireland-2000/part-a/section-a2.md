# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section A2

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## A2. Interpretation (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-bbf0adcf0191418f61b7576fce424fe4][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-54aa6d971ccfe3f73f79c566b6329fa7] In these regulations the expressions defined in Schedule A1 (general definitions) and Schedule A2 (interpretation for pension sharing on divorce) have the meanings given to them by those Schedules. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) In these regulations, any reference to a Part of these regulations includes a reference to any Schedules to these regulations referred to in that Part, in so far as they apply for its purposes. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) So far as is necessary for the purpose of regulation A1(2) to (10) the regulations shall be deemed to have effect from and including 6th April 1988. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
