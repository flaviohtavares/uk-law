# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section A1

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## A1. Citation, commencement and retrospection (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-b2bbca91dd34b2ec0e8863e5f6c09e94][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000 and shall come into operation on 1st August 2000 (“the commencement date”) and except as provided in paragraphs (2) to (10) shall have effect from that date. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) Part F, except for regulations F7 and F8, regulations G8(3) and G9 shall have effect as from 6th April 1988. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) Regulation B2(1) shall have effect as from 1st October 1989. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(4) Regulation B9(2) shall have effect as from 1st April 1990. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(5) Regulation D16(5) shall have effect as from 17th May 1990. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(6) Paragraphs 1(1)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*c*) and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] *(d*) of Schedule D3 shall have effect as from 1st November 1991. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(7) Regulation C6 shall have effect as from 1st January 1993. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(8) Paragraphs 15 and 16 of Schedule C3 shall have effect as from 1st April 1993. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(9) Regulations C12, D7, D9, D11(2)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*e*), D16(3), E1, H1(2)(*h*) and H6 shall have effect as from 2nd May 1995. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(10) Regulation K17 shall have effect as from 9th April 1997. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
