# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section F7

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## F7. Widowers: requirement for post 31st March 1972 membership and consequent adjustments to “retirement pension” (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-117d17a624f1950cadd419cf3251a7c0][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) For the purpose of determining entitlement to a widower’s pension under regulations F3(1), F4(1) and F5(1), in those regulations— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- “retirement pension” means a retirement pension of which at least part is attributable to a period of membership after 31st March 1972; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- “local government employment” means local government employment of which at least part was employment after that date; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- “preserved benefits” means preserved benefits of which at least part are attributable to a period of membership after that date. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) For the purposes of calculating a widower’s short-term pension under regulation F4(2) or long-term pension under regulation F2(2), F3(2) or F5(2), in those regulations “retirement pension” means a retirement pension calculated— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) by reference to the length in years of the wife’s period of membership after that date, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) if the widower was his wife’s husband at some time while she was in local government employment after 31st March 1972, by reference also to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (i) the length in years of any period treated under paragraph (3) as a period of membership after 31st March 1972, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] the length in years of any period of which notice is given in accordance with paragraph 1(1) of Schedule F1. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] the length in years of any period of membership in respect of which payment under regulation C14 has been or is treated as having been completed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) For the purposes of paragraph (2)(b)(i) the following periods are to be treated as periods of membership after 31st March 1972, namely— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) membership which the deceased became entitled to count after that date by virtue of regulation D7(2), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) membership which the deceased was entitled to count under regulation C9 or paragraph 5 of Schedule M2 (transitional provisions) or regulation D4, D5 or D9 of the 1992 regulations where the necessary payment was made or commenced after that date, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c) membership which the deceased was entitled to count by virtue of a resolution passed under regulation B15 or regulation D7 of the 1992 regulations after that date, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (d) membership which is treated as membership after that date by virtue of paragraph 7 of Schedule M2 (transitional provisions), or regulation D13(2) of the 1992 regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
