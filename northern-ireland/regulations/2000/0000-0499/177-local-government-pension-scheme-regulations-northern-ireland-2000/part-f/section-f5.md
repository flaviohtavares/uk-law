# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section F5

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## F5. Pensioner’s spouse’s long-term pension (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-4342f9e6f473961c950e82b3e31f2c22][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where regulation F4(1) applies the surviving spouse is entitled or, as the case may be, the surviving spouses are jointly entitled, at the end of the period in respect of which a short-term pension is payable under that regulation, to a spouse’s long-term pension. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) Subject to regulation F6 (post retirement marriages), where paragraph (1) applies and any new employment for the purposes of Part I of Schedule D5 (re-employed pensioners) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] or, as the case may be, regulation 113 (abatement) of the 2002 Regulations[^key-bfbbba086be9dd086d3e5631e165da42] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] was not a local government employment, the annual rate of the long-term pension is half the annual rate of the deceased’s retirement pension immediately before the date of death. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) For the purposes of paragraph (2)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) any increase in the deceased’s retirement pension by virtue of regulation D2(2)(i), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) any reduction in that pension under regulation D13 or H2 or by virtue of a surrender under regulation D14, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c) any extinguishment of that pension by virtue of the operation of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] regulation H6 or as the case may be, regulation 52 of the 2002 Regulations or Part 1 of Schedule D5 or, as the case may be, regulation 113 (abatement) of the 2002 Regulations[^key-421236226ef92772be5b6c08ca60eb34] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111],

shall be disregarded. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
