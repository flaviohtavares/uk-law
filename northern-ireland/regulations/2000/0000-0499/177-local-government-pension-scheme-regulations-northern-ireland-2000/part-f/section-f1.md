# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section F1

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## F1. Member’s spouse’s short-term pension (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-209a51f51d78ab72143145e03b249af6][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to regulation G8(3) if a member dies leaving a surviving spouse or spouses, that spouse is entitled or, as the case may be, they are jointly entitled, to a spouse’s short-term pension— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) if the spouse has one or more eligible children in his or her care, for six months after the member’s death, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) otherwise for three months after the member’s death. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) Where paragraph (1) applies then, subject to paragraph 21 of Schedule D5 (re-employed pensioners) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] or, as the case may be (in accordance with Schedule 2 to the Transitional Regulations), regulation 50(4) of the 2002 Regulations[^c-952fd675d5fb5464696c081022560452] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111], the annual rate of the short-term pension is a rate equal to the deceased member’s pensionable remuneration. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) Paragraph 7 of Schedule D1 (by virtue of which a member is, in respect of any period of part-time local government employment, to be treated as having received the remuneration which would have been paid in respect of a single comparable whole-time employment) does not apply to the application of regulation D1 and that Schedule to this regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
