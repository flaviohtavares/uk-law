# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section F6

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## F6. Post retirement marriages (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-464fe1349f7596a00dd96354d33adaf0][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where a surviving spouse who was not married to the deceased at some time while the member was in local government employment after 31st March 1972 and before the date on which he became entitled to a retirement pension— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) the long-term pension under regulation F3, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) the short-term pension under regulation F4, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (c) the long-term pension under regulation F5, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

shall be calculated in accordance with paragraph (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) Where paragraph (1) applies the references in regulations F3(2), F4(2) and F5(2) to the retirement pension are to be construed as references to the part of the pension which is attributable to the period of membership in contracted-out employment after 5th April 1978 in the case of a man and 5th April 1988 in the case of a woman. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
