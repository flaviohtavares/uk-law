> Text as at 2019-11-18.

## F9. Adjustments to pensions of surviving spouses of certain re-employed pensioners (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-201a7f70932cfd3af82f7f69db3ea174][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

The provisions of this Part have effect subject to Part IV of Schedule D5 (re-employed pensioners) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] or, as the case may be, regulation 50 of the 2002 Regulations (in accordance with Schedule 2 to the Transitional Regulations)[^key-afa81475726f8a91b1ea11b8eadf0e34] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111].
