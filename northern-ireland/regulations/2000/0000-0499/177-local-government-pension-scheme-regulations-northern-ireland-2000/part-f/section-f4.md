# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section F4

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## F4. Pensioner’s spouse’s short-term pension (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-222d5eabea887f0ddd9f415038b1b54d][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to regulation G8(3), if a person who— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) was entitled to receive payments in respect of a retirement pension (other than a pension under regulation D18), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) would have been so entitled but for the operation of regulation H6 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] or, as the case may be, regulation 52 of the 2002 Regulations[^key-b9b6f8f7821bdd08681be071e5daae1c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] (commutation in exceptional circumstances of ill-health) or of Part I of Schedule D5 (re-employed pensioners) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] or, as the case may be, regulation 113 (abatement) of the 2002 Regulations[^key-827c3a2b12a61ce977da065733aabfa5] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111],

dies leaving a surviving spouse or spouses, that spouse is entitled or, as the case may be, they are jointly entitled to a spouse’s short-term pension— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (i) if the spouse has one or more eligible children in his or her care, for six months after the deceased’s death, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] otherwise for three months after the deceased’s death. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) Subject to regulation F6 (post retirement marriages), where paragraph (1) applies, the annual rate of the short-term pension is a rate equal to the spouse’s retirement pension immediately before the date of death or the rate it would have been at that date apart from any payment under regulation H6 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] or, as the case may be, regulation 52 of the 2002 Regulations[^key-0379b18de00087ba304f95cdc8089ee3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] or the operation of Part I of Schedule D5 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] or, as the case may be, regulation 113 (abatement) of the 2002 Regulations[^key-e52ff19239a22664ba153e251663652c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111].
