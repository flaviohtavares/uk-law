# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section F3

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## F3. Deferred pensioner’s spouse’s long-term pension (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-0aaf29a79e0df6d9ec814527ba6c9399][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) If a person who is not a member dies leaving a surviving spouse or spouses and at the time of his death he— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) was entitled to preserved benefits, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) was in a local government employment and would have been so entitled if he had ceased to hold that employment immediately before that time, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

that spouse is entitled or, as the case may be, they are jointly entitled, to a spouse’s long-term pension. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) Subject to regulation F6 (post retirement marriages), the annual rate of the long-term pension to which a spouse is entitled under paragraph (1) is half the annual rate of the retirement pension to which the deceased would have been entitled if on the date of death he had become entitled under regulation D5. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3)[^key-8663dd65757ad5fefb93911d4ed6ed4b] Where the deceased was a pension debit member, the long-term pension is equal to half the pension to which he would have been entitled under regulation D5 if he had become entitled to a pension under that regulation on the date that he died. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
