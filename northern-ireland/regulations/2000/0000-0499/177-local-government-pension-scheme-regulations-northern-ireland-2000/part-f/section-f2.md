# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section F2

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## F2. Member’s spouse’s long-term pension (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-46f5e2b84a3eb54531180f43276f36ae][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) If a member who has a statutory pension entitlement dies leaving a surviving spouse or spouses, that spouse is entitled or, as the case may be, they are jointly entitled, at the end of the period in respect of which a short-term pension is payable under regulation F1, to a spouse’s long-term pension. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) Where paragraph (1) applies then, subject to Part IV of Schedule D5 (re-employed pensioners) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] or, as the case may be (in accordance with Schedule 2 to the Transitional Regulations), regulation 50 of the 2002 Regulations[^key-7b23d60426a85dabf086e84226b88b07] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111], the annual rate of the long-term pension is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) if the member’s total period of membership was not less than two years, half the annual rate of the retirement pension to which the spouse would have been entitled if on the date of death the spouse had become entitled under regulation D7 (ill-health, etc.), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) otherwise, one one hundred and sixtieth of the member’s pensionable remuneration, multiplied by the length in years of the member’s total period of membership. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) For the purposes of paragraph (2)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*a*)—

- (a) any increase in the deceased’s retirement pension by virtue of regulation D2(2)(i), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) any reduction in that pension under regulation H2, or by virtue of a surrender under regulation D14, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

shall be disregarded. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
