# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section F10

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## F10. Surviving spouse’s guaranteed minimum pension (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-ff14de78f68965fde8f69d0e57131a1f][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) the employment of a member in any local government employment is contracted-out employment, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) the member has a guaranteed minimum under section 10 of the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^f00028] in relation to benefits under these regulations, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

then, if the member dies at any time leaving a widow or widower, the widow or widower is entitled to a pension at a weekly rate equal to the widow’s or, as the case may be, the widower’s guaranteed minimum (within the meaning of section 13 of that Act (minimum pensions for widows and widowers)) during any period for which that section provides that a pension of not less than that amount is to be payable to the widow or, as the case may be, the widower (unless the widow or widower is entitled to a surviving spouse’s pension at a higher rate). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) The guaranteed minimum referred to in paragraph (1) shall, so far as it is attributable to earnings factors for the tax year 1988-89 or for subsequent tax years, be increased in accordance with the requirements of section 105 of the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993 and to the extent of any orders made thereunder (annual increase of guaranteed minimum pensions). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) Where this regulation applies it overrides any provision in these regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] or the 2002 Regulations or the Transitional Regulations[^key-c210298504055362139c68eb9a30ab1e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] to the extent to which it conflicts with it, except— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) regulation H4 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] or regulation 114 of the 2002 Regulations or regulation 117 of those Regulations (so far as it relates to regulation 114)[^key-7574aa2c49cf3ce494280a54d7b00430] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] (forfeiture of rights), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) regulation H5 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] or regulation 51 of the 2002 Regulations[^key-6ac9b6f25e15541e58f6da8ae2123590] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] (commutation of small pensions). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
