# Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section M5

> Text as at 2019-11-18.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## M5. Right to opt out (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8a522652ce833465bcfbc7b3306ccb16][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111][^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) apart from this regulation, any provision of these regulations, which re-enacts with any modification any provision revoked by these regulations, would place any person to whom a relevant benefit is or may become payable in a worse position in relation to that benefit than that he would have been in if that modification had not been made, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) that person so elects, by notice in writing given to the Committee within the period of six months beginning with the commencement date, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

then, subject to paragraph (3), these regulations shall have effect, in relation to him and to that benefit, as if these regulations had re-enacted the revoked provision without modification. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(2) In this regulation— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- “relevant benefit” means a benefit payable to, or in respect of, a person who before the commencement date— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (a) ceased to hold an employment in respect of which he was a member (whether or not he has subsequently become a member again); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (b) died while in such employment; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- “benefit” includes a return of contributions and any pension payable to a widow, widower or any dependant by virtue of a surrender. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

(3) If an election under paragraph (1) is made in relation to a benefit in respect of a person who is a member, or subsequently becomes a member again— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

- (a) the election shall have effect in relation to the benefit only to the extent that it accrues or has accrued by virtue— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (i) of periods of membership before the cessation referred to in paragraph (2)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*a*) (or, if there has been more than one such cessation, the last of them before the commencement date); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111] of contributions paid in respect of such periods of membership; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
- (b) in determining entitlement to, or the amount of, the benefit to that extent, he shall be treated as if he had never become a member again at any time after the cessation referred to in paragraph (2)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]*a*) (but without prejudice to the application of this paragraph); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]

and these regulations shall have effect accordingly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24f17523d80f91b5e2c8c7c012137111]
