# The Jobseeker’s Allowance (Joint Claims) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section 1

> Text as at 2016-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation[^c-1f6f994d9e43d030048e4dc5872d1c7f]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) These Regulations shall be cited as the Jobseeker’s Allowance (Joint Claims) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000 and shall come into operation on 19th March 2001.

(2) In these Regulations, “the principal Regulations” means the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00005].

(3) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00006] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.
