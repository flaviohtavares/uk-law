# Building Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section 5

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

**5.** In Part P (Sanitary appliances and unvented hot water storage systems) restrictions preventing sanitary accommodation opening directly from kitchens have been limited to buildings other than dwellings and the regulations have been extended to require sanitary accommodation which opens directly from a kitchen to contain a wash hand basin (regulation P4).
