# Building Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section R3

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## R3. Sanitary accommodation and sanitary conveniences

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) If sanitary accommodation is provided in a building, other than in a dwelling or in a shop which has a floor area of less than 50 m<sup>2</sup>, that accommodation shall include reasonable provision for disabled people.

(2) In a dwelling a sanitary convenience shall be provided—

- (a) in the entrance storey; or
- (b) where the entrance storey contains no habitable rooms – in the principal storey.
