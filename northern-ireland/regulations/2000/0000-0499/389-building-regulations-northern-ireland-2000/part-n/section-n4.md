# Building Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section N4

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## N4. Underground foul drainage

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Underground foul drainage shall—

- (a) consist of pipes and fittings—
  - (i) of such size, layout, construction and watertightness; and
  - (ii) with sufficient ventilation,

  to ensure the hygienic conveyance of foul water to a sewer, cesspool, septic tank or similar structure; and
- (b) have such means of access as is necessary to facilitate the clearance of blockages.
