# Building Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section N2

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## N2. Drainage systems

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Every building shall be provided with such sanitary pipework, underground foul drainage and rain-water drainage as may be necessary for the hygienic and adequate disposal of foul water and rain-water from that building.
