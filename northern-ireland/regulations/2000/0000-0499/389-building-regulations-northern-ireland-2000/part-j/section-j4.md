# Building Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section J4

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## J4. Deemed-to-satisfy provisions for regulations J2(1)(b) and J3

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) *The requirements of regulation J2(1)(b) shall be deemed to be satisfied, in the case of a building specified in column (2) of Table J opposite the reference to that regulation, if* —

- (a) *the waste container space is sited in accordance with the provisions specified opposite thereto in column (3); or*
- (b) *the waste container space is sited in accordance with the relevant provisions of the publications specified opposite thereto in column (3), as amended by the publications specified opposite thereto in column (4)*.

(2) *The requirements of regulation J3 shall be deemed to be satisfied, in the case of a building specified in column (2) of Table J opposite the reference to that regulation, if the waste chute system used in the building is designed and constructed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the publications specified opposite thereto in column (3), as amended by the publications specified opposite thereto in column (4)*.
