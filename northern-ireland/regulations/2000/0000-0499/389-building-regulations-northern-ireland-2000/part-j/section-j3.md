# Building Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section J3

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## J3. Waste chute systems

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

A waste chute system shall—

- (a) be of such size, layout and construction as will ensure the hygienic conveyance of waste to a waste container;
- (b) be constructed of non-combustible materials;
- (c) have non-absorbent inner surfaces;
- (d) have adequate means of ventilation to the external air; and
- (e) be so constructed as to prevent the ignition of any part of the building in the event of any waste within the system catching fire.
