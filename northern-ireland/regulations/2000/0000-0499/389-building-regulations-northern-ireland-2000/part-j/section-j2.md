# Building Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section J2

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## J2. Solid waste storage

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) A building shall be provided with adequate space for the storage of waste in a container, and such space shall be—

- (a) sited so as not to be prejudicial to the health of any person;
- (b) sited so as to be reasonably accessible for use by people in the building in the storage of waste and for emptying or removing the container; and
- (c) of sufficient capacity having regard to the quantity of waste and the frequency and method of removal.

(2) Every dwelling—

- (a) on or below the fourth storey of a building, shall have space for an individual waste container or access to a communal waste container or a waste chute system; and
- (b) above the fourth storey of a building, shall have access to a waste chute system.
