# Building Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section E3

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## E3. Internal fire spread – Linings

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

To inhibit the spread of fire within a building the internal linings shall—

- (a) offer adequate resistance to the spread of flame over their surfaces; and
- (b) where they are located in a circulation space, have a low rate of heat release when ignited.
