# Building Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section L5

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## L5. Separation distances from an existing LPG storage tank

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

A building shall be separated by a safe distance from any existing LPG storage tank within the same curtilage.
