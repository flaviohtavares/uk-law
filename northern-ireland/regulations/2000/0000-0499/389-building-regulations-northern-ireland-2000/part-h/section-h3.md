# Building Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section H3

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## H3. Stairs, ladders, ramps and landings

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

A stair, ladder and a ramp and its landings, shall offer reasonable safety to people using them to move between—

- (a) levels within the building; and
- (b) an entrance or exit of the building and the general ground level immediately outside that entrance or exit.
