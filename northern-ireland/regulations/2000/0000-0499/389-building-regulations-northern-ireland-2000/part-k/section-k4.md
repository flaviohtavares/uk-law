# Building Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section K4

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## K4. Deemed-to-satisfy provisions for regulations K2 and K3

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) *The requirements of regulation K2 shall be deemed to be satisfied, in the case of a building specified in column (2) of Table K opposite the reference to that regulation, if the ventilation for the building is designed and constructed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the publications specified opposite thereto in column (3), as amended by the publications specified opposite thereto in column (4)*.

(2) *The requirements of regulation K3 shall be deemed to be satisfied, in the case of a building specified in column (2) of Table K opposite the reference to that regulation, if the ventilation for the building is designed and constructed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the publications specified opposite thereto in column (3), as amended by the publications specified opposite thereto in column (4)*.
