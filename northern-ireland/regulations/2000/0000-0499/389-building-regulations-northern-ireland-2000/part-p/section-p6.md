# Building Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section P6

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## P6. Deemed-to-satisfy provisions for regulation P5

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

*The requirements of regulation P5 shall be deemed to be satisfied, in the case of a building specified in column (2) of Table P opposite the reference to that regulation, if the unvented domestic hot water storage system used in the building is designed, constructed and installed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the publications specified opposite thereto in column (3), as amended by the publications specified opposite thereto in column (4)*.
