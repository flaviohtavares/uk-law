# Building Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section V2

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## V2. Impact with glazing

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Reasonable provision shall be made to limit the risk of people sustaining cutting and piercing injuries from accidental impact with glazing.
