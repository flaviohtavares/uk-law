# Building Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section V4

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## V4. Safe opening and closing of windows, skylights and ventilators

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Any window, skylight or ventilator which can be opened by a person shall be so constructed or equipped that it may be opened, closed and adjusted safely.
