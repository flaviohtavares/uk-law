# Building Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section V1

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## V1. Application and interpretation

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Subject to paragraphs (2) and (3) this Part shall apply to any building or part of a building.

(2) Regulation V3 shall not apply to glazing in a dwelling.

(3) Regulation V5 shall not apply to glazing that is not intended to be cleaned.

(4) In this Part—

- glazing includes glass, plastic and other transparent or translucent materials.
