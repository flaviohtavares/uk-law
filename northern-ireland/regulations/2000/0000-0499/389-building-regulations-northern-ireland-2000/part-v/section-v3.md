# Building Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section V3

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## V3. Transparent glazing

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Transparent glazing, of which people may otherwise be unaware and with which they are likely to collide while in passage in or about a building, shall incorporate features which make it apparent.
