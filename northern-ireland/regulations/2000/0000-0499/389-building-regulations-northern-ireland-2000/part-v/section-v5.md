# Building Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section V5

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## V5. Safe means of access for cleaning glazing

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Reasonable provision shall be made for safe means of access to clean glazing in walls, ceilings and roofs.
