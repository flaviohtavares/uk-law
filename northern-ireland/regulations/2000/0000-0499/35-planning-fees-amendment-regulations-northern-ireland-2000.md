# Planning (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 35

PLANNING

Planning (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000

Made

29th February 2000

Coming into operation

1st April 2000

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 127 and 129(1) of the Planning (Northern Ireland) Order 1991[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These regulations may be cited as the Planning (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000 and shall come into operation on 1st April 2000.

## 2. Amendment of the Planning (Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995

In regulation 17(1) of the Planning (Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995[^f00002] (fees for appeals) for “£120” substitute “£126”.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 29th February 2000.

J. McConnell

A senior officer of the

Department of the Environment

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations amend the Planning (Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995 by increasing the fees payable to the Planning Appeals Commission in respect of appeals by 5% from £120 to £126.
