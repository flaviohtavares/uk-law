## SCHEDULE 7

*Extent: N.I.*

Regulations 21 and 23

### Part I — Requirements Relating to Obligatory Direction Indicators and to Optional Direction Indicators to the Extent Specified in Part II[^key-2adcab08b1abde501c38d53a5eefc52d]

<table>
<tbody>
<tr><td>1. Number (on each side of a vehicle)—</td><td></td></tr>
<tr><td>(a) A motor vehicle with three or more wheels, not being a motor bicycle combination, first used on or after 24th January 1996:</td><td>One front indicator (Category 1, la or lb), one rear indicator (Category 2, 2a or 2b) and one side repeater indicator (Category 5 or 6) or, in the case of a motor vehicle having a maximum speed not exceeding 25 mph, one front indicator (Category 1, la or lb) and one rear indicator (Category 2, 2a or 2b).</td></tr>
<tr><td>(b) A trailer manufactured on or after 24th January 1996 drawn by a motor vehicle:</td><td>One rear indicator (Category 2, 2a or 2b) or, in the case of a trailer towed by a solo motor bicycle or a motor bicycle combination, one rear indicator (Category 12).</td></tr>
<tr><td>(c) A solo motor bicycle and a motor bicycle combination, in each case first used on or after 24th January 1996:</td><td>One front indicator (Category 1, la, lb or 11) and one rear indicator (Category 2, 2a, 2b or 12).</td></tr>
<tr><td>(d) A motor vehicle first used on or after lst April 1936 and before 24th January 1996, a trailer manufactured on or after 1st January 1936 and before 24th January 1996, a pedal cycle with or without a sidecar or a trailer, a horse-drawn vehicle and a vehicle drawn or propelled by hand:</td><td>Any arrangement of indicators so as to satisfy the requirements for angles of visibility in paragraph 3.</td></tr>
<tr><td>(e) A motor vehicle first used before lst January 1936 and any trailer manufactured before that date:</td><td>Any arrangement of indicators so as to make the intention of the driver clear to other road users.</td></tr>

<tr><td>(a) Longitudinal—</td><td></td></tr>

<tr><td>(i) Maximum distance from the side of the vehicle—</td><td></td></tr>
<tr><td>(A) Any vehicle not covered by sub- head (B):</td><td>400 mm</td></tr>

<tr><td>(ii) Minimum separation distance between indicators on opposite sides of a vehicle—</td><td></td></tr>
<tr><td>(A) A motor vehicle (other than a solo motor bicycle or a motor bicycle combination or an invalid carriage having a maximum speed not exceeding 8 mph) first used on or after 24th January 1996, a trailer manufactured on or after 24th January 1996, a horse-drawn vehicle, a pedestrian-controlled vehicle and a vehicle drawn or propelled by hand:</td><td>500 mm or, if the overall width of the vehicle is less than 1400 mm, 400 mm</td></tr>
<tr><td>(B) A solo motor bicycle having an engine exceeding 50 cc and first used on or after 24th January 1996—</td><td></td></tr>
<tr><td>(1) Front indicators:</td><td>300 mm</td></tr>
<tr><td>(2) Rear indicators:</td><td>240 mm</td></tr>

<tr><td>(1) Front indicators:</td><td>240 mm</td></tr>
<tr><td>(2) Rear indicators:</td><td>180 mm</td></tr>
<tr><td>(D) A motor bicycle combination first used on or after 24th January 1996:</td><td>400 mm</td></tr>
<tr><td>(E) An invalid carriage having a maximum speed not exceeding 8 mph—</td><td></td></tr>
<tr><td>(1) Front indicators:</td><td>240 mm</td></tr>
<tr><td>(2) Rear indicators:</td><td>300 mm</td></tr>
<tr><td>(F) A motor vehicle first used before 24th January 1996 and a trailer manufactured before 24th January 1996:</td><td>No requirement</td></tr>
<tr><td>(iii) Minimum separation distance between a front indicator and any dipped-beam head-lamp or front fog lamp—</td><td></td></tr>
<tr><td>(A) Fitted to a motor vehicle, other than a solo motor bicycle or a motor bicycle combination, first used on or after 24th January 1996:</td><td>(a) in the case of a Category 1 indicator, 40 mm;<br>(b) in the case of a Category la indicator, 20 mm;<br>(c) in the case of a Category lb indicator, no requirement</td></tr>

Electrical connections—<br>(a) All indicators on one side of a vehicle together with all indicators on that side of any trailer drawn by the vehicle, while so drawn, shall be operated by one switch;<br>(b) All indicators on one side of a vehicle or combination of vehicles showing a flashing light shall flash in phase, except that in the case of a solo motor bicycle, a motor bicycle combination and a pedal cycle, the front and rear direction indicators on one side of the vehicle may flash alternately.</td></tr>
<tr><td colspan="2">11. Tell-tale—<br>(a) One or more indicators on each side of a vehicle to which indicators are fitted shall be so designed and fitted that the driver when in his seat can readily be aware when it is in operation; or<br>(b) The vehicle shall be equipped with an operational tell-tale for front and rear indicators (including any rear indicator on the rearmost of any trailers drawn by the vehicle).</td></tr>
<tr><td>12. Other requirements—<br>(a) Every indicator (other than a semaphore arm, that is an indicator in the form of an illuminated sign which when in operation temporarily alters the outline of the vehicle to the extent of at least 150 mm measured horizontally and is visible from both the front and rear of the vehicle) shall when in operation show a light which flashes constantly at the rate of not less than 60 nor more than 120 flashes per minute. However, in the event of a failure, other than a short-circuit of an indicator, any other indicator on the same side of the vehicle or combination of vehicles may continue to flash, but the rate may be less than 60 or more than 120 flashes per minute. Every indicator shall when in operation perform efficiently regardless of the speed of the vehicle;<br>(b) Where two front or rear direction indicators are fitted to a motor vehicle first used on or after 24th January 1996, and two rear direction indicators are fitted to a trailer manufactured on or after 24th January 1996, in each case they shall be fitted so as to form a pair.</td></tr>

### Part II — Requirements Relating to Optional Direction Indicators

**1.**[^key-e808827014e4d105256176714af38a17] A vehicle shall not be fitted with a total of more than one front indicator nor more than two rear indicators, on each side.

**2.**[^key-98d349c8221ea68780bc6cf842f9fb0a] Any number of side indicators may be fitted to the side (excluding the front and rear) of a vehicle.

**3.**[^key-049e72243f4bd17417edea29b754cd1a] The only other requirements prescribed by these Regulations in respect of any which are fitted are those specified in paragraphs 5, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12(*a*) and 12(*b*) of Part I.
