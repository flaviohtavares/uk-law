## SCHEDULE 24A — Requirements related to daytime running lamps[^c-377265fafe5567498b9a3f2e88ce1072]

*Extent: N.I.*

Regulations 21, 23

Daytime running lamps must comply with paragraphs 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 and 9 of ECE Regulation 87.
