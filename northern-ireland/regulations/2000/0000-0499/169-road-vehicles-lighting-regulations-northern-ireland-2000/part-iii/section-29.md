# Road Vehicles Lighting Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section 29

> Text as at 2020-09-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 29. Requirements about the use of warning beacons[^key-b9c25b8b234a33e6807bb02b19482724]

*Extent: N.I.*

A person shall not use, or cause or permit to be used, on an unrestricted dual-carriageway road a vehicle which is required to be fitted with at least one warning beacon by regulation 20 unless every such beacon is kept lit.
