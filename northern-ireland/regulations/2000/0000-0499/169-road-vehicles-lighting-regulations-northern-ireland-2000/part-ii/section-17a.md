# Road Vehicles Lighting Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section 17A

> Text as at 2020-09-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 17A. Illuminable signs on buses carrying children[^key-6a8409ef79eab7e30c889040f55a74cc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) From 1<sup>st</sup> September 2014, a person shall not use or cause or permit to be used on a road a bus first used before 1[^key-1bd8e1c218518c0e5d87cba484f54e45]<sup>st</sup> June 2011, which is being used to provide a school service when it is carrying a child to or from his school unless—

- (a) an illuminable sign is fitted to the front of the bus and is plainly visible to road users ahead of the bus; and
- (b) an illuminable sign is fitted to the rear of the bus and is plainly visible to road users behind the bus,

which both comply with the requirements of Part 1 or Part 2 of Schedule 22A and are fitted in accordance with the specifications in Schedule 22D.

(2) From 1<sup>st</sup> September 2014, a person shall not use or cause or permit to be used on a road a bus first used on or after 1[^key-5afbca055a19f93aa9421bb92df30267]<sup>st</sup> June 2011, which is being used to provide a school service when it is carrying a child to or from his school unless—

- (a) an illuminable sign is fitted to the front of the bus and is plainly visible to road users ahead of the bus; and
- (b) an illuminable sign is fitted to the rear of the bus and is plainly visible to road users behind the bus,

which both comply with the requirements of Part 2 of Schedule 22A and are fitted in accordance with the specifications in Schedule 22D.

(3) It shall not be a contravention of regulation 17(1) for a person to cause or permit to be used on a road a bus used to provide a school service where the illuminable signs specified in paragraphs (1) and (2) are fitted to a bus prior to 1<sup>st</sup> September 2014.
