# Road Vehicles Lighting Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section 17D

> Text as at 2020-09-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 17D. Switches[^key-6a8409ef79eab7e30c889040f55a74cc]

*Extent: N.I.*

A bus used to provide a school service shall be so constructed that each illuminable sign, warning light and roof bar with which the vehicle is fitted, is capable of being operated by a switch which complies with the requirements of Schedule 22E.
