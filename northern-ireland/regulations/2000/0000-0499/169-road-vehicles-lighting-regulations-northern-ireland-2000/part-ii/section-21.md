# Road Vehicles Lighting Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section 21

> Text as at 2020-09-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 21. Obligatory lamps, reflectors, rear markings and devices[^c-5de0d43f6f3103e3db09851a1e0bbfcc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Save as provided in the foregoing provisions of these Regulations , in regulation 23A(8)[^c-757005437ae22722e9584a82505edca6] and in paragraphs (2), (2A), (2B) and (2C)[^c-a1c9bce831fad1cbf1a87e55fe5b2b56], every vehicle of a class specified in a Table in Schedule 1 shall be fitted with lamps, reflectors, rear markings and devices which—

- (a) are of a type specified in column 1 of that Table, and
- (b) comply with the relevant installation, alignment and performance requirements set out in the Schedule or Part of a Schedule shown against that type in column 2 of that Table.

(2) The requirements specified in paragraph (1) do not apply in respect of a lamp, reflector, rear marking or device of a type specified in column 1 of a Table in the case of a vehicle shown against it in column 3 of that Table.

(2A)[^c-1bf6fdca0819b0c9c30e30f8562ddfef] The requirements specified in paragraph 5(c) of Schedule 2 shall not apply in the case of a front position lamp capable of emitting a flashing light which is fitted to—

- (i) a pedal cycle; or
- (ii) a trailer drawn by, or a sidecar attached to, a pedal cycle,

unless the lamp is also capable of emitting a steady light.

(2B) The requirements specified in paragraph 5(d) and (e) of Schedule 10 shall not apply in the case of a rear position lamp capable of emitting a flashing light which is fitted to—

- (i) a pedal cycle; or
- (ii) a trailer drawn by, or a sidecar attached to, a pedal cycle,

unless the lamp is also capable of emitting a steady light.

(2C)[^c-4b1c3c61e8daee02dde584e6868f135d] The requirements specified in paragraph (1) do not apply in the case of a headlamp cleaning device fitted to a lamp, other than a gas discharge headlamp or an adaptive front lighting system.

(3) The requirements specified in paragraph (1) apply without prejudice to any additional requirements specified in regulations 23 , 23A[^c-1062c6361e007fe363e0d8bf529c712d] and 24.

(4) The Schedules referred to in the Tables in Schedule 1 are Schedules 2 to 21.
