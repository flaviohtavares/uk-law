# Road Vehicles Lighting Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, SCHEDULE 14

> Text as at 2020-09-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 14

*Extent: N.I.*

### PART I — Requirements relating to Obligatory Reversing Lamps[^c-e642b25d3113aa9b0a12060cdedf7ffa]

Regulations 21, 23

**1.** Obligatory reversing lamps must comply with the requirements of section 6.4 of ECE Regulation 48.

### PART II — Requirements relating to optional reversing lamps[^c-0165a7b9aa574aa8d5f3626758c504a4]

Regulation 23

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**1.** Number: | Not more than two but if the vehicle has an overall length which exceeds 6 m and is—[^c-fabd15a8e4ce39a9e66a8b51f81dd78c]<br>- (a) a bus; or<br>- (b) a vehicle which is not a passenger vehicle,<br>four may be fitted. |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**2.** Position: | No requirement |
| 3\. Angles of visibility: | No requirement |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**4.** Alignment: | To the rear but if, in accordance with paragraph 1, four are fitted the configuration shall be—[^c-231fbd8c267e54422672df32a28222e0]<br>- (a) four to the rear; or<br>- (b) two to the rear and one on each side. |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**5.** Markings— |  |
| (a) A motor vehicle first used on or after or a trailer manufactured on or after 24th January 1996: | An approval mark |
| (b) Any other motor vehicle: | No requirement |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**6.** Size of illuminated area: | No requirement |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**7.** Colour: | White |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**8.** Wattage— |  |
| (a) A reversing lamp bearing an approval mark: | No requirement |
| (b) A reversing lamp not bearing an approval mark: | The total wattage of any one reversing lamp shall not exceed 24 watts |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**9.** Intensity: | No requirement |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**10.** Electrical connections: | No requirement except that if, in accordance with paragraph 4, reversing lamps have been fitted to the side, the following requirements shall apply in relation to those lamps—[^c-d628a5010349dd6c47317b8881530d06]<br>- (a) it shall not be possible for the reversing lamps to be illuminated unless the front and rear position lamps of the vehicle are illuminated at the same time;<br>- (b) the reversing lamps shall be switched on and off by the manual operation of one switch which shall have no other function; and<br>- (c) the reversing lamps shall switch off automatically if the vehicle is moving forward at a speed of 10 km/h or more. |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**11.** Tell-tale— |  |
| (a) A motor vehicle first used on or after 1st July 1954, provided that the electrical connections are such that any[^c-1fe58b1a911c6ecfd8ce130eb59d757b] reversing lamp or lamps fitted to the rear[^c-2b5fe8f4a3a17b17aa987620ff5bc2a5] cannot be illuminated other than automatically by the selection of the reverse gear of the vehicle: | No requirement |
| (b) Any other motor vehicle first used on or after1st July 1954: | A circuit-closed tell-tale shall be fitted |
| (c) A motor vehicle first used before 1st July 1954: | No requirement |
| (d) Any vehicle which is not a motor vehicle: | No requirement |

**12.**[^c-1ffae3a15cf8d34575de186b33ea67a4] Definitions—

In this Schedule “approval mark” means either—

- (a) a marking designated as an approval mark by regulation 5 of the Designation of Approval Marks Regulations and shown at item 21 of Schedule 4 to those Regulations; or
- (b) a marking designated as an approval mark by regulation 4 of the Designation of Approval Marks Regulations and shown at item 23 or 23A of Schedule 2 to those Regulations.
