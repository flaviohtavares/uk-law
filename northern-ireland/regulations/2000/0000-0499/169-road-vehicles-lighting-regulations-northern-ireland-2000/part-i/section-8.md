# Road Vehicles Lighting Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, section 8

> Text as at 2020-09-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 8. Exceptions — police vehicles[^c-44850cc8ebd41931dea9f0abc59cfc60]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A vehicle under the control of the Chief Constable shall be exempt from the requirements of regulations 27 and 28 while it is on patrol within three miles of the land frontier of Northern Ireland, if a Superintendent of the Royal Ulster Constabulary has certified that such exemption is necessary in the case of that vehicle for the purpose of enforcing the law.

(2) Paragraph (1) shall not affect any civil claim for injury or damage to person or property.
