# Road Vehicles Lighting Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, SCHEDULE 21

> Text as at 2020-09-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 21 — Regulations 21 and 23

*Extent: N.I.*

### Part I — Requirements Relating to Obligatory Front Retro Reflectors and to Optional Front Retro Reflectors to the Extent Specified in Part II

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**1.** Number: | Two |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**2.** Position— |  |
| (a) Longitudinal: | No requirement |
| (b) Lateral— |  |
| - (i) Maximum distance from the side of the trailer: | 150 mm |
| - (ii) Minimum separation distance between a pair of front reflectors: | 600 mm or, if the overall width of the trailer is less than 1400 mm, 400 mm |
| (c) Vertical— |  |
| - (i) Maximum height above the ground: | 900 mm or, if the structure of the trailer makes this impracticable, 1500 mm |
| - (ii) Minimum height above the ground: | 250 mm[^c-9322a1ae9c4aca471432fbb22571413b] |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**3.** Angles of visibility— |  |
| (a) Horizontal: | 30° outwards and 5° inwards |
| (b) Vertical— |  |
| - (i) Any case not covered by sub- paragraph (ii): | 10[^c-0dca3757182bad729cf0223313a62e1e]<sup>o</sup> above and below the horizontal |
| - (ii) Where the highest point of the reflecting area is less than 750 mm above the ground: | 10<sup>o</sup> above and 5° below the horizontal |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**4.** Alignment: | To the front |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**5.** Markings: | An approval mark |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**6.** Size of reflecting area: | No requirement |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**7.** Colour: | White |

**8.**[^c-e49655ac180414c471470d85c4d56816] Other requirements—

- (a) Where two front reflectors are required to be fitted they shall form a pair.
- (b) Triangular shaped retro reflectors shall not be fitted to the front of any trailer.

**9.**[^c-b57ef4bc26c51f386a2a573372ae6dcf] Definitions—

In this Schedule—

- “approval mark” means either—
  - (a) a marking designated as an approval mark by regulation 4 of the Designation of Approval Marks Regulations and shown at item 3 or 3A or 3B of Schedule 2 to those Regulations; or
  - (b) a marking designated as an approval mark by regulation 5 of the Designation of Approval Marks Regulation and shown at item 4 of Schedule 4 to those Regulations.

### Part II — Requirements Relating to Optional Front Retro Reflectors[^c-1dd90ab4281ce43307d8421650273d64]

Any number may be fitted and the only requirements prescribed by these Regulations in respect of any which are fitted are that specified in paragraph 8(*b*) of Part I and that the colour shall not be red.
