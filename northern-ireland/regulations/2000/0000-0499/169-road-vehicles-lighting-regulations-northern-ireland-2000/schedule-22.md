## SCHEDULE 22 — Standard sign for buses carrying children[^c-a015aa800ae087d342df99ce00173fcb]

*Extent: N.I.*

Regulation 2(1)

A standard sign shall—

- (a) be of a size and colour indicated in diagram 1; and
- (b) display the “schoolchild” pictogram shown in that diagram.

  [image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2000/169/images/nisr_20000169_en_epp_002.gif)

*Diagram 1*

**Colour**

Shaded areas — yellow retro reflective material

Border and silhouette — black

Dimensions

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| A.  Front— not less than 250mm<br>     Rear— not less than 400mm | B.  Front— not more than 20mm<br>     Rear— not more than 30mm. |
