# Road Vehicles Lighting Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000, SCHEDULE 20

> Text as at 2020-09-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 20

*Extent: N.I.*

Regulation 21 and 23

### Part I — Requirements Relating to Obligatory Pedal Retro Reflectors and Optional Pedal Retro Reflectors to the Extent Specified in Part II[^c-cb23224aff98a093936f2f2a1fca8296]

<table>
<tfoot>
<tr><td colspan="2">In this Schedule “British Standard mark” means the specification or photometric and physical requirements of reflective devices published by the British Standards Institution under the reference BS 6102: Part 2: 1982, namely “BS 6102/2”.</td></tr>
</tfoot>
<tbody>
<tr><td>1. Number:</td><td>Two reflectors on each pedal</td></tr>
<tr><td>2. Position—</td><td></td></tr>
<tr><td>(a) Longitudinal:</td><td>On the leading edge and the trailing edge of each pedal</td></tr>
<tr><td>(b) Lateral:</td><td>No requirement</td></tr>
<tr><td>(c) Vertical:</td><td>No requirement</td></tr>
<tr><td>3. Angles of visibility:</td><td>Such that the reflector on the leading edge of each pedal is plainly visible to the front and the reflector on the trailing edge of each pedal is plainly visible to the rear</td></tr>
<tr><td>4. Markings:</td><td>A British Standard mark</td></tr>
<tr><td>5. Size of reflecting area:</td><td>No requirement</td></tr>
<tr><td>6. Colour:</td><td>Amber</td></tr>
<tr><td>7. Definitions—</td><td></td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>

### Part II — Requirements Relating to Optional Pedal Retro Reflectors[^c-f266d89b42c60e858b39233faf6fb128]

Any number may be fitted and the only requirement prescribed by these Regulations in respect of any which are fitted is that specified in paragraph 6 of Part I.
