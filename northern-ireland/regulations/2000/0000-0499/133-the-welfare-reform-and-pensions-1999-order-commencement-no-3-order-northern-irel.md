# The Welfare Reform and Pensions (1999 Order) (Commencement No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2000

> Text as at 2001-03-15.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

2000 No. 133 (C. 5)

PENSIONS; SOCIAL SECURITY; FAMILY LAW

The Welfare Reform and Pensions (1999 Order) (Commencement No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2000

Made

17th April 2000

The Department for Social Development, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 1(2) of the Welfare Reform and Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1999[^f00001], and now vested in it[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and interpretation[^c-c0b7db214a9d4ef53b101feedbc7b6e3]

(1) This Order may be cited as the Welfare Reform and Pensions (1999 Order) (Commencement No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2000.

(2) In this Order, “the Order” means the Welfare Reform and Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1999 and any reference to a numbered Article or Schedule is to the Article of, or Schedule to, the Order.

## 2. Appointed days[^c-32247f762a362757825ce66513f02e7e]

(1) The day appointed for the coming into operation of paragraph 60 of Schedule 9 (deregulation and contracting out) is 18th April 2000.

(2) The day appointed for the coming into operation of Article 66 (child benefit: claimant to state national insurance number)—

- (a) for the purpose only of authorising the making of regulations, is 18th April 2000, and
- (b) for all other purposes, is 15th May 2000.

(3) The day appointed for the coming into operation of—

- (a) the provisions of the Order specified in Part I of the Schedule—
  - (i) for the purpose only of authorising the making of regulations, is 24th April 2000, and
  - (ii) for all other purposes, is 9th April 2001;
- (b) the provisions of the Order specified in Part II of the Schedule, in so far as not already in operation, is 25th April 2000;
- (c) the provisions of the Order specified in Part III of the Schedule, in so far as not already in operation, is 1st October 2000;
- (d) the provisions of the Order specified in Part IV of the Schedule, in so far as not already in operation, is 1st December 2000, and
- (e) the provisions of the Order specified in Part V of the Schedule, in so far as not already in operation, is 8th October 2001.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 17th April 2000.

John O'Neill

Senior Officer of the

Department for Social Development

This Order provides for the coming into operation of the following provisions of the Welfare Reform and Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1999 (“the Order”)—

- paragraph 60 of Schedule 9 which amends the Deregulation and Contracting Out (Northern Ireland) Order 1996 on 18th April 2000 (Article 2(1)),
- Article 66 (which provides for entitlement to child benefit to be conditional on the claimant’s stating, or providing information about, or applying for, his national insurance number) on 18th April 2000, for the purpose only of authorising the making of regulations, and on 15th May 2000 for all other purposes (Article 2(2)),
- Articles 51 to 53 (which make provision in connection with bereavement benefits), and related provisions in Schedules 8 and 10, and Articles 67 and 76 in so far as they relate to them, on 24th April 2000, for the purpose only of authorising the making of regulations, and on 9th April 2001 for all other purposes (Part I of the Schedule),
- certain provisions of Schedules 2 and 10 (which contain miscellaneous amendments of the law relating to pensions and repeals) in so far as those provisions are not already in operation, and Articles 17 and 76 in so far as they relate to them, on 25th April 2000 (Part II of the Schedule),
- Part II and Schedule 1 (which introduces arrangements for stakeholder pension schemes), in so far as those provisions are not already in operation, on 1st October 2000 in part (Part III of the Schedule), and fully on 8th October 2001 (Part V of the Schedule),
- Articles 21, 22, 23 (which make provision in connection with the treatment of pensions on divorce or on annulment of marriage) and Part V and Schedules 5 and 6 (which introduce arrangements for the sharing of pensions), in so far as those provisions are not already in operation, and related consequential amendments and repeals in Schedules 8, 9 and 10, and Articles 67, 74(1) and 76 in so far as they relate to them, on 1st December 2000 (Part IV of the Schedule).
