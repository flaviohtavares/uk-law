# Animal Feedingstuffs from Belgium (Control) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 233

FOOD

Animal Feedingstuffs from Belgium (Control) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000

Made

11th July 2000

Coming into operation

21st August 2000

The Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety[^f00001], being a Department designated[^f00002] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00003] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by the said section 2(2) and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Animal Feedingstuffs from Belgium (Control) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000 and shall come into operation on 21st August 2000.

## 2. Revocation

The Animal Feedingstuffs from Belgium (Control) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000[^f00004] are hereby revoked.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety on 11th July 2000.

D. A. Baker

Senior Officer of the

Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations revoke the Animal Feedingstuffs from Belgium (Control) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000 (“the revoked Regulations”) and implement, in relation to products for animal feeding, Commission Decision [2000/301/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2000/0301) repealing the protective measures with regard to contamination by dioxins of certain products of porcine or poultry origin intended for human or animal consumption (O.J. No. L97, 19.04.2000, p. 16). (The protective measures concerned were contained in Commission Decision [1999/788/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1999/0788) (O.J. No. L310, 4.12.1999, p. 62) as amended by Commission Decision [2000/150/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2000/0150) (O.J. No. L50, 23.2.2000, p. 25) and were implemented, in relation to products for animal feeding, by the revoked Regulations).
