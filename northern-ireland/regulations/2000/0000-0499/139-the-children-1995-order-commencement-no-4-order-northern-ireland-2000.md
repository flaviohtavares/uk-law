# The Children (1995 Order) (Commencement No. 4) Order (Northern Ireland) 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 139 (C. 6)

CHILDREN

The Children (1995 Order) (Commencement No. 4) Order (Northern Ireland) 2000

Made

17th April 2000

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by Article 1(2) of the Children (Northern Ireland) Order 1995[^f00001] and of all other powers in that behalf, I hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Children (1995 Order) (Commencement No. 4) Order (Northern Ireland) 2000.

(2) In this Order, “the Order” means the Children (Northern Ireland) Order 1995.

## 2. Appointed days

The day appointed for the coming into operation of—

- (a) Article 176 of the Order (children accommodated in schools)—
  - (i) for the purpose only of authorising the making of regulations, is 15th May 2000, and
  - (ii) for all other purposes, is 4th September 2000; and
- (b) Article 149(1)(i) (inspection of schools providing accommodation) and Article 178 (search warrants) in so far as it relates to Article 176, is 4th September 2000.

Peter Mandelson

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Northern Ireland Office

17th April 2000

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order provides for the coming into operation on 15th May 2000 of provisions of the Children (Northern Ireland) Order 1995 (“the 1995 Order”) enabling the making of Regulations. It also provides for the coming into operation on 4th September 2000 of Articles 149(1)(i), 176 and 178 (in so far as it relates to Article 176) of the 1995 Order which relate to children accommodated in schools.

(This note is not part of the Order.)
