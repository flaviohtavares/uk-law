# The Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000 (Commencement) Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 412 (C. 32)

POLICE

The Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000 (Commencement) Order 2000

Made

19th December 2000

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by Section 79 of the Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000 (Commencement) Order 2000.

**2.** The provisions of the Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000 specified in the Schedule to this Order shall come into force on 22nd December 2000.

Peter Mandelson

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Northern Ireland Office

19th December 2000
