# Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 13. Conditions attached to provisional licences: holders of driving permits other than licences granted under Part II of the Order

A holder of a provisional licence authorising the driving of vehicles of any class who also holds a permit by virtue of which he is at any time—

- (a) treated, by virtue of regulation 55, as the holder, for the purposes of Article 3 of the Order, of a licence authorising the driving of vehicles of that class; or
- (b) entitled, pursuant to Article 3(1) of the Motor Vehicles (International Circulation) Order (Northern Ireland) 1990[^f00023], to drive motor vehicles of that class,

need not comply with regulation 12 at that time.
