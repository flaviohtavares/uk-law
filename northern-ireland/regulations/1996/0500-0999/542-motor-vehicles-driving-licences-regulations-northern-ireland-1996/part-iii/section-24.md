# Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 24

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 24. Fees for theory tests

The fee payable for a theory test to be conducted by an appointed person in respect of any category of motor vehicle shall be £16·50 and is payable to the Department.
