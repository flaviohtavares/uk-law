# Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 44

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 44. Obligatory revocation of large goods vehicle drivers' licences and disqualification on revocation

(1) The prescribed circumstances for the purposes of Article 73(1)(*a*) of the Order are that, in the case of the holder of a large goods vehicle driver’s licence who is under the age of 21, the counterpart of his ordinary driving licence bears more than one relevant endorsement.

(2) Where a large goods vehicle driver’s licence is revoked under Article 73(1)(*a*) the cases in which the person whose licence has been revoked must be disqualified indefinitely or for a period, shall be determined by the Department.

(3) Where the Department determines that a person whose licence has been revoked under Article 73(1)(*a*) is to be disqualified for a period he shall be disqualified until he reaches 21 years of age or for such longer period as the Department may determine.
