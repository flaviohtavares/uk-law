## SCHEDULE 2 — Revocations

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Article 3

**1.**[^key-debc0775aa2f31d9f26e6a45f67173e3] The item described in the Second Schedule to the Roads (Speed Limit) Order (Northern Ireland), 1960[^f00002] as follows:

- Londonderry — Claudy Road, Class I No. A.6, Co. Londonderry, from a point approximately 385 yards east of the junction with Craig Road to a point approximately 85 yards west of this junction.
