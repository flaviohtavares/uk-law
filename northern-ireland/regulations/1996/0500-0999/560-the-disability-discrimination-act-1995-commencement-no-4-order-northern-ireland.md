# The Disability Discrimination Act 1995 (Commencement No. 4) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 560 (C. 27)

DISABLED PERSONS

The Disability Discrimination Act 1995 (Commencement No. 4) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996

Made

2nd December 1996

The Department of Health and Social Services, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 70(3) of the Disability Discrimination Act 1995[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Disability Discrimination Act 1995 (Commencement No. 4) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of section 16(3) of the Disability Discrimination Act 1995, to the extent that it is not already in operation, is 2nd December 1996.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health and Social Services on

W. B. Smith

Assistant Secretary

2nd December 1996.

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order brings into operation on 2nd December 1996 section 16(3) of the Disability Discrimination Act 1995 to the extent that it is not already in operation. Section 16(3) in so far as it relates to the definition of “sub-lease” and “sub-tenancy” was brought into operation on 30th May 1996.

(This note is not part of the Order.)
