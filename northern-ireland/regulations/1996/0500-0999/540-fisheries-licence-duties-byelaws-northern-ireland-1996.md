# Fisheries (Licence Duties) Byelaws (Northern Ireland) 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 540

FISHERIES

Fisheries (Licence Duties) Byelaws (Northern Ireland) 1996

Made

19th November 1996

Coming into operation

1st January 1997

The Fisheries Conservancy Board for Northern Ireland in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Sections 26(1), 114(1)(*b*) and 115(1)(*b*) of the Fisheries Act (Northern Ireland) 1966[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf with the approval of the Department of Agriculture hereby makes the following Byelaws:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Byelaws may be cited as the Fisheries (Licence Duties) Byelaws (Northern Ireland) 1996 and shall come into operation on 1st January 1997.

The Fisheries (Licence Duties) Byelaws (Northern Ireland) 1995[^f00003] are hereby revoked.

(This note is not part of the Byelaws.)

These Byelaws further amend the Fisheries Consolidated and Amendment Byelaws (Northern Ireland) 1989 to increase the licence duties payable from 1st January 1997 for fishing with rod and line and hand line for salmon and freshwater fish and for commercial fishing engines used for the taking of salmon and freshwater fish other than eels together with the fee payable for a licence which authorises the holder to buy and sell salmon, trout and eels.
