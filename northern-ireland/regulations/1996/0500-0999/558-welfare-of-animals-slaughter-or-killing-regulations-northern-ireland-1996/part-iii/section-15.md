# Welfare of Animals (Slaughter or Killing) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 15. The killing of animals elsewhere than in a slaughterhouse or knacker’s yard

Subject to regulations 16 to 19, where any soliped, ruminant, pig, rabbit or bird is killed elsewhere than in a slaughterhouse or knacker’s yard, that animal shall be killed in accordance with Parts I and III of Schedule 5.
