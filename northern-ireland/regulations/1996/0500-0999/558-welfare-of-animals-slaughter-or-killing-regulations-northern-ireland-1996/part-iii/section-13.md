# Welfare of Animals (Slaughter or Killing) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 13. Application of Part III

(1) This Part applies to the slaughter or killing of animals elsewhere than in a slaughterhouse or knacker’s yard.

(2) This Part does not apply in the case of any animal which has to be slaughtered or killed immediately for emergency reasons relating to the welfare of that animal.
