# Welfare of Animals (Slaughter or Killing) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Welfare of Animals (Slaughter or Killing) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996 and shall come into operation on 30th November 1996.
