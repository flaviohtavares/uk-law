# Welfare of Animals (Slaughter or Killing) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 21

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 21. Additional requirements relating to slaughter by a religious method

Schedule 12 shall have effect in relation to the slaughter of any animal by a religious method.
