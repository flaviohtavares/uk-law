# Gaming and Amusements with Prizes (Variation of Monetary Limits) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 572

BETTING, GAMING AND LOTTERIES

Gaming and Amusements with Prizes (Variation of Monetary Limits) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996

Made

10th December 1996

To be laid before Parliament under paragraph 3(3) of Schedule 1 to the Northern Ireland Act 1974

Coming into operation

27th January 1997

The Department of Health and Social Services, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Articles 75(2), 76(5), 77(4), 108(16) and 154(7) of the Betting, Gaming, Lotteries and Amusements (Northern Ireland) Order 1985[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Gaming and Amusements with Prizes (Variation of Monetary Limits) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996 and shall come into operation on 27th January 1997.

(2) In this Order “the Order” means the Betting, Gaming, Lotteries and Amusements (Northern Ireland) Order 1985.

## 2. Maximum amount of weekly winnings in games of bingo played simultaneously on different bingo club premises

For the purposes of Article 75(2)(ii) of the Order instead of £10,000[^f00002] the amount £25,000 is hereby specified.

## 3. Maximum amount by which weekly winnings on bingo club premises may exceed the stakes hazarded

For the purposes of Article 76(5) of the Order instead of £2,500[^f00003] the amount £5,000 is hereby specified.

## 4. Conditions governing gaming for prizes on bingo club premises

In Article 77(1)(*b*) and (*e*) of the Order for “£27·50”[^f00004] there shall be substituted “£30”.

## 5. Maximum entitlement to prizes in gaming by means of gaming machines on licensed premises within the meaning of the Licensing (Northern Ireland) Order 1990

In Article 108(8) and (9) of the Order for “£8”[^f00005] there shall be substituted “£10”.

## 6. Conditions governing amusements with prizes at certain commercial entertainments

In Article 154(4)(*b*) of the Order for “£25”[^f00006] there shall be substituted “£30”.

## 7. Revocations

Articles 3, 4, 5 and 10 of the Betting, Gaming, Lotteries and Amusements with Prizes (Variation of Fees and Monetary Limits) Order (Northern Ireland) 1995[^f00007] are hereby revoked.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health and Social Services on 10th December 1996.

Jeremy Harbison

Under Secretary

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order—

- (a) increases the maximum permitted aggregate amount of winnings in respect of games of bingo played in 1 week simultaneously on different bingo club premises from £10,000 to £25,000;
- (b) increases the maximum amount by which weekly winnings on bingo club premises may exceed the stakes hazarded from £2,500 to £5,000;
- (c) increases, in any 1 determination of winners in gaming for prizes on bingo club premises, the maximum permitted aggregate amount taken by way of the sale of chances from £27·50 to £30 and the maximum permitted aggregate amount or value of prizes from £27·50 to £30;
- (d) increases the maximum entitlement to prizes in gaming by means of a gaming machine installed on licensed premises (within the meaning of the Licensing (Northern Ireland) Order 1990) from £8 to £10;
- (e) increases the maximum permitted aggregate amount taken by way of the sale of chances in any 1 determination of winners in amusements with prizes at certain commercial entertainments from £25 to £30.
