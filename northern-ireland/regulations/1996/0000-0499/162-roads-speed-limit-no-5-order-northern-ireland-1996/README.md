(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order makes the roads and lengths of road in Schedule 1 restricted roads, thereby imposing a 30 miles per hour speed limit on those roads.

The lengths of road to which the items in paragraphs 1 and 2 of Schedule 2 relate are now comprised in the length of road described in item No. 17 of Schedule 1.

The lengths of road to which the items revoked under paragraphs 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 (item Nos. 393 and 441), 8 and 9 of Schedule 2 relate are now comprised in greater lengths of road described in item Nos. 81, 13, 7, 82, 28, 77, 8 and 98 respectively of Schedule 1.
