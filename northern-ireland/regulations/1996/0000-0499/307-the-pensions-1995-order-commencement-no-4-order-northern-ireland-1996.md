# The Pensions (1995 Order) (Commencement No. 4) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 307 (C. 18)

PFNSIONS

The Pensions (1995 Order) (Commencement No. 4) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996

Made

22nd July 1996

The Department of Health and Social services, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by article 1(2) of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1995[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Pensions (1995 Order) (Commencement No. 4) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996.

(2) In this Order, “the Order” means the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1995.

## 2. Appointed days

The day appointed for the coming into operation of—

- (a) the provisions of the Order specified in Part I of the Schedule, for the purpose only of authorising the making of regulations, is 23rd July 1996;
- (b) the provisions of the Order specified in Part II of the Schedule, for the purpose of authorising the making of rules, is 23rd July 1996.
- (c) Article 113 is 23rd July 1996;
- (d) the provisions of the Order specified in Part III of the Schedule is 1st August 1996.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health and Social Services on

W. G. Purdy

Assistant Secretary

22nd July 1996.

This Order provides for the coming into operation of further provisions of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1995 on the following dates—

- 23rd July 1996 for—
  - the purpose only of authorising the making of regulations under provisions relating to time limits for paying civil penalties, the requirement for an independent trustee, the Pensions Compensation Board, the review of decisions of the Occupational Pensions Regulatory Authority and the jurisdiction of the Pensions Ombudsman;
  - provisions relating to a breach of regulations;
  - the purpose only of authorising the making of rules relating to the powers of the Pensions Ombudsman as regards the payment of allowances;
- 1st August 1996 for—
  - provisions relating to the establishment of the Pensions Compensation Board.
