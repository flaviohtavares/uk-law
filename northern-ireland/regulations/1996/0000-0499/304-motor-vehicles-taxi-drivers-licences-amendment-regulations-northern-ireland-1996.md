# Motor Vehicles (Taxi Drivers' Licences) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 1996-07-17. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisr/1996/304
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

1996 No. 304

ROAD TRAFFIC AND VEHICLES

Motor Vehicles (Taxi Drivers' Licences) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996

Made

17th July 1996

Coming into operation

27th August 1996

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Articles 79A(2) and (4) and 218(1) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001], and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, and with the approval of the Department of Finance and Personnel as regards regulation 2(1), hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement[^c-7bdc59b9735a180eeae28e1d18f1bb2a]

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Taxi Drivers' Licences) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996 and shall come into operation on 27th August 1996.

## 2. Amendment of the Motor Vehicles (Taxi Drivers' Licences) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1991[^c-0ea738d929f6cc2a82f28feb66e9a7c0]

The Motor Vehicles (Taxi Drivers' Licences) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1991[^f00002] shall be amended as follows—

(1) In regulation 4 (fee for a licence) for paragraph (1) there shall be substituted—

> (1) The fee for a licence shall be £58·50.

.

(2) Part III and the Schedule are hereby revoked.

## 3. Revocation[^c-2c550744d6e9d33e84fbb0a4435d9b44]

The Motor Vehicles (Taxi Drivers' Licences) (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995[^f00003] and the Motor Vehicles (Taxi Drivers' Licences) (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00004] are hereby revoked.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 17th July 1996.

Trevor Pearson

Assistant Secretary

The Department of Finance and Personnel hereby approves regulation 2(1).

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance and Personnel on 17th July 1996.

V. N. Hewitt

Assistant Secretary

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

Article 8 of the Deregulation and Contracting Out (Northern Ireland) Order 1996 amends Article 79A(3)(*b*)(ii) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981 on 27th August 1996 by removing the requirement to pass a test of competence before the grant of a taxi driver’s licence. As a consequence of this amendment the Motor Vehicles (Taxi Drivers' Licences) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1991 are amended as follows—

(1) The two tier system of fees for licences is replaced by a single fee for a licence and the fee payable is £58·50.

(2) The provisions which dealt with tests of competence are revoked.

The Motor Vehicles (Taxi Drivers' Licences) (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995 and the Motor Vehicles (Taxi Drivers' Licences) (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996 are revoked as a consequence of these Regulations.
