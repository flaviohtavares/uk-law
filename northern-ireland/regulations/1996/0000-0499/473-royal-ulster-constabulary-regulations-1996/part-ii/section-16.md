# Royal Ulster Constabulary Regulations 1996, section 16

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 16. Transfer of personal records

Where a member transfers to another police force his personal record shall be transferred to the chief officer of police of that other police force.
