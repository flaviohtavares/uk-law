# Royal Ulster Constabulary Regulations 1996, section 14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 14. Retirement

(1) Without prejudice to the provisions mentioned in paragraph (3) and subject to paragraph (2), a member may retire only if he has given to the Police Authority a month’s written notice of his intention to retire or such shorter notice as may have been accepted by that Authority:

- Provided that, while suspended under the Discipline Regulations, a member may not, without the consent of the chief constable, give notice for the purposes of this regulation or retire in pursuance of a notice previously given.

(2) In the case of a chief constable, deputy chief constable or assistant chief constable, paragraph (1) shall have effect as if—

- (a) for “a month's” there shall be substituted “three months' ”; and
- (b) for “chief constable” there were substituted “Police Authority”.

(3) The provisions referred to in paragraph (1) are—

- (a) the provisions of section 7 of the Act relating to retirement in the interests of efficiency;
- (b) the provisions of the Pensions Regulations relating to compulsory retirement; and
- (c) the provisions of the Discipline Regulations relating to resignation as an alternative to dismissal.
