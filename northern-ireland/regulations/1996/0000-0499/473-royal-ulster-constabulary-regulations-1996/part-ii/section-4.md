# Royal Ulster Constabulary Regulations 1996, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. Ranks

The force shall consist of members of the following ranks—

- Chief Constable
- Deputy Chief Constable
- Assistant Chief Constable
- Superintendent
- Chief Inspector
- Inspector
- Sergeant
- Constable.
