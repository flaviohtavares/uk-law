# Royal Ulster Constabulary Regulations 1996, section 59

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 59. Allowances in respect of periods of suspension

This Part shall have effect in relation to a member suspended under the Discipline Regulations, subject to the provisions of paragraphs 2 and 3 of Schedule 7.
