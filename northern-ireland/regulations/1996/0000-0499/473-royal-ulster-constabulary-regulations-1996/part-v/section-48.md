# Royal Ulster Constabulary Regulations 1996, section 48

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 48. Restriction on payments for private employment of police

Without prejudice to the generality of regulation 47, a member who is engaged on duty at the request of any person who has agreed to pay the Police Authority for the member’s services shall not be entitled to any payment for those services except as provided by the regulations applicable to him.
