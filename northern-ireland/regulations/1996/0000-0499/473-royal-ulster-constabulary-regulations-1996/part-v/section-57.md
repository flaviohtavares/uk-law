# Royal Ulster Constabulary Regulations 1996, section 57

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 57. Allowance for recurring escort duty, etc.

An allowance may be paid, of such amount and under such conditions as may be determined by the Secretary of State on the recommendation of the Police Authority, in respect of recurring escort duty or other specific duties involving recurring retention on duty beyond the normal daily period and not covered by any other payment.
