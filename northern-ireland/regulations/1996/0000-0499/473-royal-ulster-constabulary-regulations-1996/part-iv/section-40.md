# Royal Ulster Constabulary Regulations 1996, section 40

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 40. Reckoning of service in an airport constabulary

Where a member of an aerodrome constabulary has been transferred to the force by an order under section 30 of the Aviation Security Act 1982[^f00011] then, for the purposes of regulation 36, his service in any rank in that constabulary shall be treated as if it were service in the corresponding rank in the force.
