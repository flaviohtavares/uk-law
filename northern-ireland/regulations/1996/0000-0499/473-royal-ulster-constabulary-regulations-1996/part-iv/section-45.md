# Royal Ulster Constabulary Regulations 1996, section 45

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 45. Calculation of monthly, weekly and daily pay

(1) A month’s pay shall be calculated, for all purposes, at a monthly rate of pay determined by dividing by 12 the annual rate.

(2) A week’s pay shall be calculated, for all purposes, at a weekly rate of pay determined by dividing by 52⅙ the annual rate.

(3) A day’s pay shall be calculated, except for the purposes of regulations 24 and 25, at a daily rate determined by dividing by 7 the weekly rate, determined as aforesaid.
