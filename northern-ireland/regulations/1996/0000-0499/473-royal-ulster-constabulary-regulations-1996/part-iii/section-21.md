# Royal Ulster Constabulary Regulations 1996, section 21

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 21. Meaning of “day” in Part III

(1) In this Part, the expression “day”, in relation to members, means a period of 24 hours commencing at such time or times as the chief constable shall fix and the chief constable may fix different times in relation to different groups of members.

(2) In discharging his functions under paragraph (1), the chief constable shall have regard to the wishes of the central committee.
