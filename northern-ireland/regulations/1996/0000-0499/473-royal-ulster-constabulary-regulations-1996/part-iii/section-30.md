# Royal Ulster Constabulary Regulations 1996, section 30

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 30. Annual leave

(1) Every member shall, so far as the exigencies of duty permit, be granted annual leave in accordance with Schedule 4.

(2) The annual leave of a member shall be additional to the days upon which he is not required to perform police duties in accordance with—

- (a) regulation 25, in the case of a member below the rank of inspector, or
- (b) regulation 26 or 27, in the case of a member of, or above, that rank;

and a member below the rank of superintendent shall, so far as the exigencies of duty permit, be allowed to take his annual leave in one period continuous with such days as aforesaid falling within the period in which he desires to take annual leave.
