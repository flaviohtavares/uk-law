# Royal Ulster Constabulary Regulations 1996, section 64

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 64. Ownership of uniform and equipment

Subject to regulation 65, the uniform and equipment issued by the Police Authority shall not become the property of the member to whom they are issued and shall be handed back by him to the Police Authority on his leaving the force.
