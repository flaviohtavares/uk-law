# Royal Ulster Constabulary Regulations 1996, section 63

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 63. Re-issue of uniform and equipment

Uniform and equipment handed back to the Police Authority shall not be re-issued to another member until it has received any necessary cleaning or renovation and is in serviceable condition.
