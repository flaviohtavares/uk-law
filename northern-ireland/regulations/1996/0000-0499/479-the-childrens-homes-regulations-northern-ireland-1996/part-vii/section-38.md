# The Children’s Homes Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 38

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 38. Revocation

The Children and Young Persons (Voluntary Homes) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1975[^f00013], insofar as they continue to have effect, are hereby revoked.
