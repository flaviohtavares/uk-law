# The Children’s Homes Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 30

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 30. Limits on number of children accommodated

The registration authority may limit the number of children to be accommodated in a registered children’s home to such number as it may specify by means of a condition imposed under Article 97 of the Order.
