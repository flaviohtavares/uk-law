# The Children’s Homes Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 29

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 29. Application for registration of children’s home

(1) An application for registration under Article 96 of the Order (registration of a children’s home) shall be made in writing.

(2) Where the applicant is an individual his application shall be accompanied by the particulars specified in Part I of Schedule 5.

(3) Where the applicant is a body corporate or unincorporate the application shall be accompanied by the particulars specified in Part II of Schedule 5.

(4) Whether the applicant is an individual or a body corporate or unincorporate his application shall also be accompanied by the particulars specified in, or referred to in, Part III of Schedule 5.
