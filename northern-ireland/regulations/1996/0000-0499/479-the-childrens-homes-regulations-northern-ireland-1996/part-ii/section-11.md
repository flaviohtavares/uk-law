# The Children’s Homes Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 11. Religious observance

The responsible authority shall ensure that each child accommodated in each children’s home is enabled, so far as is practicable, to attend the services of, to receive instruction in, and to observe any requirement (whether as to dress, diet or otherwise) of, the religious persuasion to which he belongs.
