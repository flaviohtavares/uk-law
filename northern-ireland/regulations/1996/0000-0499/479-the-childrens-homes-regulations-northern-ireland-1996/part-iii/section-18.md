# The Children’s Homes Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 18

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 18. Regulations and guidance

A copy of these Regulations and of any relevant guidance issued by the Department[^f00009] shall be kept in each children’s home and made available when required to—

- (a) all staff;
- (b) every child accommodated in the home;
- (c) the parents or guardians of any child accommodated in the home; and
- (d) any person who is not a parent of a child accommodated in the home but has parental responsibility for him.
