# The Children’s Homes Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 20

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 20. Absence of a child without permission

(1) The responsible authority shall draw up and cause to be recorded in writing the procedure to be followed when any child accommodated in a children’s home is absent without permission.

(2) The responsible authority shall ensure that the procedure mentioned in paragraph (1) is drawn to the attention of the children accommodated in the children’s home and the staff of, and others working in, the home.
