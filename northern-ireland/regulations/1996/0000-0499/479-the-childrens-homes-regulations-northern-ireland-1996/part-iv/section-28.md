# The Children’s Homes Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 28

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 28. Change of person in charge

The responsible authority shall give at least one month’s prior notice, in writing, to the registration authority of any proposal to change the person in charge of a voluntary home, giving the particulars specified in Schedule 6.
