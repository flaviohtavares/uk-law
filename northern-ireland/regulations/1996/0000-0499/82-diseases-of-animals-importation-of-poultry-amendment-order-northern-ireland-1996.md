# Diseases of Animals (Importation of Poultry) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 82

ANIMALS

Diseases of Animals (Importation of Poultry) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996

Made

7th March 1996

Coming into operation

29th March 1996

The Department of Agriculture, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Articles 24(1), 30 and 60(1) of the Diseases of Animals (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Diseases of Animals (Importation of Poultry) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996 and shall come into operation on 29th March 1996.

## 2. Amendment of the Diseases of Animals (Importation of Poultry) Order (Northern Ireland) 1965

In Article 4(1) of the Diseases of Animals (Importation of Poultry) Order (Northern Ireland) 1965[^f00002] sub-paragraphs (b) and (c) shall be revoked.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture on

P. T. Toal

Assistant Secretary

7th March 1996.

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order amends the Diseases of Animals (Importation of Poultry) Order (Northern Ireland) 1965 by revoking sub-paragraphs (b) and (c) of Article 4(1) which prohibit the importation into Northern Ireland of carcases of poultry and eggs except under licence. The licensing of the importation of those products is now provided for in the Diseases of Animals (Importation of Bird Products) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996, [S.R. 1996 No. 81](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1996/81).
