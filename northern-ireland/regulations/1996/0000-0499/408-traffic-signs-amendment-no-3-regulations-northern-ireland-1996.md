# Traffic Signs (Amendment No. 3) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 408

ROAD TRAFFIC AND VEHICLES

Traffic Signs (Amendment No. 3) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996

Made

2nd September 1996

Coming into operation

20th October 1996

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 27 of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, makes the following regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These regulations may be cited as the Traffic Signs (Amendment No. 3) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996 and shall come into operation on 20th October 1996.

## 2. Amendment

The Traffic Signs Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1979[^f00002] shall be amended by inserting after diagram 919.1 in Part IIA of Schedule 1 the following diagram and the words specified in and under it—

> Waiting place for police patrol vehicles only.**925** [image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1996/408/images/nisr_19960408_en_001)

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 2nd September 1996.

J. Carlisle

Assistant Secretary

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These regulations amend the Traffic Signs Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1979 by inserting a sign to indicate a waiting place for police patrol vehicles only.
