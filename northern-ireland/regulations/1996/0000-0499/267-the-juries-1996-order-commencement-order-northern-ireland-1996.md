# The Juries (1996 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 267 (C. 12)

JURIES

The Juries (1996 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996

Made

2nd July 1996

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by Article 1 of the Juries (Northern Ireland) Order 1996[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:—

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Juries (1996 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the Juries (Northern Ireland) Order 1996 shall be 31st July 1996.

Mackay of Clashfern, *C.*

Dated 2nd July 1996

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order appoints 31st July 1996 for the coming into operation of the Juries (Northern Ireland) Order 1996.
