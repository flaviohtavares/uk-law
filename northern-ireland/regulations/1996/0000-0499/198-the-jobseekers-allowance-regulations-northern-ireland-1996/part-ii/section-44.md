# The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 44

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 44. Appeals to the Commissioner[^c-46048a2e55cc43f264c8ba41e782b49e]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

The Social Security Commissioners Procedure Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1987[^f00045], except regulations 8, 12(2), 23, 28, 31(5), (6), (7) and (8), 32 and 33, shall apply in relation to appeals to the Commissioner under Article 13(6) of the Order as they apply in relation to appeals under section 21 of the Administration Act.
