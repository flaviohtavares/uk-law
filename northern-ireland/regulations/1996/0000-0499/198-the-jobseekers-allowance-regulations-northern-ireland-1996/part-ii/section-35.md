# The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 35

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 35. Automatic back-dating of Jobseeker’s Agreement[^c-24fd388de3c6cc26b64c8e2f26524f1e]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Where a jobseeker’s agreement is signed on a date later than the date of claim and there is no reference of that agreement to an adjudication officer under Article 11(6) of the Order, the agreement shall be treated as having effect on the date of claim.
