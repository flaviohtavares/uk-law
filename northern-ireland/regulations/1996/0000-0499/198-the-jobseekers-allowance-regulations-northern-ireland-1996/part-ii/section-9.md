# The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 9

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 9. No restrictions on pay after 6 months[^key-072ff5376eebaf4722f07218cd26910e]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

After the expiry of the 6 month period beginning with and including the date of claim, a person may not restrict his availability for employment by placing restrictions on the level of remuneration in employment for which he is available.
