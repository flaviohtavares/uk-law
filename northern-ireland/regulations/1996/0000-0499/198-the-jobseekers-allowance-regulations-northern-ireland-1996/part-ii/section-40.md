# The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 40

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 40. Notification of determinations and directions under Article 12 of the Order[^c-443212d4290e0b3cdccecb5c559839b8]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

The claimant shall be notified of—

- (a) any determination of the adjudication officer under Article 12 of the Order;
- (b) any direction of the adjudication officer under Article 12 of the Order.
