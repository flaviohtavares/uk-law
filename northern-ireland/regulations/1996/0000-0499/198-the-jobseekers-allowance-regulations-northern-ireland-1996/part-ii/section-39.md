# The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 39

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 39. Variation of Agreement: matters to be taken into account[^key-744f4784b7246b72b8592ccd652c24f2]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In giving a direction under Article 12(6)(b) or (d) of the Order an adjudication officer shall take into account the preference of the claimant if he considers that both the claimant’s proposals and those of the employment officer satisfy the requirements of Article 12(5) of the Order.
