# The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 37

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 37. Variation of Jobseeker’s Agreement[^key-cb2f0657dd8236101b2d21082a4080d5]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

The prescribed manner for varying a jobseeker’s agreement shall be in writing and signed by both parties in accordance with Article 12(2) of the Order on the proposal of the claimant or the employment officer.
