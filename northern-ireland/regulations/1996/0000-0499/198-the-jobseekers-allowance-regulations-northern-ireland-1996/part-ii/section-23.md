# The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 23

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 23. Attendance[^c-2f03cbc8a1b64a2ca05c3d4c9e199ae5]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

A claimant shall attend at such place and at such time as the Department may specify by a notice in writing given or sent to the claimant.
