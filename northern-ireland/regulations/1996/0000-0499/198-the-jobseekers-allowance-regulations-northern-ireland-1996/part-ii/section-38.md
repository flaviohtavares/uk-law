## 38. Direction to vary Agreement: time for compliance[^c-fb5245e259cc95713e070347e4d59202]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

The prescribed period for the purposes of Article 12(6)(c) of the Order shall be the period of 21 days beginning with and including the date on which the direction was issued.
