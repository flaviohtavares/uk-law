# The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 43

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 43. Direction of Social Security Appeal Tribunal: time limit for compliance[^key-0bedd73a1aa576bfe411ef3b9a804bfa]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

The prescribed period for the purposes of Article 13(5) of the Order shall be the period of 21 days beginning with and including the date on which the direction was issued.
