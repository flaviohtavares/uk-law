# The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 119

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 119. Disregard of payments treated as not relevant income[^c-a73695ec3137bf3a71de397cc10f23f2]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Where the Department treats any payment as not being relevant income for the purposes of section 72A of the Administration Act[^f00107] (payment of benefit where maintenance payments collected by Department), that payment shall be disregarded in calculating a claimant’s income.
