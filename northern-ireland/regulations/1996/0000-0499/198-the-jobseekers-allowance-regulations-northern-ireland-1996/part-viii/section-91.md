# The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 91

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 91. Calculation of income and capital of full-time students[^c-cbb8bae4746beb5be3aba72c4c95f5a7]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

The provisions of Chapters II to VI of this Part (income and capital) shall have effect in relation to full-time students and their partners subject to the modifications set out in Chapter IX (full-time students) thereof.
