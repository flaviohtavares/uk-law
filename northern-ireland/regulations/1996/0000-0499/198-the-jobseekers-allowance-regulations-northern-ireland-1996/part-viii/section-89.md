# The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 89

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 89. Liable relative payments[^key-546ae4932638b17c977b1d032c264215]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Regulations 94 to 106, 108 to 115 and Chapter IX of this Part shall not apply to any payment which is to be calculated in accordance with Chapter VII (liable relatives) thereof.
