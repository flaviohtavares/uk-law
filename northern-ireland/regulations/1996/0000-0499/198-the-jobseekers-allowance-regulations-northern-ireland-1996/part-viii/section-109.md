# The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 109

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 109. Disregard of capital of child or young person[^key-972709046c2f332df247cfaa94f70c82]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

The capital of a child or young person who is a member of the claimant’s family shall not be treated as capital of the claimant.
