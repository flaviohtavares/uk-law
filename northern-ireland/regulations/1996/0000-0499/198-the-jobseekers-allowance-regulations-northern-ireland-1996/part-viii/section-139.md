# The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 139

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 139. Disregard of changes occurring during summer vacation[^c-8f867b41588ea803ce7abd046660b132]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In calculating a student’s income an adjudication officer shall disregard any change in the standard maintenance grant occurring in the recognised summer vacation appropriate to the student’s course, if that vacation does not form part of his period of study, from and including the date on which the change occurred up to the end of that vacation.
