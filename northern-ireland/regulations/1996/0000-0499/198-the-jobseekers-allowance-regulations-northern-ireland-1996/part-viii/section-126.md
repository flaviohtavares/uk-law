# The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 126

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 126. Treatment of child support maintenance[^c-c92dad4131ab973bfda37664c6140473]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Subject to regulation 127 (disregard of payments treated as not relevant income) all payments of child support maintenance shall to the extent that they are not payments of income be treated as income and shall be taken into account on a weekly basis in accordance with regulations 127 to 129.
