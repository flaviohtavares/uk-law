# The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 107

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 107. Capital limit[^c-7cde196b58f0795a1bef11f0e4b09f6c]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

For the purposes of Article 15(1) of the Order (no entitlement to an income-based joseeker’s allowance if capital exceeds a prescribed amount), the prescribed amount is £8,000.
