# The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 127

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 127. Disregard of payments treated as not relevant income[^key-e64589078fc7702b2fdb1ef3c37f0d93]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Where the Department treats any payment of child support maintenance as not being relevant income for the purposes of section 72A of the Administration Act[^f00108] (payment of benefit where maintenance payments collected by Department), that payment shall be disregarded in calculating a claimant’s income.
