# The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 138

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 138. Income treated as capital[^c-6ef4c6ad7551af71aa2bf113355fec1e]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Any amount by way of a refund of tax deducted from a student’s income shall be treated as capital.
