## 157. Special provisions in respect of share fishermen[^c-3468033fd037f3eaebb243e42db0f75d]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

The Order and the foregoing provisions of these Regulations shall have effect in relation to share fishermen subject to the provisions of this Chapter.
