# The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 160

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 160. Modifications of Articles 21 and 22 of the Order[^key-16de71085fa7d686d28eff52fef1ae4a]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

For the purposes of Articles 21 and 22 of the Order (circumstances in which a jobseeker’s allowance is not payable) employment as a share fisherman shall be treated as employment as an employed earner.
