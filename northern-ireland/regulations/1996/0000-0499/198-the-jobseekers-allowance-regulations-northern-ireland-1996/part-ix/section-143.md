# The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 143

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 143. Conditions for hardship payments[^key-9b73f48bedeb7a5f1858a630320e9c4e]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) A jobseeker’s allowance shall not be payable in accordance with regulation 141 (circumstances in which an income-based jobseeker’s allowance is payable to a person in hardship) or, as the case may be, 142 (further circumstances in which an income-based jobseeker’s allowance is payable to a person in hardship), except where the claimant has—

- (a) furnished on a form approved for the purpose by the Department or in such other form as it may in any particular case approve a statement of the circumstances he relies upon to establish entitlement under regulation 141 or, as the case may be, regulation 142, and
- (b) signed the statement.

(2) The completed and signed form shall be delivered by the claimant to such office as the Department may specify.
