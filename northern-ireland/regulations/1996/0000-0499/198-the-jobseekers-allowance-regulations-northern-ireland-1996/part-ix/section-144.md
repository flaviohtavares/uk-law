# The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 144

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 144. Provision of information[^c-d73e4dff76e4cc232c30588178465de3]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

For the purposes of paragraph 10(3) of Schedule 1 to the Order, a claimant shall provide to the Department information as to the circumstances of the person alleged to be in hardship.
