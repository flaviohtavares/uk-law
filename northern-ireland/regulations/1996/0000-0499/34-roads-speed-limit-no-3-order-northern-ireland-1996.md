# Roads (Speed Limit) (No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 34

ROAD TRAFFIC AND VEHICLES

Roads (Speed Limit) (No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996

Made

12th February 1996

Coming into operation

25th March 1996

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 50(4)(*c*) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Roads (Speed Limit) (No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996 and shall come into operation on 25th March 1996.

## 2. Speed restrictions on certain roads

The Department hereby directs that each of the roads and lengths of road specified in the Schedule shall be a restricted road for the purposes of Article 50 of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on

E. J. Galway

Assistant Secretary

12th February 1996.
