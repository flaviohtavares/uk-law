# The Magistrates' Courts Fees (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 494

MAGISTRATES' COURTS

The Magistrates' Courts Fees (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996

Made

15th October 1996

Coming into operation

4th November 1996

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 116(1) and (4) of the Judicature (Northern Ireland) Act 1978[^f00001], after consultation with the Lord Chief Justice, and with the concurrence of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Magistrates' Courts Fees (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996 and shall come into operation on 4th November 1996.

**2.** After Section **H** in Schedule 1 to the Magistrates' Courts Fees Order (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00002], there shall be inserted the following section:

> **I.** Applications under the Children (Northern Ireland) Order 1995
>
> |  |  |  |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 56\. | An application for an order under Parts II or III not otherwise provided for | £30·00 |
> | 57\. | An application for an order under Article 50, (care or supervision order) 55, (education supervision order) or 62 (child assessment order) | £50·00 |
> | 58\. | An application for an order under Article 53 (contact or refusal of contact with a child in care) or 13 (change of child’s surname or removal from the jurisdiction) | £20·00 |
> | 59\. | An application for a financial provision order under Schedule 1 | £30·00 |
> | 60\. | An application to vary, extend or discharge an order made under any of the provisions of the Order mentioned at items 56 to 59 above | £20·00 |
> | 61\. | An application under Article 129 (affecting the registration of a child minder) or an appeal under Article 131 (appeal in relation to registration of child minder) | £50·00 |
> | 62\. | An appeal under Article 113 (appeal in relation to foster parenting) | £50·00 |
> | 63\. | An appeal under Article 145 (appeal in relation to licence to take part in public performance) | £50·00 |
>
> Note: Where an application under the Children (Northern Ireland) Order 1995 requires the leave of the Court the relevant fee applies where leave is sought but no further fee may be charged if leave is granted and the application is made.

.

Mackay of Clashfern, *C.*

Dated 10th October 1996

We concur

Patrick McLoughlin

Roger M. Knapman

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

Dated 15th October 1996

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order amends the Magistrates' Courts Fees Order (Northern Ireland) 1996 to prescribe the fees to be taken in respect of certain applications and appeals under the Children (Northern Ireland) Order 1995.
