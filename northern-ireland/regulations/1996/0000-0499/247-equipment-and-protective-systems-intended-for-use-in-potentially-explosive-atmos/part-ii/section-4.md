# Equipment and Protective Systems Intended for Use in Potentially Explosive Atmospheres Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. Excluded equipment, protective systems, devices and components

These Regulations do not apply to—

- (a) the equipment, protective systems and devices specified in Schedule 4; and
- (b) components for the equipment, protective systems or devices referred to in paragraph (*a*).
