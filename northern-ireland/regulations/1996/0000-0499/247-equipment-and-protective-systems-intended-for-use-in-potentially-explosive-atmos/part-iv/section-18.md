# Equipment and Protective Systems Intended for Use in Potentially Explosive Atmospheres Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 18

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 18. Liability of persons other than the principal offender

Where the commission by any person of an offence under regulation 16 is due to the act or default of some other person in the course of any business of his, that other person may be proceeded against and punished of the offence by virtue of this regulation whether or not proceedings for the offence are taken against the first-mentioned person.
