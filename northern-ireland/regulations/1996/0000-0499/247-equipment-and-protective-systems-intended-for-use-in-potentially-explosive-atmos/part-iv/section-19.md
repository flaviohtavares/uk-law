# Equipment and Protective Systems Intended for Use in Potentially Explosive Atmospheres Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 19

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 19. Amendment of the Electricity at Work Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1991

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In regulation 19(2) of the Electricity at Work Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1991[^f00016] for sub-paragraph (*d*) there shall be substituted the following sub-paragraph—

> - (d) equipment which has received a certificate of conformity or a certificate of inspection in accordance with Council Directive No. [82/130/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1982/0130)[^f00017] on the approximation of the laws of the Member States concerning electrical equipment for use in potentially explosive atmospheres in mines susceptible to firedamp, as adapted to technical progress by Commission Directives No. [88/35/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1988/0035)[^f00018], No. [91/269/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0269)[^f00019] and No. [94/44/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1994/0044)[^f00020];

.
