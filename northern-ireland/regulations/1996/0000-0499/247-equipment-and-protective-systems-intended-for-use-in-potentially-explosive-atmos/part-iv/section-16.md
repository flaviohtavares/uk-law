# Equipment and Protective Systems Intended for Use in Potentially Explosive Atmospheres Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 16

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 16. Offences and penalties

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Any person who contravenes regulation 6, 7 or 8 shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on summary conviction—

- (a) to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 3 months; or
- (b) to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale,

or to both.
