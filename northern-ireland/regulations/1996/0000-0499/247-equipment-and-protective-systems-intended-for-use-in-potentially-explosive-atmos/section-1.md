# Equipment and Protective Systems Intended for Use in Potentially Explosive Atmospheres Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 1

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

**1.** These Regulations implement as respects Northern Ireland the European Parliament and the Council Directive No. [94/9/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1994/0009) on the approximation of the laws of the Member States concerning equipment and protective systems intended for use in potentially explosive atmospheres (the ATEX Directive). Subject to certain limited exceptions, references in the Regulations to the Community or a Member State include respectively a reference to the EEA or an EEA State, which are defined in regulation 2(1)(*b*).
