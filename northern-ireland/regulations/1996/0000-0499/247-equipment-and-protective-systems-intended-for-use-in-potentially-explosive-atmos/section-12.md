# Equipment and Protective Systems Intended for Use in Potentially Explosive Atmospheres Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**12.** Certain amendments of existing Regulations are made by regulations 19 and 20. Regulation 19 amends the Electricity at Work Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1991 in order to incorporate references to Commission Directives No. [91/269/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0269) and No. [94/44/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1994/0044) which adapted to technical progress Council Directive No. [82/130/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1982/0130) concerning electrical equipment for use in potentially explosive atmospheres in mines susceptible to firedamp. Regulation 20 makes a consequential amendment to the Provision and Use of Work Equipment Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1993 (“the 1993 Regulations”) and provides that these Regulations are to take effect for the purposes of the enforcement of regulation 10 of the 1993 Regulations as if that amendment had been made by means of Regulations made under Article 17 of the Health and Safety at Work (Northern Ireland) Order 1978.
