# Equipment and Protective Systems Intended for Use in Potentially Explosive Atmospheres Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 11. Notified bodies

For the purposes of these Regulations, a notified body is a body which has been appointed to carry out one or more of the conformity assessment procedures specified in Article 8 of the ATEX Directive and referred to in regulation 10 which has been—

- (a) appointed as a notified body in Northern Ireland pursuant to regulation 12;
- (b) appointed as a notified body in Great Britain; or
- (c) appointed by a member State other than the United Kingdom,

and in the case of paragraph (*a*) and (*b*) has been notified by the United Kingdom and in the case of paragraph (*c*) has been notified by the member State concerned to the Commission and the other member States pursuant to Article 9(1) of the ATEX Directive.
