# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 2.22

> Text as at 2022-11-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 2.22. Particulars

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A party on whom a pleading has been served may by notice request the party whose pleading it is to give particulars of any allegation or other matter pleaded and, if that party fails to give the particulars within a reasonable time the party requiring them may apply for an order that the particulars be given.

(2) A party giving particulars whether in pursuance of an order or otherwise shall at the same time file a copy of them.
