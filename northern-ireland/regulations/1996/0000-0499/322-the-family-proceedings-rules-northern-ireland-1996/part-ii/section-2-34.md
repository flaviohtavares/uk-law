# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 2.34

> Text as at 2022-11-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 2.34. Lists in the \[... county court\][^c-0665b13aefdfd646bfd843be0eda2ae1][^f00130]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The chief clerk [^c-9653e58b0fd9aa8de14ba16978eaa399]... shall maintain a list of the causes which are for the time being set down for trial [^c-9653e58b0fd9aa8de14ba16978eaa399]....

(2) Causes shall be entered in the said list in the order in which they were set down for trial and for the purpose of this paragraph—

- (a) a cause shall, subject to sub-paragraph (b), be treated as having been set down for trial when the file of the cause is received by the chief clerk from the Matrimonial Office under rule 2.31(3);
- (b) a cause transferred for trial from another county court [^c-7f2df76014b42eb11f5edbe0f37e3642]... shall be treated as having been set down for trial at the end of the day on which it was originally set down for trial.
