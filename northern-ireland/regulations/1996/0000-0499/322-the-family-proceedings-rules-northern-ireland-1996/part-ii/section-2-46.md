# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 2.46

> Text as at 2022-11-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 2.46. Decrees and orders

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Every decree, \[[^f00150] every civil partnership order, every other order made in open court\] and every other order which is required to be drawn up shall be drawn up—

- (a) in the case of a decree or order pronounced or made in the High Court, in the Matrimonial Office;
- (b) in the case of a decree or order pronounced or made in \[[^f00151] the[^c-b59d7d5ac5ddf648de8cabfa99184d65] county court\], in the county court office;

and shall have affixed thereto the seal of the office in which it is drawn up.

(2) C.C.R. Order 33, rule 4(1) (which deals with the lodgment of a decree) shall not apply to a decree \[[^f00152] or civil partnership order\] pronounced in a cause pending in \[[^f00153] the[^c-2b29461f45eab76531d9ebca3fe68419] county court\].

(3) The chief clerk to whom the file of a cause has been sent under rule 2.31(3) shall, as soon as practicable after the cause has been tried, forward to the Matrimonial Office a copy of the decree or order pronounced or made in the cause.
