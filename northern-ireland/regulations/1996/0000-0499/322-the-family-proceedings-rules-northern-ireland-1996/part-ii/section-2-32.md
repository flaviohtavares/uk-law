## 2.32. Order for transfer of cause

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) \[[^f00129] The[^c-18b6ee12a75d48ac3aaae77b8841d2e1] county court\] may order that a cause pending therein be transferred to the High Court, if, having regard to all the circumstances including the difficulty or importance of the cause or of any issue arising therein, the court thinks it desirable that the cause should be heard and determined in the High Court.
