# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 2.43

> Text as at 2022-11-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 2.43. Rules 2.41 and 2.42 not to affect the power of the judge at the trial to refuse to admit any evidence

*Extent: N.I.*

Nothing in rule 2.41 or 2.42 shall affect the power of the judge at the trial to refuse to admit any evidence if in the interest of justice he thinks fit to do so.
