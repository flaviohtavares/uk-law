# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 2.44

> Text as at 2022-11-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 2.44. Issue of writ of subpoena or witness summons

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A writ of subpoena in a cause pending in the High Court shall be issued out of the Matrimonial Office.

(2) A witness summons in a cause pending in \[[^f00148] the[^key-051b1b0e84e5d3b8c9bf9e1ad8b54a62] county court\] shall be issued in any county court office.
