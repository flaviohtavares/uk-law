# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 2.21

> Text as at 2022-11-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 2.21. Service and amendment of pleadings

*Extent: N.I.*

Pleadings in matrimonial proceedings \[[^f00108] or civil partnership proceedings\] may be served on any day except Sunday, Good Friday and Christmas Day and may be amended on any day on which the appropriate court office is open.
