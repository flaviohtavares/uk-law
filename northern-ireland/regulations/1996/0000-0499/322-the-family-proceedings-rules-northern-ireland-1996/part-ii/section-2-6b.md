## 2.6B. Petition for nullity on ground that respondent’s gender had become acquired gender at time of marriage or civil partnership

*Extent: N.I.*

Where a petition for nullity of marriage is brought under Article 14(h) of the Order of 1978 or a petition for nullity of civil partnership is brought under section 174(1)(e) of the Act of 2004 and a full gender recognition certificate has been issued to the respondent, the petitioner shall file a copy of that full certificate with his petition, unless otherwise directed on an application made ex parte.\]
