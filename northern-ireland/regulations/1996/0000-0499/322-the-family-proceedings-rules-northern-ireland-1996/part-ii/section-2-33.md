# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 2.33

> Text as at 2022-11-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 2.33. Trial of issue

*Extent: N.I.*

Where directions are given for the separate trial of any issue, the Master shall—

- (a) if the issue arises on an application for ancillary relief or on an application with respect to any child or alleged child of the family, proceed as if the issue were a question referred to a judge on an application for ancillary relief and rule 2.70 shall apply accordingly;
- (b) in any other case, cause the issue to be set down for trial.
