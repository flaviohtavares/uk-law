# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 2.57

> Text as at 2022-11-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 2.57. Application in Form M13 or M14

*Extent: N.I.*

Where an application for ancillary relief is made by notice in Form M13 or an application under rule 2.48 is made by notice in Form M14, the notice shall—

- (a) if the cause has been set down for trial in \[[^f00207] the[^key-a9f16af21c7a10d2aae52d31b1165cbc] county court\], be filed in the county court office, or
- (b) in any other case be filed in the Matrimonial Office,

and within 7 days after filing the notice the applicant shall serve a copy on the respondent to the application.
