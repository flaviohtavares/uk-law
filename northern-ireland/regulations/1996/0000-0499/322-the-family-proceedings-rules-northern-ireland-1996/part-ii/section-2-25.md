> Text as at 2022-11-28.

## 2.25. Discovery by interrogatories

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) R.S.C. Order 26 (which deals with discovery by interrogatories) shall apply to a cause with the necessary modifications.

(2) A copy of the proposed interrogatories shall be filed when the summons for an order is issued.
