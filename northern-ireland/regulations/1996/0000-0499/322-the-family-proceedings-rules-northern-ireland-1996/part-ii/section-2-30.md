# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 2.30

> Text as at 2022-11-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 2.30. Mode ... of trial[^key-8e671c13793b69d4116e587fcfb65d8b]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to section 62(4) of the Judicature (Northern Ireland) Act 1978[^f00124], every cause and any issue arising therein shall be tried by a judge without a jury.

(2)[^key-f93bf34ee7f7231af6bc67a210cac80d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Unless the judge otherwise directs, every cause listed for hearing and any issue arising therein shall be heard by a judge in chambers and adjourned into court for judgment.
