# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 2.68

> Text as at 2022-11-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 2.68. Transfer for purpose of expedition

*Extent: N.I.*

Without prejudice to the last foregoing rule, a judge or Master may, on the application of a party or of his own motion, order that an application for ancillary relief pending in the High Court or \[[^f00214] the[^c-be4de40d028bf83f8210343783f12726] county court\] shall be transferred to \[[^f00214] the county court\] or the High Court if he is of opinion that the transfer is desirable for the purpose of expediting the hearing of the application.
