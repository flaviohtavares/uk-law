# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 2.24

> Text as at 2022-11-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 2.24. Discovery of documents

*Extent: N.I.*

A party to a cause may apply for an order for discovery and inspection of documents by an opposite party and R.S.C. Order 24 shall apply with the necessary modifications.
