# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 2.15

> Text as at 2022-11-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 2.15. Filing of reply and subsequent pleadings

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A petitioner may file a reply to an answer within 14 days after he has received a copy of the answer pursuant to rule 2.19.

(2) If the petitioner does not file a reply to an answer, he shall, unless the answer prays for a decree \[[^f00103] or civil partnership order\], be deemed on lodging the certificate of readiness to have denied every material allegation of fact made in the answer.

(3) No pleadings subsequent to a reply shall be filed without leave.
