# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 2.58

> Text as at 2022-11-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 2.58. Application for ancillary relief after order of court of summary jurisdiction

*Extent: N.I.*

Where an application for ancillary relief is made while there is in force an order of a court of summary jurisdiction for maintenance of a spouse \[[^f00208], civil partner\] or child, the applicant shall file a copy of the order on or before the hearing of the application.
