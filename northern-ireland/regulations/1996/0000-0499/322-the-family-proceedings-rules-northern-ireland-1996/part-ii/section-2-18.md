# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 2.18

> Text as at 2022-11-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 2.18. Allegation against third person in pleading

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Rules 2.8, 2.9 and 2.10 shall apply, with the necessary modifications, to a pleading other than a petition as they apply to a petition, so however that for the references in those rules to a co-respondent there shall be substituted references to a party cited.

(2) Rule 2.14 shall apply, with the necessary modifications, to a party cited as it applies to a co-respondent.
