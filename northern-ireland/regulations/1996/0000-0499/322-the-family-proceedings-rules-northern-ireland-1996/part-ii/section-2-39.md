# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 2.39

> Text as at 2022-11-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 2.39. Restoration of matters adjourned, etc. at the hearing

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where at the trial of a cause any application is adjourned by the judge it may be restored by any party or by the Master when in his opinion the matter ought to be further considered, by notice in Form M9 which shall be served on every party concerned and (when served by a party) on the proper officer or chief clerk, as the case may be.

(2) Where in proceedings for divorce, nullity of marriage or judicial separation \[[^f00142], or for dissolution, nullity or civil partnership or separation,\] the judge has made a direction under Article 44(2) of the Order of 1978 \[[^f00143] or under section 186(2) of the Act of 2004\], paragraph (1) shall, unless the judge otherwise directs, apply as if an application with respect to the arrangements for the care and upbringing or any such child had been adjourned.
