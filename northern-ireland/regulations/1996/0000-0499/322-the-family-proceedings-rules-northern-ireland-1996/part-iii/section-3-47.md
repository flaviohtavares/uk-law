# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 3.47

> Text as at 2022-11-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 3.47. \[ Application under Article 32L of the Order of 1991: Interpretation[^f00325]

*Extent: N.I.*

In rules 3.48 to 3.52—

- (a) “**child support maintenance**” has the meaning assigned to it in Article 4(6) of the Order of 1991;
- (b) “**the Department**” has the meaning assigned to it in Article 2(2) of the Order of 1991;
- (c) “**reviewable disposition**” has the meaning assigned to it in Article 32L(5) of the Order of 1991.
