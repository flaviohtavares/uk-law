# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 3.33

> Text as at 2022-11-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 3.33. Answer by the Attorney General

*Extent: N.I.*

Subject to paragraph (2) or rule 3.32, the Attorney General shall file an answer to the petition within 21 days after directions have been given under that rule and shall deliver a copy thereof to the petitioner and any other party to the proceedings.
