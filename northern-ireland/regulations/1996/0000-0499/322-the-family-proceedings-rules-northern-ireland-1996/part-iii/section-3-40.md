# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 3.40

> Text as at 2022-11-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 3.40. \[ Proceedings under the 2007 Act: Interpretation[^f00324]

*Extent: N.I.*

In rules 3.41 to 3.46—

- (a) a “**forced marriage protection order**” means an order under paragraph 1 of Schedule 1 to the Act of 2007;
- (b) “**the person who is the subject of the proceedings**” means the person who will be protected by the forced marriage protection order applied for or being considered by the court of its own motion, if that order is made, or who is being protected by such an order.
