## 3.21. Variation, extension or discharge of orders made under the Order of 1998

*Extent: N.I.*

An application to vary, extend or discharge an occupation order or non-molestation order made under the Order of 1998 shall be made in Form F8 and rules 3.15 and 3.17 to 3.20 shall apply, with the necessary modifications, to such an application.
