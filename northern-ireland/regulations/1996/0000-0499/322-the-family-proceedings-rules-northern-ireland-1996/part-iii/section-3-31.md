# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 3.31

> Text as at 2022-11-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 3.31. Answer by respondent

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A respondent who has entered an appearance may, within 14 days thereafter enter an answer to the petition.

(2) The respondent shall within 4 days of filing an answer deliver a copy of it to the petitioner and to the Crown Solicitor and any other party to the proceedings.
