# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 3.20

> Text as at 2022-11-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 3.20. Notification of occupation order or non-molestation order

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where an occupation order or non-molestation order is made under the Order of 1998, the applicant shall arrange for a copy of the order to be served by the applicant on the respondent personally.

(2) Where the application is for an occupation order under Article 11, 13 or 14 of the Order of 1998, the applicant shall arrange for a copy of any order made on the application shall be served by the applicant by first-class post on the mortgagee or, as the case may be, the landlord of the dwelling house in question.
