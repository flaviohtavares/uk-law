# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 3.34

> Text as at 2022-11-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 3.34. Joining of parties

*Extent: N.I.*

Persons given notice of proceedings pursuant to directions given in accordance with rule 3.32 shall within 21 days of service of the notice upon them be entitled to apply to the Master to be joined as parties and the matter shall not be set down for hearing until that period and the period referred to in rule 3.33 have expired.
