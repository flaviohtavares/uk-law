# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 3.7

> Text as at 2022-11-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 3.7. Application of other rules to proceedings under Article 37 or 38 of the Order of 1978 \[ or paragraph 62 or 66 of Schedule 15 to the Act of 2004\][^f00278]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Rules 2.63; 2.64(1) and (4) to (7), 2.65, 2.66 and 2.69 shall apply, with the necessary modifications, to an application under Article 37 or 38 of the Order of 1978 \[[^f00279] or paragraph 62 or 66 of Schedule 15 to the Act of 2004 as the case may be,\] as if it were an application for ancillary relief.

(2) Subject to paragraph (1) and to the provisions of rule 3.4, these Rules shall, so far as applicable, apply with the necessary modifications to an application under Article 37 or 38 of the Order of 1978 \[[^f00278] or paragraph 62 or 66 of Schedule 15 to the Act of 2004 as the case may be,\] as if the application were a cause, the originating summons a petition, and the applicant the petitioner.
