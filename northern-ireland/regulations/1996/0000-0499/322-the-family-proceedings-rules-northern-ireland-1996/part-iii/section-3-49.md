# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 3.49

> Text as at 2022-11-28.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 3.49. Parties to proceedings under Article 32L of the Order of 1991

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) In proceedings under Article 32L of the Order of 1991, the applicant is the Department and the respondent is the person who has failed to pay child support maintenance.

(2) The court may at any time direct that—

- (a) any person be made a party to proceedings; or
- (b) a party to the proceedings cease to be a party.
