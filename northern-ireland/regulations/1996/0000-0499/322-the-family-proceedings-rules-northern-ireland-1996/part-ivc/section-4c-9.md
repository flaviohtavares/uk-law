# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 4C.9

> Text as at 2022-11-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 4C.9. \[ County courts prescribed as an authorised court[^f00351]

*Extent: N.I.*

For the purposes of Article 2(2)(c) a county court specified as a family care centre in Schedule 2 to the Children (Allocation of Proceedings) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996 is prescribed as an authorised court.\]
