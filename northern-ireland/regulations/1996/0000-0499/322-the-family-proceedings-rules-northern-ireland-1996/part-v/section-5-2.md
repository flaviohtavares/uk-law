# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 5.2

> Text as at 2022-11-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 5.2. Appeal from the district judge

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) C.C.R Order 14 rule 1(1)(f) (which enables the judge to vary or rescind an order by the district judge in the course of proceedings) shall not apply to an order or decision made or given by the district judge in family proceedings in a county court but any party may appeal from such an order or decision to a judge on notice filed within 5 days after the order or decision was made or given and served not later than 2 clear days before the day fixed for hearing of the appeal, which shall be heard in chambers unless the judge otherwise orders.

(2) Except so far as the court may otherwise order, an appeal under paragraph (1) shall not operate as a stay of proceedings on the order or decision appealed from.
