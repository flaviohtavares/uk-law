# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 4.30

> Text as at 2022-11-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 4.30. Application by a party for transfer of the proceedings[^c-3d329ddb35deadd1cc802f6d9f31b922]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A party may apply to the court [^c-c5cd330add526625ff74c2b1b541ff8a]... under Article 8(1) of the 1996 Hague Convention—

- (a) to stay the proceedings, or a specified part of the proceedings, and to invite the parties to introduce a request before a court of another [^c-7f72bf1958d173856d32efdf97276d93]... Contracting State; or
- (b) to make a request to a court of [^c-babed9a2c13d6bd18eb91041c86ced52]... another Contracting State to assume jurisdiction for the proceedings, or a specified part of the proceedings.

(2) An application under paragraph (1) must be made—

- (a) to the court in which the relevant parental responsibility proceedings are pending; and
- (b) in Form C1 or C2 as appropriate.

(3)[^c-f1aad3adf6d374f4424479da05368918] The applicant must file the application and serve it on the respondent not less than 6 weeks before the hearing of the application.
