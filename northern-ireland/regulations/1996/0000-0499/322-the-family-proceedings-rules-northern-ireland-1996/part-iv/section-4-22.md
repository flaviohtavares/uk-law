# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 4.22

> Text as at 2022-11-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 4.22. Attachment of penal notice to Article 8 order

*Extent: N.I.*

C.C.R. Order 57 rule 7 shall apply to Article 8 orders as if for paragraph (1) of that rule there were substituted the following—

> (1) In the case of an Article 8 order (within the meaning of Article 8(2) of the Children (Northern Ireland) Order 1995) enforceable by committal order under rule 5 the judge or the district judge may, on the application of the person entitled to enforce the order, direct the chief clerk to issue a copy of the order endorsed with a notice in Form 270 and the copy so endorsed shall be served on the respondent personally and no copy of the order shall be issued with any such notice endorsed save in accordance with such direction.
