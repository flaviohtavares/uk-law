# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 4.32

> Text as at 2022-11-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 4.32. Exercise by the court of its own motion of powers to seek to transfer the proceedings[^key-3d329ddb35deadd1cc802f6d9f31b922]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The court having jurisdiction in relation to the proceedings may exercise its powers of its own motion under [^key-10cab8f353ff580f789907604efdd0e9]... Article 8 of the 1996 Hague Convention in relation to the proceedings, or a specified part of the proceedings.

(2) Where the court proposes to exercise its powers, the proper officer or chief clerk will give the parties not less than 5 days notice of the hearing.
