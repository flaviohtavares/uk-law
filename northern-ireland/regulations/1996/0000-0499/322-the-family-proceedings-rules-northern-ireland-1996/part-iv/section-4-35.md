> Text as at 2022-11-28.

## 4.35. Service of the court’s order or request relating to transfer of jurisdiction under ... the 1996 Hague Convention[^c-3d329ddb35deadd1cc802f6d9f31b922][^c-d4f03f81ede3a90416fd7dc6f9a4722c]

*Extent: N.I.*

—The proper officer will serve an order or request relating to transfer of jurisdiction on all parties, the Central Authority of the other [^c-e661d9cfc12c4f5ce18bdc3552ffc70a]... Contracting State, and the Central Authority for Northern Ireland.
