# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 4.31

> Text as at 2022-11-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 4.31. Application by a court of another Member State or another Contracting State for transfer of the proceedings[^key-3d329ddb35deadd1cc802f6d9f31b922]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This rule applies where a court of [^key-a9885b5c8c93a59aa17dfd57d8ea47d6]... another Contracting State makes an application [^key-19d38ee0057a9282d8a0322fd6428e65]... under Article 9 of the 1996 Hague Convention that the court having jurisdiction in relation to the proceedings transfer the proceedings, or a specified part of the proceedings, to the applicant court.

(2) When the court receives the application, the proper officer or chief clerk will—

- (a) as soon as practicable, notify the Central Authority for Northern Ireland of the application; and
- (b) serve the application, and notice of the hearing on all other parties in Northern Ireland not less than 5 days before the hearing of the application.
