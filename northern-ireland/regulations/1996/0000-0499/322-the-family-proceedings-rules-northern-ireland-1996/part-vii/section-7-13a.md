# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 7.13A

> Text as at 2022-11-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 7.13A. \[ Documents in family proceedings concerning gender recognition[^f00371]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This rule applies to all documents in family proceedings brought under—

- (a) Article 14(g) or (h) of, or paragraph 18(1)(e) of Schedule 3 to, the Order of 1978;
- (b) section 174(1)(d) or (e) of the Act of 2004; and
- (c) the Gender Recognition Act 2004.

(2) Documents to which this rule applies shall, while they are in the custody of the court, be kept in a place of special security.\]
