# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 7.7

> Text as at 2022-11-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 7.7. Filing of documents at place of hearing, etc

*Extent: N.I.*

Where the file of any matrimonial proceedings \[[^f00366] or civil partnership proceedings\] has been sent from the Matrimonial Office to a county court for the purpose of a hearing or for some other purpose, any document required to be filed shall be filed in that court.
