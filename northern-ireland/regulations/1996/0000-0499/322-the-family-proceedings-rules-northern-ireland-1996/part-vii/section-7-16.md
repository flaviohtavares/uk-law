# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 7.16

> Text as at 2022-11-28.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 7.16. Disclosure of address

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to rule 2.4 nothing in these Rules shall be construed as requiring any party to reveal the address of their private residence (or that of any child) except by order of the court.

(2) Where a party declines to reveal an address in reliance upon paragraph (1) he shall give notice to the court in Form C5 and that address shall not be revealed to any person except by order of the court.
