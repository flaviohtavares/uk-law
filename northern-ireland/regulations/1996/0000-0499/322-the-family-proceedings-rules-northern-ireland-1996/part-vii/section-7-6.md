# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 7.6

> Text as at 2022-11-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 7.6. No notice of intention to proceed after year’s delay

*Extent: N.I.*

No provision in the Rules of the Supreme Court (Northern Ireland) 1980, which requires a party to give notice of intention to proceed after a year’s delay, shall apply to any family proceedings.
