# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 7.9

> Text as at 2022-11-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 7.9. Copies of Decrees \[, civil partnership orders and other orders\][^f00368]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A copy of every decree \[[^f00368], civil partnership order or other order\] shall be sent by the proper officer or chief clerk to every party to the cause.

(2) A sealed or other copy of a decree \[[^f00368], civil partnership order or other order) pronounced or made in open court shall be issued to any person requiring it on payment of the prescribed fee.
