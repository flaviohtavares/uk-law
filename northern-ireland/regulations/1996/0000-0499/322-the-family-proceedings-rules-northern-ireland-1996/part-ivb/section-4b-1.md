> Text as at 2022-11-28.

## 4B.1. Introductory

*Extent: N.I.*

This Part shall apply to Convention proceedings and, subject to the provisions of this Part, rules 4A.2, 4A.14 to 4A.24 and Part IVC shall apply, with the necessary modifications, to Convention proceedings.
