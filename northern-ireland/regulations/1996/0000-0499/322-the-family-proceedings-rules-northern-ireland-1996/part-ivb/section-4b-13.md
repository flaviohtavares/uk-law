# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 4B.13

> Text as at 2022-11-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 4B.13. Witnessing of documents

*Extent: N.I.*

A document shall be sufficiently attested for the purposes of this Part of these rules if it is witnessed by one of the following persons—

- (a) if it is executed in Northern Ireland, the guardian ad litem, a Lay Magistrate, any officer of the Court of Judicature appointed by the Lord Chief Justice in accordance with section 112 of the Judicature (Northern Ireland) Act 1978[^f00349];
- (b) if it is executed elsewhere, any person specified in rule 4A.7(2), (3) or (4), according to the country in which it is executed.
