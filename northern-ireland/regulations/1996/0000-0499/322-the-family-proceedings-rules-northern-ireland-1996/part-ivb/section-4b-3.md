> Text as at 2022-11-28.

## 4B.3. Statement at hearing

*Extent: N.I.*

The requirements prescribed by regulations 19 and 30 of the Hague Convention Regulations may be established by a document executed by the applicant containing a statement to that effect attested in accordance with rule 4B.14 and such a statement shall be admissible in evidence without further proof of the signature of the applicant.
