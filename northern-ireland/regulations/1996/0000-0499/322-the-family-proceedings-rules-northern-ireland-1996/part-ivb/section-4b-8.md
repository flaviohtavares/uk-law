# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 4B.8

> Text as at 2022-11-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 4B.8. Application to declare overseas adoption invalid or determination invalid or affected

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An application for an order or decision under Article 55A(2) shall be made in Form A18.

(2) The respondent shall be—

- (a) the adopted person; and
- (b) any adopter, not being the applicant.
