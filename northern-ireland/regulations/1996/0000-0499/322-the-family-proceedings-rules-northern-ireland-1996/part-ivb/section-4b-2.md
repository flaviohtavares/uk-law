> Text as at 2022-11-28.

## 4B.2. Application

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An applicant for a Convention adoption order shall state in his application that he is applying for a Convention adoption order.

(2) The application—

- (a) need not contain paragraphs corresponding to paragraph 2.24 or 25 of Form A9, but,
- (b) shall contain the additional information required by Part III of Appendix 4.
