# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 8.48

> Text as at 2022-11-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 8.48. Stay of recognition proceedings by reason of an appeal[^c-01b7174459c6aa56f66a4c72a02166bc]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where recognition or non-recognition of a judgment given in another [^c-374bf36cc86e9297965914d7c669e3aa]... Contracting State is sought, or is raised as an incidental question in other proceedings, the court may stay the proceedings—

- (a) if an ordinary appeal against the judgment has been lodged; or
- (b) if the judgment was given in the Republic of Ireland, if enforcement of the judgment is suspended there by reason of an appeal.
