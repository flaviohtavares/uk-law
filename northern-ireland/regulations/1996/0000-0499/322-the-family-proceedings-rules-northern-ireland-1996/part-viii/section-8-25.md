# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 8.25

> Text as at 2022-11-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 8.25. Master may appoint receiver, etc

*Extent: N.I.*

The Master shall have power to make an order for the appointment of a receiver by way of equitable execution and to grant an injunction if, and only so far as, the injunction is ancillary or incidental to such an order.
