# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 8.34

> Text as at 2022-11-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 8.34. Interpretation[^key-01b7174459c6aa56f66a4c72a02166bc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) In rules 8.33 to 8.63—

- (a) “**Central Authority**” means, in relation to Northern Ireland, the Department of Justice;
- (b) “**Contracting State**” means a State[^key-c2b27edaa82593788623b7a4ed8b4eb3]... in relation to which the 1996 Hague Convention is in force as between that State and the United Kingdom;
- (c) “**judgment**” is to be construed—
  - (i)[^key-aef51249dda1c7aeeb3b1710724c29b1] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (ii)[^key-aef51249dda1c7aeeb3b1710724c29b1] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (iii) as meaning any measure taken by an authority with jurisdiction under Chapter II of the 1996 Hague Convention [^key-9f67291bed26f81ea558bde6dcea3125]...;
- (d)[^key-7dc305fede300af12cdcf1d52fac3f22] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (e)[^key-a8559145d14e304636d0b1dff1579643] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (f) “parental responsibility”—
  - (i)[^key-9c021b944a6cbb709d63987f7ed4145b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (ii) where the 1996 Hague Convention applies, has the meaning given in Article 1(2) of that Convention; and
- (g)[^key-feb102302b5258cb8b6ed35a52b4b364] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) References in rules 8.33 to 8.63 to registration are to the registration of a judgment in accordance with the provisions of those rules.
