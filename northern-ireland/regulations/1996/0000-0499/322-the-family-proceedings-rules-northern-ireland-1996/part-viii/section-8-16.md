## 8.16. Discharge, etc. of charging order

*Extent: N.I.*

The court, on the application of the judgment debtor or any other person interested in the securities to which an order under rule 8.11 relates, may at any time whether before or after the order is made absolute, discharge or vary the order on such terms (if any) as to costs as it thinks just.
