# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 8.10

> Text as at 2022-11-28.

## 8.10. Costs

*Extent: N.I.*

The costs of any application for an order under rule 8.2 to 8.9 and of any proceedings arising therefrom or incidental thereto, shall, unless the court otherwise directs, be retained by the judgment creditor out of the money recovered by him under the order and in priority to the judgment debt.
