# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 8.27

> Text as at 2022-11-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 8.27. Attachment of earnings

*Extent: N.I.*

Articles 73 to 79 of the Judgments Enforcement (Northern Ireland) Order 1981 and R.S.C. Order 105 which deals with attachment of earnings) shall apply to the enforcement of orders made in \[[^f00380] matrimonial proceedings or civil partnership proceedings in the[^c-6b51789df2bf6288fc286843df9bc878] county court\] as if they were orders of the High Court.
