# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 8.5

> Text as at 2022-11-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 8.5. No appearance or dispute of liability by garnishee

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where on the further consideration of the matter the garnishee does not attend or does not dispute the debt due or claimed to be due from him to the judgment debtor, the court may, subject to rule 8.8 make an order absolute under rule 8.2 against the garnishee.

(2) An order absolute under rule 8.2 against the garnishee may be enforced as if judgment for the amount payable thereunder had been given against the garnishee.
