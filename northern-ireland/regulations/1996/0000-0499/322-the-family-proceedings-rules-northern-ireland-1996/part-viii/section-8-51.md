# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 8.51

> Text as at 2022-11-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 8.51. Effect of registration under rule 8.50[^key-01b7174459c6aa56f66a4c72a02166bc]

*Extent: N.I.*

Registration of a judgment under rule 8.50 will serve for the purpose of [^key-208d193904eb4bebf4e4f0179b1c908f]... Article 24 of the 1996 Hague Convention[^key-195907546af91d525c9a2dd48dd3288d]... as a decision that the judgment is recognised.
