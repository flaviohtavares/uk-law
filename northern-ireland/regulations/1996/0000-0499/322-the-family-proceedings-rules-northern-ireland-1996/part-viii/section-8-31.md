# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 8.31

> Text as at 2022-11-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 8.31. Special provisions as to judgment summonses in \[ ... county courts\][^f00384][^c-2f6a72be6516a96d1d3abc084b1b46ca]

*Extent: N.I.*

C.C.R. Order 40 (which deals with enforcement of decrees) shall not apply to a judgment summons issued in a county court.
