# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 8.35

> Text as at 2022-11-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 8.35. Filing of applications[^key-01b7174459c6aa56f66a4c72a02166bc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Every application under rules 8.33 to 8.63, except for an application under rule 8.56, for a certified copy of a judgment or certificate, [^key-d9bf0c254e34b2686adc3d0c451c6d09]... must be filed with the Office of Care and Protection.

(2) Notwithstanding paragraph (1), where recognition of a judgment is raised as an incidental question in proceedings under the [^key-7a1397a9196c55ab57983b2a65deb633]... 1996 Hague Convention [^key-8e2da203b7a63a90656e6d713e46f5e2]... the court hearing those proceedings may determine the question of recognition.
