# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 8.41

> Text as at 2022-11-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 8.41. Application for recognition only of a judgment under the 1996 Hague Convention[^c-01b7174459c6aa56f66a4c72a02166bc]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where it is sought to apply for recognition only of a judgment under the 1996 Hague Convention, the provisions of rule 8.39 apply with the exception that the applicant is not required to produce the document referred to in rule 8.39(a)(ii).
