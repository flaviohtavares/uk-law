# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 8.18

> Text as at 2022-11-28.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 8.18. Jurisdiction of Master to grant injunction or appoint receiver to enforce charge

*Extent: N.I.*

The Master shall have power to grant an injunction if, and only, so far as, it is ancillary or incidental to an order under rule 8.11 or 8.17, and an application for an injunction under this rule may be joined with the application for the order under rule 8.11 or 8.17 to which it relates.
