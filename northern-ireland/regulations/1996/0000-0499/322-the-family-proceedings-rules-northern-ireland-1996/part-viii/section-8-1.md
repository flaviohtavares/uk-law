# The Family Proceedings Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 8.1

> Text as at 2022-11-28.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 8.1. Application

*Extent: N.I.*

In this Part, unless the context otherwise requires—

- “**matrimonial order**” means an order made in matrimonial proceedings for the periodical payment of money;
- \[[^f00375] “**civil partnership order**” means an order made in civil partnership proceedings for the periodical payment of money;\]
- “**government stock**” means any stock issued by Her Majesty’s government in the United Kingdom or any funds of or annuity granted by that body;
- “**judgment creditor**” means the person entitled to payments under an order;
- “**judgment debtor**” means the person liable to make payments under an order.
