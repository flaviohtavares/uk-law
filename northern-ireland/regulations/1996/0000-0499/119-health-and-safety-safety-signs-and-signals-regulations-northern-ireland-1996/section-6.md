# Health and Safety (Safety Signs and Signals) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 6

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 6. Transitional provisions

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

These Regulations shall not have effect in relation to any fire safety signs lawfully in use immediately before the coming into operation of these Regulations until 24th December 1998.

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

**6.** A person who contravenes the Regulations is guilty of an offence under Article 31 of the Health and Safety at Work (Northern Ireland) Order 1978 and is liable, on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum (currently £5,000) or, on conviction on indictment, to a fine.
