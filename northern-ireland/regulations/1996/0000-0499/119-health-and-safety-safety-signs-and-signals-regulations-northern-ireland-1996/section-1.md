# Health and Safety (Safety Signs and Signals) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 1

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation and commencement

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

These Regulations may be cited as the Health and Safety (Safety Signs and Signals) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996 and shall come into operation on 1st May 1996.

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

**1.** These Regulations impose requirements in relation to the provision and use of safety signs and signals and implement as respects Northern Ireland Council Directive [92/58/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0058) (O.J. No. L245, 26.8.92, p. 23) on the minimum requirements for the provision of safety and/or health signs at work.
