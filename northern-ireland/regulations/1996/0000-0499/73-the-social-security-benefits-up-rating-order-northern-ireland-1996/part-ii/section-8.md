# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 8

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 8. Earnings limits[^key-b291b9f0f56155223293b514945b2004]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In section 80(4) of the Contributions and Benefits Act[^f00016] (earnings limits in respect of child dependency increases) the sum specified in paragraph (a) is £130·00 and the sums specified in paragraph (b) are £17·00 and £130·00 respectively.
