# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 7

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 7. Increase in rate of certain workmen’s compensation in respect of employment before 5th July 1948[^key-42607c0dcc2b47dc030b901178d4507d]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In paragraph 2(6)(c) of Schedule 8 to the Contributions and Benefits Act[^f00015] (maximum weekly rate of lesser incapacity allowance supplementing workmen’s compensation) for “£35·25” there shall be substituted “£36·60”.
