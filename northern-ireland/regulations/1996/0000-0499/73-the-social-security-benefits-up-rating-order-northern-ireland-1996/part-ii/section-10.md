# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 10

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 10. Statutory maternity pay[^key-3a3d06a6e4f2aff1dcbb5d077c193031]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In regulation 6 of the Statutory Maternity Pay (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1987[^f00018] (lower rate of statutory maternity pay) the sum specified is £54·55.
