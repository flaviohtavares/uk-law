# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 15

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 15. Increase in rates of transitional invalidity allowance in long-term incapacity benefit cases[^c-055722003343e34aa82522bc98a37514]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In regulation 18(2) of the Social Security (Incapacity Benefit) (Transitional) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995[^f00024] (rate of long-term incapacity benefit in transitional cases)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a) for “£12·40” there shall be substituted “£12·90”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b) for “£7·80” there shall be substituted “£8·10”; and
- (c) in sub-paragraph (c) for “£3·90” there shall be substituted “£4·05”.
