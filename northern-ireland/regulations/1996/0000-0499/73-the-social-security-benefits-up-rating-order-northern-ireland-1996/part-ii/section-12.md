# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 12

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 12. Increase in rates of disability living allowance[^c-0f8fa381b6288b893120a4c28d1dab4e]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In regulation 4 of the Social Security (Disability Living Allowance) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992[^f00021] (rate of benefit)—

- (a) in paragraph (1)(a) for “£46·70” there shall be substituted “£48·50”;
- (b) in paragraph (1)(b) for “£31·20” there shall be substituted “£32·40”;
- (c) in paragraph (1)(c) for “£12·40” there shall be substituted “£12·90”;
- (d) in paragraph (2)(a) for “£32·65” there shall be substituted “£33·90”; and
- (e) in paragraph (2)(b) for “£12·40” there shall be substituted “£12·90”.
