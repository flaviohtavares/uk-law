# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 13

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 13. Sums specified for child benefit[^key-04b8963a0bf28a3bcb0d95ea0327e30a]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In regulation 2 of the Child Benefit and Social Security (Fixing and Adjustment of Rates) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1976[^f00022] (weekly rates of child benefit)—

- (a) in paragraph (1)(a) for “£10·40” there shall be substituted “£10·80”;
- (b) in paragraph (1)(b) for “£8·45” there shall be substituted “£8·80”; and
- (c) in paragraph (2) the sum specified is £6·30.
