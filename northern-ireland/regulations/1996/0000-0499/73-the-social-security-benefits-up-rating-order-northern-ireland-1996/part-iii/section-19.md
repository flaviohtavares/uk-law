# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 19

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 19. Income support transitional protection[^key-1dc1c2d7b6c8f3f8b3fe1ca8190407f1]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Sums which are special transitional additions to income support payable in accordance with regulation 15 of the Income Support (Transitional) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1987[^f00033] shall be increased by 3.0 per cent. of their amount apart from this Order.
