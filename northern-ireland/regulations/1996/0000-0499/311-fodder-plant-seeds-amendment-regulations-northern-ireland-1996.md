# Fodder Plant Seeds (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 311

SEEDS

Fodder Plant Seeds (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996

Made

23rd July 1996

Coming into operation

23rd August 1996

The Department of Agriculture in exercise of the powers conferred on it by sections 1(1) and 2[^f00001] of the Seeds Act (Northern Ireland) 1965[^f00002] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, after consultation with representatives of such interests as appear to it to be concerned, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Fodder Plant Seeds (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996 and shall come into operation on 23rd August 1996.

## 2. Amendment of the Fodder Plant Seeds Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1994

For sub-paragraph (*a*) of Part II of Schedule 5 (maximum weight of a seed lot and minimum weight of a submitted sample) to the Fodder Plant Seeds Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1994[^f00003] there shall be substituted the following sub-paragraph—

> - (a) The maximum weight of a seed lot shall be—
>   - (i) for seeds of Hairy vetch and Hungarian vetch, 20 tonnes;
>   - (ii) for seeds of Common vetch, Field bean, Field pea, Narrow-leaved lupin (Blue lupin), White lupin and Yellow lupin, 25 tonnes.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture on 23rd July 1996.

L. G. McKibben

Assistant Secretary

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations implement as respects Northern Ireland Commission Directive [96/18/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0018) (O.J. No. L76, 26.3.96, p. 21) amending Annex III to Council Directive [66/401/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1966/0401) (O.J. No. 125, 11.7.66, p. 2298/66) on the marketing of fodder plant seed as amended.

These Regulations, which come into operation on 23rd August 1996, amend the Fodder Plant Seeds Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1994 to increase the maximum seed lot weight for seeds of common vetch, field bean, field pea, narrow-leaved lupin (blue lupin), white lupin and yellow lupin to 25 tonnes.
