# Roads (Speed Limit) (No. 7) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 2

## 2. Speed restrictions on certain roads

The Department hereby directs that each of the roads and lengths of road specified in Schedule 1 shall be a restricted road for the purposes of Article 50 of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981.
