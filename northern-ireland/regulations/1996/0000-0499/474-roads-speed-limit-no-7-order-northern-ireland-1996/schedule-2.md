# Roads (Speed Limit) (No. 7) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996, SCHEDULE 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 2 — Revocations

Article 3

**1.** The item described in Schedule I to the Roads (Speed Limit) Order (Northern Ireland), 1956[^f00002] as follows:

The Dungannon-Benburb Road (Old Eglish Road), Class III No. C.644, Dungannon, Co. Tyrone, from the junction with Milltown, Dungannon, to the junction with Factory Lane, Dungannon.

**2.** Items 53 and 54 of the Second Schedule to the Roads (Speed Limit) Order (Northern Ireland), 1957[^f00003].

**3.** Items 27 and 28 of the Second Schedule to the Roads (Speed Limit) Order (Northern Ireland), 1961[^f00004].

**4.** Items 23 and 246 of Schedule 2 to the Roads (Speed Limit) Order (Northern Ireland) 1979[^f00005].

**5.** Item 189 of Schedule 2 to the Roads (Speed Limit) Order (Northern Ireland) 1986[^f00006].

**6.** Items 252 and 335 of Schedule 2 to the Roads (Speed Limit) Order (Northern Ireland) 1987[^f00007].

**7.** Items 27 and 186 of Schedule 1 to the Roads (Speed Limit) Order (Northern Ireland) 1990[^f00008].

**8.** Items 8, 140 and 142 of Schedule 1 to the Roads (Speed Limit) (No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 1990[^f00009].

**9.** Items 163 and 165 of Schedule 1 to the Roads (Speed Limit) (No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 1991[^f00010].
