# The Children’s Evidence (1995 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 122 (C. 5)

CRIMINAL PROCEDURE

The Children’s Evidence (1995 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996

Made

1st March 1996

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by Article 1(2) of the Children’s Evidence (Northern Ireland) Order 1995[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:—

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Children’s Evidence (1995 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the Children’s Evidence (Northern Ireland) Order 1995 shall be 8th April 1996.

P. B. B. Mayhew

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Northern Ireland Office

1st March 1996

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order appoints 8th April 1996 for the coming into operation of the Children’s Evidence (Northern Ireland) Order 1995.
