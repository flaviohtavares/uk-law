# Offshore Installations and Wells (Design and Construction, etc.) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 16

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 16. Materials

The well-operator shall ensure that every part of a well is composed of material which is suitable for achieving the purposes described in regulation 13(1).
