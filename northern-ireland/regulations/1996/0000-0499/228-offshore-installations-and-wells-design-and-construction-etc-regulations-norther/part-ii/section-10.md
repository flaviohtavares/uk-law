# Offshore Installations and Wells (Design and Construction, etc.) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 10

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 10. Decommissioning and dismantlement[^c-6fc47b2427d0a501ee6ec1a404822fd7]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

The duty holder shall ensure that an installation is decommissioned and dismantled in such a way that, so far as is reasonably practicable, it will possess sufficient integrity to enable such decommissioning and dismantlement to be carried out safely.
