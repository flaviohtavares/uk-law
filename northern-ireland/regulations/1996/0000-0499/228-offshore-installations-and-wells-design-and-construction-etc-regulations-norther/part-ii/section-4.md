# Offshore Installations and Wells (Design and Construction, etc.) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. General duty

(1) The duty holder shall ensure that an installation at all times possesses such integrity as is reasonably practicable.

(2) The provisions of regulations 5 to 10 are without prejudice to the generality of the requirement in paragraph (1).
