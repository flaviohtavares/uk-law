# Offshore Installations and Wells (Design and Construction, etc.) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, section 1

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

**1.** These Regulations contain requirements for ensuring that offshore oil and gas installations, and oil and gas wells are designed, constructed and kept in a sound structural state, and other requirements affecting them, for purposes of health and safety.
