# Miscellaneous Food Additives Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996, SCHEDULE 6

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 6 — Foods in which Miscellaneous Additives listed in Schedule 1 are generally prohibited[^key-5ba4b1ea01264a122b053f2e8a0b0956]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Regulations 3(2) and (4) and 4(3)

- Unprocessed foods
- Honey as defined in Directive [74/409/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1974/0409)[^f00059]
- Non-emulsified oils and fats of animal or vegetable origin
- Butter
- Pasteurised and sterilised (including UHT sterilisation) milk and cream (including skimmed, plain and semi-skimmed)
- Unflavoured, live fermented milk products
- Natural mineral water as defined in Directive [80/777/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1980/0777)[^f00060] and spring water
- Coffee (excluding flavoured instant coffee) and coffee extracts
- Unflavoured leaf tea
- Sugars as defined in Directive [73/437/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1973/0437)
- Dry pasta
- Natural unflavoured buttermilk (excluding sterilised buttermilk)
