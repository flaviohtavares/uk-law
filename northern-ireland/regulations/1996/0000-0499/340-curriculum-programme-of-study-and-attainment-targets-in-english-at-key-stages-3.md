# Curriculum (Programme of Study and Attainment Targets in English at Key Stages 3 and 4) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 340

EDUCATION

Curriculum (Programme of Study and Attainment Targets in English at Key Stages 3 and 4) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996

Made

29th July 1996

Coming into operation in accordance with Article 1(2) and (3)

The Department of Education, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 7(1)(*a*) and (5) of the Education Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 1989[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Curriculum (Programme of Study and Attainment Targets in English at Key Stages 3 and 4) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996.

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), this Order shall come into operation on 1st September 1996.

(3) The provisions of this Order relating to the fourth key stage shall come into operation on 1st August 1997 in respect of pupils in the second year of that key stage.

## 2. Revocation and saving

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the Curriculum (Programmes of Study and Attainment Targets in English) Order (Northern Ireland) 1990[^f00002], in so far as it relates to the third and fourth key stages, is hereby revoked.

(2) The Curriculum (Programmes of Study and Attainment Targets in English) Order (Northern Ireland) 1990 shall continue to have effect until 1st August 1997 in respect of pupils in the second year of the fourth key stage as if this Order had not been made.

## 3. Interpretation

In this Order—

- “the Document” means the document published by Her Majesty’s Stationery Office entitled “Key Stages 3 and 4, Programme of Study and Attainment Targets, English”[^f00003]; and

references to “level descriptions” are references to the levels set out in the Document in relation to each attainment target at the third key stage.

## 4. Specification of the Programme of Study and Attainment Targets

It is hereby directed that the provisions relating to the programme of study and attainment targets set out in the Document shall have effect as provided in Articles 5 to 8 for the purpose of specifying in relation to English—

- (a) a programme of study; and
- (b) attainment targets.

**5.** The programme of study for the third and fourth key stages are specified in relation to those key stages as set out in the Document.

**6.** The attainment targets described in the Document (to which the level descriptions referred to in Article 7 relate) are specified in relation to the third key stage.

**7.** The level descriptions related to the attainment targets described in the Document are specified in relation to the third key stage at levels 1 to 8.

**8.** The contents which are printed in italics in the Document or which are not printed on a tinted background serve to illustrate the programme of study and attainment targets described and do not form part of the provisions which have effect by virtue of this Order.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Education on 29th July 1996.

C. Jendoubi (Mrs.)

Assistant Secretary

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order, made under Article 7(1) of the Education Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 1989 (“the 1989 Order”) specifies a programme of study for key stages 3 and 4 and attainment targets for key stage 3 in relation to English. Concurrent Orders have been made specifying programmes of study for the other subjects of the Northern Ireland Curriculum.

Article 7(5) of the 1989 Order allows for an Order under Article 7(1), instead of containing the provisions to be made, to refer to provisions in a Document published by Her Majesty’s Stationery Office and to direct that these provisions shall have effect according to the Order.

This Order accordingly refers to “Key stages 3 and 4, Programme of Study and Attainment Targets, English” and provides for the programme of study for key stages 3 and 4 and the attainment targets for key stage 3 set out in it to have effect for those key stages.

It further provides that any contents printed in italics in the Document, or which are not printed on a tinted background, are for illustrative purposes only and do not form part of the provisions which have effect by virtue of this Order.

This Order revokes (with a saving) the Curriculum (Programmes of Study and Attainment Targets in English) Order (Northern Ireland) 1990 in so far as that Order relates to the third and fourth key stages.

This Order comes into operation from 1st September 1996 in relation to key stage 3 and the first year of key stage 4: and from 1st August 1997 in relation to the second year of key stage 4.

Copies of the Document entitled “Key Stages 3 and 4, Programme of Study and Attainment Targets, English” may be obtained from Her Majesty’s Stationery Office, 16 Arthur Street, Belfast BT1 4GD.
