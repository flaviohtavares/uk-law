# Roads (Speed Limit) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996

> Text as at 2016-01-14.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

1996 No. 2

ROAD TRAFFIC AND VEHICLES

Roads (Speed Limit) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996

Made

4th January 1996

To be laid before Parliament under paragraph 3(3) of Schedule 1 to the Northern Ireland Act 1974

Coming into operation

19th February 1996

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 50(4)(*a*) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation[^key-189054975514706129a11a52b135fb5b]

(1) This Order may be cited as the Roads (Speed Limit) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996 and shall come into operation on 19th February 1996.

(2) In this Order “the 1981 Order” means the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981.

## 2. Increase of speed limit[^key-22533b20c736eb076df399ac55c35891]

The rate of speed fixed by Article 50(1) of the 1981 Order shall be increased from 30 miles per hour to 40 miles per hour in relation to the restricted roads specified in Schedule 1.

## 3.[^key-a1739bf7cf4faff01b6687645c3a119c]

The rate of speed fixed by Article 50(1) of the 1981 Order shall be increased from 30 miles per hour to 50 miles per hour in relation to the restricted roads specified in Schedule 2.

## 4. Reduction of speed limit[^key-9e99484098d97280ba5a266d62886e60]

The rate of speed fixed by Article 50(1) of the 1981 Order shall be reduced from 40 miles per hour to 30 miles per hour in relation to the restricted roads specified in Schedule 3.

## 5. Revocations[^key-77f8d873f3f66e071caa5d6ccab2182a]

The provisions described in Schedule 4 are hereby revoked.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on

E. J. Galway

Assistant Secretary

4th January 1996.

- (d) revokes the provisions described in Schedule 4, which are spent (Article 5).
