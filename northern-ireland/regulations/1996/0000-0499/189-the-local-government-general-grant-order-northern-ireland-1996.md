# The Local Government (General Grant) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 1996-05-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisr/1996/189
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

1996 No. 189

LOCAL GOVERNMENT

The Local Government (General Grant) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996

To be laid before Parliament under paragraph 3(3) of Schedule 1 to the Northern Ireland Act 1974

Made

7th May 1996

Coming into operation

21st June 1996

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 3(1) of Part I of Schedule 1 to the Local Government &c. (Northern Ireland) Order 1972[^f00001] and now vested in it[^f00002] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement[^key-9959ecf0cd0bab4a44a05abc78681b20]

This Order may be cited as the Local Government (General Grant) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996 and shall come into operation on 21st June 1996.

## 2. General Grant[^key-00b9ee3fed830f8272ea1f8fd383561f]

For the purpose of paragraph 3(1) of Part I of Schedule 1 to the Local Government &c. (Northern Ireland) Order 1972 the districts specified for the year ending 31st March 1997 shall be the districts listed below—

|  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ards | Belfast | Dungannon | Newry and Mourne |
| Armagh | Carrickfergus | Larne | Strabane |
| Ballymena | Castlereagh | Lisburn |  |
| Ballymoney | Craigavon | Magherafelt |  |
| Banbridge | Down | Moyle |  |

## 3. Revocation[^key-f78873223cb870bd12c6fe5a33fb04e1]

The Local Government (General Grant) Order (Northern Ireland) 1992[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on .

J. McCormick

Assistant Secretary

7th May 1996

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order—

- (a) specifies those districts which are to be taken into account in calculating the standard penny rate products for the year ending 31st March 1997 for the purpose of computing the resources element of the grant made from central funds by the Department of the Environment to district councils (Article 2).
- (b) revokes a previous Order which is now spent (Article 3).
