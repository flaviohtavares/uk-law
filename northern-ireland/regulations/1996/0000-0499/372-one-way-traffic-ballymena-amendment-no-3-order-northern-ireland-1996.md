# One-Way Traffic (Ballymena) (Amendment No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 372

ROAD TRAFFIC AND VEHICLES

One-Way Traffic (Ballymena) (Amendment No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996

Made

7th August 1996

Coming into operation

15th September 1996

WHEREAS the Department of the Environment has published a notice in compliance with Article 23(1) and (2) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001];

AND WHEREAS no objection or representation has been received;

NOW THEREFORE the Department, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 21(1) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981 and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the One-Way Traffic (Ballymena) (Amendment No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996 and shall come into operation on 15th September 1996.

## 2. Amendment

The Schedule to the One-Way Traffic (Ballymena) Order (Northern Ireland) 1982[^f00002] shall be amended by the deletion therefrom of the items relating to Duke Street, from its junction with Mount Street to its junction with Park Street, and Park Street.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 7th August 1996.

J. McConnell

Assistant Secretary

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order amends the provisions of the One-Way Traffic (Ballymena) Order (Northern Ireland) 1982 by deleting the item relating to Duke Street, from its junction with Mount Street to its junction with Park Street, and the item relating to Park Street.
