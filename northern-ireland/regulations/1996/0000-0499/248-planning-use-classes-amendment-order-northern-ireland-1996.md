# Planning (Use Classes) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 248

PLANNING

Planning (Use Classes) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996

Made

25th June 1996

Coming into operation

5th August 1996

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 11(2)(*e*) of the Planning (Northern Ireland) Order 1991[^f00001] and all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Planning (Use Classes) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996 and shall come into operation on 5th August 1996.

## 2. Amendment

The Planning (Use Classes) Order (Northern Ireland) 1989[^f00002] is amended as follows—

- (a) in Class 5 (General Industrial) of the Schedule, omit “or Classes 7 to 10”; and
- (b) omit Classes 7 to 10 (Special Industrial) of the Schedule.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on

J. McConnell

Assistant Secretary

25th June 1996.

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order further amends the Planning (Use Classes) Order (Northern Ireland) 1989 by omitting Classes 7 to 10 (Special Industrial) from the Schedule.

Industrial processes previously within Classes 7 to 10 will fall within Class 5 (General Industrial).
