# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 101

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 101. Times of hearing of appeals or applications

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The hearing of appeals or applications shall be at such sittings of the court as from time to time may be directed by a district judge (magistrates’ courts)[^key-7814383bc018853e1a91fbc59da52b4b].

(2) The court may, notwithstanding that Rule 98(1) or 99 has not been complied with, order, upon such terms as it thinks fit, that the appeal or application shall be heard.
