> Text as at 2022-10-17.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 97. Signature on notice

*Extent: N.I.*

A notice under Part VII of the Order shall be signed by the appellant or applicant or by his solicitor or firm of solicitors, or in the name of the appellant or applicant or of such solicitor or firm of solicitors by some person duly authorised so to sign.
