# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 99

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 99. Copy of decision or determination appealed from to be lodged

*Extent: N.I.*

A copy of the decision or determination from which an appeal is brought shall be lodged with the clerk of petty sessions by the appellant at least seven days before the hearing of the appeal.
