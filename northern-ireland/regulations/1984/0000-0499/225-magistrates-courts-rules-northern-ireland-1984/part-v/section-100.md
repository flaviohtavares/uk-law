# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 100

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 100. Manner of service of notice

*Extent: N.I.*

A notice under Part VII of the Order may be served by the appellant or applicant or any person authorised by him to serve such notice in accordance with section 24(2) of the Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00149].
