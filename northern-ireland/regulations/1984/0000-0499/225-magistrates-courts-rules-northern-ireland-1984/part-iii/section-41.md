## 41. Authentication of statements, depositions or admissions

*Extent: N.I.*

The clerk of petty sessions shall authenticate by certificate in Form 27 the written statements admitted in evidence, any depositions[^key-12b5d32fe00ba30a638bd3a3e6d45e4d] and any formal admission made for the purpose of the inquiry.
