# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 52M

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 52M. Restriction on access to records kept under Rules 52I, 52K and 52L[^key-80154a56d1175bbe3efed0cd2a710566]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The records kept under Rules 52I, 52K and 52L[^key-24e9e19e2f9909a92ff2c04a72f12713] shall not be open to inspection by any person except—

- (a) as authorised by the Secretary of State; or
- (b) with the leave of the court.”\]
