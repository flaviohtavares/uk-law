# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 37

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 37. Court may address accused through court official

*Extent: N.I.*

It shall be sufficient compliance with the provisions of these Rules relating to a preliminary inquiry requiring the court to address the accused (whether in reading the charge, any written statement, asking any questions, giving information, administering a warning or otherwise) for the presiding district judge (magistrates’ court) or lay magistrate to cause the accused to be addressed in the appropriate manner by an official of the court.
