# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 42A

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 42A. Procedure for binding witnesses and prosecutor over to attend trial[^c-de059b9dd8185fb0554bd8ca8a0f008c]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Every recognizance under Article 39 of the Order shall be acknowledged and signed by the person entering into the recognizance and signed by the district judge (magistrates’ court) or lay magistrate.

(2) The recognizance may be on a separate form or at the foot of the deposition at the discretion of the court.
