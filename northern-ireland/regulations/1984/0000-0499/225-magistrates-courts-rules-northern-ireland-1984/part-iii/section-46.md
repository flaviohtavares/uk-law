# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 46

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 46. Conviction of offence other than that charged where indictable offence dealt with summarily

*Extent: N.I.*

Where a district judge (magistrates’ court) in exercise of the power conferred by Article 46(3) of the Order, having dealt summarily with a charge for an indictable offence, convicts the accused of an offence in the alternative to that charged, an entry to that effect shall be made in the Order Book and specifying the alternative offence of which he was convicted.
