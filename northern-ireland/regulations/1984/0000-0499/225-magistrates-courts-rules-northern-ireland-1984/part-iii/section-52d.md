# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 52D

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 52D. \[ Applications and procedures under the Crime (International Co-operation) Act 2003[^f00137]

*Extent: N.I.*

In this rule and rules 52E to 52M—

- (a) “**the Act**” means the Crime (International Co-operation) Act 2003;
- (b) “**process**” has the same meaning as in section 51(3) of the Act;
- (c) “**relevant proceedings**” means proceedings under Part 1 or Part 2[^c-547a504600b11a9885a052f61b07a683] [^c-104409ec86442bca158d01dc44eb9efa]... of Schedule 2 to the Act; and
- (d) “**external court” or “external authority**” means the court or authority from which a request under section 30 or 31[^c-f8e8932b40455921b4cfda8f5ca2cb95] [^c-31990e3d3f1c42461b00c34c06b9a77b]... of the Act is received.\]
