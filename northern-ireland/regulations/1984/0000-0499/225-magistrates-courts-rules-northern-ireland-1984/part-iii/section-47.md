# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 47

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 47. Preservation of depositions where indictable offence is dealt with summarily[^key-68f52ebc1b3917ac692543ea5dae1db1]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where a person charged with an indictable offence has been tried summarily under Article 45 of the Order by a district judge (magistrates’ courts), the clerk of petty sessions shall preserve for a period of at least three years such depositions as have been taken or admitted[^key-dfc2b5ed841d1a827debad23be0a9d74].
