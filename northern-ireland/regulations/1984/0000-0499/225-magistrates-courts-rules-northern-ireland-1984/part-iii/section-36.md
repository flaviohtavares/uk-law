# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 36

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 36. Modification of Rules where accused is a corporation

*Extent: N.I.*

Any reference in these Rules to an accused shall where the accused is a corporation be construed as a reference to the representative of the corporation within the meaning of Schedule 4 to the Order.
