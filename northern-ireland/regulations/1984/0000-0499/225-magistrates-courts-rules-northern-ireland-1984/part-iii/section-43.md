# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 43

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 43. Conditions to be complied with before preliminary ... inquiry[^key-d8e3fe35110222a38facdd3b93cf55af]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where an adult is charged with an indictable offence specified in Schedule 2 to the Order, a lay magistrate (other than a district judge (magistrates’ court)) shall not proceed to conduct a [^key-5817c420a908c9919f1437849f6c7a4e]... preliminary inquiry unless he is informed that—

- (a) the prosecutor will not in any event consent to summary trial of the charge under Article 45 of the Order; or
- (b) a district judge (magistrates’ court) has decided that it is not expedient to deal with the charge summarily.
