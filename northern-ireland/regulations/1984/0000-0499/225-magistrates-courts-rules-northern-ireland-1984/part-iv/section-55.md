# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 55

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 55. Signature on process

*Extent: N.I.*

Every process and every copy thereof for service shall be signed by the plaintiff or by his solicitor or firm of solicitors, or in the name of such solicitor by some person duly authorised to sign.
