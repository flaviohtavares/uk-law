# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 91

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 91. Issue of decree after postponement subject to payment by instalments

*Extent: N.I.*

Where the court has postponed the issue of a decree subject to terms imposed under Article 116 of the Order, whether as to payment of sums periodically or otherwise, the decree shall not issue until there has been default in compliance with such terms (which includes in relation to such payment, default in payment of an instalment) and an affidavit or statutory declaration setting out particulars of the default has been lodged with the clerk of petty sessions.
