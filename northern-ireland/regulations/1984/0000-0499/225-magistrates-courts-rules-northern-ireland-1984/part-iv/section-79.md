# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 79

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 79. Signing of formal orders in debt or ejectment proceedings

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A formal order issued by the court shall be signed by the resident magistrate who made the order or by some other resident magistrate or by the clerk of petty sessions.

(2) A formal order shall be deemed to be issued on the earliest day it may lawfully be issued whether or not in fact it is actually issued on that day and such day shall be entered on the formal order as the date of issue.
