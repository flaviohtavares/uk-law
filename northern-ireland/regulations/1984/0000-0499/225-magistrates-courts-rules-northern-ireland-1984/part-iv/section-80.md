# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 80

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 80. Transfer of proceedings to county court

*Extent: N.I.*

Where the court orders proceedings to be transferred to the county court under Article 75 of the Order it shall cause the clerk of petty sessions to transmit forthwith the original process and any notice of set-off or counterclaim or other document lodged in court in relation to the matter, together with a statement of the reasons for the transfer, to the county court, and, at the same time, to send all parties to the proceeding notice in writing of the transfer.
