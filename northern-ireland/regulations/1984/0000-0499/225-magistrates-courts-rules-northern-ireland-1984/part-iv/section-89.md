# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 89

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 89. Dismiss on proof of set-off or counterclaim

*Extent: N.I.*

Where a dismiss is given by reason of the defendant establishing a set-off or counterclaim, that reason shall be stated on the dismiss.
