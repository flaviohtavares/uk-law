# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 63A

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 63A. \[ Receipt of certificate of service of process in evidence[^f00145]

*Extent: N.I.*

Any document purporting to be given as a certificate in Form 111A shall be received in evidence and shall be deemed to be duly made until the contrary is shown.\]
