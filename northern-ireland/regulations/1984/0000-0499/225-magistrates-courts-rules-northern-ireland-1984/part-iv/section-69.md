> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 69. Withdrawal of proceeding

*Extent: N.I.*

Where the court acting under Article 73(4) of the Order allows a proceeding to be withdrawn the word “Withdrawn” shall be entered in the appropriate column of the Process Register.
