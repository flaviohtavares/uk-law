# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 83

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 83. Claims for rent joined under Article 72(3) of the Order with claims in ejectment

*Extent: N.I.*

In a proceeding in which a claim for rent or sums due under Article 69 of the Order is joined to a claim for the recovery of premises in accordance with Article 72(3) of the Order so much of Rules 85 to 90 as is applicable shall have effect in relation to the proceeding.
