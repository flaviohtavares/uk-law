# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 54

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 54. Time and issue of process

*Extent: N.I.*

A process shall be deemed to have been issued when it has been stamped, signed, and handed or sent by post to a summons server or to such other person as may be authorised to serve it, or to the clerk of petty sessions [^key-a33c6c4298dc6fcbbab595a59608ce56]... for a delivery to a summons server or when service is effected in accordance with Rule 60.
