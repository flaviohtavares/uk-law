# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 74

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 74. Dismiss

*Extent: N.I.*

Where the court dismisses a claim upon the merits or without prejudice to a further claim in the same matter, the court shall so state by its formal order and the entry of the words “Dismissed on the Merits” or, as the case may be, “Dismissed Without Prejudice” in the appropriate Process Register shall be a sufficient record of the order.
