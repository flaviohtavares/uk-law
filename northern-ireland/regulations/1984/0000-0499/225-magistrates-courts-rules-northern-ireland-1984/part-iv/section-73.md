# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 73

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 73. Order where more than one defendant

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) In a proceeding in which two or more persons are named as defendants in a process, the court may make an order for payment of the amount claimed or for the recovery of possession of premises sought to be recovered against one or more of these persons, and strike out, dismiss, or allow to be withdrawn the proceeding against the remainder.

(2) Where a proceeding is dismissed under this Rule, the court may order that any costs awarded on the dismiss, and which the plaintiff is liable to pay, shall be recovered by way of recoupment by the plaintiff from the defendant or defendants against whom an order is made, in addition to the plaintiff’s costs of the order.
