# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 93

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 93. Interest on amount awarded

*Extent: N.I.*

The amount awarded by a decree (including any interest and costs thereby awarded) or the amount of costs ordered to be paid by a dismiss shall, subject to any direction by the court, carry interest at seven per cent per annum.
