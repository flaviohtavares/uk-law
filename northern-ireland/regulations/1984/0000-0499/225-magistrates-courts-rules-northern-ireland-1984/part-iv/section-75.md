# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 75

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 75. Procedure where previous dismiss is produced

*Extent: N.I.*

Where in a proceeding a defendant produces a dismiss without prejudice obtained by him against the plaintiff for the same cause of action, the plaintiff shall not be at liberty to proceed until he has paid to, or satisfied the defendant for the amount for costs shown in such dismiss.
