# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 68

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 68. Striking out of proceeding

*Extent: N.I.*

The entry of the words “Struck Out” in the Process Register shall be a sufficient record of the striking out of a proceeding under Article 73(3) of the Order.
