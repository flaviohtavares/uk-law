## 94. Postponement of issue of decree to be stay of enforcement for purposes of Article 116(3) of the Judgments Enforcement (Northern Ireland) Order 1981

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Postponement of the issue of a decree subject to payment by instalments shall be treated as a stay of enforcement on the ground of the debtor’s inability to pay for the purposes of Article 116(3) of the Judgments Enforcement (Northern Ireland) Order 1981.

(2) Where the court makes such an order the clerk of petty sessions shall enter the order in the Debt Process Register in the column headed “Minute of Adjudication” and shall transmit a memorandum thereof to the Enforcement of Judgments Office.
