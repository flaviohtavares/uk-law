## 165. \[Interpretation of Rules 166 to 170[^f00241]

*Extent: N.I.*

In rules 166 to 170—

- (a) “**the Act**” means the Coroners and Justice Act 2009;
- (b)[^c-383fc65133c6f4ab8adb6ab34d88741f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) a section referred to by number means the section so numbered in the Act; and
- (d) expressions which are defined in the Act have the same meaning in these Rules as they have in the Act.
