# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 164

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 164. Service of copy of order to which Article 98 of the Order applies

*Extent: N.I.*

Where the court makes, revokes, discharges, revives or varies an order to which Article 98 of the Order applies, the court shall cause a copy of its order to be served on the player and on the payee under the Order delivering it to him or by sending it by ordinary post in an envelope addressed to him at his last known or usual place of abode.
