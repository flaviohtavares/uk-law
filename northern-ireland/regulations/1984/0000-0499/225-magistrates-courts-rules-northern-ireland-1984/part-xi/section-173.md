# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 173

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 173. Appeal against a decision in respect of a closure order

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A notice of appeal against a decision of the court in respect of an application under Article 41E of the Registration of Clubs (Northern Ireland) Order 1996 or Article 69E of the Licensing (Northern Ireland) Order 1996 shall be—

- (a) made in writing, setting out the grounds of appeal; and
- (b) served by the appellant on all parties to the proceedings.\]
