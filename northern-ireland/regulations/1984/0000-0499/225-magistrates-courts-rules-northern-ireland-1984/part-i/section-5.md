## 5. Saving for the Magistrates’ Courts (Children and Young Persons) Rules (Northern Ireland) 1969

*Extent: N.I.*

[^f00054] Subject to the Children and Young Persons Act (Northern Ireland) 1968 [^f00055] and the Criminal Justice (Children) (Northern Ireland) Order 1998[^f00056], nothing in these Rules shall affect the operation of the Magistrates’ Courts (Criminal Justice (Children)) Rules (Northern Ireland) 1999[^f00057].\]
