# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 4

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 4. Revocation of existing rules

*Extent: N.I.*

The following Rules are hereby revoked as from the coming into force of these Rules:—

- the Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1974 [^f00038];
- the Magistrates’ Courts (Emergency Provisions) Rules (Northern Ireland) 1976[^f00039];
- the Magistrates’ Courts (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 1976[^f00040];
- the Magistrates’ Courts (Amendment No. 2) Rules (Northern Ireland) 1976[^f00041];
- the Magistrates’ Courts (Extra-territorial Offences) Rules (Northern Ireland) 1976[^f00042];
- the Magistrates’ Courts (Amendment No. 3) Rules (Northern Ireland) 1976[^f00043];
- the Magistrates’ Courts (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 1977[^f00044];
- the Magistrates’ Courts (Amendment No. 2) Rules (Northern Ireland) 1977[^f00045];
- the Magistrates’ Courts (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 1978[^f00046]
- the Magistrates’ Courts (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 1979[^f00047]
- the Magistrates’ Courts (Attachment of Earnings) Rules (Northern Ireland) 1980[^f00048]
- the Magistrates’ Courts (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 1980[^f00049]
- the Magistrates’ Courts (Criminal Justice) (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 1980[^f00050]
- the Magistrates’ Courts (Amendment No. 2) Rules (Northern Ireland) 1980[^f00051]
- the Magistrates’ Courts (Amendment No. 3) Rules (Northern Ireland) 1980[^f00052]
- the Magistrates’ Courts (Amendment No. 4) Rules (Northern Ireland) 1980[^f00053]
