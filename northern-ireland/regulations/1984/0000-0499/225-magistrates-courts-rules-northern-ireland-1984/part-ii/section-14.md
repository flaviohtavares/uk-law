# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 14

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 14. Form of warrant or order

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2)[^c-da14d15def1bbaeae1a0ef77791fa3b7], a warrant or form of order issued to give effect to the order of a magistrates’ court shall be signed by the district judge (magistrates’ courts) or lay magistrate who made the order or by the clerk of petty sessions.

(2) A warrant to arrest or warrant to search must be signed by a district judge (magistrates’ courts) or lay magistrate.

(2A)[^c-68af9bce61ca68f7f0bcc4079dfaea75] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2B)[^c-5849be92c21a23eb5437043bddb9be1e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) No warrant shall be signed in Blank.

(4) Every warrant shall state shortly the offence charged in the complaint or the grounds on which the warrant is issued.

(5) A warrant shall name in full or otherwise describe the person against whom it is issued.

(6)[^c-bd314c3157841addde0ccfb6c5896fec] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

[(7) [^f00104] Where no other form of warrant of arrest is prescribed, either by these Rules or by any other statutory provision, the warrant in Form 8C shall be used.\]
