## 12D. Proof of service of a requirement to identify driver of vehicle under Article 15 of the Road Traffic Offenders (Northern Ireland) Order 1996

*Extent: N.I.*

Service of a requirement under Article 177 of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981, to give information as to the identity of the driver of a vehicle may be proved in any proceedings by a certificate of service in Form 109C.\]
