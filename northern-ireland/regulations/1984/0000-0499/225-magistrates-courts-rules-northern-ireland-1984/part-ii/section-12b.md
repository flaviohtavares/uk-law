# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 12B

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 12B. \[ Receipt of certificate or electronic report in evidence[^f00085]

*Extent: N.I.*

Any document purporting to be given as a certificate in [^key-87d3f9458ae23f0793470e020ca070e6]... Form 109C or Form 110A, or any information or documentation provided by means of an electronic report, shall be received in evidence and shall be deemed to be duly made until the contrary is shown.
