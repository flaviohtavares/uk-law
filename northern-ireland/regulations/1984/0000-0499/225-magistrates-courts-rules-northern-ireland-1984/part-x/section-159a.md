# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 159A

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 159A. Notification of decision in an application for case stated in relation to proceedings under section 59 of the Crime (International Co-operation) Act 2003

*Extent: N.I.*

[^f00237] Where an application has been made to a district judge (magistrates’ courts) to state a case in relation to proceedings under section 59 of the Crime (International Co-operation) Act 2003, and the Court of Appeal has suspended the recognition of the disqualification under section 62(3) of that Act, the applicant shall notify the proper officer, as soon as practicable, if —

- (a) the applicant abandons the application to state a case; or
- (b) the district judge (magistrates’ courts) refuses to state a case and the applicant does not intend to apply to the Court of Appeal to compel him or her to do so.\]
