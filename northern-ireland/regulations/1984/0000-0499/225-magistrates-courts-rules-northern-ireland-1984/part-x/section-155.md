# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 155

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 155. Documents to be sent to the chief clerk and to the parties to the appeal

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) As soon as practicable after complying with Rule 154 the clerk of petty sessions shall send to the chief clerk [^c-20cd37c35b7aeced582014859ac7bb3e]...—

- (a) the copy of the notice of appeal;
- (b) the recognizance, if any, entered into by the appellant under Article 148 or Article 149 of the Order;
- (c) the form of appeal duly completed; \[[^f00231]...\]
- (d) all other proceedings in the case (including any written statements admitted in evidence under section 1 of the Criminal Justice (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act (Northern Ireland) 1968) \[[^f00232] and any pre-sentence report or medical report, within the meaning of the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 1996, \[[^f00233] or the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2008\] which was considered at the trial of the appellant), and\];
- [(e) [^f00234] a copy of any entry in the Order Book made in pursuance of Article 10(7); 13(9); 15(4) or 19(5) of the 1996 Order, or under Rule 125A.\]

(2) The clerk of petty sessions shall at the same time send to the appellant and to the respondent a copy of the form of appeal.
