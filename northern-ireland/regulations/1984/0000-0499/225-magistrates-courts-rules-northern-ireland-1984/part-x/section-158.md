# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 158

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 158. Contents of and signature upon written application for case stated

*Extent: N.I.*

A written application under Article 146 of the Order for a case to be stated for the opinion of the Court of Appeal shall—

- (a) specify the point of law involved in the determination by the magistrates’ court of the proceedings or any issue as to its jurisdiction; and
- (b) be prepared and signed by the appellant or his solicitor or counsel and contain his address or that of his solicitor.
