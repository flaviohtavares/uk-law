# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 159

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 159. Notification of decision to grant application for case stated

*Extent: N.I.*

As soon as the court grants an application for a case stated, it shall notify the parties to the proceeding of its decision to grant the application.
