# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 126A

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 126A. Committal to Crown Court

*Extent: N.I.*

Where a court of summary jurisdiction commits an offender to the Crown Court under \[[^f00176]paragraph 2(4)\] or paragraph 9(2)(b) of Schedule 2 to the 1996 Order, the clerk of petty sessions shall (in addition to complying with paragraph 3(4) of Schedule 2 where it applies) notify the chief clerk [^key-043f213214690530e6a32ba9c9d10b62]... by sending to him—

- (a) where an offender is released on bail, a copy of any recognizance entered into; and
- (b) a certificate of the conviction or order made by the court of summary jurisdiction.
