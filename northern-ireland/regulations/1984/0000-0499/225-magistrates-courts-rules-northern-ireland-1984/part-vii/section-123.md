## 123. Evidence of conviction to be sent to the Crown Court

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where a magistrates’ court under section 20(3) of the Treatment of Offenders Act (Northern Ireland) 1968 commits an offender to the Crown Court, or, instead of doing so, furnishes written notice of the conviction to the chief clerk [^key-2a9faa16e0142e7f498f8efa82f02870]..., the clerk of petty sessions shall send to the chief clerk[^key-ed36bc232fd2746f61d909fedb8a5b83] a certificate of the conviction by the magistrates’ court.
