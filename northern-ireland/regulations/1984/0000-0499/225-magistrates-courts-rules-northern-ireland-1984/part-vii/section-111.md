> Text as at 2022-10-17.

## 111. Attachment of earnings order

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An attachment of earnings order under Article 101 of the Order shall be in Form 73.

(2) The clerk of petty sessions [^key-e2d25de599abd5a1113b15b1adbf59dd]... may sign such order.
