> Text as at 2022-10-17.

## 126E. Conviction while licence remains in force

*Extent: N.I.*

Where it appears to the court by or before which a person is convicted of an offence that the offence was committed while the person was on licence and that the person has not been recalled to prison, the court shall inform the Secretary of State of the conviction in Form 106.\]
