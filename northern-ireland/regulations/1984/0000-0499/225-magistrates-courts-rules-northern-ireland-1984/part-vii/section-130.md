# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 130

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 130. Service of process issued under Rule 128 or 129

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Service of a process issued in accordance with Rule 128 or 129 shall be effected by delivering to the defendant personally a true copy thereof.

(2)[^key-076280be186fe7bf7c047daf7de69519] A committal process may be served by the summons server of the administrative court division in which the defendant resides or in which any business premises used or occupied by him are situated and such service may be proved by affidavit.
