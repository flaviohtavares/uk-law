## 126D. Licence conditions for sentences of less than 12 months

*Extent: N.I.*

Where a court, when passing a determinate custodial sentence of less than 12 months, under Article 24(2)(a) of the 2008 Order requires particular conditions to be included in any licence granted under Article 17 or 19 of the 2008 Order, it shall send notice of its order to the Secretary of State in Form 106.
