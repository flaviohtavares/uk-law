> Text as at 2022-10-17.

## 142. Part payment of sums to clerk of petty sessions

*Extent: N.I.*

Where before a warrant is issued to enforce payment of a sum adjudged to be paid by the conviction or order of a magistrates’ court, part of that sum has been paid to the clerk of petty sessions, he shall endorse details of such payment on the warrant.
