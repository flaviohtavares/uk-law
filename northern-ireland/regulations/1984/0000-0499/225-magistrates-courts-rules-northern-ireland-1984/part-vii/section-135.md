# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 135

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 135. Duration of committal orders

*Extent: N.I.*

A committal order under Article 107 of the Judgments (Enforcement) (Northern Ireland) Order 1981 shall remain in force for one year and no longer.
