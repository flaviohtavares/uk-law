# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 115

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 115. Variation of attachment of earnings order on change of employment

*Extent: N.I.*

Where an attachment of earnings order has lapsed under Article 104(5) of the Order on the debtor’s ceasing to be in the employment of the person to whom the order was directed and it appears to the court that the defendant has subsequently entered the employment of a person (whether the same as before or another), the court may, of its own motion, vary the order by directing it to that person and may make any consequential amendment to the order made necessary by this variation.
