# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 114

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 114. Notice of application for appropriate variation order

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where an application is made under Article 104 of the Order for the appropriate variation of an attachment of earnings order, the clerk of petty sessions shall, where practicable, give notice in writing of the time and place appointed for the hearing of the application to the person entitled to receive payment under the related maintenance order (whether directly or through the officer of any court).

(2) An application for an order varying (including suspending or reviving) or discharging an attachment of earnings order shall be by way of complaint.
