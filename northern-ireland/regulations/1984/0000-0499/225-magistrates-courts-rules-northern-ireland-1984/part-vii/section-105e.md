# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 105E

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 105E. Notification of court order at default hearing[^c-62eb0fe59a1a90e24325a9d5066d92bd]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where, at a default hearing, a court exercises any of the powers conferred by section 9 of the Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 2016, the collection officer or, as the case may be, clerk of petty sessions shall serve upon the debtor notice in writing of the decision by delivering it to the debtor or by sending it to him by ordinary post in an envelope addressed to him at his last known or usual place of abode.
