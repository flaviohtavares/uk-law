# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 108

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 108. Transfer of Fine Order sent to England and Wales or Scotland

*Extent: N.I.*

Where the court makes a transfer of fine order under Article 95 of the Order (including Article 95 as applied by Article 96 of the Order), the clerk of petty sessions shall send to the clerk to the justices for the petty sessions area in England and Wales or to the sheriff or sheriff clerk of the court of summary jurisdiction in Scotland in or by which, under the order, payment of a sum adjudged to be paid is to be enforceable, a copy of the order in Form 70 or 71, as the case may be, with a statement of the offence and the steps, if any, taken to recover the sum with such former information as is available and is, in the opinion of the clerk of petty sessions, likely to assist enforcement.
