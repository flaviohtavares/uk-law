## 105F. Interpretation[^c-62eb0fe59a1a90e24325a9d5066d92bd]

*Extent: N.I.*

In rules 105C to E, “default hearing” means a hearing under section 6(1) or 8(2) of the Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 2016.
