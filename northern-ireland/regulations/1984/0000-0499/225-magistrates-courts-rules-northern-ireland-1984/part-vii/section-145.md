# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 145

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 145. Warrants of distress issued under Article 109 of the Order

*Extent: N.I.*

A warrant of distress issued under Article 109 of the Order shall be enforced in the same manner as a warrant issued in proceedings upon complaint.
