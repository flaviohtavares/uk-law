# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 121

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 121. Enforcement of Judgments Office records

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where a clerk of petty sessions causes a copy of an order or notice to be given to any person under Rule 112 of these Rules, he shall cause a copy of the order or notice to be given also to the Enforcement of Judgments Office.

(2) Where the clerk of petty sessions for the court which has made an attachment of earnings order is informed of a defendant’s change of address he shall notify the new address to the Enforcement of Judgments Office.
