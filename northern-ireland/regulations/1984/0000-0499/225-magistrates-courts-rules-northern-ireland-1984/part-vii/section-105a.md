# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 105A

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 105A. Collection order[^key-62eb0fe59a1a90e24325a9d5066d92bd]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A collection order under section 3 of the Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 2016 shall be in Form 60B and shall be served by the clerk of petty sessions on the debtor and, at the same time, a copy thereof shall be sent to the collection officer.

(2) A collection order shall be served by delivering it to the debtor or by sending it to him by ordinary post in an envelope addressed to him at his last known or usual place of abode.
