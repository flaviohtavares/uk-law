# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 147

> Text as at 2022-10-17.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 147. Application for review of warrant of commitment

*Extent: N.I.*

A person imprisoned under a warrant of commitment issued by a magistrates’ court under Article 112 of the Order who is not detained otherwise than under that Article may make an application by way of complaint in Form 120 to a district judge (magistrates’ court) requesting that the warrant be cancelled and stating the grounds of the application.
