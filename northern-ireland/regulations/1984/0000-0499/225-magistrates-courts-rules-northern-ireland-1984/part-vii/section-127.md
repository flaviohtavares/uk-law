## 127. Notification of conviction before expiration of period of deferment

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where under \[[^f00178] Article 3 of the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 1996\] a court has deferred passing sentence on an offender and before the expiration of the period of deferment he is convicted of any offence by a magistrates’ court, the clerk of petty sessions shall, [^key-c0393688ee39a6fd51d10e064717173a]... if the court which deferred passing sentence on the earlier occasion was the Crown Court, give notice of the conviction to the chief clerk [^key-c0393688ee39a6fd51d10e064717173a]....
