> Text as at 2022-10-17.

## 125A. Entry in Order Book

*Extent: N.I.*

Where a magistrates’ court makes a statement under Article 9(4); \[[^f00173]...\] 24(4) or (5) or 33(2) of the 1996 Order \[[^f00174] or Article 9(3) of the 2008 Order\], it shall cause that statement to be entered in the Order Book.
