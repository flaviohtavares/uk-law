# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 132

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 132. Signature on notice of application for the issue of decree for possession

*Extent: N.I.*

A form of notice of application under Rule 131 may be signed by the plaintiff or by his solicitor or firm of solicitors or in the name of such solicitor or firm of solicitors by some person duly authorised to sign.
