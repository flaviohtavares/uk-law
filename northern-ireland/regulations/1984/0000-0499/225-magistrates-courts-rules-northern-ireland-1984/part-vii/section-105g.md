# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 105G

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 105G. Appeals[^c-62eb0fe59a1a90e24325a9d5066d92bd]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An appeal under section 25(1) of the Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 2016 against a decision of a collection officer shall be in Form 55 and shall be served on the collection officer at least 14 days before the hearing of the appeal.

(2) At the same time, the appellant shall serve a copy of the notice on the clerk of petty sessions which shall be endorsed with the date and manner of service on the collection officer and should be accompanied by a copy of the decision being appealed.
