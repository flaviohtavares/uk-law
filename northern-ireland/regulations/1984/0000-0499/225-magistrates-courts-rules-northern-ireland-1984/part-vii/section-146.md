# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 146

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 146. Costs of warrant to be added to sum due under warrant of commitment or distress

*Extent: N.I.*

Where the warrant is a warrant of distress the sum referred to in Article 8 of the Magistrates’ Courts Fees Order (Northern Ireland) 1983[^f00181] shall be leviable in addition to any reasonable costs of making or keeping the distress or conducting the sale of goods distrained in accordance with the order of the court.
