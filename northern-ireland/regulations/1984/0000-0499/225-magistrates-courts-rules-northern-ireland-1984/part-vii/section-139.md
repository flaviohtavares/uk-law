# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 139

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 139. Direction that money found on default shall not be applied in satisfaction of sum adjudged to be paid by a conviction or in proceedings under Article 98 of the Order

*Extent: N.I.*

Where the defaulter is committed to, or ordered to be detained in a prison or other place of detention, any direction given under Article 110(2) of the Order shall be endorsed on the warrant of commitment.
