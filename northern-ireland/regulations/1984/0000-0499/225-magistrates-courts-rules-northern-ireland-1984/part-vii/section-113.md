# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 113

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 113. Particulars of defendant

*Extent: N.I.*

The particulars of the defendant for the purpose of enabling him to be identified which, so far as they are known, are to be included in an attachment of earnings order shall be—

- (a) full name and address;
- (b) place of work;
- (c) nature of work and works number, if any.
