# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 149J

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 149J. \[ Witness Summonses[^f00220]

*Extent: N.I.*

In these Rules, unless the context otherwise requires—

- “**the applicant**” means the applicant in relation to an application to which that rule applies; and
- “the directed person” and “the stipulated evidence, document or thing” have the same meaning as in Article 118A(8) of the Order.
