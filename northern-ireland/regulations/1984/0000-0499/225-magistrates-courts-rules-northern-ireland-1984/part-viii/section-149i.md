> Text as at 2022-10-17.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 149I. Discharge of live link direction

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (3), an application to discharge a live link direction under Article 21A(7) of the 1999 Order shall be made in writing.

(2) An application under paragraph (1) shall be served on the clerk of petty sessions and on each party to the proceedings as soon as reasonably practicable.

(3) Paragraphs (4) to (12) of rule 149H shall apply to an application to discharge a live link direction as they apply to an application for a live link direction.\]
