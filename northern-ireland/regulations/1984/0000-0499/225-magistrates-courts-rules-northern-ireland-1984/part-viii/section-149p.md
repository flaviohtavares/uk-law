# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 149P

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 149P. Discharge or variation of a direction allowing the examination of accused by an intermediary

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (3), an application to discharge or vary a direction allowing the examination of the accused by an intermediary shall be made in writing.

(2) Any application under paragraph (1) shall be served on the clerk of petty sessions and on each party to the proceedings as soon as reasonably practicable.

(3) Paragraphs (4) to (12) of Rule 149O shall apply to an application to discharge or vary a direction allowing the examination of the accused by an intermediary as they apply to an application for such a direction.
