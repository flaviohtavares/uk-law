# Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984, section 151

> Text as at 2022-10-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 151. Deposit of recognizance with clerk of petty sessions

*Extent: N.I.*

Without prejudice to Rule 21, where the condition of a recognizance is to appear before a magistrates’ court or to be of good behaviour or to keep the peace or to be of good behaviour and keep the peace, the recognizance shall be deposited with the clerk of petty sessions.
