# The Statutory Rules (Exemption) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 541

STATUTORY RULES

The Statutory Rules (Exemption) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1991

Made

19th December 1991

Coming into operation

1st February 1992

The Department of Finance and Personnel in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Articles 6(2)(d) and 7(1) of the Statutory Rules (Northern Ireland) Order 1979[^f00001] and paragraph 2(1) of Schedule 1 of the Northern Ireland Act 1974[^f00002] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf and with the concurrence of the Lord Chief Justice, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These regulations may be cited as the Statutory Rules (Exemption) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1991 and shall come into operation on 1st February 1992.

## 2. Exemption

The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991[^f00003] are hereby exempted from Article 6(2) of the Statutory Rules (Northern Ireland) Order 1979 (which requires statutory rules to be printed in the annual volume of statutory rules).

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance and Personnel for Northern Ireland on

Joan O. M. Frame

Assistant Secretary

19th December 1991.

I concur,

Brian Hutton

Lord Chief Justice

(This note is not part of the Regulations).

These Regulations allow the Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991 to be omitted from the 1991 annual volume of statutory rules.
