# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 12.02

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12.02. Costs

All costs incurred in the course of winding up or bankruptcy proceedings are to be regarded as expenses of the winding up or, as the case may be, of the bankruptcy.
