# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 12.06

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12.06. Evidence of proceedings at meetings

(1) A minute of proceedings at a meeting (held under the Order or the Rules) of a person's creditors, or of the members of a company, or of the contributories in a company's liquidation, signed by a person describing himself as, or appearing to be, the chairman of that meeting is admissible in insolvency proceedings without further proof.

(2) The minute is prima facie evidence that—

- (a) the meeting was duly convened and held,
- (b) all resolutions passed at the meeting were duly passed, and
- (c) all proceedings at the meeting duly took place.
