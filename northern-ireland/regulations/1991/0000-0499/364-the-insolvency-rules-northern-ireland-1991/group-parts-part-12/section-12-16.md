# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 12.16

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12.16. Right to copy documents

Where the Order or the Rules confer a right for any person to inspect documents, the right includes that of taking copies of those documents, on payment—

- (a) in the case of documents on the court's file of proceedings, of the fee chargeable under any order made under section 116 of the Judicature (Northern Ireland) Act 1978[^f00035], and
- (b) otherwise, of the appropriate fee.
