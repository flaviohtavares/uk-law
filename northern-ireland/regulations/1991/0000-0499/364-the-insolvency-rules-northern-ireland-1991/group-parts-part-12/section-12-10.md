# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 12.10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12.10. Time

The provisions of Order 3 of the Supreme Court Rules, except rules 3 and 6, apply as regards computation, extension and abridgment of time in respect of anything required or authorised by the Rules to be done.
