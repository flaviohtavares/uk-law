# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 12.18

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12.18. Non-receipt of notice of meeting

Where in accordance with the Order or the Rules a meeting of creditors or other persons is summoned by notice, the meeting is presumed to have been duly summoned and held, notwithstanding that not all those to whom the notice is to be given have received it.
