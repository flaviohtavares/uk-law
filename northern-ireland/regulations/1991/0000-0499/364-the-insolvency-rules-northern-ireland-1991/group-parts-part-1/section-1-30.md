# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 1.30

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1.30. False representations, etc.

(1) A person being a past or present officer of a company commits an offence if he makes any false representation or commits any other fraud for the purpose of obtaining the approval of the company's members or creditors to a proposal for a voluntary arrangement under Part II of the Order.

(2) For this purpose “**officer**” includes a shadow director.

(3) A person guilty of an offence under this Rule is liable to imprisonment or a fine, or both.
