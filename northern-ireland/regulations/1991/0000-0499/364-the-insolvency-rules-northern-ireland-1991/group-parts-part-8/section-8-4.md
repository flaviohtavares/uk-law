# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 8.4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 8.4. Retention of proxies

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), proxies used for voting at any meeting shall be retained by the chairman of the meeting.

(2) The chairman shall deliver the proxies, forthwith after the meeting, to the responsible insolvency practitioner (where that is someone other than himself).
