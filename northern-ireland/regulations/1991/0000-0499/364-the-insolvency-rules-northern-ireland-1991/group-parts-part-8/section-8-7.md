# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 8.7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 8.7. Company representation

(1) Where a person is authorised under Article 383 of the Companies Order to represent a corporation at a meeting of creditors or of the company or its contributories, he shall produce to the chairman of the meeting a copy of the resolution from which he derives his authority.

(2) The copy resolution must be under the seal of the corporation, or certified by the secretary or a director of the corporation to be a true copy.

(3) Nothing in this Rule requires the authority of a person to sign a proxy on behalf of a principal which is a corporation to be in the form of a resolution of that corporation.
