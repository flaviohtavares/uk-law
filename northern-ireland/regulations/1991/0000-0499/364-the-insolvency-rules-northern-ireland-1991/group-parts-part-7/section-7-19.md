# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 7.19

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7.19. Enforcement of court orders

In any insolvency proceedings, orders of the court may be enforced in the same manner as a judgment to the same effect.

\[E.R.7.19(1)\]
