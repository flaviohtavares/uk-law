# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 7.41

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7.41. Service of notices following appointment

Any notice served on, or sent to, a person appointed under Rule 7.39 has the same effect as if it had been served on, or given to, the incapacitated person.
