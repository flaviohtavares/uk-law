# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 7.48

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7.48. Responsible insolvency practitioner's solicitor

Where in any proceedings the attendance of the responsible insolvency practitioner's solicitor is required, whether in court or in chambers, the responsible insolvency practitioner himself need not attend, unless directed by the court.
