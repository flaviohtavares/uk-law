# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 7.14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7.14. Nomination and appointment of shorthand writers

(1) The Judge may in writing nominate one or more persons to be official shorthand writers to the court.[^m00241]

(2) The court may, at any time in the course of insolvency proceedings, appoint a shorthand writer to take down the evidence of a person examined under Article 113, 200,2 63 or 337.[^m00242][^m00243]

(3) Where the official receiver applies to the court for an order appointing a shorthand writer, he shall name the person he proposes for appointment; and that appointment shall be made, unless the court otherwise orders.
