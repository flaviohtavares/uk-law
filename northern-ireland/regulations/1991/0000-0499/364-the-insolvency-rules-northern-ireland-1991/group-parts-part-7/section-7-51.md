# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 7.51

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7.51. Restriction on concurrent proceedings and remedies

Where in insolvency proceedings the court makes an order staying any action, execution or other legal process against the property of a company, or against the property or person of an individual debtor or bankrupt, service of the order may be effected by sending a sealed copy of the order to whatever is the address for service of the plaintiff or other party having the carriage of the proceedings to be stayed.
