# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 7.55

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7.55. Office copies of documents

(1) Any person who has under the Rules the right to inspect the court file of insolvency proceedings may require the court to provide him with an office copy of any document from the file.

(2) A person's rights under this Rule may be exercised on his behalf by his solicitor.

(3) An office copy provided by the court under this Rule shall be in such form as the Master thinks appropriate, and shall bear the court's seal.
