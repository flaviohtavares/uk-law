# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 7.16

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7.16. Remuneration

The remuneration of the shorthand writer shall be paid by the party at whose instance the appointment was made or out of the insolvent estate, or otherwise as the court may direct, at the rates payable for taking a note of evidence and making a transcript for use in the Court of Appeal.

\[E.R.7.17\]
