# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 7.54

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7.54. Discovery

(1) Any party to insolvency proceedings may, with the leave of the court, administer interrogatories to, or obtain discovery from, any other party to those proceedings.

(2) Application under this Rule may be made ex parte.
