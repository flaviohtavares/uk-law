# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 7.29

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7.29. Application of Supreme Court Rules

Subject to provision to inconsistent effect made in this Chapter, Order 62 of the Supreme Court Rules applies to insolvency proceedings in the High Court with any necessary modifications.
