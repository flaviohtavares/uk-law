# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 7.34

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7.34. Costs paid otherwise than out of the insolvent estate

Where a bill of costs is taxed under an order of the court directing that the costs are to be paid otherwise than out of the insolvent estate, the Taxing Master shall note on the certificate of taxation by whom, or the manner in which, the costs are to be paid.
