# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 7.45

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7.45. Principal court rules and practice to apply

Except so far as inconsistent with the Rules, the Supreme Court Rules and the practice of the High Court apply to insolvency proceedings with any necessary modifications.
