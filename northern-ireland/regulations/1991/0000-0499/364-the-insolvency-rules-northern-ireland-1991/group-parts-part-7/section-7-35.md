# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 7.35

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7.35. Award of costs against official receiver or responsible insolvency practitioner

Without prejudice to any provision of the Order or the Rules by virtue of which the official receiver is not in any event to be liable for costs, where the official receiver or a responsible insolvency practitioner is made a party to any proceedings on the application of another party to the proceedings, he shall not be personally liable for costs unless the court otherwise directs.
