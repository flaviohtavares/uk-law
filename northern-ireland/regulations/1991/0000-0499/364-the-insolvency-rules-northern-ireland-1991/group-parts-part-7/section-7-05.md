# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 7.05

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7.05. Preliminary

This Chapter applies to any application made to the court under the Order or the Rules except a petition for—

- (a) an administration order under Part III,
- (b) a winding-up order under Part V, or
- (c) a bankruptcy order under Part IX

of the Order.
