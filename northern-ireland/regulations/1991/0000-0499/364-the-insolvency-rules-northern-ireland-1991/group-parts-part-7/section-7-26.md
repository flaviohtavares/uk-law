# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 7.26

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7.26. File of court proceedings

(1) In respect of all insolvency proceedings, the court shall open and maintain a file for each case; and (subject to directions of the Master) all documents relating to such proceedings shall be placed on the relevant file.

(2) No proceedings shall be filed in the Central Office of the Supreme Court.
