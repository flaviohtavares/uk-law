# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 7.43

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7.43. Appeals in bankruptcy

In bankruptcy proceedings, an appeal lies at the instance of the Department from any order of the court made on an application for the rescission or annulment of a bankruptcy order, or for a bankrupt's discharge.
