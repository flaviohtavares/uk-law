# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 7.37

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7.37. Costs and expenses of witnesses

(1) Except as directed by the court, no allowance as a witness in any examination or other proceedings before the court shall be made to the bankrupt or an officer of the insolvent company to which the proceedings relate.

(2) A person presenting any petition in insolvency proceedings shall not be regarded as a witness on the hearing of the petition, but the Taxing Master may allow his expenses of travelling and subsistence.
