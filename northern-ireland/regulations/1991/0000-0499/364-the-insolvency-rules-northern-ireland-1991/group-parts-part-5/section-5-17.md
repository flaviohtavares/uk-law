# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 5.17

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5.17. Creditors' meeting: supplementary

(1) Subject to paragraphs (2) and (3), in fixing the venue for the creditors' meeting, the nominee shall have regard to the convenience of creditors.

(2) The meeting shall be summoned for commencement between 10.00 and 16.00 hours on a business day.

(3) With every notice summoning the meeting there shall be sent out forms of proxy.[^m00144]
