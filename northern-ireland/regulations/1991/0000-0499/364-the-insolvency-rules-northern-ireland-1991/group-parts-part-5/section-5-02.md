# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 5.02

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5.02. Introductory

(1) This Chapter applies where a debtor, with a view to an application for an interim order under Chapter II of Part VIII of the Order, makes a proposal to his creditors for a voluntary arrangement.

(2) The Rules apply whether the debtor is an undischarged bankrupt (“Case 1”) or he is not (“Case 2”).
