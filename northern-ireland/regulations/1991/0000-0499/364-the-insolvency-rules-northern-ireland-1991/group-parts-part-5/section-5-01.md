# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 5.01

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5.01. Service of application under Articles 221(3) or 224

(1) An application under Article 221(3) shall be served on the trustee named in the deed.

(2) An application under Article 224 shall be served—

- (a) if made by the trustee, on the debtor and any creditor or other person to be affected thereby;
- (b) if made by the debtor, on the trustee and any creditor or other person to be affected thereby; and
- (c) if made by a creditor, on the trustee and the debtor.
