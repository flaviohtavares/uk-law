# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 5.15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5.15. Consideration of nominee's report

(1) At the hearing by the court to consider the nominee's report any of the persons who have been given notice under Rule 5.06(4) may appear or be represented.

(2) Rule 5.08 applies to any order made by the court at the hearing.
