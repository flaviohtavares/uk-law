# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 5.33

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5.33. False representations, etc.

(1) The debtor commits an offence if he makes any false representation or commits any other fraud for the purpose of obtaining the approval of his creditors to a proposal for a voluntary arrangement under Part VIII of the Order.

(2) A person guilty of an offence under this Rule is liable to imprisonment or a fine, or both.
