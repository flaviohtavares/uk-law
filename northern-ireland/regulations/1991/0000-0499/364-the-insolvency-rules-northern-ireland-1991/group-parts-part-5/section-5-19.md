# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 5.19

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5.19. The chairman as proxy-holder

The chairman shall not by virtue of any proxy held by him vote to increase or reduce the amount of the remuneration or expenses of the nominee or the supervisor of the proposed voluntary arrangement, unless the proxy specifically directs him to vote in that way.
