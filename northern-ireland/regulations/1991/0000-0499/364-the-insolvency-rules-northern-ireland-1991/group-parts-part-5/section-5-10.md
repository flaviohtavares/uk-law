# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 5.10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5.10. Discharge of interim order

If an order is made discharging an interim order, the court shall send notice of the making of the order to the Enforcement of Judgments Office.
