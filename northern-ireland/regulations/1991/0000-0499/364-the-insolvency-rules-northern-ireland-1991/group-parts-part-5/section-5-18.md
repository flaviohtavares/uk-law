# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 5.18

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5.18. The chairman at the meeting

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the nominee shall be chairman of the creditors' meeting.

(2) If for any reason the nominee is unable to attend, he may nominate another person to act as chairman in his place; but a person so nominated must be either—

- (a) a person qualified to act as an insolvency practitioner in relation to the debtor, or
- (b) an employee of the nominee or his firm who is experienced in insolvency matters.
