# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 2.20

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2.20. Notice to members of proposals to creditors

The manner of publishing—

- (a) under Article 35(2)(b), notice to members of the administrator's proposals to creditors, and
- (b) under Article 37(3)(b), notice to members of substantial revisions of the proposals, shall be by gazetting; and the notice shall also in either case be advertised once in the newspaper in which the administration order was advertised.
