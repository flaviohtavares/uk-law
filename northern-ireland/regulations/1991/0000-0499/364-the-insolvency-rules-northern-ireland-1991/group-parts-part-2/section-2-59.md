# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 2.59

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2.59. Order filling vacancy

Where the court makes an order filling a vacancy in the office of administrator, the same provisions apply in respect of giving notice of, and advertising, the order as in the case of the administration order.
