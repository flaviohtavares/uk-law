# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 2.35

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2.35. Constitution of committee

(1) Where it is resolved by a creditors' meeting to establish a creditors' committee for the purposes of the administration, the committee shall consist of at least three and not more than five creditors of the company elected at the meeting.

(2) Any creditor of the company is eligible to be a member of the committee, so long as his claim has not been rejected for the purpose of his entitlement to vote.

(3) A body corporate may be a member of the committee, but it cannot act as such otherwise than by a representative appointed under Rule 2.40.
