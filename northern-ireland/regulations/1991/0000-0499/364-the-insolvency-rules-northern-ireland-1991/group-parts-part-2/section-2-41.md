# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 2.41

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2.41. Resignation

A member of the committee may resign by notice m writing delivered to the administrator.
