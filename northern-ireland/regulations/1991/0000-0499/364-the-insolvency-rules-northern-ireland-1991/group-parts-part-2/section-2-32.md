# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 2.32

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2.32. Reports and notices under Articles 35 and 37

Any report or notice by the administrator of the result of a creditors' meeting held under Article 35 or 37 shall have annexed to it details of the proposals which were considered by the meeting and of the revisions and modifications to the proposals which were so considered.
