# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 2.13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2.13. Discharge of administration order

Where an administration order is discharged, the court shall send notice of the making of the order of discharge to the Enforcement of Judgments Office.
