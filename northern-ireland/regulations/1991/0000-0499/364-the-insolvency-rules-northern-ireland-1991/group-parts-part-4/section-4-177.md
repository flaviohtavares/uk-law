# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 4.177

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.177. Expenses of members, etc.

The liquidator shall defray out of the assets, in the prescribed order of priority, any reasonable travelling expenses directly incurred by members of the liquidation committee or their representatives in respect of their attendance at the committee's meetings, or otherwise on the committee's business.
