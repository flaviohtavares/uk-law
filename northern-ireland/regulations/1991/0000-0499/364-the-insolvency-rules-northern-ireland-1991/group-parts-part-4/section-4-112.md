# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 4.112

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.112. Authentication of liquidator's appointment

A copy of the certificate of the liquidator's appointment or (as the case may be) a sealed copy of the court's order, may in any proceedings be adduced as proof that the person appointed is duly authorised to exercise the powers and perform the duties of liquidator in the company's winding up.
