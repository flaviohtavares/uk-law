# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 4.215

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.215. Making and enforcement of the call

(1) Notice of the call shall be given to each of the contributories concerned, and shall specify—[^m00131]

- (a) the amount or balance due from him in respect of it, and
- (b) whether the call is made with the sanction of the court or the liquidation committee.

(2) Payment of the amount due from any contributory may be enforced by order of the court.[^m00132]
