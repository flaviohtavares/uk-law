# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 4.017

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.017. List of appearances

(1) The petitioner shall prepare for the court a list of the persons (if any) who have given notice under Rule 4.016, specifying their names and addresses and (if known to him) their respective solicitors.[^m00047]

(2) Against the name of each creditor in the list it shall be stated whether his intention is to support the petition, or to oppose it.

(3) On the day appointed for the hearing of the petition, a copy of the list shall be handed to the court before the commencement of the hearing.

(4) If any leave is given under Rule 4.016(5), the petitioner shall add to the list the same particulars in respect of the person to whom leave has been given.
