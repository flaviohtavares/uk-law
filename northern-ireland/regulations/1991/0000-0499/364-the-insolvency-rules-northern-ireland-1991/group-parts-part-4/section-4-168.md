# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 4.168

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.168. Resignation

A member of the liquidation committee may resign by notice in writing delivered to the liquidator.
