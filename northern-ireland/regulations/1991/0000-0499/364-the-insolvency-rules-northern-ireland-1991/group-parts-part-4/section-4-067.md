# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 4.067

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.067. Venue

(1) In fixing the venue for a meeting of creditors or contributories, the convener shall have regard to the convenience of the persons (other than whoever is to be chairman) who are invited to attend.

(2) Meetings shall in all cases be summoned for commencement between 10.00 and 16.00 hours on a business day, unless the court otherwise directs.

(3) With every notice summoning a meeting of creditors or contributories there shall be sent out forms of proxy.[^m00064]
