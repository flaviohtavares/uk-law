# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 4.028

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.028. Notice of appointment

(1) Where a provisional liquidator has been appointed the court shall forthwith give notice of the fact to the official receiver.[^m00052]

(2) A copy of that notice shall at the same time be sent by the court to the provisional liquidator where he is not the official receiver.
