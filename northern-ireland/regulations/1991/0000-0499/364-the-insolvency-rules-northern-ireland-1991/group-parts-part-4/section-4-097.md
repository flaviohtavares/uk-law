# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 4.097

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.097. Debt in foreign currency

(1) For the purpose of proving a debt incurred or payable in a currency other than sterling, the amount of the debt shall be converted into sterling at the official exchange rate prevailing on the date when the company went into liquidation.

(2) “The official exchange rate” is the middle market rate at the Bank of England, as published for the date in question. In the absence of any such published rate, it is such rate as the court determines.
