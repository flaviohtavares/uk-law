# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 4.084

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.084. Cost of proving

(1) Subject to paragraph (3), every creditor bears the cost of proving his own debt, including such as may be incurred in providing documents or evidence under Rule 4.081 (3) or 4.082-CVL.

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), costs incurred by the liquidator in estimating the quantum of a debt under Rule 4.092 (debts not bearing a certain value) are payable out of the assets, as an expense of the liquidation.

(3) Paragraphs (1) and (2) apply unless the court otherwise orders.
