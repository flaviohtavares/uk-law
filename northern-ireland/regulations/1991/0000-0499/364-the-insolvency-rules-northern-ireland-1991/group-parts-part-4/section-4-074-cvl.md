# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 4.074-CVL

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.074-CVL. Chairman's discretion to allow vote

At a creditors' meeting, the chairman may allow a creditor to vote, notwithstanding that he has failed to comply with Rule 4.073(1)(a), if satisfied that the failure was due to circumstances beyond the creditor's control.
