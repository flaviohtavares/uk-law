# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 4.048

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.048. Meaning of “creditors”

Any reference in this Chapter to creditors is to creditors of the company who are known to the official receiver or (as the case may be) the liquidator or, where a statement of the company's affairs has been submitted, are identified in the statement.
