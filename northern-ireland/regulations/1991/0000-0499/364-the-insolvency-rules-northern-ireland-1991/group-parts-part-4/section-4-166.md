# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 4.166

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.166. Quorum

(1) A meeting of the committee is duly constituted if due notice of it has been given to all the members, and at least two creditor members are present or represented. (NO CVL APPLICATION)

(2-CVL) A meeting of the committee is duly constituted if due notice of it has been given to all the members, and at least two members are present or represented.
