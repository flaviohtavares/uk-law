# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 4.103

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.103. Redemption by liquidator

(1) The liquidator may at any time give notice to a creditor whose debt is secured that he proposes, at the expiration of 28 days from the date of the notice, to redeem the security at the value put upon it in the creditor's proof.

(2) The creditor then has 21 days (or such longer period as the liquidator may allow) in which, if he so wishes, to exercise his right to re-value his security (with the leave of the court, where Rule 4.101 (2) applies).

(3) If the creditor re-values his security, the liquidator may only redeem at the new value.

(4) If the liquidator redeems the security, the cost of transferring it is payable out of the assets.

(5) A secured creditor may at any time, by a notice in writing, call on the liquidator to elect whether he will or will not exercise his power to redeem the security at the value then placed on it; and the liquidator then has 6 months in which to exercise the power or determine not to exercise it.
