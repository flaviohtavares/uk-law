# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 4.018

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.018. Affidavit by company in opposition

(1) If the company intends to oppose the petition, its affidavit in opposition shall be filed in court not less than 7 days before the hearing date.

(2) A copy of the affidavit shall be sent by the company to the petitioner, forthwith after filing.
