# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 4.108-CVL

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.108-CVL. Power to fill vacancy in office of liquidator

Where a vacancy in the office of liquidator occurs in the manner mentioned in Article 90, a meeting of creditors to fill the vacancy may be convened by any creditor or, if there were more liquidators than one, by the continuing liquidators.
