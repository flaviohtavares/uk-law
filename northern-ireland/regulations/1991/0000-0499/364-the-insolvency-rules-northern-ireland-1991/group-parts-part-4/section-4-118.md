# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 4.118

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.118. Leave to resign granted by the court

(1) If at a creditors' meeting summoned to accept the liquidator's resignation it is resolved that it be not accepted, the court may, on the liquidator's application, make an order giving him leave to resign.[^m00079]

(2) The court's order may include such provision as it thinks fit with respect to matters arising in connection with the resignation, and shall determine the date from which the liquidator's release is effective.

(3) The court shall send 3 sealed copies of the order to the liquidator, who shall forthwith send one of the copies to the official receiver and one to the registrar. (NO CVL APPLICATION)

(4-CVL) The court shall send 2 sealed copies of the order to the liquidator, who shall forthwith send one of them to the registrar.[^m00080]

(5) On sending notice of his resignation to the court, the liquidator shall send a copy of it to the official receiver. (NO CVL APPLICATION)[^m00081]
