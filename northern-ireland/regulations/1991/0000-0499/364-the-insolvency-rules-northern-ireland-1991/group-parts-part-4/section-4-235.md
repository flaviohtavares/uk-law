# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 4.235

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.235. Procedure following appeal under Article 168(4) or 169(3)

Following an appeal under Article 168(4) or 169(3) (against a decision of the Department under the applicable Article) the court shall send 2 sealed copies of its order to the person in whose favour the appeal was determined; and that party shall send one of the copies to the registrar to comply with Article 168(5) or, as the case may be, 169(4).[^m00142]
