# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 4.201

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.201. Application by interested party under Article 152(4)

Where, in the case of any property, application is made to the liquidator by an interested party under Article 152(4) (request for decision whether the property is to be disclaimed or not), the application—

- (a) shall be delivered to the liquidator personally or by registered post, and
- (b) shall be made in the form known as “notice to elect”, or a substantially similar form.[^m00127]
