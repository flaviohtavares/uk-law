# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 4.052

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.052. Winding up stayed

(NO CVL APPLICATION)

(1) If proceedings in the winding up are stayed by order of the court, any duty of the official receiver to send reports under Rules 4.047 to 4.051 ceases.

(2) Where the court grants a stay, it may include in its order such requirements on the company as it thinks fit with a view to bringing the stay to the notice of creditors and contributories.
