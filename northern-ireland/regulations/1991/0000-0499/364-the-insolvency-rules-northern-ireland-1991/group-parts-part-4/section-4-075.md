# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 4.075

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.075. Entitlement to vote (contributories)

At a meeting of contributories, voting rights are as at a general meeting of the company, subject to any provision in the articles affecting entitlement to vote, either generally or at a time when the company is in liquidation.
