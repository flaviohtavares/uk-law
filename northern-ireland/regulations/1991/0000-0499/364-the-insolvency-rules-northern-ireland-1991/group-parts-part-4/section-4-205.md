# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 4.205

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.205. Preliminary

The duties of the court with regard to the settling of the list of contributories are, by virtue of the Rules, delegated to the liquidator.
