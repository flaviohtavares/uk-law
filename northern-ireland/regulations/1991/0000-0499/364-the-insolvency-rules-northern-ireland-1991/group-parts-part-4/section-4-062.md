# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 4.062

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.062. The chairman at meetings

(NO CVL APPLICATION)

(1) This Rule applies both to a meeting of creditors and to a meeting of contributories.

(2) Where the convener of the meeting is the official receiver, he, or a person nominated by him, shall be chairman.

(3) A nomination under paragraph (2) shall be in writing, unless the nominee is another official receiver or a deputy official receiver.

(4) Where the convener is other than the official receiver, the chairman shall be he, or a person nominated in writing by him.

(5) A person nominated under paragraph (4) must be either—

- (a) one who is qualified to act as an insolvency practitioner in relation to the company, or
- (b) an employee of the liquidator or his firm who is experienced in insolvency matters.
