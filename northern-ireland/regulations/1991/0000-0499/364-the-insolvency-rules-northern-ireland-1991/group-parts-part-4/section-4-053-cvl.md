# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 4.053-CVL

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.053-CVL. Information to creditors and contributories

The liquidator shall, within 28 days of a meeting held under Article 81 or 84, send to creditors and contributories of the company—

- (a) a copy or summary of the statement of affairs, and
- (b) a report of the proceedings at the meeting.
