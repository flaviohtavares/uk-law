# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 4.037-CVL

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.037-CVL. Statement of affairs

(1) This Rule applies with respect to the statement of affairs made out by the liquidator under Article 81(3) or (as the case may be) by the directors under Article 85(1).[^m00056][^m00057]

(2) Where it is made out by the liquidator, the statement of affairs shall be delivered by him to the registrar within 7 days after the creditors' meeting summoned under Article 81(2).[^m00058]

(3) Where it is made out by the directors under Article 85(1) the statement of affairs shall be delivered by them to the liquidator in office following the creditors' meeting summoned under Article 84 forthwith after that meeting has been held; and he shall, within 7 days, deliver it to the registrar.

(4) A statement of affairs under Article 85(1) may be made up to a date not more than 14 days before that on which the resolution for voluntary winding up is passed by the company.
