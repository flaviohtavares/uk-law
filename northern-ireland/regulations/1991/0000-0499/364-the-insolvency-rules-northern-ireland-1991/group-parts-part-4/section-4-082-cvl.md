# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 4.082-CVL

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.082-CVL. Particulars of creditor's claim

The liquidator, or the convener or chairman of any meeting, may, if he thinks it necessary for the purpose of clarifying or substantiating the whole or any part of a creditor's claim made in his proof, call for details of any matter specified in sub-paragraphs (a) to (h) of Rule 4.081(1), or for the production to him of such documentary or other evidence as he may require.
