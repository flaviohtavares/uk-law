# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 4.159

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.159. Preliminary

(NO CVL APPLICATION)

For the purposes of this Chapter—

- (a) an “insolvent winding up” is where the company is being wound up on the grounds which include inability to pay its debts, and
- (b) a “solvent winding up” is where the company is being wound up on grounds which do not include that one.
