# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 4.119

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.119. Advertisement of resignation

Where a new liquidator is appointed in place of one who has resigned, the former shall, in giving notice of his appointment, state that his predecessor has resigned and (if it be the case) that he has been given his release.
