# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 4.200

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.200. Duty to keep court informed

The liquidator shall notify the court from time to time as to the persons to whom he has sent or given copies of the notice of disclaimer under Rules 4.198 and 4.199, giving their names and addresses, and the nature of their respective interests.
