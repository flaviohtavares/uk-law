# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 4.124-CVL

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.124-CVL. Procedure on removal

Where the creditors have resolved that the liquidator be removed, the chairman of the creditors' meeting shall forthwith—

- (a) if at the meeting another liquidator was not appointed, send the certificate of the liquidator's removal to the registrar, and[^m00085]
- (b) otherwise, deliver the certificate to the new liquidator, who shall send it to the registrar.
