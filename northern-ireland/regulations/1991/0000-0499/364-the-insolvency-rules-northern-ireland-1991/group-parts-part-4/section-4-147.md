# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 4.147

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.147. Appointment by the court

(1) This Rule applies where the liquidator is appointed by the court under Article 94.

(2) The court's order shall not issue unless and until the person appointed has filed in court a statement to the effect that he is an insolvency practitioner, duly qualified under the Order to be the liquidator, and that he consents so to act.[^m00101][^m00102]

(3) Thereafter, the court shall send a sealed copy of the order to the liquidator, whose appointment takes effect from the date of the order.

(4) Not later than 28 days from his appointment, the liquidator shall give notice of it to all creditors of the company of whom he is aware in that period.
