# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 4.071

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.071. Chairman of meeting as proxy-holder

Where the chairman at a meeting of creditors or contributories holds a proxy which requires him to vote for a particular resolution, and no other person proposes that resolution—

- (a) he shall himself propose it, unless he considers that there is good reason for not doing so, and
- (b) if he does not propose it, he shall forthwith after the meeting notify his principal of the reason why not.
