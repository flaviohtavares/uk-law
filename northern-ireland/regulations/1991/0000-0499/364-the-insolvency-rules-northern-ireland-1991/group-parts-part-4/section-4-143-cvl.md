# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 4.143-CVL

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.143-CVL. Vacation of office on making of winding-up order

Where the liquidator vacates office in consequence of the court making a winding-up order against the company, Rule 4.129-CVL applies as regards his obtaining his release, as if he had been removed by the court.
