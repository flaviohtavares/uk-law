# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 4.059-CVL

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.059-CVL. Effect of adjournment of company meeting

Where a company meeting at which a resolution for voluntary winding up is to be proposed is adjourned, any resolution passed at a meeting under Article 84 held before the holding of the adjourned company meeting only has effect on and from the passing by the company of a resolution for winding up.
