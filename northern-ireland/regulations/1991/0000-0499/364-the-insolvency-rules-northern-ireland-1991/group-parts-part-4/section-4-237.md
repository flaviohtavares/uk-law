# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 4.237

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.237. Application for leave under Article 180(3)

When considering an application for leave under Article 180, the court may call on the liquidator, or any former liquidator, of the liquidating company for a report of the circumstances in which that company became insolvent, and the extent (if any) of the applicant's apparent responsibility for its doing so.
