# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 4.003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.003. Time-limits

Where by any provision of the Order or the Rules about winding up, the time for doing anything is limited, the court may extend the time, either before or after it has expired, on such terms, if any, as it thinks fit.
