# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 6.163

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.163. Committee's functions vested in Department

(1) At any time when the functions of the creditors' committee are vested in the Department under Article 275(1) or (2), requirements of the Order or the Rules about notices to be given, or reports to be made, to the committee by the trustee do not apply, otherwise than as enabling the committee to require a report as to any matter.

(2) Where the committee's functions are so vested under Article 275(2), they may be exercised by the official receiver.
