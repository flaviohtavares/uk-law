# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 6.187

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.187. Action to follow making of order

(1) Where the court makes an income payments order, a sealed copy of the order shall, forthwith after it is made, be sent by the trustee to the bankrupt.[^m00226][^m00227]

(2) If the order is made under Article 283(3)(b), a sealed copy of the order shall also be sent by the trustee to the person to whom the order is directed.
