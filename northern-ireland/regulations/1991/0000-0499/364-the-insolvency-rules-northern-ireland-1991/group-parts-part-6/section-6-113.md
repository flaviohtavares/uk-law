# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 6.113

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.113. Surrender for non-disclosure

(1) If a secured creditor omits to disclose his security in his proof of debt, he shall surrender his security for the general benefit of creditors, unless the court, on application by him, relieves him from the effect of this Rule on the ground that the omission was inadvertent or the result of honest mistake.

(2) If the court grants that relief, it may require or allow the creditor's proof of debt to be amended, on such terms as may be just.
