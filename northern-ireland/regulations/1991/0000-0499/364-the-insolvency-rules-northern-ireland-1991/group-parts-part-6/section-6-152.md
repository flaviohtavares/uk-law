# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 6.152

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.152. Quorum

A meeting of the committee is duly constituted if due notice of it has been given to all the members and at least two of the members are present or represented.
