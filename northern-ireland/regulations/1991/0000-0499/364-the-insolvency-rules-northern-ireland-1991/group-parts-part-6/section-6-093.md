# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 6.093

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.093. Bankruptcy on petition under Article 238 (1)(d)

Where a bankruptcy order is made against a solicitor on a petition under Article 238(1)(d), the Law Society of Northern Ireland may be represented at any meeting of creditors and is to be given or sent any notice or report under the Order or the Rules which is required or authorised to be given or sent to creditors.
