# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 6.068

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.068. Submission and filing of accounts

(1) The accounts to be furnished under Rule 6.067 shall, if the official receiver so requires, be verified by affidavit, and (whether or not so verified) delivered to him within 21 days of the request under Rule 6.067, or such longer period as he may allow.

(2) Two copies of the accounts and (where required) the affidavit shall be delivered by the bankrupt to the official receiver, who shall file one copy in court (with the affidavit, if any).
