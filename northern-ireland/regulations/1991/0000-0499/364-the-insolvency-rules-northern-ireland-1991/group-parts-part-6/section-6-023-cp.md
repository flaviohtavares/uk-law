# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 6.023-CP

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.023-CP. Non-appearance of petitioning creditor

If the petitioning creditor fails to appear on the hearing of the petition, no subsequent petition against the same debtor, either alone or jointly with any other person, shall be presented by the same creditor in respect of the same debt, without the leave of the court.
