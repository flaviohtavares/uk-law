# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 6.227

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.227. Rescission of bankruptcy order against solicitor

Where a bankruptcy order made against a solicitor is rescinded, the court shall forthwith send to the Law Society of Northern Ireland a copy of the order that it has made.
