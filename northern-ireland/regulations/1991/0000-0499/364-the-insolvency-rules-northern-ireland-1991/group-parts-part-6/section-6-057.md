# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 6.057

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.057. The statement of affairs

The bankrupt's statement of affairs shall be in Form 6.36, and contain all the particulars required by that form.[^m00183]
