# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 6.127

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.127. Court's power to regulate meeting under Rule 6.126

Where a meeting under Rule 6.126 is to be held, or is proposed to be summoned, the court may on the application of any creditor give directions as to the mode of summoning it, the sending out and return of forms of proxy, the conduct of the meeting, and any other matter which appears to the court to require regulation or control.
