# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 6.075

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.075. General rule as to reporting

(1) The court may, on the official receiver's application, relieve him of any duty imposed on him by this Chapter, or authorise him to carry out the duty in a way other than there required.

(2) In considering whether to act under paragraph (1), the court shall have regard to the cost of carrying out the duty, to the amount of the funds available in the estate, and to the extent of the interest of creditors or any particular class of them.
