# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 6.225

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.225. Delivery up to later trustee

The existing trustee shall, as and when requested by the trustee for the purposes of the later bankruptcy, deliver up to the latter all such property and money as is in his custody or under his control in pursuance of Rule 6.224.
