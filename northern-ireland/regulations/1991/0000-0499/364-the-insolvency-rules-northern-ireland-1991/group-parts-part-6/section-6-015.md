# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 6.015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.015. Death of debtor before service

If the debtor dies before service of the petition, the court may order service to be effected on his personal representatives, or on such other persons as it thinks fit.
