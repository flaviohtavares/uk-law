# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 6.147

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.147. Membership of creditors' committee

(1) The creditors' committee shall consist of at least three, and not more than five, members.

(2) All the members of the committee must be creditors of the bankrupt; and any creditor (other than one who is fully secured) may be a member, so long as—

- (a) he has lodged a proof of his debt, and
- (b) his proof has neither been wholly disallowed for voting purposes, nor wholly rejected for the purposes of distribution or dividend.

(3) A body corporate may be a member of the committee, but it cannot act as such otherwise than by a representative appointed under Rule 6.153.
