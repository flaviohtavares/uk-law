# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 6.199

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.199. Expenses of getting in property for the estate

Any expenses incurred by the trustee in acquiring title to after acquired property shall be paid out of the estate, in the prescribed order of priority.
