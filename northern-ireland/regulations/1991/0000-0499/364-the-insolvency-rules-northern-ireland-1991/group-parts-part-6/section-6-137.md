# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 6.137

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.137. Recourse of trustee to meeting of creditors

If the trustee's remuneration has been fixed by the creditors' committee, and he considers the rate or amount to be insufficient, he may request that it be increased by resolution of the creditors.
