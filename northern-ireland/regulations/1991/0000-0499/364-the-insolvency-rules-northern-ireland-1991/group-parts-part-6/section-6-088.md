# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 6.088

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.088. Suspension of meeting

Once only in the course of any meeting, the chairman may, in his discretion and without an adjournment, declare the meeting suspended for any period up to one hour.
