# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 6.184

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.184. Purchase of replacement property

(1) A purchase of replacement property under Article 281(3) may be made either before or after the realisation by the trustee of the value of the property vesting in him under that Article.

(2) The trustee is under no obligation, by virtue of Article 281, to apply funds to the purchase of a replacement for property vested in him, unless and until he has sufficient funds in the estate for that purpose.
