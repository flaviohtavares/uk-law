# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 6.185

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.185. Money provided in lieu of sale

(1) Paragraph (2) applies where a third party proposes to the trustee that he (the former) should provide the estate with a sum of money enabling the bankrupt to be left in possession of property which would otherwise be made to vest in the trustee under Article 281.

(2) The trustee may accept that proposal, if satisfied that it is a reasonable one, and that the estate will benefit to the extent of the value of the property in question less the cost of a reasonable replacement.
