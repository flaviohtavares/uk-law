# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 6.217

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.217. Order of discharge on application

(1) An order of the court under Article 254(2)(b) (discharge absolutely) or (c) (discharge subject to conditions with respect to income or property) shall bear the date on which it is made, but does not take effect until such time as it is drawn up by the court.[^m00236]

(2) The order then has effect retrospectively to the date on which it was made.

(3) Copies of any order made by the court on an application by the bankrupt for discharge under Article 254 shall be sent by the court to the bankrupt, the trustee and the official receiver.
