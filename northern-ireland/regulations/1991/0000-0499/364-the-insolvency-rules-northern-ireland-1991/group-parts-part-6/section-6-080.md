# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 6.080

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.080. The chairman at a meeting

(1) Where the convener of a meeting is the official receiver, he, or a person nominated by him, shall be chairman.

(2) A nomination under paragraph (1) shall be in writing, unless the nominee is another official receiver or a deputy official receiver.

(3) Where the convener is other than the official receiver, the chairman shall be he, or a person nominated by him in writing to act.

(4) A person nominated under paragraph (3) must be either—

- (a) one who is qualified to act as an insolvency practitioner in relation to the bankrupt, or
- (b) an employee of the trustee or his firm who is experienced in insolvency matters.
