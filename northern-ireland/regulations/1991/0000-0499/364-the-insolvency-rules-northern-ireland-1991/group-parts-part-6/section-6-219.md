# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 6.219

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.219. Deferment of issue and advertisement of order pending appeal

An order made by the court on an application by the bankrupt for discharge under Article 254 shall not be issued, otherwise than for the purposes of an appeal, and shall not be advertised until the time allowed for appealing has expired or, if an appeal is entered, until the appeal has been determined.
