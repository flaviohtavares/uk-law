# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 6.210

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.210. Annulment under Article 235 or 256(3)(a)

Rules 6.203, 6.205, 6.207 and 6.209 apply to an application for annulment under Article 235 or 256(3) as they apply to such an application under Article 256(1)(a).
