# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 6.207

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.207. The hearing

(1) The trustee shall attend the hearing of the application.

(2) The official receiver, if he is not the trustee, may attend, but is not required to do so unless he has filed a report under Rule 6.204.
