# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 6.065

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.065. Preliminary

This Section applies with respect to the statement of affairs required in the case of a person petitioning for a bankruptcy order to be made against him and the further disclosure which is required of him in that case.
