# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 6.131

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.131. Advertisement of resignation or removal

Where a new trustee is appointed in place of one who has resigned or been removed, the new trustee shall, in the advertisement of his appointment, state that his predecessor has resigned or, as the case may be, been removed and (if it be the case) that he has been given his release.
