# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 6.016-CP

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.016-CP. Security for costs (Article 242(2) only)

(1) This Rule applies where the debt in respect of which the petition is presented is for a liquidated sum payable at some future time, it being claimed in the petition that the debtor appears to have no reasonable prospect of being able to pay it.

(2) The petitioning creditor may, on the debtor's application, be ordered to give security for the debtor's costs.

(3) The nature and amount of the security to be ordered is in the court's discretion.

(4) If an order is made under this Rule, there shall be no hearing of the petition until the whole amount of the security has been given.
