# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 6.116

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.116. Realisation of security by creditor

If a creditor who has valued his security subsequently realises it (whether or not at the instance of the trustee)—

- (a) the net amount realised shall be substituted for the value previously put by the creditor on the security, and
- (b) that amount shall be treated in all respects as an amended valuation made by him.
