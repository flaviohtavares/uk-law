# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 6.050

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.050. Order of appointment

(1) The order appointing the interim receiver shall state the nature and give a short description of the property of which the person appointed is to take possession, and the duties to be performed by him in relation to the debtor's affairs.[^m00182]

(2) The court shall, forthwith after the order is made, send 2 sealed copies of it to the person appointed interim receiver (one of which shall be sent by him forthwith to the debtor).
