# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 6.111

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.111. Debt payable at future time

A creditor may prove for a debt of which payment was not yet due at the date of the bankruptcy order, but subject to Rule 11.13 (adjustment of dividend where payment made before time).
