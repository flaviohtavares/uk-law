# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 6.221

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.221. Bankrupt's debts surviving discharge

Discharge does not release the bankrupt from any obligation arising under a confiscation order made under Article 4 or 5 of the Criminal Justice (Confiscation) (Northern Ireland) Order 1990 [^f00028].
