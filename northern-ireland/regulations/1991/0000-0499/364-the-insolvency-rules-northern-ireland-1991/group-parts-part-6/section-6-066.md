# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 6.066

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.066. Contents of statement

The statement of affairs required by Rule 6.037 to accompany the debtor's petition shall be in Form 6.31, and contain all the particulars required by that form.[^m00184]
