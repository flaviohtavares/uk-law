# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 6.097

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.097. Claim established by affidavit

(1) The trustee may, if he thinks it necessary, require a claim of debt to be verified by affidavit, for which purpose there shall be used the form known as “affidavit of debt”.[^m00193]

(2) An affidavit may be required notwithstanding that a proof of debt has already been lodged.

(3) The affidavit may be sworn before an official receiver or before an officer of the Department or of the court duly authorised in that behalf.
