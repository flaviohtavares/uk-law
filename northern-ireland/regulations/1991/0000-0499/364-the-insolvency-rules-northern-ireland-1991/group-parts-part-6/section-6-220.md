# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 6.220

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.220. Costs under this Chapter

In no case do any costs arising under this Chapter fall on the official receiver personally.
