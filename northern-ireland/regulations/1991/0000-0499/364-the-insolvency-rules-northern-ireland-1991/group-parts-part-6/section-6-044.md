# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 6.044

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.044. Expenses of voluntary arrangement

Where a bankruptcy order is made on a debtor's petition and there is at the time of the petition in force for the debtor a voluntary arrangement, any expenses properly incurred as expenses of the administration of the voluntary arrangement in question shall be a first charge on the bankrupt's estate.
