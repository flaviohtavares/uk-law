# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 6.048

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.048. Revocation of certificate for summary administration

(1) The court may under Article 249(3) revoke a certificate for summary administration, either of its own motion or on the application of the official receiver.[^m00181]

(2) If the official receiver applies for the certificate to be revoked, he shall give at least 14 days' notice of the application to the bankrupt.

(3) If the court revokes the certificate, it shall forthwith give notice to the official receiver and the bankrupt.

(4) If at the time of revocation there is a trustee other than the official receiver, the official receiver shall send a copy of the court's notice to him.
