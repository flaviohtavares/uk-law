# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 6.098

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.098. Cost of proving

(1) Subject to paragraphs (2) and (3), every creditor bears the cost of proving his own debt, including such as may be incurred in providing documents or evidence under Rule 6.096(3).

(2) Costs incurred by the trustee in estimating the value of a bankruptcy debt under Article 295(3) (debts not bearing a certain value) fall on the estate, as an expense of the bankruptcy.

(3) Paragraphs (1) and (2) apply unless the court otherwise orders.
