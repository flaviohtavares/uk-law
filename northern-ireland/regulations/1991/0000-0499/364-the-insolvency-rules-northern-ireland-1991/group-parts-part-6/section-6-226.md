# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 6.226

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.226. Existing trustee's expenses

Any expenses incurred by the existing trustee in compliance with Article 308(1) and this Chapter shall be defrayed out of, and are a charge on, all such property and money as is referred to in Article 307(3), whether in the hands of the existing trustee or of the trustee for the purposes of the later bankruptcy.
