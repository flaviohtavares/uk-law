# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 6.055

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.055. Termination of appointment

(1) The appointment of the interim receiver may be terminated by the court on the application of—

- (a) the interim receiver himself,
- (b) the official receiver,
- (c) the debtor,
- (d) any creditor, or
- (e) on a petition under Article 238 (1)(d), the petitioner.

(2) If the interim receiver's appointment terminates, in consequence of the dismissal of the bankruptcy petition or otherwise, the court may give such directions as it thinks fit with respect to the accounts of his administration and any other matters which it thinks appropriate.
