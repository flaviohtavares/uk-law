# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 6.223

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.223. Scope of this Chapter

(1) This Chapter relates to the manner in which, in the case of a second bankruptcy, the trustee in the earlier bankruptcy is to deal with property and money to which Article 307(3) applies, until there is a trustee of the estate in the later bankruptcy.

(2) “**The earlier bankruptcy**”, “**the later bankruptcy**” and “**the existing trustee**” have the meanings given by Article 307(1).
