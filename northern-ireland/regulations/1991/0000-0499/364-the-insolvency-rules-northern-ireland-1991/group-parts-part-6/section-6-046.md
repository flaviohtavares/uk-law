# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 6.046

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.046. Certificate for summary administration

(1) If the court under Article 249 issues a certificate for the summary administration of the bankrupt's estate, the certificate may be included in the bankruptcy order.[^m00180]

(2) If the certificate is not so included, the court shall forthwith send copies of it to the official receiver and the bankrupt.
