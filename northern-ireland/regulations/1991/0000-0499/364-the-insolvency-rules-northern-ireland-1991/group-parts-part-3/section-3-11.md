# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 3.11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3.11. The chairman at the meeting

(1) The chairman at the creditors' meeting shall be the administrative receiver, or a person nominated by him in writing to act in his place.

(2) A person so nominated must be either—

- (a) one who is qualified to act as an insolvency practitioner in relation to the company, or
- (b) an employee of the administrative receiver or bis firm who is experienced in insolvency matters.
