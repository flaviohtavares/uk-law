# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 3.14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3.14. Adjournment

(1) The creditors' meeting shall not be adjourned, even if no quorum is present, unless the chairman decides that it is desirable; and in that case he shall adjourn it to such date, time and place as he thinks fit.

(2) Rule 3.10(1) and (2) applies, with necessary modifications, to any adjourned meeting.

(3) If there is no quorum, and the meeting is not adjourned, it is deemed to have been duly summoned and held.
