# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 3.18

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3.18. Functions and meetings of the committee

(1) The creditors' committee shall assist the administrative receiver in discharging his functions, and act in relation to him in such manner as may be agreed from time to time.

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), meetings of the committee shall be held when and where determined by the administrative receiver.

(3) The administrative receiver shall call a first meeting of the committee not later than 3 months after its establishment; and thereafter he shall call a meeting—

- (a) if requested by a member of the committee or his representative (the meeting then to be held within 21 days of the request being received by the administrative receiver), and
- (b) for a specified date, if the committee has previously resolved that a meeting be held on that date.

(4) The administrative receiver shall give 7 days' written notice of the venue of any meeting to every member (or his representative designated for that purpose), unless in any case the requirement of notice has been waived by or on behalf of any member.

(5) For the purpose of paragraph (4), waiver may be signified either at or before the meeting.
