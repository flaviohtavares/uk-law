# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 3.01

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3.01. Scope of this Part

(1) Rule 3.02 applies to the appointment of a receiver or manager of a company's property under powers contained in an instrument.

(2) Rules 3.02 to 3.39 apply to an administrative receivership.
