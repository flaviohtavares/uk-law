# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 3.22

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3.22. Resignation

A member of the committee may resign by notice in writing delivered to the administrative receiver.
