# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 3.08

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3.08. Expenses of statement of affairs

(1) A deponent making the statement of affairs and affidavit shall be allowed, and paid by the administrative receiver out of his receipts, any expenses incurred by the deponent in so doing which the administrative receiver thinks reasonable.

(2) Any decision by the administrative receiver under this Rule is subject to appeal to the court.

(3) Nothing in this Rule relieves a deponent from any obligation with respect to the preparation, verification and submission of the statement of affairs, or to the provision of information to the administrative receiver.
