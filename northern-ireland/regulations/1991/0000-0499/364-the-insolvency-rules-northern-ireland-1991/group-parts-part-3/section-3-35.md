# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 3.35

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3.35. Receiver deceased

If the administrative receiver dies, the person by whom he was appointed shall, forthwith on his becoming aware of the death, give notice of it to—

- (a) the registrar,[^m00035]
- (b) the company or, if it is in liquidation, the liquidator, and
- (c) in any case, to the members of the creditors' committee (if any).
