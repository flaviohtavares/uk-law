# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 10.1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10.1. Appointment of official receivers

Judicial notice shall be taken of the appointment under Articles 355 to 357 of official receivers and deputy official receivers.
