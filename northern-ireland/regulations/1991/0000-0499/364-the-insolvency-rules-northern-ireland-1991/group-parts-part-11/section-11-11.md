# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 11.11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 11.11. Assignment of right to dividend

(1) If a person entitled to a dividend gives notice to the responsible insolvency practitioner that he wishes the dividend to be paid to another person, or that he has assigned his entitlement to another person, the responsible insolvency practitioner shall pay the dividend to that other accordingly.

(2) A notice given under this Rule must specify the name and address of the person to whom payment is to be made.
