# The Insolvency Rules (Northern Ireland) 1991, section 11.07

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 11.07. Notice of no, or no further, dividend

If the responsible insolvency practitioner gives notice to creditors that he is unable to declare any dividend or (as the case may be) any further dividend, the notice shall contain a statement to the effect either—

- (a) that no funds have been realised, or
- (b) that the funds realised have already been distributed or used or allocated for defraying the expenses of administration.
