# Insider Dealing (Recognised Stock Exchange) Order (Northern Ireland) 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 52

COMPANIES

Insider Dealing (Recognised Stock Exchange) Order (Northern Ireland) 1991

Made

19th February 1991

Coming into operation

1st April 1991

The Department of Economic Development, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 2(2) of the Company Securities (Insider Dealing) (Northern Ireland) Order 1986[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Insider Dealing (Recognised Stock Exchange) Order (Northern Ireland) 1991 and shall come into operation on 1st April 1991.

## 2. Recognised stock exchanges

The Department of Economic Development hereby declares the investment exchanges known as:

- (a) NASDAQ, which is an unincorporated association consisting of the National Association of Securities Dealers, Inc., NASDAQ Inc., NASD Market Services Inc. and NASDAQ International Ltd., and
- (b) OM London Limited

to be recognised stock exchanges for the purposes of the Company Securities (Insider Dealing) (Northern Ireland) Order 1986.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Economic Development on 19th February 1991.

A.H. McAlister

Assistant Secretary

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order declares the investment exchanges known as NASDAQ and OM London Limited to be recognised stock exchanges for the purposes of the Company Securities (Insider Dealing) (Northern Ireland) Order 1986. That Order provides, amongst other matters, that it shall be an offence to deal in securities on a recognised stock exchange whilst in possession of unpublished price sensitive information (as defined in Article 4) in relation to those securities, or to counsel or procure such dealing.
