# Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) (Heavy Goods and Public Service Vehicles) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1991

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1991 No. 416

ROAD TRAFFIC AND VEHICLES

Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) (Heavy Goods and Public Service Vehicles) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1991

Made

5th September 1991

Coming into operation

14th October 1991

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 2(2)[^f00001] and paragraph 7(2)(*b*) of Part I of Schedule 2 to the Road Traffic (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 1991[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) (Heavy Goods and Public Service Vehicles) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1991 and shall come into operation on 14th October 1991.

## 2. Regulations amended

In regulation 5(2)(a) (custody and production of licences) of the Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) (Heavy Goods and Public Service Vehicles) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1991[^f00003] for “7 days” substitute “5 days”.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Economic Development on 5th September 1991.

(L.S.)

Trevor Pearson

Assistant Secretary

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

Regulation 5(2)(a) of the Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) (Heavy Goods and Public Service Vehicles) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1991, provides that any person unable to produce his driving licence for a heavy goods vehicle or public service vehicle when requited by a policeman to do so, may produce that licence for examination at a police station.

These Regulations reduce from 7 days to 5 days, the period within which the licence must be produced at the police station.
