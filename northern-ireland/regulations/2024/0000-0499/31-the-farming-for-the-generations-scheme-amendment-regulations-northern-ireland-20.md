# The Farming for the Generations Scheme (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2024

> Text as at 2024-03-06.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

2024 No. 31

Agriculture

The Farming for the Generations Scheme (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2024

Made

6th March 2024

Coming into operation

6th March 2024

The Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs[^f00001] makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 5 and 6 of the Agriculture Act (Northern Ireland) 1949[^f00002].

These Regulations are made with the approval of the Department of Finance[^f00003] under sections 5 and 6 of the Agriculture Act (Northern Ireland) 1949.

## 1. Citation and commencement[^c-3a5ce791e8c14e7e702312ae2eefb371]

These Regulations may be cited as the Farming for the Generations Scheme (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2024 and shall come into operation on 6th March 2024.

## 2. Amendment to the Farming for the Generations Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2024[^c-050da06e7bc4fa5d268e0a0618927d78]

(1) The Farming for the Generations Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2024[^f00004] are amended as follows.

(2) Omit regulation 9.

(3) In regulation 11, omit paragraph (1)(c).

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs on 6th March 2024

(L.S.)

Rosemary Agnew

A senior officer of the

Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs

The Department of Finance approves the making of these Regulations.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance on 6th March 2024

(L.S.)

Patrick Neeson

A senior officer of the

Department of Finance

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Farming for the Generations Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2024 to omit regulation 9 and amend regulation 11 to remove powers of inspection.
