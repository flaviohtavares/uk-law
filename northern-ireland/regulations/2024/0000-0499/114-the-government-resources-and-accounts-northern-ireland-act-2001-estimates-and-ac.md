# The Government Resources and Accounts (Northern Ireland) Act 2001 (Estimates and Accounts) (Designation of Bodies) Order 2024

> Text as at 2025-01-06.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

**May not be in force at this date. Check the source.**

2024 No. 114

Government Resources And Accounts

The Government Resources and Accounts (Northern Ireland) Act 2001 (Estimates and Accounts) (Designation of Bodies) Order 2024

Made

16th May 2024

Coming into operation

6th June 2024

The Department of Finance [^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 8A and 8B of the Government Resources and Accounts Act (Northern Ireland) 2001 [^f00002], makes the following Order.

In accordance with section 8A(5) of the Act the Department of Finance have consulted, where they think it appropriate, with the Treasury.

## 1. Title and commencement[^key-1af941435c57da83e2e664a7e77985b8]

This Order may be cited as the Government Resources and Accounts (Northern Ireland) Act 2001 (Estimates and Accounts) (Designation of Bodies) Order 2024 and shall come into operation on 6<sup>th</sup> June 2024.

## 2. Amendments to the principal Order[^key-a5d1b00a38e361353ba9f5c9d1e29bea]

A body which is listed in a Part of the Schedule to this Order is a designated body for the purposes of sections 8A and 8B of the Government Resources and Accounts (Northern Ireland) Act 2001—

- (a) in relation to the department named in the heading to that Part, and
- (b) for the financial year which ends on 31st March 2025.

**May not be in force at this date. Check the source.**

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance on 16<sup>th</sup> May 2024

Barry Armstrong

A senior officer of the

Department of Finance

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

This Order designates specified central government bodies in relation to named government departments for the purpose of this departments’ supply estimates and resource accounts.

The designations have effect for the financial year that ends on 31<sup>st</sup> March 2025.

An impact assessment has not been prepared for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen. An Explanatory Memorandum is available alongside the instrument on www.legislation.gov.uk.
