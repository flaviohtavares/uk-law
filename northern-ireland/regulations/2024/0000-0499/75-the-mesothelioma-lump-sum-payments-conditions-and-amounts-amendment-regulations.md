# The Mesothelioma Lump Sum Payments (Conditions and Amounts) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2024

> Text as at 2024-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

Regulations made by the Department for Communities and laid before the Assembly under section 10(2) of the Mesothelioma, etc., Act (Northern Ireland) 2008 for approval of the Assembly before the expiration of six months from the date of their coming into operation

2024 No. 75

Social Security

The Mesothelioma Lump Sum Payments (Conditions and Amounts) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2024

Made

25th March 2024

Coming into operation

1st April 2024

The Department for Communities makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1(3), 9(1) and (2)(a) of the Mesothelioma, etc., Act (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00001].

## 1. Citation, commencement and application[^c-f951380132de015fba53610e291fe1a3]

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Mesothelioma Lump Sum Payments (Conditions and Amounts) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2024 and shall come into operation on 1st April 2024.

(2) In regulation 2—

- (a) paragraph (a) applies only in relation to a person—
  - (i) first diagnosed with diffuse mesothelioma on or after 1st April 2024; or
  - (ii) where the date of the first diagnosis with diffuse mesothelioma is unknown, if a claim under section 1(1) of the Mesothelioma, etc., Act (Northern Ireland) 2008 is made by that person on or after 1st April 2024; and
- (b) paragraph (b) applies only in relation to the dependant of a person who dies on or after 1st April 2024 and who, immediately before death, had diffuse mesothelioma.

## 2. Amendment of the Mesothelioma Lump Sum Payments (Conditions and Amounts) Regulations

In the Schedule to the Mesothelioma Lump Sum Payments (Conditions and Amounts) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00002]—

- (a)[^c-a4e862a2a81aaf42f7a12e348ddc7864] for Table 1 substitute the Table set out in Part 1 of the Schedule to these Regulations; and
- (b)[^c-a3a23e6d9b1f77a478502031c06dd6d7] for Table 2 substitute the Table set out in Part 2 of the Schedule to these Regulations.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Mesothelioma Lump Sum Payments (Conditions and Amounts) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008 to increase amounts of lump sums payable to people with diffuse mesothelioma or to their dependants under the Mesothelioma, etc., Act (Northern Ireland) 2008. The increase in the amounts paid from 1st April 2024 in each case is 6.7 per cent, rounded up or down to the nearest £1 as appropriate.
