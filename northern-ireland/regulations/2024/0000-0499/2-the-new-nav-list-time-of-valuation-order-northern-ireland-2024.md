# The New NAV List (Time of Valuation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2024

> Text as at 2024-01-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

2024 No. 2

Rates

The New NAV List (Time of Valuation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2024

Made

3rd January 2024

Coming into operation-

31st January 2024

The Department of Finance[^f00001] makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 39A(1) of the Rates (Northern Ireland) Order 1977[^f00002]

## 1. Citation and commencement[^key-55b569562733bc308727d48f72d0da7c]

This Order may be cited as the New NAV List (Time of Valuation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2024 and shall come into operation on 31st January 2024.

## 2. Specification of time[^key-643c72073ec15384ca4de6b2001e6baa]

1st April 2024 is the time by reference to which a net annual value to be ascribed to any hereditament in a new NAV list coming into force on 1st April 2026 is to be ascertained.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance on 3rd January 2024

(L.S.)

Sharon Magee

A senior officer of the Department of Finance

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order specifies 1st April 2024 as the time by reference to which net annual values are to be ascertained for the purposes of the new NAV list coming into force on 1st April 2026.
