## 9. Scheme design requirement[^c-aa90ea739318f742145d66da9aa7fbb3]

*Extent: N.I.*

Part 1 of Schedule 2 sets out the matters that the Regulator must take into account in deciding, for the purposes of section 63 of the 2021 Act (scheme design requirement), whether it is satisfied that the design of a collective money purchase scheme is sound.
