## 13. Communication requirement[^c-b5472a808ef414fb9f2bd22f0b08f50c]

*Extent: N.I.*

Schedule 4 sets out the matters that the Regulator must take into account in deciding, for the purposes of section 66 of the 2021 Act (communication requirement), whether it is satisfied that a collective money purchase scheme has adequate systems and processes for communicating with members and others.
