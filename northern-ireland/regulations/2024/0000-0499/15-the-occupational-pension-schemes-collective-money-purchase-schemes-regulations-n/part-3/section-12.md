# The Occupational Pension Schemes (Collective Money Purchase Schemes) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2024, section 12

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 12. Financial sustainability requirement[^c-55126be245cfd065f73b400ee278cc51]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Part 2 of Schedule 3 sets out the matters that the Regulator must take into account in deciding, for the purposes of section 65 of the 2021 Act (financial sustainability requirement), whether it is satisfied that a collective money purchase scheme is financially sustainable.

(2) Part 3 of Schedule 3 sets out for the purposes of section 65(4)(b) of the 2021 Act the requirements to be met by a collective money purchase scheme in relation to its financing.
