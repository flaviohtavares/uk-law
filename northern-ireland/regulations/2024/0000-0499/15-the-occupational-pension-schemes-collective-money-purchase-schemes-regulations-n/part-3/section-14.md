## 14. Systems and processes requirement[^key-8a857b3e7c1e7fe90f826ec6c797181a]

*Extent: N.I.*

Schedule 5 sets out the matters that the Regulator must take into account in deciding, for the purposes of section 67 of the 2021 Act (systems and processes requirements), whether it is satisfied that the systems and processes used in running a collective money purchase scheme are sufficient to ensure that the scheme is run effectively.
