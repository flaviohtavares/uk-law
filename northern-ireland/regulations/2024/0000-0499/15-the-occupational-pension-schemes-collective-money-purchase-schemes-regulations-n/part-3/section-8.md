# The Occupational Pension Schemes (Collective Money Purchase Schemes) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2024, section 8

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 8. Fit and proper persons requirement[^key-dbfd1f01583979843b76e6200fa05019]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Schedule 1 sets out the matters that the Regulator must take into account in assessing, for the purposes of section 62 of the 2021 Act (fit and proper persons requirement), whether a person is fit and proper to act in a capacity mentioned in section 62(2) of the 2021 Act.

(2) [^key-a42ff3f8b4d103e2426d1fd4b199f5c3]... Where a person in a capacity mentioned in paragraphs (a) to (d) of section 62(2) is a body corporate, the Regulator must assess whether each individual performing the functions of that person in relation to the scheme, in the exercise of a management or executive role, is a fit and proper person to act in relation to the scheme in that capacity.
