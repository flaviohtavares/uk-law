# The Legal Complaints and Regulation (Penalty) Order (Northern Ireland) 2025

> Text as at 2025-09-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

2025 No. 118

Legal Complaints And Regulation

The Legal Complaints and Regulation (Penalty) Order (Northern Ireland) 2025

Laid before the Assembly in draft.

Made

26th June 2025

Coming into operation

1st September 2025

The Department of Finance[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(6) of the Legal Complaints and Regulation Act (Northern Ireland) 2016[^f00002], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement[^key-e2cb0c7078c5cb009808c06ebb784d0c]

This Order may be cited as the Legal Complaints and Regulation (Penalty) Order (Northern Ireland) 2025 and shall come into operation on 1<sup>st</sup> September 2025.

## 2. Interpretation[^key-f525ba1cb7235191bf5eaa1cc5964c3f]

In this Order:

- “professional body” is –
  - (a) The Honourable Society of the Inn of Court of Northern Ireland; or
  - (b) The Law Society of Northern Ireland;
- “**the 2016 Act**” means the Legal Complaints and Regulation Act (Northern Ireland) 2016.

## 3. Penalty[^key-5443d21709104a4e6b4ae8c040ac4b79]

The maximum amount of a penalty the Legal Oversight Commissioner may require a professional body to pay under section 2(4) of the 2016 Act is £20,000.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance on 26<sup>th</sup> June 2025.

(L.S.)

David Hughes

A senior officer of the Department of Finance

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

This Order sets the maximum amount the Legal Services Oversight Commissioner may require the Law Society or the Honourable Society of the Inn of Court to pay as a penalty should the professional body either fail to submit a plan which the Commissioner considers adequate for the handling of complaints or fail to handle complaints in accordance with its plan.
