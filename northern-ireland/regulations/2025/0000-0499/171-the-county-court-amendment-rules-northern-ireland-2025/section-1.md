# The County Court (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2025, section 1

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation[^c-2dbffe0e456cd61b1d20c2d0923c64f0]

*Extent: N.I.*

These Rules may be cited as the County Court (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2025 and shall come into operation on 26th November 2025.

**2.**[^c-5a1bb104fffaead3a851d3f6613e89bf] In these Rules—

- (a) “**the principal Rules**” means the County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981[^f00002]; and
- (b) a reference to an Order, Rule, paragraph, Appendix or Form is a reference to that Order, Rule, paragraph, Appendix or Form so numbered in the principal Rules.
