# The County Court (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2025, section 3

> Text as at 2025-11-26.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 3. Amendments to the principal Rules[^c-02c9abedad58ae89db926e89eda52e11]

*Extent: N.I.*

The principal Rules are amended as follows—

(1) In Order 19, Rule 5 from “(a)” to “2018” substitute “£43.00”.

(2) In Order 24, Rule 9, for paragraph (7A) substitute—

> (7A) The sum to be paid or tendered under paragraph (7)(a) to—
>
> - (a) a police officer is £17.00; and
> - (b) any other person is £23.00.

(3) In Order 33, Rule 10, paragraph (6) from “(a)” to “2018” substitute “£10.00”.

(4) In Order 55, Rule 17 from “(a)” to “2018” substitute “£4.00”.

(5) For Appendix 2 substitute the new Appendix 2 set out in the Schedule to these Rules.
