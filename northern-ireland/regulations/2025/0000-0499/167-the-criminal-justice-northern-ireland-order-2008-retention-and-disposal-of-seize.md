EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations uplift the charges that shall be levied in respect of the removal and storage of vehicles seized because they have been driven carelessly, inconsiderately or illegally off-road and is causing, or is likely to cause, alarm, distress or annoyance to members of the public.

Regulation 3 amends the statutory charges for removal of a seized vehicle.

Regulation 4 amends the statutory charges for the storage of a seized vehicle.

Regulation 5 provides that the amendments made by regulations 3 and 4 only have effect in respect of a vehicle seized on or after 10th November 2025.
