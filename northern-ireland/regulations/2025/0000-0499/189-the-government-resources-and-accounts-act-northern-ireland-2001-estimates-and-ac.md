# The Government Resources and Accounts Act (Northern Ireland) 2001 (Estimates and Accounts) (Designation of Bodies) (Amendment) Order 2025

> Text as at 2025-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

2025 No. 189

Government Resources And Accounts

The Government Resources and Accounts Act (Northern Ireland) 2001 (Estimates and Accounts) (Designation of Bodies) (Amendment) Order 2025

Made

28th November 2025

Coming into operation

31st December 2025

The Department of Finance [^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 8A and 8B of the Government Resources and Accounts Act (Northern Ireland) 2001 [^f00002], makes the following Order.

In accordance with section 8A(5) of the Act the Department of Finance have consulted, where they think it appropriate, with the Treasury.

## 1. Title and commencement[^c-256567955e90bef849c8aad9650a0ef9]

This Order may be cited as the Government Resources and Accounts Act (Northern Ireland) 2001 (Estimates and Accounts) (Designation of Bodies) (Amendment) Order 2025 and shall come into operation on 31st December 2025.

## 2. Amendments to the principal Order[^c-7439fcbf4afaec6b648fe2098ef8f8e3]

The Principal Order is amended as follows:

(1) For the Schedule (designated bodies) to the principal Order, substitute the Schedule set out in the Schedule to this Order.

(2) In this Order, “**the principal Order**” means the Government Resources and Accounts (Northern Ireland) Act 2001 (Estimates and Accounts) (Designation of Bodies) Order 2025 [^f00003].

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance on 28<sup>th</sup> November 2025

Patrick Neeson

A senior officer of the

Department of Finance

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

This Order amends the Government Resources and Accounts (Northern Ireland) Act 2001 (Estimates and Accounts) (Designation of Bodies) Order 2025 (S.I. [2025 No.50](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2025/50)) (“**the principal Order**”), which designates specified central government bodies in relation to named government departments for the purpose of those departments’ supply estimates and resource accounts.

A new Schedule is substituted for the Schedule to the principal Order. The effect of this amendment is that three bodies have been added and eleven bodies have been renamed.

The designations have effect for the financial year that ends on 31st March 2026.

An impact assessment has not been prepared for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen. An Explanatory Memorandum is available alongside the instrument on www.legislation.gov.uk.
