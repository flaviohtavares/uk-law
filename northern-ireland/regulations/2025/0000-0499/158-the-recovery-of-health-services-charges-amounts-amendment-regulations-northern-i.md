# The Recovery of Health Services Charges (Amounts) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2025

> Text as at 2025-10-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

2025 No. 158

Health Services Charges

The Recovery of Health Services Charges (Amounts) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2025

Made

23rd September 2025

Coming into operation

1st October 2025

The Department of Health[^f00001] makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 5(2) and (5) and 19(3) of the Recovery of Health Services Charges (Northern Ireland) Order 2006[^f00002]:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation[^c-bc8272a430d0b393b1d99797cc355e1c]

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Recovery of Health Services Charges (Amounts) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2025 and shall come into operation on 1st October 2025.

(2) In these Regulations “**the principal Regulations**” means the Recovery of Health Services Charges (Amounts) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006[^f00003].

## 2. Amendment of regulation 2 of the principal Regulations[^c-8031321ed804a47954ac05df1dfb7128]

In regulation 2 of the principal Regulations (amount of health care charges)—

- (a) in paragraph (1)(a) for “£256” substitute “£267”;
- (b) in paragraph (1)(b)(i) for “£848” substitute “£883”;
- (c) in paragraph (1)(b)(ii) for “£1,042” substitute “£1,085”;
- (d) in paragraph (4) for “£62,272” substitute “£64,856”.

## 3. Saving[^c-18ce9d68047db23d8cf76c5308c5f4b7]

Notwithstanding regulation 2, the principal Regulations shall continue to have effect in respect of a certificate relating to an incident which occurred before 1st October 2025 as if the amendment made by these Regulations had not been made.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health on 23rd September 2025.

David Keenan

A senior officer of the Department of Health

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

The Recovery of Health Services Charges (Northern Ireland) Order 2006 provides for a scheme for the recovery of charges in cases where an injured person who receives a compensation payment in respect of his injury has received hospital treatment or ambulance services. The charges are specified in certificates issued by the Department for Communities and are payable by persons who pay compensation to the injured person. The charges in each case are calculated by reference to a tariff of charges provided for by the Recovery of Health Services Charges (Amounts) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006 (“**the principal Regulations**”).

Amendments made to the principal Regulations by regulation 2 increase the charges in respect of injuries which occur on or after 1st October 2025. Where the injured person is provided with health care ambulance services, the charge is increased from £256 to £267 for each occasion. Where the injured person receives health care treatment but is not admitted to hospital the charge is increased from £848 to £883. The daily charge for health care in-patient treatment is increased from £1,042 to £1,085. The maximum charge in respect of an injury is increased from £62,272 to £64,856.

Regulation 3 is a saving provision which maintains the current charges for incidents occurring before 1st October 2025.
