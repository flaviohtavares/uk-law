EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations uplift the charges that shall be levied in respect of the removal and storage of vehicles seized because they have been driven without insurance, by amending the charges set out in Regulation 6 of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981 (Retention and Disposal of Seized Motor Vehicles) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008.

Regulation 3 amends the statutory charges for removal of a seized motor vehicle.

Regulation 4 amends the statutory charges for the storage of a seized motor vehicle.

Regulation 5 provides that the amendments made by regulations 3 and 4 only have effect in respect of a vehicle seized on or after 10th November 2025.
