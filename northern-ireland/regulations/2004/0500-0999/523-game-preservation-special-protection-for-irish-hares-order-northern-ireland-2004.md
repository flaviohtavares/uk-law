# Game Preservation (Special Protection for Irish Hares) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 523

GAME

Game Preservation (Special Protection for Irish Hares) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004

Made

22nd December 2004

Coming into operation

19th January 2005

Whereas the Department of the Environment[^f00001] is satisfied that it is necessary to provide special protection for Irish Hares;

And whereas the Department of the Environment has caused a notice of the proposal to make an order to be published in accordance with section 7F(5) of the Game Preservation Act (Northern Ireland) 1928[^f00002] and taken into consideration the objections duly lodged in accordance with that notice;

Now, therefore, the Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 7C(1) and 7F(1) and (2) of the Game Preservation Act (Northern Ireland) 1928 and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Game Preservation (Special Protection for Irish Hares) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004, and shall come into operation on 19th January 2005.

## 2. Prohibition of killing, taking, selling or trading of Irish hares

A person shall not kill, take, sell or purchase Irish Hares at any time during the period 19th January to 31st March 2005.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 22nd December 2004.

F. Dillon

A senior officer of the

Department of the Environment

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order prohibits a person from killing, taking, selling or purchasing Irish hares during the period outlined in Article 2.

Any person who acts in contravention of or fails to comply with any provision of the Order shall be guilty of an offence under section 7F(6) of the Game Preservation Act (Northern Ireland) 1928.
