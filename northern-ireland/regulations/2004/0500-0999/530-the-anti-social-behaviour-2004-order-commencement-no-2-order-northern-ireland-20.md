# The Anti-social Behaviour (2004 Order) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 530 (C. 33)

ANTI-SOCIAL BEHAVIOUR

The Anti-social Behaviour (2004 Order) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004

Made

15th December 2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by Article 1(2) of the Anti-social Behaviour (Northern Ireland) Order 2004[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Anti-social Behaviour (2004 Order) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004.

## 2. Commencement

Article 6 of the Anti-social Behaviour (Northern Ireland) Order 2004 shall come into operation on 15th December 2004.

Paul Murphy

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Northern Ireland Office

15th December 2004

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Commencement Order brings into operation Article 6 of the Anti-social Behaviour (Northern Ireland) Order 2004 on 15th December 2004.
