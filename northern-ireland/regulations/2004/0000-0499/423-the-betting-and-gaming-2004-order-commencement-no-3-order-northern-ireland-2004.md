# The Betting and Gaming (2004 Order) (Commencement No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 423 (C. 20)

BETTING, GAMING AND LOTTERIES

The Betting and Gaming (2004 Order) (Commencement No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004

Made

5th October 2004

The Department for Social Development, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 1(4) of the Betting and Gaming (Northern Ireland) Order 2004[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Betting and Gaming (2004 Order) (Commencement No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004.

(2) In this Order “the 2004 Order” means the Betting and Gaming (Northern Ireland) Order 2004.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the following provisions of the 2004 Order is 6th January 2005 –

- (a) Article 5(1) (together with Schedules 2 and 3); and
- (b) in Schedule 4, the entries relating to Articles 174(3)(a), 178(2) and 186(1) of the Betting, Gaming, Lotteries and Amusements (Northern Ireland) Order 1985[^f00002], and Article 12(3) so far as it relates thereto.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 5th October 2004.

Henry Johnston

A senior officer of the

Department for Social Development

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order brings into operation the remaining provisions of the Betting and Gaming (Northern Ireland) Order 2004 (the 2004 Order). It appoints 6th January 2005 for the coming into operation of the following provisions of the 2004 Order –

- (a) Article 5(1) (Bookmaking office licences) (together with Schedules 2 and 3); and
- (b) the remaining repeals in Schedule 4 and Article 12(3) so far as it relates thereto.
