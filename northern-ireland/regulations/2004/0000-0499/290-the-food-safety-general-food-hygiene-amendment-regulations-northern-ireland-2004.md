# The Food Safety (General Food Hygiene) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 290

FOOD

The Food Safety (General Food Hygiene) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004

Made

6th July 2004

Coming into operation

30th July 2004

The Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety[^f00001], being a Department designated[^f00002] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00003] in relation to food in exercise of the powers conferred on it by the said section 2(2), and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as The Food Safety (General Food Hygiene) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004, and shall come into operation on 30th July 2004.

## 2. Amendment to the Food Safety (General Food Hygiene) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995

(1) The Food Safety (General Food Hygiene) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995[^f00004] shall be amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

(2) In paragraph (1) of regulation 2 (interpretation), for the definition of “list of acceptable previous cargoes for liquid oils or fats” there shall be substituted the following definition -

> - “list of acceptable previous cargoes for liquid oils or fats” means the list set out in the Annex to Commission Directive [96/3/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0003) granting a derogation from certain provisions of Council Directive [93/43/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0043) on the hygiene of foodstuffs as regards the transport of bulk liquid oils and fats by sea[^f00005] as amended by Commission Directive [2004/4/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0004) amending Directive [96/3/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0003) granting a derogation from certain provisions of Council Directive [93/43/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0043) on the hygiene of foodstuffs as regards the transport of bulk liquid oils and fats by sea[^f00006];

.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety on 6th July 2004.

D. Kenny

A senior officer of the

Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations further amend the Food Safety (General Food Hygiene) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995 (“the principal regulations”).

These Regulations implement Commission Directive [2004/4/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0004) amending Directive [96/3/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0003) granting a derogation from certain provisions of Council Directive [93/43/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0043) on the hygiene of foodstuffs as regards the transport of bulk liquid oils and fats by sea (O.J. No. L15, 22.1.2004, p. 25) by substituting a revised definition of the phrase “list of acceptable previous cargoes for liquid oils or fats” for the existing definition of that phrase in regulation 2(1) of the principal regulations (regulation 2(2)). The revised definition now refers to the list set out in the Annex to Commission Directive [96/3/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0003) (O.J. No. L21, 27.1.96, p. 42) as replaced by Commission Directive [2004/4/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0004).

Chapter IV of Schedule 1 to the principal regulations provides that the bulk transport in sea-going vessels of liquid oils or fats which are to be processed and which are intended for or are likely to be used for human consumption is permitted in tanks that are not exclusively reserved for the transport of foodstuffs if a certain number of previous cargoes transported in the tanks were from the “list of acceptable previous cargoes for liquid oils or fats” as defined in regulation 2(1) of those regulations.
