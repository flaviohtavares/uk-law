# The Protection of Water Against Agricultural Nitrate Pollution Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

2004 No. 419

EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES

WATER AND SEWERAGE

The Protection of Water Against Agricultural Nitrate Pollution Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004

Made

1st October 2004

Coming into operation

29th October 2004

The Department of the Environment and the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development, being departments designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to measures relating to the prevention, reduction and elimination of pollution of water, acting jointly in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by that section and of every other power enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement[^key-1c965bacfab2771b686c141a81c325d6]

These Regulations may be cited as the Protection of Water Against Agricultural Nitrate Pollution Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004 and shall come into operation on 29th October 2004.

## 2. Interpretation[^key-cbdf20de941f221ff1bceb5f85cfef02]

(1) In these Regulations –

- “[^key-e2859a379d3c071a63f9c9cea1a074a0]**Action Programme**” means measures regarding the protection of water against pollution caused by nutrients from agricultural sources as set out in the Nitrates Action Programme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014 [^c23534271];
- “the Departments” means the Department of the Environment and the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development acting jointly;
- “Northern Ireland” has the same meaning as in the Northern Ireland Act [1998 (c. 47)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1998/47);
- “Regulations” means Regulations made under section 2(2) of the European Communities Act [1972 (c. 68)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1972/68).

(2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00004] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

## 3. Action Programme throughout territory of Northern Ireland[^key-5279ba1fadba3d33a77112a9d7373f49]

The Departments shall by Regulations establish and apply an Action Programme throughout the territory of Northern Ireland.

## 4. Transitional[^key-58b4d574f7adb83996d07af3c588996e]

(1) The statutory provisions referred to in paragraph (2) shall continue in operation unless and until the Departments by Regulations establish and apply an Action Programme throughout the territory of Northern Ireland.

(2) The statutory provisions referred to in paragraph (1) are the following, namely –

- (a) The Protection of Water Against Agricultural Nitrate Pollution Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00005]; and
- (b) Action Programme for Nitrate Vulnerable Zones Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999[^f00006].

Department of the Environment

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations give further effect to Council Directive [91/676/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0676) (the Nitrates Directive) concerning the protection of waters against pollution caused by nitrates from agricultural sources.

Regulation 3 establishes the territory of Northern Ireland as the area to which an Action Programme will be applied.

Regulation 4 is a transitional measure and provides for the continuation of the existing Action Programme and the seven existing Nitrate Vulnerable Zones pending the application of an Action Programme to the territory of Northern Ireland.
