# Open-Ended Investment Companies (2002 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 333 (C. 13)

FINANCIAL SERVICES

Open-Ended Investment Companies (2002 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004

Made

9th August 2004

The Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 4 of the Open-Ended Investment Companies Act (Northern Ireland) 2002[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Open-Ended Investment Companies (2002 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004.

## 2. Appointed Day

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the Open-Ended Investment Companies Act (Northern Ireland) 2002, in so far as it is not already in operation, is 10th August 2004.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment on 9th August 2004.

James McKeown

A senior officer of the

Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order brings the Open-Ended Investment Companies Act (Northern Ireland) 2002 fully into operation on 10th August 2004.
