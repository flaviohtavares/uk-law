# The Protection of Children and Vulnerable Adults (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 466 (C.24)

HEALTH AND PERSONAL SOCIAL SERVICES

The Protection of Children and Vulnerable Adults (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004

Made

8th November 2004

Coming into operation

30th November 2004

The Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety, in exercise of the powers conferred upon it by Article 1(3) of the Protection of Children and Vulnerable Adults (Northern Ireland) Order 2003[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Protection of Children and Vulnerable Adults (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004.

## 2. Appointed day

The 30th November 2004 is the day appointed for the coming into operation of the following provisions of the Protection of Children and Vulnerable Adults (Northern Ireland) Order 2003 –

- (a) Articles 3, 4(1), (2)(a) to (c), (3) and (9) and 21(1) to (3) in so far as they relate to Article 10(1) and (2);
- (b) Article 10(1) and (2);
- (c) Article 20;
- (d) Articles 35, 36(1), (2)(a) to (c), (3) and (9) and 49(1) to (3) in so far as they relate to Article 41(1) and (2);
- (e) Article 41(1) and (2);
- (f) Article 48.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Health, Social Services and Public Safety on 8th November 2004.

P. Martin

A senior officer of the

Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order brings into operation those Articles of the Protection of Children and Vulnerable Adults (Northern Ireland) Order 2003 which will permit the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety to seek observations from those currently listed by that Department on the Pre-Employment Consultancy Service Register and from those organisations that referred them for inclusion on that Register.
