# The Health Services (Primary Care) (1997 Order) (Commencement No. 4) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 133 (C. 5)

HEALTH AND PERSONAL SOCIAL SERVICES

The Health Services (Primary Care) (1997 Order) (Commencement No. 4) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004

Made

25th March 2004

The Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety[^f00001] in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 1(2) of the Health Services (Primary Care) (Northern Ireland) Order 1997[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Health Services (Primary Care) (1997 Order) (Commencement No. 4) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004.

(2) In this Order:

- “the 1997 Order” means the Health Services (Primary Care) (Northern Ireland) Order 1997.

## 2. Appointed days

(1) The day appointed for the coming into operation of Article 21 of the 1997 Order (Provision of personal medical services or personal dental services), in so far as it relates to the provision of personal medical services and Article 22 of the 1997 Order (Supplementary regulations) is 1st April 2004.

(2) 1st April 2004 is the day appointed for the coming into operation of Schedule 2 to the 1997 Order insofar as those provisions relate to –

- (a) the insertion in Article 2(2) of the Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1972[^f00003] of the definitions of “health services body” and “statutory provision”; and
- (b) the amendments to Article 10 of the Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1991[^f00004].

## 3. Revocation

Article 3(2) of the Health Services (Primary Care) (1997 Order) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 1998[^f00005] is hereby revoked.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety on 25th March 2004.

Dr. J. F. Livingstone

Senior Officer of the

Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order brings into operation on 1st April 2004 certain provisions of the Health Services (Primary Care) (Northern Ireland) Order 1997, namely Article 21 (Provision of personal medical services or dental services), but only in so far as it refers to personal medical services, Article 22 (Supplementary regulations) and Schedule 2, in so far as the amendments in that Schedule are relevant to those provisions.
