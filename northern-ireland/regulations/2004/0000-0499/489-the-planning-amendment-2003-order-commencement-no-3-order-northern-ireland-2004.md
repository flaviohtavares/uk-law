# The Planning (Amendment) (2003 Order) (Commencement No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 489 (C. 27)

PLANNING

The Planning (Amendment) (2003 Order) (Commencement No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004

Made

29th November 2004

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 1(2) of the Planning (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 2003[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

This Order may be cited as the Planning (Amendment) (2003 Order) (Commencement No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004.

## 2. Appointed day

The appointed day for the coming into operation of Article 18 of the Planning (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order is 1st December 2004.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 29th November 2004.

David Ferguson

A senior officer of the

Department of the Environment

(This note is not part of the Order.)

Article 2 of this Order brings into operation on 1st December 2004, Article 18 of the Planning (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 2003.

(This note is not part of the Order.)
