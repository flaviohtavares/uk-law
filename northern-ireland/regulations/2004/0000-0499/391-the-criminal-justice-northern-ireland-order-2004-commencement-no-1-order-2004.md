# The Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2004 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 391 (C. 17)

CRIMINAL LAW

The Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2004 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2004

Made

1st September 2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by Article 1(3), of the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2004[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2004 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2004.

**2.** Articles 3, 5, 6, 7 and 8 of the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2004 shall come into operation on 6 September 2004.

**3.** Articles 4 and 9 of the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2004 shall come into operation on 1st January 2005.

John Spellar

Minister of State

Northern Ireland Office

1st September 2004

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order commences Articles 3, 5, 6, 7 and 8 of the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2004 from 6th September 2004 and Article 4 and 9 from 1st January 2005.

The Articles amend the Police and Criminal Evidence (Northern Ireland) Order 1989.
