# Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) (Amendment No. 5) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 360

ROAD TRAFFIC AND VEHICLES

Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) (Amendment No. 5) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004

Made

25th August 2004

Coming into operation

18th November 2004

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Articles 55(1) and (2) and 110(2) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1995[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) (Amendment No. 5) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004 and shall come into operation on 18th November 2004.

## 2. Amendment of the Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999

In regulation 2(1) of the Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999[^f00002], in the definition of “heavy motor car”, for “3050 kg” there shall be substituted “2540 kg”.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 25th August 2004.

Wesley Shannon

A senior officer of the

Department of the Environment

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations further amend the Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999 (“the 1999 Regulations”) in order to correct an anomaly between the definition of a “heavy motor car” in the 1999 Regulations and the definition of a “heavy motor car” in the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1995.

In the 1999 Regulations the definition of a “heavy motor car” is amended to bring it into line with the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1995 wherein a “heavy motor car” is defined as having an unladen weight exceeding 2540 kilograms.
