# Housing Renewal Grants (Reduction of Grant) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004, section 31

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 31. Capital treated as income[^key-c619721a74c7ece19b1b79f0d816f6f8]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

The following shall be treated as income –

- (a) any payment received under an annuity; and
- (b) any payment made in consequence of any personal injury to a relevant person pursuant to any agreement or court order, where such payments are to be made, wholly or partly, by way of periodical payments.
