# Housing Renewal Grants (Reduction of Grant) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004, section 35

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 35. Disregard of capital of child or young person[^key-e444d8856158f03fdca7d0bc4e0e4e7f]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

The capital of a child or young person who is a member of the relevant person’s family shall not be treated as capital of the relevant person.
