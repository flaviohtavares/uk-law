# Housing Renewal Grants (Reduction of Grant) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004, section 45

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 45. Relationship with amounts to be disregarded under Schedule 3[^c-28f5978e52d0c243a7be86885dc47956]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

No part of a student’s grant income shall be disregarded under paragraph 12 of Schedule 3 (sums to be disregarded in the determination of income other than earnings).
