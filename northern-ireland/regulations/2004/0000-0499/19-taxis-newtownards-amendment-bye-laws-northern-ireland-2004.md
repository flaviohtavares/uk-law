# Taxis (Newtownards) (Amendment) Bye-Laws (Northern Ireland) 2004

> Text as at 2004-03-03.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

2004 No. 19

ROAD TRAFFIC AND VEHICLES

Taxis (Newtownards) (Amendment) Bye-Laws (Northern Ireland) 2004

Made

20th January 2004

Coming into operation

3rd March 2004

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 65(1) and (2) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001], and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Bye-Laws:

## 1. Citation and commencement[^key-a34e7d08dcc1bd3ab078675c6d35d12d]

These Bye-Laws may be cited as the Taxis (Newtownards) (Amendment) Bye-Laws (Northern Ireland) 2004 and shall come into operation on 3rd March 2004.

## 2. Bye-Laws amended[^key-ae8b0aa668bff99ab6191dcb98ad8e18]

In the Schedule to the Taxis (Newtownards) Bye-Laws (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00002] before the first entry for Regent Street there shall be inserted –

> |  |  |  |  |
> | --- | --- | --- | --- |
> | Old Cross Street | West Side, from a point approximately 15 metres from the junction with Court Street to a point approximately 57 metres from that junction. | 7 | At any time. |

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 20th January 2004.

Wesley Shannon

A senior officer of the

Department of the Environment

(This note is not part of the Bye-Laws.)

These Bye-Laws amend the Schedule to the Taxis (Newtownards) Bye-Laws (Northern Ireland) 1996 by prescribing a new taxi stand at Old Cross Street.
