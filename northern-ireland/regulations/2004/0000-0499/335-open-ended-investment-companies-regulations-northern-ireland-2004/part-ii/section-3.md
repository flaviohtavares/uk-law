# Open-Ended Investment Companies Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004, section 3

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 3. Open-ended investment company[^key-7d26e3d1a06e4932b074567b1d711728]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) If the Authority makes an authorisation order then, immediately upon the coming into effect of the order, the body to which the authorisation order relates is to be incorporated as an open-ended investment company (notwithstanding that, at the point of its incorporation by virtue of this paragraph, the body will not have any shareholders or property).

(2) The name of an open-ended investment company is the name mentioned in the authorisation order made in respect of the company or, if it changes its name in accordance with these Regulations and FCA rules[^key-17a9141fb993bcb8fc32d9b21cf6070f], its new name.
