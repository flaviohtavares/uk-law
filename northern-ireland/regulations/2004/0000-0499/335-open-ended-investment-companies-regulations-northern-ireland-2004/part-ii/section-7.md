# Open-Ended Investment Companies Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004, section 7

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 7. Modification or waiver of FCA rules[^c-5d349255491abfd47d5a6ed965251a24][^c-17a9141fb993bcb8fc32d9b21cf6070f]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Authority may, on the application or with the consent of any person to whom any FCA rules apply, direct that all or any of the FCA rules –

- (a) are not to apply to him as respects a particular open-ended investment company; or
- (b) are to apply to him as respects such a company with such modifications as may be specified in the direction.

(2) The Authority may, on the application or with the consent of an open-ended investment company and its depositary acting jointly, direct that all or any of the FCA rules –

- (a) are not to apply to the company; or
- (b) are to apply to the company with such modifications as may be specified in the direction.

(3) Sections 138A(3) to (7) and 138B of[^c-a8ad51566d4a03606beb0c7f496fb698] the Act (modification or waiver of rules) has effect in relation to a direction under paragraph (1) as it has effect in relation to a direction under section 138A(1)[^c-82c6ffa7deaad0e6d87787c09d622289] of the Act but with the following modifications –

- (a)[^c-82e4462ddd99e30a356b853d7747f4db] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b)[^c-82e4462ddd99e30a356b853d7747f4db] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) section 138B(3)(c)[^c-4928ca8a5115f7b80ea0bb7e456aa798] is to be read, in relation to a shareholder, as if the word commercial were omitted.

(4) Sections 138A(3) to (7) and 138B of[^c-8a36b6ad752ea188d5cec2932cf1a9ce] the Act has effect in relation to a direction under paragraph (2) as it has effect in relation to a direction under section 138A(1)[^c-846b935d7dab71172f3caaba94d0e79c] of the Act but with the following modifications –

- (a) section 138A(4)(a)[^c-e2212c35b709f1922869db450085b00e] is to be read as if the words by the [^c-a5226d21c62e96653412988d45b7b56c]...person were omitted;
- (b) section 138B(3)(c) and (5)[^c-1109e498156a03659f8f1e061a569a4e] are to be read as if the reference to the person concerned[^c-9818bd2409db48188c70c9df0c2012bf] were a reference to each of the company and its depositary;
- (c) section 138B(3)(c)[^c-7a9e71a1c7e4e6b25e2c8ab00e5983ad] is to be read, in relation to a shareholder, as if the word commercial were omitted; and
- (d) section 138A(7)[^c-5de9cdeff59e27fc8a37dc60989177f2] is to be read as if the reference to the person to whom the direction relates[^c-b336e754071f88b989f12c429fba7d5e] were a reference to the company and its depositary acting jointly.
