# Open-Ended Investment Companies Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004, section 79

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 79. Contraventions[^c-032dd0552f45b0f75e5493519a5c945c]

*Extent: N.I.*

Any of the following persons, that is to say –

- (a) a person who contravenes any provision of these Regulations; and
- (b) an open-ended investment company (including any director or depositary of such a company) which contravenes any provision of FCA rules[^c-17a9141fb993bcb8fc32d9b21cf6070f],

is to be treated as having contravened rules made under section 137A of[^c-c3301ab5360ac199886166befbb315b4] the Act (FCA’s general rules) (general rule-making power).
