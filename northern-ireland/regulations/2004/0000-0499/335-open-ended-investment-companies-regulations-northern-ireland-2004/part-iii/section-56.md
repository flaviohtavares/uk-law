# Open-Ended Investment Companies Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004, section 56

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 56. Contracts[^c-0bf1a9869b8eeeb95b370a46c5d0479d]

*Extent: N.I.*

A contract may be made –

- (a) by an open-ended investment company by writing under its common seal; or
- (b) on behalf of such a company, by any person acting under its authority (whether express or implied);

and any formalities required by law in the case of a contract made by an individual also apply, unless a contrary intention appears, to a contract made by or on behalf of such a company.
