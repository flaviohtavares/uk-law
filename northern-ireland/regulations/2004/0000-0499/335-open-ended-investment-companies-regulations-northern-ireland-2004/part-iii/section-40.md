# Open-Ended Investment Companies Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004, section 40

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 40. No duty to enquire as to capacity etc.[^key-89163459b95a5ada9554d069cbffe6ed]

*Extent: N.I.*

Subject to regulation 39(4)(b)(ii), a party to a transaction with an open-ended investment company is not bound to enquire –

- (a) as to whether the transaction is permitted by these Regulations, FCA rules[^key-17a9141fb993bcb8fc32d9b21cf6070f] or the company’s instrument of incorporation; or
- (b) as to any limitation on the powers referred to in regulation 39(1)(a) or (b).
