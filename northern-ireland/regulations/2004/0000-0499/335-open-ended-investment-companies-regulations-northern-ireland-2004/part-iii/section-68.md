# Open-Ended Investment Companies Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004, section 68

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 68. Reports: voluntary revision[^key-2c14ea877d6dc9afb334555e900cd10d]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) If it appears to the directors of an open-ended investment company that any annual report of the company did not comply with the requirements of these Regulations or FCA rules[^key-17a9141fb993bcb8fc32d9b21cf6070f], they may prepare a revised annual report.

(2) Where copies of the previous report have been laid before the company in general meeting or delivered to the Authority, the revisions must be confined to –

- (a) the correction of anything in the previous report which did not comply with the requirements of these Regulations or FCA rules; and
- (b) the making of any necessary consequential alterations.
