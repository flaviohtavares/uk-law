> Text as at 2013-04-01.

## 53. Power incidental to carrying on business[^key-d3d7a378a4ff18eb625ade3f3be1428e]

*Extent: N.I.*

An open-ended investment company has power to do all such things as are incidental or conducive to the carrying on of its business.
