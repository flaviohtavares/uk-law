# The Companies (1986 Order) (International Accounting Standards and Other Accounting Amendments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004, section 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12. Participating interest no longer required to be subsidiary undertaking

(1) In Article 266(4) of the 1986 Order (parent and subsidiary undertakings)[^f00012] –

- (a) omit “it has a participating interest in the undertaking and”, and
- (b) for sub-paragraph (a) substitute –

  > - (a) it has the power to exercise, or actually exercises, dominant influence or control over it, or

  .

(2) The modification of Article 266(4) in paragraph (1) also applies for the purposes of –

- (a) the Building Societies Act 1986[^f00013], and
- (b) the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000[^f00014].
