# The Companies (1986 Order) (International Accounting Standards and Other Accounting Amendments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7. Auditors' report to be dated

In Article 244(1) of the 1986 Order (signature of auditors' report)[^f00007], after “signed” insert “and dated”.

## Annotations

[^f00007]: Footnote: Article 244 was inserted into the 1986 Order by Article 11 of the Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1990 in place of an existing Article of that number
