# The Companies (1986 Order) (International Accounting Standards and Other Accounting Amendments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004, section 9

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 9. Repeal of 3 month extension for laying and delivering accounts etc

In Article 252 of the 1986 Order (3 month extension of period allowed for laying and delivering accounts and reports where interests outside the United Kingdom, etc.)[^f00009] omit paragraph (3).

## Annotations

[^f00009]: Footnote: Article 252 was inserted into the 1986 Order by Article 13 of the Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1990 in place of an existing Article of that number
