# The Companies (1986 Order) (International Accounting Standards and Other Accounting Amendments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004, section 14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 14. Amendment of Schedules to the 1986 Order

(1) Schedule 4 to the 1986 Order (form and content of company accounts)[^f00018] is amended in accordance with Schedule 2 to these Regulations.

(2) Schedule 8 to the 1986 Order (form and content of accounts prepared by small companies)[^f00019] is amended in accordance with Schedule 3 to these Regulations.

(3) Schedule 8A to the 1986 Order (form and content of abbreviated accounts of small companies delivered to registrar)[^f00020] is amended in accordance with Schedule 4 to these Regulations.

(4) Schedule 9 to the 1986 Order (form and content of accounts of banking companies and groups)[^f00021] is amended in accordance with Schedule 5 to these Regulations.

(5) Schedule 9A to the 1986 Order (form and content of accounts of insurance companies and groups)[^f00022] is amended in accordance with Schedule 6 to these Regulations.
