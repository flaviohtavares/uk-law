# The Companies (1986 Order) (International Accounting Standards and Other Accounting Amendments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004, section 5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5. Subsidiary undertakings included in consolidation

In Article 237 of the 1986 Order (subsidiary undertakings included in the consolidation)[^f00005] –

- (a) in paragraph (3)(c), omit from “and the undertaking” to the end, and
- (b) omit paragraph (4).

## Annotations

[^f00005]: Footnote: Article 237 was inserted into the 1986 Order by Article 7 of the Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1990 in place of an existing Article of that number
