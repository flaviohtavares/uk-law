# The General Medical Services Transitional and Consequential Provisions (No. 2) (Northern Ireland) Order 2004, section 36

> Text as at 2007-11-09.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 36. Entry on to primary medical services performers list of persons whose application has been granted under regulation 12 of the 1997 Regulations[^key-f78573449f667dc1fac28bc6bf23b58f]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where a Board –

- (a) had, on 31st March 2004, granted the application of a medical practitioner under regulation 12 of the 1997 Regulations[^f00024] but had not yet entered his name on its medical list in accordance with regulation 12(2) of those Regulations; and
- (b) intends to enter into a default contract or general medical services contract with two or more individuals practising in partnership one of whom is the person so approved,

it shall add that person’s name to its primary medical services performers list.
