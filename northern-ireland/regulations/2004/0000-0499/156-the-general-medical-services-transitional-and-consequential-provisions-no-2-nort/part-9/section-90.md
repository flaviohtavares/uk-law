# The General Medical Services Transitional and Consequential Provisions (No. 2) (Northern Ireland) Order 2004, section 90

> Text as at 2007-11-09.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 90. Transitional provision in relation to the Health and Personal Social Services (Injury Benefits) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001[^c-deb729753668c8ecf046845ef04a8dbf]

*Extent: N.I.*

Notwithstanding the amendments made to the Health and Personal Social Services (Injury Benefits) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001[^f00047]. by paragraph 9 of Schedule 1, those regulations shall, in relation to any employment before 1st April 2004, continue to apply as if those amendments had not been made.
