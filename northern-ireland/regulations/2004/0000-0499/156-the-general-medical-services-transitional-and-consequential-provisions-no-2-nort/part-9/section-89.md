# The General Medical Services Transitional and Consequential Provisions (No. 2) (Northern Ireland) Order 2004, section 89

> Text as at 2007-11-09.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 89. Saving of Article 144 of the Trade Union and Labour Relations (Northern Ireland) Order 1995[^key-9c908139024b952c55f5c6af4778dfd2]

*Extent: N.I.*

Notwithstanding the coming into operation of the amendments to the definition of worker in Article 144 of the Trade Union and Labour Relations (Northern Ireland) Order 1995 (health service practitioners) made by paragraph 16 of Schedule 1 to the 2004 Order[^f00046], in relation to any complaint arising in respect of a matter which occurred before 1st April 2004, Article 144 shall have effect as if those amendments had not been brought into force.
