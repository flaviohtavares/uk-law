# The General Medical Services Transitional and Consequential Provisions (No. 2) (Northern Ireland) Order 2004, section 56

> Text as at 2007-11-09.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 56. Allocation of medical practitioners on previous lists[^c-e2045e983274701ae69ce9c5fcf94196]

*Extent: N.I.*

In the case of a medical practitioner, whose name, immediately before 1st April 2004, was included in a Board’s medical list, his name shall on that day, be included in its primary medical services performers list.
