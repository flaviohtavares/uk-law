# The Magistrates' Courts Fees (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 339

MAGISTRATES' COURTS

The Magistrates' Courts Fees (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004

Made

4th August 2004

Coming into operation

1st September 2004

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 116(1) of the Judicature (Northern Ireland) Act 1978[^f00001], after consultation with the Lord Chief Justice, and with the concurrence of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Magistrates' Courts Fees (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004 and shall come into operation on 1st September 2004.

## 2. Magistrates' Courts Fees

For Schedules 1 and 2 to the Magistrates' Courts Fees Order (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00002] there shall be substituted the new Schedules 1 and 2 set out in the Schedule to this Order.

Signed by the authority of the Lord Chancellor

I concur

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

> **SCHEDULE 2 — Fees Payable for Service of a Summons or Process**
>
> Article 6
>
> |  | Fee |
> | --- | --- |
> | **1.** (1) Subject to sub-paragraph (2) and paragraph 3, for the service of a summons or process or document | £10·00 |
> | (2) Where service of a summons or process is required to be personal | £13·00 |
> | **2.** For the service of a summons or process where there are two or more defendants or witnesses in the same proceedings there shall be a separate fee for each defendant or witness. |  |
> | **3.** Where a document relating to a summons is served together with the summons, for each document served | £ 3·00 |

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order amends the Magistrates' Courts Fees Order (Northern Ireland) 1996 so as to increase a number of the fees payable in respect of proceedings in Magistrates' Courts and increase fees payable for service of a summons or process.
