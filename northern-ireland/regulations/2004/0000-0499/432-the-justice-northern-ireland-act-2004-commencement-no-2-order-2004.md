# The Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2004 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 432 (C. 23)

JUSTICE

The Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2004 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2004

Made

7th October 2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 19 of the Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2004[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2004 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2004.

**2.** Section 10 of the Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2004 shall come into force on 29th October 2004.

Paul Murphy

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Northern Ireland Office

7th October 2004

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order brings into force section 10 of the Justice (Northern Ireland) Act [2004 (c. 4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/4) on 29th October 2004.
