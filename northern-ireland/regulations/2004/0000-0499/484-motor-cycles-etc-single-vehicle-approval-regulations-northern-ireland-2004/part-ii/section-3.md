# Motor Cycles Etc. (Single Vehicle Approval) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Application

(1) Subject to paragraph (2) these Regulations apply to diesel mopeds, electric mopeds, mopeds, motor cycles, motor tricycles, quadricycles and 350kg quadricycles.

(2) These Regulations do not apply to –

- (a) any diesel moped, electric moped or 350kg quadricycle which is manufactured before 9th November 2003; or
- (b) vehicles of a description referred to in the second paragraph of Article 1(1) of Directive 2002/24/EC as being those to which the Directive does not apply.
