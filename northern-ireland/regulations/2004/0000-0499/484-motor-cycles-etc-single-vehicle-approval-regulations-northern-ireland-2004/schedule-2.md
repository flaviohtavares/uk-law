## SCHEDULE 2 — APPROVAL REQUIREMENTS FOR RELEVANT VEHICLES

Regulation 4

A reference to a numbered paragraph in column 4 or 5 in relation to an item in column 1 (unless otherwise provided) means a reference to the paragraph so numbered in column 3 in relation to that item and expressions in bold type are defined in column 4.

<tr><td>2</td><td>Speedometers</td><td>The vehicle shall be fitted with a speedometer capable of indicating speed in miles per hour at uniform intervals not exceeding the <strong>relevant miles per hour</strong> at all speeds up to the <strong>maximum speed</strong> of the vehicle and be capable of being read by the driver at all times of the day or night.</td><td>For the purposes of this item –<br>“maximum speed” has the meaning given in regulation 2(1) of the Construction and Use Regulations; and<br>“relevant miles per hour” means –<br>(a) in the case of a <strong>low power moped</strong>, 10 miles per hour; and<br>(b) in the case of other relevant vehicles, 20 miles per hour; and, for the purposes of this definition, “low power moped” means a moped fitted with pedals and an auxiliary engine of a power output not exceeding 1 kilowatt with a maximum speed not exceeding 25 km/h.</td></tr>
<tr><td>3</td><td>Wipers and washers</td><td>1. The vehicle shall be fitted with one or more wipers which, when switched on, operate automatically to clear the windscreen of water so that the driver has an adequate view of the road ahead, both directly in front of the vehicle and in front of both sides of the vehicle, in wet weather.<br>2. The wiper or wipers shall have at least one operating frequency at at which it or they shall operate continuously at no less than 40 cycles per minute.<br>3. The wiper blade or blades shall be capable of being displaced from the windscreen to allow for windscreen cleaning.<br>4. The vehicle shall be fitted with a windscreen washer system capable of cleaning, in conjunction with the wiper or wipers, the area of windscreen swept by the wiper or wipers.<br>5. <tr><td>4</td><td>Audible warning</td><td>The vehicle shall be fitted with a <strong>horn</strong> which when operated emits a continuous uniform sound that is not strident and is capable of giving audible warning of the approach or position of the vehicle to which it is fitted.</td><td>For the purposes of this item –<br>“horn” means an audible warning device not being a bell, gong or siren.</td><td></td></tr>
<tr><td>5</td><td>Defrosting and de-misting system</td><td>1. The vehicle shall be equipped with a system for removing mist from the interior glazed surface of the windscreen which shall be sufficiently effective to ensure that the driver has an adequate view of the road ahead, both directly in front of the vehicle and in front of both sides of the vehicle, in wet and humid weather.</td><td>For the purposes of this item –<br>“body” has the same meaning as that expression has for the purposes of item 3 of this Schedule.</td><td>There are excepted –<br>(a) vehicles with less than three wheels;<br>(b) vehicles not having a <strong>body</strong>;<br>(c) three-wheel mopeds having an engine developing not more than 4kW; and<br>(d) vehicles not equipped with a windscreen through which the driver must look to obtain an adequate view of the road ahead, both directly in front of the vehicle and in front of both sides of the vehicle, from all normal seating positions.</td></tr>

Every re-treaded tyre fitted to the vehicle shall bear a mark in accordance with paragraph 6 of <strong>BS AU 144e: 1988</strong>, ECE Regulation 108 or 109 or a <strong>corresponding standard</strong> and the indication given by the mark must not be false in a material particular.<br>3. Each tyre fitted to the vehicle shall bear marks that were moulded onto or into the tyre at the time that it was manufactured which provide the following service information –<br>(a) nominal size;<br>(b) construction type (e.g. radial ply);<br>(c) load capacity; and<br>(d) speed capability.<br>4. Tyres on the same axle of the vehicle shall be of the same nominal size and of the same construction type and if radial ply tyres are fitted to the front axle, they shall also be fitted to the rear axle and if bias-belted tyres are fitted to the front axle, radial ply or bias-belted tyres shall be fitted to the rear axle.<br>6. <tr><td>7</td><td>Glazing in windscreens and other windows</td><td>Every <strong>window</strong> of the vehicle shall be fitted with transparent material which complies with the requirements applicable to “safety glazing” in <strong>Directive <a href="https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1997/0024">97/24/EC</a></strong>, ECE Regulation 43 or Council Directive <a href="https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0022">92/22/EEC</a> of 31st March 1992 on safety glazing and glazing materials on motor vehicles and their trailers.</td><td>For the purposes of this item –<br>“body” has the same meaning as that expression has for the purposes of item 3 of this Schedule;<br>“Directive <a href="https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1997/0024">97/24/EC</a>” has the same meaning as that expression has for the purposes of item 6 of this Schedule.<br>“window” includes a windscreen through which the driver must look to obtain an adequate view of the road ahead, both directly in front of the vehicle and in front of both sides of the vehicle, from all normal seating positions and a <strong>partition</strong>; and, for the purposes of this definition, “partition” means a panel comprising of transparent material including one separating the driver of a vehicle from any seating intended for passengers therein.</td><td>There is excepted –<br>(a) a vehicle which has a <strong>body</strong>;<br>(b) the transparent material fitted to a <strong>window</strong> of the vehicle if it provides a level of safety equivalent to transparent material which complies with the requirements referred to in column 3 of this item.</td></tr>
<tr><td>8</td><td>General vehicle construction</td><td>1. weight of any one axle equals its maximum permitted axle weight –<br>(a) it shall be possible to control the vehicle safely, taking account of the vehicle’s speed and acceleration capabilities;<br>(b) it shall at all times be possible for the driver, while controlling the vehicle, to have a full view of the road and traffic ahead of the vehicle;<br>(c) the clearance between any of the following parts of the vehicle, namely the tyres, wheels, other rotating components associated with the transmission of engine power or braking, moveable parts of the steering mechanism and fixed parts shall be sufficient under all conditions of suspension travel and steered wheel angles to ensure no risk of fouling and the wheels and wheel fixings shall be compatible;<br>(d) the vehicle structure (frame, chassis or integral chassis-body), shall be so designed and constructed as to withstand the forces and vibration to which it is likely to be subject;<br>(e) all entries and exits provided for the vehicle occupants (other than doors) shall be so designed as to allow easy and safe use;<br>(f) all aperture covers (other than doors) including tail gates, boot and bonnet lids, shall be capable of being securely latched in the fully closed position;<br>(g) the steering, suspension, axles and wheels shall be so designed, constructed and fitted as to withstand the forces and vibration to which which it is likely to be subject;<br>(ii) fitted so as to avoid the risk of damage, such as abrasion, due to fouling of other parts, and to minimise the risk of fire in the event of any leakage of electrical system including the battery, electrical components and wires, shall –<br>(i) be securely attached to the vehicle;<br>(ii) be so constructed and fitted as to minimise the risk of corrosion and fire;<br>(k) the wires of the electrical system shall be –<br>(i) attached in such manner that the maximum distance between attachments thereof, except those contained within hollow components, <tr><td>9</td><td>Noise and silencers</td><td>1. The vehicle shall be fitted with an exhaust system including a silencer such that the exhaust gases shall not escape into the atmosphere without first passing through the silencer.<br>2. <tr><td>10</td><td>Devices to prevent unauthorised use</td><td>1. The vehicle shall be fitted with an <strong>anti-theft device</strong> intended to prevent the unauthorised use of the vehicle.<br>2. The <strong>anti-theft device</strong> fitted to the vehicle must be such that –<br>(a) it does not act on any part of the braking system; and<br>(b) when the vehicle is set in motion and the engine is running there is no likelihood of accidental jamming which could, in particular, constitute a safety hazard.<br>3. If the <strong>anti-theft device</strong> fitted to the vehicle is a, or incorporates any, mechanical device it –<br>(a) must act positively by locking the steering or transmission system or both;<br>(b) must be such that it shall be necessary to disable it in order to point, drive or move the vehicle straight ahead; and<br>(c) may engage at the same time as the device which switches off the engine.</td><td>For the purposes of this item –<br>“anti-theft device” means a mechanical, electrical or electronic immobiliser or a combination of these (in addition to any key operated switch normally used to start the engine) that prevents the vehicle being driven or moved under its own power.</td><td>Paragraph 1 shall not apply to a diesel moped, electric moped, or moped.<br>Paragraphs 2 and 3 shall not apply to a diesel moped, electric moped, or moped to which an anti-theft device is not fitted.</td></tr>
<tr><td>11</td><td>Handholds</td><td>Where provision is made for the carriage of a passenger the vehicle shall be fitted with a passenger hand-hold system which may take the form of a strap or hand-grip.</td><td></td><td>There are excepted –<br>(a) vehicles with three or more wheels; and<br>(b) any sidecar which is attached to, or forms part of, the vehicle.</td></tr>
<tr><td>12</td><td>Stands</td><td>1. <tr><td>14</td><td>External projections for unbodied vehicles</td><td>1. <tr><td>24</td><td>Smoke emissions</td><td>The <strong>coefficient of absorption</strong> of the exhaust emissions from the engine by which the vehicle is propelled shall not, when subject to the <strong>free-acceleration test</strong>, exceed –<br>(a) if the engine of the vehicle is turbo-charged, 3.0 per metre, or<br>(b) in any other case, 2.5 per metre.</td><td>For the purposes of this item –<br>“co-efficient of absorption” shall be construed in accordance with paragraph 3.5 of Appendix 4 of Annex III to chapter 5 to <strong>Directive <a href="https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1997/0024">97/24/EC</a></strong>; and, for the purposes of this definition and that of “free acceleration test” below,<br>“Directive <a href="https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1997/0024">97/24/EC</a>” has the same meaning as that expression has for the purposes of item 6 of this Schedule;<br>“effective date” has the same meaning as that expression has for the purposes of item 23 of this Schedule; and<br>“free acceleration test” has the same meaning as in Annex III of Chapter 5 to <strong>Directive <a href="https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1997/0024">97/24/EC</a></strong>.</td><td>This item shall not apply to –<br>(a) a vehicle propelled other than by a compression ignition engine;<br>(b) a vehicle having an <strong>effective date</strong> before 17th June 1993.</td></tr>
<tr><td>25</td><td>Brakes</td><td>1. The vehicle shall comply with the requirements of paragraph 2.1 and 2.2 of the Annex to <strong>Directive <a href="https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0014">93/14/EEC</a></strong>.<br>2. If the vehicle is equipped with an <strong>anti-lock device</strong> it shall comply with paragraphs 3.1 to 3.3 of Appendix 2 of the Annex to <strong>Directive <a href="https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0014">93/14/EEC</a></strong>.<br>3. The <strong>brake efficiency</strong> shall be not less than –<br>(a) for <strong>braking devices</strong> operating on the front axle –<br>(i) in the case of motorcycles, 40%;<br>(ii) in the case of mopeds, diesel mopeds and electric mopeds, 32%;<br>(iii) in the case of motor tricycles falling within paragraph 2 of Schedule 1, <strong>handle bar steered quadricycles</strong> and <strong>motorcycle- derived vehicles</strong>, 28.5%;<br>(b) for <strong>braking devices</strong> operating on the rear axle –<br>(i) in the case of motor cycles, 27%;<br>(ii) in the case of mopeds, diesel mopeds and electric mopeds 25%;<br>(iii) in the case of motor tricycles falling within paragraph 2 of Schedule 1, <strong>handle bar steered quadricycles</strong> and <strong>motorcycle- derived vehicles</strong>, 28.5%;<br>(c) for vehicles where one or more of the braking devices is a <strong>combined braking system</strong> –<br>(i) in the case of the <strong>service braking</strong>, 50%;<br>(ii) in the case of the remaining system, 23%;<br>(d) for the <strong>parking braking device</strong> (if fitted), 18%.<br>4. The <strong>lateral brake distribution</strong> for the <strong>service braking</strong> shall –<br>(a) in relation to each steering axle; and<br>(b) for all values of <strong>total braking force</strong> and vehicle speed, be not less than 70%.<br>5. The capacity of the <strong>braking device</strong> to absorb and dissipate energy shall be sufficient to ensure that all the required aspects of braking performance can be maintained under all normal operating conditions of the vehicle, including long hill descents, taking account of the vehicle’s speed capability, weight and weight distribution.</td><td>For the purpose of this item –<br>“anti-lock device”, “braking device”, “combined braking system”, “parking braking device”, and “service braking” have the same meanings respectively as those expressions have in Appendix 2 to the Annex to <strong>Directive <a href="https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0014">93/14/EEC</a></strong>;<br>“brake efficiency” means <strong>maximum total brake force</strong> expressed as a percentage of the <strong>maximum mass</strong> and for the purposes of this definition<br>“maximum mass” has the same meaning as that expression has in the Annex to <strong>Directive <a href="https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0014">93/14/EEC</a></strong>;<br>“Directive <a href="https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0014">93/14/EEC</a>” means Council Directive <a href="https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0014">93/14/EEC</a> of 3rd April 1993 on the braking of two or three-wheel motor vehicles;<br>“handle bar steered quadricycle” means a quadricycle or 350kg quadricycle which is a <strong>saddle seat vehicle</strong> the method of steering control of which is by handle bar and for the purposes of this definition “saddle seat vehicle” has the same meaning as that expression has for the purposes of item 19 of this Schedule;<br>“lateral brake distribution”, in relation to an axle means the ratio (expressed as a percentage) of the lower to the higher of the braking forces transmitted by the tyres to the road surface for two wheels, on opposite sides of the vehicle on the same axle;<br>“low power moped” has the same meaning as that expression has for the purposes of the definition of “relevant miles per hour” in item 2 of this Schedule;<br>“maximum total brake force” means the <strong>total braking force</strong> when the brakes are fully applied;<br>“motorcycle-derived vehicle” has the same meaning as that expression has for the purposes of item 17 of this Schedule;<br>“total braking force” means the total of the braking forces transmitted by the tyres to the road surface when the brakes are applied.</td><td>The <strong>brake efficiencies</strong> referred to in paragraph 3.3 of Appendix 2 of the Annex to <strong>Directive <a href="https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0014">93/14/EEC</a></strong> shall not apply to the brakes of a relevant vehicle.<br>The requirement of paragraph 2.2.8 of the Annex to <strong>Directive <a href="https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0014">93/14/EEC</a></strong> shall not apply to <strong>low power mopeds</strong>.<br>Paragraph 1 shall not apply to a <strong>handle bar steered quadricycle</strong> or a <strong>motorcycle-derived vehicle</strong> in so far as it requires such vehicle to be fitted with other than –<br>(a) two independent <strong>service braking devices</strong> which together actuate the brakes on all of the wheels; or<br>(b) a foot-controlled <strong>service braking device</strong> which operates on all of the wheels.</td></tr>
