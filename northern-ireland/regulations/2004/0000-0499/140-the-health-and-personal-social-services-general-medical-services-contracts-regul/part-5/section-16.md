# The Health and Personal Social Services (General Medical Services Contracts) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004, section 16

> Text as at 2023-11-06.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 16. Additional services[^c-566264fe13de4c63be298990acd59b4e]

*Extent: N.I.*

A contract which includes the provision of any additional services must –

- (a) in relation to all such services as are included in the contract, contain a term which has the same effect as that specified in paragraph 1 of Schedule 1; and
- (b) in relation to each such service as is included in the contract, contain terms which have the same effect as those specified in Schedule 1 which are relevant to that service.
