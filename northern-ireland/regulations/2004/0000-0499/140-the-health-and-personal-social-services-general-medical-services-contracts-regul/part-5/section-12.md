> Text as at 2023-11-06.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 12. Health and social services contract[^key-6c6a5a04d06d5aa42b55592817ebb14c]

*Extent: N.I.*

If the contractor is to be regarded as a health services body pursuant to regulation 10, the contract must state that it is an HSS contract.
