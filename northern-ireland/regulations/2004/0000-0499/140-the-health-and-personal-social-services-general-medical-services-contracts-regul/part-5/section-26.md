# The Health and Personal Social Services (General Medical Services Contracts) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004, section 26

> Text as at 2023-11-06.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 26. Other contractual terms[^c-ee26f23c4fea570e654ec1a0d6c2c1f2]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A contract must, unless it is of a type or nature to which a particular provision does not apply, contain other terms which have the same effect as those specified in Schedule 5 except paragraphs 31(6) to (8), 35(5) to (9), 36(5) to (17), 93(5) to (14) and 94.

(2) The paragraphs specified in paragraph (1) shall have effect in relation to the matters set out in those paragraphs.
