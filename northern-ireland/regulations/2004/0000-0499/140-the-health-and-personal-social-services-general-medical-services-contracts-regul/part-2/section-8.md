## 8. Prescribed period under Article 15C(b)(iii) of the Order[^key-31edfa5264b7ebd7b5e6679021ff42f6]

*Extent: N.I.*

The period prescribed for the purposes of Article 15C(1)(b)(iii) of the Order (persons with whom agreements may be made)[^f00042] is six months.
