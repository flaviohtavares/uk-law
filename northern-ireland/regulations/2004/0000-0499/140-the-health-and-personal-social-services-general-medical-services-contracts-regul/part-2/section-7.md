# The Health and Personal Social Services (General Medical Services Contracts) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004, section 7

> Text as at 2023-11-06.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 7. Appeal[^c-6b3e70307d1bc3b078f2daa4644e4765]

*Extent: N.I.*

A person who has been served with a notice under regulation 6(1) may appeal to the Family Practitioner Services Independent Appeal Panel[^c-0d83cb320dfeb65ee67419bbc2a0e28a] against the decision of the Department[^c-d51dc2c009030c26ad0dabecc523cd49] by giving notice in writing to the Family Practitioner Services Independent Appeal Panel within the period of 28 days beginning on the day that the Department served its notice.
