# The Health and Personal Social Services (General Medical Services Contracts) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004, SCHEDULE 3

> Text as at 2023-11-06.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 3 — LIST OF PRESCRIBED MEDICAL CERTIFICATES[^key-eb4e06b766ef5fd634863e762c3041b4]

*Extent: N.I.*

Regulation 21

| Description of medical certificate | Statutory provisions under or for the purpose of which certificate required |
| --- | --- |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**1.** To support a claim or to obtain payment either personally or by proxy; to prove inability to work or incapacity for self-support for the purposes of an award by the Department; or to enable proxy to draw pensions etc. | Naval and Marine Pay and Pensions Act 1865[^f00050] Air Force (Constitution) Act 1917[^f00051]<br>Pensions (Navy, Army, Air Force and Mercantile Marine) Act 1939[^f00052]<br>Personal Injuries (Emergency Provisions) Act 1939[^f00053]<br>Pensions (Mercantile Marine) Act 1942[^f00054]<br>Polish Resettlement Act 1947[^f00055]<br>Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992[^f00056]<br>Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992[^f00057] |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**2.** To establish pregnancy for the purpose of obtaining welfare foods | Article 13 of the Social Security (Northern Ireland) Order 1988[^f00058] |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**3.** To secure registration of still-births. | Article 15 of the Births and Deaths Registration (Northern Ireland) Order 1976[^f00059] |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**4.** To enable payment to be made to an institution or other person in case of mental disorder of persons entitled to payment from public funds. | Article 128 of the Mental Health (Northern Ireland) Order 1986[^f00060] |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**5.** To establish unfitness for jury service | Juries (Northern Ireland) Order 1996[^f00061] |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**6.** To support late application for reinstatement in civil employment or notification of non-availability to take up employment owing to sickness. | Reserve Forces (Safeguarding of Employment) Act 1985[^f00062]<br>Reserve Forces Act 1980[^f00063]<br>Reserve Forces Act 1996[^f00064] |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**7.** To enable a person to be registered as an absent voter on grounds of physical incapacity | Representation of the People Act 1985[^f00065] Northern Ireland Assembly Elections Order 1982[^f00066]<br>The Local Elections (Northern Ireland) Order 1985[^f00067] |
| *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*<br>**8.** To support applications for certificates conferring exemption from charges in respect of drugs, medicines and appliances. | Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1972[^f00068] |
