# Companies (1986 Order) (Insurance Companies Accounts) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1994, section 5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5. Minor and consequential amendments

The 1986 Order shall have effect subject to the amendments specified in Schedule 2 to these Regulations (being minor amendments and amendments consequential on the provisions of these Regulations).

**5.** Regulation 6 specifies certain companies which, under the terms of the Directive, may continue to prepare accounts under Part VIII of the 1986 Order, as it applies to insurance companies and groups, without the amendments effected by these Regulations.
