# Companies (1986 Order) (Insurance Companies Accounts) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1994, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Insurance companies

(1) In paragraph (2) of Article 263 of the 1986 Order[^f00003] (special provisions for banking and insurance companies), for the word “may” there shall be substituted the word “shall”.

(2) Paragraph (5) of that Article shall be omitted.

**2.** Regulations 2 and 3 amend Articles 263 and 263A of the 1986 Order which make special provisions for the accounts of banking and insurance companies and groups. They introduce a requirement for insurance companies to prepare accounts in accordance with the new Schedule 9A, and define which companies are to prepare group accounts under the special provisions with respect to insurance groups.
