# Companies (1986 Order) (Insurance Companies Accounts) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1994, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. Form and content of accounts

For Schedule 9A to the 1986 Order[^f00005] there shall be substituted Schedule 1 to these Regulations (form and content of accounts of insurance companies and groups).

**4.** Regulation 5 of, and Schedule 2 to, the Regulations make minor and consequential amendments to the 1986 Order.

## Annotations

[^f00005]: Footnote: Parts I and II of Schedule 9 to the 1986 Order were formed into a new Schedule numbered 9A by regulation 5(l) of the Companies (1986 Order) (Bank Accounts) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992
