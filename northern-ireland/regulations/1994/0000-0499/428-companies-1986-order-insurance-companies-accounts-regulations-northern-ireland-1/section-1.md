# Companies (1986 Order) (Insurance Companies Accounts) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1994, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Companies (1986 Order) (Insurance Companies Accounts) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1994, and shall come into operation on 16th December 1994.

(2) In these Regulations “the 1986 Order” means the Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986.

**1.** These Regulations implement Council Directive [91/674/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0674) Official Journal No. L374 of 31.12.91, pages 7 to 31, on the annual accounts and consolidated accounts of insurance undertakings, in so far as that Directive is applicable to bodies corporate to which Part VIII of the Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986 (“the 1986 Order”) applies.
