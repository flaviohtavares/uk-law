# The Animal Health and Welfare (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**6.** (1) Schedule 4 amends the Zoonotic Disease Provisions.

(2) For the purposes of these Regulations, “the Zoonotic Disease Provisions” means—

- (a) the Diseases of Poultry Scheme Order (Northern Ireland) 1995[^f00030];
- (b) the Diseases of Poultry Order (Northern Ireland) 1995[^f00031];
- (c) the Tuberculosis Control Order (Northern Ireland) 1999[^f00032];
- (d) the Control of Salmonella in Poultry Scheme Order (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00033];
- (e) the Zoonoses (Monitoring) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00034];
- (f) the Scrapie (Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00035];
- (g) the Control of Salmonella in Broiler Flocks Scheme Order (Northern Ireland) 2009[^f00036];
- (h) the Control of Salmonella in Turkey Flocks Scheme Order (Northern Ireland) 2010[^f00037]; and
- (i) the Zoonoses (Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011[^f00038].
