# The Animal Health and Welfare (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**7.** (1) Schedule 5 amends the Trade Related Provisions.

(2) For the purposes of these Regulations, “the Trade Related Provisions” means—

- (a) the Rabies (Importation of Dogs, Cats and Other Mammals) Order (Northern Ireland) 1977[^f00039];
- (b) the Artificial Insemination of Cattle Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1988[^f00040];
- (c) the Artificial Insemination of Pigs (EEC) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1993[^f00041];
- (d) the Animals (Post–Import Control) Order (Northern Ireland) 1993[^f00042];
- (e) the Artificial Breeding of Sheep and Goats (EEC) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1994[^f00043];
- (f) the Bovine Embryo Collection, Production and Transplantation Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00044];
- (g) the Official Controls (Animals, Feed and Food) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00045];
- (h) the Trade in Animals and Related Products Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011[^f00046];
- (i) the Non-Commercial Movement of Pet Animals Order (Northern Ireland) 2011[^f00047]: and
- (j) the Animal By-Products (Enforcement) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015[^f00048].
