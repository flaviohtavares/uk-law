## SCHEDULE 3 — Amendments to the Exotic Disease Provisions

Regulation 5(1)

### PART 1 — Amendment to Primary Legislation

#### 1. Amendments to the Diseases of Animals (Northern Ireland) Order 1981

In the Diseases of Animals (Northern Ireland) Order 1981, in Schedule 2 in Part 1 (power to slaughter)—

- (a) in paragraph 3A(1), after “foot-and-mouth disease” insert “, as last amended by Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2018/1099[^f00049]”; and
- (b) in paragraph 3A(3)(b)(ii), after “1990/425/EEC” insert “, as last amended by Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2017/2174[^f00050]”.
