# The Animal Health and Welfare (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**4.** (1) Schedule 2 amends the Welfare Provisions.

(2) For the purposes of the these Regulations, “the Welfare Provisions” means—

- (a) the Welfare of Animals (Transport) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006[^f00010];
- (b) the Welfare of Animals (Permitted Procedures by Lay Persons) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012[^f00011]; and
- (c) the Welfare of Farmed Animals Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012[^f00012].
