# The Animal Health and Welfare (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019, SCHEDULE 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 2 — Amendments to the Welfare Provisions

Regulation 4(1)

### 1. Amendments to the Welfare of Animals (Transport) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006

In the Welfare of Animals (Transport) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, in regulation 2(1) (interpretation)—

- (a) in the definition of “Council Regulation 1/2005”, after “93/119/EC” insert “and Regulation [(EC) No 1255/97](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1997/1255)”; and
- (b) in the definition of “Council Regulation 1255/97”, after “91/628/EEC” insert “, as last amended by Council Regulation [(EC) No. 1/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/0001)”.

### 2. Amendment to the Welfare of Animals (Permitted Procedures by Lay Persons) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012

In the Welfare of Animals (Permitted Procedures by Lay Persons) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012, in regulation 2(1) (interpretation), in the definition of “conventionally reared meat chicken”, in paragraph (c), after “2092/91” insert “, as last amended by Council Regulation (EU) No 517/2013”.

### 3. Amendments to the Welfare of Farmed Animals Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012

The Welfare of Farmed Animals Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012 are amended as follows.

**4.** In regulation 2(1) (interpretation), in the definition of “conventionally reared meat chicken”, in paragraph (d), after “2092/1991” insert “, as last amended by Council Regulation (EU) No 517/2013”.

**5.** In Schedule 5 (additional conditions that apply to the keeping of conventionally reared meat chickens), in paragraph 1 (interpretation)—

- (a) in the definition of “Regulation 853/2004”, after “animal origin” insert “, as last amended by Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/1981”; and
- (b) in the definition of “Regulation 854/2004”, after “human consumption” insert “, as last amended by Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/1979”.
