# The Animal Health and Welfare (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019, section 5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**5.** (1) Schedule 3 amends the Exotic Disease Provisions.

(2) For the purposes of these Regulations, “the Exotic Disease Provisions” means—

- (a) the Diseases of Animals (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00013];
- (b) the Bovine Leucosis Scheme Order (Northern Ireland) 1995[^f00014];
- (c) the African Swine Fever Order (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00015];
- (d) the Brucellosis Control Order (Northern Ireland) 2004[^f00016];
- (e) the Brucellosis (Examination and Testing) Scheme Order (Northern Ireland) 2004[^f00017];
- (f) the Foot-and-Mouth Disease Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006[^f00018];
- (g) the Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Control of Vaccination) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006[^f00019];
- (h) the Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00020];
- (i) the Avian Influenza (Vaccination) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00021];
- (j) the Avian Influenza (Preventive Measures) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00022];
- (k) the Avian Influenza (H5N1 in Poultry) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00023];
- (l) the Avian Influenza (H5N1 in Wild Birds) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00024];
- (m) the Diseases of Animals (Approval of Disinfectants) Order (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00025];
- (n) the Bluetongue Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00026];
- (o) the Products of Animal Origin (Disease Control) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00027];
- (p) the Aujeszky’s Disease Order (Northern Ireland) 2012[^f00028]; and
- (q) the African Horse Sickness Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013[^f00029].
