# The Animal Health and Welfare (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**3.** (1) Schedule 1 amends the Identification Provisions.

(2) For the purposes of these Regulations, “the Identification Provisions” means—

- (a) the Cattle Identification (Enforcement) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1998[^f00005]
- (b) the Cattle Identification (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1998[^f00006];
- (c) the Cattle Identification (Notification of Births, Deaths and Movements) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999[^f00007];
- (d) the Cattle Passport Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999[^f00008]; and
- (e) the Sheep and Goats (Records, Identification and Movement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2009[^f00009].
