# The Waste Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2019 No. 240

Environmental Protection, Northern Ireland

The Waste Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019

Made

*23* December 2019

Coming into operation-

*16* January 2020

The Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00001].

The Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs[^f00002] is designated[^f00003] in relation to measures relating to the prevention, reduction and elimination of pollution caused by waste and in relation to the environment.

## PART 1 — Introductory

### 1. Citation, commencement and extent

These Regulations may be cited as the Waste Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019 and shall come into operation on 16 January 2020.

**2.** These Regulations extend to Northern Ireland only.

## PART 2 — Amendment of primary legislation

### 3. Amendment of the Waste and Contaminated Land (Northern Ireland) Order 1997

The Waste and Contaminated Land (Northern Ireland) Order 1997[^f00004] is amended as follows.

**4.** In Article 2(2), for “strategy” substitute “plan”.

**5.** In Article 19 in the heading, paragraph (1), (2)(a), (2)(b), (3), (4), (4A), (5), (5)(b)(iii) and (8), for “strategy” substitute “plan”.

**6.** In Article 19A in paragraph (1), (2)(a) and (2)(b), for “strategy” substitute “plan”.

**7.** In Article 19B in paragraph (1), (1)(b)(i) and (1)(b)(ii), for “strategy” substitute “plan”.

**8.** In Article 19C in paragraph (1), (2), (2)(c), (3) and (4)(a), for “strategy” substitute “plan”.

**9.** In Article 23(2)(a), for “strategy” substitute “plan”.

**10.** In Schedule 3—

- (a) in the heading of Schedule 3, for “STRATEGY” substitute “PLAN”;
- (b) the heading of Part 2, for “strategy” substitute “plan”;
- (c) in paragraph (5), (5)(b), (6), (7) and (8), for “strategy” substitute “plan”;
- (d) in the heading of Part 3, for “strategy” substitute “plan”; and
- (e) in paragraph (9), for “strategy” substitute “plan”.

### 11. Amendment of the Environment (Northern Ireland) Order 2002

The Environment (Northern Ireland) Order 2002[^f00005] is amended as follows.

**12.** In Article 7(1)(a), for “strategy” substitute “waste management plan”.

### 13. Amendment of the Waste and Emissions Trading Act 2003

The Waste and Emissions Trading Act 2003[^f00006] is amended as follows.

**14.** In section 20—

- (a) in the heading for “Strategy” substitute “Plan”; and
- (b) in paragraph (1) and (2), for “strategy” substitute “plan”.

## PART 3 — Amendment of subordinate legislation

### 15. Amendment of the Waste Management Licensing Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003

The Waste Management Licensing Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00007] are amended as follows.

**16.** In Schedule 3 in paragraph 3(1), Table 13, for “strategy” substitute “plan”.

### 17. Amendment of the Hazardous Waste Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005

The Hazardous Waste Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005[^f00008] are amended as follows.

**18.** In regulation 47(2)—

- (a) for “ waste strategy” substitute “ waste management plan”; and
- (b) for “strategy” substitute “plan”.

### 19. Amendment of the Producer Responsibility Obligations (Packaging Waste) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

The Producer Responsibility Obligations (Packaging Waste) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00009] are amended as follows.

**20.** In regulation 4(4)(d)(iv)––

- (a) for “strategy” substitute “plan”; and
- (b) for “18” substitute “19”.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs on 23 December 2019.

John Mills

A senior officer of the Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations are made in exercise of the powers in section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00010] in order to ensure that the requirements of the revised Waste Framework Directive[^f00011] have been met.

Part 2 makes amendments to primary legislation in order to ensure that the requirements of the revised Waste Framework Directive have been met.

Part 3 makes amendments to subordinate legislation in order to ensure that the requirements of the revised Waste Framework Directive have been met.
