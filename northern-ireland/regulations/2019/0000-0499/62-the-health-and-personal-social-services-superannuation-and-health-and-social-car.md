# The Health and Personal Social Services (Superannuation) and Health and Social Care Pension Scheme (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2019 No. 62

Health And Personal Social Services

Public Service Pensions

The Health and Personal Social Services (Superannuation) and Health and Social Care Pension Scheme (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019

Made

26th March 2019

Coming into operation

1st April 2019

The Department of Health makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 12(1), (2) and (2A), 14(1) and (2) of, and Schedule 3 to, the Superannuation (Northern Ireland) Order 1972[^f00001] and sections 1(1) and (2)(e) and 3 of, and paragraph 5 of Schedule 2 and Schedule 3 to, the Public Service Pensions Act (Northern Ireland) 2014[^f00002].

In accordance with Article 12(4) of the Superannuation (Northern Ireland) Order 1972 and section 21 of the Public Service Pensions Act (Northern Ireland) 2014, the Department has consulted with representatives of such persons as appear to the Department likely to be affected by these Regulations.

In accordance with Article 12(1) of the Superannuation (Northern Ireland) Order 1972 and section 3(5) of the Public Service Pensions Act (Northern Ireland) 2014, these Regulations are made with the consent of the Department of Finance.

## PART 1 — Introductory

### 1. Citation, commencement and effect

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Health and Personal Social Services (Superannuation) and Health and Social Care Pension Scheme (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019.

(2) These Regulations shall come into operation on 1 April 2019 and save as provided in paragraph (3), have effect from that date.

(3) Regulation 7 has effect from 1 June 2016.

## PART 2 — Amendment of the Health and Personal Social Services (Superannuation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995

  > - (b) in respect of each scheme year from 2015-2016, Table 2.

  ;

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Health and Personal Social Services (Superannuation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995 ([S.R. 1995 No.95](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1995/95)) (“the 1995 Regulations”), the Health and Social Care (Pension Scheme) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008 ([S.R. 2008 No.256](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2008/256)) (“the 2008 Regulations”), the Health and Social Care Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015 ([S.R. 2015 No.120](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2015/120)) (“the 2015 Regulations”).

Part 1 provides for citation, commencement and effect, including that some provisions of this rule are to take effect before the date of commencement. Article 14(1) of the Superannuation (Northern Ireland) Order 1972 and section 3(3) of the Public Service Pensions Act (Northern Ireland) 2014 provide authority for these regulations to take effect as from a date earlier than the making of these Regulations.

Parts 2, 3 and 4 make similar amendments in respect of the 1995 Regulations, the 2008 Regulations and the 2015 Regulations in that they amend the employer contribution rate and renew the existing member contribution rates from 1 April 2019.
