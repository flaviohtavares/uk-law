# The Eggs and Chicks and Poultrymeat (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2019 No. 210

Agriculture

Food

The Eggs and Chicks and Poultrymeat (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019

Made

29th October 2019

Coming into operation

19th November 2019

The Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs[^f00001], being designated[^f00002] for the purpose of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00003] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Union in exercise of the powers conferred under that section 2(2) of that Act, makes the following Regulations.

There has been open and transparent public consultation during the preparation of these Regulations as required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council[^f00004] laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety.

## PART 1 — Introductory

### 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Eggs and Chicks and Poultrymeat (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019 and shall come into operation on 19th November 2019.

(2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00005] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

## PART 2 — Amendment of Agriculture (Food) Provisions

### 2. Amendment to the Eggs and Chicks Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010

In the Eggs and Chicks Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010[^f00006], after regulation 2(8), insert—

> (9) Any reference in these Regulations to Part VI of Annex VII of Regulation (EU) 2013 includes a reference to the equivalent transitional marketing standards under Article 116 of and Annex XIV to, the Single CMO Regulation.

.

### 3. Amendment to the Poultrymeat Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011

In the Poultrymeat Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011[^f00007], after regulation 2(4), insert—

> (5) Any reference in these Regulations to Part VI of Annex VII of Regulation (EU) 2013 includes a reference to the equivalent transitional marketing standards under Article 116 of and Annex XIV to, the Single CMO Regulation.

.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs on 29th October 2019.

Colette McMaster

A senior officer of the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

These regulations update European Union legislative references.

Part 2 amends the Eggs and Chicks Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010 and the Poultrymeat Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011 to provide that any reference to a specified EU Regulation is to be construed so as to include a reference to another specified EU Regulation
