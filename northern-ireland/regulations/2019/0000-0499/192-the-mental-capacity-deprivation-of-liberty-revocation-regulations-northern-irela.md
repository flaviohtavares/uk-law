# The Mental Capacity (Deprivation of Liberty) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2019 No. 192

Mental Capacity

The Mental Capacity (Deprivation of Liberty) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019

Made

27th September 2019

Coming into operation

1st October 2019

The Department of Health[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 14(4), 39, 42, 43, 50, 57, 58, 61(1), 79(4), 132, 276, 297 and 300 and paragraphs 5, 6, 7, 8, 14 and 19 of Schedule 1, paragraphs 1, 2, 3, 4, 7, 9, 11, 13 and 14 of Schedule 2 and paragraphs 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, and 9 of Schedule 3 to the Mental Capacity Act (Northern Ireland) 2016[^f00002], makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Mental Capacity (Deprivation of Liberty) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019 and shall come into operation on 1st October 2019.

## 2. Revocations

The following Regulations are revoked—

- (a) the Mental Capacity (Deprivation of Liberty) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019[^f00003]; and
- (b) the Mental Capacity (Deprivation of Liberty) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019[^f00004].

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health on 27th September 2019.

(L.S.)

Mark Lee

A senior officer of the Department of Health.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

These Regulations revoke the Mental Capacity (Deprivation of Liberty) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019 and the Mental Capacity (Deprivation of Liberty) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019.
