# The Dundrod Circuit (Admission Charges) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2019 No. 120

Road Traffic And Vehicles

The Dundrod Circuit (Admission Charges) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019

Made

18th June 2019

Coming into operation

16th July 2019

The Department for Infrastructure[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 3(7A)(b) of the Road Races (Northern Ireland) Order 1986[^f00002] and now exercisable by it[^f00003], and of every other power enabling it in that behalf makes the following Regulations.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as The Dundrod Circuit (Admission Charges) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019 and shall come into operation on 16th July 2019.

## 2. Charges

The maximum charge for a spectator badge in respect of each person shall be £30.00.

## 3. Revocation

The Dundrod Circuit (Admission Charges) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011[^f00004] are revoked.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Infrastructure on 18th June 2019

(L.S.)

T Reid

A senior officer of the Department for Infrastructure

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

These Regulations revoke and replace the Dundrod Circuit (Admission Charges) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011.

The Regulations increase the maximum admission charge from £25.00 to £30.00.
