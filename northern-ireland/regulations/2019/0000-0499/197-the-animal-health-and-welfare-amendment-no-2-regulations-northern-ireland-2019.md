# The Animal Health and Welfare (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2019 No. 197

Agriculture

Animals

The Animal Health and Welfare (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019

Made

8th October 2019

Coming into operation

30th October 2019

The Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs[^f00001] is designated[^f00002] for the purpose of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00003] in relation to common agricultural policy of the European Union and makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of, and paragraph 1A of Schedule 2 to, that Act.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

These Regulations may be cited as the Animal Health and Welfare (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019 and shall come into operation on 30th October 2019.

**2.** The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00004] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

## 3. Amendments to the Exotic Disease Provisions

(1) Schedule 1 amends the Exotic Disease Provisions.

(2) For the purposes of these Regulations, “the Exotic Disease Provisions” means—

- (a) the Foot-and-Mouth Disease Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006[^f00005];
- (b) the Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Control of Vaccination) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006[^f00006];
- (c) the Avian Influenza (H5N1 in Poultry) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00007]; and
- (d) the Avian Influenza (H5N1 in Wild Birds) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00008].

## 4. Amendments to the Zoonotic Disease Provisions

(1) Schedule 2 amends the Zoonotic Disease Provisions.

(2) For the purposes of these Regulations, “the Zoonotic Disease Provision” means—

- (a) the Control of Salmonella in Poultry Scheme Order (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00009];
- (b) the Scrapie (Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00010]; and
- (c) the Control of Salmonella in Broiler Flocks Scheme Order (Northern Ireland) 2009[^f00011].

## 5. Amendments to the Trade in Animals and Related Products Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011

(1) The Trade in Animals and Related Products Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011[^f00012] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2(1) (interpretation), in the definition of “product”, after “97/78/EC” insert “, as last amended by Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2016/1196”.

(3) In regulation 23(4) (action following failure of checks or seizure-animals), after “embryos of equidae” insert “, as amended by Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2018/1301”.

(4) In Schedule 3, in paragraph 10 (horses), after “embryos of equidae” insert “, as amended by Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2018/1301”.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs on 8th October 2019

Catherine Fisher

A senior officer of the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs

SCHEDULES

## SCHEDULE 1 — Amendments to the Exotic Disease Provisions

Regulation 3(1)

### 1. Amendments to the Foot-and-Mouth Disease Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006

(1) The Foot-and-Mouth Disease Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006 are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2(1) (interpretation)—

- (a) in the definition of “Regulation [(EC) No. 1069/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/1069)”, for “Council Regulation (EU) No. 1385/2013” substitute “Regulation (EU) 2019/1009”; and
- (b) in the definition of “Regulation (EU) No. 142/2011”, for “Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/1262” substitute “Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/1177”.

### 2. Amendment to the Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Control of Vaccination) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006

In regulation 2(1) (interpretation) of the Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Control of Vaccination) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, in the definition of “Database”, at the end omit “)”.

### 3. Amendments to the Avian Influenza (H5N1 in Poultry) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

(1) The Avian Influenza (H5N1 in Poultry) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2(1) (interpretation)—

- (a) in the definition of “Regulation [(EC) No. 1069/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/1069)”, for “Council Regulation (EU) No. 1385/2013” substitute “Regulation (EU) 2019/1009”; and
- (b) in the definition of “Regulation (EU) No 142/2011”, for “Council Regulation (EU) 2017/1262” substitute “Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/1177”.

### 4. Amendments to the Avian Influenza (H5N1 in Wild Birds) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

(1) The Avian Influenza (H5N1 in Wild Birds) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2(1) (interpretation)—

- (a) in the definition of “Regulation [(EC) No 854/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0854)”, for “Commission Regulation (EU) 2015/2285” substitute “Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/1979”;
- (b) in the definition of “Regulation [(EC) No. 1069/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/1069)”, for “Council Regulation (EU) No. 1385/2013” substitute “Regulation (EU) 2019/1009”; and
- (c) in the definition of “Regulation (EU) No 142/2011”, for “Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/1262” substitute “Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/1177”.

## SCHEDULE 2 — Amendments to the Zoonotic Disease Provisions

Regulation 4(1)

### 1. Amendments to the Control of Salmonella in Poultry Scheme Order (Northern Ireland) 2008

(1) The Control of Salmonella in Poultry Scheme Order (Northern Ireland) 2008 is amended as follows.

(2) In the Schedule, in paragraph 5 (competent authority)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (b), for “[Commission Regulation (EU) No 517/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2011/0517)” substitute “Commission Regulation (EU) 2019/268”; and
- (b) in sub-paragraph (c), after “200/2010” insert “, as amended by Commission Regulation (EU) 2019/268”.

### 2. Amendment to the Scrapie (Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008

In regulation 2(1) (interpretation) of the Scrapie (Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008, in the definition of “the Community TSE Regulation”, for “[Commission Regulation (EU) No 2018/96](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1996/2018)9” substitute “Commission Regulation (EU) 2019/1091”.

### 3. Amendment to the Control of Salmonella in Broiler Flocks Scheme Order (Northern Ireland) 2009

In the Control of Salmonella in Broiler Flocks Scheme Order (Northern Ireland) 2009, in the Schedule, in paragraph 3(b), after “2160/2003” insert “, as amended by Commission Regulation (EU) 2019/268”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations make minor amendments to miscellaneous legislation relating to animal disease and trade in animals and animal related products. The Regulations update provisions in that legislation which refer to EU legislation that has been amended.

An impact assessment has not been produced for these Regulations as no impact on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen.
