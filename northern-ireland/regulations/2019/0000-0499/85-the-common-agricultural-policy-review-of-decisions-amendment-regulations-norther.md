# The Common Agricultural Policy (Review of Decisions) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2019 No. 85

Agriculture

The Common Agricultural Policy (Review of Decisions) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019

Made

*11*th April 2019

Coming into operation

*13*th May 2019

The Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs is a Northern Ireland Department designated[^f00001], for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002], in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community and matters relating to the promotion of rural development.

The Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

These Regulations may be cited as the Common Agricultural Policy (Review of Decisions) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019 and shall come into operation on 13<sup>th</sup> May 2019.

**2.** The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00003] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

## 3. Amendment to the Common Agricultural Policy (Review of Decisions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015

The Common Agricultural Policy (Review of Decisions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015[^f00004] shall be amended as provided by regulation 4.

**4.** In regulation 4(4)(b) for “£100” substitute “£200”.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs on 11<sup>th</sup> April 2019.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Common Agricultural Policy (Review of Decisions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015 ([S.R. 2015 No. 318](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2015/318)).

These Regulations increase the level of fee to be paid by applicants for the review of a relevant determination made by the Department by an independent Panel.
