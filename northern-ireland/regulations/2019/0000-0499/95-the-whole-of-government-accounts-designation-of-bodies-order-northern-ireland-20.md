# The Whole of Government Accounts (Designation of Bodies) Order (Northern Ireland) 2019

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2019 No. 95

Government Resourses And Accounts

The Whole of Government Accounts (Designation of Bodies) Order (Northern Ireland) 2019

Made

1st May 2019

Coming into operation

27th May 2019

The Department of Finance [^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by section 15(1) of the Government Resources and Accounts Act (Northern Ireland) 2001[^f00002] (‘the Act’), makes the following Order.

The bodies listed in the Schedule to this Order appear to the Department of Finance (“the Department”) –

- (a) to exercise functions of a public nature; or
- (b) to be entirely or substantially funded from public money.

The Department intends the accounts under section 14 of the Act for the financial year ending with 31st March 2019 to relate in part to those bodies.

## 1. Title and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Whole of Government Accounts (Designation of Bodies) Order (Northern Ireland) 2019 and shall come into operation on 27<sup>th</sup> May 2019.

## 2. Designation of Bodies

For the purposes of section 15 of the Government Resources and Accounts Act (Northern Ireland) 2001, the Department designates the bodies listed in the Schedule in respect of the financial year ending with 31st March 2019.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance on 1<sup>st</sup> May 2019.

Stuart Stevenson

A senior officer of the Department of Finance

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates bodies, for the financial year ending with 31st March 2019, for the purposes of section 15 of the Government Resources and Accounts Act (Northern Ireland) 2001. The Department of Finance (“the Department”) intends to pass the information provided by bodies so designated in the Schedule to this Order to HM Treasury for use in the preparation of the Whole of Government Accounts (WGA). The designation enables the Department to require those bodies to provide financial information in relation to the year in question. The bodies designated include the District Councils for the 11 Local Government Districts established pursuant to the Local Government (Boundaries) Act (Northern Ireland) 2008.

WGA are financial statements prepared on the basis of Generally Accepted Accounting Practice which will cover the whole of the public sector as far as practically possible.
