# The Licensing (Northern Ireland) Order 1990, section 20

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 20. Renewal of suspended licences

The renewal of a suspended licence shall not affect the suspension.
