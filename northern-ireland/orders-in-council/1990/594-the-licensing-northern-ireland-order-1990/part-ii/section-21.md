# The Licensing (Northern Ireland) Order 1990, section 21

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 21. Continuance of licence pending determination of appeal

Where a court refuses to renew a licence and the holder of the licence appeals, the licence shall, unless suspended, continue in force until the appeal is determined or abandoned.
