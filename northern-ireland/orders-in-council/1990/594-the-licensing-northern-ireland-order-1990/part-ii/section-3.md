# The Licensing (Northern Ireland) Order 1990, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Prohibition on sale of intoxicating liquor without a licence

Except as permitted by this Order, it shall be unlawful for any person to sell intoxicating liquor by retail unless he holds a licence authorising him to do so in the course of a business carried on in premises specified in the licence.
