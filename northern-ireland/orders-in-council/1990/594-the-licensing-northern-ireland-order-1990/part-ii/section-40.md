# The Licensing (Northern Ireland) Order 1990, section 40

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 40. Inspection of register

A register of licences may be inspected and copies of all or any part of any entry in the register may be taken at all reasonable hours.
