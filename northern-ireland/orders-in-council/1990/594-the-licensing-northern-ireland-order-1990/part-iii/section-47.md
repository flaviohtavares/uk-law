# The Licensing (Northern Ireland) Order 1990, section 47

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 47. General permitted hours

(1) Subject to the succeeding provisions of this Part, the permitted hours for premises of a kind mentioned in Article 6(1)(a), (c), (d), ( f) and (g) are the hours—

- (a) on week-days, other than Good Friday or Christmas Day, from half past 11 in the morning to 11 in the evening; and
- (b) on Good Friday from 5 in the afternoon to 11 in the evening.

(2) The permitted hours for premises of a kind mentioned in Article 6(1)(b) are the hours on week-days, other than Christmas Day, from half past 9 in the morning to 9 in the evening.
