## 2. Interpretation

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^c16861671] shall apply to Article 1 and the following provisions of this Order as it applies to a Measure of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

(2) In this Order—

- “the Department” means the Department of Economic Development;
- “the Companies Order” means the Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986[^c16861681];
- *Definition rep. by 1993 c.36*
- “the Insolvency Order” means the Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989[^c16861691].
