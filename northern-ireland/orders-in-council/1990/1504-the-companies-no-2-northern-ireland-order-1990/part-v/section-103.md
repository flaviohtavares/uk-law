## 103. Power to apply provisions to other cases

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The power of the Department to make provision by regulations under—

- (a) Article 92,[^c16862771]. . . or 94 (power to extend provisions relating to market contracts), or
- (b) Article 98 (power to extend provisions relating to market charges),

includes power to apply Articles 99 to 102 to any description of property provided as cover for margin in relation to contracts in relation to which the power is exercised or, as the case may be, property subject to charges in relation to which the power is exercised.

(2) The regulations may provide that those Articles apply with such exceptions, additions and adaptations as may be specified in the regulations.
