# The Companies (No. 2) (Northern Ireland) Order 1990, section 92

## 92. Certain overseas exchanges and clearing houses

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department and the Treasury[^c16862421] may by regulations provide that this Part applies in relation to contracts connected with an overseas investment exchange or clearing house which is approved by the the Treasury under section 170 of the Companies Act 1989[^c16862461] (certain overseas exchanges and clearing houses), as it applies in relation to contracts connected with a recognised investment exchange or clearing house.

(2) The Department may make regulations which, in relation to a body which is so approved, provide that the provisions of this Part apply with such exceptions, additions and adaptations as appear to the Department to be necessary or expedient.
