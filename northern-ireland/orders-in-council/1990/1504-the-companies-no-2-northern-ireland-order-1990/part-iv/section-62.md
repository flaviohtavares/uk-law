# The Companies (No. 2) (Northern Ireland) Order 1990, section 62

## 62. “Subsidiary”, “holding company” and “wholly-owned subsidiary”

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c21247491] In Part I of the Companies Order (introductory and interpretation), for Article 4 substitute— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21247491]

> **4. “Subsidiary”, “holding company” and “wholly-owned subsidiary” (affected by repeal, see note)[^c21247491]**
>
> (1) A company is a “subsidiary” of another company, its “holding company”, if that other company— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21247491]
>
> - (a) holds a majority of the voting rights in it, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21247491]
> - (b) is a member of it and has the right to appoint or remove a majority of its board of directors, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21247491]
> - (c) is a member of it and controls alone, pursuant toan agreement with other shareholders or members, amajority of the voting rights in it, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21247491]
>
> or if it is a subsidiary of a company which is itself a subsidiary of that other company. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21247491]
>
> (2) A company is a “wholly-owned subsidiary” of another company if it has no members except that other and that other's wholly-owned subsidiaries or persons acting on behalf of that other or its wholly-owned subsidiaries. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21247491]
>
> (3) In this Article “company” includes any body corporate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21247491]
>
> **4A. Provisions supplementing Article 4 (affected by repeal, see note)[^c21247491]**
>
> (1) The provisions of this Article explain expressions used in Article 4 and otherwise supplement that Article. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21247491]
>
> (2) In Article 4(1)(a) and (c) the references to the voting rights in a company are to the rights conferred on shareholders in respect of their shares or, in the case of a company not having a share capital, on members, to vote at general meetings of the company on all, or substantially all, matters. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21247491]
>
> (3) In Article 4(1)(b) the reference to the right to appoint or remove a majority of the board of directors is to the right to appoint or remove directors holding a majority of the voting rights at meetings of the board on all, or substantially all, matters; and for the purposes of that provision— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21247491]
>
> - (a) a company shall be treated as having the right to appoint to a directorship if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21247491]
>   - (i) a person's appointment to it follows necessarily from his appointment as director of the company, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21247491]
>   - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c21247491] the directorship is held by the company itself; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21247491]
> - (b) a right to appoint or remove which is exercisable only with the consent or concurrence of another person shall be left out of account unless no other person has a right to appoint or, as the case may be, remove in relation to that directorship. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21247491]
>
> (4) Rights which are exercisable only in certain circumstances shall be taken into account only— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21247491]
>
> - (a) when the circumstances have arisen, and for so long as they continue to obtain, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21247491]
> - (b) when the circumstances are within the control of the person having the rights; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21247491]
>
> and rights which are normally exercisable but are temporarily incapable of exercise shall continue to be taken into account. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21247491]
>
> (5) Rights held by a person in a fiduciary capacity shall be treated as not held by him. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21247491]
>
> (6) Rights held by a person as nominee for another shall be treated as held by the other; and rights shall be regarded as held as nominee for another if they are exercisable only on his instructions or with his consent or concurrence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21247491]
>
> (7) Rights attached to shares held by way of security shall be treated as held by the person providing the security— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21247491]
>
> - (a) where apart from the right to exercise them for the purpose of preserving the value of the security, or of realising it, the rights are exercisable only in accordance with his instructions; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21247491]
> - (b) where the shares are held in connection with the granting of loans as part of normal business activities and apart from the right to exercise them for the purpose of preserving the value of the security, or of realising it, the rights are exercisable only in his interests. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21247491]
>
> (8) Rights shall be treated as held by a company if they are held by any of its subsidiaries; and nothing in paragraph (6) or (7) shall be construed as requiring rights held by a company to be treated as held by any of its subsidiaries. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21247491]
>
> (9) For the purposes of paragraph (7) rights shall be treated as being exercisable in accordance with the instructions or in the interests of a company if they are exercisable in accordance with the instructions of or, as the case may be, in the interests of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21247491]
>
> - (a) any subsidiary or holding company of that company, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21247491]
> - (b) any subsidiary of a holding company of that company. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21247491]
>
> (10) The voting rights in a company shall be reduced by any rights held by the company itself. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21247491]
>
> (11) References in any provision of paragraphs (5) to (10) to rights held by a person include rights falling to be treated as held by him by virtue of any other provision of those paragraphs but not rights which by virtue of any such provision are to be treated as not held by him. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21247491]
>
> (12) In this Article “company” includes any body corporate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21247491]

.

(2) Any reference in any statutory provision to a “subsidiary” or “holding company” within the meaning of Article 4 of the Companies Order shall, subject to any express amendment or saving made by or under this Order, be read as referring to a subsidiary orholding company as defined in Article 4 as substituted by paragraph(1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21247491]

This applies whether the reference is specific or general, or express or implied. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21247491]

(3) In Part I of the Companies Order (introductory and interpretation) after Article 4A insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21247491]

> **4B. Power to amend Articles 4 and 4A (affected by repeal, see note)[^c21247491]**
>
> (1) The Department may by regulations amend Articles 4 and 4A so as to alter the meaning of the expressions “holding company”, “subsidiary” or “wholly-owned subsidiary”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21247491]
>
> (2) Any amendment made by regulations under this Article does not apply for the purposes of statutory provisions outside the Companies Orders unless the regulations so provide. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21247491]
>
> (3) So much of section 29(1) of the Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 (effect of repeal and re-enactment) as relates to statutory documents shall not apply in relation to any repeal and re-enactment effected by regulations made under this Article. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21247491]

.

*Para. (4)—amendments and savings *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21247491]*

(5) So much of section 29(1) of the Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21247491][^c16861791] (effect of repeal and re-enactment) as relates to statutory documents shall not apply in relation to the repeal and re-enactment by this Article of Article 4 of the Companies Order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21247491]
