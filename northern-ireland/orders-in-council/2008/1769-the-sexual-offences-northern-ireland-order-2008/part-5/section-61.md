# The Sexual Offences (Northern Ireland) Order 2008, section 61

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 61. Persistent soliciting[^c22523881]

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2010-04-01).**

(1) A person (A) commits an offence if in a street or other public place he persistently solicits another person (or different persons) for the purpose of prostitution.

(2) A person guilty of an offence under this Article shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale.

(3) Any reference in this Article to A soliciting another person for the purposes of prostitution is a reference to A soliciting that other person to provide services as a prostitute to A.
