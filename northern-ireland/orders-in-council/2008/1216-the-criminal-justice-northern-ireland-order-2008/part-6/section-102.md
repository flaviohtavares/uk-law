# The Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2008, section 102

## 102. Amendments and repeals[^c19282091]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The statutory provisions set out in Schedule 5 have effect subject to the minor and consequential amendments specified in that Schedule.

(2) The statutory provisions set out in Schedule 6 are repealed to the extent specified in the second column of that Schedule.
