## 58. Furious driving

*Extent: N.I.*

In Part 2 of Schedule 1 to the Road Traffic Offenders (Northern Ireland) Order 1996 (NI 10) (prosecution and punishment of offences: offences otherwise than under the Road Traffic Orders), after the entry relating to manslaughter insert—

> |  |  |  |  |
> | --- | --- | --- | --- |
> | An offence under section 35 of the Offences against the Person Act 1861 (furious driving). | Discretionary. | Obligatory if committed in respect of a mechanically propelled vehicle. | 3–9 |
