## 56. Driving while disqualified

*Extent: N.I.*

In Part 1 of Schedule 1 to the Road Traffic Offenders (Northern Ireland) Order 1996 (NI 10) (prosecution and punishment of offences: offences under the Road Traffic Orders), for the entry relating to Article 168A(1)(c) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981 substitute—

> |  |  |  |  |  |  |  |
> | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
> | Article 168A(1)(c) | Driving while disqualified. | - (a) Summarily.<br>- (b) On indictment | - (a) The statutory maximum or 6 months or both.<br>- (b) A fine or 2 years or both. | Discretionary. | Obligatory. | 6 |
