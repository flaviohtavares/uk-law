## 73. Removal of requirement to appoint medical officers

*Extent: N.I.*

In section 2(2) of the Prison Act (Northern Ireland) 1953 (c. 18) (requirement to appoint governors, medical officers and other officers) the words “, medical officers” shall cease to have effect.
