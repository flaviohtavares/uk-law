## 93. Applications for interim order

*Extent: N.I.*

In the Anti-social Behaviour (Northern Ireland) Order 2004 (NI 12) in Article 4 (interim orders) after paragraph (1) insert—

> (1A) An application by a relevant authority for an order under this Article may be made without notice being given to the defendant.
