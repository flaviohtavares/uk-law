# The Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2008, section 74

## 74. Abolition of right of justice of the peace to visit prisons

*Extent: N.I.*

Section 19 of the Prison Act (Northern Ireland) 1953 (c. 18) (right of justice of the peace to visit prisons) shall cease to have effect.
