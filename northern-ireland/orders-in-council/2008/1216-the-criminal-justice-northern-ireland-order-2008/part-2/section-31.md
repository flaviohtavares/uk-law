## 31. Conviction while licence remains in force

*Extent: N.I.*

Where it appears to the court by or before which a person is convicted of an offence—

- (a) that the offence was committed while the person was on licence under this Chapter, and
- (b) that the person has not been recalled to prison,

the court shall inform the Department of Justice[^c22093341] of the conviction.
