## 42. Provision of copies of electronic monitoring requirement[^c-89ac3901aaa31923093b1f56a4c4eb71]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where a court or the Secretary of State imposes an electronic monitoring requirement, the court or (as the case may be) the Secretary of State shall forthwith provide copies of the requirement—

- (a) to the person who by virtue of Article 40(3) will be responsible for the electronic monitoring; and
- (b) to any person to whom Article 40(2)(b) applies.
