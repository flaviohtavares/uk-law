# The Appropriation (No. 3) (Northern Ireland) Order 1987

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1987 No. 2204 (N.I. 23)

NORTHERN IRELAND

The Appropriation (No. 3) (Northern Ireland) Order 1987

Made

18th December 1987

Coming into Operation

18th December 1987

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 18th day of December 1987

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Whereas a draft of this Order has been approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament:

Now, therefore, Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 1 of Schedule 1 to the Northern Ireland Act 1974[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling Her in that behalf, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

## 1. Title and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Appropriation (No. 3) (Northern Ireland) Order 1987 and shall come into operation forthwith.

## 2. Interpretation

The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00002] shall apply to Article 1 and the following provisions of this Order as it applies to a Measure of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

## 3. Issue of sum out of the Consolidated Fund for year ending on 31st March 1988

The Department of Finance and Personnel may issue out of the Consolidated Fund and apply towards making good the supply granted to Her Majesty for the service of the year ending on 31st March 1988 the sum of eighteen million, four hundred and ninety-six thousand pounds.

## 4. Power for Department of Finance and Personnel to borrow

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the Department of Finance and Personnel may borrow on the credit of the aforesaid sum, any sum or sums not exceeding in the whole nine million pounds.

(2) Any money so borrowed shall be repaid, with any interest due thereon, out of the Consolidated Fund at any date not later than 31st March 1988.

## 5. Appropriation of grant

The sum granted by Article 3 out of the Consolidated Fund is appropriated for the service and purpose expressed in Schedule 1.

## 6. Repeal of spent enactments

The enactments mentioned in Schedule 2 are hereby repealed to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule.

*G. I. de Deney*

Clerk of the Privy Council
