# The Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1995, section 111

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2020-12-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/1995/2994
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 111. Savings and transitional provisions

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Nothing in this Order shall authorise any person to use on any road any vehicle so constructed or used as to cause a public or private nuisance, or shall affect any liability of the driver or owner of a vehicle whether under statute or common law.

(2) Nothing in regulations made under Article 69(5) or in Article 71 shall be taken to authorise the carriage for reward of any passengers or any luggage or goods contrary to Part 2[^c-86b28e88bf153846e2fee6a973c827e8] of the Transport Act (Northern Ireland) 1967[^c17247831] or the Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) Act (Northern Ireland) 2010[^c-b585334d710b588144258433e59ea04d] .

(3) The Department may by order make such transitional provision as appears to it necessary or expedient for the purpose of giving effect to this Order.

(4) Nothing in this Article, or in an order made under paragraph (3), shall prejudice the operation of the Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954.
