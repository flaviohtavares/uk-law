## 11A. Causing death or grievous bodily injury by careless or inconsiderate driving[^c21147931]

*Extent: N.I.*

A person who causes the death of, or grievous bodily injury to, another person by driving a mechanically propelled vehicle on a road or other public place without due care and attention, or without reasonable consideration for other persons using the road or place, is guilty of an offence.
