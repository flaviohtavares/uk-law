## 32. Leaving vehicles in dangerous positions

*Extent: N.I.*

If a person in charge of a vehicle causes or permits the vehicle or a trailer drawn by it to remain at rest on a road in such a position or in such condition or in such circumstances as to involve a danger of injury to other persons using the road[^c17247251], he is guilty of an offence.
