# The Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1995, section 35

## 35. Restrictions of carriage of persons on bicycles

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Not more than one person may be carried on a road on a bicycle not propelled by mechanical power unless it is constructed or adapted for the carriage of more than one person.

(2) In this Article references to a person carried on a bicycle include references to a person riding the bicycle.

(3) If a person is carried on a bicycle in contravention of paragraph (1), each of the persons carried is guilty of an offence.
