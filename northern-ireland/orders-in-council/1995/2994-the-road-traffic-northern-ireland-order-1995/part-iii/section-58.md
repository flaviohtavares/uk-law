# The Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1995, section 58

## 58. Breach of other construction and use requirements

*Extent: N.I.*

A person who—

- (a) contravenes any construction or use requirement other than one within Article 56(a) , 56A[^c20472851] or 57(1)(a); or
- (b) uses on a road a motor vehicle or trailer which does not comply with such a requirement, or causes or permits a motor vehicle or trailer to be so used,

is guilty of an offence.
