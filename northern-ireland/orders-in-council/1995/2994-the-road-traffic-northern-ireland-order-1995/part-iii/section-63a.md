# The Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1995, section 63A

## 63A. Display of test certificates[^c20473031]

*Extent: N.I.*

A person who–

- (a) uses on a road or other public place at any time, or
- (b) causes or permits to be so used,

a motor vehicle to which Article 63 applies and on which there is not displayed a test certificate in accordance with regulations made under Article 62(g), is guilty of an offence.
