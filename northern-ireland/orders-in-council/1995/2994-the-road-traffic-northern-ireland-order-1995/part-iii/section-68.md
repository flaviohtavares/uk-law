## 68. Supplementary provision about tests, etc., of goods vehicles

*Extent: N.I.*

Without prejudice to any regulations made under Article 65 by virtue of Article 67(1)(c), the Department may give directions with respect to the manner in which examinations under regulations under Article 65 or under Article 66 are to be carried out.
