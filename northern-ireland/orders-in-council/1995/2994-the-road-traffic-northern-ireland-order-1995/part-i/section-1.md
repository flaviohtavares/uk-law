# The Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1995, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2020-12-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/1995/2994
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 1. Title and commencement

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1995.

(2) This Order, except Article 92, shall come into operation on the expiration of 2 months from the day on which it is made.

(3) Article 92 shall come into operation on such day as the Head of the Department may by order appoint[^c17246691].

(4) This Order shall be construed as one with the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981.
