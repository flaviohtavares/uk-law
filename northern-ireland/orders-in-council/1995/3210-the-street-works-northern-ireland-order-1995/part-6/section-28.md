# The Street Works (Northern Ireland) Order 1995, section 28

## 28. Facilities to be afforded to street authority

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An undertaker executing street works shall afford the street authority reasonable facilities for ascertaining whether he is complying with his duties under this Order.

(2) An undertaker who fails to afford the street authority such facilities commits an offence in respect of each failure and is liable on summary conviction to a fine note exceeding level 4[^c20469331] on the standard scale.
