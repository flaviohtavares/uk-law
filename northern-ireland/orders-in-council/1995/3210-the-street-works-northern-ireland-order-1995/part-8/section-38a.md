# The Street Works (Northern Ireland) Order 1995, section 38A

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 38A). (not yet in force: Contributions to costs of re-surfacing by undertaker)[^c20473961]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: Where a street authority has given a re-surfacing notice to an undertaker (A)—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: the authority shall pay to A a proportion, calculated in the prescribed manner, of the costs reasonably incurred by A in executing the works specified in the notice;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: an undertaker to whom paragraph (2) applies shall pay to A a proportion, calculated in the prescribed manner, of those costs.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: This paragraph applies to an undertaker if—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: he has, after the commencement of this Article (whether or not regulations under it have been made) and before the completion of the works specified in the notice, executed street works which involved the breaking up of any part of a street, and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: the works specified in the notice include the re-surfacing of that part of the street.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: The Department may by regulations prescribe exceptions to the duty imposed by paragraph (1)(b).)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: The payments referred to in paragraph (1) shall be made in such instalments and manner, and within such period of such event, as may be prescribed.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: The Department may by regulations make provision—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: requiring a street authority, within such period of such event as may be prescribed, to give to an undertaker to whom paragraph (2) applies a notice containing such information as may be prescribed;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: requiring a street authority to pay to an undertaker to whom it has given a re-surfacing notice such sum as he has been unable to recover under paragraph (1)(b) on account of the insolvency of an undertaker;)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: requiring disputes of a prescribed description (including disputes as to whether paragraph (2) applies to an undertaker) to be determined in such manner and by such persons as may be prescribed.)*

((not yet in force: 6)) *(not yet in force: For the purposes of this Article, any costs incurred by an undertaker (including any costs of a street authority which are borne by the undertaker) in consequence of a failure by the undertaker to comply with any duty under this Order shall be treated as having been incurred unreasonably.)*

((not yet in force: 7)) *(not yet in force: The Department may by regulations make provision requiring undertakers to make payments to a street authority where—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: the authority has given a re-surfacing notice to an undertaker,)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: that undertaker has exercised a right, conferred by regulations under Article 33D, of the sort mentioned in paragraph (2)(d) of that Article, and)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: the authority has carried out any of the works specified in the notice.)*

((not yet in force: 8)) *(not yet in force: The power in paragraph (7) includes power to make provision corresponding to provision that may be made under paragraphs (1) to (5).)*

((not yet in force: 9)) *(not yet in force: The first regulations under this Article shall not be made unless a draft of them has been laid before and approved by a resolution of the Assembly.)*

((not yet in force: 10)) *(not yet in force: In paragraph (5)(b) “insolvency”—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: in relation to a company, has the meaning given by Article 6(1) of the Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989 ()**(not yet in force: NI)* *(not yet in force: 19);)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: in relation to an individual, includes the approval of a voluntary arrangement under Part VIII of that Order.)*
