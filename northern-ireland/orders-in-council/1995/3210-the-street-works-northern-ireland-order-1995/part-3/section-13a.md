# The Street Works (Northern Ireland) Order 1995, section 13A

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 13A). (not yet in force: Duty to inspect records)[^c20470831]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: The Department may make provision by regulations requiring an undertaker proposing to execute street works of a prescribed description to inspect prescribed statutory records before commencing the street works.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: The regulations may provide for the manner in which an inspection of any statutory records is to be carried out.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: The regulations may prescribe evidence which may be relied on by the undertaker to demonstrate that he has carried out an inspection required by the regulations.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: The regulations may in particular require the undertaker—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: to be in possession of prescribed evidence before commencing the street works; and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: to produce, in such manner as may be prescribed, prescribed evidence to the street authority either at the request of the authority or at or by such time as may be prescribed.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: The regulations may provide for the cases or circumstances in which a requirement under paragraph (1) or (4) does or does not apply.)*

((not yet in force: 6)) *(not yet in force: The regulations may create a summary offence, punishable with a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale, in respect of any contravention by an undertaker of a requirement of the regulations.)*

((not yet in force: 7)) *(not yet in force: In this Article “)****(not yet in force: statutory record)****(not yet in force: ” means any register or other record kept in pursuance of a requirement imposed by—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: a statutory provision; or)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: a licence or other instrument having effect under or by virtue of a statutory provision.)*
