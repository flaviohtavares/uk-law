# The Street Works (Northern Ireland) Order 1995, SCHEDULE 2A

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: SCHEDULE 2A) — (not yet in force: FIXED PENALTIES FOR CERTAIN OFFENCES)[^c20480641]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

### (not yet in force: Power to give fixed penalty notices)

**(not yet in force: 1).** ((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: An authorised officer of a street authority may, if he has reason to believe that a person is committing or has committed a fixed penalty offence, give him a fixed penalty notice in relation to that offence.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: In this Schedule “)****(not yet in force: fixed penalty notice)****(not yet in force: ” means a notice offering a person the opportunity of discharging any liability to conviction for a fixed penalty offence by payment of a penalty.)*

**(not yet in force: 2).** *(not yet in force: A fixed penalty notice for an offence may not be given after such time relating to the offence as may be prescribed.)*

### (not yet in force: Contents of fixed penalty notice)

**(not yet in force: 3).** ((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: A fixed penalty notice must identify the offence to which it relates and give reasonable particulars of the circumstances alleged to constitute that offence.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: A fixed penalty notice must also state—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: the amount of the penalty and the period within which it may be paid;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: the discounted amount and the period within which it may be paid;)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: the person to whom and the address at which payment may be made;)*
- ((not yet in force: d)) *(not yet in force: the method or methods by which payment may be made;)*
- ((not yet in force: e)) *(not yet in force: the person to whom and the address at which any representations relating to the notice may be addressed;)*
- ((not yet in force: f)) *(not yet in force: the consequences of not making a payment within the period for payment.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: The person specified under sub-paragraph (2)(c) must be the street authority or a person acting on its behalf.)*

### (not yet in force: The amount of the penalty and the period for payment)

**(not yet in force: 4).** ((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: The penalty for a fixed penalty offence is (subject to paragraph 5) such amount, not exceeding 30 per cent. of the maximum fine for that offence, as may be prescribed.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: The period for payment of the penalty is the period of 29 days beginning with the day on which the notice is given.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: The street authority may extend the period for paying the penalty in any particular case if the authority considers it appropriate to do so.)*

### (not yet in force: The discounted amount)

**(not yet in force: 5).** ((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: A discounted amount is payable instead of the amount prescribed under paragraph 4(1) if payment is made before the end of the period of 15 days beginning with the day on which the notice is given.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: The discounted amount for a fixed penalty offence is such amount, not exceeding 25 per cent. of the maximum fine for the offence, as may be prescribed.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: If the last day of the period specified in sub-paragraph (1) does not fall on a working day, the period for payment of the discounted amount is extended until the end of the next working day.)*

### (not yet in force: Effect of notice and payment of penalty)

**(not yet in force: 6).** ((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: This paragraph applies where a person is given a fixed penalty notice in respect of a fixed penalty offence.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: No proceedings for the offence may be commenced before the end of the period for payment of the penalty.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: No such proceedings may be commenced or continued if payment of the penalty is made before the end of that period or is accepted by the street authority after that time.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: Payment of the discounted amount only counts for the purposes of sub-paragraph (3) if it is made before the end of the period for payment of the discounted amount.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: In proceedings for the offence a certificate which—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: purports to be signed by or on behalf of the person having responsibility for the financial affairs of the street authority; and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: states that payment of an amount specified in the certificate was or was not received by a date so specified,)*

*(not yet in force: is evidence of the facts stated.)*

### (not yet in force: Power to withdraw notices)

**(not yet in force: 7).** ((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: If the street authority considers that a fixed penalty notice which has been given ought not to have been given, it may give to the person to whom it was given a notice withdrawing the fixed penalty notice.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: Where a notice under sub-paragraph (1) is given—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: the authority shall repay any amount which has been paid by way of penalty in pursuance of the fixed penalty notice; and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: no proceedings shall be commenced or continued against that person for the offence in question.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: The street authority shall consider any representations made by or on behalf of the recipient of a fixed penalty notice and decide in all the circumstances whether to withdraw the notice.)*

### (not yet in force: General and supplementary)

**(not yet in force: 8).** *(not yet in force: The Department may, with the consent of the Department of Finance and Personnel, make regulations about—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: the application by street authorities (other than the Department) of fixed penalties paid under this Schedule;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: the keeping by such authorities of accounts, and the preparation and publication of statements of account, relating to fixed penalties paid under this Schedule.)*

**(not yet in force: 9).** *(not yet in force: The Department may by regulations—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: prescribe circumstances in which fixed penalty notices may not be given;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: modify paragraph 4(2) or 5(1) so as to substitute a different period for the period for the time being specified there;)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: prescribe the method or methods by which penalties may be paid.)*
