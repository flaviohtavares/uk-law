# The Street Works (Northern Ireland) Order 1995, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (58).** Text as at 2025-06-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/1995/3210
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 1. Title and commencement

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This Order may be cited as the Street Works (Northern Ireland) Order 1995.

(2) This Order shall come into operation on such day or days as the Head of the Department may by order appoint[^c17049801].

(3) An order under paragraph (2) may contain such transitional provisions as appear to the Head of the Department to be necessary or expedient.
