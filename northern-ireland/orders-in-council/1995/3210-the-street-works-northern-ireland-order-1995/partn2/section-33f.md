# The Street Works (Northern Ireland) Order 1995, section 33F

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 33F). (not yet in force: Guidance about street authority inspections)[^c20477081]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: The Department may issue or approve guidance to street authorities about any matter relating to the exercise by them of any power to carry out inspections of street works.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: In exercising their powers to carry out such inspections street authorities shall have regard to any guidance issued or approved under this Article.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: In this Article any reference to inspections includes the carrying out of investigatory works.)*
