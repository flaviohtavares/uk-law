# The Street Works (Northern Ireland) Order 1995, section 33A

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 33A). (not yet in force: Power to require undertaker to re-surface street)[^c20476991]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: In prescribed circumstances, the street authority for a street may by notice (a “re-surfacing notice”) require an undertaker within paragraph (2) to execute such re-surfacing works in the street as may be specified in the notice.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: An undertaker is within this paragraph if—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: he has given notice under Article 14 or 15 of, or made a notification under paragraph 2(1)(d) of Schedule 1A in respect of, proposed street works,)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: he is executing street works, or)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: he has, within such period ending with the giving of the notice as may be prescribed (or if no period is prescribed, at any time), executed street works,)*

*(not yet in force: and the works will involve, involve or (as the case may be) involved the breaking up of any part of the street.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: The works specified in the re-surfacing notice may relate to any part of the street (including any part not, and not to be, broken up by the undertaker); but regulations may restrict the extent of the works that may be so specified.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: The re-surfacing notice relieves the undertaker to the extent (if any) specified in the notice of his duty under Article 30 to reinstate the surface of the street; but regulations may restrict the circumstances in which and the extent to which undertakers may be relieved of that duty.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: The street authority may by notice to the undertaker vary or withdraw a re-surfacing notice; but regulations may restrict the circumstances in which notices may be varied or withdrawn.)*

((not yet in force: 6)) *(not yet in force: A street authority may give a re-surfacing notice notwithstanding that the authority (in any capacity) is under a duty to undertake any of the works specified in the notice.)*

((not yet in force: 7)) *(not yet in force: In this Order—)*

- *(not yet in force: “)****(not yet in force: re-surfacing notice)****(not yet in force: ” has the meaning given by paragraph (1);)*
- *(not yet in force: “)****(not yet in force: re-surfacing works)****(not yet in force: ” means any works relating to the replacement of the surface of any part of a street;)*
- *(not yet in force: “)****(not yet in force: surface)****(not yet in force: ” includes a paved surface.)*

((not yet in force: 8)) *(not yet in force: The reference in paragraph (2)(c) to the execution of street works is a reference to the execution of such works after the commencement of this Article (whether or not regulations under it have been made).)*
