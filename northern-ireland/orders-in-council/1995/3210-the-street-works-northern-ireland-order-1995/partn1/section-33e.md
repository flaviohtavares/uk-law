# The Street Works (Northern Ireland) Order 1995, section 33E

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 33E). (not yet in force: Re-surfacing: guidance)[^c20476991]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: The Department may, for the purposes of Articles 33A to 33D (including regulations under those Articles), issue or approve a code of practice giving practical guidance as to the exercise of powers and the discharge of duties under those Articles.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: In exercising those powers and in discharging those duties, street authorities and undertakers shall have regard to the code of practice.)*
