# The Street Works (Northern Ireland) Order 1995, section 33B

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 33B). (not yet in force: Power to specify timing) (not yet in force: etc.) (not yet in force: of re-surfacing)[^c20476991]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: A re-surfacing notice may require an undertaker to—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: execute the works specified in the notice in stages so specified;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: begin the execution of those works (or any stage of them) at or by a date and time so specified;)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: execute those works (or any stage of them) at times or on days (or at times on days) so specified;)*
- ((not yet in force: d)) *(not yet in force: complete the execution of those works (or any stage of them) by a date and time so specified.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: The Department may by regulations make provision restricting, in some or all cases, the power of street authorities to include requirements within paragraph (1), including provision that—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: requires street authorities to consult an undertaker before a prescribed description of requirement is included in a notice;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: provides that any date specified in a notice for the beginning, execution or completion of works shall not be earlier than a prescribed period from the date on which the notice is given.)*
