# The Street Works (Northern Ireland) Order 1995, section 16A

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 16A). (not yet in force: Power to give directions as to placing of apparatus)[^c20472761]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: Where—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: an undertaker is proposing to execute street works consisting of the placing of apparatus in a street (“street A”),)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: placing the apparatus in street A is likely to cause disruption to traffic, and)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: it appears to the street authority that—)*
  - ((not yet in force: i)) *(not yet in force: there is another street (“street B”) in which the apparatus could be placed, and)*
  - ((not yet in force: ii)) *(not yet in force: the conditions in paragraph (2) are satisfied,)*

  *(not yet in force: the authority may by direction require the undertaker not to place the apparatus in street A (but may not require him to place the apparatus in street B).)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: The conditions referred to in paragraph (1)(c) are that—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: disruption to traffic would be avoided or reduced if the apparatus were to be placed in street B;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: placing the apparatus in street B would be a reasonable way of achieving the purpose for which the apparatus is to be placed; and)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: it is reasonable to require the undertaker not to place the apparatus in street A.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: A direction under this Article may be varied or revoked by a further such direction.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: The procedure for giving a direction under this Article shall be prescribed.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: The Department may by regulations make provision for or in connection with appeals to the planning appeals commission against directions under this Article.)*

((not yet in force: 6)) *(not yet in force: An undertaker who executes works in contravention of a direction under this Article commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale.)*

((not yet in force: 7)) *(not yet in force: An undertaker shall be taken not to have failed to fulfil any statutory duty to afford a supply or service if, or to the extent that, his failure is attributable to a direction under this Article.)*

((not yet in force: 8)) *(not yet in force: The Department may issue or approve for the purposes of this Article a code of practice giving practical guidance as to the exercise by street authorities of the power conferred by this Article; and in exercising that power street authorities shall have regard to the code of practice.)*
