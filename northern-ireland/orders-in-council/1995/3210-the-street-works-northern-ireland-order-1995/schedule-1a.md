# The Street Works (Northern Ireland) Order 1995, SCHEDULE 1A

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: SCHEDULE 1A) — (not yet in force: RESTRICTION ON WORKS FOLLOWING SUBSTANTIAL STREET WORKS)[^c20480621]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

### (not yet in force: Introductory)

**(not yet in force: 1).** ((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: This Schedule applies where a street authority receives a notice under Article 14 or 15 that an undertaker is proposing to execute substantial street works in a road.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: For the purposes of this Schedule, “)****(not yet in force: substantial street works)****(not yet in force: ” means street works of such description as may be prescribed.)*

### (not yet in force: Notice by authority of proposed restriction)

**(not yet in force: 2).** ((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: The street authority may publish a notice—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: specifying the nature and location of the proposed works and the date on which it is proposed to begin them;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: stating that the authority proposes to issue a direction under paragraph 4 imposing a restriction on street works;)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: stating the duration of the proposed restriction and the part of the road to which it relates;)*
- ((not yet in force: d)) *(not yet in force: requiring any other undertakers who propose to execute street works in that part of the road, and who have not already done so, to notify the authority of their proposed works within the period specified in the notice (“)****(not yet in force: the notice period)****(not yet in force: ”).)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: The notice period shall not be less than such period as may be prescribed.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: A notice under this paragraph shall—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: be published in the prescribed form and manner; and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: comply with such requirements as to its form and content as may be prescribed.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: A copy of a notice under this paragraph shall be given to each of the following—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: where there is a public sewer in the part of the road specified under sub-paragraph (1)(c), to the Department;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: where that part of the road is carried or crossed by a bridge vested in a transport authority, or crosses or is crossed by any other property held by or used for the purposes of a transport authority, to that authority;)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: where in any other case that part of the road is carried or crossed by a bridge, to the bridge authority;)*
- ((not yet in force: d)) *(not yet in force: any person who has given notice under Article 14 of his intention to execute street works in that part of the road;)*
- ((not yet in force: e)) *(not yet in force: any person who has apparatus in that part of the road;)*
- ((not yet in force: f)) *(not yet in force: any other person of a prescribed description.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: Notification under sub-paragraph (1)(d) shall be in such form, contain such information, and be made in such manner as may be prescribed.)*

((not yet in force: 6)) *(not yet in force: Article 15 does not apply in relation to works in the part of the road specified under sub-paragraph (1)(c) that are begun between the end of the notice period and completion of the works referred to in paragraph 3(1)(a) to (c).)*

*(not yet in force: This sub-paragraph does not apply to cases prescribed under paragraph 3(5)(b).)*

### (not yet in force: Completion of notified works)

**(not yet in force: 3).** ((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: After the expiry of the notice period the street authority may issue directions to—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: the undertaker proposing to execute the substantial street works,)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: any undertakers who have given notice under paragraph 2 in respect of works they propose to execute, and)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: any undertakers who have previously given notice of works they propose to execute in the part of the road specified under paragraph 2(1)(c).)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: A direction to an undertaker under this paragraph is a direction as to the date on which he may begin to execute the works proposed by him.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: Where—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: a direction is given to an undertaker under this paragraph as respects the date on which he may begin to execute the works proposed by him, and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: he begins to execute those works before that date,)*

*(not yet in force: he is guilty of an offence.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: After the expiry of the notice period, any undertaker who, before completion of the works referred to in sub-paragraph (1)(a) to (c), executes any other street works in the part of the road specified under paragraph 2(1)(c), commits an offence.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: Sub-paragraph (4) does not apply—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: where an undertaker executes emergency works; or)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: in such other cases as may be prescribed.)*

((not yet in force: 6)) *(not yet in force: A person guilty of an offence under this paragraph is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale.)*

### (not yet in force: Direction restricting further works)

**(not yet in force: 4).** ((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: After the expiry of the notice period and before completion of the works referred to in paragraph 3(1)(a) to (c) the authority may give a direction under this paragraph.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: A direction under this paragraph is a direction restricting the execution of street works in the part of the road specified under paragraph 2(1)(c) for such period following completion of the works referred to in paragraph 3(1)(a) to (c) as may be specified in the direction.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: The duration of the period specified under sub-paragraph (2) may not exceed the duration of the restriction proposed by the authority under paragraph 2(1)(c).)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: The period specified in a direction under this paragraph may not in any case exceed such period as may be prescribed.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: A direction under this paragraph shall—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: be given in the prescribed manner;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: comply with such requirements as to its form and content as may be prescribed.)*

((not yet in force: 6)) *(not yet in force: The street authority must send a copy of any direction under this paragraph to the persons specified in paragraph 2(4).)*

((not yet in force: 7)) *(not yet in force: A direction under this paragraph shall cease to have effect if the works referred to in paragraph 3(1)(a) to (c) to which it relates are not completed within such period as may be prescribed.)*

((not yet in force: 8)) *(not yet in force: A direction under this paragraph may be revoked at any time by the authority which gave it.)*

((not yet in force: 9)) *(not yet in force: Where a direction under this paragraph ceases to have effect by virtue of sub-paragraph (7), or is revoked by virtue of sub-paragraph (8), the street authority must notify the persons specified in paragraph 2(4).)*

((not yet in force: 10)) *(not yet in force: If the street authority decides not to give a direction under this paragraph, it must notify the persons specified in paragraph 2(4) accordingly.)*

### (not yet in force: Effect of direction imposing restriction)

**(not yet in force: 5).** ((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: Where a direction under paragraph 4 is in force, an undertaker may not during the period specified in the direction execute street works in the part of the road to which the restriction relates.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: Sub-paragraph (1) does not apply—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: where an undertaker executes emergency works;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: where an undertaker executes works with the consent of the street authority; or)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: in such other cases as may be prescribed.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: The consent of the street authority under sub-paragraph (2)(b) shall not be unreasonably withheld; and any question whether the withholding of consent is unreasonable shall be settled in such manner as may be prescribed.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: Regulations under sub-paragraph (3) may in particular make provision for the question referred to in that sub-paragraph to be settled—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: by arbitration;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: by the planning appeals commission on appeal by the undertaker.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: An undertaker who contravenes sub-paragraph (1) commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale.)*

((not yet in force: 6)) *(not yet in force: An undertaker convicted of an offence under sub-paragraph (5) is liable to reimburse the street authority any costs reasonably incurred by it in reinstating the road.)*

### (not yet in force: Supplementary)

**(not yet in force: 6).** *(not yet in force: An undertaker shall be taken not to have failed to fulfil any statutory duty to afford a supply or service if, or to the extent that, his failure is attributable to a restriction imposed under this Schedule.)*
