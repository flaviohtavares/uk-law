## (not yet in force: 52A). (not yet in force: Fixed penalties for certain offences under this Order)[^c20475761]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: Any offence under this Order relating to any street works which is listed in paragraph (3) is a fixed penalty offence for the purposes of this Order.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: Offences listed in that paragraph which are committed by virtue of section 20(2) of the Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 (c. 33) (offences by bodies corporate) are not fixed penalty offences.)*
