# The Children (Northern Ireland) Order 1995, section 120

## 120. Persons who provide day care for children under the age of twelve

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of this Part a person does not provide day care for children unless the period, or the total of the periods, during which children are looked after exceeds two hours in any day.

(2) Where a person provides day care for children under the age of twelve on different premises situated within the area of the same authority, that person shall be separately registered with respect to each of those premises.
