# The Children (Northern Ireland) Order 1995, section 134

## 134. Exemptions and saving

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The provisions of this Part and of any regulations made under it shall not apply to a child detained in a training school.

(2) The provisions of this Part are in addition to any statutory provision—

- (a) relating to the employment of children; or
- (b) for giving effect to any international convention regulating employment.
