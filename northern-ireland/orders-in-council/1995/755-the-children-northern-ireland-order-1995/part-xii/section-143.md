## 143. Licensing of training children over twelve for performances of a dangerous nature

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Education Authority[^key-e2fe12e4813a3a64ddc67af2f72653d8] may grant a licence for a child who is not under the age of twelve to be trained to take part in performances of a dangerous nature and no such child shall be so trained except under and in accordance with the terms of a licence under this Article.

(2)[^key-1febde38a5010ae7eea7962a5e64517e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) The Education Authority[^key-fbc9bbbfd1d70c40a3a5bf5050c34a26] shall not refuse to grant a licence under this Article if the Education Authority[^key-32a88b8db9a6f695184c0aa3e4632075] is satisfied—

- (a) that the child is fit and willing to be trained; and
- (b) that proper provision has been made to secure his health and kind treatment.

(4) A licence under this Article shall—

- (a) specify the place or places at which the child is to be trained;
- (b) contain such conditions as the Education Authority[^key-63285578bb5fcd3c3fb281932b181c39] considers necessary for the child's protection.

(5) The Education Authority[^key-5168fc8ecc7da150f1766202860d1b7e] may vary or revoke the licence—

- (a) if any condition subject to which the licence was granted is not observed; or
- (b) if it appears to the Education Authority[^key-a9ac904e25dfaca7582383503af29cc3] that—
  - (i) the child is no longer fit and willing to be trained; or
  - (ii) proper provision is no longer being made to secure his health and kind treatment,

  but before varying or revoking the licence, the Education Authority shall give to the holder of the licence such notice, if any, of the Education Authority's[^key-68c89aa1f2d89dbd5d9e3b5e8128f769] intentions as may be practicable in the circumstances.
