## 139. Variation and revocation of licences

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A licence under Article 138 may be varied on the application of the person holding it by the Education Authority[^c-3c18cc1da904303976ee836785cefd54] .

(2) The Education Authority[^c-4297c1f18c4614f9f74251bc7c676c25] may vary or revoke a licence under Article 138 if—

- (a) any condition subject to which the licence was granted is not observed; or
- (b) the Education Authority[^c-aca271297648928c844bb19dbb440be4] is not satisfied as to the matters mentioned in paragraph (3) of that Article,

but before varying or revoking the licence, the Education Authority shall give to the holder of the licence such notice, if any, of the Education Authority's[^c-0d1e43b3f9824fd5a876408a734594be] intentions as may be practicable in the circumstances.
