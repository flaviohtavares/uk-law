# The Children (Northern Ireland) Order 1995, section 74

## 74. Interpretation

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) In this Order—

- “voluntary home” means a children's home which is carried on by a voluntary organisation;[^c17270181]
- “voluntary organisation” means any association carrying on or proposing to carry on any activities otherwise than for the purpose of gain by the association or by individual members.
- *Paras. (2) & (3) rep. by 2003 NI 9*

(4) Any reference in this Part to an authority in relation to a voluntary home is a reference to the authority in whose area the home is, or is to be, situated.
