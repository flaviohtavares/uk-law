## 158. Repeal of Illegitimate Children (Affiliation Orders) Act (Northern Ireland) 1924

*Extent: N.I.*

The Illegitimate Children (Affiliation Orders) Act (Northern Ireland) 1924[^c17271111] shall cease to have effect.
