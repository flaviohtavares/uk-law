## 116. Avoidance of insurances on lives of privately fostered children

*Extent: N.I.*

A person who fosters a child privately and for reward shall be deemed for the purposes of the Life Assurance Act 1774[^c17270991] as extended by the Life Insurance (Ireland) Act 1866[^c17271001] to have no interest in the life of the child.
