# The Children (Northern Ireland) Order 1995, section 25

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (152).** Text as at 2024-11-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/1995/755
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 25. Interpretation[^key-ca053b75914d81ef504d6f29db029dac][^key-712553097392a0d285d87a156a3097f2]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) In this Order any reference to a child who is looked after by an authority is a reference to a child who is—

- (a) in the care of the authority; or
- (b) provided with accommodation by the authority.

(2) In paragraph (1)(b) “accommodation” means accommodation which is provided for a continuous period of more than 24 hours but does not include accommodation provided under Article 34C or 35B[^c17269611].

(3) Paragraph (1) is subject to Article 2(8).
