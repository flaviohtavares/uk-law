# The Children (Northern Ireland) Order 1995, section 181

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (152).** Text as at 2024-11-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/1995/755
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 181. Report on the operation of this Order[^key-f1dbee9390c629e4216cba1e091517cb]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-ae5e73432561a06ee8fb4335a9bf4798] The Department shall, after consultation with the Lord Chancellor, the Department of Education and the Department of Finance and Personnel, cause an annual general report on the operation of this Order to be prepared and laid before the Assembly.

(2)[^key-0c24137a4c352ae176224b060fd53724] The first report under paragraph (1) must be prepared and laid before the Assembly within the period of three years beginning on the date on which the Adoption and Children Act (Northern Ireland) 2022 receives Royal Assent.
