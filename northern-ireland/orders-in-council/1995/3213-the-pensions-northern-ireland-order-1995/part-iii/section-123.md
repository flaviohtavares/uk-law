# The Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1995, section 123

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 123. Equalisation of pensionable age and of entitlement to certain benefits and increase in pensionable age[^c21160061]

*Extent: N.I.*

Schedule 2, of which—

- (a) Part I has effect to equalise pensionable age for men and women progressively and then to increase it[^key-8cfa3ee53a3ed29f8fcdb4c2741c4b3a] ,
- (b) Part II makes provision for bringing equality for men and women to certain pension and other benefits, and
- (c) Part III makes consequential amendments of enactments,

has effect.
