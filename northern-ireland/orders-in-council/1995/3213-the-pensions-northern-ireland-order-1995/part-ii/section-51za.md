## 51ZA. Meaning of “the appropriate percentage”[^c21157411]

*Extent: N.I.*

For the purposes of Article 51(1)(c) and (2), “the appropriate percentage” in relation to an increase in the whole or part of the annual rate of a pension—

(a) in the case of a category X pension, means the higher revaluation percentage[^c21157371] for the latest revaluation period specified in the latest revaluation order (revaluation of accrued pension benefits) which is in force at the time of the increase, and

(b)[^c21158721] in the case of a category Y pension, means the lower revaluation percentage for that period.
