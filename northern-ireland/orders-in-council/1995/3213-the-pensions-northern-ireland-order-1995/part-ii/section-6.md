## 6. Removal or suspension of trustees: consequences

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A person who purports to act as trustee of a trust scheme while prohibited from being a trustee of the scheme under Article 3 or 3A[^key-0c949e3fb215daace1f5aa492fe30303] or suspended in relation to the scheme under Article 4 is guilty of an offence and liable—

- (a) on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum, and
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to a fine or imprisonment or both.

(2) An offence under paragraph (1) may be charged by reference to any day or longer period of time; and a person may be convicted of a second or subsequent offence under that paragraph by reference to any period of time following the preceding conviction of the offence.

(3) Things done by a person purporting to act as trustee of a trust scheme while prohibited from being a trustee of the scheme under Article 3 or 3A[^key-ff4d9308578abfefe0a09e0cc6a37060] or suspended in relation to the scheme under Article 4 are not invalid merely because of that prohibition or suspension.

(4) Nothing in Article 3 , 3A[^key-44fe111a7258c6b73de891c0d2a7e30a] or 4 or this Article affects the liability of any person for things done, or omitted to be done, by him while purporting to act as trustee of a trust scheme.
