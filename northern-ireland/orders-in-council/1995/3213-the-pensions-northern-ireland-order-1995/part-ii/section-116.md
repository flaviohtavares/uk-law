## 116. Calculations, etc. under regulations: sub-delegation

*Extent: N.I.*

Regulations made by virtue of Article[^c19432061]. . . 73B(4)(a)[^c17252601] or 75 may provide for the values of the assets and the amounts of the liabilities there mentioned to be calculated and verified in accordance with guidance prepared and from time to time revised by a prescribed body.[^c21159591]
