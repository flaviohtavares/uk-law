# The Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1995, section 134

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (51).** Text as at 2026-07-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/1995/3213

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 134. State scheme contributions and rebates

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) In section 36 of the Pension Schemes Act (scope of Chapter II of Part III), in paragraph (b), after “members of” insert “money purchase contracted-out schemes and members of”.

*Para.(2) rep. by 1998 NI 10*

(3)[^c-631d6e1a2c4e90606f7bba86c01b571a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^c-25823785672c667bfb8e08b12844a107] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) In Schedule 3 to that Act (priority in bankruptcy, etc), in paragraph 2(3)—

- (a) in paragraph (a), for “4.8 per cent.” substitute “the percentage for non-contributing earners”,
- (b) in paragraph (b), for “3 per cent.” substitute “the percentage for contributing earners”.

(6) In paragraph 2(5) of that Schedule—

- (a)[^c-b1cac02ea9ef901397f0efe0e7aa9acd] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) after that definition insert—

  > - “the percentage for contributing earners”
  >   - (a) in relation to a salary related contracted-out scheme, 3 per cent, and
  >   - (b) in relation to a money purchase contracted-out scheme, the percentage which is the appropriate flat-rate percentage for secondary Class 1 contributions,
  > - “the percentage for non-contributing earners” means—
  >   - (a) in relation to a salary related contracted-out scheme, 4.8 per cent, and
  >   - (b) in relation to a money purchase contracted-out scheme, a percentage equal to the sum of the appropriate flat-rate percentages for primary and secondary Class 1 contributions;

  .
