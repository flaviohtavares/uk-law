# The Trade Union and Labour Relations (Northern Ireland) Order 1995, section 149

## 149. Regulations and orders

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), all regulations and orders made by the Department under this Order shall be subject to negative resolution.

(2) Paragraph (1) does not apply to an order under Article l(2), 44B(9),[^c17262561] 90A(2)(e)[^c17262581] 91(3) or 121(2).

(3) Regulations and orders under this Order may contain such incidental, supplementary and transitional provisions as appear to the Department to be necessary or expedient.
