# The Trade Union and Labour Relations (Northern Ireland) Order 1995, section 76

## 76. Requirement of ballot on resolution

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A resolution approving the instrument of amalgamation or transfer must be passed on a ballot of the members of the trade union held in accordance with Articles 77 to 81.

(2) A simple majority of those voting is sufficient to pass such a resolution unless the rules of the trade union expressly require it to be approved by a greater majority or by a specified proportion of the members of the union.
