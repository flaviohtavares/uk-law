# The Trade Union and Labour Relations (Northern Ireland) Order 1995, section 113

## 113. Employers to be informed of ballot result

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) As soon as reasonably practicable after the holding of the ballot, the trade union shall take such steps as are reasonably necessary to ensure that every relevant employer is informed of the matters mentioned in Article 112.

(2) In paragraph (1) “relevant employer” means a person who it is reasonable for the trade union to believe (at the time when the steps are taken) was at the time of the ballot the employer of any persons entitled to vote.
