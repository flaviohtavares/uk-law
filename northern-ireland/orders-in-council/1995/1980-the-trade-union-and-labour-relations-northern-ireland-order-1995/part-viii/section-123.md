# The Trade Union and Labour Relations (Northern Ireland) Order 1995, section 123

## 123. No compulsion to work

*Extent: N.I.*

No court shall, whether by way of—

- (a) an order for specific performance of a contract of employment; or
- (b) an injunction restraining a breach or threatened breach of such a contract,

compel an employee to do any work or attend at any place for the doing of any work.
