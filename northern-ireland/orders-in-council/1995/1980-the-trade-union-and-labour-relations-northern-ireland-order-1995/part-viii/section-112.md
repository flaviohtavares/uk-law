# The Trade Union and Labour Relations (Northern Ireland) Order 1995, section 112

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 112. Information as to result of ballot

*Extent: N.I.*

As soon as reasonably practicable after the holding of the ballot, the trade union shall take such steps as are reasonably necessary to ensure that all persons entitled to vote in the ballot are informed of the number of—

- (a) votes cast in the ballot,
- (b) individuals answering “Yes” to the question, or as the case may be, to each question,
- (c) individuals answering “No” to the question, or, as the case may be, to each question, and
- (d) spoiled voting papers.
