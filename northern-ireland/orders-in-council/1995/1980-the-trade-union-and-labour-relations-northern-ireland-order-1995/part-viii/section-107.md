# The Trade Union and Labour Relations (Northern Ireland) Order 1995, section 107

## 107. Exclusion for small ballots

*Extent: N.I.*

Nothing in Article 106, Article 110(2)(a) or Article 114 shall impose a requirement on a trade union unless—

- (a) the number of members entitled to vote in the ballot, or
- (b) where separate workplace ballots are held in accordance with Article 109(1), the aggregate of the number of members entitled to vote in each of them,

exceeds 50.
