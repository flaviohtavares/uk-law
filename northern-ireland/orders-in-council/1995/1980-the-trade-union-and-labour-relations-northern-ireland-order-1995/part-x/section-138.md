## 138. Functions of the Agency

*Extent: N.I.*

*Para. (1) rep. by 1999 NI 9*

(2) For Article 89 of the 1992 Order (powers of the Agency to give advice) there shall be substituted—

> **89. Advice**
>
> (1) The Agency may, on request or otherwise, give employers, employers' associations, workers and trade unions such advice as it thinks appropriate on matters concerned with or affecting or likely to affect industrial relations.
>
> (2) The Agency may also publish general advice on matters concerned with or affecting or likely to affect industrial relations.
