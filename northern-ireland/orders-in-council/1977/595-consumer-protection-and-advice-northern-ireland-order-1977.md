# Consumer Protection and Advice (Northern Ireland) Order 1977

> Text as at 2006-01-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

1977 No. 595 (N.I. 6)

Consumer Protection and Advice (Northern Ireland) Order 1977

\[30th March 1977\]

## 1. Title and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Consumer Protection and Advice (Northern Ireland) Order 1977 … *Commencement* …

## 2. Interpretation

The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 [^m00001] shall apply to Article 1 and the following provisions of this Order as it applies to a Measure of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

*Art. 3 repeals in part s.3(2) and substitutes s.3(3)(4) of 1965 c.14 (NI)*

## 4. Consumer advice

A district council may make, or assist in the making of, arrangements (including the provision of accommodation) to provide within the district of the council advice to, or for the benefit of, consumers of goods and services.
