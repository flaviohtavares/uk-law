## 43. Distinguishment in NAV list of industrial hereditaments and freight-transport hereditaments[^c19632561]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where the Commissioner or the district valuer is satisfied that a hereditament is a hereditament of a description specified in paragraph 1 of Schedule 14, he shall distinguish the hereditament, or cause it to be distinguished, in the NAV list in accordance with the provisions of that Schedule; and, where by any provision of that Schedule the net annual value of such a hereditament is required to be apportioned, the apportionment shall be shown in the NAV list.
