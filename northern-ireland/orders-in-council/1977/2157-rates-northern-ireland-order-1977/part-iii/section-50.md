## 50. Alteration in . . . valuation list by Commissioner[^c19643271]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Commissioner may at any time—

- (a) make in a valuation list[^c19642881] any alteration which is necessary—
  - (i) to correct any clerical error in the list;
  - (ii) in consequence of any alteration in a boundary that is made under the Boundary Survey (Ireland) Act 1854 [^m00013], the Boundary Survey (Ireland) Act 1857 [^m00014], the Boundary Survey (Ireland) Act 1859 [^m00015] or the County Boundaries (Ireland) Act 1872 [^m00016] or under section 50 of the Local Government Act (Northern Ireland) 1972 [^m00017];
  - (iii) to give effect to any apportionment made by him under Article 40(4)[^c16884421];
  - (iv)[^c-67ce8bb5ade396a393aacbcb891c2380] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

  *Head (v) rep. by 1998 NI 22*
- (b) alter a valuation list[^c19642901] by deleting from it any hereditament which he is satisfied has ceased to exist.

(2) Where the Commissioner alters a valuation list[^c19643031] under paragraph (1)( *a*) or ( *b*), he shall serve certificates of the alteration on the persons mentioned in Article 56(8).

(3) The district valuer shall not cause to be made in a valuation list[^c19643041] any alteration such as is mentioned in paragraph (1)( *a*)(iii)[^c-c6a3086441add1c09dfe5501873da6ee]..., but this Article shall not prejudice his power to cause to be made any alteration such as is mentioned in paragraph (1)( *a*)(i) or (ii) or ( *b*).
