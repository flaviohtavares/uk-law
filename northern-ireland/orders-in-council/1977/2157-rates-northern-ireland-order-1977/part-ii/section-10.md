## 10. Departures from valuation list in levying rates[^c20669951]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department, in levying a rate, may make such departures not affecting value from the particulars contained in a valuation list[^c19549591] as may be necessary to enable the rate to be effectively levied in accordance with the provisions of this Order.

(2) Any departure under paragraph (1) shall be forthwith reported to the district valuer.
