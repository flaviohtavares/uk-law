# Rates (Northern Ireland) Order 1977, section 61

## 61. Regulations

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Regulations may—

- (a) prescribe the form of any valuation list, certificate, application, statement, return, notice or other document whatsoever which is required or authorised to be used under or for the purposes of this Order;
- (b) substitute for any period mentioned in Article[^c19668441]. . . 13(1)( *a*)(ii), 49(4) or (5), 51(1), (1A), (2) or (4), or 52(4A) or (4B)[^c19671611] any other period; [^c16884921]…
- (bb) [^c19668201]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) make provision with respect to the making of reviews under Article 55.

(1A) [^c19668881]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^c19668961] Regulations shall not be made under Article 18(2) or 29A or paragraph 8 of Schedule 5 or paragraph 12 of Part 1 of Schedule 12[^c21346501] unless a draft of the regulations has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, the Assembly.

(2A) Regulations made under—[^c-198cf6b8e5b715cc31df28c19d2e21dc]

- (a) Article 23A , except those made under paragraph (a) of the definition of “hereditament in the social sector” in paragraph (3) of that Article[^c21346461] ;[^c-d585f030d8ccf9728a22cbe31aa3c51d]
- (b) Article 31(5A); or
- (c) paragraph (1)(b),

shall be subject to affirmative resolution.

(2B) Regulations made under provisions of this Order other than those regulations[^c21346481] to which paragraphs (2) and (2A) apply shall be subject to negative resolution.
