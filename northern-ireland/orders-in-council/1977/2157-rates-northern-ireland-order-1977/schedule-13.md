## SCHEDULE 13 — HEREDITAMENTS EXCLUDED FROM EXEMPTION

*Extent: N.I.*

Article 41.

### Occupying bodies

- The Education Authority.[^key-0256a6f857e812298bb3f82c09a74084]
- The Northern Ireland Fire and Rescue Service Board[^c20796281]
- The Northern Ireland Library Authority.[^c20796261]
- The governing body of an institution of further education within the meaning of the Further Education (Northern Ireland) Order 1997.[^c16886251]
- *Entry rep. by 1984 NI 10*
- A Board of Governors of a maintained school[^c16886231] or a grant-maintained integrated school[^c16886291] within the meaning of the Education and Libraries (Northern Ireland) Order 1986.
- The Board of Trustees of the National Museums and Galleries of Northern Ireland.[^c16886331]
- University of Ulster[^c16886351].
- The Police Authority for Northern Ireland.
- The Queen's University of Belfast.
- *Entries rep. by 1998 NI 2*
