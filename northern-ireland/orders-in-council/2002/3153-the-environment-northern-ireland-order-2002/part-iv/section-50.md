> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 50. Statutory charges

*Extent: N.I.*

In Schedule 11 to the Land Registration Act (Northern Ireland) 1970 (c. 18) (matters which are statutory charges)—

- (a) in paragraph 39 for “17 or 24(8)(a)” substitute “ or 17 ”; and
- (b) at the end there shall be added—

  > **43.**
  >
  > Any declaration under Article 28(1) of the Environment (Northern Ireland) Order 2002, any management agreement under Article 34 of that Order or any waiver relating thereto and any management notice under Article 35 of that Order.
