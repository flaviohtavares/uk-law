# The Environment (Northern Ireland) Order 2002, section 22

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 22. Power to give effect to assimilated obligations and other international obligations, etc.[^c-531dcb84fed91270adcb95478f47f757][^c-d922ed18157a2888a62d675767de1b2f]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Regulations may provide that the provisions of this Part shall have effect with such modifications as may be prescribed for the purpose of enabling Her Majesty's Government in the United Kingdom—

- (a) to give effect to assimilated[^c-8574f34bcb08a63f409e19e551f23f9b][^c-5d40c2e31375a5029b71156d10b5d78b] obligations or exercise any related right; or
- (b) to give effect to any obligation or exercise any related right under any international agreement to which the United Kingdom is for the time being a party

(2) In this Article “related right”, in relation to an obligation, includes any derogation or other right to make more onerous provisions available in respect of that obligation.
