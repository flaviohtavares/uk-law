## 2. Interpretation

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 (c.33) applies to this Order as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

(2) In this Order—

- “the Department” means the Department of the Environment;
- “modifications” includes additions, alterations and omissions
- “statutory provision” has the meaning given in section 1(f) of the Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 (c.33).
