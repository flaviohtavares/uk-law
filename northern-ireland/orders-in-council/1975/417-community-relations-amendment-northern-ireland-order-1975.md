# Community Relations (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 1975

> Text as at 2006-01-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

1975 No. 417 (N.I. 2)

Community Relations (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 1975 [^c545697]

\[18th March 1975\]

## 1. Title and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Community Relations (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 1975 … *Commencement* …

## 2. Interpretation

(1) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 [^m00001] shall apply to Article 1 and the following provisions of this Order as it applies to a Measure of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

(2) In this Order “Department” includes the Head of a Department.

*Art. 3 rep. by 1999 NI 1*

## 4. Functions of the Department of Education

*Para.(1) rep. by 1999 NI 1*

(2) [^c545698]... The Department of Finance and Personnel[^c545698] shall be responsible for formulating and sponsoring policies for the improvement of community relations in Northern Ireland.

(3) Without prejudice to the generality of paragraph (2), the powers of the Department of Finance of Personnel[^c545698] under that paragraph include—

- (a) giving financial or other assistance to any persons or organisations appearing to the Department of Finance and Personnel[^c545698] to be concerned with community relations; and
- (b) giving financial assistance towards the conduct of research into community relations.

*Para.(4) rep. by SR 1990/77*

(5) In paragraphs (2) and (3) any reference to community relations shall be construed as including a reference to any matter connected with or affecting those relations.

**Not yet in force.**

*Art.5(a) rep. by 1986 NI 14; para.(b) amends art.67 of 1972 NI 14; para.(c), with Schedule, effects repeals*
