# The Criminal Justice (No. 2) (Northern Ireland) Order 2003

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

2003 No. 3194 (N.I. 18)

The Criminal Justice (No. 2) (Northern Ireland) Order 2003 (repealed)

[^c2050798]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## 1. Title and commencement

**No longer in force (ended 2007-03-01).**

(1) This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice (No. 2) (Northern Ireland) Order 2003.

(2) This Order shall come into operation on 29th January 2004.

## 2. Interpretation

**No longer in force (ended 2007-03-01).**

The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 (c. 33) applies to this Order as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

## 3. Power of arrest for possession of cannabis

**No longer in force (ended 2007-03-01).**

In Article 26(2) of the Police and Criminal Evidence (Northern Ireland) Order 1989 (NI 12) (list of offences to which powers of summary arrest apply) after sub-paragraph (l) insert—

> - (m) an offence under section 5(2) of the Misuse of Drugs Act 1971 (c. 38) (having possession of a controlled drug) in respect of cannabis or cannabis resin (within the meaning of that Act).

.
