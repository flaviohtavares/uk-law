# The Energy (Northern Ireland) Order 2003, section 6B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/2003/419
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 6B. Decisions of the Agency for the Cooperation of Energy Regulators and of the European Commission[^c21264271]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-ebab704ceaaf71efe86f0b7889dfb971] The Authority shall carry out its functions in the manner that it considers is best calculated to implement, or to ensure compliance with, any binding decision of the Agency or the European Commission made under the 2009 Electricity Directive,[^c-a9776b1609c78b9e172d116b568d5de4] the Electricity Directive, the Gas Directive, the 2009 Electricity Market Regulation,[^c-26494fd9aca472b6a7e9676dbdf0ad2d] the Electricity Regulation, the Gas Regulation , the 2009 Agency Regulation[^c-39e1bda95e4b6b0860bc3a80c6142e78] or the Agency Regulation.

(2)[^c-abbfdd2ac2900b03b0541c1ea696a61f] For the purposes of paragraph (1), a binding decision made under the Gas Directive or the Gas Regulation[^c-f2d74bb632cd318137a32b25c08da13d] does not include a decision that is not, or so much of a decision as is not, assimilated[^c-fc3615a6964139e31ae16d0e46d8b668] law.
