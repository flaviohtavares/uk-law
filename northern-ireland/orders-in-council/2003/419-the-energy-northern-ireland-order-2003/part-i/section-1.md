# The Energy (Northern Ireland) Order 2003, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/2003/419
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 1. Title and commencement

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This Order may be cited as the Energy (Northern Ireland) Order 2003.

(2)[^c17147521] Except as provided by paragraph (3), this Order shall come into operation on such day or days as the Department may by order appoint.

(3) The following provisions come into operation on the day after the day on which this Order is made—

- (a) this Part;
- (b) Articles 59 and 60;
- (c) Article 64;
- (d) Articles 66 and 67;
- (e) paragraph 1(2) of Schedule 3 (and Article 65 so far as relating thereto);
- (f) Schedule 4 (and Article 65 so far as relating thereto).
