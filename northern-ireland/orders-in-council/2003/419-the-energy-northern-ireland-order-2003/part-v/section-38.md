# The Energy (Northern Ireland) Order 2003, section 38

## 38. Modification of licences: general provisions

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c20602891][^c20602901][^c21264871][^c21264881][^c22560811] In exercising any power to modify an electricity licence under the Electricity Order or this Order, the Department, the Authority, the Secretary of State or the Competition and Markets Authority[^c22100371] (as the case may be) shall have regard to the requirements and prohibitions laid down in the Electricity Directive[^c21264831][^c17147571].

(2)[^c21264891][^c22560821] In exercising any power to modify a gas licence under the Gas Order or this Order, the Department, the Authority, the Secretary of State or the Competition and Markets Authority[^c22100391] (as the case may be) shall have regard to any requirements and prohibitions contained in[^key-8a68aca3cdf9cde9877c17e7c63693f8] assimilated[^key-10dac3537e364218b4490f3727cffcd9] law which correspond to prohibitions and requirements in the Gas Directive.
