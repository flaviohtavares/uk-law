> Text as at 2022-08-29.

## 135. Definition of “homelessness”

*Extent: N.I.*

In Article 3 of the Order of 1988 (homelessness and threatened homelessness), in paragraph (1) for the words “in Northern Ireland” there shall be substituted “ available for his occupation in the United Kingdom or elsewhere ”.
