> Text as at 2022-08-29.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 145. Service of certain documents

*Extent: N.I.*

In Article 104 of the Order of 1992 (service of certain documents)—

- (a) in paragraph (1) the words “Executive as” and “the Executive by” shall cease to have effect;
- (b) in paragraph (2)—
  - (i) after the words “to the Executive” there shall be inserted “ or such other body as may be prescribed ”;
  - (ii) in sub-paragraph (a) the words “by the Executive” shall cease to have effect.
