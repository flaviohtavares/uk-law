> Text as at 2022-08-29.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 13. Persons qualified to succeed tenant

*Extent: N.I.*

A person is qualified to succeed the tenant under an introductory tenancy if he occupies the dwelling-house as his only or principal home at the time of the tenant's death and either—

- (a) he is the tenant's spouse or civil partner[^c17101591], or
- (b) he is another member of the tenant's family and has resided with the tenant throughout the period of 12 months ending with the tenant's death;

unless, in either case, the tenant was himself a successor, as defined in Article 14.
