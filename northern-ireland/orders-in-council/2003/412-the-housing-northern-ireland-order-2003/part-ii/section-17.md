## 17. Right of introductory tenants of the Executive to have repairs carried out

*Extent: N.I.*

The Department may direct that any scheme for the time being approved by it under Article 38A of the Order of 1983 (see Article 133) shall apply to introductory tenants of the Executive in the same manner as it applies to secure tenants of the Executive.
