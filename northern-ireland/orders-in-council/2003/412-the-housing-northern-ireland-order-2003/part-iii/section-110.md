# The Housing (Northern Ireland) Order 2003, section 110

> Text as at 2022-08-29.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 110. Interpretation of Chapter V

*Extent: N.I.*

In this Chapter—

- “deferred action notice” shall be construed in accordance with Article 111;
- “flat”, “house” and “owner” have the meaning given in Article 2 of the Order of 1981; and
- “flat in multiple occupation” means a house in multiple occupation within the meaning of the Act of 2016 which is a flat[^key-cb528075e5d8d2ce57d0887aa42f1377] .
