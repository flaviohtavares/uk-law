# The Housing (Northern Ireland) Order 2003, section 104

> Text as at 2022-08-29.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 104. Power of Department to modify operation of Chapter

*Extent: N.I.*

If the Department so directs in the case of any scheme or any description of scheme, such of the preceding provisions of this Chapter as are specified in the direction shall not apply in relation to that scheme or, as the case may be, in relation to a scheme of that description.
