# The Housing (Northern Ireland) Order 2003, section 148

> Text as at 2022-08-29.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 148. Orders and regulations

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Any regulations or order made by the Department under this Order may contain such incidental, supplementary or transitional provisions or savings as the Department considers appropriate.

(2) Subject to paragraphs (3) and (4), an order or regulations made by the Department under this Order shall be subject to negative resolution.

(3) Paragraph (2) does not apply to—

- (a) an order made under Article 1 (commencement); or
- (b) regulations (other than regulations under Article 19A(3))[^c22062761] which only prescribe forms or particulars to be contained in forms.

(4) An order made under, or including provision for the purposes of, paragraph (a), (b) or (g) of paragraph 2(3) of Schedule 2 shall be subject to affirmative resolution.
