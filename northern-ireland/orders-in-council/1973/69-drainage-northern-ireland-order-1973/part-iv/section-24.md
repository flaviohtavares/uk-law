> Text as at 2024-01-01.

## 24. Duty of occupiers with respect to undesignated watercourses

*Extent: N.I.*

The provisions of Schedule 5 shall have effect in relation to the duties of occupiers of land with respect to undesignated watercourses, and to the other matters specified in that Schedule.
