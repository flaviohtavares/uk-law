# Drainage (Northern Ireland) Order 1973, section 42

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 42. Financial provisions

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Any expenses incurred by the Ministry under this Order, and any contributions or grants paid by the Ministry under this Order, not being expenses or sums chargeable or charged to the Erne Development Account under paragraph 11(3) of Schedule 7, may be defrayed out of money hereafter appropriated for the purpose of meeting such expenditure.

(2) Any sum received by the Ministry under Article 17(3), 25, 27, 28 or 34A[^c17525551], or under paragraph 7 of Schedule 5 or paragraph 5, 6 or 8 of Schedule 6[^c17525571]. . . , shall be appropriated in aid of the expenses incurred by the Ministry under this Order or in the payment of any contributions or grants under this Order.
