> Text as at 2024-01-01.

## 39. Protection for officers of Ministry

*Extent: N.I.*

An action shall not lie at law or in equity against the Ministry or any of its officers or servants in respect of any act, matter or thing in respect of which compensation is payable, or protection is provided, by virtue of any provision of this Order.
