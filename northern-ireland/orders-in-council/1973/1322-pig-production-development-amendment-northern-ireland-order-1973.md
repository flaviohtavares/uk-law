## 1. Title and commencement

**No longer in force (ended 2009-06-01).**

## 2. Interpretation

**No longer in force (ended 2009-06-01).**

## 3. Increase in limit on levy payable in respect of pigs sold to the Pigs Marketing Board.

**No longer in force (ended 2009-06-01).**

The limit imposed by section 4(1) of the Pig Production Development Act (Northern Ireland) 1964 [^m00002] on the amount of the levy payable under that section in respect of each pig sold to the Pigs Marketing Board shall be increased to twenty pence and accordingly … *residue amends s.4 of 1964 c.25 (NI)*
