# Planning (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 1978

Planning (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 1978

\[25th July 1978\]

## PART I — INTRODUCTORY

### 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Planning (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 1978[^c15897801]. . . .

(2) Article 4 shall come into operation on such day as the head of the Department of the Environment may by order appoint[^c15897811] and the remaining provisions of this Order shall come into operation at the expiration of the period of one month from the day on which it is made.

### 2. Interpretation

(1) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 [^m00001] shall apply to the interpretation of Article 1 and the following provisions of this Order as it applies to the interpretation of a Measure of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

(2) In this Order—

- “the Department” means the Department of the Environment;
- [^c-995a72a3f805349595ba0ca774156e05]...

(3) Any expression used in Part II and in the Planning Act (Northern Ireland) 2011[^c-abdbb900067ad29a604f63d87e30af85] has the same meaning in that Part as in that Act[^c-94a192b2f8273dab40fb1cd6980b493a].

## PART II — PLANNING

### 3. Exclusion of compensation in certain cases where planning permission is revoked

Where—

- (a) planning permission granted under Part 3 of the Planning Act (Northern Ireland) 2011[^c-c4061ecad4e0fd53d7904b62b577c8e4] is revoked with the consent of a person entitled to a compensatable estate in the land to which the permission related (in this Article referred to as “the original land”);
- (b) planning permission is granted under that Part on or after the date of the revocation referred to in paragraph ( *a*) in relation to land other than the original land, for development similar to the development permitted by the revoked permission; and
- (c) the permission first referred to in paragraph ( *b*) specifies that it is in substitution for the permission referred to in paragraph ( *a*);

no compensation shall be payable under section 26 of the Land Development Values (Compensation) Act (Northern Ireland) 1965 [^m00002] in respect of the revocation referred to in paragraph ( *a*) to a person who has consented under that paragraph to the revocation.
