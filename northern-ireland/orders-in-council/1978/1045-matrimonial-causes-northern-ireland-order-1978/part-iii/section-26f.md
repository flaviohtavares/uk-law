# Matrimonial Causes (Northern Ireland) Order 1978, section 26F

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 26F. Pension compensation sharing orders: apportionment of charges[^c-e114e6a9c14e900cf11f1dd2403894e1]

*Extent: N.I.*

The court may include in a pension compensation sharing order provision about the apportionment between the parties of any charge under section 95 of the Pensions (No. 2) Act (Northern Ireland) 2008 (charges in respect of pension compensation sharing costs), or under section 117 of the Pensions Act 2008.
