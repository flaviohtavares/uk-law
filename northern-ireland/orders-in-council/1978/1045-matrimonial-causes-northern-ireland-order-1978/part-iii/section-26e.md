# Matrimonial Causes (Northern Ireland) Order 1978, section 26E

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 26E. Pension compensation sharing orders: duty to stay[^key-e114e6a9c14e900cf11f1dd2403894e1]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) No pension compensation sharing order may be made so as to take effect before the end of such period after the making of the order as may be prescribed by regulations made by the Department of Justice.

(2) Regulations under this Article shall be subject to negative resolution.
