# Matrimonial Causes (Northern Ireland) Order 1978, section 60

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 60. Abolition of wife's agency of necessity

*Extent: N.I.*

Any rule of law or equity conferring on a wife authority, as agent of necessity of her husband, to pledge his credit or borrow money on his credit is hereby abrogated.
