# Matrimonial Causes (Northern Ireland) Order 1978, section 59

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 59. Abolition of right to claim restitution of conjugal rights

*Extent: N.I.*

After the commencement of this Article no person shall be entitled to petition the court for restitution of conjugal rights.
