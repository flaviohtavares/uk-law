# Health and Safety at Work (Northern Ireland) Order 1978, section 29

> Text as at 2024-10-21.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 29. Obtaining of information[^c17536611][^c17536621][^c21340541][^c21342791][^key-f548968fd623a9620c9b359b640f0114][^key-da3fc6d0242531b2baf00dcc8470e0fc][^key-6d169bf988d924da4ac56092ae773c4a][^key-ed5ff11fa2764c04e9a851b822d7fed0][^key-18510339c238a3352394fe1d804107be][^key-73ef94d86e08046558127a6c596b6d1c][^key-0f22c772cf0a6622cce91cf0c5e4c31d][^key-792e0e8271171141d0721ef84b384198][^key-31784603373a4fc0c18fd3ef08be5650][^key-7ab187d70da9443fa135290259e4c79c][^key-a466776691ee45f580dcdbb0fa7a0694][^key-54eb4a5e76ea50eaf657fd4ede8e5f48]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) For the purpose of obtaining—

*Sub‐para.(a) rep. by 1998 NI 18*

- (b) any information which an enforcing authority needs for the discharge of the authority's functions,

the Department concerned, or the Executive[^c17536631][^c17536631]. . . , may serve on any person a notice requiring that person to furnish to the Executive or, as the case may be, to the enforcing authority in question such information about such matters as may be specified in the notice, and to do so in such form and manner and within such time as may be so specified.

*Paras.(2)‐(4) rep. by 1988 NI 3*
