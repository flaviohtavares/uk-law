# Health and Safety at Work (Northern Ireland) Order 1978, section 9

> Text as at 2024-10-21.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 9. Duty not to interfere with or misuse things provided pursuant to certain provisions[^c17534591]

*Extent: N.I.*

[^c17534601][^c17534611]No person shall intentionally or recklessly interfere with or misuse anything provided in the interests of health, safety or welfare in pursuance of any of the relevant statutory provisions.
