## 33. Venue[^c17537151][^c17537161][^c19255881][^c21340621][^c21342851][^c22013631][^key-08fc1a01168c1dc37411512923d3ac92][^key-bec1b2cfe3b57b52d60df83b85e7369e][^key-983cd85e0ac59e1b22d22f9e4d939a02][^key-11cd1571c491ed9b0eb21a6b640c31c2][^key-534b523eff6f30bec622820ad23444aa][^key-479212d3c5be726f8eab094f98300ab0][^key-ed5ff11fa2764c04e9a851b822d7fed0][^key-ba8f75500bd4f0ac75236a0d327d3c2c][^key-6258d1f5b895883cb1afaac58524ac23][^key-373ea07e327a37ea339c0bdaa879ab56][^key-54cdf7eab6b62ab4fc63e7ee9a1aa7e8][^key-6ab2b2cc76b64054a75d3e78bc5eb504][^key-ef693c3a6bfe7a4a33f2f650755f82b3][^key-1ae93b8735696a7f1dbdaf01af842930][^key-4ce18e2be57bdfd16343a8e931510f6e][^key-33878ef0d1f2469d808dbb7481966efe][^key-283ea9d1563bc39e20599616875ab4c3]

*Extent: N.I.*

An offence under any of the relevant statutory provisions committed in connection with any plant or substance may, if necessary for the purpose of bringing the offence within the field of responsibility of any enforcing authority or conferring jurisdiction on any court to entertain proceedings for the offence, be treated as having been committed at the place where that plant or substance is for the time being.
