> Text as at 2024-10-21.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 47. Exclusion of application to domestic employment[^c17537851]

*Extent: N.I.*

[^c17537861]Nothing in this Part shall apply in relation to a person by reason only that he employs another, or is himself employed, as a domestic servant in a private household.
