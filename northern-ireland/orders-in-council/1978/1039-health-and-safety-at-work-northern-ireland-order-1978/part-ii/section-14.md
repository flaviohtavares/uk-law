# Health and Safety at Work (Northern Ireland) Order 1978, section 14

> Text as at 2024-10-21.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 14. Control of the Executive by the Department[^c17535001][^c17534981]

*Extent: N.I.*

[^c17535011]The Department may—

- (a) approve, with or without modifications, any proposals submitted to it in pursuance of Article 13(4)( *a*);
- (b) give to the Executive at any time such directions as the Department thinks fit with respect to the functions of the Executive.
