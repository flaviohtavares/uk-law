# Rent (Northern Ireland) Order 1978, section 57

> Text as at 2020-01-13.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 57. Savings (Part IX)[^c22529301]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Nothing in this Part shall affect the jurisdiction of the High Court in proceedings—

- (a) to enforce a lessor's right of re-entry or forfeiture;
- (b) to enforce a mortgagee's right of possession in a case where the former tenancy was not binding on the mortgagee.

(2) Nothing in this Part shall affect the operation of—

- (a) section 19 of the Defence Act 1842 [^m00004];
- (b) section 91 of the Lands Clauses Consolidation Act 1845 [^m00005];
- (c) section 5(1) of the Criminal Law Amendment Act 1912 [^m00006].
- (d)[^c16841281] Article 129 of, and Schedule 15 to, the Betting, Gaming, Lotteries and Amusements (Northern Ireland) Order 1985
