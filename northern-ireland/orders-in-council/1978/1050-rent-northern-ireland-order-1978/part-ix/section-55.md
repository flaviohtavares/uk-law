# Rent (Northern Ireland) Order 1978, section 55

> Text as at 2020-01-13.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 55. Restriction on re-entry without due process of law[^c22529281]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where any premises are let as a dwelling-house on a lease which is subject to a right of re-entry or forfeiture it shall not be lawful to enforce that right otherwise than in pursuance of proceedings in the court while any person is lawfully residing in the premises or any part of them.
