# Rent (Northern Ireland) Order 1978, section 73A

> Text as at 2020-01-13.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 73A. Method of serving certain documents[^c16841301]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Any document to be served under Article 73(2)[^c19286561] may be served by being sent by ordinary post.

(2) In section 24(1) of the Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 (service of documents), as it applies to the service by post of such a document, the word “registering” shall be omitted.
