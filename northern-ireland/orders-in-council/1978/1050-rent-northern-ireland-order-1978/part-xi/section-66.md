# Rent (Northern Ireland) Order 1978, section 66

> Text as at 2020-01-13.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 66. Overholding

*Extent: N.I.*

A tenant shall not be deemed for the purposes of section 76 of the Landlord and Tenant Law Amendment Act, Ireland, 1860 [^m00007] (payment of double rent for overholding) wilfully to hold over any land comprised in a tenancy by reason only of his remaining in possession thereof under or by virtue of this Order.
