## 1. Title and commencement

## 2. Interpretation

(1) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 [^m00001] shall apply to Article 1 and the following provisions of this Order (except Article 5(2)) as it applies to an Act of the Parliament of Northern Ireland.

*Para. (2) rep. in pt. by 1977 NI 28; residue spent*

(3) Expressions used in Article 3 which are defined in the [^m00002] Electoral Law Act (Northern Ireland) 1962 have the same meanings in that Article as in that Act.

*Para. (4) rep. by 1977 NI 28*

*Art. 3(a) amends ss. 11, 65 of 1962 c. 14 (NI), arts 3, 4 of 1972 NI 13; paras. (b) (c) spent; para. (d) amends sch. 2 to 1972 c. 9 (NI)*

*Art. 4(1) spent; para. (2)(a) (b) (c), with Schedules 1, 2, effects amendments; sub‐para.(d) rep. by 1977 NI 28; sub‐para. (e) repeals sch.2 (pt.) to 1972 NI 12; para. (3) spent*
