## (not yet in force: SCHEDULE 15A) — (not yet in force: DENTAL CHARGING: EXEMPTIONS)[^c20792961]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

### (not yet in force: General exemptions)

**(not yet in force: 1).** ((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: No charge is to be made under regulations under paragraph 1A(1) of Schedule 15 in respect of a relevant dental service provided for any person who at the prescribed time—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: was under the age of 18;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: was under the age of 19 and receiving qualifying full-time education;)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: was pregnant; or)*
- ((not yet in force: d)) *(not yet in force: had given birth to a child within the previous 12 months.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: In sub-paragraph (1)(b), “)****(not yet in force: qualifying full-time education)****(not yet in force: ” means full-time instruction at a recognised educational establishment or by other means accepted as comparable by the person or body making the regulations.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: For the purposes of sub-paragraph (2)—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: “)****(not yet in force: recognised educational establishment)****(not yet in force: ” means an establishment recognised by the Department as being, or as comparable to, a school, college or university; and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: regulations may prescribe the circumstances in which a person is or is not to be treated as receiving full-time instruction.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: In sub-paragraph (1)(d), “)****(not yet in force: child)****(not yet in force: ” includes a still-born child (within the meaning of the Births and Deaths Registration (Northern Ireland) Order 1976).)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: This paragraph is subject to paragraph 3.)*

### (not yet in force: Repair and replacement)

**(not yet in force: 2).** ((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: No charge is to be made under regulations under paragraph 1A(1) of Schedule 15 in respect of the repair or replacement of any appliance.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: This paragraph is subject to paragraph 3.)*

### (not yet in force: Exceptions to paragraphs 1 and 2)

**(not yet in force: 3).** *(not yet in force: Paragraphs 1 and 2 do not apply in relation to—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: the repair or replacement of any appliance of a prescribed description; or)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: the repair or replacement of any appliance where it is determined in the prescribed manner—)*
  - ((not yet in force: i)) *(not yet in force: in any case, that the repair or replacement was necessitated by an act or omission of the person supplied; or)*
  - ((not yet in force: ii)) *(not yet in force: in a case where the person supplied was under the age of 16, that the repair or replacement was necessitated by an act or omission, occurring while that person was under that age, of a person having charge of him.)*

### (not yet in force: Hospital patients)

**(not yet in force: 4).** *(not yet in force: No charge is to be made under regulations under paragraph 1A(1) of Schedule 15 in respect of any appliance supplied to a patient for the time being resident in a hospital.)*

**(not yet in force: 5).** *(not yet in force: Paragraph 4 does not apply where an appliance is supplied—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: under Article 60A(2);)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: under a general dental services contract; or)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: in accordance with Article 15B arrangements.)*

### (not yet in force: Arrest of bleeding)

**(not yet in force: 6).** *(not yet in force: No charge is to be made under regulations under paragraph 1A(1) of Schedule 15 in respect of the arrest of bleeding.)*

### (not yet in force: Declarations and evidence)

**(not yet in force: 7).** *(not yet in force: Regulations may provide, with respect to any exemption under this Schedule, that it is to be a condition of the exemption that—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: a declaration of the prescribed kind is made in the prescribed form and manner; or)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: a certificate or other evidence of the prescribed kind is supplied in the prescribed form and manner.)*
