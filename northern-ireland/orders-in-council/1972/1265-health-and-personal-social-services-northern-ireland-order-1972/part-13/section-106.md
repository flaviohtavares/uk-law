# Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1972, section 106

## 106. Miscellaneous administrative matters

*Extent: N.I.*

Regulations may—

- (a) provide for the determination by arbitration, in default of agreement, of any question arising as to whether any asset or liability has been transferred by virtue of section 27 of the Health Services Act (Northern Ireland) 1948 [^m00026] or section 6 of the Health Services (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 1967 [^m00027] or as to the person to whom it has been transferred by virtue of those sections;
- (b) prescribe anything which is necessary or expedient for the purpose of giving due effect to the provisions of this Order.
