## 47. Loans to certain officers

*Extent: N.I.*

The Ministry may make loans to such officers employed for the purposes of any of the health or social care[^c23379631] and subject to such conditions as the Ministry, with the approval[^c17171681]. . . of the Ministry of Finance, may determine to facilitate such officers in the purchase of motor cars or motor cycles essential to the efficient carrying out of their official duties.
