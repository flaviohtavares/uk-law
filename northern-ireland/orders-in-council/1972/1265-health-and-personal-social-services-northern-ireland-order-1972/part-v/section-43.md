## 43. Powers of Ministry to conduct or assist research

*Extent: N.I.*

The Ministry may conduct or promote, or assist (by grant or otherwise) any person in conducting, research into—

- (a) any matter relating to the causation, prevention, diagnosis or treatment of illness, or into such other matters relating to the health care[^c23379511] as it thinks fit;
- (b) any matter relating to the social care[^c23379501].
