## 87. Expenses of the special agencies[^c23385191]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c23385221] The Department shall pay to the special agencies such sums as may be necessary to defray the expenditure of those bodies, being expenditure approved by the Ministry[^c17173741]. . . .

(2) [^c23385251]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Payments made under this Article shall be made at such times and in such manner, and subject to such conditions as to records, certificates or otherwise, as the Ministry may determine.
