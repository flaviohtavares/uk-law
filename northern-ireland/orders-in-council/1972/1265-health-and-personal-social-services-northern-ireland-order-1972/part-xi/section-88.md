# Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1972, section 88

## 88. Regulation of financial arrangements of certain bodies

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Ministry may give directions to each special agency[^c23385261] restricting the making of payments by or on behalf of each of those bodies otherwise than on such authorisation and subject to such conditions as may be specified in the directions, but such provision may be made subject to such exceptions as may be so specified.

(2) Directions under paragraph (1) may contain such other provisions as to the making and carrying out by all or any of those bodies of such arrangements with respect to financial matters as the Ministry thinks necessary for the purpose of securing that the affairs of such bodies are conducted, so far as reasonably practicable, in such manner as to prevent financial loss and to ensure and maintain efficiency.
