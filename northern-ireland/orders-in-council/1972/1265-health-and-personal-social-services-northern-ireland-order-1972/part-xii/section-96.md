## 96. Preservation of associations of denominational hospitals

*Extent: N.I.*

Where the character and associations of any hospital through which services are being or are to be provided under this Order are such as to link that hospital with a particular religious denomination, regard shall be had in the general administration of the hospital and in the making of appointments to the HSC trust[^c23386721] administering the hospital to the preservation of the character and associations of the hospital.
