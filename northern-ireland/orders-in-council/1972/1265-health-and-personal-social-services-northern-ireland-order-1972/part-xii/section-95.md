## 95. Exercise, in certain cases, of right of choice of person by whom services are to be provided

*Extent: N.I.*

Where a right to choose the person by whom services are to be provided under this Order is conferred by or under any provision of this Order, that right shall, in the case of such persons as may be prescribed, be exercisable on their behalf by other prescribed persons.
