# Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1972, section 81

## 81. Transfer of certain property held by health and welfare authorities

*Extent: N.I.*

*Para.(1) rep. by SLR 1980 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fceb68af5de84e4dab0ae8bde9c4b32f]*

(2) The property and income transferred under paragraph (1) shall be held by the Health and Social Services Board and administered by that Board in accordance with the terms of the agreement made between the authority concerned and the organisation or, as the case may be, the trustees. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fceb68af5de84e4dab0ae8bde9c4b32f]

(3) An agreement made under section 42(4) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fceb68af5de84e4dab0ae8bde9c4b32f][^c17173481] of, or under section 28 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fceb68af5de84e4dab0ae8bde9c4b32f][^c17173491] of, the said Acts of 1971 may be varied in the like manner and subject to the like consents and conditions as are provided for in, or by virtue of, that section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fceb68af5de84e4dab0ae8bde9c4b32f]

(4) Where the Attorney-General is satisfied that an organisation is no longer in existence or, as the case may be, the trustees are dead, he may consent to the variation of the terms of an agreement under paragraph (3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fceb68af5de84e4dab0ae8bde9c4b32f]
