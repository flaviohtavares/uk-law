## 85. Power for certain health bodies to accept property on trust, etc[^key-49710b8022a3767ada4eb6b8ffc3661c]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) [^key-8f8dc5639557456df2772401cb362359]... A special agency[^c17173521] may accept, hold and administer any property on trust for purposes relating to any service which it is that body's function to make arrangements for, administer or provide.

(2)[^c17173561] For the purposes of giving effect to the provisions of any will, deed or other like instrument—

- (a) any reference therein to a management committee, a health authority or a welfare authority shall be construed as a reference to the appropriate HSC trust[^key-55c8e9bf8fe1ef2bc333ca16936e13fe] ;
- (b)[^key-2d4c60cd3d316ed9a1b5dc95bd27e719] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) any reference therein to a hospital or other establishment or facility managed by an HSC trust[^c23384831] shall be construed as a reference to that trust.

(3) Nothing in Article 87 shall affect the operation of any such provisions as are referred to in paragraph (2).
