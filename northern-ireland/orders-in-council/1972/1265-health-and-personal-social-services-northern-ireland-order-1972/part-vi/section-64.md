# Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1972, section 64

## 64. Persons authorised to provide pharmaceutical services

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c23367241] Except as provided by or under regulations[^c23367261], an arrangement shall not be made by the Department[^c-aa6de5497d15f7e5b5000f7401cfec31] —

- (a) with a medical or dental practitioner, under which he is required or agrees to provide pharmaceutical services to any person to whom he is rendering primary medical services[^c23367281] or general dental services; or
- (b) with a person who is not a pharmacist, for the dispensing of medicines.

(1A)[^c23367301] Regulations shall provide for the preparation and publication by the Department[^c-64b8e729cb1872eb43542186a3e41b31] of one or more lists of medical practitioners who are required or agree to provide drugs, medicines or listed appliances [^c-f90bc50d0956b0901de6bd7033890ce0]....

(1B) In paragraph (1A) “listed” has the same meaning as in Article 63.

(1C) The regulations shall include provision for the removal of an entry from a list in prescribed circumstances.

(2)[^c23367321] No arrangements for the provision of pharmaceutical services falling within Article 63(1)(c), or additional pharmaceutical services provided in accordance with[^c23367331] [^c-dea80f0691f5f295d36028aec0e728bd]... Article 63A, shall be made with persons other than those who are pharmacists or are of a prescribed description.
