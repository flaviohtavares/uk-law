## 66. Former dispensary property, etc.

*Extent: N.I.*

The provisions of Schedule 12 shall apply to any property which was transferred to and vested in the Northern Ireland General Health Services Board consequent on section 19 of the Health Services Act (Northern Ireland) 1948 [^m00021] and transferred to and vested in the Ministry by virtue of section 6(1)( *b*) of the Health Services (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 1967 [^m00022].
