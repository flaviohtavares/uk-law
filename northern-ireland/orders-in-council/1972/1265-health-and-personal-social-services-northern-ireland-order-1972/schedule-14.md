## SCHEDULE 14 — ENDOWMENTS OF CERTAIN HOSPITALS (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-103413dcf048cc0bd6ce3cedf82d4b8f]

*Extent: N.I.*

Articles 79(2), 83(2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-103413dcf048cc0bd6ce3cedf82d4b8f]

**1.** Any endowment to which this Schedule applies shall, notwithstanding anything contained in any enactment or rule of law, be applied and used by the Health and Social Services Board— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-103413dcf048cc0bd6ce3cedf82d4b8f]

- (a) firstly, in the discharge, to the extent to which the endowment could, immediately before 5th July 1948, have been lawfully applied and used for that purpose, of so much of the liabilities transferred to the management committee under section 25(3), (4) and (7) of the Health Services Act (Northern Ireland) 1948 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-103413dcf048cc0bd6ce3cedf82d4b8f] [^m00032] as may be prescribed; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-103413dcf048cc0bd6ce3cedf82d4b8f]
- (b) secondly, subject to paragraph 2, for such the purposes to which it could, immediately before 5th July 1948, have been lawfully applied or used as the Health and Social Services Board may think proper. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-103413dcf048cc0bd6ce3cedf82d4b8f]

**2.** Where the Health and Social Services Board is satisfied that it has become impracticable or unnecessary to apply and use the endowment in accordance with paragraph 1( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-103413dcf048cc0bd6ce3cedf82d4b8f] *b*), that Board may, notwithstanding anything contained in the trusts upon which the endowment is held, apply and use the capital or income of the whole or any part of the endowment for such of the purposes connected with the hospital as it may think proper. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-103413dcf048cc0bd6ce3cedf82d4b8f]
