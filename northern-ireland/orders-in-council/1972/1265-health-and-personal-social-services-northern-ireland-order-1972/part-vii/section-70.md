## 70. Assistance to bodies providing certain housing accommodation, etc.

*Extent: N.I.*

The Ministry may make a contribution of such amount as it considers appropriate towards any expenditure incurred by the Northern Ireland Housing Executive or a housing association in respect of the provision, maintenance and management of housing accommodation for, or the provision of special facilities for, persons in need.
