## 67. Co-operation between Health and Social Services Boards, district councils, etc.[^key-5685f68e7137bfad476769d2d28f5563]

*Extent: N.I.*

In exercising their respective functions, health and social care bodies[^c23380631], district councils, the Education Authority[^key-75149bcd921d1fb3ea2aeb27c9c589ec] , the Northern Ireland Library Authority[^c20743331], \[[^c17173281] and\] the Northern Ireland Housing Executive …[^c17173291] shall co-operate with one another in order to secure and advance the health and social welfare of the people of Northern Ireland.
