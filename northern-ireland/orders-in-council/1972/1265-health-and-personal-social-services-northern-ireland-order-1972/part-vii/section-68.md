## 68. Supply of goods and services to certain persons and bodies

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Ministry may anda health and social care body[^c23382531] may, and if directed by the Ministry, shall—

- (a) purchase, store and supply to persons—[^c17173321]
  - (i) providing general medical services, general dental services, general opthalmic services or pharmaceutical services, or
  - (ii) providing, in accordance with Article 15B arrangements, personal medical services, personal dental services or other services of a kind that may be provided under Part VI,

  such equipment, goods or materials as may be prescribed;
- (b) purchase, store and supply to government departments and public bodies, any equipment, goods or materials of a kind which is used in the health or social care[^c23380651];
- (c) provide government departments and public bodies with any administrative, professional or other services of persons employed by the Ministry, or[^c17173361] a health and social care body;
- (d) permit government departments and public bodies to use premises occupied for the purposes of the health or social care;

on such terms and conditions as may be agreed.

(2) In paragraph (1)( *a*) and ( *b*), the power to supply equipment, goods and materials includes a power to make arrangements with third parties for the supply by them of those things.
