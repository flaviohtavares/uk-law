## 69. Supply of goods and services between Health and Social Services Boards and district councils

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c17173401] For any purpose connected with matters concerning public or environmental health the Ministry may directa health and social care body[^c23384421] and a district council

- (a) to make arrangements such as are mentioned in section 105 of the Local Government Act (Northern Ireland) 1972 [^m00023] in circumstances specified by the Ministry; and
- (b) to furnish to the Ministry or to the other party to the arrangements such information as appears to the Ministry to be expedient for purposes of the arrangements;

and that body and the district council shall comply with any such direction.
