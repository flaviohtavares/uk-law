# Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1972, section 12

## 12. Family planning

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Ministry shall make arrangments, to such extent as it considers necessary, for the giving of advice on contraception, the medical examination of persons seeking advice on contraception, the treatment of such persons and the supply of contraceptive substances or appliances.

(2) The Ministry may recover from persons availing themselves of any service under this Article such charges (if any) as the Ministry considers appropriate.
