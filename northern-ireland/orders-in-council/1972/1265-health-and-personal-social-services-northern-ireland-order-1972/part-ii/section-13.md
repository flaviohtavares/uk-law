## 13. Educational and research facilities

*Extent: N.I.*

The Ministry shall make available such facilities, in any premises provided by it under this Order or the 2009 Act[^c23378201], as appear to the Ministry to be reasonably required for under-graduate and post-graduate clinical teaching and research.
