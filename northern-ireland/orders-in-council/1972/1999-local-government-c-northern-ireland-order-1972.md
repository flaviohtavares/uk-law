Local Government &c. (Northern Ireland) Order 1972[^key-6efcd20181624752fcb97f0626d8338e]

\[20th December 1972\]

## 1. Title and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Local Government &c. (Northern Ireland) Order 1972.

(2) *Commencement*

## 2. Interpretation

(1) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 [^m00001] shall apply to Article 1 and the following provisions of this Order as it applies to an Act of the Parliament of Northern Ireland.

(2) In this Order—

- “the Ministry” means the Ministry of Development[^c11570651];
- “transferred provision” has the meaning assigned to it by section 1( *g*) of the Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954.
