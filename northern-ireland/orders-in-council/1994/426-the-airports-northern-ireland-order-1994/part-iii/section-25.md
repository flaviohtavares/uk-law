## 25. Status of certain airport operators as statutory undertakers

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This Article applies to—

- (a) any regulated airport;
- (b) any airport (other than a regulated airport) which is managed by a district council under Article 17; [^c22329021]. . .
- (c) [^c22329021]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) Schedule 5 shall have effect with respect to the application of the statutory provisions mentioned in that Schedule (which relate to statutory undertakers) to the airport operators of airports to which this Article applies.
