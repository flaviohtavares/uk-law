# The Airports (Northern Ireland) Order 1994, section 70

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2021-07-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/1994/426
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 70. Regulations and orders

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Regulations made by the Department under this Order shall be subject to negative resolution.

(2) Orders made by the Department under this Order (other than orders under Article 1(3), 3, 51(1), 54(2), or paragraph 1 of Schedule 7 and orders described in paragraph (2A)[^c-88927ac4c0860a5104bc7f25b350547d]) shall be subject to negative resolution.

(2A)[^c-bc31f2b717dc684f456652441894b11b] An order under Article 2A(9) increasing the specified sum by more than is necessary to reflect changes in the value of money may not be made unless a draft of the order has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, the Assembly.

(3) Regulations and orders under this Order may contain consequential, incidental, supplementary and transitional provisions.
