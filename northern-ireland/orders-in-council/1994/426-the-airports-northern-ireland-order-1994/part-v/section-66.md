## 66. Financial limits on borrowings, etc.

*Extent: N.I.*

The aggregate of any amounts outstanding by way of principal in respect of—

- (a) sums issued under Article 64 in fulfilment of guarantees given in respect of loans made to such companies; and
- (b) relevant loans within the meaning of Article 65,

shall not exceed £10 million.
