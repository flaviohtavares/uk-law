# The Airports (Northern Ireland) Order 1994, section 51

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2021-07-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/1994/426
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 51. The successor company

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department may by order nominate as the successor company for the purposes of this Order a company which—

- (a) is a company limited by shares (within the meaning of section 3 of the Companies Act 2006)[^c21401061]; and
- (b) is wholly owned by the Crown;

and references in this Order to “the successor company” are references to the company so nominated.

(2) In the Northern Ireland Assembly Disqualification Act 1975[^c5480131], in Part III of Schedule 1 (other disqualifying offices) there shall be inserted (at the appropriate place in alphabetical order) the following entry—

> Director of the successor company (within the meaning of Part V of the Airports (Northern Ireland) Order 1994), being a director nominated or appointed by a Northern Ireland department or by a person acting on behalf of a Northern Ireland department

.
