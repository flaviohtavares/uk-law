# The Airports (Northern Ireland) Order 1994, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2021-07-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/1994/426
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 1. Title and commencement

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This Order may be cited as the Airports (Northern Ireland) Order 1994.

(2) Subject to paragraph (3) and to Article 56(3), this Order shall come into operation on the expiration of 2 months from the day on which it is made.

(3) The following provisions shall come into operation on such day or days as the Department may by order appoint[^c5479141], namely—

- (a) Part IV;
- (b) Schedule 6;
- (c) paragraphs 2, 4, 5, 7, 8, 10 to 12 and 14 of Schedule 9, and Article 71(2) so far as relating thereto.
