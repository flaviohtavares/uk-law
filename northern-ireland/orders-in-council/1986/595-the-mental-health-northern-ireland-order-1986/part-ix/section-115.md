## 115. Appointment of approved social workers by authorised HSC trusts[^c-23fea8ac79bbbb41fe79c3c9e9cc2d53]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An authorised HSC trust[^c-f4ed5687209d9034e99d9e0901406268] shall appoint a sufficient number of approved social workers for the purpose of discharging the functions conferred on them by this Order.

(2) No person shall be appointed by a trust[^c-05356bb2c41e9c1e163900447c4735cc] as an approved social worker unless he is approved by the trust[^c-523e35beaab17350e5358b502cdd9154] as having appropriate competence in dealing with persons who are suffering from mental disorder.

(3) In approving a person for appointment as an approved social worker a trust[^c-741caeef2630686d77c0e1e60e53169e] shall have regard to such matters as the Department may direct.
