# The Mental Health (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 62

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 62. Patients to whom the provisions of this Part apply

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Article 63 and, so far as relevant to that Article, Articles 65, 66 and 68 apply to all patients.

(2) The other provisions of this Part apply to any patient liable to be detained under this Order except—

- (a) a patient who is liable to be detained by virtue of Article 7(2) or (3), 7A(2)[^c17125221], 42, 129 or 130 or by virtue of directions under Article 46(4); and
- (b) a patient who has been conditionally discharged under Article 48(2), 78 or 79 and has not been recalled to hospital.
