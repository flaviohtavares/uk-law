# The Mental Health (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 95

## 95. Application of this Order to private hospitals[^key-0dcaf1cd1dedc9d5c3a40f98b72a02c9][^key-07c9aba0cbd9e438d237becd43d73cad]

*Extent: N.I.*

The Department may by regulations made subject to affirmative resolution apply to, or in relation to, private hospitals such of the provisions of this Order (other than this Part) as the Department may think fit, subject to such adaptations and modifications as appear to the Department to be necessary.
