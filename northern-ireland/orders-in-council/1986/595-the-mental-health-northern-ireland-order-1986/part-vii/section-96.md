# The Mental Health (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 96

>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 96. Offences under Part VII[^key-c0e8ee42d1b050db01be21c6e78f2653]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Any person who carries on a private hospital which is not registered under this Part shall be guilty of an offence and liable—

- (a) on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum; or
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to a fine.

(2) Any person guilty of an offence under this Part (other than the offence mentioned in paragraph (1)) shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 1 on the standard scale.
