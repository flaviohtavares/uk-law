## 109. Interpretation of Part VIII

*Extent: N.I.*

In this Part—

- “the court” means the High Court;
- “patient” has the meaning assigned to it by Article 97(1);
- “property” includes any thing in action, and any interest in real or personal property;
- “rules of court” means rules of court made under section 55 of the [^m00021] Judicature (Northern Ireland) Act 1978.
