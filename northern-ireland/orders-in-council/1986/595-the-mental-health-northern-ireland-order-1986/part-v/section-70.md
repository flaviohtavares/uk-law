## 70. Constitution of the Review Tribunal

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Review Tribunal[^key-3985b43051f86314b1cdf1bd307ef93d] shall be constituted in accordance with Schedule 3.

(2)[^c22527131] The Department may pay to the members of the Review Tribunal such remuneration and allowances as the Department, with the approval of the Department of Finance and Personnel, may determine, and defray the expenses of the tribunal to such amount as the Department, with the approval of the Department of Finance and Personnel, may determine, and may provide for the tribunal such officers and servants, and such accommodation, as the tribunal may require.
