# The Mental Health (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 33

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (153).** Text as at 2024-04-17. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/1986/595
>

## 33. Children and young persons in care[^c-b981a25554f9bc333255f4e171d76546][^c-af92e68968cd6af7d536d2dd6f4bd32c][^c21355381]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where a patient who is a child or young person is in the care of an[^c-2612636c81d712b1b2f81e74525be93f] HSC[^c21355371] trust by virtue of a care order within the meaning of the Children (Northern Ireland) Order 1995, the [^c-83ec346775a789bdac5dbd55a0e82acc]... trust shall be deemed to be the nearest relative of the patient in preference to any person except the patient's spouse or civil partner[^c-4a33235819a7501ab0f441b62b79df93] (if any).
