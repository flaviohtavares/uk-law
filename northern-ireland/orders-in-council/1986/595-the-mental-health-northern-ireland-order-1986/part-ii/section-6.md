# The Mental Health (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 6

## 6. General provisions as to medical recommendation[^c-1873dedb163874b630f31c9d40b66ff5][^c-5a7355a757314f6863a0c035a1adbb4a][^c-42056f7475fc7e2edadb439c11c20616]

*Extent: N.I.*

The medical recommendation required for the purposes of an application for assessment shall be in the prescribed form and shall satisfy the following requirements, namely—

- (a)[^c-7535d63db3e445fd21824c9ba5f085f7] the recommendation shall be given and signed by a medical practitioner who has personally examined the patient not more than two days *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2b17288d09a33380871b30f200923d9a] five days before the date on which he signs the recommendation;
- (b) the recommendation shall, if practicable, be given by the patient's medical practitioner or by a medical practitioner who has previous acquaintance with the patient;
- (c) the recommendation shall not, except in a case of urgent necessity, be given by a medical practitioner on the staff of the hospital to which admission is sought;
- (d) the recommendation shall not be given by any of the persons described in Schedule 1.
