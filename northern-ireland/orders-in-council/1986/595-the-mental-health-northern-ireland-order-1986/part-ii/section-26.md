## 26. Regulations as to guardianship

*Extent: N.I.*

Subject to the provisions of this Part, the Department may make regulations—

- (a) for regulating the exercise by the guardians of patients received into guardianship under this Part of their powers as guardians; and
- (b) for imposing on those guardians and upon [^c-82e5af97e17c40eb39a4510c3a68cfe5]... HSC[^c21355891][^c17123821] trusts such duties as the Department considers necessary or expedient in the interests of the patients; and
- (c) requiring the patients to be visited, on such occasions or at such intervals as may be prescribed.
