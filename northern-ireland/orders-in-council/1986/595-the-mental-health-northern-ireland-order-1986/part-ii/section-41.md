# The Mental Health (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 41

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (153).** Text as at 2024-04-17. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/1986/595
>

## 41. Applications, recommendations and reports under Part II[^c-38c0e0d3256b8bd818bec865a19f6945][^c-6d09385791369d39e03f916a89adfb3f]

*Extent: N.I.*

Any application for assessment, guardianship application, recommendation or report which appears to be duly made under this Part may be acted upon without further proof of the signature or qualification of the person by whom the application, recommendation or report is made or given, or of any matter of fact or opinion stated therein.
