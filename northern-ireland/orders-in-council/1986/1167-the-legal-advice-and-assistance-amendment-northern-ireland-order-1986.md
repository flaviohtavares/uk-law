# The Legal Advice and Assistance (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 1986

> Text as at 2015-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

1986 No. 1167 (N.I. 12)

The Legal Advice and Assistance (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 1986 (REPEALED)

[^c-20877e46288b3cc3ad7c454a8cf3ce82]8th July 1986

## 1. Title and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Legal Advice and Assistance (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 1986. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-20877e46288b3cc3ad7c454a8cf3ce82]

(2) This Order shall come into operation on the expiration of twenty-one days from the day on which it is made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-20877e46288b3cc3ad7c454a8cf3ce82]

## 2. Interpretation

The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-20877e46288b3cc3ad7c454a8cf3ce82] [^m00001] Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 shall apply to Article 1 and the following provisions of this Order as it applies to a Measure of the Northern Ireland Assembly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-20877e46288b3cc3ad7c454a8cf3ce82]

## 3. Eligibility for legal advice and assistance

In Article 3 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-20877e46288b3cc3ad7c454a8cf3ce82] [^m00002] Legal Aid, Advice and Assistance (Northern Ireland) Order 1981 (paragraph (2)(b) of which enables the disposable capital limit on eligibility for legal advice and assistance to be altered by regulations) after paragraph (2) there shall be inserted— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-20877e46288b3cc3ad7c454a8cf3ce82]

> (3) Regulations made by virtue of paragraph (2)(b) may provide for the substitution of different capital sums in relation to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-20877e46288b3cc3ad7c454a8cf3ce82]
>
> - (a) advice and assistance other than assistance by way of representation; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-20877e46288b3cc3ad7c454a8cf3ce82]
> - (b) assistance by way of representation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-20877e46288b3cc3ad7c454a8cf3ce82]

.
