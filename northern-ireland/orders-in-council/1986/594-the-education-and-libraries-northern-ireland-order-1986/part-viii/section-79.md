## 79. Payments to members of boards, etc.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c19592571] A board shall make payments by way of travelling allowance or subsistence allowance,[^c17008961]. . . to any person to whom this paragraph applies[^key-76c7f5906fcfc9a09dc6a581f263086e] who necessarily incurs expenditure on travelling or, as the case may be, subsistence for the purpose of enabling him to perform any duty specified in the conditions.

(1A) Payments under paragraph (1) shall be made at such rates and on such conditions as may be—

- (a) determined by the Authority[^key-b411148a1ff6ad3f10e8cd22b37183f6] ; and
- (b) approved by the Department.

(2) Paragraph (1) shall apply to members of, or of a committee of,[^c17008991]

- (a) a Board of Governors of a controlled school;
- (b) a Board of Governors of a maintained school;[^c17009011]. . .
