## 109. Power of Head of the Department to appoint advisory bodies or committees[^c17009791]

*Extent: N.I.*

The Head of the Department may appoint such advisory bodies or committees as he considers necessary to assist the Department in the exercise and performance of the functions conferred on the Department by the Education Orders[^c17009801].
