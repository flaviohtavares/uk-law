# The Education and Libraries (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 107

## 107. Transfer to board of certain assets of Department

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department may, where it thinks fit, transfer to the Authority[^c-b411148a1ff6ad3f10e8cd22b37183f6] any assets or liabilities to which it is for the time being entitled or subject to for the purposes of the Education Orders[^c-acd4ae3df22c7a616894d4602515ea65] .

(2) The transfer to the Department [^c-961e7f478e9b6489524f3f422d425808]... of an endowment or other charitable gift shall be effective notwithstanding any provision to the contrary in any instrument making the endowment or gift but the Department shall, so far as practicable, ensure that the benefits accruing from any such endowment or gift are applied for the purposes for which the endowment or gift was made.
