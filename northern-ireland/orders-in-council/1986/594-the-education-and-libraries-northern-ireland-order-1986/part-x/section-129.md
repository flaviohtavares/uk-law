## 129. Publication of notices

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Notices and other matters required to be published under the Education Orders[^c17010161] shall, when no particular method is provided or indicated, be published either by advertisement in newspapers, or in such other manner as the Department may either generally or with respect to any particular area, place or notice, or class of area, place or notice, direct as being in its opinion sufficient for giving information to all persons interested.

(2) If any person wilfully removes, injures or defaces any notice exhibited in pursuance of the Education Orders he shall, in respect of each offence, be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale.
