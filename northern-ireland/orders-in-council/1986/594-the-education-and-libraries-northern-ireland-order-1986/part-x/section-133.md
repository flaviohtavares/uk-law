## 133. Transitional and saving provisions

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The transitional provisions set out in Schedule 19 shall have effect.

(2)[^c17010301] No power or duty conferred or imposed by this Order on the Department or boards or parents shall be construed as relating to any person who is detaimed in pursuance of an order made by any court.
