# The Education and Libraries (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 134

## 134. Regulations[^c17010321]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c17010331] The Department may make all such regulations as it is required or empowered to make under the Education Orders[^c17010341] or as appear to it to be necessary or proper for giving full effect to the Education Orders.

(2) All regulations under this Order shall be subject to negative resolution.
