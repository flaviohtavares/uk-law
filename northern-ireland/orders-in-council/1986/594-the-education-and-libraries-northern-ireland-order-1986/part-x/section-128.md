## 128. Use of land provided or maintained under this Order

*Extent: N.I.*

Land provided or maintained for any of the purposes of the Education Orders[^c17010081] may be used for any of the other purposes of the Education Orders or for any of the purposes of the Recreation and Youth Service (Northern Ireland) Order 1986[^c17010121] Youth Service (Northern Ireland) Order 1989[^c17010141].
