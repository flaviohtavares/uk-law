## 44. Pupils to be educated in accordance with wishes of their parents[^c17007491]

*Extent: N.I.*

In the exercise and performance of all powers and duties conferred or imposed on them by the Education Orders[^c17007501], the Department and boards shall have regard to the general principle that, so far as is compatible with the provision of efficient instruction and training and the avoidance of unreasonable public expenditure, pupils shall be educated in accordance with the wishes of their parents.
