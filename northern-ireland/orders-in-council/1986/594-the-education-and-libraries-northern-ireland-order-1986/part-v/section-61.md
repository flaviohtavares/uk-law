## 61. Facilities for medical and dental inspection, etc.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Trustees and managers of grant-aided schools shall afford reasonable facilities for such inspection, supervision and education as is provided for under paragraph (1) or (1A) of Article 9 of the Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1972[^c17008021].

*Para. (2) rep. by 1988 NI 24*
