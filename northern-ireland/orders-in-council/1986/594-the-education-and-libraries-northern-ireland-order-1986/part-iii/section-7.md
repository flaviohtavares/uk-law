## 7. Provision, maintenance and management of controlled schools by boards

*Extent: N.I.*

For the purposes of fulfilling its duties under the Education Orders[^c17005491], the Authority[^key-b411148a1ff6ad3f10e8cd22b37183f6] may provide primary, secondary and special schools [^key-24f018d508954c43aa0fd5e192d695ae]... and shall maintain and manage any such school provided by it or transferred to its management [^key-24f018d508954c43aa0fd5e192d695ae]....
