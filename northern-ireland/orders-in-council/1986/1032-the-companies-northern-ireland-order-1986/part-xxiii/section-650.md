# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 650

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 650. Part XXIII company's financial year and accounting reference periods

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

(1) Sections 390 to 392 of the Companies Act 2006[^c21250791] (financial year and accounting reference periods) apply to a Part XXIII company, subject to the following modifications.

(2) For the references to the incorporation of the company substitute references to the company establishing a place of business in Northern Ireland.

(3) Omit section 392(3)[^c21250821] (restriction on frequency with which current accounting reference period may be extended).
