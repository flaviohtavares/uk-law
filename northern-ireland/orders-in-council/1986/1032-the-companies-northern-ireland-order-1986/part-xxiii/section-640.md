# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 640

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 640. Application of this Part

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

(1) This Part applies to—

- (a) a company incorporated outside Northern Ireland which after the commencement of this Order, establishes a place of business in Northern Ireland, and
- (b) a company so incorporated which has, before the commencement of this Order, established a place of business in Northern Ireland and continues to have an established place of business in Northern Ireland at the commencement of this Order.

(2) A company to which this Part applies is in this Order referred to as a “Part XXIII company”.
