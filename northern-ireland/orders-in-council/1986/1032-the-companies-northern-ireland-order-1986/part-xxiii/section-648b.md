# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 648B

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 648B. Scope of Articles 649 to 652[^c23384941]

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

Articles 649 to 652 shall not apply to any institution to which Article 648A applies or to any limited company which is incorporated outside the United Kingdom and Gibraltar and has a branch in the United Kingdom[^c17579171].
