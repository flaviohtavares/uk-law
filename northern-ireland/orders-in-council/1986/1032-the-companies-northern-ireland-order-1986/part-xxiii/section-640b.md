# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 640B

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 640B. Scope of Articles 641 and 642[^c22234601]

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

Article 641 and 642 shall not apply to any limited company which—

- (a) is incorporated outside the United Kingdom and Gibraltar, and
- (b) has a branch in the United Kingdom.
