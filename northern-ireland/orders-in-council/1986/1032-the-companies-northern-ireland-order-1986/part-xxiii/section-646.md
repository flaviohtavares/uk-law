# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 646

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 646. Documents to be filed on cessation of business

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

If a Part XXIII company to which Article 641 applies[^c17578901] ceases to have a place of business in Northern Ireland, it shall forthwith give notice of that fact to the registrar; and as from the date on which notice is so given the obligation of the company to deliver any document to the registrar ceases.
