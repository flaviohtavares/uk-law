# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 20

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 20. Alteration of articles by special resolution

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

(1) Subject to the provisions of this Order and to the conditions contained in its memorandum, a company may by special resolution alter its articles.

(2) Alterations so made in the articles are (subject to this Order) as valid as if originally contained in them and are subject in like manner to alteration by special resolution.
