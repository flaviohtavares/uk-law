# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 50

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 50. Official seal for share certificates, etc.

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

[^c17554421]A company which has a common seal[^c17554431] may have, for use for sealing securities issued by the company and for sealing documents creating or evidencing securities so issued, an official seal which is a facsimile of its common seal with the addition on its face of the word “Securities”. The official seal when duly affixed to a document has the same effect as the company's common seal.
