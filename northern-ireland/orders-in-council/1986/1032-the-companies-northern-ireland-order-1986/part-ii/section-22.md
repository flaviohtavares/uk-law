# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 22

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 22. Minimum authorised capital (public companies)

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

When a memorandum delivered to the registrar under Article 21 states that the association to be registered is to be a public company, the share capital stated in the memorandum to be that with which the company proposes to be registered—[^c21127901]

- (a) must have a nominal value of not less than the authorised minimum (as defined in section 763 of the Companies Act 2006); and
- (b) to the extent necessary to comply with that requirement, must be denominated in accordance with section 765 of that Act (allotted share capital of public company to be denominated either in sterling or in euros but not partly in one and partly in the other).
