# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 15

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 15. Resolution to alter objects[^c21550151]

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

(1) A company may by special resolution alter its memorandum with respect to the statement of the company's objects.

(2) If an application is made under Article 16, an alteration does not have effect except in so far as it is confirmed by the court.
