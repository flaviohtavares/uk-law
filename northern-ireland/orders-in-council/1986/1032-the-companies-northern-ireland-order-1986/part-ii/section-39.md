# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 39

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 39. Regulations about names

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

(1)[^c19831451] The Department may by regulations— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19831451]

- (a) prescribe words or expressions for the registration of which as or as part of a company's corporate name the Department's approval is required under Article 36(2)(b), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19831451]
- (b) in relation to any such word or expression, prescribe a government department or other body as the relevant body for the purposes of paragraph (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19831451]

(2) Where a company proposes to have as, or as part of, its corporate name any such word or expression in relation to which a relevant body has been prescribed under paragraph (1)(b), a request shall be made (in writing) to the relevant body to indicate whether (and if so why) it has any objections to the proposal; and the person to make the request is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19831451]

- (a) in the case of a company seeking to be registered under this Part, the person making the statutory declaration *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19831451] under Article 23(3) or statement under Article 23(4) (as the case may be)[^c17554061] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19831451],
- (b) in the case of a company seeking to be registered under Article 629, the persons making the statutory declaration *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19831451] under Article 635(2) or statement under Article 635(2A) (as the case may be) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19831451], and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19831451]
- (c) in any other case, a director or secretary of the company concerned. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19831451]

(3) The person who has made that request to the relevant body shall submit to the registrar a statement that it has been made and a copy of any response received from that body, together with— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19831451]

- (a) the requisite statutory declaration *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19831451] or statement *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19831451], or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19831451]
- (b) a copy of the special resolution changing the company's name, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19831451]

according as the case is the one or the other of those mentioned in paragraph (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19831451]

(4) Articles 658 and 659 (public inspection of documents kept by registrar) do not apply to documents sent under paragraph (3) other than documents mentioned in sub-paragraphs (a) and (b) of that paragraph. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19831451]

(5) Regulations under this Article may contain such transitional provisions and savings as the Department thinks appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19831451]

(6) The regulations shall be laid before the Assembly after being made and shall cease to have effect at the end of the statutory period next after the regulations have been so laid (but without prejudice to anything previously done by virtue of the regulations or to the making of new regulations) unless during that period they are approved by a resolution of the Assembly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19831451]
