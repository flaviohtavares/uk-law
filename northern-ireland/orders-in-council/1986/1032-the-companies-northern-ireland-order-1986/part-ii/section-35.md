# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 35

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 35. Name as stated in memorandum

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

(1) The name of a public company must end with the words “public limited company” and those words may not be preceded by the word “limited”.

(2) In the case of a company limited by shares or by guarantee (not being a public company), the name must have “limited” as its last word, subject to Article 40 (exempting, in certain circumstances, a company from the requirement to have “limited” as part of the name).
