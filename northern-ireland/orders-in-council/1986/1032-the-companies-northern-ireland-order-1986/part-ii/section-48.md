# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 48

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 48. Execution of deeds abroad[^c17554321]

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

(1) A company may, by writing under its common seal, empower any person, either generally or in respect of any specified matters, as its attorney, to execute deeds on its behalf in any place elsewhere than in the United Kingdom.

(2)[^c17554331] A deed executed by such an attorney on behalf of the company has the same effect as if it were executed under the company's common seal.
