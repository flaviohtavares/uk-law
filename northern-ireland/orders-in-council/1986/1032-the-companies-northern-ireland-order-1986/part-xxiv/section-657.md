# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 657

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 657. Fees payable to registrar[^c17579651]

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

(1) The Department may by regulations require the payment to the registrar of such fees as may be specified in the regulations in respect of—

- (a) the performance by the registrar of such functions under the Companies Orders as may be so specified, including the receipt by him of any document which under those Orders is required to be delivered to him[^c17579661];
- (b) the inspection of documents[^c17579661]. . . kept by him under those Orders.

(2) Regulations made under paragraph (1)(a) requiring the payment of a fee in respect of a matter for which no fee was previously payable or increasing a fee shall be subject to affirmative resolution.

(3) Fees paid to the registrar under the Companies Orders shall be paid into the Consolidated Fund[^c17579691].

(4) It is hereby declared that the registrar may charge a fee for any services provided by him otherwise than in pursuance of any obligation imposed on him by law.
