# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 659

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 659. Certificate of incorporation[^c17579751][^c23384541]

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

Any person may require a certificate of the incorporation of a company, signed by the registrar or authenticated by his official seal.
