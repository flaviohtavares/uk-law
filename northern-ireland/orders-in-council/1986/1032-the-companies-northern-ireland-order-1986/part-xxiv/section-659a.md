# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 659A

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 659A. Provision and authentication by registrar of documents is non-legible form[^c17579761][^c23384571]

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

(1) Any requirement of the Companies Orders as to the supply by the registrar of a document may, if the registrar thinks fit, be satisfied by the communication by the registrar of the requisite information in any non-legible form prescribed for the purposes of this Article by regulations or approved by him.

(2) Where the document is required to be signed by him or sealed with his official seal, it shall instead be authenticated in such manner as may be prescribed by regulations or approved by the registrar.
