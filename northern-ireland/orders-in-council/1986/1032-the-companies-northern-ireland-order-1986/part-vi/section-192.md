# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 192

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 192. Nature, transfer and numbering of shares

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

(1)[^c20676431] The shares or other interest of any member in a company— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20676431]

- (a) are personal estate and are not in the nature of real estate, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20676431]
- (b) are transferable in manner provided by the company's articles, but subject to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20676431] [^m00112] Stock Transfer Act (Northern Ireland) 1963 (which enables securities of certain descriptions to be transferred by a simplified process) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20676431] and to regulations made under section 207 of the Companies Act 1989 (which enable title to securities to be evidenced and transferred without a written instrument).[^c17556681] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20676431]

(2) Each share in a company having a share capital shall be distinguished by its appropriate number; except that, if at any time all the issued shares in a company, or all the issued shares in it of a particular class, are fully paid up and rank pari passu for all purposes, none of those shares need thereafter have a distinguishing number so long as it remains fully paid up and ranks pari passu for all purposes with all shares of the same class for the time being issued and fully paid up. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20676431]
