# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 168

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 168. Time for giving financial assistance under Article 165

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

(1)[^c20692841] This Article applies as to the time before and after which financial assistance may not be given by a company in pursuance of Article 165. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692841]

(2) Where a special resolution is required by that Article to be passed approving the giving of the assistance, the assistance shall not be given before the expiry of the period of 4 weeks beginning with— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692841]

- (a) the date on which the special resolution is passed, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692841]
- (b) where more than one such resolution is passed, the date on which the last of them is passed, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692841]

unless, as respects that resolution (or, if more than one, each of them) every member of the company which passed the resolution who is entitled to vote at general meetings of the company voted in favour of the resolution. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692841]

(3) If application for the cancellation of any such resolution is made under Article 167, the financial assistance shall not be given before the final determination of the application unless the court otherwise orders. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692841]

(4) The assistance shall not be given after the expiry of the period of 8 weeks beginning with— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692841]

- (a) the date on which the directors of the company proposing to give the assistance made their statutory declaration under Article 165, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692841]
- (b) where that company is a subsidiary and both its directors and the directors of any of its holding companies made such a declaration, the date on which the earliest of the declarations is made, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692841]

unless the court, on an application under Article 167, otherwise orders. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692841]
