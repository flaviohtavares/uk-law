# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 163

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 163. Transactions not prohibited by Article 161

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

(1)[^c20692761] Article 161(1) does not prohibit a company from giving financial assistance for the purpose of an acquisition of shares in it or its holding company if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692761]

- (a) the company's principal purpose in giving that assistance is not to give it for the purpose of any such acquisition, or the giving of the assistance for that purpose is but an incidental part of some larger purpose of the company, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692761]
- (b) the assistance is given in good faith in the interests of the company. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692761]

(2) Article 161 (2) does not prohibit a company from giving financial assistance if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692761]

- (a) the company's principal purpose in giving the assistance is not to reduce or discharge any liability incurred by a person for the purpose of the acquisition of shares in the company or its holding company, or the reduction or discharge of any such liability is but an incidental part of some larger purpose of the company, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692761]
- (b) the assistance is given in good faith in the interests of the company. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692761]

(3) Article 161 does not prohibit— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692761]

- (a) a distribution of a company's assets by way of dividend lawfully made or a distribution made in the course of the company's winding up, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692761]
- (b) the allotment of bonus shares, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692761]
- (c) a reduction of capital confirmed by order of the court under Article 147, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692761]
- (d) a redemption or purchase of shares made in accordance with Chapter VII, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692761]
- (e) anything done in pursuance of an order of the court under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692761] section 899 of the Companies Act 2006[^c21249941] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692761] (compromises and arrangements with creditors and members), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692761]
- (f) anything done under an arrangement made in pursuance of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692761] Article 96 of the Insolvency Order[^c17555661] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692761] (acceptance of shares by liquidator in winding up as consideration for sale of property), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692761]
- (g) anything done under an arrangement made between a company and its creditors which is binding on the creditors by virtue of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692761] Part II of the Insolvency Order *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692761] (winding up imminent or in progress). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692761]

(4) Article 161 does not prohibit— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692761]

- (a) where the lending of money is part of the ordinary business of the company, the lending of money by the company in the ordinary course of its business, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692761]
- (b)[^c17555701] the provision by a company, in good faith in the interests of the company, of financial assistance for the purposes of an employees' share scheme, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692761]
- (bb (affected by repeal, see note))[^c17555721][^c20692761] without prejudice to sub-paragraph (b), the provision of financial assistance by a company or any of its subsidiaries for the purposes of or in connection with anything done by the company (or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692761] a company in the same group *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692761]) for the purpose of enabling or facilitating transactions in shares in the first-mentioned company between, and involving the acquisition of beneficial ownership of those shares by, any of the following persons— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692761]
  - (i) the bona fida employees or former employees of that company or of another company in the same group; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692761]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c20692761] the wives, husbands, widows, widowers, children, step-children or adopted children under the age of eighteen of such employees or former employees. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692761]
- (c) the making by a company of loans to persons (other than directors) employed in good faith by the company with a view to enabling those persons to acquire fully paid shares in the company or its holding company to be held by them by way of beneficial ownership. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692761]

(5) For the purposes of paragraph (4)(bb) a company is in the same group as another company if it is a holding company or subsidiary of that company, or a subsidiary of a holding company of that company. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692761]
