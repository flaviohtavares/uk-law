# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 165

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 165. Relaxation of Article 161 for private companies

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

(1)[^c20692781] Article 161 does not prohibit a private company from giving financial assistance in a case where the acquisition of shares in question is or was an acquisition of shares in the company or, if it is a subsidiary of another private company, in that other company if the following provisions of this Article, and Articles 166 to 168, are complied with as respects the giving of that assistance. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692781]

(2) The financial assistance may only be given if the company has net assets which are not thereby reduced or, to the extent that they are reduced, if the assistance is provided out of distributable profits. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692781]

Article 164(2) applies for the interpretation of this paragraph. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692781]

(3) This Article does not permit financial assistance to be given by a subsidiary in a case where the acquisition of shares in question is or was an acquisition of shares in its holding company, if it is also a subsidiary of a public company which is itself a subsidiary of that holding company. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692781]

(4) Unless the company proposing to give the financial assistance is a wholly-owned subsidiary, the giving of assistance under this Article must be approved by special resolution of the company in general meeting. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692781]

(5) Where the financial assistance is to be given by the company in a case where the acquisition of shares in question is or was an acquisition of shares in its holding company, that holding company and any other company which is both the company's holding company and a subsidiary of that other holding company (except, in any case, a company which is a wholly-owned subsidiary) shall also approve by special resolution in general meeting the giving of the financial assistance. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692781]

(6) The directors of the company proposing to give the financial assistance and, where the shares acquired or to be acquired are shares in its holding company, the directors of that company and of any other company which is both the company's holding company and a subsidiary of that other holding company shall before the financial assistance is given make a statutory declaration in the prescribed form complying with Article 166. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692781]
