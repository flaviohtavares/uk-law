# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 172G

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 172G. Treasury shares: penalty for contravention

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

If a company contravenes any provision of Articles 172A to 172F every officer of it who is in default is liable to a fine.
