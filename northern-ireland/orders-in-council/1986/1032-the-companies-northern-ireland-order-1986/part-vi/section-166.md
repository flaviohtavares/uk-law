# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 166

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 166. Statutory declaration under Article 165

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

(1)[^c20692801] A statutory declaration made by a company's directors under Article 165(6) shall contain such particulars of the financial assistance to be given, and of the business of the company of which they are directors, as may be prescribed, and shall identify the person to whom the assistance is to be given. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692801]

(2) The declaration shall state that the directors have formed the opinion, as regards the company's initial situation immediately following the date on which the assistance is proposed to be given, that there will be no ground on which it could then be found to be unable to pay its debts; and either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692801]

- (a) if it is intended to commence the winding up of the company within 12 months of that date, the company will be able to pay its debts in full within 12 months of the commencement of the winding up, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692801]
- (b) in any other case, that the company will be able to pay its debts as they fall due during the year immediately following that date. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692801]

(3) In forming their opinion for the purposes of paragraph (2), the directors shall take into account the same liabilities (including contingent and prospective liabilities) as would be relevant under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692801] Article 102 of the Insolvency Order[^c17555801] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692801] (winding up by the court) to the question whether the company is unable to pay its debts. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692801]

(4) The directors' statutory declaration shall have annexed to it a report addressed to them by their company's auditors stating that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692801]

- (a) they have enquired into the state of affairs of the company, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692801]
- (b) they are not aware of anything to indicate that the opinion expressed by the directors in the declaration as to any of the matters mentioned in paragraph (2) is unreasonable in all the circumstances. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692801]

(5) The statutory declaration and auditors' report shall be delivered to the registrar— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692801]

- (a) together with a copy of any special resolution passed by the company under Article 165 and delivered to the registrar in compliance with *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692801] section 30 of the Companies Act 2006[^c20572741] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692801], or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692801]
- (b) where no such resolution is required to be passed, within 15 days after the making of the declaration. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692801]

(6) If a company fails to comply with paragraph (5), the company and every officer of it who is in default is liable to a fine and, for continued contravention, to a daily default fine. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692801]

(7) A director of a company who makes a statutory declaration under Article 165 without having reasonable grounds for the opinion expressed in it is liable to imprisonment or a fine, or both. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692801]
