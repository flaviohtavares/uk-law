# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 161

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 161. Financial assistance generally prohibited

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

(1)[^c20691631] Subject to the following provisions of this Chapter, where a person is acquiring or is proposing to acquire shares in a company, it is not lawful for the company or any of its subsidiaries to give financial assistance directly or indirectly for the purpose of that acquisition before or at the same time as the acquisition takes place. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20691631]

(2) Subject to those provisions, where a person has acquired shares in a company and any liability has been incurred (by that or any other person) for the purpose of that acquisition, it is not lawful for the company or any of its subsidiaries to give financial assistance directly or indirectly for the purpose of reducing or discharging the liability so incurred. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20691631]

(3) If a company acts in contravention of this Article, it is liable to a fine, and every officer of it who is in default is liable to imprisonment or a fine, or both. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20691631]
