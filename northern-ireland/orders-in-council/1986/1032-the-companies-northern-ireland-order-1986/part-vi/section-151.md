# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 151

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 151. Penalty for concealing name of creditor, etc.

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

If an officer of the company—

- (a) wilfully conceals the name of a creditor entitled to object to the reduction of capital;
- (b) wilfully misrepresents the nature or amount of the debt of claim of any creditor; or
- (c) aids, abets or is privy to any such concealment or misrepresentation,

he is guilty of an offence and liable to a fine.
