# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 162

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 162. Interpretation for this Chapter

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

(1)[^c20692741] In this Chapter— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692741]

- (a) “financial assistance” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692741]
  - (i) financial assistance given by way of gift; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692741]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c20692741] financial assistance given by way of guarantee, security or indemnity, other than an indemnity in respect of the indemnifier's own neglect or default, or by way of release or waiver; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692741]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c20692741] financial assistance given by way of a loan or any other agreement under which any of the obligations of the person giving the assistance are to be fulfilled at a time when in accordance with the agreement any obligation of another party to the agreement remains unfulfilled, or by way of the novation of, or the assignment of rights arising under a loan or such other agreement; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692741]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c20692741] any other financial assistance given by a company the net assets of which are thereby reduced to a material extent or which has no net assets; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692741]
- (b) “distributable profits”, in relation to the giving of any financial assistance— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692741]
  - (i) means those profits out of which the company could lawfully make a distribution equal in value to that assistance, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692741]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c20692741] includes, in a case where the financial assistance is or includes a non-cash asset, any profit which, if the company were to make a distribution of that asset, would under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692741] section 846 of the Companies Act 2006[^c21249721] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692741] (distributions in kind) be available for that purpose, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692741]
- (c) “distribution” has the meaning given by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692741] section 829 of the Companies Act 2006[^c21249921] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692741].

(2) In paragraph (1)(a)(iv) “net assets” means the aggregate of the company's assets, less the aggregate of its liabilities ( “liabilities” to include any *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692741] provision for liabilities[^c17555621] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692741] within paragraph 88 of Schedule 4 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692741] that is made in Companies Order individual accounts and any provision that is made in IAS individual accounts *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692741] ).

(3) In this Chapter— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692741]

- (a) a reference to a person incurring a liability includes his changing his financial position by making an agreement or arrangement (whether enforceable or unenforceable, and whether made on his own account or with any other person) or by any other means, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692741]
- (b) a reference to a company giving financial assistance for the purpose of reducing or discharging a liability incurred by a person for the purpose of the acquisition of shares includes its giving such assistance for the purpose of wholly or partly restoring his financial position to what it was before the acquisition took place. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20692741]
