# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 136

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 136. Saving for court's powers under other provisions

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

Nothing in Article 135(2) to (5) derogates from the powers of the court under the following Articles, namely—

- Articles 15 to 17 (company resolution to alter objects),
- Article 64 (litigated objection to public company becoming private by re-registration),
- section 899 of the Companies Act 2006 (court sanction for compromise or arrangement with creditors or members)[^c21249601],
- section 900 of that Act (powers of court to facilitate reconstruction or amalgamation)[^c21249621],
- Part 30 of the Companies Act 2006 (protection of members against unfair prejudice)[^c20572601].
