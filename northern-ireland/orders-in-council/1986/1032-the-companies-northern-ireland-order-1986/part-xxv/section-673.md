# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 673

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 673. Service of documents

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

A document may be served on a company by leaving it at, or sending it by post to, the company's registered office.
