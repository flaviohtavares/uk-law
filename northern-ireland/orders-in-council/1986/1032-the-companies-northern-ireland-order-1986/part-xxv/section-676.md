# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 676

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 676. Enforcement of High Court orders[^c17580411]

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

Orders made by the High Court under this Order may be enforced in the same manner as orders made in an action pending in that court.
