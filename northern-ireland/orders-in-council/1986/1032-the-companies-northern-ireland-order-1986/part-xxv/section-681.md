# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 681

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 681. Regulations[^c17580721]

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

(1) Subject to paragraph (4), the Department may make regulations for prescribing anything which is authorised or required by the Companies Orders to be prescribed.

(2) Regulations under those Orders may contain such consequential, incidental or supplementary provisions as the Department thinks appropriate.

(3) Save as otherwise expressly provided by those Orders, all regulations under them shall be subject to negative resolution.

*Para. (4) rep. by 1989 NI 19*
