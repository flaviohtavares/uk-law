# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 103

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 103. Saving for other restrictions as to offers

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

(1) Articles 99 to 102 are without prejudice to any statutory provision by virtue of which a company is prohibited (whether generally or in specified circumstances) from offering or allotting equity securities to any person.

(2) Where a company cannot by virtue of such a statutory provision offer or allot equity securities to a holder of relevant shares or relevant employee shares, those Articles have effect as if the shares held by that holder were not relevant shares or relevant employee shares.
