# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 108

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 108. Apart from Article 107, commissions and discounts barred

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

(1) Except as permitted by Article 107, no company shall apply any of its shares or capital money either directly or indirectly in payment of any commission, discount or allowance to any person in consderation of his subscribing or agreeing to subscribe (whether absolutely or conditionally) for any shares in the company, or procuring or agreeing to procure subscriptions (whether absolute or conditional) for any shares in the company.

(2) This applies whether the shares or money be so applied by being added to the purchase money of any property acquired by the company or to the contract price of any work to be executed for the company, or the money be paid out of the nominal purchase money or contract price, or otherwise.

(3) Nothing in Article 107 or this Article affects the power of a company to pay such brokerage as has heretofore been lawful.

(4) A vendor to, or promoter of, or other person who receives payment in money or shares from, a company has and is deemed always to have had power to apply any part of the money or shares so received in payment of any commission, the payment of which, if made directly by the company, would have been lawful under Article 107 and this Article.
