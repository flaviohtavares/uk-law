# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 110

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 110. Prohibition on allotment of shares at a discount

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

(1) A company's shares shall not be allotted at a discount.

(2) If shares are allotted in contravention of this Article, the allottee is liable to pay the company an amount equal to the amount of the discount, with interest at the appropriate rate.
