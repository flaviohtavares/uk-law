# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 415

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 415. Company's register of charges

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

(1)[^c17574761] Every limited company shall keep at the same office as its register of members is kept a register of charges and enter in it all charges specifically affecting property of the company and all floating charges on the company's undertaking or any of its property.

(2) The entry shall in each case give a short description of the property charged, the amount of the charge and, except in the case of securities to bearer, the names of the persons entitled to the charge.

(3) If an officer of the company knowingly and wilfully authorises or permits the omission of any entry required to be made in pursuance of this Article, he is liable to a fine.
