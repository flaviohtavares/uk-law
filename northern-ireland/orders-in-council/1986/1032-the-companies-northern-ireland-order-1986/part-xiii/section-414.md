# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 414

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 414. Companies to keep copies of instruments creating charges[^c17574741]

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

(1) Every company shall cause a copy of every instrument creating a charge requiring registration under this Part, including every order or notice a copy of which has been delivered to the company under Article 408, to be kept at the same office as its register of members is kept.

(2) In the case of a series of uniform debentures, a copy of one debenture of the series is sufficient.
