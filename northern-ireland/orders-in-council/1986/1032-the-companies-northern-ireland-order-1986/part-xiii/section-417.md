# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 417

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 417. Application of this Part to companies incorporated outside Northern Ireland

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

(1) This Part extends to charges on property in Northern Ireland which are created, and to charges on property in Northern Ireland which is acquired, by a company (whether a company within the meaning of this Order or not) incorporated outside Northern Ireland which has an established place of business in Northern Ireland.

(2) In relation to such a company, Articles 414 and 415 apply with the substitution, for the reference to the office at which the register of members is kept, of a reference to the company's principal place of business in Northern Ireland.
