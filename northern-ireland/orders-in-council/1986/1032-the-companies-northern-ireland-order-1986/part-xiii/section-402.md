# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 402

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 402. Certain charges void if not registered[^c17574361]

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

(1) Subject to the provisions of this Part, a charge created by a company and being a charge to which this Article applies is, so far as any security on the company's property or undertaking is conferred by the charge, void against the liquidator or administrator[^c17574371] and any creditor of the company, unless the prescribed particulars of the charge together with the instrument (if any) by which the charge is created or evidenced, are delivered to or received by the registrar for registration in the manner required by this Part, within 21 days after the date of the charge's creation.

(2) Paragraph (1) is without prejudice to any contract or obligation for repayment of the money secured by the charge; and when a charge becomes void under this Article, the money secured by it immediately becomes payable.
