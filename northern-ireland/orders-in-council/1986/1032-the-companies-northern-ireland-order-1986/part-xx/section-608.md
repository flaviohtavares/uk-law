# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 608

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 608. Effect of Crown disclaimer under Article 607[^c17578201]

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

[^c17578211][^c17578221]Where notice of disclaimer is executed under Article 607 as respects any property, that property is deemed not to have vested in the Crown under Article 605 and as regards that property, Article 152(3) and Articles 153 to 156 of the Insolvency Order shall apply[^c17578231] as if the property had been disclaimed by the liquidator under that Article 152 immediately before the dissolution of the company.
