# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 609

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 609. Liability for rentcharge on company's land after dissolution[^c17578271]

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

(1)[^c17578281][^c17578291] Article 154 of the Insolvency Order shall apply[^c17578301] to land which by operation of law vests subject to a rentcharge in the Crown or any other person on the dissolution of a company as it applies to land so vesting on a disclaimer under that Article.

(2) In this Article “company” includes any body corporate.
