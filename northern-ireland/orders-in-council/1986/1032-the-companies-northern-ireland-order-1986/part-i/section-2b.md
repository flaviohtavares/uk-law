# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 2B

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 2B. Relationship of this Order to Parts IV and V of the Financial Services Act 1986[^c21091101]

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

In Articles 653(4) and (5)[^c17553111], 655(1), 656(1), 656A(1), 657(1)(a) and (3), 658(1) and (3), 659A and 662(1) references to the Companies Orders include Part 6 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000[^c17553131].
