# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 7

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 7. “Non-cash asset”[^c17553251]

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

(1) In this Order “non-cash asset” means any property or interest in property other than cash; and for this purpose “cash” includes foreign currency.

(2) A reference to the transfer or acquisition of a non-cash asset includes the creation or extinction of an estate or interest in, or a right over, any property and also the discharge of any person's liability, other than a liability for a liquidated sum.
