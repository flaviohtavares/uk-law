# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 11

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 11. “Employees' share scheme”

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

For the purposes of this Order, an employees' share scheme is a scheme for encouraging or facilitating the holding of shares or debentures in a company by or for the benefit of—

- (a) the bona fide employees or former employees of the company, the company's subsidiary or holding company or a subsidiary of the company's holding company, or
- (b) the spouses, civil partners, surviving spouses, surviving civil partners[^c17553501] or children, step-children or adopted children under the age of 18 of such employees or former employees.
