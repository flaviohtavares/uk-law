# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 298

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 298. Particulars of secretaries to be registered under Article 296

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

(1) The register to be kept by a company under Article 296 shall contain the following particulars with respect to the secretary (if any)[^c21250171] or, where there are joint secretaries, with respect to each of them—

- (a) in the case of an individual, his present name[^c17571521], any former name and his usual residential address, and
- (b) in the case of a body corporate or a Scottish firm, its corporate or firm name and registered or principal office.

(2) Where all the partners in a firm are joint secretaries, the name and principal office of the firm may be stated instead of the particulars mentioned in paragraph (1).

(3) Article 297(2)(a) and (b) apply for the purposes of the obligation under paragraph (1)(a) of this Article to state the name or former name of an individual.
