# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 446

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 446. Investigation of bodies incorporated outside Northern Ireland

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

(1)[^c19947541] The provisions of this Part apply to bodies corporate incorporated outside Northern Ireland which are carrying on business in Northern Ireland, or have at any time carried on business there, as they apply to companies under this Order; but subject to the following exceptions, adaptations and modifications. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19947541]

(2) The following provisions do not apply to such bodies— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19947541]

- (a) Article 424 (investigation on application of company or its members), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19947541]
- (b) Article 431 (power to bring civil proceedings on the company's behalf), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19947541]
- (c) Articles 435 to 438 (investigation of company ownership and power to obtain information as to those interested in shares, &c.), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19947541]
- (d) Article 439 (investigation of share dealings). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19947541]

(3) The other provisions of this Part apply to such bodies subject to such adaptations and modifications as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19947541]
