# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 627

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 627. Companies re-registered with altered status under former Companies Acts

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

(1) This Order applies to every unlimited company registered or re-registered as limited in pursuance of the [^m00117] Companies Act 1879, section 57 of the [^m00118] Companies (Consolidation) Act 1908, section 16 of the [^m00119] Companies Act (Northern Ireland) 1932, section 16 of the Act of 1960 or Article 119 of the Order of 1978 as it (this Order) applies to an unlimited company re-registered as limited in pursuance of Part III.

(2) But reference, express or implied, to the date of registration or re-registration is to be read as referring to the date at which the company was registered or re-registered as a limited company under the relevant statutory provision.
