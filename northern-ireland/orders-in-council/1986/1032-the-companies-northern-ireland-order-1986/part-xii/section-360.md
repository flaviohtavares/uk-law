# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 360

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 360. Obligation to keep and enter up register

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

(1)[^c20679711] Every company shall keep a register of its members and enter in it the particulars required by this Article. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20679711]

(2) There shall be entered in the register— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20679711]

- (a) the names and addresses of the members; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20679711]
- (b) the date on which each person was registered as a member; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20679711]
- (c) the date at which any person ceased to be a member. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20679711]

(3) This paragraph applies in the case of a company having a share capital— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20679711]

- (a) with the names and addresses of the members there shall be entered a statement— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20679711]
  - (i) of the shares held by each member, distinguishing each share by its number (so long as the share has a number) and, where the company has more than one class of issued shares, by its class, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20679711]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c20679711] of the amount paid or agreed to be considered as paid on the shares of each member; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20679711]
- (b) where the company has converted any of its shares into stock and given notice of the conversion to the register, the register shall show the amount and class of stock held by each member, instead of the amount of shares and the particulars relating to shares specified in sub-paragraph (a). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20679711]

(3A)[^c17572791] Where a company purchases one or more of its own shares in circumstances in which Article 172A applies— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20679711]

- (a) the requirements of paragraphs (2) and (3) must be complied with unless the company cancels all of the shares forthwith after the purchase in accordance with Article 172D(1), but *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20679711]
- (b) any share which is so cancelled must be disregarded for the purposes of paragraph (3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20679711]

(4) In the case of a company which does not have a share capital but has more than one class of members, there shall be entered in the register, with the names and addresses of the members, the class to which each member belongs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20679711]

(5) If a company makes default in complying with this Article, the company and every officer of it who is in default is liable to a fine and, for continued contravention, to a daily default fine. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20679711]

(6) An entry relating to a former member of a company may be removed from the register after the expiration of 20 years from the date on which he ceased to be a member. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20679711]

(7) Liability incurred by a company from the making or deletion of an entry in its register of members or debenture holders, or from a failure to make or delete any such entry, is not enforceable more than 20 years after the date on which the entry was made or deleted or, in the case of any such failure, the failure first occurred. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20679711]

This is without prejudice to any lesser period of limitation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20679711]
