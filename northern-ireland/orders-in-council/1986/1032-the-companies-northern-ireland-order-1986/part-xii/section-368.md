# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 368

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 368. Trusts not be entered on register

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

No notice of any trust, expressed, implied or constructive, shall be entered on the register, or be receivable by the registrar.
