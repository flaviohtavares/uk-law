# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 358

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 358. Company seal[^c17572691]

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

(1)[^c17572701] A company which has a common seal shall have its name engraved in legible characters on the seal; and if it fails to comply with this paragraph it is liable to a fine.

(2) If an officer of a company or a person on its behalf uses or authorises the use of any seal purporting to be a seal of the company on which its name is not engraved as required by paragraph (1), he is liable to a fine.
