# The Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986, section 387A

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 387A. Elective resolution of private company[^c22226031]

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

(1)[^c19944422] An election by a private company for the purposes of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19944422]

- (a) Article 90A (election as to duration of authority to allot shares), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19944422]
- (b) Article 260 (election to dispense with laying of accounts and reports before general meeting), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19944422]
- (c) Article 374A (election to dispense with holding of annual general meeting), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19944422]
- (d) Article 377(4) or 386(3) (election as to majority required to authorise short notice of meeting), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19944422]
- (e) Article 394 (election to dispense with appointment of auditors annually), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19944422]

shall be made by resolution of the company in general meeting in accordance with this Article. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19944422]

Such a resolution is referred to in this Order as an “elective resolution”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19944422]

(2) An elective resolution is not effective unless— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19944422]

- (a) at least 21 days' notice in writing is given of the meeting, stating that an elective resolution is to be proposed and stating the terms of the resolution, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19944422]
- (b) the resolution is agreed to at the meeting, in person or by proxy, by all the members entitled to attend and vote at the meeting. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19944422]

(2A)[^c17573361] An elective resolution is effective notwithstanding the fact that less than 21 days' notice in writing of the meeting is given if all the members entitled to attend and vote at the meeting so agree. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19944422]

(2B)[^c17573381] For the purposes of this Article, notice in writing of the meeting is to be taken as given to a person where notice of the meeting is sent using electronic communications to such address as may for the time being be notified by that person to the company for that purpose. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19944422]

(2C) For the purposes of this Article a notice in writing of the meeting is also to be treated as given to a person where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19944422]

- (a) the company and that person have agreed that notices of meetings required to be given to that person may instead be accessed by him on a web site; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19944422]
- (b) the meeting is a meeting to which that agreement applies; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19944422]
- (c) that person is notified, in manner for the time being agreed between him and the company for the purpose, of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19944422]
  - (i) the publication of the notice on a web site; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19944422]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c19944422] the address of that web site; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19944422]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c19944422] the place on that web site where the notice may be accessed, and how it may be accessed; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19944422]
- (d) the notice continues to be published on that web site throughout the period beginning with the giving of that notification and ending with the conclusion of the meeting; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19944422]

and for the purposes of this Article a notice treated in accordance with this paragraph as given to any person is to be treated as so given at the time of the notification mentioned in sub-paragraph (c). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19944422]

(2D) A notification given for the purposes of paragraph (2C)(c) must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19944422]

- (a) state that it concerns a notice of a company meeting at which an elective resolution is to be proposed, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19944422]
- (b) specify the place, date and time of the meeting. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19944422]

(2E) Nothing in paragraph (2C) shall invalidate the proceedings of a meeting where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19944422]

- (a) any notice that is required to be published as mentioned in sub-paragraph (d) of that paragraph is published for a part, but not all, of the period mentioned in that sub-paragraph; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19944422]
- (b) the failure to publish that notice throughout that period is wholly attributable to circumstances which it would not be reasonable to have expected the company to prevent or avoid. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19944422]

(2F) In so far as the articles of the company do not provide for notices and notifications to be served using electronic communications, the provisions of Table A (as for the time being in operation) as to such service shall apply. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19944422]

(3) The company may revoke an elective resolution by passing an ordinary resolution to that effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19944422]

(4) An elective resolution shall cease to have effect if the company is re-registered as a public company. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19944422]

(5) An elective resolution may be passed or revoked in accordance with this Article, and the provisions referred to in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19944422] paragraphs (1) and (2B) to (2E) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19944422] have effect, notwithstanding any contrary provision in the company's articles of association. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19944422]

(6) In this article, “address” includes any number or address used for the purposes of electronic communications. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19944422]
