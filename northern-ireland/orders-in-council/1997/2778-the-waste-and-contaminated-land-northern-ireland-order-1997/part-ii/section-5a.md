## (not yet in force: 5A). (not yet in force: Fixed penalty notices for certain offences under Article 5(8))[^c20491731]

*Extent: N.I.*

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: This Article applies where it appears to)* *(not yet in force: an authorised officer of an enforcing authority[^c-1db0222d6778d796361b6daa918463a3])* *(not yet in force: that a person has failed to comply with a duty to furnish documents)* *(not yet in force: to the enforcing authority)* *(not yet in force: imposed under regulations made at any time under Article 5(7).)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: The)* *(not yet in force: authorised officer[^c-5fe0ff3b7498cf1bf98c2754f95c7d1d])* *(not yet in force: may serve on that person a notice offering him the opportunity of discharging any liability to conviction for an offence under Article 5(8) by payment of a fixed penalty.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: Where a person is given a notice under this Article in respect of an offence—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: no proceedings may be instituted for that offence before expiration of the period of 14 days following the date of the notice; and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: he may not be convicted of that offence if he pays the fixed penalty before the expiration of the period.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: A notice under this Article must give such particulars of the circumstances alleged to constitute the offence as are necessary for giving reasonable information of the offence.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: A notice under this Article must also state—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: the period during which, by virtue of paragraph (3), proceedings will not be taken for the offence;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: the amount of the fixed penalty; and)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: the person to whom and the address at which the fixed penalty may be paid.)*

((not yet in force: 6)) *(not yet in force: Without prejudice to payment by any other method, payment of the fixed penalty may be made by pre-paying and posting a letter containing the amount of the penalty to the person mentioned in paragraph (5)(c) at the address so mentioned.)*

((not yet in force: 7)) *(not yet in force: Where a letter is sent in accordance with paragraph (6) payment is to be regarded as having been made at the time at which that letter would be delivered in the ordinary course of post.)*

((not yet in force: 8)) *(not yet in force: The form of a notice under this Article is to be such as the Department may by order prescribe.)*

((not yet in force: 9)) *(not yet in force: The fixed penalty payable to)* *(not yet in force: an enforcing authority[^c-e0132565b9a0ada8eded4dd1226335d5])* *(not yet in force: under this Article is, subject to paragraph (10), £300.)*

((not yet in force: 10)) *(not yet in force: The Department may by order substitute a different amount for the amount for the time being specified in paragraph (9).)*

((not yet in force: 11)) *(not yet in force: An enforcing authority may[^c-f7404176a0f13ba9a69b3450445ae97f])* *(not yet in force: make provision for treating a fixed penalty as having been paid if a lesser amount is paid before the end of a period specified)* *(not yet in force: by the enforcing authority)* *(not yet in force: .)*

((not yet in force: 12)) *(not yet in force: In any proceedings a certificate which—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: purports to be signed by an authorised officer; and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: states that payment of a fixed penalty was or was not received by a date specified in the certificate,)*

*(not yet in force: is evidence of the facts stated.)*

((not yet in force: 12A))[^c-f96024e5e5a9175d16da495827c59203] *(not yet in force: Article 22C (use of fixed penalty receipts by a district council) applies in relation to amounts received by a council under this Article as it applies in relation to amounts received under Article 22A.)*

((not yet in force: 13)) *(not yet in force: In this Article—)*

- *(not yet in force: “)****(not yet in force: authorised officer)****(not yet in force: ” means an officer of the enforcing authority who is authorised in writing by the enforcing authority for the purposes of this Article;)*
- *(not yet in force: “)****(not yet in force: enforcing authority)****(not yet in force: ” means—)*
  - (a) *(not yet in force: the Department; and)*
  - (b) *(not yet in force: in relation to an offence committed within its district, a district council.)*
