# The Waste and Contaminated Land (Northern Ireland) Order 1997, section 19A

## 19A. Application of public participation procedures[^c21428591]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Articles 19B and 19C apply to the modification of the waste management plan[^c-0b485185756fb7ee7d9246e6e9cee34e] as they apply to the preparation of such a plan .

(2) Articles 19B and 19C do not apply to—

- (a) a waste management plan[^c-861f1369e97979a49fd4634fa0904670] designed for the sole purpose of serving national defence or prepared in case of civil emergency; or
- (b) a waste management plan[^c-62eaeed029fd75422e5c84a44170f570] for which a public participation procedure is carried out under the Environmental Assessment of Plans and Programmes Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004.
