## (not yet in force: 5E). (not yet in force: Power to search and seize vehicles) (not yet in force: etc)[^c20497751]

*Extent: N.I.*

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: This Article applies where an authorised officer or a constable reasonably believes that the grounds in paragraph (2) or (3) exist.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: The grounds in this paragraph are that—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: an offence under Article 4 or 5 has been committed,)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: a vehicle was used in the commission of the offence, and)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: proceedings for the offence have not yet been brought against any person.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: The grounds in this paragraph are that—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: an offence under Article 4 or 5 is being or is about to be committed, and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: a vehicle is being or is about to be used in the commission of the offence.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: The authorised officer or constable may—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: search the vehicle;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: seize the vehicle and any of its contents.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: In acting under paragraph (4) the authorised officer or constable may—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: stop the vehicle;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: enter any premises for the purpose of searching or seizing the vehicle.)*

((not yet in force: 6)) *(not yet in force: A vehicle or its contents seized under paragraph (4) are seized on behalf of the Department.)*

((not yet in force: 7)) *(not yet in force: A person commits an offence if—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: he fails without reasonable excuse to give any assistance that an authorised officer or constable may reasonably request in the exercise of a power under paragraph (4) or (5);)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: he otherwise intentionally obstructs an authorised officer or constable in exercising that power.)*

((not yet in force: 8)) *(not yet in force: Where an authorised officer or constable has stopped a vehicle under paragraph (5)(a), he may require any occupant of the vehicle to give him—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: the occupant's name and address;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: the name and address of the registered owner of the vehicle;)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: any other information he may reasonably request.)*

((not yet in force: 9)) *(not yet in force: A person commits an offence if—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: he fails without reasonable excuse to comply with a requirement under paragraph (8);)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: he gives information required under that paragraph that is—)*
  - ((not yet in force: i)) *(not yet in force: to his knowledge false or misleading in a material way, or)*
  - ((not yet in force: ii)) *(not yet in force: given recklessly and is false or misleading in a material way.)*

((not yet in force: 10)) *(not yet in force: A person guilty of an offence under this Article is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale.)*

((not yet in force: 11)) *(not yet in force: In this Article and Article 5F—)*

- *(not yet in force: “)****(not yet in force: authorised officer)****(not yet in force: ” means an officer of the Department who is authorised in writing by the Department for the purposes of this Article;)*
- *(not yet in force: “)****(not yet in force: vehicle)****(not yet in force: ” includes any mobile plant.)*
