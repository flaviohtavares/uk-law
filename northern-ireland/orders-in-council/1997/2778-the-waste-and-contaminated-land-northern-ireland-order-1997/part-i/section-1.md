# The Waste and Contaminated Land (Northern Ireland) Order 1997, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (27).** Text as at 2025-01-24. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/1997/2778

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 1. Title and commencement

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This Order may be cited as the Waste and Contaminated Land (Northern Ireland) Order 1997.

(2)[^c21160031] This Order shall come into operation on such day or days as the Head of the Department may by order appoint.
