## 53. Disposal of vehicles by district council

*Extent: N.I.*

Where a vehicle is delivered to a district council under Article 48(5) or 49(5), Article 31 of the [^m00019] Pollution Control and Local Government (Northern Ireland) Order 1978 (disposal of removed vehicles) shall apply to that vehicle as it applies to a vehicle in the custody of the district council in pursuance of Article 30 of that Order [^key-0fcbaff05d9d24a047447b5c31e88e4b]....
