## 64. Power to install equipment for detection of traffic offences

*Extent: N.I.*

After Article 65 of the [^m00022] Roads (Northern Ireland) Order 1993 (road humps and other traffic calming works) there shall be inserted the following Article—

> **65A. Equipment for detection of traffic offences**
>
> The Department may install and maintain on or near a road structures and equipment for the detection of traffic offences.
