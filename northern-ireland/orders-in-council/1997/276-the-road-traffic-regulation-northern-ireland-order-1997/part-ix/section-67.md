# The Road Traffic Regulation (Northern Ireland) Order 1997, section 67

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2021-05-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/1997/276
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 67. Prospective exercise of powers

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Any power under this Order to make an order or give a direction relating to a road may be exercised before the road is open for public use, so as to take effect immediately on the road's becoming open for public use.

(2) The procedure for making an order or giving a direction applies in such a case with such modifications as may be prescribed.
