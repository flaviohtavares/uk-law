# The Road Traffic Regulation (Northern Ireland) Order 1997, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2021-05-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/1997/276
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 1. Title and commencement

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic Regulation (Northern Ireland) Order 1997.

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), this Order shall come into operation on the expiration of two months from the day on which it is made.

(3) Articles 55 and 56 and Article 75(2) so far as it relates to—

- (a) the [^m00001] Chronically Sick and Disabled Persons (Northern Ireland) Act 1978;
- (b) the provisions of the Order of 1981 and Article 4 of the [^m00002] Disabled Persons (Northern Ireland) Order 1982 relating to Article 174A of the Order of 1981;
- (c) paragraph 22 of Schedule 3 to the Order of 1995; and
- (d) Article 61(1), (2) and (4),

shall come into operation on such day or days as the Department may by order appoint[^c17052591].
