## 39. Speed limits for particular classes of vehicles

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department may make regulations with respect to the maximum speed of motor vehicles of any class specified in the regulations on any class, length or part of road so specified.

(2) The powers conferred by this Article are in addition to and not in derogation of the powers conferred on the Department by Article 8.
