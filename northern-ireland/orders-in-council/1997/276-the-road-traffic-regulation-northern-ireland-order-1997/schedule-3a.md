## (not yet in force: SCHEDULE 3A) — (not yet in force: PROHIBITION OR RESTRICTION OF USE OF PUBLIC ROADS: SPECIAL EVENTS)[^c22070001]

*Extent: N.I.*

### (not yet in force: Interpretation)

**(not yet in force: 1).** ((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: This paragraph applies for the purposes of this Schedule.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: Subject to sub-paragraph (3), a “special event” is—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: any sporting event, social event or entertainment which is held on a public road; or)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: the making of a film on a public road.)*

- *(not yet in force: And for the purpose of this paragraph “)****(not yet in force: film)****(not yet in force: ” includes a recording on any medium from which a moving image may by any means be produced.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: The following are not special events —)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: a public procession (within the meaning of the Public Processions (Northern Ireland) Act 1998);)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: a motor race falling within the Road Races (Northern Ireland) Order 1986 (motor races on roads);)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: a race or trial falling within Article 45 of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1995 (cycle racing on roads).)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: The “relevant authority”—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: for a special road, is the Department;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: for any other public road, is the district council for the district in which the road is situated.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: Where the special event is the making of a film, references to the holding of the event are to be read as references to the making of the film.)*

((not yet in force: 6)) *(not yet in force: References to the promoter, in relation to a special event, are references to the person organising the event.)*

((not yet in force: 7)) *(not yet in force: References to an order are to an order under paragraph 2.)*

((not yet in force: 8)) *(not yet in force: A public road is “affected” by an order or proposed order if the order contains or would contain provisions restricting or prohibiting the use of the road.)*

### (not yet in force: Prohibition or restriction on public roads in connection with special events)

**(not yet in force: 2).** ((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: Sub-paragraph (2) applies if the relevant authority for a public road is satisfied that—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: traffic on the road should be restricted or prohibited for the purpose of—)*
  - ((not yet in force: i)) *(not yet in force: facilitating the holding of a special event,)*
  - ((not yet in force: ii)) *(not yet in force: enabling members of the public to watch a special event, or)*
  - ((not yet in force: iii)) *(not yet in force: reducing the disruption to traffic likely to be caused by a special event; and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: it is not reasonably practicable for the event to be held otherwise than on a public road.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: The relevant authority may by order restrict or prohibit temporarily the use of that road, or any part of it, by vehicles or vehicles of any class or by pedestrians, to such extent and subject to such conditions or exceptions as it may consider necessary or expedient.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: An order may relate to the public road on which the special event is to be held or to any other public road.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: No order shall be made with respect to any public road which would have the effect of preventing at any time access for pedestrians—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: to any premises situated on or adjacent to the road, or)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: to any other premises accessible for pedestrians from, and only from, the road.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: An order may suspend or modify any statutory provision relating to the affected road or its use by traffic or pedestrians.)*

((not yet in force: 6)) *(not yet in force: An order may contain provision —)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: requiring the promoter to insure against such risks in connection with the holding of the event on a public road as the relevant authority may specify;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: requiring the promoter to produce to the relevant authority such certificates as it may require as to the safety of any structures, equipment or other apparatus to be used in association with the special event;)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: requiring the promoter to erect such barriers and place such signs in such places on or in the vicinity of any affected road as the relevant authority may specify;)*
- ((not yet in force: d)) *(not yet in force: requiring any such barriers and signs to be erected no earlier than, and removed no later than, such times as the relevant authority may specify;)*
- ((not yet in force: e)) *(not yet in force: requiring the promoter to comply with such other conditions as may be specified in the order.)*

((not yet in force: 7)) *(not yet in force: A district council may not make an order except with the consent of the Department.)*

### (not yet in force: Procedure for making orders)

**(not yet in force: 3).** ((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: An order shall not be made except on an application made to the relevant authority by the promoter of the special event.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: An application shall be in writing and give such details of the event as the relevant authority may require.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: Where—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: an application for an order is made to a relevant authority, and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: the relevant authority is minded to make an order,)*

*(not yet in force: the relevant authority shall publish in at least one local newspaper circulating in the district in which any affected road is situated a notice complying with sub-paragraph (4).)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: The notice must—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: identify the promoter of the special event;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: identify any affected road;)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: specify any restrictions or prohibitions which the relevant authority proposes to include in the order;)*
- ((not yet in force: d)) *(not yet in force: specify the dates on which and times between which the restrictions or prohibitions would apply;)*
- ((not yet in force: e)) *(not yet in force: specify any alternative routes for traffic or pedestrians;)*
- ((not yet in force: f)) *(not yet in force: state the address where copies of the application may be inspected by any person free of charge at all reasonable times;)*
- ((not yet in force: g)) *(not yet in force: state that representations in writing may be sent to the relevant authority within such period as is specified in the notice (not being less than 21 days from the date of the last publication of the notice) at such address as is so specified.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: Before making an order the relevant authority must consult—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: the district commander of the police district in which any affected road is situated;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: the Northern Ireland Fire and Rescue Service;)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: the Northern Ireland Ambulance Service.)*

((not yet in force: 6)) *(not yet in force: When considering whether to make an order, the relevant authority must—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: consider any representations made in accordance with a notice published under sub-paragraph (3) and the outcome of consultations under sub-paragraph (5);)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: have regard to the safety and convenience of alternative routes suitable for traffic and pedestrians affected by the order; and)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: if the relevant authority is a district council, also have regard to any guidance issued by the Department under paragraph 5.)*

### (not yet in force: Recovery of certain costs)

**(not yet in force: 4).** *(not yet in force: The relevant authority may recover from the promoter the whole of the costs incurred by the relevant authority in connection with or in consequence of the making of an order, including in particular the cost to the relevant authority of complying with the requirement in paragraph 3(3) to publish notices.)*

### (not yet in force: Guidance)

**(not yet in force: 5).** *(not yet in force: The Department may issue guidance—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: to district councils as to the exercise of their functions under this Schedule; and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: to promoters of events as to the procedure for making an order and as to the conduct of events in relation to which an order has been made.)*

### (not yet in force: Offences in relation to orders)

**(not yet in force: 6).** ((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: A person who contravenes, or who uses or permits the use of a vehicle in contravention of, a restriction or prohibition imposed by an order is guilty of an offence.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: A promoter who fails to comply with any requirement imposed under paragraph 2(6) is guilty of an offence.)*
