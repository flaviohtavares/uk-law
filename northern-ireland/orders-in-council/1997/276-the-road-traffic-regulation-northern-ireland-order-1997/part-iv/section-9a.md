## 9A. Parking places: introductory[^key-b1230746ffcfc1190dd7e15b0ab7b055]

*Extent: N.I.*

In this Part “**the parking authority**” means—

- (a) in relation to off-street parking places operated as “park and ride” or “park and share” parking places, the Department;
- (b) in relation to any other off-street parking places, the district council for the district in which the parking places are situated;
- (c) in relation to parking places on roads, the Department.
