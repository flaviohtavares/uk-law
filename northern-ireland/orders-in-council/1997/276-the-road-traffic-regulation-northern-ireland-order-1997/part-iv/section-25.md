## 25. Parking attendants[^c-dc2e42a6dc2efe10f60963a69ccbd288]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The parking authority[^c-61332f24686553e6161cdca027a990d6] may appoint such persons as may be necessary for the superintendence of parking places.

(2) Persons appointed under paragraph (1) shall be known as parking attendants.
