## 27. Protection of parking authority from liability[^c-dc2e42a6dc2efe10f60963a69ccbd288][^c-519d34d4fcf2919cc145adae51111937]

*Extent: N.I.*

The exercise by the parking authority of its functions under this Part shall not render the parking authority subject to any liability in respect of the loss of or damage to any vehicle in a parking place or the fittings or contents of any such vehicle, unless such loss or damage is directly attributable to a negligent act of the parking authority or its servant or agent.
