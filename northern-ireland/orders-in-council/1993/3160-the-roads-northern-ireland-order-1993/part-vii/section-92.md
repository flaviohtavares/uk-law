> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 92. Playing games on a road

*Extent: N.I.*

Any person who plays a game on a road to the danger of any person using the road, shall be guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 2 on the standard scale.
