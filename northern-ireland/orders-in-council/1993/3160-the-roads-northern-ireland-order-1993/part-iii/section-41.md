> Text as at 2024-01-01.

## 41. Powers under this Part to be cumulative

*Extent: N.I.*

The powers conferred on the Department by this Part shall, unless the context otherwise requires, be construed as being in addition to and not in derogation of the powers conferred on the Department by any other Part of this Order or by any other statutory provision.
