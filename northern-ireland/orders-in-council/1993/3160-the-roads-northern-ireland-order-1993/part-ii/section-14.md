# The Roads (Northern Ireland) Order 1993, section 14

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 14. Trunk roads

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where the Department considers it expedient for the purpose of extending, improving or reorganising the trunk system that any road should be designated as a trunk road, the Department may by order direct that—

- (a) any existing road;
- (b) any road in the course of construction; or
- (c) any road proposed to be constructed,

shall become a trunk road; and the trunk system shall be modified accordingly.

(2) In this Article “the trunk system” means the main system of routes for through traffic in Northern Ireland.
