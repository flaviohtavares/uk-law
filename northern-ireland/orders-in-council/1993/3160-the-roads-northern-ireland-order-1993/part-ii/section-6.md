> Text as at 2024-01-01.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 6. Extinguishment of certain public rights over navigable waters and foreshore

*Extent: N.I.*

The Department may, if it considers it necessary or desirable to do so in connection with any matter mentioned in Article 5(1), by order extinguish—

- (a) any public rights over or in relation to such part of the foreshore as is specified in the order;
- (b) any public rights of navigation over such part of any navigable waters (whether the sea, a river or other navigable waters) as is specified in the order.
