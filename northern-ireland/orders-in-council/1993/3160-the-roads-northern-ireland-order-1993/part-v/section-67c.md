> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 67C. Application[^c20624991]

*Extent: N.I.*

This Part shall not apply to projects where an environmental statement or the details of the project were published before the coming into operation of the Roads (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999.
