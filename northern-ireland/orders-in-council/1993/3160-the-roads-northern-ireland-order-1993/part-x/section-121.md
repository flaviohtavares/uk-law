> Text as at 2024-01-01.

## 121. Expenses of Department

*Extent: N.I.*

Any expenses incurred by the Department under this Order shall be defrayed either out of moneys appropriated for the purposes of defraying such expenses or, if the Department of Finance and Personnel so directs, by means of sums charged on and issued out of the Consolidated Fund.
