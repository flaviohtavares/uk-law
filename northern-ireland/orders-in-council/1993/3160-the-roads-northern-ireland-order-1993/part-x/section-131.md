# The Roads (Northern Ireland) Order 1993, section 131

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 131. Arbitration

*Extent: N.I.*

Subject to the provisions of this Order, Part I of the Arbitration Act 1996[^c16868111] shall, in relation to arbitrations under this Order, have effect as if the parties had entered into an arbitration agreement within the meaning of that Act providing for reference to a single arbitrator.
