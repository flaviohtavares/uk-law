# The Roads (Northern Ireland) Order 1993, section 134

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 134. Saving provisions, consequential amendments, repeals and revocations

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The saving provisions set out in Schedule 9 shall have effect.

- *Para. (2)—Amendments*
- *Para. (3)—Repeals*

(4) In the Schedule to the Local Government (Modifications and Repeals) Order (Northern Ireland) 1973[^c16868161], the entries relating to the statutory provisions mentioned in Schedule 11 are hereby revoked to the extent that they modify those provisions.

(5) The Roads (Assessment of Environmental Effects) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1988[^c16868171] are hereby revoked.
