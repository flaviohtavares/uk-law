## 72. Issue of process

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A process issued in debt proceeding shall require the defendant to appear before a court of summary jurisdiction [^c-49a649cebe466bd548f35831d0352323]....

(2) A process issued in ejectment proceedings shall require the defendant to appear before a court of summary jurisdiction [^c-49a649cebe466bd548f35831d0352323]....

(3) A claim for the recovery of arrears of rent or sums due under Article 69 not exceeding £100 due in respect of lands or premises may be joined together with a claim in ejectment proceedings and commenced by the issue of the same process.
