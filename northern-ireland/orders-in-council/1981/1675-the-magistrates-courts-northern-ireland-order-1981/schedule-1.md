## SCHEDULE 1 — MATTERS WHICH MAY BE DEALT WITH BY A JUSTICE OF THE PEACE OUT OF PETTY SESSIONS

*Extent: N.I.*

Articles 3(2) and 18(3).

### PART I — Enactments passed before 1st January 1936, under which functions may be exercised by a justice of the peace

**1.** The Justices of the Peace Act 1361[^c16971711] in so far as it authorises the taking of security for good behaviour or keeping the peace.

**2.** Any enactment authorising a lay magistrate[^c16971721] to sign summonses warrants, certificates or other documents or make or sign declarations, administer oaths or take affidavits, declarations, affirmations, informations, bonds or recognizances.

**3.** Section 5(1) of the General Dealers (Ireland) Act 1903[^c16971741].

**4.** Section 2[^c16971751]. . . of the Game Preservation Act (Northern Ireland) 1928[^c16971761].

*Para. 5 rep. by 1985 NI 2*

*Part. 2 rep. by 2002 c. 26*
