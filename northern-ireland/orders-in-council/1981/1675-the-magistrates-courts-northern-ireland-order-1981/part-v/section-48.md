## 48. Continuous bail

*Extent: N.I.*

Where a person is remanded on bail any recognizance or condition of bail may provide[^c20377261] for his appearance at every time and place to which during the course of proceedings the hearing may be adjourned, without prejudice, however, to the power of the court to vary the order at any subsequent hearing.
