## 52. Power of court to sentence person convicted by another court in same county court division

*Extent: N.I.*

Where a person has been convicted of an offence by a court of summary jurisdiction and the case has been adjourned or where a person has been remanded under Article 51 he may be sentenced or otherwise dealt with for that offence by any court of summary jurisdiction [^key-6270a9b2d57a735498d5e99000300236]....
