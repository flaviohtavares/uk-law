## 117. Postponement of issue of warrants or decrees until appeal determined

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2) where a person has given notice of appeal to the county court or has applied for a case to be stated for the opinion of the Court of Appeal and where he is required to enter into a recognizance under Article 149 he has entered into that recognizance, a decree or warrant for the purpose of enforcing the order shall not be issued until—

- (a) the order has been affirmed, amended or varied on appeal; or
- (b) it appears to the court that the appellant has failed to perform the conditions of a recognizance entered into under Article 149; or
- (c) the appellant has abandoned the appeal in accordance with Article 150.

(2) Nothing in this Article shall apply to a person sentenced to imprisonment or other form of detention in custody or prejudice the operation of any enactment which expressly authorises or directs the levy of any sum notwithstanding the appeal or of Article 148 respecting persons in custody.
