## 152. Enforcement of orders made, affirmed or varied on appeal or of original order where appeal abandoned

*Extent: N.I.*

Without prejudice to Article 147, or, in the case of an appeal to the county court, to Article 28 of the County Courts (Northern Ireland) Order 1980, after an appeal has been decided by the Court of Appeal or county court or, where an appeal has been abandoned (including where an applicant for a case stated has failed to take delivery of it or to transmit it to the Court of Appeal), an order, decree or warrant to enforce the order made on appeal, affirming, reversing or varying the original order, or (as the case may be) to enforce the original order, may be issued by any resident magistrate[^c16971481]. . . and, in the case of an appeal to the county court, by the chief clerk.
