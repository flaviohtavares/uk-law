## 145A. Immunity of county court judges hearing appeals under this Part.[^c16971301]

*Extent: N.I.*

Articles 5, 6,6A[^c16971461] and 10 shall apply in relation to a county court judge sitting in connection with an appeal under this Part as they apply in relation to a resident magistrate.
