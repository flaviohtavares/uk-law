# The Magistrates' Courts (Northern Ireland) Order 1981, section 15

>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 15. Rules under or for the purpose of particular enactments[^c22031281]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The power under section 32A(1) of the Treatment of Offenders Act (Northern Ireland) 1968[^c16968621] with respect to the making of rules under that Act regarding persons charged before magistrates' courts shall be exercised by the Secretary of State after consultation with the Rules Committee.

(2) Magistrates' courts rules may assign to juvenile courts the hearing of any applications for orders or licences relating to children[^c16968641]. . . being applications cognizable by courts of summary jurisdiction, if in the opinion of the Lord Chief Justice[^c20293831] after consultation with the Rules Committee it is desirable in the interests of the children[^c16968641]. . . concerned that such applications should be heard by juvenile courts.

(3) For the purposes of paragraph (2), any complaint under Schedule 13[^c16968681] to the Education and Libraries (Northern Ireland) Order 1986 (which relates to compulsory attendance at school), shall be deemed to be an application for an order relating to a child.
