## 157. Summons or process lawfully issued may be served anywhere in Northern Ireland

*Extent: N.I.*

Any summons, notice or other process lawfully issued in connection with any proceedings before a magistrates' court may be served in any part of Northern Ireland upon the person to whom it is addressed.
