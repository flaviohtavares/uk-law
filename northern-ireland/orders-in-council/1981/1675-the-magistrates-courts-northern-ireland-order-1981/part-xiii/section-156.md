## 156. Validity of documents issued in proceedings

*Extent: N.I.*

A summons, warrant, decree or other document issued by a resident magistrate or lay magistrate[^c16971511] shall not be void by reason of the person who signed the document subsequently dying or ceasing to hold or becoming disqualified for holding office.
