## 79. Issue of summons upon civil complaint

*Extent: N.I.*

Where a complaint in a civil matter is made to a justice of the peace [^c-19feb487985904f8dfc9d4afbca098a8]... upon which a court of summary jurisdiction [^c-19feb487985904f8dfc9d4afbca098a8]... has power to make an order against any person, the justice may issue a summons directed to that person requiring him to appear before that court to answer to the complaint.
