## 7. Where warrant on conviction is issued by clerk or another justice

*Extent: N.I.*

Where a conviction or order is made by a magistrates' court[^c16968311] and a warrant to enforce it is signed by[^c16968331]. . . a clerk of petty sessions, no action shall be brought against the[^c16968331]. . . clerk who signed the warrant by reason of any lack of jurisdiction in the magistrates' court which made the conviction or order.
