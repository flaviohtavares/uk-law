## 8. Acts performed pursuant to decision of High Court or on appeal

*Extent: N.I.*

*Para. (1) rep. by 1990 c. 41*

(2) Where a warrant or other document signed by a[^c16968351]. . . clerk of petty sessions is issued upon any conviction or order which is confirmed upon appeal no action shall be brought against that[^c16968351]. . . clerk in respect of anything done pursuant to the warrant or document.
