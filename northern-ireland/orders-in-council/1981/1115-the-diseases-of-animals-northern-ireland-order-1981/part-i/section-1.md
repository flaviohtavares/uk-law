# The Diseases of Animals (Northern Ireland) Order 1981, section 1

## 1. Title and commencement

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This Order may be cited as the Diseases of Animals (Northern Ireland) Order 1981.

(2) This Order shall come into operation on the expiration of one month from the day on which it is made.
