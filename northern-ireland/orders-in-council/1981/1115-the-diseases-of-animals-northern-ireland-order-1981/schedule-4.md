## SCHEDULE 4 — SUBSTANCES TO WHICH PART III APPLIES

*Extent: N.I.*

Articles 33, 34, 36(1).

*Paras. 1‐3 rep. by SR 1981/408*

**4.** Any of the following substances to be administered to swine:-

(a) Para-aminophenylarsonic acid.

- Sodium Para-aminophenylarsonic acid.
- 4-Hydroxy-3-Nitrophenylarsonic acid.
- 4-Nitrophenylarsonic acid.
- 4-Ureidophenylarsonic acid.
- Arsenosobenzene.

(b) Any other form of arsenic.

**5.** Any substance containing any proportion of any of the substance specified in paragraph 4 if such substance is to be administered to swine.

**6.** The prohibition in Article 33 shall not apply to any person who has in his possession a substance specified in paragraphs 4 and 5 for administration to animals other than swine (whether by himself or by any other person) or who administers any such substance to any animal other than swine.
