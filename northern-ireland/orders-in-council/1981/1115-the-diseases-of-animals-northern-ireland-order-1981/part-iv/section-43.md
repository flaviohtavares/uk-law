## 43. Agricultural marketing boards to furnish information to the Department

*Extent: N.I.*

It shall be the duty of every board administering a scheme under[^c16923071] the Agricultural Marketing Act (Northern Ireland) 1964[^c16923081] or the Agricultural Marketing (Northern Ireland) Order 1982[^c16923091] to furnish to the Department at any time such information as the Department may reasonably require for the purposes of its functions under this Order.
