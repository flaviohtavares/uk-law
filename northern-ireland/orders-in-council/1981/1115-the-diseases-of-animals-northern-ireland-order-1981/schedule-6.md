## SCHEDULE 6 — TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS

*Extent: N.I.*

Article 62(2).

**1.** For the purposes of an order under the Diseases of Animals Act (Northern Ireland) 1958[^c16924131] made before the coming into operation of section 55(6) of the Criminal Law Act 1977[^c16924141] and expressed to be made for the purpose of preventing the introduction or spreading of rabies into or within Northern Ireland, Article 52(3) *(a)* *(i)* shall have effect as if for “£500” there were substituted “ £1,000 ”.

**2.** Without prejudice to section 17 of the Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 an order under the Diseases of Animals Act (Northern Ireland) 1958 imposing prohibitions or restrictions shall be deemed always to have been enabled to provide that the Department may do so by means of the issue of licences in such form and subject to such conditions as may be specified in the Order.
