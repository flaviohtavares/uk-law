## 32J. Interpretation[^c22000931]

*Extent: N.I.*

In this Part—

- “**breeding**” includes the placing in any creature of an embryo which has been created otherwise than by fertilisation;
- “**keeper**”, in relation to livestock, includes an owner of the livestock;
- “**livestock**” means—
  - (a) any creature which is kept, fattened or bred for the production of food, wool, skin or fur;
  - (b) any creature, other than a dog, which is kept for use in the farming of land; and
  - (c) any equine animal;
- “**TSE**” means transmissible spongiform encephalopathy.
