## 34. Records and returns

*Extent: N.I.*

The Department may make orders for the purposes of—

- (a) ensuring the keeping of such records of the administration to animals (other than animals kept in a laboratory such as is referred to in Article 33 (2) *(f)*) of substances specified in Schedule 4 as may be required by the orders; and
- (b) providing for the inspection, verification and copying of such records by officers of the Department;

and orders made under this Article may require registered veterinary surgeons or registered veterinary practitioners or other persons specified in the orders to make such returns to the Department giving such details of any substances so specified which have been administered, directed to be administered or prescribed by them to or in respect of any animals (including details sufficient to identify the animals concerned) as may be so required.
