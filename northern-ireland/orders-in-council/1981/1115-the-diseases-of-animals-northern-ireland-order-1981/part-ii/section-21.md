## 21. Protection of animals and poultry from suffering, (affected by repeal, see note) etc. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-d3e49d946829786f3e9f4ba7cd3bd3d5][^c-d3e49d946829786f3e9f4ba7cd3bd3d5][^c-d3e49d946829786f3e9f4ba7cd3bd3d5]

*Extent: N.I.*

The Department may by order make provision— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d3e49d946829786f3e9f4ba7cd3bd3d5]

- (a) for protecting animals and poultry from unnecessary suffering— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d3e49d946829786f3e9f4ba7cd3bd3d5]
  - (i) during inland transit, or in an aircraft engaged in a journey or part of a journey or part of a journey beginning and ending in Northern Ireland; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d3e49d946829786f3e9f4ba7cd3bd3d5]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d3e49d946829786f3e9f4ba7cd3bd3d5] during passage by sea or air and on landing; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d3e49d946829786f3e9f4ba7cd3bd3d5]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d3e49d946829786f3e9f4ba7cd3bd3d5] in connection with their exposure for sale and their disposal after sale; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d3e49d946829786f3e9f4ba7cd3bd3d5]
- (b) for securing a proper supply of water and food to animals and poultry during any period of their detention. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d3e49d946829786f3e9f4ba7cd3bd3d5]
