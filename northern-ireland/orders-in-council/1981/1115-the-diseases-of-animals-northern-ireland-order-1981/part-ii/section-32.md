## 32. Export of animals and poultry

*Extent: N.I.*

The Department, in the interests of animal health or of human health, may by order—

- (a) regulate the exportation from Northern Ireland [^c22004991]. . . of animals or poultry or carcases of animals or poultry, and, in particular, prohibit exportation without such certificate or licence as may be prescribed by the order; and
- (b) make provisions as to the circumstances in which, and the conditions on which, a certificate or licence may be obtained.
