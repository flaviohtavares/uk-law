# The Diseases of Animals (Northern Ireland) Order 1981, section 23A

## 23A. Animal welfare in exportation (affected by repeal, see note)[^c16922741][^key-d3e49d946829786f3e9f4ba7cd3bd3d5][^key-d3e49d946829786f3e9f4ba7cd3bd3d5]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department, in the interests of animal welfare, may by order— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d3e49d946829786f3e9f4ba7cd3bd3d5]

- (a) regulate the exportation from Northern Ireland of animals or poultry, and, in particular, prohibit exportation without such licence or certificate as may be prescribed by the order; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d3e49d946829786f3e9f4ba7cd3bd3d5]
- (b) make provision as to the circumstances in which, and the conditions on which, a certificate or licence may be obtained. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d3e49d946829786f3e9f4ba7cd3bd3d5]

(2) Without prejudice to the generality of paragraph (1), an order under that paragraph may include provision for requiring persons proposing to export animals or poultry from Northern Ireland to furnish information about— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d3e49d946829786f3e9f4ba7cd3bd3d5]

- (a) the intended ultimate destination of the animals or poultry; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d3e49d946829786f3e9f4ba7cd3bd3d5]
- (b) the arrangements for conveying them to that destination; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d3e49d946829786f3e9f4ba7cd3bd3d5]
- (c) any other matters which may be specified in the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d3e49d946829786f3e9f4ba7cd3bd3d5]
