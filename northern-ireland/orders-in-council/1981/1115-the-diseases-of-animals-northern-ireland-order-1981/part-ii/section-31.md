## 31. Compensation not payable for animals temporarily in Northern Ireland for quarantine purposes

*Extent: N.I.*

Notwithstanding any other provision of this Order, no compensation shall be payable under this Order in respect of any imported animal which enters Northern Ireland for quarantine purposes in the course of transit to any place out of Northern Ireland.
