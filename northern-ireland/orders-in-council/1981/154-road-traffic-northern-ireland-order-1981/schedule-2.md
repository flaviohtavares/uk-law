## SCHEDULE 2 — STATUTORY PROVISIONS RELATING TO VEHICLES AND THEIR DRIVERS

*Extent: N.I.*

Article 84.

| *Statutory provision* | *Effect* |
| --- | --- |
| Section 4 of the Transport Act (Northern Ireland) 1967. | To require users of motor vehicles carrying passengers and their luggage for reward to hold road service licences. |
