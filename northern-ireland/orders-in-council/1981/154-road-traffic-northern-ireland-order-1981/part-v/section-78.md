# Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981, section 78

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (101).** Text as at 2025-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/1981/154
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 78. Interpretation[^c17616791][^c17616431][^c17617271]

*Extent: N.I.*

In this Article, Articles 70 to 77 and Article 180A[^c20403851]—

- “[^c17617281]**conduct**” means—
  - (a) in relation to an applicant for or the holder of a large goods vehicle driver's licence or the holder of an LGV Community licence, his conduct as a driver of a motor vehicle, and
  - (b) in relation to an applicant for or the holder of a passenger-carrying vehicle driver's licence or the holder of a PCV Community licence, his conduct both as a driver of a motor vehicle and in any other respect relevant to his holding a passenger-carrying vehicle driver's licence or (as the case may be) his authorisation by virtue of Article 15A(1) to drive in Northern Ireland a passenger-carrying vehicle of any class,

  including, in either case, such conduct in Great Britain;
- “**counterpart**” in relation to a licence to drive under Part II or a Community licence has the same meaning as in that Part;
- “**full licence**” means a large goods vehicle or passenger-carrying vehicle driver's licence other than a provisional licence;
- “**large goods vehicle**” means a motor vehicle (not being a medium-sized goods vehicle within the meaning of Part II) which is designed and constructed for the carriage of no more than 8 passengers in addition to the driver and[^key-07e46edfd732b5fd5b8b6be1c2cc1b7c] which is constructed or adapted to carry or to haul goods and the permissable maximum weight of which exceeds 7.5 tonnes;
- “**passenger-carrying vehicle**” means—
  - (a) a large passenger-carrying vehicle, that is to say, a vehicle used for carrying passengers which is constructed or adapted to carry more than 16 passengers, or
  - (b) a small passenger-carrying vehicle, that is to say, a vehicle used for carrying passengers for hire or reward which is constructed or adapted to carry more than 8 but not more than 16 passengers and includes a combination of such a motor vehicle and a trailer;
- “**notice**” means notice in writing and notify shall be construed accordingly;
- “**provisional licence**” means a licence granted by virtue of Article 13(2);
- “**the special provisions**” has the meaning given in Article 70;
- and “**permissible maximum weight**” has the same meaning as in Part II.
