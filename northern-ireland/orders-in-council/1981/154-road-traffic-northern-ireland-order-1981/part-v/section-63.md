## 63. Limitation of continuous hours of duty of drivers of public service vehicles[^c17616721][^c-e8f83d28dba9eba785ba62c4e7da031e]

*Extent: N.I.*

Article 56 shall apply to the driving of public service vehicles as it applies to the driving of goods vehicles and paragraph (2)( *b*) of that Article shall in its application to public service vehicles have effect as if that sub-paragraph included a reference to any time spent by a driver on a public service vehicle in any capacity other than that of a passenger.
