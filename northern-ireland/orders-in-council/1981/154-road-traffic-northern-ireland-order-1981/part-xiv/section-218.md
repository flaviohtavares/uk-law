## 218. General provisions as to regulations[^c17619401]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department may make regulations prescribing anything which is to be prescribed and providing for any matter in regard to which regulations may be made under the provisions of this Order.

(2) Save as is otherwise expressly provided by this Order, all regulations and orders made under this Order shall be subject to negative resolution.

(3) Without prejudice to the provisions of section 17(3) of the Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 [^m00019], any regulations made under this Order may be of a local, as opposed to public general, nature and may be limited in their application to a particular area.
