# Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981, section 217

## 217. Savings[^c17619401]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Nothing in this Order shall authorise any person to use on any road any vehicle so constructed or used as to cause a public or private nuisance, or shall affect any liability of the driver or owner of a vehicle whether under statute or common law.

(2) The provisions of this Order relating to public service vehicles shall, except as is otherwise expressly provided by this Order, be in addition to and not in derogation of any other enactment (including this Order) applicable to motor vehicles or to any class of such vehicles.

*Para. (3) rep. by 1995 NI 18*

(4) Nothing in this Order shall prejudice or affect any powers exercisable by Order in Council under section 2 of the Motor Vehicles (International Circulation) Act 1952 [^m00018].

(5) Schedule 6 (which contains futher savings) shall have effect.
