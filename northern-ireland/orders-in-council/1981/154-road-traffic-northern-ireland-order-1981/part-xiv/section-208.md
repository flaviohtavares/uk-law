## 208. Inquiries and applications

*Extent: N.I.*

The provisions of Schedule 5 shall have effect in relation to any inquiries or applications authorised by any provision of this Order, and section 23 of the Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 [^m00016] shall not apply in relation to any such inquiry or application.
