## 178. Identification of pedal cyclists[^c20507091]

*Extent: N.I.*

A constable may require any[^c17618881]. . . cyclist who appears to him to have committed an offence under Article 42, 43 or 44 of the Order of 1995 to give his correct name and address, and if that person fails to do so he shall be guilty of an offence under this Order.
