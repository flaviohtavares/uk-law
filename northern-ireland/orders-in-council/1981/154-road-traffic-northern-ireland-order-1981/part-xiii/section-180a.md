## 180A. Powers of vehicle examiners[^c20515071][^c17618951]

*Extent: N.I.*

An examiner appointed under Article 74 of the Order of 1995 may, on production if required of his authority, exercise all such powers as are exercisable by a constable under–

- (a) Article 177(1)(a) and (c); and
- (b) Article 180.
