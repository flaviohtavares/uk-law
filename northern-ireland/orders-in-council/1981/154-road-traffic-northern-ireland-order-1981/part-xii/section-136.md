## 136. Surrender of certificates and licences

*Extent: N.I.*

Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22100801]

- (a) the name of a person to whom a certificate prescribed under Article 132(2)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22100801] *g*) has been issued is removed from the register of approved driving instructors in pursuance of regulations made under that Article; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22100801]
- (b) a licence granted under Article 135 to a person expires or is revoked; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22100801]

he shall, if so required by the Department by notice in writing, surrender the certificate or licence, as the case may be, to the Department within the period of 14 days beginning with that on which the notice is given, and if he fails to do so he shall be guilty of an offence under this Order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22100801]
