# Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981, section 135

## 135. Licences to give instruction restricted by Article 133

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) For the purpose of enabling a person to acquire practical experience in giving instruction in driving motor cars with a view to undergoing the examination prescribed under Article 132(2)( *b*) the Department may by regulations provide for the granting, renewal and revocation of licences to give instruction in the driving of a motor car being instruction the giving of which is restricted by Article 133.

(2) In particular, and without prejudice to the generality of paragraph (1), regulations made under that paragraph—

- (a) may prescribe [^c20408631]. . . the fees to be paid on each application for a licence or renewal of licence;
- (b) may provide for special exemptions in connection with the granting or renewal of licences to persons not residing in Northern Ireland or persons becoming resident in Northern Ireland;
- (c) may provide for the refusal to grant a licence under this Article to a person to whom two or more such licences have previously been issued.
