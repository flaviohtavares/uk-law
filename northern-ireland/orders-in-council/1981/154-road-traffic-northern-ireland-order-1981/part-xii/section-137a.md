## 137A. Meaning of motor car[^c17618041]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c22100821] Notwithstanding Article 3 of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1995, in this Part “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22100821]**motor car *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22100821]**” means a motor vehicle (other than an invalid carriage or motor cycle)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22100821]

- (a) which is not constructed or adapted to carry more than nine persons inclusive of the driver, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22100821]
- (b) which has a maximum gross weight not exceeding 3.5 tonnes. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22100821]

(2) In paragraph (1) “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22100821]**maximum gross weight *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22100821]**” has the same meaning as in Part II. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22100821]
