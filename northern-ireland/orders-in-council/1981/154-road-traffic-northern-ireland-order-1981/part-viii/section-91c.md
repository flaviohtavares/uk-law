# Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981, section 91C

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 91C). (not yet in force: Fixed penalty notices)[^c20404321]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: Where on any occasion the Department has reason to believe that a person has committed an offence under Article 91A, the Department may give the person a notice offering him the opportunity of discharging any liability to conviction for that offence by payment of a fixed penalty to the Department.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: Where a person is given a notice under this Article in respect of an offence under Article 91A–)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: no proceedings may be instituted for that offence before the end of the period of 21 days following the date of the notice, and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: he may not be convicted of that offence if he pays the fixed penalty before the end of that period.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: A notice under this Article must give such particulars of the circumstances alleged to constitute the offence as are necessary for giving reasonable information of the offence.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: A notice under this Article must also state–)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: the period during which, by virtue of paragraph (2), proceedings will not be taken for the offence,)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: the amount of the fixed penalty, and)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: the person to whom and the address at which the fixed penalty may be paid.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: Without prejudice to payment by any other method, payment of the fixed penalty may be made by pre-paying and posting a letter containing the amount of the penalty to the person mentioned in paragraph (4)(c) at the address so mentioned.)*

((not yet in force: 6)) *(not yet in force: Where a letter is sent in accordance with paragraph (5) payment is to be regarded as having been made at the time at which that letter would be delivered in the ordinary course of post.)*

((not yet in force: 7)) *(not yet in force: Regulations may make provision as to any matter incidental to the operation of this Article, and in particular–)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: as to the form of a notice under this Article,)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: as to the information to be provided in such a notice by virtue of this Article, and)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: as to any further information to be provided in a such notice.)*

((not yet in force: 8)) *(not yet in force: The fixed penalty payable under this Article is, subject to paragraph (9), £100.)*

((not yet in force: 9)) *(not yet in force: Regulations may substitute a different amount for the amount for the time being specified in paragraph (8).)*

((not yet in force: 10)) *(not yet in force: Regulations may make provision for treating a fixed penalty payable under this Article as having been paid if a lesser amount is paid before the end of a prescribed period.)*

((not yet in force: 11)) *(not yet in force: In any proceedings a certificate which–)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: purports to be signed on behalf of the Department, and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: states that payment of a fixed penalty was or was not received by a date specified in the certificate,)*

*(not yet in force: is evidence of the facts stated.)*
