# Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981, section 91D

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 91D). (not yet in force: Article 91A offence: supplementary)[^c20404321]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: Schedule 2A makes provision about the immobilisation of vehicles as regards which it appears that an offence under Article 91A is being committed and about their removal and disposal.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: A person authorised by the Department for the purposes of this paragraph may on behalf of the Department conduct and appear in any proceedings by or against the Department in any court of summary jurisdiction in connection with the enforcement of an offence under Article 91A or under regulations made under Article 103 by virtue of Schedule 2A.)*
