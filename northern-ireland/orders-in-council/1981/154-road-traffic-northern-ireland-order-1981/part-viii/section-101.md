# Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981, section 101

## 101. Meaning of authorised insurer for purposes of this Part

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c17617971] In this Part “**"authorised insurer”**” has the same meaning as in Article 12.

(2) If any person or body of persons ceases to be a member of the Motor Insurers' Bureau[^c17617971]. . . , that person or body shall not thereby cease to be treated as an authorised insurer for the purposes of this Part[^c19327901]. . .

- (a) in relation to any policy issued by the insurer before ceasing to be such a member; or
- (b) in relation to any obligation (whether arising before or after the insurer ceased to be such a member) which the insurer may be called upon to meet under or in consequence of any such policy[^c19327931]. . . by virtue of making a payment in pursuance of such an obligation.
