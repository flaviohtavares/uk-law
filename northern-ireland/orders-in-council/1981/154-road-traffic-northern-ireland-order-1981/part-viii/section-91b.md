# Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981, section 91B

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 91B). (not yet in force: Exceptions to Article 91A offence)[^c20404321]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: A person (“the registered keeper”) in whose name a vehicle which does not meet the insurance requirements is registered at any particular time (“)****(not yet in force: the relevant time)****(not yet in force: ”) does not commit an offence under Article 91A at that time if any of the following conditions are satisfied.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: The first condition is that at the relevant time the vehicle is owned as described in paragraph (2) or (2A) of Article 90 (whether or not at the relevant time it is being driven as described in that provision).)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: The second condition is that the registered keeper–)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: is not at the relevant time the person keeping the vehicle, and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: if previously he was the person keeping the vehicle, he has by the relevant time complied with any requirements under paragraph (6)(a) that he is required to have complied with by the relevant or any earlier time.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: The third condition is that–)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: the registered keeper is at the relevant time the person keeping the vehicle,)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: at the relevant time the vehicle is not used on a road or other public place, and)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: the registered keeper has by the relevant time complied with any requirements under paragraph (6)(a) that he is required to have complied with by the relevant or any earlier time.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: The fourth condition is that–)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: the vehicle has been stolen before the relevant time,)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: the vehicle has not been recovered by the relevant time, and)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: any requirements under paragraph (6)(b) that, in connection with the theft, are required to have been complied with by the relevant or any earlier time have been complied with by the relevant time.)*

((not yet in force: 6)) *(not yet in force: Regulations may make provision–)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: for the purposes of paragraph (3)(b) and (4)(c), requiring a person in whose name a vehicle is registered to furnish such particulars and make such declarations as may be prescribed, and to do so at such times and in such manner as may be prescribed, and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: for the purposes of paragraph (5)(c), as to the persons to whom, the times at which and the manner in which the theft of a vehicle is to be notified.)*

((not yet in force: 7)) *(not yet in force: Regulations may make provision amending this Article for the purpose of providing for further exceptions to Article 91A (or varying or revoking any such further exceptions).)*

((not yet in force: 8)) *(not yet in force: A person accused of an offence under Article 91A is not entitled to the benefit of an exception conferred by or under this Article unless evidence is adduced that is sufficient to raise an issue with respect to that exception; but where evidence is so adduced it is for the prosecution to prove beyond reasonable doubt that the exception does not apply.)*
