# Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981, section 19E

## 19E. Provisions as to Great Britain drivers' licences

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The holder of a Great Britain driving licence may drive, and a person may cause or permit the holder of such a licence to drive, in Northern Ireland in accordance with that licence,[^c17615941], a motor vehicle of any class which he is authorised by that licence to drive, and which he is not disqualified from driving under this Part or the special provisions set out in Articles 70 to 79, notwithstanding that he is not the holder of a licence under this Part.

(2) Any driver holding a Great Britain driving licence shall be under the like obligation to produce such a licence as if it[^c-4279fb22af5dca86d1d19b523897bccc] had been a licence granted under this Part [^c-b1165c3fc069654b37ff4c066356e0d8]...; and the provisions—[^c17615961]

- (a) of this Order,[^c17615971]. . .

*Sub-para. (b) rep. by 2003 NI 16*

as to the production of licences [^c-d11f2bd22f3b5fb26554968166c77c06]... granted under this Part shall apply accordingly.

*Paras. (3)-(5) rep. by 2003 NI 16*
