# Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981, section 8A

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 8A). (not yet in force: Approved test assistants)[^c20378281]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: The Department may make regulations permitting any person wishing to be accompanied at a relevant test by another person (a “test assistant”) to be so accompanied if–)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: he submits himself for the relevant test in any circumstances in which the Department considers it appropriate that he should be entitled to be so accompanied; and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: the test assistant is approved in accordance with regulations made under this Article to accompany people at relevant tests in such circumstances in order to assist them in undergoing the relevant tests.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: The circumstances in which the Department considers it appropriate that a person should be entitled to be accompanied by a test assistant at a relevant test may include, for example, circumstances in which he is likely to have difficulty in hearing, understanding or responding to instructions or questions in the course of the relevant test without assistance.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: The regulations may make provision in relation to the approval of test assistants and may, in particular, include provision–)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: in relation to the making of applications for approval;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: for the payment in respect of applications for approval, or in connection with approvals, of fees of a prescribed amount;)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: in relation to the period for which an approval is to have effect and withdrawing approval;)*
- ((not yet in force: d)) *(not yet in force: authorising the imposition of conditions on an approval;)*
- ((not yet in force: e)) *(not yet in force: for an appeal to lie to a court of summary jurisdiction acting for the petty sessions district in which the person resides against a refusal of an application for approval, the imposition of conditions on an approval or the withdrawal of approval;)*
- ((not yet in force: f)) *(not yet in force: prescribing circumstances in which an approved test assistant may not act as such;)*
- ((not yet in force: g)) *(not yet in force: as to the evidencing by persons of their status as approved test assistants;)*
- ((not yet in force: h)) *(not yet in force: authorising the Department to make available (with or without charge) information about approved test assistants.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: In this Article “)****(not yet in force: relevant tests)****(not yet in force: ” means–)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: tests of competence to drive a motor vehicle prescribed by virtue of Article 5(3) of this Order or Article 41(5) of the Offenders Order;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: examinations of ability and fitness (or continued ability and fitness) to give driving instruction for which provision is made by virtue of Article 62 of the Order of 2007;)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: emergency control assessments under Article 65 of the Order of 2007.)*
