# Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981, section 3

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (101).** Text as at 2025-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/1981/154

## 3. Drivers of motor vehicles to have driving licences

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) It is an offence under this Order for a person to drive on a road a motor vehicle of any class otherwise than in accordance with[^c20371801] a licence authorising him to drive a motor vehicle of that class.

(2) It is an offence under this Order for a person to cause or permit another person to drive on a road a motor vehicle of any class otherwise than in accordance with a licence authorising that other person[^c20371881] to drive a motor vehicle of that class.
