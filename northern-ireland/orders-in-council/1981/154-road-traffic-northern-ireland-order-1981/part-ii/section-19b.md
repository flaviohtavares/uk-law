## 19B. Speed limit on certain vehicles displaying distinguishing mark referred to in Article 19A(3)(a)[^c17615001]

*Extent: N.I.*

Any person who drives on a road at a speed exceeding 45 miles per hour, or, as the case may be, such other speed as may be prescribed under Article 19A, a motor vehicle displaying such a distinguishing mark as is referred to in paragraph (5)(a)[^c17615081] of that Article shall, where he is not subject to the specified restrictions within the meaning of that Article while driving that vehicle, be guilty of an offence under this Order.
