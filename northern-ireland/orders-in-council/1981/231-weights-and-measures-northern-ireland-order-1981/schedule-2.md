### PART VI — SPECIAL RESTRICTIONS ON USE FOR TRADE

**1.** No person shall use the ounce troy for trade except for the purposes of transactions in, or in articles made from, gold, silver or other precious metals, including transactions in gold or silver thread, lace or fringe.

**2.** No person shall use the carat (metric) for trade except for the purposes of transactions in precious stones or pearls.

**3.**[^c16915301] No person shall use the pint for trade execpt for—

(a) the purposes of the sale of draught beer and cider; or

(b) the purposes of the sale of milk in returnable containers;[^c16915301]. . .
