# Weights and Measures (Northern Ireland) Order 1981, section 49

> Text as at 2017-01-11.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 49. Department to report to Assembly

*Extent: N.I.*

The Department shall not less than once in every three years lay before the Assembly a report of its proceedings under this Order and generally about the operation of this Order.
