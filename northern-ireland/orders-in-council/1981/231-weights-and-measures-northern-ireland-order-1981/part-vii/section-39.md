# Weights and Measures (Northern Ireland) Order 1981, section 39

> Text as at 2017-01-11.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 39. Function of Department

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-399ed7272679775136214a0133717f5c] It shall be the duty of the Department to enforce the provisions of this Order.

(2)[^c-210d7dc13b213d8bfe34371d71f08ca7] For the investigatory powers available to the Department for the purposes of the duty in paragraph (1) see Schedule 5 to the Consumer Rights Act 2015.
