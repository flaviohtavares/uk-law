# Weights and Measures (Northern Ireland) Order 1981, section 47

> Text as at 2017-01-11.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 47. Penalties

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A person guilty of an offence under Article 6(3), 7(4), 8(2), 9(2), 9(11), 10(3), 10(4), 11(4), 12(3), 12(4), 13(1), 13(4), 13A(3),[^c16914901] 16(3), 18(2), 18(4) or 18(5), paragraphs 4 and 5 of Schedule 4 or paragraph 5 of Schedule 5, shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding £200.

(2) A person guilty of an offence under Article 15(3) or 18(3)(b) or paragraph 6 of Part I of Schedule 5 shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale[^c16914921] or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months or to both.

(2A)[^key-03039f8817143da3add10719bb3a9392] A person guilty of an offence under Article 42(1) or (2) shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale.

(5) A person guilty of an offence under any provision of this Order other than those mentioned in paragraphs (1) to (2A)[^key-535094409566a47d4ec41571d6421a75] [^c22526261]. . . shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale.
