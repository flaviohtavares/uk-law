# Housing (Northern Ireland) Order 1981, section 90

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2019-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/1981/156
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 90. Land acquired compulsorily in re-development areas and housing action areas[^c17551581]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where the Department makes or has made a vesting order to which this paragraph applies, compensation shall be assessed in accordance with the provisions of the Land Compensation (Northern Ireland) Order 1982.

(2) Paragraph (1) applies to any vesting order made on or after 1st April 1990 to vest in the Executive land comprising a house, for the purpose of any of the functions of the Executive under Chapter III or IV of Part III.
