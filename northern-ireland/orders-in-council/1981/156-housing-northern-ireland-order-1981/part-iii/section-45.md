# Housing (Northern Ireland) Order 1981, section 45

## 45. Effect of Rent (Northern Ireland) Order 1978[^c17551101]

*Extent: N.I.*

Nothing in the Rent (Northern Ireland) Order 1978 shall be deemed to affect the provisions of this Chapter relating to obtaining possession of a house with respect to which a clearance or demolition order has been made, or to prevent possession being obtained of any house which is required for the purpose of enabling the Executive to exercise its powers under this Chapter.
