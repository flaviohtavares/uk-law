# Housing (Northern Ireland) Order 1981, section 162

>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 162. Regulations

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department may make regulations prescribing anything which is to be prescribed and providing for any matter in regard to which regulations may be made under this Order and generally for carrying this Order into effect, and, without prejudice to the generality of the foregoing, may make regulations prescribing the form of any notice, advertisement, statement or other document which is required or authorised to be used under or for the purposes of this Order.

(2) Regulations under this Order other than those made under Article 22A(3)[^c17551981] shall be subject to negative resolution.
