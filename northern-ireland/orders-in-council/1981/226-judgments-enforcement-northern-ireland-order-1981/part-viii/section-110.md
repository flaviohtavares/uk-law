## 110. Appeal from committal order

*Extent: N.I.*

Any person aggrieved by an order under Article 107(1) committing him to prison may appeal therefrom—

- (a) where the order was made by the High Court, to the Court of Appeal; or
- (b) where the order was made by a county court, to the High Court; or
- (c) where the order was made by a court of summary jurisdiction, to the [^key-496b2f80c6a3e3d6c01b9b6004b47ac5]... county court;

in accordance with rules of court, and the decision of the court shall, except as provided by Article 61 or 62 of the County Courts (Northern Ireland) Order 1980 [^m00027], be final and conclusive.
