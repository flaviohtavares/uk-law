## 11. General jurisdiction of Office

*Extent: N.I.*

Subject to the provisions of this Order, the jurisdiction relating to the enforcement of judgments which immediately before 15th February 1971 was exercised by any court, and which, by virtue of section 8 of the Judgments (Enforcement) Act (Northern Ireland) 1969 [^m00010], became vested in the Office, shall continue to be vested in the Office.
