## 117. Registration of pending actions, etc.

*Extent: N.I.*

Pending actions (including cases stated)—

- (a) affecting unregistered land, may be registered in the Registry of Deeds in accordance with section 3 of the Registration of Deeds Act (Northern Ireland) 1970 [^m00028];
- (b) affecting registered land, may be registered only in the Land Registry in accordance with the Land Registration Act (Northern Ireland) 1970 [^m00029].
