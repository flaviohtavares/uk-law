# Judgments Enforcement (Northern Ireland) Order 1981, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/1981/226

## 1. Title and commencement

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This Order may be cited as the Judgments Enforcement (Northern Ireland) Order 1981.

(2) Part VII shall come into operation on such day as the Lord Chancellor may by order appoint[^c16845971], and the remaining provisions of this Order shall come into operation on 19th March 1981.
