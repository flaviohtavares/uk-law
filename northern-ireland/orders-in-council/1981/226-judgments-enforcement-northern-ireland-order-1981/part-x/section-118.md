## 118. Obstruction and personation of enforcement officers

*Extent: N.I.*

Any person who—

- (a) obstructs or impedes an enforcement officer in the performance of his duties; or
- (b) impersonates an enforcement officer;

shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on summary conviction to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months or to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale[^c16847711], or to both such imprisonment and such fine.
