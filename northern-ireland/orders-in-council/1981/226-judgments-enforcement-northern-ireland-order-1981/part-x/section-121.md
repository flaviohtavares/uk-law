# Judgments Enforcement (Northern Ireland) Order 1981, section 121

## 121. Rescue of or interference with goods in custody of Office

*Extent: N.I.*

Any person who rescues or attempts to rescue or interferes with any goods which are or are deemed to be in the custody and possession of the Office shall, unless he satisfies the court that he had no reason to believe that the goods were in such custody and possession, be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale[^c16847771] or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months or to both.
