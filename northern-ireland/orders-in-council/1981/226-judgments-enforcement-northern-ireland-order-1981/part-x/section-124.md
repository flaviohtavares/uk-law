## 124. Conduct of prosecutions

*Extent: N.I.*

A civil servant in the Department of Justice who is[^c22020641] of a class designated by that Department[^c22020661], although not of counsel or a solicitor may, and if the Master so directs shall, prosecute or conduct before a court of summary jurisdiction any proceedings in respect of any offence under this Part.
