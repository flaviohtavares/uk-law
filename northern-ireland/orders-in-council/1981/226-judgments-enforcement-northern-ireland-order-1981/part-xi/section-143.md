# Judgments Enforcement (Northern Ireland) Order 1981, section 143

## 143. Provisions as to distress

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Notwithstanding anything contained in any other statutory provision, a person shall not distrain on any property for the enforcement of any recognizance or bond, or for rent (including rent-charge) in arrear, or for tithes.

(2) Paragraph (1) shall not affect the relationship of landlord and tenant save in so far as that paragraph restricts the remedy of distress.
