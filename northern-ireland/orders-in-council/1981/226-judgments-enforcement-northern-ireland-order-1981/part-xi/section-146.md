## 146. Application to the Crown

*Extent: N.I.*

Without prejudice to any Order in Council made under section 53 of the Crown Proceedings Act 1947 [^m00032] (extension of that Act to Northern Ireland), Articles 3, 73 to 79 and 97 to 105 and Schedule 1 to bind the Crown to the extent necessary to enable the enforcement of the duties imposed on chief officers by virtue of Articles 75 and 101.
