## 64. Stop order on money standing to credit of pending proceeding

*Extent: N.I.*

Where any money stands to the credit of any proceeding to which the debtor is a party in any court, the Office may pending the determination of that proceeding make a stop order under Article 62 prohibiting the payment of any such money to the debtor.
