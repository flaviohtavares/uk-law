## 54. Notice of eviction; removal of goods to place of safety; notice to Health and Social Care trust[^key-ec903b4ae82da4fc1d63cc1727fa9bc4]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) For the purpose of delivering vacant possession of land, the Office shall, upon giving to every person in occupation or possession of the land or any part thereof such notice as may be prescribed by rules, evict all persons in occupation or possession of the land and shall remove the goods of all such persons to a place of safety.

(2) Before proceeding to eviction from land which contains a building or structure used as a dwelling, the Office shall give to the Health and Social Care trust[^key-2b103b96c4d1dc9bd0e223b8ff69e9d6] for the area in which the land is situated notice of not less than seven days or such longer period as may be prescribed by rules.
