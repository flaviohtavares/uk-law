# Judgments Enforcement (Northern Ireland) Order 1981, section 33

## 33. Property exempt from seizure

*Extent: N.I.*

An order of seizure shall not be authority for the seizure of property of any of the following descriptions:—

- (a) such wearing apparel, furniture, bedding and household equipment of the debtor and his spouse or civil partner[^c16846521] as appear to the Office to be essential for the domestic purposes of the debtor, his spouse or civil partner and his dependants residing with him, or any of them;
- (b) the tools and implements of the debtor's trade to the value of £200[^c16846561] or of such greater amount as may be fixed by rules;
- (c) any property which has, at the date when the order takes effect, been seized under any other statutory provision;
- (d) any property held by the debtor in trust for or on behalf of any other person or body;
- (e) any property in the hands of a receiver appointed by a court, except with the leave of the court which appointed the receiver;
- (f) any property exempted from seizure by any other statutory provision.
