## 38. Power of entry under order of seizure

*Extent: N.I.*

During the continuance in force of an order of seizure any land occupied or used by the debtor or his spouse or civil partner[^c16846601] or any of his dependants or by any other person such as is mentioned in Article 36( *a*)(ii) may at any reasonable time be entered, by force if necessary, by an enforcement officer on production, if required, of his credentials for the purpose of identifying or of removing for sale or to a place of safety any property which is liable to be seized in pursuance of the order or of ascertaining whether or not any such property has been interfered with.
