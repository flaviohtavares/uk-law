# Judgments Enforcement (Northern Ireland) Order 1981, section 92

## 92. Charge in respect of costs and expenses of enforcement[^key-5388866300629fe7f9516b7115185de1]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where, pursuant to this Part, money or property is paid or delivered to the official receiver[^c16847301] or trustee, or, where the debtor is a company, to the liquidator, such costs and expenses of enforcement as may be approved by the Office shall be a first charge on the money or property so paid or delivered; and the official receiver or trustee or, as the case may be, the liquidator may realise the property or an adequate part thereof for the purpose of satisfying the charge.
