## 87B. Notice of administration order ceasing to be in force[^c22020621]

*Extent: N.I.*

In a case where an administration order ceases to be in force in accordance with Article 208F of the Order the Office shall send notice of the fact to every person to whom a debt scheduled to the administration order is owed.
