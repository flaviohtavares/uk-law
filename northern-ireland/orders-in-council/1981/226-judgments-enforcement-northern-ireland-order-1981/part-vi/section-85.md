## 85. Appropriation of money paid under administration order

*Extent: N.I.*

Money paid to the Office under an administration order shall be appropriated—

- (a) first in satisfaction of the expenses of the Office incurred in administering the order (but not in excess of 10p in the pound on the total amount of the scheduled debts); and
- (b) then (without prejudice to Article 90 (bankruptcy supervening)) in liquidation of the debts in accordance with the order and Article 83(4).
