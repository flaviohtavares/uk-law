# The Firearms (Northern Ireland) Order 2004, section 38A

## 38A. Recording of transactions in firearms by Chief Constable[^c-927b74056287d80c963d05e71aa53b87]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This Article applies where the Chief Constable receives a copy of a record of a transaction involving a firearm in accordance with Article 38(2)(a).

(2) The Chief Constable must keep a record which includes the following information—

- (a) a description of the firearm which includes the matters mentioned in paragraph 6(a) and (b)[^c-6380a348ceb7dfd08a22fceb080c5ac2] of Schedule 3;
- (b) the names and addresses of the seller or transferor and the purchaser or transferee , together with the date of the transaction[^c-35fc44aadee01b4ddacbd2ff764f941d] .

(3) The record mentioned in paragraph (2) must be kept—

- (a) on a computerised data filing system;
- (b) for a period of not less than 30 years after the destruction of the firearm in question[^c-95f74fa225d7533dca5dda13bcca1fcd] .
