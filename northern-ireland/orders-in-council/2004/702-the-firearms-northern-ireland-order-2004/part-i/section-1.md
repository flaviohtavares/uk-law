# The Firearms (Northern Ireland) Order 2004, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2021-07-14. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/2004/702
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 1. Title and commencement

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This Order may be cited as the Firearms (Northern Ireland) Order 2004.

(2) This Part and Articles 79 to 81 shall come into operation on the expiration of one month from the day on which this Order is made.

(3) The remaining provisions of this Order shall come into operation on such day or days as the Secretary of State may by order appoint[^c17102661].
