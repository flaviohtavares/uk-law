# The Firearms (Northern Ireland) Order 2004, section 71

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2021-07-14. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/2004/702
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 71. Power by order to exclude application of minimum sentence to those under 18

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department of Justice[^c21953871] may by order—

- (a) amend Article 70(1)(b) by substituting for the word “16” the word “18”, and
- (b) make such other provision as the Department of Justice[^c21953881] considers necessary or expedient in consequence of, or in connection with, the provision made by virtue of sub-paragraph (a).

(2) The provision that may be made by virtue of paragraph (1)(b) includes, in particular, provision amending or repealing any statutory provision (whenever passed or made).
