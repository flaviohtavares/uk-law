# The Firearms (Northern Ireland) Order 2004, section 73

>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 73. False statements

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Any person who knowingly or recklessly makes any statement which is false in any material particular for the purpose of procuring, whether for himself or another person, the grant or variation of any certificate under this Order shall be guilty of an offence.

(2) In this Article—

- “certificate” includes licence, permit or authorisation;
- “grant” includes issue;
- “variation of any certificate”, in relation to a firearms dealer's certificate, includes the addition of a place of business under Article 31.
