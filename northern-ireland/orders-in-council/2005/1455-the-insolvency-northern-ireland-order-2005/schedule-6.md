## SCHEDULE 6 — EFFECT OF BANKRUPTCY RESTRICTIONS ORDER AND UNDERTAKING

*Extent: N.I.*

Article 13(3).

### Disqualification for acting as receiver or manager

**1.**[^c20290961] or Article 41 of the 1989 Order (receiver and manager: disqualification) substitute—

> **41. Disqualification of bankrupt**
>
> (1) A person shall be guilty of an offence if he acts as receiver or manager of the property of a company on behalf of debenture holders while—
>
> - (a) he is an undischarged bankrupt, or
> - (b) a bankruptcy restrictions order is in force in respect of him.
>
> (2) This Article does not apply to a receiver or manager acting under an appointment made by the High Court.

.

### Bankruptcy offences after discharge

**2.**[^c20290971] After Article 321(3) of the 1989 Order (bankruptcy offences: general: no liability after discharge) insert—

> (3A) Paragraph (3) is without prejudice to any provision of this Chapter which applies to a person in respect of whom a bankruptcy restrictions order is in force.

.

**3.**[^c20290981] At the end of Article 331 of the 1989 Order (obtaining credit and doing business) insert—

> (5) This Article applies to the bankrupt after discharge while a bankruptcy restrictions order is in force in respect of him.
>
> (6) For the purposes of paragraph (1)(a) as it applies by virtue of paragraph (5), the relevant information about the status of the person in question is the information that a bankruptcy restrictions order is in force in respect of him.

.

### Disqualification for acting as insolvency practitioner

**4.**[^c-e983ed9dcc345b05a8f5043d80e2b786] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

### Prohibition against involvement in company

**5.**[^c20291001] For Article 15(1) of the Company Directors Disqualification (Northern Ireland) Order 2002 (NI 4) (bankrupt) substitute—

> (1) It is an offence for a person to act as director of a company or directly or indirectly to take part in or be concerned in the promotion, formation or management of a company, without the leave of the High Court, at a time when—
>
> - (a) he is an undischarged bankrupt, or
> - (b) a bankruptcy restrictions order is in force in respect of him.

.
