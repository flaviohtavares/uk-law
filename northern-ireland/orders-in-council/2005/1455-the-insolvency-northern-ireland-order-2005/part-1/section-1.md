# The Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 2005, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2020-06-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/2005/1455
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 1. Title and commencement

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This Order may be cited as the Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 2005.

(2) This Article and Article 2 shall come into operation on the expiration of 7 days from the day on which this Order is made.

(3)[^c20259431] The other provisions of this Order shall come into operation on such day or days as the Department may by order appoint.
