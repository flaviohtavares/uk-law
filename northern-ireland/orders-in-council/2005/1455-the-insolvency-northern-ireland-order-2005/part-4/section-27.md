# The Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 2005, section 27

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2020-06-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/2005/1455
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 27. Insolvency Account: interest[^c20272141]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The following shall be inserted after paragraph 16 of Schedule 5 to the 1989 Order (company insolvency rules: money)—

> **16A.**
>
> Provision enabling the Department to set the rate of interest paid on sums which have been paid into the Insolvency Account.

.

(2) The following shall be inserted after paragraph 19 of Schedule 6 to the 1989 Order (individual insolvency rules: money)—

> **19A.**
>
> Provision enabling the Department to set the rate of interest paid on sums which have been paid into the Insolvency Account.

.
