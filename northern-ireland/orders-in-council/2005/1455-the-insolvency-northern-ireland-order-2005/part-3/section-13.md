# The Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 2005, section 13

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2020-06-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/2005/1455
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 13. Post-discharge restrictions[^c20271661]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The following shall be inserted after Article 255 of the 1989 Order (bankruptcy: effect of discharge)—

> **255A. Post-discharge restrictions**
>
> Schedule 2A (bankruptcy restrictions order and bankruptcy restrictions undertaking) shall have effect.

.

(2) The Schedule 2A set out in Schedule 5 to this Order shall be inserted after Schedule 2 to the 1989 Order.

(3) The amendments set out in Schedule 6 (which specify the effect of a bankruptcy restrictions order or undertaking) shall have effect.
