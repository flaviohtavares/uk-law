## 22. Disqualification for office: the Assembly[^c20272041]

*Extent: N.I.*

The following shall be substituted for Article 370 of the 1989 Order (Northern Ireland Assembly disqualification)—

> **370. Assembly disqualification**
>
> (1) If the High Court makes a bankruptcy restrictions order or interim order in respect of a member of the Assembly, the Court shall notify the presiding officer of the Assembly.
>
> (2) If the Department accepts a bankruptcy restrictions undertaking made by a member of the Assembly, the Department shall notify the presiding officer of the Assembly.
>
> **370A. Irrelevance of privilege**
>
> A statutory provision about insolvency applies in relation to a member of the Assembly irrespective of any privilege of the Assembly.
