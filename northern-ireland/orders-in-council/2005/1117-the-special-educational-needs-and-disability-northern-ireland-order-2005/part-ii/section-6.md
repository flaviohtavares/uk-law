## 6. Compliance with orders of Tribunal

*Extent: N.I.*

In the 1996 Order after Article 23 insert—

> **23A. Compliance with orders**
>
> If the Tribunal makes an order, the board concerned must comply with the order before the end of the prescribed period beginning with the date on which it is made.
