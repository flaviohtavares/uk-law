## 200. Pension schemes to which this Part applies

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c17105821] The provisions of this Part apply to every occupational pension scheme other than—

- (a)[^c17105821] a money purchase scheme, or
- (b)[^c17105821] a prescribed scheme or a scheme of a prescribed description.

(2)[^c17105821] Regulations under paragraph (1)(b) may provide for exemptions from all or any of the provisions of this Part.
