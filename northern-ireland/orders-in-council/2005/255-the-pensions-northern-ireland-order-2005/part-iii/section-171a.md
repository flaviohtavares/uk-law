# The Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 2005, section 171A

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 171A). (not yet in force: Fraud compensation levy: interest for late payment)[^c21179451]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: Regulations may make provision for interest to be charged at the prescribed rate in the case of late payment of a fraud compensation levy.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: Interest is payable by or on behalf of the person or persons by or on behalf of whom the levy is payable.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: Interest payable by a person by virtue of this Article is a debt due from the person to the Board.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: Interest is recoverable by the Board or, if the Board so determines, by the Regulator on its behalf.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: Without prejudice to the generality of paragraph (1), regulations under this Article may include provision relating to—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: the collection and recovery of interest;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: the circumstances in which interest may be waived.)*
