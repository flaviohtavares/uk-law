# The Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 2005, section 231

## 231. Representative of non-UK scheme to be treated as trustee[^c-387b3e47b402b4c0f54cdf7313462cf5]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) In the case of an occupational pension scheme that has its main administration outside the United Kingdom[^c-edb9fd6d20b5917a213ed9dcb2fd2cb5], a reference in pensions legislation to the trustees, or a trustee, of the scheme includes a person who is for the time being appointed by the trustees of the scheme to be a representative of the scheme for the purposes of this Article.

(2) Paragraph (1) does not apply to a prescribed reference.

(3) In paragraph (1) “pensions legislation” means any statutory provision contained in or made by virtue of—

- (a) the Pension Schemes Act,
- (b) the 1995 Order,
- (c) Parts II to V of the 1999 Order, [^c-2ca5439611b2b670974bf29b81d3124e]...
- (d) this Order,[^c-ccde5431d36a6e17a06d48ae7715d37d] or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8c6ec7710fcf78a73bf1d254218b1b9c]
- (e) the Pension Schemes Act (Northern Ireland) 2016 , or[^c-13fef48431d9dde5c22a68ffe1ec398b]
- (f) Part 2 of the Pension Schemes Act 2021.
