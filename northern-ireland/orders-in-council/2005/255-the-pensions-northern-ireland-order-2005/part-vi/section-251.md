## 251. Deputy Pensions Ombudsman

*Extent: N.I.*

After section 141(3) of the Pension Schemes Act (the Pensions Ombudsman) add—

> (4) The Department may reimburse the Pensions Ombudsman in respect of any expenses incurred by a Deputy Pensions Ombudsman in the performance of any of the Pensions Ombudsman's functions.
>
> (5) In this section “Deputy Pensions Ombudsman” means a person appointed under section 145A of the Pension Schemes Act 1993.
