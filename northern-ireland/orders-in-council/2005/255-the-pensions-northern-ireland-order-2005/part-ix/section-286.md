## 286. Breach of regulations

*Extent: N.I.*

The following provisions of the 1995 Order apply to regulations under this Order as if they were regulations made by virtue of Part II of that Order—

- (a) Article 10(3) to (9) (power to impose civil penalties for contravention of regulations under Part II of that Order);
- (b) Article 113 (power to provide for contravention of regulations under that Part to be criminal offence).
