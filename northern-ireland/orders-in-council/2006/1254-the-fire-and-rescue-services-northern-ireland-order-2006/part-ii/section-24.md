# The Fire and Rescue Services (Northern Ireland) Order 2006, section 24

> Text as at 2019-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 24. Interpretation of Part II

*Extent: N.I.*

In this Part–

- “**emergency**” means an event or situation that causes or is likely to cause–
  - (a) a person to die, be seriously injured or become seriously ill, or
  - (b) serious harm to the environment (including the life and health of plants and animals and the fabric of buildings);
- “**premises**” includes any place and, in particular includes–
  - (a) any vehicle, ship or aircraft,
  - (b) any installation on land, any offshore installation and any other installation, and
  - (c) any tent or movable structure;
- “**road**” has the meaning given by Article 2(2) of the Road Traffic Regulation (Northern Ireland) Order 1997 (NI 2);
- “**ship**” includes every description of vessel used in navigation.
