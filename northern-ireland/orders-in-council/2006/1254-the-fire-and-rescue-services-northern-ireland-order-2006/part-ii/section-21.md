# The Fire and Rescue Services (Northern Ireland) Order 2006, section 21

> Text as at 2019-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 21. Exercise of powers under Articles 19 and 20: securing of premises[^c-6f2fe6160bb7ff06af0fed1d2001a581]

*Extent: N.I.*

An authorised officer who, by virtue of Article 19 or 20, enters premises–

- (a) which are unoccupied; or
- (b) from which the occupier is temporarily absent,

shall on departure ensure that all reasonable measures have been taken to leave the premises as effectively secured against unauthorised entry as he found them.
