# The Fire and Rescue Services (Northern Ireland) Order 2006, section 43

> Text as at 2019-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 43. Offence due to fault of other person

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where the commission by any person (“A”) of an offence under this Part is due to the act or default of some other person (“B”), B shall be guilty of the offence.

(2) B may be charged with and convicted of an offence by virtue of paragraph (1) whether or not proceedings are taken against A.
