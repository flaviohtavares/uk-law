# The Fire and Rescue Services (Northern Ireland) Order 2006, section 45

> Text as at 2019-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 45. Prohibition on charging employees

*Extent: N.I.*

No employer shall charge, or permit the charging of, any employee of his in respect of anything done or provided in pursuance of any of the fire safety duties.
