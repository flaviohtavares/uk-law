# The Private Tenancies (Northern Ireland) Order 2006, section 16

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 16. Application of this Part

*Extent: N.I.*

This Part applies in relation to any dwelling-house which is for the time being let under a private tenancy.
