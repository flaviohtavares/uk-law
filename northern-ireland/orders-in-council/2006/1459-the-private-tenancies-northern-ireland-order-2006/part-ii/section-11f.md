## 11F. Knowledge of disrepair[^c-856b36ea6a8654676ebd47db71066e4f]

*Extent: N.I.*

A landlord is not under a duty to carry out works by virtue of Article 11B unless the landlord has actual knowledge (whether because of notice given by the tenant or otherwise) of the need for those works
