> Text as at 2025-04-01.

## 11E. General qualification on landlord’s duties[^key-856b36ea6a8654676ebd47db71066e4f]

*Extent: N.I.*

The duties imposed on the landlord by Article 11B do not require the landlord to carry out works or repairs for which the tenant is liable by virtue of Article 11C.
