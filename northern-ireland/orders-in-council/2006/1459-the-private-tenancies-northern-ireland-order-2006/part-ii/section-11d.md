# The Private Tenancies (Northern Ireland) Order 2006, section 11D

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 11D. Landlord’s duties: private tenancy of part of a building[^c-856b36ea6a8654676ebd47db71066e4f]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where a dwelling-house let under a private tenancy consists of a part of a building, the duties imposed on the landlord by Article 11B may require the landlord to position appliances in a part or parts of the building not comprised in the tenancy.
