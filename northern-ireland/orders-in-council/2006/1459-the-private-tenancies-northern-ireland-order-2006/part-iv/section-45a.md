# The Private Tenancies (Northern Ireland) Order 2006, section 45A

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 45A. Power to amend Articles 42 to 45[^c22383801]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department may by regulations modify any provision of Articles 42 to 45 and Schedule 2.

(2) Regulations under this Article may make such consequential modifications of other provisions of this Chapter as appear to the Department to be necessary or appropriate.
