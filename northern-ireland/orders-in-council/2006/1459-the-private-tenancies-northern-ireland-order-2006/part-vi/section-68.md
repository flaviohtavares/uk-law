# The Private Tenancies (Northern Ireland) Order 2006, section 68

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 68. Prosecution of offences

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A person who is guilty of an offence under Article [^c22386051]. . . 4A(4), 4B(5), 4C(3),[^c-ee120410778f6b36815ddd11547c97a9] 5(7), 5ZA(3)[^c-18511e23ade22228b38cb0a5917e9458] , 5ZC(4)[^c-a9d6c4b2c450d9dc4af5ca77ecfd1367], 11B(4),[^c-4f7ea756a63d361d2624d927fa3546ba] 24(1), 33(5) or 50(2) or (3) or paragraph 1(2) of Schedule 2 shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 4 on the standard scale.

(2) A person who is guilty of an offence under Article 28, 65(3) or 66(3) shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale.

(3) Proceedings for any offence under this Order (including any offence created by regulations under Article 11G[^c-6d309ef9ee1843c02c053fb5c0674f95] or 11I[^c-4dd4474562f0a42137d6416dbe4e4d76]) may be instituted by the appropriate district council.
