# The Private Tenancies (Northern Ireland) Order 2006, section 71

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 71. Application to Crown property

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to Article 3(2), this Order shall apply in relation to premises in which there subsists, or at any material time subsisted, a Crown estate as it applies in relation to premises in which no such estate subsists or ever subsisted.

(2) In this Article “**Crown estate**” means an estate–

- (a) which belongs to the Crown in right of Her Majesty's Government in the United Kingdom or in Northern Ireland; or
- (b) which is held in trust for Her Majesty for the purposes of a government department.
