# The Private Tenancies (Northern Ireland) Order 2006, section 1

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 1. Title and commencement

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This Order may be cited as the Private Tenancies (Northern Ireland) Order 2006.

(2) This Article and Article 2 shall come into operation on the expiration of 7 days from the day on which this Order is made.

(3)[^c20246151] The other provisions of this Order shall come into operation on such day or days as the Department may by order appoint.
