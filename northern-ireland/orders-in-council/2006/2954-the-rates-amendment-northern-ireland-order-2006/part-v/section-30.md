# The Rates (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 2006, section 30

> Text as at 2015-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 30. Frivolous or vexatious applications

*Extent: N.I.*

In Article 49 of the principal Order (revision of valuation list, and alteration, by district valuer), after paragraph (5) there shall be added the following paragraph—

> (6) If the district valuer decides that an application served on him is frivolous or vexatious—
>
> - (a) he shall serve on the applicant notice of his decision; and
> - (b) sub-paragraphs (a) and (b) of paragraph (1) shall not have effect in relation to that application.
