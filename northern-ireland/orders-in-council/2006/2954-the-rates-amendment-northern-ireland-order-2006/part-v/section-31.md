# The Rates (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 2006, section 31

> Text as at 2015-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 31. Transfer of application from the district valuer to the Commissioner

*Extent: N.I.*

After Article 49 of the principal Order there shall be inserted the following Article—

> **49A. Transfer to the Commissioner of application under Article 49**
>
> (1) The district valuer may, with the consent of the applicant, transfer to the Commissioner an application served on the district valuer under Article 49.
>
> (2) Where an application is transferred under this Article, the functions of the district valuer in relation to the application served on him shall be exercisable by the Commissioner.
