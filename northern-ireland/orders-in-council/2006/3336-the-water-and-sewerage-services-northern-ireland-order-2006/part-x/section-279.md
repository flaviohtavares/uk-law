# The Water and Sewerage Services (Northern Ireland) Order 2006, section 279

## 279. Exercise of certain functions of the Department under this Part

*Extent: N.I.*

The Department shall not, except with the consent of DFP, exercise any power under any of the following provisions of this Part, namely—

- (a) Article 269(1);
- (b) Article 270(1);
- (c) Article 271(1) to (4);
- (d) Article 272;
- (e) Article 274;
- (f) Article 275(1);
- (g) Article 278(1) to (3) and (7);
- (h) paragraph 3 of Schedule 11.
