## 72. Water resources management plans: supplementary[^c17810111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Directions given under Article 70 , 70A[^key-ed8be8a9f3f4992548e56e7f3a230133] or 71 may be—

- (a) general directions applying to all water undertakers; or
- (b) directions applying only to one or more water undertakers specified in the directions,

and shall be given by an instrument in writing.

(2) It shall be the duty of each water undertaker to whom directions apply to comply with the directions.

(3) The duties of a water undertaker under Articles 70 , 70A[^key-ca289077b6fa6450a49b496ccfb78a6a] and 71 and under this Article shall be enforceable by the Department under Article 30.
