## 289. Matters to be taken into account in exercising certain functions under Part III of Water Order[^c20620961]

*Extent: N.I.*

In Article 40(1) of the Water Order (matters to be taken into account in exercising functions under Part III of that Order) after sub-paragraph (e) insert—

> - (ea) the duties imposed under Parts III to VI of the Water and Sewerage Services (Northern Ireland) Order 2006 on any relevant undertaker (within the meaning of that Order) which is likely to be affected by the exercise of the function in question;

.
