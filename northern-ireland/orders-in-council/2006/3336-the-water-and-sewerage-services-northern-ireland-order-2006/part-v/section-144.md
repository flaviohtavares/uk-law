# The Water and Sewerage Services (Northern Ireland) Order 2006, section 144

## 144. Interpretation of this Chapter[^c20597031]

*Extent: N.I.*

In this Chapter—

- (a) references to the taking of water include references to the collection, impounding, diversion or appropriation of water; and
- (b) references to an obligation or to a restriction include references to an obligation or, as the case may be, to a restriction which is imposed by or under any statutory provision (including this Order) or any agreement.
