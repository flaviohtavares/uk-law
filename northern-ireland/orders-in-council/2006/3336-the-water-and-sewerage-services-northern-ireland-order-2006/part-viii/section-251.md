## 251. Rights as to sewage[^c20605521]

*Extent: N.I.*

A sewerage undertaker—

- (a) shall be entitled to all sewage discharged into its sewers or waste water treatment works; and
- (b) may process, sell or otherwise dispose of such sewage.
