# The Gas (Northern Ireland) Order 1996, section 8A

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 8A). (not yet in force: Licences under Article 8(1)(a))[^c21345231]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: A licence under Article 8(1)(a) may authorise the holder to participate in the conveyance of gas in any area, or only in an area specified in the licence.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: The Authority may, with the consent of the holder of a licence under Article 8(1)(a), modify any term included in the licence in pursuance of paragraph (1).)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: Conditions included in a licence under Article 8(1)(a) by virtue of Article 10(1)(a) may (without prejudice to the generality of that sub-paragraph)—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: require the licence holder not to carry on an activity which he would otherwise be authorised by the licence to carry on; or)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: restrict where he may carry on an activity which he is authorised by the licence to carry on.)*
