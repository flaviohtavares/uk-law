## 8H. Designation for the purposes of the Gas Regulation[^c21259511][^key-eed9f6568786a2cfce86f3704d61b749]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This Article applies for any period during which a person—

- (a) holds a licence under Article 8(1)(a); and
- (b) is certified.

(2) If the person is certified on the first [^key-3e876621e13aea5079262ead3f39f73b]... certification ground in Article 8F, the Department shall designate that person as a gas transmission system operator for the purposes of the Gas Regulation[^key-028132b469334dd6872a76bf285cf04a].

(3) If the person is certified on the second certification ground in Article 8F, the Department shall designate the independent system operator nominated in the application for certification as a gas transmission system operator for the purposes of the Gas Regulation[^key-a66ef6d0443a6c560bf888ac9e519a88].

(4) As soon as is reasonably practicable after a person is designated by virtue of this Article, the Department must give notice of the designation to—

- (a) the person so designated;
- (b) the applicant (if different); and[^key-7c381f4455ecdac22f74b837b7283d9c]
- (c) the Authority; [^key-17509c600e7dfcf750344b20ef3f25de]...
- (d)[^key-17509c600e7dfcf750344b20ef3f25de] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) Where, in the case of a person certified on the first certification ground, there is a related conveyance licensee, conditions included pursuant to Article 10A(1) in the licences under Article 8(1)(a) of the certified person and the related conveyance licensee may make provision for determining which of them is to act as transmission system operator for the purposes of any provision of the Gas Regulation and any designation under this Article is without prejudice to any such provision.
