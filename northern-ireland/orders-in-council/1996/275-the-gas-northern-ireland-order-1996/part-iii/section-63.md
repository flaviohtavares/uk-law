## 63. Provisions as to requirements and prohibitions imposed under this Part

*Extent: N.I.*

Any power, exercisable by notice, conferred by this Part on the Department to impose a requirement or prohibition shall be construed as including a power, exercisable in the like manner, to vary or revoke the requirement or prohibition.
