# The Gas (Northern Ireland) Order 1996, section 70

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2025-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/1996/275

## 70. Directions

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) It shall be the duty of any person to whom a direction is given under this Order to give effect to that direction.

(2) Any power conferred by this Order to give a direction shall include power to vary or revoke the direction.

(3) Any direction given under this Order shall be in writing.
