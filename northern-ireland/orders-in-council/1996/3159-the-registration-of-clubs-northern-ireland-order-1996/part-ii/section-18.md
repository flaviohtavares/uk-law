## 18. Returns to Department

*Extent: N.I.*

The Department of Justice shall, in respect of each such period as the Department may specify, arrange for a statement to be sent to the Department showing—[^c-dfe619119434ca12c929abf0a4decaef]

- (a) the number of clubs having a current certificate of registration;
- (b) the number of voting and non-voting members of each club;

and containing such other information as the Department may require.
