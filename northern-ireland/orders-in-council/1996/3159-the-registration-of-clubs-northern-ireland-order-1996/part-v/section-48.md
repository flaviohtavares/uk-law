## 48. Appeals

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Without prejudice to Articles 61 and 65 of the [^m00022] County Courts (Northern Ireland) Order 1980, any party to the proceedings who is dissatisfied with a decision of a county court on an application made to it under this Order may appeal from that decision as if the decision had been made in exercise of the jurisdiction conferred by Part III of that Order and the appeal were brought under Article 60 of that Order.

(2) For the purposes of—

- (a) paragraph (1);
- (b) Articles 61 and 62 of the County Courts (Northern Ireland) Order 1980;
- (c) Articles 143 and 146 of the [^m00023] Magistrates' Courts (Northern Ireland) Order 1981,

a person who appears as an objector in any proceedings under this Order shall be deemed to be a party to the proceedings.
