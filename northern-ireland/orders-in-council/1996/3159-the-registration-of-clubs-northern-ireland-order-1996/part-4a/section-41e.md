## 41E. Application to court of summary jurisdiction by police[^key-4fad6ecf672d502c6ae9fd3d86f11d98]

*Extent: N.I.*

The responsible senior police officer must, as soon as reasonably practicable after a closure order comes into force in respect of the premises of a registered club, apply to a [^key-3d824ae785fc4fd312b4b8a16bc49ed8]... court of summary jurisdiction for it to consider the order and any extension of it.
