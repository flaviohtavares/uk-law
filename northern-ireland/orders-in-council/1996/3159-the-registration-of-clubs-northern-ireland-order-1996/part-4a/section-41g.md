## 41G. Appeal from decision of court of summary jurisdiction[^key-4fad6ecf672d502c6ae9fd3d86f11d98]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Any person aggrieved by a decision of a court of summary jurisdiction under Article 41F may, within 21 days from the day the decision is made, appeal against the decision to the county court in accordance with magistrates' court rules.

(2) Where an order is made under Article 41F(2)(b), the order shall not have effect from the time a person brings an appeal under paragraph (1) against the making of the order until the appeal is abandoned or determined.
