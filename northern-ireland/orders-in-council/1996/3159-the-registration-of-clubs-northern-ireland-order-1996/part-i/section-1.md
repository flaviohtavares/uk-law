# The Registration of Clubs (Northern Ireland) Order 1996, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2022-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/1996/3159

## 1. Title and commencement

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This Order may be cited as the Registration of Clubs (Northern Ireland) Order 1996.

(2) This Order shall come into operation on the expiration of 2 months from the day on which it is made.
