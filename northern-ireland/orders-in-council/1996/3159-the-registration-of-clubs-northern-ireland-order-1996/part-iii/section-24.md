# The Registration of Clubs (Northern Ireland) Order 1996, section 24

## 24. General permitted hours

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to the succeeding provisions of this Part, the permitted hours in a registered club other than a nightworkers club are the hours—

- (a) on week-days, other than [^c-3ea497182e351eac9e5ce2255de65989]... Christmas Day, from half past 11 in the morning to 11 in the evening; and
- (aa)[^c-87d19c04e09135f7cb84a80cb4f2bb72] on Sundays other than Christmas Day, from half past 12 in the afternoon to 11 in the evening; and
- (b)[^c-4d64e176bb4fe7cb27943fccc8040994] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) on [^c-839ffe45022e5150c7a62aa616af894d]... Christmas Day from half past 12 in the afternoon to 10 in the evening.

(2) The hours for the supply of intoxicating liquor in a nightworkers club shall be fixed by the county court on the application for the grant of registration and may be varied by a court of summary jurisdiction on renewal of the registration and the hours so fixed or varied shall—

- (a) not exceed the total of—
  - (i) on week days, other than [^c-c9cf4289498b4689ed905154560c66be]... Christmas Day, 11½;
  - (ii)[^c-8c328d7a62fd48fe4ab189edbd29349b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (iii) on Sunday and Christmas Day, 9½; and
- (b)[^c-e4d4bfd344c1b888de477cf6875f1b71] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) not include any period between 1 in the morning and 6 in the morning of any day.
