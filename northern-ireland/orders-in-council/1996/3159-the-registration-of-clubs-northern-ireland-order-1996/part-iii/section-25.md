## 25. Consumption after permitted hours of liquor supplied during those hours

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-c90eab695bbfc0cb56a7e0e3a217559a] Where intoxicating liquor is supplied in the premises of a registered club during the permitted hours, Article 22 shall not prohibit—

- (a) during the first 60 minutes[^c-c25c25e7b20d9151d1ac1d5e7a7b57d9] after the conclusion of the permitted hours, [^c-27719c0b4e0e6b08045855b9739494d2]...
- (b)[^c-27719c0b4e0e6b08045855b9739494d2] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

the consumption or permitting consumption of the liquor in those premises.

(2)[^c-f4c0dc7eabcee142d342597ed926653a] Regulations may modify paragraph (1)(a) so as to substitute “30 minutes” for “60 minutes”.

(3) Regulations may not be made under paragraph (2) unless a draft of the regulations has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, the Assembly.
