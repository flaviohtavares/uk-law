# The Registration of Clubs (Northern Ireland) Order 1996, section 23

## 23. Closing of bar outside permitted hours

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Any bar on the premises of a registered club shall be kept closed except during the permitted hours.

(2) If paragraph (1) is contravened the registered club shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on summary conviction to a tine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale.
