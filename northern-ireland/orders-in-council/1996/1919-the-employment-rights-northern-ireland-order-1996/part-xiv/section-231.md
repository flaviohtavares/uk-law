# The Employment Rights (Northern Ireland) Order 1996, section 231

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (68).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/1996/1919

## 231. Limit on amount payable under Article 227

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The total amount payable to an employee in respect of any debt to which this Part applies, where the amount of the debt is referable to a period of time, shall not exceed—

- (a)[^c20309041] £783[^key-ca1ee46ecafa96b3972e332d978973be] in respect of any one week, or
- (b)[^c20309041] in respect of a shorter period, an amount bearing the same proportion to £783 as that shorter period bears to a week.

*Para. (2) rep. by 1999 NI 9*
