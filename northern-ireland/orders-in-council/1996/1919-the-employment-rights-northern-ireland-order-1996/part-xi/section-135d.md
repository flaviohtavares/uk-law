# The Employment Rights (Northern Ireland) Order 1996, section 135D

## (not yet in force: 135D). (not yet in force: Pension enrolment)[^c21151491]

*Extent: N.I.*

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: An employee who is dismissed shall be regarded for the purposes of this Part as unfairly dismissed if the reason (or, if more than one, the principal reason) for the dismissal is that—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: any action was taken, or was proposed to be taken, with a view to enforcing in favour of the employee a requirement to which this Article applies;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: the employer was prosecuted for an offence under section 45 of the Pensions ()**(not yet in force: No.)* *(not yet in force: 2) Act (Northern Ireland) 2008 as a result of action taken for the purposes of enforcing in favour of the employee a requirement to which this Article applies; or)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: any provision of Chapter 1 of that Part of that Act applies to the employee, or will or might apply.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: It is immaterial for the purposes of sub-paragraph (a) or (b) of paragraph (1)—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: whether or not the requirement applies in favour of the employee, or)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: whether or not the requirement has been contravened,)*

*(not yet in force: but, for that paragraph to apply, the claim that the requirement applies and, if applicable, the claim that it has been contravened must be made in good faith.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: This Article applies to any requirement imposed on the employer by or under any provision of Chapter 1 of Part 1 of the Pensions ()**(not yet in force: No.)* *(not yet in force: 2) Act (Northern Ireland) 2008.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: In this Article references to enforcing a requirement include references to securing its benefit in any way.)*
