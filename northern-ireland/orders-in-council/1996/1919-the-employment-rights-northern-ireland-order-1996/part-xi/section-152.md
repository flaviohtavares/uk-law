## 152. General

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) [^c17626491]. . . Where a tribunal makes an award of compensation for unfair dismissal under Article 146(4) or 151(3)(a) the award shall consist of—

- (a) a basic award (calculated in accordance with Articles 153 to 156,160 and 161), and
- (b) a compensatory award (calculated in accordance with Articles 157, 158, 158A[^c20515041], 160 and 161[^c17626521]. . . ).

- *Paras. (2), (3) rep. by 1999 NI 9*
- *Para. (4) rep. by 2003 NI 15*
