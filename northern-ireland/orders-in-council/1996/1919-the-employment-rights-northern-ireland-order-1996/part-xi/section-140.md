## 140. Qualifying period of employment

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Article 126 does not apply to the dismissal of an employee unless he has been continuously employed for a period of not less than one year[^c17625121] ending with the effective date of termination.

(2) If an employee is dismissed by reason of any such requirement or recommendation as is referred to in Article 96(2), paragraph (1) has effect in relation to that dismissal as if for the words one year there were substituted the words “one month”.

(3) Paragraph (1) does not apply if—
