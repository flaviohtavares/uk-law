# The Employment Rights (Northern Ireland) Order 1996, section 134A

## 134A. Protected disclosure[^c20490451][^c20508551]

*Extent: N.I.*

An employee who is dismissed shall be reguarded for the purposes of this Part as unfairly dismissed if the reason (or, if more than one, the principal reason) for the dismissal is that the employee made a protected disclosure.
