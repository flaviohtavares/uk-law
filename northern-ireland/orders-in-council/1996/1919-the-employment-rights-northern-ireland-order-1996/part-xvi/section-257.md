## 257. Repeals and revocations

*Extent: N.I.*

*Para. (1)—Repeals*

(2) The following statutory provisions are revoked—

- (a) the [^m00041] Insolvency of Employers (Excluded Classes) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1983;
- (b) the [^m00042] Employment Protection (Part-time Employees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995;
- (c) the [^m00043] Collective Redundancies and Transfer of Undertakings (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995.
