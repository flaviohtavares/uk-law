# The Employment Rights (Northern Ireland) Order 1996, section 251

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (68).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/1996/1919
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 251. Orders and regulations

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to[^c17628941] paragraphs (1A), (1B) and (7)[^c-255461dc00703a06f7eaa174c873a0e0] , all regulations under this Order shall be subject to negative resolution.

(1A) Regulations under Article 70C, 103, 104, 105, 107A, 107B,[^c17628971] 107E, 107F(16), 107G, 107I(16), 107J(1) or (2),[^c-e6cff4a83d850ccde4ecf40e35839741] 108,[^c20310071] 112A, 112B, 112BAA,[^c-dfa6331ab2a78d520020ba5e054c30ee] 112EA,[^c-8350ff3c929907c33c6891e4271ea504] 112G , or 131 shall—

- (a) be laid before the Assembly as soon as may be after they are made;
- (b) come into operation on such date as may be specified therein; and
- (c) cease to have effect on the expiration of a period of six months from the date on which they came into operation unless, before the expiration of that period, they are approved by a resolution of the Assembly.

(1B)[^c-611287c171a30fb3acfeb095e5246d61] Subject to paragraph (1C), regulations under Article 59A or 67FA , and the first regulations under Article 112EF,[^c-e2fc754c40ad98582f3f22faea6423e5] shall not be made unless a draft of the regulations has been laid before, and approved by resolution of, the Assembly.

(1C) Paragraph (1B) does not apply to regulations under Article 67FA that contain only the provision mentioned in Article 67FA(2), (3) or (4).

(2) An order to which this paragraph applies shall—

- (a) be laid before the Assembly as soon as may be after it is made;
- (b) come into operation on such date as may be specified therein; and
- (c) cease to have effect on the expiration of a period of six months from the date on which it came into operation unless, before the expiration of that period, it is approved by a resolution of the Assembly.

(3) Paragraph (2) applies to—

- (a) an order under Article[^c17628941]. . . 63(7), 92A(11)[^c17629031],[^c17628941]. . . [^c17628941]. . . or 237(3);
- (b) an order under Article 250, other than an order under paragraph (1)(b) of that Article which specifies only provisions contained in Part XII or an order under paragraph (1)(c) of that Article which falls within paragraph (5A)[^c-68c1198d1a1de3ca6ece567fb10d2f79].

(4) Subject to paragraphs (5) and (5A)[^c-f8ce68468aa10994714bb9c685a882aa] all other orders made by the Department under this Order shall be subject to negative resolution.

(5) Paragraph (4) does not apply to—

- (a) an order under Article 67; or
- (b) an order under Part II of Schedule 2.
- (c)[^c-6bb016156e78b6880d8406d5a87d10dc] an order under Article 85ZS(6) or 107AB(4);

(5A)[^c-6b08a14b7a5ee794a86b243bcfd626fe] An order to which this paragraph applies[^c-2a1e8f9adfee56e34e75fbb260df9bbf] shall not be made unless a draft has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, the Assembly.

(5B)[^c-fb4ae59e9b6a1fdd8d64c4c18e74a4f8] Paragraph (5A) applies to—

- (a) an order under Article 67K(4), 85ZS(6) or 107AB(4);
- (b) an order under Article 250(1)(c) which varies or excludes the operation of Article 124(3) or 140(1).

(6) Regulations and orders under this Order may contain incidental, supplementary and transitional provisions.

(7)[^c-668193c18cae5d820be3290291fdec10] Regulations to which this paragraph applies shall not be subject to negative resolution but shall be subject to the confirmatory procedure.

(8) Paragraph (7) applies to regulations under this Order which—

- (a) but for that paragraph would be subject to negative resolution; and
- (b) are contained in a statutory rule which includes regulations subject to the confirmatory procedure.

(9) In this Article “**the confirmatory procedure**” means the procedure described in paragraph (1A).
