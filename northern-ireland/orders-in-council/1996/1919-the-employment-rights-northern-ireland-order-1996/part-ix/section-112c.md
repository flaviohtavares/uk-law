## 112C. Rights during and after paternity leave[^c20289071][^c22340171]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Regulations under Article 112A [^key-bc03e89febf92cfe90cc56fe1cf12643]... shall provide—

- (a) that an employee who is absent on leave under that Article is entitled, for such purposes and to such extent as may be specified[^key-15a101e052092b2f013f8f18f37d6220] , to the benefit of the terms and conditions of employment which would have applied if he had not been absent;
- (b) that an employee who is absent on leave under that Article is bound, for such purposes and to such extent as may be specified[^key-8895ce7dfc704a98975bf05b88dd8924] , by obligations arising under those terms and conditions (except in so far as they are inconsistent with paragraph (1) of that Article), and
- (c) that an employee who is absent, on leave under that Article is entitled to return from leave to a job of a kind that may be specified[^key-831eb110e7b7d74ee72cb0e1298aa890] , subject to Article 112D(1).

(2) The reference in paragraph (1)(c) to absence on leave under Article 112A [^key-d934628aee25c4c19476ba3a3176c5f2]... includes, where appropriate, a reference to a continuous period of absence attributable partly to leave under that Article and partly to any one or more of the following—

- (za)[^key-914ccce06ddc42224bd9ded1491069f3] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (a) maternity leave,
- (b) adoption leave, [^key-836cf4a2c4ac6bf1897c2019fec394fc]...
- (ba)[^key-fef5aa9a4070db631729225a54ec658b] shared parental leave
- (bb)[^key-9e686fb31c4856e2b3553b0d2df95cf0] parental bereavement leave,, and
- (c) parental leave.

(3) Paragraph (1) shall apply to regulations under Article 112B or 112BAA as it applies to regulations under Article 112A[^key-6bf6b26cfb5c3d2e0d5d553191d3eb93][^c20291271] .

(4) In the application of paragraph (1)(c) to regulations under Article l12B or[^c20291331] 112BAA[^key-205d2f7530c13d7a171a589d96dfab1e] , the reference to absence on leave under that Article includes, where appropriate, a reference to a continuous period of absence attributable partly to leave under that Article and partly to anyone or more of the following—

- (za)[^key-1f3beea2ffcb284d2242510c37959806] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (a) maternity leave,
- (b) adoption leave,
- (ba)[^key-434da4168f655a5afc148be200a45bb4] shared parental leave,
- (bb)[^key-635f54fdda50e159799ff2d92a18209f] parental bereavement leave,
- (c) parental leave, and
- (d) leave under Article 112A [^key-7a054af10c2e24b9b53d6d96bc3620f5]... .

(5) In paragraph (1)(a), “terms and conditions of employment”

- (a) includes matters connected with an employee's employment whether or not they arise under his contract of employment, but
- (b) does not include terms and conditions about remuneration.

(6) Regulations under Article 112A or 112B[^key-8cdbe134dce64ca9edaa5fec4c9a0f39] may specify matters which are, or are not, to be treated as remuneration for the purposes of this Article.

(7) Regulations under Article 112A or 112B[^key-94497f6186c3cce6516ef05142b517ec] may make provision, in relation to the right to return mentioned in paragraph (1)(c), about—

- (a) seniority, pension rights and similar rights;
- (b) terms and conditions of employment on return.
