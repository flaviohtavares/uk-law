# The Employment Rights (Northern Ireland) Order 1996, section 72A

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 72A. Application to police of Article 68 and related provisions[^c20272051]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of Article 68, and of Articles 71 and 72 so far as relating to that Article, the holding, otherwise than under a contract of employment, of the office of constable -shall be treated as employment by the relevant officer under a contract of employment.

(2)[^c20272071] In this Article “**the relevant officer**”, in relation to a person holding the office of constable, means the person who under Article 47A of the Health and Safety at Work (Northern Ireland) Order 1978 is to be treated as his employer for the purposes of Part II of that Order.
