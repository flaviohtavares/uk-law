# The Employment Rights (Northern Ireland) Order 1996, section 194

## 194. Public offices etc.

*Extent: N.I.*

A person does not have any right to a redundancy payment in respect of any employment which—

- (a) is employment in a public office within the meaning of section 39 of the [^m00026] Superannuation Act 1965 or section 39 of the [^m00027] Superannuation (Northern Ireland) Act 1967, or
- (b) is treated for the purposes of pensions and other superannuation benefits as service in the civil service of Northern Ireland or the civil service of the United Kingdom.
