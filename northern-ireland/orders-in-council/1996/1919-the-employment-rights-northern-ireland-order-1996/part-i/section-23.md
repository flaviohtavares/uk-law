# The Employment Rights (Northern Ireland) Order 1996, section 23

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 23. Maximum amount[^c20262581][^c20262591]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c20262711][^c20262771] For the purpose of calculating—

- (za)[^c17621271] an award of compensation under Article 112I(1)(b),
- (a) a basic award of compensation for unfair dismissal,
- (b) an additional award of compensation for unfair dismissal,
- (ba)[^c17621291] an award under Article 146(5); or
- (c) a redundancy payment,

the amount of a week's pay shall not exceed £783[^key-7940118541453397c8a89a2510d344cd].

*Paras. (2)‐(4) rep. by 1999 NI 9*
