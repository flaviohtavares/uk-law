# The Road Traffic Offenders (Northern Ireland) Order 1996, section 38D

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 38D). (not yet in force: Programmes in Great Britain)[^c2178316]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: The Department may enter into arrangements with persons in Great Britain who provide programmes which are approved programmes within the meaning of section 34D(8) of the Road Traffic Offenders Act 1988 for the purpose of treating those programmes as approved programmes within the meaning of Articles 38A to 38E of this Order, and in this Article such programmes in respect of which such arrangements have been entered into are referred to as “)****(not yet in force: recognised programmes)****(not yet in force: ”)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: Such arrangements may include provision for any matters for which provision is made in Articles 38A to 38E in relation to approved programmes.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: A court—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: may treat recognised programmes as approved programmes for the purposes of Article 38A,)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: may treat any certificates received from programme providers of recognised programmes as certificates received from programme providers of approved programmes for the purposes of Article 38B.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: Where a court has made an order in respect of a person under Article 38A, that person may apply to the court to vary the order by substituting a recognised programme for the programme specified in the order, and if the court grants that application, it shall vary the order accordingly.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: The Department may by regulations make such further provision in respect of recognised programmes as it considers necessary or expedient)*
