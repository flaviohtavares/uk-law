# The Road Traffic Offenders (Northern Ireland) Order 1996, section 32E

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 32E). (not yet in force: Provisions supplementary to Articles 32A to 32D)[^c2079629]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: The Department may issue guidance to course providers, or to any category of course provider, as to the conduct of courses approved for the purposes of Article 32; and–)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: course providers shall have regard to any guidance given to them under this paragraph, and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: in determining for the purposes of Article 32B whether any instructions or requirements of a course provider were reasonable, a court shall have regard to any guidance given to him under this paragraph.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: The Department may by regulations make provision–)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: amending Article 32A(1)(c) by substituting for the lower number of penalty points for the time being specified there a different number of penalty points, or)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: amending Article 32A(6)(a) by substituting for the period for the time being specified there a different period.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: In Articles 32A to 32C and this Article–)*

- *(not yet in force: “)****(not yet in force: course provider)****(not yet in force: ”, in relation to a course, means the person by whom it is, or is to be, provided;)*
- *(not yet in force: “)****(not yet in force: probationary period)****(not yet in force: ” has the meaning given in Article 2 of the Road Traffic (New Drivers) (Northern Ireland) Order 1998;)*
- *(not yet in force: “)****(not yet in force: proper officer)****(not yet in force: ” means–)*
  - (a) *(not yet in force: in relation to a magistrates' court, the clerk of petty sessions for the petty sessions district for which the court acts, and)*
  - (b) *(not yet in force: otherwise, the chief clerk;)*
- *(not yet in force: “)****(not yet in force: relevant local court)****(not yet in force: ”, in relation to an order made under Article 32A in the case of an offender, means a magistrates' court acting for the petty sessions district in which the offender resides;)*
- *(not yet in force: “)****(not yet in force: rules of court)****(not yet in force: ” means–)*
  - (a) *(not yet in force: in relation to an application to a magistrates' court, magistrates' court rules, and)*
  - (b) *(not yet in force: in relation to an application to the Crown Court, Crown Court rules;)*
- *(not yet in force: “)****(not yet in force: supervising court)****(not yet in force: ”, in relation to an order under Article 32A means–)*
  - (a) *(not yet in force: if the Crown Court made the order, the Crown Court, and)*
  - (b) *(not yet in force: otherwise a magistrates' court acting in the same petty sessions district as the court which made the order.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: Orders or regulations made by the Department under Article 32A to 32D or this Article may include such incidental or supplementary provision as appears to the Department to be necessary or expedient.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: Orders made under Article 32A(3) and regulations made under this Article shall be subject to affirmative resolution.)*

((not yet in force: 6)) *(not yet in force: Regulations made under Article 32B, 32C or 32D shall be subject to negative resolution.)*
