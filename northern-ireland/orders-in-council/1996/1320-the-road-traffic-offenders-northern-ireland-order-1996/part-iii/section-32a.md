# The Road Traffic Offenders (Northern Ireland) Order 1996, section 32A

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 32A). (not yet in force: Reduced penalty points for attendance on course)[^c2079629]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: This Article applies where–)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: a person is convicted of a specified offence by or before a court,)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: penalty points are to be attributed to the offence and the court does not order him to be disqualified, and)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: at least 7 but no more than 11 penalty points are to be taken into account on the occasion of the conviction.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: In this Article “)****(not yet in force: specified offence)****(not yet in force: ” means–)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: an offence under Article 20(4) of the Roads (Northern Ireland) Order 1993 (use of special road contrary to regulations),)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: an offence under Article 12 of the Order of 1995 (careless, and inconsiderate, driving),)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: an offence under Article 50 of the Order of 1995 (contravention of traffic signs),)*
- ((not yet in force: d)) *(not yet in force: an offence under Article 43(1) of the Road Traffic Regulation (Northern Ireland) Order 1997 (contravening speed limit), or)*
- ((not yet in force: e)) *(not yet in force: an offence under Article 7 of the Road Traffic Regulation (Northern Ireland) Order 1997 committed by contravening a temporary speed restriction under paragraph (3)(b) of that Article.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: The Department may by order amend paragraph (2) by making additions to or deletions from the list of offences for the time being set out there.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: Where this Article applies, the court may make an order that 3 of the penalty points attributed to the offence (or all of them if 3 or fewer are so attributed) shall not be taken into account under Article 31(1)(b) on the occasion of any conviction of an offence after the end of the period of 12 months beginning with the date of the order if, by the relevant date, the offender completes an approved course specified in the order.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: In paragraph (4)–)*

- *(not yet in force: “)****(not yet in force: an approved course)****(not yet in force: ” means a course approved by the Department for the purposes of this Article in relation to the description of offence of which the offender is convicted; and)*
- *(not yet in force: “)****(not yet in force: the relevant date)****(not yet in force: ” means such date, no later than 10 months after the day on which the order is made, as is specified in the order.)*

((not yet in force: 6)) *(not yet in force: A court shall not make an order under this Article in the case of an offender convicted of an offence if–)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: the offender has, during the period of 3 years ending with the date on which the offence was committed, committed a specified offence and successfully completed an approved course pursuant to an order made under this Article or Article 36 on conviction of that offence, or)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: the offence was committed during his probationary period.)*

((not yet in force: 7)) *(not yet in force: A court shall not make an order under this Article in the case of an offender unless–)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: it is satisfied that a place on the course specified in the order will be available for the offender,)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: the offender appears to the court to be of or over the age of 17,)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: the court has informed the offender (orally or in writing and in ordinary language) of the effect of the order and of the amount of the fees which he is required to pay for the course and when he must pay them, and)*
- ((not yet in force: d)) *(not yet in force: the offender agrees that the order should be made.)*
