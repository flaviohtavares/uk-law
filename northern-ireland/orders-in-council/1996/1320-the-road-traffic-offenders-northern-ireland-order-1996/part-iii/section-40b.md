# The Road Traffic Offenders (Northern Ireland) Order 1996, section 40B

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 40B). (not yet in force: Effect of custodial sentence in other cases)[^c2178341]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: This Article applies where a person is convicted of an offence for which a court proposes to order the person to be disqualified under Article 35 or 40 and—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: the court proposes to impose on the person a custodial sentence (other than a suspended sentence) for another offence, or)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: at the time of sentencing for the offence, a custodial sentence imposed on the person on an earlier occasion has not expired.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: In determining the period for which the person is to be disqualified under Article 35 or 40, the court must have regard to the consideration in paragraph (3) if and to the extent that it is appropriate to do so.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: The consideration is the diminished effect of disqualification as a distinct punishment if the person who is disqualified is also detained in pursuance of a custodial sentence.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: If the court proposes to order the person to be disqualified under Article 35 or 40 and to impose a custodial sentence for the same offence, the court may not in relation to that disqualification take that custodial sentence into account for the purposes of paragraph (2).)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: In this Article “)****(not yet in force: custodial sentence)****(not yet in force: ” and “)****(not yet in force: suspended sentence)****(not yet in force: ” have the same meaning as in Article 40A.)*
