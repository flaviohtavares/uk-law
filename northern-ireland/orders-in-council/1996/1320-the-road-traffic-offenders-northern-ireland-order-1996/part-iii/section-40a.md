# The Road Traffic Offenders (Northern Ireland) Order 1996, section 40A

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 40A). (not yet in force: Extension of disqualification where custodial sentence also imposed)[^c2178341]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: This Article applies where a person is convicted of an offence for which the court—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: imposes a custodial sentence, and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: orders the person to be disqualified under Article 35 or 40.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: The order under Article 35 or 40 must provide for the person to be disqualified for the appropriate extension period, in addition to the discretionary disqualification period.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: The discretionary disqualification period is the period for which, in the absence of this Article, the court would have disqualified the person under Article 35 or 40.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: The appropriate extension period is—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: where a court imposes a sentence under Article 45(2) of the Criminal Justice (Children) (Northern Ireland) Order 1998 ()**(not yet in force: S.I.)* *(not yet in force: 1998/1504 (N.I. 9)) (punishment of grave crimes: indeterminate sentences), a period equal to the period specified in the sentence under Article 45(2) of that Order less any relevant discount;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: where an order under Article 5(1) of the Life Sentences (Northern Ireland) Order 2001 ()**(not yet in force: S.I.)* *(not yet in force: 2001/2564 (N.I. 2)) (determination of tariffs) is made in relation to the custodial sentence, a period equal to the part of the sentence specified in that order less any relevant discount;)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: where Article 8(1) of the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2008 ()**(not yet in force: S.I.)* *(not yet in force: 2008/1216 (N.I. 1)) (sentence for a determinate term) applies in relation to the custodial sentence, a period equal to the custodial period specified pursuant to Article 8(2) of that Order less any relevant discount;)*
- ((not yet in force: d)) *(not yet in force: where a court imposes a sentence under Article 13(3) of the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2008 (indeterminate custodial sentences for serious offences), a period equal to the period specified pursuant to Article 13(3)(b) of that Order less any relevant discount;)*
- ((not yet in force: e)) *(not yet in force: where Article 14(3) of the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2008 (extended custodial sentences for certain offences where the offender is aged over 21) applies in relation to the custodial sentence, a period equal to half of the term imposed pursuant to Article 14(3)(a) of that Order calculated after that term has been reduced by any relevant discount;)*
- ((not yet in force: f)) *(not yet in force: where Article 14(5) of the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2008 (extended custodial sentences for certain offences where the offender is aged under 21) applies in relation to the custodial sentence, a period equal to half of the term imposed pursuant to Article 14(5)(a) of that Order calculated after that term has been reduced by any relevant discount;)*
- ((not yet in force: g)) *(not yet in force: in any other case, a period equal to half the custodial sentence imposed calculated after that sentence has been reduced by any relevant discount.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: If a period determined under paragraph (4) includes a fraction of a day, that period is to be rounded up to the nearest number of whole days.)*

((not yet in force: 6)) *(not yet in force: The “relevant discount” is the number of days by which the custodial sentence is treated as reduced by virtue of section 26(2) of the Treatment of Offenders Act (Northern Ireland) 1968 (c. 29) (periods in custody before sentence passed)* *(not yet in force: etc)**(not yet in force: ).)*

((not yet in force: 7)) *(not yet in force: This Article does not apply where—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: the custodial sentence was a suspended sentence, or)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: the court has made an order under Article 5(3) of the Life Sentences (Northern Ireland) Order 2001 (life sentence: no early release) in relation to the custodial sentence.)*

((not yet in force: 8)) *(not yet in force: Paragraph (9) applies where an amending order provides that the proportion of a prisoner's sentence referred to in Article 18(2)(b) of the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2008 (duty to release prisoners serving extended custodial sentences) is to be read as a reference to another proportion (“the new proportion”).)*

((not yet in force: 9)) *(not yet in force: The Secretary of State may by order provide that the proportion specified in paragraph (4)(e) and (f) of this Article is to be read, in the case of a custodial sentence to which the amending order applies, as a reference to the new proportion.)*

((not yet in force: 10)) *(not yet in force: An order under paragraph (9) is subject to annulment by a resolution of either House of Parliament in like manner as a statutory instrument and section 5 of the Statutory Instruments Act 1946 (instruments subject to annulment by resolution of either House of Parliament) applies accordingly.)*

((not yet in force: 11)) *(not yet in force: In this Article—)*

- *(not yet in force: “)****(not yet in force: amending order)****(not yet in force: ” means an order under Article 18(9) of the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2008 (alteration by order of relevant part of sentence);)*
- *(not yet in force: “)****(not yet in force: custodial sentence)****(not yet in force: ” has the meaning given by Article 4 of the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2008;)*
- *(not yet in force: “)****(not yet in force: suspended sentence)****(not yet in force: ” means a suspended sentence or order for detention under section 18 of the Treatment of Offenders Act (Northern Ireland) 1968.)*
