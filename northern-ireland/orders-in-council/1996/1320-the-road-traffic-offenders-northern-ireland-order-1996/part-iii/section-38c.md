# The Road Traffic Offenders (Northern Ireland) Order 1996, section 38C

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 38C). (not yet in force: Approval of programmes)[^c2178316]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: If an application is made to the Department for the approval of a programme for the purposes of Article 38A, the Department must decide whether to grant or refuse the application.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: In reaching that decision the Department must have regard to—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: the nature of the programme, and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: whether the programme provider is an appropriate person to provide the programme and administer its provision efficiently and effectively,)*

*(not yet in force: and may take into account any recommendations made by any persons appointed to consider the application.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: A programme may be approved subject to conditions specified by the Department.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: An approval of a programme is for the period specified by the Department (which must not exceed 7 years), subject to withdrawal of approval.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: Regulations made by the Department may make provision in relation to the approval of programmes and may, in particular, include provision—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: in relation to the making of applications for approval,)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: for the payment in respect of applications for approval, or of approvals, (or of both) of fees of such amounts as are prescribed by the regulations,)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: specifying the maximum fees that a person may be required to pay for a programme and by when they are to be paid,)*
- ((not yet in force: d)) *(not yet in force: for the monitoring of programmes and programme providers,)*
- ((not yet in force: e)) *(not yet in force: in relation to withdrawing approval,)*
- ((not yet in force: f)) *(not yet in force: for an appeal to lie to a court of summary jurisdiction against a refusal of an application for approval, the imposition of conditions on the grant of such an application or the withdrawal of approval, and)*
- ((not yet in force: g)) *(not yet in force: authorising the Department to make available (with or without charge) information about programmes and programme providers.)*
