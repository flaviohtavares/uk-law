# The Road Traffic Offenders (Northern Ireland) Order 1996, section 38E

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 38E). (not yet in force: Provisions supplementary to Articles 38A to 38D)[^c2178316]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: The Department may issue guidance to programme providers, or to any category of programme provider, as to the conduct of programmes approved for the purposes of Article 38A; and—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: programme providers shall have regard to any guidance given to them under this paragraph, and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: in determining for the purposes of Article 38B whether any instructions or requirements of a programme provider were reasonable, a court shall have regard to any guidance given to him under this paragraph.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: The Department may by regulations make provision—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: amending Article 38A(1)(b) by substituting for the period for the time being specified there a different period,)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: amending Article 38A(1)(d) by substituting for the period for the time being specified there a different period, or)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: amending Article 38A(4) by substituting for the period for the time being specified there a different period, or by substituting for the fraction of the unreduced period for the time being specified there a different fraction of that period, (or by doing both).)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: In Articles 38A to 38D and this Article—)*

- *(not yet in force: “)****(not yet in force: contravention)****(not yet in force: ” includes failure to comply;)*
- *(not yet in force: “)****(not yet in force: programme provider)****(not yet in force: ”, in relation to an alcohol ignition interlock programme, means the person by whom it is, or is to be, provided;)*
- *(not yet in force: “)****(not yet in force: proper officer)****(not yet in force: ” means—)*
  - (a) *(not yet in force: in relation to a magistrates' court, the clerk of petty sessions for the petty sessions district for which the court acts, an)*
  - (b) *(not yet in force: otherwise, the chief clerk;)*
- *(not yet in force: “)****(not yet in force: relevant local court)****(not yet in force: ”, in relation to an alcohol ignition interlock programme order in the case of an offender, means a magistrates' court acting for the petty sessions district in which the offender resides,)*
- *(not yet in force: “)****(not yet in force: rules of court)****(not yet in force: ” means—)*
  - (a) *(not yet in force: in relation to an application to a magistrates' court, magistrates' court rules; and)*
  - (b) *(not yet in force: in relation to an application to the Crown Court, Crown Court rules;)*
- *(not yet in force: “)****(not yet in force: supervising court)****(not yet in force: ”, in relation to an alcohol ignition interlock programme order, means—)*
  - (a) *(not yet in force: if the Crown Court made the order, the Crown Court; and)*
  - (b) *(not yet in force: otherwise a magistrates' court acting for the same petty sessions district as the court which made the order.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: Regulations under Article 38A, 38B, 38C or 38D or this Article may include such incidental or supplementary provision as appears to the Department to be necessary or expedient.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: Regulations under Article 38A, 38B, 38C or 38D shall be subject to negative resolution.)*

((not yet in force: 6)) *(not yet in force: No regulations shall be made under this Article unless a draft of the regulations has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, the Assembly.)*
