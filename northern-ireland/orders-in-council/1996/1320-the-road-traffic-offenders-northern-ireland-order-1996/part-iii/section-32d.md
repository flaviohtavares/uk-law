# The Road Traffic Offenders (Northern Ireland) Order 1996, section 32D

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 32D). (not yet in force: Courses in Great Britain)[^c2079629]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: The Department may enter into arrangements with persons in Great Britain who provide courses which are approved courses within the meaning of section 30A(5) of the Road Traffic Offenders Act 1988 for the purpose of treating those courses as approved courses within the meaning of Articles 32A to 32E and in this Article such courses in respect of which such arrangements have been entered into shall be known as “)****(not yet in force: recognised courses)****(not yet in force: ”.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: Such arrangements may include provision for any matters for which provision is made in Articles 32A to 32E in relation to approved courses.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: A court–)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: may treat recognised courses as approved courses for the purposes of Article 32A,)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: may treat any certificates received from course providers of recognised courses as certificates received from course providers of approved courses for the purposes of Article 32B,)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: may treat a notice of such course provider not to give a certificate as a notice within the meaning of Article 32B(4) for the purposes of Article 32B(5), (6) and (10), provided that, the offender may only appeal to the supervising court,)*
- ((not yet in force: d)) *(not yet in force: may treat a failure to give such a notice and a failure to give such a certificate in the same way as it would treat such a failure under Article 32B(7) for the purposes of Article 32B(7), (8) and (10), provided that, the offender may only appeal to the supervising court.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: Where a court has made an order in respect of a person under Article 32A, that person may apply to the court to vary the order by substituting a recognised course for the course specified in the order, and if the court grants that application, it shall vary the order accordingly.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: The power to prescribe periods by rules of court under Article 32B(5) and (7) shall include power to prescribe periods for the purposes of paragraph (2)(c) and (d) of this Article.)*

((not yet in force: 6)) *(not yet in force: The Department may by regulations make such further provision in respect of recognised courses as it considers necessary or expedient.)*
