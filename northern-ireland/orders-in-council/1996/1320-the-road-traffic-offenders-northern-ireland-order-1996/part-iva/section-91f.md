## 91F. Orders about financial penalty deposits[^c2103094]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Before making an order under any provision of this Part, the Department or the Department of Finance and Personnel (as the case may be) shall consult with such representative organisations as it thinks fit.

(2) An order under any provision of this Part shall be subject to negative resolution.
