# The Road Traffic Offenders (Northern Ireland) Order 1996, section 62

## 62. Licence receipts

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A person[^c2048589] to whom a person surrenders or delivers[^c2048591] his licence and its counterpart on receiving a fixed penalty notice given to him under Article 60 must issue a receipt for the licence and its counterpart under this Article.

(2) Where the duty in Article 60(7) applies, the[^c2048595] fixed penalty clerk may, on the application of a person who has surrendered or delivered[^c2048599] his licence and its counterpart in those circumstances, issue a new receipt for them.

(3) A receipt issued under this Article ceases to have effect—

- (a) if issued under paragraph (1)[^c2048608], on the expiration of the period of one month beginning with the date of issue or such longer period as may be prescribed, and
- (b) if issued under paragraph (2)[^c2048612], on such date as he may specify in the receipt,

or, if earlier, on the return of the licence and its counterpart to the licence holder.
