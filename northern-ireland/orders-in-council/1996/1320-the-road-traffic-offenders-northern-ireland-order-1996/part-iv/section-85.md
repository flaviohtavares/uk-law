# The Road Traffic Offenders (Northern Ireland) Order 1996, section 85

## 85. Certificates about payment

*Extent: N.I.*

In any proceedings a certificate—

- (a) that payment of a fixed penalty was or was not received, by a date specified in the certificate, by the person to whom it was required to be paid[^c2082818], or
- (b) that a letter containing an amount sent by post in payment of a fixed penalty was marked as posted on a date so specified,

shall, if the certificate purports to be signed by the person to whom it was required to be paid[^c2082821], be evidence of the facts stated.
