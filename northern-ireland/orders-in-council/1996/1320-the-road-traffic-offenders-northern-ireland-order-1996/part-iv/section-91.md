## 91. Procedure for making regulations and orders under this Part

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Before making—

- (a) an order under Article 57 or 59, or
- (b) regulations under Article 88(1),

the Department must consult with such representative organisations as it thinks fit.

(2) [^c2177360]. . . an order or regulations under any provision of this Part shall be subject to negative resolution.
