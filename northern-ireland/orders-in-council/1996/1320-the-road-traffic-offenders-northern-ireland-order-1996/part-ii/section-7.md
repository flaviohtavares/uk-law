## 7. Restriction on institution of proceedings for certain offences

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c1725530] Proceedings for an offence under Article 11(3) of the Order of 1981 (notice about relevant or prospective disability) shall not be instituted except by the Department or by a constable acting with the approval of the Department.

(2) In paragraph (1) the reference to Article 11(3) of the Order of 1981 includes a reference to that provision as applied by Article 15D or 19H[^c1725533] of that Order.
