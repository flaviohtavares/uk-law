## 12. Duty to include date of birth and sex in written plea of guilty

*Extent: N.I.*

A person who gives notification to the clerk of petty sessions in pursuance of Article 24(2) of the [^m00016] Magistrates' Courts (Northern Ireland) Order 1981 (written pleas of guilty) in respect of an offence involving obligatory or discretionary disqualification or such other offence as may be prescribed by regulations made under Article 19C of the Order of 1981 (regulations under Part II of that Order), must include in the notification a statement of the date of birth and sex of the accused.
