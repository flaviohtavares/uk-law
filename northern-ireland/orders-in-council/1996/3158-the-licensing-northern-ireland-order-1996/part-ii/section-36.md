## 36. Information for Department[^key-2b44ceb3abd14685c0e208cdc9c9219f]

*Extent: N.I.*

The Department of Justice shall, in respect of each such period as the Department may specify, arrange for a statement to be sent to the Department showing the number of premises of each of the kinds mentioned in Article 5 having a current licence and containing such other information as the Department may require
