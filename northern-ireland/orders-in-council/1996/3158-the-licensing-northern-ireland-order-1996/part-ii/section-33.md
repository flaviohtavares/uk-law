# The Licensing (Northern Ireland) Order 1996, section 33

## 33. Form of licence

*Extent: N.I.*

A licence shall be in such form as may be prescribed by regulations and shall specify—

- (a) the name and address of the holder of the licence;
- (b) the address of the premises for which it is granted;
- (c) the kind of premises for which it is granted;
- (d) the name and address of the owner of the premises;
- (e) such other matters as may be prescribed by regulations.
