## 42. General permitted hours

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to the succeeding provisions of this Part, the permitted hours for premises of a kind mentioned in Article 5(1), except premises of a kind mentioned in Article 5(1)(b) or (m)[^c-4789b7987f55db9a06f949f4949e530c] (subject to paragraph (6))[^c-4ba9e28b048db1127d346f9beba0e483] or a place of public entertainment, are the hours—

- (a) on week-days, other than [^c-1925372669e976180337d24cfc9f771d]... Christmas Day, from half past 11 in the morning to 11 in the evening; and
- (aa)[^c-032625cd98e56b8707578334da25e7dc] on Sundays, other than Christmas Day, from half past 12 in the afternoon to 11 in the evening; and
- (b)[^c-6be4a78bd26e09a34075dad79c4baa98] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) on Christmas Day,[^c-223bd17b8b01cac1f0f2e04b833a6dd1] from half past 12 in the afternoon to 10 in the evening.

(1A)[^c-4b3fa773d4982434a085a5c5e29f3ab6] Neither paragraph (1)(aa) nor, in a year when Christmas Day is on a Sunday, paragraph (1)(c) applies in the case of premises of a kind mentioned in Article 5(1)(a) with respect to which a direction under Article 7(10) or 15(5)(a) is in force.

(2) The permitted hours for premises of a kind mentioned in Article 5(1)(b) or (m)[^c-323e8c48d5778e1352903813a9829afe] (subject to paragraph (6))[^c-b3aa07adf50c6c8a4eae7430ae0934a5] are the hours—

- (a) on week-days, other than Christmas Day, from 8 in the morning to 11 in the evening; and
- (b) on Sunday, not being Christmas Day [^c-71055224a5f3be5c237922cb2d109d61]..., from 10 in the morning to 10 in the evening.

(3) The permitted hours for a place of public entertainment are that part of the hours mentioned in paragraph (1)(a) [^c-83826803facd2b1152a48c7cb0196e41]... and, in the case of a cinema,[^c-92b65f2b1b6d96a47a878758f8b32539] a theatre or (subject to paragraph (4)) premises on a licensed track[^c-7dc121fc42ef7e270d95a62ca6107446], paragraph (1)(aa) and (c)[^c-e2a94706a224b764088b5dde3b1782fa] which comprises—

- (a) the period of an entertainment; and
- (b) periods, not exceeding 30 minutes in each case, which immediately precede the commencement and immediately succeed the termination of an entertainment.

(4)[^c-e353d1474b436216a0070c16d805c0f6] The permitted hours for premises on a licensed track do not include any time on Christmas Day.

(5) In this Article, “licensed track” has the meaning given in the Betting, Gaming, Lotteries and Amusements (Northern Ireland) Order 1985.

(6)[^c-6fb5d87f51dc2f8cc62e078e124428c5] In the case of premises of a kind mentioned in Article 5(1)(m) to which an order under Article 52E applies, the permitted hours for a part of the premises specified in the order on a day on which an authorisation under Article 52F has effect are the hours on that day from 4 in the afternoon to 10 in the evening.
