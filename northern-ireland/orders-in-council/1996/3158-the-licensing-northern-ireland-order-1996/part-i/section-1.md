# The Licensing (Northern Ireland) Order 1996, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2023-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/1996/3158
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 1. Title and commencement

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This Order may be cited as the Licensing (Northern Ireland) Order 1996.

(2) This Order shall come into operation on the expiration of 2 months from the day on which it is made.
