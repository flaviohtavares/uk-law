## 2AA. Meaning of “outdoor stadium”[^key-40ebc1e3d66ed16a3ea894d80f8cdf44]

*Extent: N.I.*

In this Order outdoor stadium means any premises—

- (a) which are structurally adapted and used, or intended to be used, for the purpose of providing a venue primarily for a variety of outdoor sporting events and activities,
- (b) which include one or more areas for indoor events and activities, and
- (c) which are designated in regulations as a stadium which the Department considers to be of importance to the whole of Northern Ireland.
