# The Licensing (Northern Ireland) Order 1996, section 76

## 76. Appeals against suspension of licence, etc.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where a licence is suspended under Article 72 and the holder of the licence appeals, the licence shall continue in force until the appeal is determined or abandoned.

(2)[^c-8910f546d330327debb7186c96967b74] When on conviction of an offence a licence is suspended the suspension shall not take effect—

- (a) until the expiry of the time for bringing an appeal against the conviction or against the suspension; and
- (b) if such an appeal is brought, until the appeal has been determined or abandoned.
