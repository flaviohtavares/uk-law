## 69E. Application to court of summary jurisdiction by police[^key-ef70bbf2419afa46388f7b74ad26523a]

*Extent: N.I.*

The responsible senior police officer must, as soon as reasonably practicable after a closure order comes into force in respect of any licensed premises, apply to a [^key-140a9370e10497f83d0c30402e757c55]... court of summary jurisdiction for it to consider the order and any extension of it.
