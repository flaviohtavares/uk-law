# The Education Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 1989, section 149

## 149. Programmes of training by boards

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A board shall[^c17610781]. . . prepare[^c17610781]. . . a programme of the provision it proposes to secure—

- (a) for the further training of teachers, in pursuance of its duty under a scheme under Article 29;

*Sub-para. (b) rep. by 1997 NI 15*

- (c) for the training of persons mentioned in paragraph (1)(a) to (c)[^c17610801] of Article 148, in pursuance of its duty under that paragraph.

(2) Before preparing a programme under paragraph (1) the Authority[^key-c9eaa1f9dd0129b0080c4bcc4e3ac22a] shall consult—

- (a) the Board of Governors of—
  - (i) every controlled school under its management;
  - (ii) every maintained school maintained by it;
  - (iii) every other voluntary or grant‐maintained integrated school situated in its area;

- *Sub-para. (b) rep. by 1993 NI 12*
- *Sub-para. (c) rep. by 1997 NI 15*
- (d) the Council for Catholic Maintained Schools;
- (e)[^c17610821] the Northern Ireland Council for the Curriculum, Examinations and Assessment;
- (f) such other persons as the Authority considers appropriate.

*Paras.(3), (4) rep. by 2003 NI 12*

(5) It shall be the duty of the Authority to give eﬀect to the provisions of any programme which has been prepared by it under this Article.

(6) A board may at any time prepare a revised programme or an amendment to an existing programme; and paragraphs (2) and (5) apply in relation to any such revised programme or amendment as they apply in relation to the original programme.
