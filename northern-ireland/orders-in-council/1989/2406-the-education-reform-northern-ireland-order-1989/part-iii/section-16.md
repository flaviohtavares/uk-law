# The Education Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 1989, section 16

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 16. Pupils with statements of special educational needs

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2006-08-01).**

The special educational provision for any pupil speciﬁed in a statement under Article 16 of the Education (Northern Ireland) Order 1996[^c17609041] of his special educational needs may include provision—

- (a) applying Articles 5, 6 and 8 with such modiﬁcations as may be speciﬁed in the statement; or
- (b) excluding the application of those Articles.
