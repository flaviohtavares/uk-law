# The Education Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 1989, section 15

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 15. Exceptions by regulations

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2006-08-01).**

The Department may by regulations provide that Articles 5, 6 and 8—

- (a) shall apply with such modiﬁcations as may be speciﬁed in the regulations; or
- (b) shall not apply;

in such cases or circumstances as may be so speciﬁed.
