## 2. Interpretation

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^c17608731] shall apply to Article 1 and the following provisions of this Order as it applies to a Measure of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

(2) In this Order “the principal Order” means the Education and Libraries (Northern Ireland) Order 1986[^c17608741].

(3) In any provision of this Order “the appointed day” means the day appointed under Article 1(3) for the coming into operation of that provision.

(4) This Order shall be construed as one with the principal Order and accordingly Article 2(2) of that Order, in so far as it relates to the deﬁnition of words or expressions used in that Order and in this Order, shall apply for the purposes of this Order as it applies for the purposes of that Order.
