## 88. Management of controlled integrated schools

*Extent: N.I.*

The scheme of management for a controlled integrated school shall require the Board of Governors to use its best endeavours, in exercising its functions under the Education Orders, to ensure that the management, control and ethos of the school are such as are likely to ensure the provision of integrated education[^c-2435b767afe98a5ffea3e45dc514d488].
