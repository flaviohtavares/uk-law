## 66. Management of grant‐maintained integrated schools

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Each grant‐maintained integrated school shall be under the control and management of a Board of Governors constituted in accordance with the provisions of Schedule 5.

(2) The scheme of management for a grant‐maintained integrated school shall require the Board of Governors to use its best endeavours, in exercising its functions under the Education Orders, to ensure that the management, control and ethos of the school are such as are likely to ensure the provision of integrated education[^c-565aa3de4d45b13ba5ec52db1a3bccdf].
