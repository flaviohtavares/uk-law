# The Food (Northern Ireland) Order 1989, section 26

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 26. Saving for certain licensed uses of premises

Neither a closure order nor an emergency order shall have effect in respect of the use of any premises to the extent that there is a licence in force under—

- (a) the Milk (Northern Ireland) Order 1983[^f00009]; or
- (b) the Slaughter-houses Act (Northern Ireland) 1953[^f00010],

in respect of that use of the premises.
