# The Food (Northern Ireland) Order 1989, section 32

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 32. Power to provide cold stores

A district council may provide a cold store for the storage and preservation of meat and other articles of food, and may make charges in respect of the use of any such store.
