# The Food (Northern Ireland) Order 1989, section 19

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 19. Extension to other food businesses of provisions relating to registration, etc.

(1) The Department may—

- (a) by regulations prescribe that persons intending to use any premises for the purposes of any food business specified in those regulations shall give notice to the district council of that intention;
- (b) by order apply, with or without modifications, the provisions of Article 18 to any food business specified in the order.

(2) An order made under paragraph (1)(b) shall be subject to affirmative resolution.
