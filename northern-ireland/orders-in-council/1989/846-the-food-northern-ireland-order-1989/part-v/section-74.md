# The Food (Northern Ireland) Order 1989, section 74

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 74. Expenses of sampling officers

Any expense incurred under this Order by a sampling officer in procuring samples and causing samples to be analysed shall be defrayed by the authority whose officer he is.
