# The Food (Northern Ireland) Order 1989, section 76

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 76. Amendments, repeals, revocations and savings

(1) The statutory provisions specified in Schedule 3 shall have effect subject to the amendments specified in that Schedule (being amendments consequential on this Order).

(2) The statutory provisions specified in Schedule 4 are hereby repealed or revoked to the extent shown in column 3 of that Schedule. [1958 c. 27 (N.I.)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/apni/1958/27)

(3) In the application of section 29 of the Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00020] to any repeal or revocation made by or under this Order or the Food and Drugs Act (Northern Ireland) 1958[^f00021], subsection (1) and paragraph (a) of subsection (3) shall have effect as if the word “statutory” were omitted wherever it occurs.

(4) Any premises which, immediately before the commencement of this Article, are deemed to be registered under section 17 of the Food and Drugs Act (Northern Ireland) 1958[^f00022] by virtue of subsection (14) of that section, shall, notwithstanding the repeal of that subsection, be deemed to be registered under Article 18, and the provisions of that Article shall have effect accordingly.
