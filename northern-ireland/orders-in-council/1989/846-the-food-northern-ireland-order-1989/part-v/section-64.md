# The Food (Northern Ireland) Order 1989, section 64

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 64. Application of Order for purposes of importation orders and regulations

The provisions of Schedule 2 shall apply for the purposes of the enforcement and execution of any order or regulation made under the Food Act 1984[^f00019], and applying to Northern Ireland.
