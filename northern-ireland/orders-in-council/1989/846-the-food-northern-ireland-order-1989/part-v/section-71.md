# The Food (Northern Ireland) Order 1989, section 71

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 71. Power of Department to hold inquiries and investigations

The Department may cause inquiries or investigations to be held in any case where it appears to the Department advisable to do so in connection with any matter arising under this Order.
