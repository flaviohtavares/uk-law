# The Food (Northern Ireland) Order 1989, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Title and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Food (Northern Ireland) Order 1989.

(2) This Order shall come into operation on the expiration of three months from the day on which it is made.
