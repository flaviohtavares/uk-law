# The Food (Northern Ireland) Order 1989, section 37

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 37. Facilities for examination of food and drugs

A district council may provide facilities for bacteriological and other examinations of samples of food and drugs.
