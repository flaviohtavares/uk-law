# The Food (Northern Ireland) Order 1989, section 46

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 46. Quarterly reports of analysts

(1) Every public analyst shall, as soon as may be after the last day of March, the last day of June, the last day of September and the last day of December in every year, report to the district council by whom he was appointed the number of articles which have been analysed by him or on his behalf in his capacity as public analyst for the district of that council during the preceding quarter of a year, and the result of each analysis.

(2) Every district council shall transmit to the Department, at such times as the Department may direct, a copy of each quarterly report received by it from a public analyst, together with details of any action taken in each case.
