### (not yet in force: Part 4) — (not yet in force: General and supplementary)

#### (not yet in force: Requirement to have power to take fingerprints or sample)

**(not yet in force: 15).** *(not yet in force: A power conferred by this Schedule to require a person to attend a police station for the purposes of taking fingerprints or a sample under any provision of this Order may be exercised only in a case where the fingerprints or sample may be taken from the person under that provision (and, in particular, if any necessary authorisation for taking the fingerprints or sample under that provision has been obtained).)*

#### (not yet in force: Date and time of attendance)

**(not yet in force: 16).** ((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: A requirement under this Schedule—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: shall give the person a period of at least seven days within which he must attend the police station; and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: may direct him so to attend at a specified time of day or between specified times of day.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: In specifying a period or time or times of day for the purposes of sub-paragraph (1), the constable shall consider whether the fingerprints or sample could reasonably be taken at a time when the person is for any other reason required to attend the police station.)*
