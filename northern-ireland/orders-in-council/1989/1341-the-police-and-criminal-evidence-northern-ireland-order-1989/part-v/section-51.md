# The Police and Criminal Evidence (Northern Ireland) Order 1989, section 51

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (146).** Text as at 2026-02-10. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/1989/1341
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 51. Savings

*Extent: N.I.*

Nothing in this Part shall affect—

- (a) the powers conferred on immigration officers by section 4 of and Schedule 2 to the Immigration Act 1971[^c17128961] (administrative provisions as to control on entry etc.);
- (b)[^c-f8e808828f95e6b8efae1f716cecc863][^c-63ad7210573aeff94f620939b9d2294f][^c17128971] the powers conferred by virtue of section 41 or 43B[^c-b3204d38c2c50cd3fb6968029755901e] of, or Schedule 7 to, the Terrorism Act 2000 (powers of arrest and detention);
- (ba)[^c-7f2295d158080a60588bb6c3dbfd9af0] the powers conferred by virtue of Part 1 of Schedule 3 to the Counter-Terrorism and Border Security Act 2019 (powers of detention);
- (bb)[^M_F_b9a26ff7-4130-4ed7-b34f-027f66c5d33b] the powers conferred by virtue of section 27 of and Schedule 6 to the National Security Act 2023;
- (c) [^c19406541]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d)[^c20419201][^c21390631][^c-f8e808828f95e6b8efae1f716cecc863] any right of a person in police detention to apply for a writ of habeas corpus or other prerogative remedy.
