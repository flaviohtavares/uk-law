# The Police and Criminal Evidence (Northern Ireland) Order 1989, section 52

## 52. Children[^c20419221]

*Extent: N.I.*

This Part does not apply to a child apparently under the age of 14 who is arrested without a warrant for an offence other than homicide and to whom Articles 7 and 8 of the Criminal Justice (Children) (Northern Ireland) Order 1998 accordingly apply[^c17129031].
