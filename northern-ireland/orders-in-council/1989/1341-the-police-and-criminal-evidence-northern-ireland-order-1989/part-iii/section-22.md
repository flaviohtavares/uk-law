# The Police and Criminal Evidence (Northern Ireland) Order 1989, section 22

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (146).** Text as at 2026-02-10. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/1989/1341
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 22. Extension of powers of seizure to computerised information[^c20410891][^c21390311][^c-d6356901327b6df45e0b28acda58d712][^c-1ee3464f17b48b22784be39afbcd7cf6]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Every power of seizure which is conferred by a statutory provision to which this Article applies on a constable who has entered premises in the exercise of a power conferred by a statutory provision shall be construed as including a power to require any information stored in any electronic form[^c17127621] and accessible from the premises to be produced in a form in which it can be taken away and in which it is visible and legible or from which it can readily be produced in a visible and legible form[^c17127641].

(2) This Article applies—

- (a) to any statutory provision passed or made before the making of this Order;
- (b) to Articles 10 and 20;
- (c) to paragraph 10 of Schedule 1; and
- (d) to any statutory provision passed or made after the making of this Order.
