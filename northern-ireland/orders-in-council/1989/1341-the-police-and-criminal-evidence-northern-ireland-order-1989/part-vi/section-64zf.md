# The Police and Criminal Evidence (Northern Ireland) Order 1989, section 64ZF

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 64ZF). (not yet in force: Destruction of data relating to persons under 16 not convicted: qualifying offences)[^c22059131]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: This Article applies to material falling within paragraph (2) relating to a person who—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: has no previous convictions or only one exempt conviction,)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: is arrested for or charged with a qualifying offence, and)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: is aged under 16 at the time of the alleged offence.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: Material falls within this paragraph if it is—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: fingerprints or impressions of footwear taken from the person in connection with the investigation of the offence, or)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: a)* *(not yet in force: DNA)* *(not yet in force: profile derived from a)* *(not yet in force: DNA)* *(not yet in force: sample so taken.)*

((not yet in force: 3))[^c-340451c22d59c2701f43d1793c864403][^c-96541e414999ba48e118939aa200b5d1] *(not yet in force: The material must be destroyed—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: in the case of fingerprints or impressions of footwear, before the end of the period of 3 years beginning with the date on which the fingerprints or impressions were taken,)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: in the case of a)* *(not yet in force: DNA)* *(not yet in force: profile, before the end of the period of 3 years beginning with the date on which the)* *(not yet in force: DNA)* *(not yet in force: sample from which the profile was derived was taken (or, if the profile was derived from more than one)* *(not yet in force: DNA)* *(not yet in force: sample, the date on which the first of those samples was taken).)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: But if, before the material is required to be destroyed by virtue of this Article, the person is arrested for or charged with a recordable offence—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: where the person is aged 18 or over at the time of the alleged offence, the material may be further retained until the end of the period of 6 years beginning with the date of the arrest or charge,)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: where—)*
  - ((not yet in force: i)) *(not yet in force: the alleged offence is not a qualifying offence, and)*
  - ((not yet in force: ii)) *(not yet in force: the person is aged under 18 at the time of the alleged offence,)*

  *(not yet in force: the material may be further retained until the end of the period of 3 years beginning with the date of the arrest or charge,)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: where—)*
  - ((not yet in force: i)) *(not yet in force: the alleged offence is a qualifying offence, and)*
  - ((not yet in force: ii)) *(not yet in force: the person is aged under 16 at the time of the alleged offence,)*

  *(not yet in force: the material may be further retained until the end of the period of 3 years beginning with the date of the arrest or charge,)*
- ((not yet in force: d)) *(not yet in force: where—)*
  - ((not yet in force: i)) *(not yet in force: the alleged offence is a qualifying offence, and)*
  - ((not yet in force: ii)) *(not yet in force: the person is aged 16 or 17 at the time of the alleged offence,)*

  *(not yet in force: the material may be further retained until the end of the period of 6 years beginning with the date of the arrest or charge,)*
- ((not yet in force: e)) *(not yet in force: where—)*
  - ((not yet in force: i)) *(not yet in force: the person is convicted of the offence,)*
  - ((not yet in force: ii)) *(not yet in force: the offence is not a qualifying offence,)*
  - ((not yet in force: iii)) *(not yet in force: the person is aged under 18 at the time of the offence, and)*
  - ((not yet in force: iv)) *(not yet in force: the person has no previous convictions,)*

  *(not yet in force: the material may be further retained until the end of the period of 5 years beginning with the date of the arrest or charge.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: This Article ceases to have effect in relation to the material if, before the material is required to be destroyed by virtue of this Article, the person—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: is convicted of a recordable offence and is aged 18 or over at the time of the offence,)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: is convicted of a qualifying offence, or)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: having a previous exempt conviction, is convicted of a recordable offence.)*
