# The Police and Criminal Evidence (Northern Ireland) Order 1989, section 64ZK

>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 64ZK). (not yet in force: Retention for purposes of national security)[^c22059131]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: Paragraph (2) applies if the Chief Constable determines that it is necessary for—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: a)* *(not yet in force: DNA)* *(not yet in force: profile to which Article 64 applies, or)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: fingerprints to which Article 64 applies, other than fingerprints taken under Article 61(6A),)*

*(not yet in force: to be retained for the purposes of national security.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: Where this paragraph applies—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: the material is not required to be destroyed in accordance with Articles 64ZB to 64ZH, and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: Article 64ZN(2) does not apply to the material,)*

*(not yet in force: for as long as the determination has effect.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: A determination under paragraph (1) has effect for a maximum of 2 years beginning with the date on which the material would otherwise be required to be destroyed, but a determination may be renewed.)*
