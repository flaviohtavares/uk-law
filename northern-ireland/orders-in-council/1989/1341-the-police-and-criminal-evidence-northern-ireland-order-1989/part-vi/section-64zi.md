# The Police and Criminal Evidence (Northern Ireland) Order 1989, section 64ZI

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 64ZI). (not yet in force: Articles 64ZB to 64ZH: supplementary provision)[^c22059131]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: Any reference in Article 64ZB or Articles 64ZD to 64ZH to a person being charged with an offence includes a reference to a person being informed that he will be reported for an offence.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: For the purposes of those Articles—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: a person has no previous convictions if the person has not previously been convicted of a recordable offence, and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: if the person has been previously convicted of a recordable offence, the conviction is exempt if it is in respect of a recordable offence other than a qualifying offence, committed when the person is aged under 18.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: For the purposes of those Articles, a person is to be treated as having been convicted of an offence if he has been given a caution in respect of the offence which, at the time of the caution, he has admitted.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: If a person is convicted of more than one offence arising out of a single course of action, those convictions are to be treated as a single conviction for the purpose of any provision of those Articles relating to an exempt, first or subsequent conviction.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: Subject to the completion of any speculative search that the Chief Constable considers necessary or desirable, material falling within any of Articles 64ZD to 64ZH must be destroyed immediately if it appears to the Chief Constable that—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: the arrest was unlawful,)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: the taking of the fingerprints, impressions of footwear or)* *(not yet in force: DNA)* *(not yet in force: sample concerned was unlawful,)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: the arrest was based on mistaken identity, or)*
- ((not yet in force: d)) *(not yet in force: other circumstances relating to the arrest or the alleged offence mean that it is appropriate to destroy the material.)*
