# The Police and Criminal Evidence (Northern Ireland) Order 1989, section 54

## 54. Abolition of certain powers of constables to search persons

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) [^c17129291]. . . , there shall cease to have effect any statutory provision made before the date of the coming into operation of this Order in so far as it authorises—

- (a) any search by a constable of a person in police detention at a police station; or
- (b) an intimate search of a person by a constable;

and any rule of common law which authorises a search such as is mentioned in sub‐paragraph (a) or (b) is abolished.

*Para. (2) rep. by 2000 c. 11*
