# The Police and Criminal Evidence (Northern Ireland) Order 1989, section 89

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (146).** Text as at 2026-02-10. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/1989/1341
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 89. Orders and regulations

*Extent: N.I.*

[(1)][^c22059951] Orders made by the Secretary of State[^key-2140d65f6e3428f1ef56ae36a2bf9414] under Article[^c22059911] 53A or[^key-30f68c609e3ba5602e34eb29bfc2b7c3] 85(1) shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament and section 5 of the Statutory Instruments Act 1946[^c17131651] shall apply accordingly.

(2)[^c22059931] Orders made by the Department of Justice under Article 53, 53A,[^key-2f8b6216bab9b3ad2f2e2e03d14c8f26] 60, 60A , 66 or 81[^key-4c7c10582cb3979b80ff4a708ce8882c] shall be subject to negative resolution.

(3) Regulations made by the Department of Justice under Article 29(4) or 46A shall be subject to negative resolution.
