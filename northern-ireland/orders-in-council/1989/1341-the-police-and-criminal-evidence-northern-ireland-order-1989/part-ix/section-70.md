# The Police and Criminal Evidence (Northern Ireland) Order 1989, section 70

## 70. Interpretation of Part IX[^c21392131]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) In this Part—

- “confession” includes any statement wholly or partly adverse to the person who made it, whether made to a person in authority or not and whether made in words or otherwise;
- “Service court” means the Court Martial or the Service Civilian Court[^c19407821]; and
- “sexual offence” has the meaning given in Schedule 1 to the Criminal Justice (Children) (Northern Ireland) Order 1998[^c17130671].

(2) [^c19408001]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Nothing in this Part shall prejudice any power of a court to exclude evidence (whether by preventing questions from being put or otherwise) at its discretion.
