# The Police and Criminal Evidence (Northern Ireland) Order 1989, section 79A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (146).** Text as at 2026-02-10. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/1989/1341

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 79A. Rule where accused’s spouse or civil partner not compellable[^c22059451][^c22059871]

*Extent: N.I.*

The failure of the spouse or civil partner[^c22059851] of a person charged in any proceedings to give evidence in the proceedings shall not be made the subject of any comment by the prosecution.
