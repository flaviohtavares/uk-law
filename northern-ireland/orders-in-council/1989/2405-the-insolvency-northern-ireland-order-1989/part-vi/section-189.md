## 189. Company incorporated outside Northern Ireland may be wound up though dissolved

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c17599651] Where a company incorporated outside Northern Ireland which has been carrying on business in Northern Ireland ceases to carry on business in Northern Ireland, it may be wound up as an unregistered company under this Order, notwithstanding that it has been dissolved or otherwise ceased to exist as a company under or by virtue of the laws of the country under which it was incorporated.

(2) This Article is subject to the EU Regulation[^c-3f7523a4142682bb4f51ba3020ae6d01].
