## 13DO. Contravention of certain requirements imposed under this Chapter[^c-1531dc9ea9123ea203c78422d93482c3]

*Extent: N.I.*

The fact that a company contravenes Article 13DA or any of Articles 13DG to 13DN does not—

- (a) make any transaction void or unenforceable, or
- (b) affect the validity of any other thing.
