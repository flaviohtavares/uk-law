# The Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989, section 358A

## 358A. Adjustment of balances[^c20147441]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department of Finance and Personnel may direct the payment out of the Consolidated Fund of sums into the Insolvency Account.

(2) The Department of Finance and Personnel shall certify to the Assembly the reason for any payment under paragraph (1).

(3) The Department may pay sums out of the Insolvency Account into the Consolidated Fund.
