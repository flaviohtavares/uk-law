# The Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989, section 364

## 364. Insolvent partnerships[^c20427111][^c17603291]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department of Justice[^c22530241] may, by order made with the concurrence of the Lord Chief Justice and[^c20259701] the Department, provide that such provisions of this Order, Part VI of the Judgments Enforcement (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^c17603311], the Land Registration Act (Northern Ireland) 1970[^c17603321] or the Registration of Deeds Acts as may be specified in the order shall apply in relation to insolvent partnerships with such modifications as may be so specified.

(1ZA)[^c20260381] The Lord Chief Justice of Northern Ireland may nominate any of the following to exercise his functions under paragraph (1)—

- (a) the holder of one of the offices listed in Schedule 1 to the Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2002;
- (b) a Lord Justice of Appeal (as defined in section 88 of that Act).

(1A)[^c17603341] An order under this Article may make provision in relation to the EU Regulation[^c-42999298e76890f1625df36730f1d0b4].

(1B) Provision made by virtue of this Article in relation to the EU Regulation[^c-2d84cbe049fb65a059f301435eb4478d] may not create a new relevant offence (see Article 366A)[^c-793cc7de3c8e3bd9b0636bf8efd4c947].

(2) An order under this Article shall be subject to negative resolution[^c22530261].
