## 344. Time‐limits

*Extent: N.I.*

Where by any provision in Parts 7A to 10[^c22530011] [^c-f215d17792df59d1b20d1a6e5c286916]... or by the rules the time for doing anything is limited, the High Court may extend the time, either before or after it has expired, on such terms, if any, as it thinks fit.
