## 387. Application of filing obligations to overseas companies[^c21831901][^c21113501]

*Extent: N.I.*

The provisions of this Order requiring documents to be forwarded or delivered to, or filed with, the registrar apply in relation to an overseas company that is required to register particulars under section 1046 of the Companies Act 2006 as they apply in relation to a company registered under that Act in Northern Ireland.
