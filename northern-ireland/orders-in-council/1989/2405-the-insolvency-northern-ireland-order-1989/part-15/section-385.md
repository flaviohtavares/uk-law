## 385. Legal professional privilege[^key-939eccd6883b9cc9bc0390377ac16bce][^c21113501]

*Extent: N.I.*

In proceedings against a person for an offence under this Order nothing in this Order is to be taken to require any person to disclose any information that he is entitled to refuse to disclose on grounds of legal professional privilege.
