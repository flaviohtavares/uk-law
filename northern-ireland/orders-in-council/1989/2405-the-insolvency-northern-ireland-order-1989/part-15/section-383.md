## 383. Introductory[^c21113501]

*Extent: N.I.*

The provisions of this Part have effect for the purposes of—

- (a) Parts 1A to 7[^c-2a879bc0884b409ce43133bff12a325d] (company insolvency; companies winding-up),
- (b) Articles 2 and 5 to 8 (interpretation), and
- (c) Articles 359, 360, 361 and 362 in Part 13.
