## 208W. Register of debt relief orders, etc.[^c22531021]

*Extent: N.I.*

The Department must maintain a register of matters relating to—

- (a) debt relief orders;
- (b) debt relief restrictions orders; and
- (c) debt relief restrictions undertakings.
