# The Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989, section 226

## 226. Interim order of High Court

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) In the circumstances specified in Articles 227 and 229, the High Court may in the case of a debtor (being an individual) make an interim order under this Article.

(2) An interim order has the effect that, during the period for which it is in force—

- (a) no bankruptcy petition relating to the debtor may be presented or proceeded with,
- (aa)[^c17600231] no landlord or other person to whom rent is payable may exercise any right of forfeiture by peaceable re-entry in relation to premises let to the debtor in respect of a failure by the debtor to comply with any term or condition of his tenancy of such premises, except with the leave of the High Court, and
- (b) no other proceedings, and no execution or other legal process, may be commenced or continued and no distress may be levied against the debtor or his property except with the leave of the High Court.
