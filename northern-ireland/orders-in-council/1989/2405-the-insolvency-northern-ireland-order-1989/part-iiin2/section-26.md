# The Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989, section 26

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 26. Appointment of administrator

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2006-03-27).**

(1) The administrator of a company shall be appointed either by the administration order or by an order under paragraph (2).

(2) If a vacancy occurs by death, resignation or otherwise in the office of the administrator, the High Court may by order fill the vacancy.

(3) An application for an order under paragraph (2) may be made—

- (a) by any continuing administrator of the company; or
- (b) where there is no such administrator, by a creditors' committee established under Article 38; or
- (c)[^c17597481] where there is no such administrator and no such committee, by the company or the directors or by any creditor or creditors of the company.
