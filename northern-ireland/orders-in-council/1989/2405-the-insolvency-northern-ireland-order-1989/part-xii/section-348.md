## 348. Acting as insolvency practitioner without qualification[^c-0c39cefc5935db25635b45944f7b7ace]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) [^c17602891] A person who acts as an insolvency practitioner in relation to a company or an individual at a time when he is not qualified to do so shall be guilty of an offence.

(1A)[^c-e652d22d7a92413f47983ffde3db836a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) This Article does not apply to the official receiver.
