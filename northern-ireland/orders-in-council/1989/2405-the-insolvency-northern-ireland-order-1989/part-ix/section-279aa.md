((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: This Article applies where—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: property is excluded from the bankrupt's estate by virtue of section 423(2)(b) of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (property detained under certain provisions),)*
