## (not yet in force: 279BA). (not yet in force: Property in respect of which realisation order made)[^c21832061][^c20234791]

*Extent: N.I.*

**May not be in force at this date. Check the source.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: This Article applies where—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: property is excluded from the bankrupt's estate by virtue of section 423(2)(d) of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (property in respect of which an order has been made authorising realisation of the property by an appropriate officer),)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: a confiscation order is made under section 6, 92 or 156 of that Act,)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: the amount payable under the confiscation order is fully paid, and)*
- ((not yet in force: d)) *(not yet in force: any of the property remains in the hands of the appropriate officer.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: The property vests in the trustee as part of the bankrupt's estate.)*
