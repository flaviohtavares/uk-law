## 246. Grounds of debtor's petition[^c18665841]

*Extent: N.I.*

A joint debtor's petition in Form 8 in Schedule 3 to the Insolvent Partnerships Order (Northern Ireland) 1991 may be presented to the High Court by individual members only on the grounds that the partnership is unable to pay its debts.
