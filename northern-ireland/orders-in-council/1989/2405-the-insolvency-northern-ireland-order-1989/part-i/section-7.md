## 7. “Connected with a company”[^c17596031][^c20062581][^key-7f31e900ba1465187ac77a95c346f415]

*Extent: N.I.*

For the purposes of any provision in Parts 1A to 7[^key-5066748cacf5718016339fa0b6eb490e], a person is connected with a company if—

- (a) he is a director or shadow director of the company or an associate of such a director or shadow director, or
- (b) he is an associate of the company.
