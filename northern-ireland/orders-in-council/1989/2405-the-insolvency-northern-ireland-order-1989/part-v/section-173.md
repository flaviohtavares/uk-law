# The Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989, section 173

>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 173. Falsification of company's books[^c-bf0a8fcbe65c4e6192a6490eb2676843][^c-5382c63d9681977c1c8e6a38bc2e3841][^c-5382c63d9681977c1c8e6a38bc2e3841]

*Extent: N.I.*

When a company is being wound up, an officer or contributory of the company who destroys, mutilates, alters or falsifies any books, papers or securities, or makes or is privy to the making of any false or fraudulent entry in any register, accounting records or document belonging to the company with intent to defraud or deceive any person shall be guilty of an offence.
