## 133. Inspection of books by creditors, etc.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The High Court may, at any time after making a winding‐up order, make such order for inspection of the company's books and papers by creditors and contributories as the Court thinks just; and any books and papers in the company's possession may be inspected by creditors and contributories accordingly, but not further or otherwise.

(2) Nothing in this Article excludes or restricts any statutory rights of—

- (a) a Northern Ireland department; or
- (b) a department of the Government of the United Kingdom; or
- (c) a person acting under the authority of either such department.
