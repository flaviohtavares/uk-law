## 65. Unlimited company formerly limited[^c17597851][^c-4077657929f72ef1e7530aa74b970501][^c-5382c63d9681977c1c8e6a38bc2e3841][^c-d839741c181161b0cf3928de3146e834][^c-5382c63d9681977c1c8e6a38bc2e3841]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This Article applies in the case of a company being wound up which was at some former time registered as limited but has been re‐registered as unlimited[^c21826991]. . . .

(2) A person who, at the time when the application for the company to be re‐registered was lodged, was a past member of the company and did not after that again become a member of it is not liable to contribute to the assets of the company more than he would have been liable to contribute had the company not been re‐registered.
