## 136. Powers of High Court to be cumulative

*Extent: N.I.*

Powers conferred on the High Court by this Order[^c21829611] are in addition to, and not in restriction of, any existing powers of instituting proceedings against any contributory or debtor of the company, or the estate of any contributory or debtor, for the recovery of any call or other sum.
