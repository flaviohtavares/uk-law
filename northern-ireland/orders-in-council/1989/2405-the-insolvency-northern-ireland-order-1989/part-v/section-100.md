## 100. Expenses of voluntary winding up

*Extent: N.I.*

After the payment of any liabilities to which Article 148A applies,[^key-6cafffdc18c111e8883353478bb7bcb4] all expenses properly incurred in the winding up, including the remuneration of the liquidator, are payable out of the company's assets in priority to all other claims.
