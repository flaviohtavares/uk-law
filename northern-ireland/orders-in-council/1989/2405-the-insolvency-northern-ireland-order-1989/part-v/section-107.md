# The Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989, section 107

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (422).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/1989/2405
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 107. Avoidance of property dispositions, etc.[^c21829431][^c17598861]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c20103631] In a winding up by the High Court, any disposition of the company's property, and any transfer of shares, or alteration in the status of the company's members, made after the commencement of the winding up is, unless the Court otherwise orders, void.

(2)[^c20103491] This Article has no effect in respect of anything done by an administrator of a company while a winding-up petition is suspended under paragraph 41 of Schedule B1.

(3)[^key-fe2365cfb14012129c6b43c1ced5bfd7] This Article has no effect in respect of anything done during a moratorium under Part 1A, or during a period mentioned in Article 18(4)(a) following the end of a moratorium, where the winding-up order was made on a petition presented before the moratorium begins, unless the petition was presented under section 367 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 on the ground mentioned in section 367(3)(b) of that Act.
