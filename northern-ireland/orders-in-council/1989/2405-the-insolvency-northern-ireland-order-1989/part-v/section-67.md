# The Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989, section 67

## 67. Contributories in case of death of a member[^c-4077657929f72ef1e7530aa74b970501][^c-5382c63d9681977c1c8e6a38bc2e3841][^c-5382c63d9681977c1c8e6a38bc2e3841]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) If a contributory dies either before or after he has been placed on the list of contributories, his personal representatives are liable in a due course of administration to contribute to the assets of the company in discharge of his liability and are contributories accordingly.

(2) If the personal representatives make default in paying any money ordered to be paid by them, proceedings may be taken for administering the estate of the deceased contributory and for compelling payment out of it of the money due.
