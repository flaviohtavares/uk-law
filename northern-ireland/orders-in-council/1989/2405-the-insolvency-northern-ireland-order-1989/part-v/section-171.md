# The Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989, section 171

## 171. Transactions in fraud of creditors[^key-00b7acd053da2cffe4c93112859aef73][^key-5382c63d9681977c1c8e6a38bc2e3841][^key-5382c63d9681977c1c8e6a38bc2e3841]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) [^c17599191] When a company is ordered to be wound up by the High Court or passes a resolution for voluntary winding up, a person who, being at the time an officer of the company,—

- (a)[^key-4b4b9444884d18be91c3e6f9e7ba2d2b] within the 5 years immediately preceding the commencement of the winding up, has made or caused to be made any gift or transfer of, or charge on, or has caused or connived at the enforcement of a judgment against, the company's property, or
- (b) has concealed or removed any part of the company's property since, or within the 2 months immediately preceding, the date of any unsatisfied judgment or order for the payment of money obtained against the company,

shall be guilty of an offence.

(2) It is a defence for a person charged under paragraph (1) to prove that, at the time of the conduct constituting the offence, he had no intent to defraud the company's creditors.
