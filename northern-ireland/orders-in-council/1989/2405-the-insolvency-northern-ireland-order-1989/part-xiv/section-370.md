# The Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989, section 370

## 370. Assembly disqualification[^c20166781]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c22530411] If the High Court makes any order mentioned in paragraph (1A) in respect of a member of the Assembly, the Court shall notify the presiding officer of the Assembly.

(1A) The orders are—

- (a) a bankruptcy restrictions order;
- (b) a debt relief restrictions order;
- (c) an interim bankruptcy restrictions order; or
- (d) an interim debt relief restrictions order.

(2) If the Department accepts a bankruptcy restrictions undertaking or a debt relief restrictions undertaking[^c22530431] made by a member of the Assembly, the Department shall notify the presiding officer of the Assembly.
