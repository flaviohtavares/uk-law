## 20B. Arrangements coming to an end prematurely

*Extent: N.I.*

For the purposes of this Part, a voluntary arrangement the approval of which has taken effect under Article 17A [^key-163bff7913e7f9808b8563e7e3162ecc]... comes to an end prematurely if, when it ceases to have effect, it has not been fully implemented in respect of all persons bound by the arrangement by virtue of Article 18(2)(b)(i) [^key-3af8a37a030e55582b53e2f6859a3ed9]....
