## 17A. Approval of arrangement[^c18663811]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This Article applies to a decision, under Article 17, with respect to the approval of a proposed voluntary arrangement.

(2)[^c17596591] The decision has effect if, in accordance with the rules—

- (a) it has been taken by both meetings summoned under Article 16, or
- (b) (subject to any order made under paragraph (6)[^c-d334a967af162201e6865b82be9ced75]) it has been taken by the creditors' meeting summoned under that Article.

(3)[^c17596591] If the decision taken by the creditors' meeting differs from that taken by the company meeting, a member of the company may apply to the High Court.

(4)[^c17596591] An application under paragraph (3) shall not be made after the end of the period of 28 days beginning with—

- (a) the day on which the decision was taken by the creditors' meeting, or
- (b) where the decision of the company meeting was taken on a later day, that day.

(5)[^c17596591] Where a member of a regulated company, as defined by Article 13H(13)[^c-9176464ee8163192ff441cd02f3fc11a], applies to the High Court under paragraph (3), the Financial Conduct Authority and, where the regulated company is a[^c-c50c94400905cb628056fd39cf75f2dd] PRA-regulated company[^c-114ed9af58b7760a996234de1e384a2b] as defined by Article 13H(13)[^c-32f2ef58c0b0a2fe6bee53595aa62bb6], the Prudential Regulation Authority is entitled to be heard on the application.

(6)[^c17596591] On an application under paragraph (3), the High Court may—

- (a) order the decision of the company meeting to have effect instead of the decision of the creditors' meeting, or
- (b) make such other order as it thinks fit.
