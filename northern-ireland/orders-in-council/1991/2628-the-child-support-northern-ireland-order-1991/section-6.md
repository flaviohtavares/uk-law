## 6. Welfare of children: the general principle

*Extent: N.I.*

Where in any case which falls to be dealt with under this Order, the Department [^c16849831]. . . is considering the exercise of any discretionary power conferred by this Order, the Department [^c16849831]. . . shall have regard to the welfare of any child likely to be affected by its [^c16849831]. . . decision.
