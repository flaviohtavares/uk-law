## 45. Conduct of proceedings

*Extent: N.I.*

Any officer of the Department who is authorised[^c16852571] by the Department for the purposes of this Article may, although not of counsel or a solicitor, prosecute or conduct before a court of summary jurisdiction any proceedings under this Order.
