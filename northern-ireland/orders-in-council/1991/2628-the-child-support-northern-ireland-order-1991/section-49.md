## 49. Co-ordination with Great Britain

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department may make arrangements with the Secretary of State with a view to securing, to the extent allowed for in the arrangements, that—

- (a) the provision made by or under this Order ( “the provision made for Northern Ireland”); and
- (b) the provision made by or under the Child Support Act 1991[^c16852791] ( “the provision made for Great Britain”),

provide for a single system within the United Kingdom.

*Paras. (2), (3) rep. by 1998 c. 47*
