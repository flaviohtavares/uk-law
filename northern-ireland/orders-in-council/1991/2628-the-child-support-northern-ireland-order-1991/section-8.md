## 8. Child support maintenance: supplemental provisions

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where—

- (a) there is more than one person with care of a qualifying child; and
- (b) one or more, but not all, of them have parental responsibility for the child;

no application may be made for a maintenance calculation[^c20963541] with respect to the child by any of those persons who do not have parental responsibility for the child.

(2) Where more than one application for a maintenance calculation[^c20963591] is made with respect to the child concerned, only one of them may be proceeded with.

(3) The Department may by regulations make provision as to which of two or more applications for a maintenance calculation[^c20963641] with respect to the same child is to be proceeded with.
