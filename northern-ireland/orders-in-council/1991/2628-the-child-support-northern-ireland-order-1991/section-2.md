# The Child Support (Northern Ireland) Order 1991, section 2

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (58).** Text as at 2023-07-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/1991/2628

## 2. Interpretation

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^c16849411] shall apply to Article 1 and the following provisions of this Order as it applies to a Measure of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

(2) In this Order—

- “non-resident parent[^c20427261]” has the meaning given in Article 4(2);
- *Definition rep. by S.I. 1998/1506 (N.I. 10)*
- “appeal tribunal” means an appeal tribunal constituted under Chapter 1 of the Social Security (Northern Ireland) Order 1998;[^c16849421]
- “application for a[^c16849441] variation[^c20415991]” means an application under Article 28A or 28G[^c20424821];
- [^c20424911]...
- “benefit Acts” means the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992 and the Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992
- “child benefit” has the same meaning as in the Child Benefit (Northern Ireland) Order 1975[^c16849511];
- *Definitions rep. by S.I. 1998/1506 (N.I. 10)*
- “Child Support Commissioner” means a Child Support Commissioner for Northern Ireland appointed under section 23 of the Child Support Act 1991[^c16849521];
- “child support maintenance” has the meaning given in Article 4(6);
- “[^c-a74c92b0456ecf24d98786486d9428bb]**curfew order**” has the meaning given in Article 36G(1);
- *Definition rep. by S.I. 1998/1506 (N.I. 10)*
- [^c20426781]...
- “deduction from earnings order” has the meaning given in Article 31(2);
- “default maintenance decision” has the meaning given in Article 14;[^c20427011]
- “the Department” means the Department of Health and Social Services;
- “**deposit taker**” means a person who, in the course of a business, may lawfully accept deposits in the United Kingdom;
- [^c20426891]...
- “disability living allowance” has the same meaning as in the Social Security (Northern Ireland) Act 1975[^c16849601];
- *Definition rep. by 2002 c. 21*
- “income support” has the same meaning as in the benefit Acts;
- “income-based jobseeker's allowance” has the same meaning as in the Jobseekers (Northern Ireland) Order 1995;[^c16849611]
- “[^c-90da7f1742d8d62235cbabb9c527d44e]**income-related employment and support allowance**” means an income-related allowance under Part 1 of the Welfare Reform Act (Northern Ireland) 2007 (employment and support allowance);
- “interim maintenance decision[^c20427051]” has the meaning given in Article 14;
- “liability order” has the meaning given in Article 33(2);
- “maintenance agreement” has the meaning given in Article 11(1);
- “maintenance calculation” means a calculation of maintenance made under this Order and, except in prescribed circumstances, includes a default maintenance decision and an interim maintenance decision;[^c20427061]
- “maintenance order” has the meaning given in Article 10(11);
- [^c20426921]...
- “parent”, in relation to any child, means any person who is in law the mother or father of the child and, in the case of an illegitimate child, includes the father;
- “parent with care” means a person who is, in relation to a child, both a parent and a person with care;
- “parental responsibility” has the same meaning as in the Children (Northern Ireland) Order 1995[^c16849681];
- “person with care” has the meaning given in Article 4(3);
- “prescribed”, except in Article 12(5), means prescribed by regulations made by the Department;
- “qualifying child” has the meaning given in Article 4(1);
- “statutory provision” has the meaning assigned to it by section 1(f) of the Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^c16849701].
- “voluntary payment” has the meaning given in Article 28J.[^c20427141]

(3)[^c-082dbf82524b4c05046d999c7016ffa3] The definition of “deposit-taker” in paragraph (2) is to be read with—

- (a) section 22 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000;
- (b) any relevant order under that section; and
- (c) Schedule 2 to that Act.
