# The Statistics (Confidentiality) (Northern Ireland) Order 1991

> Text as at 2006-01-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

1991 No. 1721 (N.I. 20)

The Statistics (Confidentiality) (Northern Ireland) Order 1991

24th July 1991

## 1. Title and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Statistics (Confidentiality) (Northern Ireland) Order 1991.

(2) This Order shall come into operation on the expiration of 2 months from the day on which it is made.

## 2. Interpretation

The [^m00001] Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 shall apply to Article 1 and the following provisions of this Order as it applies to a Measure of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

## 3. Disclosure of information

*Para. (1) rep. by 2004 NI 7*

(2) In Article 7(1) of the [^m00002] Statistics of Trade and Employment(Northern Ireland) Order 1988 (restriction on disclosure of information), after sub-paragraph (a)(iv) there shall be inserted—

> - (v) to any person who provides, or is employed in the provision of, services to that Northern Ireland department;

.
