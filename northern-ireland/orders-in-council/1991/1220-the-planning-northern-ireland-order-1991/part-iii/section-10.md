# The Planning (Northern Ireland) Order 1991, section 10

## 10. Regulations as to development plans

*Extent: N.I.*

Without prejudice to Articles 4 to 9, the Department may make regulations with respect to—

- (a) the form and content of development plans; and
- (b) the procedure to be followed in connection with the making, alteration, repeal and replacement of development plans.
