## 9. Incorporation in development plans of certain orders and schemes

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Any development plan for an area which includes land to which an order or scheme listed in paragraph (2) relates shall have effect as if the provisions of the order or scheme were included in the plan.

(2) The orders and schemes mentioned in paragraph (1) are—

- (a) an order under section 1 of the [^m00019] New Towns Act (Northern Ireland) 1965;
- (b) an order under Article 14 or 15[^c18019941] of the Roads (Northern Ireland) Order 1993;
- (c) a redevelopment scheme approved under Article 49 of the [^m00020] Housing (Northern Ireland) Order 1981;
- (d) an enterprise zone scheme;
- (e) a simplified planning zone scheme; and
- (f) a development scheme adopted under Article 86.
