# The Fair Employment and Treatment (Northern Ireland) Order 1998, section 81

> Text as at 2022-05-12.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 81. The Fair Employment Tribunal

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department shall by regulations provide for the establishment of a tribunal, to be known as the Fair Employment Tribunal for Northern Ireland, to exercise the jurisdiction conferred on it by this Order or any other statutory provision[^c17085241].

(2) Regulations under this Article may provide for the jurisdiction of the Tribunal to be exercised by a single tribunal or by two or more tribunals.
