# The Fair Employment and Treatment (Northern Ireland) Order 1998, section 83

> Text as at 2022-05-12.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 83. Remuneration and allowances

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department may pay to members of the Tribunal (other than the President and the Vice-President) such remuneration, fees and allowances as it may, with the consent of the Department of Finance and Personnel, determine.

(2) The Department may pay to any other persons such allowances as it may, with the consent of the Department of Finance and Personnel, determine for the purposes of, or in connection with, their attendance at the Tribunal.
