# The Fair Employment and Treatment (Northern Ireland) Order 1998, section 104

> Text as at 2022-05-12.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 104. Regulations and orders

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) No regulations under Article 48(10), 53 or 54 or order under Article 6(3), 48(5)[^key-e6be884074d7af08a954c969420e1cc8]... or 101(1) and no regulations which include provision under Article 84B(2)(a)[^key-e9d4cd7509ca363e542666be78e4e1e3] shall be made unless a draft of the regulations or order has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Assembly.

(2) Regulations and orders made by the Department under any other provision of this Order (except orders under paragraph 1(5) or 2(7) of Schedule 1 and regulations which include provision under Article 84B(2)(a)[^key-ba730a0d7caf5ed2098cc1ccc0b174a1] ) shall be subject to negative resolution.

(3) Regulations and orders made by the Department under this Order may contain such incidental, supplementary, consequential and transitional provisions as the Department thinks fit.
