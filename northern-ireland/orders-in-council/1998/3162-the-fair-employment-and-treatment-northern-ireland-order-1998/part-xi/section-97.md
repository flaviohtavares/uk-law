# The Fair Employment and Treatment (Northern Ireland) Order 1998, section 97

> Text as at 2022-05-12.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 97. Selection of unemployed persons

*Extent: N.I.*

After Article 36 of the [^m00030] Race Relations (Northern Ireland) Order 1997 there shall be inserted—

> **36A. Selection of unemployed persons**
>
> (1) The application of any requirement or condition to any person applying to fill a vacancy for employment where the requirement or condition is one that the person applying to fill the vacancy has not been in employment for a specified period of time is not by virtue of Article 3(1)(b) unlawful under any provision of Parts II and IV.
>
> (2) The Commission shall, on request, advise any person who proposes to apply any requirement or condition described in paragraph (1).
>
> (3) In paragraph (1) “specified” means specified by the person applying the requirement or condition in question.
