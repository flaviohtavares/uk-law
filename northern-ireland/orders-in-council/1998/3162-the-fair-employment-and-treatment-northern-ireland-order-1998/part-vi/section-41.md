# The Fair Employment and Treatment (Northern Ireland) Order 1998, section 41

> Text as at 2022-05-12.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 41. Persistent discrimination

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) If, during the period of 5 years beginning on the date on which a finding by the Tribunal or a court under Article 38 or 40 that he has committed an act of unlawful discrimination or unlawful harassment[^c17084671] becomes final in respect of any person, it appears to the Commission that unless restrained that person is likely to commit one or more such acts the Commission may apply to a county court for an injunction restraining him from doing so; and the court, if satisfied that the application is well-founded, may grant the injunction in the terms applied for or in more limited terms.

(2) For the purposes of this Article and Article 42 a finding by a court or the Tribunal becomes final when—

- (a) an appeal against the finding is abandoned or determined; or
- (b) the time for appealing expires without an appeal having been brought.
