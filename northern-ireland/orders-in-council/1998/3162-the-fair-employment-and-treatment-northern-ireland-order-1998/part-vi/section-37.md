# The Fair Employment and Treatment (Northern Ireland) Order 1998, section 37

> Text as at 2022-05-12.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 37. Restriction on proceedings for breach of this Order

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Except as provided by this Order or regulations thereunder, no proceedings whether civil or criminal shall be brought against any person in respect of a contravention of any provision of this Order or of such regulations.

(2) Nothing in paragraph (1) prevents any application for judicial review or the investigation or determination of any matter in accordance with Part X (investigations: the Pensions Ombudsman) of the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993 by the Pensions Ombudsman[^c17084371].
