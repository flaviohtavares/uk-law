# The Fair Employment and Treatment (Northern Ireland) Order 1998, section 70

> Text as at 2022-05-12.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 70. Excepted employments, etc.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This Order does not apply to or in relation to—

- (a) any employment or occupation as a clergyman or minister of a religious denomination;[^c17084851]. . .

*Sub.-para.(b) rep. by SR 2003/520*

(2) Part VII does not apply to or in relation to any employment or occupation where the essential nature of the job requires it to be done by a person holding, or not holding, a particular religious belief.

(3)[^key-e177d62819bca68c0f8a8c8cc80efaf2] So far as they relate to discrimination on the ground of religious belief, Parts III and V do not apply to or in relation to any employment or occupation where -

- (a) the holding, or not holding, of a particular religious belief is an occupational requirement;
- (b) the application of the requirement is a proportionate means of achieving a legitimate aim; and
- (c) either -
  - (i) the person (A) to whom that requirement is applied does not meet it; or
  - (ii) the person who applies the requirement has reasonable grounds for not being satisfied that A meets it.

(4) So far as they relate to discrimination on the ground of political opinion, Parts III and V do not apply to or in relation to an employment or occupation where the essential nature of the job requires it to be done by a person holding, or not holding, a particular political opinion.
