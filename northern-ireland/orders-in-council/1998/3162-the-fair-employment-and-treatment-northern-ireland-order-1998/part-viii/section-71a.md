# The Fair Employment and Treatment (Northern Ireland) Order 1998, section 71A

> Text as at 2022-05-12.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 71A. The police and the police support staff[^c20642831][^c22070081][^c18596711]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Nothing in Part III or V shall render unlawful anything done by the Chief Constable in order to comply with the requirements of section 46(1) to (1F)[^c20642911] of the Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000 (selection of persons for appointment as police trainees).

(2) Nothing in Part III or V shall render unlawful anything done by the Chief Constable in order to comply with the requirements of section 46(5) to (5F)[^c20642921] of the Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000 (selection of persons for certain posts in police support staff).

(3) The application of any provision, criterion or practice[^c17084961] to any person applying for appointment as a police reserve trainee with a view to service in the Police Service of Northern Ireland Reserve on a part-time basis where the provision, criterion or practice is one that the person applying is resident in a particular area is not by virtue of Article 3(2A) unlawful under any provision of Part III or V.
