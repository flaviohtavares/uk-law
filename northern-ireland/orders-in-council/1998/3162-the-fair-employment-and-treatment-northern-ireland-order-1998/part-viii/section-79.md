# The Fair Employment and Treatment (Northern Ireland) Order 1998, section 79

> Text as at 2022-05-12.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 79. Acts safeguarding national security, etc.

*Extent: N.I.*

No act done by any person shall be treated for the purposes of any provision of Parts III to V as unlawfully discriminating if—

- (a) the act is done for the purpose of safeguarding national security or protecting public safety or public order; and
- (b) the doing of the act is justified by that purpose.
