# The Fair Employment and Treatment (Northern Ireland) Order 1998, section 1

> Text as at 2022-05-12.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 1. Title and commencement

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This Order may be cited as the Fair Employment and Treatment (Northern Ireland) Order 1998.

(2) This Order comes into operation on such day or days as the Secretary of State may by order appoint[^c17083101].
