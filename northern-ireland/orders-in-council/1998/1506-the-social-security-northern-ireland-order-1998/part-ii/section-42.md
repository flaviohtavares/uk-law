# The Social Security (Northern Ireland) Order 1998, section 42

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (56).** Text as at 2024-02-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/1998/1506

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 42. Child support: appeals to appeal tribunals

*Extent: N.I.*

For Articles 22 to 23 of the Child Support Order there shall be substituted the following Article— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21053521]

> **22. Appeals to appeal tribunals (affected by repeal, see note)[^c21053521]**
>
> (1) Where an application for a maintenance assessment is refused, the person who made that application shall have a right of appeal to an appeal tribunal against the refusal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21053521]
>
> (2) Where a maintenance assessment is in force, the absent parent or person with care with respect to whom it was made shall have a right of appeal to an appeal tribunal against the amount of the assessment or the date from which the assessment takes effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21053521]
>
> (3) Where a maintenance assessment is cancelled, or an application for the cancellation of a maintenance assessment is refused, the absent parent or person with care with respect to whom the maintenance assessment in question was, or remains, in force, shall have a right of appeal to an appeal tribunal against the cancellation or refusal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21053521]
>
> (4) A person with a right of appeal under this Article shall be given such notice of that right and, in the case of a right conferred by paragraph (1) or (3), such notice of the decision as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21053521]
>
> (5) Regulations may make— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21053521]
>
> - (a) provision as to the manner in which, and the time within which, appeals are to be brought; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21053521]
> - (b) such provision with respect to proceedings before appeal tribunals as the Department considers appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21053521]
>
> (6) The regulations may in particular make any provision of a kind mentioned in Schedule 4 to the Social Security (Northern Ireland) Order 1998. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21053521]
>
> (7) In deciding an appeal under this Article, an appeal tribunal— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21053521]
>
> - (a) need not consider any issue that is not raised by the appeal; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21053521]
> - (b) shall not take into account any circumstances not obtaining at the time when the decision or assessment appealed against was made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21053521]
