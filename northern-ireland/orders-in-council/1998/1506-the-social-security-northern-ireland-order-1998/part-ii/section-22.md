# The Social Security (Northern Ireland) Order 1998, section 22

## 22. Suspension and termination for failure to furnish information, etc.[^c17081651]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c17081661] The powers conferred by this Article are exercisable in relation to persons who fail to comply with information requirements.

(2) Regulations may provide for—

- (a) suspending payments of a relevant benefit, in whole or in part;
- (b) the subsequent making in prescribed circumstances of any or all of the payments so suspended.

(3) In this Article and Article 23 “information requirement” means a requirement, made in pursuance of regulations under section 5(1A)[^key-8ad67a1a9ce22b496a0266bf8736f51a] of the Administration Act, to furnish information or evidence needed for a determination whether a decision on an award of benefit to which that section applies should be revised under Article 10 or superseded under Article 11.

(4)[^c17081671] Subsection (2A) of section 5 of the Administration Act shall apply in relation to paragraph (3) as it applies in relation to paragraph (hh) of subsection (1) of that section.
