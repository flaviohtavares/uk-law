# The Social Security (Northern Ireland) Order 1998, section 36

## 36. Appropriate officers[^c17082111]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c17082121][^key-670086d294e62bae40409d73417322f4] In this Article and Article 38, “appropriate officer” means an officer of the Department who, acting under its authority, is exercising functions of the Department in relation to payments from the social fund such as are mentioned in section 134(1)(b) of the Contributions and Benefits Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-670086d294e62bae40409d73417322f4]

(2) The Department may nominate for an area an appropriate officer who shall issue general guidance to other such officers in the area about such matters relating to the social fund as the Department may specify. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-670086d294e62bae40409d73417322f4]

(3) In relation to any decision of an appropriate officer, Article 38 shall apply in substitution for Articles 10 and 11. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-670086d294e62bae40409d73417322f4]
