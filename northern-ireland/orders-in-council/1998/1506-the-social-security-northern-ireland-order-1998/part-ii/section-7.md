## 7. Panel for appointment to appeal tribunals

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Northern Ireland Judicial Appointments Commission[^c21644511] shall constitute for Northern Ireland a panel of persons to act as members of appeal tribunals.

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), the panel shall be composed of persons appointed by the[^c19498081] Northern Ireland Judicial Appointments Commission.

(3) The panel shall include persons possessing such qualifications as may be prescribed by regulations made with the concurrence of the Department of Justice[^c21750331][^c21644561]; and such concurrence may be given only after consultation with the Lord Chief Justice[^c19498311].

(3A)[^c19498431] The Lord Chief Justice may nominate any of the following to exercise his functions under paragraph (3)—

- (a) the holder of one of the offices listed in Schedule 1 to the Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2002;
- (b) a Lord Justice of Appeal (as defined in section 88 of that Act).”

(3B) As part of the selection process for the appointment of a medical practitioner as a member of the panel, the Northern Ireland Judicial Appointments Commission shall consult the Chief Medical Officer of the Department.

(4) The terms and conditions of appointments to the panel[^c21644581] shall be determined by the Northern Ireland Judicial Appointments Commission with the consent of the Department.
