# The Social Security (Northern Ireland) Order 1998, section 38

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (56).** Text as at 2024-02-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/1998/1506

## 38. Reviews of determinations[^c17082151]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c17082161][^c-5ee1a6fb72ae5d5c0669135294c262e0] An appropriate officer— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5ee1a6fb72ae5d5c0669135294c262e0]

- (a) shall review a social fund determination, if an application for a review is made, within such time and in such form and manner as may be prescribed, by or on behalf of the person who applied for the payment to which the determination relates; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5ee1a6fb72ae5d5c0669135294c262e0]
- (b) may review such a determination on the ground that the person who applied for the payment to which the determination relates misrepresented, or failed to disclose, any material fact; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5ee1a6fb72ae5d5c0669135294c262e0]
- (c) may review such a determination in such other circumstances as he thinks fit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5ee1a6fb72ae5d5c0669135294c262e0]

(2) The power to review a social fund determination conferred by paragraph (1) includes power to review a determination made on a previous review. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5ee1a6fb72ae5d5c0669135294c262e0]

(3) A social fund determination which has been reviewed under paragraph (1) shall be further reviewed by a social fund inspector if an application is made, within such time and in such form and manner as may be prescribed, by or on behalf of the person who applied for the payment to which the determination relates. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5ee1a6fb72ae5d5c0669135294c262e0]

(4) On a review under paragraph (3) a social fund inspector shall have the following powers— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5ee1a6fb72ae5d5c0669135294c262e0]

- (a) power to confirm the determination made by the appropriate officer; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5ee1a6fb72ae5d5c0669135294c262e0]
- (b) power to make any determination which an appropriate officer could have made; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5ee1a6fb72ae5d5c0669135294c262e0]
- (c) power to refer the matter to such an officer for determination. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5ee1a6fb72ae5d5c0669135294c262e0]

(5) A social fund inspector may review a determination under paragraph (3) made by himself or some other social fund inspector. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5ee1a6fb72ae5d5c0669135294c262e0]

(6) In making a determination on a review an appropriate officer or a social fund inspector need not consider— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5ee1a6fb72ae5d5c0669135294c262e0]

- (a) in the case of a determination on a review under paragraph (1)(a), any issue that is not raised by the application; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5ee1a6fb72ae5d5c0669135294c262e0]
- (b) in the case of a determination on a review under paragraph (1)(b), any issue that is not raised by the material fact; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5ee1a6fb72ae5d5c0669135294c262e0]
- (c) in the case of a determination on a review under paragraph (1)(c), any issue that did not cause him to carry out the review. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5ee1a6fb72ae5d5c0669135294c262e0]

(7) In making a determination on a review under paragraph (1)(a) or (c) an appropriate officer or a social fund inspector shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5ee1a6fb72ae5d5c0669135294c262e0]

- (a) subject to sub-paragraphs (b) and (c), have regard to whichever of the following are applicable, namely— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5ee1a6fb72ae5d5c0669135294c262e0]
  - (i) all the circumstances of the case and, in particular, the criteria specified in paragraphs (a) to (e) of subsection (1) of section 136 of the Contributions and Benefits Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5ee1a6fb72ae5d5c0669135294c262e0] and[^c20568521] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5ee1a6fb72ae5d5c0669135294c262e0]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-5ee1a6fb72ae5d5c0669135294c262e0] the criteria mentioned in paragraphs (a) and (b) of subsection (1A) of that section; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5ee1a6fb72ae5d5c0669135294c262e0] [^c20568561]. . .
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-5ee1a6fb72ae5d5c0669135294c262e0] [^c20568561]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) act in accordance with any general directions issued by the Department under subsection (2) of that section, and any general directions issued by it with regard to reviews; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5ee1a6fb72ae5d5c0669135294c262e0]
- (c) take account of any general guidance issued by the Department under that subsection or with regard to reviews. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5ee1a6fb72ae5d5c0669135294c262e0]

(8) In making a determination on a review under paragraph (1)(b) an appropriate officer or a social fund inspector shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5ee1a6fb72ae5d5c0669135294c262e0]

- (a) act in accordance with any general directions issued by the Department; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5ee1a6fb72ae5d5c0669135294c262e0]
- (b) take account of any general guidance issued by the Department. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5ee1a6fb72ae5d5c0669135294c262e0]

(9) Any reference in paragraph (6), (7) or (8) to a determination on a review under a particular provision of paragraph (1) shall be construed, in relation to a social fund inspector, as a reference to a determination on a further review of a determination which has been reviewed under that provision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5ee1a6fb72ae5d5c0669135294c262e0]

(10) Directions under this Article may specify— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5ee1a6fb72ae5d5c0669135294c262e0]

- (a) the circumstances in which a social fund determination is to be reviewed; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5ee1a6fb72ae5d5c0669135294c262e0]
- (b) the manner in which a review is to be conducted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5ee1a6fb72ae5d5c0669135294c262e0]

(11) In making a determination on a review under paragraph (1)(a) or (c) an appropriate officer shall take account (subject to any directions or guidance issued by the Department under this Article) of any guidance issued by the appropriate officer nominated for his area under Article 36(2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5ee1a6fb72ae5d5c0669135294c262e0]

(12) A social fund inspector reviewing a social fund determination which has been reviewed under paragraph (1)(a) or (c) shall be under the same duties in relation to such guidance as the appropriate officer or social fund inspector who made the determination. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5ee1a6fb72ae5d5c0669135294c262e0]

(13) In this Article “social fund determination” means a determination made under the Contributions and Benefits Act by an appropriate officer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5ee1a6fb72ae5d5c0669135294c262e0]
