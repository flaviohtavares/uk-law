# The Social Security (Northern Ireland) Order 1998, section 63

## 63. Daily rate of maternity allowance[^c19537611]

*Extent: N.I.*

In subsection (5) of section 35 of the Contributions and Benefits Act (state maternity allowance), for paragraphs (a) and (b) there shall be substituted the words “the amount payable by way of that allowance for any day shall be taken as one seventh of the weekly rate of the allowance.”.
