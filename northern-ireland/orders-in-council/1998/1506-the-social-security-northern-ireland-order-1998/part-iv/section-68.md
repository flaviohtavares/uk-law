## 68. Power to reduce child benefit for lone parents[^c17082301]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Regulations may revoke any provision of regulations which prescribes a higher rate of child benefit in the case of a lone parent, notwithstanding anything in section 141(4) of the Contributions and Benefits Act (which precludes regulations from prescribing a rate lower than the rate it replaces).

(2) In this Article “lone parent” means a parent who—

- (a) has no spouse or civil partner[^c17082311] or is not living with his spouse or civil partner; and
- (b) is not living with any other person as if spouses of each other[^c-f5dc577cdfc1a23932ba02d73f4697c2] .
