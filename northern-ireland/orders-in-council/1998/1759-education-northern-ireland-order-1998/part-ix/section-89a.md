# Education (Northern Ireland) Order 1998, section 89A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2025-02-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/1998/1759

## 89A. Ulster Scots in education[^key-2f7845f26344ce76631366bb5f4711ff]

*Extent: N.I.*

It shall be the duty of the Department to encourage and facilitate the use and understanding of Ulster Scots in the education system.
