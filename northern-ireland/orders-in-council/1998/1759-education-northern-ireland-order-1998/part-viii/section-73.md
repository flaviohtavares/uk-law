## 73. The Northern Ireland Council for the Curriculum, Examinations and Assessment

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) There shall continue to be a body known as the Northern Ireland Council for the Curriculum, Examinations and Assessment (referred to in this Part as “the Council”).

(2) Schedule 3 shall have effect in relation to the Council.
