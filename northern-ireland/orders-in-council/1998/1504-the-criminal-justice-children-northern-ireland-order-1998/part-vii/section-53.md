## 53. Parental responsibility for children in juvenile justice centres

*Extent: N.I.*

While a child is being detained by the managers of a juvenile justice centre[^c17146981], they shall—

- (a) have parental responsibility for him; and
- (b) if they are satisfied that it is necessary to do so in order to safeguard or promote his welfare, have the power to determine the extent to which a parent of his may meet his parental responsibility for him.
