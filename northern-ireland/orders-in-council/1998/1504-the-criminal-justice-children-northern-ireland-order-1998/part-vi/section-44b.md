# The Criminal Justice (Children) (Northern Ireland) Order 1998, section 44B

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 44B). (not yet in force: Period in secure accommodation under custody care order)[^c22021411]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: This Article makes provision about the application of the Children (Northern Ireland) Order 1995 (N.I. 2) in relation to a child during any period for which he is kept in secure accommodation by the appropriate authority under a custody care order (or under any other order under this Order or as a place of safety).)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: Of the provisions about a child looked after by an authority (within the meaning of Article 25) those specified in paragraph (3) (and no others) apply.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: Those provisions are—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: Article 26 (duty to safeguard and promote welfare);)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: Article 27(1), (2)(b), (e) and (f), (8) and (9) and Article 28(2) (accommodation and maintenance);)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: Article 29(1), (2) and (4) to (6) (promotion and maintenance of contact with family);)*
- ((not yet in force: d)) *(not yet in force: Articles 30 and 31 (visits);)*
- ((not yet in force: e)) *(not yet in force: Article 34 (death);)*
- ((not yet in force: f)) *(not yet in force: Article 35(1) and Article 36(1) and (4) (advice, assistance and befriending);)*
- ((not yet in force: g)) *(not yet in force: Article 45 (reviews and representations); and)*
- ((not yet in force: h)) *(not yet in force: Articles 72 and 73 (provision of homes).)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: In their application by virtue of paragraph (2)—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: Article 29(4) has effect with the omission of sub-paragraph (a); and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: Article 34(1)(a) has effect as if the reference to the Department were to the Department and the)* *(not yet in force: Department of Justice[^c22022951])**(not yet in force: .)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: The following provisions—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: Article 5(7) (person having parental responsibility not to act inconsistently with order);)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: Article 52(3) to (6), (7)(a) and (9) (effect of care order); and)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: Article 53(1) to (9) (parental contact),)*

*(not yet in force: apply as if the custody care order (or the other order or the placing of the child in a place of safety) were a care order and the appropriate authority were the authority designated by it and in whose care the child is.)*

((not yet in force: 6)) *(not yet in force: Articles 8 to 14 (residence, contact etc. orders) and Articles 17 to 24 (children in need) do not apply.)*

((not yet in force: 7)) *(not yet in force: No care order or supervision order under Part 5 may be made or, if such an order has already been made, it does not have effect.)*
