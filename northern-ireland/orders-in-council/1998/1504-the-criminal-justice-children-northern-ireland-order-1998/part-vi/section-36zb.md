# The Criminal Justice (Children) (Northern Ireland) Order 1998, section 36ZB

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 36ZB). (not yet in force: Parental compensation orders: the compensation)[^c22021541]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: When specifying the amount of compensation for the purposes of Article 36ZA(3), the magistrates' court shall take into account—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: the value of the property taken or damaged, or whose loss was caused, by the child;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: any further loss which flowed from the taking of or damage to the property, or from its loss;)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: whether the child, or any parent or guardian of his, has already paid any compensation for the property (and if so, how much);)*
- ((not yet in force: d)) *(not yet in force: whether the child, or any parent or guardian of his, has already made any reparation (and if so, what it consisted of);)*
- ((not yet in force: e)) *(not yet in force: the means of those to be specified in the order as liable to pay the compensation, so far as the court can ascertain them;)*
- ((not yet in force: f)) *(not yet in force: whether there was any lack of care on the part of the person affected by the taking of the property or its loss or damage which made it easier for the child to take or damage the property or to cause its loss.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: If property taken is recovered before compensation is ordered to be paid in respect of it—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: the court shall not order any such compensation to be payable in respect of it if it is not damaged;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: if it is damaged, the damage shall be treated for the purposes of making a parental compensation order as having been caused by the child, regardless of how it was caused and who caused it.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: The court shall specify in the order how and by when the compensation is to be paid (for example, it may specify that the compensation is to be paid by instalments, and specify the date by which each instalment must be paid).)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: For the purpose of ascertaining the means of the parent or guardian, the court may, before specifying the amount of compensation, order him to provide the court, within such period as it may specify in the order, such a statement of his financial circumstances as the court may require.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: A person who without reasonable excuse fails to comply with an order under paragraph (4) is guilty of an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale.)*

((not yet in force: 6)) *(not yet in force: If, in providing a statement of his financial circumstances pursuant to an order under paragraph (4), a person—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: makes a statement which he knows to be false in a material particular;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: recklessly provides a statement which is false in a material particular; or)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: knowingly fails to disclose any material fact,)*

*(not yet in force: he is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 4 on the standard scale.)*

((not yet in force: 7)) *(not yet in force: Proceedings in respect of an offence under paragraph (6) may, despite anything in Article 19 of the Magistrates' Courts (Northern Ireland) Order 1981 (limitation of time), be commenced at any time within two years from the date of the commission of the offence or within six months of its first discovery by the applicant, whichever period expires earlier.)*

((not yet in force: 8)) *(not yet in force: Paragraphs (1)(e) and (4) to (7) do not apply in the case of an order specifying an authority as liable to pay the compensation.)*
