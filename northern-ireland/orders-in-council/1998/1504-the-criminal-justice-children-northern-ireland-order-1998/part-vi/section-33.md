## 33. Power to notify appropriate authority if child's welfare requires it

*Extent: N.I.*

Where a child is charged with an offence the court before which he is charged may, if it[^c21160431] considers that his welfare requires it, notify the appropriate authority of such matters as the court thinks fit.
