# The Criminal Justice (Children) (Northern Ireland) Order 1998, section 44F

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 44F). (not yet in force: Breach of supervision requirements)[^c22021411]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: Where a custody care order has been made in respect of a child and it appears, on a complaint made to a lay magistrate, that the child has failed to comply with any requirements under Article 44E(2) or (3), the lay magistrate may—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: issue a summons directed to the child requiring him to appear before a youth court specified in the summons; or)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: if the complaint is in writing and on oath, issue a warrant for the child’s arrest requiring him to be brought before a youth court specified in the warrant.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: If it is proved to the satisfaction of the court before which the child appears or is brought under this Article that he has failed without reasonable excuse to comply with requirements under Article 44E(2) or (3), the court may—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: if he has not attained the age of 14, deal with him as specified in paragraph (3); and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: if he has attained that age, deal with him as specified in paragraph (4).)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: If the child has not attained the age of 14, the court may either—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: impose on him a fine not exceeding £200; or)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: order him to be placed in secure accommodation by the appropriate authority and kept there by the appropriate authority for a period not exceeding 30 days;)*

*(not yet in force: but the appropriate authority may, with the consent of the)* *(not yet in force: Department of Justice[^c22023011])**(not yet in force: , at any time discharge a child who is being so kept.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: If the child has attained the age of 14, the court may either—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: impose on him a fine not exceeding £1,000; or)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: order him to be detained in a juvenile justice centre for a period not exceeding 30 days.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: Where the court imposes a fine on the child under paragraph (3)(a) or (4)(a), it shall order that the fine be paid by the parent or guardian of the child instead of by the child, unless it is satisfied that there is good reason for not so doing.)*

((not yet in force: 6)) *(not yet in force: A fine ordered under paragraph (5) to be paid by a parent or guardian may be recovered from him by distress, or he may be imprisoned in default of payment, in like manner as if the order had been made on the conviction of the parent or guardian of the offence for which the custody care order was made.)*

((not yet in force: 7)) *(not yet in force: A parent or guardian may appeal to a county court against an order under paragraph (5).)*

((not yet in force: 8)) *(not yet in force: Any period of supervision shall not be reduced by any period during which the child is detained under this Article.)*
