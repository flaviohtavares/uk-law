# The Criminal Justice (Children) (Northern Ireland) Order 1998, section 36ZC

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 36ZC). (not yet in force: Parental compensation orders: supplemental)[^c22021541]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: Before deciding whether or not to make a parental compensation order in favour of any person, the magistrates' court shall take into account the views of that person about whether a parental compensation order should be made in his favour.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: Before making a parental compensation order, the magistrates' court shall obtain and consider information about the child's family circumstances and the likely effect of the order on those circumstances.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: Before making a parental compensation order, a magistrates' court shall explain to the parent or guardian of the child in ordinary language—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: the effect of the order and of the requirements proposed to be included in it;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: the consequences which may follow (under paragraph (4)(b)) as a result of failure to comply with any of those requirements;)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: that the court has power (under paragraph (4)(a)) to review the order on the application either of the parent or guardian or of the applicant.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: A magistrates' court)* *(not yet in force: [^c-01ec4e6d61a01847e1164f70381970be]...)* *(not yet in force: may make an order under paragraph (5) if while the order is in force—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: it appears to the court, on the application of the applicant, or the parent or guardian subject to the order, that it is appropriate to make an order under paragraph (5); or)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: it is proved to the satisfaction of the court, on the application of the applicant, that the parent or guardian subject to it has failed to comply with any requirement included in the order.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: An order under this paragraph is an order discharging the parental compensation order or varying it—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: by cancelling any provision included in it; or)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: by inserting in it (either in addition to or in substitution for any of its provisions) any provision that could have been included in the order if the court had then had power to make it and were exercising the power.)*

((not yet in force: 6)) *(not yet in force: Where an application under paragraph (4) for the discharge of a parental compensation order is dismissed, no further application for its discharge shall be made under that paragraph by any person except with the consent of the court which made the order.)*
