# The Criminal Justice (Children) (Northern Ireland) Order 1998, section 36ZE

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 36ZE). (not yet in force: Effect of parental compensation order on subsequent award of damages in civil proceedings)[^c22021541]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: This Article has effect where—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: a parental compensation order has been made in favour of any person in respect of any taking or loss of property or damage to it; and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: a claim by him in civil proceedings for damages in respect of the taking, loss or damage is then to be determined.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: The damages in the civil proceedings shall be assessed without regard to the parental compensation order, but the claimant may recover only an amount equal to the aggregate of the following—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: any amount by which they exceed the compensation; and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: a sum equal to any portion of the compensation which he fails to recover.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: The claimant may not enforce the judgment, so far as it relates to such a sum as is mentioned in paragraph (2)(b), without the leave of the court.)*
