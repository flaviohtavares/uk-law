# The Criminal Justice (Children) (Northern Ireland) Order 1998, section 44G

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 44G). (not yet in force: Effect of subsequent conviction where custody care order is in effect)[^c22021411]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: Where a child in respect of whom a custody care order is (or but for Article 44A(8) would be) in effect is convicted by or before a court of an offence and the court imposes a custodial sentence on the child for the offence, the court shall—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: revoke the order; and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: in dealing with the child for the offence take into account the period for which, but for the revocation, the order would have continued in effect.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: Where in such a case the court decides to make a custody care order, Article 44A shall have effect as if—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: in paragraph (3) for the words from “a period of six months” to “two years” there were substituted “such period not exceeding two years as the court specifies in the order”; and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: in paragraph (6) for the words “one half of the period of the order” there were substituted “such part of the period of the order as the court specifies in the order”.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: Where in such a case the court decides to make a juvenile justice centre order, Article 39 shall have effect as if—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: in paragraph (2) for the words from “a period of six months” to “two years” there were substituted “such period not exceeding two years as the court specifies in the order”; and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: in paragraph (5) for the words “one half of the period of the order” there were substituted “:such part of the period of the order as the court specifies in the order”.)*
