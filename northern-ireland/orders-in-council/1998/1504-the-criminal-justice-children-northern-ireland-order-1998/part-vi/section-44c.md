# The Criminal Justice (Children) (Northern Ireland) Order 1998, section 44C

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 44C). (not yet in force: Escape from secure accommodation)[^c22021411]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: If a child who has been ordered to be kept in secure accommodation under a custody care order—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: escapes from secure accommodation in which he is being kept or from any hospital or institution in which he is receiving medical treatment;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: being absent from secure accommodation on temporary leave of absence or under supervision, runs away from the person in whose charge he is or fails to return to the secure accommodation at the end of his leave; or)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: being absent from secure accommodation under supervision, fails to return to the secure accommodation on being recalled,)*

*(not yet in force: he may be arrested without warrant by a constable or any person authorised by the appropriate authority and taken to any secure accommodation, or (if he has attained the age of 14) to any juvenile justice centre, or returned to any hospital or institution from which he escaped or to any person in whose charge he was.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: A child arrested under paragraph (1) may at any time be brought with the authority of the)* *(not yet in force: Department of Justice[^c22022971])* *(not yet in force: before a court of summary jurisdiction having jurisdiction where the child is found or where the secure accommodation, hospital or institution is situated.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: Where a child is brought before a court under paragraph (2), the court—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: may order the period for which he is to be detained under the custody care order to be increased by a further period not exceeding 30 days; but)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: if it does not do that, shall revoke the custody care order and deal with the child in any manner in which the court could deal with him if he had just been found guilty of the offence by the court.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: In dealing with a child under paragraph (3)(b) the court shall take into account the period for which the custody care order would, but for its revocation, have continued in effect.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: If any person—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: knowingly assists a child who escapes, runs away or fails to return as mentioned in paragraph (1) or knowingly induces any child to so escape, run away or fail to return;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: without lawful authority takes a child away from any accommodation, hospital, institution or person as is mentioned in that paragraph; or)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: knowingly harbours or conceals a child who escapes, runs away or fails to return as mentioned in paragraph (1), or prevents him from returning,)*

*(not yet in force: he shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale, or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months, or to both.)*
