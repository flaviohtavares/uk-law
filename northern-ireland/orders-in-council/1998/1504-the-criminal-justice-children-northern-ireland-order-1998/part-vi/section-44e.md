# The Criminal Justice (Children) (Northern Ireland) Order 1998, section 44E

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 44E). (not yet in force: Supervision under custody care order)[^c22021411]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: During the period of supervision under a custody care order, the child shall be under the supervision of a probation officer or such other person as the)* *(not yet in force: Department of Justice[^c22023001])* *(not yet in force: may designate.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: Before the commencement of the period of supervision—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: the appropriate authority shall give him a notice specifying—)*
  - ((not yet in force: i)) *(not yet in force: the period of supervision; and)*
  - ((not yet in force: ii)) *(not yet in force: the person under whose supervision he will be; and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: the person under whose supervision he will be shall give him a notice specifying any requirements with which he must comply.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: During the period of supervision the person under whose supervision the offender is or another person designated by the)* *(not yet in force: Department of Justice)* *(not yet in force: may give the child a notice specifying any alteration to the matters mentioned in paragraph (2)(a)(ii) or (b).)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: The)* *(not yet in force: Department of Justice)* *(not yet in force: may make rules regulating the supervision of a child subject to a custody care order.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: Rules under paragraph (4) are subject to)* *(not yet in force: negative resolution[^c22023021])**(not yet in force: .)*

((not yet in force: 6)) *(not yet in force: The)* *(not yet in force: Department of Justice)* *(not yet in force: may pay the expenses incurred by any person designated under paragraph (1) arising from the supervision of a child under this Article.)*
