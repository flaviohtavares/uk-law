## 47. Limitation on punishment for contempt of court[^key-97db0fa0915e02408d844b05bb30ec60]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A child shall not be ordered to be detained in custody—

- (a) for contempt of court; [^key-4f3c1f5c1916f806c22203e555ce97e0]...
- (b)[^key-4f3c1f5c1916f806c22203e555ce97e0] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) Paragraph (1) shall not apply in the case of a child who has attained the age of 16 if the court certifies that his behaviour is such that no other method of dealing with him is appropriate.
