## 1. Title and commencement

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice (Children) (Northern Ireland) Order 1998.

(2) This Order shall come into operation on such day or days as the Secretary of State may by order appoint[^c17146051].
