## 1. Title and commencement

**No longer in force (ended 2010-11-15).**

(1) This Order may be cited as the Fire Services (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 1998.

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), this Order shall come into operation on the expiration of 2 months from the day on which it is made.

(3) Articles 5 and 6 shall come into operation on such day as the Department of the Environment may by order appoint[^c548577].

## 2. Interpretation

**No longer in force (ended 2010-11-15).**

(1) The [^m00001] Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 shall apply to Article 1 and the following provisions of this Order as it applies to a Measure of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

(2) In this Order “the principal Order” means the [^m00002] Fire Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1984.

## 4. Pensions

**No longer in force (ended 2010-11-15).**

In Article 10(5) of the principal Order (pensions)—

  ;
