# The Credit Unions (Northern Ireland) Order 1985, section 1

> Text as at 2020-12-08.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 1. Title and commencement

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This Order may be cited as the Credit Unions (Northern Ireland) Order 1985.

(2) This Order shall come into operation on such day or days as the Head of the Department may by order appoint[^c17048581].
