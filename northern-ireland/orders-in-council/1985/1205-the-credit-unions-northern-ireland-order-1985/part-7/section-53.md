## 53. Duties of receiver or manager of credit union's property

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^M_F_3d882fe6-e5f3-4027-bf0d-29b7d96d6274] Every receiver or manager of the property of a credit union who has been appointed under the powers contained in any instrument shall—

- (a) within 1 month from the date of his appointment, notify [^c-238923728ca672b303321762d2ba5010]...the appropriate person[^c-2348464611aa7d8c7ac14fc536e7d2a6] of his appointment; and
- (b) within 1 month [^c-42b12672fa70f69015ea5eeb9305aa4d]... after the expiration of the period of 6 months from that date, and of every subsequent period of 6 months, deliver to [^c-238923728ca672b303321762d2ba5010]... the appropriate person a return showing his receipts and his payments during that period of 6 months; and
- (c) within 1 month after he ceases to act as receiver or manager, notify [^c-238923728ca672b303321762d2ba5010]...the appropriate person of that fact and deliver to [^c-238923728ca672b303321762d2ba5010]... the appropriate person a return showing his receipts and his payments during the final period and the aggregate amount of his receipts and of his payments during all preceding periods since his appointment.

(2)[^c-c563c47850dddfa0ae7ed1617397537c] In this article, “the appropriate person” means—

- (a)[^c-da09c7a940f70fcba0535e4926e2c23d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) the FCA ; and
- (c) if the society is a PRA -authorised person, the PRA .

(3) [^c-0b9cf6bd018ccb965a8f298d868d7810]... the FCA and, if the society is a PRA -authorised person, the PRA may each allow a period of longer than 1 month for the delivery of returns to it under paragraph (1)(b).
