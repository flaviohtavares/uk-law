# The Credit Unions (Northern Ireland) Order 1985, section 67

> Text as at 2020-12-08.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 67. Saving for rights of creditors

*Extent: N.I.*

An amalgamation or transfer of engagements in pursuance of Article 65 or 66 shall not prejudice any right of a creditor of any credit union which is a party to such amalgamation or transfer of engagements.
