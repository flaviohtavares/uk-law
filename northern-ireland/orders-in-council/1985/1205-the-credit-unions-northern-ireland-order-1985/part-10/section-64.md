# The Credit Unions (Northern Ireland) Order 1985, section 64

> Text as at 2020-12-08.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 64. Restrictions on amalgamations and transfers of engagements

*Extent: N.I.*

For the purposes of Articles 65 and 66, a credit union with which another credit union may amalgamate or to which that other credit union may transfer its engagements or from which that other credit union may accept a transfer of engagements shall be a credit union with a membership which has a common bond similar to the common bond between the members of that other credit union.
