> Text as at 2020-12-08.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 43. Obligation to appoint auditors

*Extent: N.I.*

Every credit union shall in each year of account appoint a qualified auditor or qualified auditors to audit its accounts and balance sheet for that year.
