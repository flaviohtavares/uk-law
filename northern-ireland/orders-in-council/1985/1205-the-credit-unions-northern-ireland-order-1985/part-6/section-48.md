# The Credit Unions (Northern Ireland) Order 1985, section 48

> Text as at 2020-12-08.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 48. Remuneration of qualified auditors

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Regulations may prescribe the maximum rates of remuneration to be paid by credit unions for the audit of their accounts and balance sheets by qualified auditors.

(2) Where the maximum rate of remuneration which may be paid by a credit union for the audit of its accounts and balance sheet has been prescribed under paragraph (1), an auditor shall not, in respect of his services to a credit union, ask for, receive or be entitled to receive remuneration in excess of the rate so prescribed.
