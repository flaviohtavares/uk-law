# The Credit Unions (Northern Ireland) Order 1985, section 56

> Text as at 2020-12-08.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 56. Inspection of books by order of FCA[^c-58d514900aab2c6474c5e9282e931f41]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the FCA[^c-4c5ecf6995eb372a3db522d5cb8c9900] may, on the application of 10 members of a credit union each of whom has been a member of the credit union for not less than 12 months immediately preceding the date of the application, appoint an accountant or actuary to inspect, and to report on, the books of the credit union.

(2) The members making an application under paragraph (1) shall deposit with the FCA[^c-29ae4113c73fbc287cf6f6fd597e1add] as security for the costs of the proposed inspection such sum as the FCA[^c-b179358adb5908a73e05ece5bd24e6ac] may require; and all expenses of and incidental to the inspection shall be defrayed by the applicants, or out of the funds of the credit union, or by the members or officers, or former members or officers, of the credit union, in such proportions as the FCA shall direct.

(3) A person appointed under this Article may make copies of, and take extracts from, any books of the credit union at all reasonable hours at the credit union's registered office or at any other place where those books are kept.

(4) The FCA[^c-fc160ec92ff941c291dbd3c5e2765b38] shall communicate the results of any inspection under this Article to the applicants and to the credit union.
