# The Credit Unions (Northern Ireland) Order 1985, section 70

> Text as at 2020-12-08.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 70. Instrument of dissolution

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The following provisions of this Article shall have effect where a credit union is to be dissolved by an instrument of dissolution under Article 68(b).

(2) The instrument of dissolution shall set forth—

- (a) the liabilities and assets of the credit union in detail;
- (b) the number of the members and the nature of their respective interests in the credit union;
- (c) the claims of creditors, if any, and the provision to be made for their payment; and
- (d) unless stated in the instrument of dissolution to be left to the award of the FCA[^c-37e4b338cfab5273409d9626fa3e1dcc] , the intended appropriation or division of the funds and property of the credit union.

(3) Alterations in the instrument of dissolution may be made by the consent of not less than three-fourths of the members of the credit union testified by their signatures to the alteration.

(4) The instrument of dissolution shall be sent to the FCA[^c-23b93ca63c02641cbabd996b37b7bd0e] accompanied by a statutory declaration made by 3 members and the secretary of the credit union that all relevant provisions of this Order have been complied with; and any person knowingly making a false or fraudulent declaration in the matter shall be guilty of an offence and liable to the penalties provision by Article 10 of the [^m00011] Perjury (Northern Ireland) Order 1979.

(5) The instrument of dissolution and any alterations to the instrument shall be registered in like manner as an amendment of the rules of the credit union and shall be binding upon all the members of the credit union, but shall not be so registered until the FCA[^c-e5b2f8599d08c94a5138bce7f3ae541a] has received such a final return from the credit union as is referred to in Article 49(8)[^c22313891].

(6) The FCA[^c-ee9ca784383003d075df6c92059a7f47] shall cause notice of the dissolution to be advertised at the expense of the credit union in the Belfast Gazette and in any other manner which the FCA considers necessary for bringing such notice to the attention of persons affected by the dissolution: and unless—

- (a) within 3 months from the date of the Belfast Gazette in which that advertisement appears a member or other person interested in or having any claim on the funds of the credit union commences in the county court [^c-5c12c3a223607a24eb2791f33b141f14]... proceedings to set aside the dissolution of the credit union: and
- (b) that dissolution is set aside accordingly,

then, subject to paragraph (7), the credit union shall be legally dissolved from the date of the advertisement and the requisite consents to the instrument of dissolution shall be deemed to have been duly obtained without proof of the signatures to the instrument.

(7) If the certificate referred to in Article 71(1) has not been lodged with the FCA[^c-66f1f0ccbef058ca8059fca32c90e7e1] by the date of the advertisement referred to in paragraph (6), the credit union shall be legally dissolved only from the date when that certificate is so lodged.

(8) Notice of any proceedings to set aside the dissolution of a credit union shall be sent to the FCA[^c-3d6d15037d51f332554c6ab96cbf2edc] by the person taking those proceedings not later than 7 days after they are commenced or not later than the expiration of the period of 3 months referred to in paragraph (6), whichever is the earlier; and notice of any order setting the dissolution aside shall be sent by the credit union to the FCA within 7 days after the making of the order.
