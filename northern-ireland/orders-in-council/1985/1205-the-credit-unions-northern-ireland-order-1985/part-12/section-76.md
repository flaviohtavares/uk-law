# The Credit Unions (Northern Ireland) Order 1985, section 76

> Text as at 2020-12-08.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 76. Prosecution of offences

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Summary proceedings for an offence under this Order shall not be instituted except by—

- (a) in the case of proceedings under Article 74,—
  - (i) the credit union; or
  - (ii) any member of that credit union authorised by the credit union or its board of directors or by the FCA[^key-896736d91fcba4d0b7e481f60c94b93e] ; or
  - (iii) the FCA ;
- (b) in any other case, the FCA or any person aggrieved.

(2) Notwithstanding Article 19(1)(a) of [^m00012] the Magistrates' Courts (Northern Ireland) Order 1981 (limitation of time for taking proceedings) summary proceedings for an offence under this Order[^c22313971]. . . may be instituted by the FCA at any time within one year after the first discovery of the offence by the FCA , but shall not in any case be instituted after the expiration of 3 years from the commission of the offence.
