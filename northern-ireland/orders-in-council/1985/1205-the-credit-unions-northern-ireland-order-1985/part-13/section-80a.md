# The Credit Unions (Northern Ireland) Order 1985, section 80A

> Text as at 2020-12-08.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 80A. Form etc of electronic documents[^c-6d115e8ea284bd12e2b6fe58dee5b57b]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A document or information required for the purposes of this Order, must, if sent in electronic form, be sent in a form, and by a means, that the sender reasonably considers will enable the recipient to read it and retain a copy of it.

(2) For the purposes of this Article, a document or information can be read only if—

- (a) it can be read with the naked eye; or
- (b) to the extent that it consists of images (for example photographs, pictures, maps, plans or drawings), it can be seen with the naked eye.
