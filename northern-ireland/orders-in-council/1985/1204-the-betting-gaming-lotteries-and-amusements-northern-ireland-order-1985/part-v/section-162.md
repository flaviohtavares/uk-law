## 162. Continuance of pleasure permits pending determination of appeal

*Extent: N.I.*

Where a district council refuses to renew a pleasure permit and the holder of the permit appeals the pleasure permit shall, unless it is cancelled, continue in force until the appeal is determined or abandoned.
