# The Betting, Gaming, Lotteries and Amusements (Northern Ireland) Order 1985, section 135

## 135. Societies' lotteries

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to the provisions of this Order, a society's lottery is not unlawful if—

- (a) it is promoted in Northern Ireland; and
- (b) the society is registered under Article 136; and
- (c) it is promoted in accordance with a lottery scheme approved by the registered society.

(2) The whole proceeds of a society's lottery, after deducting sums lawfully appropriated on account of expenses or for the provision of prizes, shall be applied to the purposes of the registered society.
