## 150. Continuance of lottery certificates pending determination of appeal against revocation

*Extent: N.I.*

Where a court revokes a lottery certificate and the holder of the certificate appeals, the certificate shall, unless it is a certificate to which a disqualification order under Article 152 applies or it ceases to be in force under Article 173, continue in force until the appeal is determined or abandoned.
