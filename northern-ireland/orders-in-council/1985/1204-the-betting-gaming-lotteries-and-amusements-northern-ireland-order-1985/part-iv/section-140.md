# The Betting, Gaming, Lotteries and Amusements (Northern Ireland) Order 1985, section 140

## 140. Prohibition on acting as a lottery consultant or manager without a certificate

*Extent: N.I.*

It shall be unlawful for any person to act in relation to any society's lottery[^c17003401] as an external lottery consultant or manager unless he holds a lottery certificate authorising him to do so and any person acting in contravention of this Article shall be guilty of an offence.
