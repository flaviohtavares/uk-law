# The Betting, Gaming, Lotteries and Amusements (Northern Ireland) Order 1985, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2025-01-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/1985/1204

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 1. Title and commencement

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This Order may be cited as the Betting, Gaming, Lotteries and Amusements (Northern Ireland) Order 1985.

(2) This Order shall come into operation on such day or days as the Head of the Department may by order appoint[^c17000151].
