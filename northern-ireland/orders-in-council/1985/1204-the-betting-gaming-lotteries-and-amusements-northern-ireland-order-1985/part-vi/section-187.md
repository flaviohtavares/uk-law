# The Betting, Gaming, Lotteries and Amusements (Northern Ireland) Order 1985, section 187

## 187. Modifications of Schedules, amendments, transitional provisions and repeals

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The provisions of Schedule 2 may be modified by county court rules and the provisions of Schedules 1, 3[^c17004051] to 7, 9 to 14, 16 and 17 may be modified by magistrates' courts rules and any rules under this paragraph may make consequential modifications of this Order.

*Para. (2)—Amendments*

(3) The transitional provisions in Schedule 20 shall have effect for the purposes of this Order.

*Para. (4)—Repeals*

(5) The rules of law relating to common gaming houses are hereby abolished.
