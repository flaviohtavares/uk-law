## 73. Continuance of bingo club licences pending determination of appeal against revocation

*Extent: N.I.*

Where a court revokes a bingo club licence and the holder of the licence appeals, the licence shall, unless it is a licence to which a disqualification order under Article 79 applies or it ceases to be in force under Article 173, continue in force until the appeal is determined or abandoned.
