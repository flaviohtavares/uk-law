## 101. Continuance of registration of clubs pending determination of appeal

*Extent: N.I.*

Where a court refuses an application for the renewal of the registration of a club and the applicant for the renewal appeals, the registration of the club shall, unless cancelled, continue in force until the appeal is determined or abandoned.
