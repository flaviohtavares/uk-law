## 60. Gaming to which Chapter II applies

*Extent: N.I.*

This Chapter applies to—

- (a) gaming by way of bingo,
- (b) gaming to which Article 77 applies,

which takes place on bingo club premises and which is not gaming by means of a gaming machine.
