# The Betting, Gaming, Lotteries and Amusements (Northern Ireland) Order 1985, section 6

## 6. Prohibition on acting as a bookmaker without a licence

*Extent: N.I.*

It shall be unlawful for any person to—

- (a) carry on business or act as a bookmaker, or
- (b) hold himself out or represent himself to be a bookmaker,

unless he holds a bookmaker's licence authorising him to do so and any person acting in contravention of this Article shall be guilty of an offence.
