# The Betting, Gaming, Lotteries and Amusements (Northern Ireland) Order 1985, section 9

## 9. Prohibition on using certain premises without a bookmaking office licence

*Extent: N.I.*

It shall be unlawful for any licensed bookmaker to carry on or represent himself as carrying on the business of a bookmaker in any premises unless he holds a bookmaking office licence authorising him to do so in those premises and any licensed bookmaker acting in contravention of this Article shall be guilty of an offence.
