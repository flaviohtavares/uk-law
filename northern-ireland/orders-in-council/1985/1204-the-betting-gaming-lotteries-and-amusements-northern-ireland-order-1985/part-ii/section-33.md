# The Betting, Gaming, Lotteries and Amusements (Northern Ireland) Order 1985, section 33

## 33. Employment of agents

*Extent: N.I.*

A person who, in connection with the business carried on in a licensed office, employs any other person directly or indirectly as his agent or collector of bets at any place other than that office shall be guilty of an offence.
