# The Foyle and Carlingford Fisheries (Northern Ireland) Order 2007, section 14

> Text as at 2012-09-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 14. Reinstatement of polluted waters

*Extent: N.I.*

In section 41 of the 1952 Act (penalty for pollution)—

- (a) in subsection (3)(i) for “restocking to restore the fish population” there shall be substituted “ reinstatement ”;
- (b) in subsection (4) for “the Arbitration Act (Northern Ireland) 1937” there shall be substituted “ Part I of the Arbitration Act 1996 ”;
- (c) in subsections (4)(a) and (5) for “restocking” there shall be substituted “ reinstatement ”.
