# The Foyle and Carlingford Fisheries (Northern Ireland) Order 2007, section 12

> Text as at 2012-09-17.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 12. Metrication

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) In section 37(1) of the 1952 Act (mesh of nets for the taking of salmon)—

- (a) for the words “one and three-quarter inches” there shall be substituted the words “ 4.5 centimetres ”;
- (b) for the words “seven inches” there shall be substituted the words “ 18 centimetres ”.

(2) In section 38(1) for the words “half a mile” (where they twice occur) there shall be substituted the words “ 805 metres ”.
