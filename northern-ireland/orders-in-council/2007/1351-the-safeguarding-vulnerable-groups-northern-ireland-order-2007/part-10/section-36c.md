# The Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups (Northern Ireland) Order 2007, section 36C

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 36C). (not yet in force: Articles 36A and 36B: notification of outcome)[^c21524031]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: Paragraph (2) applies if ISA—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: has notified a person (A) under Article 36A or 36B that it proposes to include another (B) in a barred list, and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: includes B in the barred list or decides not to do so.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: ISA must notify A that it has included B in the barred list or that it has decided not to do so (as the case may be).)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: In a case where A is registered in relation to B under Article 34 (or has ceased to be so registered by virtue of B's inclusion in the barred list), the requirement in paragraph (2) is satisfied if notification is sent to any address recorded (or, as the case may be, formerly recorded) against A's name in the register.)*
