## 40. Duty to provide information: offences[^c21194001]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A person commits an offence if—

- (a) he is required under Article 37 or 38 or in pursuance of Article 39 to provide information to DBS[^key-2b869d4b5be798483539b4a52ce1eeec] , and
- (b) he fails, without reasonable excuse, to provide the information.

(2) A person guilty of an offence under paragraph (1) is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale.
