## 48. Supervisory authorities: duty to provide information on request[^c21195841]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This Article applies if DBS[^key-b98d72648b237250484235c621f9ad09] is considering—

- (a) whether to include in a barred list a person in relation to whom DBS thinks that a supervisory authority may have prescribed information;
- (b) whether to remove such a person from a barred list.

(2) DBS may require the supervisory authority to provide it with any prescribed information it holds relating to the person.

(3) The supervisory authority must comply with a requirement under paragraph (2).
