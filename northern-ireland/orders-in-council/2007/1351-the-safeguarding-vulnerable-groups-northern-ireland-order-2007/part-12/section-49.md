## 49. Supervisory authorities: power to apply for certain barring information[^c21195851][^c-21cebca518c9a62b82425c266e04b99b]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-d22314847aaccebaf2907947758803e7] If a supervisory authority applies to the DBS[^c-b98d72648b237250484235c621f9ad09] for information within paragraph (2) or (3) relating to a person (B), the DBSmust provide the supervisory authority with that information.

(2)[^c-d22314847aaccebaf2907947758803e7] The information within this paragraph is—

- (a) whether B is barred from regulated activity relating to children,
- (b)[^c-a315f3dc68a934516e81c723e5b7af4d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c)[^c-a315f3dc68a934516e81c723e5b7af4d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d)[^c-a315f3dc68a934516e81c723e5b7af4d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (e)[^c-a315f3dc68a934516e81c723e5b7af4d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c-d22314847aaccebaf2907947758803e7] The information within this paragraph is—

- (a) whether B is barred from regulated activity relating to vulnerable adults,
- (b)[^c-d0658b18fb829a36e0a83beefb4712b6] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c)[^c-d0658b18fb829a36e0a83beefb4712b6] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d)[^c-d0658b18fb829a36e0a83beefb4712b6] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (e)[^c-d0658b18fb829a36e0a83beefb4712b6] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^c-d22314847aaccebaf2907947758803e7] A supervisory authority may apply for information under this Article only if the information is required in connection with the exercise of a function of the supervisory authority mentioned in Article 47(7).

(5)[^c-41c3e2d0f38f1d26667bd4716c052683] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6) The Secretary of State may by order amend this Article for the purpose of altering the information within paragraph (2) or (3).

(7)[^c-a8fa18edfa0ac42c49f4262fd26d0886] DBS[^c-b581bf95abc7e38def0bb88e65835999] may determine[^c-ae79d8941c726c98abe00d77a8a476f0] the form, manner and contents of an application for the purposes of this Article.
