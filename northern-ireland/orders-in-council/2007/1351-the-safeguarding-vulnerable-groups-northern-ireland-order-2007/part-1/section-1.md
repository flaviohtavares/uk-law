# The Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups (Northern Ireland) Order 2007, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (34).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/2007/1351

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 1. Title and commencement

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This Order may be cited as the Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups (Northern Ireland) Order 2007.

(2) This Article and Articles 2 to 4, 58 and 61 come into operation two weeks after the day on which this Order is made.

(3)[^c21190011] The other provisions of this Order come into operation on such day or days as the Secretary of State may by order appoint.
