## 42. The Education Authority and HSC bodies: duty to provide information on request[^c21194021][^key-fcfe61884410d745a2c5c9f7d59b27e5][^c21480821]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This Article applies if DBS[^key-2b869d4b5be798483539b4a52ce1eeec] is considering—

- (a) whether to include any person in a barred list;
- (b) whether to remove any person from a barred list.

(2) If DBS thinks that the Education Authority[^key-9ae361f71e606a9f9abf67650e0437dd] or an HSC[^c21480831] body holds any prescribed information relating to the person, it may require the Authority or that[^key-f0ea6b67e8d555cf7b00bf4cbbc30d6b] body to provide it with the information.

(3) The Education Authority or the[^key-9a279a32d7d8005b0c0229d987b3b57b] body must comply with a requirement under paragraph (2).
