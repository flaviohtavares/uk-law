# The Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups (Northern Ireland) Order 2007, section 54

## 54. Referrals: findings of fact immaterial

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of the provisions mentioned in paragraph (2), it is immaterial whether there is a finding of fact in any proceedings.

(2) The provisions are—

- (a) Article 37(3)(b) and (c);
- (b) Article 38(4)(b) and (c);
- (c) Article 41(2)(b) and (c) and (5)(a);
- (d) Article 43(2)(b) and (c) and (5)(a);
- (e) Article 47(2)(b) and (c) and (5)(a).
