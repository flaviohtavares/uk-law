# The Industrial Relations (Northern Ireland) Order 1992, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-08. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/1992/807
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 1. Title and commencement

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This Order may be cited as the Industrial Relations (Northern Ireland) Order 1992.

(2) This Order shall come into operation on such day or days as the Head of the Department of Economic Development may by order appoint[^c16878371].

(3) Without prejudice to Article 107(3), an order under paragraph (2) may contain such transitional and supplementary provisions as appear to the Head of the Department of Economic Development to be necessary or expedient.
