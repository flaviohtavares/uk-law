# The Industrial Relations (Northern Ireland) Order 1992, section 96

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-08. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/1992/807
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 96. Meaning of “trade dispute” in Part XI

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) In this Part “trade dispute” means a dispute between employers and workers, or between workers and workers, which is connected with one or more of the following, that is to say—

- (a)[^key-bf6fc3d37c4c3687c41201c0e16e2f40] terms and conditions of employment, or the physical conditions in which any workers are required to work;
- (b)[^key-bf6fc3d37c4c3687c41201c0e16e2f40] engagement or non-engagement, or termination or suspension of employment or the duties of employment, of one or more workers;
- (c)[^key-bf6fc3d37c4c3687c41201c0e16e2f40] allocation of work or the duties of employment as between workers or groups of workers;
- (d)[^key-bf6fc3d37c4c3687c41201c0e16e2f40] matters of discipline;
- (e)[^key-bf6fc3d37c4c3687c41201c0e16e2f40] the membership or non-membership of a trade union on the part of a worker;
- (f)[^key-bf6fc3d37c4c3687c41201c0e16e2f40] facilities for officials of trade unions;
- (g)[^key-bf6fc3d37c4c3687c41201c0e16e2f40] machinery for negotiation or consultation, and other procedures, relating to any of the foregoing matters, including the recognition by employers or employers' associations of the right of a trade union to represent workers in any such negotiation or consultation or in the carrying out of such procedures.

(2) A dispute between a Minister of the Crown or Department of the Government of Northern Ireland and any workers shall, notwithstanding that the Minister or the Department is not the employer of those workers, be treated for the purposes of this Part as a dispute between an employer and those workers if the dispute relates—

- (a) to matters which have been referred for consideration by a joint body on which, by virtue of any provision made by or under any statutory provision, that Minister or that Department is represented; or
- (b) to matters which cannot be settled without that Minister or that Department exercising a power conferred by or under any statutory provision.

(3) There is a trade dispute for the purposes of this Part even though it relates to matters occurring outside Northern Ireland.

(4) A dispute to which a trade union or employers' association is a party shall be treated for the purposes of this Part as a dispute to which workers or, as the case may be, employers are parties.

(5) In this Article—

- “employment” includes any relationship whereby one person personally does work or performs services for another;
- “worker”, in relation to a dispute to which an employer is a party, includes any worker even if not employed by that employer.
