# The Industrial Relations (Northern Ireland) Order 1992, section 107

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-08. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/1992/807
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 107. Orders and regulations

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), all orders and regulations under this Order shall be subject to negative resolution.

(2) Paragraph (1) does not apply to an order under Article 1(2),[^c16879801] 84A(1)(b),[^c16879811] 90(14) , 90AA(6)[^c22320611] or 95(12).

(3) Regulations and orders under this Order may contain incidental, supplementary and transitional provisions.
