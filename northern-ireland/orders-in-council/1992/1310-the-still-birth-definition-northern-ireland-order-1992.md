# The Still-Birth (Definition) (Northern Ireland) Order 1992

> Text as at 2006-01-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

1992 No. 1310 (N.I. 10)

The Still-Birth (Definition) (Northern Ireland) Order 1992

4th June 1992

## 1. Title and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Still-Birth (Definition) (Northern Ireland) Order 1992 and shall come into operation on 1st October 1992.

## 2. Interpretation

The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^c547816] shall apply to Article 1 and the following provisions of this Order as it applies to a Measure of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

## 3. Meaning of “still-birth”

In Article 2(2) of the Births and Deaths Registration (Northern Ireland) Order 1976[^c547817] (interpretation), in the definition of “still-birth”, for the words “twenty-eighth week” there shall be substituted the words “twenty-fourth week”.

## 4. Meaning of “confinement” for certain social security purposes

In the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992[^c547818]

- (a) in section 35(6) (definition of “confinement” for the purpose of maternity allowance), and
- (b) in section 167(1) (interpretation of Part XII-statutory maternity pay), in the definition of “confinement”,

for the words “28 weeks” there shall be substituted the words “24 weeks”.
