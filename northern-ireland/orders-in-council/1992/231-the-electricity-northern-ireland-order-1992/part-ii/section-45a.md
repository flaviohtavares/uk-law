# The Electricity (Northern Ireland) Order 1992, section 45A

## 45A. Information to be given to customers about overall performance[^c20662761]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c20667501] Each electricity supplier and each electricity distributor[^c20667511] shall, in such form and manner and with such frequency as the Director may direct, take steps to inform customers of electricity suppliers[^c20667531] of—

- (a) the standards of overall performance determined under Article 43 or, as the case may be Article 43A[^c20667551] which are applicable to that supplier or distributor[^c20667571]; and
- (b) that supplier's or that distributor's[^c20667601] level of performance as respects each of those standards.

(2) In giving any such direction, the Director shall not specify a frequency of less than once in every period of 12 months.
