## 36. Fossil fuel levy

*Extent: N.I.*

Where the Department has made one or more orders under Article 35 in relation to holders of a licence under Article 10(1)(b) or (c), regulations may provide—

- (a)[^c20660011] for the imposition on such licence holders, and on other persons authorised by a licence under Article 10(1)(c), of a levy;
- (b) for the collection of payments in respect of that levy by a prescribed person; and
- (c) for the making by that person of payments out of that levy.
