## 72. Supplementary provisions as to transfers under Article 69

*Extent: N.I.*

The provisions of Schedule 10 shall apply, to the extent there mentioned, to any transfer which is effected by Article 69(4); and that paragraph shall have effect subject to the provisions of that Schedule.
