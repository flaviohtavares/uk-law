# The Electricity (Northern Ireland) Order 1992, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (9).** Text as at 2025-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/1992/231
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 1. Title and commencement

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This Order may be cited as the Electricity (Northern Ireland) Order 1992.

(2) This Order shall come into operation on such day or days as the Head of the Department may by order appoint[^c17136301].

(3) An order under paragraph (2) may also appoint a day for the coming into operation of any provision of an order made under section 38(2) of the Northern Ireland Constitution Act 1973[^c17136311] as necessary or expedient in consequence of this Order which appears to the Head of the Department to be consequential on any provision of this Order brought into operation by the order.
