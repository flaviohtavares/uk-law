## 2. Interpretation

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^c17136321] shall apply to Article 1 and the following provisions of this Order as it applies to a Measure of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

(2) In this Order—

- “[^c22107811]**the CMA**” means the Competition and Markets Authority;
- “the Department” means the Department of Economic Development;
- *Definition rep. by 2003 NI 6*
- “modifications” includes additions, omissions, amendments and substitutions;
- “prescribed” means prescribed by regulations;
- “regulations”
  - (a) except in Article 42 and Schedule 7, means regulations made by the Department;
  - (b) in Article 42 and Schedule 7, means regulations made by the Director, with the consent of the Department;
- “statutory provision” has the meaning assigned to it by section 1(f) of the Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954.
