> Text as at 2020-08-29.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 9. Tenancy agreements, etc.

*Extent: N.I.*

Articles 25 and 26A of the Order of 1981 (which relate to the substitution or variation of tenancies of houses let by the Executive and to the length of notice to quit required in respect of such tenancies) shall apply to registered housing associations and their tenants in the same manner as they apply to the Executive and its tenants.
