# The Housing (Northern Ireland) Order 1992, section 10

> Text as at 2020-08-29.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 10. Persons eligible for housing accommodation

*Extent: N.I.*

Except with the consent of the Department, a housing association shall not—

- (a) if it is a registered housing association, accept as a tenant of any housing accommodation provided by it; or
- (b) if it is not a registered housing association—
  - (i) accept as a tenant of housing accommodation provided by it under section 1 of the Housing Act (Northern Ireland) 1945[^c11665451] as applied by section 12 of the Housing and Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act (Northern Ireland) 1946[^c11665461], or
  - (ii) accept as an occupier of accommodation in a hostel provided by it in respect of which any contribution has been made to it under section 15 of the Housing Act (Northern Ireland) 1963[^c11665471],

any person whom the Executive would be precluded by a scheme made under Article 22 of the Order of 1981 from accepting as a tenant of housing accommodation provided by it.
