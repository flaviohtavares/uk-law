# The Housing (Northern Ireland) Order 1992, section 7

> Text as at 2020-08-29.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 7. Recovery of possession of premises let by a housing association

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), on the termination of the tenancy of any premises let by a housing association, possession of the premises may (without prejudice to any other method of recovery) be recovered by the housing association in a summary manner under Part VI of the Magistrates' Courts (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^c11665381] relating to ejectment proceedings, whatever may be the rent or term of the tenancy.

(2) Paragraph (1) does not apply to any tenancy which is a secure tenancy within the meaning of Article 25 of the Order of 1983.
