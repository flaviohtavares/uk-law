# The Housing (Northern Ireland) Order 1992, section 106

> Text as at 2020-08-29.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 106. Orders and regulations

*Extent: N.I.*

Any order (other than a vesting order or an order under Article 1(3) or 25) or regulations made by the Department under this Order shall be subject to negative resolution.
