## 108. Repeals, transitional provisions and savings

*Extent: N.I.*

*Para. (1)—Repeals*

(2) The Department may by order make such transitional provisions and savings (whether or not involving the modification of any statutory provision) as it considers necessary or expedient in consequence of the provision made by this Order.
