# The Housing (Northern Ireland) Order 1992, section 91

> Text as at 2020-08-29.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 91. Continuance of certain powers to deal with unoccupied premises

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The provisions of Chapter V of Part III of the Order of 1981 (unoccupied premises) which are in force immediately before the day of the coming into operation of this Article, shall continue in force for the time being.

(2) Article 64 of the Order of 1981 (which provides as to the duration of that Chapter) shall cease to have effect.
