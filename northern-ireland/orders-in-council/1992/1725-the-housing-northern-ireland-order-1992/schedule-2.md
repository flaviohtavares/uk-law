# The Housing (Northern Ireland) Order 1992, SCHEDULE 2

> Text as at 2020-08-29.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 2 — GRANT-AIDED LAND

*Extent: N.I.*

Article 13A(1)[^key-9ebeec239c222c7deaed00f743077e0f]

**1.** For the purposes of Article 13A[^key-db4cc69e02b0c577fcd58eb3799b86c0] “grant-aided land” means land—

- (a) in respect of which such payment as is specified in paragraph 2 falls to be made in respect of a period ending after 10th November 1976; or
- (b) on which is or has been secured a loan which is of a description specified in paragraph 3 and in respect of which any repayment (whether by way of principal or interest or both) falls to be made after that date.

**2.** The payments referred to in paragraph 1(a) are contributions which became payable before 10th November 1976—

- (a) by way of exchequer contributions under section 1 of the Housing Act (Northern Ireland) 1945[^c11665881] as applied to housing associations by virtue of section 12 of the Housing and Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act (Northern Ireland) 1946[^c11665891];
- (b) by way of exchequer contributions under section 15 of the Housing Act (Northern Ireland) 1963[^c11665901] (contributions for hostels).

**3.** The loans referred to in paragraph 1(b) are—

- (a) loans under section 14 of the Housing (Ireland) Act 1919[^c11665911], as applied to the Executive by virtue of section 3(1) of the Housing Executive Act (Northern Ireland) 1971[^c11665921] (powers of promoting and assisting public utility societies); and
- (b) loans under section 44 of the Housing Act (Northern Ireland) 1956[^c11665931] (power to make loans to certain associations).
