## 101. Service of documents under Rent (Northern Ireland) Order 1978

*Extent: N.I.*

After Article 73 (service of notices on landlord's agents) of the Rent (Northern Ireland) Order 1978[^c11665731] there shall be inserted the following Article—

> **73A. Method of serving certain documents**
>
> (1) Any document to be served under any of the following provisions of this Order, namely Articles 8, 9, 10, 25(6), 25A(2), 26(4), 46 and 73(2) and paragraph 2(1) of Schedule 6 may be served by being sent by ordinary post.
>
> (2) In section 24(1) of the Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 (service of documents), as it applies to the service by post of such a document, the word “registering” shall be omitted.
