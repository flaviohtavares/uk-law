## 5. Duty of employers to facilitate access to stakeholder pension schemes

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Except in so far as regulations otherwise provide, it shall be the duty of an employer of relevant employees to comply with the requirement in paragraph (5)[^c-fd48978174a29a06f15f3b8bb7b7fe10] .

(1A)[^c-675b73039d5f1da69fe5dc39413e8a90] A relevant employee, in relation to an employer, is an employee of the employer who, on the relevant date, satisfies the conditions in paragraph (1B).

(1B) The conditions are that—

- (a) the employee is a member of a stakeholder pension scheme;
- (b) the employee made a request under paragraph (5) before the relevant date and that request has not been withdrawn;
- (c) the employee pays contributions (which are deducted in accordance with that request) to the scheme at regular intervals;
- (d) at least one deduction has been made before the relevant date in accordance with that request.

(1C) A person ceases to be a relevant employee—

- (a) on ceasing to be employed by the employer;
- (b) on withdrawing a request under paragraph (5);
- (c) on ceasing to pay contributions at regular intervals.

(2)[^c-362b825ba76fa786da06da8c1fca7926] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c-362b825ba76fa786da06da8c1fca7926] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^c-362b825ba76fa786da06da8c1fca7926] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) The [^c-5e060c5e30203d466f8d7145996e414d]... requirement is that, subject to such exceptions and qualifications as may be prescribed, the employer shall, if he is requested to do so by a relevant employee [^c-feff6d11563df86a0f39694f9fcefd8d]...—

- (a) deduct the employee's contributions to the stakeholder pension scheme[^c-ca83c6bc64c4511cbe91996e609af2bf] from his remuneration; and
- (b) pay them to the trustees or managers of the scheme or, if regulations so provide, to a prescribed person.

(5A)[^c-3f5c9b72fd01682e3e6f134c92a4de10] That requirement only applies in relation to a request to make deductions made before the relevant date (whether or not that request is varied after that date).

(6)[^c-ad08c627cd30687ccf7db51d0310dd0c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7) Article 10 of the Pensions Order (civil penalties) applies to an employer who fails to comply with the requirement[^c-527633e7025bd7f990c40f9c8cc000cf] set out above.

(8) An employer is not, while subject to the requirement in paragraph (5)[^c-3ecdf02ef2052ae1399524fbb8989e11] , under any duty—

- (a) to make any enquiries, or act on any information, about the scheme for any purpose not connected with—
  - (i) ascertaining whether the scheme is for the time being registered under Article 4,
  - (ii)[^c-992c69127e62cffcaccfc4e4e8845d82] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (iii)[^c-8b3d838671419a0cdca18d5adcb45956] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) in particular, to investigate or monitor, or make any judgment as to, the past, present or future performance of the scheme.

(9) In this Article—

- “employer” means any employer, whether or not resident or incorporated in any part of the United Kingdom;
- [^c-2e2eed0abc68d24042bfe5d895a9f069]...
- “regulations” means regulations made by the Department;
- “[^c-bfea0c3e767a3f972c220a48d76ec561]**relevant date**” means the date on which section 69 of the Pensions (No. 2) Act (Northern Ireland) 2008 comes into operation.
- [^c-a0ac63c12fb45135f2b46c693c509dbf]...
