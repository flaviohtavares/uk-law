# The Welfare Reform and Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1999, section 62

## 62. Abolition of severe disablement allowance

*Extent: N.I.*

Sections 68 and 69 of the Contributions and Benefits Act (severe disablement allowance) shall cease to have effect.
