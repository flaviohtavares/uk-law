# The Welfare Reform and Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1999, section 41

## 41. Disapplication of restrictions on alienation

*Extent: N.I.*

Nothing in any of the following provisions (restrictions on alienation of pension rights) applies in relation to any order or provision falling within Article 25(1)—

- (a) section 155(4) and (4A) of the Pension Schemes Act,
- (b) Article 89 of the Pensions Order,
- (c) any statutory provision (whether passed or made before or after this Order is made) corresponding to any of the statutory provisions mentioned in paragraphs (a) and (b), and
- (d) any provision of a pension arrangement corresponding to any of those statutory provisions.
