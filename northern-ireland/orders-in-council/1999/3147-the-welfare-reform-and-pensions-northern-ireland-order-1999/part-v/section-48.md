# The Welfare Reform and Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1999, section 48

## 48. Interpretation of Chapter II

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-35ad9d0db5f9e785e66d2e7a48979cf8] In this Chapter—

- "shareable state scheme rights”, and related expressions, have the meaning given by Article 44;[^c-e88b35cc5696f933c33c62b76eb2ad59] and
- “tax year” has the meaning given by section 121(1) of the Contributions and Benefits Act.

(2)[^c-8b822ad09eb9d6695a3e26a9f5f3e568] For the purposes of this Chapter—

- (a) a person is in the old state pension system if the person reached pensionable age before 6 April 2016 (or would have done so if the person had lived until pensionable age), and
- (b) a person is in the new state pension system if the person reached pensionable age on or after 6 April 2016 (or will do so if the person lives until pensionable age).
