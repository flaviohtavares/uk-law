# The Water (Northern Ireland) Order 1999, section 33

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 33. Maps of fresh-water limits of waterways

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department shall—

- (a) maintain maps showing what appear to the Department to be the fresh-water limits of every waterway; and
- (b) make such maps available, at all reasonable times, for inspection by the public free of charge.

(2) This Article does not apply to any waterway of a description specified in an order made by the Department subject to negative resolution.
