> Text as at 2024-01-01.

## 55. Financial provision[^key-80effcb8f6c831bf6595d84cd37f8620]

*Extent: N.I.*

Any sum received by the Department under this Part shall be appropriated in aid of the expenses incurred by the Department under this Part or in the payment of any contributions or grants under this Part.
