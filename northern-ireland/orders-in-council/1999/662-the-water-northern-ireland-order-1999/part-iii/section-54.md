# The Water (Northern Ireland) Order 1999, section 54

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 54. Power to give effect to assimilated obligations, etc.[^c-80effcb8f6c831bf6595d84cd37f8620][^c-bd24b03164924fa2fc319398949ec0a8]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Regulations may provide that the provisions of this Part shall have effect with such modifications as may be prescribed for the purpose of giving effect to any[^c-345ad1d74def30c8256a48d1b6b8a759] assimilated[^c-7054cfa578995e6373e87c9fcccdd5e3] obligation or enabling Her Majesty’s Government in the United Kingdom to exercise any related right.

(2) In this Article “related right”, in relation to an obligation, includes any derogation or other right to make more onerous provisions available in respect of that obligation.
