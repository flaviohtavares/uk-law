> Text as at 2024-01-01.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 60. Protection of watercourses – penalty in relation to nuisance

*Extent: N.I.*

In paragraph 6(ii)(A) of Schedule 6 to the Drainage Order for the words “not exceeding £100” there shall be substituted the words “not exceeding £1,000 or such other sum as the Department may specify by order subject to affirmative resolution”.
