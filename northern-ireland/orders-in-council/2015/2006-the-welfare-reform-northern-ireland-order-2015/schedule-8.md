# The Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015, SCHEDULE 8

## SCHEDULE 8 — Social fund discretionary payments: consequential amendments

*Extent: N.I.*

Article 76(8)

**1.**[^key-fab27ed686bcd9f7016471d2e07c5a89] The Administration Act is amended as follows.

**2.**[^key-d69e1c6fea8a7f314071fdca13ade13b] In section 74(4) (recovery of funeral payments out of social fund), after “Payments” insert “ out of the social fund ”.

**3.**[^key-b8b6515d61e1596c68e29a675ac743b8][^key-c2bb6e38a27c3908972c15d4d294724b] In section 75 (recovery of Great Britain payments), in paragraph (c), for the words from “under subsection” to the end substitute “ under section 69(8) ”.

**4.**[^key-d92bdbf77f6ec29ff51dcbbd29178e68] In Part 1 of Schedule 4 (persons employed in social security administration), under the heading “Former statutory bodies”, after the entry for “A social fund officer” insert—

> The social fund Commissioner. A social fund inspector. A member of any staff employed in connection with the social fund.
