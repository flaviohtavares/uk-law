# The Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015, section 127

## 127. Collection of child support maintenance

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-528c6aa2b7bed40235fc3098eede346e] The Child Support (Northern Ireland) Order 1991 is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 7 (child support maintenance)—

- (a)[^key-528c6aa2b7bed40235fc3098eede346e] in paragraph (2), the words from “or” to “made” are repealed;
- (b)[^key-0755bae0026eaf0ec8b0a673e559c71c] after paragraph (2) insert—

  > (2A) The Department may only make arrangements under paragraph (2)(a) if—
  >
  > - (a) the non-resident parent agrees to the arrangements, or
  > - (b) the Department is satisfied that without the arrangements child support maintenance is unlikely to be paid in accordance with the calculation.

(3)[^key-96da14df1452ad6e27ae2e90281dee1a] In Article 29 (collection of child support maintenance), in paragraph (1) after “may” insert “ (subject to Article 7(2A)) ”.
