## 139. Duty to report on operation of this Order[^c-97268af18770157f077c1b9b1ad21a55]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department must, not later than 3 years after this Order is made, publish a report on the operation of this Order.

(2) The Department must lay the report before the Assembly.
