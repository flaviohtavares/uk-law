# The Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015, section 106

>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 106. Payments on account[^c-ed5907f0f565ed5c6ebd94510affc886]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) In section 5(1) of the Administration Act (regulations about claims and payments), for paragraph (s) substitute—

> - (s) for the making of a payment on account of such a benefit—
>   - (i) in cases where it is impracticable for a claim to be made or determined immediately, or for an award to be determined or paid in full immediately,
>   - (ii) in cases of need, or
>   - (iii) in cases where the Department considers in accordance with prescribed criteria that the payment can reasonably be expected to be recovered;

.

(2) Section 18 of the Welfare Reform Act (Northern Ireland) 2010 (payments on account), which has not been brought into operation, is repealed.
