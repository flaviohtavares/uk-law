## 105. Payments to joint claimants[^c-1e64cd951a3d10f47e29b3cda446d774]

*Extent: N.I.*

In section 5 of the Administration Act (regulations about claims and payments), after subsection (2A) insert—

> (2B) The power in subsection (1)(j) to make provision for the person to whom a benefit is to be paid includes, in the case of a benefit awarded to persons jointly, power to make provision for the Department to determine to which of them all or any part of a payment should be made, and in particular for the Department—
>
> - (a) to determine that payment should be made to whichever of those persons they themselves nominate, or
> - (b) to determine that payment should be made to one of them irrespective of any nomination by them.
