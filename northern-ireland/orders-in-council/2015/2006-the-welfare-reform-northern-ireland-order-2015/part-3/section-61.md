## 61. Work experience etc.[^key-9837da5d869fa1670531861051d6b31c]

*Extent: N.I.*

In section 13 of the Welfare Reform Act (Northern Ireland) 2007 (work-related activity), after subsection (7) insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ea252cc7-147b-48af-b97f-02f3fd61d697]

> (8) The reference to activity in subsection (7) includes work experience or a work placement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ea252cc7-147b-48af-b97f-02f3fd61d697]
