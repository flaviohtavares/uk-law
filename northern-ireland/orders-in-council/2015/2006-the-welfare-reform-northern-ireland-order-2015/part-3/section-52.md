> **21. Higher-level sanctions (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_db124ccf-3b85-47a5-8e89-d5d51946e53b]**
>
> (1) The amount of an award of a jobseeker's allowance is to be reduced in accordance with this Article in the event of a failure by the claimant which is sanctionable under this Article. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_db124ccf-3b85-47a5-8e89-d5d51946e53b]
>
> (2) It is a failure sanctionable under this Article if a claimant— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_db124ccf-3b85-47a5-8e89-d5d51946e53b]
>
> - (a) through misconduct loses employment as an employed earner; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_db124ccf-3b85-47a5-8e89-d5d51946e53b]
> - (b) without a good reason voluntarily leaves such employment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_db124ccf-3b85-47a5-8e89-d5d51946e53b]
> - (c) without a good reason refuses or fails to apply for, or accept if offered, a situation in any employment which an employment officer has informed him is vacant or about to become vacant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_db124ccf-3b85-47a5-8e89-d5d51946e53b]
> - (d) without a good reason neglects to avail himself of a reasonable opportunity of employment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_db124ccf-3b85-47a5-8e89-d5d51946e53b]
> - (e) without a good reason fails to participate in any scheme within Article 19A(1) which is prescribed for the purposes of this Article. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_db124ccf-3b85-47a5-8e89-d5d51946e53b]
>
> (3) For the purposes of paragraph (2)(b), in such circumstances as may be prescribed, including in particular where a person has been dismissed by his employer by reason of redundancy within the meaning of Article 174(1) of the Employment Rights (Northern Ireland) Order 1996 after volunteering or agreeing to be so dismissed, a person who might otherwise be treated as having left his employment voluntarily is to be treated as not having left voluntarily. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_db124ccf-3b85-47a5-8e89-d5d51946e53b]
>
> (4) Regulations are to provide for— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_db124ccf-3b85-47a5-8e89-d5d51946e53b]
>
> - (a) the amount of a reduction under this Article; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_db124ccf-3b85-47a5-8e89-d5d51946e53b]
> - (b) the period for which such a reduction has effect, not exceeding 18 months in relation to any failure sanctionable under this Article. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_db124ccf-3b85-47a5-8e89-d5d51946e53b]
>
> (5) Regulations under paragraph (4)(b) may in particular provide for the period of a reduction to depend on either or both of the following— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_db124ccf-3b85-47a5-8e89-d5d51946e53b]
>
> - (a) the number of failures by the claimant sanctionable under this Article; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_db124ccf-3b85-47a5-8e89-d5d51946e53b]
> - (b) the period between such failures. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_db124ccf-3b85-47a5-8e89-d5d51946e53b]
>
> (6) Regulations may provide— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_db124ccf-3b85-47a5-8e89-d5d51946e53b]
>
> - (a) for cases in which no reduction is to be made under this Article; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_db124ccf-3b85-47a5-8e89-d5d51946e53b]
> - (b) for a reduction under this Article made in relation to an award that is terminated to be applied to any new award made within a prescribed period of the termination. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_db124ccf-3b85-47a5-8e89-d5d51946e53b]
>
> (7) During any period for which the amount of a joint-claim jobseeker's allowance is reduced under this Article by virtue of a failure by one of the claimants which is sanctionable under this Article, the allowance is payable to the other member of the couple. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_db124ccf-3b85-47a5-8e89-d5d51946e53b]

> **21A. Other sanctions (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_db124ccf-3b85-47a5-8e89-d5d51946e53b]**
>
> (1) The amount of an award of a jobseeker's allowance is to be reduced in accordance with this Article in the event of a failure by the claimant which is sanctionable under this Article. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_db124ccf-3b85-47a5-8e89-d5d51946e53b]
>
> (2) It is a failure sanctionable under this Article if a claimant— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_db124ccf-3b85-47a5-8e89-d5d51946e53b]
>
> - (a) without a good reason fails to comply with regulations under Article 10(1) or (1A); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_db124ccf-3b85-47a5-8e89-d5d51946e53b]
> - (b) without a good reason fails to comply with regulations under Article 19A; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_db124ccf-3b85-47a5-8e89-d5d51946e53b]
> - (c) without a good reason refuses or fails to carry out a jobseeker's direction which was reasonable having regard to his circumstances; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_db124ccf-3b85-47a5-8e89-d5d51946e53b]
> - (d) without a good reason neglects to avail himself of a reasonable opportunity of a place on a training scheme or employment programme; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_db124ccf-3b85-47a5-8e89-d5d51946e53b]
> - (e) without a good reason refuses or fails to apply for, or accept if offered, a place on such a scheme or programme which an employment officer has informed him is vacant or about to become vacant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_db124ccf-3b85-47a5-8e89-d5d51946e53b]
> - (f) without a good reason gives up a place on such a scheme or programme or fails to attend such a scheme or programme having been given a place on it; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_db124ccf-3b85-47a5-8e89-d5d51946e53b]
> - (g) through misconduct loses a place on such a scheme or programme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_db124ccf-3b85-47a5-8e89-d5d51946e53b]
>
> (3) But a failure is not sanctionable under this Article if it is also sanctionable under Article 21. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_db124ccf-3b85-47a5-8e89-d5d51946e53b]
