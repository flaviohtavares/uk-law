# The Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015, section 60

## 60. Claimant commitment for employment and support allowance[^c-41db15b31786da2cefb8e32d4da1e08e]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-e962afb3fa7890a319961d9c186d0728][^c-d7ac1a2feb362e15ecfd2c33d2ca8c5f] The Welfare Reform Act (Northern Ireland) 2007 is amended as follows.

(2)[^c-e962afb3fa7890a319961d9c186d0728][^c-d7ac1a2feb362e15ecfd2c33d2ca8c5f] In section 1(3) (employment and support allowance) after paragraph (a) insert—

> - (aa) has accepted a claimant commitment,

.

(3) After section 1B (as inserted by Article 58) insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2791ddb5-1361-4b91-adef-1a74dd4c1082]

> **1C. Claimant commitment (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_2791ddb5-1361-4b91-adef-1a74dd4c1082]**
>
> (1) For the purposes of this Part a “claimant commitment” is a record of the claimant's responsibilities in relation to an award of an employment and support allowance. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2791ddb5-1361-4b91-adef-1a74dd4c1082]
>
> (2) A claimant commitment is to be prepared by the Department or the Department for Employment and Learning and may be reviewed and updated as that department thinks fit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2791ddb5-1361-4b91-adef-1a74dd4c1082]
>
> (3) A claimant commitment is to be in such form as the Department thinks fit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2791ddb5-1361-4b91-adef-1a74dd4c1082]
>
> (4) A claimant commitment is to include— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2791ddb5-1361-4b91-adef-1a74dd4c1082]
>
> - (a) any prescribed information, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2791ddb5-1361-4b91-adef-1a74dd4c1082]
> - (b) any other information the Department or the Department for Employment and Learning considers it appropriate to include. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2791ddb5-1361-4b91-adef-1a74dd4c1082]
>
> (5) For the purposes of this Part a claimant accepts a claimant commitment if, and only if, the claimant accepts the most up-to-date version of it in such manner as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2791ddb5-1361-4b91-adef-1a74dd4c1082]
>
> (6) Regulations may provide that, in prescribed circumstances, a claimant is to be treated as having satisfied the condition mentioned in section 1(3)(aa). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2791ddb5-1361-4b91-adef-1a74dd4c1082]

(4) In section 15(2)(b) (directions about work-related activity) for the words from “by” to “14” substitute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_f676decb-8c70-4371-b8c0-bf51d3c012e8] “ in such manner as the Department or the Department for Employment and Learning thinks fit ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_f676decb-8c70-4371-b8c0-bf51d3c012e8].

(5) In section 16(1) (contracting out) before paragraph (a) insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_279a3d59-4d9f-4bae-e326-ca6bde9a05cf]

> - (za (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_279a3d59-4d9f-4bae-e326-ca6bde9a05cf] any function under section 1C in relation to a claimant commitment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_279a3d59-4d9f-4bae-e326-ca6bde9a05cf]

.

(6) In Schedule 2 (supplementary provisions) after paragraph 4 insert—

> **Exemption**
>
> **4A.**
>
> Regulations may prescribe circumstances in which a person may be entitled to employment and support allowance without having accepted a claimant commitment.

(7) In section 25(2) of the Welfare Reform Act (Northern Ireland) 2010 (action plans: well-being of children), in subsection (5) inserted into section 14 of the Welfare Reform Act (Northern Ireland) 2007—

- (a) after “preparing any” insert “ claimant commitment or ”;
- (b) after “action plan,” insert “ the Department or ”.
