# The Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015, section 95

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 95. Abolition of disability living allowance

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Sections 71 to 76 of the Contributions and Benefits Act (disability living allowance) are repealed.
