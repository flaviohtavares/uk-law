## 97. Power to make supplementary and consequential provision[^key-32d257b0c29135ca4e6197f4e3bc41df]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Regulations may make such consequential, supplementary or incidental provision in relation to any provision of this Part as the Department considers appropriate.

(2) Regulations under this Article may amend, repeal, revoke or otherwise modify any statutory provision.
