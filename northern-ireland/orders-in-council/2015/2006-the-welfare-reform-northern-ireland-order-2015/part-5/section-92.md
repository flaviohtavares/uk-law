# The Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015, section 92

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (30).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/2015/2006
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 92. Prisoners[^c-1a6a91bc56feacedcea5ad0591509f32][^c-1da0daf57bda1317d697bbf574f92eee][^c-a5b450590807f4644b913ee701d6917e][^c-b153d8f1eac8b200669b6543f06b420e][^c-0fbcebe3ee28d8b56b5452bdba5fdd75]

*Extent: N.I.*

Except to the extent that regulations provide otherwise, no amount in respect of personal independence payment is payable in respect of a person for a period during which the person is undergoing imprisonment or detention in legal custody.
