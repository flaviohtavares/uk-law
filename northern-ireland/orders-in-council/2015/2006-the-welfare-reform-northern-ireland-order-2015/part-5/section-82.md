## 82. Personal independence payment

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-a8b7f62aa9bdb4176beb01d8a4d4aee4] An allowance known as personal independence payment is payable in accordance with this Part.

(2)[^key-a8b7f62aa9bdb4176beb01d8a4d4aee4] A person's entitlement to personal independence payment may be an entitlement to—

- (a) the daily living component (see Article 83);
- (b) the mobility component (see Article 84); or
- (c) both those components.

(3)[^key-56a26a62ac3f5afbfc02aedcfe16a439][^key-43d30e000c11e2a377f6b9f7564d91a0] A person is not entitled to personal independence payment unless the person meets prescribed conditions relating to residence and presence in Northern Ireland.

(4)[^key-c1c978c610faa3642f6ca39d2f315e47] A person is not entitled to personal independence payment while the person is entitled to—

- (a) child disability payment,
- (b) adult disability payment, [^key-acb63b9ef305f5fd981164c86b21d418]...
- (c) pension age disability payment , or[^key-26d371b54ffb9d5e2e445a6d8e247391]
- (d)[^key-e2e8944caef7a2a2b2cf6c4fda401dad] Scottish adult disability living allowance.
