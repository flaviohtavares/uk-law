## 88. Persons of pensionable age[^key-d44f389f9aa55bbaeb9482c85ff778e9][^key-2986ca2507fb64c6b4c7280107fc1ffe]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-dc70e025247507ee7935218238cb0824][^key-b5b013c7fbecb98d6ee600441bcbb009][^key-78e7ff7b2b66988f5eba83d8335c15d1][^key-c48b3ff0b2ae52af1bd23c2747075881][^key-fcf6b7d3d18ee4819a6fb8d6ef611dd2] A person is not entitled to the daily living component or the mobility component for any period after the person reaches the relevant age.

(2) In paragraph (1) “**the relevant age**” means—

- (a) pensionable age (within the meaning given by the rules in paragraph 1 of Schedule 2 to the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1995); or
- (b) if higher, 65.

(3) Paragraph (1) is subject to such exceptions as may be provided by regulations.
