## 94. Report to the Assembly[^c-b6c6ffbb53649b410cad5737428ed586]

*Extent: N.I.*

The Department must lay before the Assembly an independent report on the operation of assessments under Article 85—

- (a) within 2 years beginning with the date on which the first regulations under that Article come into operation; and
- (b) within 4 years beginning with that date.
