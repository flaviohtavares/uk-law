# The Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015, section 39

## 39. Abolition of benefits

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The following benefits are abolished—

- (a)[^key-408caeeb623be8e74e8253d9834ac0d4][^key-05fcf8dc6392b2bdc39fc6c70f74b33b][^key-e9e2465b8628566a76eec0dc7fe995df][^key-c400a23e9c31136ceff60b615e4ab360][^key-9be5fff7a6385b2aa9d0a8c03e49b366][^key-4049f4252e51878acd8028cdf50e936d][^key-d8cb59d88c2ecd615dc4021e3b9aa6cb][^key-a7bc89cd337bf79e2d496164a314d605][^M_I_3e03dc13-433f-424f-e29e-9f33a5925fec] income-based jobseeker's allowance under the Jobseekers Order;
- (b)[^key-408caeeb623be8e74e8253d9834ac0d4][^key-05fcf8dc6392b2bdc39fc6c70f74b33b][^key-e9e2465b8628566a76eec0dc7fe995df][^key-c400a23e9c31136ceff60b615e4ab360][^key-9be5fff7a6385b2aa9d0a8c03e49b366][^key-4049f4252e51878acd8028cdf50e936d][^key-d8cb59d88c2ecd615dc4021e3b9aa6cb][^key-a7bc89cd337bf79e2d496164a314d605] income-related employment and support allowance under Part 1 of the Welfare Reform Act (Northern Ireland) 2007;
- (c)[^key-525278bc0bb8263123f8d9879afd4aaa] income support under section 123 of the Contributions and Benefits Act;
- (d)[^key-07b52b8ee10424dd0e1dbbd461bafe76][^M_I_e63b7373-d3de-4ab4-c0ac-65d87db7d665] housing benefit under section 129 of that Act.

(2)[^key-408caeeb623be8e74e8253d9834ac0d4][^key-05fcf8dc6392b2bdc39fc6c70f74b33b][^key-e9e2465b8628566a76eec0dc7fe995df][^key-c400a23e9c31136ceff60b615e4ab360][^key-9be5fff7a6385b2aa9d0a8c03e49b366][^key-4049f4252e51878acd8028cdf50e936d][^key-d8cb59d88c2ecd615dc4021e3b9aa6cb][^key-a7bc89cd337bf79e2d496164a314d605] In paragraph (1)—

- (a) “**income-based jobseeker's allowance**” has the same meaning as in the Jobseekers Order;
- (b) “**income-related employment and support allowance**” means an employment and support allowance entitlement to which is based on section 1(2)(b) of the Welfare Reform Act (Northern Ireland) 2007.

(3)[^key-6442c943d2fca089d5cbe575539ba5c1][^key-d8cb59d88c2ecd615dc4021e3b9aa6cb][^key-a7bc89cd337bf79e2d496164a314d605] Schedule 3 contains consequential amendments.
