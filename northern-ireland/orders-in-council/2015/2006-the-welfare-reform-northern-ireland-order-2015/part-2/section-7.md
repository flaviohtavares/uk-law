## 7. Claims[^c-301cf38f8ce4a785e6163912e423062e][^c-b8d32d6e1e16d3ec612c4afc25382969][^c-bc568d7a2604030ce95c1a4c4f4c7027][^c-73c04cfeca744347f85c4c64d16ff2c7][^c-f17e4fac8d7a751c0fe6b970df4a0f98][^c-bb3013233eedc424350816dac1a120de][^c-7133fec6bbc149fcc72311e86f76aca2][^c-21fb1dbce9e255f94a9ae13b4c6de589]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A claim may be made for universal credit by—

- (a) a single person, or
- (b) members of a couple jointly.

(2) Regulations may specify circumstances in which a member of a couple may make a claim as a single person.
