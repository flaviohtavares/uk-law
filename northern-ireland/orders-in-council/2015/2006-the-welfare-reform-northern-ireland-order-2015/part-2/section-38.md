## 38. Power to make supplementary and consequential provision etc.[^c-69bec839c095812e9768b1745aec8ae3]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department may by regulations make such consequential, supplementary, incidental or transitional provision in relation to any provision of this Part as the Department considers appropriate.

(2) Regulations under this Article may amend, repeal or revoke any statutory provision (whenever passed or made).
