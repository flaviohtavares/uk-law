## 15. Responsibility for children and young persons[^key-bf772b7681959ae090f83cf14ae2d343][^key-1d7ea1821b2e0a6544b1cf9ea94337df][^key-96dc7c8f387f3b7a35396c0aa49657b0][^key-edddd31b1efee997a37bb5e04379eb02][^key-7b9d3a89400740a9fe445cc856ed29d9][^key-96f52b5abfdd8a1dd43646e9de52e2ca][^key-dd5b38cd464672a76feacbf23f200e72][^key-63b7451e20b7067d14db2309284aef52]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The calculation of an award of universal credit is to include an amount for each child or qualifying young person for whom a claimant is responsible.

(1A)[^key-8db239fbde2b69e2e0c8e686ed728bdb] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) Regulations may make provision for the inclusion of an additional amount for each[^key-e44c5a09bd7b59574a0a9f4918898793] child or qualifying young person for whom a claimant is responsible who[^key-fa8fb5dc52d351846055ae33af67f0c1] is disabled.

(3) Regulations are to specify, or provide for the calculation of, amounts to be included under paragraph (1) or (2).

(4) Regulations may provide for exceptions to paragraph (1) [^key-749c5fcb52b30703ca58b39bb80d1318]....

(5) In this Part, “**qualifying young person**” means a person of a prescribed description.
