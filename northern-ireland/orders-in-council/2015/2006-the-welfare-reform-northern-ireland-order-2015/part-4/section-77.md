## 77. Purposes of discretionary payments[^key-becc053fead30d9f5e8431e4a95784cb]

*Extent: N.I.*

In section 134 of the Contributions and Benefits Act (payments out of the social fund), in subsection (1)(b), for “to meet other needs” substitute—

> to meet—
>
> - (i) other needs, and
> - (ii) in the case of payments by way of budgeting loan, those needs for which provision is made by paragraph (a),

.
