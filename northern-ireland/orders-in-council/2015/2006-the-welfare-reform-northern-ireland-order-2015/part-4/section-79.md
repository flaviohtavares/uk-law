## 79. Community care grants[^key-044b0f4608a44b4b315a7414952896e4]

*Extent: N.I.*

Sections 15 to 17 of the Welfare Reform Act (Northern Ireland) 2010 (provisions relating to community care grants), which have not been brought into operation, are repealed.
