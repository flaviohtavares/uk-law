# The Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015, section 74

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (30).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/2015/2006
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 74. Determinations[^key-be71d72dfb83d1ef48042c987fd14d1a]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Article 29(2) of the Social Security (Northern Ireland) Order 1998 (which provides for decisions as to whether an accident is an industrial accident in the absence of a claim for benefit) is repealed.

(2) In Article 30 of that Order (effect of decision), in paragraph (1), the words from “(given” to “otherwise)” are repealed.
