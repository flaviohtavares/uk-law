## 25. Other discrimination by the Education Authority[^key-3994fad3627178dc0c9660de7417d2da]

*Extent: N.I.*

It is unlawful for the Education Authority[^key-0cbdb4312be55df475194a317efb39ab] , in carrying out such of its functions under the Education Orders, as defined in[^c17233741] the Education and Libraries (Northern Ireland) Order 1986[^c17233761] as do not fall under Article 24, to do any act which constitutes sex discrimination.
