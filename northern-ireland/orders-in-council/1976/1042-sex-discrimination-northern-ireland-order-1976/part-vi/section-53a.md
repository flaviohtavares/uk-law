## 53A. Construction of references to vocational training[^c17234141]

*Extent: N.I.*

In Article[^c17234321] 53ZA[^c17234341] “vocational training” includes advanced vocational training and retraining; and any reference to vocational training in those provisions shall be construed as including a reference to vocational guidance.
