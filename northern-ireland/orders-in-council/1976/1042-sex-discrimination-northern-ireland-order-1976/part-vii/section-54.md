## 54. Establishment and duties of Commission

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Commission shall have the following duties[^c17234381]

- (a) to work towards the elimination of discrimination,
- (aa)[^c17234401] to work towards the elimination of harassment that is contrary to any of the provisions of this Order,
- (b) to promote equality of opportunity between men and women generally,[^c17234421]. . .
- (bb) to promote equality of opportunity [^c20779031]. . . for persons who intend to undergo, are undergoing or have undergone gender reassignment, and
- (c) to keep under review the working of this Order and the Equal Pay Act and, when the Commission is so required by the Department for Communities[^c-29795eb087fe6d1f43ec030111b56822] or otherwise thinks it necessary, draw up and submit to the Department for Communities proposals for amending them.

*Paras. (2)‐(4) rep. by 1998 c. 47*
