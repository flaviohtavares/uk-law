## 38. Discriminatory practices

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c17234011] In this Article "discriminatory practice" means—

- (...)[^c-faf0e4deb014dca5aec0e27aeb9b9226] the application of a provision, criterion or practice which results in an act of discrimination which is unlawful by virtue of any provision of Part III or IV taken with Article 3A[^c-1cb4671efa55456fc8aa969d79e9f4ad] or 5(1)(b) or which would be likely to result in such an act of discrimination if the persons to whom it is applied were not all of one sex,[^c-09377f4f0b7d60a59c2ca12c33f87d9d]...
- (b)[^c-461779bce23f46c137783abede1f7e74] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) A person acts in contravention of this Article if and so long as—

- (a) he applies a discriminatory practice, or
- (b) he operates practices or other arrangements which in any circumstances would call for the application by him of a discriminatory practice.

(3) Proceedings in respect of a contravention of this Article may be brought[^c22299931] by the Commission in accordance with Articles 67 to 71.
