# Solicitors (Northern Ireland) Order 1976, section 9A

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 9A). (not yet in force: Authorisation of Society conferring additional rights of audience)[^c22299771]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: A person who is qualified to act as a solicitor may apply to the Society for an authorisation under this Article.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: An application under paragraph (1)—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: shall be made in such manner as may be prescribed;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: shall be accompanied by such information as the Society may reasonably require for the purpose of determining the application; and)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: shall be accompanied by such fee (if any) as may be prescribed.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: At any time after receiving the application and before determining it the Society may require the applicant to provide it with further information.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: The Society shall grant an authorisation under this Article if it appears to the Society, from the information furnished by the applicant and any other information it may have, that the applicant has complied with the requirements applicable to him by virtue of regulations under Article 6(1A).)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: An authorisation granted to a person under this Article ceases to have effect if, and for so long as, that person is not qualified to act as a solicitor.)*

((not yet in force: 6)) *(not yet in force: The Society may by regulations provide that any person who has completed such education, training or experience as may be prescribed, before such date as may be prescribed shall be taken to hold an authorisation granted under this Article.)*
