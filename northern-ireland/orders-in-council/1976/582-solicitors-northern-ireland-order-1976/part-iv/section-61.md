# Solicitors (Northern Ireland) Order 1976, section 61

## 61. Power to make regulations as to procedure[^c-082446a2d95d85e59b0d756708919581]

*Extent: N.I.*

The Council may make regulations with respect to the procedure to be followed in giving effect to the provisions of this Part and Schedule 2 and with respect to any matters incidental, ancillary or supplemental to those provisions or concerning the administration or protection of the Compensation Fund.
