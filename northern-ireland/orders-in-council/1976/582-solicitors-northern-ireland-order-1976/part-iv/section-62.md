# Solicitors (Northern Ireland) Order 1976, section 62

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/1976/582
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 62. Grants may be made whether or not solicitor had practising certificate[^c-d6b07ea8792c1d67b8f2d8ec0508c96f][^c-d0fffeb6eab2b79fb7981a63a3a28acd]

*Extent: N.I.*

A grant may be made under this Part whether or not the solicitor in respect of whose act or default or in respect of whose employee's[^c17115201] act or default an application is made had a practising certificate in force at the time of the act or default and notwithstanding the fact that he has since such time died or ceased to practise or has been suspended from practice or had his name removed from or struck off the roll.
