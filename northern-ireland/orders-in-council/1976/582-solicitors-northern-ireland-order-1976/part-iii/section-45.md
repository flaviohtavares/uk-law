## 45. Procedure on application by solicitor or former solicitor[^c-2610eccc17d892d05c84d1f8ba792f72][^c17114251]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where an application is made under Article 44(1)(a), (b), (c) or (d), the Tribunal—

- (a) shall inform the Society of the application; and
- (b) shall hold an inquiry.
