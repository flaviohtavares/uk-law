## 50. Application of Bankers' Books Evidence Act 1879[^key-5be9c47f8b24d514d3b938a8199c61f2]

*Extent: N.I.*

An application to or an inquiry or other proceeding before the Tribunal[^c17114681] shall be a legal proceeding within the meaning of section 10 of the Bankers' Books Evidence Act 1879 [^m00017].
