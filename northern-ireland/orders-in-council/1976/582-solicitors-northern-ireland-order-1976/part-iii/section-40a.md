# Solicitors (Northern Ireland) Order 1976, section 40A

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 40A). (not yet in force: Duty to advise client as to representation in court)[^c22299811]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: Paragraph (2) applies where—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: it appears to a solicitor that a client requires, or is likely to require, legal representation in any proceedings in the High Court or the Court of Appeal;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: either—)*
  - ((not yet in force: i)) *(not yet in force: that solicitor is minded to arrange for another solicitor who is an authorised solicitor to provide that representation; or)*
  - ((not yet in force: ii)) *(not yet in force: that solicitor is an authorised solicitor and is minded to provide that representation; and)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: in representing that client in the High Court or Court of Appeal, a solicitor would need to exercise the right of audience conferred by section 106(3A) of the Judicature (Northern Ireland) Act 1978.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: The solicitor must advise the client in writing—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: of the advantages and disadvantages of representation by an authorised solicitor and by counsel, respectively; and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: that the decision as to whether an authorised solicitor or counsel is to represent the client is entirely that of the client.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: The Society shall make regulations with respect to the giving of advice under paragraph (2).)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: A solicitor shall—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: in advising a client under paragraph (2), act in the best interest of the client; and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: give effect to any decision of the client referred to in paragraph (2)(b).)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: For the purposes of this Article compliance with paragraph (2) in relation to any proceedings in a court in any cause or matter is to be taken to be compliance with that paragraph in relation to any other proceedings in that court in the same cause or matter.)*

((not yet in force: 6)) *(not yet in force: If a solicitor contravenes this Article, any person may make a complaint in respect of the contravention to the Tribunal.)*

((not yet in force: 7)) *(not yet in force: In this Article and Article 40B “)****(not yet in force: authorised solicitor)****(not yet in force: ” means a solicitor who holds an authorisation under Article 9A.)*
