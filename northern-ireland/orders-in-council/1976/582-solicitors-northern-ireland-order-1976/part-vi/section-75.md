# Solicitors (Northern Ireland) Order 1976, section 75

## 75. Regulations and rules

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to the provisions of this Order, the Society may make regulations—

- (a) for the purpose of the due execution of those provisions;
- (b) with respect to any matter which under this Order may or is to be prescribed or is to be provided for by regulations;

and regulations may contain such provisions as the Society may think proper for facilitating the due enforcement thereof.

(1A)[^c-b11c1bebc57d771d7d0ecee427ae1415] Before making regulations the Society shall consult the Legal Services Oversight Commissioner.

(2) All regulations shall be made with the concurrence of the Lord Chief Justice.

(3) The powers conferred by \[[^c17115391] section 55 of the Judicature (Northern Ireland) Act 1978 [^m00018]\] on the Northern Ireland Court of Judicature[^c21337621] Rules Committee to make rules of court shall be exercisable in relation to any function conferred on the Lord Chief Justice by this Order.
