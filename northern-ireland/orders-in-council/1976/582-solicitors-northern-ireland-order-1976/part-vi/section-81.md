# Solicitors (Northern Ireland) Order 1976, section 81

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/1976/582
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 81. Savings

*Extent: N.I.*

Nothing in this Order shall operate to prejudice or affect—

- (a) any inherent or other jurisdiction exercisable by any court, division or judge of the High Court or by the Court of Appeal or \[[^c17115531] the Crown Court\] or any judge of either of those Courts or of any other Court in relation to a solicitor whether by reason of his being deemed to be an officer of the Court of Judicature[^c21337631] or otherwise; or
- (b) any enactment empowering an unqualified person to conduct, defend or otherwise act in relation to any legal proceedings; or
- (c) any rights or privileges of the solicitor to any government department (including a department of the Government of the United Kingdom);

and nothing in this Order shall operate to require any solicitor such as is mentioned in paragraph ( *c*) or any clerk or officer appointed to act for him to be admitted or enrolled or to hold a practising certificate in any case where it would not have been necessary for him to be admitted or enrolled or to hold such a certificate if this Order had not been made.
