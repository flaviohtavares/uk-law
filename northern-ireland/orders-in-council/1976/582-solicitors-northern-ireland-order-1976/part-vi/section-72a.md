## 72A. Exercise of solicitors' lien on documents by Society[^c17115291]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where, under any provision of this Order or otherwise, a solicitor has delivered any document to the Society or to a solicitor appointed by the Society, the Society shall be deemed to have a lien on that document of the same nature and to the same extent as the solicitor would have had if the document had remained in his possession.
