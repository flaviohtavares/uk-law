# Financial Provisions (Northern Ireland) Order 1976

> Text as at 2006-01-01.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

1976 No. 1212 (N.I. 21)

Financial Provisions (Northern Ireland) Order 1976

\[30th July 1976\]

## 1. Title and Commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Financial Provisions (Northern Ireland) Order 1976.

(2) *Commencement*

## 2. Interpretation

(1) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 [^m00001] shall apply to Article 1 and the following provisions of this Order as it applies to a Measure of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

(2) In this Order the expression “statutory provision” has the meaning assigned to it by section 1( *f*) of the Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954.

## 15. Fee for certificate of birth, marriage, civil partnership or death for purposes of certain Acts[^c1711619][^c1711619]

(1)[^c1711621] The fee payable under section 10 of the Savings Banks Act 1887 [^m00002] for the issue of a certificate of birth, marriage, civil partnership[^c1711619] or death for the purposes of the Acts relating to the National Savings Bank,[^c1711625]... and government annuities shall be 5p or such larger sum as the Department of Finance and Personnel may by order prescribe[^c1711621] and, accordingly, … *residue amends s.10 of 1887 c.40*

(2) No order shall be made under paragraph (1) unless a draft of the order has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Assembly.[^c1711621]

*Art.16 repeals in part s.188 of 1878 c.52.*
