# Pharmacy (Northern Ireland) Order 1976, section 2

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (17).** Text as at 2026-01-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/nisi/1976/1213

## 2. Interpretation

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 [^m00001] shall apply to Article 1 and the following provisions of this Order as it applies to a Measure of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

(2)[^c-fd8d75377d5b53b5da2829d540d1c918] In this Order—

- “the Act of 1925” means the Pharmacy and Poisons Act (Northern Ireland) 1925 [^m00002];
- “the Act of 1945” means the Medicines, Pharmacy and Poisons Act (Northern Ireland) 1945 [^m00003];
- “approved bye-laws” means bye-laws—
  - (a) submitted by the Council to a meeting of the Society called, after at least fourteen days' notice of the meeting has been served on every person appearing to be a member of the Society, for the purpose of approving bye-laws so submitted (whether or not the meeting has been called for any other purposes in addition), being a meeting at which at least thirty members of the Society are present; and
  - (b) approved by a majority of the members present and voting at such a meeting;
- “associate” has the meaning assigned to it by paragraph 3(1) of Part I of Schedule 1;
- “certificate of registration” has the meaning assigned to it by Article 12(3);
- [^c-99cab26f05b1364fd9a83eb773ddb4d3]...
- “the Council” means the Council of the Society;
- “the Department” means the Department of Health and Social Services;
- [^c-8234c388b54e52d6383f8d005065ed82] ...
- [^c-99cab26f05b1364fd9a83eb773ddb4d3]...
- [^c-99cab26f05b1364fd9a83eb773ddb4d3]...
- “electronic communication” has the meaning given in section 15(1) of the Electronic Communications Act (Northern Ireland) 2001 (general interpretation);[^c-32a983e164931ff3ce56bea460f5996b]
- “enactment” has the meaning assigned to it by section 1( *b*) of the Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954;
- [^c-99cab26f05b1364fd9a83eb773ddb4d3]...
- [^c-99cab26f05b1364fd9a83eb773ddb4d3]...
- [^c-99cab26f05b1364fd9a83eb773ddb4d3]...
- [^c-99cab26f05b1364fd9a83eb773ddb4d3]...
- [^c-99cab26f05b1364fd9a83eb773ddb4d3]...
- “lay member” means a person who–[^c-326d3cd4f5c5f4111267232bfc7b7817]
  - (a) is not and has never been a registered person: and
  - (b) does not hold qualifications which would entitle him to apply for registration under this Order.
- “the Medicines Act” means the Medicines Act 1968 [^m00004];
- “member” has the meaning assigned to it by paragraph 2 of Part I of Schedule 1;
- [^c-99cab26f05b1364fd9a83eb773ddb4d3]...
- “the necessary knowledge of English”, in relation to a person registered, or desirous of being registered, in the register of pharmaceutical chemists, means a knowledge of English which is necessary for the safe and effective practice as a pharmaceutical chemist in Northern Ireland;[^c-8e097672b21dc7e80568ecdd12b13548]
- “the Pharmacy Inspector” means the inspector appointed under Article 24(1);
- “prescribed” means prescribed by regulations under Article 5;
- “professional traineeship” means a period of professional practice, carried out under supervision, that—[^c-a56136e8b18ddd2f96027c6e7cc0a864]
  - (a) constitutes a condition for access to the profession of pharmacy in the country in which it is carried out; and
  - (b) takes place during or after completion of a course of education leading to an educational qualification pursued for the purpose of entry to that profession;
- “registered” means in relation to a pharmaceutical chemist, [^c-b9021c367ff205380ff14e43fc918967]... druggist or student, registered in the appropriate register under Article 6;
- “registered person” means a person registered as a pharmaceutical chemist [^c-b0a3deb6210be7acd003bdb9731f4f3c]... or druggist;
- “registered pharmacy” has the meaning assigned to it by section 74 of the Medicines Act;
- “the registrar” means the registrar appointed under Article 9(1);
- [^c-99cab26f05b1364fd9a83eb773ddb4d3]...
- “regulatory body” means a regulatory body which has the function of authorising persons to practise as a member of a health or social care profession;[^c-b3e2fba28bd2f4b59cbda3e793c10fc6]
- [^c-99cab26f05b1364fd9a83eb773ddb4d3]...
- “retail pharmacy business” has the meaning assigned to it by regulation[^c-89f628fb7e2879583bee312b14202f6b] 8(1) of the Human Medicines Regulations 2012;
- “Scrutiny Committee” means the committee established under paragraph 2(4) of Schedule 2;[^c-896d2285060cb7b94f5ea269eb18dc7c]
- “the Society” means the Pharmaceutical Society of Northern Ireland;
- “the Statutory Committee” means the Committee continued under Article 19(1);
- “student” means a registered student.
- [^c-99cab26f05b1364fd9a83eb773ddb4d3]...
