## 20. Fitness to practice[^key-c12a8be79b76ac6ec604a6d9c1a121f3]

*Extent: N.I.*

The provisions of Schedule 3 shall have effect with respect to fitness to practise functions and proceedings of the Statutory Committee and the Scrutiny Committee.
