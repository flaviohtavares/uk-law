## 16. Penalty for falsification of registers

*Extent: N.I.*

If the registrar wilfully makes or causes to be made any falsification in any matter relating to any register under this Order, he shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable—

- (a) on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale[^c16838111];
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for any term not exceeding twelve months or to a fine, or to both.
