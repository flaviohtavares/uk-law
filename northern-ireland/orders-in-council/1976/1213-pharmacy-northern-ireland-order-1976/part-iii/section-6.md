# Pharmacy (Northern Ireland) Order 1976, section 6

## 6. The registers

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c20780661] In relation to the registers mentioned in sub-paragraphs (a), (b) and (c) there shall continue to be kept[^c-11ed7d1367a2a79e789120b38acacfe0]... in accordance with the succeeding provisions of this Order,—

- (a) a register of pharmaceutical chemists;
- (b) a register of druggists; and[^c-5620466494d9fc1fd65abc43e673850f]
- (c) a register of students; [^c-7088535e9f2946ebddc9b69cfec6d11d]...
- (d)[^c-7088535e9f2946ebddc9b69cfec6d11d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) Registration under this Order shall not entitle any person so registered to practice medicine or surgery or any branch of medicine or surgery.
