# Commission Regulation (EC) No 889/2008 of 5 September 2008 laying down detailed rules for the implementation of Council Regulation (EC) No 834/2007 on organic production and labelling of organic products with regard to organic production, labelling and control, section Article 16

> Text as at 2026-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 16. Prohibition of landless livestock production

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Landless livestock production, by which the operator of the livestock does not manage agricultural land and/or has not established a written cooperation agreement with another operator according to Article 3(3), is prohibited.
