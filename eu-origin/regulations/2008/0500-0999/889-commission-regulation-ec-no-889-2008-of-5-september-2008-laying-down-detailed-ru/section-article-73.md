# Commission Regulation (EC) No 889/2008 of 5 September 2008 laying down detailed rules for the implementation of Council Regulation (EC) No 834/2007 on organic production and labelling of organic products with regard to organic production, labelling and control, section Article 73

> Text as at 2026-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 73. Several production units run by the same operator

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where an operator runs several production units in the same area, the units producing non-organic crops, together with storage premises for farm input products shall also be subject to the general and the specific control requirements laid down in Chapter 1 and this Chapter of this Title.
