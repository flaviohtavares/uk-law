# Commission Regulation (EC) No 889/2008 of 5 September 2008 laying down detailed rules for the implementation of Council Regulation (EC) No 834/2007 on organic production and labelling of organic products with regard to organic production, labelling and control, section Article 48

> Text as at 2026-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 48. Database

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. The relevant authority[^c-64fdf5966eca888f4dfc8ca0359e6e46] shall ensure that a computerised database is established for the listing of the varieties for which seed or seed potatoes obtained by the organic production method are available in Great Britain[^c-99e69d2b4a32e72d020eed0a6a332b25].

2. The database shall be managed either by the competent authority [^c-6411e6197ffa9660b72094dca00b5a68]... or by an authority or body designated for this purpose by the relevant authority[^c-407e2c8959f074d40d55b3e13bf4c9ec], hereinafter referred to as ‘manager of the database’. The relevant authority[^c-cbb78c52e1aabc2d8f4f3c3baf7d3024] may also designate an authority or a private body in another country.
