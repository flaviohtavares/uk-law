# Commission Regulation (EC) No 889/2008 of 5 September 2008 laying down detailed rules for the implementation of Council Regulation (EC) No 834/2007 on organic production and labelling of organic products with regard to organic production, labelling and control, section Article 25la

> Text as at 2026-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 25la. Specific rules on feeds for organic juveniles[^c000016][^c000003]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the larval rearing of organic juveniles, conventional phytoplankton and zooplankton may be used as feed.
