# Commission Regulation (EC) No 555/2008 of 27 June 2008 laying down detailed rules for implementing Council Regulation (EC) No 479/2008 on the common organisation of the market in wine as regards support programmes, trade with third countries, production potential and on controls in the wine sector, ANNEX XX

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## ANNEX XX — Collection of samples in the context of assistance between control bodies as referred in Article 92[^c-cf1c6cc0846c956ff05312fc59b52ae1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force.**

[^c-cf1c6cc0846c956ff05312fc59b52ae1] 1.

When samples of wine, grape must or another liquid wine product are taken in the context of assistance between control bodies, the competent body shall ensure that:

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

[^c-cf1c6cc0846c956ff05312fc59b52ae1] 2.

Samples shall be taken by pouring the product in question into at least five clean containers each having a nominal capacity of not less than 75 cl. In the case of products as referred to in the first indent of paragraph 1, sampling may also take the form of removing at least five containers having a nominal capacity of not less than 75 cl from the lot to be examined.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

[^c-cf1c6cc0846c956ff05312fc59b52ae1] 3.

Each sample shall bear a label which complies with part A of Annex XXI.

[^c-cf1c6cc0846c956ff05312fc59b52ae1] 4.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

[^c-cf1c6cc0846c956ff05312fc59b52ae1] 5.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

[^c-cf1c6cc0846c956ff05312fc59b52ae1] 6.

Consignments of samples shall bear on the external packaging a red label complying with the model in part B of Annex XXI. The label shall be 50 mm by 25 mm.
