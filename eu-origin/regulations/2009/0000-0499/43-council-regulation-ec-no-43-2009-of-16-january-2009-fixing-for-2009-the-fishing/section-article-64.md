# Council Regulation (EC) No 43/2009 of 16 January 2009 fixing for 2009 the fishing opportunities and associated conditions for certain fish stocks and groups of fish stocks, applicable in Community waters and, for Community vessels, in waters where catch limitations are required, section Article 64

> Text as at 2010-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 64. Measures for the protection of deep water sharks

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Directed fishing for deep water sharks in the SEAFO Convention Area shall be prohibited.
