# Council Regulation (EC) No 43/2009 of 16 January 2009 fixing for 2009 the fishing opportunities and associated conditions for certain fish stocks and groups of fish stocks, applicable in Community waters and, for Community vessels, in waters where catch limitations are required, section Article 56

> Text as at 2010-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 56. Obligations for authorised vessels

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. Vessels shall comply with all the relevant SEAFO conservation and management measures.

2. Authorised vessels shall keep on board valid certificates of vessel registration and valid authorisation to fish and/or tranship.
