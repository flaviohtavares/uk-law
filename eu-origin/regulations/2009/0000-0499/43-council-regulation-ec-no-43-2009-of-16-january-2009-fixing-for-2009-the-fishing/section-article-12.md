# Council Regulation (EC) No 43/2009 of 16 January 2009 fixing for 2009 the fishing opportunities and associated conditions for certain fish stocks and groups of fish stocks, applicable in Community waters and, for Community vessels, in waters where catch limitations are required, section Article 12

> Text as at 2010-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 12. Determination of mesh size and twine thickness

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Mesh size and twine thickness referred to in this Regulation shall be determined in accordance with Commission Regulation (EC) No 517/2008 of 10 June 2008 laying down detailed rules for the implementation of Council Regulation (EC) No 850/98 as regards the determination of the mesh size and assessing the thickness of twine of fishing nets[^f00042], when Community vessels are inspected by Community inspectors, Commission inspectors and national inspectors.
