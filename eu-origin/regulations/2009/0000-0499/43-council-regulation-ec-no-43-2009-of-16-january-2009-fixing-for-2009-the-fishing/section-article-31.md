# Council Regulation (EC) No 43/2009 of 16 January 2009 fixing for 2009 the fishing opportunities and associated conditions for certain fish stocks and groups of fish stocks, applicable in Community waters and, for Community vessels, in waters where catch limitations are required, section Article 31

> Text as at 2010-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 31. Transmission of data

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. Member States shall submit to the Executive Secretary of the GFCM, by 30 June 2009, the data of Task 1.1 and Task 1.2 of the GFCM statistical matrix as set out in Annex X.

2. Member States shall submit to the Executive Secretary of the GFCM, by 30 June 2009 and to the extent possible, the data of Tasks 1.3, 1.4 and 1.5 of the GFCM statistical matrix as set out in Annex X.

3. Member States shall use the GFCM data-entry system as available at the GFCM web-site[^f00044] for the submission of data referred to in paragraphs 1 and 2.

4. Member States shall inform the Commission of the data submitted on the basis of this Article.
