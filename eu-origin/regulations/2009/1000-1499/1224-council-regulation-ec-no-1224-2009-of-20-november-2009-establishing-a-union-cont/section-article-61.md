## Article 61. Weighing of fisheries products after transport from the place of landing

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. By way of derogation from Article 60(2), a fisheries administration[^key-3276f70674685c17cbaa3f5a9859a67c] may permit fisheries products to be weighed after transport from the place of landing provided that they are transported to a destination in the United Kingdom[^key-5b64ce81029c50b5d75083857212fb6c] and that the fisheries administration[^key-d150cfb35c2dcf8abbdd55b14287e44f] has adopted a control plan in accordance with the risk-based methodology described in Annex XIX to Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 404/2011[^key-bd7a687c7b7484fc0bd634d0e1da6ef9].
