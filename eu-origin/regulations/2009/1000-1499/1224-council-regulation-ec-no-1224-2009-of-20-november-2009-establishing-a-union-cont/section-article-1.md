## Article 1. Subject matter

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Regulation establishes a [^c-92433a18e765cc1dac14f9e7317d2869]... system for control, inspection and enforcement[^c000001] [^c-b226200a1572d3a1166c1e8603c88a99]... to ensure compliance with Fisheries Rules[^c-62638afbee5fb300f2da4a267f3466fa].
