## Article 49. Catch composition

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. If catches which have been retained on board any United Kingdom[^c-7dad420852c9c5153e8b465045f3d90f] fishing vessel have been taken with nets with different minimum mesh sizes during the same voyage, the species composition shall be calculated for each part of the catch which has been taken under different conditions. To that end, all changes from the mesh size previously used as well as the catch composition on board at the moment of any such change shall be entered into the fishing logbook.
