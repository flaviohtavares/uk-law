# Council Regulation (EC) No 1224/2009 of 20 November 2009 establishing a Union control system for ensuring compliance with the rules of the common fisheries policy, amending Regulations (EC) No 847/96, (EC) No 2371/2002, (EC) No 811/2004, (EC) No 768/2005, (EC) No 2115/2005, (EC) No 2166/2005, (EC) No 388/2006, (EC) No 509/2007, (EC) No 676/2007, (EC) No 1098/2007, (EC) No 1300/2008, (EC) No 1342/2008 and repealing Regulations (EEC) No 2847/93, (EC) No 1627/94 and (EC) No 1966/2006, section Article 78

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 78. Electronic database

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. A fisheries administration must[^c-ec78731c4f0f2c5163e63d68f0230917] set up and keep up to date an electronic database where they upload all inspection and surveillance reports drawn up by their officials.

2.[^c-008f70450938695b87f84c798c23bb70] A fisheries administration may make regulations specifying detailed rules for the application of this Article.
