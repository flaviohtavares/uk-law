> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 49c. Landing of catches below the minimum conservation reference sizes[^c000004]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where catches below the applicable minimum conservation reference size are landed, those catches shall be stored separately and be treated in such a way that they are distinguished from fishery products destined for direct human consumption. [^c-d128772f04170fc8bc074f8175438253]...
