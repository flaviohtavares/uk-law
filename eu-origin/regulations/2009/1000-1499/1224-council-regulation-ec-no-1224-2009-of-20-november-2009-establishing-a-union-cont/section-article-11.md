# Council Regulation (EC) No 1224/2009 of 20 November 2009 establishing a Union control system for ensuring compliance with the rules of the common fisheries policy, amending Regulations (EC) No 847/96, (EC) No 2371/2002, (EC) No 811/2004, (EC) No 768/2005, (EC) No 2115/2005, (EC) No 2166/2005, (EC) No 388/2006, (EC) No 509/2007, (EC) No 676/2007, (EC) No 1098/2007, (EC) No 1300/2008, (EC) No 1342/2008 and repealing Regulations (EEC) No 2847/93, (EC) No 1627/94 and (EC) No 1966/2006, section Article 11

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 11. Vessel detection system

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where a fisheries administration has[^c-16f63999e812106c794abf29dd72de9e] clear evidence of a cost benefit in relation to the traditional control means in the detection of fishing vessels, it must[^c-8ed10e8b2bbf04fa90ee0ae55c971a99] use a vessel detection system allowing it[^c-0b5df9b92644c1a9510489bab7cb79f0] to match the positions derived by remotely sensed images sent to earth by satellites or other equivalent systems with the data received by vessel monitoring system or automatic identification system, in order to assess the presence of fishing vessels in the area. A fisheries administration must[^c-fc2a5ef9bf1f67fc2d27c53f98db8c40] ensure that its[^c-9cf63e1555adc65abce000cad8db926b] fisheries monitoring centres possess the technical capacity to use a vessel detection system.
