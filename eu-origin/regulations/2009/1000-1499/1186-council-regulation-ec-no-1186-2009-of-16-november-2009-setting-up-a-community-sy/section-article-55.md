# Council Regulation (EC) No 1186/2009 of 16 November 2009 setting up a Community system of reliefs from customs duty (codified version), section Article 55

> Text as at 2021-08-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 55.** Relief shall be limited to products which:

- ((a)) are intended for institutions or laboratories approved by the competent authorities, for use exclusively for non-commercial medical or scientific purposes;
- ((b)) are accompanied by a certificate of conformity issued by a duly authorised body in the third country of departure;
- ((c)) are in containers bearing a special label identifying them.
