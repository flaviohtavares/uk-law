# Council Regulation (EC) No 1186/2009 of 16 November 2009 setting up a Community system of reliefs from customs duty (codified version), section Article 99

> Text as at 2021-08-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 99.** 1. The relief referred to in Article 95 shall cover goods which are not completely used up or destroyed during examination, analysis or testing, provided that the products remaining are, with the agreement and under the supervision of the competent authorities:

- (a) completely destroyed or rendered commercially valueless on completion of examination, analysis or testing; or
- (b) surrendered to the State without causing it any expense, where this is possible under national law; or
- (c) in duly justified circumstances, exported outside the customs territory of the Community.

2. For the purposes of paragraph 1, ‘products remaining’ means products resulting from the examination, analysis or tests or goods not actually used.
