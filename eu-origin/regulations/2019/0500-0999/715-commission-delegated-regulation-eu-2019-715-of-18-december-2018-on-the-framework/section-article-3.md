# Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/715 of 18 December 2018 on the framework financial regulation for the bodies set up under the TFEU and Euratom Treaty and referred to in Article 70 of Regulation (EU, Euratom) 2018/1046 of the European Parliament and of the Council, section Article 3

> Text as at 2018-12-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 3. Periods, dates and time limits

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Unless otherwise provided, Council Regulation (EEC, Euratom) No 1182/71[^f00007] shall apply to the deadlines set out in this Regulation.
