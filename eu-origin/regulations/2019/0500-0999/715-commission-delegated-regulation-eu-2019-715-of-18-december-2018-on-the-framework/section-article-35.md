# Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/715 of 18 December 2018 on the framework financial regulation for the bodies set up under the TFEU and Euratom Treaty and referred to in Article 70 of Regulation (EU, Euratom) 2018/1046 of the European Parliament and of the Council, section Article 35

> Text as at 2018-12-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 35. Structure of the budget of the Union body

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The budget of the Union body shall consist of a statement of revenue and a statement of expenditure.
