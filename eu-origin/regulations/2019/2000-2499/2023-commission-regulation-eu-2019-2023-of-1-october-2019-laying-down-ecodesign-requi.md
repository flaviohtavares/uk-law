# Commission Regulation (EU) 2019/2023 of 1 October 2019 laying down ecodesign requirements for household washing machines and household washer-dryers pursuant to Directive 2009/125/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council, amending Commission Regulation (EC) No 1275/2008 and repealing Commission Regulation (EU) No 1015/2010 (Text with EEA relevance)

Commission Regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5] (EU) 2019/2023 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]

Having regard to Article 114 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]

Having regard to Directive 2009/125/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 21 October 2009 establishing a framework for the setting of ecodesign requirements for energy-related products *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5][^f00001], and in particular Article 15(1) thereof, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]

Whereas: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]

(1)

Pursuant to Directive 2009/125/EC the Commission should set ecodesign requirements for energy-related products which account for significant volumes of sales and trade in the Union and which have a significant environmental impact and presenting significant potential for improvement through design in terms of their environmental impact, without entailing excessive costs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]

(2)

The Communication from the Commission COM(2016)773 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5][^f00002] (ecodesign working plan) established by the Commission in application of Article 16(1) of Directive 2009/125/EC sets out the working priorities under the ecodesign and energy labelling framework for the period 2016-2019. The Working Plan identifies the energy-related product groups to be considered as priorities for the undertaking of preparatory studies and eventual adoption of implementing measures, as well as the review of Commission Regulation (EU) No 1015/2010 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5][^f00003], Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) No 1061/2010 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5][^f00004] and Commission Directive 96/60/EC *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5][^f00005].

(3)

Measures from the Working Plan have an estimated potential to deliver a total in excess of 260 TWh of annual final energy savings in 2030, which is equivalent to reducing greenhouse gas emissions by approximately 100 million tonnes per year in 2030. Household washing machines and household washer-dryers are among the product groups listed in the Working Plan, with estimated annual electricity savings of 2,5 TWh, leading to GHG emission reductions of 0,8 Mt CO *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]<sub>2</sub> eq/year, and estimated water savings of 711 million m *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]<sup>3</sup> in 2030. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]

(4)

The Commission established ecodesign requirements for household washing machines in Regulation (EU) No 1015/2010 and pursuant to that Regulation, the Commission should review it in light of technological progress. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]

(5)

The Commission has reviewed Regulation (EU) No 1015/2010 and analysed the technical, environmental and economic aspects of household washing machines and household washer-dryers as well as real-life user behaviour. The review was carried out in close cooperation with stakeholders and interested parties from the Union and third countries. The results of the review were made public and presented to the Consultation Forum established by Article 18 of Directive 2009/125/EC. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]

(6)

It appears from the review study that there is a need to revise the ecodesign requirements for household washing machines and to establish ecodesign requirements for household washer-dryers. The requirements relate to the use of essential resources such as energy and water. There is also a need to introduce requirements related to resource efficiency such as reparability and recyclability. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]

(7)

The environmental aspects of household washing machines and household washer-dryers, which have been identified as significant for the purposes of this Regulation are the consumption of energy and water during the use phase, the generation of waste at the end of life and the emissions to air and water in the production phase (due to the extraction and processing of raw materials) and in the use phase (because of the consumption of electricity and water discharge). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]

(8)

The annual energy and water consumption of products subject to this Regulation in the Union was estimated at 35,3 TWh and 2 496 million m *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]<sup>3</sup> respectively in the Union in 2015. The projected electricity consumption of household washing machines and household washer-dryers in a business as usual scenario is estimated to decrease to 33,5 TWh in 2030 and the water consumption to decrease to 1 764 million m *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]<sup>3</sup> in 2030. That decrease in energy and water consumption may be accelerated if the existing ecodesign requirements are updated. Finally, the service life-time of household washing machines and household washer-dryers has been estimated to have decreased in recent years to around 12,5 years and the trend is likely to continue in the absence of incentives. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]

(9)

The Communication from the Commission to the European Parliament, the Council, the European Economic and Social Committee and the Committee of the Regions COM(2015) 614 final *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5][^f00006] (circular economy action plan) and the Communication on the ecodesign working plan *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5][^f00007] underline the importance of using the ecodesign framework in order to support the move towards more resource efficient and circular economy. Directive 2012/19/EU *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5][^f00008] refers to Directive 2009/125/EC and indicate that ecodesign requirements should facilitate the re-use, dismantling and recovery of waste electrical and electronic equipment (WEEE) by tackling the issues upstream. Therefore this Regulation should lay down appropriate requirements contributing to circular economy objectives. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]

(10)

Non-household washing machines and non-household washer-dryers have distinct characteristics and uses. They are subject to other regulatory work, in particular Directive 2006/42/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council on machinery *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5][^f00009], and should not be included in the scope of this Regulation. Provisions for household washing machines and household washer-dryers should apply to washing machines and washer-dryers with the same technical characteristics, regardless of the setting they are used in. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]

(11)

Household washing machines and household washer-dryers with more than one drum should be subject to specific rules only if all their drums perform the same function. Otherwise, each drum should be considered as a separate household washing machine or as a separate household washer-dryer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]

(12)

Specific requirements for the low power modes of household washing machines and household washer-dryers should be laid down. The requirements of Commission Regulation (EC) No 1275/2008 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5][^f00010] should not apply to products covered by the scope of this Regulation. Regulation (EC) No 1275/2008 should be amended accordingly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]

(13)

The relevant product parameters should be measured using reliable, accurate and reproducible methods. Those methods should take into account the recognised state-of-the-art measurement methods including, where available, harmonised standards adopted by the European standardisation organisations, as listed in Annex I to Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5][^f00011].

(14)

In accordance with Article 8 of Directive 2009/125/EC, this Regulation should specify the applicable conformity assessment procedures. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]

(15)

To facilitate compliance checks, manufacturers, importers or authorised representatives should provide information in the technical documentation referred to in Annexes IV and V to Directive 2009/125/EC in so far as that information relates to the requirements laid down in this Regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]

(16)

Where parameters of the technical documentation, as defined by this Regulation, are identical to parameters of the product information sheet defined by Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/2014 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5][^f00012], manufacturers, importers or authorised representatives should enter the corresponding data into the product database defined by Regulation (EU) 2017/1369 of the European Parliament and of the Council *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5][^f00013] and should no longer need to provide them to market surveillance authorities as part of the technical documentation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]

(17)

To ensure the effectiveness and credibility of the Regulation and to protect consumers, products that automatically alter their performance in test conditions to improve the declared parameters should not be allowed to be placed on the market. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]

(18)

In addition to the requirements laid down in this Regulation, indicative benchmarks for best available technologies should be identified to make information on the life-cycle environmental performance of products subject to this Regulation widely available and easily accessible, in accordance with point (2), part 3 of Annex I to Directive 2009/125/EC. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]

(19)

This Regulation should be reviewed in order to assess the appropriateness and effectiveness of its provisions in achieving its goals. The timing of the review should be sufficient for all provisions to be implemented and show an effect on the market. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]

(20)

Regulation (EU) No 1015/2010 should be repealed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]

(21)

In order to facilitate the transition between Regulation (EU) No 1015/2010 and this Regulation, the new name ‘eco 40-60’ should be allowed to be used as from the entry into force of this Regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]

(22)

The measures provided for in this Regulation are in accordance with the opinion of the Committee established by Article 19 of Directive 2009/125/EC, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]

## Article 1 (affected by repeal, see note). Subject matter and scope (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5][^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]

1. This Regulation establishes ecodesign requirements for the placing on the market or the putting into service of electric mains-operated household washing machines and household washer-dryers, including built-in household washing machines and household washer-dryers and electric mains-operated household washing machines and household washer-dryers that can also be powered by batteries. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]

2. This Regulation shall not apply to: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]

- (a)[^c000001] washing machines and washer-dryers belonging to the scope of Directive 2006/42/EC *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5][^f00014];
- (b) battery-operated household washing machines and household washer-dryers that can be connected to the mains through an AC/DC converter purchased separately. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]

3. The requirements in points 1 to 6, 9(1)(a) and (c), and 9(2)(i) and (vii) of Annex II shall not apply to: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]

- (a) household washing machines with a rated capacity lower than 2 kg; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]
- (b) household washer-dryers with a rated washing capacity lower than 2 kg. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]

## Article 2 (affected by repeal, see note). Definitions (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5][^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]

For the purpose of this Regulation the following definitions shall apply: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]

- ((1)) ‘mains’ or ‘electric mains’ means the electricity supply from the grid of 230 (± 10 %) volts of alternating current at 50 Hz; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]
- ((2)) ‘automatic washing machine’ means a washing machine where the load is fully treated by the washing machine without the need for user intervention at any point during the programme; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]
- ((3)) ‘household washing machine’ means an automatic washing machine which cleans and rinses household laundry by using water, chemical, mechanical and thermal means, which also has a spin extraction function, and which is declared by the manufacturer in the Declaration of Conformity as complying with Directive 2014/35/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5][^f00015] or with Directive 2014/53/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5][^f00016];
- ((4)) ‘household washer-dryer’ means a household washing machine which, in addition to the functions of an automatic washing machine, in the same drum includes a means for drying the textiles by heating and tumbling, and which is declared by the manufacturer in the Declaration of Conformity as complying with Directive 2014/35/EU or with Directive 2014/53/EU; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]
- ((5)) ‘built-in household washing machine’ means a household washing machine that is designed, tested and marketed exclusively: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]
  - ((a)) to be installed in cabinetry or encased (top and/or bottom, and sides) by panels; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]
  - ((b)) to be securely fastened to the sides, top or floor of the cabinetry or panels; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]
  - ((c)) to be equipped with an integral factory-finished face or to be fitted with a custom front panel; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]
- ((6)) ‘built-in household washer-dryer’ means a household washer-dryer that is designed, tested and marketed exclusively: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]
  - ((a)) to be installed in cabinetry or encased (top and/or bottom, and sides) by panels; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]
  - ((b)) to be securely fastened to the sides, top or floor of the cabinetry or panels; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]
  - ((c)) to be equipped with an integral factory-finished face or to be fitted with a custom front panel; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]
- ((7)) ‘multi-drum household washing machine’ means a household washing machine equipped with more than one drum, whether in separate units or in the same casing; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]
- ((8)) ‘multi-drum household washer-dryer’ means a household washer-dryer equipped with more than one drum, whether in separate units or in the same casing; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]
- ((9)) ‘equivalent model’ means a model which has the same technical characteristics relevant for the technical information to be provided, but which is placed on the market or put into service by the same manufacturer, importer or authorised representative as another model with a different model identifier; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]
- ((10)) ‘model identifier’ means the code, usually alphanumeric, which distinguishes a specific product model from other models with the same trade mark or the same manufacturer's, importer's or authorised representative's name; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]
- ((11)) ‘product database’ means a collection of data concerning products, which is arranged in a systematic manner and consists of a consumer-oriented public part, where information concerning individual product parameters is accessible by electronic means, an online portal for accessibility and a compliance part, with clearly specified accessibility and security requirements, as laid down in Regulation (EU) 2017/1369; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]
- ((12)) ‘eco 40-60’ means the name of the programme declared by the manufacturer, importer or authorised representative as able to clean normally soiled cotton laundry declared to be washable at 40 °C or 60 °C, together in the same washing cycle, and to which the ecodesign requirements on energy efficiency, washing efficiency, rinsing effectiveness, programme duration and water consumption relate; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]
- ((13)) ‘programme’ means a series of operations that are pre-defined and which are declared by the manufacturer, importer or authorised representative as suitable for washing, drying or continuously washing and drying certain types of textile; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]
- ((14)) ‘washing cycle’ means a complete washing process as defined by a selected programme, consisting of a series of different operations including washing, rinsing, and spinning. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]

For the purpose of the annexes, additional definitions are set out in Annex I. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]

## Article 6 (affected by repeal, see note). Circumvention (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5][^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]

The manufacturer, importer or authorised representative shall not place on the market products designed to be able to detect they are being tested (e.g. by recognising the test conditions or test cycle), and to react specifically by automatically altering their performance during the test with the aim of reaching a more favourable level for any of the parameters declared by the manufacturer, importer or authorised representative in the technical documentation or included in any documentation provided. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]

## Article 8 (affected by repeal, see note). Review (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5][^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]

The review shall in particular focus on the following: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]

## ANNEX VI (affected by repeal, see note) — Multi-drum household washing machines and multi-drum household washer-dryers (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5][^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]

For multi-drum household washing machines and multi-drum household washer-dryers, the provisions of points 1 to 6 and 9(2) of Annex II, following the measurement and calculation methods set out in Annex III, shall apply to any drum. The provisions of points 7, 8, 9(1) and 9(3) of Annex II, apply to all multi-drum household washing machines and all multi-drum household washer-dryers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]

The provisions of points 1 to 6 and 9(2) of Annex II, shall apply to each of the drums independently, except when the drums are built in the same casing and can, in the eco 40-60 programme or in the wash and dry cycle, only operate simultaneously. In the latter case, these provisions shall apply to the multi-drum household washing machine or to the multi-drum household washer-dryer as a whole, as follows: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]

- ((a)) the rated washing capacity is the sum of the rated washing capacities of each drum; for multi-drum household washer-dryers, the rated capacity is the sum of the rated capacities of each drum; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]
- ((b)) the energy and water consumption of the multi-drum household washing machine and of the washing cycle of the multi-drum household washer-dryer is the sum of the energy consumption, or water consumption, of each drum; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]
- ((c)) the energy and water consumption of the complete cycle of the multi-drum household washer-dryer is the sum of the energy consumption, or water consumption, of each drum; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]
- ((d)) the Energy Efficiency Index (EEI *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]<sub>W</sub>) is calculated using the rated washing capacity and energy consumption; for multi-drum household washer-dryers, the Energy Efficiency Index (EEI *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]<sub>WD *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]</sub>) is calculated using the rated capacity and energy consumption; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]
- ((e)) each drum shall comply individually with the minimum washing efficiency and the minimum rinsing effectiveness requirements; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]
- ((f)) each drum shall comply individually with the requirement on duration applicable to the drum with the largest rated capacity; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]
- ((g)) the requirements on low power modes apply to the whole household washing machine or the whole household washer-dryer; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]
- ((h)) the residual moisture content after washing is calculated as the weighted average, according to each drum’s rated capacity; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]
- ((i)) for household multi-drum washer-dryers, the requirement on final moisture content after drying applies individually to each drum. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]

The verification procedure set out in Annex IV applies to the multi-drum household washing machine and to the multi-drum household washer-dryer as a whole, with the verification tolerances applying to each of the parameters determined in application of this annex. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d101743197c5b5aed8128d8ad77c23a5]
