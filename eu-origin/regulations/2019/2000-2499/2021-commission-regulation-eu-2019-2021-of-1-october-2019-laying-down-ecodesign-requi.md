# Commission Regulation (EU) 2019/2021 of 1 October 2019 laying down ecodesign requirements for electronic displays pursuant to Directive 2009/125/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council, amending Commission Regulation (EC) No 1275/2008 and repealing Commission Regulation (EC) No 642/2009 (Text with EEA relevance)

of 1 October 2019 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]

laying down ecodesign requirements for electronic displays pursuant to Directive 2009/125/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council, amending Commission Regulation (EC) No 1275/2008 and repealing Commission Regulation (EC) No 642/2009 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]

(Text with EEA relevance) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]

Having regard to Article 114 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]

Having regard to Directive 2009/125/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 21 October 2009 establishing a framework for the setting of ecodesign requirements for energy-related products *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0][^f00001], and in particular Article 15(1) thereof, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]

Whereas: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]

(1)

Pursuant to Directive 2009/125/EC the Commission should set ecodesign requirements for energy-related products which account for significant volumes of sales and trade, in the Union and which have a significant environmental impact and presenting significant potential for improvement through design in terms of their environmental impact, without entailing excessive costs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]

(2)

The Commission established ecodesign requirements for televisions in Commission Regulation (EC) No 642/2009 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0][^f00002] and pursuant to that Regulation, the Commission should review the Regulation in the light of technological progress. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]

(3)

The Communication from the Commission COM(2016) 773 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0][^f00003] (ecodesign working plan), established by the Commission in application of Article 16(1) of Directive 2009/125/EC, sets out the working priorities under the ecodesign and energy labelling framework for the period 2016-2019. The ecodesign working plan identifies the energy-related product groups to be considered as priorities for the undertaking of preparatory studies and eventual adoption of implementing measures, as well as the review of Regulation (EC) No 642/2009. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]

(4)

Measures from the Ecodesign Working Plan have an estimated potential to deliver a total in excess of 260 TWh of annual primary energy savings in 2030, which is equivalent to reducing greenhouse gas emissions by approximately 100 million tonnes per year in 2030. Electronic displays are one of the product groups listed in the working plan. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]

(5)

Pursuant to Article 6 of Regulation (EC) No 642/2009, the Commission has reviewed the Regulation in light of technological progress and analysed the technical, environmental and economic aspects of televisions and other electronic displays. The review was carried out in close cooperation with stakeholders and interested parties from the Union and third countries. The results of the review were made public and presented to the Consultation Forum established by Article 18 of Directive 2009/125/EC. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]

(6)

The review concluded that there was a need for the introduction of new ecodesign energy-related requirements for televisions and that the same requirements should also apply to other displays, such as computer monitors, because of the rapidly increasing functionality overlap between different display types. Projectors use very different technologies and consequently should be out of scope of this Regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]

(7)

Digital signage displays are used in public spaces such as airports, metro and train stations, retail stores, shop windows, restaurants, museums, hotels, conference centres or in prominent positions outside buildings and represent a relevant emerging market. Their energy needs are different and generally higher than those of other electronic displays because they are often used in luminous places and continuously on. Minimum requirements for digital signage displays in on-mode should be evaluated once additional data will be available, however they should at least have minimum requirements on off, standby and networked standby modes and on material efficiency. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]

(8)

The annual energy consumption in 2016 of televisions in the Union constituted more than 3 % of the European Union’s electricity consumption. The projected energy consumption of televisions, monitors and digital signage displays would be expected be close to 100 TWh/yr in 2030. This Regulation, together with the accompanying energy labelling regulation, is estimated to reduce the overall consumption by 39 TWh/yr by 2030. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]

(9)

Specific requirements should be laid down for standby, networked standby and off mode electric power demand of electronic displays. Therefore, the requirements of Commission Regulation (EC) No 1275/2008 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0][^f00004] that does not apply to televisions, should no longer apply to the additional electronic displays types covered by the scope of this Regulation. Regulation (EC) No 1275/2008 should be amended accordingly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]

(10)

Electronic displays for professional use such as video-editing, computer-aided design, graphics or for the broadcast sector, possess enhanced performance and very specific features that, although usually involving higher energy use, should be not subject to on-mode energy efficiency requirements set for more generic products. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]

(11)

The Commission Communication on the circular economy *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0][^f00005] and the Communication on the ecodesign working plan *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0][^f00006] underline the importance of using the ecodesign framework to support the move towards a more resource efficient and circular economy. Recital (11) and Article 4 of Directive 2012/19/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0][^f00007] refer as well to Directive 2009/125/EC and indicate that ecodesign requirements should facilitate the re-use, dismantling and recovery of waste electrical and electronic equipment (WEEE) by tackling the issues upstream, thus facilitating the objectives of waste prevention and recovery in Member States as from Directive (EU) 2018/851 of the European Parliament and of the Council *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0][^f00008]. In addition, Decision No 1386/2013/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0][^f00009] on a General Union Environment Action Programme to 2020 includes the goal ‘to turn the Union into a resource-efficient, green and competitive low-carbon economy’. Implementable and enforceable requirements at the product design phase may be appropriate for optimising resource and material efficiency at end of life. Finally, in accordance with the Union action plan for the Circular Economy *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0][^f00010], the Commission should make sure that special emphasis is placed on aspects relevant to the circular economy when setting out or revising ecodesign criteria. This Regulation should therefore lay down appropriate non-energy related requirements contributing to circular economy objectives including requirements to facilitate repair and the availability of spare parts. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]

(12)

Liquid crystal screens (LCD) with a screen area greater than 100 square centimetres are in the scope of the requirements set in Article 8 and Annex VII of the Directive 2012/19/EU in relation to the selective treatment for materials and components of WEEE which means that such displays need to be removed from the product integrating them. Considering, in addition, that screens with a screen area smaller than or equal to 100 square centimetres have very limited energy use, all such electronic displays should be outside the scope of this Regulation both for energy and for requirements contributing to circular economy objectives. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]

(13)

Once delivered to an electrical and electronic equipment waste collection facility at the end of their life, televisions, computer monitors, digital signage displays, professional displays, broadcast displays, security displays, as well as displays integrated into tablets, ‘all-in-one’ desktop or portable computers are, generally, not distinguishable from each other. Therefore they should all be subject to the same requirements for proper end of life treatment and they should also facilitate circular economy objectives. However electronic displays integrated into computers, such as tablets, laptops or all-in-one desktops, although hardly distinguishable from other electronic displays, should be covered in a review of Commission Regulation (EU) No 617/2013 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0][^f00011] on computers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]

(14)

Shredding of electronic displays causes large losses of resources and hinders circular economy objectives such as recovery of some rare and precious materials. Moreover, Article 8(1) and (2) of the Directive 2012/19/EU require Member States to ensure that all separately collected waste undergoes proper treatment including, as a minimum, a selective treatment of a number of components – typically present in electronic displays – in preparation for recovery or recycling and before schredding. Dismantling of at least the specific components listed in Annex VII of that Directive should therefore be facilitated. Furthermore, Article 15 makes provision for information to be provided free of charge by producers to facilitate the preparation for re-use and the correct and environmentally sound treatment of WEEE, which can be provided using a voluntary electronic platform *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0][^f00012].

(15)

Presence of halogenated flame retardants represents a major issue in the recycling of plastics of electronic displays. Some halogenated compounds have been restricted by Directive 2011/65/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0][^f00013] because of their high toxicity, but may be still found in old displays and others are still allowed. Control on maximum content of non permitted compounds in recycled plastic is not cost-effective, resulting in all being incinerated. Alternative solutions would exist for the bulk of the plastic part in an electronic display, such as the enclosure and the stand, permitting higher yields of recycled plastics. Use of halogenated flame retardants in these parts should be limited. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]

(16)

Presence of cadmium, a highly toxic and carcinogenic substance in display panels is an additional obstacle to efficient management of the waste stream. Use of certain hazardous substances in electrical and electronic equipment, including cadmium, is restricted by Directive 2011/65/EU. Use of cadmium in electronic displays, however, is among the applications in Annex III exempted from the restriction for a limited time. A specific marking on displays that contain cadmium, to facilitate the correct and environmentally sound treatment at end of life, should therefore be provided by manufacturers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]

(17)

The relevant product parameters should be measured using reliable, accurate and reproducible methods, which take into account recognised state-of-the-art measurement methods and, where available, harmonised standards adopted by the European standardisation bodies, as listed in Annex I to Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0][^f00014].

(18)

In line with Article 8 of Directive 2009/125/EC, this Regulation should specify the applicable conformity assessment procedures. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]

(19)

To facilitate compliance checks, manufacturers, importers or authorised representatives should provide information in the technical documentation referred to in Annexes IV and V to Directive 2009/125/EC in so far as that information relates to the requirements laid down in this Regulation. For market surveillance purposes, manufacturers, importers or authorised representatives should be allowed to refer to the product database if the technical documentation as per Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/2013 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0][^f00015] contains the same information. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]

(20)

To improve the effectiveness of this Regulation and to protect consumers, products that automatically alter their performance in test conditions to improve the declared parameters should be prohibited from being placed on the market. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]

(21)

In addition to the legally binding requirements laid down in this Regulation, indicative benchmarks for best available technologies should be identified to make information on products environmental performance over their life-cycle subject to this Regulation widely available and easily accessible, in accordance with Directive 2009/125/EC, Annex I, part 3, point (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]

(22)

A review of this Regulation should assess the appropriateness and effectiveness of its provisions in achieving its goals. The timing of the review should take into account the fast rate of technological progress in the products covered by this Regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]

(23)

Regulation (EC) No 642/2009 should therefore be repealed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]

(24)

The measures provided for in this Regulation are in accordance with the opinion of the Committee established by Article 19 of Directive 2009/125/EC, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]

## Article 1 (affected by repeal, see note). Subject matter and scope (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0][^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]

1. This Regulation establishes ecodesign requirements for the placing on the market and putting into service of electronic displays, including televisions, monitors and digital signage displays. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]

2. This Regulation shall not apply to the following: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]

- (a) any electronic display with a screen area smaller than or equal to 100 square centimetres; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]
- (b) projectors; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]
- (c) all-in-one video conference systems; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]
- (d) medical displays; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]
- (e) virtual reality headsets; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]
- (f) displays integrated or to be integrated into products listed into Article 2, point 3(a) and point 4 of Directive 2012/19/EU; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]
- (g) displays that are components or subassemblies of products covered by implementing measures adopted under Directive 2009/125/EC. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]

3. The requirements in points A and B of Annex II shall not apply to the following displays: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]

- (a) broadcast displays; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]
- (b) professional displays; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]
- (c) security displays; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]
- (d) digital interactive whiteboards; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]
- (e) digital photo frames; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]
- (f) digital signage displays. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]

4. The requirements in points A, B and C of Annex II shall not apply to the following displays: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]

- (a) status displays; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]
- (b) control panels. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]

## Article 2 (affected by repeal, see note). Definitions (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0][^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]

For the purpose of this Regulation the following definitions shall apply: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]

- ((1)) ‘*electronic display *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]*’ means a display screen and associated electronics that, as its primary function, displays visual information from wired or wireless sources; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]
- ((2)) ‘*television *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]*’ means an electronic display designed primarily for the display and reception of audiovisual signals and which consists of an electronic display and one or more tuners/receivers; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]
- ((3)) ‘*tuner/receiver *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]*’ means an electronic circuit that detects television broadcast signal, such as terrestrial digital or satellite, but not internet unicast, and facilitates the selection of a TV channel from a group of broadcast channels; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]
- ((4)) ‘*monitor *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]*’*or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]*‘*computer monitor *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]*’ or ‘ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]*computer display *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]*’ means an electronic display intended for one person for close viewing such as in a desk-based environment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]
- ((5)) ‘*digital signage display *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]*’ means an electronic display that is designed primarily to be viewed by multiple people in non-desktop based and non domestic environments. Its specifications shall include all of the following features: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]
  - ((a)) unique identifier to enable addressing a specific display screen; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]
  - ((b)) a function disabling unauthorised access to the display settings and displayed image; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]
  - ((c)) network connection (encompassing a hard-wired or wireless interface) for controlling, monitoring or receiving the information to display from remote unicast or multicast but not broadcast sources; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]
  - ((d)) designed to be installed hanging, mounted or fixed to a physical structure for viewing by multiple people and not placed on the market with a ground stand; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]
  - ((e)) does not integrate a tuner to display broadcast signals; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]
- ((6)) ‘*screen area *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]*’ means the viewable area of the electronic display calculated by multiplying the maximum viewable image width by the maximum viewable image height along the surface of the panel (both flat or curved); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]
- ((7)) ‘*digital photo frame *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]*’ means an electronic display that displays exclusively still visual information; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]
- ((8)) ‘*projector *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]*’ means an optical device for processing analogue or digital video image information, in any format, to modulate a light source and project the resulting image onto an external surface; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]
- ((9)) ‘*status display *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]*’ means a display used to show simple but changing information such as selected channel, time or power consumption. A simple light indicator is not considered a status display; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]
- ((10)) ‘*control panel *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]*’ means an electronic display whose main function is to display images associated with product operational status; it may provide user interaction by touch or other means to control the product operation. It may be integrated into products or specifically designed and marketed to be used exclusively with the product; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]
- ((11)) ‘*all-in-one video conference system *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]*’ means a dedicated system designed for video conferencing and collaboration, integrated within a single enclosure, whose specification shall include all of the following features: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]
  - ((a)) support for specific videoconference protocol ITU-T H.323 or IETF SIP as delivered by the manufacturer; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]
  - ((b)) camera(s), display and processing capabilities for two-way real-time video including packet loss resilience; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]
  - ((c)) loudspeaker and audio processing capabilities for two-way real-time hands-free audio including echo cancellation; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]
  - ((d)) an encryption function; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]
  - ((e)) HiNA; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]
- ((12)) ‘*HiNA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]*’ means High Network Availability as defined in Article 2 of Regulation (EC) No 1275/2008; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]
- ((13)) ‘*broadcast display *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]*’ means an electronic display designed and marketed for professional use by broadcasters and video production houses for video content creation. Its specifications shall include all of the following characteristics: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]
  - ((a)) colour calibration function; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]
  - ((b)) input signal analysis function for input signal monitoring and error detection, such as wave-form monitor/vector scope, RGB cut off, facility to check the video signal status at actual pixel resolution, interlace mode and screen marker; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]
  - ((c)) Serial Digital Interface (SDI) or Video over internet Protocol (VoIP) integrated with the product; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]
  - ((d)) not intended for use in public areas; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]
- ((14)) ‘*digital interactive whiteboard *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-432de2249d7c101cb6e2d35ab76a5eb0]*’ means an electronic display which allows direct user interaction with the displayed image.
