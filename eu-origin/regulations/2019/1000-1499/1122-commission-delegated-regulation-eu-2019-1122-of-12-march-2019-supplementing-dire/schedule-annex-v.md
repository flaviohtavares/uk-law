# Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/1122 of 12 March 2019 supplementing Directive 2003/87/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards the functioning of the Union Registry (Text with EEA relevance), ANNEX V

> Text as at 2021-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## ANNEX V — Additional information to be provided for registering verifiers

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A document proving that the verifier requesting registration is accredited as a verifier in accordance with Article 15 of Directive 2003/87/EC.
