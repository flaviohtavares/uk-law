# Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/1122 of 12 March 2019 supplementing Directive 2003/87/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards the functioning of the Union Registry (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 62

> Text as at 2021-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 62. Authentication of the Union Registry

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The identity of the Union Registry shall be authenticated by the EUTL having regard to the data exchange and technical specifications provided for in Article 75.
