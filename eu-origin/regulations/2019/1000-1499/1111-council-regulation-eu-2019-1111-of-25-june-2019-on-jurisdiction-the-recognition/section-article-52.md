# Council Regulation (EU) 2019/1111 of 25 June 2019 on jurisdiction, the recognition and enforcement of decisions in matrimonial matters and the matters of parental responsibility, and on international child abduction (recast), section Article 52

> Text as at 2019-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 52. Authorities competent for enforcement

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The application for enforcement shall be submitted to the authority competent for enforcement under the law of the Member State of enforcement as communicated by that Member State to the Commission pursuant to Article 103.
