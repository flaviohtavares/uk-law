# Council Regulation (EU) 2019/1111 of 25 June 2019 on jurisdiction, the recognition and enforcement of decisions in matrimonial matters and the matters of parental responsibility, and on international child abduction (recast), section Article 92

> Text as at 2019-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 92. Amendments to the Annexes

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Commission is empowered to adopt delegated acts in accordance with Article 93 concerning the amendment of Annexes I to IX in order to update or to make technical changes to those Annexes.
