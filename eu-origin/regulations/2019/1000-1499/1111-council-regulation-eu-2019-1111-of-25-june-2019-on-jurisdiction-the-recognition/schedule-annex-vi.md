## ANNEX VI

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

CERTIFICATE CONCERNING CERTAIN DECISIONS ON THE SUBSTANCE OF RIGHTS OF CUSTODY GIVEN PURSUANT TO ARTICLE 29(6) OF THE REGULATION AND ENTAILING THE RETURN OF THE CHILD

(Article 29(6), point (b) of Article 42(1) and point (b) of Article 47(1) of Council Regulation (EU) 2019/1111[^f00076])

IMPORTANT

To be issued, upon application by a party, by the court that has given the decision pursuant to Article 29(6) in so far as this decision entails the return of the child and only if the conditions of Article 47(3) and (4) of the Regulation, as indicated in points 11 to 15, are met.
