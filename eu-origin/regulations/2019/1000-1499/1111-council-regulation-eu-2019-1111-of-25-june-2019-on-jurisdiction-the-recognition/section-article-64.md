# Council Regulation (EU) 2019/1111 of 25 June 2019 on jurisdiction, the recognition and enforcement of decisions in matrimonial matters and the matters of parental responsibility, and on international child abduction (recast), section Article 64

> Text as at 2019-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 64. Scope

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Section applies in matters of divorce, legal separation and parental responsibility to authentic instruments which have been formally drawn up or registered, and to agreements which have been registered, in a Member State assuming jurisdiction under Chapter II.
