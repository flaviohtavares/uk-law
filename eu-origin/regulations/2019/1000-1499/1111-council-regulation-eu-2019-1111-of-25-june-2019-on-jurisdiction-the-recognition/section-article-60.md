# Council Regulation (EU) 2019/1111 of 25 June 2019 on jurisdiction, the recognition and enforcement of decisions in matrimonial matters and the matters of parental responsibility, and on international child abduction (recast), section Article 60

> Text as at 2019-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 60. Expeditious procedures

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The authority competent for enforcement or the court shall act without undue delay in procedures concerning the application for refusal of enforcement.
