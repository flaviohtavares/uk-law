# Council Regulation (EU) 2019/1111 of 25 June 2019 on jurisdiction, the recognition and enforcement of decisions in matrimonial matters and the matters of parental responsibility, and on international child abduction (recast), ANNEX IV

> Text as at 2019-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## ANNEX IV

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

CERTIFICATE CONCERNING DECISIONS ORDERING THE RETURN OF A CHILD TO ANOTHER MEMBER STATE PURSUANT TO THE 1980 HAGUE CONVENTION[^f00048] AND ANY PROVISIONAL, INCLUDING PROTECTIVE, MEASURES TAKEN IN ACCORDANCE WITH ARTICLE 27(5) OF THE REGULATION ACCOMPANYING THEM

(Point (c) of Article 36(1) of Council Regulation (EU) 2019/1111[^f00049])

IMPORTANT

To be issued, upon application by a party, by the court of a Member State of origin of a return decision as communicated to the Commission pursuant to Article 103 of the Regulation, where the return decision needs to be enforced in another Member State due to a further abduction of the child(ren) after return was ordered, or where the return decision contains a provisional, including a protective, measure based on Article 27(5) of the Regulation to protect the child from the grave risk referred to in point (b) of Article 13(1) of the 1980 Hague Convention.
