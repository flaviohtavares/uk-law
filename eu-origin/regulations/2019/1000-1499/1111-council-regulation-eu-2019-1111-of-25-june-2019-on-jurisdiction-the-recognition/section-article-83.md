# Council Regulation (EU) 2019/1111 of 25 June 2019 on jurisdiction, the recognition and enforcement of decisions in matrimonial matters and the matters of parental responsibility, and on international child abduction (recast), section Article 83

> Text as at 2019-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 83. Costs of Central Authorities

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. The assistance provided by the Central Authorities pursuant to this Regulation shall be free of charge.

2. Each Central Authority shall bear its own costs in applying this Regulation.
