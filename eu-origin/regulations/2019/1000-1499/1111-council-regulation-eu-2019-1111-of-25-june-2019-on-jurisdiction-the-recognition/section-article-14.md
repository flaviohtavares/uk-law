# Council Regulation (EU) 2019/1111 of 25 June 2019 on jurisdiction, the recognition and enforcement of decisions in matrimonial matters and the matters of parental responsibility, and on international child abduction (recast), section Article 14

> Text as at 2019-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 14. Residual jurisdiction

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where no court of a Member State has jurisdiction pursuant to Articles 7 to 11, jurisdiction shall be determined, in each Member State, by the laws of that Member State.
