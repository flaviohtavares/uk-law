# Council Regulation (EU) 2019/1111 of 25 June 2019 on jurisdiction, the recognition and enforcement of decisions in matrimonial matters and the matters of parental responsibility, and on international child abduction (recast), section Article 70

> Text as at 2019-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 70. Differences in applicable law

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The recognition of a decision in matrimonial matters may not be refused because the law of the Member State in which such recognition is invoked would not allow divorce, legal separation or marriage annulment on the same facts.
