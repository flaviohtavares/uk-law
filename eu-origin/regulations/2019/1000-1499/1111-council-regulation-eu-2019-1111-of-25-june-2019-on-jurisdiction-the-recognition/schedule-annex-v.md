## Annotations

[^f00063]: Footnote: Council Regulation (EU) 2019/1111 of 25 June 2019 on jurisdiction, the recognition and enforcement of decisions in matrimonial matters and the matters of parental responsibility, and on international child abduction ([OJ L 178, 2.7.2019, p. 1](https://webarchive.nationalarchives.gov.uk/eu-exit/https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=uriserv:OJ.L_.2019.178.01.0001.01.ENG)) ('the Regulation').

[^f00064]: Footnote: Fields marked with (\*) are mandatory fields.

[^f00065]: Footnote: If more than three children are concerned, please attach an additional sheet.

[^f00066]: Footnote: If more than two parties were granted rights of access, please attach an additional sheet.

[^f00067]: Footnote: Please copy the relevant part of the order.

[^f00068]: Footnote: If the enforcement is sought against more than two parties, please attach an additional sheet.

[^f00069]: Footnote: If more than one party is concerned, please attach an additional sheet.

[^f00070]: Footnote: If more than two parties are concerned, please attach an additional sheet.

[^f00071]: Footnote: If more than three children are concerned, please attach an additional sheet.

[^f00072]: Footnote: If more than one party is concerned, please attach an additional sheet.

[^f00073]: Footnote: If more than two parties are concerned, please attach an additional sheet.

[^f00074]: Footnote: This point also covers situations where the costs are awarded in a separate decision. The mere fact that the amount of the costs has not been fixed yet should not prevent the court from issuing the certificate if a party wishes to seek recognition or enforcement of the substantive part of the decision.

[^f00075]: Footnote: If more than one party has been ordered to bear the costs, please attach an additional sheet.
