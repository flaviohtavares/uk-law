# Regulation (EU) 2019/6 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 11 December 2018 on veterinary medicinal products and repealing Directive 2001/82/EC (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 117

> Text as at 2018-12-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 117. Collection and disposal of waste of veterinary medicinal products

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Member States shall ensure that appropriate systems are in place for the collection and disposal of waste of veterinary medicinal products.
