# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/1787 of 24 October 2019 amending Implementing Regulation (EU) 2016/6 imposing special conditions governing the import of feed and food originating in or consigned from Japan following the accident at the Fukushima nuclear power station (Text with EEA relevance)

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/1787

of 24 October 2019

amending Implementing Regulation (EU) 2016/6 imposing special conditions governing the import of feed and food originating in or consigned from Japan following the accident at the Fukushima nuclear power station

(Text with EEA relevance)

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30c66f1abc7cb1f7730c6e54b5092c84]

Having regard to Regulation (EC) No 178/2002 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 28 January 2002 laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30c66f1abc7cb1f7730c6e54b5092c84][^f00001], and in particular Article 53(1)(b)(ii) thereof, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30c66f1abc7cb1f7730c6e54b5092c84]

Whereas: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30c66f1abc7cb1f7730c6e54b5092c84]

(1)

Article 53 of Regulation (EC) No 178/2002 provides for the possibility to adopt appropriate Union emergency measures for food and feed imported from a third country in order to protect public health, animal health or the environment, where the risk cannot be contained satisfactorily by means of measures taken by the Member States individually. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30c66f1abc7cb1f7730c6e54b5092c84]

(2)

Following the accident at the Fukushima nuclear power station on 11 March 2011, the Commission was informed that radionuclide levels in certain food products originating in Japan exceeded the action levels in food applicable in Japan. Such contamination may constitute a threat to public and animal health in the Union and therefore Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 297/2011 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30c66f1abc7cb1f7730c6e54b5092c84][^f00002] was adopted. That Regulation was replaced by Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 961/2011 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30c66f1abc7cb1f7730c6e54b5092c84][^f00003] which was later replaced by Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 284/2012 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30c66f1abc7cb1f7730c6e54b5092c84][^f00004]. The latter was replaced by Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 996/2012 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30c66f1abc7cb1f7730c6e54b5092c84][^f00005] which was later replaced by Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 322/2014 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30c66f1abc7cb1f7730c6e54b5092c84][^f00006] and which was in turn replaced by Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2016/6 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30c66f1abc7cb1f7730c6e54b5092c84][^f00007].

(3)

Since Implementing Regulation (EU) 2016/6, as amended by Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2017/2058 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30c66f1abc7cb1f7730c6e54b5092c84][^f00008] provides that the measures provided therein are to be reviewed by 30 June 2019 and in order to take into account further development of the situation and occurrence data for 2017 and 2018 on radioactivity in feed and food, it is appropriate to amend Implementing Regulation (EU) 2016/6. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30c66f1abc7cb1f7730c6e54b5092c84]

(4)

The existing measures have been reviewed taking into account more than 100 000 occurrence data on radioactivity in feed and food other than beef and more than 534 000 occurrence data on radioactivity in beef, provided by the Japanese authorities concerning the seventh and eight growing season (January 2017 until December 2018) after the accident. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30c66f1abc7cb1f7730c6e54b5092c84]

(5)

The data submitted by the Japanese authorities for 2017 and 2018 provide evidence that no exceedance of the maximum levels of radioactivity were observed in feed and food originating from Chiba, Tochigi and Iwate during the eighth growing season after the accident and it is no longer necessary to require the sampling and analysis of feed and food originating in the prefectures of Chiba, Tochigi and Iwate regarding the presence of radioactivity before export to the Union. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30c66f1abc7cb1f7730c6e54b5092c84]

(6)

For feed and food products originating in the prefecture of Fukushima, taking into account the occurrence data provided by the Japanese authorities for 2017 and 2018, it is appropriate to lift the requirement of sampling and analysis before export to the Union for soybeans, giant butterbur, bracken, Japanese royal fern and ostrich fern and products derived thereof. For the other feed and food originating in that prefecture, it is appropriate to maintain the requirement of sampling and analysis before export to the Union. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30c66f1abc7cb1f7730c6e54b5092c84]

(7)

As regards the prefectures of Miyagi, Ibaraki and Gunma, it is currently required to sample and analyse mushrooms, fishery products and certain edible wild plants and derived products thereof, before export to the Union. The occurrence data for the eighth growing season provide evidence that it is appropriate to no longer require sampling and analysis before export to the Union for fish and fishery products and certain edible wild plants and derived products thereof from the prefectures of Miyagi, Ibaraki and Gunma and also for mushrooms from the prefecture of Ibaraki. With regard to edible wild plants and derived products thereof, the sampling and analysis should no longer be required for bamboo shoots for the prefectures Ibaraki and Gunma, but it should be maintained for the prefecture Miyagi; and the sampling and analysis should no longer be required for Ostrich fern and Japanese royal fern for the prefecture Miyagi. On the other hand, non-compliances have been found in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30c66f1abc7cb1f7730c6e54b5092c84] *Aralia *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30c66f1abc7cb1f7730c6e54b5092c84]* spp. from the prefecture Gunma during the eighth growing season and therefore it is appropriate to require sampling and analysis for *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30c66f1abc7cb1f7730c6e54b5092c84] *Aralia *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30c66f1abc7cb1f7730c6e54b5092c84]* spp. and derived products thereof from the prefecture Gunma before its export to the Union. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30c66f1abc7cb1f7730c6e54b5092c84]

(8)

As regards the prefectures of Nagano and Niigata, it is currently required to sample and analyse mushrooms and certain edible wild plants and the processed and derived products thereof before export to the Union. The occurrence data for the eight growing season provide evidence that it is appropriate to no longer require sampling and analysis before export to the Union for mushrooms for both prefectures and also for the edible wild plants Ostrich fern, Japanese royal fern and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30c66f1abc7cb1f7730c6e54b5092c84] *Aralia *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30c66f1abc7cb1f7730c6e54b5092c84]* spp, and derived products thereof from the prefecture of Nagano. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30c66f1abc7cb1f7730c6e54b5092c84]

(9)

The occurrence data from the seventh and eighth growing season provide evidence that it is appropriate to maintain the requirement for sampling and analysis before export to the Union for mushrooms and koshiabura and derived products thereof originating from the prefectures of Shizuoka, Yamanashi and Yamagata. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30c66f1abc7cb1f7730c6e54b5092c84]

(10)

Taking into account the occurrence data from the seventh and eighth growing season, it is appropriate to structure the provisions of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2016/6 in a manner that prefectures of which the same feed and food has to be sampled and analysed before export to the Union are grouped together. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30c66f1abc7cb1f7730c6e54b5092c84]

(11)

The controls performed at import show that the special conditions provided for by Union law are correctly implemented by the Japanese authorities and non-compliance has not been found at import controls for more than seven years. Therefore, it is appropriate to keep the low frequency of controls at import. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30c66f1abc7cb1f7730c6e54b5092c84]

(12)

It is appropriate to provide for a review of the provisions of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2016/6 when the results of sampling and analysis on the presence of radioactivity of feed and food of the ninth and tenth growing season (2019 and 2020) after the accident are available, i.e. by 30 June 2021. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30c66f1abc7cb1f7730c6e54b5092c84]

(13)

Implementing Regulation (EU) 2016/6 should therefore be amended accordingly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30c66f1abc7cb1f7730c6e54b5092c84]

(14)

The measures provided for in this Regulation are in accordance with the opinion of the Standing Committee on Plants, Animals, Food and Feed, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30c66f1abc7cb1f7730c6e54b5092c84]
