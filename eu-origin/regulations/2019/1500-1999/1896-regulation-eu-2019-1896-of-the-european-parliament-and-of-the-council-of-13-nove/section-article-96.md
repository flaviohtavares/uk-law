# Regulation (EU) 2019/1896 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 November 2019 on the European Border and Coast Guard and repealing Regulations (EU) No 1052/2013 and (EU) 2016/1624, section Article 96

> Text as at 2019-11-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 96. Privileges and immunities

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Protocol No 7 on the Privileges and Immunities of the European Union annexed to the Treaty on European Union (TEU) and to the TFEU shall apply to the Agency and its statutory staff.
