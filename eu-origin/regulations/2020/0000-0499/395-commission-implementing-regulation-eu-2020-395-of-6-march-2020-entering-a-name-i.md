# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/395 of 6 March 2020 entering a name in the register of traditional specialities guaranteed ‘Amatriciana Tradizionale’ (TSG)

> Text as at 2020-03-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/395

of 6 March 2020

entering a name in the register of traditional specialities guaranteed ‘Amatriciana Tradizionale’ (TSG)

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) No 1151/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 21 November 2012 on quality schemes for agricultural products and foodstuffs[^f00001], and in particular Article 52(2) thereof,

Whereas:

(1)

Pursuant to Article 50(2)(b) of Regulation (EU) No 1151/2012, Italy’s application to register the name ‘Amatriciana Tradizionale’ was published in the *Official Journal of the European Union* [^f00002].

(2)

As no statement of opposition under Article 51 of Regulation (EU) No 1151/2012 has been received by the Commission, the name ‘Amatriciana Tradizionale’ should therefore be entered in the register,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

**Article 1.** The name ‘Amatriciana Tradizionale’ (TSG) is hereby entered in the register.

The name specified in the first paragraph denotes a product in Class 2.21. – prepared meals, as listed in Annex XI to Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 668/2014[^f00003].

**Article 2.** This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Union*.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 6 March 2020.

*For the Commission,*

*On behalf of the President,*

Janusz Wojciechowski

*Member of the Commission*
