# Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2020/692 of 30 January 2020 supplementing Regulation (EU) 2016/429 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards rules for entry into the Union, and the movement and handling after entry of consignments of certain animals, germinal products and products of animal origin (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 160

> Text as at 2024-09-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 160. The third country or territory of origin or zone thereof of the egg products

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Consignments of egg products shall only be permitted to enter the Union if the egg products of the consignment originate from a third country or territory or zone thereof which applies a disease surveillance programme for highly pathogenic avian influenza that complies with the requirements established in either:

- ((a)) Annex II to this Regulation;

  or
- ((b)) the relevant Chapter of the Terrestrial Animal Health Code of the World Organisation for Animal Health (OIE).
