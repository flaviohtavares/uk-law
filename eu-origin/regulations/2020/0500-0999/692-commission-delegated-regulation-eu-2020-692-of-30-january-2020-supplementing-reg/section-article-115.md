# Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2020/692 of 30 January 2020 supplementing Regulation (EU) 2016/429 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards rules for entry into the Union, and the movement and handling after entry of consignments of certain animals, germinal products and products of animal origin (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 115

> Text as at 2024-09-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 115. The hatching eggs of the consignment

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Consignments of hatching eggs of captive birds shall only be permitted to enter the Union if they were obtained from captive birds which comply with the requirements for entry into the Union set out in Articles 55 to 58.
