# Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2020/692 of 30 January 2020 supplementing Regulation (EU) 2016/429 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards rules for entry into the Union, and the movement and handling after entry of consignments of certain animals, germinal products and products of animal origin (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 53

> Text as at 2024-09-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 53. Requirements concerning the identification of captive birds

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Consignments of captive birds shall only be permitted to enter the Union if the animals of the consignment are identified with an individual identification number by means of a unique marked closed leg-ring or an injectable transponder, which contains at least the following information:

- ((a)) the code of the third country or territory of origin conforming with ISO Standard 3166 in the format of two-letter;
- ((b)) a unique serial number.
