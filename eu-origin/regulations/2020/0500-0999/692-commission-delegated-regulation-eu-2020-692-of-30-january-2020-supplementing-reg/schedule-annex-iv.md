### PART B

**Specific conditions** to be provided by the competent authority of the third country or territory where the third country or territory or zone thereof has been free from certain diseases for less than the period set out in the table in Part A of this Annex as referred to in Article 22(3):

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Foot and mouth disease | Supplementary information to determine the date from which the third country or territory or zone thereof is considered to be free from foot and mouth disease. |
| Classical swine fever | - ((a)) supplementary information to determine the date from which the third country or territory or zone thereof is considered to be free from classical swine fever;<br>- ((b)) the animals intended for entry into the Union have reacted negatively to a test for the detection of classical swine fever, carried out within a period of 30 days prior to the date of dispatch to the Union. |
| Infection with *Burkholderia mallei* (Glanders) | - ((a)) the disease not reported in the establishment of origin during a period of at least 6 months prior to the date of dispatch to the Union;<br>- ((b)) the Commission has recognised the surveillance programme carried out in breeding equine animals in the establishment of origin to demonstrate absence of infection during that period of 6 months. |
| Dourine | - ((a)) the disease not reported in the establishment of origin for a period at least 6 months prior to the date of dispatch to the Union;<br>- ((b)) the Commission has recognised the surveillance programme carried out to demonstrate the absence of infection in the establishment of origin during that period of 6 months. |
| Surra (*Trypanosoma evansi*) | - ((a)) the disease has not been reported in the establishment of origin for a period of at least the 6 months prior to the date of dispatch to the Union;<br>- ((b)) the Commission has recognised the surveillance programme carried out to demonstrate the absence of infection in the establishment of origin during that period of 6 months. |
