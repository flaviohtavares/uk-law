# Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2020/688 of 17 December 2019 supplementing Regulation (EU) 2016/429 of the European Parliament and of the Council, as regards animal health requirements for movements within the Union of terrestrial animals and hatching eggs (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 104

> Text as at 2019-12-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 104. Requirements for advance notification by operators of movements of wild terrestrial animals to other Member States

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Operators other than transporters moving wild terrestrial animals to another Member State shall notify the competent authority of the Member State of origin at least 24 hours before the departure of the consignment.
