# Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2020/688 of 17 December 2019 supplementing Regulation (EU) 2016/429 of the European Parliament and of the Council, as regards animal health requirements for movements within the Union of terrestrial animals and hatching eggs (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 28

> Text as at 2019-12-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 28. Derogation for movements of kept cervid animals intended for slaughter to other Member States

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

By way of derogation from the requirements set out in Articles 26, operators may move kept cervid animals intended for slaughter to another Member State or zone thereof where those animals come from an establishment

- ((a)) in which infection with rabies virus in kept terrestrial animals has not been reported during the last 30 days prior to departure;
- ((b)) in which anthrax in ungulates has not been reported during the last 15 days prior to departure;
- ((c)) in which no infection with Bluetongue virus (serotypes 1-24) has been reported during the last 30 days prior to departure.
