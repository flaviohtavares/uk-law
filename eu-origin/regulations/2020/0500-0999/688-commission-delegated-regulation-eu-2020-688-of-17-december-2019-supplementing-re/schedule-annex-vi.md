# Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2020/688 of 17 December 2019 supplementing Regulation (EU) 2016/429 of the European Parliament and of the Council, as regards animal health requirements for movements within the Union of terrestrial animals and hatching eggs (Text with EEA relevance), ANNEX VI

> Text as at 2019-12-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## ANNEX VI — CRITERIA FOR VACCINES AGAINST INFECTION WITH NEWCASTLE DISEASE VIRUS

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Live attenuated vaccines against infection with Newcastle disease virus must be prepared from a Newcastle disease virus strain for which the master seed has been tested and shown to have an intracerebral pathogenicity index (ICPI) of:

- ((a)) less than 0,4 if not less than 10<sup>7</sup> EID<sub>50</sub> (50 % Embryo Infectious Dose) are administrated to each bird in the ICPI test; or
- ((b)) less than 0,5 if not less than 10<sup>8</sup> EID<sub>50</sub> are administered to each bird in the ICPI test.
