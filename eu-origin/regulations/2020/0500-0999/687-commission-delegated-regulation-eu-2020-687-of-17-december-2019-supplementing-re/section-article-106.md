# Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2020/687 of 17 December 2019 supplementing Regulation (EU) 2016/429 of the European Parliament and the Council, as regards rules for the prevention and control of certain listed diseases (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 106

> Text as at 2019-12-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 106. Extension of measures

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The competent authority may decide that relevant measures in Articles 102 to 105 also apply to aquatic animals of non-listed species.
