# Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2020/687 of 17 December 2019 supplementing Regulation (EU) 2016/429 of the European Parliament and the Council, as regards rules for the prevention and control of certain listed diseases (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 40

> Text as at 2019-12-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 40. Measures to be applied in establishments in the surveillance zone

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The competent authority shall order the application, without delay, of the measures provided for in Article 25 in all the establishments in the surveillance zone keeping animals of listed species.
