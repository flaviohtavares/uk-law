# Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2020/687 of 17 December 2019 supplementing Regulation (EU) 2016/429 of the European Parliament and the Council, as regards rules for the prevention and control of certain listed diseases (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 77

> Text as at 2019-12-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 77. Official confirmation of a category A disease in aquaculture animals

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The competent authority shall officially confirm an outbreak of a category A disease in aquaculture animals when the conditions set out in Article 9(2), (3) and (4) of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2020/689 are met.
