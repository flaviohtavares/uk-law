> Text as at 2019-12-17.

## Article 112. Repeals

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Directive 92/66/EEC, Directive 2001/89/EC, Directive 2003/85/EC and Directive 2005/94/EC as well as the acts adopted on the basis thereof, shall cease to apply with effect from 21 April 2021.
