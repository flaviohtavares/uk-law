# Commission Regulation (EC) No 261/2003 of 12 February 2003 concerning the provisional authorisation of new uses of additives in feedingstuffs (Text with EEA relevance)

> Text as at 2017-07-19.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Commission Regulation (EC) No 261/2003

of 12 February 2003

concerning the provisional authorisation of new uses of additives in feedingstuffs

(Text with EEA relevance)

**Article 1.**[^c000001] The preparation specified in Annex I, belonging to the group ‘ Enzymes ’ is authorised for use as additive in animal nutrition under the conditions laid down in that Annex.

**Article 2.** This Regulation shall enter into force on the day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.
