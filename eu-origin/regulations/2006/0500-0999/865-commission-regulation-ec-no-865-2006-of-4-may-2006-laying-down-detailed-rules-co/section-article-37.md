# Commission regulation (EC) No 865/2006 of 4 May 2006 laying down detailed rules concerning the implementation of Council Regulation (EC) No 338/97 on the protection of species of wild fauna and flora by regulating trade therein, section Article 37

> Text as at 2026-06-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 37. Issue

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1.[^c000001] The management authority[^key-f8edebfc30840c75df5b662d290f785e] may issue personal ownership certificates to the legal owner of legally acquired live animals, held for personal non-commercial purposes.

2. A personal ownership certificate shall cover only one specimen.

3. A continuation sheet shall be attached to the certificate for use in accordance with Article 42.
