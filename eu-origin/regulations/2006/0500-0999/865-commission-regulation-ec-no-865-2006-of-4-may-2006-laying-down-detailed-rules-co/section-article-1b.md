# Commission regulation (EC) No 865/2006 of 4 May 2006 laying down detailed rules concerning the implementation of Council Regulation (EC) No 338/97 on the protection of species of wild fauna and flora by regulating trade therein, section Article 1b

> Text as at 2026-06-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 1b. Electronic forms[^key-996fed6e7a7dcc712910de59325e69e7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Any form mentioned in Article 4 (including an application form, continuation sheet, label, notification, annex to a form and supporting document) may be submitted to the management authority in paper or electronic format or both.
