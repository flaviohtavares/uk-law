# Commission regulation (EC) No 865/2006 of 4 May 2006 laying down detailed rules concerning the implementation of Council Regulation (EC) No 338/97 on the protection of species of wild fauna and flora by regulating trade therein, section Article 63

> Text as at 2026-06-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 63. Pre-issued certificates under Article 8(3) of Regulation (EC) No 338/97

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. For the purposes of Article 8(3)(d) of Regulation (EC) No 338/97, the management authority may make pre-issued certificates available to breeders approved by the management authority for that purpose[^c-18ca9d9afdc1084bb47ee9cd3e8b5bde], provided that they maintain breeding records and that they produce those records, on request, to the [^c-a748ba4b074dcc4b34c12a1d77a330d2]... management authority.

Such certificates shall, in box 20, include the following statement:

‘Certificate only valid for the following taxon/taxa: …’

2. For the purposes of Article 8(3)(d) and (h) of Regulation (EC) No 338/97, the management authority may make pre-issued certificates available to persons who have been approved by the management authority[^c-8e3ee5ba522f0de8308e7353abc142a6] to sell on the basis of such certificates dead captive-bred specimens and/or small numbers of dead specimens legally taken from the wild within Great Britain[^c-d95bf6eab53be037731766c6e9939a26], provided that any such person meets the following requirements:

- (a) he maintains a record, which is produced on request to the [^c-09515bd2f1a5db7de2f1957c01ef7eb9]... management authority and which contains details of specimens/species sold, the cause of death if known, the persons from whom specimens were acquired and the persons to whom they were sold;
- (b) he submits an annual report to the [^c-71bdfa6c290607f3120a2f44f443e443]... management authority which contains details of sales during that year, the type and number of specimens, the species concerned and how the specimens were acquired.

3.[^c000003] Pre-issued certificates shall only be valid once they have been completed and a copy of the certificate is transmitted to the issuing management authority by the applicant.
