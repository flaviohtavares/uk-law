# Commission regulation (EC) No 865/2006 of 4 May 2006 laying down detailed rules concerning the implementation of Council Regulation (EC) No 338/97 on the protection of species of wild fauna and flora by regulating trade therein, section Article 44c

> Text as at 2026-06-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 44c. Issuing authority[^c000005]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. Where the sample collection originates within Great Britain[^c-26eec86a61f7dc82599824ddcc6fe5ca] the issuing authority for a sample collection certificate shall be the management authority [^c-c302fbddd0ff81fa89b6860f57fc6cf3]....

2. Where the sample collection originates in a third country, the issuing authority for a sample collection certificate shall be the management authority [^c-fa2fd61f9d0bac31945645017dbf084a]... and the issuance of that certificate shall be based on the provision of an equivalent document, issued by that third country.
