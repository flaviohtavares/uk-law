# Commission regulation (EC) No 865/2006 of 4 May 2006 laying down detailed rules concerning the implementation of Council Regulation (EC) No 338/97 on the protection of species of wild fauna and flora by regulating trade therein, section Article 44i

> Text as at 2026-06-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 44i. Use[^c000008]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The certificate may be used in either of the following ways:

- ((a)) as an import permit in accordance with Article 4 of Regulation (EC) No 338/97;
- ((b)) as an export permit or re-export certificate in accordance with Article 5 of Regulation (EC) No 338/97.
