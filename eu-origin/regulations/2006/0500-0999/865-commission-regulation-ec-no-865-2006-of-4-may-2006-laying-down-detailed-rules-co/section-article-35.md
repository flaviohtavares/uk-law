## Article 35. Documents to be surrendered to the customs office by the holder

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. For[^c-d86f61ff0b97f8f71b8ae46cf7142f28] a travelling exhibition certificate issued in accordance with this Regulation, Regulation 338/97 (pre-exit), or the Convention[^c-9d02beba0f4f3d15741ff79ffd5eab62], the holder or his authorised representative shall, for verification purposes, surrender the original of that certificate (form 1), and the original and a copy of the continuation sheet, to a customs office designated in accordance with Article 12(1) of Regulation (EC) No 338/97.
