# Commission regulation (EC) No 865/2006 of 4 May 2006 laying down detailed rules concerning the implementation of Council Regulation (EC) No 338/97 on the protection of species of wild fauna and flora by regulating trade therein, section Article 31

> Text as at 2026-06-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 31. Use

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A travelling exhibition certificate may be used as follows:

- ((1)) as an import permit, in accordance with Article 4 of Regulation (EC) No 338/97;
- ((2)) as an export permit or re-export certificate, in accordance with Article 5 of Regulation (EC) No 338/97;
- ((3)) as a certificate in accordance with Article 8(3) of Regulation (EC) No 338/97 for the sole purpose of allowing the specimens to be displayed to the public for commercial purposes.[^c000004]
