# Commission regulation (EC) No 865/2006 of 4 May 2006 laying down detailed rules concerning the implementation of Council Regulation (EC) No 338/97 on the protection of species of wild fauna and flora by regulating trade therein, section Article 5b

> Text as at 2026-06-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 5b. Specific content of permits and certificates for live rhinoceros and live elephants[^c000007]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Permits and certificates issued under Article 4 or Article 5 of Regulation (EC) No 338/97 for import or re-export of live rhinoceroses or live elephants from populations included in Annex B to that Regulation shall contain a condition stating that horn or ivory from those animals or from their progeny may not enter commercial trade or commercial activities within Great Britain[^key-377ba2b88b7d0fe431fe34747a287686]. In addition, live rhinoceroses or live elephants from those populations shall not be subject to trophy hunting outside of their historic range.
