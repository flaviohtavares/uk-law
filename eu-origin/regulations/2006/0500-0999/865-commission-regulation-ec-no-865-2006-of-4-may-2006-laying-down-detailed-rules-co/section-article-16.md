# Commission regulation (EC) No 865/2006 of 4 May 2006 laying down detailed rules concerning the implementation of Council Regulation (EC) No 338/97 on the protection of species of wild fauna and flora by regulating trade therein, section Article 16

> Text as at 2026-06-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 16. Specimens in transit through Great Britain[^c-2c7011e8cbfe5a869e92ec74424ab941]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Articles 14 and 15 of this Regulation shall apply *mutatis mutandis* to specimens of species listed in Annexes A and B to Regulation (EC) No 338/97 which are in transit through Great Britain[^c-6e1cec90c9f32b468ba12f70988b888b] where that transit is otherwise in accordance with the latter Regulation.
