# Commission regulation (EC) No 865/2006 of 4 May 2006 laying down detailed rules concerning the implementation of Council Regulation (EC) No 338/97 on the protection of species of wild fauna and flora by regulating trade therein, section Article 26a

> Text as at 2026-06-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 26a. Rejection of applications for export permits[^c000005]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The management authority[^c-001aca2621cb813697e3c1242943cce2] shall reject applications for export permits for caviar and meat of sturgeon species ( *Acipenseriformes* spp.) from shared stocks unless export quotas have been set for the species in accordance with the procedure approved by the Conference of the Parties to the Convention.
