# Commission regulation (EC) No 865/2006 of 4 May 2006 laying down detailed rules concerning the implementation of Council Regulation (EC) No 338/97 on the protection of species of wild fauna and flora by regulating trade therein, section Article 57

> Text as at 2026-06-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 57. Introduction and reintroduction into Great Britain of personal and household effects[^key-e3fadc314f2e472f450317f120ea8f94]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. The derogation from Article 4 of Regulation (EC) No 338/97 for personal or household effects, provided for in Article 7(3) of that Regulation, shall not apply to specimens used for commercial gain, sold, displayed for commercial purposes, kept for sale, offered for sale or transported for sale.

That derogation shall only apply to specimens, including hunting trophies, if they are not subject to any additional measures, conditions or restrictions set out in guidance published under Article 10(2) of Regulation[^key-9f4958ffc125439f5592268cfe325460] [(EC) No 338/97](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1997/0338) and meet one of the following conditions:

- (a) they are contained in the personal luggage of travellers coming from a third country;
- (b) they are contained in the personal property of a natural person transferring his normal place of residence from a third country to Great Britain[^key-3a291c5ee49984a6b3e9b3b02c3f9ced];
- (c) they are hunting trophies taken by a traveller and imported at a later date.

2. The derogation from Article 4 of Regulation (EC) No 338/97 for personal or household effects, provided for in Article 7(3) of that Regulation, shall not apply to specimens of species listed in Annex A thereto where they are introduced into Great Britain[^key-2ea204a11c841a26bd536b94247f9f5c] for the first time by a person normally residing in, or taking up residence in, Great Britain.

3. The first introduction into Great Britain[^key-90ffd406275d776c766c200c2cc0de34] of personal or household effects, including hunting trophies, by a person normally residing in Great Britain and involving specimens of species listed in Annex B to Regulation (EC) No 338/97 shall not require the presentation to customs of an import permit, provided that the original of a (re-)export document and a copy thereof are presented.

[^key-1addebdc6a67290c4eaf1311d2e7304f]...

3a.[^c000008] By way of derogation from paragraph 3, the first introduction into Great Britain[^key-0b5e08b2b5154f859f0ae3be8b4dc184] of hunting trophies of specimens of species or populations listed in Annex B to Regulation (EC) No 338/97 and in Annex XIII to this Regulation shall be subject to Article 4 of Regulation (EC) No 338/97.

4. The reintroduction into Great Britain[^key-2b43d3225cdbb344e1df6cfd9d8e656e], by a person normally residing in Great Britain, of personal or household effects, including hunting trophies, that are specimens of species listed in Annex A or B to Regulation (EC) No 338/97 shall not require the presentation to customs of an import permit, provided that one of the following is presented:

- (a) the customs-endorsed ‘copy for the holder’ (form 2) of a previously used import or export permit issued in Great Britain[^key-79c4c9db98b0f570b1fd94649e7833a7];
- (b) the copy of the (re-)export document referred to in paragraph 3;
- (c) proof that the specimens were acquired within Great Britain.

5.[^c000004] By way of derogation from paragraphs 3 and 4, the introduction or re-introduction into Great Britain[^key-c1382fb9b3ddad8eafee1833da013e46] of the following items listed in Annex B to Regulation (EC) No 338/97 shall not require the presentation of a (re-)export document or an import permit:

- (a) caviar of sturgeon species ( *Acipenseriformes* spp.), up to a maximum of 125 grams per person, in containers that are individually marked in accordance with Article 66(6);
- (b) rainsticks of *Cactaceae* spp., up to three per person;
- (c) dead worked specimens of *Crocodylia* spp., excluding meat and hunting trophies, up to four per person;
- (d) shells of *Strombus gigas* , up to three per person;
- (e) *Hippocampus* spp. up to four dead specimens per person;
- (f) shells of *Tridacnidae* spp. up to three specimens per person not exceeding three kg in total, where a specimen may be one intact shell or two matching halves;[^c000002]
- (g) specimens of agarwood ( *Aquilaria* spp. and *Gyrinops* spp.) — up to 1 kg woodchips, 24 ml oil, and two sets of beads or prayer beads (or two necklaces or bracelets) per person.
