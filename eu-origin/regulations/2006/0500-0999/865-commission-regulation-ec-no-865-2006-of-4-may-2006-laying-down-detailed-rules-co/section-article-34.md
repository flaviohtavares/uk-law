# Commission regulation (EC) No 865/2006 of 4 May 2006 laying down detailed rules concerning the implementation of Council Regulation (EC) No 338/97 on the protection of species of wild fauna and flora by regulating trade therein, section Article 34

> Text as at 2026-06-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 34. Applications

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1a.[^key-4e74cf2e6d98a6b1965421cd5c97fb0e] The applicant for a travelling-exhibition certificate must complete boxes 3 and 9 to 18 of the application form (form 3) and boxes 3 and 9 to 18 of the original and all copies.

(1b.) The applicant must include in the application form the name and details of the owner of the specimen and, if relevant, the name and details of a person authorised by the owner of the specimen to act on their behalf.

(1c.) Where the applicant for a certificate is not the owner of the specimen subject to the application, evidence of authority to make the application on behalf of the owner must be submitted in written form to the management authority together with the application form;

(1d.) The management authority may authorise the completion of a single application form in which case such an application may be for more than one certificate.

2. The duly completed form shall be submitted to the management authority [^key-1df98c0902a159ebd9c639652000d6cc]..., together with the necessary information and the documentary evidence that that authority deems necessary so as to enable it to determine whether a certificate should be issued.

The omission of information from the application must be justified.

3. Where an application is made for a certificate relating to specimens for which such an application has previously been rejected, the applicant shall inform the management authority of that fact.
