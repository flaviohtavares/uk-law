# Commission regulation (EC) No 865/2006 of 4 May 2006 laying down detailed rules concerning the implementation of Council Regulation (EC) No 338/97 on the protection of species of wild fauna and flora by regulating trade therein, section Article 55

> Text as at 2026-06-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 55. Establishment of ancestry

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where, for the purposes of Articles 54, 62(1) or 63(1), a competent authority considers it necessary to establish the ancestry of an animal through the analysis of blood or other tissue, such analysis or the necessary samples shall be made available in a manner established by that authority.
