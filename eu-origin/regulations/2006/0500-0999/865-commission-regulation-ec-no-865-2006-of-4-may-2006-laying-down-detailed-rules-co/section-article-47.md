# Commission regulation (EC) No 865/2006 of 4 May 2006 laying down detailed rules concerning the implementation of Council Regulation (EC) No 338/97 on the protection of species of wild fauna and flora by regulating trade therein, section Article 47

> Text as at 2026-06-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 47. Certificates provided for in Article 5 (3) and (4) of Regulation (EC) No 338/97 (certificates required for export or re-export)[^key-f9e601f1aace539a4ffc079ce9a2c982]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The certificates provided for in Article 5[^key-aa15d0d5347e3f861ae5f4b5c880987e] (3) and (4) of Regulation (EC) No 338/97 shall indicate which of the following statements apply in the case of the specimens covered:

- ((1)) they were taken from the wild in accordance with the legislation of the United Kingdom (as that legislation applies in Great Britain)[^key-089705b1da470741d5e2272fc8fed7dc];
- ((2)) they were abandoned or had escaped and were recovered in accordance with the legislation of the United Kingdom (as that legislation applies in Great Britain)[^key-35b7ed41da4a336d636a74ab95ef93fe];
- ((3)) they were acquired in, or introduced into Great Britain after IP completion day[^key-983396f1c36b1ce93724dc5fd4cfc4a6], in accordance with Regulation (EC) No 338/97;
- ((3A)) they were acquired in, or were introduced into, the United Kingdom before IP completion day, in accordance with Regulation 338/97 (pre-exit);[^key-8d91ec34a08c051a493aba618c3d60e9]
- ((4)) they were acquired in, or introduced into the United Kingdom[^key-6210804aac59d43a4c31e5786cce1b78], before 1 June 1997 in accordance with Regulation (EEC) No 3626/82;
- ((5)) they were acquired in, or introduced into, the United Kingdom[^key-3216d34525f5efbca32049566410eff5] before 1 January 1984 in accordance with the Convention;
- ((6)) they were acquired in, or introduced into, the territory of a Member State before the Regulations referred to in points (3A)[^key-ef066367ee96fd5251465372ac42ab36] or (4) or the Convention became applicable to them, or became applicable in that Member State , and were acquired in, or were introduced into, the United Kingdom before IP completion day[^key-33d959b4f190a93096df1075bc48d2a3].
