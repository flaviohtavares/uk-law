# Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 18 December 2006 concerning the Registration, Evaluation, Authorisation and Restriction of Chemicals (REACH), establishing a European Chemicals Agency, amending Directive 1999/45/EC and repealing Council Regulation (EEC) No 793/93 and Commission Regulation (EC) No 1488/94 as well as Council Directive 76/769/EEC and Commission Directives 91/155/EEC, 93/67/EEC, 93/105/EC and 2000/21/EC (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 120

> Text as at 2026-08-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 120. Cooperation with other countries and international organisations[^c000001][^key-118bd0ef92d1775595a3c903722b4564]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Notwithstanding Articles 118 and 119, information received by the Agency under this Regulation may be disclosed to any government or national authority of another country or an international organisation in accordance with an agreement concluded between the United Kingdom and the other country or international organisation,[^key-01b620f268e619dfa48261c0d5be6603] provided that both the following conditions are met:

- ((a)) the purpose of the agreement is cooperation on the implementation or management of legislation concerning chemicals covered by this Regulation;
- ((b)) the other country or international organisation concerned[^key-2758ad7fe3a63aa4f359ba7a37058847] protects the confidential information as mutually agreed.
