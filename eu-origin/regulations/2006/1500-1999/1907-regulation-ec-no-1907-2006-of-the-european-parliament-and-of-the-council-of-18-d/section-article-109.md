# Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 18 December 2006 concerning the Registration, Evaluation, Authorisation and Restriction of Chemicals (REACH), establishing a European Chemicals Agency, amending Directive 1999/45/EC and repealing Council Regulation (EEC) No 793/93 and Commission Regulation (EC) No 1488/94 as well as Council Directive 76/769/EEC and Commission Directives 91/155/EEC, 93/67/EEC, 93/105/EC and 2000/21/EC (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 109

> Text as at 2026-08-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 109. Rules on transparency[^c000001]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

To ensure transparency, the Agency must[^key-038d71dd4c31044716eca8695e0ff831] adopt rules to ensure the availability to the public of regulatory, scientific or technical information concerning the safety of substances on their own, in mixtures[^c000002] or in articles which is not of a confidential nature.
