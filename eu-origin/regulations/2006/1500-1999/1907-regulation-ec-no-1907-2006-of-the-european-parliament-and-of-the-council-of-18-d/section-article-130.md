## Article 130. Statement of reasons for decisions[^c000001]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The appropriate authorities and[^key-e35a2ba982511ea62208b0da6c4ca812] the Agency [^key-bf49215239f3800e5b7682d0a3db4fce]... shall state the reasons for all decisions they take under this Regulation.
