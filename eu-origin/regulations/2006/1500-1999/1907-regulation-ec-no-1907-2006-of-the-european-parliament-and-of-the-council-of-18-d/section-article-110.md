# Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 18 December 2006 concerning the Registration, Evaluation, Authorisation and Restriction of Chemicals (REACH), establishing a European Chemicals Agency, amending Directive 1999/45/EC and repealing Council Regulation (EEC) No 793/93 and Commission Regulation (EC) No 1488/94 as well as Council Directive 76/769/EEC and Commission Directives 91/155/EEC, 93/67/EEC, 93/105/EC and 2000/21/EC (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 110

> Text as at 2026-08-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 110. Relations with relevant public bodies[^c000001][^key-529bf487338f6a9b5506fc8fffc441c4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. The Agency shall cooperate with other public[^key-26e13064bd3b21917b3e80bdb7a01894] bodies to ensure mutual support in the accomplishment of their respective tasks in particular to avoid duplication of work.

2. The Agency, having consulted the Food Standards Agency and Food Standards Scotland[^key-56baeb6451bef58e7e35e363ac7dd8c8] shall establish rules of procedure concerning substances for which an opinion has been sought in a food safety context. [^key-4bb73802f63c31e3ebc64ae32e801e44] ...

This Title shall not otherwise affect the competences vested in the Food Standards Agency and Food Standards Scotland[^key-8565578edbe658c99697deaf34b6b2ce] .

3. This Title shall not affect the competences vested in the Medicines and Healthcare products Regulatory Agency[^key-b8dc9774ac8d66454010f824b8f9468f] .
