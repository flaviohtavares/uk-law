# Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 18 December 2006 concerning the Registration, Evaluation, Authorisation and Restriction of Chemicals (REACH), establishing a European Chemicals Agency, amending Directive 1999/45/EC and repealing Council Regulation (EEC) No 793/93 and Commission Regulation (EC) No 1488/94 as well as Council Directive 76/769/EEC and Commission Directives 91/155/EEC, 93/67/EEC, 93/105/EC and 2000/21/EC (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 35

> Text as at 2026-08-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 35. Access to information for workers[^c000001]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Workers and their representatives shall be granted access by their employer to the information provided in accordance with Articles 31 and 32 in relation to substances or mixtures[^c000002] that they use or may be exposed to in the course of their work.
