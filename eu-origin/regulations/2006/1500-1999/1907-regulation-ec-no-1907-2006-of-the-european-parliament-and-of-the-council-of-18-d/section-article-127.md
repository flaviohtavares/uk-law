## Article 127. Report[^c000001]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The report referred to in Article 117(2)[^key-6ed48f1830e2fda9d5d4e095b7b70c8b] shall, in relation to enforcement, include the results of the official inspections, the monitoring carried out, the penalties provided for and the other measures taken [^key-2b5a9a436f56297a124bbbd44f67f53f]... during the previous reporting period.
