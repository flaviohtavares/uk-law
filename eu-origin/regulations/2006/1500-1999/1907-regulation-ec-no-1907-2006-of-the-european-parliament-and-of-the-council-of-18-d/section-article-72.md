# Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 18 December 2006 concerning the Registration, Evaluation, Authorisation and Restriction of Chemicals (REACH), establishing a European Chemicals Agency, amending Directive 1999/45/EC and repealing Council Regulation (EEC) No 793/93 and Commission Regulation (EC) No 1488/94 as well as Council Directive 76/769/EEC and Commission Directives 91/155/EEC, 93/67/EEC, 93/105/EC and 2000/21/EC (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 72

> Text as at 2026-08-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 72. Submission of an opinion to the appropriate authorities[^c000001][^key-2fd57e59383bf0336b8141f6d3923f26]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. The Agency shall submit to the appropriate authorities[^key-865b502b7a6aa121805e8e5934a119dd] without delay its opinions on risk assessment and socio-economic analysis[^key-30b6b182ee7d0b4db305f809dfa08e21] on restrictions suggested for substances on their own, in mixtures[^c000002] or in articles. If the Agency does[^key-daf51ec7954f2840fda6ef22062bc091] not formulate an opinion by the deadline set in Article 70 and Article 71(1) the Agency shall inform the appropriate authorities[^key-02ab792086e59d87a53044da97047a82] accordingly, stating the reasons.

2. Without prejudice to Articles 118 and 119 the Agency shall publish its opinions[^key-b90738c80140985bb391882ca5af5211] on its website without delay.

3. The Agency shall provide the appropriate authorities that request them[^key-4dcb2de3fe9b14302fe7b0a47489090e] with all documents and evidence submitted to or considered by it.
