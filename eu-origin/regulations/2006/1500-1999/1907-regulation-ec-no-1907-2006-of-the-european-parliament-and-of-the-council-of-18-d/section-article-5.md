# Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 18 December 2006 concerning the Registration, Evaluation, Authorisation and Restriction of Chemicals (REACH), establishing a European Chemicals Agency, amending Directive 1999/45/EC and repealing Council Regulation (EEC) No 793/93 and Commission Regulation (EC) No 1488/94 as well as Council Directive 76/769/EEC and Commission Directives 91/155/EEC, 93/67/EEC, 93/105/EC and 2000/21/EC (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 5

> Text as at 2026-08-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 5. No data, no market[^c000001]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Subject to Articles 6, 7 and 21[^key-33ca8a48ebb5241e174b0294780c7263] , substances on their own, in mixtures[^c000002] or in articles shall not be manufactured in Great Britain[^key-ab103c7d5e7be5c132bf18c47eee4b92] or placed on the market unless they have been registered in accordance with the relevant provisions of this Title where this is required.
