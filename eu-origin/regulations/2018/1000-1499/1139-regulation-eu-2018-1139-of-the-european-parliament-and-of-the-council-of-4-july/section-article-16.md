# Regulation (EU) 2018/1139 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 4 July 2018 on common rules in the field of civil aviation and establishing a European Union Aviation Safety Agency, and amending Regulations (EC) No 2111/2005, (EC) No 1008/2008, (EU) No 996/2010, (EU) No 376/2014 and Directives 2014/30/EU and 2014/53/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council, and repealing Regulations (EC) No 552/2004 and (EC) No 216/2008 of the European Parliament and of the Council and Council Regulation (EEC) No 3922/91 (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 16

> Text as at 2026-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 16. Personnel

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. Personnel responsible for the release of a product, a part or non-installed equipment after maintenance shall be required to hold a licence, except for situations in which, as a result of regulations made under[^key-72d91fa4287c2b66a603bc179ff2f442] point (d) of Article 17(1), taking into account the objectives and principles set out in Articles 1 and 4, and in particular the nature and risk of the activity concerned, such licences are not required.

That licence shall be issued upon application, when the applicant has demonstrated that he or she complies with regulations made under[^key-5b9fcd6e49ab4abf716a8e36b196a8a4] Article 17 [^key-182f860eb46491c63021ea2cfce4b7b9]... to ensure compliance with the essential requirements referred to in Article 9.

2. The licence referred to in paragraph 1 of this Article shall specify the privileges granted to the personnel. The licence may be amended to add or remove privileges, in accordance with regulations made under[^key-3e3140c4df4a780f68b432b9b7703694] point (d) of Article 17(1).

3. The licence referred to in paragraph 1 of this Article may be limited, suspended or revoked when the holder no longer complies with the rules and procedures for issuing and maintaining such licence, in accordance with regulations made under[^key-183ad0a4ebd9fdc144409f60cfd8653a] point (d) of Article 17(1).
