> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 8. State Plan for Aviation Safety

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. The State Safety Programme shall include or be accompanied by a State Plan for Aviation Safety. Based on the assessment of relevant safety information, the Secretary of State, in consultation with the[^key-60ad1d4eb7ef2b349522188eb30e853d] CAA and other relevant stakeholders, shall identify in that plan the main safety risks affecting the[^key-1663507535bda8b844275f342da9f5d8] national civil aviation safety system and shall set out the necessary actions to mitigate those risks.
