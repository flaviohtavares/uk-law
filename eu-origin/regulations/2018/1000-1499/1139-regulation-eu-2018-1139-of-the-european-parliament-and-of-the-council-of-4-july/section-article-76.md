## Article 76. CAA measures[^key-66b0ae7e1e7438603cb1a7c1a1687f46]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. The[^key-fc71c6af91ea825b59362f7d89dfa0d4] CAA must, upon request, assist the Secretary of State in the preparation of proposals for amendments to this Regulation and of regulations to be made under it[^key-29bbbcd7bb6d8e4e08305d967a2c89c4], in accordance with the principles laid down in Article 4. The documents that the[^key-17396e6b7a3aeff2595c8f9222661a28] CAA submits to the Secretary of State for those purposes shall take the form of opinions.

2.[^key-4700aa576887044d1314045a88a24e86] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

3. The CAA[^key-0d2c5f6083a6e82ca24790618a08e69c] shall, in accordance with Article 115 and with the applicable regulations made under[^key-c2cb3505617c7b2ddcfbde9d18ac54e9] this Regulation, issue certification specifications and other detailed specifications, acceptable means of compliance and guidance material for the application of this Regulation and of regulations made under it[^key-b244f7983a21b5712f7cb354f28e5175].

4. The[^key-04805c379d599aababf620f7c3552575] CAA shall take the appropriate decisions for the application of Articles 77 and 79 to 83[^key-64aa4cef212d96b601e68a16eb10b603].
