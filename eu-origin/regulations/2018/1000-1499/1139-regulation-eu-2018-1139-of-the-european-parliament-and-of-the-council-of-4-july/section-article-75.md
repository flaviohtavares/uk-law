# Regulation (EU) 2018/1139 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 4 July 2018 on common rules in the field of civil aviation and establishing a European Union Aviation Safety Agency, and amending Regulations (EC) No 2111/2005, (EC) No 1008/2008, (EU) No 996/2010, (EU) No 376/2014 and Directives 2014/30/EU and 2014/53/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council, and repealing Regulations (EC) No 552/2004 and (EC) No 216/2008 of the European Parliament and of the Council and Council Regulation (EEC) No 3922/91 (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 75

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 75. Functions of the CAA under this Regulation[^key-0b1020ab6cba3cb73b2013127127206c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1.[^key-2aa865e203ab544dbff1143179448b0e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

2. For the purposes of ensuring the proper functioning and development of civil aviation in the United Kingdom[^key-169263d01f5b9e291c4738c65cebaaed] in accordance with the objectives set out in Article 1, the CAA[^key-44c92e4f7d5f8e6d706acfda88b6cf07] shall:

- (a) undertake any task and formulate opinions on all matters covered by this Regulation;
- (b) assist the Secretary of State[^key-6345f001156fd262f8a2c9f639e87c48] by preparing measures to be taken under this Regulation. Where those measures comprise technical rules, the Secretary of State may not change their content without prior coordination with the CAA[^key-ed348053fb97f617f929f648a22607f9];
- (c) provide the Secretary of State[^key-25865320c4396bb51e44e6e0a964a0af] with the necessary technical, scientific and administrative support to carry out its tasks;
- (d) take the necessary measures within the powers conferred on it by this Regulation or other [^key-b5ed1cfc0df4715a37d4344998159bb8]... legislation;
- (e) conduct inspections, other monitoring activities and investigations as necessary to fulfil its tasks under this Regulation[^key-93862673f244a2297e634e189dc2a47d]...;
- (f) within its field of competence, carry out, on behalf of the United Kingdom[^key-225750c0847fde5bf31dea3771bd9cc3], functions and tasks ascribed to the United Kingdom[^key-144e6abab8ead04c2b7caf8e96138f14] by applicable international conventions, in particular the Chicago Convention;
- (g) assist the Secretary of State[^key-b74a33df07e68b2953cd987be778ae9d] in carrying out the tasks of the Secretary of State[^key-28cc1d88e3ab220b36eb73a8860a3710], in particular by providing a forum for exchanges of information and expertise;
- (h) contribute, for matters covered by this Regulation, upon request by the Secretary of State[^key-c39653d81d7365a88e564f7c2d6c1d07], to the establishment, measurement, reporting and analysis of performance indicators, where any relevant[^key-c51a06daad89104e1c7bb45f4e32bd4f] law establishes performance schemes relating to civil aviation;
- (i) promote United Kingdom[^key-e7c0d3b4da7ab2c94e9f37e3b211aea7] aviation standards and rules at international level by establishing appropriate cooperation with the competent authorities of third countries and international organisations;
- (j) cooperate with other [^key-938d15d4c7c9bc45dbc76ef254f77990]... institutions, bodies, offices and agencies in areas where their activities relate to technical aspects of civil aviation.
