# Regulation (EU) 2018/1139 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 4 July 2018 on common rules in the field of civil aviation and establishing a European Union Aviation Safety Agency, and amending Regulations (EC) No 2111/2005, (EC) No 1008/2008, (EU) No 996/2010, (EU) No 376/2014 and Directives 2014/30/EU and 2014/53/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council, and repealing Regulations (EC) No 552/2004 and (EC) No 216/2008 of the European Parliament and of the Council and Council Regulation (EEC) No 3922/91 (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 115

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 115. Procedures for the development of opinions, certification specifications and other detailed specifications, acceptable means of compliance and guidance material

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. The CAA[^key-d11d27d3e73fd36806a87c89330a165f] shall establish transparent procedures for issuing opinions, certification specifications and other detailed specifications, acceptable means of compliance and guidance material referred to in Article 76(1) and (3). Those procedures shall:

- (a) draw on the expertise of [^key-bd4e506ab6701f527c67f46dbdce6d4c]..., where appropriate, military aviation authorities [^key-3dafc4a1ac7c26895d01e88f2c6f0c5f]...;
- (b) whenever necessary, involve experts from relevant interested parties or draw on the expertise of [^key-e2a853ae42bcd2b3ff1be0745014d158]... standardisation bodies or other specialised bodies;
- (c) ensure that the CAA[^key-41f11a74ce8fe01f98f183e120455f8e] publishes documents and widely consults interested parties, in accordance with a timetable and a procedure which includes an obligation on the CAA to give a written response to the consultation process.

2. When the CAA[^key-c28214fb0da69eb3966ee74c212f9ccf], pursuant to Article 76(1) and (3), develops opinions, certification specifications and other detailed specifications, acceptable means of compliance and guidance material, it shall establish a procedure for the prior consultation of relevant stakeholders[^key-611989d707e27ecf3ef225f4fd993b0a]. [^key-89dffba3eb904ce9f3be64ed8a622e40]...

3. The CAA[^key-1ff86c40e5506e9445e9930a15ee9c59] shall publish the opinions, certification specifications and other detailed specifications, acceptable means of compliance and guidance material developed pursuant to Article 76(1) and (3) and the procedures established pursuant to paragraph 1 of this Article on the[^key-0b6df675715a41e784fa39b41c6b77b4] CAA’s website.
