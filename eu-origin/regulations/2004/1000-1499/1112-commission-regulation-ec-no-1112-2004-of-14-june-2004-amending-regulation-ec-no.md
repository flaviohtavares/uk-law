# Commission Regulation (EC) No 1112/2004 of 14 June 2004 amending Regulation (EC) No 1096/2004 fixing the export refunds in the milk and milk products sector

> Text as at 2004-06-14.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Commission Regulation (EC) No 1112/2004

of 14 June 2004

amending Regulation (EC) No 1096/2004 fixing the export refunds in the milk and milk products sector

THE COMMISSION OF THE EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES,

Having regard to the Treaty establishing the European Community,

Having regard to Council Regulation (EC) No 1255/1999 of 17 May 1999 on the common organization of the market in milk and milk products[^f00001], and in particular Article 31(3) thereof,

Whereas:

Commission Regulation (EC) No 1096/2004[^f00002] sets export refunds on products in the milk and milk products sector. A check has shown that its Annex is not consistent with the measures presented for an opinion to the management committee; therefore, the Regulation should be corrected,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

**Article 1.** In the Annex to Regulation (EC) No 1096/2004, the amount of refund for product codes 0401 30 31 9100 are replaced by the following:

| Product code | Destination | Unit of measurement | Amount of refund |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0401 30 31 9100 | L02 | EUR/100 kg | 20,79 |

**Article 2.** This Regulation shall enter into force on 15 June 2004.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 14 June 2004.

*For the Commission*

Franz Fischler

*Member of the Commission*
