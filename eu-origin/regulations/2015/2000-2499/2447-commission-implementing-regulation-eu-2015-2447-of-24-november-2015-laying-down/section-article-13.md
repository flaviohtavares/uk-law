# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2015/2447 of 24 November 2015 laying down detailed rules for implementing certain provisions of Regulation (EU) No 952/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the Union Customs Code, section Article 13

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-06-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/eur/2015/2447
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 13. Storage of information relating to decisions(Article 23(5) of the Code)

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Commissioners for Her Majesty's Revenue and Customs[^c-60bbe2cbd78493802e7771cc8b043994] shall retain all data and supporting information which was relied upon when taking a[^c-a3936d4acff30e49de903e31a87ad0d8] decision for at least 3 years after the end date of its validity.
