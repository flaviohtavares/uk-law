# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2015/2447 of 24 November 2015 laying down detailed rules for implementing certain provisions of Regulation (EU) No 952/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the Union Customs Code, section Article 344

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-06-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/eur/2015/2447
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 344. Goods for which a re-export notification has been lodged that do not leave the customs territory of the Union(Article 174 of the Code)

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where goods for which a re-export notification has been lodged are no longer intended to be taken out of the customs territory of the Union, the person who removes the goods from the customs office of exit for carriage to a place within that territory shall inform the customs office of exit that the goods will not be taken out of the customs territory of the Union and specify the MRN of the re-export notification.
