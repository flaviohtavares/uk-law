# Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2015/2446 of 28 July 2015 supplementing Regulation (EU) No 952/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards detailed rules concerning certain provisions of the Union Customs Code, section Article 225

> Text as at 2025-01-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 225. Sound-, image- or data-carrying media and publicity material(Article 250(2)(d) of the Code)

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Total relief from import duty shall be granted for the following goods:

- ((a)) media carrying sound, image or data supplied free of charge and used for the purposes of demonstration prior to commercialisation, producing sound track, dubbing or reproduction;
- ((b)) material used exclusively for publicity purposes, which includes means of transport specially equipped for those purposes.
