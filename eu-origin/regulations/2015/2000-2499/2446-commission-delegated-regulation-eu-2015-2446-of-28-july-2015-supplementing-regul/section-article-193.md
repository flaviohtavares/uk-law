# Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2015/2446 of 28 July 2015 supplementing Regulation (EU) No 952/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards detailed rules concerning certain provisions of the Union Customs Code, section Article 193

> Text as at 2025-01-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 193. Authorisations for the status of authorised consignor for placing goods under the Union transit procedure (Article 233(4)(a) of the Code)[^c000002]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The status of authorised consignor referred to in Article 233(4)(a) of the Code shall only be granted to applicants who are authorised in accordance with Article 89(5) of the Code to provide a comprehensive guarantee or to use a guarantee waiver in accordance with Article 95(2) of the Code.
