# Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2015/2446 of 28 July 2015 supplementing Regulation (EU) No 952/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards detailed rules concerning certain provisions of the Union Customs Code, section Article 125

> Text as at 2025-01-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 125. Proof of the customs status of Union goods for travellers other than economic operators(Article 6(3)(a) of the Code)

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A traveller, other than an economic operator, may make a request on paper for a proof of the customs status of Union goods.
