# Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2015/2446 of 28 July 2015 supplementing Regulation (EU) No 952/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards detailed rules concerning certain provisions of the Union Customs Code, section Article 153

> Text as at 2025-01-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 153. Release not conditional upon provision of a guarantee(Article 195(2) of the Code)

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where, before the release of goods which are the subject of a request for the granting of a tariff quota, the tariff quota in question is not considered critical, the release of the goods shall not be conditional upon the provision of a guarantee in respect of those goods.
