# Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2015/2446 of 28 July 2015 supplementing Regulation (EU) No 952/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards detailed rules concerning certain provisions of the Union Customs Code, section Article 143

> Text as at 2025-01-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 143. Paper-based customs declarations(Article 158(2) of the Code)

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Travellers may lodge a paper-based customs declaration in respect of goods carried by them.
