# Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2015/2446 of 28 July 2015 supplementing Regulation (EU) No 952/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards detailed rules concerning certain provisions of the Union Customs Code, section Article 36

> Text as at 2025-01-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 36. Neutral elements and packing(Article 60 of the Code)

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. In order to determine whether goods originate in a country or territory, the origin of the following elements shall not be taken into account:

- (a) energy and fuel;
- (b) plant and equipment;
- (c) machines and tools;
- (d) materials which neither enter into the final composition of the goods nor are intended to do so.

2. Where, under general rule 5 for the interpretation of the combined nomenclature set out in Annex I to Council Regulation (EEC) No 2658/87[^f00016], packing materials and packing containers are considered as part of the product for classification purposes, they shall be disregarded for the purpose of determining origin, except where the rule in Annex 22-01 for the goods concerned is based on an added value percentage.
