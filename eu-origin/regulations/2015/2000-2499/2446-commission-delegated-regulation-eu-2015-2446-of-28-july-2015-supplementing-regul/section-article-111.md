# Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2015/2446 of 28 July 2015 supplementing Regulation (EU) No 952/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards detailed rules concerning certain provisions of the Union Customs Code, section Article 111

> Text as at 2025-01-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 111. Time-limits for lodging the entry summary declaration in case of force majeure(Article 127(3) and (7) of the Code)

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The time-limits referred to in Articles 105 to 109 shall not apply in the case of *force majeure*.
