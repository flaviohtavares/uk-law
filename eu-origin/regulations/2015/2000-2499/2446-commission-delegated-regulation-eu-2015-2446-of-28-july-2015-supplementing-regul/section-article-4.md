# Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2015/2446 of 28 July 2015 supplementing Regulation (EU) No 952/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards detailed rules concerning certain provisions of the Union Customs Code, section Article 4

> Text as at 2025-01-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 4. Submission of particulars for EORI registration(Article 6(4) of the Code)

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Customs authorities may allow persons to submit the particulars necessary for the EORI registration by means other than electronic data-processing techniques.
