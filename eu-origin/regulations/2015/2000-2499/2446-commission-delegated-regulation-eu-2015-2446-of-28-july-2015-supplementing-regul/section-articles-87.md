# Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2015/2446 of 28 July 2015 supplementing Regulation (EU) No 952/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards detailed rules concerning certain provisions of the Union Customs Code, section Articles 87

> Text as at 2025-01-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Articles 87. Means of notification of the customs debt(Article 6(3)(a) of the Code)

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The notification of the customs debt in accordance with Article 102 of the Code may be made by means other than by electronic data-processing techniques.
