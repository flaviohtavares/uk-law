# Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2015/2446 of 28 July 2015 supplementing Regulation (EU) No 952/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards detailed rules concerning certain provisions of the Union Customs Code, section Article 160

> Text as at 2025-01-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 160. Means of communication of information sheet INF 3(Article 6(3)(a) of the Code)[^c-b8d6864d2bfa23c259bfcfeb6f128e6c][^c-aa81c2bade219992103912f8b4c54a0d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A document certifying that the conditions for the relief from import duty have been fulfilled (‘information sheet INF 3’) may be communicated using means other than electronic data-processing techniques.
