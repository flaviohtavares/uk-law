# Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2015/2446 of 28 July 2015 supplementing Regulation (EU) No 952/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards detailed rules concerning certain provisions of the Union Customs Code, section Article 203

> Text as at 2025-01-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 203. Type of storage facilities(Article 211(1)(b) of the Code)

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Authorisations for the operation of storage facilities for the customs warehousing of goods shall specify which of the following types of customs warehouses is to be used under each authorisation:

- ((a)) public customs warehouse type I;
- ((b)) public customs warehouse type II;
- ((c)) private customs warehouse.
