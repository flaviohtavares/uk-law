# Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2015/2446 of 28 July 2015 supplementing Regulation (EU) No 952/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards detailed rules concerning certain provisions of the Union Customs Code, section Article 198

> Text as at 2025-01-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 198. Authorisation for the use of a transit declaration with reduced data requirements(Article 233(4)(d) of the Code)

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Authorisations in accordance with Article 233(4)(d) of the Code to use a customs declaration with reduced data requirements to place goods under the Union transit procedure shall be granted for:

- ((a)) transport of goods by rail;
- ((b)) transport of goods by air and sea where an electronic transport document is not used as a transit declaration.
