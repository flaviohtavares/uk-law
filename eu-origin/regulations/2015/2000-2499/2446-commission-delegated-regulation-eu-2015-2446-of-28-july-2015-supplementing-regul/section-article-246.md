# Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2015/2446 of 28 July 2015 supplementing Regulation (EU) No 952/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards detailed rules concerning certain provisions of the Union Customs Code, section Article 246

> Text as at 2025-01-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 246. Means for the exchange of information in cases of presentation of goods at the customs office of exit(Article 6(3)(a) of the Code)

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where goods are presented at the customs office of exit in accordance with Article 267(2) of the Code means for the exchange of information other than electronic data-processing techniques may be used for the following:

- ((a)) identification of the export declaration;
- ((b)) communications regarding discrepancies between the goods declared and released for the export procedure and the goods presented.
