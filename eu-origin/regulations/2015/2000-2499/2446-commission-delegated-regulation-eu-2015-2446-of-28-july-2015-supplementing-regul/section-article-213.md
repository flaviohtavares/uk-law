# Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2015/2446 of 28 July 2015 supplementing Regulation (EU) No 952/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards detailed rules concerning certain provisions of the Union Customs Code, section Article 213

> Text as at 2025-01-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 213. Spare parts, accessories and equipment for non-Union means of transport(Article 250(2)(d) of the Code)

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Total relief from import duty shall be granted for spare parts, accessories and equipment for means of transport where they are temporarily imported to be re-exported separately or as part of means of transport.
