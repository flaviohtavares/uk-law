# Regulation No 31 (EEC), 11 (EAEC), laying down the Staff Regulations of Officials and the Conditions of Employment of Other Servants of the European Economic Community and the European Atomic Energy Community, section Article 31a

> Text as at 2021-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 31a.**[^c000039] The spouse or persons recognized as dependants of a former official within the meaning of Article 18a of Annex VIII, or of a former official entitled to an allowance either under Article 50 of the Staff Regulations or under Regulation (EEC) No 1857/89[^c000007][^f00014], Regulation (EC, Euratom) No 1746/2002[^f00015], Regulation (EC, Euratom) No 1747/2002[^f00016] or Regulation (EC, Euratom) No 1748/2002[^f00017] may, if the former official's whereabouts are unknown for more than one year, provisionally receive a survivor's pension to which they would be entitled under this Annex.
