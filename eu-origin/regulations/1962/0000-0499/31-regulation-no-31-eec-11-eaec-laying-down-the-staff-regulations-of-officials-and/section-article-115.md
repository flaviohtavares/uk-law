# Regulation No 31 (EEC), 11 (EAEC), laying down the Staff Regulations of Officials and the Conditions of Employment of Other Servants of the European Economic Community and the European Atomic Energy Community, section Article 115

> Text as at 2021-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 115.**[^c000008] The provisions of Article 85a of the Staff Regulations, relating to subrogation in favour of the Union[^c000001], shall apply by analogy.

[^c000008]: Textual amendment: Inserted by [Council Regulation (EC, Euratom) No 723/2004 of 22 March 2004 amending the Staff Regulations of officials of the European Communities and the Conditions of Employment of other servants of the European Communities](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/723).
