# Regulation No 31 (EEC), 11 (EAEC), laying down the Staff Regulations of Officials and the Conditions of Employment of Other Servants of the European Economic Community and the European Atomic Energy Community, section Article 13a

> Text as at 2021-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 13a.**[^c000008] Detailed rules for the application of Articles 11, 12 and 13 of this Annex shall be laid down by the appointing authorities of the various institutions[^c000006] under the general implementing provisions.

[^c000008]: Textual amendment: Inserted by [Council Regulation (EC, Euratom) No 723/2004 of 22 March 2004 amending the Staff Regulations of officials of the European Communities and the Conditions of Employment of other servants of the European Communities](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/723).

[^c000006]: Textual amendment: Substituted by [Regulation (EU, Euratom) No 1023/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 22 October 2013 amending the Staff Regulations of Officials of the European Union and the Conditions of Employment of Other Servants of the European Union](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2013/1023).
