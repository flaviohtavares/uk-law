# Regulation No 31 (EEC), 11 (EAEC), laying down the Staff Regulations of Officials and the Conditions of Employment of Other Servants of the European Economic Community and the European Atomic Energy Community, section Article 116

> Text as at 2021-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 116.**[^c000008] The provisions of Article 85 of the Staff Regulations on the recovery of undue payment shall apply.

## Annotations

[^c000008]: Textual amendment: Inserted by [Council Regulation (EC, Euratom) No 723/2004 of 22 March 2004 amending the Staff Regulations of officials of the European Communities and the Conditions of Employment of other servants of the European Communities](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/723).
