# Regulation No 31 (EEC), 11 (EAEC), laying down the Staff Regulations of Officials and the Conditions of Employment of Other Servants of the European Economic Community and the European Atomic Energy Community, section Article 133

> Text as at 2021-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 133.**[^c000069] The scale of basic salaries shall be as provided for in the following table:

|  |  |  |  |  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grade[^c000022] | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
| Full-time basic salary | 1 886,92 | 2 198,26 | 2 383,36 | 2 584,07 | 2 801,67 | 3 037,62 | 3 293,42 |
| Grade | 8 | 9 | 10 | 11 | 12 | 13 | 14 |
| Full-time basic salary | 3 570,78 | 3 871,47 | 4 197,48 | 4 550,94 | 4 934,19 | 5 349,69 | 5 800,2 |
| Grade | 15 | 16 | 17 | 18 | 19 |  |  |
| Full-time basic salary | 6 288,64 | 6 818,22 | 7 392,39 | 8 014,89 | 8 689,85 |  |  |
