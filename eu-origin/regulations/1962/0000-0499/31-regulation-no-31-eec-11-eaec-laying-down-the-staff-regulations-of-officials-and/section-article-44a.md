# Regulation No 31 (EEC), 11 (EAEC), laying down the Staff Regulations of Officials and the Conditions of Employment of Other Servants of the European Economic Community and the European Atomic Energy Community, section Article 44a

> Text as at 2021-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 44a.**[^c000039] The provisions of Article 85a of the Staff Regulations, relating to subrogation in favour of the Union[^c000001] shall apply by analogy.

## Annotations

[^c000039]: Textual amendment: Inserted by [Council Regulation (ECSC, EEC, Euratom) No 2799/85 of 27 September 1985 amending the Staff Regulations of officials and the conditions of employment of other servants of the European Communities](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1985/2799).

[^c000001]: Textual amendment: Substituted by [Regulation (EU, Euratom) No 1080/2010 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 24 November 2010 amending the Staff Regulations of Officials of the European Communities and the Conditions of Employment of Other Servants of those Communities](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2010/1080).
