# Regulation No 31 (EEC), 11 (EAEC), laying down the Staff Regulations of Officials and the Conditions of Employment of Other Servants of the European Economic Community and the European Atomic Energy Community, section Article 49

> Text as at 2021-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 49.** An official may be required to resign only where he ceases to fulfil the conditions laid down in Article 28 (a), or in the cases provided for in Articles[^c000017] 13,[^c000010] 39, 40 and 41 (4) and (5) and in the second paragraph of Article 14 of Annex VIII.

Reasoned decisions requiring officials to resign shall be taken by the appointing authority after consulting the Joint Committee and hearing the official concerned.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 49.** Where an official has exercised his option to withdraw from his account with the temporary joint provident scheme of the institutions of the Union[^c000001] sums which he was required to contribute in his country of origin in order to maintain his pension rights there, his pension rights shall, in respect of the period when he was a member of the temporary provident scheme, be reduced in proportion to the sums withdrawn from his account.

The preceding paragraph shall not apply where an official has asked, within three months of the Staff Regulations being applied to him, to be allowed to repay those sums plus compound interest at the rate of 3·5 % per annum.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 49.** 1.[^c000020] After the disciplinary procedure provided for in Annex IX to the Staff Regulations, which shall apply by analogy, has been followed, employment may be terminated without notice on disciplinary grounds in serious cases of intentional or negligent failure of temporary staff to comply with their obligations. A reasoned decision shall be taken by the authority referred to in the first paragraph of Article 6, after the servant concerned has been given an opportunity of submitting his defence.

Before his employment is teminated, a member of temporary staff may be suspended, in accordance with Articles 23 and 24 of Annex IX to[^c000007] the Staff Regulations, which shall apply by analogy.

2. Where employment is terminated in accordance with paragraph 1, the authority referred to in the first paragraph of Article 6 may decide:

- (a) to limit the severance grant provided for in Article 39 to repayment of the contribution provided for in Article 83 of the Staff Regulations, plus compound interest at the rate of 3·5 % per annum;
- (b) to withhold in whole or in part the resettlement allowance provided for in Article 24 (2).
