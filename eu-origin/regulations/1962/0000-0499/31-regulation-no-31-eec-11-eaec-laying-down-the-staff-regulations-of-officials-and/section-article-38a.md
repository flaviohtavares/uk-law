# Regulation No 31 (EEC), 11 (EAEC), laying down the Staff Regulations of Officials and the Conditions of Employment of Other Servants of the European Economic Community and the European Atomic Energy Community, section Article 38a

> Text as at 2021-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 38a.**[^c000039] The rules relating to ceilings and apportionment set out in Article 81 a of the Staff Regulations shall apply by analogy.

## Annotations

[^c000039]: Textual amendment: Inserted by [Council Regulation (ECSC, EEC, Euratom) No 2799/85 of 27 September 1985 amending the Staff Regulations of officials and the conditions of employment of other servants of the European Communities](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1985/2799).
