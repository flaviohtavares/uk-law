# Regulation No 31 (EEC), 11 (EAEC), laying down the Staff Regulations of Officials and the Conditions of Employment of Other Servants of the European Economic Community and the European Atomic Energy Community, section Article 68a

> Text as at 2021-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 68a.**[^c000007] An official who is authorised to work part time shall be entitled to remuneration calculated as provided for in Annex IVa.

## Annotations

[^c000007]: Textual amendment: Substituted by [Council Regulation (EC, Euratom) No 723/2004 of 22 March 2004 amending the Staff Regulations of officials of the European Communities and the Conditions of Employment of other servants of the European Communities](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/723).
