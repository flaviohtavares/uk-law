# Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016 on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data and on the free movement of such data (United Kingdom General Data Protection Regulation) (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 52

> Text as at 2026-09-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 52. Independence

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. The[^c-cec8e729b3195f93f303adaa88258a78] Commission[^c-459d0d1c750be1b8e0e6a21a2007b708] shall act with complete independence in performing [^c-1e0da15542c0b0d887a5b899a66d9f31]... tasks and exercising [^c-1e0da15542c0b0d887a5b899a66d9f31]... powers in accordance with this Regulation.

2. The[^c-644a6bddf1b47415a7ad9f0cb20ea3bf] Commission shall, in the performance of [^c-04e5999a5dd01708807710680a2d94cc]... tasks and exercise of [^c-04e5999a5dd01708807710680a2d94cc]... powers in accordance with this Regulation, remain free from external influence, whether direct or indirect, and shall neither seek nor take instructions from anybody.

3. The[^c-d73408e4df7fef51b1cce320e894b2a6] Commission shall refrain from any action incompatible with the[^c-63ffd4c641a31e5156c438afe341b663] Commission's duties and its members shall refrain from any action incompatible with the Commission’s duties.[^c-8cb19e298f29aee55b5dcbca06079b75]

4.[^c-197da3da61d09b231d4a8816f222cd3d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

5.[^c-197da3da61d09b231d4a8816f222cd3d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

6.[^c-197da3da61d09b231d4a8816f222cd3d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
