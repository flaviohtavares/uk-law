# Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016 on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data and on the free movement of such data (United Kingdom General Data Protection Regulation) (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 1

> Text as at 2026-09-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 1. Subject-matter and objectives

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. This Regulation lays down rules relating to the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data and rules relating to the free movement of personal data.

2.[^c-bae99d46f616322455fcf3d0583707a3] This Regulation contributes to the protection of individuals’ fundamental rights and freedoms.
