# Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016 on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data and on the free movement of such data (United Kingdom General Data Protection Regulation) (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 57

> Text as at 2026-09-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 57. Tasks

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. Without prejudice to other tasks set out under this Regulation, the[^c-c87634ed585b4844c2305455dbe92df9] Commission[^c-4d4f1c63a6a3423599a1576faf797997] must:

- (a) monitor and enforce the application of this Regulation;
- (b) promote public awareness and understanding of the risks, rules, safeguards and rights in relation to processing. Activities addressed specifically to children shall receive specific attention;
- (c) advise Parliament[^c-70e456acba137026b029acfd4b9c6b7b], the government, and other institutions and bodies on legislative and administrative measures relating to the protection of natural persons' rights and freedoms with regard to processing;
- (d) promote the awareness of controllers and processors of their obligations under this Regulation;
- (e) upon request, provide information to any data subject concerning the exercise of their rights under this Regulation and, if appropriate, cooperate with foreign designated authorities[^c-efe6edd30846e33367b17b8072635561] to that end;
- (f)[^c-c72a7c5142ef507ed685c91ef127ad07] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (g)[^c-916c297a62f7b4dced66b29b5ac4545f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (h) conduct investigations on the application of this Regulation, including on the basis of information received from a foreign designated authority[^c-2c656c264644912fe8a8c9de3c189147] or other public authority;
- (i) monitor relevant developments, insofar as they have an impact on the protection of personal data, in particular the development of information and communication technologies and commercial practices;
- (j) adopt standard contractual clauses referred to in Article 28(8) and issue standard data protection clauses referred to[^c-3ce8d7a4c25cf1c2186e8bf1c74e39bd] in point (d) of Article 46(2);
- (k) establish and maintain a list in relation to the requirement for data protection impact assessment pursuant to Article 35(4);
- (l) give advice on the processing operations referred to in Article 36(2);
- (m) encourage the drawing up of codes of conduct pursuant to Article 40(1) and provide an opinion and approve such codes of conduct [^c-d7a4848d81c94c22aec5bf6d7aaf74f0]... pursuant to Article 40(5);
- (n) encourage the establishment of data protection certification mechanisms and of data protection seals and marks pursuant to Article 42(1), and approve the criteria of certification pursuant to Article 42(5);
- (o) where applicable, carry out a periodic review of certifications issued in accordance with Article 42(7);
- (oa)[^c-5f542c7d08b44cd7a93ec1c1a80e4a23] maintain a public register of certification mechanisms and data protection seals and marks pursuant to Article 42(8) and of controllers or processors established in third countries and certified pursuant to Article 42(7);
- (p)[^c000001] draft and publish the requirements for accreditation of a body for monitoring codes of conduct pursuant to Article 41 and of a certification body pursuant to Article 43;
- (q) conduct the accreditation of a body for monitoring codes of conduct pursuant to Article 41 and of a certification body pursuant to Article 43;
- (r) authorise contractual clauses and provisions referred to in Article 46(3);
- (s) approve binding corporate rules pursuant to Article 47;
- (sa)[^c-b9e89b68c17db3591825ecf7a6e8d2f9] provide authorisation required under regulations made under Article 47A;
- (t)[^c-b76382febfe88fac598c362a93f6ace2] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (u) keep internal records of infringements of this Regulation and of measures taken in accordance with Article 58(2); and
- (v) fulfil any other tasks related to the protection of personal data.

2.[^c-dfad14f434ec6939117fefbc0367c801] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

3. The performance of the[^c-ed4a4464d5e8fc7d0d07ae65b4c67bef] Commission's[^c-2d1519d9b04db4ab0422ed9cc8aa7fed] tasks is to be free of charge for the data subject and, where applicable, for the data protection officer.
