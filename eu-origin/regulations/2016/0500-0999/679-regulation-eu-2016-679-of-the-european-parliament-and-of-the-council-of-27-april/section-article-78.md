# Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016 on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data and on the free movement of such data (United Kingdom General Data Protection Regulation) (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 78

> Text as at 2026-09-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 78. Right to an effective judicial remedy against the Commission[^key-c70bc06dfe7ce242a97335ed61bca719][^key-248207d287dd716d55526418c527e9d9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. Without prejudice to any other administrative or non-judicial remedy, each natural or legal person shall have the right to an effective judicial remedy against a legally binding decision of the[^key-b9e4ac28424f6dba200647735e3648c4] Commission[^key-a9a944355c75c579f0cb2a11a2f0b374] concerning them.

2. Without prejudice to any other administrative or non-judicial remedy, each data subject shall have the right to a an effective judicial remedy where the[^key-7d2e221ea4ff04072d5d604fe2d84982] Commission does not handle a complaint or does not inform the data subject within three months on the progress or outcome of the complaint lodged pursuant to Article 77.
