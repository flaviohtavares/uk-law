# Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016 on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data and on the free movement of such data (United Kingdom General Data Protection Regulation) (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 79

> Text as at 2026-09-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 79. Right to an effective judicial remedy against a controller or processor

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. Without prejudice to any available administrative or non-judicial remedy, including the right to lodge a complaint with the[^c-52619e56cd65ad9e2ffd76c81d1233fc] Commission[^c-082c71327d81606f4271858392afca4c] pursuant to Article 77, each data subject shall have the right to an effective judicial remedy where he or she considers that his or her rights under this Regulation have been infringed as a result of the processing of his or her personal data in non-compliance with this Regulation.
