## Article 85. Processing and freedom of expression and information

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1.[^key-de756e2d3e06c42a25160dbc642a9291] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

2. For processing carried out for journalistic purposes or the purpose of academic artistic or literary expression, the Secretary of State may[^key-d489385fe27c174986ff236e9a480125] provide for exemptions or derogations from Chapter II (principles), Chapter III (rights of the data subject), Chapter IV (controller and processor), Chapter V (transfer of personal data to third countries or international organisations), Chapter VI (the[^key-a3cfa4d48b27568c713a7fc4599cd85a] Commission[^key-a1df06dfc0eff35faf0d90dd645468e4]) [^key-4d01ddf61f4ff84af367e7cb2a4be476]... and Chapter IX (specific data processing situations) if they are necessary to reconcile [^key-4adeef7664a54071015d5df4465dd49b]... the protection of personal data with the freedom of expression and information.

(2A.)[^key-f1031792a08558724d96bb017f5009ef] The Secretary of State may exercise the power under paragraph 2 of this Article only by making regulations under section 16 of the 2018 Act.
