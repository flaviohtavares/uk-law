# Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016 on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data and on the free movement of such data (United Kingdom General Data Protection Regulation) (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 31

> Text as at 2026-09-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 31. Cooperation with the Commission[^key-299bc854c5ca1621b6777d52c6e933ac][^key-2061390a94fa9f26de693bc601546ac8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The controller and the processor and, where applicable, their representatives, shall cooperate, on request, with the[^key-86ba5d29ddf87df41ba5885e90c83de9] Commission in the performance of the Commission's tasks.
