# Regulation (EU) 2016/2031 of the European Parliament of the Council of 26 October 2016 on protective measures against pests of plants, amending Regulations (EU) No 228/2013, (EU) No 652/2014 and (EU) No 1143/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Council Directives 69/464/EEC, 74/647/EEC, 93/85/EEC, 98/57/EC, 2000/29/EC, 2006/91/EC and 2007/33/EC, section Article 4

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 4. Definition of GB quarantine pests[^c-a0a4668da8325e8e20c2e9e99f57e5d2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A quarantine pest is a ‘GB[^c-2811a74a0a0996f2f74658727c73481e] quarantine pest’ if the defined territory referred to in the introductory part of Article 3 is Great Britain[^c-8a6407a1199b54b8b940ebdfd86e93c6] and if it is included in the list in Annex 2 to the Phytosanitary Conditions Regulation[^M_F_f18deb80-143c-43e7-bf61-ad6c88347841].
