# Regulation (EU) 2016/2031 of the European Parliament of the Council of 26 October 2016 on protective measures against pests of plants, amending Regulations (EU) No 228/2013, (EU) No 652/2014 and (EU) No 1143/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Council Directives 69/464/EEC, 74/647/EEC, 93/85/EEC, 98/57/EC, 2000/29/EC, 2006/91/EC and 2007/33/EC, section Article 86

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 86. Substantive requirements for a UK plant passport for movement into and within a GB pest-free area[^c-f51404fbe7e5845833cd9611db83bae8][^c-1a29f1f56d1a24775b548dd177b8c5dc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A1.[^c-72aee7ba6324e8a90a900f0e6649dd1d] This Article applies to any plant, plant product or other object of a description specified in any entry in column (1) of the table in Annex 14 to the Phytosanitary Conditions Regulation which is to be introduced into, or moved within, a GB pest-free area specified in the corresponding entry in column (2) of the table.

1. A UK plant passport may only be issued for the introduction into, or movement within, a GB pest-free area of a plant, plant product or other object if it[^c-554315478b1f3ddcf0e70c1c771ffdc2] fulfils all of the requirements of Article 85, and in addition the following requirements:

- (a) it is free from the PFA quarantine pest which relates to that GB pest-free area[^c-c85a843d31878112f28edca2cd2838d2]; and
- (b) it complies with the requirements referred to in Article 54(1)[^c-cd6ff5eee3c683bc6995b9956ff47185].

2. But where Article 33(2) applies, a UK plant passport may[^c-96822f7a48c7d0c2310acd919446df33] not be issued for plants, plant products or other objects originating in the demarcated area concerned and which may host the PFA quarantine[^c-81278a2156e5d38cf8a4da5ab6afcf4e] pest concerned.
