# Regulation (EU) 2016/2031 of the European Parliament of the Council of 26 October 2016 on protective measures against pests of plants, amending Regulations (EU) No 228/2013, (EU) No 652/2014 and (EU) No 1143/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Council Directives 69/464/EEC, 74/647/EEC, 93/85/EEC, 98/57/EC, 2000/29/EC, 2006/91/EC and 2007/33/EC, section Article 36

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 36. Definition of GB regulated non-quarantine pests[^key-154cfba6d7bf870237023492fb41bfdf]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A pest is a ‘GB[^key-e8eecd84516a0d51915e2ee32be47d34] regulated non-quarantine pest’ if it fulfils all of the following conditions and it is included in column (1) of the tables in Annex 4 to the Phytosanitary Conditions Regulation[^key-295f0c82895dd8bd2fcecc07d5d14955]:

- ((a)) its identity is established in accordance with point (1) of Section 4 of Annex I;
- ((b)) it is present in Great Britain[^key-93a0f4dc4efa05b3fcbcf0effe4874d5];
- ((c)) it is not a GB[^key-726e91488552cb089f835c621caaf8f5] quarantine pest or a provisional GB quarantine pest[^key-e3690ab77d0fc0a8f732163ba48d4e0a];
- ((d)) it is transmitted mainly through specific plants for planting, in accordance with point (2) of Section 4 of Annex I;
- ((e)) its presence on those plants for planting has an unacceptable economic impact, as regards the intended use of those plants for planting, in accordance with point (3) of Section 4 of Annex I;
- ((f)) feasible and effective measures are available to prevent its presence on the plants for planting concerned.
