# Regulation (EU) 2016/2031 of the European Parliament of the Council of 26 October 2016 on protective measures against pests of plants, amending Regulations (EU) No 228/2013, (EU) No 652/2014 and (EU) No 1143/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Council Directives 69/464/EEC, 74/647/EEC, 93/85/EEC, 98/57/EC, 2000/29/EC, 2006/91/EC and 2007/33/EC, section Article 85

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 85. Substantive requirements for a UK plant passport ...[^key-e2c200bd14b3e9be6e79a3b6fc207a27][^key-3df166e10f0d5b9d37c98e86a25e19f9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A UK plant passport may only be issued for the movement of[^key-fad4d3829c036c8babc144d96fc7eabe] a plant, plant product or other object within Great Britain if the plant, plant product or other object[^key-b0f186ca79a989abc4ba94488edb8ea2] fulfils the following requirements:

- ((a)) it is free from GB quarantine pests and provisional GB quarantine pests[^key-6edc20c9c3478fa673ea769b30805123];
- ((b)) it complies with the provisions of Article 37(1) concerning the presence of GB[^key-3306be2897bca3dc848efa751bcfb058] regulated non-quarantine pests on plants for planting and the provisions of Article 37(4A)[^key-db4c32abe37d651a23c3f173458c19d9] concerning the measures to be taken;
- ((c)) it complies with the requirements [^key-ebbe92fdf8474597b957a85978ec7f3e]... referred to in Article 41(1B)[^key-265e26ac8a6cba77b2ff930e532beb9d];
- ((d)) where applicable, it complies with any requirements specified in regulations made under Article 28(1) or 30(1)[^key-d384641b0de5e8d396d75fedc9e5bdf4];
- ((e)) where applicable, it complies with any[^key-9cc5f756b743061381bdcca2bce31dae] measures adopted by the competent authority[^key-c653b44bc58abeccc6e161b494c7d4f3] for the eradication of GB[^key-66fdbb660df57c9748a57de039753a92] quarantine pests pursuant to Article 17(1) or the eradication of provisional GB quarantine pests pursuant to regulations made under Article 30(1)[^key-e73f878dcdd2ecf1274ff8c9a92c3ae1].
