# Regulation (EU) 2016/2031 of the European Parliament of the Council of 26 October 2016 on protective measures against pests of plants, amending Regulations (EU) No 228/2013, (EU) No 652/2014 and (EU) No 1143/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Council Directives 69/464/EEC, 74/647/EEC, 93/85/EEC, 98/57/EC, 2000/29/EC, 2006/91/EC and 2007/33/EC, section Article 88

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 88. Attaching of the UK plant passports[^key-ade94ce83acb2ccde0236202045c561c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

UK plant[^key-4c3ca3833ce3efaf2876a7c0e4f5d7e5] passports issued in Great Britain[^key-f4ba191b49d504dc7368ea748d403b99] shall be attached by the professional operators concerned to the trade unit of the plants, plant products and other objects concerned before they are moved within Great Britain[^key-f432e6315e1e8dc49ccdea0071f83f40] pursuant to Article 79 or into or within a GB pest-free area[^key-37882ebd48b4cb6cbaf4a448645b5232] pursuant to Article 80 , or before they are introduced into a CD territory[^key-b7cdb7dbd40a3d81bba648a422a1dc65]. Where such plants, plant products or other objects are moved in a package, bundle or container, the UK[^key-aa3849fa0e427b17d6129649d55a8e3a] plant passport shall be attached to that package, bundle or container.
