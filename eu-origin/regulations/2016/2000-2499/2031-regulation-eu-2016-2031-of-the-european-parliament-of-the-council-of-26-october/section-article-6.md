# Regulation (EU) 2016/2031 of the European Parliament of the Council of 26 October 2016 on protective measures against pests of plants, amending Regulations (EU) No 228/2013, (EU) No 652/2014 and (EU) No 1143/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Council Directives 69/464/EEC, 74/647/EEC, 93/85/EEC, 98/57/EC, 2000/29/EC, 2006/91/EC and 2007/33/EC, section Article 6

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 6. Priority pests

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. GB[^key-14c195377b973323dc575ca3aa6311e8] quarantine pests are ‘priority pests’ if they fulfil all of the following conditions:

- (a) they fulfil, as regards Great Britain[^key-46114162a476800d450d6eb93079d090], one or more of the conditions set out in point (2) of Section 1 of Annex I;
- (b) their potential economic, environmental or social impact is the most severe in respect of Great Britain[^key-65bfeaaedd987c294e5cd6a79701683f] as set out in Section 2 of Annex I;
- (c) they are included in the list in the Annex to Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/1702[^key-c3404070c26bda023c1a101bfe7f6d4f].

2.[^key-30e99b54ef49c760462a7688497ce49b] The appropriate authority may by regulations amend the list of priority pests in the Annex to Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/1702 where the results of an assessment show that a GB quarantine pest which is not included in that list fulfils the conditions referred to in points (a) and (b) of paragraph 1, or that a pest which is included in that list no longer fulfils either of those conditions.
