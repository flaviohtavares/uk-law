# Regulation (EU) 2016/2031 of the European Parliament of the Council of 26 October 2016 on protective measures against pests of plants, amending Regulations (EU) No 228/2013, (EU) No 652/2014 and (EU) No 1143/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Council Directives 69/464/EEC, 74/647/EEC, 93/85/EEC, 98/57/EC, 2000/29/EC, 2006/91/EC and 2007/33/EC, section Article 81

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 81. Exception for direct supply to final users

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A1.[^c-246b50e44a0325b0f9e06128b9aee2ba] This Article applies to the movement of plants, plant products or other objects in Great Britain and the introduction of plants, plant products or other objects into Great Britain from a CD territory.

1. No UK[^c-82471c118f8750eb507a8e06a1875c8e] plant passport or other permitted plant passport[^c-be21add16cf7d62e5d9aea74841e606d] shall be required for the movement of plants, plant products or other objects which are[^c-07ceb0b7c276d816283e42efc2f22db7] supplied directly to a final user, including home gardeners.

(1A)[^c-ff42c2f5a410184624dab197bac8961c] That exception shall not apply to:

- (a) final users receiving those plants, plant products or other objects by means of sales through distance contracts; or
- (b) final users of plants, plant products or other objects described in an entry (if any) in column (1) of the table in Annex 14 to Phytosanitary Conditions Regulation, who are situated in a GB pest-free area specified in the corresponding entry in column (2) of the table[^c-843b2668706c62b58ddedfcb45689f67].

1B.[^c-7cd0749882c10254ad474b3be0b29533] The appropriate authority may by regulations specify that point (b) of paragraph 1A only applies to particular PFA quarantine pests and plants, plant products or other objects.

2. The appropriate authority may by regulations supplement[^c-97899fc40b5ad4a1af5bd9b3338a64b8] this Regulation by setting out the cases where, for particular plants, plant products or other objects, the exception of paragraph 1 of this Article shall only apply to small quantities. Those regulations must[^c-1eb4947fcd786664d9a86b805d8ba933] define those quantities per period of time as appropriate to the plant, plant product or other object concerned and the respective pest risks.
