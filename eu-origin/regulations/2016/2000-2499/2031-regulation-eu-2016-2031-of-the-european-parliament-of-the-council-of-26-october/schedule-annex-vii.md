# Regulation (EU) 2016/2031 of the European Parliament of the Council of 26 October 2016 on protective measures against pests of plants, amending Regulations (EU) No 228/2013, (EU) No 652/2014 and (EU) No 1143/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Council Directives 69/464/EEC, 74/647/EEC, 93/85/EEC, 98/57/EC, 2000/29/EC, 2006/91/EC and 2007/33/EC, ANNEX VII

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## ANNEX VII — UK PLANT PASSPORTS[^key-d62c3589543415597edf8936c4335efd]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

PART A

***UK plant passports[^key-b6c50ea76cdcb5136b2d77fed298915a] [^key-7c9eee2035ead442b5dd94fa79fe4e8f]... as referred to in the first subparagraph of Article 83(2)***

(1)

The UK plant passport for the movement of plants, plant products and other objects within Great Britain, the introduction of plants, plant products and other objects into Great Britain from a CD territory or the introduction of plants, plant products and other objects into a CD territory from Great Britain[^key-bf8750256b61b4a9467a2e2d5c565169] shall contain the following elements:

- ((a)) the words ‘UK Plant Passport’ in its upper right-hand corner in English;[^key-187aa59393bbd77c3e4a35b502a0fd2f]
- ((b)) [^key-af71243558a7c1933fee588edf62002b]...
- ((c)) the letter ‘A.’, followed by the botanical name of the plant species or taxon concerned, in the case of plants and plant products, or, where appropriate, the name of the object concerned, and, optionally, the name of the variety;
- ((d)) the letter ‘B.’, followed by [^key-b9e5f05f7476892d32f10aa28ffce954]... the registration number of the professional operator concerned who issues the UK plant passport[^key-e5cc111c6af06bf938de0802174f8f35] or for whom the UK plant passport is issued by the competent authority or the CD authority[^key-6f8a1dceec62c0c4326116c1a455a46d];
- ((e)) the letter ‘C.’, followed by the traceability code of the plant, plant product or the other object concerned;
- ((f)) the letter ‘D.’, where applicable followed by:
  - ((i)) the name of the third country of origin, or
  - ((ii)) if originating in or from Great Britain or a CD territory, ‘GB’[^key-a256ffa190dc218698f7b7a99a9d224b] ;[^key-af1c1faedd4a4d7cb242fa01e978f6d2]
- ((g)) the letter ‘E.’, where applicable followed by ‘GB(NI)’.[^key-50be437a9c005ece483a42ddc874f935]

(2)

The traceability code referred to in point (1)(e) may also be supplemented by a reference to a unique traceability barcode, hologram, chip or other data carrier, present on the trade unit.

PART B

***UK plant passports for movement into GB pest-free areas and CD pest-free areas, as referred to in Article 83(4)[^key-b4efa8ae32af1d2f433b542418aa2a4d]***

(1)

The UK[^key-7fdb47c6f6e1c5db832d68a11d662da6] plant passport for movement into and within GB pest-free areas and for movement into CD pest-free areas[^key-b554403b509ada5656c4506ff91a4141] shall contain the following elements:

- ((a)) the words ‘UK Plant Passport — PFA’ in its upper right-hand corner in English;[^key-954e4db7f6f962494fbcbed28f5148f3]
- ((b)) immediately underneath those words, the scientific name(s) or the code(s) of the respective PFA quarantine pest(s) or CD quarantine pest(s)[^key-a7f22e565a47da28dd16e680299c9639];
- ((c)) [^key-6046e9588391779c289351867f61d97c]...
- ((d)) the letter ‘A.’, followed by the botanical name of the plant species or taxon concerned, in the case of plants and plant products, or, where appropriate, the name of the object concerned and, optionally, the name of the variety;
- ((e)) the letter ‘B.’, followed by [^key-de495aa06371a59501b03ced85083b8b]... the registration number of the professional operator concerned who issues the UK plant passport[^key-39c746fa58cb767989700071527a7c57] or for whom the UK plant passport is issued by the competent authority or the CD authority[^key-00b711d75672163236b87c32eaeee71a];
- ((f)) the letter ‘C.’, followed by the traceability code of the plant, plant product or the other object concerned;
- ((g)) the letter ‘D.’, where appropriate followed by:
  - ((i)) the name of the third country of origin, or
  - ((ii)) if originating in or from Great Britain or a CD territory, ‘GB’[^key-aaf12a15ca4f7506122b494c83e36ac5], in the case of replacement of a UK plant passport[^key-1d359c27e61f3f013b8128cd520d403a], the registration number of the professional operator concerned who issued the initial UK[^key-28763cd54e6856453e08ba69299ada1c] plant passport or for whom the initial UK plant passport was issued by the competent authority as referred in Article 93(1) and (2) ;[^key-e39334be8d88781da22a05795eb44743]
- ((h)) the letter ‘E.’, where applicable followed by ‘GB(NI)’.[^key-0ffcf5e0cb4bb2ecda81cdf26765459f]

(2)

The traceability code referred to in point (1)(f) may also be supplemented by a reference to a unique traceability barcode, hologram, chip or other data carrier, present on the trade unit.

PART C

***UK plant passports[^key-82f98618cb866f1387e84ed31faaeb97] [^key-eaed9dc39a2ca7fca6134a327a905774]... , combined with a certification label, as referred to in the second subparagraph of Article 83(5)***

(1)

The UK plant passport for the movement of plants, plant products and other objects within Great Britain, the introduction of plants, plant products and other objects into Great Britain from a CD territory or the introduction of plants, plant products and other objects into a CD territory from Great Britain[^key-c4b017f10256406326d24ec5e5c3253d], combined in a joint label with the official label for seeds or other propagating material referred to in the relevant legislation on the marketing of seeds and other propagating material[^key-0b352c22e405f85eabb993c857a8b7b3], shall contain the following elements:

- ((a)) the words ‘UK Plant Passport’ in its upper right-hand corner in English;[^key-effb0514309ab231d7aa3af6ebfcc6f0]
- ((b)) [^key-424f8244fff83770977826d34fabc198]...

The UK plant passport[^key-e76e3662eede2250f11f7eba669a6b5d] shall be positioned in the joint label immediately above, and have the same width as, that official label.

(2)

Point (2) of Part A shall apply accordingly.

PART D

***UK plant passports[^key-93d59a84e653d036b03e868eab09b42c] for movement into and within GB pest-free areas and for movement into CD pest-free areas[^key-e7ff1d69b076d5298b5b0b3b2be2a30c], combined with a certification label, as referred to in the third subparagraph of Article 83(5)***

(1)

The UK plant passport for movement into and within GB pest-free areas and for movement into CD pest-free areas[^key-c2e9817dffc39d6f0d3bb3fb6218f609], combined in a joint label with the official label for seeds or other propagating material referred to in the relevant legislation on the marketing of seeds and other propagating material[^key-b5f4b17cdfb0ab893d11a45bda2cb666], shall contain the following elements:

- ((a)) the words ‘UK Plant Passport — PFA’ in its upper right-hand corner in English;[^key-deed7340d3066f8332ebba00343f548a]
- ((b)) immediately underneath those words, the scientific name(s) or code(s) of the PFA quarantine pest(s) or CD quarantine pest(s)[^key-d54140221cfceab1e61fe3a5bbb79e7d] concerned;
- ((c)) [^key-8074dbaa7e5266828dea84589d5013cb]...

The UK plant passport[^key-23a9b1fa1bb97002a0c7d68e7d7cb677] shall be positioned in the joint label immediately above, and have the same width as, that official label or, where applicable, that master certificate.

(2)

Point (2) of Part B shall apply accordingly.
