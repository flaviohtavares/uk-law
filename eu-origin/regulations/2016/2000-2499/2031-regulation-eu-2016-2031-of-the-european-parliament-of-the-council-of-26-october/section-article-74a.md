# Regulation (EU) 2016/2031 of the European Parliament of the Council of 26 October 2016 on protective measures against pests of plants, amending Regulations (EU) No 228/2013, (EU) No 652/2014 and (EU) No 1143/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Council Directives 69/464/EEC, 74/647/EEC, 93/85/EEC, 98/57/EC, 2000/29/EC, 2006/91/EC and 2007/33/EC, section Article 74a

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 74a. Commodity classification[^key-0aa4ac8f5b7271fe5750f0ddf2006c66]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The appropriate authority may by regulations amend Annex 11 to the Phytosanitary Conditions Regulation to:

- (a) remove the references to any CN codes used in that Annex to identify the plants plant products and other objects listed; or
- (b) identify the plants, plant products and other objects listed in that Annex by any alternative commodity code classification system that the appropriate authority considers appropriate.
