# Regulation (EU) 2016/2031 of the European Parliament of the Council of 26 October 2016 on protective measures against pests of plants, amending Regulations (EU) No 228/2013, (EU) No 652/2014 and (EU) No 1143/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Council Directives 69/464/EEC, 74/647/EEC, 93/85/EEC, 98/57/EC, 2000/29/EC, 2006/91/EC and 2007/33/EC, section Article 39

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 39. GB regulated non-quarantine pests used for scientific or educational purposes, trials, varietal selection, breeding or exhibitions[^key-0923ed4795509ec78240c43efbce290d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The prohibition provided for in Article 37 shall not apply to GB[^key-0b79ea994f781706b29ba93ee306480b] regulated non-quarantine pests which are present on plants for planting used for scientific or educational purposes, trials, varietal selection, breeding or exhibitions.
