# Regulation (EU) 2016/2031 of the European Parliament of the Council of 26 October 2016 on protective measures against pests of plants, amending Regulations (EU) No 228/2013, (EU) No 652/2014 and (EU) No 1143/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Council Directives 69/464/EEC, 74/647/EEC, 93/85/EEC, 98/57/EC, 2000/29/EC, 2006/91/EC and 2007/33/EC, section Article 58

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 58. Plants, plant products and other objects used for official testing, scientific or educational purposes, trials, varietal selection or breeding as regards GB pest-free areas[^c-0b03e2bc2b3d50d8248e6827331e6e12]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

By way of derogation from [^c-8a1e2ad05e394429e0c16862edfe9bf7]... Article 53(1) and Article 54(1), Article 48 shall apply *mutatis mutandis* to the introduction into, and the movement within, a GB pest-free area of plants, plant products and other objects of a description specified in any entry in column (1) of the tables in Annex 9 and 10 to the Phytosanitary Conditions Regulation which relate to that GB pest-free area[^c-88c123afa5756761db372597c96cdc32] and used for official testing, scientific or educational purposes, trials, varietal selection or breeding.
