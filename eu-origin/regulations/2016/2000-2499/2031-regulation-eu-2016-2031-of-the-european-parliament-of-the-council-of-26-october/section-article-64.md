# Regulation (EU) 2016/2031 of the European Parliament of the Council of 26 October 2016 on protective measures against pests of plants, amending Regulations (EU) No 228/2013, (EU) No 652/2014 and (EU) No 1143/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Council Directives 69/464/EEC, 74/647/EEC, 93/85/EEC, 98/57/EC, 2000/29/EC, 2006/91/EC and 2007/33/EC, section Article 64

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 64. Release of plants, plant products and other objects from quarantine stations and confinement facilities

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. Plants, plant products and other objects shall only leave a quarantine station or a confinement facility[^c-e50276292de8224770cb7e89178f870c], upon authorisation by the competent authority[^c-ace402f54ad75549ca86732d11c85906], if it is confirmed that they are free from GB quarantine pests and provisional GB quarantine pests, or, where applicable, PFA[^c-1f2cbe8b1591d50ff92762bcfeb239ed] quarantine pests.

2. The competent authority may authorise the movement of plants, plant products or other objects infested by a GB quarantine pest or provisional GB quarantine pest[^c-97e297ca9477d2cefa795a46f94c24be] from a quarantine station or a confinement facility to another quarantine station or confinement facility if that movement is justified by official testing or scientific reasons and takes place under the conditions set by the competent authority.

3. The appropriate authority may by regulations[^c-8457141cbd46bb49c9c1cd4b33bef113] lay down specific rules concerning the release of plants, plant products and other objects from quarantine stations and confinement facilities, and, where appropriate, labelling requirements related to that release or to the movement referred to in paragraph 2.
