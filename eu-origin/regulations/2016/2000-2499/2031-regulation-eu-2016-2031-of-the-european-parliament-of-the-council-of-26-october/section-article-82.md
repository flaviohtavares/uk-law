# Regulation (EU) 2016/2031 of the European Parliament of the Council of 26 October 2016 on protective measures against pests of plants, amending Regulations (EU) No 228/2013, (EU) No 652/2014 and (EU) No 1143/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Council Directives 69/464/EEC, 74/647/EEC, 93/85/EEC, 98/57/EC, 2000/29/EC, 2006/91/EC and 2007/33/EC, section Article 82

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 82. Exceptions for movements within and between the premises of a registered operator

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

No UK[^key-06563cfa5aa0814d045d1502c72e5127] plant passport or other permitted plant passport[^key-b87645c58bd4590a8b76eef2f64292f0] shall be required for the movements of plants, plant products and other objects within and between the premises of the same registered operator which are in close proximity to each other.
