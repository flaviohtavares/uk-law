# Regulation (EU) 2016/2031 of the European Parliament of the Council of 26 October 2016 on protective measures against pests of plants, amending Regulations (EU) No 228/2013, (EU) No 652/2014 and (EU) No 1143/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Council Directives 69/464/EEC, 74/647/EEC, 93/85/EEC, 98/57/EC, 2000/29/EC, 2006/91/EC and 2007/33/EC, section Article 11

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 11. Notification of GB quarantine pests by competent authorities[^c-5d2afd424d6da404cb2babe8dad73eb5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1.[^c-29bbe5b85cfe3c208c66b53678437b11] A competent authority that officially confirms any of the situations described in paragraph 2 must immediately provide details of the situation to the other competent authorities.

2. The situations are:

- ((a)) the presence in its territory of a GB[^c-b82f7cc923ea606315f1cf7d44dadf32] quarantine pest or provisional GB quarantine pest[^c-437f55e5cf4017e4e576d44565e33be9] not known to be present there;
- ((b)) the presence of a GB[^c-584eb8df966190a1b3b2832945847d12] quarantine pest or provisional GB quarantine pest in a part of its territory where it was previously not present;
- ((c)) the presence in its territory of a GB[^c-647cd772d0ff17cc39847d7863398e81] quarantine pest or provisional GB quarantine pest in a consignment of plants, plant products or other objects introduced into, intended to be introduced into, or moved within, Great Britain[^c-129a919de9087f46ed27cce68e62d6fc].
