# Regulation (EU) 2016/2031 of the European Parliament of the Council of 26 October 2016 on protective measures against pests of plants, amending Regulations (EU) No 228/2013, (EU) No 652/2014 and (EU) No 1143/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Council Directives 69/464/EEC, 74/647/EEC, 93/85/EEC, 98/57/EC, 2000/29/EC, 2006/91/EC and 2007/33/EC, section Article 47

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 47. Requirements for phytosanitary transit

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. By way of derogation from Article 40(1), Article 41(1), Article 42(2), Article 72(1) and Article 73, plants, plant products and other objects may be introduced into, and be passed through, Great Britain[^key-c14d9f1d29eb281e6f8314e763bc34dc] to a place outside Great Britain[^key-de0fbb15ec690361d12ed256b2bc5a62], either in the form of transit or transhipment (‘phytosanitary transit’), if they fulfil both of the following conditions:

- (a) they are accompanied by a signed declaration of the professional operator in control of those plants, plant products and other objects stating that those plants, plant products or other objects are in phytosanitary transit;
- (b) they are packed and moved in such a way that there is no risk of spreading of GB[^key-ed9e011f4c2194d128bd7d14570d5f57] quarantine pests during their introduction into, and passing through, Great Britain[^key-0f348964e919bb377ac841adcec22c59].

2. The competent authority[^key-67be03cd6aec5a28e9773b9b25484f31] shall prohibit phytosanitary transit if the plants, plant products or other objects concerned do not comply, or there is reasonable evidence that they will not comply, with paragraph 1.
