# Regulation (EU) 2016/2031 of the European Parliament of the Council of 26 October 2016 on protective measures against pests of plants, amending Regulations (EU) No 228/2013, (EU) No 652/2014 and (EU) No 1143/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Council Directives 69/464/EEC, 74/647/EEC, 93/85/EEC, 98/57/EC, 2000/29/EC, 2006/91/EC and 2007/33/EC, section Article 79

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 79. Plants, plant products and other objects for which a UK plant passport is required for movement within or into Great Britain[^c-3856d1c0739fe30aad32d3f87868745a][^c-453e9326905478d13d8c5d68d9007619]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1.[^c-9b0ce5f046280c4b80820ca72d851103] Plants, plant products and other objects listed in Annex 13 to the Phytosanitary Conditions Regulation may not be moved within Great Britain or introduced into Great Britain from a CD territory, unless they are accompanied by a UK plant passport.

1A.[^c-a879dbc5a9234a0da9f92be6977cd60d] Paragraph 1 is subject to Articles 81 and 82 and, in relation to qualifying Northern Ireland goods, to Article 95a(2)[^c-309f3f70d952eea5c5fe5d2e75e16f1f].

2.[^c-eac71c2888b57af818663d4297efe451] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

3.[^c-61feb5f654d62cbcb7c78bdfce89baa1] The appropriate authority may by regulations amend Annex 13 to the Phytosanitary Conditions Regulation in accordance with the principles set out in Section 2 of Annex 2:

- (a) to include a plant, plant product or other object that is not included in Annex 13 to the Phytosanitary Conditions Regulation, where there is a risk that the plant, plant product or other object hosts a GB quarantine pest or a provisional GB quarantine pest;
- (b) to remove a plant, plant product or other object listed in that Annex, where the risk that it hosts a GB quarantine pest or a provisional GB quarantine pest no longer exists.

4.[^c-41ebe3c129d10072c3e88241483b193c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

5. By way of derogation from paragraphs 1[^c-f666819069689ca7ab15e9e28db14869]... and 3, no UK[^c-4579eac9c96aa895e405f76cae0bba0f] plant passport shall be required for the plants, plant products or other objects which are subject to Article 47, 48 or 75(1)[^c-4771d7203b18885734a9dc16fa3f2398].

6.[^c-9cb08691492c23c88e4e177ebc7e2510] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
