# Commission Regulation (EC) No 1017/2002 of 13 June 2002 concerning the classification of certain goods in the Combined Nomenclature

> Text as at 2013-06-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Commission Regulation (EC) No 1017/2002

of 13 June 2002

concerning the classification of certain goods in the Combined Nomenclature

**Article 1.** In the table in the Annex, the goods described in column 1 shall be classified, in the Combined Nomenclature, in the corresponding CN codes indicated in column 2.

**Article 2.** Subject to the measures in force in the Community relating to double checking systems and to prior and retrospective Community surveillance of textile products on importation into the Community, binding tariff information issued by the customs authorities of Member States which does not conform to the provisions of this Regulation, can continue to be invoked for a period of 60 days, under the provisions of Article 12(6) of Regulation (EEC) No 2913/92.

**Article 3.** This Regulation shall enter into force on the 20th day following its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Communities*.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.
