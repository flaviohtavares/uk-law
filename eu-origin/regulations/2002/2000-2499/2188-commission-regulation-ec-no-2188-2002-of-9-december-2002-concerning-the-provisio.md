# Commission Regulation (EC) No 2188/2002 of 9 December 2002 concerning the provisional authorisation of new uses of additives in feedingstuffs (Text with EEA relevance)

> Text as at 2017-07-19.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Commission Regulation (EC) No 2188/2002

of 9 December 2002

concerning the provisional authorisation of new uses of additives in feedingstuffs

(Text with EEA relevance)

**Article 1.** The preparations belonging to the group ‘Enzymes’ listed in Annexes I and II to this Regulation are authorised for use as additives in animal nutrition under the conditions laid down in these Annexes.

**Article 2.** The preparation belonging to the group ‘Enzymes’ listed in Annex III to this Regulation is authorised for use as additive in animal nutrition under the conditions laid down in this Annex.

**Article 3.** This Regulation shall enter into force on the day following that of its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Communities*.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.
