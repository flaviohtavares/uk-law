# Council Regulation (EC) No 6/2002 of 12 December 2001 on Community designs, section Article 19

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 19. Rights conferred by the supplementary unregistered design[^c-02bee2be96b0a46fdae1e5b5b37b0641]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. A supplementary unregistered[^c-315799b3b881757aba33acd38d7cc9b4] design shall confer on its holder the exclusive right to use it and to prevent any third party not having his consent from using it. The aforementioned use shall cover, in particular, the making, offering, putting on the market, importing, exporting or using of a product in which the design is incorporated or to which it is applied, or stocking such a product for those purposes.

2. A supplementary[^c-3b79e9c19da2046a6f259b13507a3b00] unregistered [^c-094f4a43c78ee471b0baf947d08c9766]... design shall, however, confer on its holder the right to prevent the acts referred to in paragraph 1 only if the contested use results from copying the protected design.

The contested use shall not be deemed to result from copying the protected design if it results from an independent work of creation by a designer who may be reasonably thought not to be familiar with the design made available to the public by the holder.
