# Council Regulation (EC) No 6/2002 of 12 December 2001 on Community designs, section Article 5

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 5. Novelty

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. A design shall be considered to be new if no identical design has been made available to the public:

- (a) in the case of a supplementary[^c-a0237463027e5fe9fa1469b31df6ca88] unregistered [^c-db1eacd9e42bcfedb0c7056d4a581910]... design, before the date on which the design for which protection is claimed has first been made available to the public;
- (b)[^c-34010f3e8693b46f697e72fc98dc2805] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

2. Designs shall be deemed to be identical if their features differ only in immaterial details.
