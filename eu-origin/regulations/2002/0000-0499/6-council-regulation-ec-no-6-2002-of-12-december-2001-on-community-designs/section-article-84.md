# Council Regulation (EC) No 6/2002 of 12 December 2001 on Community designs, section Article 84

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 84. Action or counterclaim for a declaration of invalidity of a supplementary unregistered design[^key-7570323fdbf2eb8c839d350199fc134f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. An action or a counterclaim for a declaration of invalidity of a supplementary unregistered[^key-24266555997eda4a3ce9d8fad8085191] design may only be based on the grounds for invalidity mentioned in Article 25.

2. In the cases referred to in Article 25(2), (3), and[^key-b0d1c7d4720add2a93f866899d9fa13a] (4) [^key-ebfe3d05b58a8c9ee5d7ff04979518aa]... the action or the counterclaim may be brought solely by the person entitled under those provisions.

3. If the counterclaim is brought in a legal action to which the right holder of the supplementary unregistered[^key-44fbc9928e6f8c724abd151f3655b1fe] design is not already a party, he shall be informed thereof and may be joined as a party to the action in accordance with the laws applying to the design courts[^key-2c8ee9b732ae6c20fca8cfe43a163a4a].

4. The validity of a supplementary unregistered[^key-6e29c63942528b6e3af8014d9526c8bb] design may not be put in issue in an action for a declaration of non-infringement.
