# Council Regulation (EC) No 6/2002 of 12 December 2001 on Community designs, section Article 25

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 25. Grounds for invalidity

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. A supplementary unregistered[^key-172693a3b7aa93f84c72c63135aaa994] design may be declared invalid only in the following cases:

- (a) if the design does not correspond to the definition under Article 3(a);
- (b) if it does not fulfil the requirements of Articles 4 to 9;
- (c) if, by virtue of a court decision, the right holder is not entitled to the [^key-ea88d9c3da11194a7854adeec8648019]... design under Article 14;
- (d)[^key-d5ee954b4be3679f135a855782236726] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (e) if a distinctive sign is used in a subsequent design, and [^key-82c0ff70ed23bfafd8b4b2b38dd7c3ad]... the law [^key-cc0aea7910017aa55ff1015dcb28d36f]... governing that sign confers on the right holder of the sign the right to prohibit such use;
- (f) if the design constitutes an unauthorised use of a work protected under [^key-f613807d2180ecb061f7495cc3184e71]... copyright law [^key-9ee2fe9bd3c66aef6fde74dc69fe923f]...;
- (g) if the design constitutes an improper use of any of the items listed in Article 6ter of the ‘Paris Convention’ for the Protection of Industrial Property hereafter referred to as the ‘Paris Convention’, or of badges, emblems and escutcheons other than those covered by the said Article 6ter and which are of particular public interest in the United Kingdom, a qualifying country or a qualifying territory[^key-4ef2d518de8c3763c94e581aa5aaa2a1].

2. The ground provided for in paragraph (1)(c) may be invoked solely by the person who is entitled to the supplementary unregistered[^key-ab6386068e55dac3afd091225a3cf8e4] design under Article 14.

3. The grounds provided for in paragraph (1) [^key-4f5d8c8ad564de85eff066dd62910b1d]..., (e) and (f) may be invoked solely by [^key-db64f93b13c55b016bb9134627e9ca6c]... holder of the earlier right.

4. The ground provided for in paragraph (1)(g) may be invoked solely by the person or entity concerned by the use.
