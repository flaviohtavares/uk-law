# Council Regulation (EC) No 6/2002 of 12 December 2001 on Community designs, section Article 94

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 94. Obligation of the design court[^key-7be31a5c55fdf0d0458458d7be65d3cd]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A design court which is dealing with an action relating to a supplementary unregistered[^key-84a5bdf8fb4f41c8891d1522ac7212fe] design other than the actions referred to in Article 81 shall treat the design as valid. Articles 85(2) and 90(2) shall, however, apply *mutatis mutandis*.
