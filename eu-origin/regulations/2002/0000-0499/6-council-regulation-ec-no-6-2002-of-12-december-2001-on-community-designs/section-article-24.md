## Article 24. Declaration of invalidity

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1.[^c-01a988d1523bce6eb61e62a389b06a77] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

2. A supplementary unregistered[^c-307a616a5515fa0c261ce7665699ac3a] design may be declared invalid even after it[^c-3372ae65d715f44ee5b490145184fff7] has lapsed [^c-efa425ae9873deb0890cb45d4e0aa098]....

3. An unregistered supplementary unregistered[^c-399ad45bb839badedcee9d65bf0faa6f] design shall be declared invalid by a [^c-b903ac50cf771abf273196442abb9611]... design court on application to such a court or on the basis of a counterclaim in infringement proceedings.
