# Council Regulation (EC) No 6/2002 of 12 December 2001 on Community designs, section Article 26

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 26. Consequences of invalidity

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. A supplementary unregistered[^key-2e706ba786a117d797388b041308b941] design shall be deemed not to have had, as from the outset, the effects specified in this Regulation, to the extent that it has been declared invalid.

2. Subject to [^key-2686f0e1bea9b2316190934b93470972]... claims for compensation for damage caused by negligence or lack of good faith on the part of the holder of the supplementary unregistered[^key-b798b4454ca0a906f081d57a57274928] design, or to unjust enrichment, the retroactive effect of invalidity of the supplementary unregistered design shall not affect:

- (a) any decision on infringement which has acquired the authority of a final decision and been enforced prior to the invalidity decision;
- (b) any contract concluded prior to the invalidity decision, in so far as it has been performed before the decision; however, repayment, to an extent justified by the circumstances, of sums paid under the relevant contract may be claimed on grounds of equity.
