# Council Regulation (EC) No 6/2002 of 12 December 2001 on Community designs, section Article 21

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 21. Exhaustion of rights

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The rights conferred by a supplementary unregistered[^c-7405a99cc1be0667a334603f64b8aace] design shall not extend to acts relating to a product in which a design included within the scope of protection of the supplementary unregistered design is incorporated or to which it is applied, when the product has been put on the market in the United Kingdom or the European Economic Area[^c-27527100ee7f9022e9e9d6698757b5ee] by the holder of the supplementary unregistered design or with his consent.
