# Council Regulation (EC) No 6/2002 of 12 December 2001 on Community designs, section Article 27

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 27. Property right[^key-68bd97fd80c328a102954486d03b7a5c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

 A supplementary unregistered design is personal property (in Scotland, incorporeal moveable property) which subsists in accordance with this Regulation.[^key-c636822382c0e27a519b14649e7b7e2b]
