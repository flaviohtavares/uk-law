# Council Regulation (EC) No 6/2002 of 12 December 2001 on Community designs, section Article 85

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 85. Presumption of validity — defence as to the merits

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1.[^c-7d43d9914041990a9c221f9eb9abacdf] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

2. In proceedings in respect of an infringement action or an action for threatened infringement of a supplementary[^c-fcbf5cdc2af19a47011561d62d1413d0] unregistered [^c-e2716b7af271d55e615453d3eb477a4c]... design, the [^c-e2716b7af271d55e615453d3eb477a4c]... design court shall treat the supplementary unregistered[^c-badfca94a90cc988acfb70760ffb3270] design as valid if the right holder produces proof that the conditions laid down in Article 11 have been met and indicates what constitutes the individual character of his supplementary unregistered design. However, the defendant may contest its validity by way of a plea or with a counterclaim for a declaration of invalidity.
