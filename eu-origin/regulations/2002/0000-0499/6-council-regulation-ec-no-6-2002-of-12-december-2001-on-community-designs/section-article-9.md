# Council Regulation (EC) No 6/2002 of 12 December 2001 on Community designs, section Article 9

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 9. Designs contrary to public policy or morality

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A supplementary unregistered[^c-ffa82084d511b0e2efa0edcc27a2fd93] design shall not subsist in a design which is contrary to public policy or to accepted principles of morality.
