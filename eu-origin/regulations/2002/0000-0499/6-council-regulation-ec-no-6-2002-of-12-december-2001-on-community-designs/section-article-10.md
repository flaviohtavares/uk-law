# Council Regulation (EC) No 6/2002 of 12 December 2001 on Community designs, section Article 10

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 10. Scope of protection

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. The scope of the protection conferred by a supplementary unregistered[^c-7921eccecf3b02546435ba4c03f4e6c2] design shall include any design which does not produce on the informed user a different overall impression.

2. In assessing the scope of protection, the degree of freedom of the designer in developing his design shall be taken into consideration.
