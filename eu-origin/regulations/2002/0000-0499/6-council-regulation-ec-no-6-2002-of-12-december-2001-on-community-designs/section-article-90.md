# Council Regulation (EC) No 6/2002 of 12 December 2001 on Community designs, section Article 90

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 90. Provisional measures, including protective measures

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. Application may be made to the courts [^key-e2d1ea7da3d901e13b4ec6400bd18e22]..., including [^key-f4aff4d3938941b05ad09eaba091ff84]... design courts, for such provisional measures, including protective measures, in respect of a supplementary unregistered[^key-4581f44054a9ee4ab7aefd31587cb35e] design as may be available [^key-633090bbadb212d5a02b3f685cbf1b70]....

2. In proceedings relating to provisional measures, including protective measures, a plea otherwise than by way of counterclaim relating to the invalidity of a supplementary unregistered[^key-267e16e681c943ec5a9b9073bccd7c07] design submitted by the defendant shall be admissible. Article 85(2) shall, however, apply *mutatis mutandis*.

3. A [^key-4fab854c8ffb10fa8c8fa79e28009403]... design court [^key-27fb375c9ded68a7e73a31e75b99b652]... shall have jurisdiction to grant provisional measures, including protective measures [^key-2009b84748ebf7e24cd9591a630a3037]....
