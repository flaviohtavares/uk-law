## Article 71. Exchange of information[^c-b8d2af2faf97c443ad0a58b78cf61457]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1.[^c-85b0cccfa4e81a45d73c60a09bf2ad06] The competent authority shall establish and maintain a system for the exchange of information between the competent authority and applicants.

2.[^c-f69106159645a99eb2829bc167583bbd] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

3. Applicants shall use the system referred to in paragraph 1[^c-379e2c69b7ff051ee77ed4f5be53131b] to submit applications and data for all procedures covered by this Regulation.

4. Upon submission of applications and data by applicants, the competent authority[^c-5469455bab5e9c13dc95562f92d9a88a] shall check that these have been submitted in the correct format [^c-71d6c4e988818e690550728b15e4baff]....

Where the competent authority decides that the application has not been submitted in the correct format, it shall reject the application and inform the applicant accordingly.
