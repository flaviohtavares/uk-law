# Regulation (EU) No 528/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 22 May 2012 concerning the making available on the market and use of biocidal products (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 48

> Text as at 2026-05-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 48. Cancellation or amendment of an authorisation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. Without prejudice to Article 23, the competent authority [^c-7a3ec6b0bb8bd9252dacf41ce5411528]... shall at any time cancel or amend an authorisation it has granted where it considers that:

- (a) the conditions referred to in Article 19 or, where relevant, in Article 25 are not satisfied;
- (b) the authorisation was granted on the basis of false or misleading information; or
- (c) the authorisation holder has failed to comply with its obligations under the authorisation or this Regulation.

2. Where the competent authority [^c-7366b8c5191ba35a79a022978cd0f454]... intends to cancel or amend an authorisation, it shall inform the authorisation holder thereof and give it the opportunity to submit comments or additional information within a specified time limit. The competent authority[^c-83ecdd3cf18919b2e8b946f62a07d05e] shall take due account of those comments when finalising its decision.

3. Where the competent authority [^c-a932e544796eba706dc2607c64d90815]... cancels or amends an authorisation in accordance with paragraph 1, it shall without delay notify the authorisation holder [^c-1ddb5807eeb3c7147df96524bdef90f9]....
