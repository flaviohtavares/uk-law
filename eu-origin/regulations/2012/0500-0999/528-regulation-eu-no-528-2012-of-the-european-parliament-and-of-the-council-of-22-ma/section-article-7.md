# Regulation (EU) No 528/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 22 May 2012 concerning the making available on the market and use of biocidal products (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 7

> Text as at 2026-05-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 7. Submission and validation of applications

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1.[^key-806408bfe8e4619831864236e080f73a] The applicant shall submit an application for approval of an active substance, or for making subsequent amendments to the conditions of approval of an active substance, to the competent authority.

2.[^key-f5903cefafa63399e33738bdd80add80] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

3. Within 30 days of the competent authority receiving[^key-eec11105098cafaa9c377b370cc336b8] an application, it[^key-925a54e563b060568bc5efad5d7185ef] shall validate the application if the data required in accordance with points (a) and (b) and, where relevant, point (c) of Article 6(1), and any justifications for the adaptation of data requirements, have been submitted.

In the context of the validation referred to in the first subparagraph, the [^key-6c3ba39293a62e299d0033a230e71093]... competent authority shall not make an assessment of the quality or the adequacy of the data or justifications submitted.

The [^key-453e9863ebf9d3f423288bfb1a103477]... competent authority shall, as soon as possible after it has received[^key-97cbb8721fd3f9c25957f498c1c407f1] an application, inform the applicant of the fees payable [^key-b7cf7983978f64c47d186fac0e923799]... and shall reject the application if the applicant fails to pay the fees within 30 days. It shall inform the applicant accordingly.

4. Where the [^key-c4e28dc76032acee784e80cb70922737]... competent authority considers that the application is incomplete, it shall inform the applicant as to what additional information is required for the validation of the application and shall set a reasonable time limit for the submission of that information. That time limit shall not normally exceed 90 days.

The [^key-c4e28dc76032acee784e80cb70922737]... competent authority shall, within 30 days of receipt of the additional information, validate the application if it determines that the additional information submitted is sufficient to comply with the requirement laid down in paragraph 3.

The [^key-c4e28dc76032acee784e80cb70922737]... competent authority shall reject the application if the applicant fails to submit the requested information within the deadline and shall inform the applicant [^key-4a45262b6145b559fc5b7d2b3c59ff13].... In such cases, part of the fees paid [^key-b97d85bf1d021e0b3110a71177c7822d]... shall be reimbursed.

5. On validating an application in accordance with paragraph 3 or 4, the [^key-6ed6a1825b79e08ea416d32d3abf4642]... competent authority shall without delay inform the applicant [^key-adf75a80127de4e181586283b6c66382]... accordingly, indicating the date of the validation.

6.[^key-deae9a19e8104658cea84ef7e4c8d993] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
