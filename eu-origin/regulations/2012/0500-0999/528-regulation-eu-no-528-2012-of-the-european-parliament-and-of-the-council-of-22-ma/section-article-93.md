# Regulation (EU) No 528/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 22 May 2012 concerning the making available on the market and use of biocidal products (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 93

> Text as at 2026-05-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 93. Transitional measures concerning biocidal products not covered by the scope of Directive 98/8/EC[^c000001]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

By way of derogation from Article 17(1), the[^key-58b237a8a0d971c542a21ba51ebf9d87] current system or practice of making available on the market and using a biocidal product not covered by the scope of Directive 98/8/EC, but falling within the scope of this Regulation, and consisting of, containing or generating only active substances that were available on the market, or used in biocidal products, on 1 September 2013 , shall continue to apply—[^key-2238c9901e4fd3e420f57d36ed6fa9aa][^key-37769c56e1acc49c3b604d66b131603b]...

- ((a)) where applications for approval of all those active substances, which the biocidal product consists of, contains or generates, were[^key-645b20deabe4d4630310c0109f71cdcd] submitted for the relevant product-type by 1 September 2016, the deadlines provided for in the second subparagraph Article 89(7), in Article 89(8) to (10) and in Article 89(11); or[^key-cdd760842eb6046ee80aa6888a08892d]
- ((b)) where an application was[^key-874582c9161c391945c8fd442e35a94b] not submitted in accordance with point (a) for one of the active substances, until 1 September 2017.
