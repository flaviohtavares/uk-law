# Regulation (EU) No 528/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 22 May 2012 concerning the making available on the market and use of biocidal products (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 50

> Text as at 2026-05-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 50. Amendment of an authorisation at the request of the authorisation holder

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1.[^key-b9c39b7714afd77c770fc2ba2c5c67c1] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

2. An authorisation holder seeking to change any of the information submitted in relation to the initial application for authorisation of the product shall apply to the competent authority[^key-0e29819ef3ffbabdc5b681f7e5cb719f]. The competent authority shall[^key-08d3d310cc1e02ad1e521e343ab3cb12] decide whether the conditions of Article 19 or, where relevant, Article 25 are still met and whether the terms and conditions of the authorisation need to be amended.

The application shall be accompanied by the appropriate fees[^key-3eb7dc46a864ad6add23269db1476baa].

3. An amendment to an existing authorisation shall fall under one of the following categories of changes:

- (a) administrative change;
- (b) minor change; or
- (c) major change.
