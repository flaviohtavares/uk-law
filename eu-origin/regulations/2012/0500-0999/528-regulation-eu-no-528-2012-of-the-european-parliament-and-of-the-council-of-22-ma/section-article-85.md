# Regulation (EU) No 528/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 22 May 2012 concerning the making available on the market and use of biocidal products (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 85

> Text as at 2026-05-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 85. Adaptation to scientific and technical progress[^c-7d4125cb78d1c398a0ab463fdc013707]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1.) The Secretary of State may by regulations amend Annexes II, III and IV to this Regulation to take account of current scientific and technical knowledge.

(2.) Regulations made under paragraph 1 above shall be subject to the consent requirement.
