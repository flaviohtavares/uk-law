# Regulation (EU) No 528/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 22 May 2012 concerning the making available on the market and use of biocidal products (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 8A

> Text as at 2026-05-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 8A. The GB List[^key-4192843e7f9abd23ec1e45fffb9a8e85]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The competent authority shall establish, maintain and make electronically available to the public a list of approved active substances (“the GB List”).
