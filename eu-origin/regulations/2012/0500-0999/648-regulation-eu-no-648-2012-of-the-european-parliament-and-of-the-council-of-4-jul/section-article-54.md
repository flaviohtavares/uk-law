# Regulation (EU) No 648/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 4 July 2012 on OTC derivatives, central counterparties and trade repositories (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 54

## Article 54. Approval of interoperability arrangements

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. An interoperability arrangement shall be subject to the prior approval of the CCP's competent authority[^c-bdff3211beb57354e096ca3275ff0dfc]. The procedure under Article 17 shall apply.

2. The competent authority[^c-e8fbb4ab2a39596f5ce60433950a5f26] shall grant approval of the interoperability arrangement only where the CCPs involved have been authorised to clear under Article 17 or recognised under Article 25 [^c-9aa8f192f2a9ebf72a61f7ac617b85f7]... , the requirements laid down in Article 52 are met and the technical conditions for clearing transactions under the terms of the arrangement allow for a smooth and orderly functioning of financial markets and the arrangement does not undermine the effectiveness of supervision.

3.[^c-11c814a16699ab85ab4d372bb9b56ef6] Where the competent authority considers that the requirements laid down in paragraph 2 are not met, it shall provide explanations in writing regarding its risk considerations to the CCPs involved.
