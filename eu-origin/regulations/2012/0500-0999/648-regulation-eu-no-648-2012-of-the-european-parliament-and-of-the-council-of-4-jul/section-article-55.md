## Article 55. Registration of a trade repository

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. A trade repository shall register with the FCA[^c-516960312a7bc5ee9ad9b27fe09d118f] for the purposes of Article 9.

2. To be eligible to be registered under this Article, a trade repository shall be a legal person established in the United Kingdom[^c-de19166eab789af2469a3d3453426929] and meet the requirements laid down in Title VII.

3.[^c-3527ebdada7b1af84459a97add27b472] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

4. A registered trade repository shall comply at all times with the conditions for registration. A trade repository shall, without undue delay, notify the FCA[^c-71b45c47641e94f61abc784772ce707d] of any material changes to the conditions for registration.
