# Regulation (EU) No 648/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 4 July 2012 on OTC derivatives, central counterparties and trade repositories (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 12

## Article 12. Penalties

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1.[^key-ce08081a8536295f01f4926993da4a56] Regulation 9 of the 2013 Regulations sets out the penalties applicable to infringements of the rules under this Title.

2.[^key-a0e6119a9a10ccc91d9857aab88b2234] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

3. An infringement of the rules under this Title shall not affect the validity of an OTC derivative contract or the possibility for the parties to enforce the provisions of an OTC derivative contract. An infringement of the rules under this Title shall not give rise to any right to compensation from a party to an OTC derivative contract.
