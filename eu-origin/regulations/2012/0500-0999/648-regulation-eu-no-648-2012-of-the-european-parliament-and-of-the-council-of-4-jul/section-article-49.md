## Article 49. Review of models, stress testing and back testing

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1.[^c000013] A CCP shall regularly review the models and parameters adopted to calculate its margin requirements, default fund contributions, collateral requirements and other risk control mechanisms. It shall subject the models to rigorous and frequent stress tests to assess their resilience in extreme but plausible market conditions and shall perform back tests to assess the reliability of the methodology adopted. The CCP shall obtain independent validation, shall inform the Bank of England[^M_F_ccd0f100-2f20-4ffa-c406-ea3332d8bfc0] of the results of the tests performed and shall obtain its[^c-46f8c158f7d113066d409fb196ae8896] validation in accordance with paragraphs 1a, [^M_F_0d96527b-00ef-44ac-9919-15a03182c3fb]... 1d and 1e before adopting any significant change to the models and parameters.

[^c-fcf504469de9d9f1f47db9d5737dc508]...

[^c-fcf504469de9d9f1f47db9d5737dc508]...

1a.[^c000015] Where a CCP intends to adopt any significant change to the models and parameters referred to in paragraph 1, it shall apply to the Bank of England[^c-42a76c4801775eca17450721f2b43b59] for validation of that change. The CCP shall enclose an independent validation of the intended change to its applications. The Bank of England shall [^c-b5cabfcd6cba0e1d633bda4b8a4d5f91]... confirm the receipt of the complete application to the CCP.

1b.[^c-60a843076b41c8673c3f1a886cf5ff4a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

1c.[^c-6e6d212a037fd05e9ca7604726ae364f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

1d. Within 90 working days of the receipt of the applications referred to in paragraph 1a, the Bank of England shall inform the CCP[^c-0bec9e6c1ba31d7fda03b1c05067aa6b] in writing, including a fully reasoned explanation, whether the validation has been granted or refused.

1e. The CCP may not adopt any significant change to the models and parameters referred to in paragraph 1 before obtaining the validations by the Bank of England[^c-4f0737d755ea2a92c38ecd879cb149c1]. The Bank of England[^c-649bcb839fd7e3a08589f68e141385ad] may allow for a provisional adoption of a significant change of those models or parameters prior to their validations where duly justified.

2. A CCP shall regularly test the key aspects of its default procedures and take all reasonable steps to ensure that all clearing members understand them and have appropriate arrangements in place to respond to a default event.

3. A CCP shall publicly disclose key information on its risk-management model and assumptions adopted to perform the stress tests referred to in paragraph 1.

4. The Bank of England may make[^c-9aa67aacaa1098be0cd8d872e971cc27] technical standards specifying:

- (a) the type of tests to be undertaken for different classes of financial instruments and portfolios;
- (b) the involvement of clearing members or other parties in the tests;
- (c) the frequency of the tests;
- (d) the time horizons of the tests;
- (e) the key information referred to in paragraph 3.

5.[^M_F_77ae410f-e871-4bf4-e1c5-b64ddce519f1]  The Bank of England may make technical standards specifying the conditions under which changes to the models and parameters referred to in paragraph 1 are significant.
