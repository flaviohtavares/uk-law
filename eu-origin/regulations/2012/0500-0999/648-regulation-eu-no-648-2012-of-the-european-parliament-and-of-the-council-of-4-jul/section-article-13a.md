## Article 13a. Conditions for making technical standards[^key-9f014b8e3e399948ec6228a24d84e60f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The FCA, the PRA and the Bank of England, as appropriate, must co-ordinate the exercise of their functions when making technical standards under:

- (a) the second sub-paragraph of paragraph 4 of Article 4,
- (b) paragraph 5 of Article 9,
- (c) paragraph 6 of Article 9,
- (d) paragraph 15 of Article 11, or
- (e) paragraph 17 of Article 11,

with a view to ensuring that the technical standards made under each of those provisions are compatible.
