# Regulation (EU) No 648/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 4 July 2012 on OTC derivatives, central counterparties and trade repositories (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 25b

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (25).** Text as at 2025-11-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/eur/2012/648
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 25b. Ongoing compliance with the conditions for recognition[^c000015]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. The Bank of England[^c-f54c2bb28920f10a4807f4fc31e94089] shall be responsible for carrying out the duties resulting from this Regulation for the supervision on an ongoing basis of the compliance of recognised Tier 2 CCPs with the requirements referred to in point (a) of Article 25(2b). [^c-9611a5d9415b0740aa962cdd86972a98]...

The Bank of England shall require confirmation from each Tier 2 CCP at least on a yearly basis that the requirements referred to in points (a), (c) and (d) of Article 25(2b) continue to be fulfilled.

[^c-34e07c277a012f1a84a3da73d4f36680]...

2. Where a Tier 2 CCP fails to provide the Bank of England with the confirmation referred to in the second subparagraph of paragraph 1 [^c-dd5c2c78754beef9705039a33dc48ee7]... , the CCP shall be considered as no longer meeting the conditions for recognition pursuant to Article 25(2b) and the procedure set out in Article 25p(2) and (3)[^c-0978391fe9214ff081ef1123672d24cd] shall apply.

3.[^c-2ab29b8c3de65d7ae7215ae6cb3a845c] The Bank of England shall carry out assessments of the resilience of recognised Tier 2 CCPs to adverse market developments.
