# Regulation (EU) No 648/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 4 July 2012 on OTC derivatives, central counterparties and trade repositories (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 56

## Article 56. Application for registration

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1.[^c000006] For the purposes of Article 55(1), a trade repository shall submit either of the following to the FCA[^c-d54d65a7ff6d6d17929597b10b60cc0f]:

- (a) an application for registration;
- (b) an application for an extension of the registration where the trade repository is already registered under Chapter III of Regulation (EU) 2015/2365.

2. The FCA[^c-9b2f48fa58670bc63dc606ada5702417] shall assess whether the application is complete within 20 working days of receipt of the application.

Where the application is not complete, the FCA[^c-b128c3f745e7ab02efb4385f7928308c] shall set a deadline by which the trade repository is to provide additional information.

After assessing an application as complete, the FCA shall notify the trade repository accordingly.

3.[^c-ffda53fee399c596ccb51e92c23e0ebd] The FCA may make technical standards specifying the form and content of applications under paragraph 1.
