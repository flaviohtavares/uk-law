## Article 17. Procedure for granting and refusing authorisation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. The applicant CCP shall submit an application for authorisation to the competent authority [^key-4f6bacdcda55ef2b6889f8667f23f839]... .

2. The applicant CCP shall provide all information necessary to satisfy the competent authority that the applicant CCP has established, at the time of authorisation, all the necessary arrangements to meet the requirements laid down in this Regulation. [^key-77c0b2d90147b3bd88c9004b163d8176]...

3.[^c000013] Within 30 working days of receipt of the application, the competent authority shall assess whether the application is complete. Where the application is not complete, the competent authority shall set a deadline by which the applicant CCP has to provide additional information. [^key-ec3c4231a1f330232bce117491223213]... After assessing that an application is complete, the competent authority shall notify the applicant CCP [^key-73e652aa6bd73e1036e36617edfa5df6]...

4. The competent authority shall grant authorisation only where it is fully satisfied that the applicant CCP complies with all the requirements laid down in this Regulation and that the CCP is a designated system as defined in regulation 2(1) of the Financial Markets and Insolvency (Settlement Finality) Regulations 1999[^key-6f7c2d6d0075370870c45ac5fe6ef129].

[^key-c162452bd9eb25b0d6cea7b3ae43d196]...

5.[^key-94a0883d34c1915ea95b8b1ce7f988f3] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

6.[^key-8a5575ddef6a8b30d3f583ca6feed86e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

7. Within six months of the submission of a complete application, the competent authority shall inform the applicant CCP in writing, with a fully reasoned explanation, whether authorisation has been granted or refused.
