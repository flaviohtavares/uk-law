# Commission Regulation (EC) No 1718/2005 of 19 October 2005 amending the rates of refunds applicable to certain products from the cereals and rice sector exported in the form of goods not covered by Annex I to the Treaty

> Text as at 2005-10-19.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Commission Regulation (EC) No 1718/2005

of 19 October 2005

amending the rates of refunds applicable to certain products from the cereals and rice sector exported in the form of goods not covered by Annex I to the Treaty

THE COMMISSION OF THE EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES,

Having regard to the Treaty establishing the European Community,

Having regard to Council Regulation (EC) No 1784/2003 of 29 September 2003 on the common organisation of the market in cereals[^f00001], and in particular Article 13(3) thereof,

Having regard to Council Regulation (EC) No 1785/2003 of 29 September 2003 on the common organisation of the market in ric[^f00002], and in particular Article 14(3) thereof,

Whereas:

(1)

The rates of the refunds applicable from 30 September 2005 to the products listed in the Annex, exported in the form of goods not covered by Annex I to the Treaty, were fixed by Commission Regulation (EC) No 1585/2005[^f00003].

(2)

It follows from applying the rules and criteria contained in Regulation (EC) No 1585/2005 to the information at present available to the Commission that the export refunds at present applicable should be altered as shown in the Annex hereto,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

**Article 1.** The rates of refund fixed by Regulation (EC) No 1585/2005 are hereby altered as shown in the Annex hereto.

**Article 2.** This Regulation shall enter into force on 20 October 2005.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 19 October 2005.

*For the Commission*

Günter Verheugen

*Member of the Commission*
