# Commission Regulation (EC) No 2154/2005 of 23 December 2005 supplementing the Annex to Regulation (EC) No 2400/96 as regards the entry of a name in the ‘Register of protected designations of origin and protected geographical indications’(‘Sidra de Asturias’ or ‘Sidra d’Asturies’) [PDO]

> Text as at 2005-12-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Commission Regulation (EC) No 2154/2005

of 23 December 2005

supplementing the Annex to Regulation (EC) No 2400/96 as regards the entry of a name in the ‘Register of protected designations of origin and protected geographical indications’*(‘Sidra de Asturias’* or *‘Sidra d’Asturies’)* \[PDO\]

THE COMMISSION OF THE EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES,

Having regard to the Treaty establishing the European Community,

Having regard to Council Regulation (EEC) No 2081/92 of 14 July 1992 on the protection of geographical indications and designations of origin for agricultural products and foodstuffs[^f00001], and in particular Article 6(3) and (4) thereof,

Whereas:

(1)

In accordance with Article 6(2) of Regulation (EEC) No 2081/92, the request from Spain to enter the name ‘*Sidra de Asturias*’ or ‘*Sidra d’Asturies*’ has been published in the *Official Journal of the European Union* [^f00002].

(2)

Since the Commission has received no statement of objection pursuant to Article 7 of Regulation (EEC) No 2081/92, this name must be entered in the ‘Register of protected designations of origin and protected geographical indications’,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

**Article 1.** The name in the Annex to this Regulation is hereby added to the Annex to Commission Regulation (EC) No 2400/96[^f00003].

**Article 2.** This Regulation shall enter into force on the 20th day following its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Union*.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 23 December 2005.

*For the Commission*

Mariann Fischer Boel

*Member of the Commission*

## ANNEX — Products listed in Annex I to the Treaty, intended for human consumption

Other Annex I products (spices, etc.)

SPAIN

‘Sidra de Asturias’ or ‘Sidra d’Asturies’ (PDO)
