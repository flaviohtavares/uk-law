# Regulation (EU) No 1306/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 December 2013 on the financing, management and monitoring of the common agricultural policy and repealing Council Regulations (EEC) No 352/78, (EC) No 165/94, (EC) No 2799/98, (EC) No 814/2000, (EC) No 1290/2005 and (EC) No 485/2008, section Article 30

## Article 30. No double funding

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Measures financed by support for rural development[^c-d8823701e851de4d3578cf6fdaf43de1] shall not be the subject of any other agricultural support[^c-fff5af874abf647b72a9795adb0c3b9f].
