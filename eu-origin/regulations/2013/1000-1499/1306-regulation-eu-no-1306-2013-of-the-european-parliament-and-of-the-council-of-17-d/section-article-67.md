## Article 67. Scope and terms used[^M_E_034cf595-c7e1-4fb9-c6d0-e9a7c6db2e79]

*Extent: S*

1. The relevant authority must[^key-0170300b5aff8ea672c86cefb7ddb221] operate an integrated administration and control system (the 'integrated system').

2.[^key-d4f0b6d0feed2f648c69a061f6e71c09] The integrated system shall apply *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d4f0b6d0feed2f648c69a061f6e71c09] [^key-0918615c3764edc043a1e7037dd68942]... to the support granted in accordance with points (a) and (b) of Article 21(1) and Articles 28 to 31, 33, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d4f0b6d0feed2f648c69a061f6e71c09] and 34[^key-4471ccc196605835c7c699b6f862ba72] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d4f0b6d0feed2f648c69a061f6e71c09] of Regulation 1305/2013 and where applicable points (b) and (c) of Article 35(1) of Regulation (EU) 1303/2013. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d4f0b6d0feed2f648c69a061f6e71c09]

This Chapter shall not, however, apply to measures referred to in Article 28(9) of Regulation (EU) No 1305/2013. Nor shall it apply to, measures under points (a) and (b) of Article 21(1) of that Regulation as far as the establishment cost is concerned. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d4f0b6d0feed2f648c69a061f6e71c09]

2. The integrated system shall apply to the support schemes listed in Annex I of Regulation (EU) No 1307/2013.

3. To the extent necessary, the integrated system shall also apply to the control of cross-compliance as laid down in Title VI.

4. For the purpose of this Chapter:

- (a) "agricultural parcel" means a continuous area of land, declared by one farmer, which does not cover more than one single crop group; however, where a separate declaration of the use of an area within a crop group is required in the context of Regulation (EU) No 1307/2013, that specific use shall if necessary further limit the agricultural parcel; Member States may lay *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f8c1c89e7782478e481e01fdc85eb281]nothing in this Regulation prevents the relevant authority from laying down additional criteria for further delimitation of an agricultural parcel;
- (b)[^c000001] "area-related direct payment" means the basic payment scheme; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0dcbd86f0e8da7ff82d6b67842fd610b] [^key-5ae876e13e0be2b453d86fd1c316cb65]...
