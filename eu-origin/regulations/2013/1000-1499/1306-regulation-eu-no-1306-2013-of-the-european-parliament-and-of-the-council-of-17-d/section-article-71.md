## Article 71 (affected by repeal, see note). System for the identification and registration of payment entitlements[^key-4f0b0bacac0a1af16296e93680cbea88]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. The system for the identification and registration of payment entitlements shall allow for verification of the entitlements and for cross-checks with the aid applications and the identification system for agricultural parcels. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4f0b0bacac0a1af16296e93680cbea88]

2.[^key-eed80a17a08d5949c3ceef7fed75b004] The system referred to in paragraph 1 shall allow direct and immediate consultation, through the competent authority of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-eed80a17a08d5949c3ceef7fed75b004] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4f0b0bacac0a1af16296e93680cbea88] constituent nation[^key-d181c51c068695209b6ac5e5fabfa8f7] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-eed80a17a08d5949c3ceef7fed75b004] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4f0b0bacac0a1af16296e93680cbea88], of the data relating to at least the previous four consecutive calendar years. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-eed80a17a08d5949c3ceef7fed75b004] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4f0b0bacac0a1af16296e93680cbea88]
