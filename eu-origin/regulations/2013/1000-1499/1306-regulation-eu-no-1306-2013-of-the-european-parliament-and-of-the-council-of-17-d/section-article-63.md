## Article 63. Undue payments and administrative penalties

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. Where it is found that a beneficiary does not comply with the eligibility criteria, commitments or other obligations relating to the conditions for the granting of the aid or support, as provided for in the sectoral agricultural legislation, the aid shall *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b2eb6c888a698dba4c50660847f030b0] may not be paid or shall *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b2eb6c888a698dba4c50660847f030b0] may be withdrawn in full or in part and, where relevant, the corresponding payment entitlements as referred to in Article 21 of Regulation (EU) No 1307/2013 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22fd5305da57ad0b741d140cdb0cab93] shall *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b2eb6c888a698dba4c50660847f030b0] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22fd5305da57ad0b741d140cdb0cab93] may *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22fd5305da57ad0b741d140cdb0cab93] not be allocated or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22fd5305da57ad0b741d140cdb0cab93] shall *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b2eb6c888a698dba4c50660847f030b0] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22fd5305da57ad0b741d140cdb0cab93] may *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22fd5305da57ad0b741d140cdb0cab93] be withdrawn *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22fd5305da57ad0b741d140cdb0cab93].

Where the non-compliance concerns[^c000005] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-78e1c4a7c49bce2907d7ddfc925d781d] [^c-fd9b2da5308257cd5b23feb568f64c31]... rules on public procurement, the part of the aid not to be paid or to be withdrawn *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-78e1c4a7c49bce2907d7ddfc925d781d] shall *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b2eb6c888a698dba4c50660847f030b0] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-78e1c4a7c49bce2907d7ddfc925d781d]may *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-78e1c4a7c49bce2907d7ddfc925d781d] be determined on the basis of the gravity of the non-compliance and in accordance with the principle of proportionality. The legality and regularity of the transaction *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-78e1c4a7c49bce2907d7ddfc925d781d] shall *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b2eb6c888a698dba4c50660847f030b0] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-78e1c4a7c49bce2907d7ddfc925d781d] may *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-78e1c4a7c49bce2907d7ddfc925d781d] only be affected up to the level of the part of the aid not to be paid or to be withdrawn. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-78e1c4a7c49bce2907d7ddfc925d781d]

2. Moreover, where sectoral agricultural legislation so provides, the[^c-67132d2f7367ad856b035a1d7099844a] relevant authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-784d27bb5a724190971aa77e25ce1bf6] shall *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-784d27bb5a724190971aa77e25ce1bf6] relevant authority may also impose administrative penalties, in accordance with the rules laid down in Article 64 and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a28d324e8ce88caf55f2843b437839ec] Article 77. This shall be without prejudice to the provisions set out in Articles 91 to 101 of Title VI. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6afbd995f29238bc8b4f1834478865e8]

3. Without prejudice to Article 54(3), the amounts, including interest thereon, and payment entitlements, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-396b36a49da4e8aed772071d562205b3] concerned by the withdrawal referred to in paragraph 1 and by the penalties referred to in paragraph 2 shall *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-55b4413bc4fd089b794241978208d215] may be recovered.

4. The appropriate authority may make regulations[^c-917504d826860b5ce7f4ede3d2bd3f66] laying down the conditions for the partial or total withdrawal referred to in paragraph 1.

5. The appropriate authority may make regulations[^c-fd2d6ea454d81fa0338c965fa7ad3b91] laying down detailed procedural and technical rules on:

- (a) the application and calculation of the partial or total withdrawal referred to in paragraph 1;
- (b) the recovery of undue payments and penalties as well as in respect of unduly allocated payment entitlements and[^c-a25c727e256c3c864af9f07f7e65a092][^c-04b222bb6071810713e8fc4940ff5d20]... the application of interest.
