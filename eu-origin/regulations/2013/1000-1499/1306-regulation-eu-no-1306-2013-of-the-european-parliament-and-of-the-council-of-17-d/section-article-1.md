# Regulation (EU) No 1306/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 December 2013 on the financing, management and monitoring of the common agricultural policy and repealing Council Regulations (EEC) No 352/78, (EC) No 165/94, (EC) No 2799/98, (EC) No 814/2000, (EC) No 1290/2005 and (EC) No 485/2008, section Article 1

## Article 1. Scope

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Regulation lays down the rules on:

- ((a)) the financing of expenditure for direct payments[^key-fafb12181afafe16d3ec97d3e21c403c] on agricultural support[^key-5d6d9a0aaab6f34dbcc1b2f20de01d32];
- ((b)) the farm advisory system;
- ((c)) the management and control systems to be put in place by the relevant authority[^key-004af12e393313654590580bc5efe0f1];
- ((d)) the cross-compliance system; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-437d722f1cb986f900f66e4af22d8701]
- ((e)) [^key-5c5129bbe6a26df195c023d6075fbc84]...

This Regulation does not apply to direct payments in England in respect of any year falling after the end of 2023.[^key-2b5fe7c9a8fe0a3f856b6c7c0e61c84d]
