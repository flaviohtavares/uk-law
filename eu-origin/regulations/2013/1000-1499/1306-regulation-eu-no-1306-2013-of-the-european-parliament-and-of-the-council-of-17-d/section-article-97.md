## Article 97 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note). Application of the administrative penalty (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e][^c-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note).[^c-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e] The administrative penalty provided for in Article 91 shall be imposed where the rules on cross-compliance are not complied with at any time in a given calendar year ('the calendar year concerned'), and where the non-compliance in question is directly attributable to the beneficiary who submitted the aid application or the payment claim in the calendar year concerned. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e]

[^c-2a6256aa75c406d94c55e370366ea280]... *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e]

2 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note).[^c-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e] In cases in which the land is transferred during the calendar year concerned or the years concerned, paragraph 1 shall also apply where the non-compliance in question is the result of an act or omission directly attributable to the person to whom or from whom the agricultural land was transferred. By way of derogation from the first sentence, where the person to whom the act or omission is directly attributable has submitted an aid application or a payment claim in the calendar year concerned or the years concerned, the administrative penalty shall be imposed on the basis of the total amounts of the payments referred to in Article 92 granted or to be granted to that person. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e]

For the purpose of this paragraph, 'transfer' means any type of transaction whereby the agricultural land ceases to be at the disposal of the transferor. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e]

3 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note).[^c-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e] Notwithstanding paragraph 1, and subject to the rules to be adopted pursuant to Article 101, *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e] the relevant authority[^c-2f4b3ecb48037ebedabffcdfb3cefe98] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e] may decide not to apply an administrative penalty per beneficiary and per calendar year when the amount of the penalty is *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e] EUR 100 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2012020cca2554f1ed49833857911193] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e]£100 *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e] or less. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e]

Where *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e] the relevant authority[^M_F_c693c8c2-f8e5-4f01-de29-d37b9f22949e] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e] decides to make use of the option provided for in the first subparagraph, the competent authority shall, for a sample of beneficiaries, take in the following year the actions necessary to verify that the beneficiary has remedied the findings of non-compliance concerned. The finding and the obligation to take remedial action shall be notified to the beneficiary. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e]

4 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note).[^c-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e] The imposition of an administrative penalty shall not affect the legality and regularity of the payments to which it applies. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e]
