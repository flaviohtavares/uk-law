# Regulation (EU) No 1306/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 December 2013 on the financing, management and monitoring of the common agricultural policy and repealing Council Regulations (EEC) No 352/78, (EC) No 165/94, (EC) No 2799/98, (EC) No 814/2000, (EC) No 1290/2005 and (EC) No 485/2008, section Article 84

## Article 84. Programming[^M_E_5pfpkb18-oknk-0j95-ovk6-664751c1730b3]

*Extent: W+S+N.I.*

1. The relevant authority[^c-b7e3efecfebe24e2cd4ec4c6209a3997] shall draw up programmes for scrutiny to be carried out pursuant to Article 80 during the subsequent scrutiny period.

2. Each year, before 15 April, the relevant authority[^c-65642eeae7a0d13dc839728a5bdfd44c] shall send the relevant accounting officer[^c-d21e982a02a909aa77129d828020d29e] their programme as referred to in paragraph 1 and shall specify:

- (a) the number of undertakings to be scrutinised and their breakdown by sector on the basis of the amounts relating to them;
- (b) the criteria adopted for drawing up the programme.

3. The programmes established by the relevant authority[^c-87e5e19b97764c5c1797fc9855181f1e] and forwarded to the relevant accounting officer[^c-c8f9fc84c3c28d7911b0312e2af5daa3] shall be implemented by the relevant authority, if, within eight weeks, the relevant accounting officer has not made known its comments.

4. Paragraph 3 shall apply mutatis mutandis to the amendments to the programme made by the relevant authority[^c-9bc08c3d39f5c21202bc2a1268e9970b].

5. At any stage, the relevant accounting officer[^c-2bd65b630f850a2420f4d30dd32941c6] may request the inclusion of a particular category of undertaking in the programme of the relevant authority[^c-50890ea8a922487e76c91bb9aff2b443].

6. Undertakings for which the sum of the receipts or payments amounted to less than EUR 40 000 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-22c617e421b1402cfdd8f6ff53cd8a73]£40 000 shall be scrutinised in accordance with this Chapter only for specific reasons to be indicated by the relevant authority in its[^c-49f7129891c8b94b484c729362e5e0eb] annual programme referred to in paragraph 1 or by the relevant accounting officer[^c-6d004a7b6c31e4c8d3b01253fc32eb05] in any proposed amendment to that programme. In order to take account of economic developments, the appropriate authority may make regulations modifying the threshold. Such regulations may specify the threshold in Sterling.[^c-3df21c2e2fd562572a8627a9f27485fb]

## Article 84. Programming[^M_E_wv3t99t6-jv4a-lnm3-tp6p-664751c173024]

*Extent: E*

1. The relevant authority[^V_E_de27d510-885e-77d9-cea1-2efa2a6a7b47] may[^c-2200659c3a92d4de7b50b58f7b8c464a] draw up programmes for scrutiny to be carried out pursuant to Article 80 during the subsequent scrutiny period.

2. Each year, before 15 April, the relevant authority[^V_E_8c15e683-a7ab-d4f7-f19b-5c509bcbac94] may[^c-3083d86415d40a8c31022a1783076496] send the relevant accounting officer[^V_E_62020417-c8a7-6e59-ccc5-a9c7861fba34] their programme as referred to in paragraph 1 and may specify:

- (a) the number of undertakings to be scrutinised and their breakdown by sector on the basis of the amounts relating to them;
- (b) the criteria adopted for drawing up the programme.

3. The programmes established by the relevant authority[^V_E_cf35bd67-5d36-2357-4010-c91597f274fe] and forwarded to the relevant accounting officer[^V_E_54ad50cd-9f31-7d4c-2d94-665247d55c04] may[^c-5e2fb246c83e35a835a21835ddcba212] be implemented by the relevant authority, if, within eight weeks, the relevant accounting officer has not made known its comments.

4. Paragraph 3 shall apply mutatis mutandis to the amendments to the programme made by the relevant authority[^V_E_3e7e9886-0027-4baf-fc5b-7003963e0c73].

5. At any stage, the relevant accounting officer[^V_E_6c0dc19e-a8c1-3bf9-a21d-b709222ad009] may request the inclusion of a particular category of undertaking in the programme of the relevant authority[^V_E_9688d240-7d65-c34f-13d8-61d501a0752f].

6. Undertakings for which the sum of the receipts or payments amounted to less than EUR 40 000 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^V_E_1a0d28a3-ba37-e496-386b-e4bc074c1609]£40 000 shall be scrutinised in accordance with this Chapter only for specific reasons to be indicated by the relevant authority in its[^V_E_4acdf251-8b5c-3aff-1d65-5d2870aab8d8] annual programme referred to in paragraph 1 or by the relevant accounting officer[^V_E_ccf0a8af-2f1f-736c-3576-308ed615ecba] in any proposed amendment to that programme. In order to take account of economic developments, the appropriate authority may make regulations modifying the threshold. Such regulations may specify the threshold in Sterling.[^V_E_a396e9ff-4e43-c434-b9c3-9a107dbd57ed]
