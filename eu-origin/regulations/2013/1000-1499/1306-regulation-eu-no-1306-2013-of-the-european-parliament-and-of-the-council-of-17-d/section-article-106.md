## Article 106 (affected by repeal, see note). Exchange rate and operative event[^key-e6fdd4aab54f43e243ab32c30dcb9381]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1.[^key-5ecbc010928674e8bee47cf6723fb91d] Where the prices or amounts referred to in Article 105(2) are expressed in euro, they shall be converted into sterling by means of an exchange rate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e6fdd4aab54f43e243ab32c30dcb9381]

2. The operative event for the exchange rate shall be: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e6fdd4aab54f43e243ab32c30dcb9381]

- (a) the completion of customs import or export formalities in the case of amounts collected or granted in trade with third countries; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e6fdd4aab54f43e243ab32c30dcb9381]
- (b) the event whereby the economic objective of the operation is attained in all other cases. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e6fdd4aab54f43e243ab32c30dcb9381]

3.[^key-b4450dca531f81d5ca4102dbe4cb80a0] Where a direct payment as provided for in Regulation (EU) No 1307/2013 is made to a beneficiary in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b4450dca531f81d5ca4102dbe4cb80a0] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e6fdd4aab54f43e243ab32c30dcb9381] sterling, the relevant authority shall convert the amount of aid expressed in euro into sterling on the basis of the average of the exchange rates set by the European Central Bank during the month prior to 1 October 2019.[^key-8dec2e639aefa2d2af04c0708ae556f2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b4450dca531f81d5ca4102dbe4cb80a0] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e6fdd4aab54f43e243ab32c30dcb9381].

[^key-ec41c50d0b6dbee842ca9aed5c83f7a4]...

4.[^key-771f93bfeac36151cce75e9c6eab7a26] As regards *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-771f93bfeac36151cce75e9c6eab7a26] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e6fdd4aab54f43e243ab32c30dcb9381] direct payment support[^key-3620be869e7d6d60d9bc9633df116eef] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-771f93bfeac36151cce75e9c6eab7a26] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e6fdd4aab54f43e243ab32c30dcb9381]CMO support[^key-ebd2fb4fcbf6db301bd955efbf694700] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-771f93bfeac36151cce75e9c6eab7a26] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e6fdd4aab54f43e243ab32c30dcb9381], when drawing up their declarations of expenditure, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-771f93bfeac36151cce75e9c6eab7a26] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e6fdd4aab54f43e243ab32c30dcb9381] the relevant authority[^key-0d29b06b61d9900b08324ef729446746] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-771f93bfeac36151cce75e9c6eab7a26] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e6fdd4aab54f43e243ab32c30dcb9381] shall apply the same exchange rate as that which they used to make payments to beneficiaries or receive revenue, in accordance with the provisions of this Chapter. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-771f93bfeac36151cce75e9c6eab7a26] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e6fdd4aab54f43e243ab32c30dcb9381]

5. In order to specify the operative event referred to in paragraph 2 or to fix it for reasons peculiar to the market organisation or the amount in question, the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e6fdd4aab54f43e243ab32c30dcb9381] appropriate authority may make regulations[^key-6c43262225ac528528e415bfaf68072c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e6fdd4aab54f43e243ab32c30dcb9381], containing rules on those operative events and the exchange rate to be used. The specific operative event shall be determined taking account of the following criteria: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e6fdd4aab54f43e243ab32c30dcb9381]

- (a) actual applicability as soon as possible of adjustments to the exchange rate; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e6fdd4aab54f43e243ab32c30dcb9381]
- (b) similarity of the operative events for analogous operations carried out under the market organisation; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e6fdd4aab54f43e243ab32c30dcb9381]
- (c) coherence in the operative events for the various prices and amounts relating to the market organisation; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e6fdd4aab54f43e243ab32c30dcb9381]
- (d) practicability and effectiveness of checks on the application of suitable exchange rates. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e6fdd4aab54f43e243ab32c30dcb9381]

6.[^key-ba9a94feb853b3a2af38afd97258b41e] In order to avoid the application *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ba9a94feb853b3a2af38afd97258b41e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e6fdd4aab54f43e243ab32c30dcb9381] [^key-286d637c2cc044a7a8425e5face678a7]... of different exchange rates in accounts of revenue received or aid paid to beneficiaries in a currency other than the euro, on the one hand, and in the establishment of the declaration of expenditure drawn up by the paying agency, on the other, the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ba9a94feb853b3a2af38afd97258b41e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e6fdd4aab54f43e243ab32c30dcb9381] appropriate authority may make regulations[^key-b509e5ee4c6c217cefd10c2e9b6208b9] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ba9a94feb853b3a2af38afd97258b41e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e6fdd4aab54f43e243ab32c30dcb9381] laying down rules on the exchange rate applicable when declarations of expenditure are drawn up and when public storage operations are recorded in the accounts of the paying agency. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ba9a94feb853b3a2af38afd97258b41e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e6fdd4aab54f43e243ab32c30dcb9381]
