## Article 32. Core contribution[^c-b44137b369bc5cea4936d7e58df20577]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The core contribution referred to in Article 58(1) of Regulation (EU) No 1305/2013 shall be determined for each programme, within the ceilings established by Annex 1 to Regulation (EU) No 1305/2013. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ca8ab8f4ee0c6f6e056c677561405cc9]
