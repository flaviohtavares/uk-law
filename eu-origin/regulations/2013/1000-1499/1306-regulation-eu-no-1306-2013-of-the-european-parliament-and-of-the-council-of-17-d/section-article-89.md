## Article 89. Other checks and penalties related to marketing rules

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. The relevant authority[^key-1f75162436a46aebe30f1ebda204cd06] shall take measures to ensure that the products referred to in Article 119(1) of Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013 which are not labelled in conformity with the provisions of that Regulation are not placed on, or are withdrawn from, the market.

In this paragraph and in paragraph 4, ‘relevant authority’ means:[^key-ddca2fde7564b3704e1a8763eb938c77]

- (a)) in England, the Secretary of State;
- (b)) in Wales, the Welsh Ministers;
- (c)) in Scotland, the Scottish Ministers.

2. Without prejudice to any specific provisions in[^key-34e01aceb7e3b71d9bcd9be4fc34ed5b] assimilated direct[^key-8dc75431e6665a4fcd8ad7728ce6ed5e] legislation, imports into the United Kingdom[^key-c77812116f4d495903ca413368036b6e] of the products specified in points (a) and (b) of paragraph 1 of Article 189 of Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013 shall be subject to checks to determine whether the conditions provided for in paragraph 1 of that Article are met.

3.[^c000001] The relevant authority[^key-dc59bbf0564e75dc010c38034ae5d4ad] shall carry out checks, based on a risk analysis, in order to verify whether products referred to in Annex I to Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013 conform to the rules laid down in Section I of Chapter I of Title II of Part II of Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013 and shall apply administrative penalties as appropriate.

4. Without prejudice to provisions regarding the wine sector made under, or adopted on the basis of, Article 64, in the event of infringement of rules in the wine sector, the relevant authority shall apply penalties in accordance with the law applying in the constituent nation.[^key-fb0b04dddf7d075547241494d4a541e5] Such penalties shall not apply in the cases set out in points (a) to (d) of Article 64(2) and where the non-compliance is of a minor nature.

5. In order to protect [^key-39e22a671b9cb43f6b4d4443df948406]... the identity, provenance and quality of wine originating in Great Britain[^key-41b7cad0ad43871c16915f9efe7a849a], the Secretary of State may make regulations[^key-b145c57c2056c281a4f83d36ba73ac43] relating to:

- (a) the establishment of an analytical databank of isotopic data to help detect fraud to be constructed on the basis of samples collected by the relevant authority[^key-b5b6a152e43fd0d695f9d6ab2a962667];
- (b) rules on control bodies and the mutual assistance between them;
- (c)[^key-777a44ce85f2f6362cfc537a4e762658] rules on the use of findings.

6. The Secretary of State may make regulations[^key-39932bee736388f2766e92f5cc56388f] laying down all measures necessary for:

- (a) the procedures relating to [^key-e4dc71f63164f0022cc01b7a665a66d3]... the analytical databank of isotopic data that will help detect fraud;
- (b) the procedures relating to cooperation and assistance between control authorities and bodies;
- (c) as regards the obligation referred to in paragraph 3, rules for performing the checks of compliance with marketing standards, rules on the authorities responsible for performing the checks, as well as on the content, the frequency and the marketing stage to which those checks are to apply.

[^key-4bb4af8b836cc66a4b833297bbac526d]...

7.[^key-857acc4637d7686bceb4152454352060] The Secretary of State may not make regulations under paragraph 5 or 6 without the consent of the relevant authorities for Wales and Scotland.

8. Where the relevant authority for Wales or Scotland requests that the Secretary of State makes regulations under paragraph 5 or 6, the Secretary of State must have regard to that request.
