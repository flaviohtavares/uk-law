## Article 111. Publication of beneficiaries

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. The relevant authority[^c-941d2a7e16a21b06c4ea823f9fdc5216] shall *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1143ae053cb860f75a7b1fc1a21ccd5] may ensure annual ex-post publication of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b585582f7018cf150d22c7973f513936] publish a list of beneficiaries of direct payments[^c-1c325fd928c1961b1008655704202c6c] agricultural support[^c-e2d597de57fd32f0c6b5aafc496c743a] annually[^c-7438c0a35a7f6e5de109735b80561aef]. The publication shall *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1143ae053cb860f75a7b1fc1a21ccd5]may contain:

- (a) without prejudice to the first paragraph of Article 112 of this Regulation, the name of the beneficiary, as follows:
  - ((i)) the first name and the surname where the beneficiary is a natural person;
  - ((ii)) the full legal name as registered where the beneficiary is a legal person with the autonomous legal personality pursuant to the legislation applying in the constituent nation[^c-40f2493c73ae45a95cb2def1a2182836];
  - ((iii)) the full name of the association as registered or otherwise officially recognised where the beneficiary is an association without an own legal personality;
- (b) the municipality where the beneficiary is resident or is registered and, where available, the postal code or the part thereof identifying the municipality;
- (c) the amounts of payment corresponding to each measure under which direct payment support is granted, which is[^c-7eaa01e85b055b5158ac5a23278a1c65] under which agricultural support is granted[^c-2a6c48bd7aa7a277c4aa72da88b15040] received by each beneficiary in the financial year concerned;
- (d) the nature and the description of the measures under which direct payment support is granted[^c-698fb4f88e114e626b97127703f14949] under which agricultural support is granted[^c-ba60d0a0de831b63c7186187429ed9ee] and under which the payment referred to in point (c) is awarded.

The information referred to in the first subparagraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f617021d29161d336158040ba0478b23] shall *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1143ae053cb860f75a7b1fc1a21ccd5] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f617021d29161d336158040ba0478b23] may *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f617021d29161d336158040ba0478b23] be made available on a single website *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f617021d29161d336158040ba0478b23] for the United Kingdom administered jointly by the relevant authorities[^c-88539bef8a8785212215428e4439c52b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f617021d29161d336158040ba0478b23]. It *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f617021d29161d336158040ba0478b23] shall *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1143ae053cb860f75a7b1fc1a21ccd5] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f617021d29161d336158040ba0478b23] may *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f617021d29161d336158040ba0478b23] remain available for two years from the date of the initial publication. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f617021d29161d336158040ba0478b23]

2.[^c-7ddb760733a47725711cb20a1530a4e8] As regards the payments corresponding to the measures *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7ddb760733a47725711cb20a1530a4e8] under which support for rural development is granted[^c-86afd9aba44211626698682d44469e76] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7ddb760733a47725711cb20a1530a4e8] as referred to in point (c) of the first subparagraph of paragraph 1, the amounts to be published shall correspond to the total public funding *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7ddb760733a47725711cb20a1530a4e8] [^c-c9e845c974129824af3580688434333a]....
