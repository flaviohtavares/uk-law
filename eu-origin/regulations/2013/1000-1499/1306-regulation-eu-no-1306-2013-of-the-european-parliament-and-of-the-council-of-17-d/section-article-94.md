## Article 94 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note). Obligations (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note) of Member States (affected by repeal, see note) (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note) relating to good agricultural and environmental condition (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e][^key-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e][^key-500a82f984d76082beec579196d3400d][^key-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e][^key-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The relevant authority[^key-71d81b83981d8a4d5193dc570ab7d556] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e] shall ensure that all agricultural area, including land which is no longer used for production purposes, is maintained in good agricultural and environmental condition. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e] The minimum standards for beneficiaries for good agricultural and environmental conditions of land are as set out in the legislation applying in the constituent nation.[^key-b90077d0ccb4d7a4d7ab3952e462bc6a] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e]

The relevant authority[^key-c1507a31f9f04f9d86a6e3fa868270f9] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e] shall not define minimum requirements which are not established in Annex II. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e]
