## Article 75. Payment to beneficiaries[^M_E_5e78b0b1-accb-4aad-a574-70cda2a2542d]

*Extent: E*

1. The payments under the support schemes and the measures referred to in Article 67(2) shall be made within any deadlines specified by the Secretary of State.[^c-d55cbe5b34a7e0fa6d882a7aa356ac85]

[^c-f420d5523ac67471e4355de1c1ff4184]...

Notwithstanding the[^c000004] first subparagraph[^c-8ba0312c80ccbea01e12406160876a67] of this paragraph, the relevant authority[^c-edbfec3e0b0e4296aa85bf27e5ffddec] may:

- (a)[^c-f445ea87b7a9f8c9535d5c822eb69a0f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) [^c-9b862a005fdcb8d51e349a6c011b0b6d]... pay advances of up to 75 % for the support granted under rural development as referred to in Article 67(2).

With regard to support granted under rural development, as referred to in Article 67(2), the first subparagraph of this paragraph shall apply in respect of the aid applications or payment claims submitted from claim year 2019.

2. Where a payment referred to in paragraph 1 is made in multiple instalments, the final instalment of such payment[^c-a33b761f539a5ed5a2869c77e7f4fade][^c-70f8a5bee16ddd1004936d858c1f1403]... shall not be made before the verification of eligibility conditions, to be carried out by the relevant authority[^c-e5873aaa578975d4f630f5732f430d88] pursuant to Article 74, has been finalised.

Advances[^c-719fe945e721d0ac857e7eea4a5aa2a2] for support granted under rural development as referred to in Article 67(2) may be paid after the administrative checks pursuant to Article 59(1) have been finalised.

3. In the event of an emergency, the appropriate authority may make regulations[^c-1bd1c7c69e4195a051dc8f6a7a6dee38] which are both necessary and justifiable, in order to resolve specific problems in relation to the application of this Article. Such regulations[^c-f2f3ab2190169bc2fec5d3791764b0a7] may derogate from paragraphs 1 and 2, but only to the extent that, and for such a period, as is strictly necessary.

## Article 75. Payment to beneficiaries[^M_E_01105225-71cd-43ea-b9e9-ad54e09c2a6a]

*Extent: W*

1. The payments under the support schemes and the measures referred to in Article 67(2) shall be made within the period from 1 December to 30 June of the following calendar year or in cases where it has not been possible to complete the verification of eligibility conditions by 30 June, upon completion.[^V_W_78f4939d-c8ac-9a3f-bf0b-2dd8e66f4418]

Payments shall be made in a maximum of two instalments within that period.

Notwithstanding the first and second subparagraphs of this paragraph,[^V_W_2ddb28d9-4697-df24-80a3-cf017a40ffc6] the relevant authority[^V_W_60a06fba-7876-690b-7c73-27fbbee89e5a] may:

- (a)[^V_W_c5752c69-0c58-d9ea-9ccf-134addbfb10d] prior to 1 December *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^V_W_c5752c69-0c58-d9ea-9ccf-134addbfb10d] [^V_W_dce20428-6506-e0c2-2109-2b128161c93a]..., pay advances *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^V_W_c5752c69-0c58-d9ea-9ccf-134addbfb10d] [^V_W_bf6f1b72-ffae-3c38-1206-49f6d3efc1ef]... for direct payments *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^V_W_c5752c69-0c58-d9ea-9ccf-134addbfb10d] before the verification of eligibility conditions, to be carried out by the relevant authority pursuant to Article 74, has been finalised[^V_W_d85e04fb-eec1-bf94-dc54-45c9ac07e6f0] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^V_W_c5752c69-0c58-d9ea-9ccf-134addbfb10d];
- (b) prior to 1 December, pay advances of up to 75 % for the support granted under rural development as referred to in Article 67(2).

With regard to support granted under rural development, as referred to in Article 67(2), the first and second subparagraphs of this paragraph shall apply in respect of the aid applications or payment claims submitted from claim year 2019.

2. Payments referred to in the paragraph 1 (excluding advances for direct payments under paragraph 1(a))[^V_W_8028e33f-8e68-44dd-de00-7b0a72049eeb] shall not be made before the verification of eligibility conditions, to be carried out by the relevant authority[^V_W_b713ff68-bf92-c777-0e54-3fe8f75fe55b] pursuant to Article 74, has been finalised.

By way of derogation from the first subparagraph, advances for support granted under rural development as referred to in Article 67(2) may be paid after the administrative checks pursuant to Article 59(1) have been finalised.

3. In the event of an emergency, the appropriate authority may make regulations[^V_W_9a321a9e-db1f-4996-ebdd-6b96f3064b8d] which are both necessary and justifiable, in order to resolve specific problems in relation to the application of this Article. Such regulations[^V_W_0a0a25b3-4ef8-5866-b3a8-2a5010c30dc2] may derogate from paragraphs 1 and 2, but only to the extent that, and for such a period, as is strictly necessary.
