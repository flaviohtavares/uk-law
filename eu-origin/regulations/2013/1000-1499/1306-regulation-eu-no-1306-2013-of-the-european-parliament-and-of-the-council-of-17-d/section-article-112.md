# Regulation (EU) No 1306/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 December 2013 on the financing, management and monitoring of the common agricultural policy and repealing Council Regulations (EEC) No 352/78, (EC) No 165/94, (EC) No 2799/98, (EC) No 814/2000, (EC) No 1290/2005 and (EC) No 485/2008, section Article 112

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (32).** Text as at 2025-06-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/eur/2013/1306
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 112. Threshold[^M_E_b7e00c4c-55b2-470f-af22-3cda0f2f8c7d]

*Extent: S+N.I.*

The relevant authority shall not publish the name of a beneficiary as provided for in point (a) of the first subparagraph of Article 111(1) of this Regulation where the amount of aid from direct payment support and[^c-34b0779f2b22adb5c2476b9a3b03c505] agricultural support[^c-6833b8d6af9c283cd4ad11a8c57fbb4b] received in one year by a beneficiary is equal to or less than EUR 1250.

The relevant authority shall not publish the name of a beneficiary as provided for in point (a) of the first subparagraph of Article 111(1) of this Regulation where the amount of aid received in one year by a beneficiary is equal to or less than EUR 1250.[^c-2afb3588184df93d10dc90449fd13d10]

Where the first paragraph of this Article applies the relevant authority[^c-78706e243a99a806ebbe42dddece1aa0] shall publish the information referred to in points (b), (c) and (d) of the first subparagraph of Article 111(1) and the beneficiary shall be identified by a code. The relevant authority[^c-7c70ddaebbd406d79d7d8a387b10d346] shall decide on the form of that code.

## Article 112. Threshold[^M_E_34t7e0u5-2pf3-le5j-0gw6-6634b282e3cde]

*Extent: W*

The relevant authority shall not publish the name of a beneficiary as provided for in point (a) of the first subparagraph of Article 111(1) of this Regulation where the amount of aid from direct payment support and[^V_W_71a70481-9142-e4c7-bdea-9f5e7c1d7aaa] received in one year by a beneficiary is equal to or less than £1250.[^V_W_217cef64-4ce9-ecee-ef09-518c5886abf9].

The relevant authority shall not publish the name of a beneficiary as provided for in point (a) of the first subparagraph of Article 111(1) of this Regulation where the amount of aid received in one year by a beneficiary is equal to or less than[^V_W_a34c1513-616e-ff51-0a10-27f1b30af8e0] EUR 1250 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8064e0f3d20e0c1436d3e8ba6071846a] £1250.

Where the first paragraph of this Article applies the relevant authority[^V_W_3a12559d-3323-3e3d-dd95-b8c2a7d78d6c] shall publish the information referred to in points (b), (c) and (d) of the first subparagraph of Article 111(1) and the beneficiary shall be identified by a code. The relevant authority[^V_W_05ee1b9b-50dd-c746-9182-02eb3ab90953] shall decide on the form of that code.

## Article 112. Threshold[^V_E_62c8b17f-4121-34d0-c149-1b63b9d55c72]

*Extent: E*

The relevant authority shall not publish the name of a beneficiary as provided for in point (a) of the first subparagraph of Article 111(1) of this Regulation where the amount of aid from direct payment support and[^V_E_6e6b5d47-c9b8-a3d1-b96f-4cc05e67dbd1] agricultural support[^V_E_d7a73d76-5787-a29b-de5a-76d972d69e0b] received in one year by a beneficiary is equal to or less than EUR 1250.

The relevant authority[^V_E_352e4d4b-1804-cabc-0a59-93c61345acfc] may[^c-a6d60003de44fac87ca4c12bc0e1bf7c] not publish the name of a beneficiary as provided for in point (a) of the first subparagraph of Article 111(1) of this Regulation where the amount of aid received in one year by a beneficiary is equal to or less than EUR 1250.

Where the first paragraph of this Article applies the relevant authority[^V_E_22e8fdcd-874c-2606-881b-86f301e30ea1] shall publish the information referred to in points (b), (c) and (d) of the first subparagraph of Article 111(1) and the beneficiary shall be identified by a code. The relevant authority[^V_E_8df60dc6-d54f-b9f3-dfb6-90f0d0f1be04] shall decide on the form of that code.
