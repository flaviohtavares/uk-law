## Article 96 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note). Checks of cross-compliance (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e][^key-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note).[^key-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e] The relevant authority[^key-bcb5fca8c8b358ba0d6e134e9adc1786] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e] shall make use, where appropriate, of the integrated system laid down in Chapter II of Title V and in particular of points (a), (b), (d), (e) and (f) of Article 68(1). *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e]

The relevant authority *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e] may make use of their existing administration and control systems to ensure compliance with the rules on cross-compliance. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e]

Those systems, and notably the system for the identification and registration of animals set up *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e] in the constituent nation[^key-8d1703710f01cffeb7d478b4ccd8769a] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e] in accordance with Council Directive 2008/71/EC *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e][^f00040] and Regulations (EC) No 1760/2000 and (EC) No 21/2004, shall be compatible with the integrated system referred to in Chapter II of Title V of this Regulation. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e]

2 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note).[^key-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e] Depending on the requirements, standards, acts or areas of cross-compliance in question, *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e] the relevant authority[^key-070a1292fbcba5d990b7cb3a1507aa5d] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e] may decide to carry out administrative checks, in particular those already provided for under the control systems applicable to the respective requirement, standard, act or area of cross-compliance. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e]

3 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note).[^key-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e] The relevant authority[^key-4be3d79bde9e86b089b258aeb9616cbd] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e] shall carry out on-the-spot checks to verify whether a beneficiary complies with the obligations laid down in this Title. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e]

4 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note).[^key-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e] The appropriate authority may make regulations[^key-a4c3fb0837643144729a122c12022eb9] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e], laying down rules on the carrying out of checks in order to verify compliance with the obligations referred to under this Title, including rules allowing that risk analysis takes account of the following factors: *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e]

- (a (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e] a farmer's participation in the farm advisory system as provided for in Title III of this Regulation; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e]
- (b (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e] a farmer's participation in a certification system, if it covers the requirements and standards concerned. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-16008c6846e55803ae32449f90d0a84e]
