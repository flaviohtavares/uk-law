# Regulation (EU) No 1303/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 December 2013 laying down common provisions on the European Regional Development Fund, the European Social Fund, the Cohesion Fund, the European Agricultural Fund for Rural Development and the European Maritime and Fisheries Fund and laying down general provisions on the European Regional Development Fund, the European Social Fund, the Cohesion Fund and the European Maritime and Fisheries Fund and repealing Council Regulation (EC) No 1083/2006, section Article 49

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 49 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Functions of the monitoring committee[^key-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note).[^key-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0] The monitoring committee shall meet at least once a year and shall review implementation of the programme and progress made towards achieving its objectives. In doing so, it shall have regard to the financial data, common and programme-specific indicators, including changes in the value of result indicators and progress towards quantified target values *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0] [^key-da6d93ae757a7364b46a8633dc7caae8]... and, where relevant, the results of qualitative analyses. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0]

2 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note).[^c000002][^key-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0] The monitoring committee shall examine all issues that affect the performance of the programme, including the conclusions of the performance review. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0]

3 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note).[^key-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0] The monitoring committee shall be consulted and shall, if it considers it to be appropriate, give an opinion on any amendment of the programme proposed by the managing authority. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0]

4 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note).[^c000001][^key-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0] The monitoring committee may make observations to the managing authority regarding implementation and evaluation of the programme including actions related to the reduction of the administrative burden on beneficiaries. It may also make observations on the visibility of support *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0] for rural development[^key-00426ece0134040ca31a013a299c05f8] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0] and on raising awareness about the results of such support. It shall monitor actions taken as a result of its observations. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0]
