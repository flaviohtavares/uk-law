# Regulation (EU) No 1303/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 December 2013 laying down common provisions on the European Regional Development Fund, the European Social Fund, the Cohesion Fund, the European Agricultural Fund for Rural Development and the European Maritime and Fisheries Fund and laying down general provisions on the European Regional Development Fund, the European Social Fund, the Cohesion Fund and the European Maritime and Fisheries Fund and repealing Council Regulation (EC) No 1083/2006, section Article 138

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-06-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/eur/2013/1303
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 138 (affected by repeal, see note). Submission of information (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4][^c-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For each year from 2016 until and including 2025, Member States shall submit, by the deadline set out in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4] Article 63(5), and the second subparagraph of Article 63(7), of the Financial Regulation[^c000001] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4], the documents referred to in that Article namely: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]

- ((a)) the accounts, referred to in Article 137(1) of this Regulation, for the preceding accounting year; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]
- ((b)) the management declaration and the annual summary referred to in point (e) of the first subparagraph of Article 125(4) of this Regulation, for the preceding accounting year; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]
- ((c)) the audit opinion and the control report referred to in points (a) and (b) of the first subparagraph of Article 127(5) of this Regulation, for the preceding accounting year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]
