1 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note).[^key-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0] The managing authority shall send to the *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0] Programme Monitoring Committee[^key-947ecd71481f1ea5fe3c6816581cf175] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0] a specific report covering the operations comprising financial instruments as an annex to the annual implementation report. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0]

2 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note).[^key-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0] The specific report referred to in paragraph 1 shall include, for each financial instrument, the following information: *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0] identification of the programme and of the priority or measure from which support *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0] for rural development[^key-efc91009e5514dc65ee9e5052cceec47] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0] is provided; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0] description of the financial instrument and implementation arrangements; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0]
- (c (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c000001][^key-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0] identification of the bodies implementing financial instruments, and the bodies implementing funds of funds where applicable, as referred to under points (a), (b) and (c) of Article 38(1); *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0]
- (d (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0] total amount of programme contributions by priority or measure paid to the financial instrument; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0]
- (e (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0] total amount of support paid to the final recipients or to the benefit of final recipients, or committed in guarantee contracts by the financial instrument for investments in final recipients, as well as management costs incurred or management fees paid, by programme and priority or measure; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0]
- (f (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0] the performance of the financial instrument including progress in its set-up and in selection of bodies implementing the financial instrument, including the body implementing a fund of funds; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0]
- (g (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0] interest and other gains generated by support *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0] for rural development *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0] to the financial instrument and programme resources paid back to financial instruments from investments as referred to in Articles 43 and 44 and amounts used for differentiated treatment as referred to in Article 43a; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0]
- (h (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0] progress in achieving the expected leverage effect of investments made by the financial instrument; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0]
- (i (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0] the value of equity investments, with respect to previous years; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0]
- (j (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0] contribution of the financial instrument to the achievement of the indicators of the priority or measure concerned. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0]

The information in points (h) and (j) of the first subparagraph may be included only in the annex to the annual implementation reports submitted in 2017 and 2019 as well as in the final implementation report. The reporting obligations set out in points (a) to (j) of the first subparagraph shall not be applied at the level of final recipients. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0]
