# Regulation (EU) No 1303/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 December 2013 laying down common provisions on the European Regional Development Fund, the European Social Fund, the Cohesion Fund, the European Agricultural Fund for Rural Development and the European Maritime and Fisheries Fund and laying down general provisions on the European Regional Development Fund, the European Social Fund, the Cohesion Fund and the European Maritime and Fisheries Fund and repealing Council Regulation (EC) No 1083/2006, section Article 125

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-06-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/eur/2013/1303
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 125 (affected by repeal, see note). Functions of the managing authority (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4][^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. The managing authority shall be responsible for managing the operational programme in accordance with the principle of sound financial management. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]

2. As regards the management of the operational programme, the managing authority shall: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]

- (a) support the work of the monitoring committee referred to in Article 47 and provide it with the information it requires to carry out its tasks, in particular data relating to the progress of the operational programme in achieving its objectives, financial data and data relating to indicators and milestones; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4efda85c54380f24a7be55e7a744084a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]
- (b) draw up and, after approval by the monitoring committee, submit to the Commission annual and final implementation reports referred to in Article 50; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-42c38865ce3a1042240022eb67c1d3dd] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]
- (c) make available to intermediate bodies and beneficiaries information that is relevant to the execution of their tasks and the implementation of operations respectively; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]
- (d) establish a system to record and store in computerised form data on each operation necessary for monitoring, evaluation, financial management, verification and audit, including data on individual participants in operations, where applicable; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]
- (e) ensure that the data referred to in point (d) is collected, entered and stored in the system referred to in point (d), and that data on indicators is broken down by gender where required by Annexes I and II of the ESF Regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]

3. As regards the selection of operations, the managing authority shall: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]

- (a) draw up and, once approved, apply appropriate selection procedures and criteria that: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]
  - ((i)) ensure the contribution of operations to the achievement of the specific objectives and results of the relevant priority; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]
  - ((ii)) are non-discriminatory and transparent; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]
  - ((iii)) take into account the general principles set out in Articles 7 and 8; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]
- (b)[^c000002] ensure that a selected operation falls within the scope of the EMFF, the Fund or Funds concerned and can be attributed to a category of intervention or, in the case of the EMFF, a measure identified in the priority or priorities of the operational programme; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]
- (c)[^c000001] ensure that the beneficiary is provided with a document setting out the conditions for support for each operation including the specific requirements concerning the products or services to be delivered under the operation, the financing plan, the time limit for execution, as well as the requirements regarding information, communication and visibility; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]
- (d) satisfy itself that the beneficiary has the administrative, financial and operational capacity to fulfil the conditions referred to in point (c) before approval of the operation; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]
- (e) satisfy itself that, where the operation has started before the submission of an application for funding to the managing authority, applicable law relevant for the operation has been complied with; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]
- (f) ensure that operations selected for support from the Funds or the EMFF do not include activities which were part of an operation which has been or should have been subject to a procedure of recovery in accordance with Article 71 following the relocation of a productive activity outside the programme area; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]
- (g) determine the categories of intervention or, in the case of the EMFF, the measures to which the expenditure of an operation shall be attributed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]

4. As regards the financial management and control of the operational programme, the managing authority shall: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]

- (a) verify that the co-financed products and services have been delivered, that the operation complies with applicable law, the operational programme and the conditions for support of the operation and: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]
  - ((i)) where costs are to be reimbursed pursuant to point (a) of the first subparagraph of Article 67(1), that the amount of expenditure declared by the beneficiaries in relation to those costs has been paid; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]
  - ((ii)) in the case of costs reimbursed pursuant to points (b) to (e) of the first subparagraph of Article 67(1), that the conditions for reimbursement of expenditure to the beneficiary have been met; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]
- (b) ensure that beneficiaries involved in the implementation of operations reimbursed on the basis of eligible costs actually incurred maintain either a separate accounting system or an adequate accounting code for all transactions relating to an operation; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]
- (c) put in place effective and proportionate anti-fraud measures taking into account the risks identified; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]
- (d) set up procedures to ensure that all documents regarding expenditure and audits required to ensure an adequate audit trail are held in accordance with the requirements of point (g) of Article 72; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]
- (e) draw up the management declaration and annual summary referred to in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4] points (a) and (b) of Article 63(5) and Article 63(6) and (7) of the Financial Regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4].

By way of derogation from point (a) of the first subparagraph, the ETC Regulation may establish specific rules on verification applicable to cooperation programmes. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]

5. Verifications pursuant to point (a) of the first subparagraph of paragraph 4 shall include the following procedures: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]

- (a) administrative verifications in respect of each application for reimbursement by beneficiaries; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]
- (b) on-the-spot verifications of operations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]

The frequency and coverage of the on-the-spot verifications shall be proportionate to the amount of public support to an operation and to the level of risk identified by such verifications and audits by the audit authority for the management and control system as a whole. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]

6. On-the-spot verifications of individual operations pursuant to point (b) of the first subparagraph of paragraph 5 may be carried out on a sample basis. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]

7. Where the managing authority is also a beneficiary under the operational programme, arrangements for the verifications referred to in point (a) of the first subparagraph of paragraph 4 shall ensure adequate separation of functions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]

8. The Commission shall be empowered to adopt delegated acts, in accordance with Article 149, laying down rules specifying the information in relation to the data to be recorded and stored in computerised form within the monitoring system established under point (d) of paragraph 2 of this Article. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]

The Commission shall adopt implementing acts laying down the technical specifications of the system established under point (d) of paragraph 2 of this Article. Those implementing acts shall be adopted in accordance with the examination procedure referred to in Article 150(3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]

9. The Commission shall be empowered to adopt delegated acts, in accordance with Article 149, laying down the detailed minimum requirements for the audit trail referred to in point (d) of the first subparagraph of paragraph 4 of this Article in respect of the accounting records to be maintained and the supporting documents to be held at the level of the certifying authority, managing authority, intermediate bodies and beneficiaries. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]

10. The Commission shall, in order to ensure uniform conditions on the implementation of this Article, adopt implementing acts concerning the model for the management declaration referred to in point (e) of the first subparagraph of paragraph 4 of this Article. Those implementing acts shall be adopted in accordance with the advisory procedure referred to in Article 150(2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]
