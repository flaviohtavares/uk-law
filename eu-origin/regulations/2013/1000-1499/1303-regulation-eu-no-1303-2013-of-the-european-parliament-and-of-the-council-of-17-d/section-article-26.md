# Regulation (EU) No 1303/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 December 2013 laying down common provisions on the European Regional Development Fund, the European Social Fund, the Cohesion Fund, the European Agricultural Fund for Rural Development and the European Maritime and Fisheries Fund and laying down general provisions on the European Regional Development Fund, the European Social Fund, the Cohesion Fund and the European Maritime and Fisheries Fund and repealing Council Regulation (EC) No 1083/2006, section Article 26

## Article 26 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Preparation of programmes (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-89dc04f895112ad63c0e89827e8679a9][^key-45f0588b8a671a076579a96026205fe8][^key-9202ae8b3b1d89c905a530aad59f19a91][^key-45f0588b8a671a076579a96026205fe8]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

Support for rural development must be implemented through programmes. Each programmes shall cover the period from 1 January 2014 to 31 December 2020. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-45f0588b8a671a076579a96026205fe8]
