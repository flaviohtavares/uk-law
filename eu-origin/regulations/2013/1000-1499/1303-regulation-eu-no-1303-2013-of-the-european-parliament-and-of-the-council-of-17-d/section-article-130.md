# Regulation (EU) No 1303/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 December 2013 laying down common provisions on the European Regional Development Fund, the European Social Fund, the Cohesion Fund, the European Agricultural Fund for Rural Development and the European Maritime and Fisheries Fund and laying down general provisions on the European Regional Development Fund, the European Social Fund, the Cohesion Fund and the European Maritime and Fisheries Fund and repealing Council Regulation (EC) No 1083/2006, section Article 130

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-06-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/eur/2013/1303
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 130 (affected by repeal, see note). Common rules for calculating interim payments and payment of the final balance (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4][^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. The Commission shall reimburse as interim payments 90 % of the amount resulting from applying the co-financing rate for each priority, laid down in the decision adopting the operational programme, to the eligible expenditure for the priority included in the payment application. The Commission shall determine the remaining amounts to be reimbursed as interim payments or to be recovered in accordance with Article 139. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]

2.[^c000002] The contribution from the Funds or the EMFF to a priority through the interim payments and payment of the final balance shall not be higher than: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]

- (a) the eligible public expenditure indicated in the payment applications for the priority; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]
- (b) the contribution from the Funds or the EMFF for the priority laid down in the decision of the Commission approving the operational programme, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]

whichever is the lower. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]

3.[^c000007] By way of derogation from paragraph 2, the contribution from the Funds or the EMFF through payments of the final balance for each priority per Fund and per category of regions in the final accounting year shall not exceed, by more than 10 %, the contribution from the Funds or the EMFF for each priority per Fund and per category of regions as laid down in the decision of the Commission approving the operational programme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]

The contribution from the Funds or the EMFF through payments of the final balance in the final accounting year shall not exceed the eligible public expenditure declared or the contribution from each Fund and category of regions to each operational programme as laid down in the decision of the Commission approving the operational programme, whichever is the lower. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]
