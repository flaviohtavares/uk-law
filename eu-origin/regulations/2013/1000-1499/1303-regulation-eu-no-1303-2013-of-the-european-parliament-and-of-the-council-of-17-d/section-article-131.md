# Regulation (EU) No 1303/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 December 2013 laying down common provisions on the European Regional Development Fund, the European Social Fund, the Cohesion Fund, the European Agricultural Fund for Rural Development and the European Maritime and Fisheries Fund and laying down general provisions on the European Regional Development Fund, the European Social Fund, the Cohesion Fund and the European Maritime and Fisheries Fund and repealing Council Regulation (EC) No 1083/2006, section Article 131

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-06-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/eur/2013/1303
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 131 (affected by repeal, see note). Payment applications (affected by repeal, see note)[^c000001][^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4][^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. Payment applications shall include, for each priority: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]

- (a) the total amount of eligible expenditure incurred by beneficiaries and paid in implementing operations, as entered in the accounting system of the certifying authority; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]
- (b) the total amount of public expenditure incurred in implementing operations, as entered in the accounting system of the certifying authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]

With regard to the amounts to be included in payment applications for the form of support referred to in point (e) of the first subparagraph of Article 67(1), the payment applications shall include the elements set out in the delegated acts adopted in accordance with Article 67(5a) and shall use the model for payment applications set out in the implementing acts adopted in accordance with paragraph 6 of this Article. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]

2. Eligible expenditure included in a payment application shall be supported by receipted invoices or accounting documents of equivalent probative value, except for the forms of support referred to in points (b) to (e) of the first subparagraph of Article 67(1) of this Regulation, Articles 68, 68a and 68b of this Regulation, Article 69(1) of this Regulation and Article 109 of this Regulation and in Article 14 of the ESF Regulation. For those forms of support, the amounts included in a payment application shall be the costs calculated on the applicable basis. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]

3. In the case of State aid, the public contribution corresponding to the expenditure included in a payment application shall have been paid to the beneficiaries by the body granting the aid or, where Member States have decided that the beneficiary is the body granting the aid pursuant to point (10)(a) of Article 2, paid by the beneficiary to the body receiving the aid. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]

4. By way of derogation from paragraph 1 of this Article, in the case of State aid, the payment application may include advances paid to the beneficiary by the body granting the aid or, where Member States have decided that the beneficiary is the body granting the aid pursuant to point (10)(a) of Article 2, paid by the beneficiary to the body receiving the aid, under the following cumulative conditions: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]

- (a) those advances are subject to a guarantee provided by a bank or other financial institution established in the Member State or are covered by a facility provided as a guarantee by a public entity or by the Member State; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]
- (b) those advances do not exceed 40 % of the total amount of the aid to be granted to a beneficiary for a given operation or, where Member States have decided that the beneficiary is the body granting the aid pursuant to point (10)(a) of Article 2, of the total amount of the aid to be granted to the body receiving the aid as part of a given operation; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]
- (c) those advances are covered by expenditure paid by the beneficiary or, where Member States have decided that the beneficiary is the body granting the aid pursuant to point (10)(a) of Article 2, expenditure paid by the body receiving the aid in implementing the operation, and supported by receipted invoices or accounting documents of equivalent probative value within three years of the year of the payment of the advance or on 31 December 2023, whichever is earlier. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]

Where the conditions set out in point (c) of the first subparagraph are not met, the next payment application shall be corrected accordingly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]

5. Each payment application which includes advances of the type referred to in paragraph 4 of this Article shall separately disclose: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]

- (a) the total amount paid from the operational programme as advances; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]
- (b) the amount which, within three years of the payment of the advance in accordance with point (c) of the first subparagraph of paragraph 4, has been covered by expenditure paid by the beneficiary or, where Member States have decided that the beneficiary is the body granting the aid pursuant to point (10)(a) of Article 2, by the body receiving the aid; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]
- (c) the amount which has not been covered by expenditure paid by the beneficiary or, where Member States have decided that the beneficiary is the body granting the aid pursuant to point (10)(a) of Article 2, by the body receiving the aid, and for which the three year period has not yet elapsed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]

6. The Commission shall, in order to ensure uniform conditions for the implementation of this Article, adopt implementing acts laying down the model for payment applications. Those implementing acts shall be adopted in accordance with the examination procedure referred to in Article 150(3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b676935048cfd925335e1c66c82fd0d4]
