# Regulation (EU) No 1303/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 December 2013 laying down common provisions on the European Regional Development Fund, the European Social Fund, the Cohesion Fund, the European Agricultural Fund for Rural Development and the European Maritime and Fisheries Fund and laying down general provisions on the European Regional Development Fund, the European Social Fund, the Cohesion Fund and the European Maritime and Fisheries Fund and repealing Council Regulation (EC) No 1083/2006, section Article 80

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-06-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/eur/2013/1303
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 80 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Use of the euro[^M_E_bc41fac2-ba8b-486d-b02e-7bc3ffc02749][^c-baf650bfb9dced042c98eadcf1cd7c41][^c-72cf4f981c2ba4e34614775056d25da4]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

Amounts set out in programmes relating to support for rural development, forecasts of expenditure, payment applications, accounts and expenditure mentioned in the annual and final implementation reports shall be denominated in euros. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72cf4f981c2ba4e34614775056d25da4]

## Article 80. Currency[^M_E_3c4e3ab8-b649-4ac4-cdb3-eddf6db82af7][^V_S_74393df1-5b61-4c15-c358-b2c196a18c50][^c-f828d1ab31d44877f6cad8ba1c49cffd]

*Extent: S*

Amounts set out in programmes relating to support for rural development, forecasts of expenditure, statutory reporting[^c-9a0bf4db1cf0632673c1cfde6a84967a] and expenditure mentioned in the annual and final implementation reports shall be denominated in sterling (GBP)[^c-b4997c6464fedbd80de6694e63fab979].
