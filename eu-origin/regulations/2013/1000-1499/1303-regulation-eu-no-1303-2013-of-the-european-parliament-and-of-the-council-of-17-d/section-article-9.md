# Regulation (EU) No 1303/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 December 2013 laying down common provisions on the European Regional Development Fund, the European Social Fund, the Cohesion Fund, the European Agricultural Fund for Rural Development and the European Maritime and Fisheries Fund and laying down general provisions on the European Regional Development Fund, the European Social Fund, the Cohesion Fund and the European Maritime and Fisheries Fund and repealing Council Regulation (EC) No 1083/2006, section Article 9

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 9 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Thematic objectives (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-5b4bdf7cefeb917bd7634ae1394956ca][^key-5b4bdf7cefeb917bd7634ae1394956ca]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In order to contribute to the Fund-specific missions, support for rural development must support the following thematic objectives:[^key-8903fb8848c749402213958471bf0b7d] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5b4bdf7cefeb917bd7634ae1394956ca]

- ((1)) strengthening research, technological development and innovation; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5b4bdf7cefeb917bd7634ae1394956ca]
- ((2)) enhancing access to, and use and quality of, ICT; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5b4bdf7cefeb917bd7634ae1394956ca]
- ((3)) enhancing the competitiveness of SMEs, of the agricultural sector *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5b4bdf7cefeb917bd7634ae1394956ca] (for support for rural development)[^key-782ef51b00ba9f3f5534f9389adbc86b] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5b4bdf7cefeb917bd7634ae1394956ca] and of the fishery and aquaculture sector (for the EMFF); *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5b4bdf7cefeb917bd7634ae1394956ca]
- ((4)) supporting the shift towards a low-carbon economy in all sectors; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5b4bdf7cefeb917bd7634ae1394956ca]
- ((5)) promoting climate change adaptation, risk prevention and management; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5b4bdf7cefeb917bd7634ae1394956ca]
- ((6)) preserving and protecting the environment and promoting resource efficiency; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5b4bdf7cefeb917bd7634ae1394956ca]
- ((7)) promoting sustainable transport and removing bottlenecks in key network infrastructures; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5b4bdf7cefeb917bd7634ae1394956ca]
- ((8)) promoting sustainable and quality employment and supporting labour mobility; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5b4bdf7cefeb917bd7634ae1394956ca]
- ((9)) promoting social inclusion, combating poverty and any discrimination; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5b4bdf7cefeb917bd7634ae1394956ca]
- ((10)) investing in education, training and vocational training for skills and lifelong learning; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5b4bdf7cefeb917bd7634ae1394956ca]
- ((11)) enhancing institutional capacity of public authorities and stakeholders and efficient public administration. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5b4bdf7cefeb917bd7634ae1394956ca]

Thematic objectives shall be translated into priorities that are specific to *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5b4bdf7cefeb917bd7634ae1394956ca] support for rural development[^key-e427fb5c17548abb4cc5617afac32e68] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5b4bdf7cefeb917bd7634ae1394956ca] and are set out in the Fund-specific rules. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5b4bdf7cefeb917bd7634ae1394956ca]

The priorities established for[^c000003] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5b4bdf7cefeb917bd7634ae1394956ca] support for rural development *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5b4bdf7cefeb917bd7634ae1394956ca] in the Fund-specific rules shall in particular cover the appropriate use of *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5b4bdf7cefeb917bd7634ae1394956ca] support for rural development[^key-e500bf426198825d832300b4326acec0] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5b4bdf7cefeb917bd7634ae1394956ca] in the areas of migration and asylum. In that context, coordination with the Asylum, Migration and Integration Fund established by Regulation (EU) No 516/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5b4bdf7cefeb917bd7634ae1394956ca][^f00032] shall be ensured, where appropriate. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5b4bdf7cefeb917bd7634ae1394956ca]
