# Regulation (EU) No 1303/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 December 2013 laying down common provisions on the European Regional Development Fund, the European Social Fund, the Cohesion Fund, the European Agricultural Fund for Rural Development and the European Maritime and Fisheries Fund and laying down general provisions on the European Regional Development Fund, the European Social Fund, the Cohesion Fund and the European Maritime and Fisheries Fund and repealing Council Regulation (EC) No 1083/2006, section Article 45

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-06-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/eur/2013/1303
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 45. Re-use of resources after the end of the eligibility period[^M_E_28c30c17-5423-4594-d39d-6441947b9c30][^c000002]

*Extent: E+S+N.I.*

The relevant authority[^c-624d641535778ba7cda3ce4719b0d72e] shall adopt the necessary measures to ensure that resources paid back to financial instruments, including capital repayments and gains and other earnings or yields generated during a period of at least eight years after the end of the eligibility period, which are attributable to support for rural development[^c-b839a705589900bf1b4042555bd4f8dc] to financial instruments pursuant to Article 37, are re-used in accordance with the aims of the programme or programmes, either within the same financial instrument or, following the exit of those resources from the financial instrument, in other financial instruments provided that, in both cases, an assessment of market conditions demonstrates a continuing need for such investment, or in other forms of support.

## Article 45. Re-use of resources[^M_E_e83d0d9b-33b3-42b1-881c-03fca878079c][^V_W_4b24fe13-ee38-3da9-1e82-8e7a2fecf10d][^c-ca2c43a02be9f567101e5a32fd804c8b]

*Extent: W*

The relevant authority[^V_W_a619f006-95a7-1df0-8256-de9350f1b451] shall adopt the necessary measures to ensure that resources paid back to financial instruments, including capital repayments and gains and other earnings or yields generated during a period of at least eight years [^c-3e32ef6984f7f9a50c43fdbefb653f66]..., which are attributable to support for rural development[^V_W_c1b8e75d-d504-1302-40b1-e6f639eec6fc] to financial instruments pursuant to Article 37, are re-used in accordance with the aims of the programme [^c-29bef779f457e5675960d36e16536f1a]..., either within the same financial instrument or, following the exit of those resources from the financial instrument, in other financial instruments provided that, in both cases, an assessment of market conditions demonstrates a continuing need for such investment, or in other forms of support.
