## Article 62. PPPs

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Support for rural development[^c-9bf780996068bcf8547570439a7f3dec] may be used to support PPP operations. Such PPP operations shall comply with applicable law, in particular concerning [^c-2f9a158e3f5c70059f9cae0baf0126b1]... public procurement.
