## Article 54 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). General Provisions[^c-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note).[^c-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0] Evaluations shall be carried out to improve the quality of the design and implementation of programmes, as well as to assess their effectiveness, efficiency and impact. The impact of programmes shall be evaluated, in the light of the mission of *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0] support for rural development[^c-3ba790a9dfc2e9dd36002552f1f62a31] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0], *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0] [^c-310523d8226bfbabd6a2c4d5e9b70eaf]... , having regard to the size of the programme, in relation to GDP and unemployment in the programme area concerned, where appropriate. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0]

2 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note).[^c-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0] The relevant authority[^c-4ea4a51263bef28b4a0afb5e0d1280b1] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0] shall provide the resources necessary for carrying out evaluations, and shall ensure that procedures are in place to produce and collect the data necessary for evaluations, including data related to common and where appropriate programme-specific indicators. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0]

3 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note).[^c-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0] Evaluations shall be carried out by internal or external experts that are functionally independent of the authorities responsible for programme implementation. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0] [^c-f45eea4d5e10f77850b56c28941a8588].... *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0]

4 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note).[^c-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0] All evaluations shall be made available to the public. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c6514eb875195dfd55335095e30ee7c0]
