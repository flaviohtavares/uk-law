## Article 221. Measures to resolve specific problems

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A1.[^c-7d9e26582f2c2d24de3fbd5e4c3e7a3e] This Article does not apply in relation to agricultural producers in England.

A2.[^c-85d11a00a3c929ab7076071de3620567] This Article does not apply in relation to agricultural producers in Wales.

1. The appropriate authority may make regulations[^c-b37939313fe71f580cad17d7ac9ad3ca] taking necessary and justifiable emergency measures to resolve specific problems. Those measures may derogate from the provisions of this Regulation only to an extent that is strictly necessary and for a period that is strictly necessary. [^c-4eb2a7135f431f20ac669bfc19901b9f]...

2. The appropriate authority may make regulations to[^c-21d7de5c3854161dd732770035e9a267] resolve specific problems, and on duly justified imperative grounds of urgency, relating to situations likely to cause a rapid deterioration of production and market conditions which could be difficult to address if the adoption of measures were delayed[^c-d8994145fcc8ed4a6d4d41846a6c510c]....

3. The appropriate authority may make regulations[^c-b1c39dcd7427320e70f025a97c0ecb31] under paragraph 1 [^c-19aca5b49ea8386ed11ddddd3c136287]... only if it is not possible to adopt the required emergency measures in accordance with Article 219 or 220.

4.[^c-e130683deb43200a574dda5f0f7c1860] Regulations made under paragraph 1 or 2 may remain in force for a period not exceeding 12 months.

If it appears to the appropriate authority that the specific problems which led to the making of the regulations under paragraph 1 or 2 will persist beyond the period for which they are in force, the appropriate authority may make further regulations to address the problems. Such regulations may only remain in force for a period not exceeding 12 months.

The power to make further regulations under the second subparagraph may not be exercised more than twice.
