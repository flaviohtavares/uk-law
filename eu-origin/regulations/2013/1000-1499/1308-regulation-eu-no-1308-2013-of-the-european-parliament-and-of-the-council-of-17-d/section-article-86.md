# Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 December 2013 establishing a common organisation of the markets in agricultural products and repealing Council Regulations (EEC) No 922/72, (EEC) No 234/79, (EC) No 1037/2001 and (EC) No 1234/2007, section Article 86

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-02-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/eur/2013/1308
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 86. Reservation, amendment and cancellation of optional reserved terms[^c-856b6965dbb8eb3dc14f1c3a267837e8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Article and Articles 87 and 88 do not apply in relation to products marketed in England (see section 37(1) of the Agriculture Act 2020).[^c-b9bfbef78a96e22e81e7ac4885d6b5e5]

This Article and Articles 87 and 88 do not apply in relation to products marketed in Wales[^c-c944cf316539765ba66dfbe7b9fc513b] (see section 34(1) of the Agriculture (Wales) Act 2023)[^c-82d87cfc144cf01d1803ae5dab085e99].

This Article and Articles 87 and 88 do not apply in relation to products marketed in Scotland (see section 9 of the Agriculture (Retained EU Law and Data) (Scotland) Act 2020). *(Extent: S)*[^c-b81a09feaacd373fe570a335d12c01c5]

In order to take account of the expectations of consumers, developments in scientific and technical knowledge, the situation in the market and developments in marketing standards and in international standards, the appropriate authority in England, Wales or Scotland may make regulations[^c-78e41aded014ad5a0034f77909f0b8fb]:

- ((a)) reserving an additional optional reserved term, laying down its conditions of use;
- ((b)) amending the conditions of use of an optional reserved term; or
- ((c)) cancelling an optional reserved term.
