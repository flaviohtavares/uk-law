## Article 215. ... Payments for apiculture[^M_C_95a506c9-ba9c-4194-e61f-d1fa2a9cdf9e][^key-7fcf5d72dce808b6e03aa7d2a44d7913]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The relevant authority[^key-14190db4b0ddf9fc7e0d7d110eda4efd] may make [^key-de4f1c373b84da3f29238fde61c58bf4]... payments for the protection of apiaries disadvantaged by structural or natural conditions or under economic development programmes, except for those allocated for production or trade.
