## Article 90. Special provisions for the imports of wine[^c-856b6965dbb8eb3dc14f1c3a267837e8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. Save as otherwise provided for in international agreements to which the United Kingdom is a party[^c-4f188389053cb84aa361c4a47e53c64b], the provisions concerning designation of origin and geographical indications and labelling of wine set out in Section 2 of this Chapter, and the definitions, designations and sales descriptions referred to in Article 78 of this Regulation and in regulations made under section 9 of the Agriculture (Retained EU Law and Data) (Scotland) Act 2020, insofar as they relate to wine, *(Extent: S)*[^c-f015f62245689a652136dde334e88719] shall apply to products imported into Great Britain[^c-20cf97231117cc3cf5e7fb4c55d2d6b5] and falling within CN codes 2009 61, 2009 69 and 2204.

2. Save as otherwise provided for in international agreements to which the United Kingdom is a party, products referred to in paragraph 1 of this Article shall be produced in accordance with oenological practices authorised [^c-53bcb67464fdccd786b7d0286a6a0e14]... pursuant to this Regulation or regulations made under section 9 of the Agriculture (Retained EU Law and Data) (Scotland) Act 2020, or *(Extent: S)*[^c-5eea712e610710dc620a522105b21ba1], prior to the authorisation pursuant to Article 80(3) *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-947eb86c1859ea98afcc0a8b55af7739], produced in accordance with oenological practices recommended and published by the OIV.

(2A) By way of derogation from paragraph 2, products referred to in paragraph 1:[^c-be06520b1f79a002ac2895a535a42e18]

- (a) which are produced before IP completion day, or which are still in the finishing process immediately before IP completion day, may be placed or remain on the market until stocks are exhausted provided that they are produced in accordance with oenological practices authorised pursuant to this Regulation as it had effect immediately before IP completion day;
- (b) other than products falling within point (a), which are imported into Great Britain on or before IP completion day, may be produced in accordance with oenological practices authorised pursuant to this Regulation as it had effect immediately before IP completion day.
