## Article 206. ... The application of competition rules to agriculture[^key-8f8345f250bf87aa00f5e1f20e93000c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Save as otherwise provided in this Regulation and subject, in particular, to Articles 207 to 210 of this Regulation, the Competition Act 1998 shall apply to all agreements, decisions and practices referred to in sections 2(1) and 18(1) of that Act which relate to the production of, or trade in, agricultural products.[^key-0277d132e2dfbdb7221966d0799b7c90]
