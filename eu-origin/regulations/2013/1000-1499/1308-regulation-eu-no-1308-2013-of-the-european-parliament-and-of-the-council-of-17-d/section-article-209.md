# Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 December 2013 establishing a common organisation of the markets in agricultural products and repealing Council Regulations (EEC) No 922/72, (EEC) No 234/79, (EC) No 1037/2001 and (EC) No 1234/2007, section Article 209

## Article 209. Exceptions for the objectives set out in Article 39 TFEU and farmers and their associations[^c-6cb10de3e8425e31858c74432e25db4b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. Section 2(1) of the Competition Act 1998[^c-aee9e16bb0ff2d7292a58b5ffc4430b8] shall not apply to the agreements, decisions and practices referred to in Article 206 of this Regulation necessary for the attainment of the objectives set out in Article 39 TFEU.

Section 2(1) of the Competition Act 1998[^c000005] shall not apply to agreements, decisions and concerted practices of farmers, farmers' associations, or associations of such associations, or producer organisations recognised under Article 152 or Article 161 of this Regulation, or associations of producer organisations recognised under Article 156 of this Regulation, which concern the production or sale of agricultural products or the use of joint facilities for the storage, treatment or processing of agricultural products, unless the objectives set out in Article 39 TFEU are jeopardised.

This paragraph shall not apply to agreements, decisions and concerted practices which entail an obligation to charge an identical price or by which competition is excluded.

2. Agreements, decisions and concerted practices which fulfil the conditions referred to in paragraph 1 of this Article shall not be prohibited, no prior decision to that effect being required.

However, farmers, farmers’ associations, or associations of such associations, or producer organisations recognised under Article 152 or Article 161 of this Regulation, or associations of producer organisations recognised under Article 156 of this Regulation, may request an opinion from the[^c000006] Competition and Markets Authority[^c-fcae0ceb0f2b340d67ef23390c6ca346] on the compatibility of those agreements, decisions and concerted practices with the objectives set out in Article 39 TFEU.

The Competition and Markets Authority[^c-870f742812acc35bd2998681ff249ee2] shall deal with requests for opinions promptly and shall send the applicant its opinion within four months of receipt of a complete request. The Competition and Markets Authority may [^c-617d822e6aad1fa32d747388962fe868]... change the content of an opinion, in particular if the applicant has provided inaccurate information or misused the opinion.

In any [^c-ba0c8610c9a13c1b330d6a3c30d597f9]... proceedings for the application of section 2 of the Competition Act 1998[^c-bd42003e9947998cc95cff20371a87fc], the burden of proving an infringement of section 2(1) of the Competition Act 1998[^c-897ef5e994d9846bce377b2b9f8d9231] shall rest on the party or the authority alleging the infringement. The party claiming the benefit of the exemptions provided in paragraph 1 of this Article shall bear the burden of proving that the conditions of that paragraph are fulfilled.
