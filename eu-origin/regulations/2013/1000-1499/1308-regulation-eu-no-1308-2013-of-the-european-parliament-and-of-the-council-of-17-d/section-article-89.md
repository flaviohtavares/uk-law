# Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 December 2013 establishing a common organisation of the markets in agricultural products and repealing Council Regulations (EEC) No 922/72, (EEC) No 234/79, (EC) No 1037/2001 and (EC) No 1234/2007, section Article 89

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-02-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/eur/2013/1308
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 89. General provisions[^key-856b6965dbb8eb3dc14f1c3a267837e8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In order to take into account the specific characteristics of trade between Great Britain and certain third countries, movement between Great Britain and Northern Ireland,[^key-16b71e3fecb3a1661deef6e7cbf8b346] and the special character of certain agricultural products, the Secretary of State may make regulations[^key-160612efb541efcefb31e5efb3c674b5] concerning:

- ((a)) the conditions under which imported products are considered to have an equivalent level of conformity to the [^key-a3f03146d1801657269e221e09fa9cb5]... marketing standards applicable in Great Britain[^key-f4abc63e0419aec96189bffef96992ce] and conditions allowing derogation from Article 74; and
- ((b)) the rules concerning the application of the marketing standards to products exported from Great Britain[^key-c09cd34debe426043bcd1148f95c4250].
