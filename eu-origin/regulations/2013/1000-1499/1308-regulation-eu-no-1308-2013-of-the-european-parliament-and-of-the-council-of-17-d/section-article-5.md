## Article 5. Conversion rates for rice

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Secretary of State may make regulations[^c-322c6318ca6f1294a0c00b9c27777083]:

- ((a)) fixing the conversion rates for rice at various stages of processing, the processing costs and the value of by-products;
- ((b)) setting out[^c-0e2836f8775e0de965233a35528a7fbc] necessary measures regarding the application of conversion rates for rice.
