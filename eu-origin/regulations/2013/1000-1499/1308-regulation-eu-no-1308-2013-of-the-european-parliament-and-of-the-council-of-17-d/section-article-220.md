## Article 220. Measures concerning animal diseases and loss of consumer confidence due to public, animal or plant health risks

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A1.[^c-1db913ad20008b8b992f4940b210b95f] This Article does not apply in relation to agricultural producers in England.

A2.[^c-1e510f84d033dbdef4b8bdc16b972249] This Article does not apply in relation to agricultural producers in Wales.

1. The appropriate authority may make regulations[^c-0b1b73a4cf919da783151bf0e179aee5] taking exceptional support measures for the affected market in order to take account of:

- (a) restrictions on trade within the United Kingdom and international[^c-803f24e732040f5ac50a6ac2fdfa9742] trade which may result from the application of measures for combating the spread of diseases in animals; and
- (b) serious market disturbances directly attributed to a loss in consumer confidence due to public, animal or plant health and disease risks.

[^c-f13fb47ac10adfea3fda5a1626285910]...

2. The measures provided for in paragraph 1 shall apply to any of the following sectors:

- (a) beef and veal;
- (b) milk and milk products;
- (c) pigmeat;
- (d) sheepmeat and goatmeat;
- (e) eggs;
- (f) poultrymeat.

The measures provided for in point (b) of the first subparagraph of paragraph 1 related to a loss in consumer confidence due to public or plant health risks shall also apply to all other agricultural products except those listed in Section 2 of Part XXIV of Annex I.

The appropriate authority may make regulations[^c-b7f3730582b8f2c5769e43a0c7f9f37f], extending the list of products in the first two subparagraphs of this paragraph.

3.[^c-23074b5bf336ca72444d731e22b7ec53] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

4. The measures provided for in point (a) of the first subparagraph of paragraph 1 may be taken only if health and veterinary measures have been taken[^c-1f779cf0802d5bd39cef57399b4a3e3a] to stamp out the disease, and only to the extent and for the duration strictly necessary to support the market concerned.
