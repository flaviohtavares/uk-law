# Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 December 2013 establishing a common organisation of the markets in agricultural products and repealing Council Regulations (EEC) No 922/72, (EEC) No 234/79, (EC) No 1037/2001 and (EC) No 1234/2007, section Article 82

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-02-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/eur/2013/1308
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 82 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note). Specific use of wine not conforming to the categories listed in Part II of Annex VII[^c-856b6965dbb8eb3dc14f1c3a267837e8][^c-f4550a16e2a265d5333ca7e01378cf30]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Except for bottled wine in respect of which there is evidence that bottling was performed before 1 September 1971, wine produced from wine grape varieties listed in the classifications drawn up in accordance with the first subparagraph of Article 81(2) but not conforming to one of the categories laid down in Part II of Annex VII shall be used only for consumption by individual wine-producers' households, for the production of wine vinegar or for distillation. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4550a16e2a265d5333ca7e01378cf30]
