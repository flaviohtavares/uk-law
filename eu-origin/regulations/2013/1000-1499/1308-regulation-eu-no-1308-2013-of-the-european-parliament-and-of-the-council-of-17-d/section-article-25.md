## Article 25. Implementing powers in accordance with the examination procedure[^c000002]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The appropriate authority may make regulations laying down[^c-b37acad694d75c7104b07c4313e84ec0] the measures necessary for the application of this Section, including those concerning:

- ((a)) the information to be contained in the strategy referred to in Article 23(8)[^c-af3a50c6cf9db1b9de00fc7958fee643];
- ((b)) the aid applications and payments, including the simplification of procedures resulting from the common framework for the school scheme;
- ((c)) the methods of publicising, and networking measures in respect of, the school scheme;
- ((d)) the format and content of monitoring and evaluation reports[^c-c6e2da4a51925e3c5d8cdc3f7b0ef76b]
