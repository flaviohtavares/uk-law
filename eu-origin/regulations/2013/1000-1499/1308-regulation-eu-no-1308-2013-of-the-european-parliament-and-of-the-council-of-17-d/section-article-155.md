## Article 155. Outsourcing

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Secretary of State[^key-a2c8cb6b4475f9929f1e4c3d124572f5] may permit a recognised producer organisation or a recognised association of producer organisations in the sectors specified [^key-9d12cafb24cd9895ccfec0b3c5d517d4]... in accordance with point (f) of Article 173(1) to outsource any of its activities other than production, including to subsidiaries, provided that the producer organisation or association of producer organisations remains responsible for ensuring the carrying out of the outsourced activity and overall management control and supervision of the commercial arrangement for the carrying out of the activity.
