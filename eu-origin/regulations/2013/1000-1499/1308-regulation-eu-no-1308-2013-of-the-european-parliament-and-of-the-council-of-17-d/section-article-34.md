## Article 34 (affected by repeal, see note). ... Financial assistance (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-6604ed46c589cd98188f7507e7f25a3a][^V_NI_3b4e02a9-c34a-e2d1-d642-79ede9ef889d][^M_E_7e3459e1-1490-40fb-ebc4-f65e482232e0][^key-12c82a854a011846e2323ec0d77aad99][^key-a5ce9d65af511fe4057d8a92b5d41da4][^key-12c82a854a011846e2323ec0d77aad99]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

1. The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-12c82a854a011846e2323ec0d77aad99] [^key-20e788e00d6ae73e0da693a943a9ae6d]... financial assistance shall be equal to the amount of the financial contributions referred to in point (a) of Article 32(1) actually paid and limited to 50 % of the actual expenditure incurred. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-12c82a854a011846e2323ec0d77aad99]

2. The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-12c82a854a011846e2323ec0d77aad99] [^key-dedb6ec264bf27952f88893b07bfaf69]... financial assistance shall be limited to 4,1 % of the value of the marketed production of each producer organisation or of their association. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-12c82a854a011846e2323ec0d77aad99]

However, in the case of producer organisations, that percentage may be increased to 4,6 % of the value of the marketed production, provided that the amount in excess of 4,1 % of the value of the marketed production is used solely for crisis prevention and management measures. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-12c82a854a011846e2323ec0d77aad99]

In the case of associations of producer organisations, that percentage may be increased to 4,7 % of the value of the marketed production, provided that the amount in excess of 4,1 % of the value of the marketed production is used solely for crisis prevention and management measures implemented by the association of producer organisations on behalf of its members. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-12c82a854a011846e2323ec0d77aad99]

3. At the request of a producer organisation, the 50 % limit provided for in paragraph 1 shall be increased to 60 % for an operational programme or part of an operational programme satisfying at least one of the following conditions: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-12c82a854a011846e2323ec0d77aad99]

- (a)[^key-288c4d67c9b84044aefdd216a3ff2a6a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) it is submitted by one or more producer organisations engaged in schemes operated on an interbranch basis; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-12c82a854a011846e2323ec0d77aad99]
- (c) it covers solely specific support for the production of organic products covered by Council Regulation (EC) No 834/2007 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-12c82a854a011846e2323ec0d77aad99][^f00024];
- (d) it is the first to be submitted by a recognised producer organisation which is the result of a merger between two recognised producer organisations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-12c82a854a011846e2323ec0d77aad99]
- (e) it is the first to be submitted by a recognised association of producer organisations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-12c82a854a011846e2323ec0d77aad99]
- (f)[^key-dc13ba7421d10a60084aa0c0cbdc2021] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (g)[^key-56227f9a4633a321225046ba96f5c820] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
