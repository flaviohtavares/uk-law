2. The relevant authority must ensure that there is a strategy in place in respect of the constituent nation for sustainable operational programmes in the fruit and vegetable market. Where the relevant authorities agree, the strategy may be a single strategy in respect of the United Kingdom as a whole[^c-4e973f02a12352daa713006604f72735] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-12c82a854a011846e2323ec0d77aad99]. Such a strategy shall include: : *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-12c82a854a011846e2323ec0d77aad99]

- (a) an analysis of the situation in terms of strengths and weaknesses and the potential for development; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-12c82a854a011846e2323ec0d77aad99]
- (b) justification of the priorities chosen; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-12c82a854a011846e2323ec0d77aad99]
- (c) the objectives of operational programmes and instruments, and performance indicators; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-12c82a854a011846e2323ec0d77aad99]
- (d) assessment of operational programmes; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-12c82a854a011846e2323ec0d77aad99]
- (e) reporting obligations for producer organisations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-12c82a854a011846e2323ec0d77aad99]

The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-12c82a854a011846e2323ec0d77aad99] [^c-e161322231c30a0fd1dde20620d2e670]... strategy shall also integrate the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-12c82a854a011846e2323ec0d77aad99] [^c-e161322231c30a0fd1dde20620d2e670]... framework referred to in paragraph 1. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-12c82a854a011846e2323ec0d77aad99]
