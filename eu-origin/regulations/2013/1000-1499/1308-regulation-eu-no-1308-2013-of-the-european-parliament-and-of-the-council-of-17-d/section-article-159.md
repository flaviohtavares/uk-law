## Article 159. Mandatory recognition[^c000005]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

By way of derogation from Articles 152 to 158, the Secretary of State[^key-c29720335284d70dbbcd1a6c64aa9e64] shall, on request, recognise:

- ((a)) producer organisations in:
  - ((i)) the fruit and vegetables sector in respect of one or more products of that sector and/or such products solely intended for processing,
  - ((ii)) [^key-f8925e0e89bea8709b27ec681d4f964f]...
  - ((iii)) [^key-f8925e0e89bea8709b27ec681d4f964f]...
  - ((iv)) the hops sector;
- ((b)) [^key-8113440d618a22587be91631c86b449a]...
