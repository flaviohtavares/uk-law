# Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 December 2013 establishing a common organisation of the markets in agricultural products and repealing Council Regulations (EEC) No 922/72, (EEC) No 234/79, (EC) No 1037/2001 and (EC) No 1234/2007, section Article 17

## Article 17. Eligible products[^c-0099223a29501e388e0f6404d120f843][^c-a5a5a1e24768cb94ff2941d2f9406a8a][^c-eb97c9a50312edb8b27403b38f4b1e62][^c-a4e86130302b89eb906ef8f1c5751b54][^c-ec2b8529f3f072d8f7abc37bda38cecd]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Aid for private storage may be granted in respect of the following products in accordance with the conditions set out in this Section and any further requirements and conditions prescribed in legislation made[^c-a7764a0a38bc7ec67fcb3775b16b6256] pursuant to Articles 18, 19 or 20 *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-951e084a6515d8c62c6b47194c49d7d0]under any of Articles 18, 19 and 20 of this Regulation and section 11 of the Agriculture (Retained EU Law and Data) (Scotland) Act 2020 *(Extent: S)*:

- ((a)) white sugar;
- ((b)) olive oil;
- ((c)) flax fibre;
- ((d)) fresh or chilled meat of bovine animals aged eight months or more;
- ((e)) butter produced from cream obtained directly and exclusively from cow's milk;
- ((f)) cheese;
- ((g)) skimmed milk powder made from cow's milk;
- ((h)) pigmeat;
- ((i)) sheepmeat and goatmeat.

Point (f) of the first paragraph is restricted to cheese benefiting from a protected designation of origin or from a protected geographical indication under Regulation (EU) No 1151/2012 that is stored beyond the period of maturation laid down in the product specification for the product referred to in Article 7 of that Regulation and/or a period of maturation that contributes to increasing the value of the cheese.

The second paragraph does not apply in relation to England. *(Extent: E+W)*[^c-1366e5e6b88941e13f8813d7561ecb6d]

The second paragraph ceases to apply in relation to Scotland on 1 July 2023. *(Extent: S)*[^c-0479902da9510432f6b6e2f7e8ba8e45]

This Article does not apply in relation to private storage aid schemes in Wales.[^c-bd0ddb95a0d2dae020d99f9bc0c8e0a2]
