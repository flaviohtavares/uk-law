## Article 57. Regulations relating to apiculture[^key-227e1603108af133d0274da5cfa868b9][^key-5c2ae1ebe922bec7268ba320cb0db667][^key-c64fdfd05fb6ae07b0443024b8bff580][^key-f6f4fd37a2435d8674156aeeddc25b87][^key-dfa6dfec1befcb0afad7a466d517cdad]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The appropriate authority may make regulations relating to the content of apiculture programmes and the content of the studies under Article 55(3).
