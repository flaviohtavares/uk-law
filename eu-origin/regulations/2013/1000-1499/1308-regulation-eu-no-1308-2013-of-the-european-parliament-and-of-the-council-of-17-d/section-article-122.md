# Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 December 2013 establishing a common organisation of the markets in agricultural products and repealing Council Regulations (EEC) No 922/72, (EEC) No 234/79, (EC) No 1037/2001 and (EC) No 1234/2007, section Article 122

## Article 122. Delegated powers[^key-31c0fa691bc7edc0cedbc2f3fe7733e7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(A1.)[^key-5cbb2825dbce3343ae2b2e06da6bd113] This Article does not apply in relation to products marketed in England (see section 37(1) of the Agriculture Act 2020).

A2.[^key-ff34cb7c9d6a19f8b4611c026dda8fb5] This Article does not apply in relation to products marketed in Wales (see section 34(1) of the Agriculture (Wales) Act 2023)[^key-11c1f30a65dc5b9b972722b307e4e7a9].

(A1A. (Extent: S))[^key-6f1159b2fd3e3321ac73f99439455cd2] This Article does not apply in relation to products marketed in Scotland (see section 9 of the Agriculture (Retained EU Law and Data) (Scotland) Act 2020). *(Extent: S)*

1. In order to take into account the specific characteristics of the wine sector, the appropriate authority in England, Wales or Scotland may make regulations[^key-0539ae6b039623af56cee14432ba37c7] concerning rules and restrictions on:

- (a) the presentation and use of labelling particulars other than those provided for in this Section;
- (b) compulsory particulars concerning:
  - ((i)) terms to be used to formulate the compulsory particulars and their conditions of use;
  - ((ii)) terms referring to a holding and the conditions for their use;
  - ((iii)) [^key-fd97a46a2da1b753cda8f2df77dc0248]...
  - ((iv)) provisions allowing further derogations in addition to those referred to in Article 119(2) as regards the omission of the reference to the category of the grapevine product; and
  - ((v)) provisions on the use of languages;
- (c) optional particulars concerning:
  - ((i)) terms to be used to formulate the optional particulars and their conditions of use;
  - ((ii)) [^key-8416948df344621757bbee80bfcc78c5]...
- (d) the presentation concerning:
  - ((i)) the conditions of use of certain bottle shapes, and a list of certain specific bottle shapes;
  - ((ii)) the conditions of use of "sparkling wine"-type bottles and closures;
  - ((iii)) [^key-e9f828e7ced7d58669e34cb8237bae72]...
  - ((iv)) provisions on the use of languages.

2. In order to ensure the protection of the legitimate interests of operators, the appropriate authority in England, Wales or Scotland may make regulations[^key-fc86571586cd8be98e86f3fc1b9ab1c6] concerning rules as regards temporary labelling and presentation of wines bearing a designation of origin or a geographical indication, where that designation of origin or geographical indication fulfils the necessary requirements.

3.[^key-bccc4d0778f679e1e68869249c25637b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

4. In order to take account of the specific characteristics in trade between the United Kingdom[^key-feebb10b5c97dc646ebe2e5d973d56d4] and certain third countries, the appropriate authority in England, Wales or Scotland may make regulations[^key-20a65d63cb336ab806cbe82bdbee4873] concerning derogations from this Section as regards products to be exported where required by the law of the third country concerned.
