## Article 88. Restrictions on use of optional reserved terms[^c-856b6965dbb8eb3dc14f1c3a267837e8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. An optional reserved term may only be used to describe products that conform to the applicable conditions of use.

2.[^c-83b591602e1899010a55ff69688ce270] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

3. In order to ensure that products described by means of optional reserved terms conform to the applicable conditions of use, the appropriate authority in England, Wales or Scotland may make regulations[^c-af6b043c41f96b773d298f88aacda634] laying down additional rules on the use of optional reserved terms.
