# Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 December 2013 establishing a common organisation of the markets in agricultural products and repealing Council Regulations (EEC) No 922/72, (EEC) No 234/79, (EC) No 1037/2001 and (EC) No 1234/2007, section Article 85

## Article 85. Existing optional reserved terms[^M_E_y3sn3bxd-22ho-fhyj-1j0k-67320a94e31ba][^c-856b6965dbb8eb3dc14f1c3a267837e8]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

1. The optional reserved terms covered by this scheme on 20 December 2013 are listed in Annex IX and the conditions of their use shall be laid down pursuant to point (a) of Article 86 *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1208ad47c23b574ba197857376480a4].

2. The optional reserved terms referred to in paragraph 1 of this Article shall remain in force, subject to any amendment, unless cancelled pursuant to Article 86 *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5fb56a960f22f0b54404a28064a3c322].

## Article 85. Existing optional reserved terms[^M_E_d4pfy41d-m1ui-nv5r-jewf-67320a94e312e]

*Extent: S*

1. The optional reserved terms covered by this scheme on 20 December 2013 are listed in Annex IX and the conditions of their use may be laid down in regulations made under section 9 of the Agriculture (Retained EU Law and Data) (Scotland) Act 2020[^c-0bf3ad4bb99e6b80017938117fcdb490].

2. The optional reserved terms referred to in paragraph 1 of this Article shall remain in force, subject to any amendment, unless cancelled [^c-f949728cf1d51270cc52c11f4801f092]....
