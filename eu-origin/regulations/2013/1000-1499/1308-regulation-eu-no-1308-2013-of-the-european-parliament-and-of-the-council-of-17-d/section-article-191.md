## Article 191. Derogations for imported products and special security in the wine sector

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Secretary of State may, pursuant to international obligations, make regulations setting out derogations from point 5 of Section B[^c-135770416389904f7b5222f3eda38d74] or from Section C *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a67376f393a25a6d8ec287d72be77e92] of Part 2 of Annex VIII for products imported into Great Britain.

In the case of derogations from point 5 of Section B of Part II of Annex VIII, importers shall lodge a security for those products with the designated customs authorities at the time of release into free circulation. The security shall be released on the presentation of proof by the importer, to the satisfaction of the customs authorities of the [^c-b33f7c221fec1e817ce93895f2fb264e]..., that:

- ((a)) the products have not benefited from the derogations; or,
- ((b)) if they have benefited from the derogations, the products have not been vinified, or if they have been vinified, the resulting products have been appropriately labelled.

The Secretary of State may make regulations[^c-26ba2c38b51e363e1b9f94fe35186d40] laying down rules to ensure the uniform application of this Article, including on the amounts of the security and appropriate labelling. [^c-53264fb8b84930c67b728dd029ad9de9]...
