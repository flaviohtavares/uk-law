## ANNEX VIII — OENOLOGICAL PRACTICES REFERRED TO IN ARTICLE 80

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

PART I

**Enrichment, acidification and de-acidification in certain wine-growing zones**

A.

**Enrichment limits**

1\.

Where climatic conditions have made it necessary , the Secretary of State, with the agreement of the relevant authorities for Wales and Scotland,[^c-94a6272f117c2e3ee5ae18ba4439cdb4] may allow to supplement the natural alcoholic strength by volume of fresh grapes, grape must, grape must in fermentation, new wine still in fermentation and wine obtained from wine grape varieties classifiable according to Article 81.

2\.

The increase in natural alcoholic strength by volume shall be achieved by means of the oenological practices referred to in Section B and shall not exceed 3% volume[^c-219d88a3acdce4e1de0b1d81aa89f605].

3.[^c000005]

In years when climatic conditions have been exceptionally unfavourable, the limit(s) laid down in point 2 may be raised by 0,5 % by the Secretary of State, with the agreement of the relevant authorities for Wales and Scotland,[^c-04d27aae0b921280f035f74d7282e28e] as an exception for the regions concerned. [^c-dce9420be8c63082973c0d8b25b600b9]...

B.

**Enrichment processes**

1\.

The increase in natural alcoholic strength by volume provided for in Section A shall only be effected:

- ((a)) in respect of fresh grapes, grape must in fermentation or new wine still in fermentation, by adding sucrose, concentrated grape must or rectified concentrated grape must;
- ((b)) in respect of grape must, by adding sucrose, concentrated grape must or rectified concentrated grape must, or by partial concentration, including reverse osmosis;
- ((c)) in respect of wine, by partial concentration through cooling.

2\.

The processes referred to in point 1 shall be mutually exclusive where wine or grape must is enriched with concentrated grape must or rectified concentrated grape must and an aid has been paid under Article 103y of Regulation (EC) No 1234/2007.

3\.

The addition of sucrose provided for in points 1(a) and (b) may only be performed by dry sugaring [^c-4c6434ef61c06de43d915b929196baa7]....

4\.

The addition of concentrated grape must or rectified concentrated grape must shall not have the effect of increasing the initial volume of fresh crushed grapes, grape must, grape must in fermentation or new wine still in fermentation by more than 11 % [^c-e5f94936ef96bd2931fdedf01c0f7bf3]....

5\.

The concentration of grape must or of wine subjected to the processes referred to in point 1:

- ((a)) shall not have the effect of reducing the initial volume of these products by more than 20 %;
- ((b)) shall, notwithstanding point 2(c) of Section A, not increase the natural alcoholic strength of these products by more than 2 % volume.

6\.

The processes referred to in points 1 and 5 shall not raise the total alcoholic strength by volume of the fresh grapes, grape must, grape must in fermentation, new wine still in fermentation, or wine to more than 11.5% volume[^c-9baf48ca689ff652d56750cd4e852687].

7\.

Nothing in this Regulation prevents the Secretary of State, with the agreement of the relevant authorities for Wales and Scotland, from[^c-0d73c07912ffdd80d9dc590e5ea03293]:

- ((a)) in relation to red wine, raising[^c-304fc5cf91a574752b5b6c564852c459] the upper limit of total alcoholic strength by volume of the products referred to in point 6 to 12 % volume [^c-67a2d35e3b8a09b907b017b82a0ee3b4]...;
- ((b)) raising the total alcoholic strength by volume of the products referred to in point 6 for the production of wines with a designation of origin to a level to be determined by the Secretary of State[^c-3f5f5939d9992c3a08fe00e30cdcd798].

C.

**Acidification and de-acidification**

1\.

Fresh grapes, grape must, grape must in fermentation, new wine still in fermentation and wine may be subject to de-acidification[^c-e21711c4cf6db5a240f2416b16880d61].

2\.

Acidification of the products, other than wine, referred to in point 1 may be carried out only up to a limit of 1,50 g/l expressed as tartaric acid, or 20 milliequivalents per litre.

3\.

Acidification of wines may be carried out only up to a limit of 2,50 g/l expressed as tartaric acid, or 33,3 milliequivalents per litre.

4\.

De-acidification of wines may be carried out only up to a limit of 1 g/l expressed as tartaric acid, or 13,3 milliequivalents per litre.

5\.

Grape must intended for concentration may be partially de-acidified.

6\.

Notwithstanding point 1, in years when climatic conditions have been exceptional, the Secretary of State, with the agreement of the relevant authorities for Wales and Scotland,[^c-ece704d9e53cbf5a6fc746abec5e45e9] may authorise acidification of the products referred to in point 1 [^c-33bbcbc4bedf5bf95f4b6cdcf69763c0]..., under the conditions referred to in points 2 and 3.

7\.

Acidification and enrichment, *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ef80aa7aaa0b2efca56e7279c07b39b4] subject to any exceptions[^c-38a6a9bdef8fcfd00a5a639ef6961229] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ef80aa7aaa0b2efca56e7279c07b39b4] made under *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c869fd2a245921d2c1d1f56f060e04a1] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ef80aa7aaa0b2efca56e7279c07b39b4] Article 75(2) *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c869fd2a245921d2c1d1f56f060e04a1] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ef80aa7aaa0b2efca56e7279c07b39b4]in regulations made under section 9 of the Agriculture (Retained EU Law and Data) (Scotland) Act 2020 *(Extent: S)* *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c869fd2a245921d2c1d1f56f060e04a1], and acidification and de-acidification of one and the same product shall be mutually exclusive processes.

D.

**Processes**

1\.

None of the processes referred to in Sections B and C, with the exception of the acidification and de-acidification of wines, shall be authorised unless carried out, under conditions prescribed[^c-74ffe2d20b3fa2721533f7ffff8379f8] under *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-30f0855d36f50c6031dfa7ad036ab0ce] Article 75(2) *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-30f0855d36f50c6031dfa7ad036ab0ce]in Regulation (EU) 2019/934 *(Extent: E)*in Regulation (EU) 2019/934 or in regulations made under section 9 of the Agriculture (Retained EU Law and Data) (Scotland) Act 2020 *(Extent: S)*[^c-57847bad2bce9715c640df20cbea5c93], at the time when the fresh grapes, grape must, grape must in fermentation or new wine still in fermentation are being turned into wine or into any other wine sector beverage intended for direct human consumption other than sparkling wine or aerated sparkling wine in the wine-growing zone where the fresh grapes used were harvested.

2\.

The concentration of wines shall take place in Great Britain[^c-7105f5c0fa922f5ac84bdd2c6f776dc7].

3\.

Acidification and de-acidification of wines shall take place only in the wine-making undertaking [^c-f10b096b41e97d566ff6e88dde9f0516]... where the grapes used to produce the wine in question were harvested.

4\.

Each of the processes referred to in points 1, 2 and 3 shall be notified to the competent authorities. The same shall apply in respect of the quantities of concentrated grape must rectified concentrated grape must or sucrose held in the exercise of their profession by natural or legal persons or groups of persons, in particular producers, bottlers, processors and merchants prescribed[^c-44579deb51d48d34f6bb48506ef8f514] under *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-57847bad2bce9715c640df20cbea5c93] Article 75(2) *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-57847bad2bce9715c640df20cbea5c93]in Regulation (EU) 2019/934 or in regulations made under section 9 of the Agriculture (Retained EU Law and Data) (Scotland) Act 2020 *(Extent: S)*, at the same time and in the same place as fresh grapes, grape must, grape must in fermentation or wine in bulk. The notification of these quantities may, however, be replaced by entry in a goods inwards and stock utilisation register.

5\.

Each of the processes referred to in Sections B and C shall be recorded on the accompanying document, as provided for in Article 147, under cover of which the products having undergone the processes are put into circulation.

6\.

The processes referred to in Sections B and C, subject to derogations justified by exceptional climatic conditions, shall not be carried out:

- ((a)) [^c-32dcb094de86b03f9b41c36523dc59dd]...
- ((b)) [^c-0278886728970ac4c0809708ef830d57]... after 16 March, and they shall be carried out only for products of the grape harvest immediately preceding those dates.

7\.

Notwithstanding point 6, concentration by cooling and acidification and de-acidification of wines may be practised throughout the year.

**Restrictions**

**General**

1\.

All authorised oenological practices shall exclude the addition of water, except where required on account of a specific technical necessity.

2\.

All authorised oenological practices shall exclude the addition of alcohol, except for practices related to obtaining fresh grape must with fermentation arrested by the addition of alcohol, liqueur wine, sparkling wine, wine fortified for distillation and semi-sparkling wine.
