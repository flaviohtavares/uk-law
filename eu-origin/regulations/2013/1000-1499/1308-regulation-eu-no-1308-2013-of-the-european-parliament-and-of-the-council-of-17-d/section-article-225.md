## Article 225. Reporting obligation of the appropriate authority[^c-fd441f541e7e07a6e7fa37008d9f9f89]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The appropriate authority shall publish a report[^c-ac176e65108d276ec92086ee69e37a8f]:

- ((a)) every three years on[^c-73ec7633b5350c26e67152491f38dbab] the implementation of the measures concerning the apiculture sector as set out in Articles 55, 56 and 57, including on the latest developments on beehive identification systems;
- ((b)) [^c-14e724ee5fa6d345003b26d9e2e2e736]...
- ((c)) [^c-14e724ee5fa6d345003b26d9e2e2e736]...
- ((d)) [^c-14e724ee5fa6d345003b26d9e2e2e736]...
- ((e)) by 31 July 2023, on the application of the allocation criteria referred to in Article 23a(2);[^c000010]
- ((f)) [^c-b2b40b06404960772999900f8e5ef4c7]...
