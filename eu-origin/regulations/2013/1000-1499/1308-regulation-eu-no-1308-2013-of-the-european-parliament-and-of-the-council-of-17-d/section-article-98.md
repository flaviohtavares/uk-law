# Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 December 2013 establishing a common organisation of the markets in agricultural products and repealing Council Regulations (EEC) No 922/72, (EEC) No 234/79, (EC) No 1037/2001 and (EC) No 1234/2007, section Article 98

## Article 98. Objection procedure[^c-065191af70980d1e5228e24a6ecc8025]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Within two months from the date of the publication of the single document as referred to in point (d) of Article 94(1), any [^c-b07617b4fce72887d47e726703f8f271]... country, or any natural or legal person having a legitimate interest [^c-f43ecfd381148f02dfaa74e674adc974]..., may object to the proposed protection by submitting to the Secretary of State[^c-0a18eb7dfd67d1efc85772c5bdd91991] a duly substantiated statement concerning the conditions of eligibility as laid down in this Subsection.

In the case of natural or legal persons resident or established in another country[^c-d368b0414b42eda15bed2988d7b3d497], such a statement shall be submitted, either directly or via the authorities of the [^c-2e9e2430e2a28c461c020d2f9f504297]... country concerned, within the two month period referred to in the first paragraph.
