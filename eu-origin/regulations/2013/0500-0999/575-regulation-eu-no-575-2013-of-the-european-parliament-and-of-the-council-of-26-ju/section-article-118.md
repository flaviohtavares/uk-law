# Regulation (EU) No 575/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 26 June 2013 on prudential requirements for credit institutions and investment firms and amending Regulation (EU) No 648/2012 (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 118

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/eur/2013/575
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 118. Exposures to international organisations[^c000001]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Exposures to the following international organisations shall be assigned a 0 % risk weight:

- ((a)) the European Union[^c000002] [^key-d000b94a25ca520c420f70fc00aead23]...;
- ((b)) the International Monetary Fund;
- ((c)) the Bank for International Settlements;
- ((d)) the European Financial Stability Facility;
- ((e)) the European Stability Mechanism;
- ((f)) [^key-71a71bc7e9f4127a35fddda79876cc58]...
