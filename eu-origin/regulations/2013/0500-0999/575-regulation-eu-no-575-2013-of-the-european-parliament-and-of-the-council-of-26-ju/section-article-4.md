## Article 4. Definitions[^c000001]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. For the purposes of this Regulation, the following definitions shall apply:

- ((1)) ‘credit institution’ means an undertaking the business of which is to take deposits or other repayable funds from the public and to grant credits for its own account;
- ((2)) ‘investment firm’ means a person as defined in paragraph 1A of Article 2 of Regulation 600/2014/EU, as that Article has effect subject to the requirements imposed by the United Kingdom legislation that implemented Directive 2014/65/EU, as amended from time to time[^key-02e7a12ba356ef911c654046910168b0], other than a credit institution[^key-6baea607e6d1c6535184d3cc380eb0dd];
- ((2A)) ‘CRR firm’ means a person that satisfies the following conditions—[^key-9c0c6d929db293d592db18c9dc7adf41]

    - ((i)) is a credit institution which has permission under Part 4A of FSMA to carry on the regulated activity of accepting deposits; or
    - ((ii)) is a designated investment firm[^key-019a3900583e04ab6191d6f46b4353d4];
  - ((b)) its registered office, or if it has no registered office, its head office, is in the United Kingdom; and
  - ((c)) it is not a credit union within the meaning of the Credit Unions Act 1979 or the Credit Unions (Northern Ireland) Order 1985, or a friendly society within the meaning of section 417(1) of FSMA;

  and for the purposes of this definition, ‘regulated activity’ has the meaning in section 22 of FSMA, and ‘accepting deposits’ has the meaning in Regulation 5 of the Regulated Activities Order;
