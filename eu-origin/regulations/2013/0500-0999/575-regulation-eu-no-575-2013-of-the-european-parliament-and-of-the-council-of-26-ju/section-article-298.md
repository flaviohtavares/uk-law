# Regulation (EU) No 575/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 26 June 2013 on prudential requirements for credit institutions and investment firms and amending Regulation (EU) No 648/2012 (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 298

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/eur/2013/575
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 298. Effects of recognition of netting as risk-reducing[^c-271c1beb00dfcd5305d622e053e14fa2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Netting for the purposes of Section 6 of this Chapter, and Sections 3 to 5 of Chapter 3 of the Counterparty Credit Risk (CRR) Part of the PRA Rulebook[^c-5b098a089b6df93432aa209ec58b7fb2] shall be recognised as set out in those Sections.
