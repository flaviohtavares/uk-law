## Article 81. Minority interests that qualify for inclusion in consolidated Common Equity Tier 1 capital[^c000001]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1.[^c000002] Minority interests shall comprise the sum of Common Equity Tier 1 items of a subsidiary where the following conditions are met:

- (a) the subsidiary is one of the following:
  - (i)[^key-47dd74b358394b06c466fa7b2d5009da] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (ii)[^key-47dd74b358394b06c466fa7b2d5009da] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (iia)[^key-47dd74b358394b06c466fa7b2d5009da] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (iib)[^key-47dd74b358394b06c466fa7b2d5009da] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (iic)[^key-47dd74b358394b06c466fa7b2d5009da] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (iii) an intermediate financial holding company in a third country that is subject to prudential requirements which are[^key-7771cc5949bda8c129195eae746d905d] as stringent as those applied to credit institutions of that third country and which[^key-b500cc9c62907b2fef76ca9cf7713509] the Treasury have[^key-f698359e895195eddf7308eaeebf9abe] determined[^key-0988ea6edb651f7df2156ca3739795c0] in accordance with Article 107(4) [^key-497a92cfed0e611c7b9a8be964254143]... are at least equivalent to those of this Regulation;
- (b)[^key-47dd74b358394b06c466fa7b2d5009da] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c)[^key-47dd74b358394b06c466fa7b2d5009da] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
