## Article 6. General principles[^c000001]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. Institutions shall comply with the obligations laid down in Part Two,[^c-834a8aaa35832eec79c0ac6c5440c9e6] and Part Three of this Regulation and in chapter 2 of the securitisation part of the PRA Rulebook, as that chapter has effect from time to time,[^c-7211c513b6e8366f0f27a92281919ec9] on an individual basis.
