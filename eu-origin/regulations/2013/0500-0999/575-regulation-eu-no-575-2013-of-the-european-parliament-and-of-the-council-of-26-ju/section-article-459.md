## Article 459. Prudential requirements[^c000001]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Treasury may by regulations impose[^key-eefecfbe479bd29667dec1da95e304df], for a period of one year, stricter prudential requirements for exposures where this is necessary to address changes in the intensity of microprudential and macroprudential risks which arise from market developments in the United Kingdom or outside the United Kingdom[^key-2f7e426bd8640aeeb0b2b7063402750c], and where the instruments of this Regulation and Directive 2013/36/EU UK law[^key-37321cd4378a8cf65681f6acf0ff3ffd] are not sufficient to address these risks [^key-b507823c811528dc71f980ada69cd093]... concerning:

- ((a)) the level of own funds laid down in Article 92;
