# Regulation (EU) No 575/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 26 June 2013 on prudential requirements for credit institutions and investment firms and amending Regulation (EU) No 648/2012 (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 2

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/eur/2013/575
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 2. Supervisory powers[^c000002][^c000001]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. For the purpose of ensuring compliance with this Regulation, competent authorities shall have the powers and shall follow the procedures set out in Directive 2013/36/EU UK law[^c-41505aab95901c6bc4231bbc8f3c6fe9] and in this Regulation.

2. For the purpose of ensuring compliance with this Regulation, the resolution authority[^c-fac965dc6933b7bfbd9af16de996ce01] shall have the powers and shall follow the procedures set out in the United Kingdom legislation that implemented Directive 2014/59/EU, as amended from time to time,[^c-dc1b4dea267ada012ad8d5ca5ff99d65] and in this Regulation.

3. For the purpose of ensuring compliance with the requirements concerning own funds and eligible liabilities, competent authorities and the resolution authority shall cooperate.
