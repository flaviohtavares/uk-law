## Article 456. Regulations modifying this Regulation[^c000001][^key-2c1d646b6ddb837eb8072fdbdbe790ad]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. The Treasury may make regulations[^key-89bca18f01480f4c7d5cca0f70d65353] concerning the following matters:

- (a) clarification of the definitions set out in Articles 4, 5, 142, 153, 192, 242, 272 and 381[^key-56796249cd1fb89c6345d5e78997e49f] [^key-ebec92d4f8b0e8d17d185017306edb9f]...;
- (b) clarification of the definitions set out in Articles 4, 5, 142, 153, 192, 242, 272 and 381[^key-8fd53297b78f3da45d5c32b5342a35cc] in order to take account, in the application of this Regulation, of developments on financial markets;
- (c) amendment of the list of exposure classes in Articles 112 and 147 in order to take account of developments on financial markets;
- (d) the amount specified in point (c) of Article 123, Article 147(5)(a), Article 153(4) and Article 162(4), to take into account the effects of inflation;
- (e) the list and classification of the off-balance sheet items in Annexes I and II, in order to take account of developments on financial markets;
- (f)[^key-14a3a28be307f16c4cb2cade445ce7f4] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (g)[^key-e59ff5739fde64b2f978d50da3a615d4] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (h) amendment of the own funds requirements as set out in [^key-437880fd27f33faaa6ad0ecbe5482b18]... Articles 50a to 50d of Regulation (EU) No 648/2012 to take account of developments or amendments of the international standards for exposures to a central counterparty;
- (i)[^key-a383d831754a9f034bc056dafa92891b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (j)[^key-a383d831754a9f034bc056dafa92891b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

2. The Treasury may by regulations amend Articles 381, 382(1) to (3)[^key-e305d5a5de722bdcd4988714c760d3d0] , 383, 384 and 386[^key-20974d452206e0e033fdc86d68225e50] concerning—

- (a) the treatment of CVA risk as a stand-alone charge versus an integrated component of the market risk framework;
- (b) the scope of the CVA risk charge including the exemption in Article 482;
- (c) eligible hedges;
- (d) calculation of capital requirements of CVA risk.
