# Regulation (EU) No 575/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 26 June 2013 on prudential requirements for credit institutions and investment firms and amending Regulation (EU) No 648/2012 (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 270a

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/eur/2013/575
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 270a. Additional risk weight[^c000003][^c000001]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. Where an institution does not meet the requirements in PRA securitisation rules[^c-dac4bc8ea16ca0f91b4665eaff711ca6] in any material respect by reason of negligence or omission by the institution, the competent authority[^c-e54b17d9a6e5e7fee9f943c76f1945dd] shall impose a proportionate additional risk weight of no less than 250 % of the risk weight, capped at 1 250  %, which shall apply to the relevant securitisation positions in the manner specified in Article 247(6) or Article 337(3) of this Regulation respectively. The additional risk weight shall progressively increase with each subsequent infringement of the due diligence and risk management provisions. The competent authority shall take into account the exemptions for certain securitisations provided for in PRA securitisation rules from risk retention requirements imposed by those rules[^c-5e19613f4569dfc50faab8e5b7b839e3] by reducing the risk weight they would otherwise impose under this Article in respect of the securitisation if that exemption did not apply.[^c-1cf02150c6d18c7ad66c34859c4769af]

1A.[^c-805181a972350770a61092d679946223] In paragraph 1, “**PRA securitisation rules**” means rules made by the PRA under section 137G of FSMA which relate to securitisation.

2. The PRA may[^c-c79d0431b1b64eb44c2bea694a93b61c][^c-6f5f2ddd36e40ee844c10d8ef7b43f30] make technical standards to facilitate the convergence of supervisory practices with regard to the implementation of paragraph 1, including the measures to be taken in the case of breach of the due diligence and risk management obligations.
