# Regulation (EU) No 575/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 26 June 2013 on prudential requirements for credit institutions and investment firms and amending Regulation (EU) No 648/2012 (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 465

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/eur/2013/575
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 465. Own funds requirements[^c000001]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. By way of derogation from points (a) and (b) of Article 92(1) the following own funds requirements shall apply during the period from 1 January 2014 to 31 December 2014 :

- (a) a Common Equity Tier 1 capital ratio of a level that falls within a range of 4 % to 4,5 %;
- (b) a Tier 1 capital ratio of a level that falls within a range of 5,5 % to 6 %.

2. Competent authorities shall determine and publish the levels of the Common Equity Tier 1 and Tier 1 capital ratios in the ranges specified in paragraph 1 that institutions shall meet or exceed.
