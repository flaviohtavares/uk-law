# Regulation (EU) No 575/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 26 June 2013 on prudential requirements for credit institutions and investment firms and amending Regulation (EU) No 648/2012 (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 341

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 341. Net positions in equity instruments[^c000001]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. The institution shall separately sum all its net long positions and all its net short positions in accordance with Article 327. The sum of the absolute values of the two figures shall be its overall gross position.

2. The institution shall calculate, separately for each market, the difference between the sum of the net long and the net short positions. The sum of the absolute values of those differences shall be its overall net position.

3. The[^c-71015b94afa55ce0db13ec728d2d52dc] PRA may[^c-824c4dec077a75f3f57ab36f0275a544] make technical standards defining the term market referred to in paragraph 2.
