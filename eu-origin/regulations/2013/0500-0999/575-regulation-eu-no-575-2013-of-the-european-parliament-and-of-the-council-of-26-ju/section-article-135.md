# Regulation (EU) No 575/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 26 June 2013 on prudential requirements for credit institutions and investment firms and amending Regulation (EU) No 648/2012 (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 135

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/eur/2013/575
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 135. Use of credit assessments by ECAIs[^c000001]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. An external credit assessment may be used to determine the risk weight of an exposure under this Chapter only if it has been issued by an ECAI or has been endorsed by an ECAI in accordance with Regulation (EC) No 1060/2009.

2. The competent authorities must each publish a list[^c-91a022260d3f8b48ad2b7d4e9e367b04] of ECAIs [^c-6d780af84beddc605c6579e6118e5474]... on its website.
