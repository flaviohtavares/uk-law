# Regulation (EU) No 575/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 26 June 2013 on prudential requirements for credit institutions and investment firms and amending Regulation (EU) No 648/2012 (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 14

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/eur/2013/575
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 14. Application of due-diligence rules on a consolidated basis[^c000002][^c000001][^key-15cf8ca9be412921a0364654da6793cb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. Parent undertakings and their subsidiaries that are subject to this Regulation shall be required to meet the obligations laid down in due-diligence rules[^key-b8bffcfa2bfd51e517d4251c9ae6b065] on a consolidated or sub-consolidated basis, to ensure that their arrangements, processes and mechanisms required by those provisions are consistent and well-integrated and that any data and information relevant to the purpose of supervision can be produced. In particular, they shall ensure that subsidiaries that are not subject to this Regulation implement arrangements, processes and mechanisms to ensure compliance with those provisions.

2. Institutions shall apply an additional risk weight in accordance with Article 270a of this Regulation when applying Article 92 of this Regulation on a consolidated or sub-consolidated basis if the requirements laid down in due-diligence rules[^key-b4a488656a791440a0dfc2dd32d842aa] are breached at the level of an entity established in a third country included in the consolidation in accordance with Article 18 of this Regulation if the breach is material in relation to the overall risk profile of the group.

3.[^key-afca5e2eb8c2a77bfe308ea4f649ef1c] “**Due-diligence rules**” means rules made by the PRA under section 137G of FSMA in accordance with regulation 33 of the Securitisation Regulations 2024 (rules relating to due-diligence requirements of institutional investors).
