# Regulation (EU) No 952/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 9 October 2013 laying down the Union Customs Code (recast), section Article 9

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2022-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/eur/2013/952
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 9. Registration

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. Economic operators established in the customs territory of the Union shall register with the customs authorities [^c-a012aea33f912284b931aebcf51c1831]....

2. In specific cases, economic operators which are not established in the customs territory of the Union shall register with the customs authorities [^c-752adc9a0ea898385ac05b1f8604042f]....

3. Persons other than economic operators shall not be required to register with the customs authorities unless otherwise provided.

[^c-33d1fe306a53d286a6fc7f0e6dfa57f6]...

4. In specific cases, the customs authorities shall invalidate the registration.
