# Regulation (EU) No 952/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 9 October 2013 laying down the Union Customs Code (recast), section Article 281

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2022-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/eur/2013/952
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 281. Conferral of implementing powers

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Commission shall adopt, by means of implementing acts, the work programme referred to in Article 280.

Those implementing acts shall be adopted in accordance with the examination procedure referred to in Article 285(4).

Where the committee delivers no opinion, the Commission shall not adopt the implementing acts referred to in the first paragraph of this Article, and the third subparagraph of Article 5(4) of Regulation (EU) No 182/2011 shall apply.[^c000003]
