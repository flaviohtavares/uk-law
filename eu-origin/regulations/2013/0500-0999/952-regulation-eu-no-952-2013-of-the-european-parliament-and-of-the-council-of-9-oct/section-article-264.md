# Regulation (EU) No 952/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 9 October 2013 laying down the Union Customs Code (recast), section Article 264

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 264. Risk analysis

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The customs office to which the pre-departure declaration referred to in Article 263 is lodged shall ensure that, within a specific time-limit, a risk analysis is carried out, [^key-8d85d2dbcad9ec515e01004ab2266e94]... for security and safety purposes, on the basis of that declaration and shall take the necessary measures based on the results of that risk analysis.
