# Regulation (EU) No 952/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 9 October 2013 laying down the Union Customs Code (recast), section Article 180

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2022-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/eur/2013/952

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 180. Delegation of power

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Commission shall be empowered to adopt delegated acts, in accordance with Article 284, in order to determine the conditions for granting the authorisation referred to in the first subparagraph of Article 179(1).
