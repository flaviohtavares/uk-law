# Regulation (EU) No 952/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 9 October 2013 laying down the Union Customs Code (recast), section Article 35

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2022-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/eur/2013/952
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 35. Decisions relating to binding information with regard to other factors

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In specific cases, the customs authorities shall, upon application, take decisions relating to binding information with regard to other factors referred to in Title II, on the basis of which import or export duty and other measures in respect of trade in goods are applied.
