# Regulation (EU) No 952/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 9 October 2013 laying down the Union Customs Code (recast), section Article 217

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2022-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/eur/2013/952
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 217. Conferral of implementing powers

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Commission shall specify, by means of implementing acts, the procedural rules for the discharge of a special procedure referred to in Article 215.[^c000003]

Those implementing acts shall be adopted in accordance with the examination procedure referred to in Article 285(4).
