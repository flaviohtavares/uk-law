# Regulation (EU) No 952/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 9 October 2013 laying down the Union Customs Code (recast), section Article 207

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2022-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/eur/2013/952
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 207. Conferral of implementing powers[^c-822c3fbe288dcf495c199d5dd190d444][^c-d5b6ab9cef700eb9b0fa86e2a9078c45]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Commission shall specify, by means of implementing acts, the procedural rules for the provision of information referred to in Article 203(6).

Those implementing acts shall be adopted in accordance with the examination procedure referred to in Article 285(4).
