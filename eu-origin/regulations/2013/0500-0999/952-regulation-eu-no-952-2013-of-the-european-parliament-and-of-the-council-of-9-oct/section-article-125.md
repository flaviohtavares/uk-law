# Regulation (EU) No 952/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 9 October 2013 laying down the Union Customs Code (recast), section Article 125

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2022-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/eur/2013/952
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 125. Application of penalties

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where the customs debt is extinguished on the basis of point (h) of Article 124(1), Member States shall not be precluded from the application of penalties for failure to comply with the customs legislation.
