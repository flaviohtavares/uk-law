## Article 16. General obligations of the FCA[^key-d6b8d0e4d1fd7978be4271d5677bb124]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

When assessing the AIFM's compliance with rules 2.1.4 and 18.5A.3 of the Conduct of Business sourcebook and rules 4.1.2C and 10.1.24 in the Senior Management Arrangements, Systems and Controls sourcebook, the FCA shall use at least the criteria laid down in this Section.
