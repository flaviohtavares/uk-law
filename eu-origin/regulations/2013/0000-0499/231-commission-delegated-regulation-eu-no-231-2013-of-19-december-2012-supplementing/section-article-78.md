## Article 78. Delegation of portfolio or risk management

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. This Article shall apply where the delegation of portfolio management or risk management is concerned.

2. The following entities shall be deemed to be authorised or registered for the purpose of asset management and subject to supervision in accordance with regulation 26(1)(a) and (2) of the AIFM Regulations 2013, and rules 3.10.2(2)(c) and 3.10.7G of the Investment Funds sourcebook[^key-7d0f603252fe889071b18d2d2b7224ae]:

- (a) management companies authorised under FSMA[^key-9b7958a26fb6d63129473580ff4d544d];
- (b) investment firms authorised under FSMA with permission under Part 4A of that Act to carry out the regulated activity of managing investments under article 37 of the Regulated Activities Order 2001[^key-926acb02a79a1b9302646e828170173a];
- (c) credit institutions authorised under FSMA with permission under Part 4A of that Act to carry out the regulated activity of managing investments under article 37 of the Regulated Activities Order 2001[^key-72f4da2711bd8d74069e902a38081a38];
- (d) external AIFMs authorised under the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000[^key-efc91c9973a86910513958478fffc26f];
- (e) third country entities authorised or registered for the purpose of asset management and effectively supervised by a competent authority in those countries.

3. Where the delegation is conferred on a third-country undertaking the following conditions shall be fulfilled in accordance with rules 3.10.2(2)(d) of the Investment Funds sourcebook[^key-7082849b541e88b9043d8b4c138e8f19]:

- (a) a written arrangement shall exist between the FCA[^key-711742305fd816ccb652aa603c9f82b2] and the supervisory authorities of the undertaking to which delegation is conferred;
- (b) with respect to the undertaking to which delegation is conferred, the arrangement referred to in point (a) allows the FCA[^key-c2ee357225f76d5bad8b3bb1499783b9] to:
  - ((i)) obtain on request the relevant information necessary to carry out their supervisory tasks as provided for in the UK provisions which implemented[^key-8c3dd4766fda0ba70c46d7da45eda073] Directive 2011/61/EU;
  - ((ii)) obtain access to the documents relevant for the performance of their supervisory duties maintained in the third country;
  - ((iii)) carry out on-site inspections on the premises of the undertaking to which functions were delegated. The practical procedures for on-site inspections shall be detailed in the written arrangement;
  - ((iv)) receive as soon as possible information from the supervisory authority in the third country for the purpose of investigating apparent breaches of the requirements of the UK provisions which implemented[^key-30ffd8846d6149614a270918c394328a] Directive 2011/61/EU and its implementing measures;
  - ((v)) cooperate in enforcement in accordance with the national and international law applicable to the supervisory authority of the third country and the EU competent authorities in cases of breach of the requirements of the UK provisions which implemented[^key-6004cfc4ae37260ed271501dec02fa0e] Directive 2011/61/EU and its implementing measures [^key-c744945affc0f05f5563a166c6e0eba7]....
