## Article 56. Interpretation[^key-0e3ec9b4efa787b1bcc3ca4adb80b02d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the absence of guidance issued by the FCA, the provisions of this Section shall be interpreted in a consistent manner with the corresponding provisions of rules made by the FCA under section 137A of FSMA in accordance with regulation 33 of the Securitisation Regulations 2024 (due-diligence requirements of institutional investors)[^key-86e97e638ef65ca2aafe87d25b982af6].
