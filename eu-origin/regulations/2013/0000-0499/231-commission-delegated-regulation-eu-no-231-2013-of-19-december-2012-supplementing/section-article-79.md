# Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) No 231/2013 of 19 December 2012 supplementing Directive 2011/61/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council with regard to exemptions, general operating conditions, depositaries, leverage, transparency and supervision (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 79

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/eur/2013/231
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 79. Effective supervision

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A delegation shall be deemed to prevent the effective supervision of the AIFM where:

- ((a)) the AIFM, its auditors and the FCA[^c-5c6bb05d804a70d8c80c27cf46498b30] do not have effective access to data related to the delegated functions and to the business premises of the delegate, or the FCA are not able to exercise those rights of access;
- ((b)) the delegate does not cooperate with the FCA[^c-e901c5395fc5b4cc034fdd77642584ed] in connection with the delegated functions;
- ((c)) the AIFM does not make available on request to the FCA[^c-163599b7828f372f00ff2ebc62996dc0] all information necessary to enable authorities to supervise the compliance of the performance of the delegated functions with the requirements of the UK provisions which implemented[^c-8fed0c72a681f8238c43dcbd79fb3a98] Directive 2011/61/EU and its implementing measures.
