# Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) No 231/2013 of 19 December 2012 supplementing Directive 2011/61/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council with regard to exemptions, general operating conditions, depositaries, leverage, transparency and supervision (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 111

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/eur/2013/231
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 111. Use of leverage on a ‘substantial basis’

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. Leverage shall be considered to be employed on a substantial basis for the purposes of rule 3.4.5 of the Investment Funds sourcebook[^key-81702e9b7ace8a94cdad17782f14e50c] when the exposure of an AIF as calculated according to the commitment method under Article 8 of this Regulation exceeds three times its net asset value.

2. Where the requirements referred to in paragraph 1 of this Article are fulfilled, AIFMs shall provide information in accordance with rule 3.4.5 of the Investment Funds sourcebook[^key-0a02897f7bb54ec07c35bd4c363dfc4d] to the FCA[^key-89f7f532ac7eace6ddd0f65055d8b530] in accordance with the principles laid down in Article 110(3) of this Regulation.
