## Article 27. Closure of accounts

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Subject to Article 32(1), within 10 working days of the receipt of a request from the account holder [^key-38b60120c47c7b4eaf185942a4f62567]... the national administrator must[^key-d268e114e9db5df99792cc5cef75ddad] close the account.
