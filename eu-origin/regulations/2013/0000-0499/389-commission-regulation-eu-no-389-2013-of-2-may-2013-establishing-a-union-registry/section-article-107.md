# Commission Regulation (EU) No 389/2013 of 2 May 2013 establishing a Union Registry pursuant to Directive 2003/87/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council, Decisions No 280/2004/EC and No 406/2009/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Commission Regulations (EU) No 920/2010 and No 1193/2011 (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 107

> Text as at 2021-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 107. Processing of information and personal data

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. The Secretary of State and national administrator must[^c-eb4b0c3d11595ba1de91b94046fca8ac] ensure that the Registry only stores and processes[^c-a4b5baa1fdc17c898248eb5d97830d46] the information concerning the accounts, account holders and account representatives as set out in Table III-I of Annex III [^c-0b1d05795cf069f89aae8239fbae1c67]... and Table VIII-I of Annex VIII.

2.[^c-5ba3b69bcceeb2b8e09b24d8cd52747b] The Secretary of State and the national administrator must ensure that no special categories of personal data (within the meaning given in Article 9(1) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, as amended from time to time) are recorded in the Registry.

3. The Secretary of State and national administrator must[^c-5d0e831f447f716e9e9ae333111c6047] ensure that only personal data related to transactions that transfer Kyoto units are transferred to the ITL.
