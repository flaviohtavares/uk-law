# Commission Regulation (EU) No 389/2013 of 2 May 2013 establishing a Union Registry pursuant to Directive 2003/87/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council, Decisions No 280/2004/EC and No 406/2009/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Commission Regulations (EU) No 920/2010 and No 1193/2011 (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 32

> Text as at 2021-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 32. Positive balance on accounts under closure

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. If there is a positive balance of [^key-f40a9a7091a95385e42aef9e8ce541bd]... Kyoto units on an account which the national administrator[^key-e60598e7028e630c22484cb09ed62b17] is to close in accordance with Article 27[^key-fa5f0d50b11137c0a6ea04033899996e], the national administrator[^key-a6ccfd727b0e2ab82d584e3199ff6b82] must[^key-6e03d8cd18e5b9c6082d27024117afcb] request the account holder to specify another account to which such [^key-f40a9a7091a95385e42aef9e8ce541bd]... Kyoto units shall be transferred. If the account holder has not responded to the national administrator’s[^key-f1ee913a3b4320015169de8bb4d54a56] request within 40 working days, the national administrator must transfer the [^key-fd38ac2a4de12cc043af646b51d9cdcc]... Kyoto units to a party holding account[^key-f98c990f72a1b69b40e64beff0609a27].

2. If there is a positive balance of [^key-97abd074a967a6a906a9ed32be323ba6]... Kyoto units on an account to which access was suspended in accordance with Article 34, the Secretary of State[^key-87766dabf67875bdf972f1bddf1dca63] may require that the [^key-031e63f0fc578ea28285b1802b8168a0]... Kyoto units are transferred immediately to a party holding account[^key-af92ea241bb9ef3c00f00128c76b4e3d].
