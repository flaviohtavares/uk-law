# Commission Regulation (EU) No 389/2013 of 2 May 2013 establishing a Union Registry pursuant to Directive 2003/87/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council, Decisions No 280/2004/EC and No 406/2009/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Commission Regulations (EU) No 920/2010 and No 1193/2011 (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 96

> Text as at 2021-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 96. Suspension of all access due to a security breach or a security risk

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. The Secretary of State[^key-d1015b4e8139dd8fc33b1970413638e3] may [^key-cf4c851b2e396f6b261cd50226ba7d67]... suspend access to the Registry[^key-7ef9e175d975981ef8b968c8adc57c0b] or any part thereof where the Secretary of State[^key-6b48904ed14ce67c8f9c2b17d7f5ef60] has a reasonable suspicion that there is a breach of security of the Registry or that there exists a serious security risk to the Registry that threatens the integrity of the system [^key-b43d3560fa8e163fcb1a54b86c785323]....

2.[^key-4fdbe53cf4dc89877e0ce26aace6869e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

3. If the national administrator[^key-7e5ec3ffcf37d4873fbe9ea4a8314635] becomes aware of a situation that requires the suspension of all access to the accounts that it manages in accordance with this Regulation, it must[^key-aa4651dbac550a87abae9b49bedf8448] inform [^key-1d3865f32844cee968bbbcce74bd1c74]... account holders with such prior notice of the suspension as is practicable. [^key-0642d80cb9090e12e4eb42f550c0f50c]...

4. The notice referred to in paragraph 3 must[^key-1e25573e47bd5d08c0924edf061887f3] include the likely duration of the suspension and must be clearly displayed on the public area of the Registry’s[^key-c492b308363c5de80a7c4926e9076211] web site.
