# Commission Regulation (EU) No 389/2013 of 2 May 2013 establishing a Union Registry pursuant to Directive 2003/87/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council, Decisions No 280/2004/EC and No 406/2009/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Commission Regulations (EU) No 920/2010 and No 1193/2011 (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 18

> Text as at 2021-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 18. Opening person holding ... accounts ...[^c-659baa5745dcc6405a31aba64d699834][^c-0225079e79e135d3326f35fd2fb5b45d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. A request for opening a person holding account [^c-cb86097ecf0660182918f5f6fd3a6b58]... must[^c-bdece77448ff97f8417fd495e0400cec] be submitted to the national administrator by the prospective account holder. The prospective account holder must provide information as required by the national administrator, which must include, at a minimum, the information set out in Annex IV.

2. The Secretary of State may[^c-e6488a505c3459010405fe4cdd492d1c] require as a condition for opening a person holding [^c-818ff0d3afe42c9a4a62251f69204caf]... account that the prospective account holders have their permanent residence or registration in the United Kingdom[^c-091bd91f7e72339b3213c21e69acf936].

3. The Secretary of State may[^c-4d2219a571bbc4194ee24b7b5ed19215] require as a condition for opening a person holding [^c-de93b49303df2b9c91c7f06e935edca5]... account that prospective account holders are registered for value added tax (VAT) in the United Kingdom[^c-15629015973022414bd8070edafa30de].

4. Within 20 working days of the receipt of a complete set of information in accordance with paragraph 1 of this Article and Article 24, the national administrator must[^c-de69ea82e92c672c3efba919d7ca520b] open a person holding account [^c-3af9ea6607bdfe5793016280424dfeb3]... in the [^c-6815dc085dcc78fbc639e6986012207f]... Registry or inform the prospective account holder of the refusal to open the account, pursuant to Article 22.
