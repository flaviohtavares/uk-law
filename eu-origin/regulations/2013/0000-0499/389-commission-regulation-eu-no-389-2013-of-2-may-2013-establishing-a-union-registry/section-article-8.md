# Commission Regulation (EU) No 389/2013 of 2 May 2013 establishing a Union Registry pursuant to Directive 2003/87/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council, Decisions No 280/2004/EC and No 406/2009/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Commission Regulations (EU) No 920/2010 and No 1193/2011 (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 8

> Text as at 2021-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 8. National administrator[^c-a153d65d09dfc6c1e75f4b38eb3e6d23]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The national administrator must administer the Registry in accordance with this Regulation and relevant international climate law.
