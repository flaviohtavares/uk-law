# Commission Regulation (EU) No 389/2013 of 2 May 2013 establishing a Union Registry pursuant to Directive 2003/87/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council, Decisions No 280/2004/EC and No 406/2009/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Commission Regulations (EU) No 920/2010 and No 1193/2011 (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 33

> Text as at 2021-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 33. Closure of accounts and removal of authorised representative on the national administrator’s initiative[^c-b3f5b86ae81c60f5c61dfd5064916bb3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. If the situation giving rise to the suspension of access to accounts pursuant to Article 34 is not resolved within a reasonable period despite repeated notifications, the Secretary of State[^c-9b26be01d8a24d7a6a6f4b596009040d] may instruct the national administrator to close [^c-6bde44147cfbfd4361193c2d6d640d1c]... those accounts for which access is suspended until the Secretary of State determines that the situation giving rise to the suspension no longer subsists.

2. If a person holding account [^c-daa512b589869f824eb5ea706a6ffbd0]... has a zero balance and no transactions have been recorded for a period of one year, the national administrator may notify the account holder that the person holding account [^c-daa512b589869f824eb5ea706a6ffbd0]... will be closed within 40 working days unless the national administrator receives a request that the account be maintained. If the national administrator does not receive any such request from the account holder, the national administrator may close the account.

3.[^c-41ce1f3b0f5de275da0db3e36f7e62de] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

4. The national administrator may remove an authorised representative [^c-db776c0525cb8f58873e7e33ca777869]... if it considers that the approval of the authorised representative [^c-db776c0525cb8f58873e7e33ca777869]... should have been refused in accordance with Article 24(3), and in particular if it discovers that the documents and identification information provided upon nomination were incomplete, out-of-date or otherwise inaccurate or false.

5.[^c-1126885fc38c90ca208359a33a9d90f7] In accordance with Article 110c, the account holder may appeal against—

- (a) a decision to close an account under paragraph 1 within the period of 30 days beginning with the day on which the account is closed; or
- (b) a decision to remove an authorised representative under paragraph 4 within the period of 30 days beginning with the day on which the authorised representative is removed.
