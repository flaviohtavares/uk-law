# Commission Regulation (EU) No 389/2013 of 2 May 2013 establishing a Union Registry pursuant to Directive 2003/87/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council, Decisions No 280/2004/EC and No 406/2009/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Commission Regulations (EU) No 920/2010 and No 1193/2011 (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 25

> Text as at 2021-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 25. Updating of account information and information on authorised representatives

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. All account holders must[^c-7bd9c9a5c80f0067fd0f813fa2b7ae53] notify the national administrator within 10 working days of changes to the information submitted for the opening of an account. In addition, account holders must confirm to the national administrator by 31 December each year that the information for their account remains complete, up-to-date, accurate and true.

2.[^c-0774d46d138a147890f6c72e040871f2] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

3. The notification of change must[^c-5060062109367a19019b0fc1bff1bf07] be supported by information as required by the national administrator in conformity with this Section. Within 15 working days of the receipt of such a notification and supporting information, the national administrator[^c-b46b92c2015de044cf26ce93330a4e59] must determine whether to[^c-890edb1a35944a18bbc8eac4a33c6def] approve the update of the information. The national administrator[^c-e4f4597af4e3dfb0c6128b3965f881ab] may refuse to update the information in accordance with Article 24(4) and (5). The account holder must be notified of any such refusal. If the national administrator refuses to update the information, the account holder may appeal against the decision in accordance with Article 110c.[^c-7227e80465e9d2b82f2de6b730eeca1d]

4. At least once every three years, the national administrator must[^c-ae0621e26ed600a4da50e1796179dc66] review whether the information submitted for the opening of an account remains complete, up-to-date, accurate and true, and must request that the account holder notify any changes as appropriate.

5.[^c-1ee88ad25af963c7d11b97b9305942db] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

6. No[^c-d31dbe70e1e871404606e4ca104d6350] account holder may sell or divest of the ownership of its account to another person.

7. An authorised representative [^c-adf537d725c0c9d78fe2175648abd53f]... may not transfer its status as such to another person.

8. An account holder may request the removal of an authorised representative from an account. Upon receipt of the request the national administrator must[^c-9a4ea8a9ab5bf48c94677ccab3b35717] suspend the access of the authorised representative [^c-917ddae2a849ac1d004b7cc85b31c1eb].... Within 10 working days of the receipt of the request, the national administrator[^c-0cef0dd11273c104629188b7e0b00793] must remove the authorised representative.

9. An account holder may nominate new authorised representatives [^c-2caebb608dbc5c1e5d66edf83f959f15]... in accordance with Article 24.

10.[^c-e8a383178f55e8158f9d3b0538f1f07e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

11.[^c-0c852b888657f249b708d83036c40979] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
