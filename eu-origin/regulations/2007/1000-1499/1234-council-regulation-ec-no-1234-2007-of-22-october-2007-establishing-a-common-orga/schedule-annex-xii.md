# Council Regulation (EC) No 1234/2007 of 22 October 2007 establishing a common organisation of agricultural markets and on specific provisions for certain agricultural products (Single CMO Regulation), ANNEX XII

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## ANNEX XII — DEFINITIONS AND DESIGNATIONS IN RESPECT OF MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS REFERRED TO IN ARTICLE 114(1)[^c000038]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force.**

[^c000038]I.

Definitions

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

[^c000038]II.

Use of the term ‘milk’

[^c000038]1.

The term ‘milk’ means exclusively the normal mammary secretion obtained from one or more milkings without either addition thereto or extraction therefrom.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

[^c000038]2.

For the purposes of this Annex, ‘milk products’ means products derived exclusively from milk, on the understanding that substances necessary for their manufacture may be added provided that those substances are not used for the purpose of replacing, in whole or in part, any milk constituent.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

[^c000038]3.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

[^c000038]4.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

[^c000038]III.

Use of designations in respect of competing products

[^c000038]1.

The designations referred to in point II of this Annex may not be used for any product other than those referred to in that point.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

[^c000038]2.

In respect of a product other than those described in point II of this Annex, no label, commercial document, publicity material or any form of advertising as defined in Article 2(1) of Council Directive 84/450/EEC of 10 September 1984 concerning misleading and comparative advertising or any form of presentation, may be used which claims, implies or suggests that the product is a dairy product.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

[^c000038]IV.

Lists of products; communications

[^c000038]1.

Member States shall make available to the Commission an indicative list of the products which they regard as corresponding in their territory to the products referred to in the second subparagraph of point III(1).

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
