## Article 111. Preparation, update and review of MANCPs

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. The person designated from time to time in accordance with Article 109(2)[^key-ae35e9ec9f7d74bc0acc8d12f9871944] shall ensure that the MANCP provided for in Article 109(1) is made available to the public, with the exception of those parts of the plan the disclosure of which could undermine the effectiveness of official controls.

2. The MANCP shall be regularly updated to adjust it to changes to the rules referred to in Article 1(2), and reviewed to take account at least of the following factors:

- (a) the emergence of new diseases, pests of plants or other risks to human, animal or plant health, animal welfare or, in the case of GMOs and plant protection products, also to the environment;
- (b) significant changes to the structure, management or operation of the competent authorities in Great Britain[^key-0b5b98a6af1763c69533a8a2f07b1c3e];
- (c) the outcome of the competent authorities'[^key-0c07e500fc4e9d789ad935bfbe7969a7] official controls;
- (d)[^key-67b22f7b8eb621ee803e0ce49521f272] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (e) scientific findings; and
- (f) the outcome of any[^key-e00f30ed6c16f5b4e6b7ed0fa90a9ec3] official controls performed by the competent authorities of a third country in Great Britain[^key-1cb53108c2ed8c653c2b5fd9b0f297c2].
