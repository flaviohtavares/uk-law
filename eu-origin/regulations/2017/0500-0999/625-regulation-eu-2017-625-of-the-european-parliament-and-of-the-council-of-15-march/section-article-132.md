## Article 132. General functionalities of the computerised information management system[^key-cee40dbd1bd6a5816c76d7ad5d41cee0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The computerised information management system[^key-00226a3b3610ee46420c5550f98d0ac3] shall:

- ((a)) allow for the computerised handling and exchange of information, data and documents necessary for the performance of official controls, resulting from the performance of official controls or the recording of the performance or outcome of official controls in all cases where this Regulation, the rules referred to in Article 1(2) or the regulations[^key-70758e9e360cf7ec45fcb4c76af491e5] provided for in Articles 16 to 27 provide for the exchange between competent authorities[^key-b0c7652cf479e8465b9c824b6b4125a3], and where appropriate with other authorities and the operators, of such information, data and documents;
- ((b)) provide a mechanism for the exchange of data, information and documents in accordance with Articles 102 to  107[^key-aec4d30cc2d6388621d818cc0b766139];
- ((c)) [^key-f845fc15b1c891a9ec91ff1cde1b955f]...
- ((d)) allow for the production, handling and transmission, including in electronic form, of the journey log referred to in Article 5(4) of Regulation (EC) No 1/2005, of the records obtained by the navigation system referred to in Article 6(9) of that Regulation, of official certificates and of the CHED referred to in Article 56 of this Regulation; [^key-d9c266d64cea9c2430a1ee197a232274]...
- ((e)) [^key-d9c266d64cea9c2430a1ee197a232274]...
