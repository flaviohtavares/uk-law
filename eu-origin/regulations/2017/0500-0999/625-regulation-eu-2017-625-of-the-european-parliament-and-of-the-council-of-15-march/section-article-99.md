## Article 99. Obligations of the appropriate authority[^key-b04f340f20b5df8540ce3c4934ebabc2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. The appropriate authority[^key-9820d07dfe6410d1746d188160d82724] shall publish and update, whenever necessary, the list of:

- (a)[^key-d845af9fcc59c282ed81bc2b3a9b4266] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) national[^key-fd10c3d03af7ac41513c68d8a6c0fd77] reference centres for animal welfare provided for in Article 95;
- (c) national[^key-3495789a236433bcf6e7ea8f9993dcc1] reference centres for the authenticity and integrity of the agri-food chain provided for in Article 97.

2.[^key-58418735d7207a0a6d82e3f1fd74011f] Subject to paragraph 2A, the appropriate authority may make regulations supplementing this Regulation concerning the establishment of requirements, responsibilities and tasks for the national reference centre for animal welfare and the national reference centre for the authenticity and integrity of the agri-food chain, in addition to those laid down in Articles 95 to 98.

(2A.) The appropriate authority may only exercise the power to make regulations under paragraph 1 where the appropriate authority considers that new or emerging risks, new or emerging animal diseases or pests of plants, or new legal requirements make the exercise of this power appropriate.
