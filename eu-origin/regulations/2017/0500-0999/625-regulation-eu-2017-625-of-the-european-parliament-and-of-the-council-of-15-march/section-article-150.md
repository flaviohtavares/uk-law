## Article 150. Transitional measures related to the repeal of Directive 96/23/EC

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. Competent authorities shall continue to perform the official controls necessary to detect the presence of the substances and groups of residues listed in legislation which transposed[^key-a2d625f3c34825f033c557e4d4129458] Annex I to Directive 96/23/EC, in accordance with [^key-50169dba04d008391cd577020420dccc]... Annexes II, III and IV to that Directive, instead of the corresponding provisions of this Regulation, until the date specified in[^key-b4463426105972a95a288b3aa784fbb8] regulations made under[^key-46b3664fe71bdf933f61be43f057b2f9] paragraph 3 of this Article.

2. Legislation which transposed[^key-04c0472220e1a6faffdbbedc7d00e393] Article 29(1) and (2) of Directive 96/23/EC shall continue to apply instead of the corresponding provisions of this Regulation until  the date specified in[^key-ef5eb6343b1ba526dbd42de17e16f110] regulations made under[^key-71ed6947ff5a2d85ef9f036c6a6c815c] paragraph 3 of this Article.

3.[^key-dabcdcf35928fd92debaaa4c103056d7] The appropriate authority may make regulations specifying—

- (a)) the date for the purposes of paragraph 1;
- (b)) the date for the purposes of paragraph 2.
