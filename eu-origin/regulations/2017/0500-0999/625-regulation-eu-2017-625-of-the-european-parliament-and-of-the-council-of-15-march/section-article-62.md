## Article 62. Withdrawal of the designation of border control posts

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. Where border control posts cease to comply with the requirements referred to in Article 64, the competent authorities[^c-9aab160205f4878b730897e430a3ee10] shall:

- (a) withdraw the designation provided for in Article 59(1) for all or for certain categories of animals and goods for which the designation was made; and
- (b) remove those border control posts from the lists referred to in Article 60(1), for the categories of animals and goods for which the designation is withdrawn.

2. The competent authority must inform the other competent authorities of the constituent territories of the British Islands[^c-c96a9e520e55a0f2ee40cfc12124c83e] of the withdrawal of the designation of a border control post as provided for in paragraph 1 and of the reasons for such withdrawal.

3.[^c-d6ecd23eaa267c4f073a828c399348c3] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

4. This Article shall be without prejudice to competent authorities'[^c-bbd8c3cb48a27e4abdf4671c315e2b72] competence to decide on the withdrawal of designation of border control posts for reasons other than those referred to in this Regulation.
