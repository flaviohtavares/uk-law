## Article 123. Programme of ... controls in third countries[^c-0e34c0e746e8a001c95f8c719c218239]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The competent authority[^c-025a5852eb5e67cc0d8131aeaedf0b0a] shall communicate its programme of controls in third countries to other competent authorities in Great Britain[^c-eac84ef6f271c36d0d3ea164eaad7f60] in advance and shall report on the results. The competent authority may amend that programme to take account of developments in the areas governed by the rules referred to in Article 1(2). Any such amendment shall be communicated to the other competent authorities in Great Britain in advance.
