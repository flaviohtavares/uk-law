# Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/1485 of 2 August 2017 establishing a guideline on electricity transmission system operation (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 98

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 98. Validation of year-ahead availability plans ...[^c-538611427006f43f5098d89fccb347a2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. Each TSO shall analyse whether any outage planning incompatibility arises when taking into account all the preliminary year-ahead availability plans.

2.[^c-df32c5a189e057433f68c73a81cb4629] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

3. If a TSO detects an outage planning incompatibility, the involved TSOs [^c-825982517ed5de0dcbbe1fd447002566]... shall jointly identify a solution in coordination with the concerned outage planning agents, DSOs and CDSOs, using the means at their disposal, while respecting to the extent possible the availability plans submitted by outage planning agents, which are neither a TSO [^c-0c66e430754c7e8456e4241924ed3df2]... nor a DSO or a CDSO, and developed in accordance with Articles 95 and 96. Where a solution is identified, all TSOs [^c-0dd716395ba4990b8f3a2527b890756d]... shall update and validate the year-ahead availability plans for all relevant assets.

4. Where no solution is found for an outage planning incompatibility each concerned TSO, subject to approval by the regulatory authority[^c-3107ba37e2324dde82840ca9c4027791], shall:

- (a) force to ‘available’ status all the ‘unavailable’ or ‘testing’ statuses for the relevant assets involved in an outage planning incompatibility during the period concerned; and
- (b) notify to the regulatory authority[^c-ed3d8f91a4f824e08cb156c4610c1be2], the affected DSOs or CDSOs, if any, and the affected outage planning agents of the actions taken including the rationale for such actions, the impact reported by affected outage planning agents and the DSOs or CDSOs where relevant.

5. All TSOs [^c-45db2b4b787db488b36bc809217e4664]... shall consequently update and validate the year-ahead availability plans for all relevant assets.
