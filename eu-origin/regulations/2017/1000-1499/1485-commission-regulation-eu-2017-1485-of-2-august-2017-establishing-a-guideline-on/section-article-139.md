# Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/1485 of 2 August 2017 establishing a guideline on electricity transmission system operation (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 139

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 139. Basic structure

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. All TSOs of the GB synchronous area[^c-9b0b2aed1fa4e0cdb17866ecb347a15a] shall specify the load-frequency-control structure for the synchronous area in the synchronous area operational agreement. Each TSO shall be responsible for implementing the load-frequency-control structure of the GB synchronous area[^c-244239ef4f74eb4c680d563cfae07514] and operating in accordance with it.

2. The load-frequency control structure of the GB synchronous area[^c-d06809599900d05711d9ae7a745464ff] shall include:

- (a) a process activation structure in accordance with Article 140; and
- (b) a process responsibility structure in accordance with Article 141.
