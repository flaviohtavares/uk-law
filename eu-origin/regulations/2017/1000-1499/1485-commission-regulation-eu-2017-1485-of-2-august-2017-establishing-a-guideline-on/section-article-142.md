# Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/1485 of 2 August 2017 establishing a guideline on electricity transmission system operation (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 142

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 142. Frequency containment process

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. The control target of FCP shall be the stabilization of the system frequency by activation of FCR.

2. The overall characteristic for FCR activation in the GB synchronous area[^key-01cbf579b83c8ffbf2b59f17e37b6b98] shall reflect a monotonic decrease of the FCR activation as a function of the frequency deviation.
