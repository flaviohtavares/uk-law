# Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/1485 of 2 August 2017 establishing a guideline on electricity transmission system operation (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 2

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 2. Scope

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. The rules and requirements set out in this Regulation shall apply to the following SGUs within the jurisdiction of Great Britain[^key-09134872985d6363aa4a144ff5f5b3a9]:

- (a) existing and new power generating modules that are, or would be, classified as type B, C and D in accordance with the criteria set out in Article 5 of Commission Regulation (EU) 2016/631[^f00003];
- (b) existing and new transmission-connected demand facilities;
- (c) existing and new transmission-connected closed distribution systems;
- (d) existing and new demand facilities, closed distribution systems and third parties if they provide demand response directly to the TSO [^key-68d41f338b865eeb6f8ba182fbf1d53a]...;
- (e) providers of redispatching of power generating modules or demand facilities by means of aggregation and providers of active power reserve in accordance with Title 8 of Part IV of this Regulation; and
- (f) existing and new high voltage direct current (‘HVDC’) systems in accordance with the criteria in Article 3(1) of Commission Regulation (EU) 2016/1447[^f00005].

2. Except for Article 13,[^key-012111449a9fdb99da38d7bbf1bf3a63] this Regulation shall apply to all transmission systems, distribution systems and interconnections within the jurisdiction of Great Britain[^key-707fb01e58bf2362c9067385d6963259], except transmission systems and distribution systems or parts of the transmission systems and distribution systems located in islands which are not part of the GB synchronous area[^key-0d1ba11d6dd0ad6605e73a691ccd67a2].

3. Except for Article 13, this Regulation applies to all TSOs that are certified by the regulatory authority, to the extent they are operating within the jurisdiction of Great Britain.[^key-c1c351bddf2f28c5298a4c7318193709] Where a TSO does not have a function relevant to one or more obligations under this Regulation, the regulatory authority may[^key-a65cae8e6b7a90c1961ffb476a903bb2] provide that the responsibility of a TSO to comply with one or some or all obligations under this Regulation is assigned to one or more specific TSOs.

4.[^key-2e61d1cf9dfbb21a5603c37bba945a7a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

5. Where the requirements under this Regulation are to be established by a relevant system operator that is not a TSO, the regulatory authority[^key-a9b93f1f83d0a0f0f665ecdddbee442a] may provide that instead the TSO be responsible for establishing the relevant requirements.
