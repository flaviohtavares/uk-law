# Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/1485 of 2 August 2017 establishing a guideline on electricity transmission system operation (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 16

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 16. Annual report on load-frequency control

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. By 30 September, the[^c-fabe013801cef198fc7e2d76e5396d1d] transmission system operator[^c-d6128d20e7e3e47d3d0b2a843f1f6a24] shall publish an annual report on load-frequency control based on the information provided by the TSOs in accordance with paragraph 2. The annual report on load-frequency control shall include the information listed in paragraph 2 [^c-f956d4327cbe326dcd19bc7d36e92713]....

2. TSOs shall notify to the[^c-820364f9137187bbd7cad12f15fdccd5] transmission system operator, by 1 March every year, the following information for the previous year:

- (a) the identification of the LFC blocks, LFC areas and monitoring areas [^c-b16be3fd54c734a9e3d472e1ef04c49c]...;
- (b) the identification of LFC blocks that [^c-e817b9015486575f21d3a3255e378749]... contain LFC areas and monitoring areas [^c-ef5325550728b8da09daa73a4c6f2b7c]...;
- (c)[^c-6ac1635e17c9c8c1803e2ee861371f75] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d) the data related to the frequency quality evaluation criteria for each synchronous area and each LFC block in subparagraphs (a) and (b)[^c-175017af674de48b66a0bd34e40286f4] covering each month of at least 2 previous calendar years;
- (e) the FCR obligation and the initial FCR obligation of each TSO [^c-f52889ed8a22a78f15fcf03f08f1de63]... covering each month of at least 2 previous calendar years; and
- (f) a description and date of implementation of any mitigation measures and ramping requirements to alleviate deterministic frequency deviations taken in the previous calendar year in accordance with Articles 137 and 138, in which TSOs [^c-0534ca21fc767a472bad89eb1af5a9f0]... were involved.

3. The data provided by the TSOs shall cover the preceding year. The information concerning [^c-473661b994f6418b54fc869536b6e986]... LFC blocks, LFC areas and monitoring areas in subparagraphs (a) and (b)[^c-d8b0f25d2e653cf47aa3115afd979195] shall be reported once. Where these areas change, this information shall be reported by 1 March of the following year.

4. Where appropriate, all TSOs of a synchronous area or LFC block shall cooperate in collecting the data listed in paragraph 2.
