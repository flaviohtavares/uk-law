# Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/1485 of 2 August 2017 establishing a guideline on electricity transmission system operation (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 184

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 184. Information on operational agreements

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. Each TSO shall share the contents of its synchronous area operational agreement with the regulatory authority[^key-9055a1baf2e78ef032fec6fefed84036] no later than 1 month before its entry into force.

2. All TSOs in the GB synchronous area shall publish[^key-dc7445884bbe60e5b56e0060110067b6] the contents of their synchronous area operational agreement [^key-96f50722222539e82a90242461222cce]... no later than 1 week after its entry into force.

3. Each TSO of each LFC block shall share the contents of its LFC block operational agreement with the regulatory authority[^key-bfca2930c7bdba951eb7c3a90b77f57c].
