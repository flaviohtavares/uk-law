# Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/1485 of 2 August 2017 establishing a guideline on electricity transmission system operation (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 187

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 187. Information on FCR

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. All TSOs of the GB synchronous area[^c-43c287b79e70da82aa9040c15882a5b5] shall publish[^c-18a1839801d8755389cc8d7381a3316c] the dimensioning approach for FCR for their synchronous area in accordance with Article 153(2) [^c-54ec97c57cd9e5c031ae043c038f5a15]... at least 1 month before its applicability.

2. Where applicable, all TSOs of the GB synchronous area shall publish the total amount of reserve capacity on FCR and the shares of reserve capacity on FCR required for each TSO specified in accordance with Article 153(1) as the initial FCR obligation [^c-54ec97c57cd9e5c031ae043c038f5a15]... at least 1 month before their applicability.

3. All TSOs of the GB synchronous area shall publish the FCR properties established for their synchronous area in accordance with Article 154(2) and the additional requirements for FCR providing groups in accordance with Article 154(3) [^c-54ec97c57cd9e5c031ae043c038f5a15]... at least 3 months before their applicability.
