# Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/1485 of 2 August 2017 establishing a guideline on electricity transmission system operation (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 4

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 4. Objectives and regulatory aspects

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. This Regulation aims at:

- (a) determining common operational security requirements and principles;
- (b) determining common [^c-aedafaa5e692ce497fa94b379b5e582e]... system operational planning principles;
- (c) determining common load-frequency control processes and control structures;
- (d) ensuring the conditions for maintaining operational security [^c-25a3b302ba0fb697f41e6f2e69402aa1]...;
- (e) ensuring the conditions for maintaining a frequency quality level in the GB synchronous area[^c-bb6a1c9032f4a0cc9e49a00b247bbab9];
- (f) promoting the coordination of system operation and operational planning;
- (g) ensuring and enhancing the transparency and reliability of information on transmission system operation;
- (h) contributing to the efficient operation and development of the electricity transmission system and electricity sector [^c-a0bc9b310d095830b949a1b8f0029045]....

2. When applying this Regulation, the Secretary of State, the regulatory authority[^c-7edb3d99597bb0154e23351171d7ee16], and system operators shall:

- (a) apply the principles of proportionality and non-discrimination;
- (b) ensure transparency;
- (c) apply the principle of optimisation between the highest overall efficiency and lowest total costs for all parties involved;
- (d) ensure TSOs make use of market-based mechanisms as far as possible, to ensure network security and stability;
- (e) respect the responsibility assigned to the relevant TSO in order to ensure system security [^c-95f0612c94872be56491d61c7f3c19d3]...;
- (f) consult with relevant DSOs and take account of potential impacts on their system; and
- (g) take into consideration agreed [^c-6f38ffb1ccb69b59043fbf38feab2fc0]... standards and technical specifications.
