# Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/1485 of 2 August 2017 establishing a guideline on electricity transmission system operation (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 129

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 129. Criteria application process

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The criteria application process shall comprise:

- ((a)) the collection of frequency quality evaluation data; and
- ((b)) the calculation of frequency quality evaluation criteria.
