# Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/1485 of 2 August 2017 establishing a guideline on electricity transmission system operation (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 30

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 30. Short-circuit current

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Each TSO shall determine:

- ((a)) the maximum short-circuit current at which the rated capability of circuit breakers and other equipment is exceeded; and
- ((b)) the minimum short-circuit current for the correct operation of protection equipment.
