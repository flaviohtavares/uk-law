# Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/1485 of 2 August 2017 establishing a guideline on electricity transmission system operation (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 66

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 66. Year-ahead individual grid models

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. Each TSO shall determine a year-ahead individual grid model for each of the scenarios developed pursuant to Article 65, using its best estimates of the variables defined in Article 65(1). [^key-970ee3b3888f0fbf967fd8876fa2fd30]...

2. When defining its year-ahead individual grid model, each TSO shall:

- (a)[^key-7bfbeddc65fa4fcf469537b09335215e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) balance for each scenario the sum of:
  - ((i)) net exchanges on AC lines;
  - ((ii)) estimated power flows on HVDC systems;
  - ((iii)) load, including an estimation of losses; and
  - ((iv)) generation.

3. Each TSO shall include in its year-ahead individual grid models the aggregated power outputs for power generating facilities connected to distribution systems. Those aggregated power outputs shall:

- (a) be consistent with the structural data provided in accordance with the requirements of Articles [^key-f79cbe554ccea7df1eb35a96a0a26194]... 43, 45 and 48;
- (b) be consistent with the scenarios developed in accordance with Article 65; and
- (c) distinguish the type of primary energy source.
