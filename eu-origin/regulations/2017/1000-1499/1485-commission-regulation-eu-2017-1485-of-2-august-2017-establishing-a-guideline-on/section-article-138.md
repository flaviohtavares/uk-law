# Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/1485 of 2 August 2017 establishing a guideline on electricity transmission system operation (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 138

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 138. Mitigation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where the values calculated for the period of one calendar year concerning the frequency quality target parameters or the FRCE target parameters are outside the targets set for the GB synchronous area[^key-b1828dd1bef432f181a7e7069c07da07] or for the LFC block, all TSOs of the GB synchronous area[^key-bc60a2eef209d359dfafe30ba205ba7a] or of the relevant LFC block shall:

- ((a)) analyse whether the frequency quality target parameters or the FRCE target parameters will remain outside the targets set for the GB synchronous area or for the LFC block and in case of a justified risk that this may happen, analyse the causes and develop recommendations; and
- ((b)) develop mitigation measures to ensure that the targets for the GB synchronous area or for the LFC block can be met in the future.
