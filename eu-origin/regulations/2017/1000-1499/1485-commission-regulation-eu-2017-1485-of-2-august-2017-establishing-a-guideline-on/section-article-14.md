# Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/1485 of 2 August 2017 establishing a guideline on electricity transmission system operation (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 14

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 14. Monitoring

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. The regulatory authority[^c-f3f808ec8a61071dc0d177d1f0fb15a1] shall monitor the implementation of this Regulation [^c-1ce072adefe45bd28b662801d74af81f].... Monitoring shall cover at least the following matters:

- (a) operational security indicators in accordance with Article 15;
- (b)[^c-74913d60c8250dce2ac4e408b59ad461] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c)[^c-74913d60c8250dce2ac4e408b59ad461] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d)[^c-74913d60c8250dce2ac4e408b59ad461] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (e)[^c-74913d60c8250dce2ac4e408b59ad461] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (f)[^c-74913d60c8250dce2ac4e408b59ad461] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (g) identification of any difficulties concerning cooperation on secure system operation with neighbouring[^c-949b27a261cac318ad4ac792116c1281] TSOs.

2.[^c-b9cee3881cfa5e096835aa5e213cfa33] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

3. Relevant TSOs shall submit to the regulatory authority[^c-771b5287ad28d0837ee44bb77713996b] the information required to perform the tasks referred to in paragraph 1[^c-436a779be90577411905e0555b9737ea].

4.[^c-7b8a6a8ddac84c1784e193a8fe7cb56a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
