# Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/1485 of 2 August 2017 establishing a guideline on electricity transmission system operation (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 94

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 94. Provision of year-ahead availability plan proposals

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. Before 1 August of each calendar year, an outage planning agent other than a TSO [^key-c4e5b4bcd79ed83e64210ce6e1217c44]..., a DSO or a CDSO, shall submit to the TSO(s) [^key-c4e5b4bcd79ed83e64210ce6e1217c44]..., and where relevant to the DSO(s) or CDSO(s), an availability plan covering the following calendar year for each of its relevant assets.

2. The TSO(s) referred to in paragraph 1 shall endeavour to examine the requests for amendment of an availability plan when received. Where this is not possible, it shall examine the requests for amendment of an availability plan after the year-ahead outage coordination has been finalised.

3. The TSO(s) referred to in paragraph 1 shall examine the requests for amendment of an availability plan after the year-ahead outage coordination has been finalised:

- (a) following the order in which the requests were received; and
- (b) applying the procedure established in accordance with Article 100.
