# Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/1485 of 2 August 2017 establishing a guideline on electricity transmission system operation (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 153

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 153. FCR dimensioning

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. All TSOs of the GB synchronous area[^c-bad9fa4a6ea8bb74e73141ec4dcd32d1] shall determine, at least annually, the reserve capacity for FCR required for the synchronous area and the initial FCR obligation of each TSO in accordance with paragraph 2.

2. All TSOs of the GB synchronous area[^c-0a1d388efb453d17dec0e2a3407f96bc] shall specify dimensioning rules in the synchronous area operational agreement in accordance with the following criteria:

- (a) the reserve capacity for FCR required for the synchronous area shall cover at least the reference incident [^c-feb7851580886fb3117d2c6e4f95e87e]...
- (b)[^c-63fdf99880e704e0e98fd3dfe285bad2] the size of the reference incident shall be either—
  - (i) the largest imbalance that may result from an instantaneous change of active power such as that of a single power generating module, single demand facility, or single HVDC interconnector or from a tripping of an AC line; or
  - (ii) the maximum instantaneous loss of active power consumption due to the tripping of one or two connection points,

  and the reference incident shall be determined separately for positive and negative direction;
- (c)[^c-7dc28f8c6e73909975c6f15f0124cde5] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d) the shares of the reserve capacity on FCR required for each TSO as initial FCR obligation shall be based on the sum of the net generation and consumption of its control area divided by the sum of net generation and consumption of the GB synchronous area[^c-4f83445174f2a2d45d999e334e864d0f] over a period of 1 year.
