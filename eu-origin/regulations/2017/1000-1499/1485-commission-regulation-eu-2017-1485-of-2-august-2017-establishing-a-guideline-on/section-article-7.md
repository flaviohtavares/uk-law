# Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/1485 of 2 August 2017 establishing a guideline on electricity transmission system operation (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 7

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 7. Amendments to the terms and conditions or methodologies of TSOs

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. Where the regulatory authority requires[^key-6d2d0dd5319b93227acd993f5b3127b5] an amendment in order to approve the terms and conditions or methodologies submitted in accordance with paragraphs 3 and 4[^key-c134ad58c0afc4ca4fc2efb169bfa051] of Article 6, the relevant TSOs shall submit a proposal for amended terms and conditions or methodologies for approval within 2 months following the requirement from the regulatory authority[^key-281687534cfddac7f5d32677308f2ddc]. The regulatory authority[^key-256e4a963d6e795d6fd05b62533b963c] shall decide on the amended terms and conditions or methodologies within 2 months following their submission.

2. Where the regulatory authority[^key-284810db78d81f9ae03e08deb0a3540c] requires an amendment in order to approve the terms and conditions or methodologies submitted in accordance with Article 6(4), the relevant TSO shall submit a proposal for amended terms and conditions or methodologies for approval within 2 months following the requirement from the regulatory authority[^key-c412ae19b0a2c700dcdde0c0afded113]. The regulatory authority[^key-b4a199399fcd7b674e53f12facf3f78c] shall decide on the amended terms and conditions or methodologies within 2 months following their submission.

3.[^key-93553fd47143828aa841b5548fe8c136] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

4. TSOs responsible for developing a proposal for terms and conditions or methodologies or the regulatory authority[^key-60db05a7d502785130b4f81b26b1cc37] in accordance with paragraphs 2, 3 and 4 of Article 6 may request amendments of those terms and conditions or methodologies. Proposals for amendment to the terms and conditions or methodologies shall be submitted to consultation if applicable in accordance with the procedure set out in Article 11 and approved in accordance with the procedure set out in Articles 5 and 6.
