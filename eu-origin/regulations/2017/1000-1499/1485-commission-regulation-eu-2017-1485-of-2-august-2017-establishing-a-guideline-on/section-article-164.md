# Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/1485 of 2 August 2017 establishing a guideline on electricity transmission system operation (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 164

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 164. Sharing of FCR within a synchronous area

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A TSO shall not share FCR with other TSOs of the GB synchronous area[^c-c4e2a914763aa0fb8f159bc3b83009a7] to fulfil its FCR obligation and to reduce the total amount of FCR of the synchronous area in accordance with Article 153.
