# Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/1485 of 2 August 2017 establishing a guideline on electricity transmission system operation (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 144

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 144. Reserve replacement process

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. The control target of the RRP shall be to fulfil at least one of the following goals by activation of RR:

- (a) progressively restore the activated FRR;
- (b) support FRR activation;
- (c) [^key-79455a8d8d32d65056c8df768997615e]... to progressively restore the activated FCR and FRR.

2. The RRP shall be operated through instructions for manual RR activation in order to fulfil the control target in accordance with paragraph 1.
