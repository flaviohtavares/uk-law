# Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/1485 of 2 August 2017 establishing a guideline on electricity transmission system operation (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 190

> Text as at 2024-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 190. Information on sharing and exchange

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. All TSOs of the GB synchronous area[^key-1891c2b840b47542ecc68aa293fed8eb] shall publish[^key-5eaf1c928271cb40c8b472b90356d93f] the annual compilations of the agreements for the sharing of FRR and for the sharing of RR for each LFC block within the synchronous area [^key-91501f0ae6ddf866b0931f94e1a9e721]... in accordance with Articles 188(3) and 189(2). Those compilations shall include the following information:

- (a) the identity of the LFC blocks where there is an agreement for the sharing of FRR or RR; and
- (b) the share of FRR and RR reduced due to each agreement for the sharing of FRR or RR.

2.[^key-004accc76a3c089e241b137939a34db0] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

3. Where applicable, all TSOs shall publish the information on the exchange of [^key-5a90dfcbbcbd65dcfe9277cc9dc89d8e]... FRR and RR.
