# Council Regulation (EU) 2017/1939 of 12 October 2017 implementing enhanced cooperation on the establishment of the European Public Prosecutor’s Office (‘the EPPO’), section Article 112

> Text as at 2017-10-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 112. Administrative inquiries

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The administrative activities of the EPPO shall be subject to the inquiries of the European Ombudsman in accordance with Article 228 TFEU.
