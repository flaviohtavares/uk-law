# Council Implementing Regulation (EU) No 282/2011 of 15 March 2011 laying down implementing measures for Directive 2006/112/EC on the common system of value added tax (recast), section Article 3

> Text as at 2020-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 3.** Without prejudice to point (b) of the first paragraph of Article 59a of Directive 2006/112/EC, the supply of the following services is not subject to VAT if the supplier demonstrates that the place of supply determined in accordance with Subsections 3 and 4 of Section 4 of Chapter V of this Regulation is outside the Community:

- ((a)) from 1 January 2013, the service referred to in the first subparagraph of Article 56(2) of Directive 2006/112/EC;
- ((b)) from 1 January 2015, the services listed in Article 58 of Directive 2006/112/EC;
- ((c)) the services listed in Article 59 of Directive 2006/112/EC.
