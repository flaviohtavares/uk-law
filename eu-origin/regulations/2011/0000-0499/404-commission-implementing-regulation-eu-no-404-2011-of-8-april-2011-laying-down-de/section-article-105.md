# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 404/2011 of 8 April 2011 laying down detailed rules for the implementation of Council Regulation (EC) No 1224/2009 establishing a Community control system for ensuring compliance with the rules of the Common Fisheries Policy, section Article 105

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 105. Preparation of inspection

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. Without prejudice to benchmarks defined in specific control and inspection programmes and in Article 9 of Regulation (EC) No 1005/2008, an inspection of a fishing vessel shall take place in port or on landing, on the following occasions:

- (a) routinely subject to a sampling methodology based on a risk-based management; or
- (b) where it is suspected of failing to comply with the Fisheries Rules[^key-fab8a5128c0fc5ffad00be17893e0621].

2. In cases referred to in paragraph 1(b) and without prejudice to the last sentence of Article 106(2) of this Regulation the competent authorities of a fisheries administration must[^key-6d6ad61bc9f6faa70c22b9c8deb42430] ensure that the fishing vessel to be inspected in port is met by their officials on arrival.

3. Paragraph 1 does not exclude the possibility for a fisheries administration[^key-27c716a5bb13c47bb391cacde047f9c7] to undertake random inspections.
