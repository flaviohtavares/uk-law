# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 404/2011 of 8 April 2011 laying down detailed rules for the implementation of Council Regulation (EC) No 1224/2009 establishing a Community control system for ensuring compliance with the rules of the Common Fisheries Policy, section Article 146b

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 146b. Definitions[^c000002]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purpose of this Chapter the following definitions shall apply:

- ((a)) ‘Transportation layer’ means the electronic network for fisheries data exchanges as made available by the Commission to all Member States and the body designated by it to exchange data in a standardised way;
- ((b)) ‘Report’ means the information recorded by electronic means;
- ((c)) ‘Message’ means the report in its format for transmission;
- ((d)) ‘Request’ means an electronic message containing a query for a set of reports.
