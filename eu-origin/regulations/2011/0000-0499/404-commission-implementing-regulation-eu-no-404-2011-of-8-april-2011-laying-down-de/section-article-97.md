# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 404/2011 of 8 April 2011 laying down detailed rules for the implementation of Council Regulation (EC) No 1224/2009 establishing a Community control system for ensuring compliance with the rules of the Common Fisheries Policy, section Article 97

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 97. Officials authorised to conduct inspections at sea or on land

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. Officials responsible for carrying out inspections, as referred to in Article 74 of the Control Regulation shall be authorised by the competent authorities of a fisheries administration[^key-15cf8bbbfc1ac9720f186fd7d8b59821]. To this end, a fisheries administration must[^key-15849feb6f2bc549255a999e1523950e] provide their officials with a service card stating their identity and the capacity under which they operate. Each official on duty shall carry that service card and present it during an inspection at the earliest opportunity.
