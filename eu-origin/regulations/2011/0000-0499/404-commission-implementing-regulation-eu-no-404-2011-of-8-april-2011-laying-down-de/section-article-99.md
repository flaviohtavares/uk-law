## Article 99. Duties of officials during the pre-inspection phase

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

During the pre-inspection phase officials shall, where possible, collect all appropriate information, including:

- ((a)) fishing licences and fishing authorisations;
- ((b)) VMS information corresponding to the current fishing trip;
