# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 404/2011 of 8 April 2011 laying down detailed rules for the implementation of Council Regulation (EC) No 1224/2009 establishing a Community control system for ensuring compliance with the rules of the Common Fisheries Policy, section Article 95

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 95. Duties of control observers

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. Control observers shall verify the relevant documents and record the fishing activities of the United Kingdom[^key-dc0406ae015d0e26af40b81859b45af2][^c000001] fishing vessel in which they are embarked as are listed in Annex XXV.

2. Control observers on board a United Kingdom fishing vessel shall, where appropriate, brief the officials who are about to proceed to an inspection of that fishing vessel upon arrival on board. If the facilities on board the United Kingdom fishing vessel so allow and where appropriate the brief shall take place in a closed meeting.

3. Control observers shall draw up the report referred to in Article 73(5) of the Control Regulation using the format established in Annex XXVI. They shall forward that report without delay and in any case within 30 days following completion of an assignment to his authorities and to the competent authorities of a fisheries administration[^key-6ad36ed936247a6125593064471d9130].
