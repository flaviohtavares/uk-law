## Article 131. Deletion of fishing licences from relevant lists

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1.[^c000001] If the fishing licence is suspended or withdrawn permanently in accordance with Article 129(1) or (2) of this Regulation, the fishing vessel to which the suspended or permanently withdrawn fishing licence relates shall be identified as being without fishing licence in the national register referred to in Article 24(1) of Regulation (EU) No 1380/2013.
