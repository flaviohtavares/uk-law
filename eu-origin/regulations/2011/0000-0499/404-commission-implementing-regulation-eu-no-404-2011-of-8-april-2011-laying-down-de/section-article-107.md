# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 404/2011 of 8 April 2011 laying down detailed rules for the implementation of Council Regulation (EC) No 1224/2009 establishing a Community control system for ensuring compliance with the rules of the Common Fisheries Policy, section Article 107

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 107. Inspection of certain pelagic landings[^c000001]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For landings of herring, mackerel, horse mackerel and blue whiting as referred to in Article 78 of this Regulation the competent authorities of a fisheries administration must[^c-8215e41b6192799311f1092e86bcb281] ensure that at least 7,5 % of the quantities landed for each species and at least 5 % of the landings are fully inspected.
