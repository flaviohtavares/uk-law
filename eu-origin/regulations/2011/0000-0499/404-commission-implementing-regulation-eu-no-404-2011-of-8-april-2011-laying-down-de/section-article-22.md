# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 404/2011 of 8 April 2011 laying down detailed rules for the implementation of Council Regulation (EC) No 1224/2009 establishing a Community control system for ensuring compliance with the rules of the Common Fisheries Policy, section Article 22

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 22. Frequency of data transmission

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. A fisheries administration must[^c-c9b02c98b424eac1849566e10d33debf] ensure that its FMC receives, at least once every 2 hours, through the VMS the information referred to in Article 19 of this Regulation concerning fishing vessels in its fleet[^c-a7bb4a64d510ae46fdd884ef933075ca]. The FMC may require the information at shorter time intervals.

2. The FMC shall have the capacity of polling the actual position of each of its fishing vessels in its fleet.
