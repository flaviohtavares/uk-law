# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 404/2011 of 8 April 2011 laying down detailed rules for the implementation of Council Regulation (EC) No 1224/2009 establishing a Community control system for ensuring compliance with the rules of the Common Fisheries Policy, section Article 7

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 7. Documents carried on board a United Kingdom fishing vessel[^c000001][^key-0397b708e7a8c28a4ed97a9778f5a252]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. The master of a United Kingdom[^key-c8592b9236f456457b0a4f357d435d75] fishing vessel of 10 metres length overall or more shall carry on board documents, issued by a competent authority of a fisheries administration[^key-bf49bb943d47b800d8eeb35478bfe82c], showing at least the following elements of the vessel:

- (a) the name if any;
- (b) the letters of the port or district in which it is registered, and the number(s) under which it is registered;
- (c) the international radio call sign, if any;
- (d) the names and addresses of the owner(s) and, where applicable, the charterer(s);
- (e) the length overall, propulsion engine power, gross tonnage and, for United Kingdom[^key-5186ffd914ab639f89f4cfd54b6ae07f] fishing vessels which entered into service from 1 January 1987 onwards, date of entry into service.

2. On United Kingdom[^key-074048f888be6e2737aceec88dcc8f41] fishing vessels of 17 metres length overall or more with fish rooms the master shall keep on board accurate drawings with description of its fish rooms, including the indication of all access points and of their storage capacity in cubic metres.

3. The master of a United Kingdom fishing[^key-c2d9b5a771ac2ae42658fa754bb3b7c4] vessel with chilled or refrigerated seawater tanks shall keep on board an up-to-date document indicating the calibration of the tanks in cubic metres at 10 centimetre intervals.

4. The documents referred to in paragraphs 2 and 3 shall be certified by the competent authority of a fisheries administration[^key-63e907ee38170cb5b5cab07edc9434eb]. Any modification of the characteristics contained in the documents referred to in paragraphs 1 to 3, shall be certified by a competent authority of a fisheries administration.

5. The documents referred to in this Article shall be presented for the purposes of control and inspection at the request of the officials.
