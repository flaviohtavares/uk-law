# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 404/2011 of 8 April 2011 laying down detailed rules for the implementation of Council Regulation (EC) No 1224/2009 establishing a Community control system for ensuring compliance with the rules of the Common Fisheries Policy, section Article 1

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 1. Subject matter

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Regulation lays down detailed rules for the application of the control system of the United Kingdom[^c-461b58ee8ff0752543d5ff0b841b14a9] as established by the Control Regulation.

This Regulation applies without prejudice to special provisions contained in fisheries agreements concluded between the United Kingdom and other countries or applicable in the framework of regional fisheries management organisations or similar agreements to which the United Kingdom is a Contracting Party or a non-contracting Cooperating Party.[^c-3cd4077a9a743ff5566d8f2bf699232d]
