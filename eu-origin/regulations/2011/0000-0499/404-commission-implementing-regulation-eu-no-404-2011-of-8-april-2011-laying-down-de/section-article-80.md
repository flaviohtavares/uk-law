# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 404/2011 of 8 April 2011 laying down detailed rules for the implementation of Council Regulation (EC) No 1224/2009 establishing a Community control system for ensuring compliance with the rules of the Common Fisheries Policy, section Article 80

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 80. Entry into a port ...[^c-b162dbe6b941a66208be045825a738ea]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. For the purpose of weighing, the master of a fishing vessel or his representative shall inform the competent authorities of a fisheries administration[^c-9c2683b74d828a5c53b2568127da6f88] at least 4 hours in advance of entry to a United Kingdom[^c-d16e0fd98e794ac70c5dc21b52e6f9c4] port of landing [^c-571afb76fae3cab630454ea8ab27eab9]... of the following:

- (a) the port he intends to enter, the name of the vessel and its external registration letters and numbers;
- (b) the estimated time of arrival at that port;
- (c)[^c000001] the quantities in kilograms live weight of herring, mackerel, horse mackerel and blue whiting retained on board;
- (d) relevance geographical area(s) where the catch was taken; the zone shall refer to the sub-area and division or sub-division in which catch limits apply pursuant to Fisheries Rules[^c-68a40ed8868e64fe6e8b3b41d162f3be].

2. The master of a United Kingdom[^c-577edd93fa1fde8f1670ffd1811219d8] fishing vessel which is under the obligation to record fishing logbook data electronically shall send the information referred to in paragraph 1 electronically to a fisheries administration[^c-06542239d3e0d0e496edd20f0c8789f5]. A fisheries administration must[^c-ac963fdec72746fa4064ad7cf012bd1f] transmit this information without delay to the country where the landing is to be made[^c-b00337c0d9d1f243f574117b49355fab]. The electronic fishing logbook data referred to in Article 15 of the Control Regulation and the information referred to in paragraph 1 may be sent in a single electronic transmission.

3.[^c-16008d7c3c69a6292fb034cb7007a643] A fisheries administration may provide for a shorter notification period than laid down in paragraph 1. In such a case, the fisheries administration must put this information on its official website.
