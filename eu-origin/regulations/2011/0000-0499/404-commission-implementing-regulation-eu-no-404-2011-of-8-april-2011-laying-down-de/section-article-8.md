# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 404/2011 of 8 April 2011 laying down detailed rules for the implementation of Council Regulation (EC) No 1224/2009 establishing a Community control system for ensuring compliance with the rules of the Common Fisheries Policy, section Article 8

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 8. Marking of crafts and fish aggregating devices

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Any craft carried on board United Kingdom[^c-dba7ef2d84929da3febdef612570af5c][^c000001] fishing vessels and fish aggregating devices shall be marked with external registration letters and numbers of the United Kingdom fishing vessel(s) which use them.
