# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 404/2011 of 8 April 2011 laying down detailed rules for the implementation of Council Regulation (EC) No 1224/2009 establishing a Community control system for ensuring compliance with the rules of the Common Fisheries Policy, section Article 26

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 26. Non-receipt of data

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. When the FMC of a fisheries administration[^c-5cead86f1d2005a10575f584553d09ae] has not received data transmissions in accordance with Article 22 or Article 25(1) of this Regulation for 12 consecutive hours it shall notify the master or the operator of the United Kingdom[^c-68f6a58ef8fb1427dd7fa0a39c0eb95f][^c000001] fishing vessel or their representative(s) thereof as soon as possible. If, in respect of a[^c-4fbb971eb6355851761a36bd84ceb44c] particular fishing vessel, that situation occurs more than three times within a period of a calendar year, the fisheries administration must[^c-f58f76424c05c45c6a0137981870c4ae] ensure that the satellite-tracking device of the fishing vessel is thoroughly checked. The fisheries administration must investigate the matter in order to establish whether the equipment has been tampered with. By way of derogation from Article 20(2)(d) of this Regulation, that investigation may entail the removal of such equipment for examination.

2. When the FMC of a fisheries administration[^c-decaa1346bcd8119eceecb8a544cec4d] has not received data transmissions for 12 hours in accordance with Article 22 or Article 25(1) of this Regulation and the last received position was from within the waters of a third country[^c-e64b72eafd3d25d1675d324f1e2227bb] it shall notify the competent authority of that country[^c-f43c5ca6ad8b157eb3563fffb8f0f0e9] thereof as soon as possible.
