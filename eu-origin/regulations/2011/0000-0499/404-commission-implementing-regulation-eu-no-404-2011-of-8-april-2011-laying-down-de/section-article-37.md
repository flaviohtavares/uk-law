# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 404/2011 of 8 April 2011 laying down detailed rules for the implementation of Council Regulation (EC) No 1224/2009 establishing a Community control system for ensuring compliance with the rules of the Common Fisheries Policy, section Article 37

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 37. Format for transmission of data from a United Kingdom fishing vessel to the competent authority of a fisheries administration[^c000001][^key-dad97bf0cd6f4a7b3c94c4e54655654d][^key-f3456d4df5e03a0d75abbf44f1efa4e0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A fisheries administration must[^key-c3e38733b323ce778cffc1f6a7132fde] determine the format to be used between United Kingdom fishing vessels[^key-546f0e0b8e0bc563b85f20f6cbb85cb0] and their competent authorities for the completion and transmission of fishing logbook, transhipment declaration and landing declaration data as referred to in Articles 15, 21 and 24 of the Control Regulation.

The fishing logbook, transhipment declaration and landing declaration in electronic format shall be completed in accordance with the instructions set out in Annex X.[^c000002]
