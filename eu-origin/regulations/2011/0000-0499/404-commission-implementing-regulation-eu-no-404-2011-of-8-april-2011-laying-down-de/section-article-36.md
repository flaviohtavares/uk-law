# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 404/2011 of 8 April 2011 laying down detailed rules for the implementation of Council Regulation (EC) No 1224/2009 establishing a Community control system for ensuring compliance with the rules of the Common Fisheries Policy, section Article 36

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 36. Requirement of electronic recording and reporting system on ... fishing vessels[^key-2463fb42f5788f898af8476f5d221ab2][^c000001]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. Without prejudice to Article 39(4) of this Regulation a United Kingdom[^key-5eb98fcc2a3d753afcbc7664541c2171] fishing vessel subject to electronic completion and transmission of fishing logbook, transhipment declaration and landing declaration in accordance with Articles 15, 21 and 24 of the Control Regulation shall not be allowed to leave port without a fully operational electronic recording and reporting system installed on board.

2. This Chapter shall not apply to [^key-f54407b7fed29ebce0921f34a8b72fcb]... fishing vessels used exclusively for the exploitation of aquaculture.
