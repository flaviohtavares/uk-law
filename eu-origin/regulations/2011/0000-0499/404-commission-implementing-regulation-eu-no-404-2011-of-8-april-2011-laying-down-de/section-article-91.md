# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 404/2011 of 8 April 2011 laying down detailed rules for the implementation of Council Regulation (EC) No 1224/2009 establishing a Community control system for ensuring compliance with the rules of the Common Fisheries Policy, section Article 91

## Article 91. Formats of sales notes and take-over declarations[^c000001]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. A fisheries administration must[^key-b5b878887d5f3b107bc1653876c56b17] determine the format to be used for the completion and transmission of sales notes and take-over declarations between registered buyers, registered auctions, or other bodies or persons authorised by a fisheries administration and its[^key-f3752a94b9c9b83ab36e88253731578e] competent authorities as referred to in Articles 63 and 67 of the Control Regulation.

2.[^key-5e8ad19cdb69352c088cc8d7dcebe86c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

3.[^key-8c6d8b2de9d441ed9a1ff66d109bea48] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

4. A fisheries administration must[^key-22ed32ca492615b1d171af2230cf49dc]:

- (a) ensure that data received according to this Chapter are recorded in computer-readable form and safely stored in computerised databases for at least 3 years;
- (b) take all necessary measures to ensure that the data are only used for purposes as provided for in this Regulation; and
- (c) take all necessary technical measures to protect such data against any accidental or illicit destruction, accidental loss, deterioration, distribution or unauthorised consultation.
