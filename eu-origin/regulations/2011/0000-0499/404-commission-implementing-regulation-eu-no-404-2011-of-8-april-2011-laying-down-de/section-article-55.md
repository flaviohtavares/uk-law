# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 404/2011 of 8 April 2011 laying down detailed rules for the implementation of Council Regulation (EC) No 1224/2009 establishing a Community control system for ensuring compliance with the rules of the Common Fisheries Policy, section Article 55

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 55. Fishing Operations involving two or more ... fishing vessels[^c-8987376f2b5ce0254b04e8d0d0f29319][^c000001]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Without prejudice to special rules in the case of fishing operations involving two or more United Kingdom[^c-ae475b786b1d60a06cd6a8599103a533] fishing vessels[^c-cf4f216c17a5163a7fd9a404caedd660]... the landed catch shall be attributed to the United Kingdom fishing vessel landing the fisheries products.
