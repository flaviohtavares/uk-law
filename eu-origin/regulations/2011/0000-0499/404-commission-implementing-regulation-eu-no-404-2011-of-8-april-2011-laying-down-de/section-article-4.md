# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 404/2011 of 8 April 2011 laying down detailed rules for the implementation of Council Regulation (EC) No 1224/2009 establishing a Community control system for ensuring compliance with the rules of the Common Fisheries Policy, section Article 4

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 4. Fishing authorisations

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. A fishing authorisation referred to in Article 7 of the Control Regulation shall be valid for one [^key-e632989cab081e0f319b9ce0c74c20fb]... fishing vessel[^c000001] only.

2. Fishing authorisations referred to in Article 7 of the Control Regulation shall contain as a minimum the information set out in Annex III. A fisheries administration must[^key-32b166a0f910b0366b0acd2a39742d7a] ensure that the information contained in the fishing authorisation is accurate and consistent with Fisheries Rules[^key-7c64a536f933d65835979ed79df86ee7].

3. Special fishing permits issued in accordance with Council Regulation (EC) No 1627/94[^f00022] shall be considered as fishing authorisations issued in accordance with this Regulation if they contain the minimum information required by paragraph 2 of this Article.

4. A fishing authorisation as referred to in paragraph 2 and a fishing licence as referred to in Article 3(2) of this Regulation may be contained in the same document.

5. Without prejudice to special rules United Kingdom[^key-a862bdac973b9393e953d27693baf60d] fishing vessels of less than 10 metres’ length overall which fish exclusively in United Kingdom waters[^key-9b3e0e97c099ca0e409103f6946a40f3] shall be excluded from the obligation to a have a fishing authorisation.

6. Paragraph 2 and paragraph 5 of Article 3 of this Regulation shall apply correspondingly.
