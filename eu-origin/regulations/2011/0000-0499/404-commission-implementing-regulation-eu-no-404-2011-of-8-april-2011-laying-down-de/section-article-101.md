# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 404/2011 of 8 April 2011 laying down detailed rules for the implementation of Council Regulation (EC) No 1224/2009 establishing a Community control system for ensuring compliance with the rules of the Common Fisheries Policy, section Article 101

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 101. Obligations of a fisheries administration[^c-5141857d55196993f4c0d16468772e43]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. The competent authorities of a fisheries administration must[^c-3773207f875d0c3ecf3e5543c16eef52] ensure that their officials, whilst being courteous and sensitive, conduct inspections professionally and to a high standard.

2. The competent authorities of a fisheries administration must[^c-37ff01ac5147aeaec1053eb038612231] establish procedures to ensure that any complaint made by operators regarding the conduct of inspections carried out by their officials is investigated in a fair and thorough manner in accordance with applicable laws[^c-9931062c80c519f8eb14c11610cc653d].
