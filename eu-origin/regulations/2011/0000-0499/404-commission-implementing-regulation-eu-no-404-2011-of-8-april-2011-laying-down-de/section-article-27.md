# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 404/2011 of 8 April 2011 laying down detailed rules for the implementation of Council Regulation (EC) No 1224/2009 establishing a Community control system for ensuring compliance with the rules of the Common Fisheries Policy, section Article 27

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 27. Monitoring and recording of the fishing activities

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1.[^key-76a4dc6f3a85d0be0bd36fd282ffde2f] A fisheries administration shall use relevant data received from other authorities for the effective monitoring of the activities of fishing vessels.

2. A fisheries administration must[^key-bacfad1d668bbab56d12aa5fe4fe719f]:

- (a) ensure that data received according to this Chapter are recorded in computer-readable form and safely stored in computerised databases for at least 3 years;
- (b) take all necessary measures to ensure that they are only used for official purposes; and
- (c) take all necessary technical measures to protect such data against any accidental or illicit destruction, accidental loss, deterioration, distribution or unauthorised consultation.
