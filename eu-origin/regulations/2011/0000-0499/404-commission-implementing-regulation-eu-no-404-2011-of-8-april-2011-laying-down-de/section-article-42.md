# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 404/2011 of 8 April 2011 laying down detailed rules for the implementation of Council Regulation (EC) No 1224/2009 establishing a Community control system for ensuring compliance with the rules of the Common Fisheries Policy, section Article 42

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 42. Data on the functioning of the electronic recording and reporting system

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. A fisheries administration must[^key-6f6fb319f3325e104d7354d6e54af507] maintain databases on the functioning of their electronic recording and reporting system. Those databases shall contain at least and be capable to generate automatically the following information:

- (a) the list of fishing vessels in its fleet[^key-23b750e3a37061e24d636a4917ec5b87] whose electronic recording and reporting systems have experienced technical failure or have failed to function;
- (b) the number of vessels that have not made daily electronic fishing logbook transmissions and the average number of electronic fishing logbook transmissions received per fishing vessel, broken down by flag state[^key-582ad2c8e5abcd27c010637e57de6118];
- (c) the number of transhipment declaration, landing declaration, takeover declaration and sales note transmissions received, broken down by flag state[^key-d9b08a811057294aa471947962bd2e97].
