# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 404/2011 of 8 April 2011 laying down detailed rules for the implementation of Council Regulation (EC) No 1224/2009 establishing a Community control system for ensuring compliance with the rules of the Common Fisheries Policy, ANNEX XXVIII

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## ANNEX XXVIII — MARKING OF FISHERY INSPECTION MEANS

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

[image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2011/404/images/eur_20110404_2020-07-14_en_007)

INSPECTION PENNANT OR SYMBOL

All vessels used for fisheries control inspection and enforcement shall carry the inspection pennant or symbol clearly displayed on the sides of the unit used in such a way to be clearly visible. Vessels engaged in these duties shall fly the inspection pennant as to be clearly visible at all times.

The words ‘FISHERY INSPECTION’ may also be affixed on the sides of units.
