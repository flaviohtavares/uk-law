# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 449/2011 of 6 May 2011 entering certain names in the register of protected designations of origin and protected geographical indications ( (Shaanxi ping guo) (PDO), (Longjing Cha) (PDO), (Guanxi Mi You) (PDO), (Lixian Ma Shan Yao) (PGI))

> Text as at 2011-05-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 449/2011

of 6 May 2011

entering certain names in the register of protected designations of origin and protected geographical indications ([image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2011/449/images/eur_20110449_2011-05-06_en_001) (Shaanxi ping guo) (PDO), [image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2011/449/images/eur_20110449_2011-05-06_en_002) (Longjing Cha) (PDO), [image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2011/449/images/eur_20110449_2011-05-06_en_003) (Guanxi Mi You) (PDO), [image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2011/449/images/eur_20110449_2011-05-06_en_004) (Lixian Ma Shan Yao) (PGI))

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Council Regulation (EC) No 510/2006 of 20 March 2006 on the protection of geographical indications and designations of origin for agricultural products and foodstuffs[^f00001], and in particular the first subparagraph of Article 7(4) thereof,

Whereas:

(1)

Pursuant to the first subparagraph of Article 6(2) of Regulation (EC) No 510/2006, the applications of the People’s Republic of China to register the names ‘[image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2011/449/images/eur_20110449_2011-05-06_en_005) (Shaanxi ping guo)’, ‘[image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2011/449/images/eur_20110449_2011-05-06_en_006) (Longjing Cha)’, ‘[image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2011/449/images/eur_20110449_2011-05-06_en_007) (Guanxi Mi You)’ and ‘[image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2011/449/images/eur_20110449_2011-05-06_en_008) (Lixian Ma Shan Yao)’ were published in the *Official Journal of the European Union* [^f00002].

(2)

As no statement of objection under Article 7 of Regulation (EC) No 510/2006 has been received by the Commission, these names should therefore be entered in the register,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

**Article 1.** The names contained in the Annex to this Regulation are hereby entered in the register.

**Article 2.** This Regulation shall enter into force on the 20th day following its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Union.*

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 6 May 2011.

*For the Commission, On behalf of the President*,

Dacian Cioloş

*Member of the Commission*
