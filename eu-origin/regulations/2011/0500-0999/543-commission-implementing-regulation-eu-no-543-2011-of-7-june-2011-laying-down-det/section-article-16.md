# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 543/2011 of 7 June 2011 laying down detailed rules for the application of Council Regulation (EC) No 1234/2007 in respect of the fruit and vegetables and processed fruit and vegetables sectors, section Article 16

> Text as at 2023-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 16. Suspension of approval of the conformity checks

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Secretary of State[^key-0164c2b32b935df43aa5fdbd65646cac] may suspend approval of the conformity checks if it is found that, in a significant number of lots and/or quantities, the goods do not correspond to the information in the certificates of conformity issued by the third country inspection bodies.
