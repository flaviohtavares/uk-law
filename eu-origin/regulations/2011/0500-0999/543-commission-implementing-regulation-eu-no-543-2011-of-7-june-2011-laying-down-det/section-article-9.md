# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 543/2011 of 7 June 2011 laying down detailed rules for the application of Council Regulation (EC) No 1234/2007 in respect of the fruit and vegetables and processed fruit and vegetables sectors, section Article 9

> Text as at 2023-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 9. Coordinating authorities and inspection bodies

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. [^key-af6ded0b1fb96a718a5106cbc5b9ed07]...

- (a) The Secretary of State shall designate[^key-e65da602cb754f8ece14463c2ccbb73f] a single competent authority for Great Britain[^key-0e3aa62b835a323cabdb33a1b96a9a14] responsible for coordination and contacts in the areas covered by this Chapter, hereinafter called ‘the coordinating authority’; and
- (b) Each appropriate authority shall designate[^key-7c03fd3178bfe9dcce25c4813ef044a4] an inspection body or bodies responsible for the application of this Chapter, hereinafter called ‘the inspection bodies’.

The coordinating authorities and inspection bodies referred to in the first subparagraph may be public or private. However, the the Secretary of State or the appropriate authority that designates them (as the case may be)[^key-d20aa1cc1a2d9efa65e770ac89f02450] shall be responsible for them in either case.

The Secretary of State may not designate the coordinating authority referred to in point (a) of the first subparagraph without the consent of—[^key-00000c0fefed994a05304cb33ea2de8c]

- (a) in relation to Wales, the Welsh Ministers;
- (b) in relation to Scotland, the Scottish Ministers.

Nothing in this Regulation affects the validity of any designation of a coordinating authority or inspection body made before IP completion day.

2.[^key-17374073caa93dfb6cc38984c0de3d2b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

3. The coordinating authority may be the inspection body or one of the inspection bodies or any other body designated pursuant to paragraph 1.

4. The Secretary of State[^key-c5816b1f00f718479c51008769bffc8c] shall make publicly available the name of the coordinating authority[^key-2a8de35591c3062bdff95121f9575df6] designated by the Secretary of State[^key-27a5404967b57faae639d49086c4a97a] in the manner the Secretary of State[^key-3a0642376451c188c5203a7068280ba5] considers appropriate.
