Regulation (EU) No 1299/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 December 2013 on specific provisions for the support from the European Regional Development Fund to the European territorial cooperation goal *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc][^f00002], and in particular the second subparagraph of Article 8(2) thereof, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

Whereas: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

(1)

The provisions in this Regulation are closely linked, since they all deal with aspects required for the preparation of operational programmes under the Investment for growth and jobs goal and under the European territorial cooperation goal. To ensure coherence between those provisions, which should enter into force at the same time, and to facilitate a comprehensive view and compact access to them by all Union residents, it is desirable to include provisions on categories of intervention for the European territorial cooperation goal into this implementing act as the procedure with regard to the consultation of the Coordination Committee for the European Structural and Investment Funds established by Article 150(1) of Regulation (EU) No 1303/2013 is the same as for the other provisions covered by this implementing act, whereas categories of intervention for the Investment for growth and jobs goal are subject to a different procedure. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

(2)

Pursuant to Article 74(4) of Regulation (EU) No 1303/2013 all official exchanges of information between the Member State and the Commission shall be carried out using an electronic data exchange system. It is therefore necessary to establish the terms and conditions with which that electronic data exchange system should comply. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

(3)

The modalities of exchange of information between the Member State and the Commission should be considered distinct from those established for the exchanges of information between beneficiaries and relevant authorities pursuant to Article 122(3) of Regulation (EU) No 1303/2013 and which are subject to a different implementing act. In order to guarantee enhanced quality of information on the implementation of operational programmes, improved usefulness of the system and simplification, it is necessary to specify basic requirements for the form and scope of the information to be exchanged. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

(4)

It is necessary to specify principles, as well as applicable rules for operation of the system with regard to the identification of the party responsible for uploading the documents and making any updates thereto. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

(5)

In order to guarantee the reduction of the administrative burden for the Member States and the Commission while ensuring the efficient and effective electronic exchange of information, it is necessary to establish technical characteristics for the system. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

(6)

Member States and the Commission should also have a possibility to encode and transfer data in two different ways to be specified. It is also necessary to provide for rules in the event of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc] *force majeure *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]* hindering the use of the electronic data exchange system, to ensure that both Member States and the Commission can continue to exchange information by alternative means. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

(7)

Member States and the Commission should ensure that transfer of data through the electronic data exchange system is performed in a secured manner allowing for availability, integrity, authenticity, confidentiality and non-repudiation of information. Therefore rules on security should be set out. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

(8)

This Regulation should respect the fundamental rights and observe the principles recognised by the Charter of Fundamental Rights of the European Union, and notably the right to protection of personal data. This Regulation should therefore be applied in accordance with these rights and principles. Concerning personal data processed by Member States, Directive 95/46/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc][^f00003] applies. Concerning the processing of personal data by the Union institutions and bodies and the free movement of such data, Regulation (EC) No 45/2001 of the European Parliament and of the Council *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc][^f00004] applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

(9)

Pursuant to the second subparagraph of Article 8(2) of Regulation (EU) No 1299/2013, it is necessary to specify common categories of intervention for programmes under the European territorial cooperation goal to enable Member States to submit to the Commission consistent information on the programmed use of the European Regional Development Fund (ERDF), as well as information on the cumulative allocation and expenditure of the ERDF by category and the number of operations throughout the application period of a programme. This is to enable the Commission to inform the other Union institutions and citizens of the Union in an appropriate manner about the use of the ERDF. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

(10)

In order to allow for the prompt application of the measures provided for in this Regulation, this Regulation should enter into force on the day following that of its publication in the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc] *Official Journal of the European Union *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]*.

(11)

The measures provided for in this Regulation are in accordance with the first subparagraph of Article 150(3) of Regulation (EU) No 1303/2013, as the Coordination Committee for the European Structural and Investment Funds established by Article 150(1) of that Regulation delivered an opinion, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

## CHAPTER I (affected by repeal, see note) — Provisions implementing Regulation (EU) No 1303/2013 with regard to the ESI Funds (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc][^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

### ELECTRONIC DATA EXCHANGE SYSTEM (affected by repeal, see note) — (Empowerment under Article 74(4) of Regulation (EU) No 1303/2013) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc][^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

#### Article 1 (affected by repeal, see note). Establishment of electronic data exchange system (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc][^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

The Commission shall establish an electronic data exchange system for all official exchanges of information between the Member State and the Commission. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

#### Article 2 (affected by repeal, see note). Content of electronic data exchange system (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc][^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

1. The electronic data exchange system (hereinafter referred to as ‘SFC2014’) shall contain at least information specified in the models, formats and templates established in accordance with Regulation (EU) No 1303/2013, Regulation (EU) No 1299/2013, Regulation (EU) No 1305/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc][^f00005] and the future Union legal act establishing the conditions for the financial support for maritime and fisheries policy for the programming period 2014-2020 (the ‘EMFF Regulation’). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

2. The information provided in the electronic forms embedded in SFC2014 (hereinafter referred to as ‘structured data’) may not be replaced by non-structured data, including the use of hyperlinks or other types of non-structured data such as attachment of documents or images. Where a Member State transmits the same information in the form of structured data and non-structured data, the structured data shall be used in case of inconsistencies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

#### Article 3 (affected by repeal, see note). Operation of SFC2014 (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc][^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

1. The Commission, the authorities designated by the Member State pursuant to Article 59(3) of Regulation (EU, Euratom) No 966/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc][^f00006], Article 123 of Regulation (EU) No 1303/2013 and Article 65(2) of Regulation (EU) No 1305/2013 as well as the bodies to which tasks of those authorities have been delegated shall enter into SFC2014 the information for the transmission of which they are responsible and any updates thereto. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

2. Any transmission of information to the Commission shall be verified and submitted by a person other than the person who entered the data for that transmission. This separation of tasks shall be supported by SFC2014 or by Member State’s management and control information systems connected automatically with SFC2014. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

3. Member States shall appoint, at national or regional level or at both national and regional levels, a person or persons responsible for managing access rights to SFC2014 who shall fulfil the following tasks: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

- (a) identifying users requesting access, making sure those users are employed by the organisation; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]
- (b) informing users about their obligations to preserve the security of the system; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]
- (c) verifying the entitlement of users to the required privilege level in relation to their tasks and their hierarchical position; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]
- (d) requesting the termination of access rights when those access rights are no longer needed or justified; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]
- (e) promptly reporting suspicious events that may bring prejudice to the security of the system; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]
- (f) ensuring the continued accuracy of user identification data by reporting any changes; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]
- (g) taking the necessary data protection and commercial confidentiality precautions in accordance with Union and national rules; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]
- (h) informing the Commission of any changes affecting the capacity of the Member State authorities or users of SFC2014 to carry out the responsibilities referred to in paragraph 1 or their personal capacity to carry out responsibilities referred to in points (a)-(g). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

4. Exchanges of data and transactions shall bear a compulsory electronic signature within the meaning of Directive 1999/93/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc][^f00007]. The Member States and the Commission shall recognise the legal effectiveness and admissibility of the electronic signature used in SFC2014 as evidence in legal proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

Information processed through SFC2014 shall respect the protection of privacy and personal data for individuals and commercial confidentiality for legal entities in accordance with Directive 2002/58/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc][^f00008], Directive 2009/136/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc][^f00009], Directive 1995/46/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc][^f00010] and Regulation (EC) No 45/2001. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

#### Article 4 (affected by repeal, see note). Characteristics of SFC2014 (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc][^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

In order to ensure the efficient and effective electronic exchange of information, SFC2014 shall have the following characteristics: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

- ((a)) interactive forms or forms pre-filled by the system on the basis of the data already recorded in the system previously; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]
- ((b)) automatic calculations, where they reduce the encoding effort of users; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]
- ((c)) automatic embedded controls to verify internal consistency of transmitted data and consistency of this data with applicable rules; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]
- ((d)) system generated alerts warning SFC2014 users that certain actions can or cannot be performed; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]
- ((e)) online status tracking of the treatment of information entered into the system; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]
- ((f)) availability of historical data in respect of all information entered for an operational programme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

#### Article 5 (affected by repeal, see note). Transmission of data through SFC2014 (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc][^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

1. SFC2014 shall be accessible to the Member States and the Commission either directly through an interactive user-interface (i.e. a web-application) or via a technical interface using pre-defined protocols (i.e. web-services) that allows for automatic synchronisation and transmission of data between Member States information systems and SFC2014. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

2. The date of electronic transmission of the information by the Member State to the Commission and vice versa shall be considered to be the date of submission of the document concerned. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

3. In the event of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc] *force majeure *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]*, a malfunctioning of SFC2014 or a lack of a connection with SFC2014 exceeding one working day in the last week before a regulatory deadline for the submission of information or in the period from 23 to 31 December, or five working days at other times, the information exchange between the Member State and the Commission may take place in paper form using the models, formats and templates referred to in Article 2(1) of this Regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

When the electronic exchange system ceases to malfunction, the connection with that system is re-established or the cause of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc] *force majeure *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]* ceases, the party concerned shall enter without delay the information already sent in paper form also into SFC2014. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

4. In cases referred to in paragraph 3 the date stamped by the post shall be considered to be the date of submission of the document concerned. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

#### Article 6 (affected by repeal, see note). Security of data transmitted through SFC2014 (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc][^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

1. The Commission shall establish an information technology security policy (hereinafter referred to as ‘SFC IT security policy’) for SFC2014 applicable to personnel using SFC2014 in accordance with relevant Union rules, in particular Commission Decision C(2006) 3602 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc][^f00011] and its implementing rules. The Commission shall designate a person or persons responsible for defining, maintaining and ensuring the correct application of the security policy to SFC2014. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

2. Member States and European institutions other than the Commission, who have received access rights to SFC2014, shall comply with the IT security terms and conditions published in the SFC2014 portal and the measures that are implemented in SFC2014 by the Commission to secure the transmission of data, in particular in relation to the use of the technical interface referred to in Article 5(1) of this Regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

3. Member States and the Commission shall implement and ensure the effectiveness of the security measures adopted to protect the data they have stored and transmitted through SFC2014. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

4. Member States shall adopt national, regional or local information security policies covering access to SFC2014 and automatic input of data into it, ensuring a minimum set of security requirements. These national, regional or local IT security policies can refer to other security documents. Each Member State shall ensure that these IT security policies apply to all authorities using SFC2014. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

5. These national, regional or local IT security policies shall include: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

- (a) the IT security aspects of the work performed by the person or persons responsible for managing the access rights referred to in Article 3(3) of this Regulation in case of application of direct use; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]
- (b) in case of national, regional or local computer systems connected to SFC2014, through a technical interface referred to in Article 5(1) of this Regulation the security measures for those systems allowing to be aligned with SFC2014 security requirements. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

For the purposes of point (b) of the first subparagraph, the following aspects shall be covered, as appropriate: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

- (a) physical security; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]
- (b) data media and access control; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]
- (c) storage control; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

- (g) communication infrastructure; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]
- (h) human resources management prior to employment, during employment and after employment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]
- (i) incident management. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

6. These national, regional or local IT security policies shall be based on a risk assessment and the measures described shall be proportionate to the risks identified. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

7. The documents setting out the national, regional or local IT security policies shall be made available to the Commission upon request. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e94288d3be37af0769c5733f260472dc]

8. Member States shall designate, at a national or regional level, a person or persons responsible for maintaining and ensuring the application of the national, regional or local IT security policies. Both the SFC IT security policy and the relevant national, regional and local IT security policies shall be updated in the event of technological changes, the identification of new threats or other relevant developments.
