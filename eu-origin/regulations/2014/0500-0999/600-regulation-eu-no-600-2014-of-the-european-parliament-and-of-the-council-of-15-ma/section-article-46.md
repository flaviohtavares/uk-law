# Regulation (EU) No 600/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 May 2014 on markets in financial instruments and amending Regulation (EU) No 648/2012 (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 46

## Article 46 (affected by repeal, see note). General provisions (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945][^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. A third-country firm may provide investment services or perform investment activities with or without any ancillary services to eligible counterparties and to professional clients within *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945] Part 2 of Schedule 1 to this Regulation[^c-5ca162353100c004f1831e9ab6558265] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]in the United Kingdom[^c-d26c41b7e82eace1787478f7c0a984c3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945] without the establishment of a branch where it is registered in the register of third-country firms kept by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945] the FCA[^c-159e6cf9544f4c7aa61e4b22a98aeea6] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945] in accordance with *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945] Article 48[^c-3693fb9a8e9ee3c12d4e6194d10d02e0] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945] .

2. The FCA[^c-50e415e7e8103d1ae7e1d035880bad7c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945] shall register a third-country firm that has applied for the provision of investment services or performance of activities *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945] in the United Kingdom[^c-ca10d28e0cdd11f1ba3837f0b9d0e456] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945] in accordance with paragraph 1 only where the following conditions are met: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

- (a)[^c-8454ac22201224ce0f6b3036a8606b71] the Treasury has made a determination under Article 47(1) in respect of the third country; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]
- (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945] the firm, and the services or activities, fall within the scope of the determination; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]
- (b) the firm is authorised in the jurisdiction where its head office is established to provide the investment services or activities to be provided in the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945] United Kingdom[^c-9b845cbc636d2cd5a06009c6808d3fa5] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945] and it is subject to effective supervision and enforcement ensuring a full compliance with the requirements applicable in that third country; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]
- (c) cooperation arrangements have been established pursuant to Article 47(2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]
- (d)[^c-513c2508347ce4d38aeca5402df8bff8] the firm has established the necessary arrangements and procedures to provide the information required by rules made under paragraph 6B of this Article; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]
- (e) the firm has established the necessary arrangements and procedures to comply with requirements imposed under Article 48A. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

3.[^c-bc689b17bc4e3075e3ea7dafbf25e13a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

4. The third-country firm referred to in paragraph 1 shall submit its application to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945] the FCA[^c-e0f113a6d17f51487248fb74e101136f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945] after the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945] making of a determination by the Treasury under Article 47(1) that the legal and supervisory arrangements of the third country in which the third-country firm is authorised satisfy the requirements described in Article 47(1).[^c-40cbdb1555242d43d46a51dfe7ba5d56] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

An application for registration must—[^c-0ea230c7d3afae984401d3db43cf8cb5] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

- (a) be made in such form and manner as the FCA may direct, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]
- (b) contain, or be accompanied by, such information as the FCA may direct. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

The applicant third-country firm shall provide *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945] the FCA[^c-9cbe310ffde6ed3fb33422ec4214a88c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945] with all *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945] further[^c-63da950c825dd076ff04c533018f9270] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945] information necessary for its registration. Within 30 working days of receipt of the application, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945] the FCA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945] shall assess whether the application is complete. If the application is not complete, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945] the FCA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945] shall set a deadline by which the applicant third-country firm is to provide additional information. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

The registration decision shall be based on the conditions set out in paragraph 2. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

Within 180 working days of the submission of a complete application, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945] the FCA[^c-675ef57ddfe52d029474de45fb7a026d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945] shall inform the applicant third-country firm in writing with a fully reasoned explanation whether the registration has been granted or refused. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

[^c-c3dc2ce119e9982810d2b92b0dd89a23]...

5. Third-country firms providing services in accordance with this Article shall inform clients established in the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945] United Kingdom[^c-bbed71bd663c8302a98067cf861faf50] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945], before the provision of any investment services, that they are not allowed to provide services to clients other than eligible counterparties and professional clients within the meaning of Section I of Annex II to Directive 2014/65/EU and that they are not subject to supervision in the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945] United Kingdom *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]. They shall indicate the name and the address of the competent authority responsible for supervision in the third country. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

The information in the first subparagraph shall be provided *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945] in writing, in a prominent way and in such form as the FCA may direct[^c-6b206628d5fe0e4a297db3ef83636de9] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945] .

[^c-3c1e9e5a7a1c776e2d7d43eac8d4edf7]... Where an eligible counterparty or professional client within *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945] Part 2 of Schedule 1 to this Regulation[^c-d19a3a3209a33c029f1635325601d734] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945] established or situated in the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945] United Kingdom *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945] initiates at its own exclusive initiative the provision of an investment service or activity by a third-country firm, this Article does not apply to the provision of that service or activity by the third-country firm to that person including a relationship specifically related to the provision of that service or activity. An initiative by such clients shall not entitle the third-country firm to market new categories of investment product or investment service to that individual. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

(5A.)[^c-b2f0c319e2d4043f443e1fb41dd9c9a3] For the purposes of paragraph 5, where a third-country firm or a person acting on behalf of a third-country firm solicits a person, the provision of an investment service or activity by the third-country firm to the person is not initiated at the person's own exclusive initiative. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

6. Third-country firms providing services or performing activities in accordance with this Article shall, before providing any service or performing any activity in relation to a client established in the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945] United Kingdom[^c-12ec415dfdfa600aa1131b8e1ceb59ee] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945], offer to submit any disputes relating to those services or activities to the jurisdiction of a court or arbitral tribunal in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945] the United Kingdom[^c-c8e2be8939c5512de88ecad66d46ff3e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945].

(6A.)[^c-54ea15a9955b71ae6cbb78e52ec98b94] Third-country firms providing services or performing activities in accordance with this Article must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

- (a) keep the data relating to all orders and transactions in the United Kingdom in financial instruments which they have carried out, whether on own account or on behalf of a client, for a period of five years, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]
- (b) make that data available to the FCA on request. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

(6B.) The FCA may make rules requiring third-country firms providing services or performing activities in accordance with this Article to provide information specified in the rules to the FCA at intervals specified in the rules. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]
