# Regulation (EU) No 600/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 May 2014 on markets in financial instruments and amending Regulation (EU) No 648/2012 (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 49A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-07-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/eur/2014/600
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 49A (affected by repeal, see note). Temporary prohibitions and restrictions: procedure (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-adde2cd88683b08e11b87c7b9b1761a6][^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945][^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. A temporary prohibition or restriction under Article 49(A1) takes effect— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

- (a) immediately, if the notice given under paragraph 3 states that that is the case, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]
- (b) on such date as may be specified in the notice, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]
- (c) if no date is specified in the notice, when the matter to which it relates is no longer open to review. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

2. A temporary prohibition or restriction under Article 49(A1) may be expressed to take effect immediately, or on a specified date, only if the FCA, having regard to the ground on which it is taking that action, reasonably considers that it is necessary for it to take effect immediately or (as appropriate) on that date. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

3. The FCA must give written notice to a third-country firm if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

- (a) it proposes to take action in relation to the firm under Article 49(A1), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]
- (b) it takes action in relation to the firm under Article 49(A1) with immediate effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

4. The notice must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

- (a) give details of the temporary prohibition or restriction, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]
- (b) inform the firm when the prohibition or restriction takes effect, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]
- (c) state the FCA's reasons for taking the action and for its determination of when the prohibition or restriction takes effect, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]
- (d) inform the firm that it may make representations to the FCA within such period as may be specified in the notice (whether or not the firm has referred the matter to the Upper Tribunal), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]
- (e) inform the firm of its right to refer the matter to the Upper Tribunal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

5. The FCA may extend the period allowed under the notice for making representations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

6. The FCA must give the third-country firm written notice if, having considered any representations made by the firm, the FCA decides— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

- (a) to impose the proposed prohibition or restriction, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]
- (b) if the prohibition or restriction has been imposed, not to rescind it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

7. The FCA must give the third-country firm written notice if, having considered any representations made by the firm, the FCA decides— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

- (a) not to impose the proposed prohibition or restriction, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]
- (b) to impose a different prohibition or restriction, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]
- (c) to rescind a prohibition or restriction that has been imposed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

8. A notice under paragraph 6 must inform the third-country firm of its right to refer the matter to the Upper Tribunal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

9. A notice under paragraph 7(b) must comply with paragraph 4. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

10. If a notice informs a third-country firm of its right to refer a matter to the Upper Tribunal, it must give an indication of the procedure on such a reference. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

11. A third-country firm that is aggrieved by action taken by the FCA under Article 49(A1) may refer the matter to the Upper Tribunal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

12. Part 9 of FSMA (hearings and appeals) applies in relation to references to the Upper Tribunal made under this Article as it applies in relation to references made to that Tribunal under that Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

13. For the purposes of paragraph 1(c), section 391(8) of FSMA (matters open to review) applies as if the notice under paragraph 3 were a supervisory notice (as defined in section 395 of that Act). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]
