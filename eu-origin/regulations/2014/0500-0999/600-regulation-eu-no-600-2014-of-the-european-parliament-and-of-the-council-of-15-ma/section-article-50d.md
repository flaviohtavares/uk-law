# Regulation (EU) No 600/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 May 2014 on markets in financial instruments and amending Regulation (EU) No 648/2012 (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 50D

## Article 50D (affected by repeal, see note). FCA rules (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-7286db8c3126d335aef618c948ae3434][^key-c192450003b8b4b40afcaee3d153cf62][^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945][^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. The provisions of Part 9A of FSMA (rules and guidance) listed in paragraph 2 apply in relation to rules made by the FCA under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945] this Regulation[^key-314705a5af5a359cf51f75af608efc03] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945] as they apply in relation to rules made by the FCA under that Part of that Act, subject to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945] modifications in paragraphs 2A and 3[^key-049bb0aae46c2a60002481027abd2b5d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945].

2. The provisions are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

- (a) section 137T (general supplementary powers); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]
- (b) Chapter 2 (modification, waiver, contravention and procedural provisions), with the exception of section 138D (actions for damages) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945] and section 138EA (matters to consider when making rules)[^key-141f579aa4db9bd0d2f9f44538f139cb] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945];
- (c) section 141A (power to make consequential amendments of references to rules etc). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

2A.[^key-b28ad1d4053d6a36e059a3f9b08d0673] In its application to rules made under Article 31, section 138I has effect as if the reference to the PRA in subsection (1)(a) were a reference to the Bank of England. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

3. Section 137T applies as if the reference to authorised persons *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945] included a reference to persons who are not authorised persons but to whom any of the rules made by the FCA under this Regulation apply[^key-ba59a70c412688f265666e27728d5099] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945].
