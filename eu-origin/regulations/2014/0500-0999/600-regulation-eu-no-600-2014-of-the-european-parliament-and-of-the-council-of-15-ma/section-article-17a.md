# Regulation (EU) No 600/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 May 2014 on markets in financial instruments and amending Regulation (EU) No 648/2012 (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 17a

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-07-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/eur/2014/600
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 17a (affected by repeal, see note). Tick sizes (affected by repeal, see note)[^c000005][^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945][^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Systematic internalisers’ quotes, price improvements on those quotes and execution prices shall comply with tick sizes set in accordance with *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945] paragraph 3G of Schedule 1 to the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Recognition Requirements for Investment Exchanges, Clearing Houses and Central Securities Depositories) Regulations 2001, as if that paragraph applied to the systemic internaliser in the course of its operations as such and sub-paragraph (1A) were omitted[^c-0e1f0213aa9a489612f38b4977c50050] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945].

Application of tick sizes shall not prevent systematic internalisers matching orders *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945] [^c-9f5a94524ddc61fa1063d8d183de19aa]... at mid‐point within the current bid and offer prices. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]
