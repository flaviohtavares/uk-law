# Regulation (EU) No 600/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 May 2014 on markets in financial instruments and amending Regulation (EU) No 648/2012 (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 6

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-07-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/eur/2014/600
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 6 (affected by repeal, see note). Post-trade transparency requirements for trading venues in respect of shares, depositary receipts, ETFs, certificates and other similar financial instruments (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945][^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. Market operators and investment firms operating a trading venue shall make public the price, volume and time of the transactions executed in respect of shares, depositary receipts, ETFs, certificates and other similar financial instruments traded on that trading venue. Market operators and investment firms operating a trading venue shall make details of all such transactions public as close to real-time as is technically possible. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

2. Market operators and investment firms operating a trading venue shall give access, on reasonable commercial terms and on a non-discriminatory basis, to the arrangements they employ for making public the information under paragraph 1 of this Article to investment firms which are obliged to publish the details of their transactions in shares, depositary receipts, ETFs, certificates and other similar financial instruments pursuant to Article 20. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]
