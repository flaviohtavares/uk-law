# Regulation (EU) No 600/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 May 2014 on markets in financial instruments and amending Regulation (EU) No 648/2012 (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 50B

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 50B (affected by repeal, see note). FCA Directions (affected by repeal, see note) identifying relevant area of the UK (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-43c2c693cb12bff145fe0e60ed883c6e][^key-c192450003b8b4b40afcaee3d153cf62][^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945][^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945][^M_F_13893002-6255-4539-aa02-9c00e37d63a3][^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. A direction may only be given, amended or revoked by the FCA under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945] [^key-a84f67067383f6d8e41f5511e3b3adfe]... Article 14 (“an FCA direction”) with the approval of the Treasury. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

2. An FCA direction— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

- (a) may specify different countries in relation to different financial instruments; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]
- (b) must specify the date on which the direction comes into effect and the financial instruments or class of instruments to which it applies; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]
- (c) may be amended or revoked. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

3. The Treasury may refuse to approve an FCA direction if it appears to the Treasury that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

- (a) the giving of that direction would prejudice any current or proposed negotiations for an international agreement between the United Kingdom and one or more other countries, international organisations or institutions; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]
- (b) there are grounds under section 410 (international obligations) of FSMA to direct the FCA not to give that direction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

4. For the purposes of paragraph 3, “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]**international organisations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]**” includes the European Union. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

5. The Treasury must notify the FCA in writing whether or not they approve an FCA direction within four weeks from the day on which that direction is submitted to the Treasury for approval (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]**the relevant period *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]**”).

6. If the Treasury do not give notice under paragraph 5 before the end of the relevant period the Treasury are deemed to have approved the direction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

7. Provision of a draft direction to the Treasury for consultation does not amount to submission of the direction for approval. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]

8. A copy of each FCA direction given under this Article must be must be laid before Parliament and published in a way appearing to the FCA to be best calculated to bring it to the attention of the public. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d902aeb0-be53-47f1-c9c0-dd03b0be1945]
