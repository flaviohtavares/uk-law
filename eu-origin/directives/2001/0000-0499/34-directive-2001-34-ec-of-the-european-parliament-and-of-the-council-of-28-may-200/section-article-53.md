# Directive 2001/34/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 28 May 2001 on the admission of securities to official stock exchange listing and on information to be published on those securities, section Article 53

> Text as at 2007-01-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 53.** The legal position of the debt securities must be in conformity with the laws and regulations to which they are subject.
