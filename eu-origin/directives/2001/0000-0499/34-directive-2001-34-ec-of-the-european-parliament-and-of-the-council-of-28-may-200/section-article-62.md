# Directive 2001/34/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 28 May 2001 on the admission of securities to official stock exchange listing and on information to be published on those securities, section Article 62

> Text as at 2007-01-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 62.** The application for admission to official listing must cover all the securities ranking *pari passu*.
