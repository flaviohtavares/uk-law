# Directive 2001/83/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 6 November 2001 on the Community code relating to medicinal products for human use, section Article 127b

> Text as at 2019-07-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 127b.**[^c000007] Member States shall ensure that appropriate collection systems are in place for medicinal products that are unused or have expired.

## Annotations

[^c000007]: Textual amendment: Inserted by [Directive 2004/27/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 31 March 2004 amending Directive 2001/83/EC on the Community code relating to medicinal products for human use](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2004/27).
