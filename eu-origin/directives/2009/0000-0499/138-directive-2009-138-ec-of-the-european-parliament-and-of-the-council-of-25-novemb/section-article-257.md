# Directive 2009/138/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 November 2009 on the taking-up and pursuit of the business of Insurance and Reinsurance (Solvency II) (recast) (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 257

> Text as at 2019-01-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 257. Administrative, management or supervisory body of insurance holding companies and mixed financial holding companies[^c000001]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Member States shall require that all persons who effectively run the insurance holding company or the mixed financial holding company are fit and proper to perform their duties.

Article 42 shall apply *mutatis mutandis* .
