# Directive 2009/138/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 November 2009 on the taking-up and pursuit of the business of Insurance and Reinsurance (Solvency II) (recast) (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 208

> Text as at 2019-01-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 208. Prohibition on compulsory ceding of part of underwriting

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Member States shall not require life insurance undertakings to cede part of their underwriting of activities listed in Article 2(3) to an organisation or organisations designated by national law.
