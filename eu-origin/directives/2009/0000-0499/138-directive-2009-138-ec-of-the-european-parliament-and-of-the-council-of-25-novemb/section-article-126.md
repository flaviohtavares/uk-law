# Directive 2009/138/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 November 2009 on the taking-up and pursuit of the business of Insurance and Reinsurance (Solvency II) (recast) (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 126

> Text as at 2019-01-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 126. External models and data

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The use of a model or data obtained from a third party shall not be considered to be a justification for exemption from any of the requirements for the internal model set out in Articles 120 to 125.
