# Directive 2009/138/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 November 2009 on the taking-up and pursuit of the business of Insurance and Reinsurance (Solvency II) (recast) (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 3

> Text as at 2019-01-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 3. Statutory systems

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Without prejudice to Article 2(3)(c), this Directive shall not apply to insurance forming part of a statutory system of social security.
