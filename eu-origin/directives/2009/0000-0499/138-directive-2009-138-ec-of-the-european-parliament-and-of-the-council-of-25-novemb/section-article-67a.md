# Directive 2009/138/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 November 2009 on the taking-up and pursuit of the business of Insurance and Reinsurance (Solvency II) (recast) (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 67a

> Text as at 2019-01-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 67a. European Parliament powers of investigation[^c000002]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Articles 64 and 67 shall be without prejudice to the powers of investigation conferred on the European Parliament by Article 226 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union (TFEU).
