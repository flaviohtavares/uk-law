## ANNEX — TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS FOR LATERAL PROTECTION[^c000001]

[^c000001]1.

**General prescriptions and definitions**

[^c000001]1.1.

Every vehicle of categories N<sub>2</sub>, N<sub>3</sub>, O<sub>3</sub> and O<sub>4</sub> shall be so constructed and /or equipped as to offer, when a complete entity, effective protection to unprotected road users (pedestrians, cyclists, motorcyclists) against the risk of falling under the sides of the vehicle and being caught under the wheels.
