# Directive (EU) 2017/1132 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 June 2017 relating to certain aspects of company law (codification) (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 27

> Text as at 2020-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 27. Persons carrying out disclosure formalities

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Each Member State shall determine by which persons the disclosure formalities are to be carried out.
