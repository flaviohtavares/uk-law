# Directive (EU) 2017/1132 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 June 2017 relating to certain aspects of company law (codification) (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 41

> Text as at 2020-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 41. Persons carrying out disclosure formalities

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Each Member State shall determine who shall carry out the disclosure formalities provided for in Sections 2 and 3.
