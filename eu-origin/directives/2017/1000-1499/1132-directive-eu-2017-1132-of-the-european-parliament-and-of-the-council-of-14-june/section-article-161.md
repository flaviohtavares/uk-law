# Directive (EU) 2017/1132 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 June 2017 relating to certain aspects of company law (codification) (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 161

> Text as at 2020-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 161. Data protection[^c000001]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The processing of any personal data carried out in the context of this Directive shall be subject to Regulation (EU) 2016/679.
