# Council Directive 2013/59/Euratom of 5 December 2013 laying down basic safety standards for protection against the dangers arising from exposure to ionising radiation, and repealing Directives 89/618/Euratom, 90/641/Euratom, 96/29/Euratom, 97/43/Euratom and 2003/122/Euratom, ANNEX XIII

> Text as at 2014-01-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## ANNEX XIII — Indicative list of types of building materials considered with regard to their emitted gamma radiation as referred to in Article 75

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1\.

Natural materials

- ((a)) Alum-shale.
- ((b)) Building materials or additives of natural igneous origin, such as:
  - granitoides (such as granites, syenite and orthogneiss),
  - porphyries;
  - tuff;
  - pozzolana (pozzolanic ash);
  - lava.

2\.

Materials incorporating residues from industries processing naturally-occurring radioactive material, such as:

- fly ash;
- phosphogypsum;
- phosphorus slag;
- tin slag;
- copper slag;
- red mud (residue from aluminium production);
- residues from steel production
