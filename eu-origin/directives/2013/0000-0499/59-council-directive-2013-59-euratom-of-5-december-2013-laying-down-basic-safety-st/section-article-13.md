# Council Directive 2013/59/Euratom of 5 December 2013 laying down basic safety standards for protection against the dangers arising from exposure to ionising radiation, and repealing Directives 89/618/Euratom, 90/641/Euratom, 96/29/Euratom, 97/43/Euratom and 2003/122/Euratom, section Article 13

> Text as at 2014-01-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 13. Estimation of the effective and equivalent dose

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the estimation of effective and equivalent doses, the appropriate standard values and relationships shall be used. For external radiation, the operational quantities defined in section 2.3 of ICRP Publication 116 shall be used.
