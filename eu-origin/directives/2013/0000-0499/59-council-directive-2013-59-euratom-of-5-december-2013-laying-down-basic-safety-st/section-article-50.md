# Council Directive 2013/59/Euratom of 5 December 2013 laying down basic safety standards for protection against the dangers arising from exposure to ionising radiation, and repealing Directives 89/618/Euratom, 90/641/Euratom, 96/29/Euratom, 97/43/Euratom and 2003/122/Euratom, section Article 50

> Text as at 2014-01-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 50. Appeals

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Member States shall lay down the procedure for appeal against the findings and decisions made pursuant to Articles 46, 47 and 49.
