# Directive (EU) 2018/1972 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 11 December 2018 establishing the European Electronic Communications Code (Recast) (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 116

> Text as at 2018-12-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 116. Adaptation of annexes

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Commission is empowered to adopt delegated acts in accordance with Article 117 amending Annexes V, VI, IX, X, and XI in order to take account of technological and social developments or changes in market demand.
