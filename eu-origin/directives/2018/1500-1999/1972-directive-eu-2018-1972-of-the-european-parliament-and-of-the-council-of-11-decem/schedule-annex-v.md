# Directive (EU) 2018/1972 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 11 December 2018 establishing the European Electronic Communications Code (Recast) (Text with EEA relevance), ANNEX V

> Text as at 2018-12-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## ANNEX V — MINIMUM SET OF SERVICES WHICH THE ADEQUATE BROADBAND INTERNET ACCESS SERVICE IN ACCORDANCE WITH ARTICLE 84(3) SHALL BE CAPABLE OF SUPPORTING

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

- ((1)) E-mail
- ((2)) search engines enabling search and finding of all type of information
- ((3)) basic training and education online tools
- ((4)) online newspapers or news
- ((5)) buying or ordering goods or services online
- ((6)) job searching and job searching tools
- ((7)) professional networking
- ((8)) internet banking
- ((9)) eGovernment service use
- ((10)) social media and instant messaging
- ((11)) calls and video calls (standard quality)
