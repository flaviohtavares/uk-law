# Directive (EU) 2018/1972 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 11 December 2018 establishing the European Electronic Communications Code (Recast) (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 92

> Text as at 2018-12-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 92. Additional mandatory services

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Member States may decide to make services additional to those included in the universal service obligations referred to in Articles 84 to 87, publicly available on their territories. In such cases, no compensation mechanism involving specific undertakings shall be imposed.
