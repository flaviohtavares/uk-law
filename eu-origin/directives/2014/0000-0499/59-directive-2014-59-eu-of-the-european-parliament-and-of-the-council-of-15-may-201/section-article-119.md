# Directive 2014/59/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 May 2014 establishing a framework for the recovery and resolution of credit institutions and investment firms and amending Council Directive 82/891/EEC, and Directives 2001/24/EC, 2002/47/EC, 2004/25/EC, 2005/56/EC, 2007/36/EC, 2011/35/EU, 2012/30/EU and 2013/36/EU, and Regulations (EU) No 1093/2010 and (EU) No 648/2012, of the European Parliament and of the Council (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 119

> Text as at 2020-01-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 119. Amendment to Directive 2004/25/EC

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 4(5) of Directive 2004/25/EC, the following subparagraph is added:

> Member States shall ensure that Article 5(1) of this Directive does not apply in the case of use of resolution tools, powers and mechanisms provided for in Title IV of Directive 2014/59/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council[^f00043].

.
