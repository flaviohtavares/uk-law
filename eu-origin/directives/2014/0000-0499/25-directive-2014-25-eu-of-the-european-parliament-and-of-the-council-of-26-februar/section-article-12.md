# Directive 2014/25/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 26 February 2014 on procurement by entities operating in the water, energy, transport and postal services sectors and repealing Directive 2004/17/EC (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 12

> Text as at 2020-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 12. Ports and airports

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Directive shall apply to activities relating to the exploitation of a geographical area for the purpose of the provision of airports and maritime or inland ports or other terminal facilities to carriers by air, sea or inland waterway.
