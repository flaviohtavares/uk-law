# Directive 2014/25/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 26 February 2014 on procurement by entities operating in the water, energy, transport and postal services sectors and repealing Directive 2004/17/EC (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 107

> Text as at 2020-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 107. Repeal

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Directive 2004/17/EC is repealed with effect from 18 April 2016.

References to the repealed Directive shall be construed as being made to this Directive and shall be read in accordance with the correlation table in Annex XXI.
