# Directive 2014/25/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 26 February 2014 on procurement by entities operating in the water, energy, transport and postal services sectors and repealing Directive 2004/17/EC (Text with EEA relevance), section Article 69

> Text as at 2020-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 69. Contract notices

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Contract notices may be used as a means of calling for competition in respect of all procedures. They shall contain the information set out in the relevant part of Annex XI and shall be published in accordance with Article 71.
