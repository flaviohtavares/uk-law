# Council Directive 2006/112/EC of 28 November 2006 on the common system of value added tax, section Article 192a

> Text as at 2020-12-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 192a.**[^c000004] For the purposes of this Section, a taxable person who has a fixed establishment within the territory of the Member State where the tax is due shall be regarded as a taxable person who is not established within that Member State when the following conditions are met:

- ((a)) he makes a taxable supply of goods or of services within the territory of that Member State;
- ((b)) an establishment which the supplier has within the territory of that Member State does not intervene in that supply.
