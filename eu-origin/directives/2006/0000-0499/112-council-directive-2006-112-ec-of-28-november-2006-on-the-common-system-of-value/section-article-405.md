# Council Directive 2006/112/EC of 28 November 2006 on the common system of value added tax, section Article 405

> Text as at 2020-12-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 405.** For the purposes of this Chapter, the following definitions shall apply:

- ((1)) ‘Community’ means the territory of the Community as defined in point (1) of Article 5 before the accession of new Member States;
- ((2)) ‘new Member States’ means the territory of the Member States which acceded to the European Union after 1 January 1995, as defined for each of those Member States in point (2) of Article 5;
- ((3)) ‘enlarged Community’ means the territory of the Community as defined in point (1) of Article 5 after the accession of new Member States.
