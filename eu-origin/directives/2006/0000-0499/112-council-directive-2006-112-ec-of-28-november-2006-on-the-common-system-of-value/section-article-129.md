# Council Directive 2006/112/EC of 28 November 2006 on the common system of value added tax, section Article 129

> Text as at 2020-12-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 129.** 1.[^c000022] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

2. Slovenia may, until[^c000023] 31 December 2010, continue to apply a reduced rate of not less than 5 % to the supply of construction, renovation and maintenance work for residential housing not provided as part of a social policy, excluding building materials.
