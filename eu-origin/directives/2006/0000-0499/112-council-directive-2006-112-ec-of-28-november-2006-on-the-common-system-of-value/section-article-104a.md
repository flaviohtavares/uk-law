# Council Directive 2006/112/EC of 28 November 2006 on the common system of value added tax, section Article 104a

> Text as at 2020-12-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 104a.**[^c000020] Cyprus may apply one of the two reduced rates provided for in Article 98 to the supply of liquid petroleum gas (LPG) in cylinders.

## Annotations

[^c000020]: Textual amendment: Inserted by [Council Directive 2009/47/EC of 5 May 2009 amending Directive 2006/112/EC as regards reduced rates of value added tax](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2009/47).
