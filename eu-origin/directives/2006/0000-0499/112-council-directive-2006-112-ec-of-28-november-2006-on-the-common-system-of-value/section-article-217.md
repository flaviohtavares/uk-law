# Council Directive 2006/112/EC of 28 November 2006 on the common system of value added tax, section Article 217

> Text as at 2020-12-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 217.**[^c000011] For the purposes of this Directive, ‘ electronic invoice ’ means an invoice that contains the information required in this Directive, and which has been issued and received in any electronic format.

## Annotations

[^c000011]: Textual amendment: Substituted by [Council Directive 2010/45/EU of 13 July 2010 amending Directive 2006/112/EC on the common system of value added tax as regards the rules on invoicing](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2010/45).
