# Council Directive 2006/112/EC of 28 November 2006 on the common system of value added tax, section Article 302

> Text as at 2020-12-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 302.** If a flat-rate farmer is entitled to flat-rate compensation, he shall not be entitled to deduction of VAT in respect of activities covered by this flat-rate scheme.
