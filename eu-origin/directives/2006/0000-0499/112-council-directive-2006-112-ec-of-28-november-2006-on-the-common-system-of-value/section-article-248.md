# Council Directive 2006/112/EC of 28 November 2006 on the common system of value added tax, section Article 248

> Text as at 2020-12-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 248.** Member States may, subject to conditions which they lay down, require the storage of invoices received by non-taxable persons.
