# Council Directive 2006/112/EC of 28 November 2006 on the common system of value added tax, section Article 181

> Text as at 2020-12-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 181.**[^c000011] Member States may authorise a taxable person who does not hold an invoice drawn up in accordance with Sections 3 to 5 of Chapter 3 of Title XI to make the deduction referred to in Article 168(c) in respect of his intra-Community acquisitions of goods.

## Annotations

[^c000011]: Textual amendment: Substituted by [Council Directive 2010/45/EU of 13 July 2010 amending Directive 2006/112/EC on the common system of value added tax as regards the rules on invoicing](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2010/45).
