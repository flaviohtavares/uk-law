# Council Directive 2006/112/EC of 28 November 2006 on the common system of value added tax, section Article 6

> Text as at 2020-12-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 6.** 1. This Directive shall not apply to the following territories forming part of the customs territory of the Community:

- (a) Mount Athos;
- (b) the Canary Islands;
- (c)[^c000007] the French territories referred to in Article 349 and Article 355(1) of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union;
- (d) the Åland Islands;
- (e) the Channel Islands;[^c000008]
- (f)[^c000009] Campione d'Italia;
- (g) the Italian waters of Lake Lugano.

2. This Directive shall not apply to the following territories not forming part of the customs territory of the Community:

- (a) the Island of Heligoland;
- (b) the territory of Büsingen;
- (c) Ceuta;
- (d) Melilla;
- (e) Livigno.
