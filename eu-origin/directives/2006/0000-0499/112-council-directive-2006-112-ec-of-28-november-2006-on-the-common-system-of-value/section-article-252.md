# Council Directive 2006/112/EC of 28 November 2006 on the common system of value added tax, section Article 252

> Text as at 2020-12-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 252.** 1. The VAT return shall be submitted by a deadline to be determined by Member States. That deadline may not be more than two months after the end of each tax period.

2. The tax period shall be set by each Member State at one month, two months or three months.

Member States may, however, set different tax periods provided that those periods do not exceed one year.
