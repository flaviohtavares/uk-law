# Council Directive 2006/112/EC of 28 November 2006 on the common system of value added tax, section Article 215

> Text as at 2020-12-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 215.** Each individual VAT identification number shall have a prefix in accordance with ISO code 3166 — alpha 2 — by which the Member State of issue may be identified.

Nevertheless, Greece may use the prefix ‘EL’.

The prefix[^c000036] ‘ XI ’ shall be used for Northern Ireland.

## Annotations

[^c000036]: Textual amendment: Inserted by [Council Directive (EU) 2020/1756 of 20 November 2020 amending Directive 2006/112/EC on the common system of value added tax as regards the identification of taxable persons in Northern Ireland](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2020/1756).
