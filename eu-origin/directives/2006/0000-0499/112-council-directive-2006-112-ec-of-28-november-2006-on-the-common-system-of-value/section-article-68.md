# Council Directive 2006/112/EC of 28 November 2006 on the common system of value added tax, section Article 68

> Text as at 2020-12-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 68.** The chargeable event shall occur when the intra-Community acquisition of goods is made.

The intra-Community acquisition of goods shall be regarded as being made when the supply of similar goods is regarded as being effected within the territory of the relevant Member State.
