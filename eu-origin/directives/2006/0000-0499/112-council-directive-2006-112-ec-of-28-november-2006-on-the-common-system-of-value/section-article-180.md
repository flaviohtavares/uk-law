# Council Directive 2006/112/EC of 28 November 2006 on the common system of value added tax, section Article 180

> Text as at 2020-12-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 180.** Member States may authorise a taxable person to make a deduction which he has not made in accordance with Articles 178 and 179.
