# Council Directive 2006/112/EC of 28 November 2006 on the common system of value added tax, section Article 260

> Text as at 2020-12-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 260.** Member States shall lay down detailed rules for the submission of VAT returns in respect of the importation of goods.
