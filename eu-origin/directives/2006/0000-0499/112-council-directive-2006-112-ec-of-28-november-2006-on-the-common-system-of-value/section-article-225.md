# Council Directive 2006/112/EC of 28 November 2006 on the common system of value added tax, section Article 225

> Text as at 2020-12-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 225.**[^c000011] Member States may impose specific conditions on taxable persons in cases where the third party, or the customer, who issues invoices is established in a country with which no legal instrument exists relating to mutual assistance similar in scope to that provided for in Directive 2010/24/EU [^f00018] and Regulation (EC) No 1798/2003 [^f00019] .
