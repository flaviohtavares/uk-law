# Council Directive 2006/112/EC of 28 November 2006 on the common system of value added tax, section Article 49

> Text as at 2020-12-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 49.**[^c000003] The place of supply of the transport of goods, other than the intra-Community transport of goods, to non-taxable persons shall be the place where the transport takes place, proportionate to the distances covered.

## Annotations

[^c000003]: Textual amendment: Substituted by [Council Directive 2008/8/EC of 12 February 2008 amending Directive 2006/112/EC as regards the place of supply of services](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2008/8).
