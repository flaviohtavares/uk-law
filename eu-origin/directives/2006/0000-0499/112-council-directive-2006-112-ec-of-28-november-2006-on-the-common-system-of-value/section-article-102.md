# Council Directive 2006/112/EC of 28 November 2006 on the common system of value added tax, section Article 102

> Text as at 2020-12-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 102.**[^c000006] After consultation of the VAT Committee, each Member State may apply a reduced rate to the supply of natural gas, electricity or district heating.

## Annotations

[^c000006]: Textual amendment: Substituted by [Council Directive 2009/162/EU of 22 December 2009 amending various provisions of Directive 2006/112/EC on the common system of value added tax](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2009/162).
