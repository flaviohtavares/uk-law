# Council Directive 2006/112/EC of 28 November 2006 on the common system of value added tax, section Article 115

> Text as at 2020-12-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 115.**[^c000021] Member States which, at 1 January 1991 , were applying a reduced rate to children’s clothing, children’s footwear or housing may continue to apply such a rate to the supply of those goods or services.

## Annotations

[^c000021]: Textual amendment: Substituted by [Council Directive 2009/47/EC of 5 May 2009 amending Directive 2006/112/EC as regards reduced rates of value added tax](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2009/47).
