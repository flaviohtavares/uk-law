# Agreement on the withdrawal of the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland from the European Union and the European Atomic Energy Community, section Article 133

> Text as at 2020-06-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 133. Currency to be used between the Union and the United Kingdom

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Without prejudice to the applicable Union law concerning the Union's own resources, all amounts, liabilities, calculations, accounts and payments referred to in this Part shall be drawn up and implemented in euro.
