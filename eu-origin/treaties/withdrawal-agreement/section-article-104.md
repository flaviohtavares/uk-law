# Agreement on the withdrawal of the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland from the European Union and the European Atomic Energy Community, section Article 104

> Text as at 2020-06-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 104. Taxation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Article 3 of the Protocol on the Privileges and Immunities shall apply in respect of the Union's assets, revenues and other property in the United Kingdom at the end of the transition period, until they are no longer in official use or have been removed from the United Kingdom.
