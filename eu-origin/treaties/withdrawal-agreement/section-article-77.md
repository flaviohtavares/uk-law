# Agreement on the withdrawal of the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland from the European Union and the European Atomic Energy Community, section Article 77

> Text as at 2020-06-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 77. Review procedures

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Council Directives 89/665/EEC[^f00103] and 92/13/EEC[^f00104] shall apply in respect of the public procurement procedures referred to in Article 76 of this Agreement which fall within the scope of those Directives.

[^f00103]: Footnote: Council Directive 89/665/EEC of 21 December 1989 on the coordination of the laws, regulations and administrative provisions relating to the application of review procedures to the award of public supply and public works contracts ([OJ L 395, 30.12.1989, p. 33](https://webarchive.nationalarchives.gov.uk/eu-exit/https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=uriserv:OJ.L_.1989.395.01.0033.01.ENG)).

[^f00104]: Footnote: Council Directive 92/13/EEC of 25 February 1992 coordinating the laws, regulations and administrative provisions relating to the application of Community rules on the procurement procedures of entities operating in the water, energy, transport and telecommunications sectors ([OJ L 76, 23.3.1992, p. 14](https://webarchive.nationalarchives.gov.uk/eu-exit/https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=uriserv:OJ.L_.1992.076.01.0014.01.ENG)).
