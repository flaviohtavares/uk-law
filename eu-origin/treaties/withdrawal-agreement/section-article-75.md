# Agreement on the withdrawal of the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland from the European Union and the European Atomic Energy Community, section Article 75

> Text as at 2020-06-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 75. Definition

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of this Title, "relevant rules" means the general principles of Union law applicable to the award of public contracts, Directives 2009/81/EC[^f00093], 2014/23/EU[^f00094], 2014/24/EU[^f00095] and 2014/25/EU[^f00096] of the European Parliament and of the Council, Regulations (EC) No 2195/2002[^f00097] and (EC) No 1370/2007[^f00098] of the European Parliament and of the Council, Article 4 of Council Regulation (EEC) No 3577/92[^f00099], Articles 11 and 12 of Council Directive 96/67/EC[^f00100], Articles 16, 17 and 18 of Regulation (EC) No 1008/2008 of the European Parliament and of the Council[^f00101], Articles 6 and 7 of Regulation (EU) 2017/352 of the European Parliament and of the Council[^f00102], and any other specific provisions of Union law governing public procurement procedures.
