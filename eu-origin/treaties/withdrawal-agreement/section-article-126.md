# Agreement on the withdrawal of the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland from the European Union and the European Atomic Energy Community, section Article 126

> Text as at 2020-06-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 126. Transition period

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

There shall be a transition or implementation period, which shall start on the date of entry into force of this Agreement and end on 31 December 2020.
