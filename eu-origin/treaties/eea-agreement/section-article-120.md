# Agreement on the European Economic Area, section Article 120

> Text as at 2019-12-14.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 120.** Unless otherwise provided in this Agreement and in particular in Protocols 41 and 43[^c000010], the application of the provisions of this Agreement shall prevail over provisions in existing bilateral or multilateral agreements binding the European Economic Community, on the one hand, and one or more EFTA States, on the other, to the extent that the same subject matter is governed by this Agreement.
