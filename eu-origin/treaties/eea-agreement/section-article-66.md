# Agreement on the European Economic Area, section Article 66

> Text as at 2019-12-14.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 66.** The Contracting Parties agree upon the need to promote improved working conditions and an improved standard of living for workers.
