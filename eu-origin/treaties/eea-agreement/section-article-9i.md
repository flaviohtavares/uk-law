# Agreement on the European Economic Area, section Article 9i

> Text as at 2019-12-14.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 9i. Safeguard measures and suspension of the provisions of this Chapter[^c000021]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. If a Contracting Party does not respect the conditions stipulated in this Chapter or if the equivalency of the customs security measures in the Contracting Parties is no longer assured, after consultations in the EEA Joint Committee and only for a scope and duration strictly necessary for settling the situation, another Contracting Party may suspend partially or completely the application of the provisions of this Chapter or take appropriate measures. Articles 112 to 114 of the Agreement apply mutatis mutandis.

2. If the equivalency of the customs security measures is no longer assured because the amendments referred to in Article 9h(3) have not been decided, the application of this Chapter is suspended on the date when the Community legislation concerned is applied, unless the EEA Joint Committee, having examined the measures to maintain its application, decides otherwise.
