# Agreement on the European Economic Area, section Article 41

> Text as at 2019-12-14.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 41.** Current payments connected with the movement of goods, persons, services or capital between Contracting Parties within the framework of the provisions of this Agreement shall be free of all restrictions.

## Article 41. Transitional arrangements regarding the accession of the Republic of Croatia to the European Union (affected by repeal, see note)[^c000019][^c000019][^c000018]
