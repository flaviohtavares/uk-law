# Consolidated version of the Treaty establishing the European Atomic Energy Community 2016/C 203/01, section Article 36

> Text as at 2019-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 36.** The appropriate authorities shall periodically communicate information on the checks referred to in Article 35 to the Commission so that it is kept informed of the level of radioactivity to which the public is exposed.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 36.** Judgments shall state the reasons on which they are based. They shall contain the names of the Judges who took part in the deliberations.
