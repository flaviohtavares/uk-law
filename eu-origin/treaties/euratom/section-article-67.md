# Consolidated version of the Treaty establishing the European Atomic Energy Community 2016/C 203/01, section Article 67

> Text as at 2019-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 67.** Save where exceptions are provided for in this Treaty, prices shall be determined as a result of balancing supply against demand as provided in Article 60; the national regulations of the Member States shall not contravene such provisions.
