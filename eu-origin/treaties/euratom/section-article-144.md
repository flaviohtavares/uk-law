# Consolidated version of the Treaty establishing the European Atomic Energy Community 2016/C 203/01, section Article 144

> Text as at 2019-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 144.** The Court of Justice of the European Union shall have unlimited jurisdiction in:

- ((a)) proceedings instituted under Article 12 to have the appropriate terms fixed for the granting by the Commission of licences or sub licences;
- ((b)) proceedings instituted by persons or undertakings against sanctions imposed on them by the Commission under Article 83.
