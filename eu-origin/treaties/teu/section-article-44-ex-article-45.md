# Consolidated version of the Treaty on European Union, section Article 44 (ex Article 45)

> Text as at 2020-03-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 44 (ex Article 45). The General Council of the ECB

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

44.1. Without prejudice to Article 129(1) of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, the General Council shall be constituted as a third decision-making body of the ECB.

44.2. The General Council shall comprise the President and Vice-President of the ECB and the Governors of the national central banks. The other members of the Executive Board may participate, without having the right to vote, in meetings of the General Council.

44.3. The responsibilities of the General Council are listed in full in Article 46 of this Statute.
