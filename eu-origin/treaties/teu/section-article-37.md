# Consolidated version of the Treaty on European Union, section Article 37

> Text as at 2020-03-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 37. (ex Article 24 TEU)

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Union may conclude agreements with one or more States or international organisations in areas covered by this Chapter.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 37.** Judgments shall be signed by the President and the Registrar. They shall be read in open court.
