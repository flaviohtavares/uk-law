# Consolidated version of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, section Article 76

> Text as at 2020-03-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 76.** The acts referred to in Chapters 4 and 5, together with the measures referred to in Article 74 which ensure administrative cooperation in the areas covered by these Chapters, shall be adopted:

- ((a)) on a proposal from the Commission, or
- ((b)) on the initiative of a quarter of the Member States.
