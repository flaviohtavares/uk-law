# Consolidated version of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, section Article 277

> Text as at 2020-03-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 277. (ex Article 241 TEC)

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Notwithstanding the expiry of the period laid down in Article 263, sixth paragraph, any party may,[^c000002] in proceedings in which an act of general application adopted by an institution, body, office or agency of the Union is at issue, plead the grounds specified in Article 263, second paragraph, in order to invoke before the Court of Justice of the European Union the inapplicability of that act.
