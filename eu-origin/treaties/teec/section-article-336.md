# Consolidated version of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, section Article 336

> Text as at 2020-03-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 336. (ex Article 283 TEC)

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The European Parliament and the Council shall, acting by means of regulations in accordance with the ordinary legislative procedure and after[^c000004] consulting the other institutions concerned, lay down the Staff Regulations of Officials of the European Union and the Conditions of Employment of other servants of the Union.
