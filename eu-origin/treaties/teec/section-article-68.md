# Consolidated version of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, section Article 68

> Text as at 2020-03-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 68.** The European Council shall define the strategic guidelines for legislative and operational planning within the area of freedom, security and justice.
