# COMMISSION DECISION of 10 February 1997 concerning the information to be entered in the computerized file of consignments of animals or animal products from third countries which are re-dispatched (Text with EEA relevance) (97/152/EC)

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**COMMISSION DECISION**

**of 10 February 1997**

**concerning the information to be entered in the computerized file of consignments of animals or animal products from third countries which are re-dispatched**

**(Text with EEA relevance)**

**(97/152/EC)**

THE COMMISSION OF THE EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES,

Having regard to the Treaty establishing the European Community,

Having regard to Council Decision 92/438/EEC of 13 July 1992 on computerization of veterinary import procedures (Shift project), amending Directives 90/675/EEC, 91/496/EEC, 91/628/EEC and Decision 90/424/EEC, and repealing Decision 88/192/EEC [^f00001], as last amended by the Act of Accession of Austria, Finland and Sweden, and in particular Article 12 thereof,

Whereas an information system must be established for consignments of animals re-dispatched by veterinary officials at border inspection posts;

Whereas each authority concerned must have random access to a computerized file of consignments of animals or products which are re-dispatched;

Whereas the information to be entered in the computerized file should be laid down in order to be able accurately to identify re-dispatched consignments and the reasons for re-dispatch;

Whereas the measures provided for in this Decision are in accordance with the opinion of the Standing Veterinary Committee,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

**Article 1.** The information to be entered in the computerized file of consignments of animals or animal products from third countries which are re-dispatched shall be as set out in the Annex.

In this Decision, “[^c-a86d7ff088f739f541167045c8d485bc]**third country**” means any country or territory other than the British Islands.

**Article 2.**[^c-f72c9ae56920ed163b206bc540665949] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

Done at Brussels, 10 February 1997.

*For the Commission*

Franz FISCHLER

*Member of the Commission*

## ANNEX — INFORMATION TO BE ENTERED IN THE COMPUTERIZED FILE OF CONSIGNMENTS OF ANIMALS OR ANIMAL PRODUCTS FROM THIRD COUNTRIES WHICH ARE RE-DISPATCHED

1\.

Border Control Post[^c-8c783a087ad58b7be17c83119677ab58]

- (a) Name and code of Border Control Post[^c-224e0a0e4f3d830ab86d8cf230292bcc] (*Animo*)
- (b) Name of veterinarian responsible.

2\.

Consignment

- (a) Nature and code of goods (*Animo*)
- (b) Number/quantity/units
- (c) Health certificate:
  - (i) serial number,
  - (ii) date,
  - (iii) name and service of veterinarian signing certificate,
  - (iv) service of origin of veterinarian,
  - (v) country of origin
- (d) Serial number of ‘Annex B’

3\.

Traders

- (a) Name and address of holding and/or establishment of origin (authorization number where appropriate)
- (b) Name and address of exporter
- (c) Name and address of importer

4\.

Consignment movements

- (a) Third country of origin
- (b) Third country of dispatch
- (c) Region of origin (where appropriate)
- (d) Country of destination[^c-57a719046fe6bb6f6af1ade30d247c4f]
- (e) Consignee and address of destination (authorization number where appropriate)
- (f) Means of transport:

  <table>
  <tbody>
  <tr><td>(i)</td><td>air:</td><td>flight number,</td></tr>
  <tr><td rowspan="2">(ii)</td><td rowspan="2">road and rail:</td><td>— registration number of vehicle,</td></tr>
  <tr><td>— number of waggon,</td></tr>
  <tr><td>(iii)</td><td>sea:</td><td>— name of ship,</td></tr>
  <tr><td>(iv)</td><td colspan="2">number of container (if applicable)</td></tr>
  </tbody>
  </table>

5\.

Re-dispatch

- (a) Date and time (if possible) of re-dispatch
- (b) Departure point (if different from Border Control Post[^c-e784f19faad86ba3506473ff388609fb] of entry)
- (c) Destination (if possible)
- (d) Means of transport:

  <table>
  <tbody>
  <tr><td>(i)</td><td>air:</td><td>flight number,</td></tr>
  <tr><td rowspan="2">(ii)</td><td rowspan="2">road and rail:</td><td>— registration number of vehicle,</td></tr>
  <tr><td>— number of waggon,</td></tr>
  <tr><td>(iii)</td><td>sea:</td><td>— name of ship,</td></tr>
  <tr><td>(iv)</td><td colspan="2">number of container</td></tr>
  </tbody>
  </table>

6\.

Reason for re-dispatch

- (a) Documentary checks:
  - (i) outside competence of Border Control Post[^c-4fc6eb2a484542bc2d136d7cb8630392],
  - (ii) absence of certificate/licence,
  - (iii) invalid certificate/licence,
    - copy rather than original,
    - other formal errors,
  - (iv) documentary errors,
    - third country,
    - region,
    - establishment,
    - additional guarantees,
    - protective clause
- (b) Identity[^c-4fc29dc0c06044f49f9386d7ebf6109e] checks.
  - (i) discrepancy between certificate/licence and goods.
  - (ii) absence of regulatory mark or brand.
  - (iii) results of visual examination negative,
    - goods,
    - means of transport,
    - animal welfare
- (c) Physical checks.
  - Goods not complying with rules,
    - examination by veterinarian,
    - laboratory tests,
    - animal welfare

7\.
