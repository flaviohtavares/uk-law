# Council Decision (EU) 2016/1351 of 4 August 2016 concerning the Staff Regulations of the European Defence Agency, and repealing Decision 2004/676/EC, section Article 50

> Text as at 2016-08-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 50.** Special allowances may be granted to certain members of temporary staff to compensate for particularly arduous working conditions.

The Agency shall determine the categories of beneficiaries, and the rates and conditions of such special allowances.
