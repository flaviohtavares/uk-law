# Council Decision (EU) 2016/1351 of 4 August 2016 concerning the Staff Regulations of the European Defence Agency, and repealing Decision 2004/676/EC, section Article 171

> Text as at 2016-08-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 171.** Articles 1(2), Articles 6, 11, 12, 13 and 14, the first paragraph of Article 18, Articles 19, 20, Article 21(1), Articles 22, 27 and 28, the second paragraph of Article 32, and Article 36, concerning the rights and obligations of staff members of the Agency, and Articles 168 and 169, concerning appeals, shall apply by analogy.
