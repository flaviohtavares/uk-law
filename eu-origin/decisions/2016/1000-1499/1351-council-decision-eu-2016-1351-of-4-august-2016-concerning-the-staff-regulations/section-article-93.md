# Council Decision (EU) 2016/1351 of 4 August 2016 concerning the Staff Regulations of the European Defence Agency, and repealing Decision 2004/676/EC, section Article 93

> Text as at 2016-08-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 93.** 1. Payment of benefits shall be made in accordance with Articles 84 and 85 of these Staff Regulations and with Article 28 of Annex V.

2. Any sums due from a member of the temporary staff to the Agency under this insurance scheme at the date when the benefits are payable shall be deducted from the amount of his benefit or from the benefits payable to those entitled under him. The deduction may be spread over a number of months.
