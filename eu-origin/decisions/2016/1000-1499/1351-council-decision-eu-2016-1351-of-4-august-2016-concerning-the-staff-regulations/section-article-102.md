# Council Decision (EU) 2016/1351 of 4 August 2016 concerning the Staff Regulations of the European Defence Agency, and repealing Decision 2004/676/EC, section Article 102

> Text as at 2016-08-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 102.** 1. Contract staff shall be paid from the total appropriations for the purpose under the budget of the Agency.

2. The AACC shall adopt specific provisions governing the use of contract staff.

3. The Agency shall provide indicative yearly forecasts for the use of contract staff per function group, in the context of the budget procedure.
