# Council Decision (EU) 2016/1351 of 4 August 2016 concerning the Staff Regulations of the European Defence Agency, and repealing Decision 2004/676/EC, section Article 7

> Text as at 2016-08-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 7.** 1. Members of temporary staff in active employment shall have access to measures of a social nature, including specific measures to reconcile working life with family life, adopted by the Agency and to services provided by the Staff Committee. Former members of temporary staff may have access to limited specific measures of a social nature.

2. Members of temporary staff in active employment shall be accorded working conditions complying with appropriate health and safety standards at least equivalent to the minimum requirements applicable under measures adopted in these areas pursuant to the Treaties.
