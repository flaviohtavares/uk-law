# Council Decision (EU) 2016/1351 of 4 August 2016 concerning the Staff Regulations of the European Defence Agency, and repealing Decision 2004/676/EC, section Article 99

> Text as at 2016-08-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 99.** 1. The employment of a member of the temporary staff shall be terminated by the Agency without notice if the AACC finds:

- (a) that at the time of his engagement he deliberately furnished false information concerning his professional qualifications and experience or his ability to fulfil the requirements of Article 37(2); and
- (b) that the false information furnished was a determining factor in his being engaged.

2. In such cases the AACC shall, after hearing the member of temporary staff concerned, and after the disciplinary procedure provided for in Title V has been followed, declare that his employment is terminated.

Before his employment is terminated, a member of temporary staff may be suspended in accordance with Article 161.

The provisions of Article 98(2) shall apply.
