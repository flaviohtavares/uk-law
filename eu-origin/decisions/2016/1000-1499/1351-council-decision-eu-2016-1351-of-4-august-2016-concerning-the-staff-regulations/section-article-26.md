# Council Decision (EU) 2016/1351 of 4 August 2016 concerning the Staff Regulations of the European Defence Agency, and repealing Decision 2004/676/EC, section Article 26

> Text as at 2016-08-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 26.** A member of temporary staff may be required to make good, in whole or in part, any damage suffered by the Agency as a result of serious misconduct on his part in the course of or in connection with the performance of his duties.

A reasoned decision shall be given by the AACC authority in accordance with the procedure laid down in regard to disciplinary matters.

The Court of Justice of the European Union shall have unlimited jurisdiction in disputes arising under this provision.
