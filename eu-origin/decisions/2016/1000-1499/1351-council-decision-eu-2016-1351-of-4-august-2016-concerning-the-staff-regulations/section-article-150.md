# Council Decision (EU) 2016/1351 of 4 August 2016 concerning the Staff Regulations of the European Defence Agency, and repealing Decision 2004/676/EC, section Article 150

> Text as at 2016-08-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 150.** 1. The AACC shall submit a report to the Disciplinary Board, stating clearly the facts complained of and, where appropriate, the circumstances in which they arose, including any aggravating or extenuating circumstances.

2. The report shall be communicated to the staff member concerned and to the chairman of the Disciplinary Board, who shall bring it to the attention of the members of the Disciplinary Board.
