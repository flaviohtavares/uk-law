# Council Decision (EU) 2016/1351 of 4 August 2016 concerning the Staff Regulations of the European Defence Agency, and repealing Decision 2004/676/EC, section Article 160

> Text as at 2016-08-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 160.** 1. After hearing the member of staff, the AACC shall take its decision as provided for in Articles 147 and 148 within two months of receipt of the opinion of the Disciplinary Board. Reasons must be given for the decision.

2. If the AACC decides to close the case without imposing any disciplinary penalty, it shall so inform the staff member concerned in writing without delay. The staff member concerned may request that this decision be inserted in his personal file.
