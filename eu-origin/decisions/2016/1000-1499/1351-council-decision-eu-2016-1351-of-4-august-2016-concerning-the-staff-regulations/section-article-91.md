# Council Decision (EU) 2016/1351 of 4 August 2016 concerning the Staff Regulations of the European Defence Agency, and repealing Decision 2004/676/EC, section Article 91

> Text as at 2016-08-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 91.** In accordance with conditions to be laid down by the Agency, a member of the temporary staff may request the Agency to effect any payments which he is required to make in order to constitute or maintain pension rights in his country of origin. The Agency may also decide to effect any payments which a member of the temporary staff is required to make in order to constitute or maintain pension rights in his country of origin, even in absence of request from such member of the temporary staff. In such case, the Agency must duly justify its decision.

Such payments shall not exceed twice the rate provided for in Article 90 and shall be charged to the budget of the Agency.
