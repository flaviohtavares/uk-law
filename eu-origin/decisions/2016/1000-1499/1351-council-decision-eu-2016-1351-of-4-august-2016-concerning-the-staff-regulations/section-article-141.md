# Council Decision (EU) 2016/1351 of 4 August 2016 concerning the Staff Regulations of the European Defence Agency, and repealing Decision 2004/676/EC, section Article 141

> Text as at 2016-08-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 141.** On the basis of the investigation report, after having notified the staff member concerned of all evidence in the files and after hearing the staff member concerned, the AACC may:

- ((a)) decide that no case can be made against the staff member, in which case he shall be informed accordingly in writing; or
- ((b)) decide, even if there is or appears to have been a failure to comply with obligations, that no disciplinary measure shall be taken and, if appropriate, address a warning to the staff member; or
- ((c)) in the case of failure to comply with obligations within the meaning of Article 139:
  - ((i)) decide to initiate the disciplinary proceedings provided for in Section D of this Title; or
  - ((ii)) decide to initiate disciplinary proceedings before the Disciplinary Board.
