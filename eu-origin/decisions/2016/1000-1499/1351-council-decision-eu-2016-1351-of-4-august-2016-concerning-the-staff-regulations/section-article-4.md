# Council Decision (EU) 2016/1351 of 4 August 2016 concerning the Staff Regulations of the European Defence Agency, and repealing Decision 2004/676/EC, section Article 4

> Text as at 2016-08-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 4.** With the exception of the Chief Executive and the Deputy Chief Executive of the Agency who are subject to the provisions of Article 10(1) of Decision (CFSP) 2015/1835, temporary staff shall not be engaged for more than four years, but their engagement may be limited to any shorter duration.

Their contracts may only be renewed once for a maximum period of four years. The Steering Board shall adopt general provisions for giving effect to this Article.
