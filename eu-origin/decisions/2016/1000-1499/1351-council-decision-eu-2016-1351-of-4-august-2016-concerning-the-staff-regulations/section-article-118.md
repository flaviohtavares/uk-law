# Council Decision (EU) 2016/1351 of 4 August 2016 concerning the Staff Regulations of the European Defence Agency, and repealing Decision 2004/676/EC, section Article 118

> Text as at 2016-08-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 118.** Gifts, loans or advances may be made to contract staff during the term of their contract or after expiry of the contract where, as a result of serious protracted illness contracted or a disability, or of an accident sustained, during his employment, the member of contract staff is incapable of working and proves that such illness or accident is not covered by another social security scheme.
