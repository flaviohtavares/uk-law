# Council Decision (EU) 2016/1351 of 4 August 2016 concerning the Staff Regulations of the European Defence Agency, and repealing Decision 2004/676/EC, section Article 47

> Text as at 2016-08-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 47.** A member of temporary staff may not be required to work overtime except in cases of urgency or exceptional pressure of work; night work and all work on Sundays or public holidays may be authorised only in accordance with the procedure laid down by the AACC. The total overtime which a member of temporary staff may be asked to work shall not exceed 150 hours in any six months.

Overtime worked by members of temporary staff in function group AD, and in function group AST 5 to 11 shall carry no right to compensation or remuneration.

As provided for in Annex III, overtime worked by members of temporary staff in grade AST 1 to AST 4 shall entitle them either to compensatory leave or to remuneration where the requirements of the service do not allow compensatory leave during the month following that in which the overtime was worked.
