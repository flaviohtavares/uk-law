# Council Decision (EU) 2016/1351 of 4 August 2016 concerning the Staff Regulations of the European Defence Agency, and repealing Decision 2004/676/EC, section Article 142

> Text as at 2016-08-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 142.** A staff member who, for objective reasons, cannot be heard under the provisions of this Title may be asked to comment in writing or may be represented by a person of his choice.
