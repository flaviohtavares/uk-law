# Council Decision (EU) 2016/1351 of 4 August 2016 concerning the Staff Regulations of the European Defence Agency, and repealing Decision 2004/676/EC, section Article 154

> Text as at 2016-08-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 154.** 1. The staff member concerned shall be heard by the Disciplinary Board; at the hearing, he may submit observations in writing or orally, whether in person or through a representative. He may call witnesses.

2. The Agency shall be represented before the Disciplinary Board by a staff member mandated by the AACC to this effect and having rights equivalent to those of the staff member concerned.
