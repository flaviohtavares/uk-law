# Council Decision (EU) 2016/1351 of 4 August 2016 concerning the Staff Regulations of the European Defence Agency, and repealing Decision 2004/676/EC, section Article 43

> Text as at 2016-08-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 43.** In the case of medically certified serious illness or disability of a member of temporary staff's spouse, relative in the ascending line, relative in the descending line, brother or sister, the member of temporary staff shall be entitled to a period of family leave without basic salary. The total period of such leave shall not exceed nine months over the member of temporary staff's entire career.

The second paragraph of Article 42 shall apply.
