# Council Decision (EU) 2016/1351 of 4 August 2016 concerning the Staff Regulations of the European Defence Agency, and repealing Decision 2004/676/EC, section Article 100

> Text as at 2016-08-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 100.** Without prejudice to Articles 98 and 99, any intentional or negligent failure by a member of the temporary staff or of a former member of the temporary staff to comply with his obligations under these Staff Regulations shall render him liable to disciplinary action in accordance with Title V.
