# Council Decision (EU) 2016/1351 of 4 August 2016 concerning the Staff Regulations of the European Defence Agency, and repealing Decision 2004/676/EC, section Article 115

> Text as at 2016-08-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 115.** Articles 68 to 70 shall apply by analogy. However, Article 68(4) and (5) shall not apply to a member of the contract staff who has remained in the service of the Agency until the age of 63, unless he has been employed for more than 3 years as a member of such staff.
