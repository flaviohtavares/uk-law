# Council Decision (EU) 2016/1351 of 4 August 2016 concerning the Staff Regulations of the European Defence Agency, and repealing Decision 2004/676/EC, section Article 5

> Text as at 2016-08-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 5.** Temporary staff shall not be engaged for any purpose other than that of filling, in accordance with these Staff Regulations, the vacant posts within the maximum number of temporary posts authorised in the budget of the Agency.
