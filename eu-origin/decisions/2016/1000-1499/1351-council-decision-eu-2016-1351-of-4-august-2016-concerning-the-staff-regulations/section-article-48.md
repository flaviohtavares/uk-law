# Council Decision (EU) 2016/1351 of 4 August 2016 concerning the Staff Regulations of the European Defence Agency, and repealing Decision 2004/676/EC, section Article 48

> Text as at 2016-08-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 48.** A member of temporary staff who is expected to work regularly at night, on Saturdays, Sundays or public holidays shall be entitled to special allowances when doing shiftwork which is required by the Agency because of the exigencies of the service or safety rules and which is regarded by it as a regular and permanent feature.

The AACC shall determine the categories of members of temporary staff entitled to such allowances, and the rates and conditions thereof.

The normal working hours of a member of temporary staff on shiftwork must not exceed the annual total of normal working hours.
