# Council Decision (EU) 2016/1351 of 4 August 2016 concerning the Staff Regulations of the European Defence Agency, and repealing Decision 2004/676/EC, section Article 101

> Text as at 2016-08-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 101.** For the purposes of these Staff Regulations, ‘contract staff’ means staff not assigned to a post included in the list of posts appended to the budget of the Agency and engaged for the performance of full-time or part-time duties.
