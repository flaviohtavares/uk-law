# Council Decision (EU) 2016/1351 of 4 August 2016 concerning the Staff Regulations of the European Defence Agency, and repealing Decision 2004/676/EC, section Article 2

> Text as at 2016-08-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 2.** A staff member whose contract is for more than one year shall be entitled to vote in elections and stand for election to the Staff Committee provided for in Article 138.

A staff member whose contract is for less than one year shall also be entitled to vote if he has been employed for at least six months.
