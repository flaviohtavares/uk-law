# Council Implementing Decision 2012/126/CFSP of 28 February 2012 implementing Decision 2010/639/CFSP concerning restrictive measures against Belarus

> Text as at 2012-02-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Council Implementing Decision 2012/126/CFSP

of 28 February 2012

implementing Decision 2010/639/CFSP concerning restrictive measures against Belarus

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on European Union, and in particular Article 31(2) thereof,

Having regard to Council Decision 2010/639/CFSP[^f00001], and in particular Article 4(1) thereof,

Whereas:

(1)

On 25 October 2010, the Council adopted Decision 2010/639/CFSP.

(2)

In view of the gravity of the situation in Belarus, certain persons should be included in Annex V to Decision 2010/639/CFSP.

(3)

The information relating to persons on the list in Annex IIIA to Decision 2010/639/CFSP should be updated.

(4)

Annexes IIIA and V to Council Decision 2010/639/CFSP should be amended accordingly,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

**Article 1.** In Annex IIIA to Decision 2010/639/CFSP, the entries for the following persons:

- 1.. Khadanevich, Aliaksandr Alyaksandrauvich
- 2.. Pykina, Natallia
- 3.. Saikouski, Uladzimir
- 4.. Chubkavets, Kiril
- 5.. Khrobastau, Uladzimir Ivanavich
- 6.. Shylko, Alena Mikalaeuna
- 7.. Stsiapurka, Uladzimir Mikhailavich

shall be replaced by the entries set out in Annex I to this Decision.

**Article 2.** The persons listed in Annex II to this Decision shall be added to Annex V to Decision 2010/639/CFSP.

**Article 3.** This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its adoption.

Done at Brussels, 28 February 2012.

*For the Council*

*The President*

N. Wammen
