# Council Decision of 24 February 2014 appointing a member of the Court of Auditors (2014/111/EU)

> Text as at 2014-02-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Council Decision

of 24 February 2014

appointing a member of the Court of Auditors

(2014/111/EU)

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, and in particular Article 286(2) thereof,

Having regard to the opinion of the European Parliament[^f00001],

Whereas:

(1)

The term of office of Mr Harald NOACK expires on 28 February 2014.

(2)

A new appointment should therefore be made,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

**Article 1.** Mr Klaus-Heiner LEHNE is hereby appointed member of the Court of Auditors for the period from 1 March 2014 to 29 February 2020.

**Article 2.** This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its adoption.

Done at Brussels, 24 February 2014.

*For the Council*

*The President*

K. Arvanitopoulos
