# Council Decision of 1 December 2014 concerning the notification of the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland of its wish to take part in some of the provisions of the Schengen acquis which are contained in acts of the Union in the field of police cooperation and judicial cooperation in criminal matters and amending Decisions 2000/365/EC and 2004/926/EC (2014/857/EU)

of 1 December 2014 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a]

concerning the notification of the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland of its wish to take part in some of the provisions of the Schengen *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a] *acquis *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a]* which are contained in acts of the Union in the field of police cooperation and judicial cooperation in criminal matters and amending Decisions 2000/365/EC and 2004/926/EC *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a]

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a]

Having regard to the Protocol No 36 on transitional provisions (hereinafter ‘Protocol No 36’), annexed to the Treaty on European Union, to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union and to the Treaty establishing the European Atomic Energy Community, and in particular to Article 10(5) thereof, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a]

Having regard to the notification, under Article 10(4) of Protocol No 36, by the Government of the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland (hereinafter ‘the United Kingdom’), by its letter to the President of the Council of 24 July 2013, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a]

Having regard to the notification, under Article 10(5) of Protocol No 36, by the Government of the United Kingdom, by its letter to the President of the Council and to the President of the Commission taking effect on 1 December 2014, of its wish to take part in some of the provisions of the Schengen *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a] *acquis *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a]* which are contained in acts of the Union in the field of police cooperation and judicial cooperation in criminal matters, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a]

Whereas: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a]

(1)

Article 10(4) of Protocol No 36 allowed the United Kingdom, at the latest six months before the expiry of the five year transitional period after the entry into force of the Treaty of Lisbon, to notify to the Council that it does not accept, with respect to acts of the Union in the field of police cooperation and judicial cooperation in criminal matters which had been adopted before the entry into force of the Treaty of Lisbon, the powers of the Commission and of the Court of Justice as referred to in Article 10(1) of Protocol No 36. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a]

(2)

By letter to the President of the Council of 24 July 2013, the United Kingdom made use of the above possibility by notifying the Council that it did not accept the said powers of the Commission and of the Court of Justice, with the consequence that the relevant acts in the field of police cooperation and judicial cooperation in criminal matters would cease to apply to the United Kingdom on 1 December 2014. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a]

(3)

Article 10(5) of Protocol No 36 allows the United Kingdom to notify the Council of its wish to participate in the above acts, including those acts which are part of the Schengen *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a] *acquis *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a]*, in which case the relevant provisions of the Schengen Protocol should apply. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a]

(4)

By letter to the President of the Council and to the President of the Commission taking effect on 1 December 2014, the United Kingdom made use of the above possibility by notifying the Council of its wish to participate in some of the provisions of the Schengen *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a] *acquis *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a]* which are contained in acts of the Union in the field of police cooperation and judicial cooperation in criminal matters to which it was already taking part in accordance with Council Decision 2000/365/EC *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a][^f00001], Council Decision 2004/926/EC *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a][^f00002] and Article 5(1) of the Schengen Protocol. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a]

(5)

It is therefore necessary to identify the acts and provisions in the field of police cooperation and judicial cooperation in criminal matters which are part of the Schengen *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a] *acquis *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a]* in which the United Kingdom will continue to participate following the above notification of its wish to do so and therefore to amend Decision 2000/365/EC and Decision 2004/926/EC. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a]

(6)

Decision 2000/365/EC and Decision 2004/926/EC, as amended, will thus continue to apply, in particular concerning those provisions of the Schengen *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a] *acquis *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a]* in which the United Kingdom was authorised to take part and which are not acts and provisions in the field of police cooperation and judicial cooperation in criminal matters as referred to in Article 10(1) of Protocol No 36. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a]

(7)

Likewise, the United Kingdom will continue to participate in those acts and provisions of the Schengen *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a] *acquis *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a]* for which it was authorised to take part, which although they are acts and provisions in the field of police cooperation and judicial cooperation in criminal matters adopted before the entry into force of the Treaty of Lisbon, have been amended by an act, applicable to the United Kingdom, adopted after that entry into force and which therefore fall under Article 10(2) of Protocol No 36. This is the case with regard to the Decision of the Executive Committee of 16 September 1998 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a][^f00003] on the Schengen Evaluation Mechanism, which was amended by Council Regulation (EU) No 1053/2013 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a][^f00004], and to Articles 48 to 53 of the 1990 Convention implementing the Schengen Agreement of 14 June 1985 as well as the Convention on Mutual Assistance in Criminal Matters between the Member States of the European Union *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a][^f00005] and its Protocol *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a][^f00006], which were amended by Directive 2014/41/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a][^f00007]. This is also the case with regard to the Agreement between the European Union, the European Community and the Swiss Confederation on the Swiss Confederation's association with the implementation, application and development of the Schengen *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a] *acquis *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a]*[^f00008], which was amended by the Protocol on the accession of the Principality of Liechtenstein to that Agreement *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a][^f00009] (hereinafter ‘the Agreement of 2008 on Switzerland's association with the Schengen *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a] *acquis *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a]*’).

(8)

As recalled in Decision 2000/365/EC, the United Kingdom has a special position in respect of matters covered by Title V of Part Three of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union (TFEU), as recognised in the Schengen Protocol, as well as in Protocol No 20 on the application of certain aspects of Article 26 of the TFEU to the United Kingdom and to Ireland (hereinafter ‘Protocol No 20’) and Protocol No 21 on the position of the United Kingdom and Ireland in respect of the area of Freedom, Security and Justice (hereinafter ‘Protocol No 21’), annexed to the Treaty on European Union and to the TFEU. Due to this special position, the Schengen Protocol provides for the possibility of the United Kingdom to participate in some of the provisions of the Schengen *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a] *acquis *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a]*.

(9)

In accordance with Article 10(5) of Protocol No 36, the participation of the United Kingdom in some of the provisions of the Schengen *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a] *acquis *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a]* which are contained in acts of the Union in the field of police cooperation and judicial cooperation in criminal matters as provided in this Decision re-establishes the widest possible measure of participation of the United Kingdom in the Schengen *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a] *acquis *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a]* of the Union in the area of freedom, security and justice without seriously affecting the practical operability of the various parts thereof, while respecting their coherence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a]

(10)

As recalled in Decision 2000/365/EC, the Schengen *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a] *acquis *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a]* was conceived and functions as a coherent ensemble which has to be fully accepted and applied by all States supporting the principle of the abolition of checks on persons at their common borders. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a]

(11)

The implementing power to adopt the decisions under Articles 5(1) and 6(1) of Decision 2000/365/EC, whereby the Schengen *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a] *acquis *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a]* will be applied to the Channel Islands and the Isle of Man and the relevant *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a] *acquis *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a]* on the Schengen Information System will be put into effect for the United Kingdom, should be conferred on the Council. This is in view of the specific role entrusted to the Council by the Schengen Protocol when it comes to unanimously agreeing on new participants into the Schengen *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a] *acquis *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a]*, but also in view of the high level of mutual trust between the Member States which is required when examining that the preconditions for the implementation of the provisions on the Schengen Information System have been fulfilled and when taking the subsequent decision by which those provisions will be put into effect for the United Kingdom. The Council should act with the unanimity of its members referred to in Article 1 of the Schengen Protocol and of the representative of the Government of the United Kingdom. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a]

(12)

In accordance with Article 2 of the Agreement concluded by the Council of the European Union and the Republic of Iceland and the Kingdom of Norway on the establishment of rights and obligations between Ireland and the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland, on the one hand, and the Republic of Iceland and the Kingdom of Norway, on the other, in areas of the Schengen *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a] *acquis *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a]* which apply to these States *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a][^f00010], the Mixed Committee established pursuant to Article 3 of the Agreement concluded by the Council of the European Union and the Republic of Iceland and the Kingdom of Norway concerning the latters' association with the implementation, application, and development of the Schengen *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a] *acquis *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a]*[^f00011] has been consulted in accordance with Article 4 thereof about the preparation of this Decision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a]

(13)

In accordance with Article 5 of the Agreement of 2008 on Switzerland's association with the Schengen *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a] *acquis *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a]*, the Mixed Committee established pursuant to Article 3 thereof has been informed about the preparation of this Decision, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-582e1626ba4d45ef0307e176a90b1a1a]
