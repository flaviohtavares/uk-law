# Council Decision (EU) 2020/2001 of 4 December 2020 appointing two members, proposed by the Portuguese Republic, of the Committee of the Regions

> Text as at 2020-12-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Council Decision (EU) 2020/2001

of 4 December 2020

appointing two members, proposed by the Portuguese Republic, of the Committee of the Regions

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, and in particular Article 305 thereof,

Having regard to the proposal of the Portuguese Government,

Whereas:

(1)

On 10 December 2019, 20 January 2020, 3 February 2020 and 26 March 2020 the Council adopted Decisions (EU) 2019/2157[^f00001], (EU) 2020/102[^f00002], (EU) 2020/144[^f00003] and (EU) 2020/511[^f00004] appointing the members and alternate members of the Committee of the Regions for the period from 26 January 2020 to 25 January 2025. On 8 June 2020, the Council adopted Decision (EU) 2020/766 appointing the members and alternate members of the Committee of the Regions for the period from 1 February 2020 to 25 January 2025[^f00005]. On 30 July 2020, the Council adopted a further Decision (EU) 2020/1153 appointing members and alternate members of the Committee of the Regions[^f00006].

(2)

A member’s seat on the Committee of the Regions has become vacant following the end of the term of office of Ms Cristina de Fátima SILVA CALISTO.

(3)

A member’s seat on the Committee of the Regions has become vacant following the end of the mandate on the basis of which Mr Vasco Ilídio ALVES CORDEIRO (Member of a Regional Executive: *Governo Regional dos Açores*) was proposed,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

**Article 1.** The following are hereby appointed as members to the Committee of the Regions for the remainder of the current term of office, which runs until 25 January 2025:

- Mr José Manuel Cabral Dias BOLIEIRO, Member of a Regional Executive: *President of the Regional Government of Azores*,
- Mr Vasco Ilídio Alves CORDEIRO, Member of a Regional Assembly: *Regional Parliament of Azores* (change of mandate).

**Article 2.** This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its adoption.

Done at Brussels, 4 December 2020.

*For the Council*

*The President*

M. Roth
