# Council Decision (EU) 2020/1601 of 28 October 2020 appointing an alternate member, proposed by the Republic of Estonia, of the Committee of the Regions

> Text as at 2020-10-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Council Decision (EU) 2020/1601

of 28 October 2020

appointing an alternate member, proposed by the Republic of Estonia, of the Committee of the Regions

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, and in particular Article 305 thereof,

Having regard to the proposal of the Estonian Government,

Whereas:

(1)

On 10 December 2019, 20 January 2020, 3 February 2020 and 26 March 2020 the Council adopted Decisions (EU) 2019/2157[^f00001], (EU) 2020/102[^f00002], (EU) 2020/144[^f00003] and (EU) 2020/511[^f00004] appointing the members and alternate members of the Committee of the Regions for the period from 26 January 2020 to 25 January 2025. On 8 June 2020, the Council adopted Decision (EU) 2020/766[^f00005] appointing the members and alternate members of the Committee of the Regions for the period from 1 February 2020 to 25 January 2025. On 30 July 2020, the Council adopted a further Decision (EU) 2020/1153[^f00006] appointing members and alternate members of the Committee of the Regions.

(2)

An alternate member’s seat on the Committee of the Regions has become vacant following the end of the term of office of Mr Aivar ARU,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

**Article 1.** The following is hereby appointed as an alternate member to the Committee of the Regions for the remainder of the current term of office, which runs until 25 January 2025:

- Mr Andres JAADLA, Representative of a local body with political accountability to an elected Assembly: *Rakvere City Council.*

**Article 2.** This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its adoption.

Done at Brussels, 28 October 2020.

*For the Council*

*The President*

M. Roth
