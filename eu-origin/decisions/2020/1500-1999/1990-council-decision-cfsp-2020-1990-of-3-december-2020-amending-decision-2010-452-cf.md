# Council Decision (CFSP) 2020/1990 of 3 December 2020 amending Decision 2010/452/CFSP on the European Union Monitoring Mission in Georgia, EUMM Georgia

> Text as at 2020-12-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Council Decision (CFSP) 2020/1990

of 3 December 2020

amending Decision 2010/452/CFSP on the European Union Monitoring Mission in Georgia, EUMM Georgia

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on European Union, and in particular Article 42(4) and Article 43(2) thereof,

Having regard to the proposal from the High Representative of the Union for Foreign Affairs and Security Policy,

Whereas:

(1)

On 12 August 2010, the Council adopted Decision 2010/452/CFSP[^f00001], which extended the European Union Monitoring Mission in Georgia (EUMM Georgia) established by Council Joint Action 2008/736/CFSP[^f00002].

(2)

On 3 December 2018, the Council adopted Decision (CFSP) 2018/1884[^f00003], extending EUMM Georgia until 14 December 2020.

(3)

Following a strategic review of the Mission, the Political and Security Committee recommended that the mandate of EUMM Georgia be extended until 14 December 2022.

(4)

Decision 2010/452/CFSP should therefore be amended accordingly.

(5)

The Mission will be conducted in the context of a situation which may deteriorate and could impede the achievement of the objectives of the Union’s external action as set out in Article 21 of the Treaty,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

**Article 1.** Decision 2010/452/CFSP is amended as follows:

- ((1)) in Article 14(1), the following subparagraph is added:

  > The financial reference amount intended to cover the expenditure related to the Mission between 15 December 2020 and 14 December 2022 shall be EUR 44 823 402,79.

  ;
- ((2)) in Article 18, the second paragraph is replaced by the following:

  > It shall expire on 14 December 2022.

  .

**Article 2.** This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its adoption.

It shall apply from 15 December 2020.

Done at Brussels, 3 December 2020.

*For the Council*

*The President*

M. Roth
