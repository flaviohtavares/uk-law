# Council Decision of 9 October 2009 appointing one Lithuanian member and one Lithuanian alternate member of the Committee of the Regions (2009/749/EC)

> Text as at 2009-10-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Council Decision

of 9 October 2009

appointing one Lithuanian member and one Lithuanian alternate member of the Committee of the Regions

(2009/749/EC)

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty establishing the European Community, and in particular Article 263 thereof,

Having regard to the proposal of the Lithuanian Government,

Whereas:

(1)

On 24 January 2006, the Council adopted Decision 2006/116/EC appointing the members and alternate members of the Committee of the Regions for the period from 26 January 2006 to 25 January 2010[^f00001].

(2)

A member’s seat on the Committee of the Regions has become vacant following the end of the term of office of Mr Vitas MATUZAS. An alternate member’s seat has become vacant following the end of the term of office of Mr Alvydas ŠEDŽIUS,

HAS DECIDED AS FOLLOWS:

**Article 1.** The following are hereby appointed to the Committee of the Regions for the remainder of the current term of office, which runs until 25 January 2010:

- ((a)) as member:
  - Mr Viktor TROFIMOV, Chairman of Panevėžys Regional Development Council;
- ((b)) as alternate member:
  - Ms Daiva MATONIENĖ, Council Member of Šiauliai City Municipality.

**Article 2.** This Decision shall take effect on the day of its adoption.

Done at Luxembourg, 9 October 2009.

*For the Council*

*The President*

Å. Torstensson
