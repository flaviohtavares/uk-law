# Council Decision of 16 March 2009 appointing the Chairperson of the European Statistical Governance Advisory Board (2009/249/EC)

> Text as at 2009-03-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Council Decision

of 16 March 2009

appointing the Chairperson of the European Statistical Governance Advisory Board

(2009/249/EC)

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to Decision No 235/2008/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 11 March 2008 establishing the European Statistical Governance Advisory Board[^f00001], and in particular Article 3 thereof,

Having regard to the opinion of the Commission,

Having regard to the approval of the European Parliament,

HAS DECIDED AS FOLLOWS:

**Article 1.** Mr Johnny ÅKERHOLM is hereby appointed Chairperson of the European Statistical Governance Advisory Board for a period of three years.

**Article 2.** This Decision shall take effect on the third day following its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Union*.

Done at Brussels, 16 March 2009.

*For the Council*

*The President*

A. Vondra
