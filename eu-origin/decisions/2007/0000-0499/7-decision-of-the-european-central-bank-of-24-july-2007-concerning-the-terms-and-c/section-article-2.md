# Decision of the European Central Bank of 24 July 2007 concerning the terms and conditions of TARGET2-ECB (ECB/2007/7) (2007/601/EC), section Article 2

> Text as at 2019-11-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Article 2. Intraday credit

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1. The ECB may only grant intraday credit to customers that are European or international organisations through overdrafts on such customers' account(s). Overdrafts shall not at any time during the day exceed the amount defined in the agreement with the customer for each of the accounts. Intraday credit granted by the ECB shall remain limited to the day in question, with no possibility of an extension to overnight credit.

2.[^c000001] Any granting of intraday credit by the ECB shall be done in accordance with the rules on the provision of intraday credit laid down in Annex III to Guideline ECB/2012/27 [^f00003] .
