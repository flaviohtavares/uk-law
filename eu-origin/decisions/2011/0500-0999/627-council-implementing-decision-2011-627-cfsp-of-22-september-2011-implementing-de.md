# Council Implementing Decision 2011/627/CFSP of 22 September 2011 implementing Decision 2010/656/CFSP renewing the restrictive measures against Côte d’Ivoire

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2011-09-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/eudn/2011/627
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Council Implementing Decision 2011/627/CFSP

of 22 September 2011

implementing Decision 2010/656/CFSP renewing the restrictive measures against Côte d’Ivoire

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to Council Decision 2010/656/CFSP of 29 October 2010 renewing the restrictive measures against Côte d’Ivoire[^f00001], and in particular Article 6(2) thereof, in conjunction with Article 31(2) of the Treaty on European Union,

Whereas:

(1)

On 29 October 2010, the Council adopted Decision 2010/656/CFSP.

(2)

In view of the developments in Côte d’Ivoire, the list of persons and entities subject to restrictive measures set out in Annex II to Decision 2010/656/CFSP should be amended,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

**Article 1.** The persons listed in the Annex to this Decision shall be deleted from the list set out in Annex II to Decision 2010/656/CFSP.

**Article 2.** This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its adoption.

Done at Brussels, 22 September 2011.

*For the Council*

*The President*

M. Dowgielewicz
