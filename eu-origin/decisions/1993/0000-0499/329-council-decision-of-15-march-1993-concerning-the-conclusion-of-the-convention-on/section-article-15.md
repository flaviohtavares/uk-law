# Council Decision of 15 March 1993 concerning the conclusion of the Convention on Temporary Admission and accepting its annexes (93/329/EEC), section Article 15

> Text as at 1993-05-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 15.** Each Contracting Party shall reduce to a minimum the customs formalities required in connection with the facilities provided for in this Convention. All regulations concerning such formalities shall be promptly published.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 15.** Where Article 7 (3) of this Convention applies, the customs authorities shall, as far as possible, notify the guaranteeing association of seizures made by them or on their behalf of goods (including means of transport) admitted under cover of temporary admission papers guaranteed by that association and shall advise it of the measures they intend to take.
