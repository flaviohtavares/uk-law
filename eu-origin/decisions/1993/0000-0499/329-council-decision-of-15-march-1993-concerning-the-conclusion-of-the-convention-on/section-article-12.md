# Council Decision of 15 March 1993 concerning the conclusion of the Convention on Temporary Admission and accepting its annexes (93/329/EEC), section Article 12

> Text as at 1993-05-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 12.** Temporary admission may be terminated with the agreement of the competent authorities, by placing the goods (including means of transport) in a free port or free zone, in a customs warehouse or under a customs transit procedure with a view to their subsequent exportation or other authorized disposal.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 12.** Customs endorsements on temporary admission papers used under the conditions laid down in this Annex shall not be subject to the payment of charges for customs attendance at customs offices during the normal hours of business.
