# Council Decision of 15 March 1993 concerning the conclusion of the Convention on Temporary Admission and accepting its annexes (93/329/EEC), section Article 20

> Text as at 1993-05-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 20.** 1. Any breach of the provisions of this Convention shall render the offender liable in the territory of the Contracting Party where the offence was committed to the penalties prescribed by the legislation of that Contracting Party.

2. When it is not possible to establish in which territory an irregularity occurred, it shall be deemed to have been committed in the territory of the Contracting Party where it is detected.
