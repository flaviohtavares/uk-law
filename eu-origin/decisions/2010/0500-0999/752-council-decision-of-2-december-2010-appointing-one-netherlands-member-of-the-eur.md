# Council Decision of 2 December 2010 appointing one Netherlands member of the European Economic and Social Committee (2010/752/EU)

> Text as at 2010-12-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Council Decision

of 2 December 2010

appointing one Netherlands member of the European Economic and Social Committee

(2010/752/EU)

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, and in particular Article 302 thereof,

Having regard to the proposal of the Netherlands government,

Having regard to the opinion of the European Commission,

Whereas:

(1)

On 13 September 2010, the Council adopted Decision 2010/570/EU appointing the members of the European Economic and Social Committee for the period from 21 September 2010 to 20 September 2015[^f00001].

(2)

A member’s seat on the European Economic and Social Committee has become vacant following the end of the term of office of Ms Melanie BOUWKNEGT,

HAS DECIDED AS FOLLOWS:

**Article 1.** Mr Leon MEIJER, *senior beleidsadviseur Europese Zaken*, is hereby appointed as a member of the European Economic and Social Committee for the remainder of the current term of office, which is until 20 September 2015.

**Article 2.** This Decision shall take effect on the day of its adoption.

Done at Brussels, 2 December 2010.

*For the Council*

*The President*

M. Wathelet
