# Council Decision of 26 April 2010 appointing nine members of the Court of Auditors (2010/246/EU)

> Text as at 2010-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Council Decision

of 26 April 2010

appointing nine members of the Court of Auditors

(2010/246/EU)

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, and in particular Article 286(2) thereof,

Having regard to the opinions of the European Parliament[^f00001],

Whereas:

(1)

The terms of office of Mr Jan KINŠT, Ms Kersti KALJULAID, Mr Igors LUDBORŽS, Ms Irena PETRUŠKEVIČIENĖ, Mr Gejza HALÁSZ, Mr Josef BONNICI, Mr Jacek UCZKIEWICZ, Mr Vojko Anton ANTONČIČ and Mr Július MOLNÁR expire on 6 May 2010.

(2)

New appointments should therefore be made,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

**Sole Article.** The following are hereby appointed members of the Court of Auditors for the period from 7 May 2010 to 6 May 2016:

- Mr Jan KINŠT,
- Ms Kersti KALJULAID,
- Mr Igors LUDBORŽS,
- Ms Rasa BUDBERGYTĖ,
- Mr Szabolcs FAZAKAS,
- Mr Louis GALEA,
- Mr Augustyn KUBIK,
- Mr Milan Martin CVIKL,
- Mr Ladislav BALKO.

Done at Luxembourg, 26 April 2010.

*For the Council*

*The President*

M. Á. Moratinos
