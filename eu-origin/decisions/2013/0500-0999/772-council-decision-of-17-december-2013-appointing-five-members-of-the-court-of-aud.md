# Council Decision of 17 December 2013 appointing five members of the Court of Auditors (2013/772/EU)

> Text as at 2013-12-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Council Decision

of 17 December 2013

appointing five members of the Court of Auditors

(2013/772/EU)

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, and in particular Article 286(2) thereof,

Having regard to the opinion of the European Parliament[^f00001],

Whereas:

(1)

The terms of office of Mr Gijs DE VRIES, Mr Henri GRETHEN, Mr Michel CRETIN, Mr Ioannis SARMAS and Mr David BOSTOCK are due to expire on 31 December 2013.

(2)

New appointments should therefore be made,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

**Article 1.** The following are hereby appointed members of the Court of Auditors for the period from 1 January 2014 to 31 December 2019:

- Mr Alex BRENNINKMEIJER,
- Mr Henri GRETHEN,
- Ms Danièle LAMARQUE,
- Mr Nikolaos MILIONIS,
- Mr Phil WYNN OWEN.

**Article 2.** This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its adoption.

Done at Brussels, 17 December 2013.

*For the Council*

*The President*

L. Linkevičius
