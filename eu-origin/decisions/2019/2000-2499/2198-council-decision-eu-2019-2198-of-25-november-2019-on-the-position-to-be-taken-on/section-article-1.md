# Council Decision (EU) 2019/2198 of 25 November 2019 on the position to be taken on behalf of the European Union within the Joint Committee established by the Regional Convention on pan-Euro-Mediterranean preferential rules of origin as regards the amendment of the Convention, section Article 1

> Text as at 2019-11-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 1.** The position to be taken on the Union’s behalf within the Joint Committee established by the Regional Convention on pan-Euro-Mediterranean preferential rules of origin (the ‘Convention’) shall be to support the amendment of the Convention as set out in the Annex to the draft Decision of the Joint Committee attached to this Decision.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 1.** 1. The Convention is amended as set out in the Annex to this Decision.

2. The amendments to the Convention shall become applicable on \[1 January 2021\] among the Contracting Parties that introduce those amendments to the Convention, or references to them, in their protocols on rules of origin before that date.
