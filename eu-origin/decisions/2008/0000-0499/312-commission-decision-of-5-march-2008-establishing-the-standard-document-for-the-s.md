# Commission Decision of 5 March 2008 establishing the standard document for the supervision and control of shipments of radioactive waste and spent fuel referred to in Council Directive 2006/117/Euratom (notified under document number C(2008) 793) (2008/312/Euratom)

of 5 March 2008 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]

establishing the standard document for the supervision and control of shipments of radioactive waste and spent fuel referred to in Council Directive 2006/117/Euratom *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]

THE COMMISSION OF THE EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]

Having regard to the Treaty establishing the European Atomic Energy Community, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]

Having regard to Council Directive 2006/117/Euratom of 20 November 2006 on the supervision and control of shipments of radioactive waste and spent fuel *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c][^f00001], and in particular Article 17(2) thereof, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]

After having obtained the opinion of the Advisory Committee established in accordance with the procedure laid down in Article 21, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]

Whereas: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]

(1)

The Commission is required to establish a new standard document to be used for the shipments of radioactive waste and spent fuel within the scope of Directive 2006/117/Euratom. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]

(2)

The new standard document is required to apply to shipments of radioactive waste and spent fuel between Member States, as well as to imports into and exports out of the Community of such radioactive waste/spent fuel and to its transit through the Community from a third country to another third country. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]

(3)

The measures provided for in this Decision are in line with the opinion of the Advisory Committee established in accordance with the procedure laid down in Article 21, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]

**Article 1 (affected by repeal, see note).**[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c] The standard document set out in the Annex shall be used in respect of any shipments of radioactive waste or spent fuel between Member States or into, out of and through the Community within the scope of Directive 2006/117/Euratom. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]

**Article 2 (affected by repeal, see note).**[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c] The standard document shall be made available in electronic form, in the format presented by the Commission. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]

**Article 3 (affected by repeal, see note).**[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c] Member States shall take the measures necessary to comply with this Decision not later than 25 December 2008. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]

**Article 4 (affected by repeal, see note).**[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c] Commission Decision 93/552/Euratom *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c][^f00002] shall be repealed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]

**Article 5 (affected by repeal, see note).**[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c] This Decision is addressed to the Member States. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]

## ANNEX (affected by repeal, see note) — Standard document for the supervision and control of shipments of radioactive waste and spent fuel (affected by repeal, see note) — (Council Directive 2006/117/Euratom) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c][^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c][^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]

General notes *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]

Sections A-1 to A-6: to be completed for shipments of radioactive waste. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]

Sections B-1 to B-6: to be completed for shipments of spent fuel (including spent fuel destined for final disposal and as such categorised as waste) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]

**Section A-1 or B-1 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]** (Application for authorisation of shipments): to be completed by the applicant, this is the following, depending on the type of the shipment: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]

- the holder in case of a shipment between Member States (type MM) or an export from the Community into a third country (type ME), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]
- the consignee in case of an import into the Community from a third country (type IM), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]
- the person responsible for the shipment in the Member State by which the radioactive waste or spent fuel enters the Community in case of a transit through the Community (type TT). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]

**Section A-2 or B-2 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]** (Acknowledgement of receipt of application): to be completed by the relevant competent authorities concerned, which are — depending on the type of the shipment — the competent authorities: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]

- of origin in case of a shipment type MM or ME, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]
- of destination in case of a shipment type IM, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]
- where the shipment first enters the Community in case of a shipment type TT, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]

and all competent authorities of the Member States of transit, if any. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]

**Section A-3 or B-3 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]** (Refusal or consent): to be completed by all competent authorities concerned. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]

**Section A-4a/A-4b or B-4a/B-4b *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]** (Authorisation or refusal of shipment): to be completed by the competent authority responsible for issuing the authorisation, which is — depending on the type of the shipment — the competent authority of: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]

- the Member State of origin in case of shipments type MM and ME shipments, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]
- the Member State of destination in case of type IM shipments, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]
- the first Member State of transit, where the shipment enters the Community in case of type TT shipments. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]

**Section A-5 or B-5 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]** (Description of consignment/List of packages): to be completed by the applicant as referred to in Section A-1 or B-1. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]

**Section A-6 or B-6 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]** (Acknowledgement of receipt of shipment): to be completed by the consignee (in case of MM and IM shipments) or the holder (in case of ME shipments) or the person responsible for the shipment (in case of TT shipments). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]

Explanatory notes to each item of the standard document Sections A-1 to A-6 and B-1 to B-6: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]

Definition of a duly completed application: an application for the authorisation of a shipment of radioactive waste or spent fuel is duly completed in accordance with Directive 2006/117/Euratom, if – in case of shipments of radioactive waste – each item of Section A-1, or – in case of shipments of spent fuel – each item of Section B-1, contains the required information, either by ticking the relevant box, deleting (= crossing out) the non-applicable option or filling in the relevant data and values. In case of an application for several shipments, items 8 and 9 may contain estimates. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]

- 1.. The applicant must duly complete all items 1 to 14. In item 1 tick one of the boxes as applicable to define the type of shipment and fill in the relevant frontier posts in case third countries are concerned in the shipment: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]
  - ((a)) tick type MM for shipments between Member States, eventually passing through one or more other Member States or third countries; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]
  - ((b)) tick type IM for shipments from a third country to a Member State (= import into the Community), bearing in mind, that the application must include evidence that the consignee has made an arrangement with the holder established in the third country, and which has been accepted by the competent authority of that third country, obliging that holder to take back the radioactive waste or spent fuel where a shipment cannot or may not be completed; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]
  - ((c)) tick type ME for shipments from a Member State to a third Country (= export from the Community); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]
  - ((d)) tick type TT for shipments from one third country to another third country passing one or more Member States, bearing in mind that the application must include evidence that the consignee established in the third country has made an arrangement with the holder established in the third country, and accepted by the competent authority of that third country, obliging that holder to take back radioactive waste or spent fuel where a shipment cannot or may not be completed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]
- 2.. By ticking the relevant box, the applicant must state clearly whether the application covers only one shipment within a period of time (e.g. 05/2010, 2009 or 2010-2011) or whether the application covers more than one shipment within a period of time, but not more than a period of three years from the date of authorisation. A single application may be sent in respect of more than one shipment, provided the following conditions are met as set out in Article 6(2) of the Directive 2006/117/Euratom: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]
  - ((a)) the radioactive waste or spent fuel to which it relates essentially has the same physical, chemical and radioactive characteristics; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]
  - ((b)) the shipments are to be made from the same holder to the same consignee and involve the same competent authorities; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]
  - ((c)) where the shipments involve transit through third countries, such transit is made via the same frontier post of entry to and/or exit from the Community and via the same frontier post(s) of the third country or countries concerned, unless otherwise agreed between the competent authorities concerned. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]
- 3.. The applicant must fill in the relevant frontier posts in case one or more third countries are concerned in the shipment. These frontier posts must be identical for all shipments covered by the application unless otherwise agreed by the competent authorities. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]
- 4.. The applicant must fill in his/her trade name, address and contact details. The trade name, also known as a trading name or a business name, is the name which a business trades under for commercial purposes, although its registered, legal name, used for contracts and other formal situations, may be another. The applicant must tick the appropriate box to define his/her function, which is the following, depending on the type of shipment: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]
  - ((a)) the holder in case of a shipment between Member States (type MM) or an export from the Community into a third country (type ME); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]
  - ((b)) the consignee in case of an import into the Community from a third country (type IM); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]
  - ((c)) the person responsible for the shipment in the Member State by which the radioactive waste or spent fuel enters the Community in case of a transit through the Community (type TT). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]
- 5.. The applicant must fill in the trade name, address and contact details of the location, where the radioactive waste or spent fuel is held before the shipment, which can be different from the address of the applicant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]
- 6.. The applicant must fill in the trade name, address and contact details of the consignee. In case of shipment type IM, this information is identical with item 4. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]
- 7.. The applicant must fill in the trade name, address and contact details of the location, where the radioactive waste or spent fuel will be held after the shipment, which can be different from the address of the consignee. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]
- 8.. The applicant must complete all fields either by ticking the appropriate box (more than one answer is possible) or filling in the specific characteristics and values of the radioactive waste or spent fuel. These values may be estimates in case of several shipments. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]
- 9.. The applicant must complete item 9, the values may be estimates. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]
- 10.. The applicant must tick and define the type of activity giving rise to the radioactive waste or spent fuel and tick the appropriate box(es) or specify any other activity. More than one answer is possible. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]
- 11.. The applicant must define the purpose of the shipment and tick the appropriate box (only one answer is possible) or specify any other purpose. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]
- 12.. The applicant must list up, as planned, the different modes of transport foreseen for the shipment (road, rail, sea, air, inland waterway) and add accordingly the relevant point of departure, point of arrival and the planned carrier (if already known). Changes of this schedule at a later point during the application procedure are possible and should be notified to the competent authorities but do not require a new application for approval. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]
- 13.. The applicant must list up all countries concerned in the shipment, starting with the first Member State or third country where the radioactive waste or spent fuel is held and ending with the last Member State or third country, where it will be held after the completion of the shipment. Should the applicant whish to change the sequential list of countries, a new application is required. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]
- 14.. The applicant must declare who takes back the radioactive waste or spent fuel in case the shipment(s) cannot take place or if the conditions for shipment cannot be fulfilled. Where the shipment is of type IM or TT, the applicant must attach to the application evidence of an arrangement between the consignee in the Member State or third country of destination and the holder of the radioactive waste or spent fuel in the third country which has been approved by the competent authorities of the third country. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]

  Having completed items 1 to 14 the applicant must send the Section 1 of the standard document to the competent authority, which is responsible for issuing the authorisation for the shipment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]

  The competent authority responsible for issuing the shipment authorisation or refusal is the following, depending on the type of the shipment is: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]

  - the competent authority of the Member State of origin in case of shipments between Member States (type MM) and exports out of the Community (type ME), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]
  - the competent authority of the Member State of destination in case of an import into the Community (type IM), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]
  - the competent authority of the first Member State of transit, where the shipment enters the Community in case of a transit through the Community (type TT). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]

    The relevant contact data can be retrieved from the electronic communication platform established and maintained by the Commission or from the published list of competent authorities. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]
- 15.. Immediately upon receipt of the application, the competent authority responsible for issuing the authorisation for the shipment must: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]
  - ((a)) enter the registration number at the top of each section of the standard document, starting with Section 1; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]
  - ((b)) verify that all items of Section 1 have been duly completed by the applicant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]
  - ((c)) complete item 15 of Section 2 and make sufficient copies of Sections 1, 2 and 3 for each Member State or Country concerned. Third countries of transit are consulted for information only. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]
- 16.. The competent authority responsible for the authorisation must: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]
  - ((a)) complete, as appropriate, item 16 of Section 2 (and 18 of Section 3) for each competent authority of the Member States or Countries concerned as listed in item 13, whose consent is required for the shipment(s) to be authorised; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]
  - ((b)) send the duly completed application (Section 1) together with Section 2 – without delay – for consent to each competent authority concerned named in item 16. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]
- 17.. Item 17 to be completed by the competent authority of the Member State(s) concerned. The date of the application and of receipt must be entered immediately upon receipt of the application. Within 20 days after the date of receipt the competent authority of the Member States concerned must verify, if the application is duly completed (all items 1 to 14 must be completed and no information may be missing; some values may be estimates). Only 17(a) or 17(b) can apply, please delete as applicable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f5b078c8d1846122c9defef9bfd01b5c]
  - ((a)) If the competent authority of the Member State(s) of transit, if any, or of destination consider the application not duly completed, they must complete item 17(a), delete item 17(b) and notify their request for missing information to the competent authority responsible for issuing the authorisation (referred to as in item 15). The competent authority requesting the missing information must send copies of Section 2 to all other competent authorities of the Member States concerned referred to as in item 13 within a period of 20 days from the date of receipt of the application.
