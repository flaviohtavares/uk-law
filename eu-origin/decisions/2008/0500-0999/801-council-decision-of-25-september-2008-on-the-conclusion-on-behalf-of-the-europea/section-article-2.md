# Council Decision of 25 September 2008 on the conclusion, on behalf of the European Community, of the United Nations Convention against Corruption (2008/801/EC), section Article 2

> Text as at 2008-09-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Article 2.** The President of the Council is hereby authorised to designate the person(s) empowered to deposit the Community's instrument of formal confirmation in order to bind the Community. The instrument of formal confirmation shall comprise a declaration of competence according to Article 67(3) of the Convention, as set out in Annex II to this Decision. It shall also contain a statement as set out in Annex III to this Decision.
