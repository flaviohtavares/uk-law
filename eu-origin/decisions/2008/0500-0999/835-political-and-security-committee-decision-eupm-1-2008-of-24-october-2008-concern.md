# Political and Security Committee Decision EUPM/1/2008 of 24 October 2008 concerning the appointment of the Head of Mission/Police Commissioner of the European Union Police Mission (EUPM) in Bosnia and Herzegovina (2008/835/CFSP)

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2008-10-24. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/eudn/2008/835
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Political and Security Committee Decision EUPM/1/2008

of 24 October 2008

concerning the appointment of the Head of Mission/Police Commissioner of the European Union Police Mission (EUPM) in Bosnia and Herzegovina

(2008/835/CFSP)

THE POLITICAL AND SECURITY COMMITTEE,

Having regard to the Treaty on European Union and in particular the third paragraph of Article 25 thereof,

Having regard to Joint Action 2007/749/CFSP of 19 November 2007 on the European Union Police Mission (EUPM) in Bosnia and Herzegovina (BiH)[^f00001], and in particular Article 10(1) thereof,

Whereas:

(1)

Under Article 10(1) of Joint Action 2007/749/CFSP, the Political and Security Committee (PSC) is authorised, in accordance with Article 25 of the Treaty, to take the relevant decisions, including the decision to appoint a Head of Mission/Police Commissioner.

(2)

The Secretary-General/High Representative has proposed to the PSC that Mr Stefan FELLER should be appointed as Head of Mission of the EUPM,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

**Article 1.** Mr Stefan FELLER is hereby appointed Head of Mission/Police Commissioner of the European Union Police Mission (EUPM) in Bosnia and Herzegovina with effect from 1 November 2008.

**Article 2.** This Decision shall take effect on the day of its adoption.

It shall apply until 31 December 2009.

Done at Brussels, 24 October 2008.

*For the Political and Security Committee*

*The Chairman*

C. Roger
